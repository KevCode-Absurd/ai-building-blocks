# The questions

Ask all of these in one message, before any code. Suggest an answer for each from the skills and workflows you found. Skip any the files already answer.

1. **The job.** In one sentence, what does the app get done? (Example: "Turn our reviews into five video scripts a week.")
2. **The skills.** Which skills or workflows does it run, and in what order?
3. **What goes in.** What does a person give it each time? (A link, a file, a few choices, some text.)
4. **What comes out.** What do they get back, and where does it go next? (On screen, a download, a shared folder.)
5. **Who uses it.** Just me, a few people I trust, or a team with different jobs? Who must approve work before it's used?
6. **Where it runs.** On my computer for now, or online so others can reach it?
7. **Data it needs.** Does it read from other tools (ad accounts, a store, a sheet, a drive)? Read only, or does it ever change them?
8. **How often.** When someone presses a button, or on a schedule too (every Monday at 9)?
9. **Spend.** Which AI models, and the most it may spend a month? (Start at 0 in mock mode.)
10. **Done means.** What would make you say "this works"? (Example: "My editor makes five scripts without asking me anything.")
