# General-business mode

The book addresses startups broadly. These skills default to SaaS as requested; the general mode is a maintained adaptation, not a claim that the book concerns only software.

Identify the real product, service, geography, buying process and capacity. Replace trial activation with a meaningful customer action such as a qualified enquiry, completed consultation, first order or successful first use. Keep enquiries separate from purchases. Replace recurring-revenue assumptions with order-level contribution after fulfilment, returns, commissions and support. Account for reorder cycles, delivery capacity and working capital. Do not force an app, subscription or enterprise sales process onto a local business.

Keep the same 19-channel exploration, evidence ledger, small tests and focus decision. Include a plausible idea for every channel before ranking; a low fit score should have a business-specific reason rather than an automatic software-versus-offline rule.
