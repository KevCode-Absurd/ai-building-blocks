## Validation checklist

Before marking this DNA document complete:

- [ ] Every hex code is a real, tested color
- [ ] Every typography family is owned or correctly licensed
- [ ] Every rule in Section 9 has been broken by a past ad that underperformed (evidence-based)
- [ ] Every claim in Section 8c is vetted with legal/compliance
- [ ] Section 3c phrases came from real customer or founder language, not generic marketing
- [ ] Section 6c proof points are specific and independently verifiable
- [ ] Logo files (section 5a) are actually accessible by the team

---

## When to refresh

Refresh this document when:

- Rebrand or visual identity change (major)
- New product line launch (material)
- Customer persona shift (minor-to-major)
- Regulatory change in category (mandatory)
- 90 days passed with no refresh (calendar)

Incremental updates go in the Refresh Log. Full rewrites get a new document with a new date.
