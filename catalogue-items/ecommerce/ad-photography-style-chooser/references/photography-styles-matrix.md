# Photography Style Matrix

Shared reference. Match style to context fast.

---

## The 12 Styles

| # | Style | Best For | Budget Range |
|---|-------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | Studio product | Features, ecom standard | Low |
| 2 | Flat lay / overhead | Lifestyle adjacency | Low |
| 3 | Macro close-up | Texture, craftsmanship | Medium |
| 4 | Documentary lifestyle | Skeptical audiences | Medium |
| 5 | Editorial magazine | Premium brands | High |
| 6 | UGC / iPhone | Cold prospecting | Very low |
| 7 | Split comparison | Before/after claims | Medium |
| 8 | Infographic hybrid | Features-heavy claims | Medium |
| 9 | Meme / graphic | Cultural moments | Very low |
| 10 | Fashion editorial | Apparel | Very high |
| 11 | Food photography | Food/bev | High |
| 12 | Interior photography | Home goods | High |

## Style by Funnel Position

| Funnel | Primary | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Cold prospecting | Documentary or UGC | Authenticity wins new trust |
| Warm retargeting | Split comparison or Infographic | Evidence for the hesitant |
| Bottom-funnel | Studio or Editorial | Final polish for buyers |

## Platform Fit

| Platform | Works Well | Works Poorly |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Meta feed (1:1) | Studio, Documentary, Editorial | UGC vertical (wrong ratio) |
| Meta Reels / IG Stories (9:16) | UGC, Documentary | Editorial (feels stiff vertical) |
| TikTok | UGC, Meme | Editorial, Studio |
| YouTube pre-roll | Editorial, Documentary | UGC (looks amateur on TV) |
| Google Display | Studio, Infographic | UGC |
| Programmatic CTV | Editorial, Documentary | UGC, Meme |

## Mixing Rules

1. Never mix more than 2 styles per campaign. Confuses brand.
2. If you test editorial + UGC, run them in separate ad sets, not same set.
3. UGC and documentary can coexist if talent is consistent. Editorial and UGC cannot.
4. Split comparison and Infographic are formats, not styles. They can sit over any other style.

## Migration Rules

When a brand outgrows UGC (brand maturity shift):
- Phase 1: Documentary (keeps authenticity, raises production)
- Phase 2: Editorial (locks premium positioning)

Don't jump from UGC to Editorial directly. Audience perceives it as selling out.
