## Photography Style Spec

**Product:** VitaReds Pomegranate Greens
**Brand positioning:** Premium-but-accessible. Skeptic-friendly.
**Audience:** Female, 32-48, wellness-literate, anti-hype
**Platform:** Meta feed + Reels

### Primary Style: Documentary lifestyle

Why: The audience rejects polished studio work. They respond to candid, real-people-in-real-kitchens photography. Documentary lifestyle signals "this is a customer, not a model" and that is the brand's core positioning asset.

Reference brands doing this well:
- Athletic Greens (2022-2023 era, before overproduction)
- Ritual vitamins
- Seed Probiotics (early creative)

### Secondary Style (for variation testing): Editorial magazine

Why: Test whether the audience responds to a more elevated aesthetic. Some portion of the 40+ segment may prefer polish over authenticity. Run 20% of spend here to see.

Reference: Saie Beauty, Necessaire

### Avoid: UGC/iPhone aesthetic

Why: Low-fidelity vertical UGC worked for this category in 2021-2023 but is now heavily saturated. The audience pattern-recognizes it as "ad". Documentary lifestyle gives the authenticity of UGC with slightly higher production that reads as trustworthy rather than manipulative.

[ANNOTATION: This is a case where UGC is the obvious choice but wrong for this brand. Audience fatigue matters.]

### Execution Notes

- **Lighting:** Natural morning light preferred. Soft diffuse, never direct overhead or harsh. Avoid fluorescent.
- **Lens feel:** 50mm equivalent. Slight shallow depth of field but not extreme. No portrait-mode blur.
- **Color grading:** Warm neutral. Push cream tones slightly orange. Avoid teal-and-orange grading (feels manipulated).
- **Talent:** Real customers when possible. If models, they must be age 32-48 and not model-type. Hair slightly undone. Makeup minimal.
- **Props:** Kitchen counter, linen napkin, glass of water, ceramic mug, the product sachet clearly visible. Never stage fruit that isn't pomegranate.
- **Clothing:** Neutrals, linens, cotton. No branded athleisure.
- **Location:** Real kitchens, real bathrooms, real living rooms. Not rented studios dressed as homes.

## Production Budget Implications

Documentary lifestyle is cheaper than editorial but more expensive than UGC:
- Real customer shoot: $800-1,500 per customer (day rate + release)
- Staged documentary with model: $2,000-3,500 per shoot day
- Editorial test variation: $4,000-6,000 per shoot day

Budget the primary style for 80% of spend, secondary for 20%.

## Rollout Plan

- Month 1: Produce 6 documentary pieces (3 customer, 3 staged)
- Month 1 test: 2 editorial pieces to run at 20% spend
- Month 2: Pick winner. Lock style for Q3.
