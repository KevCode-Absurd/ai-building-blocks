# Audit narrative support: knowledge-library-sales-doc-template-pitch-deck.md

Ten-slide structure: situation1–2; diagnosis3–5; rubric/coverage6; ordered action plan7–8; genuine proof or validation plan9; actual scope/fee/terms10.

Each finding needs evidence and business relevance, not necessarily a dollar estimate. Keep material unpriced risks. Only use supplied permissioned case studies. Do not invent managed-account benchmarks or promise fee payback. Optional value/ROI scenarios require explicit supported inputs, arithmetic, uncertainty and timing; otherwise impact remains unknown.

Discovery questions: what is observed, what is missing, what must be fixed first, who owns it, what would change the decision, and what proof can be shared? Return source IDs and unanswered questions with the slides.
