# Awareness Stage Copy Matrix

The definitive reference for matching copy framework to audience awareness.

---

## The 5 Stages (Eugene Schwartz)

| Stage | What They Know | What They Need From Copy |
|-------|---------------|--------------------------|
| Unaware | Nothing about problem or category | Surprise, education, context |
| Problem-Aware | Pain exists, no solution category | Validation, name the pain, hint at fix |
| Solution-Aware | Category exists, comparing options | Differentiation, proof, fit |
| Product-Aware | Knows your brand exists | Switching reasons, comparison, objection-handling |
| Most-Aware | Ready to buy, needs push | Offer, deadline, clarity |

---

## Framework Per Stage

### Unaware
**Framework:** Story/Education
**Goal:** Reveal the hidden problem they didn't know they had
**Structure:**
1. Pattern interrupt or surprising claim
2. Expose the problem
3. Introduce category as answer
4. Soft CTA ("Learn more", "See how")

**Copy length:** Longer. Educational. 30-60s video, 3-5 paragraphs static.

**Metrics:** Top-funnel CTR, video view rate. Don't judge by CVR yet.

### Problem-Aware
**Framework:** PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)
**Goal:** Validate the pain and guide to the category
**Structure:**
1. Name the specific pain
2. Deepen with specifics (cost of inaction, frequency, impact)
3. Present category as the relief
4. Soft CTA with guarantee ("Try it", "See if it works for you")

**Copy length:** Medium. 15-30s video, 2-3 lines static body.

**Metrics:** Landing page time-on-site, email capture, add-to-cart.

### Solution-Aware
**Framework:** Feature > Benefit > Proof
**Goal:** Differentiate your solution from alternatives
**Structure:**
1. Name the category outcome
2. What makes yours different (specific metric)
3. Proof (number, testimonial, study)
4. Direct CTA ("Try [product]", "See results")

**Copy length:** Medium. 15-20s video, scannable bullets on static.

**Metrics:** CVR, CPA.

### Product-Aware
**Framework:** Us vs Them
**Goal:** Close the comparison shopping
**Structure:**
1. Acknowledge alternatives (or lead with direct comparison)
2. Concrete comparison (price, metric, ingredient)
3. Switching reason + objection handling (guarantee, trial, cancel anytime)
4. Direct CTA ("Switch today", "Try risk-free")

**Copy length:** Medium. 10-15s video, side-by-side static.

**Metrics:** CVR, new customer acquisition rate, subscription rate.

### Most-Aware
**Framework:** Urgency + Offer
**Goal:** Get them to complete
**Structure:**
1. Direct offer (percentage, bundle, free shipping)
2. Deadline or scarcity
3. Clear CTA ("Claim now", "Shop the sale")

**Copy length:** Minimal. 5-10s video, headline-heavy static.

**Metrics:** CVR, AOV, revenue.

## Stage Identification Checklist

### Is the audience Unaware?
- Did they land from cold prospecting?
- Is your product in a non-obvious category (microbiome, chronic inflammation, circadian misalignment)?
- Are they lookalikes of new customers with broad interests?

### Is the audience Problem-Aware?
- Do they search for symptoms but not solutions?
- Are they in interest-based targeting (e.g., "skin concerns")?
- Did they visit your blog but not product page?

### Is the audience Solution-Aware?
- Did they search category terms ("greens powder", "collagen")?
- Are they retargeting pool visitors who didn't buy?
- Do they read reviews / compare articles?

### Is the audience Product-Aware?
- Did they visit your product page multiple times?
- Are they in branded search retargeting?
- Did they engage with comparison content?

### Is the audience Most-Aware?
- Did they add to cart without buying?
- Did they start checkout?
- Are they email subscribers who haven't bought?

## Common Mistakes

1. **Urgency copy on cold audiences.** "30% off for 24 hours" to someone who doesn't know you = ignored.
2. **Educational copy on hot audiences.** They don't need the lesson. They need the CTA.
3. **One ad for all stages.** Pick one. Produce variants for other stages separately.
4. **Assuming awareness from demographics.** A 45-year-old might be Unaware; a 22-year-old might be Most-Aware. Behavior signals stage, not age.
5. **Not separating campaigns by stage.** Same copy in prospecting and retargeting = leaving money on the table.

## Funnel Mapping

| Funnel Position | Typical Stage | Copy Framework |
|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| Cold prospecting | Unaware to Problem-Aware | Story / PAS |
| Warm prospecting | Problem-Aware to Solution-Aware | PAS / Feature-Benefit |
| Retargeting (visitors) | Solution-Aware to Product-Aware | Feature-Benefit / Us vs Them |
| Retargeting (cart abandoners) | Most-Aware | Urgency + Offer |
| Email nurture | Varies, often Solution-Aware | Education / Feature-Benefit |

## The 30-Second Audit

Look at a campaign's ads. Ask:
1. What stage is this audience in?
2. What framework does the copy use?
3. Do they match?

If they don't match, that's your highest-leverage fix. Bigger impact than any budget change.
