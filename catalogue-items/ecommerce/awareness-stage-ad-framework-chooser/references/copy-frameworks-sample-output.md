# Sample output: `/copy-frameworks`

Same product (VitaReds pomegranate greens), five stages, five different copy sets. Redacted.

---

## Stage 1: Unaware

**Framework:** Attention-grab + Education
**Audience:** Doesn't think about greens powder. May know they feel tired but not why.

```
Headline: Your afternoon crash has a cause.
Subheadline: It's not age. It's not sleep. It's your morning.
Body:
Most adults are micronutrient-deficient by 11am.
Not because they eat badly. Because modern food is depleted.
One scoop of VitaReds in water covers the gap.
22 vegetables. Actually absorbable.
CTA: Learn How
```

[ANNOTATION: Starts with reveal, educates, soft CTA. Would fail on Most-Aware because they already know this.]

---

## Stage 2: Problem-Aware

**Framework:** PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)
**Audience:** Feels tired, knows diet is part of it, hasn't figured out greens powders yet.

```
Headline: Tired by 3pm? It's nutrient gaps.
Subheadline: 22 vegetables, one scoop, fixes it.
Body:
You eat okay. You sleep enough. Still crashing.
The problem: modern produce has 40% less minerals than 1950s.
Greens powders aren't a trend. They're the fix.
VitaReds is the one that actually tastes like pomegranate.
CTA: Try 30 Days
```

[ANNOTATION: Names pain, deepens with the 40% stat, introduces category as fix, light CTA with guarantee.]

---

## Stage 3: Solution-Aware

**Framework:** Feature-Benefit-Proof + Differentiation
**Audience:** Knows greens powders exist. Comparing options.

```
Headline: Why most greens fail.
Subheadline: 80% sugar. Fake color. 5x lower absorption.
Body:
- 22 real vegetables (not 3 extracts + fillers)
- 0g added sugar (most competitors have 12g+)
- Pomegranate-forward taste (not lawn water)
- Third-party tested for heavy metals
CTA: Compare Labels
```

[ANNOTATION: Now we can lead with comparison. Audience knows the category, we differentiate with specifics.]

---

## Stage 4: Product-Aware

**Framework:** Us vs Them + Objection Handling
**Audience:** Knows VitaReds exists. Weighing it against competitors.

```
Headline: VitaReds vs AG1. Read the labels.
Subheadline: Same daily dose. Half the price. Different absorption.
Body:
AG1: 12g powder, 75 ingredients, $99/month.
VitaReds: 8g powder, 22 whole-food ingredients, $39/month.
Independent study: VitaReds absorbs at 97%. Comparable brand at 62%.
Cancel anytime. 30 day guarantee.
CTA: Make the Switch
```

[ANNOTATION: Direct comparison, specific numbers, no hedging. Objection handling built into the offer ("cancel anytime").]

---

## Stage 5: Most-Aware

**Framework:** Urgency + Offer
**Audience:** Has seen VitaReds multiple times. Ready to buy with the right nudge.

```
Headline: 30% off your first pouch. Today.
Subheadline: Code LIVTRY. Ends midnight.
Body:
You've been thinking about it. Take the trial.
Free shipping. 30 day guarantee. Cancel anytime.
CTA: Claim 30%
```

[ANNOTATION: No preamble, no education, no proof. Audience already has all that. Just offer + deadline + CTA.]

---

## Cross-Stage Warning

Writing the Most-Aware copy for Unaware audience would fail because:
- "30% off your first pouch" means nothing without context
- No reason to care
- "Take the trial" assumes prior consideration

Writing the Unaware copy for Most-Aware audience would fail because:
- "Your afternoon crash has a cause" is something they already know
- Education is wasted spend
- No direct CTA

This is the #1 mistake in prospecting campaigns: using Most-Aware copy on a cold audience.
