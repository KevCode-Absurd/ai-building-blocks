# Theme Library Catalog

**4 starter themes. Each carries a different job. Pick the theme based on where the buyer is, what the gap is, and what the brand has already proven. Then build the creative around the theme's spine.**

Start with these four. Add more (Urgency, Educational, Us vs Them, Features-Benefits, etc.) as your brand voice demands and as your account needs more coverage. Four is enough to cover most buyer stages.

---

## Why themes, not templates

A template says "use this layout." A theme says "this is the psychological job this ad is doing." A theme fits any layout. A layout without a theme is decoration.

Every ad in your account should trace to exactly one theme. Mixing themes in one ad is how ads end up saying nothing.

---

## Theme 1, LIFESTYLE

**Job:** Insert the product into a moment they already want.

**Mechanism:** Aspirational identity transfer.

**Best TEEP element:** Trigger, Payoff
**Best Schwartz stage:** Unaware, Problem Aware
**Avoid at:** Most Aware

**Copy spine:**
- Headline: a moment or scene statement
- Body: product named inside the scene
- CTA: soft (see more, discover, learn)

**Visual spine:** Environmental, editorial lighting, person in a moment, product secondary

**Example:** "6 AM. One drop. Out the door."

**Fatigue signal:** CTR holds, but CVR drops, too soft, not moving to purchase.

**When to use:** Always in rotation. Every account needs a lifestyle workhorse.

---

## Theme 2, PROBLEM-SOLUTION

**Job:** Name the pain, sharpen it, offer the fix.

**Mechanism:** Recognition plus agitation plus resolution.

**Best TEEP element:** Trigger, Emotion
**Best Schwartz stage:** Problem Aware
**Avoid at:** Most Aware

**Copy spine:**
- Headline: name the pain in their words
- Body: why the current fix fails, then offer the solution category, then name the product
- CTA: soft for cold, direct for warm

**Visual spine:** Problem visualization (before), then product, then outcome

**Example:** "Your skin feels tight by noon. By 3 PM it is flaking. You have tried 3 serums. None lasted. There is a reason."

**Fatigue signal:** Hook rate stays strong, CVR sags, you are agitating but not landing the solution. Tighten the transition.

**When to use:** Best for cold audiences with a specific pain. Runs consistently.

---

## Theme 3, SOCIAL PROOF

**Job:** Replace doubt with volume.

**Mechanism:** Herd signal, authority, safety in numbers.

**Best TEEP element:** Evidence, Payoff
**Best Schwartz stage:** Solution Aware, Product Aware
**Avoid at:** Unaware

**Copy spine:**
- Headline: specific number or rating
- Body: quote plus context
- CTA: direct (Shop, Buy, Try)

**Visual spine:** Quote card, review screenshot, rating graphic, customer-count hero

**Example:** "12,000 reviews. 4.8 stars. The one serum most customers reorder within 60 days."

**Fatigue signal:** Same number seen too often loses salience. Rotate the number you lead with.

**When to use:** Every account, every month. Most undervalued theme.

---

## Theme 4, FOUNDER-STORY

**Job:** Put a human behind the product. Explain why it exists.

**Mechanism:** Narrative trust plus identity.

**Best TEEP element:** Trigger, Emotion
**Best Schwartz stage:** Problem Aware, Solution Aware
**Avoid at:** Most Aware

**Copy spine:**
- Headline: "I built this because..." or a specific origin cue
- Body: the moment the founder realized the gap, the decision, the result
- CTA: soft (learn the story, try it)

**Visual spine:** Founder talking to camera, archival photo, handwritten note overlay, workshop or origin imagery

**Example:** "My son had eczema for three years. Every serum we tried made it worse. So I made this one."

**Fatigue signal:** Works until people stop responding to the face. Refresh with a new story beat, not a new founder.

**When to use:** Brand-building months, relaunches, category entries. Rarely the scale winner, often the trust-builder.

## How to pick a theme

```
Start with TEEP element -> narrow to 1-2 themes -> pick based on gap and voice
```

| TEEP Element | First-choice themes |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Lifestyle, Problem-Solution, Founder-Story |
| Emotion | Problem-Solution, Founder-Story |
| Evidence | Social Proof |
| Payoff | Lifestyle, Social Proof |

---

## Theme mixing rules

- **One theme per ad.** Never mix. "Urgency plus Social Proof" ads are weaker than pure urgency OR pure social proof.
- **Themes within a campaign should cover 3+ TEEP elements.** Never run 5 of the same theme in a single rotation. Mix.
- **Theme diversity over creative diversity.** Two different visuals of the same theme are less useful than the same visual in two different themes.

---

## Theme lifespan in the account

| Theme | Typical half-life | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lifestyle | 30-60 days | Workhorse, rotate slowly |
| Problem-Solution | 14-30 days | Fatigues when the pain becomes recognized |
| Social Proof | 30-45 days | Refresh the number, not the ad |
| Founder-Story | 30-60 days | Refresh with new story beats |

Use this as a guide for refresh cadence, not an arbitrary kill trigger. Fatigue shows in metrics, half-life is a starting assumption.
