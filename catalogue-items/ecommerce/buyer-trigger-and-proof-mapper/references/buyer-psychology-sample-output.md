## Buyer Psychology Map: Skeptical Wellness Buyer

### Triggers
- 3pm energy crash despite good sleep
- Realizing current greens powder tastes bad and they stopped using it
- Doctor mentioned micronutrient gap
- Friend or podcaster recommended a "real" greens powder
- End of January (reset season)

### Emotions
- Primary: Frustration ("I've tried 3 greens powders, none worked")
- Secondary: Skepticism ("All wellness brands overclaim")
- Tertiary: Cautious optimism ("If this one is different, I want in")

### Evidence Required
- Third-party clinical testing (not just brand claims)
- Ingredient transparency (full label, not proprietary blend)
- Real customer reviews with specific results
- Founder credibility (why they made it)
- Absence of hype language (no "miracle", no "revolutionary")

### Payoff Expected
- Consistent energy through afternoon
- Not dramatic. Just reliable.
- Something she can tell a friend about without feeling like a sales pitch

## Recommendations

### Prospecting (cold audience)
Lead with **Trigger + Emotion**. Evidence in supporting role.
- Winners: Ad B (founder story), variations of 3pm crash trigger
- Loser: Ad C (discount-led, wrong stage)

### Mid-funnel (retargeting site visitors)
Lead with **Evidence + Payoff**. Trigger assumed.
- Winners: Reviews-forward ads, ingredient transparency, lab results

### Bottom-funnel (cart abandoners, email subs)
Lead with **Payoff + Urgency**. TEEP already in their head.
- Winners: Ad C and similar offer-driven creative

---

## What This Audience Will Reject

- "Miracle formula" language
- Unverified claims (e.g., "absorbs 10x better" with no source)
- Generic before/afters without specific timelines
- Celebrity endorsements without substance
- "Limited time" urgency without real scarcity

[ANNOTATION: The "will reject" section is as important as the positive recommendations. Skeptical audiences punish overclaim more than they reward underselling. This is why founder-led and evidence-led creative outperforms hype creative for this persona.]

---

## Creative Brief Guidance

When briefing any new ad for this audience, include all four TEEP elements:

```
Brief: New VitaReds video ad
- Trigger: 3pm energy crash (mention at 0:05)
- Emotion: Frustration (show the moment, real voice)
- Evidence: 97% absorption + founder story (at 0:15-0:22)
- Payoff: "Consistent energy, not magic" (closing line)
```

No element optional. Missing one = the ad underperforms.
