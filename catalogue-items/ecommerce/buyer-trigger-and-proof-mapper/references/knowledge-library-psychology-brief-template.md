## 1. Persona snapshot (1 paragraph)

Write a single paragraph describing this person. Age range, life stage, job type or life role, income tier, one defining habit, one defining frustration. Write it like you are describing a friend.

**Template:**
> [Name], [age range], [life role]. [Key daily habit]. [Defining frustration]. [One specific thing about their relationship to the category]. [One aspiration that sits behind the buying decision].

**Example:**
> Jessica, 34, full-time mom of two. Wakes at 6 AM, skin routine done in 90 seconds. Tried 11 serums in 4 years, still has cyclical flaking. Stopped believing skincare ads can be honest. Wants to look like herself at 34, not a filtered version.
