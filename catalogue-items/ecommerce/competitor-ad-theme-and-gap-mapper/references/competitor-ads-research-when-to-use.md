# When to use `/competitor-ads-research`

**What it does:** Analyzes 2-5 competitors' Meta Ad Library presence, finds creative gaps, produces 5-7 specific ad briefs exploiting those gaps.

**Runtime:** 20-30 minutes (includes manual Ad Library review).

## Run this when

1. **New client kickoff.** Baseline the competitive landscape before the first creative sprint.
2. **Creative refresh.** Current concepts plateauing. Find a gap lane.
3. **Before `/creative-brief`.** Feed gap analysis into concept generation for sharper differentiation.
4. **Quarterly strategy review.** Landscape shifts. Re-check gap positions.
5. **New product launch.** Identify how competitors frame similar products, position around them.

## Before you run

- Brand name + what you sell
- 2-5 competitors (same category, overlapping audience)
- Current ad strategy (if any)
- Access to `facebook.com/ads/library`

## What you get back

- Per-competitor: active ad count, format mix, themes, hooks, positioning
- Cross-competitor: oversaturated angles, open lanes, format gaps
- Theme heat map (competitors x 13 themes)
- 5-7 ad briefs exploiting specific gaps
- Competitive positioning statement

## Chains well with

- `/creative-brief` to flesh gap-exploit concepts into full production briefs
- `/hook-generator` to stress-test the hook copy for each opportunity
- `/audit` to understand the user's own ad weaknesses before planning the counter
- `/brand-dna` to ensure differentiation doesn't break brand coherence
