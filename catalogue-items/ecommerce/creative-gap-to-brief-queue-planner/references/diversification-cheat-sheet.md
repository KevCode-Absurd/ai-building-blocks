# Creative Diversification Cheat Sheet

The shortlist of ad types, hooks, and visual styles to reach for when diversifying.

---

## The 4 Formats

| Format | Best For | Worst For |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Video | Storytelling, demo, founder voice | Quick reads, sale promos |
| Static | Bold claims, comparisons, offers | Complex products, demos |
| Carousel | Multi-step explanations, before/after sequences | Single-punchline ads |
| Collection | Catalog products, mobile shoppers | Single-SKU brands |

An account needs at least 3 of these 4 with meaningful spend (>10% each) to pass Format Diversity.

---

## The 9 Hook Types

### Problem/Solution
"Tired of [pain]? Here's why it's happening."
- Best for: Functional products, health, productivity
- Common mistake: Pain is generic. Be specific.

### Testimonial
"I lost 12 pounds in 8 weeks. Here's what changed."
- Best for: Trust-heavy categories, results products
- Common mistake: Using paid actors. Audiences smell this.

### UGC
Real customer, phone camera, moment-in-life.
- Best for: Relatability, broad audiences
- Common mistake: Too polished. If it looks like an ad, it fails.

### Founder
Founder on camera explaining why the product exists.
- Best for: Brand-heavy categories, early-stage brands
- Common mistake: Too corporate. Founder should feel like a person.

### Comparison
Side-by-side with alternative.
- Best for: Competitive categories where differentiation matters
- Common mistake: Naming competitor directly (legal risk). Use "typical" or generic.

### Demo
Watch it work in real time.
- Best for: Visual products, unusual mechanisms
- Common mistake: Boring after 5 seconds. Add stakes or surprise.

### Educational
Teach a concept. Product is the resolution.
- Best for: Category-education plays, awareness stage
- Common mistake: Too long. Keep it under 30s.

### Lifestyle
Product in aspirational context.
- Best for: Identity-driven purchases, apparel, premium
- Common mistake: Aspirational becomes generic. Be specific about who.

### Social Proof
Numbers, reviews, credibility markers.
- Best for: Crowded categories, trust-building
- Common mistake: "4.8 stars" alone is noise. Pair with specific numbers.

## The 7 Visual Styles

### Polished
Studio lighting, retouched, intentional composition.
- Signals: premium, established brand

### Authentic
Natural light, imperfect framing, real environment.
- Signals: approachable, honest

### Lifestyle
Product in use with human subjects and context.
- Signals: "this is for people like me"

### Product on White
Clean seamless white background, product only.
- Signals: e-commerce clarity, product-first

### Studio
Controlled lighting, designed backdrop, polished but not retouched.
- Signals: craft, intentionality

### Raw UGC
Customer-filmed, unedited feel, vertical.
- Signals: trust, native-feeling, not-an-ad

### Graphic Design
Text-led, illustrated, designed layout.
- Signals: clarity, explanation, claim-driven

---

## The Balance Rule

No single category should exceed 40% of spend.

If it does:
- Format concentration: deliberately brief non-dominant formats
- Hook concentration: deliberately brief non-dominant hooks
- Visual concentration: deliberately brief non-dominant styles

"Diversify" doesn't mean "add one new variant." It means "make sure no single thing is more than 40%."

## Production Cost by Brief Type

Rough guide for budgeting a diversification sprint:

| Brief Type | Cost | Ship Time |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Static with existing assets | $150-300 | 1-2 days |
| Carousel (designer only) | $200-400 | 1-2 days |
| UGC with existing roster | $300-500 | 2-4 days |
| Founder-led video | $500-800 | 4-7 days |
| Custom photoshoot | $800-2,000 | 5-10 days |
| Custom video shoot | $1,500-5,000 | 10-21 days |
| Animated explainer | $1,000-2,500 | 7-14 days |

Ship the cheap + fast ones first. They close the Andromeda score gap while the expensive ones are in production.

---

## The "What To Produce Next" Shortlist

If you only have time to pick 3 briefs, always include:

1. **One UGC-style vertical video.** 9:16, authentic, real customer voice.
2. **One comparison static.** Side-by-side with category alternative.
3. **One founder-led video.** Founder POV, authentic visual, 15-30s.

This trio covers Format (video + static), Hook (UGC + comparison + founder), Visual (authentic + polished), and Aspect Ratio (9:16 + 1:1). It's the most gap-fill per production hour in most accounts.
