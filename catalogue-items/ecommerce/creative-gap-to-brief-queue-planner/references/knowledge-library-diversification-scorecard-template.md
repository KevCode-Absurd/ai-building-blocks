# Diversification Scorecard Template (3-Dimension Public Version)

A single-page scorecard for grading any ad account on creative diversity. Three dimensions: format, message, audience. If any dimension scores below 50%, that dimension determines the brief for next sprint.

Once you have a month of data, you can extend this to 4-5 dimensions (visual style, hook type, aspect ratio). Start with three.

|---|---|---|

## When to extend beyond 3 dimensions

Add a 4th dimension when:

1. You have 30+ active ads in the account (enough to populate sub-categories without thinning each one too far).
2. Coverage on all 3 base dimensions is at 75%+ (you have earned the right to look at finer-grained signals).
3. You have a real hypothesis about which 4th dimension matters in your account (visual style, hook type, aspect ratio, etc.).

Premature dimension expansion produces a low score that does not actually mean anything. Resist it.
