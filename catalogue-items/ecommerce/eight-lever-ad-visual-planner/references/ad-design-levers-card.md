# The 8 Levers of Ad Design (Reference Card)

Print or pin. The full language-free version is on the back.

---

## The 8 Levers

| # | Lever | Controls |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | Mood & Emotion | Emotional temperature |
| 2 | Color Palette | Dominant colors + contrast |
| 3 | Lighting Style | How light falls |
| 4 | Product Placement | Where + how prominent |
| 5 | Composition Layout | Element arrangement |
| 6 | Background Environment | Setting / context |
| 7 | Style Approach | Photographic / artistic feel |
| 8 | Product Angle | Camera angle / lens perspective |

---

## Theme-to-Lever Defaults

| Theme | L1 Mood | L3 Lighting | L4 Placement | L5 Composition | L6 Background |
|-------|---------|-------------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| Urgency | Energetic | Dramatic | Hero | Diagonal | Studio/bold |
| Lifestyle | Calm | Natural daylight | In-scene | Rule of thirds | Lifestyle |
| Social Proof | Trustworthy | Natural morning | Secondary to person | Left/right split | Everyday |
| Problem/Solution | Tense-then-resolved | Split contrast | Split comparison | Split screen | Before/after |
| Features | Clean | Flat even | Isolated hero | Grid | Clean |
| Us vs Them | Bold | Contrasting | Split | Mirror split | Brand vs competitor |
| FOMO | Exciting | Dramatic | Secondary | Dynamic | Crowd/social |

| Aspirational | Elevated | Golden hour | Scene-hero | Symmetric | Premium |

---

## Override Priority (Top Wins)

1. User's explicit lever selections
2. Brand visual vocabulary (from `/brand-dna`)
3. Theme defaults (from the table above)

---

## The Lock/Flex Rule

- Lock 2-3 brand non-negotiables (usually L1 Mood, L2 Color, L7 Style)
- Leave 4-5 levers flexible for variation spread
- Variations must differ on at least 2 flexible levers each

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Locking too much** → variations look identical → increase spend for no learning
2. **Locking nothing** → variations look unrelated → no brand coherence
3. **Mood + Color both flexible** → random brand expressions → always lock at least one
4. **Angle flexible + Placement flexible** → same scene, different camera → not real spread

---

## Quick Diagnostic

Given a bad ad output, which lever is wrong?

| Symptom | Likely Lever |
|---------|--------------|
| Feels off-brand | L1 Mood or L7 Style |
| Colors clash | L2 Color |
| Flat or ugly | L3 Lighting |
| Product hidden | L4 Placement |
| Cluttered | L5 Composition |
| Wrong vibe / wrong setting | L6 Background |
| Looks fake / too polished | L7 Style |
| Product looks wrong size | L8 Angle |

Fix one at a time. Don't change three levers and regenerate.
