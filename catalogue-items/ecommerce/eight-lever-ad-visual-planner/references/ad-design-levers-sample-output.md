---

## Why These Locks

- **Mood locked** because this brand's skeptical audience rejects energetic/hype moods
- **Color locked** because pomegranate red is the brand's category differentiator (not green)
- **Style locked** because UGC-style breaks brand premium positioning

## Why These Flexes

- **Lighting flexible** to test morning vs afternoon energy
- **Placement flexible** to test product-forward vs customer-forward
- **Composition flexible** to test grid vs single vs split
- **Background flexible** to test clean vs lived-in
- **Angle flexible** to test tight product vs wider scene

---

## Production Rule

If variations come back and they look too similar, unlock Mood OR Style (but not both at once). If they look too wild, re-lock one flexible lever. Goal: distinguishable variations that all pass the brand test.
