## Test Recommendation

**Test Angle 1 vs Angle 5 first.**

Why:
- Angle 1 is the gentle positive ("actually tastes like pomegranate")
- Angle 5 is the aggressive contrarian ("your greens aren't green")
- Same visual, same offer, same audience
- Isolates positive-frame vs challenge-frame

If Angle 1 wins: audience responds to product-forward positioning. Lean into the taste story.
If Angle 5 wins: audience responds to category critique. Lean into competitor-contrast creative.

[ANNOTATION: The real value of this skill is the 5 distinct angles. Most copy processes only generate variants of one angle. This forces the test to span rhetorical approaches, which tells you something about the audience's psychology, not just which headline is catchy.]
