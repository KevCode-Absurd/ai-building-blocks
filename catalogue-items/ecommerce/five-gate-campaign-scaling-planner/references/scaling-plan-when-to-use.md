# When to use `/scaling-plan`

**What it does:** Runs the 5-point scale-readiness check on every campaign, plus pacing analysis, plus saturation watchlist. Recommends specific scaling method and timeline per qualified campaign.

**Runtime:** 8 minutes.

## Run this when

1. **Weekly cadence.** Every Monday to set the week's scaling moves.
2. **Mid-month pacing check.** Day 15 to confirm on-pace spend.
3. **New creative just won.** Before scaling the winner, run scale-readiness.
4. **Budget unlocked.** Client doubled monthly budget; need deployment plan.

## Before you run

- 14-30 days of campaign performance per campaign
- Target CPA + Target ROAS
- Monthly budget cap
- Frequency and CTR trend data

## What you get back

- Per-campaign scale-ready YES/NO + blocking issue if NO
- Budget pacing status for the month
- Recommended scaling method per qualified campaign
- 20% rule timeline per campaign
- Saturation watchlist
- 30-day spend + revenue projection

## Chains well with

- `/budget` for the portfolio-level Scale/Maintain/Reduce/Kill decisions
- `/diversification` to ensure scaling isn't concentrating risk
- `/leading-indicators` to watch for saturation earlier
- `/ab-test` if scaling is blocked by creative fatigue
