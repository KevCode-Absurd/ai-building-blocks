# Awareness Stage × Copy Framework Matrix

**5 awareness stages × 4 copy frameworks = 20 populated cells. Use this matrix to pick the right framework for the right reader.**

The reader's awareness stage determines what they need to hear. Framework is how you deliver it. A PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution) ad crashes in Unaware because the reader does not know they have the problem. A Social Proof ad crashes in Most Aware because the reader already believes. This matrix maps what works where.

---

## The 5 awareness stages (Schwartz)

| Stage | What they know | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Unaware** | No awareness of problem or product | Name the problem so they recognize it |
| **Problem Aware** | Knows the problem. Does not know solution type | Agitate + introduce solution category |
| **Solution Aware** | Knows solution type. Shopping | Prove you are the best option |
| **Product Aware** | Knows your product. Not convinced yet | Specific proof + risk reversal |
| **Most Aware** | Knows and trusts. Needs reason to buy now | Offer + urgency |

---

## The 4 frameworks used in this matrix

1. **PAS** — Problem, Agitate, Solution
2. **Social Proof** — Numbers, quotes, ratings, volume
3. **Us vs Them** — Comparison, contrast, head-to-head
4. **Urgency** — Deadline, scarcity, offer expiring

---

## The matrix

### UNAWARE

| Framework | Fit | Example headline / hook |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** | Poor | "If your skin flakes by 3 PM, you are already dehydrated." → Problem is too direct before they have named it. Use PAS only if the pain is a physical sensation they can recognize instantly. |
| **Social Proof** | Moderate | "12,000 women made this their one serum this year." → Works if the proof names a tribe they already belong to. Weak on its own. |
| **Us vs Them** | Poor | Requires them to know the category. Unaware readers do not have a "them" in mind. Skip. |
| **Urgency** | Poor | Urgency before desire is manipulation. Skip. |

**Winner for Unaware:** Curiosity + Story. Not in this matrix. Use hook-system-master-reference.

---

### PROBLEM AWARE

| Framework | Fit | Example headline / hook |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** | Excellent | "Your skin feels tight by noon. By 3 PM it is flaking. You have tried 3 serums. None lasted. There is a reason." |
| **Social Proof** | Good | "89 out of 100 women with cyclical flaking finished the full bottle." Pair with PAS opener. |
| **Us vs Them** | Moderate | "Hydrating serums treat the wrong layer. Barrier serums repair the layer that matters." Works if they can name a competitor category. |
| **Urgency** | Poor | Still too early. They are not ready to buy. |

**Winner for Problem Aware:** PAS. Agitation is legal here because the problem is real to them.

---

### SOLUTION AWARE

| Framework | Fit | Example headline / hook |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** | Moderate | They already know the pain. PAS feels like you are wasting their time. Skip the "problem" step. Jump to "solution with proof." |
| **Social Proof** | Excellent | "4.8 stars. 12,000 reviews. One repeat buyer every 43 seconds." Solution Aware is shopping. Proof closes the shop. |
| **Us vs Them** | Excellent | "Their $85 moisturizer. Our $42 serum. Same active at 2x the concentration." This is where comparison wins. |
| **Urgency** | Moderate | Works only if paired with proof. Pure urgency here feels pushy. |

**Winner for Solution Aware:** Social Proof + Us vs Them. They are comparing. Give them comparison fuel.

---

### PRODUCT AWARE

| Framework | Fit | Example headline / hook |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** | Poor | They already moved past the problem. PAS here resets them to step 1. Do not do that. |
| **Social Proof** | Excellent | "You have been on the page 3 times. Here is what 12,000 buyers said about their first jar." Retargeting + social proof is the highest-converting combination in this stage. |
| **Us vs Them** | Good | "You compared us to [competitor]. Here is the side-by-side we do not advertise." Works for cart abandoners. |
| **Urgency** | Excellent | "Your cart is about to expire. 20% off ends at midnight." They were close. Urgency closes the gap. |

**Winner for Product Aware:** Social Proof + Urgency. They are on the fence. Push them with proof and a clock.

---

### MOST AWARE

| Framework | Fit | Example headline / hook |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** | Poor | Insults their intelligence. Skip. |
| **Social Proof** | Moderate | They already trust you. Proof is redundant unless it is a new category they are considering. |
| **Us vs Them** | Poor | They picked you. They do not need to be resold on the category. |
| **Urgency** | Excellent | "New restock. First 200 only." or "Member price ends Sunday." They buy. Just give them the reason to buy today. |

**Winner for Most Aware:** Urgency + Offer. Keep it short. One headline. One CTA.

## Decision tree

Use this when you are not sure which stage + framework pair to pick:

```
Where is the traffic coming from?
├── Cold / prospecting (TOF)
│   └── Unaware or Problem Aware
│       └── Use Curiosity, Story, or PAS
├── Retargeting product viewers (MOF)
│   └── Solution Aware or Product Aware
│       └── Use Social Proof or Us vs Them
└── Email list / repeat customers (BOF)
    └── Most Aware
        └── Use Urgency + Offer
```

## Common mistakes

1. **Running PAS ads to Most Aware.** Waste. They already bought the problem. They need the offer.
2. **Running Urgency ads to Unaware.** Manipulative and ineffective. They have no desire to urgency-close on.
3. **Running Us vs Them to Unaware.** Requires mental comparison. They have no reference point.
4. **Running Social Proof without a number.** "Thousands of happy customers" is not proof. "12,000 reviews, 4.8 stars" is proof.
5. **Same ad for every stage.** The biggest mistake. One ad, one stage. Always.

## Pairing with tactics

If you also use the 5-tactic hook system (Confession, Bold Claim, Relatability, Contrast, Curiosity):

| Stage | Framework | Compatible Tactics |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | (skip matrix, use hook system) | Curiosity, Relatability |
| Problem Aware | PAS | Confession, Relatability |
| Solution Aware | Social Proof / Us vs Them | Bold Claim, Contrast |
| Product Aware | Social Proof / Urgency | Bold Claim |
| Most Aware | Urgency | Bold Claim |

Framework is the STRUCTURE of the ad. Tactic is the TONE of the hook. Pick both. They must agree.
