# Awareness Stage Quiz

**10 questions. Answer each, then score. The output tells you which stage your audience is in RIGHT NOW, and which framework + hook tactic to run this week.**

---

## How to use this quiz

1. Pick ONE audience you are about to target (prospecting, retargeting, email list, etc).
2. Answer the 10 questions below. Each answer is one of A, B, C, D, or E.
3. Tally your answers.
4. The letter you pick most is the awareness stage.
5. Cross-reference the matrix at the bottom for framework + tactic recommendations.

---

## The 10 questions

### Q1: Where did this audience come from?

A) Cold prospecting on broad interest targets
B) Blog, article, or organic content about a general problem
C) Product category comparison searches or competitor retargeting
D) Your product page viewers or cart abandoners
E) Email list, past buyers, or existing customers

---

### Q2: Does the audience know they have a problem?

A) No. They are living with it, do not name it
B) They know they have it. Complain about it. Have not searched yet
C) They have Googled it. Know solutions exist
D) They have looked at your product specifically
E) They already bought from you, or similar

---

### Q3: Does the audience know solutions exist?

A) Not really. Problem feels normal
B) Starting to. Read an article, saw a TikTok
C) Yes. They know there is a category of products that solve this
D) Yes. They know your product. They know 1-2 competitors
E) Yes. They have their own opinion on what works

---

### Q4: Do they know your specific product?

A) No
B) Maybe. Saw the brand name once
C) They know the category. May or may not know your brand
D) Yes. Seen the product page
E) Own it or know it well

---

### Q5: What would make them buy TODAY?

A) Something that stops them scrolling, not a sales pitch
B) Realizing this problem is fixable and there is a category for it
C) Proof that your option is better than the 2-3 they are comparing
D) Specific risk reversal (returns, guarantees, money-back)
E) A reason to act NOW (offer, restock, member price)

---

### Q6: What is their trust level in your brand?

A) Zero. Never heard of you
B) Low. May have scrolled past once
C) Medium. Considering along with 2-3 others
D) High. They are on the fence
E) Very high. They are in your ecosystem

---

### Q7: What is their level of urgency to solve the problem?

A) None. Problem is not acknowledged
B) Low. Annoyed but not acting
C) Medium. Actively researching
D) High. They are close to buying
E) High for a repeat purchase, low for new

---

### Q8: What content have they consumed?

A) None from you
B) One piece of organic or ad content
C) Multiple ads, maybe a landing page
D) Product page, reviews, comparison page
E) Email sequence, loyalty program, past order

---

### Q9: If you asked them "why haven't you bought?" what would they say?

A) "Bought what? I don't know what you're asking"
B) "I haven't found something that works"
C) "I'm still deciding between 2-3 options"
D) "I'm not sure if it will work for me specifically"
E) "I will, when the timing is right / when you run a sale"

---

### Q10: What is the goal of the ad?

A) Introduce the problem and start the relationship
B) Agitate the pain and introduce the category
C) Prove you are the best option in the category
D) Close the sale with specific proof and risk reversal
E) Get the next purchase or reactivate

---

## Scoring

Count your A, B, C, D, and E answers:

- A = Unaware
- B = Problem Aware
- C = Solution Aware
- D = Product Aware
- E = Most Aware

Your dominant stage is whatever letter you picked most.

---

## What to do based on your result

### Result: MOSTLY A (Unaware)

**Your audience does not know the problem yet.**

- **Framework to use:** Story + Curiosity
- **Hook tactic:** Curiosity or Relatability
- **Opener example:** "The reason your [daily symptom] gets worse by 3 PM has nothing to do with [obvious cause]."
- **Avoid:** Urgency, Us vs Them, direct CTAs
- **Goal of the ad:** Get them to name the problem

---

### Result: MOSTLY B (Problem Aware)

**Your audience knows the problem. They do not know the solution yet.**

- **Framework to use:** PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution)
- **Hook tactic:** Confession, Relatability, or Curiosity
- **Opener example:** "If your [problem] gets worse every [time period], you are not imagining it."
- **Avoid:** Heavy proof stacks, direct urgency
- **Goal of the ad:** Introduce the solution category, hint at your product

---

### Result: MOSTLY C (Solution Aware)

**Your audience is shopping the category.**

- **Framework to use:** Social Proof + Us vs Them
- **Hook tactic:** Bold Claim or Contrast
- **Opener example:** "What I used to spend on [category] vs what I spend now."
- **Avoid:** PAS (they know the pain), broad curiosity hooks
- **Goal of the ad:** Prove you are the best option

---

### Result: MOSTLY D (Product Aware)

**Your audience knows your product. They are on the fence.**

- **Framework to use:** Social Proof + Risk Reversal + Urgency
- **Hook tactic:** Bold Claim
- **Opener example:** "You have seen this product. Here is what happened when 500 people tried it."
- **Avoid:** Introducing the problem (they moved past it)
- **Goal of the ad:** Close the sale

---

### Result: MOSTLY E (Most Aware)

**Your audience is already in your ecosystem.**

- **Framework to use:** Urgency + Offer
- **Hook tactic:** Bold Claim
- **Opener example:** "New restock. First 500 only at launch pricing."
- **Avoid:** Long-form explanation, any "introduction"
- **Goal of the ad:** Get the next purchase

---

## What if your answers are mixed?

A 4-3-2-1-0 split is common. Use the dominant letter, but note:

- **A/B split:** Audience is transitioning. Run 60% Problem Aware content, 40% Unaware curiosity.
- **B/C split:** Classic MOF audience. Run a mix of PAS and Social Proof.
- **C/D split:** Solution Aware leaning into Product Aware. Retargeting. Lead with proof + comparison.
- **D/E split:** Cart abandoners + buyers. Urgency is your primary lever.

---

## Quick reference card

| Stage | Framework | Tactic | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Story + Curiosity | Curiosity, Relatability | Urgency, PAS, Us vs Them |
| Problem Aware | PAS | Confession, Relatability, Curiosity | Heavy urgency |
| Solution Aware | Social Proof + Us vs Them | Bold Claim, Contrast | PAS, pure curiosity |
| Product Aware | Social Proof + Urgency | Bold Claim | Broad problem intros |
| Most Aware | Urgency + Offer | Bold Claim | Long-form, new intros |

---

## Run this quiz once per audience per month

Audiences shift over time. Someone who was Unaware 4 weeks ago may be Solution Aware today because they saw 3 of your ads. Re-running this quiz monthly keeps your creative calibrated to where the audience actually is, not where you wish they were.
