# Sample output: `/ugc-script`

30-second UGC script for VitaReds greens powder. Creator: skeptical 38-year-old mom, converted after 3 weeks. Redacted.

---

## Script: "Three Weeks In"

### Hook (0-3s) [Pattern Interrupt]
```
"Okay, I owe greens powder an apology."
```
*Selfie, close-up, natural light, holding the sachet.*

### Setup (3-10s)
```
"I've been on this pomegranate one for three weeks.
Honestly? Didn't think it would do anything.
I've tried the big one, you know the one."
```
*Walks to kitchen counter. Product visible in frame.*

### Demonstration (10-25s)
```
"So I tore open a packet this morning at 6:30.
Mixed it in water. Actually tasted like pomegranate.
Not pond water, not lawn trimmings. Pomegranate.
Took it before my first coffee, like the label said."
```
*Tears open sachet. Mixes. Tastes. Pauses. Surprised reaction.*

### Turn (25-30s)
```
"Week three, I've stopped reaching for my 11am coffee.
That's the thing I notice. Not dramatic. Just consistent."
```
*Medium shot, natural environment, honest face.*

### CTA (30s)
```
"If you want to try it, link's somewhere on the screen.
30 day guarantee or whatever. Okay, bye."
```
*Wave, end. Natural, not produced.*

---

## Delivery Notes

- **Tone:** Honest-and-slightly-amused. Not enthusiastic. Not flat.
- **Pace:** Medium-slow. She's thinking as she talks.
- **Camera:** Selfie for hook and CTA. Tripod medium for demo.
- **Wardrobe:** Cotton tee, no makeup, hair up. Morning.
- **Setting:** Own kitchen, natural window light.
- **Props:** Sachet, glass of water, spoon. Visible existing coffee maker.

---

## Shot List

```
Shot 1 (0-3s): Selfie close-up. Hook delivery. Product in hand.
Shot 2 (3-10s): Medium, walking toward kitchen. Setup lines.
Shot 3 (10-14s): Close-up on sachet tearing open. No voice, just sound.
Shot 4 (14-20s): Medium, mixing + first sip. Honest reaction.
Shot 5 (20-25s): Selfie, medium close. The "tasted like pomegranate" line.
Shot 6 (25-30s): Medium, kitchen with morning light behind. The turn.
Shot 7 (30s): Selfie, wave, end.
```

---

[ANNOTATION: The hook is a pattern interrupt (owed-an-apology rhythm). The turn is where most UGC scripts fail. Most say "and now I'm obsessed!" This one says "not dramatic. Just consistent." That honesty is the trust builder. The CTA is soft ("or whatever") which paradoxically works better than a hard "try it now."]
