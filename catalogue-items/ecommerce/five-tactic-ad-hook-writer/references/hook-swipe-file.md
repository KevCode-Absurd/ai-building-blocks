## Annotation: Patterns That Recur

Across all 68 hooks, notice:

### Numbers dominate
About 60% of the strongest hooks contain a specific number. "8 greens powders", "97% absorption", "6 weeks", "3pm", "200 calories."

### First-person works
"I tried", "I drink", "My skin", "My kitchen." Creates intimacy. Makes the next line feel like a friend, not an ad.

### Short is better
Almost every hook is under 10 words. Most are 5-7.

### Specific identities beat broad demos
"People who hate waistbands" > "Women 25-54 who care about fashion."

### Question-plants doubt
"Your X is doing Y" plants a seed the viewer has to investigate.

### Contrarian openings grab
"Stop stretching", "Not athleisure", "Dishwashers wreck your knives" all challenge assumptions.

## Study Checklist

When you read a hook you like:
1. What tactic is it using?
2. What specific number or specificity anchor is in it?
3. What's the implied next sentence?
4. What category default is it breaking?

Do this for 10 swipe hooks before writing your own. Pattern recognition builds faster than intuition.
