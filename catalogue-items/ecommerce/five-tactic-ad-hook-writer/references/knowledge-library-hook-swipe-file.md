## How to use this swipe file

1. **Find your tactic.** Based on your awareness stage mix (check the guardrails in the master reference).
2. **Pick 3 hooks** from that section whose pattern fits your product.
3. **Extract the structure, not the words.** Example: "The ingredient every serum brand hides" has structure = [The <specific noun> + every <category> + hides/skips/overdoses]. Apply that to your product.
4. **Write 3 variants** of your own using the extracted pattern.
5. **Run them through the hook-generator** with your product context to validate tactic, visual, and voiceover fit.
6. **A/B test two variants** that differ on ONE dimension (same tactic, different visual OR same visual, different tactic).

## Filtering by awareness stage

Need to test across funnel? Use this quick map:

- **Unaware:** Pull 3 Curiosity hooks + 3 Relatability hooks. Avoid Contrast and Confession.
- **Problem Aware:** Pull 2 from each of Confession, Relatability, Curiosity. Your strongest pool.
- **Solution Aware:** Pull 3 Contrast + 3 Bold Claim. Comparison and specificity win here.
- **Product Aware:** Pull 4 Bold Claim + 2 Contrast. Proof is the bottleneck.
- **Most Aware:** Pull 5 Bold Claim. Skip the rest. Just give them the offer.
