## Production Brief: Concept 3 — "Side-by-Side VitaReds vs AG1"

### Hook
- Primary hook text: "We did the math."
- Visual: Two glasses side by side — VitaReds pomegranate red on left, AG1 green on right. Clean white studio background. Hard side light.
- Vibe tags: confident, analytical, under-the-hood, no-BS, clinical

### Body Structure
- Framework: Us vs Them (comparison table)
- Key points:
  - Ingredient count and quality (51 vs 75, but bioavailability difference)
  - Price per serving ($2.33 vs $3.47)
  - Sourcing transparency (batch-tested vs proprietary blend)
  - Taste (pomegranate vs "grass")
- Tone: Confident, factual, peer-to-peer. No "we're better" language. Let numbers do it.

### Visual Direction
- Format: 1:1 static, 1080x1080
- Style: Clean product photography with comparison table overlay. Editorial, not cluttered.
- Color: VitaReds side uses brand pomegranate/cream palette. AG1 side desaturated slightly to pull focus.
- Product visibility: Hero left, secondary right. VitaReds glass occupies 60% visual weight.

### CTA
- CTA text: "See the full breakdown"
- CTA placement: Bottom of creative, button-style, brand pomegranate.
