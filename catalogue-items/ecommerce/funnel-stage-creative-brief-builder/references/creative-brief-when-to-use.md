# When to use `/creative-brief`

**What it does:** Produces 10-15 ad concepts with full production briefs, prioritized by messaging angle and awareness stage.

**Runtime:** 15-25 minutes.

## Run this when

1. **New client kickoff.** You need a creative strategy baseline.
2. **Quarterly refresh.** Current concepts are fatiguing, time for new angles.
3. **After `/audit`.** The audit revealed creative gaps. This fills them.
4. **Persona launch.** New persona identified via `/buyer-psychology`, needs concept lineup.
5. **Competitive gap exploit.** `/competitor-ads-research` surfaced an open lane, brief the concepts to fill it.

## Before you run

- Persona definition (from `/buyer-psychology` ideally)
- Brand URL or DNA (`/brand-dna` output works great)
- 30-60 days of performance data (what's winning, what's fatigued)
- Competitor ad review (optional but sharpens angles)

## What you get back

- 5 messaging angles ranked by priority
- 5-stage funnel adaptation matrix per angle
- 10-15 ad concepts with full production briefs
- Preflight quality report
- Top 3-5 concepts to produce first

## Chains well with

- `/persona-stage-theme` to identify which cells in the matrix are empty
- `/brand-dna` to inject visual vocabulary into production briefs
- `/generate-ad` to actually produce the statics from the briefs
- `/ugc-script` to convert UGC-format briefs into full scripts
