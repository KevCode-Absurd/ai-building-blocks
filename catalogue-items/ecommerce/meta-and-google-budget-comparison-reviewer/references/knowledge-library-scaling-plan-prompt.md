# Cross-channel support: knowledge-library-scaling-plan-prompt.md

Use matched dates, account scope, currency and metric definitions. Keep Meta attribution, Google attribution and store records separate. Split branded demand only where observed. Never sum overlapping attributed conversions as unique sales.

A spend-share/reported-value-share ratio is descriptive, not incremental return. A lag correlation can arise from promotions, seasonality, other channels and shared demand. It does not prove Meta caused Google results, prove a higher trueROAS or prove independence when absent.

## Budget worksheet

For each proposal record current and proposed amount, total before/after, margin or approved payback evidence, exposure cap, experiment exclusions, observation window, uncertainty, stop rule and owner. Keep lift unknown without a supported marginal-response model. No fixed platform minimum or universal demand-creation role applies.
