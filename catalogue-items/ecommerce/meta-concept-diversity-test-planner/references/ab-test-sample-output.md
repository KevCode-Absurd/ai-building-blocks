# Test-planning support: ab-test-sample-output.md

Use the thirteen-concept classification and complete planning sequence in SKILL.md.

## Worksheet

Record hypothesis; control; variant; main changed variable; source evidence; target audience; assignment method; primary outcome; guardrails; baseline rate; useful effect size; sample/uncertainty method; conversion delay; spend cap; observation window; review owner; stopping rule.

Spend or calendar thresholds alone cannot establish significance. Ten-times CPA, one thousand impressions and seven days are examples, not universal minimums. A small pilot may be useful for feasibility while inconclusive about conversion impact. An ordinary platform allocation is observational.

## Result record

Record actual exposure, outcomes, uncertainty, missing data and whether the intended comparison remained valid. Deliver continue, revise, stop or inconclusive with its reason. Do not invent a winner or a revenue forecast.
