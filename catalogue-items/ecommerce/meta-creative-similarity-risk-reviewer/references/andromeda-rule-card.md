# Observed creative diversity support: andromeda-rule-card.md

Record ad IDs, dates, accessible media, format, hook, visual style, aspect ratio, text density and spend. Mark provisional name-derived labels. Use the four25-point heuristic dimensions in SKILL.md and state the actual scoring configuration.

Pair record: adA, adB, observed matching features, observed differences, evidence IDs, confidence and candidate test. The output is a creative-repetition observation. It cannot reveal proprietary platform IDs or establish auction cannibalization, algorithmic penalty or CPM impact.

A high-repetition group should produce a specific new format, hook or visual brief and a bounded evaluation plan. Missing media means no claimed visual inspection.
