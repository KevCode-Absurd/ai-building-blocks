# One-Colour-World Product Photo Set

A set of photographs of one real product that belong together visually, plus, if asked, a silent 9-second montage that flashes through them. No words on any picture.

## Inputs
- **Product and variant** (required).
- **Real product photos** (required): by job; an in-use photo if any frame shows use.
- **Brand look** (required): colour palette with the role of each colour and which one is the signature, photography style, places and props, never-show list. If no colour is marked signature, ask.
- **Buyer and moment** (optional): who uses it, where, when.
- **Use and size** (required): product page (1:1), feed (4:5), stories or montage (9:16).
- **Montage?** (optional): yes or no; and whether the brand holds rights to a music track.

## Steps
1. Ask two things if unknown: photographs only, or photographs and montage; which exact product and variant.
2. Run `product-truth-sheet-from-photos`. No true photo of the exact variant, no shoot.
3. Make frame 1, the hero: the product alone, whole, on a plain ground in the main colour, soft shadow. This is the test frame. Check it against the truth sheet (label words, shape, colours, closure) and get the person's approval before anything else is made. Once approved, attach it to every later frame beside the real photos.
4. Decide the direction once for the whole set:
   - **Colour world**, one of four, with one to three hex colours from the brand palette:
     - Single-hue wash (default): ground, surface, props and clothes all in the signature colour and its tones.
     - Rotating bold grounds: a different flat saturated ground per frame; for a product whose variety is the message (flavours, shades).
     - Quiet material tones: muted tones taken from the product's own materials; for clothes, bags, tools, hard goods.
     - Natural earth tones: clay, olive, cream, stone, warm daylight; for a product sold on what it is made of.
   - **Prop kit**: 3 to 6 real things the product is made of or used with, repeated across the set. Never another brand's product, never an ingredient it does not contain.
   - **Places**: 3 to 6 where this buyer really uses or keeps it, plus plain surfaces tinted to the colour world.
   - **Light**: one recipe for all frames, with one frame in deliberate contrast.
5. Choose the frames from these 15 kinds, each with its job and place of use, weighted by how the product is met:
   1 clean hero (always first); 2 held (adult hand, label to camera, scale); 3 on a body (worn products: front, back, detail, motion); 4 at the skin (applied: real amount, swatch); 5 in action (the pour, the bite, the task at its key moment); 6 very close (texture or finish, for a carousel card); 7 beside what it is made of (real ingredient or raw material); 8 on a surface (stone, oak, linen, plaster, a calm second hero); 9 in its place (banner, email header); 10 from above (laid flat with a few props, or everything in the box); 11 colour ground (one bold flat colour and clear space, a base for an ad); 12 moody (directional light, deep shadow, the contrast frame); 13 group (two or three of the same product or its real variants); 14 small in space (product small, lots of clean room for words set later); 15 in motion (a pour, a drop, steam, cloth in wind, only motions the product really makes).
   Weighting: worn = about half on a body, plus cloth and seam close, flat from above, one in its place; applied = at the skin, texture close, a drop in motion, the shelf, the routine from above; eaten or drunk = the food close, the pour, served, the bite or sip, with what it is made of; used by hand = in action three times (key moment, result, another use), the grip, a joint close, open or stored; placed in a room = in its place three times (wide, closer, in its state of use), material close, held once for size, few or no people. Every set has the hero, one scale frame, one very close frame and one frame with clear space. No two frames share a composition.
6. Show the direction (one paragraph) and the numbered frame list (kind, what it shows, job) and wait for changes or "go ahead". Write each scene so it: names the exact product in the same words every time; sets colour, prop, place and light from the direction; says who is in it and what each hand does, or says nobody is; ends with the camera (real lens, shallow focus, true texture, fine grain) and "no words, no logos, no marks".
7. Make two or three frames, check them, then the rest. Name them in montage order, hero is `01`. Lay all on one sheet and check: product identical and true, one colour world, no words, no two alike, each doing its job.
8. Montage (only if asked): about 9 seconds, a hard cut 3 or 4 times a second. First frame is the hero. The product holds still while the world changes: plan the montage frames so the product sits at about the same height and size (soft things like bedding cannot hold still; the strict colour world does that work). No two neighbours alike (clean, then a hand, then a close one, then a place); neighbours close in brightness; last frame is the hero again or the range; in 9:16 keep the product in the safe area. Delivered silent; with music only if the brand holds the right to use the track in paid advertising and a person has listened to it.

## Output
- 8 to 15 photographs (12 at most per set), one size, named in order with job, plus a contact sheet.
- The direction paragraph and frame list.
- Optional: the montage file, its length and the track licence.
- Generated pictures marked as generated and kept apart from the real reference photos.

## Checks
- Product identical in every frame: label, shape, colours, finish.
- The colour world floods the set, never the product. The product keeps its own colours; if it matches the flood, the ground is a lighter or darker tone so its edge stays clear.
- One colour world, one light, one prop kit; props are real and none carries another brand's mark.
- No words, borders or logos added. Only words printed on the product.
- Every frame has the product in it and a stated job. At least one frame has nobody in it.
- Fictional adults only, no child or part of a child, hands whole, no finger over the label.

## If something is missing
Write `MISSING: <photo | signature colour | rights to music>`. Without a rights-cleared track, deliver the montage silent.

## Avoid
- A frame outside the colour world, or a prop typical of the category but not of this product.
- The product tinted by the flood or lost against it.
- A result the brand does not claim; a scene the brand avoids.
- A glossy rendered look: aim for a real lens, true textures, fine grain.
