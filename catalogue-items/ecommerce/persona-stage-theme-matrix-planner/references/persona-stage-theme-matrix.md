# Persona x Stage x Theme Reference Matrix

Shared reference. Use as a starting template.

---

## The 5 Awareness Stages (Eugene Schwartz)

| Stage | Buyer State | What They Need From the Ad |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Unaware | Doesn't know the problem exists | Story, lifestyle, curiosity hook |
| Problem-Aware | Knows the pain, no solution yet | Name the pain specifically, validate it |
| Solution-Aware | Knows solutions exist, not your brand | Show your category is best path |
| Product-Aware | Knows your brand, hasn't bought | Differentiate, handle objections |
| Most-Aware | Ready to buy | Urgency, guarantee, final nudge |

---

## The 9 Themes

| Theme | What It Does | Works Best At Stage |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|
| Urgency | Time pressure, scarcity | Most-Aware |
| Lifestyle | Product in life context | Unaware, Problem |
| Social Proof | Reviews, counts, names | Solution, Product, Most |
| Problem/Solution | Name pain, present fix | Problem, Solution |
| Features/Benefits | What it does, why it matters | Solution, Product |
| Us vs Them | Comparison, positioning | Product |
| FOMO | Missing out | Most-Aware |
| Educational | Teach something useful | Unaware, Problem, Solution |
| Aspirational | Identity, status | Problem, Solution, Product |

## Persona Overlay

Personas modify this base map. A skeptical persona kills FOMO and Aspirational regardless of stage. A biohacker persona demands Educational at every stage.

Always adjust the generic heat map per persona before prioritizing.

---

## Cell Prioritization Formula

Score each cell 0-10:
- Persona fit (0-3)
- Stage match to funnel position (0-3)
- Theme strength for this persona (0-2)
- Asset availability (0-2)

Produce cells scoring 7+ first. Defer 4-6. Skip 0-3.

---

## Portfolio Spread Rule

Across all active creative for a brand:
- At least 3 personas represented
- At least 3 stages covered
- At least 4 themes used

If fewer than this, creative diversity is dangerously low. Run `/diversification` to confirm.
