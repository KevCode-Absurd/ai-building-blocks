# 5 Photography Styles — Reference Card

**Every ad you generate sits in one of 5 photography styles. Pick deliberately. Mixing styles in one campaign is the fastest way to look incoherent.**

---

## 1. EDITORIAL

**Feel:** Magazine spread. Considered. Refined.

**Lighting:** Warm natural, soft diffused, or dramatic side light. No harsh flash.
**Composition:** Negative space. Rule of thirds. Subject often off-center.
**Color:** Muted, warm neutrals. Brand color as accent, not dominant.
**Subject:** Product as hero OR person in thoughtful pose. Rarely both.
**Props:** Minimal. One or two elements that support the story.
**Background:** Textured fabric, concrete, natural stone, or soft gradient.

**Best for brands that are:**
- Premium-priced
- Wellness-aligned
- Purist positioning
- Long-consideration purchases

**Best TEEP stages:** Trigger, Exploration
**Best themes:** Aspirational, Lifestyle, Educational

**Avoid for:**
- Budget brands
- Fast-moving impulse categories
- Youth-heavy audiences

**Example prompt snippet:**
```
Editorial magazine style. Warm natural morning light, side-diffused. Product off-center on textured linen. Negative space left 60%. Muted cream palette with one deep-brown accent. Depth of field shallow on product edges.
```

---

## 2. CLINICAL / SCIENTIFIC

**Feel:** Lab. Precise. Evidence-based.

**Lighting:** Even, bright, studio. Minimal shadow. Often high-key.
**Composition:** Centered or grid-aligned. Rule-of-thirds less relevant.
**Color:** Whites, pale blues, clean grays. Brand color sparingly.
**Subject:** Product front-and-center OR ingredient close-up.
**Props:** Lab equipment (beakers, gloves), ingredient callouts, technical overlays.
**Background:** Clean white, pale gray, or scientific environment.

**Best for brands that are:**
- Performance-driven
- Ingredient-forward
- Health / medical adjacent
- Founded by a scientist / researcher

**Best TEEP stages:** Evaluation, Purchase
**Best themes:** Features/Benefits, Social Proof (when backed by data), Educational

**Avoid for:**
- Lifestyle / wellness-first brands (feels too sterile)
- Emotional buying contexts

**Example prompt snippet:**
```
Clinical studio photography. Even high-key lighting, no hard shadows. Pure white seamless background. Product centered, label front. Technical annotations in sans-serif. Clinical blue #E8F4FA background accent.
```

---

## 3. LIFESTYLE

**Feel:** A day in a life. Candid. Believable.

**Lighting:** Natural ambient, golden hour, or kitchen/bathroom practical light.
**Composition:** Fill-frame. Subject in context, not isolated.
**Color:** Warm, saturated, real-world palettes.
**Subject:** Person using the product OR product in a daily scene.
**Props:** The things that naturally surround that scene (mug, towel, phone, fabric, plants).
**Background:** A real room. A real setting. Not staged to look staged.

**Best for brands that are:**
- Mass-market consumer
- Aspiration-adjacent
- Daily-use categories
- Wellness + everyday

**Best TEEP stages:** Trigger, Exploration, Evaluation
**Best themes:** Lifestyle, Aspirational, PAS, Social Proof

**Avoid for:**
- Clinical / scientific positioning
- Hyper-premium (lifestyle can feel budget if wrong)

**Example prompt snippet:**
```
Lifestyle photography. Morning kitchen scene, soft natural window light from the left. Woman mid-30s, no makeup, coffee mug in one hand, product on the counter. Warm neutral palette. Candid feel. Not over-styled.
```

---

## 4. STUDIO / COMMERCIAL

**Feel:** Catalog. Campaign. Clean.

**Lighting:** Controlled studio lighting. Key + fill + rim.
**Composition:** Centered or rule-of-thirds. Product isolated.
**Color:** Brand color dominant. Simple palette.
**Subject:** Product only OR model + product in stylized pose.
**Props:** Colored paper, simple geometric shapes, gradient backgrounds.
**Background:** Solid color, gradient, or minimal geometric set.

**Best for brands that are:**
- Launch campaigns
- Product-focused pitches
- Seasonal or promotional moments
- Static ad archetypes (Bold Statement, Number Hero)

**Best TEEP stages:** Purchase, (transitioning to Most Aware)
**Best themes:** Urgency, Social Proof (when rating/number is hero), Features/Benefits

**Avoid for:**
- Deep brand-story ads
- Trust-building exploration content

**Example prompt snippet:**
```
Studio commercial style. Product centered on seamless brand-primary-color background. Single key light from upper-left, soft fill from right. Shadow falls naturally. No props. Typography overlay in brand typeface. Bold composition.
```

---

## 5. PURIST / MINIMAL

**Feel:** One element. One message. Nothing else.

**Lighting:** Soft even or single-directional natural.
**Composition:** Extreme negative space. Product off-center or corner.
**Color:** Single neutral + single brand color. That is it.
**Subject:** Product only. Or single ingredient.
**Props:** None.
**Background:** Pure neutral. Marble, linen, paper, or solid color.

**Best for brands that are:**
- Minimalist positioning
- "One product does the work of many"
- Premium-but-quiet
- Design-forward

**Best TEEP stages:** Exploration, Evaluation
**Best themes:** Aspirational, Features/Benefits (when one ingredient is hero), Lifestyle

**Avoid for:**
- Brands that need explanation
- Complex products with multiple components
- Urgency-led or FOMO moments

**Example prompt snippet:**
```
Purist minimal photography. Single product on warm cream linen. 80% negative space top. Soft diffused natural light from the right. One small brand mark bottom-left. Warm cream + single deep-brown brand accent. No props. No text overlay.
```

## Which style matches which brand?

Use this lookup:

| Brand vibe | Style |
|---|---|
| Premium wellness, quiet confidence | Editorial or Purist |
| Science-backed, performance-driven | Clinical |
| Mass-market, daily-use, aspirational | Lifestyle |
| Launch or promo-driven | Studio |
| Minimalist, "less is more" positioning | Purist |
| Youth-focused, energetic | Lifestyle or Studio |
| Traditional, ancestral, artisanal | Editorial |

---

## Style × theme fit (quick)

| Theme | Best style |
|---|---|
| Urgency | Studio |
| Lifestyle | Lifestyle |
| Social Proof | Studio, Lifestyle |
| Educational | Editorial, Clinical |
| PAS | Lifestyle |
| Us vs Them | Studio, Clinical |
| FOMO | Lifestyle, Studio |
| Aspirational | Editorial, Purist |
| Features/Benefits | Clinical, Studio |

---

## Mixing rules (and anti-rules)

- **Within one ad:** Pick ONE style. Never mix.
- **Within one campaign / sprint:** 80% in the primary style, up to 20% in a supporting style (e.g., primary Lifestyle + 20% Studio for product shots). This keeps the campaign coherent.
- **Across the account:** Pick ONE primary style per brand. Support with ONE secondary. Never run a brand in 3+ styles concurrently.

---

## How the pipeline uses this

In the prompt pipeline (step 4: design levers), photography style is set by the brand DNA (primary) and optionally overridden by the theme (secondary). The pipeline generates explicit prompt language for that style (lighting, composition, palette, subject, props, background) so the image model renders in the right register.

Style is not a vibe. It is a set of 6 explicit decisions, every time.
