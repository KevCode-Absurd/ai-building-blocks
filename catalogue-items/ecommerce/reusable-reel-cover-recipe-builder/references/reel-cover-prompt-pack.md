# Prompt pack

Paste this after the skill, filling in the brackets.

```
Build my Reel cover recipe.
Portrait: [attached, I have the right to use it]
Reel title or topic: [title, or topic if you want options]
Brand colors (optional): [background, text, accent]
Accent word (optional): [word]
Logo (optional): [attached]
Mood (optional): [serious, premium, dark]
```

Then ask: "Give me the title lines, the cover recipe card, the image prompt, the checks and the reuse notes. Do not claim you drew the cover."
