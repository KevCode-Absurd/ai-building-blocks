# Reel Cover Style Recipe

Locked visual identity. Do not drift.

---

## Canvas
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1920 (9:16)
- Color space: sRGB
- File format: PNG
- DPI: 72 (social-optimized)

---

## Background
- Base color: `#0B0D0E` (near-black charcoal)
- Grain: 8-12% film grain overlay
- Vignette: soft radial darkening from edges, 15% intensity
- No gradients, no photos, no patterns beyond grain

**Rationale:** Dark background pushes headshot and text forward. Grain adds texture without noise. Consistent across batch = feed identity.

---

## Headshot
- Placement: Bottom 40% of canvas, right-aligned
- Crop: Chest up
- Alignment: Right edge of headshot ~40px from right edge of canvas
- Color: Natural warm tone, slight saturation lift
- Edge: Clean cutout (no background artifacts)
- Scale: Headshot height = ~55% of canvas height

---

## Title Text
- Placement: Top 55% of canvas
- Alignment: Left, 60px from left edge
- Typography: Bold condensed sans-serif (Druk, Oswald, or similar heavy weight)
- Case: ALL CAPS (consistent within batch)
- Size: 140-180px primary, accent word 1.0-1.5x
- Line height: 0.95-1.0 (tight)
- Color: Cream white `#F2EFE8`
- Accent color: Turquoise `#2CC5B8`
- Max lines: 4

---

## Accent Word
- Exactly one per cover
- Picked by emotional or numeric punch
- Same position in stack as its natural place in sentence
- Can be 1.0-1.5x size of surrounding text for numbers or single-word punches

---

## Logo Bug
- Placement: Bottom-left, 60px from left, 60px from bottom
- Width: 60px
- Opacity: 80%
- File: logo bug SVG (provided in assets)

---

## Safe Zones

Instagram Reel cover UI overlays:
- Bottom 240px: username, caption overlay (keep critical text above)
- Top 120px: safe
- Right-side icons: last 80px right edge obstructed in feed

Design rule: critical text stays in the middle 60% vertically. Headshot OK to extend into bottom safe zone since it's decorative.

---

## Batch Consistency Checklist

When producing a series:
- [ ] Same background recipe
- [ ] Same headshot (or same photo session)
- [ ] Same font family and weight
- [ ] Same accent color
- [ ] Same logo placement
- [ ] One accent word per cover
- [ ] Title reads on mobile at thumbnail size (test 150px wide preview)

---

## What Not To Do

- No emojis in title
- No em dashes
- No drop shadows on text
- No text outlines or strokes
- No multiple accent colors (only turquoise)
- No centered text
- No headshot on left side
- No gradient backgrounds
- No stock photos behind headshot
- No logos bigger than 80px

---

## Off-Brand Examples (DO NOT ship)

- Centered headshot + centered text = looks like a motivational quote card
- Red or orange accents = breaks feed identity
- Light background = breaks feed identity
- Drop shadow on text = amateur design tell
- 3 accent words = dilutes the one moment of emphasis
