# When to use this skill

**What it does:** Builds a repeatable Instagram Reel cover style: the title in lines with one accent word, a cover recipe card you reuse, and an image prompt for a tool that draws.

## Run this when

1. You post Reels often and every cover looks different.
2. An editor or helper asks what a cover should look like.
3. You are starting a series and want the whole set to look like one brand.
4. You want to refresh the covers of your best old Reels in one style.

## Before you run

- An approved portrait: a photo you own or have consent to use.
- A Reel title, or the topic if you want title options.
- Your brand colors (optional). Without them, the starting colors are used.

## What you get back

- The title split into lines, with one accent word.
- A cover recipe card with every layout value.
- An image prompt to paste into a tool that draws, such as ChatGPT or Gemini, with your portrait attached.
- Checks and reuse notes.

The skill does not draw the cover itself.

## Chains well with

- The Short video hook writer, when the title itself needs work.
- The Thumbnail and title planner, for YouTube thumbnails instead.
