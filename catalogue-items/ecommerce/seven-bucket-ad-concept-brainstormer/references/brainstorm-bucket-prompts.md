# Brainstorm Bucket Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for generating concepts in each of the 7 buckets. Use these when you're stuck.

---

## Bucket 1: Problem-first

> "Give me 5 specific moments where [persona] feels the pain that [product] solves. Moments should be time-bound (e.g., 'Tuesday at 3pm', 'Sunday evening') and physical, not abstract."

## Bucket 2: Identity-first

> "Give me 3 'if you're the kind of person who...' hooks for [persona] that would make them feel seen. The identity should be specific enough that non-[persona] would scroll past."

## Bucket 3: Myth-bust

> "Give me 3 widely held beliefs about [product category] that are actually wrong. For each, what's the truth and how does [product] prove it?"

## Bucket 4: Founder story

> "Give me 2 founder-origin hooks. One about why the founder started the company (what was broken). One about the formulation/creation process (what standard was applied)."

## Bucket 5: Customer story

> "Give me 3 single-customer transformation stories. Not before/after hype. One specific small change (e.g., 'stopped needing 3pm coffee') that would be believable to a skeptic."

## Bucket 6: Comparison

> "Give me 2 direct comparisons to a competitor. One on price. One on ingredient/feature quality. Name the competitor explicitly (legal will review)."

## Bucket 7: Demonstration

> "Give me 3 silent-video concepts that show the product working without a voiceover. Each must be shootable in one take with minimal props."

---

## Bucket Mixing Rules

- Problem-first + Identity-first = strongest for cold prospecting
- Myth-bust + Founder = strongest for Solution-Aware retargeting
- Customer + Comparison = strongest for Product-Aware
- Demonstration = fills any stage but needs great visuals

---

## When You're Stuck

If you can't generate 5 in a bucket:
1. Re-read persona interview notes
2. Look at top-performing competitor ads in that category
3. Ask a real customer: "what almost made you not buy?"
4. Skip the bucket. Some products have weak buckets and that's fine.

## When You Have Too Many

If you have 30+ concepts and can't rank, apply this filter:
1. Kill any concept where you can't name the evidence
2. Kill any concept with the same hook as an existing ad
3. Kill any concept the brand voice rules reject
4. Whatever's left, rank by production ease first, persona fit second
