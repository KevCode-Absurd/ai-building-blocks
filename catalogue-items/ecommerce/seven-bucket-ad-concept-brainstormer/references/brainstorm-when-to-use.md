# When to use `/brainstorm`

**What it does:** Generates 20+ ad concept directions in 10 minutes, ranked by fit and production ease.

**Runtime:** 10 minutes.

## Run this when

1. **Production pipeline is empty.** You need ideas by end of week.
2. **Winners are fatiguing.** Need fresh angles that aren't just copies of the winner.
3. **Entering a new audience or persona.** Need concepts calibrated to them.
4. **Briefing an agency or freelancer.** Send them 8 pre-ranked concepts instead of one vague request.

## Before you run

- Product + description
- Persona profile (1 minimum, 3 ideal)
- Awareness stages needed
- List of past ads (to avoid re-generating the same concept)

## What you get back

- 20+ concepts across 7 buckets
- Each concept with hook, angle, persona fit, stage fit, evidence requirement, risk
- Top 8 ranked to produce
- Kill list with reasons

## Chains well with

- `/persona-stage-theme` first to know which cells need filling
- `/teep-framework` to structure each top concept
- `/hook-generator` to sharpen the hook line for each concept
- `/creative-brief` to convert the top concepts into production specs
