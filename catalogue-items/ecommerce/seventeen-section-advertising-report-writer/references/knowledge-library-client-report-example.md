## 1. Executive Summary

March was the first month Lumen Wellness crossed $155,000 in total monthly revenue. Blended MER held at 3.1x against a 3.0x target, and new-customer revenue rose to $58,000, up 18% from February. New-customer CAC dropped to $156 blended from $178 the prior month.

The lift came from two moves: the Lifestyle creative refresh Sage shipped in week 2, which scaled TOF_Cold_Lifestyle_V2 from $400/day to $1,100/day while holding 3.2 ROAS, and the Klaviyo welcome flow rewrite Jordan completed in week 3, which added $4,200 in attributed revenue in the last 14 days of the month.

In April, the big bet is scaling Google non-brand paid search from $4,000/month to $10,000/month. Non-brand is the single most under-invested lever in the account right now.

## Why this report is structured this way

A few notes for operators studying this sample:

1. **Executive summary is section 1, written last.** The client reads this first. You cannot write it honestly until you have done the rest.
2. **KPI dashboard on page 2.** The client does not scroll to find the headline. It is immediately visible.
3. **Reconciliation is explicit.** We show the delta between platform-reported and Shopify revenue. This is how trust is built over time.
4. **Named people and named things.** "Priya shipped 3 LP improvements" and "Sage shipped the creative refresh" are concrete. Vague agencies say "we optimized the funnel." Specific agencies say what happened, who did it, when.
5. **Losses are honest.** A report with no losses section is a report the client stops trusting by month 3.
6. **Next-month plan is 1 page.** The client finishes this section feeling like the agency has a plan. That is the single most important feeling to leave them with.
7. **Appendix is available, not shoved.** Clients who want data get it. Clients who don't aren't overwhelmed.

The report is not the deliverable. The review call is the deliverable. The report is the artifact the call is built on.
