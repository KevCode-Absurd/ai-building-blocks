# Monthly Report Template

**17 sections. Every month. Every client. No exceptions.**

The report is not a recap. It is the single document that proves the work, frames the next 30 days, and gets the client to stay.

---

## COVER PAGE

- Client name
- Report period (e.g., "March 1-31, 2026")
- Prepared by [your agency name]
- Date issued
- Main contact + cc

---

## 1. Executive Summary (1 page)

**3 paragraphs, in order:**

1. **What happened.** Revenue, ROAS, new customers. The headline number.
2. **Why it happened.** 1 sentence of attribution (creative, audience, offer, seasonality, or platform).
3. **What is next.** The 1 thing we will do in the coming month that matters most.

Never write more than one page. Executives do not read page 2.

## How this report ships

1. **Draft on the 1st of the month** by the lead strategist
2. **Internal review** by the media buyer and account lead on the 2nd
3. **Client sent** by end of day on the 3rd
4. **Review call** with the client in the first week of the month (30-45 min)

Never skip the review call. The call is where the client experiences the value of the work — not the PDF.

---

## Voice + style rules

- **No em dashes.** Use commas or periods.
- **Plain English.** No agency jargon (leverage, unlock, optimize, synergy).
- **Specific numbers, not adjectives.** "ROAS up 18%" beats "ROAS significantly improved."
- **Named people + named things.** "Mia ran the creative refresh" is better than "our team refreshed creative."
- **Acknowledge losses.** A report with no losses is a report the client does not trust.
- **End with momentum, not recap.** Section 16 is where the client feels the future.

---

## Common mistakes

1. Burying the headline number in section 5 instead of page 1
2. Reporting platform-attributed revenue without reconciling to Shopify
3. No year-over-year comparison (the client wants the context)
4. Padding with unanswered screenshots
5. No "losses" section (feels dishonest)
6. No next-month plan (client has nothing to look forward to)
7. Sending the report without a review call booked

The monthly report is not paperwork. It is the most important document in the client relationship.
