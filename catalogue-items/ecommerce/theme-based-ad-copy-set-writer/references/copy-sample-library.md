# Copy Sample Library

50 annotated examples of headline + body + CTA combos across the 9 frameworks. Study, adapt, don't copy verbatim.

## Urgency (examples 1-5)

### 1. Flash Sale
- **Headline:** 48 hours. 30% off.
- **Body:** No code needed. Ends Sunday at midnight.
- **CTA:** Shop the Sale
- **Why it works:** Time + discount + clarity in 10 words.

### 2. Product Launch
- **Headline:** First 500 get it today.
- **Body:** The new Elara 2.0 ships Monday. Reserve yours now.
- **CTA:** Reserve Yours
- **Why it works:** Scarcity is specific (500), not vague ("limited").

### 3. Restock
- **Headline:** Back in stock. Won't last.
- **Body:** 3x back-orders last drop. Don't miss this one.
- **CTA:** Shop Now
- **Why it works:** Social proof in the scarcity (3x backordered).

### 4. Holiday Deadline
- **Headline:** Order by Dec 15. Arrives by Christmas.
- **Body:** Free 2-day shipping on orders over $60.
- **CTA:** Shop Now
- **Why it works:** Clear deadline, clear benefit, no fluff.

### 5. Seasonal
- **Headline:** Summer price ends Friday.
- **Body:** Last chance to lock in $24. Back to $36 on Saturday.
- **CTA:** Lock In $24
- **Why it works:** Specific prices beat percentages when the number is friendly.

## Educational (examples 41-45)

### 41. How It Works
- **Headline:** How magnesium glycinate works.
- **Body:** Most magnesium is oxide. It passes through. Glycinate binds to glycine, reaches deep tissue, actually absorbs.
- **CTA:** Learn More
- **Why it works:** Teaches a real mechanism in 2 sentences.

### 42. The Science
- **Headline:** The science behind 97% absorption.
- **Body:** Peptide size matters. Standard collagen is 5,000 daltons. Too big for gut. Hydrolyzed to sub-1,000 daltons, it absorbs in minutes.
- **CTA:** Read the Research
- **Why it works:** Technical authority without being opaque.

### 43. Myth Bust
- **Headline:** Myth: all vitamin C is the same.
- **Body:** Ascorbic acid oxidizes in 20 minutes. Liposomal is stable for hours and delivers 4x more to cells.
- **CTA:** See the Difference
- **Why it works:** Myth-bust opens mental doors.

### 44. 3 Facts
- **Headline:** 3 things about [category] most brands won't tell you.
- **Body:** 1) Most is diluted. 2) Lab reports are rare. 3) Real dosing costs more.
- **CTA:** Read All 3
- **Why it works:** Numbered promise + transparency hook.

### 45. Glossary
- **Headline:** What "clinical strength" actually means.
- **Body:** It's not regulated. It's marketing. Here's how to read a real label.
- **CTA:** See the Guide
- **Why it works:** Gives the customer a new skill.
