# Ad Copy Framework Card

**Use this as a printable reference. Tape it to the wall next to your desk. Every ad copy set you write should pass this page first.**

---

## The Four Components (with hard rules)

### Headline (required)
- 4 to 6 words
- 40 character hard limit (Meta mobile truncates past this)
- No em dashes, no exclamation marks in Google RSA
- Front-load the value. No fluff.

**Variant 1 — Direct Benefit.** "Glow From Within"
**Variant 2 — Pain Pattern.** "Tired of Dry, Flaky Skin?"
**Variant 3 — Proof Number.** "23,000 Customers. 4.8 Stars."
**Variant 4 — Offer Lead.** "40% Off Ends Tonight"

### Subheadline (optional)
- 6 to 12 words
- Must add a new dimension, never restate the headline
- If headline is emotional, subhead carries the proof
- If headline is proof, subhead carries the feeling

**Variant 1 — Evidence Add.** "Clinically proven collagen peptides"
**Variant 2 — Audience Name.** "Built for people who do not mess around"
**Variant 3 — Mechanism.** "Repairs the moisture barrier in 7 days"
**Variant 4 — Stakes.** "Because nobody else is going to do it for you"

### Body (format dependent)
- Static ads: 1 to 2 lines
- Video: scripted hook, not walls of text
- Carousel: per-slide copy
- Universal: scannable, benefit-forward, no paragraphs

**Variant 1 — PAS.** "Tired of [problem]? You have tried [failed fix]. Here is what actually works."
**Variant 2 — Feature-Benefit.** "[Feature] that [benefit] so [outcome]."
**Variant 3 — Testimonial Pull.** "'Skin cleared in 3 weeks' — Sarah R., verified buyer."
**Variant 4 — Educational.** "The reason your routine fails comes down to one thing."

### CTA (required)
- 2 to 4 words
- Standard verbs: Shop, Get, Try, Learn, Claim, See, Discover, Explore
- Avoid "Buy" (creates friction)
- CTA style matches awareness stage

**Variant 1 — Conversion.** "Shop Now" / "Get Yours"
**Variant 2 — Offer.** "Claim Offer" / "Redeem 40% Off"
**Variant 3 — Awareness.** "Learn More" / "See How It Works"
**Variant 4 — Trial.** "Try Risk-Free" / "Start Your Trial"

## The 9 Theme Frameworks (pick one per copy set)

| Theme | Framework | Headline Pattern | Body Approach | CTA Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urgency | Scarcity + Deadline + Direct CTA | LAST CHANCE: [X] off ends [TIME] | Short, punchy, deadline-driven | Shop Now, Claim Before Midnight |
| Lifestyle | Identity + Aspiration | For the [Tribe] / Made for [Moment] | Minimal or none, let the image carry | Discover, Explore, Join |
| Social Proof | Testimonial + Result + Validation | 23,000 switched this month | Customer quote + specific number | See Why, Shop the Favorite |
| Problem/Solution | PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve) | Tired of [pain]? | Name pain, twist knife, fix | Fix It, Get Relief |
| Features/Benefits | Feature > Benefit > Proof | The [Feature] that [Benefit] | Clear outcome, no filler | Learn How, See It |
| Us vs Them | Direct Comparison | Why [X] people switched | 3-4 comparison points | Compare, Make the Switch |
| FOMO | Social Momentum + Scarcity | Selling fast / Almost gone | Momentum + urgency | Get Yours, Last Few |
| Educational | Teach + Position as Expert | The science behind [mechanism] | Numbered points, expert tone | Learn More, Explore |
| Aspirational | Elevated Identity + Premium Position | For people who [X] | Minimal, brand-driven | Discover, Explore |

## Compliance Guardrails (FAIL if violated)

- Never fabricate countdown timers, stock counts, or deadlines
- All scarcity claims must be verifiable
- No false claims or unsubstantiated guarantees
- Never use ALL CAPS for full sentences
- Banned phrases: game-changer, revolutionary, leverage, unlock, skyrocket, delve, at the end of the day
- No em dashes (use commas or periods)

---

## Quality Gate (run before delivering)

- [ ] Headline 4-6 words, under 40 characters
- [ ] CTA present on every set
- [ ] Zero banned phrases
- [ ] Zero em dashes
- [ ] Minimum 3 copy sets generated
- [ ] At least 3 different frameworks used across sets
- [ ] All headlines unique across sets

If any item fails, regenerate. No exceptions.
