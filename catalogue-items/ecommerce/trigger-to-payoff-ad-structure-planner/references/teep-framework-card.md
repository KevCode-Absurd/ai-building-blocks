## Quick Reference

| Element | What It Is | When to Lead With |
|---------|------------|-------------------|
| T - Trigger | Moment that makes them notice | Cold prospecting |
| E - Emotion | Feeling attached to trigger | Cold prospecting |
| E - Evidence | Proof that solution works | Mid-funnel retargeting |
| P - Payoff | Specific expected outcome | Bottom-funnel retargeting |

## The 4 TEEP Failures to Avoid

1. **Payoff-only** ("Feel amazing!") - no trigger, no evidence, nothing lands
2. **Evidence-stacking** - "97% absorption! 2,400 reviews!" without reason to care
3. **Trigger without payoff** - names the pain but doesn't close the loop
4. **Emotion without evidence** - heart-tugging but too-good-to-be-true

## The TEEP Audit (2 minutes)

Score any ad 0-2 per element:
- 0 = absent
- 1 = weak / implied
- 2 = strong / explicit

| Total | Verdict |
|-------|---------|
| 6-8 | Strong ad, scale it |
| 4-5 | Missing critical element, fix before scaling |
| 0-3 | Rebuild |

## Funnel Playbook

- **Cold:** Lead Trigger + Emotion. Light Evidence. State Payoff once.
- **Warm:** Lead Evidence + Payoff. Assume Trigger is known.
- **Hot:** Lead Payoff + Urgency. Evidence in background.

Never skip the element that matches the funnel position. Common failure: running warm-audience creative (evidence-heavy) in cold placements.
