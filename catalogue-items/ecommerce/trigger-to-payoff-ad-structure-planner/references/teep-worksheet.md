## P — Payoff

What specific outcome does the buyer expect?

Types:
- [ ] Functional (sleep better, more energy, etc.)
- [ ] Identity (be a certain kind of person)
- [ ] Social (compliments, status)
- [ ] Relief (stop worrying about X)
- [ ] Time/money saved

**Primary payoff:** ___________________________________

**Specificity check:** Is this modest and believable? Or grandiose?
- If "life-changing", "transformational", "revolutionary" - rewrite.
- Better: specific, modest, verifiable ("consistent energy", "40 minutes more sleep").

---

## Structure Assignment

For each TEEP element, assign position in the ad:

| Element | Video Timestamp | Static Position | Carousel Card |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Trigger | 0-3s | Headline | Card 1 |
| Emotion | 3-10s | Visual | Card 2 |
| Evidence | 10-20s | Body copy | Card 3 |
| Payoff | 20-25s | CTA area | Card 4 |

---

## Final Check

Before handing to production, answer:

- [ ] Does the ad cover ALL 4 elements?
- [ ] Is the element order aligned to funnel position?
- [ ] Is each element specific (not vague)?
- [ ] Does the ad avoid hype language?
- [ ] Would a skeptical audience trust this?

If yes to all 5, produce. If any no, revise.
