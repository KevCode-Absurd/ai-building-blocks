# 12 Visual Hooks Gallery

The full reference of first-frame patterns. Use as a starting point, not a template.

---

## 1. Bold Text Over Solid Color
- Text: 4-8 words max
- Color: Brand or provocative
- Use: Verbal hook is the differentiator, no photo needed

## 2. Product Hero, Clean White
- Product centered, premium feel
- Use: Beautiful packaging, clean-label positioning

## 3. Split Screen (Before/After)
- Two states, divider line
- Use: Visible transformation, comparison

## 4. Extreme Close-Up
- One detail fills frame
- Use: Texture, unique feature, sensory cue

## 5. In-Hand Shot
- Real hand, real environment
- Use: Scale, tactile feel, UGC-native

## 6. Pattern Interrupt
- Something unexpected, anti-ad aesthetic
- Use: Feed-noise competition, non-obvious categories

## 7. Lifestyle Moment
- Human in real context with product
- Use: Identity purchase, aspiration

## 8. Circled/Highlighted Detail
- Arrow/circle pointing at one feature
- Use: Education, spec highlight

## 9. Comparison Chart
- Side-by-side with competitors
- Use: Product-aware audience, strong differentiator

## 10. Chaos Then Product
- Messy problem frame, product as resolution
- Use: PAS framing, problem-first

## 11. Floating Ingredients
- Product + raw ingredients in air
- Use: Clean-label, ingredient-forward

## 12. Raw UGC Thumbnail
- Phone-quality, imperfect
- Use: Authenticity over polish

## Selection Rules

### By theme:
- Urgency: #1, #10
- Lifestyle: #7, #11
- Social Proof: #5, #12
- Problem/Solution: #3, #10
- Features/Benefits: #8, #9
- Us vs Them: #9, #1
- FOMO: #5, #12
- Educational: #8, #11
- Aspirational: #2, #7

### By audience stage:
- Unaware: #6, #10, #11
- Problem-Aware: #10, #3
- Solution-Aware: #9, #8, #11
- Product-Aware: #9, #1
- Most-Aware: #2, #1 (offer-driven)

### By production capacity:
- Fastest (1-2 hours): #1, #8, #9
- Medium (half-day): #2, #3, #4, #11
- Longest (full shoot): #5, #6, #7, #10, #12

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Brand logo as first focus.** Audience trained to ignore branded frames. Brand in frame 2, not frame 1.
2. **Too much text.** 5-7 words is the hard limit. If more, you need a different hook.
3. **Using the same hook across all ads.** Visual diversity is part of Andromeda.
4. **Lifestyle without specificity.** Generic "happy woman with product" is forgettable.
5. **Comparison chart without real differentiator.** If the differentiator is weak, this hook backfires.

---

## The 30-Second Hook Audit

Look at your active ad set thumbnails. Ask:
1. Can I identify which of the 12 patterns each uses?
2. Are more than 50% of my ads using the same pattern?
3. Do my thumbnails look like my competitors' thumbnails?

If yes to #2 or #3, time to diversify visual hooks.
