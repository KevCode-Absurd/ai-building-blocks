## Visual Hook #3: Lifestyle Moment

**Pattern:** Lifestyle Moment (Human in Context)
**Subject:** Woman, 38ish, at kitchen counter, 7am morning light. Holding VitaReds glass mid-sip.
**Composition:** Rule of thirds. Subject in left third. Product glass in center.
**Background:** Kitchen at dawn, warm golden light, slight blur.
**Text Overlay:** "Actually tastes like pomegranate." Bottom third, cream color, 72pt.
**Lighting:** Natural window light, east-facing, golden hour.
**Palette:** Warm tones, pomegranate-red accent from the drink itself.
**Production Type:** Lifestyle photography (existing library or new shoot).
**Asset Requirements:** Customer model or UGC creator. Kitchen location.
**Estimated Time:** Half-day shoot + 2 hours edit.

**Why it works:** Specific moment (7am, kitchen, morning light) is relatable. Drink color echoes brand. Text confirms the visual story.

## A/B Test Recommendation

**Test #1 vs #3 first.**

Why:
- #1 is the extreme graphic (text-only, no product)
- #3 is the extreme lifestyle (real human, real moment)
- Both use the same verbal positioning (pomegranate = actually good)
- Isolates "bold claim" vs "relatable moment"

If #1 wins: audience responds to bold challenger framing. Lean into graphic-design static creative.
If #3 wins: audience wants to see themselves. Lean into lifestyle/UGC.

[ANNOTATION: These three hooks span the full range from abstract graphic to human moment. Testing them tells you which creative direction to invest in next, not just which frame is better.]
