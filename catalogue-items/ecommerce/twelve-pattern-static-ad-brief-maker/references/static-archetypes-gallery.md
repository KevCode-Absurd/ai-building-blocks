# Static Archetype Gallery

All 12 archetypes with visual + copy spec + when-to-use. Use as a picking reference.

---

## Cold Prospecting

### 1. Problem Moment
- Visual: Specific time-bound pain moment, no product
- Copy: 5-word caption
- When: Audience is Problem-Aware and needs to feel seen
- Example: "3pm. Again."

### 2. Identity Mirror
- Visual: Single portrait matching the persona
- Copy: "If you're the kind of person who..."
- When: Audience is Unaware or Problem-Aware, you want self-selection
- Example: "If you track your sleep and read ingredient labels..."

### 3. Myth Bust
- Visual: Side-by-side or contrasting visual that breaks a category belief
- Copy: Claim + counter-claim headline
- When: Category has a widespread myth you can leverage
- Example: "Every greens drink is green. This one's red. Here's why."

### 4. Founder on Camera
- Visual: Portrait of founder, slight intensity, looking at camera
- Copy: Single founder quote (max 15 words)
- When: Founder is an asset (credibility, story, category authority)
- Example: "I rejected 23 formulas before this one. - Alex, founder"

---

## Warm Retargeting

### 5. Single Testimonial
- Visual: Customer photo + verified badge
- Copy: Quote + attribution + rating
- When: You have one killer customer story
- Word count: Quote max 20 words

### 6. Multi-Proof Grid
- Visual: 3x2 or 2x3 grid of customer photos
- Copy: Big number headline
- When: You have volume but no single standout story
- Example: "12,400 women switched this month"

### 7. Comparison Table
- Visual: Us vs Them grid with check/X icons
- Copy: Feature labels + checkmarks
- When: Competitor comparison is a strong positioning play
- Legal: Always confirm competitor name use with brand legal

### 8. Feature Annotation
- Visual: Product hero center, 4 callouts radiating
- Copy: Feature labels (2-4 words) + descriptions (5-10 words)
- When: Product has 3-4 distinct features worth naming
- Warning: Don't annotate 6+ features; visual gets cluttered

---

## Bottom Funnel

### 9. Price Anchor
- Visual: Price large, product small, guarantee icon
- Copy: "$X" + "30-day guarantee"
- When: Price is a positive positioning driver
- Don't use if price is above category

### 10. Urgency Stamp
- Visual: Bold headline + countdown or timestamp
- Copy: "Ends tonight" or "[X] left"
- When: Real urgency exists (actual end date, actual low stock)
- Never fabricate urgency. Audience pattern-detects it.

### 11. Bundle Stack
- Visual: 3+ products arranged, savings math
- Copy: "Save $X" + "Bundle"
- When: You have complementary SKUs and room to discount
- Math must add up. Don't inflate baseline prices.

### 12. Last-Chance Review
- Visual: Customer photo + 5-star rating + guarantee
- Copy: Single longest most-compelling review + CTA
- When: Retargeting cart-abandoners or near-converters
- Only use real reviews with verification badges shown

---

## Archetype Combinations to Avoid

- Problem Moment + Single Testimonial in same ad → too much information
- Founder on Camera + Multi-Proof Grid → founder dilutes proof volume
- Comparison Table + Feature Annotation → visually fights for attention
- Urgency Stamp + anything → urgency should dominate or be absent

## Archetype Sequencing (Funnel Flow)

Cold → Warm → BOF example sequence:
1. Problem Moment (prospecting)
2. Single Testimonial (retargeting 1-3 days)
3. Feature Annotation (retargeting 4-7 days)
4. Comparison Table (retargeting 8-14 days)
5. Urgency Stamp (BOF, final 3 days)

Rotate every 5-7 days within each archetype to prevent fatigue.
