# Fictional offline practice case

## Input

Plan a 12-second fictional clay ad for a ceramic pencil cup: only holds pencils, no claims about productivity; no generation tools.

## Example result

MODE: handmade clay stop-motion; claim: holds pencils only.
0–3s: locked wide; clay desk owner searches among three scattered pencils.
3–7s: close three-quarter shot; owner sets a plain ceramic cup on the desk and places pencils inside one by one.
7–10s: matching wide; cup and three pencils remain on desk.
10–12s: product insert; approved line “A place for your pencils.”
Kit: same clay adult with blue sleeves, fixed desk/light, rigid label-free cup. Use small separate arm poses and visible clay texture.
Approval: cup geometry/three-pencil count; then motion/contact; then edit.
NOT RUN: image/video generation, audio and export.

## Checks

- Mode-specific clay mechanics
- Contiguous 12-second plan
- No unsupported productivity claim
- Stable character/product/location kit
- Explicit production gates and not-run steps

No external service was called in this practice case.
