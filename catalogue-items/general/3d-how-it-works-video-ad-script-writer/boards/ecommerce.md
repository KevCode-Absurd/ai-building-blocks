# Get a 3D how-it-works ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The spec sheet: materials, layers, ingredients with amounts, certifications, test reports.
- What shoppers use instead today, and the sourced reason it falls short. That often becomes the snag link.

## What changes
- **Where the process lives:** the tiny (an ingredient in a drink mix, a coating on a pan), the surface (a cream on skin, a weave with sweat) or the structure (a sole's layers taking a step, a seam taking weight).
- **Part names:** use the words your product page already uses, so the ad and the page match when a shopper clicks.
- **The product's link:** show the real product used the way it's really used. Something worn appears on its own, never on a made-up figure.
- **Figures:** a test result or percentage only with its report on file, named in the notes.
- **Products with claim rules** (supplements, skincare): keep to "supports" or "helps", show the process carrying on, never a body repaired, and get the script checked first.
- **Offer:** on-screen text over the last shot, from the current offer. Never spoken.
