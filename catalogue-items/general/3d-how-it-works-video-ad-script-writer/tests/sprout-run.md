## 5. Notes

**Inferred links for the brand to confirm**
- That the branded fiber blend in Sprout passes the upper gut largely undigested and reaches gut bacteria (inferred from "prebiotic").
- That "low-fiber meals leave them little" is acceptable wording for the snag.
- That the offer text is correct for Low Sugar Adults, 28 packs, and still live on the air date. Each discount claim is live at the same time on the site, so check which one applies.

**Claims left out**
- "61% of Americans experience weekly digestive issues": no real source given.
- "Fix the Hot Girl Tummy": "fix" breaks the "supports" rule.
- Survey figures (67% better digestion, 52% more energized): outcome claims beyond this chain.
- Study numbers and "Clinically tested for nutrient absorption": a different process.
- "Breaking down almost immediately" and "bioavailability": unsupported.
- "60+ ingredients" and "21 vitamins & minerals": a feature list, not part of this chain.
- Money-back guarantee: it covers the first order only, and it stays out of the ad.
- Star ratings: not used.

**Missing information**
- `MISSING: grams of sugar per pack` (not captured). Needed if the brand wants to answer "is the sugar a problem for my gut".
- `MISSING: breakdown of the 6 g fiber blend` beyond inulin.
