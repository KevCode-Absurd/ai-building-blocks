# Stop image tools redrawing your product and words: for online stores

**Extra things to give it**
- The product's truth sheet (`product-image-spec-writer`, "Product image spec writer"), and above all its lock lines. They go into the subject block and the keep line word for word.
- Five plain product photos by role: the pack front as sold, straight on; out of the pack; in a hand, for true size; in use; a close view of the surface. No text, badges or collage. A role with no photo is a gap to report, never one to fill from a render or a designed image.

**Extra rules for a store**
- Name the exact variant in the subject block, and describe the lookalike's opposite ("a stand-up foil pouch" rather than "not a jar").
- Give size against a hand, body or furniture, not in units, unless the image is a dimensions image. How much a pack holds says nothing about how big it looks.
- A shiny or mirrored product reflects only the scene it stands in.
- One use is one real amount, coming out of the real opening. Packs open only where they really open.
- With a pack photo attached, the keep line says the label stays as printed, and no hand covers it.
- Every image in a set shows the same pack shape, size and design.
- A visible change after use is a result claim, even with no words. It needs real evidence on file, never a convincing generated face.
