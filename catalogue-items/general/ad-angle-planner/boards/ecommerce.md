# For online stores: know how your ad should open and ask

Some settings change with how the product is bought:

| Setting | Used up (cream, snack, supplement) | Bought once (pan, jacket, bag) |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware: the opening moment | A daily routine that quietly fails | The moment the old one lets them down |
| Product aware: what to compare on | A dose, an amount, the price per day | A material, a size, a build, the price per year of use (only if the brand states how long it lasts) |
| Product aware: removing the risk | A money-back guarantee, how to pause or cancel | Returns, exchanges, warranty |
| Most aware: the one term | What it costs to start, plus the guarantee | The delivery date or the returns window |
| Market stages 3 and 4: the how | What's in it and what each part does | How it's made and why it lasts |

Image ads: for unaware and problem-aware buyers, the picture shows the buyer's moment, not the pack. From solution aware up, show the real product exactly as photographed.

If the org context names no doubt for the product, assume the one that goes with how it's bought: "will it work for me" for something used up, "will it fit and last" for something bought once. Label it as assumed.
