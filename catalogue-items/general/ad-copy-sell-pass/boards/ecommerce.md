# Ecommerce add-on: ad copy sell pass

**Facts to supply:** ingredient or materials list, how it is made, batch or origin facts, returns window and conditions, one-time vs subscription price, bundle per-unit price.

**Online-store checks**
- Price: a missing price makes people leave; state it with the offer's terms.
- Discount: a discount can bring poorer customers. For a premium product do not let it be the whole message; price signals quality.
- Trial: a starter size, guarantee or first-order price lowers risk better than a price cut.
- Picture: show the end result (the finished dish, the made bed) rather than the parts, and the product where it is really used.
- Proof: a demonstration of the product doing its job beats a describing word. Never imply an ingredient or process proves an outcome.
- Process fact: a verified ordinary process can make value concrete without being exclusive; never claim it is unique without evidence.
