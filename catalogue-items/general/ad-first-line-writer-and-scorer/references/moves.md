# Six moves for a hook

Every hook makes one move. The forms under each move are starting points: rewrite each in the buyer's words, and never fill a bracket with something you can't back. Example lines use Sprout, the example business, and only facts on its file.

## Which moves fit which buyer

| How aware the buyer is | What the hook must name | Lead with | Also fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware (doesn't see the problem) | Their moment or a feeling they'd know. No product, no offer. | Mirror | Gap |
| Problem aware | The problem, and that it can be solved | Mirror, Gap | Gain, Flip |
| Solution aware (comparing fixes) | What's different about this way of solving it | Gain, Gap, Flip | Mirror, Proof, News |
| Product aware (knows you, not sold) | New proof, a new fact or a new reason | Proof, News | Gain, Flip |
| Most aware (ready, waiting for a reason) | The deal, in its real terms | Gain (the deal), Proof | News, Timing |

## Mirror: they see their own moment
They stop because the line describes their day, or something they own, before it mentions you.
- **The moment:** a point in their routine and what goes wrong in it. "Morning, keys in hand, greens skipped again."
- **The object:** something they own that proves the problem. "If your shaker bottle has been in the sink since Monday..."
- **Their sentence:** a thing they'd say to themselves, taken from reviews. Said as a thought, never as a quote from a person who doesn't exist.
Needs: buyer notes with real moments and objects.

## Gain: what they get (the default)
Says plainly what the buyer gets, so the right person knows to stay.
- **The gain in their terms:** "Your greens, done before you reach the car." Only a gain the product really gives (here: tear the pack, eat the gummies, no water).
- **The preview:** "Here's a whole day of greens, from pack to palm." The ad must show it.
- **The deal:** for most-aware buyers only, in the offer's exact terms, with its conditions kept.
Needs: a gain or a demonstration the ad delivers.

## News: something true and new
A new product, flavour, price or finding. Only if the facts hold it, and only while it is still new.
- **The launch:** "New flavour: Raspberry Lemonade, in lemon-slice gummies." Only while that flavour is on sale.
- **The change:** a price or format that really changed, with the date it changed.
- **The finding:** a study or test result, said as news. It must be on file.
Needs: a dated fact.

## Proof: something they could check
Leads with a fact or a real voice.
- **The number:** a fact on file with its source. "Blood vitamin C up 40.1% in Sprout's 12-week study." Or, with its label kept: "80% of surveyed users take it daily."
- **The real line:** a review quoted exactly, shown on screen, with where it came from. "[Sprout] has been the one thing I've been able to stick to." (review on the brand site)
- **The live test:** something done in frame from start to end, without a cut.
Needs: the fact, the review or the footage.

## Gap: a question the ad answers
Curiosity is a gap the viewer believes will close soon. Use it only when the answer is the gain, and write the body line that closes it next to it.
- **The why:** why something they've noticed happens. "Why does the greens tub always end up at the back of the cupboard?" (only if the body answers it)
- **The doubt, asked:** their own doubt as a question. "How much fits in one small pack of gummies?" Closed by "60+ ingredients, 21 vitamins & minerals and 6 g fiber."
- **The hidden step:** a part of making or using it they never see.
- **The second thing:** "Two things to check before you buy greens. The second matters more." The body must give both.
Needs: a body line that closes it.

## Flip: a belief turned over
Names what the buyer believes, then the fact or buyer truth that turns it.
- **The usual belief:** what buyers are told or assume, turned. "Everyone picks greens by the label. Pick the one you'll keep taking." Backed by buyers who say the issue was never the nutrients.
- **Off the hook:** takes the blame off the buyer. "Maybe you're not bad at supplements. Maybe the powder is."
Needs: a fact or buyer truth behind the turn. Never a claim about a named rival.

## Add-on: Timing
Joins another move, only with a real date or offer end, quoted exactly. A season, a restock, an offer that ends. Never invent urgency. (Sprout has no end date on file, so it gets no timing hooks.)

## Who may say what

| Speaker | Can use | Can't use |
|---|---|---|
| AI-made presenter or narrator | Any move, in "you" or as an observer | "I tried it", "I was sceptical", any personal result |
| Real customer, own footage | Any move, and their own result | Claims the brand can't back |
| Real founder, own footage | Any move, plus what only the maker knows: a choice made, a step kept, a thing refused | Results they didn't measure |
| Real expert, own footage | Proof and Flip, with a credential that can be checked | Anything outside their field |
