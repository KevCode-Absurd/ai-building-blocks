# Ad Hook Opener Shape Bank

A library of opening shapes, each a way in and never a line to fill, plus rules for naming the right buyer fast and for founder-led openings.

## Inputs
- **Buyer read** (required): what they live with, the moment they are in, how they see themselves, what they want instead. From evidence only.
- **Stage** (required): awareness stage.
- **Speaker** (required): real founder, real customer, real expert, or generated presenter/narrator.
- **Proof on file** (optional): numbers, counts, results the business can show.
- **Occasion/niche** (optional): gift, sale, season.

## Steps
1. **Name the buyer in the first three seconds.** The opening is also the targeting: who stops is who the platform finds more of. Name them by what they live with, the moment they are in, how they see themselves, or what they want instead, using the buyer read only. A behaviour beats a demographic: "if you have a double shot before nine" beats "busy professionals". Never assert a condition the viewer has, least of all a health one ("why your partner ended up on the couch", not "do you snore?").
2. **Pick shapes** from `references/shapes.md`: stage-matched first, then any-stage; occasion shapes only for that occasion; one of each kind before a second. Skip shapes that need a real person if the speaker is generated, and shapes that need real proof if you hold none.
3. **Use a shape as a way in.** Say it wholly in the buyer's own words. Put nothing in a blank that the ad does not support. If the result could open any ad in the category, rewrite it.
4. **Founder openings** (only a real founder on their own footage; if a needed fact is missing, it becomes a question for the founder to answer on camera):
   - Fed-up maker: "I got tired of [problem], so I built [product]." Needs the real story.
   - Insider's trade: states a real checkable credential, then what the trade knows that buyers are not told.
   - Opposite practice: "Most [category] do [habit]. We do the reverse." Needs a true difference, about a kind of product, never a named one.
   - Low point rescued: a real hard moment and what turned it. Needs the true event.
   - Open workshop: "This is how we make [product]." Needs real footage.
   Rough shape of a 40-second founder video: problem or hook 5s, what was tried 10s, what changed 10s, what makes this different 10s, action 5s.
5. **Write the line.** Something a person says to a friend in one breath: aim for about 15 words, never over about 22. No offer or call to action for a cold buyer; no feature list early. Cut: really, very, just, actually, unique, amazing, incredible, high-quality, game-changing. Put a number the business holds in their place, or a customer's words, or nothing.
6. **Batch rule.** For one proven body, make about 10 hooks that each pull a different lever; 10 hooks that open the same way are one hook tested ten times. Change only the first three seconds in tests.

## Output
A table: hook text, shape kind, stage, needs (none / real person / real proof), and the on-screen version. Then a one-line test note per hook (what it tests).

## Checks
- No hook lets a generated presenter say "I tried it" or "I was sceptical".
- Every number or result matches proof on file.
- No two hooks use the same lever.
- No hook tells the viewer they have a health or body condition.

## If something is missing
Write `MISSING: real speaker` and move to second-person or narrator shapes. Write `MISSING: proof` and skip number-led shapes.

## Avoid
- Hype lines ("the secret they don't want you to know").
- Shapes tied to a year or a platform feature.
- Filling the blank in a shape with a claim you invented.
