# Ad performance drop diagnosis: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
Store orders and revenue for the same dates (first-party), stock status by product, price and offer changes, shipping and returns changes, checkout step counts, page speed on phone, refunds and returns by period (match tax, shipping and refund definitions across periods).

## Extra checks in the after-click step
- A best-selling product out of stock, a size or colour sold out, or a price or offer that changed after the ad was made.
- The ad's offer not matching the first buying option on the page.
- Checkout steps: where the count drops; payment methods and delivery estimate showing.
- Page speed on a phone since the last theme or app change.
- Discount codes that no longer apply.

## Store-level reading
- Use first-party revenue for store efficiency. Show platform-attributed sales separately; they can overlap each other.
- Blended efficiency: first-party revenue divided by total ad spend. Average order value: store revenue divided by orders.
- A rise in refunds or returns changes what a "good" return on spend means; check before calling a campaign profitable.
- Product feed or catalogue errors can look like a performance drop in catalogue-driven ads; check the feed's health before blaming creative.
