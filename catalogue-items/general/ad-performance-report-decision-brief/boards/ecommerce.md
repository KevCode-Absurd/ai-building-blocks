# Ad performance report: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
Store orders and revenue (first-party) for both periods, refunds, tax and shipping definitions, best-selling products and any stock-outs in the period.

## Extra lines for the KPI table
- **AOV:** store revenue / orders.
- **MER:** first-party revenue / total ad spend (use for blended store efficiency).
- **Refund rate** by period, because refunds change what a good return on spend means.

## Extra checks
- State whether revenue is gross or net of refunds, tax and shipping, the same way for both periods.
- Note any stock-out or price change that could explain a revenue move before crediting or blaming the ads.
