# Ad sales drop analyst: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
Your store's own orders and revenue for the same dates, stock levels by product, price and offer changes, shipping and returns changes, how many people reach each checkout step, page speed on a phone, and refunds and returns by period. Make sure tax, shipping and refunds are counted the same way in both periods.

## Extra checks when visitors click but don't buy
- A best-seller out of stock, a size or colour sold out, or a price or offer that changed after the ad was made.
- The ad's offer not matching the first buying option on the page.
- Checkout: where people drop out, and whether payment options and the delivery estimate show.
- Page speed on a phone since the last theme or app change.
- Discount codes that no longer work.

## Reading the whole store
- Use your store's own revenue to judge the store. Show what each ad platform claims separately, because platforms can claim the same sale.
- Overall return: store revenue divided by all ad spend. Average order value: store revenue divided by orders.
- A rise in refunds or returns changes what a "good" return on ad spend is. Check it before calling a campaign profitable.
- Errors in your product feed (the product list your ad platform reads) can look like a drop in catalogue ads. Check the feed before blaming the ads.
