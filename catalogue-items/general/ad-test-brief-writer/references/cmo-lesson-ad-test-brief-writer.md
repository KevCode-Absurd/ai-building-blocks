# Optional five-awareness message map

Use unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware and most-aware as planning labels when evidence supports them. Do not infer awareness solely from a click, open or demographic, and mark uncertain assignments.

Unaware may need an original curious scene and useful category explanation. Problem-aware needs an accurate description and possible route forward. Solution-aware needs supported differentiation and mechanism. Product-aware needs actual fit, objections and proof. Most-aware may need practical purchase information or a real offer; it does not require fake scarcity or a discount.

For each proposed brief show stage hypothesis, evidence, message job, proof needed and destination. Buyer stages are not a promise to cover all five every sprint. Use adjacent-stage alternatives where helpful, and leave unsupported cells empty. Claims about customer counts, fast results, savings or deadlines must still trace to real evidence.
