# Creative-state and deliberate sprint modes

For recurring work, add a dated creative-state record: active ad IDs, buyer stage/awareness, situation, blocking belief, evidence status, concept history and observed results. Counts describe the supplied set, not the whole market.

Declare the mode: PIVOT changes a weak argument; ITERATE changes a bounded execution; SCALE expands an actually supported result; PROMO serves real terms and dates; HOLD requests evidence or avoids a poor-value batch. Map these to the existing explore/extend/refresh choices and retain their evidence rules.

Before headlines, write the current belief, supported new understanding, customer moment, evidence and message job. Check whether copy blames the buyer for contextual friction. For a diversity sprint, variants differ on at least two of situation, message move, format or visual execution. Record the axes. A deliberate one-variable experiment remains a separate mode. Offer two or three suitable structures, not cosmetic recolours. Review the stage mix without forcing an old fixed percentage, and use stable IDs without invented history.
