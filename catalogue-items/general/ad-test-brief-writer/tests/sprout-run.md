## Judge list

- Met: The header grades the evidence, and anything missing (like a target) is written as MISSING, not guessed.
  "Evidence grade: Measured." ... "MISSING: T1 frequency and T1 cost per sale week by week." ... "No target was given, so 'winner' means best in this account"
- Met: Every idea names a doubt in the buyer's words and the fact that answers it. No fact, no idea.
  Idea 2: "A gummy can't hold enough to replace my powder." (brand file, quitter) ... held up by 60+ ingredients, the 2025 study, 95% survey.
- Met: No two ideas answer the same doubt.
  Idea 1 "Will it work for me?", Idea 2 "Will it work?", Idea 3 "Can I trust them?"
- Met: Within each idea, every version has its own first frame and says what else it changes.
  Idea 3: pouch with price stickers / 28-pack calendar grid / hand holding torn pack; each has "What changes:" line.
- Met: Every customer quote is word for word with its source, and no photo is named that you don't have.
  "My bowel movements are normal for the 1st time in 15 years!" Source: brand file ... every Photo file: "To be made".
