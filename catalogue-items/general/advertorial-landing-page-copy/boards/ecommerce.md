# Advertorial landing page: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Variant the ad showed, pack sizes, what is in the box.
- Returns window and who pays postage, warranty, delivery time and cost, subscription terms.

## What changes
- **Bought again (serum, snack, coffee, supplement):** the offer card shows the single pack, one-time against subscribe with the saving in money, and a starter or routine bundle. Say how long a pack lasts and how to pause or cancel in the store's own words. FAQ: taste or feel, who it suits, when a change is expected (only as the store states it), pausing.
- **Bought once (pan, jacket, luggage, mattress):** one price with a size or colour picker, what is in the box, the set that completes it, delivery date, returns window and warranty. FAQ: which size, what it works with, care, how a return works, what the warranty covers.
- **Risk line:** guarantee and cancel any time (again); returns and warranty (once).
- **Buy button follows the reader** from the product section onward on phones.
- **Images:** hero shows the ad's product and pack; a clean product shot beside the offer; a close-up of an ingredient, material or making step; customer photos only if real and cleared.
