# Ecommerce add-on: AI UGC video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front, in-hand and in-use photos; stated handling (how the pack opens, what comes out and its colour); `Visible result`; `Never show`.

- Handling decides the setup: a stick you tear and tip needs two hands, so the phone is propped.
- Before opening a pack, the contents' shape, colour and texture must come from a real out-of-pack or in-use photo; a label illustration does not show what is inside.
- A worn product is on the presenter from frame 1; its photo is named as what they wear.
- An applied product goes on the back of a hand or forearm unless the brand says more.
- With display-only evidence, keep the verified sealed front on a support; nobody holds, opens or uses it.
- Offer and price: on-screen text added afterwards from the current offer, not spoken by the presenter unless the approved script says so.
