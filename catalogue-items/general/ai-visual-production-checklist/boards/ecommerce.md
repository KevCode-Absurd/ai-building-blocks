# AI visual production checklist: online store add-on

## Photo jobs to attach (one per job)
Pack front (as sold, straight on); the product out of its pack; in a hand (true size); in use; a close view of the surface. Only plain photographs count: no text, collage or badges. Name the file for each job in the prompt. A missing job is a gap to report to the brand, never to fill from a designed image.

## Words repeated verbatim
From the product record: Looks like and Pack, in the same words in every prompt that shows the product. Also read: How it is used (steps as hand actions), Visible result, Moves and sounds, Never show.

## Product placement
- Use a sharp master reference.
- Scale: use verified dimensions plus a visible anchor (a hand, a countertop item). Capacity never establishes exterior size.
- Give it a feasible support or grip, and scene-consistent light, shadows, reflections, perspective and focus.
- Cleanup preserves original geometry and printing.
- Through motion, size, grip, rigid packaging and label identity stay coherent as reflections and occlusion change.
- Prominence comes from camera and framing, not from inflating the object.

## Sound for products
Plan the product's own sounds (tear, pour, click, zip). Mark any sound nobody has heard as "reasoned", not recorded.

## Before and after
A visible improvement after use is a result claim even with no text. It needs approved evidence, not a convincing generated face. A season gives context, never a new offer or benefit.

## Per-format judging
- Hook: first frame and whether the eye stays to the end of the motion.
- Demonstration: could a stranger say what happened.
- Proof picture: is the source visible and real.
- Product page image: is one buyer question answered without reading the page.

## Extra checks
- Product colour is its own colour, not the background's.
- The label is readable and never covered by a hand.
- Packaging opens only at its real opening.
- A set of images keeps one pack shape, size and design.
