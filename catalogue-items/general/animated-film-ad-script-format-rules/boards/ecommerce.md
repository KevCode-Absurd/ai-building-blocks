# Animated film ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photo and the exact label lines with their colours.
- How the product is used and a visible result, if the product page states one.

## What changes
- **Product framing:** hand, countertop or home scene beside the product, never the pack floating alone; label toward the viewer and readable.
- **Mechanism close-ups:** only what the product page claims (layers, fibre, a bead of water). No people in the frame.
- **Offer overlay:** price, bundle, shipping or guarantee as an editing overlay in current terms.
- **Character scenes for stores:** a kitchen, hallway, bathroom shelf or desk where the problem lives; pick the room from the customer's "moment" field.
