# Anime story ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photo with the label sharp, and the pack's exact label lines in order.
- The old way a shopper uses today (the thing it replaces), in the shopper's words.

## What changes
- **Draw the pack from the photo:** same shape, colours and label lines; keep effect lines off it. Never a lookalike pack for the rival.
- **Good devices for stores:** rival (a drawer of mismatched tools), routine shift (a morning with one pack on the counter), reveal (a launch).
- **Offer close:** price, bundle or guarantee as an editing overlay in current terms; nothing invented.
- **Reviews:** a buyer's words can appear as on-screen text with the source; the drawn hero never delivers them as a testimonial.
