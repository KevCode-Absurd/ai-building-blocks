# Ecommerce add-on: anime video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, product out of pack, how it is opened and used, current offer, "never show" list.

- Draw the product with its true shape, proportions, colours and label from the photo. A drawn treatment never redesigns the pack.
- Show use only as the brand states it ("tear the top, tip into water"). Put those steps in the beat where the hero uses it.
- A metaphor for the mechanism (a grey tower of chores, a spark of light) is fine; do not draw a body change, an ingredient doing something the claims do not state, or a result the brand has no evidence for.
- Keep the pack front toward the lens in the payoff shot, with room beside it for an offer line added in editing.
- Offers, prices and discount wording are on-screen text added later from the current offer; they are not drawn or spoken by the hero.
