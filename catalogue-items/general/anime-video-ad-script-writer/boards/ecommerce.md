# Get an original anime ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- A product photo with a sharp label, the label lines in order, and a photo of it being opened.
- The old way a shopper does it today (the thing your product replaces), in the shopper's words.

## What changes
- **Draw the pack from the photo:** same shape, colours and label lines, with effect lines kept off it. Never a lookalike pack for the old way.
- **Which exaggeration suits stores:** size for a cluttered old routine (a drawer of mismatched tools, a towering tub), feeling for a daily annoyance, speed for a product whose use is the fun part (a tear, a click, a pour).
- **Payoff pose:** a hand holding the pack, front to camera, with clear space beside it for the offer line added in editing.
- **Offer at the end:** price, bundle or guarantee as on-screen text in current terms. Nothing invented.
- **Reviews:** a buyer's words can appear as on-screen text with the source. The drawn hero never says them as a testimonial.
