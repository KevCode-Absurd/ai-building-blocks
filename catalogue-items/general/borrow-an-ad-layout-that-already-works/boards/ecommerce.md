# For online stores: Borrow an ad layout that already works

## Extra things to give it
- Product photos named by what each shows (front, out of pack, in hand, in use, close view) for every variant in the ad, plus the product's truth sheet if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`).
- The store's live offer (price, bundle, dates) and, for sale layouts, the range of products in the catalogue.

## Extra rules for a store
- The product must be exact: the right colourway, pattern, material and shape. No invented variant, no near miss. If a layout shows more items than the store sells, repeat the real one or show fewer. Several products in one frame are scaled to a common height, never cropped to fit.
- A studio shot doesn't tell you how the product looks worn, held or on a table. A scene of use needs a photo of use.
- A generated image is placed beside its reference photo and confirmed as the same product, zoomed in 3 to 7 times, before it passes.
- Offer layouts use the live offer in its own words. With no offer on record, pick a layout without one.
- Press, "as seen in" and "award-winning" layouts need real placements on file. Otherwise use a review or product-fact layout.

## Limits
- Sale and promo layouts are for people who already know the brand. Keep them to a small share of a set for people new to you (see "Get image ads strangers stop scrolling for").
