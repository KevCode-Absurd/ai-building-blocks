# Borrow a page layout: for online stores

## Section checklist for a typical store "reasons to buy" or article-style page
Tick only what the reference really has: announcement bar, hero with an author or product badge, press strip, alternating reason blocks (grid of small tags, before and after, timeline, image and text, certification block), testimonial row, guarantee, review-site section, closing band with an offer card, footer, sticky bottom bar.

## Slots on these pages that state a fact
Watch the top bar (a sale or deadline), any byline or badge in the hero, the press strip, results timelines and before-and-after blocks, ingredient and certificate blocks, customer photos, the guarantee, any review-site score, and the offer card at the bottom. Each one needs your own fact behind it, or it comes out.

## Extra things to give it
- The variant and pack shown, store policies (returns, shipping, warranty), the logo file, and footer contact details.

## Extra checks
- Check the finished page at phone width. Most store visitors arrive on a phone, and a desktop screenshot isn't a phone layout.
- Don't add tracking scripts while copying a layout.
