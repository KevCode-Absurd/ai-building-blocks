# Brand guide: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
For each product you advertise (main product first, four at most), its entry in `offers.md`: what it looks like, the pack, how it's used, what visibly changes, how it moves and sounds, what never to show, and its reference photos. From `voice-and-look.md`: the brand world (places, surfaces, light, people, what's never in frame) and motion and sound (pace, camera, sound, words on screen).

## Extra pages
Add these after Pictures:

- **Each product on camera.** A strip of the brand's own photos, each labelled with its job: pack front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use, close-up. Then how it looks, the pack, how it's used as numbered hand steps, what changes, how it moves and sounds, and what never to show, set in the guide's "don't" style. A field with nothing on file prints "not on file yet". A missing photo prints as an empty frame labelled with the shot it should be, so the gap can't be missed.
- **World and motion.** On one side: places, surfaces, light, people and what's never in frame, beside two to four of the brand's own photos. On the other: pace, camera, sound and words on screen. Use the brand's photos only. If there are none, the lists stand alone.

## Quick sheet additions
- Each product in two lines: how it looks, and the one thing never to show.
- The brand world in one line: where, what light, who.

## Extra check
Someone who reads only the product page would draw the right pack shape and finish. When two products look alike, the page names what tells them apart.
