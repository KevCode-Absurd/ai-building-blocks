# Brand knowledge folder: online store add-on

## Extra pages to read
Collections, 5 to 10 product pages, shipping and returns policies, size guide or ingredients and materials page, subscription terms.

## Extra inputs
- Store data: best sellers by revenue, repeat rate, products that get traffic but do not sell.
- Review export (any reviews app) or a paste of the reviews page.
- Email platform results: top subject lines, active flows, list size.
- Product photos. Pull them from the store's own product pages or catalogue feed, one folder per product, filenames in display order. Skip non-product line items (shipping protection, gift cards). If photos cannot be fetched, ask the user to drop them in.

## Extra fields per product
- **Kind:** how it is used (eaten, applied, worn, used with hands, placed in a room) and how it is bought (once, again when it runs out, subscription), with how long one unit lasts.
- **Made of / size and fit / in the box / lasts and care.** Used-up goods: ingredients with amounts, serving, pack size. Kept goods: materials, dimensions, weight, sizes, care, warranty.
- **Camera record**, written only from looking at the photos (never from a title or file name):
  - Looks like: shape, the product's own colour (not the background), finish, size against a hand, what it must never be mistaken for.
  - Pack: what it is sold in, colours, every large printed word and where it sits; versions that look different.
  - How it is used: steps as hand actions.
  - Visible result: what a viewer can see change, or "Nothing a camera can show" and what can be shown instead.
  - Moves and sounds: 2 to 4 real motions with their sounds (mark unheard sounds "reasoned").
  - Never show: mistakes particular to this product.
  - Reference photos: one per job.

## Photo jobs
Sort plain photographs (no text, collage or badges) by job: pack front, out of pack, in hand (size), in use, close up. Designed images stay in the folder but fill no job. Report any missing job to the user: those photos decide whether generated pictures and clips get the product right. A phone photo on a plain surface is enough.

## Extra file content
- `brand-guidelines.md`: **Brand world** (places, surfaces and props, light and time of day, who appears, what never appears) and **Motion and sound** (pace, camera, sound, on-screen text, formats in use), taken from lifestyle photos and 2 or 3 of the brand's videos. Write "Not captured" for what could not be watched.
- `products-and-positioning.md`: delivery (cost, free-over threshold, time), returns (window, who pays), guarantee (exact terms and exclusions), warranty (kept goods only), subscription (pause, skip, cancel).
- Customer profiles also carry **On camera**: what this person would be doing if filmed.

## Extra checks
- The hero product and every best seller have Looks like, Pack and How it is used written from photos, and at least two photo jobs filled.
- Every policy line quotes the site; "Not captured" otherwise.
