# Build trust with ten weeks of founder videos: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The products the founder can hold, open or demonstrate; how they're made or packed; one real customer message.

## What changes
- **Week 4 (inside the making):** film the real packing room, workshop or supplier visit. If the store doesn't make or stock its own products (for example, it drop-ships), tell the true story instead: how products are chosen and checked.
- **Week 6 (the myth):** use a claim your product facts support, such as how a material wears or what an ingredient does. Check it against your list of claims first.
- **Week 7 (a message I kept):** a customer review or email, with permission, read as written.
- **Reasons to post that aren't discounts,** to mix in: explain an ingredient or material, show the official way to use and care for it, answer a real question, show sourcing you can prove, tell a true team moment, help people choose between your real products.
- An unexpected use you found in a review needs the official compatibility checked before you show it.
