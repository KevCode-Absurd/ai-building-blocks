# Online store add-on: Buyer-Quote Static Ads

Extra inputs
- Product reviews exported from the store or review app with a flag for "received the product free", the reviewer name as published and verified-buyer status.
- The product photo for the taped-note format (a real photograph under the note).

Added rules for a store
- The two-sided scene uses objects (the old pan beside the new one, the old routine beside the new), never a body.
- The crossed-out line quotes the store's own product-page sentence nearest the buyer's phrase.
- Star ratings and review counts appear only if the store's review data holds them; a free-product reviewer is disclosed beside the quote.
- Pair the feed headline and description with the most common returns reason or pre-purchase question as the "doubt, answered" line.
