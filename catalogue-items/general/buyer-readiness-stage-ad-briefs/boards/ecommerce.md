# Buyer readiness stage ad briefs: online store add-on

Where a line differs for something used up versus something bought once:

| Stage / style | Used up (cream, snack, supplement) | Bought once (pan, jacket, bag) |
|---|---|---|
| Weighing this one: what to compare on | A dose, an amount, price per day | A material, a size, a build, price per year of use (only if the brand states how long it lasts) |
| Weighing this one: removing risk | Money-back guarantee, how to pause or cancel a subscription | Returns, exchanges, warranty |
| Ready: the single term to say | What it costs to start plus the guarantee | Delivery date or returns window |
| Explain the cause / Prove a better cause | What is in it and what each part does | How it is made and why it lasts |
| Not aware: the opening moment | A daily routine that quietly fails | The moment the old one lets them down |

Image ads: the still for stages 1 and 2 shows the buyer's moment, not the pack. From stage 3 up, the real product appears, exactly as photographed.

If the brand brain has no "what almost stops them" for the product, assume the first doubt of the way it is bought: "will it work for me" for something used up, "will it fit and last" for something bought once, and state the assumption.
