# Ecommerce add-on: carousels

**Extra inputs:** bundle contents and per-unit prices, sizes or variants, returns window, shipping thresholds.

**Store-specific card ideas**
- Range or bundle: one card per item, its job in the bundle, then the framed price. The per-unit or per-use price must come from the store's own numbers.
- Failed fixes for durable goods: the cheap one replaced twice, then the one that lasts, with the build fact (material, thickness, join) as the mechanism card.
- Before you buy: size or fit card, care-routine card (the real steps), returns card with conditions.
- What to expect: first use, tenth use, first season, only where the store can support it.

**Last card for stores:** action + what ships when + guarantee in the store's words, for example "Tap Shop now. Ships Monday. 60 days to send it back."
