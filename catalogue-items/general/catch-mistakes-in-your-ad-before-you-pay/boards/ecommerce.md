# For online stores: Catch mistakes in your ad before you pay

## Extra checks
- The product shown matches its photo (shape, colour, label) and is shown opening and being used the way it really does.
- The price, offer and any deadline in the ad match the store right now.
- The ad and its landing page make the same promise on the first screen.
- Any shipping, returns or subscription claim matches the store's own terms.
- The opening fits the kind of product. For things people buy again, show the result first. For things bought once, show the problem it solves or how well it's made.
