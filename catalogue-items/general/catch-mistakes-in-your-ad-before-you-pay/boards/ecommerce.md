# For online stores: Catch mistakes in your ad before you pay

## Extra things to give it
The product's real photos, and its truth sheet if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`), so each ad can sit beside the real thing.

## Extra stop-ship checks
- In step 6, check the product piece by piece against its real photos: variant, colour, every printed word, the lid or closure, the count, its size against a hand, and the place it opens.
- The pack shown is the pack being sold now, not an old design.
- A bundle or offer ad shows exactly what the order holds, no more and no fewer items.
- The price, discount, gift and any deadline match the store today, at checkout, not only on the product page. A holiday look doesn't make a sale or a delivery promise.
- A subscription price in the ad shows what it renews at, or the ad sends people to a page that does.
- Shipping, returns and guarantee lines match the store's own policy word for word.
- A before-and-after of a body, or a generated reviewer, fails as proof.

## Extra things worth a look
- The ad and the page it links to make the same promise in their first screen.
- For things people buy again, the result or the daily habit shows early. For things bought once, the build, the fit or the problem it solves shows early.
