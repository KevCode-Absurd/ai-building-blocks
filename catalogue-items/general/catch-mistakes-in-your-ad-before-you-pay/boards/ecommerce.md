# For online stores: Catch mistakes in your ad before you pay

## Extra stop-ship checks
- The product matches its photos in `offers.md`: shape, colour, label, size against a hand, and the place it opens.
- The price, discount, gift and any deadline match the store today, at checkout, not only on the product page.
- A subscription price in the ad shows what it renews at, or the ad sends people to a page that does.
- Shipping, returns and guarantee lines match the store's own policy word for word.

## Extra things worth a look
- The ad and the page it links to make the same promise in their first screen.
- For things people buy again, the result or the daily habit shows early. For things bought once, the build, the fit or the problem it solves shows early.
- The pack shown is the pack being sold now, not an old design.
