# CGI explainer ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product spec sheet: materials, layers, ingredients with amounts, certifications, test reports.
- The usual alternative in the category and the supported reason it falls short.

## What changes
- **Technical noun:** pick the material or part the product page already names (a fibre, a coating, a mineral, a layer), so the ad and the page share one word.
- **Product beat:** from beat 6, show the real product used as it is really used. A worn item appears real on its own, never on a generated figure.
- **Figures:** a test result or percentage only with its report on file; show the source in the notes.
- **Category examples:** a pan (oil baking into a film on hot steel), a wool top (fibre taking up vapour), a mineral drink (what the mineral does, as far as the claim goes), a water bottle (coating against bare steel).
- **Health-adjacent products:** use "supports" or "helps", show the process continuing, never a body repaired.
