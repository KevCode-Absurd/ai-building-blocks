# Ecommerce add-on: CGI mechanism explainer video ad

Extra inputs: clean pack photo (front, and out of pack), `Made of`, `How it works`, `Key features`, `Visible result` (if there is one, reconsider the format), claims register and forbidden claims.

- Mechanism hunting by product: a drink mix (what one ingredient does, only as far as the claim), a skin product (what it does at the surface), a fabric (what the weave does with heat or water), cookware (what the material does with heat or grease), luggage or shoes (how a joint or sole takes load).
- Categories with claim rules (supplements, skincare, anything for a condition) need the brand to read the script before pictures are made.
- Beats 6 and 7 use the pack in use as the brand states; the product is named only from beat 6.
- Offer and price: caption-style on-screen text in the last picture, from the current offer; never spoken.
