# For online stores: Get believed with a text someone else sent

**What the other person notices**
- Things people use up: the tub that's been open for months, the shaker still in the sink, the cupboard of bottles, "which ones do you actually take".
- Things people buy once: "is yours peeling too?", "did you get soaked again", "who took the good pan", "that's the third umbrella".

**One-to-one or group.** A partner or flatmate suits a habit only they see. A family chat suits a problem everyone has noticed for years.

**Build it, don't generate it.** Type the messages into plain bubbles in a design tool and export a square image. Every word stays exact and nothing looks like a real app.

**Product photos.** Send none. The product never appears in the chat.
