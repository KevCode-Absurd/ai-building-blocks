# Ecommerce add-on: cinematic 3D video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, product out of pack, how it is used, label copy line by line, "never show" list.

- Attach the pack photo to every shot that shows the product and write each label line with its colour (for example: a matte tube whose label reads LIP BALM in cream letters on teal).
- Put a hand, a countertop or a doorway in a product shot if you want it to read as animation rather than a product photograph.
- Mechanism shot example: a macro cross-section of a sneaker sole, three stacked foam layers, a soft wave of compression travelling through them. Use only the layers or ingredients the brand lists.
- For a consumable, show use in the brand's steps one action per clip.
- Prices and offers are on-screen text added in editing, never rendered into the scene.
