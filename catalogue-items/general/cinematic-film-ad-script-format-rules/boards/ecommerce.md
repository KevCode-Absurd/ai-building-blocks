# Cinematic film ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photos from several angles and the pack's exact label lines.
- How the product is really used, step by step, and its size next to a hand.

## What changes
- **Payoff shot:** the real pack or product in use, label to camera, held long enough to recognise. Never a different colourway or size from the SKU on the page.
- **Product role:** the product belongs to the turn (the gift opened, the pan lifting the egg cleanly), not a logo card at the end.
- **Offer text:** price, bundle, shipping or guarantee as a post-production overlay, using the store's current terms only.
- **Customer result:** a fictional character never "reports" a result. If a review quote is used, it appears as on-screen text with its source, not in a character's mouth.
