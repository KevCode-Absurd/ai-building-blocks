# Ecommerce add-on: cinematic photoreal video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, product out of pack, how it is held and used, visible result (if any), "never show" list, current offer.

- The pack photo fixes shape, size and artwork. Characters hold the real object; scale it against a hand or a mug.
- A "negotiation" or "ceremony" film works well for small daily products (a sachet, a lid, a cloth): treat the inconvenience as serious, resolve it with the product's real feature, end on the understated reaction.
- Show the product's visible result only if the brand states one ("nothing visible" means show the routine, not a result).
- Characters may doubt or react; they never claim a personal result or play a customer.
- Offer and price appear as on-screen text in the closing shot, from the current offer.
