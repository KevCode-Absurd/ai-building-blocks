# Clay town ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photo with the label sharp and every label line written out with colours.
- The shopper's moment: where and who else is there (a commute, an office kitchen, a dinner table).

## What changes
- **Product beat:** the clay product under a real printed paper label, so the shopper recognises the pack on the product page.
- **Scenes that suit stores:** a queue at a till, a shared kitchen, a gift being opened, a morning rush.
- **Offer close:** price, bundle or guarantee as narration or overlay in current terms; nothing invented.
- **Proof:** a crowd of clay faces is never reviews or sales volume. Use real review counts as on-screen text with their source.
