# Clay-town image ad: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Sharp photos of the pack's front, side and top, and every label line written out with its colours.
- The shopper's moment: where it happens and who else is there (a commute, an office kitchen, a dinner table).
- The live offer, its terms and its end date.

## Good fits
- Hassles that suit stores: the queue for the one good kettle, the school-gate rush, wrapping a gift at midnight, the gym bag that smells by Thursday.
- Everyday goods with a playful brand voice.

## Store rules
- The clay pack wears a printed paper label, so shoppers know the pack when they land on the product page. Lay the real pack, cut out from its photo, over it before the ad runs.
- A crowd of clay faces never stands in for reviews or sales. Use a real review count as a small line, with its source.
- Price and offer terms go on the image in editing, checked against the live offer that day.
