# Ecommerce add-on: clay world video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, label copy line by line, how it is used, product size against a person, "never show" list.

- Sculpt the pack in clay at its true shape and colours; print the label as real paper with every line stated, so no clay lettering appears.
- Handling uses clay hands with thick rounded fingers; keep the product opening to the brand's real steps.
- Playful look suits snacks, kids-adjacent adult products and giftables; refined look suits premium skincare, home and food.
- Crowd shots (a queue, an audience) are good for "everyone has this problem" openings; describe each face as distinct.
- Offer and price are on-screen text on the last scene, from the current offer.
