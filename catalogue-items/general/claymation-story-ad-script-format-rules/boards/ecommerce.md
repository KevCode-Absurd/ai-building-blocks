# Claymation story ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photo (front, label sharp) and real size, so the clay pack-to-character scale is right (a pack waist to shoulder high).
- The visible result on the product page, or a note that nothing is visible.

## What changes
- **Edge to object:** pick the one product benefit a store customer asks about (heat spread, water beading, fewer pots) and make the changing object that benefit.
- **Product photo:** use the real photo as the product in the frame, lit by the set; never redraw label letters.
- **Offer close:** price and guarantee as overlay text, current terms only; no invented scarcity.
- **Thumb-size test for a still:** at feed size the clay world and the single wrong or corrected thing must read before the headline does.
