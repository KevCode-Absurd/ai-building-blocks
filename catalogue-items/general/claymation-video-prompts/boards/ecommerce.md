# Claymation video shots: for online stores

## Give it as well
A photo of the pack front (required), every label line in order, the product out of its pack, how it's used, and your never-show list.

## Changing objects that work for stores

| What your product does better | Clay object that changes | Who or what moves |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer steps (one cleanser replaces a shelf) | a leaning stack of grey bottles that squashes down to one | the stack |
| Even heat (heavy-base skillet) | a pale clay patch under the pan that spreads warm orange to the rim | the patch |
| Keeps feet dry (waterproof boots) | blue clay drops that bead and roll off | the drops |

- Pick the product line once: a clay prop wrapped in the printed label, or the real photo lit to match the set. Keep its size against the character the same in every shot.
- No visible result: show the routine or a plain metaphor, never a result on someone's body.
- Offer and price go on screen over the last shots in editing, from the live offer.
