# Ecommerce add-on: code-rendered product offer reel

Extra inputs: pack-front on a clean ground (required), out-of-pack, in-hand, in-use photos; `Made of` and key features; bundle and offer terms with effective dates; guarantee wording; `Never show`.

- Reasons are brand facts with the photo that shows each: product out of its pack for what it is made of, in use for what it does.
- An offer reel names the offer only as the live offer states it (price, dates, terms); no added urgency or counters.
- A bundle reel shows each item from its own photo on one baseline, then the bundle price from the offer.
- End card: pack, logo and the action in the brand's words, 2 seconds or less, never static.
- Cut-outs need clean edges; add the contact shadow in the film, not in the photo.
