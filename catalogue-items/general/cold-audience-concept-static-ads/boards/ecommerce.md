# Online store add-on: Cold-Audience Concept Static Ads

Extra inputs
- Product photos by job (pack front, out of pack, in hand, in use) and a truth sheet if available (`product-truth-sheet-from-photos`).
- Visible result recorded by the brand, to bound how much the picture may show the product doing.

Added rules for a store
- Product-truth test for goods: the argument ends in what it is made of, how it works, what it replaces or the guarantee. If every competitor could say it, the ad sells the category.
- Used-up products (consumables) are decided quickly, so lean on the daily moment. Bought-once products (cookware, luggage, furniture) are decided slowly, so give Evaluation arguments more room: price per year of use, fit, materials, what happens if it is wrong.
- Buyers who are comparing or ready to buy get different ads: one kind per ad (a real customer quote, a fair comparison, a price said plainly, a deadline), each with its real evidence; quote ads use a word-for-word customer quote with no face; labels on a product are 4 at most, a button 3 words or fewer. Cold sets keep offer ads under one in ten; a set for ready buyers can be all offer ads.
- A made-up quote or deadline is worse than no ad. If the facts do not hold what the kind needs, say so and make the nearest honest ad.
- If the layout shows more variants than the store sells, repeat the real one or use fewer, scaled to a common height.
