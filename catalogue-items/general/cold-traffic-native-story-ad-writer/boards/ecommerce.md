# Cold-Traffic Native Story Ad Writer: add-on for online stores

**Account balance:** run this beside direct ads (product, benefit, offer, button). The cold story finds new people; direct ads close the ones who now know the brand.

**Fit by product**
- Pains stores can use: a stained collar, a loose seam, a pan that sticks, hair in the brush, lint in the dryer filter.
- Fashion bought because it is beautiful has no pain; write a direct ad. Garments fixing something (sweat-proof, compression, insoles) qualify.

**Destination:** an article-style advertorial that teaches the problem, with the product and offer near the end. If none exists, drafting it comes first.

**Product in the picture:** none. If a story truly needs the product in frame, use the real one from its reference photos, small and to one side.
