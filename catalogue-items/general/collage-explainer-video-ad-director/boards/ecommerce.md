# Ecommerce add-on: collage explainer video ad

Extra inputs: product photos by job (front, out of pack, in use), the brand's stated mechanism, supplied figures (sizes, weights, counts) with sources.

- Cut the product out in full colour from its photo, front on, and make it larger in frame if its label blurs.
- Storyboard example for a travel mug: "Most mugs leak at the lid." (cutout of a mug lid, ring round the seam) / "This one seals in two places." (diagram of two rings, arrow from a blank strip to each) / "So it rides upside down in a bag." (old mug slides off left, the product slides to centre).
- Compare with "the old way" or "the category", never a named rival.
- Prices, offers and guarantee text are set in editing from the current offer, on the last scene.
