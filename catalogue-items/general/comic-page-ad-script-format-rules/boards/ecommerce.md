# Comic page ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photo with label sharp (the one photographic element) and its true colour for the accent.
- The "visible result" note from the product page, or "nothing visible".

## What changes
- **Accent colour:** use the pack's own colour so the turn lands on the shelf image the shopper will see next.
- **Nothing-visible products** (supplements, skincare that works over weeks): the changed panel is a changed evening or morning, never a measured outcome.
- **Photo placement:** the real pack photograph overlaps the last panel's edge; keep the label legible at thumb size.
- **Offer strip:** price, bundle or guarantee in the store's current terms, set in the brand's typefaces under the last panel.
- **Reviews:** a buyer's thought bubble may use one word-for-word phrase from a verified review, 6 words or fewer.
