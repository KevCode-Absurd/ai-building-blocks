# Ecommerce add-on: comic page desk video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo and product-out-of-pack photo (both on every still and clip), pack colour, how it is used, visible result, "never show" list.

- The real product stands or lies on the desk beside the page the way its photo shows it: a pouch upright, a pan flat with handle out, a garment folded.
- Accent colour: the pack colour. If the pack is black, white, grey or multi-colour, use the brand's main colour.
- Beat sheet example for a lip balm: page 1 a woman at a desk with a cracked-lip wince, then the same woman outdoors in wind, worse; page 2 diagram of the stick in cross-section (wax, oil, three empty tags), then the balm arrives and the accent with it; page 3 the same scenes, comfortable.
- "Visible result: nothing visible" means the turn page shows the routine going differently, not a measured outcome.
- Prices and offers: on-screen text on the final product frame, from the current offer.
