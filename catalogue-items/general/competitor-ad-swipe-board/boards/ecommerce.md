# Swipe board: online store add-on

## Extra bucket
Add one bucket for your own product category, filled with category-typical ads that fit no other bucket (for example `supplements` for a supplement brand, `cookware` for a pan brand, `outerwear` for a jacket brand). Leave it out if you cannot name the category.

## What changes
- **Set** is a bundle, kit, duo or routine; **deal** includes percent off, buy-one-get-one, free shipping with a threshold, and gift with purchase.
- **Gift:** require explicit recipient or occasion framing ("gift for him", "stocking filler"). Brand-awareness ads that only mention a holiday do not qualify. If your own category has no gift ads, look at jewellery, beauty and candles for the structure.
- **Skip as non-creative:** ads whose link goes to a blog post and whose image is a blog thumbnail; boosted personal posts with no product call-out; publisher or news pages.
- **Near-duplicates:** treat as one ad when advertiser and landing page match (strip tracking parameters), or the first 80 characters of text match, or the image is identical. Keep the longest-running version.
- **Re-running the board:** list existing IDs first and add only new ads.
