# Competitor teardown: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Your hero products, price band, current offers and which are being pushed this month.

## What changes
- **Brief purpose changes the search:** one product means weight your search to competitors selling that kind of product; a sale means weight toward similar offer shapes (buy-one-get-one, percent off, free gift).
- **Offer mechanics worth recording per ad:** the offer wording, whether the saving is in money or percent, any deadline, any gift, subscription terms.
- **Ingestibles and skincare:** record how claims are phrased and flag any that your own claims register would not allow, so recreation notes avoid them.
- **Landing page:** note where the ad's link goes (product page, collection, advertorial) because format and page type often pair.
- **Paid ads only:** public ad libraries show paid ads; organic posts need a different method (see `founder-video-series-plan`).
