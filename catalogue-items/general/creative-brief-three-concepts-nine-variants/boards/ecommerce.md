# Online store add-on: Creative Brief, Three Concepts, Nine Variants

Extra inputs
- Product price, visible result and reference photos per product; what is live (promotions ended, products out of stock).
- Used-up or bought-once, per product, because it moves the work between stages.

Worked belief pairs for physical goods (illustrative)
| Product | Belief in the way | Belief to install | Held up by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A bar of soap, used up | "Handmade soap dries my skin out." | "A gentle base keeps the lather soft." | Ingredient list, review count |
| A carbon-fibre carry-on, bought once | "A light case cracks in the first year." | "The shell flexes and recovers, and a crack is covered." | Warranty terms word for word |
| A standing mat, bought once | "I'll stop using it after a month." | "It is comfortable enough that you forget it is there." | A real review, return terms |

Added rules for a store
- Bought-once products get at least one Evaluation concept (price against years of use, fit, materials, returns and warranty).
- Offers are stated only as in the live offer file; dates and limits are real.
