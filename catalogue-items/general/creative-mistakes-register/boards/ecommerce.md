# Creative mistakes register: online store add-on

## Product-use lessons are brand lessons
For physical products, "how it is opened, held and used" is the largest source of repeat mistakes, and every answer is specific to one product. Record each in that brand's register and in the product's record (how it is used, never show), for example:

- "The sachet is torn straight across its top edge. Never from the side, the bottom or a corner."
- "The jacket zips from the hem up. Never shown zipped from the collar down."
- "The pan's non-stick face is the inside; never shown cooking on the outer base."

These three are illustrative wording only. Real entries come from the brand's own feedback.

## Where the facts come from
Per product in the brand brain: how it is used, never show, looks like, pack. If a product has no recorded opening or use, the first lesson applies: show it closed or at rest, and ask the brand.

## Extra checks
- A pack, part or label described in a prompt uses the same words every time it appears in a set.
- A reference photo outranks a description written from file names; where they disagree, ask.
- Photos of the product show it as sold; the colour of the product is its own colour, not the colour of the background it was photographed on.
