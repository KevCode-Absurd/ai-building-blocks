## 3. Disclosure line and how to check it

**Wording to give every creator:** say plainly, out loud and on screen, that Sprout sent it to you for free. Example: "Sprout sent me this for free."

**How to check each post:**
1. Watch the video with sound on.
2. Confirm the disclosure is spoken in the video and shown as on-screen text, in plain words.
3. A tag, hashtag, caption or line under the video does not count on its own.
4. If it's missing or unclear, put the post in **Fix first**. Ask the creator to fix it, and don't share or run the post until they have.

**Groups:**
- **Ask to run:** the post is disclosed, above the creator's own median, and has at least one buyer comment. Ask for platform permission and a written agreement.
- **Fix first:** the post has no clear disclosure. Ask the creator to fix it, and don't share or run it until then.

**Running a winner:**

3. Record both before the ad goes live.
4. Run the ad from the creator's account, keeping their caption and disclosure as posted.

**After this round:** write down how many creators posted, how many posts you'd run, and the cost per usable video. Use those numbers to size round two.
