# Creator seeding: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- The product to send, its value and postage cost, stock available for gifting, the store link or code creators may use.

## What changes
- **What ships:** the real product as sold, with the note on top. Add a small insert only if it costs little.
- **Cost check:** work out cost per creator (product, packaging, postage) before choosing how many to send.
- **Commission link:** if you pay a share of sales, give each creator a unique link or code so sales can be traced.
- **Product claims:** health, results and "before and after" claims stay in the claims register. Creators describe their own experience.
- **Ads from winners:** ask permission, then run as a partnership ad from their handle; keep their original caption and disclosure.
