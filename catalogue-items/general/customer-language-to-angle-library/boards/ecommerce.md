# Customer language to angle library: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
A review export from any reviews app with: rating, title, body, date, product, verified-purchase flag, helpful votes, and "received free product" if present.

## What changes
- **Rating filter:** keep 5, 4 and every 3 star. A 3-star reviewer is lukewarm but took the time to explain, and the explanation usually mirrors what holds a first-time buyer back. Read 1 and 2 star for gripes and refund reasons.
- **Verified first:** the best phrase is a verified purchase where one exists. Unverified phrases are shown but marked. Reviewers given the product or paid are never the best phrase and must be disclosed wherever quoted.
- **Split by product** once the store has more than four products; angles do not transfer cleanly.
- **Volume:** a few hundred reviews per pass; over about 1,000, split by product.

## Where each role earns its money
| Role | Use in a store |
|---|---|
| Starting point, Spark | Cold-traffic hooks, welcome email, seasonal campaigns |
| Past fixes | Us-versus-them ads, comparison sections on the product page |
| Hesitation | Product page FAQ, cart abandonment message, "this is not for you if" ads |
| Rough patch | First post-purchase email at day two, refund prevention |
| In their words | Mid-funnel education, advertorial lead |
| Turning point | Review-led ads, subject lines |
| What stayed | Retargeting, winback, repositioning |
| Gripe | Pre-empting ads, FAQ, product roadmap |
| Pass it on | Cross-sell, gifting, subscription |

Product page: mechanism and "what stayed" become bullets; hesitations and gripes ranked by count become the FAQ. Review-led static ads: one angle per image, the quote in quotation marks with its source.

## Extra legal flags (store context)
- A quoted result is still a claim the brand must hold; supplements, skincare and health products go past whoever signs off claims.
- An unusual result (one reviewer lost a large amount of weight) is not shown as typical.
- Before-and-after imagery and personal-attribute claims are restricted by ad platforms whoever said them.
- An unexpected use belongs under "What stayed" with the product and source ID. Check the product's official use and compatibility before proposing an ad or demonstration; a customer's experiment is not permission to recommend the use.
- Hand a PDP doubt to the page writer as: the question, the number of distinct sources, the source IDs. Word overlap alone does not show the page already answers it; read the actual answer.
