# Match customer doubts to your page: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The product page's gallery images in order, plus how the size or variant picker looks in each state.
- Return reasons from the store's own records, if any.

## What changes
- **Where store questions usually belong:** size or compatibility (the picker, an image showing scale), contents (beside the price, an image of the contents), material or ingredient (a close-up image or an explanation), use and care (an instructions image), returns and warranty (under the add-to-cart button), delivery cost and time (the buy box or delivery selector).
- **Gallery rows become image briefs:** one question, one supported answer, the source IDs, and the real product photo as the reference. An illustration of the product in use doesn't prove a test or customer result happened.
- **Keep every fact the same** across the ad, the page and the gallery. Don't use this skill to build an ad scorecard or a sale plan.
- **Fit and compatibility answers** need real measurements and the limits of what it works with, not a generated demonstration.
