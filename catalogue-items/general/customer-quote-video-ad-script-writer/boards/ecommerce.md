# Video ad script from customer quotes: for online stores

**Where each timeline point hides in store data**
| Point | Look in |
|---|---|
| Before | Reviews that start "I used to..." or "Before this I...", and product-page questions |
| Trigger | Post-purchase survey answers to "what made you buy now?" |
| Tries | Reviews that name a kind of product they switched from |
| Last doubt | 2 to 4 star reviews, pre-sale chat logs, abandoned-cart replies |
| After | 5-star reviews written after a few weeks, and reorder notes |

**Reordered or kept.** Say which, because it changes four beats.
| Beat | Reordered (eaten, applied, used up) | Kept (worn, cooked on, carried) |
|---|---|---|
| Doubt | Will it suit me? Will the habit stick? Is stopping easy? | Is it worth the price? Which size? Will it last? Can I send it back? |
| Proof | The first use on camera; people who came back for more | The product put through a test; how it's made; how long owners have had theirs |
| Offer | The first order, and what it costs after | One price, set beside the thing it replaces or spread over its years of use |
| Call to action | Start | Pick the right one |

In store data: reordered means subscription and reorder rate, pack size, first-order price and the real steps to pause and cancel. Kept means size and fit data, returns policy, warranty, and how long customers say theirs has lasted.

**Where "how it works" and proof come from:** the materials list, how it's made, what was tried and dropped while making it, test results the store holds, a demonstration the store can film, reorder numbers, and review counts or the word buyers use most (only if counted).

**Offer terms to say out loud**
| Term | Say it plainly | Careful |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | What it costs, how often it arrives, the steps to pause or stop | "Cancel anytime" only if it's true and easy |
| First-order price | Today's price and the price after | Conditions in small print (a sign-up, a code) are said aloud |
| Bundle | Price per unit or per day, and why that amount | "Save 20%" alone tells the buyer little |
| Guarantee | How long, what they do, what they get back | Say "first order only" or "unopened only" if that's the rule |
| Returns and size | How long, whether used items qualify, how they find their size | Never guess a fit |
| Warranty | How long and what it covers | It isn't a lifespan claim |
| Shipping and reason for now | A date the store can keep; stock, batch, season or a price change | It has to be true |

**A rule from refunds and returns:** if they come from people who expected something the product doesn't do, no beat may hint that it does.

**In the feed.** Many people start with sound off, so the first frame and overlay must work silent. Shoot vertical first. Hold the end card (action, price, guarantee) for about two seconds; the word budget already leaves room for it.

**What each format needs from the script**
| Format | The script supplies |
|---|---|
| Hired creator | Lines they can say honestly, brackets for their own details, delivery notes, extra shots, openings to record, lines never to say |
| AI presenter | Who the person is, one shot per line, the exact words per clip, the product's real photos as reference, an adult |
| Animation | A character and a world, one picture for how it works, a scene per beat; it can act out the problem but never invent proof |
| Long sales video | A picture per sentence, the second round of how it works and proof, proof items shown on screen |
| Founder | The real story of starting, and what the founder shows on camera |
| Image ads cut from it | Opening as the overlay, how it works as the subline, proof as a badge, offer as the last card |
