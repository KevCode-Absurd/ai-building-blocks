# Cutout argument ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Clean product cutout (transparent background) from the real photo, with label sharp.
- A photo of the old way or the category's usual product, to cut out as the comparison.

## What changes
- **Thesis ideas for stores:** why the usual version fails (the old pan, the flat sheet, the clear bottle), what the product is made of, what a bundle contains.
- **Comparison:** the usual product as a plain cutout labelled "the usual way" next to yours. Never a rival's pack or name.
- **Charts:** only figures from lab tests, review counts or measured specs on file, with the source in the notes.
- **Offer close:** price, bundle and guarantee as on-screen text in current terms; the cutout large and still.
