# A quiet photo of what buyers dread: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Paperwork a store's buyers dread**
- Something people buy once: the receipt for the third replacement, a repair quote, a "return window closed" email on a phone face up.
- Something people subscribe to: the renewal charge on a card statement, after the first-order deal.

**The step** in the story is something the buyer can check before paying: the real renewal price or the guarantee terms. Never a promise the fear won't happen.

**Product photos:** send none unless you want the product in the picture. If you do, use the real one, small and to one side.
