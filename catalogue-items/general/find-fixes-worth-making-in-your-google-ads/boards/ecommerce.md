# For online stores: Find fixes worth making in your Google Ads

## Extra module: Shopping and the product feed
- **Extra data it needs:** results per product (SKU) with category, the product facts and feed fields behind each SKU, and policy or disapproval status.
- **What you get:** fixes to the feed, titles and images, plus products to look into, all based on the product facts. Never add a claim to a title that the product page doesn't support.
- **Check:** compare the feed's price and stock status with the product page. A mismatch can get products disapproved and waste spend.

## Performance Max for a store
These campaigns run from the product feed. Review asset groups per product group. Before reading the total return, check that searches for your brand and product names are kept apart from other searches.

## Extra reading rules
- Total your store's own revenue and the sales Google takes credit for separately. Some overlap between channels is expected.
- Count returns and refunds when deciding what a "good" return is.
- When the range is wide, winners and losers per product matter more than campaign averages. Check each product has enough volume before judging it.
