# Find offers hiding in what you already sell: for online stores

## Also give it
- Each product's price and pack size, and for things that run out, how many days one unit lasts.
- Sizes or bundles you sell only in shops or on marketplaces.
- The free-delivery level, usual delivery time, returns window and any warranty.
- Subscription terms: the renewal price, how often it bills, how to skip or cancel.
- Your average order value, if you know it.

## Offer shapes most stores already hold
- **Runs out (food, skincare, refills):** the price per day; a longer supply at a lower unit price; a trial pack; a reward on order two; a subscription only if the store already sells one.
- **Bought once (gear, clothing, home):** a matching set; a care kit; a second one to give away; size swaps made clear; the warranty in the headline.
- **Any store:** free delivery set just above the usual order; a named gift with its real price; first look for the email list; a seasonal offer tied to a real shipping cut-off.

## Extra checks
- The store can fulfil it today: ship it, send a download, grant access or do the work.
- A free-delivery level sits above the average order, with the sum shown.
- A bundle shows its saving one way, in money or as a percent, never both.
- The price shown at checkout for a subscription matches the renewal price in the offer.
