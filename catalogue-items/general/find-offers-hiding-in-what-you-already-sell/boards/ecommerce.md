# Find offers hiding in what you already sell: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
Product prices and pack sizes; whether each product gets used up or is bought once (and how long one unit lasts); the free-delivery level and delivery times; the returns window and warranty; subscription terms (if you sell subscriptions).

## Store offers worth proposing
- **Used up:** a longer supply at a lower price per unit; a trial size; a subscription (only if the store already sells one); a reward on the second order.
- **Bought once:** a pair or one to give; a set that completes it; a care kit; a trial at home or an exchange for size; the warranty as the headline.
- **Either:** free delivery over a level just above the typical order; a gift with a named listed price; early access for the email list; a seasonal deal tied to real shipping cut-off dates.

## Extra checks
- The store can deliver it: shipped, downloaded, access granted or done for the buyer.
- A first-order price states the ongoing price in the same sentence.
- A bundle shows the saving once, as money or as a share, never both.
