# For online stores: Find the story ad worth writing first

**Extra things to give it**
- Customer reviews with star ratings, dates, and whether each customer agreed to ad use.
- A note on how much your market already hears the same message (how many rivals say the same thing).

**Fit examples**
- Used up: a cream, a supplement, a pet food. Look for the shelf of half-used things.
- Bought once: a pan, a jacket, a suitcase. Look for the ones thrown away.

**Rough starting figures for a test set.** These are illustrations, not measured and not any brand's numbers. Replace them with your own after one set has run.
- A cost per 1,000 views of about $30 to $40, and under $60.
- 20 to 25% of viewers clicking anything, and about 2.5% clicking through to the page.
- Run one campaign with one ad set per tension, and judge the first 3 days on potential, not profit.

**Button:** "Shop now" to the product page, or "Learn more" when it leads to an article-style page.
