# 30-point landing page check: for online stores

## Extra checks for a product page
- The variant picker and size guide can be reached in the first screen when the product can't be bought without them.
- The returns window, who pays return postage, delivery time and shipping cost are on the page, near the price.
- For something bought again: what's in it, how to use it, how long a pack lasts. For something bought once: dimensions, materials, what's in the box, care.
- Reviews with photos come first where the store has them; no default rating.
- Subscription terms (how often, the saving in money, how to cancel) are stated before purchase.

## Thresholds
- Shipping, tax and fees added at checkout stay under about 10% of the order value (a rule of thumb).
- Generic security seals and footer trust badges don't count as proof.
