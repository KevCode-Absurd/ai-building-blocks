# Landing page that finishes your ad's sale: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The variants (size, colour, flavour) and which one the ad showed.
- What's in the box, item by item.
- Shipping cost and speed, any free-shipping minimum, the returns window and who pays return postage, warranty terms.
- Subscription terms: how often it ships, the first and later prices, how to pause or cancel.
- Your top pre-sale questions from support or chat, with where each came from.

## What changes on a product page
- **The buy area sits in the first phone screen,** right under the gallery: the picked variant, its price from the store, the button and the risk line. When it scrolls away, a button that follows the shopper keeps the next step in reach.
- **The gallery answers doubts too.** Order the photos so they show what it is, what's inside or what it's made of, the product in use, and its size next to something familiar.
- **Used up and bought again:** pack or bundle choices ("most popular" only if true), one-time against subscribe with both prices visible, a price per day only if the facts give it, and how to pause or cancel in the store's own words.
- **Bought once and kept:** one price, a size or colour picker with the size guide one tap away, a delivery date range, the returns window and the warranty. Never "3 of the same item".
- **Bundles:** for something used up, a set where each item has a job in the routine. For something kept, the set that completes it (a lid and a care kit with a pan). State what the bundle saves.
- **Fold-out panels near the buy area:** used up: ingredients, how to use, shipping and returns. Kept: dimensions and materials, what's in the box, care, shipping and returns, warranty. Keep the price, renewal price and returns line outside the panels, where they're always seen.
- **Shipping cost** goes near the buy area but not inside it, with any free-shipping minimum spelled out beside it.
