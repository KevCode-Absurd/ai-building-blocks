# For online stores: finish your sales page with proof and one ask

## Extra things to give it
- The returns window and who pays for return shipping.
- The subscription terms and the real steps to cancel.
- The price after a first-order discount ends.
- The price per item in bundles.
- Any true reason to buy now: a stock level, a batch, a season.

## Rules for the close in an online store
- Show the one-time price beside the subscription price, and say how to cancel in the real steps.
- A renewal price that surprises buyers comes back as refunds. State both the first price and the renewal price.
- Name every guarantee condition (first order only, unused, in its box). A warranty period is not a promise of how long it lasts.
- Reasons to buy now must be real: stock level, batch, season, a price change. Otherwise leave them out.
- Bundles: tie the quantity to how the product gets used up (for example, a 3-month supply).
