# For online stores: Fix what's wrong in finished ads before launch

## Extra things to give it
The product's real photos, and its truth sheet if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`), to compare with each finished image side by side.

## Extra rules for a store
- Check the product first: the right variant, colour, printed words, lid or closure, count, and size against a hand. A wrong product fails the ad however good the design is.
- Check pack counts. The items shown in a bundle or offer ad must match what the order contains.
- Check prices, savings and dates against the live offer. A seasonal look doesn't create a sale or a delivery promise.
- A before and after of a body, or a generated reviewer, fails as proof.
