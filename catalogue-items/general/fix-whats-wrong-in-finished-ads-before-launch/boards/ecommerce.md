# For online stores: Fix what's wrong in finished ads before launch

## Extra things to give it
The product's real photos, and its truth sheet if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`), so each finished image can sit beside the real thing.

## Extra rules for a store
- In step 2, check the product piece by piece: variant, colour, every printed word, the lid or closure, the count, and its size against a hand.
- A bundle or offer ad shows exactly what the order holds, no more and no fewer items.
- Prices, savings and dates match the live offer. A holiday look doesn't make a sale or a delivery promise.
- A before-and-after of a body, or a generated reviewer, fails as proof.
