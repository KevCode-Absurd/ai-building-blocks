# Founder decision ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- A "made this way because" list of 2 to 5 decisions per product: what was chosen, what was turned down, and why, in the founder's words.
- Footage or photos on file of the workshop, packing area, testing or sourcing.

## What changes
- **Good decision topics for physical goods:** the fabric or steel chosen and the cheaper one rejected; the number of samples turned down; the dose or amount and why that amount; the part that costs more and stayed; the step in the making that looks boring.
- **Cutaways to film:** the product cut open, rejected samples beside the final one, raw material in a hand, the test, the label at the moment it is mentioned.
- **Still note:** place the real pack beside the note on the bench, label sharp. Put the guarantee in its real terms as the second line.
- **Proof pairing:** each founder claim gets a review count, a lab test with its source or a demonstration, or it is softened.
- **Opening hooks:** "I was sick of X, so I built it" suits a founder who was a customer first; "the process, shown" needs real workshop footage.
