# Founder interview: online store add-on

## Extra pre-call asks
Ask the founder to have the product, in its packaging, on the desk. Request five photos per product before the call (below) and any size, materials or ingredients documents.

## Product questions nothing else can answer (always ask for the main product, then each product to be advertised)
- **Looks like (core).** Take it out of its packaging and hold it. Shape, colour, shiny or matt, soft or hard, size next to your hand (or on a person, if worn).
- **Pack.** What it arrives in (box, pouch, bottle, jar, bag, sleeve), the colour, and the words printed largest on the front, top to bottom.
- **How it is used (core).** Show it one step at a time, as if we had never seen it: where it opens, what comes out or goes on, what you do, how long it takes, what is left or put away. Ask the founder to hold it to the camera and use it once, narrating. Write down the order and which hand does what. The recording is a handling reference, not ad footage, unless the founder agrees on camera use.
- **Moves and sounds.** Two to four real movements (pours, tears, clicks, fizzes, folds, zips) and the sound of each.
- **Visible result.** How long before a customer notices, and could a camera see it now, in a week, in a month, or is it only felt? "Nothing to see" is a useful answer.
- **Never show.** The most common wrong use, and what pictures of it usually get wrong (held, opened, worn or served in a way that never happens; wrong amount, colour or size).
- **Five photos (core).** Pack from the front as sold; out of the pack; in a hand or on a person; in use; a close view of the surface. Say which you do not have.

## Used up versus bought once
Listen for different answers:

| Question | Used up (cream, snack, supplement) | Bought once (pan, jacket, bag) |
|---|---|---|
| What they notice and when | Often felt, not seen, after days or weeks | Often seen at once (fit, finish), and again after a year |
| How it is used | A short daily routine | First use, care, storage differ |
| Guarantee, returns, warranty | Money-back on the first order | Returns, size exchanges, a warranty in years; ask how often each is used |
| After the first order | How long one unit lasts, subscription, pausing | How long it lasts, and what they buy next (second colour, accessory, a gift) |
| Why people leave | Stopped using, cancelled, did not reorder | Sent it back, never bought the second thing |
| What the sale is lost to | Doing nothing or the brand they already buy | Keeping the one they already own |

## Store-specific money and honesty questions
- Cost to make one unit and cost to ship an order (if willing).
- Stock, shipping and last-order-date limits in the next 90 days.
- Retail or marketplace logos, certification marks and exact trademark spellings.

## Where answers go (extra rows)
Looks like, Pack, How it is used, Visible result, Moves and sounds, Never show and Reference photos go to the product's entry in the brand brain; a step that must never be shown wrongly also goes to the creative mistakes register. Brand world (places, surfaces, light, people, never in frame) and Motion and sound go to brand guidelines.

## Before the first picture
No picture or clip of a product is made until Looks like, How it is used and the five photos are answered for it, or the output is marked "product not confirmed". Where a photo, the founder's words and a description a model wrote from file names disagree, the photo wins, then the founder's words; ask.
