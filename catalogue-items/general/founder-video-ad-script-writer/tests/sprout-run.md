## Interview guide (founder, to camera or phone)

**For whoever films:** chat off camera for ten minutes first, about anything. Leave pauses unfilled. The best line often comes after a silence. Ask the founder to answer in full sentences that name the subject, so each answer stands alone as a clip. For example, "We put each day in its own pack because…", not "Because…". If they say "sachet", "strip" or "packet", keep their word in the script.

1. Tell me about the day you decided Sprout wouldn't come in a tub. Where were you, and who was in the room?
2. How do most gummy vitamins handle a day's amount? Tell me what you saw when you looked at them. (Describe the category only. Don't name a brand.)
3. How many gummies are in one day's pack, and how did you land on that number?
4. Tell me what the packs cost you compared with a tub: money per unit, packing time, machines, anything.
5. Why didn't you go with the cheaper option when it was right there?
6. What do people who use a powder say to you about gummies, in their own words?
7. Tell me what someone gets from opening one pack in the morning that they don't get from a tub.
8. Tell me about the 2025 study: why you ran it, and what you were most nervous about before the results came in.
