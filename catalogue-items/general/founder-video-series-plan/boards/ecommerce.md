# Founder video series: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- The product(s) the founder can hold, open or demonstrate; how it is made or packed; one real customer message.

## What changes
- **Week 4 (inside the making):** film the real packing room, workshop or supplier visit. If the store drop-ships, use the true story instead (how it is chosen and checked).
- **Week 6 (the myth):** use a claim your product facts support, such as how a material wears or what an ingredient does. Check with the claims list first.
- **Week 7 (a message I kept):** a customer review or email with permission, read as written.
- **Non-discount reasons to post** to mix in: explain an ingredient or material, show official use and care, answer a real question, show verified sourcing, tell a true team moment, help choose between your real products.
- A review-derived unexpected use needs the official compatibility checked before you show it.
