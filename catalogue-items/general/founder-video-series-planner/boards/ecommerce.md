# Build trust with ten weeks of founder videos: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Your last 50 reviews and your most common support emails. Store buyers ask the same few questions again and again, and those are your weeks.
- The products the founder can hold, open or use on camera, and how they're made, packed or checked.

## Where store questions usually come from
- **Cost:** "Why does it cost more than the one at the supermarket?" Show what the price pays for: the material, the testing, the size per day. Say renewal prices and terms exactly as the store states them.
- **Problems:** the top complaint in your reviews. Answer it in a "what we fixed" or "who it's for" week. If it isn't fixed yet, say what you're doing about it, or park it.
- **Comparisons:** against a kind of product (powders, pills, the cheap version), never a named brand.
- **How to use it:** the real steps, filmed in the founder's hands, the way the label says.

## What changes for stores
- **How it's made:** film the real packing table, workshop or supplier visit. If you drop-ship, film how you choose and check products instead.
- **Shown claims:** anything about what a product does to a body stays inside the claims you can back.
- **Your own site:** put each week's question and answer in your product FAQ too. Buyers ask it there as well.
