## 3. Filming setup

- Hold the phone upright (9:16) at eye height, propped up, not handheld.
- Face the light.
- Film in a quiet room with a phone or clip mic within arm's reach.
- Keep your face and any on-screen text out of the top and bottom sixth of the frame, where the app's buttons sit.
- Talk from three bullet points on a card, never a script or a teleprompter.
- Do one practice run, then film.
- Add captions for people watching with the sound off. CapCut can generate them from your speech, and you can fix them by hand.
- Keep the pouch, packs, label and old tub on the table before you start, so every show moment is in reach.
