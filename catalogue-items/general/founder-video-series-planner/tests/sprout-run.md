## 2. Ten-week plan

**Thread order:** How → Why → Who → How → Why → Fixed → Why → How → Who → Why. No thread runs two weeks in a row. The cost question is in weeks 2 and 5. "What we fixed" is not week 1.

### Week 1: How it's made
- **First line (said and on screen):** "Is this just candy?"
- **Short answer:** "No. Each pack carries 21 vitamins and minerals, 6 grams of fiber and 60-plus ingredients, and it's third-party tested. It just happens to taste like a treat."
- **Show:**
  - Tear one pack and pour the small handful of gummy bears into your palm.
  - Turn the pouch to the label.
  - Point at the vitamin and mineral panel, the 6g fiber line, then the ingredient list.
- **What it means for you:** It eats like fruit snacks, but it reads like a supplement label. Check the label, not the shape.
- **Next week:** "Why does it cost more than drugstore gummies?"
- **[Questions for the founder]:**
  - How much sugar is in one Low Sugar pack?
  - How many gummies are in one pack?
  - What does the label call the product (e.g. dietary supplement)?

### Week 2: Why we chose it (cost)
- **First line:** "Why does it cost more than drugstore gummies?"
- **Short answer:** "You're paying for what's on this label, 60-plus ingredients, 21 vitamins and minerals and 6 grams of fiber, and for outside testing behind it."
- **Show:**
  - Point down the label: 60+ ingredients, 21 vitamins & minerals, 6g fiber, and "methylfolate".
  - Then point to where the testing is stated: 70 pesticides, 4 heavy metals, 16 contaminants, 9 microbial contaminants.
  - Hold up the pouch and say the price exactly: "The subscription is $49.99 every 4 weeks. That's this pouch, 28 daily packs."

## 3. Filming setup

- **Phone:** upright (9:16), at eye height, propped. Not handheld.
- **Light:** face the window or lamp.
- **Sound:** quiet room, phone or clip mic within arm's reach.
- **Framing:** keep your face, the pack and any on-screen text out of the top and bottom sixth of the frame, where the app's buttons sit. Hold the pouch and label at chest height, mid-frame, so the panel you point at is readable.
- **Talking:** speak from three bullet points on a card, never a script or teleprompter.
- **Takes:** one practice run, then film.
- **Captions:** add them for people watching with the sound off.
