# Get a 3D how-it-works ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The product's spec sheet: materials, layers, ingredients with amounts, certifications, test reports.
- The usual alternative in your category, and the supported reason it falls short.

## What changes
- **The technical word:** pick the material or part your product page already names (a fibre, a coating, a mineral, a layer), so the ad and the page use the same word.
- **The product beat:** from beat 6, show the real product used the way it's really used. Something worn appears real on its own, never on a made-up figure.
- **Figures:** a test result or percentage only with its report on file. Give the source in the notes.
- **Examples by category:** a pan (oil baking into a coating on hot steel), a wool top (the fibre taking up moisture), a mineral drink (what the mineral does, only as far as the claim goes), a water bottle (the coating against bare steel).
- **Products close to health:** use "supports" or "helps", show the process carrying on, and never show a body repaired.
