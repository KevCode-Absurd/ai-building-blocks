# Get a short film ad script with a twist: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Product photos from several angles and the pack's exact label lines.
- How the product is really used, step by step, and its size next to a hand.

## What changes
- **Payoff shot:** the real pack or product in use, label to camera, held long enough to recognise. Never a different colour or size from the one on the product page.
- **The product's part:** it belongs to the turn in the story (the gift opened, the pan lifting the egg cleanly), not a logo card at the end.
- **Offer text:** price, bundle, shipping or guarantee as on-screen text added afterwards, using the store's current terms only.
- **Customer results:** a made-up character never "reports" a result. If you use a review quote, it appears as on-screen text with its source, not in a character's mouth.
