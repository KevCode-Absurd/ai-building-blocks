# Get a warm animated film ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- A product photo and every label line, with its colours.
- How the product is opened and used, step by step, as your product page says it.
- Two or three review lines that describe the moment people reach for it.

## What changes
- **The moment:** pick the room from your reviews (the hallway at 7:30, the desk at 3 pm, the gym bag). That room is the "and" part.
- **The product's entrance:** at "therefore", in a hand or in use, label to camera. Not floating, not on a plain background.
- **Inside views:** only the parts your product page names (layers, fibres, a seal). No people in those shots.
- **The offer:** price, bundle, shipping or guarantee as on-screen text in the last 2 seconds, from your current terms.
