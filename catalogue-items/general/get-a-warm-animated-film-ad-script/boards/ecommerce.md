# Get a warm animated film ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- A product photo and the exact label lines, with their colours.
- How the product is used, and a visible result if the product page states one.

## What changes
- **Showing the product:** a hand, a countertop or a home scene beside the product, never the pack floating on its own. The label faces the viewer and can be read.
- **How-it-works close-ups:** only what the product page claims (layers, fibres, a bead of water). No people in the frame.
- **Offer:** price, bundle, shipping or guarantee as on-screen text added in editing, using the current terms.
- **Scenes for stores:** a kitchen, hallway, bathroom shelf or desk where the problem happens. Pick the room from the customer's "moment" in the brand file.
