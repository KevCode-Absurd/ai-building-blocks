# Get an original anime ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- A product photo with a sharp label, and the pack's exact label lines in order.
- The old way a shopper does it today (the thing your product replaces), in the shopper's words.

## What changes
- **Draw the pack from the photo:** same shape, colours and label lines, with effect lines kept off it. Never a lookalike pack for the rival.
- **Devices that suit stores:** rival (a drawer of mismatched tools), routine shift (a morning with one pack on the counter), reveal (a launch).
- **Offer at the end:** price, bundle or guarantee as on-screen text added in editing, using current terms. Nothing invented.
- **Reviews:** a buyer's words can appear as on-screen text with the source. The drawn hero never says them as a testimonial.
