# Printable brand guide: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
For each product (main product first, 4 at most): what the camera sees, from the brand file (Looks like, Pack, How it's used, Visible result, Moves and sounds, Never show, Reference photos), and the brand's own photo files. Also the brand world (places, surfaces and props, light, people, what's never in frame) and motion and sound (pace, camera, sound, words on screen, video formats).

## Extra pages
Put these after Voice and before Components:

- **The product on camera.** One block per product: a row of its reference photos, each captioned with its job (pack front, out of pack, in hand, in use, close-up), then Looks like, Pack, How it's used as numbered steps, Visible result, Moves and sounds, and Never show in the "don't" style. A blank field prints "not yet described". A missing photo leaves an outlined empty box with the job named, so the gap is visible.
- **The world, motion and sound.** Left side: places, surfaces and props, light, people, never in frame, beside 2 to 4 of the brand's own photos. Right side: pace, camera, sound, words on screen, formats in use. No stock pictures and nothing generated; if the brand has no photo showing its world, the rows stand on their own.

## Cheat sheet additions
- The product in 2 lines: what it looks like, and the one thing never to show.
- The world in 1 line: the place, the light, the people.

## Extra check
Someone who reads only the product page wouldn't draw a pouch as a tub or a matt pan as a shiny one. If two products look alike, the page says what tells them apart.
