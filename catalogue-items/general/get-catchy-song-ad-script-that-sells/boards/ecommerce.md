# Song video ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Product details: the name, the benefit in one plain phrase, and what it replaces.
- Review lines. The best hooks often come straight from how buyers describe what they got.
- Your current offer and its end date, for the tag's on-screen text.

## Where it fits
- Everyday buys with a clear feeling and a short benefit phrase: a snack, a pet treat, a sleep drink, a cleaning refill.
- Products bought again and again, where a sung tag helps shoppers recall the brand at the shelf or in the search bar.
- Not for products that sell on their specifications.

## Rules for stores
- Keep the hook about what the customer gets. Product names, sizes and prices don't go in sung lines; the name belongs in the tag.
- Show the pack on screen within the first 5 seconds.
- Check platform rules and music rights for the generated track before running it as a paid ad.
- The offer, price and shipping terms appear only as on-screen text over the tag, checked against the current offer.
