# Song video ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Product details: the name, the benefit in one plain phrase, and what it replaces.
- Review lines. The chorus should echo the buyer's own words about what they got.
- Your current offer and its end date, for the last line's on-screen text.

## Where it fits
- Products with a clear feeling attached and a short benefit phrase (a snack, a pet product, a sleep drink).
- Cheaper, quick buys where a catchy line is enough to be remembered. Not for products that sell on their specifications.

## Rules for stores
- Keep the chorus about what the customer gets, not the product listing. Product names, sizes and prices don't belong in sung lines.
- Check platform rules and music rights for the generated track before running it as a paid ad.
- The offer, price and shipping terms appear only as the last line's on-screen text, checked against the current offer.
