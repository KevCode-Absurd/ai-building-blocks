# Claymation video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A product photo (front, label sharp) and the product's real size, so the clay set is built to the right scale. A pack usually stands between the character's waist and shoulder.
- The visible result your product page describes, or a note that nothing is visible.

## What changes
- **Benefit to object:** pick the one benefit shoppers ask about most (heat spread, water beading, fewer pots) and make the changing object show that benefit.
- **Product photo:** use the real photo as the product in the frame, lit to match the set. Never redraw the label letters.
- **Offer close:** price and guarantee go in as text over the picture, in the current terms only. No made-up scarcity.
- **Feed-size test for a still:** shrink it to phone-feed size. The clay world and the one wrong or fixed thing should read before anyone reads the headline.
