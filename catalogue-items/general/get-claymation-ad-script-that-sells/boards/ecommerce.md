# Claymation ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A photo of the pack front with the label sharp, every label line typed out, and the pack's real size next to a person. A pack usually stands between the character's waist and shoulder.
- The visible result your product page describes, or "nothing visible".

## What changes
- **Benefit to object:** pick the benefit shoppers ask about most (heat spread, water beading, fewer steps) and let the changing object show it.
- **The finish:** crisp for premium skincare, home and food; handled for most everyday goods; rough for snacks, gifts and playful brands.
- **The product:** a clay prop wrapped in its printed label, or your real photo lit to match the set. Never clay lettering, and it opens only your way.
- **Offer close:** price and guarantee as on-screen text in your current terms. No made-up scarcity.
- **Feed-size test for a still:** shrink it to phone-feed size. The clay set and the one changed thing should read before anyone reads the headline.
