# Comic page video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A product photo with a sharp label (the one photographic element), and its true colour for the accent.
- The visible result your product page describes, or "nothing visible".

## What changes
- **Accent colour:** use the pack's own colour, so the turn lands on the same colour the shopper sees next on the product page.
- **Products with nothing visible** (supplements, skincare that works over weeks): the changed panel is a changed evening or morning, never a measured outcome.
- **Photo placement:** the real pack photo overlaps the last panel's edge. Keep the label readable at phone-feed size.
- **Offer strip:** price, bundle or guarantee in your current terms, set in the brand's typefaces under the last panel.
- **Reviews:** a buyer's thought bubble may use one phrase, word for word, from a verified review, 6 words or fewer.
