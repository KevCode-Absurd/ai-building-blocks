# Cutout explainer video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A clean cutout of the product (transparent background) from the real photo, with the label sharp.
- A photo of the old way, or the usual product in your category, to cut out as the comparison.

## What changes
- **Main point ideas for stores:** why the usual version fails (the old pan, the flat sheet, the clear bottle), what the product is made of, what a bundle contains.
- **Comparison:** the usual product as a plain cutout labelled "the usual way" next to yours. Never a rival's pack or name.
- **Charts:** only figures from lab tests, review counts or measured specs you have on file, with the source in the notes.
- **Offer close:** price, bundle and guarantee as on-screen text in your current terms, with the cutout large and still.
