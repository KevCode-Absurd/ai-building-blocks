# Get different video ads from the same research

The same research written with the same recipe gives the same ad again and again. A script type is a different shape for the same truth: a different way in, beat order and thing doing the persuading. Two types for one buyer and one reason to buy are different ads to the viewer and to the platform.

This skill chooses types and gives each one's recipe. Pair it with a script-writing skill; the type wins on the opening, beat order, person and tense, and what carries the script. The rules on facts never change.

## What you need
- **Concept** (required): the buyer, the reason they buy, the offer.
- **How much the buyer knows, and the length** (required).
- **What the business can supply** (required): how buyers cope now, failed fixes, what nearly stopped them buying, a true cause, process facts, a real customer account, something visible, time points, offer terms, a real shortcoming.
- **How many scripts** (optional): default a batch of 4.

## Steps
1. **Shortlist types** from the table. A type that needs something the business lacks is skipped, not faked. Drop types that don't suit how much the buyer knows, and types the length can't hold (stories need about 60 seconds; a timeline needs longer).

| Carried by | Type | Opens with | Suits buyers who | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem | straight argument | How they cope, said to "you" | know the problem, or the fixes | how they cope |
| | confession | Something the speaker did and never said | don't see a problem yet, or know it | how they cope, with stakes |
| | unaware observation | An observation about their life, no problem named | don't see a problem yet | a behaviour customers describe from before they knew why |
| Doubt | failed solutions | What they tried, in order | know the problem, or the fixes | two failed fixes from research |
| | objection first | The doubt, conceded before they think it | know the fixes, or the product | what nearly stopped them |
| | honest review | What isn't perfect, before what's good | know the product, or the fixes | a real shortcoming |
| How it works | discovery | "Here's what I didn't know" | know the problem, or the fixes | one true cause |
| | three things | A count and an open question | know the fixes | two or three features, each with "which means" |
| | category call-out | What the category gets wrong | know the fixes | a true flaw of the usual product |
| | timeline | The first time point | know the problem, or the fixes | time points you can back |
| | days in | A day count and where things stand | know the fixes, or the product | a day count the business holds |
| | how it's made | A decision made in making it | know the fixes, or the product | a real process fact |
| Proof | witness | Someone else noticing | know the problem | a real customer's account |
| | demonstration | The thing working | know the fixes, or the product | something visibly done |
| | daily routine | A moment of an ordinary day | know the fixes, or the product | how and when customers use it |
| | several side by side | The line-up and what it's judged on | know the fixes, or the product | a real test or checkable facts |
| Offer | offer first | The terms | know the product, or are ready to buy | terms worth leading with |
| | price of the problem | What the problem has already cost | know the problem, or the fixes | a cost the research or business states |
| Identity | identity led | Who they are | know the product, or are ready to buy; tired markets | a feature that says something about its buyer |

2. **Spread the batch** across what carries the script: one problem type, one type carried by how it works, one proof type, one offer or doubt type. Three scripts carried by the same beat read as one ad. Don't use two types that open on the same fact for one concept (straight argument and confession on one way of coping; demonstration and how it's made on one process fact; failed solutions and price of the problem on the same two tries). Give the second a different fact or another type.
3. **Load each chosen type's recipe** from `references/types.md` and write the script with the chosen script-writing skill.
4. **Set the feeling.** A script has a lead feeling (opening and problem) and an ending feeling (promise onward); the move between them is its shape. Every feeling starts from something the research shows customers feel, and works only on a true fact.

| Feeling | Sounds like | Natural ending |
|---|---|---|
| fear of loss | What is being lost while they wait; quiet and exact, never loud | relief or change |
| frustration | Fed up with what they tried; dry, a little angry on their behalf | relief |
| matter of fact | Calm, plain; facts carry it | matter of fact or pride |
| relief | The weight coming off | joy |
| change | Who they become; forward-looking, earned | pride |
| joy | Light, playful, pleased | joy |
| pride | Quiet self-respect | pride |

Rules: true first (fear with nothing at stake is made-up urgency); dwell on how they cope, never on the affliction (no pity, shame or claim that the viewer has a condition); the brand's voice wins (if it rules out fear or guilt, take the nearest allowed feeling and say so); lead with one feeling, end on another, and let how it works be the turn; words carry feeling, not adjectives; the picture track follows. By default: opening and problem = lead feeling; promise and how it works = the turn, plain and clear; doubt = respect for how they felt; proof, offer, guarantee = steady and specific; call to action and identity line = ending feeling.

## What you hand back
```
Batch: <n> scripts
1. <type> | carried by <beat> | opens: <pattern> | feeling: <lead> -> <end> | needs met: <fact>
...
Skipped types and why
```
Then the scripts themselves, if a writing skill is paired.

## Check before you hand it back
- Each script would be called a different ad from its neighbour.
- Each type's needs are met by facts that were supplied.
- No beat carries more than two scripts in the batch.
- No example opening from the library appears in a script.

## If something is missing
Write `MISSING: <need>` and skip the type that needs it. A witness type without a real account becomes a brief for a real customer to film, with the blanks listed.

## Avoid
- Faking a process fact, shortcoming, test or customer account to fit a type.
- Picking types because they sound fresh rather than because they fit.
