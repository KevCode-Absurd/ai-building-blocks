# Plan a batch of different video ad scripts: for online stores

Each type works for a product that's used up and reordered, and for one that's kept. The openings below are made-up examples; none goes into a real script.

| Type | Used-up product opening | Kept product opening |
|---|---|---|
| straight argument | "You buy a coffee on the way in. Not because you like it. Because the one at home is bad." | "You check the forecast before you decide whether to walk." |
| confession | "I'd been buying a coffee every day and telling myself I liked the walk." | "I own a rain jacket. I still take the car when it looks grey." |
| failed solutions | "First it was the pod machine. Every cup tasted burnt." | "My last jacket was half the price. It leaked by its second winter." |
| objection first | "You think concentrate tastes like the stale stuff in a carton. Most does." | "You're between a medium and a large. Nearly everyone is." |
| honest review | "The first glass you make will be too strong. Everyone over-pours." | "It's heavier than the jacket you have now, and it isn't cheap." |
| discovery | "Coffee isn't bitter. Hot water makes it bitter." | "Jackets don't leak through the fabric. They leak at the seams." |
| three things | "Two things make coffee bitter. The second is the one nobody fixes." | "Three things decide whether a jacket keeps you dry. The label tells you one." |
| category call-out | "Most cold brew is brewed hot and chilled." | "Most rain jackets are tested as fabric, not as a jacket." |
| timeline | "Week 1, your skin argues with it." | "Wash 1, wash 20, wash 100. Here's what happens to the seams." |
| days in | "Day 10. The bottle is half gone and you've stopped measuring." | "30 days in. It's been rained on four times and washed once." |
| how it's made | "We steep it for 18 hours. We tried 12 and poured it away." | "We hose every jacket for 10 minutes before it ships." |
| witness | "My sister asked what I'd changed." | "The man at the next pitch asked what pan that was." |
| demonstration | "1 part this, 2 parts milk, ice. 15 seconds." | "10 minutes under a garden hose. Now look inside." |
| daily routine | "6:50. The kettle is cold and you're already drinking it." | "It lives on the hook by the door, so it's the one you take out with the dog." |
| several side by side | "Three ways to have cold coffee at home: a carton, a pod, a concentrate." | "Three rain jackets on a fence. One hose. Ten minutes each." |
| offer first | "The two-bottle first box is back." | "The 26 cm is back in stock, and it's the last run this year." |
| price of the problem | "A coffee on the way in, five days a week. Add that up." | "Three rain jackets in six years." |
| identity led | "Some people make tomorrow's coffee tonight. They're just done queueing." | "There are people who own one good jacket and people who own four bad ones." |
| unaware observation | "There's a cafe on your way to work that knows your order." | "You look at the sky before you look at your calendar." |

**How it's bought changes the type**
- Timeline: used up, the time points are the first weeks and the offer is the pack that covers them; kept, they're the life of the thing (first use, a season, years; washes, miles, meals) and the offer is the price against that life, with the warranty.
- Days in: used up, the count sits inside the first pack and the question is whether to reorder; kept, the count is days owned or uses, and the question is whether it has earned its place.
- Demonstration: kept goods are put through something (water, heat, weight, a wash); used-up goods show the use (the pour, the texture, one step), never an effect on a body.
- Offer first: used up, the first order, what it costs after, how to pause; kept, one price against what it replaces, and the returns.
- Honest review: used up, the shortcoming is taste, a preparation step, a getting-used-to-it period or the cost per use; kept, weight, fit, breaking in, care or price.
- Several side by side: used up, the options are kinds of the same thing judged on what's in them; kept, they're put through the same test.

**Extra things to give it:** how often it's bought or how long it lasts (and the warranty), how it's made, the visible result, the moves and sounds when it's used, how long a pack lasts and the returns window.
