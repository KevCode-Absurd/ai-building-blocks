# Get honest videos from small creators: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The product to send, what one box costs you (product, packaging, postage), how much stock you can give away, and the store link or code creators may use.

## What changes
- **What ships:** the product as a buyer gets it, with the card on top. If the product needs handling rules (how it opens, what never to do), put them on the card in one line.
- **Round size:** your budget for the round divided by the cost of one box. Ship a small first round so you learn your own post rate before spending more.
- **Share-of-sales link:** if you pay a share of sales, give each creator their own link or code so sales can be traced to them.
- **Product claims:** health claims, results and "before and after" stay inside the claims the brand can back. Creators describe what they saw, tasted or did.
- **Running a winner:** get the platform permission and the written agreement first, then run it from their account. Keep their caption and their disclosure as posted.
