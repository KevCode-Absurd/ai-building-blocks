# Get honest videos from small creators: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- The product to send, its value and postage cost, how much stock you can give away, and the store link or code creators may use.

## What changes
- **What ships:** the real product as sold, with the note on top. Add a small insert only if it costs little.
- **Cost check:** work out the cost per creator (product, packaging, postage) before deciding how many to send.
- **Share-of-sales link:** if you pay a share of sales, give each creator their own link or code so sales can be traced.
- **Product claims:** health claims, results and "before and after" claims stay within the claims the brand can make. Creators describe their own experience.
- **Ads from winners:** ask permission, then run the post as a partnership ad from their account, keeping their original caption and disclosure.
