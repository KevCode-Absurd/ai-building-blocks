# For online stores: Get image ads strangers stop scrolling for

## Extra things to give it
- Product photos named by what each shows (pack front, out of pack, in hand, in use), and a truth sheet if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`).
- Any visible result the brand has recorded, which sets how much the picture may show the product doing.

## Extra rules for a store
- Product-truth test for physical goods: the argument ends in what it is made of, how it works, what it replaces or the guarantee. If every competitor could say it, the ad sells the category.
- Products that get used up (consumables) are bought on a quick decision, so lean on the daily moment. Products bought once (cookware, luggage, furniture) are bought slowly, so give more room to arguments buyers weigh up: price per year of use, fit, materials, what happens if it's wrong.
- Buyers who are comparing or ready to buy get different ads, one kind per ad (a real customer quote, a fair comparison, a price said plainly, a deadline), each with its real evidence. Quote ads use a word-for-word customer quote with no face. Labels on a product are 4 at most, and a button is 3 words or fewer. Batches for people new to you keep offer ads under one in ten. A batch for ready buyers can be all offer ads.
- A made-up quote or deadline is worse than no ad. If the facts don't hold what that kind of ad needs, say so and make the nearest honest ad.
- If the layout shows more variants than the store sells, repeat the real one or show fewer, scaled to a common height.
