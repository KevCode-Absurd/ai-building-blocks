# Long narrated video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- Product data: what's inside, how it's made or used, and a timeline of use you can source (first use, week 1, month 1).
- Proof you hold, counted by type: real review count and rating, lab or test results, press, before and after photos with consent, customer clips with ad consent, the guarantee.
- Footage on file: unboxing, factory or ingredient footage, customer clips.
- Current offer and guarantee terms, with dates.

## Where it fits
- Pricier products, or ones buyers think hard about (supplements, mattresses, skincare devices, kitchen appliances), where the buyer needs to understand how it works.
- Ads to cold audiences that lead to a product page or an ad written like an article, where teaching earns the click.

## Rules for stores
- Count proof before choosing the length: 5 to 6 real pieces for 5 to 10 minutes. A store with one rating and one guarantee gets 2 to 3 minutes.
- Show review screenshots or quotes at the moment they're cited, with the source readable. Never invent a rating, a count or a "bought today" line.
- Timelines (day 7, day 30) only where you hold data for them.
- Place the offer once, after the last cycle, plus a light call to action after each cycle. Check price, bundle and end date against the live offer.
