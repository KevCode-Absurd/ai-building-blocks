# For online stores: Get new ads in your winners' style

## Extra things to give it
Photos of the product by view (pack front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use), the stock level, any small print every ad must carry, and the visible results the product can honestly show.

## What changes
- **Product shape matters most.** Re-proportion layouts for each product and show it as it is sold. If the winner showed its product open, held or in use, show this product the way it really works, or closed and at rest.
- **Offer.** Only a current offer from the facts, with its exact name and dates. A winner's discount doesn't carry over.
- **Not yet tried** often looks like a proven bundle layout with a different product, or a customer-quote layout with a different buyer.
- **Results to record per ad**: spend, return on ad spend or cost per purchase, dates, and how it compares with your account's usual.
