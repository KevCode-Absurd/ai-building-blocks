# For online stores: Get new ads in your winners' style

## Extra things to give it
Photos of the product by view (pack front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use), the stock level, any small print every ad must carry, and the visible results the product can honestly show.

## What changes
- **The product's shape matters most.** Redraw each skeleton for the new product's proportions and show it as it's sold. If a winner showed its product open or in use, show this one only as it truly gets used, or else shut and still.
- **Changing the product counts as the one change.** A winning style moved to a second product keeps the type, argument and audience the same, so the product is the only new thing.
- **Offers.** Only a current offer from the facts, with its exact name and dates. A winner's discount never carries over.
- **Results to record per ad**: spend, purchases, cost per purchase or return on ad spend, and dates. Rank on one of them, the same one every run.
