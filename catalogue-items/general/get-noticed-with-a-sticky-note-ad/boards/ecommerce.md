# For online stores: Get noticed with an ad that's a sticky note

**Rules a store's buyers end up writing**
- Things people buy once: care rules and warnings stuck on the thing itself, like "NOT in the dishwasher" on a pan, "eggs: OTHER pan", "zip sticks, pull gently".
- Things people use up: reminders and deadlines, like "cancel by the 14th", "take with food", "is this one still open?".

**The cheapest honest version.** Write the note yourself in thick marker, stick it on a real surface and take the photo on your phone. It takes five minutes and nothing in it is generated.

**Product photos.** Send none unless the brief asks for the product.
