# For online stores: Get one image ad ready to make

## Extra things to give it
Product photos labelled by job, the pack and how the product is used, the visible result (only where the store records one), and what must never be shown.

## Offer terms to check and say plainly

| Offer | Show | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | Price, how often it ships, how to pause or cancel | If research shows a fear of being locked in, put the real cancel steps in the main text. Never "cancel anytime" unless it's true |
| First-order discount | The price today and after | A surprise at the renewal price comes back as refunds. Show both |
| Bundle | Price per unit or per use, and why that quantity | Tie the quantity to how fast the product is used up |
| Sale | The sale price (not only the %), why it's worth it, the real end date | "Was" and "lowest ever" only if confirmed |
| Guarantee or returns | The window, what the buyer does, what they get back | Every condition (unused, in the box, first order). A warranty is not a lifespan |
| Free gift | What it is and what it's for | Only if it helps them use the product |
| Reason for now | Stock, batch, season, price change | Must be true |

## Price frames for products
- Per use ("63 cents a morning"), only if the price and how long a pack lasts both come from the store.
- Against what they already spend.
- Effort as the price ("wipe it, oil it, hang it up").

## Extra platform risks for physical products
- Green, safety and origin words ("sustainable", "non-toxic", "made in") only with proof.
- A before-and-after of a person only from real, consented uploads, and never for weight or health.
- The product drawn from its real photos, shown open or in use only as the store's facts say.

## Ad types that suit products
- A close-up of the material or part that does the work.
- Old way against new way, shown with objects.
- A sensory close-up (texture, pour, weave).
- A price truth bomb for premium items ("Yes, it's $89. Here's what that buys." then up to three reasons).
