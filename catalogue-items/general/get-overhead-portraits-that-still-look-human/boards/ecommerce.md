# Get overhead portraits that still look human: for online stores

**Extra things to give it**
- The product's photos by job (front on, in a hand) and its truth sheet, if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`, "Stop AI pictures getting your product wrong").

**Extra rules for a store**
- Rest the product against the cheek or jaw, at face height. Held up toward the lens it looks bigger than it is. State its real colour and printing in the prompt, with the label facing the lens and clear of the mouth.
- Good fit: lipsticks, small skincare, jewellery, phone cases and other things a hand holds near the face. Poor fit: bulky products, and anything whose label can't sit beside the face.
- Keep these out of the first product page image and catalogue ads. The close wide lens changes proportions, so shoppers can't judge the product from it. Use them for feed posts and ad images.
- A made-up adult is never shown as a customer or reviewer. Show a visible result only if the brand's own facts and photos record one.
