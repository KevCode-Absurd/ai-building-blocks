# Get overhead portraits that still look human: for online stores

**Extra things to give it**
- The product's photos by job (front on, in a hand) and its truth sheet, if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`, "Stop AI pictures getting your product wrong").

**Extra rules for a store**
- Hold the product beside the lower cheek at a size a hand really holds. The label faces the lens and stays clear of the mouth. State the exact size, colour and printing in the prompt.
- Good fit: beauty products, fashion accessories and small hand-held goods. Poor fit: bulky products, and anything whose label can't sit beside the face.
- Never use the portrait as the first image on a product page or in a catalogue ad, because the wide lens bends proportions. Use it for feed posts, social and ad images.
- A generated adult is never shown as a customer or reviewer. Show a result only if the brand's own facts record a visible one.
