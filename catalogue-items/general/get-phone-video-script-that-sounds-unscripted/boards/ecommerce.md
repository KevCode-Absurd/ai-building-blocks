# Phone video script: for online stores

**Extra things to give it:** how the product opens, is held and used (so the shown use is accurate), the visible result if you record one, packaging that must be readable on camera, and anything that must never be shown.

**Used up or kept**
- Used up (cream, coffee, supplement): the shown use is the real step (the pour, the pump, the amount). The one number is how many days a pack lasts, or a real count. The verdict is something noticed on first use, never an effect on a body.
- Kept (case, pan, jacket): the shown use puts the thing through its job (closing, rolling, a hose, a wash). The one number is something the store holds (trips, washes, a returns window). The verdict is "nothing went wrong".

**Display-only products:** if the handling rules say display only, show a verified sealed detail on a surface. Nobody holds, opens or uses it, and nothing implies size or what it does. If the words need a use that can't be shown, change the words first.

**Handoff for stores:** name the action and what happens next ("it's on their site; I'd start with the small one"), and state the offer only the way the store words it.
