# Phone video script: for online stores

**Extra things to give it:** how the product opens, is held and used, step by step (so the hands part is accurate); the visible result if you've recorded one; packaging that must be readable on camera; anything that must never be shown.

**Used up or kept**
- Used up (cream, coffee, supplement): the hands part is the real dose or pour. The proof moment is the label or the amount, held to the lens. Noticed is what they tasted, smelled or how easy it was, never an effect on a body.
- Kept (case, pan, jacket): the hands part puts it through its job (closing, cooking, a wash). The proof moment is the job done on camera. Noticed is what held up and what didn't.

**Display-only products:** if the handling rules say display only, the hands part becomes a sealed product on a surface, turned to show the label. Nobody opens or uses it, and nothing implies size or what it does. If the words need a use that can't be shown, change the words first.

**Pointer for stores:** say where it's sold and the one thing a buyer should know first, such as the price after the first order, which size to start with, or the returns window, worded as the store words it.
