# Podcast clip video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- Review excerpts with source and date. The Host's problem line and doubt should come from real reviews.
- Product data: what sits on the table (size, colour, pack), and a two-line reason it works.
- Your current offer with its end date (shown as text beside the product).

## Where it fits
- Products where buyers believe a common myth or have been let down by the usual fix (cookware coatings, supplements, mattress toppers, cleaning products).
- Ads for cold or problem-aware shoppers, where a product shot would be skipped.

## Rules for stores
- The Guest can pass on what the reviews keep saying, attributed. Never turn a review into the Guest's own result.
- A product that has to be seen in use (fit, texture, size) belongs in another format. This one carries the argument; the product page carries the proof.
- Price, bundle and shipping terms stay as on-screen text on the last clip. Check them against the current offer before use.
