# Podcast clip video ad script: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Review lines with source and date. The Host's problem line and doubt should come from them.
- What stands on the table: size, colour and pack, plus a two-line reason the product works.
- Today's offer with its end date, for the end text beside the product.

## Good jobs for it in a store
- Products whose buyers hold a common myth, or were let down by the usual fix (pan coatings, supplements, mattress toppers, cleaning products).
- Ads for cold shoppers, or shoppers who know the problem, who would skip a plain product shot.

## Store rules
- The Guest may pass on what reviews keep saying, credited. A review never becomes the Guest's own result.
- If shoppers need to see fit, texture or size in use, pick another format. This one makes the argument; the product page carries the proof.
- Price, bundle and shipping stay as end text on the last clip. Check them against the live offer first.
