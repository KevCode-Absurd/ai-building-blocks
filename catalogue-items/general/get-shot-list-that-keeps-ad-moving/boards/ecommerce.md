# Video ad shot list: for online stores

Give it as well: product photos labelled by what they show (pack front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use), how it's opened and held, what never to show, and the live offer.

- **Attach the photo of what the shot is about.** A photo outweighs any words in the prompt. Send a 28-serving pouch as "the product" and the clip may rip the pouch open and pour from it. For a shot about one serving, send the photo of one stick, one capsule, or the product out of its pack.
- **Single-serve products:** that serving's own pack is the object; the shipping box stays out.
- **Open and use it the brand's way,** in the brand's own words, one step per shot.
- **A pack sitting in every shot becomes furniture.** Bring it in on the line that names it.
- **Things people wear:** on an adult cropped below the face, or laid flat, unless the brand has approved footage on a body.
- **Offer:** never spoken by an AI voice. It goes on screen over the last shot, from the live offer only.
