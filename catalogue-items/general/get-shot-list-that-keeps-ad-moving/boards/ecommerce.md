# Video ad shot list: for online stores

Extra things to give it: product photos by what they show (pack front, product out of the pack, in hand, in use), how it's opened and held, anything never to show, and the current offer.

- **A photo beats the words.** A pack photo is the strongest thing in a request. Send a 28-serving pouch as "the product" and the clip may tear the pouch and pour from it. Send the photo of the thing the shot is about (one stick, one capsule, the product out of its pack).
- **Single-serve products:** that serving's own pack is the object; the shipping box stays out of the shot.
- **Open and use it the brand's way.** Write how it opens and what comes out in the brand's own words, one step per shot.
- **A pack that sits on the counter in every shot is furniture.** Bring it in on the line that names it.
- **Things people wear:** show them on an adult cropped below the face, or laid flat, unless the brand has approved footage on a body.
- **Offer:** never spoken by an AI voice. Add it as on-screen text on the last shot, from the current offer only.
