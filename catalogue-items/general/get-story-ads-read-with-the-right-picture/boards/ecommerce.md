# Pick the right picture for a story ad: for online stores

**Extra things to give it**
- Your product photos (pack front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use). Send one to the image tool only if you asked for the product in the picture, and say what it's for ("the product as sold: copy its shape, colours and printing exactly").
- Your brand's "never show" list for the product, and the facts sheet on how it's used.

**Which buyer history to look for**
- Something people use up (a cream, a supplement, pet food): the routine that kept failing, the shelf of half-used things. Types that fit well: phone reminders, sticky note, the old thing that failed, phone note.
- Something people buy once (a pan, a jacket, a suitcase): the ones bought before and thrown away, or the day the last one let them down. Types that fit well: the old thing that failed, sticky note, review card, don't buy this.
- Impulse or novelty products: nobody reads a long story to buy a novelty. Say so, and suggest a direct ad instead.

**Wording**
- "The old way" in the picture is a kind of product, never a rival's pack, shape or label.
- Words on a review card come only from a real review, with the customer's permission recorded.
