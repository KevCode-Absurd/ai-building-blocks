# Talking object video ad script: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Review lines that name objects ("graveyard of tubs under my sink", "my wobbly suitcase"), with where each came from.
- Product details: what it replaces, its true size next to the object, the pack and label.
- Your current offer and its end date (shown as on-screen text, never said by the object).

## Where it fits
- Products that replace something: the old item, kept and failing, is the best narrator (the scratched pan, the leaking bottle, the broken zip bag).
- Home and kitchen problems where a witness sits in the room (a hook, a lamp, a spoon, a shelf).

## Rules for stores
- Pick an object your reviews already mention. A type of product people bought and gave up on is the strongest source.
- Never use a competitor's pack, shape or colour as the failing object. Use the general type.
- Size matters in a shelf shot: the product stands beside the object at true scale, label readable.
- The offer, price and shipping terms stay in on-screen text on the last shot, checked against the live offer.
