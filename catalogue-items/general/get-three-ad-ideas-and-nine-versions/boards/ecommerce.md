# For online stores: Get three ad ideas and nine versions

## Extra things to give it
- For each product: the price, the visible result and reference photos. Say what is live (which promotions have ended, which products are out of stock).
- For each product: whether it is used up or bought once, because that moves the work between stages.

## Worked belief pairs for physical products (illustrations only)

| Product | Belief in the way | Belief to install | Held up by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A bar of soap, used up | "Handmade soap dries my skin out." | "A gentle base keeps the lather soft." | Ingredient list, review count |
| A carbon-fibre carry-on, bought once | "A light case cracks in the first year." | "The shell flexes and recovers, and a crack is covered." | Warranty terms word for word |
| A standing mat, bought once | "I'll stop using it after a month." | "It is comfortable enough that you forget it is there." | A real review, return terms |

## Extra rules for a store
- A bought-once product gets at least one Evaluation idea (price against years of use, fit, materials, returns and warranty).
- Offers are stated only as they appear in the live offer file. Dates and limits are real.
