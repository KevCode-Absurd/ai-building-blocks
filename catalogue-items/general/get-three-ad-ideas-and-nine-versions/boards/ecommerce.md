# For online stores: Get three ad ideas and nine versions

## Extra things to give it
- For each product: the price, what the buyer can see change, and reference photos. Say what is live (which promotions have ended, what is out of stock).
- For each product: used up (food, skincare, supplements) or kept (a pan, a case, a mat). It moves where the doubts sit.

## Where the doubts usually sit
- **Used up:** "Will it work for me?" and "Why switch?" lead. Comparing is quick (taste, feel, what's in it), so the work is the daily moment, a safe first try and a reason to reorder.
- **Kept:** "Is it worth the money?" and "Will it work?" lead. Comparing is long: price against years of use, fit, materials, what happens if it's wrong. Give at least one idea to the comparing stage, and let the risk link (returns, warranty) carry real weight.

## Worked doubt and answer pairs (illustrations only)

| Product | Doubt | Answer to plant | Held up by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A face cream, used up | "Will it work for me?" "Rich creams break me out." | "It sinks in without a greasy film." | Ingredient list, reviews from oily-skin buyers |
| A cast-iron pan, kept | "Is it worth the money?" "It'll rust and I'll bin it." | "Rust wipes off and the pan comes back." | Care guide, warranty terms word for word |
| A desk lamp, kept | "Why switch?" "My ceiling light is fine." | "The light lands on the page, not in your eyes." | A real review, the return window |

## Extra rules for a store
- Offers appear only as the live offer file states them, with real dates and limits.
- Pack counts in a bundle version match what the order holds.
