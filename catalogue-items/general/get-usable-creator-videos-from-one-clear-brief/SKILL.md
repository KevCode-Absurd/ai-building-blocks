# Get usable creator videos from one clear brief

You write the brief a real person films from. The points are the rail; the words are theirs. It never asks anyone to say a result they didn't have, or to copy another channel's video.

## What you need
- **Approved script** (required): the selected openings, body points, the one number, the verdict and the handoff.
- **Delivery needs** (required): selected openings, length, shapes (for example 9:16), extra shots wanted, raw-file needs, deadline, and the agreed usage and channels. Confirm anything missing; never copy another brief's clip count, deadline or testimonial claims.
- **How the product is handled** (required): how it opens, is held and used.
- **Never-say list** (required): banned words and claims.
- **Brand file** (optional): voice.

## Steps
1. **Write the concept** in 3 to 5 sentences: who is talking, where, what happens, what the viewer is left thinking. Then one line on why it should read as a person's post, not an advert.
2. **List the openings to film:** only the approved, selected ones, each a different way in, each joining naturally to the body. Tell the creator: start right on the approved opening, no greeting; show the product when the concept calls for it; film a first-person opening only if it's true of them, otherwise skip it and say so.
3. **Write the body points** as a table of beats (setup, shown use, the number, verdict, handoff), each with bracketed blanks for their own true details. Say: use your own words; if a line isn't something you'd say, change it and keep the point; if you have no true detail for a blank, leave that beat out and tell us. List the words and claims they must not say. State the length.
4. **List extra shots** (no talking, clean, well lit, no seasonal decorations): the product where they use it, picking it up, opening it the way it really opens, using it close up, the result if there is one, the product held to the lens with its name readable, and one or two shots particular to this concept.
5. **Add filming rules** as do and don't: framing (the agreed shapes, space for cropping and captions, eye level); light (face a window or lamp, never a window behind); sound (quiet room, no music or fan); phone (clean lens, the agreed settings, no filters or beauty mode); product (name to the lens, fingers off the name, no other brands in frame); clothes (plain, no logos or words, a different top for extra shots); editing (none: raw files only).
6. **Write the sending instructions:** raw files in three folders (Hooks, Body, Extra shots); simple names ("Hook 1", "Body", "Extra 1 opening the jar"); with the files, one line on which opening felt most like them and anything they wouldn't say; a moment of stillness before and after each take; a list of missing takes and unresolved factual lines, never filled with invented experience. State the agreed paid or organic use, length of time and channels, and note that filming alone doesn't grant usage rights.

## What you hand back
A brief in this order: Concept; Openings (table: opening, what to say, also do); Body (beat table with blanks); Extra shots (checklist); Filming (do and don't table); Sending files.

## Check before you hand it back
- Every spoken line in the brief is in the approved script or is a bracketed blank.
- No result, health or money claim that isn't in the approved script.
- Every opening the creator is asked to film is true of a creator who used the product, or is marked skippable.
- Usage terms and deadline come from the delivery needs, not invented.

## If something is missing
Write `[CONFIRM: item]` for any missing requirement (length, shape, deadline, usage) and list them at the top for the sender to sort out before it goes out. The brief sends and posts nothing.

## Avoid
- Asking the creator to copy another channel's video.
- Scripting their result for them.
- Treating filming or posting as proof of the right to use it in paid ads.
