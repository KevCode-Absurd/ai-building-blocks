# Get usable creator videos from one clear brief

You write the brief a real person films from, laid out in the order they'll use it: what to know before they start, what to film in what order, and how to send it. The points are fixed; the words are theirs. It never asks anyone to say a result they didn't have.

## What you need
- **Approved script** (required): the spine, the openings or snags chosen, the talking points, the proof moment, the facts allowed and the pointer line.
- **Delivery needs** (required): length, shape (for example 9:16), raw files, deadline, and the agreed use (paid or organic, channels, how long). Anything missing becomes `[CONFIRM]`. Never borrow another brief's numbers.
- **How the product is handled** (required): how it opens, is held and used, and what never appears on camera.
- **Never-say list** (required): banned words and claims.
- **Brand voice** (optional).

## Steps
1. **Before you start** (top of the brief): deliverables, deadline, how the video will be used and for how long, and payment terms if any. List every `[CONFIRM]` here so the sender clears them before it goes out. Say plainly that filming doesn't by itself grant the right to run the video as an ad.
2. **The idea in two or three sentences:** who is talking, where, what happens with the product, and what the viewer should be left thinking. One line on why it should look like their own post.
3. **The run order for the shoot.** Put it in the order that wastes the fewest takes:
   - *Test clip:* 10 seconds of talking, played back to check light, sound and framing.
   - *Talk takes:* the talking points as bullets with `[blanks]` for their own true details. "Use your own words. Keep the point. No true detail for a blank? Drop that line and tell us."
   - *Hands takes:* silent, close, well lit, one action each, in the order it's really used, following the handling rules. Include the proof moment (for example the label held to the lens) and one or two shots particular to this idea.
   - *Openings last,* when they're warmed up: each approved opening, two takes each. First-person openings are marked "film only if true for you".
   - *Room sound:* 5 seconds of the room with no one talking, for the edit.
4. **Must and maybe.** Split the rules so the creator knows what's fixed.
   - *Must:* the agreed shape; face the light; quiet room; no music, TV or fan; product name readable, fingers off it; no other brands in frame; the never-say list; raw files, no filters or edits.
   - *Maybe:* where in the home, what they wear (plain, no logos is best), whether to sit or stand, their own way into each talking point.
5. **Sending.** One naming pattern, no folders: `brand_name_part_take` (for example `sprout_ana_hands-tear_t1`). With the files: which opening felt most like them, any line they changed or dropped and why, and any shot they couldn't get.

## What you hand back
A brief in this order: Before you start; The idea; Run order (test clip, talk takes, hands takes, openings, room sound); Must and maybe; Sending.

## Check before you hand it back
- Every spoken line is in the approved script or is a bracketed blank.
- No result, health or money claim that isn't in the approved script.
- Every first-person opening is marked "film only if true for you".
- Deadline and usage come from the delivery needs, or are flagged `[CONFIRM]`.
- The hands takes open and hold the product the way it really opens.

## If something is missing
Write `[CONFIRM: item]` and list it under Before you start. The brief sends and posts nothing.

## Avoid
- Asking the creator to copy another channel's video.
- Scripting their result for them.
- Treating filming or posting as permission to run it in paid ads.
