# Make your ad and its page tell one story: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the ad sells a physical product.

**Extra thing to give it:** a screenshot or the text of your landing page's first screen, including its first image. The scent check needs it.

**Ideas for physical products**
- Something people buy once: photograph the old one in the state that made them shop (the cracked handle, the bald brush), and open the page on the same photo.
- If the page is a product page, its title is usually the product name. Add a first line that names the situation, or build a separate page for the ad.

**Reminder:** leave the product out of the photo unless asked. If asked, use the real one, small and to one side. The picture must still work with it taken out.
