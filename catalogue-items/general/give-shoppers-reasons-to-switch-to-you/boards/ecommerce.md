# Reasons-to-switch page: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Variants, what's in each bundle, free gifts (only real ones), shipping and returns terms, warranty.
- Your top return reasons and the questions people ask before buying. They fill the doubt pile.

## What changes
- **Offer rows** (up to three) are bundles or packs the store really sells, under their own names. A compare-at price is one the store really charged. A "free gift" shows a value only if the store states one.
- **Product detail block** (gallery, options, add-to-cart) comes after the reasons. For something bought once, it shows the size picker, what's in the box and returns.
- **Habit reasons for stores** often answer "I already own one": what happens to the old one, how long the new one lasts, what the old kind costs over time (only with figures the store holds).
- **Building it in Shopify:** hand the finished copy to the skill that turns page copy into a Shopify page.
