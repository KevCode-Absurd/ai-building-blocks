# Five-reasons page: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Variants, what's in each bundle, free gifts (only real ones), shipping and returns terms, warranty.

## What changes
- **Offer rows** (up to three) are bundles or packs the store really sells, under their own names. A compare-at price is a real former or separate price. A "free gift" shows a value only if the store states one.
- **Product detail block** (gallery, price options, gift grid, add-to-cart) comes after the reasons. For something bought once, it shows the size picker, what's in the box and returns instead of a "supply".
- **Reasons for stores** often come from: the material or ingredient, how it's made, how long it lasts, what the cheaper kind costs over time, and returns or warranty.
- **Building it in Shopify:** hand the finished copy to the skill that turns page copy into a Shopify page (section file, page template and install steps).
