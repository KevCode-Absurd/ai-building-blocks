# Google Ads export review: online store add-on

## Extra module: Shopping and product feed
- **Required extra data:** SKU-linked metrics and category, the product facts and feed fields behind each SKU, policy or disapproval status.
- **Useful output:** feed, title and image repairs, plus investigation candidates, all grounded in the product facts. Do not add claims to a title that the product page does not support.
- **Check:** compare feed price and availability with the product page; a mismatch can cause disapproval and wasted spend.

## Performance Max for a store
Feed-driven campaigns: review asset groups per product group; check that brand and product search terms are separated from non-brand terms before reading total return.

## Extra reading rules
- First-party store revenue and platform-attributed sales are totalled separately; overlap between channels is expected.
- Treat return rates and refunds as part of what a "good" return means.
- Product-level winners and losers matter more than campaign-level averages when the catalogue is wide; check SKU volume before judging.
