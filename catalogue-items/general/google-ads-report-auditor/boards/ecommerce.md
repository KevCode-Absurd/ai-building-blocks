# For online stores: Find fixes worth making in your Google Ads

## Extra area: Shopping and the product feed
- **Columns it needs:** results per product (SKU) with its category, the feed fields behind each SKU, and any disapproval or warning.
- **What you hand back:** feed, title and image fixes, plus products to look into, all based on the product facts in `offers.md`. Never add a claim to a title that the product page doesn't support.
- **Check:** compare the feed's price and stock with the product page. A mismatch can get products disapproved and waste spend.

## Performance Max for a store
These campaigns run from the product feed. Review asset groups by product group. Before reading the overall return, split searches for your brand and product names from all other searches, using the search terms view.

## Extra reading rules
- Total your store's own revenue and the sales Google claims separately. Some overlap with other channels is normal.
- Count refunds and returns when deciding what a good return is.
- Across a big catalogue, winners and losers by product matter more than campaign averages. Check each product has enough sales to judge before calling it either.
