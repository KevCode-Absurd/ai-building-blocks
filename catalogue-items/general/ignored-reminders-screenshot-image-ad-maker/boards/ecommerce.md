# A phone screen of ignored reminders: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Reminders a store's buyers set**
- Something people use up: the routine itself, repeating ("cream, twice a day").
- Something people buy once: only when the old one makes a chore that comes round, such as "re-proof jacket (again)", "soak the pan overnight" or "descale kettle", spread over days in a list.

**Product:** no reminder names it, and the screen shows no product photo.
