# For online stores: Get your story ad read with the right picture

**Where a store's evidence usually lives**
- **Things people use up** (a cream, a supplement, pet food): on a thing. The half-used tub at the back of the cupboard, the organiser still full on Sunday, the reminder that fires every morning.
- **Things people buy once** (a pan, a coat, a suitcase): on a thing or in words. The one that wore out, the note stuck on it ("other pan for eggs"), the warning you'd give a friend before they buy another.
- **Things that solve something on the body** (sweat, chafing, sore feet): on the body only if the visible-symptom gate passes. Otherwise the workaround, as a moment.
- **Bought for looks alone or on impulse**: no push. Run a direct ad.

**Product photos.** Send them to the image tool only when the brief asks for the product, and say what they're for: "the product as sold, copy its shape, colours and print".

**The old way in the picture** is a kind of product, never a rival's pack, shape or label.
