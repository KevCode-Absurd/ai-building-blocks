# Knitted Wool Ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: exact pack shape, colours, every label line, size, how it is used.
- A product photo, so the knitted pack matches the true shape.
- Ingredient or part list, if a mechanism shot is wanted.
- Current offer with end date.

## Where it fits
- Awkward or sensitive categories that a camera makes uncomfortable (digestion, sleep, pests, household grime), told gently through a doll and a room.
- Supplements and wellness drinks where the mechanism is a simple ingredient story, within the claims allowed.
- Home products where a problem-in-a-room story works.

## Store-specific rules
- Health and supplement products: mechanism wording stays inside approved claims; no body before-and-after; "supports" stays "supports".
- The pack's label lines are stated exactly in every prompt; a busy label gets a shorter, cleaner label with the key lines only.
- Food and fabric products that must look good to eat or wear are weak here; use another format for the product page.
- The offer, price and shipping terms go in the final overlay only, checked against the live offer.
