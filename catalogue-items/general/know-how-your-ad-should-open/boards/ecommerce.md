# For online stores: know how your ad should open and ask

Some lines change depending on whether the product gets used up or is bought once:

| Stage or style | Used up (cream, snack, supplement) | Bought once (pan, jacket, bag) |
|---|---|---|
| Weighing this one: what to compare on | A dose, an amount, the price per day | A material, a size, a build, the price per year of use (only if the brand states how long it lasts) |
| Weighing this one: removing the risk | A money-back guarantee, how to pause or cancel a subscription | Returns, exchanges, warranty |
| Ready: the one term to say | What it costs to start, plus the guarantee | The delivery date or the returns window |
| Explain the cause / Prove a better cause | What's in it and what each part does | How it's made and why it lasts |
| Not aware: the opening moment | A daily routine that quietly fails | The moment the old one lets them down |

Image ads: for stages 1 and 2, the picture shows the buyer's moment, not the pack. From stage 3 up, the real product appears, exactly as photographed.

If the brand file has no "what almost stops them" for the product, assume the first doubt that goes with the way it's bought: "will it work for me" for something used up, "will it fit and last" for something bought once. Say that it's an assumption.
