# 30 quick checks: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Screenshots of the buy area with each option picked (size, flavour, plan), so checks 19 and 21 can be judged on the real price shown.
- Your shipping and returns policy pages, so checks 20 to 22 can be compared with what the product page says.

## How some checks read on a product page
- **Check 5:** on a phone, the add-to-cart button should sit right under the gallery and option pickers. If an option must be picked first, the page should say so instead of showing a grey button with no hint.
- **Check 9:** if the product can't be bought without a size or fit choice, the size guide should be one tap from the picker.
- **Check 15:** shoppers in usability studies lean on the star breakdown more than on single reviews, and read a wall of only five-star reviews as a sign they're fake.
- **Check 20:** shipping cost belongs near the buy area but outside the box itself, with any free-shipping minimum stated beside it.
- **Check 24:** a theme can't show checkout settings. Mark it can't tell unless you have a screenshot of checkout, and note whether guest checkout and express ways to pay show up there.

## Why the commit checks matter in a store
In large shopper surveys, surprise costs at checkout are the most common reason people leave a cart they meant to buy, followed by slow delivery, not trusting the site with a card, and being made to create an account. Checks 19 to 24 look for each of these on the page itself, before checkout. Generic "secure checkout" badges in the footer don't pass check 18.
