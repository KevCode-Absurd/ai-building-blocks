# Landing page copy: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Variants (size, colour) and which one the ad showed.
- What is in the box, item by item.
- Shipping cost and speed, free-shipping threshold, returns window and who pays postage, warranty terms.
- Subscription terms: how often it ships, the saving in money, how to pause or cancel.

## What changes
- **Product page order:** announcement line (proof, risk removal or the offer), gallery that already works as a mini page (benefit, proof, in use), buy box, real customer content, benefit strip, two or three value-prop sections, differentiators, guarantee, questions, closing block. On a phone the buy box sits right under the gallery so price and button are in the first screen, and a button follows the visitor once the buy box scrolls away.
- **Buy box, bought again:** pack or bundle rows ("most popular" only if true), one-time against subscribe with the saving in money, price per day only if the facts give it, "pause or cancel" in the store's words.
- **Buy box, bought once:** one price, a size and colour picker with the size guide one tap away, delivery date, returns window, warranty. Never "3x" of the same item.
- **Bundles:** for something bought again, a routine whose steps are each needed for the outcome; for something bought once, the set that completes it (lid and care kit with a pan). The saving is the shipping you save by sending together.
- **Below about 100 in the store's currency,** a flat bundle price reads stronger than a percentage. Suggest it; the owner confirms.
- **Accordions in the buy box:** again: ingredients, how to use, shipping and returns. Once: dimensions and materials, what is in the box, care, shipping and returns, warranty.
- **Hero image:** the same product, pack and variant the ad showed.
- **Cart friction to keep in mind when writing the risk line:** extra costs, slow delivery, unclear returns and forced accounts are the commonest reasons for abandoned carts. State cost, delivery time and returns on the page. Keep everything added at checkout under about 10% of order value (rule of thumb).
