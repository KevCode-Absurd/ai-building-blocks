# CRO audit: online store add-on

## Surfaces a whole-store audit must cover (do not skip silently)
1. Home page
2. Navigation menu (open)
3. Collection page
4. Product pages
5. Landing pages (advertorial, reasons-why), if any
6. FAQ, About and story pages
7. Cart (with an item in it)
8. Checkout, as far as it goes without paying

For each surface give one to three test ideas that pull different levers; never pad to three. If a surface does not exist (no landing page, no story page), say so and propose what to build. An audit of one page covers that page only.

## Data request (paste-ready)
Ask the store for, from its own analytics for a stated window (30, 60 or 90 days) and the same window a year ago if available:
- Sessions, overall conversion rate, average order value, orders, revenue.
- Funnel: added-to-cart rate, reached-checkout rate, checkout completion rate.
- Sessions and conversion rate for mobile against desktop.
- Top five products by units and revenue; top five landing pages by sessions with each one's conversion rate.
- New against returning share of orders; return or refund rate and reasons; share of orders on subscription.
- Trend against last year, roughly.
Minimum for sized mode: sessions, overall conversion rate, AOV and the mobile/desktop split. Mark every gap; never fill one.

## What each number adds (say this in a page-only audit)
| Number | What it lets you say |
|---|---|
| Sessions, conversion rate, AOV | The size of a leak in money |
| Mobile against desktop | Whether the loss is on the phone |
| Funnel rates | Which step loses most |
| Landing pages by sessions | Which page to fix first |
| New against returning | Page problem or traffic mix |
| Return rate | Whether a converting page over-promises |

## Capture notes
- Phone viewport about 390 px wide at 2x. Clear age or region gates, then decline non-essential cookie banners, then hide pop-ups for the shot. Check the open menu and cart shots really show the open panel.
- Frame each BEFORE as a 9:16 crop with the whole section in view; a tall section needs several frames.
- By purchase type, the offer to look for: bought again, a starter pack, bundle, subscribe saving in money; bought once, one clear price, contents, the set that completes it, instalments, delivery date.
