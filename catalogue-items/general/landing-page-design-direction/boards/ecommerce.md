# Landing page design direction: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photos by view (front, detail, in use, scale), variants, the size or option picker states.
- Shipping, returns, subscription and compatibility terms that must stay visible.

## What changes
- Show the actual variant the ad showed, with accurate proportions and useful cropping.
- Group price, variant choices and terms around the add-to-cart button. Keep a button within thumb reach on phones.
- Do not hide delivery, return, subscription or compatibility information to make the layout cleaner.
- Equivalent catalogue products can repeat in cards; unrelated arguments should not.
- Local previews establish rendering and link wiring only. Real cart and payment behaviour needs a test inside the store.
