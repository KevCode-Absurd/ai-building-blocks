# Plan three design directions: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Product photos by view (front, detail, in use, next to something for scale), the variants, and how the size or option picker looks in each state.
- Shipping, returns, subscription and compatibility terms that must stay visible.

## What changes
- Show the exact variant the ad showed, at its true proportions, cropped so the useful detail shows.
- Group the price, variant choices and terms around the add-to-cart button. Keep a button within thumb reach on phones.
- Don't hide delivery, returns, subscription or compatibility information to make the layout cleaner.
- Products that really are alike can repeat as cards; different arguments shouldn't.
- A local preview only shows how the page looks and whether links work. Real cart and payment behaviour needs a test inside the store.
