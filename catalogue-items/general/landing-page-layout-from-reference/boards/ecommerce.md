# Layout from reference: online store add-on

## Section checklist for a typical store reasons or advertorial page
Tick only what the reference really has: announcement bar, hero with author or product badge, press strip, alternating reason blocks (chip grid, before/after, timeline, image and text, certification block), testimonial row, guarantee, review-site section, closing band with offer card, footer, sticky bottom bar.

## Slots that state a fact on such pages
Announcement timer or sale ribbon (a deadline or offer), hero author or badge (who wrote it, what it is certified for), press strip, before/after and results timelines, ingredient and certification blocks, testimonial avatars, guarantee terms, review-site score, closing offer card (price and offer). Each is filled from your facts or removed.

## Extra inputs
- Variant and pack shown, store policies (returns, shipping, warranty), logo file, footer contact details.

## Extra checks
- Check the finished page at phone width; most store traffic arrives on a phone and a desktop screenshot is not a phone layout.
- Do not add tracking scripts while copying a layout.
