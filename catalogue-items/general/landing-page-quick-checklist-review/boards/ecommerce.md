# Landing page quick checklist: online store add-on

## Extra checks for a product page
- Variant picker and size guide are reachable in the first screen when the product cannot be bought without them.
- Returns window, who pays postage, delivery time and shipping cost are on the page, near the price.
- For a product bought again: what is in it, how to use it, how long a pack lasts. For a product bought once: dimensions, materials, what is in the box, care.
- Reviews with photos come first where the store holds them; no default rating.
- Subscription terms (frequency, saving in money, how to cancel) are stated before purchase.

## Thresholds
- Shipping, tax and fees added at checkout under about 10% of order value (rule of thumb).
- Do not count generic security seals or footer trust badges as proof.
