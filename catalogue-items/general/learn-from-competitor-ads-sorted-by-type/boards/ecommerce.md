# Study board: for online stores

## One extra group
Add a group for your own product category, filled with typical ads from that category that fit no other group (for example `supplements` for a supplement brand, `cookware` for a pan brand, `outerwear` for a jacket brand). Leave it out if you can't name the category.

## What changes
- **Set** means a bundle, kit, duo or routine. **Deal** includes percent off, buy-one-get-one, free shipping over a threshold, and a free gift with purchase.
- **Gift** needs a clear recipient or occasion ("gift for him", "stocking filler"). Brand ads that only mention a holiday don't count. If your category has no gift ads, look at jewellery, beauty and candles for how they're built.
- **Skip as not real ads:** ads that link to a blog post with a blog thumbnail as the image, boosted personal posts with no product, and publisher or news pages.
- **Near-duplicates:** treat as one ad when the advertiser and landing page match (ignore tracking codes at the end of the link), or the first 80 characters of text match, or the image is identical. Keep the longest-running version.
- **Rerunning the board:** list the IDs already on it first and add only new ads.
