# For online stores: Learn what sells from 50 different image ads

## Extra things to give it
- A photo list for each product and each variant, named by what each photo shows. Products and variants each get their own slots.
- The live offers for each product, in the offer's own words, so offer-first and price-anchor slots use the real terms.

## Extra rules for a store
- Slots are shared out between products by arithmetic. A reason to buy that fits only one product is used only in that product's slots.
- Children's products: write to the adult buyer and show the product, with no child.
- Ads for a physical product show it open, held, worn or in use only the way the product facts say. Otherwise show it closed and at rest.
- Run `stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong` for any product with fewer than three usable photos, and suggest `get-a-full-product-photoshoot-from-phone-photos` before the sprint.
- A before and after for a store is the old way against the new, shown with objects, never a body or a face.
