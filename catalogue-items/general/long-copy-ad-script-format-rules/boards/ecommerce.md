# Long copy ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Real customer reviews with source, verified-buyer status and date; the founder's story if one exists.
- Product page terms: price, bundle, shipping, returns, guarantee wording.

## What changes
- **Photo ideas for stores:** the hallway floor with the muddy boots, the kitchen bench after the fifth failed attempt, the bathroom shelf with too many bottles. The product appears as it would in a real home, unposed.
- **Proof:** review count, a verified-buyer quote word for word, a retailer or press mention only if real. No "bought today" or default-rating claims.
- **Offer close:** current terms only, in the store's wording, with the link after the story, never inside the first three paragraphs.
- **Whose story:** with no real customer or founder account, use third person ("customers keep telling us...") and mark it inferred.
