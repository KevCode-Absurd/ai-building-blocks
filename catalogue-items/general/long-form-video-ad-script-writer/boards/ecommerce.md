# Long narrated video ad script: for online stores

## Give it as well
- What's inside, how it's made or used, and any sourced change over time (first use, week 1, month 1).
- Your proof sorted by kind: review count and rating with the source named, lab or test results, press, before and after photos with consent, customer clips cleared for ads, the guarantee.
- Footage you own: unboxing, the making or the raw materials, customer clips.
- Today's offer and guarantee terms, with dates.

## Good jobs for it in a store
- Pricier products, or ones people think hard about (supplements, mattresses, skin devices, kitchen machines), where the buyer has to understand how it works first.
- Ads to cold shoppers that lead to a product page or an article-style page, where teaching earns the click.

## Store rules
- Take stock before you pick a length. Each round needs its own problem, explanation and proof. One rating and a guarantee make a short script, not a long one.
- Show a review screenshot or quote when it's cited, with its source readable. Never invent a rating, a count or a "bought today" line.
- Use time points (day 7, day 30) only where you hold the data.
- Say the full offer once, after the last round, with a light ask after each round. Check price, bundle and end date against the live offer.
