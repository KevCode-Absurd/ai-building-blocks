**What would add a round** (each is about 60 to 90 seconds, toward the 5:00 asked):
- **A taste and texture round.** Needs the texts of the 20 review quotes that mention taste or chew. It also needs a sourced fact on flavour or texture.
- **A "knowing what's in it" round.** Needs buyer research showing contamination or quality worry, so testing becomes a problem the research names, not just a transition. Add the lab name and how to see the results.
- **A time-point beat.** Needs real data on when users notice a change. Neither the FAQ line nor the 30/60/90-day timeline can be used.
- **A ratings proof card.** Needs one rating with its source, once the four conflicting numbers are settled.

**MISSING:**
- Study details: citation and who ran it; confirmation it tested Sprout; what was measured (the script says "levels" `[inferred]`).
- Survey date and method; confirmation the survey wording is cleared for ads.
- Lab name for third-party testing; exact NSF wording (does it apply to the product or the facility?).
- Permission and source for the review quote; texts of the other 19 quotes.
- Pack and product photos and Supplement Facts artwork (if none exist, the `product photo` rows become `to film`).
- Screenshots of the brand photos: they are unseen. Also who is in them and whether they agreed to appear in ads.
- Price, bundle, offer dates, guarantee terms link, landing URL.
