# Long Narrated Video Ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: what is inside, how it is made or used, a usage timeline you can source (first use, week 1, month 1).
- Proof assets counted by type: review count and rating (real), lab or test results, press, before/after photos with consent, customer clips with ad consent, guarantee.
- Footage on file: unboxing, factory or ingredient footage, customer clips.
- Current offer and guarantee terms with dates.

## Where it fits
- Higher-priced or higher-consideration products (supplements, mattresses, skincare devices, kitchen appliances) where the buyer needs the mechanism explained.
- Cold-traffic ads to a product page or advertorial where education earns the click.

## Store-specific rules
- Count proof before choosing length: 5 to 6 real pieces for 5 to 10 minutes. A store with one rating and one guarantee gets 2 to 3 minutes.
- Show review screenshots or quotes at the moment they are cited, with source readable. Never invent a rating, count or "bought today" line.
- Timelines (day 7, day 30) only where the brand holds data for them.
- Place the offer once, after the last loop, plus a light call to action after each loop. Check price, bundle and end date against the live offer.
