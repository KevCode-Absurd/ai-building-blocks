# Knitted wool image ad: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Sharp photos of the pack's front, side and top, its label text word for word, its size and how it's used.
- The ingredient or part list, if the picture shows what's inside.
- The live offer, its terms and its end date.

## Good fits
- Subjects people would rather not look at in a photo: digestion, sleep, pests, cleaning. Wool makes them kind to look at.
- Home goods, pet products and gifts that suit a warm, cosy world.
- Supplements, as long as the picture stays inside your approved claims.

## Store rules
- Health and supplement products: put the approved claim in the words and picture the product in use. Never picture a body changing.
- Lay the real pack or label, cut out from its photo, over the crocheted one before the ad runs.
- Food that must look tasty and clothes that must fit look weak in yarn. Use another format for those.
- Price and offer terms go on the image in editing, checked against the live offer that day.
