# Make each carousel card earn the next swipe: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
- Product photos by role: pack front, open or in use, a close-up. One photo of each product shown, to check the cards against.
- Bundle and price facts, shipping and returns terms.

## What changes
- **Product cards:** check each card's product, colour and label against its photo. One variant per carousel, unless a card says otherwise.
- **What to expect over time:** weeks for something used up (skincare, supplements, coffee), years for something kept (cookware, bags). Only points in time the brand can back.
- **Offer card:** price, bundle contents, shipping and returns in the store's own wording. A code only if it works today.
- **Catalogue carousels** (one product per card) are a different job. This skill makes one argument across the cards.
- **Reviews:** a quote card uses a real review as posted, with no face and no invented first name.
