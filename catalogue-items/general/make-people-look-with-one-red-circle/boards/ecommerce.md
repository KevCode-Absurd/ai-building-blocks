# A photo with one red circle: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Extra thing to give it:** your product photos, but only if you want the product in the picture. Otherwise send no product photo to the image tool.

**Ideas for physical products**
- Something people buy once: mark the spot where the old one gives out, such as the seam, the hinge or the worn heel of a sole.
- Take the note from reviews and return reasons, in the buyer's own words.
- Photograph the flaw on a generic old item, never a rival's recognisable one.

**Reminder:** leave the product out unless asked. If asked, use the real one, small and to one side. The picture must still work with it taken out.
