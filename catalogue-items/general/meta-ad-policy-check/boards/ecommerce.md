# Meta ad policy check: online store add-on

## Read these first for supplement, food, cosmetic and household brands
1. **Health and wellness:** supplements, weight products and cosmetic procedures run to 18+ only; no put-down of looks, no pinched fat, no cure claim for a listed disease, no sensational or timed result without a qualifier; a shown result states how long it took.
2. **Deceptive commercial practices:** the price, discount, "free", delivery and refund terms must be what the shop charges and does; no endorsement a person or brand did not give; a subscription states its terms before the sale.
3. **Personal attributes:** describe the product and who it is for, never what the viewer is. Breach: "Do you have diabetes?", "Tired of your acne?". Fix: "New treatment available", "A cream for acne-prone skin".
4. **Body image and eating disorders:** no before-and-after weight pictures that are not about the product, no hollow-cheeked imagery, no admission of extreme weight-loss behaviour, no sad-mood hook.
5. **Adult content:** crops on breasts, groin or buttocks, suggestive poses or revealing clothing need 18+; sexual-health products run to 18+ when the focus is health, never pleasure.
6. **Third-party IP:** another brand's logo, pack or music needs a licence.
7. **Profanity:** no swearing, however disguised.
8. **Misinformation and vaccines:** no miracle-cure or fact-checked-false claim.
9. **Relevance:** picture, words and landing page sell the same product, price and offer.
10. **Drugs and medicines:** a supplement presented as a drug, a prescription product, an over-the-counter medicine or CBD has its own conditions.

## Store inputs
- Landing page text and offer, subscription terms, refund policy, the countries and age range of the ad set.
