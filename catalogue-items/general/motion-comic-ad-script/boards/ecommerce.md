# Comic ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A photo of the pack front and of the product out of its pack, with the label sharp. It sets the drawn product and becomes the photo panel.
- The visible result your product page describes, or "nothing visible".

## What changes
- **The drawn product:** copies the photo's shape and colours, with no letters. A pouch stands upright, a pan lies flat with its handle out, a shirt is folded.
- **Standing out:** the product is the brightest thing in its panels. If the pack is black, white or grey, give it a thin outline in your brand colour.
- **Nothing visible** (supplements, skincare that works over weeks): the land panels show a changed morning or evening, never a measured outcome.
- **The photo panel:** your real pack photo in the same border, with price, bundle or guarantee in your current terms, set in editing.
- **Reviews:** a caption may use one phrase, word for word, from a verified review, 6 words or fewer.
