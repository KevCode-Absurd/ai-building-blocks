# Ecommerce add-on: motion graphics video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front cutout photo, product-out-of-pack photo, `Made of` list, supplied specs (weights, sizes, counts, ratings sources), how it is used.

- One icon per ingredient or material the brand lists (`Made of`) or per use step; a row of three or four.
- Spec comparison: two bars on one baseline only if both figures are supplied (yours and a stated category or old-way figure with a source).
- Layered-product diagram example: a backpack in section, three bands (shell, foam, lining) drawing in one after another, an accent band widening where the stated benefit happens.
- Do not animate the pack opening unless the brand's use steps show how.
- Price, discount and guarantee text: set in editing on the last shot from the current offer.
