# Motion graphics video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A clean photo of the product on a plain background, sharp enough to read the label.
- The facts from your product page: what's inside or what it's made of, the steps of use, what comes in each bundle, the price and the guarantee.
- Any test or study figures you want charted, each with where it came from.

## What changes
- **Best fit for stores:** shoppers who already know the product or are close to buying. Lead with what's inside, how to use it in a few steps, or the offer in plain terms.
- **What's inside:** one item per shot, each named in one to four words copied from your product page.
- **Price and terms:** the price, the bundle, shipping and the guarantee as they stand today, held still on screen. A first-order price always shows what it renews at.
- **Never:** redraw the pack as an icon, set its label in a new font, or chart a star rating as if it were a lab result.
