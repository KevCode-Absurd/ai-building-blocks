# Native Ad Image Brief Writer: add-on for online stores

**Extra inputs**
- Product reference photos (pack front, out of pack, in hand, in use). Send one to the image model only if the user asked for the product in the picture, and name its job ("the product as sold: copy its shape, colours and printing exactly").
- The brand's "never show" list for the product, and the truth sheet for how it is used.

**Which kind of buyer history to look for**
- Used-up product (cream, supplement, pet food): the routine that kept failing, the shelf of half-used things. Types that fit well: reminders, sticky note, worn object, notes screenshot.
- Bought-once product (pan, jacket, suitcase): the ones bought before and thrown away, or the day the last one let them down. Types that fit well: worn object, sticky note, one-star review card, don't-buy-this.
- Impulse or novelty products: nobody reads a long story to buy a novelty. Say so and recommend a direct ad instead.

**Wording**
- "The old way" in the picture is a kind of product, never a rival's pack, shape or label.
- Words on a review card come only from a real review with the customer's permission recorded.
