# Ecommerce add-on: native story ads

**Product bought once vs used up** changes the story:
- Used up and reordered: the history is a run of products started and dropped; the first experience is the first week; "what changed" is over the period customers report.
- Bought once and kept: the history is the ones bought before, each with what it cost and how it ended; "first experience" becomes first use, then the tenth use; the number-then-family form works with a cost or lifespan fact and a family story of the one that lasted.

**Extra inputs:** offer page or article the story points to, returns terms, reviews that are cleared for use (with name and permission on record).

**Form fit for stores:** short review suits a single product with real reviews; letter about someone suits gifts; forum post suits durable goods with long use; expert voice only where the store has a real, agreed professional.

**Destination:** product page for a ready buyer; a teaching article for the indirect story. Never put a price or discount in the indirect story.
