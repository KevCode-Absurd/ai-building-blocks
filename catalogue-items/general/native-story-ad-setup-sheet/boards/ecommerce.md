# Native Story Ad Setup Sheet: add-on for online stores

**Extra inputs**
- Customer reviews with star ratings, dates and whether the customer agreed to ad use.
- The brand's market awareness note (how many rivals say the same thing).

**Fit examples**
- Used-up: a cream, a supplement, a pet food. Look for the shelf of half-used things.
- Bought-once: a pan, a jacket, a suitcase. Look for the ones thrown away.

**Illustrative starting figures for a test set (not measured, not any brand's numbers; replace with the brand's own after one set has run)**
- Cost per thousand views of about $30 to $40, and under $60.
- 20 to 25% of viewers clicking anything; about 2.5% clicking through to the page.
- Run one campaign with one ad set per tension; judge the first 3 days on potential, not profit.

**Button:** "Shop now" to the product page, or "Learn more" when it leads to an article.
