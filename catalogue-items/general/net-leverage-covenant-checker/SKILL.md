# Net leverage covenant checker

Net leverage, permitted debt and signed covenant headroom.

## What you need

Signed test definition, dated included debt, eligible unrestricted cash, cash-netting cap, contractual EBITDA and maximum leverage ratio.

## Method

1. Map included debt, cash eligibility, netting cap and EBITDA to the same covenant test date; never substitute accounting net debt.

2. Net only the lesser of eligible cash and contractual cash cap from included debt.

3. Divide by contractual positive EBITDA and compare net debt with maximum ratio times EBITDA; identify negative headroom without asserting legal default.

## Limits

- One leverage test and static cash cap; no springing trigger, EBITDA cure or cross-default determination.
- Headroom is arithmetic evidence for the responsible reviewer, not borrowing availability.
- Arithmetic does not establish legal applicability or authorize a transaction.
