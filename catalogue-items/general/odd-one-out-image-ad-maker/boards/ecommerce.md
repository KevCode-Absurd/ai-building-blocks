# One odd photo: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Mismatches a store's buyers know**
- Wrong use: the old one doing another job, such as a cracked mug holding brushes or a dead charger used as a twist tie.
- Wrong place: a spare kept somewhere odd, such as a jacket in the car boot in July.
- Wrong amount: three half-used tubs at the back of the shelf, or a drawer of cables.

**Product photos:** send none unless you want the product in the picture. If you do, use the real one, small and to one side.
