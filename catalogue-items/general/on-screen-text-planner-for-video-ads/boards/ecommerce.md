# On-screen text for a finished video ad: for online stores

Extra things to give it: the product name as printed, the current offer and guarantee wording, and your never-say list.

- First overlay: the product's name or a plain description beside the pack, in the script's words, the first time the pack is clearly seen.
- Numbers worth a badge in a product ad: size, weight, count, ingredient amount, guarantee length, delivery or return terms. Each only with its source.
- The offer or guarantee goes in the final scene as a badge, only as worded in the current offer. No added urgency.
- Demonstration labels name the step in 2 to 4 words ("tear the top", "stir 10 seconds"), using the brand's own instructions.
- On a podcast or talking-head ad, use labels only and keep every overlay clear of the mouth.
