# One-period residual income bridge

A one-period equity-charge schedule with a clean-surplus cross-check.

## What you need

Opening common book equity, ordinary net income, common dividends and a dated cost-of-equity assumption.

## Method

1. Use beginning common book equity and matching ordinary earnings; identify OCI, new capital and repurchases before asserting clean surplus.

2. Multiply opening book equity by required equity return and subtract the charge from ordinary earnings.

3. Roll book value through earnings and distributions, reconcile other equity movements and label the one-period discounted contribution separately from complete value.

## Limits

- Fixture assumes clean surplus with dividends only; other movements require extra rows.
- One period of residual income is not intrinsic value of the whole company.
