# Online store add-on: Overhead Close Portrait Image Prompt

Extra inputs
- The product's photo by job (front on, in hand) and its truth sheet, if you have one (`product-truth-sheet-from-photos`).

Added rules for a store
- Hold the product beside the lower cheek at a size a hand really holds; the label faces the lens and stays clear of the mouth. State the exact size, colour and printing in the prompt.
- Good fit: beauty, fashion accessories and small hand-held goods. Poor fit: bulky products and anything whose label cannot sit beside the face.
- Never use the portrait as the first image of a product page or a catalogue ad; the perspective bends proportions. Use it for feed, social and ad creative.
- A generated adult is never shown as a customer or reviewer. Show results only if the brand's own facts record a visible one.
