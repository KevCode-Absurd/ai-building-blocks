# Objection answer map: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- The product page's gallery images in order, plus the size or variant picker states.
- Returns reasons from the store's own records, if any.

## What changes
- **Where store questions usually land:** size or compatibility (picker, scale image), contents (beside price, contents image), material or ingredient (close-up image or explanation), use and care (instructions image), returns and warranty (under the add-to-cart button), delivery cost and time (buy box or delivery control).
- **Gallery rows become image briefs:** one question, one supported answer, source IDs, and the real product photo as the reference. An illustrated use does not prove a test or customer result happened.
- **Keep the ad, page and gallery consistent** on every fact. Do not use this to build an ad scorecard or a sale plan.
- **Evidence for fit and compatibility** needs real measurements and the limits of compatibility, not a generated demonstration.
