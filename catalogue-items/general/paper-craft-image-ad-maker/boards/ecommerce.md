# Paper-craft image ad: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Sharp photos of the pack's front, side and top, its label text word for word, its size and how it's used.
- The live offer, its terms and its end date.

## Good fits
- Gifts and premium goods: a pop-up reveal of the product, or layered planes for a rich scene.
- Small problem-solvers for busy days: a flat-cut figure, with a flap or concertina for how the product fits the day.
- Ranges with many flavours or colours: quilled or layered pieces, as long as each one is real and on sale.

## Store rules
- Show one variant at a time and name it in the label lines.
- A flap that turns a messy scene into a tidy one is a claim. Use it only if your approved claims say the product does that.
- Lay the real pack or label, cut out from its photo, over the card one before the ad runs.
- Price and offer terms go on the image in editing, checked against the live offer that day.
