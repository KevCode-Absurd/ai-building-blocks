# Cutout explainer video ad script: for online stores

## Also give it
- A clean cutout of the product (background removed) from the real photo, with the label sharp.
- Plain photos of the old way: the usual tools or products in your category, with no brand marks, to cut out for the swap.

## What changes
- **Points that suit stores:** the old routine takes more steps, the pack replaces several things, what the product is made of, what a bundle holds laid out flat.
- **The swap:** the usual product as a plain cutout, then yours drops into its place. Never a rival's pack or name.
- **Counts and charts:** only from lab tests, label facts or measured specs you have on file, with the source in the notes.
- **Offer close:** price, bundle and guarantee as on-screen text in your current terms, over the product cutout held large and still.
