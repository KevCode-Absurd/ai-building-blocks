# Cutout explainer video shots: for online stores

## Also give it
Product photos by job (front, out of the pack, in use), how the product works in your own words, and any figures (sizes, weights, counts) with sources.

## What changes
- Cut the product from its front photo in full colour. If the label blurs in a still, make the cutout larger in the layer stack, not sharper in the prompt.
- Example parts list for a travel mug ad: the mug cutout (front photo), the lid cutout (lid photo), a plain unbranded mug (to shoot), two paper rings, one paper seal diagram with a pin at its hinge, three blank strips.
- Compare with "a plain mug" or "the usual way", never a named rival.
- Prices, offers and guarantee text are set in editing from your current offer, over the last frame.
