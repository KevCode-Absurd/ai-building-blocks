# Paper World: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: exact pack shape, colours, every label line, size or count, how it is used.
- A product photo, so the paper pack matches the true shape.
- The current offer with its end date.

## Where it fits
- A gift or premium product: `layered-stack` or `folded`, product as hero.
- A small problem-solver: `flat-cutout` or `torn-and-glued`, the problem as a mini storybook, 20 to 30 seconds.
- Products with variants: paint one variant only and name its flavour or colour in the label lines.

## Store-specific rules
- The pack is the one thing that must stay exact. Repeat its label lines in every clip prompt.
- Worn items, fabric drape and food texture are weak in paper. Show the setting and the problem; let the real product photo appear on the product page.
- The offer (price, bundle, shipping) goes as on-screen text on the last shot only, with its current end date, never as narration over the paper.
