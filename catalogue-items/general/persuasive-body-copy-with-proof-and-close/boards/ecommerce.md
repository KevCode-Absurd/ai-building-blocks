# Ecommerce add-on: body and close

**Extra inputs:** returns window and who pays shipping, subscription and cancel steps, price after a first-order discount, per-unit price of bundles, stock or batch reasons that are true.

**Close wording rules for stores**
- Show the one-time price beside the subscription price and say how to cancel in the real steps.
- A renewal price that surprises buyers comes back as refunds: state both.
- Name every guarantee condition (first order only, unused, in its box). A warranty period is not a lifespan.
- Reasons for now must be real: stock level, batch, season, price change. Otherwise omit.
- Bundle: tie quantity to how the product is used up (for example a 3-month supply).
