# Ecommerce add-on: podcast clip video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, product height reference (as tall as a glass, a mug), how it is held and opened, `Never show` list, the doubts customers voice in reviews.

- Host's first line is a real buyer complaint in the buyer's own words (from reviews), turned into a question.
- Speakers state product facts as facts about the product, never as their own use.
- Illustrative exchange for a desk cushion (invented figures, to be replaced by the brand's real facts): Host: "Why does every seat cushion go flat by spring?" Guest: "Cheap foam gives up. This one is a gel grid, no foam in it." Host: "Wait. Nothing to squash?"
- Pick up only small single-hand items with stated handling (a sachet, a tube); big or worn items stay displayed.
- Offer and price are on-screen text on the last clip only.
