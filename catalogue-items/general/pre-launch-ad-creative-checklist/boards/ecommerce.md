# Pre-launch ad checklist: online store add-on

## Extra checks
- The product shown matches its photo (shape, colour, label) and is shown the way it really opens and is used.
- Price, offer and deadline in the ad match the store right now.
- The ad and its landing page repeat the same promise in the first screen.
- Any shipping, returns or subscription claim matches the store's own terms.
- Fit of the opening to the category: show the result first for things bought again; show the problem the product solves or the make quality for things bought once.
