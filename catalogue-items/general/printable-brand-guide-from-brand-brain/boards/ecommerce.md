# Printable brand guide: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
Per product (hero first, 4 at most): the camera record from the brand brain (Looks like, Pack, How it is used, Visible result, Moves and sounds, Never show, Reference photos) and the brand's own photo files. Brand world (places, surfaces and props, light, people, never in frame) and Motion and sound (pace, camera, sound, on-screen text, formats).

## Extra pages
Insert after Voice and before Components:

- **The product on camera.** One block per product: a row of its reference photos, each captioned with its job (pack front, out of pack, in hand, in use, close up), then Looks like, Pack, How it is used as numbered steps, Visible result, Moves and sounds, and Never show in the "don't" style. A blank field prints "not yet described". A missing photo leaves an outlined empty box with the job named so the gap is visible.
- **The world, motion and sound.** Left: places, surfaces and props, light, people, never in frame, beside 2 to 4 of the brand's own photographs. Right: pace, camera, sound, on-screen text, formats in use. No stock pictures, nothing generated; if the brand has no photo showing its world, the rows stand alone.

## Cheat sheet additions
- The product in 2 lines: what it looks like, and the one thing never to show.
- The world in 1 line: the place, the light, the people.

## Extra check
A maker who reads only the product page would not draw a pouch as a tub or a matt pan as a shiny one. If two products look alike, the page says what tells them apart.
