# Online store add-on: Problem-First Static Ad Concept

Extra inputs
- Product photos by job, so a scene of use is built from a photo of use. The truth sheet if you have one (`product-truth-sheet-from-photos`).
- Visible result recorded by the brand, if any, to decide whether the product may be shown working.

Added rules for a store
- The product may be shown working only when that is the turn of the ad and a person would really see it (an egg sliding in a pan, rain beading on a sleeve). Otherwise show it closed and at rest.
- A product block names the product and the mechanism line; it carries an offer only when the store runs one, in its own words.
- Examples of problem scenes for physical goods: a phone charger cable fraying at the neck while a tape-wrapped spare lies beside it; a mug ring-stained on the inside of a "dishwasher safe" mug.
- Physical-product honesty: no result drawn that the product facts do not state; no cutaway of a part the product does not have.
