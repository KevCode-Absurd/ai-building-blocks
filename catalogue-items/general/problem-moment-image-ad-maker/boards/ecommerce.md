# A snapshot of the moment the problem hits: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Instants a store can show**
- Something people buy once: the instant the old one gives out, such as scraping a pan at the sink or wringing out a soaked sleeve at the bike shed.
- Something people use up: the instant the routine slips, such as the open tub pushed to the back of the counter.

**Product photos:** send none unless you want the product in the picture. If you do, use the real one from its photos, small and to one side, never held up or centred. The picture must still work with it taken out.

**The old way** is generic and unbranded, never a rival's pack or label.
