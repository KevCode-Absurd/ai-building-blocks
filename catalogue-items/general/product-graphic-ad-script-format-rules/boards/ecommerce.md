# Product graphic ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Clean product cutout from the real photo (true colour, label sharp).
- Spec facts: contents or materials, steps of use, bundle contents, price and guarantee.
- Test or review figures that may be charted, each with its source.

## What changes
- **Best fit for stores:** product-aware and most-aware shoppers: what is in the bundle, how to use it in three steps, the offer in plain terms.
- **Icons:** one icon per ingredient, material or step, with a 1 to 4 word label from the product page.
- **Offer strip:** price, bundle, shipping and guarantee in current terms, one line with a button.
- **Never:** redraw the pack as an icon, reset its label in another typeface, or chart a review score as if it were a lab measurement.
