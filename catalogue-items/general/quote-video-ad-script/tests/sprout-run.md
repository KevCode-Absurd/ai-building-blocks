# Test run on Sprout, 2026-10-20

Result: **pass**. Ran on claude-opus-5-5 (effort medium), graded by claude-fable-5-1. Judge list: 6 of 6 met. Invented facts: 0.

A disciplined, usable 45-second AI-presenter script that lands exactly on the 129-word budget, keeps every price, survey figure and quote to the brief, and names Sprout only at the offer. The owner gets three testable openings, honest MISSING lines for cancel steps and guarantee process, and a flagged compliance risk on the personal-health 'you' lines; the opening itself is generic enough that a rival gut gummy could run it, which is the main weakness.
