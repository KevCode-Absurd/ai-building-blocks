# A photo of a visible symptom: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Extra thing to give it:** your product photos, but only if you want the product in the picture. It is never shown on the skin.

**Ideas for physical products**
- Something worn: the mark the old one leaves at the end of the day, such as a red line from a strap, a rub on the heel from a boot, or a dent from a watch band. Show it at the moment it comes off.
- Run the body gate even for these. A shoulder or ankle passes; a hip or chest does not.

**Reminder:** leave the product out unless asked. If asked, use the real one, small and to one side. The picture must still work with it taken out.
