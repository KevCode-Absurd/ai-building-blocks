# Reasons-why page: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Variants, bundle contents, free gifts (only real ones), shipping and returns terms, warranty.

## What changes
- **Offer rows** (up to three) are bundles or packs the store really sells, under its own names. A compare-at price is a real former or separate price. A "free gift" has a value stated only if the store states it.
- **Product detail block** (gallery, price options, gift grid, add-to-cart) comes after the reasons. For a product bought once it shows the size picker, contents and returns instead of a "supply".
- **Reasons for stores** often map to: material or ingredient, how it is made, how long it lasts, what the cheaper kind costs over time, and returns or warranty.
- **Shopify export:** hand the finished copy to `shopify-landing-page-theme-export`.
