# Same ad, new format: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product photos by job (pack front, out of pack, in a hand, in use) and what the pack looks like.
- Current offer terms, if the original names an offer, to flag expired ones.

## What changes
- Rebuild the product from its own photos with shape, colours and label kept, whatever the new look. Show it opening and being used as the store's facts say.
- If the original names an offer that has ended or changed, flag it and leave the words as they are; the owner decides.
- For carousels, keep price and offer on the last card with the call to action.
