# Selfie-style video ad script: for online stores

## Give it as well
- How the product arrives, opens, is held and first used, step by step, plus the sizes or versions you sell. This sets the Show beat and the length.
- Packaging that must be readable on camera, and anything that must never be shown.
- Clips or photos from real customers or creators, with written permission to use them in ads.
- Review lines with their source and date, for the doubt and for any quote.

## Used up or kept
- **Used up** (cream, coffee, supplement): the Show beat is the real dose or pour. The proof moment is the label or the amount, held to the lens. The honest result is what they tasted, smelled or how easy it was, never an effect on a body.
- **Kept** (case, pan, jacket): the Show beat puts it through its job (closing, cooking, a wash). The proof moment is the job done on camera. The honest result is what held up and what didn't.
- **Display only:** if the handling rules say so, the product sits sealed on a surface, turned to show the label. Nobody opens or uses it, and nothing implies its size or what it does. If the words need a use that can't be shown, change the words first.

## Good jobs for it in a store
- A first unboxing, or a first use, filmed in hand.
- One common doubt from your reviews ("I thought it would feel cheap"), said in the Doubt beat and answered in the Show beat.
- Size, fit or "which one do I get" questions, settled by holding the options side by side.

## Store rules
- The anchor is often a size, a count, a time to first use, or a week number from real customer data. Take it from the product page, a test or reviews.
- A creator who has used the product may give their own result. Leave it as a blank for them to fill.
- An AI presenter never says they bought, own or use the product.
- The hand-off says where it's sold and the one thing a buyer should know first: the price after the first order, which size to start with, or the returns window, worded as the store words it.
- Price, bundle and shipping go in the end text, checked against today's offer. No made-up star rating or "sold today" counter.
