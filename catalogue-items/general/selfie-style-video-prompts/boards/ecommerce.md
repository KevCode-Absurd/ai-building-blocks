# Turn your selfie-style script into consistent clips: for online stores

Give it as well: photos of the pack front, the pack in a hand and the product in use; how the pack opens, what comes out and its colour; any visible result; your never-show list.

- How the product is handled decides the set-up. A pack you tear and tip needs two hands, so the phone is propped on the counter.
- Before a pack opens on screen, what's inside (shape, colour, texture) must come from a real photo of it out of the pack. A drawing on the label doesn't count.
- Something the presenter wears is on them from the first frame, and its photo is attached as "what they're wearing".
- Something applied goes on the back of a hand or a forearm unless the brand says more.
- Only a photo of the sealed front: the pack stays on a surface, unopened and untouched.
- Offers and prices are on-screen text added in editing, from the live offer. The presenter says them only if the approved script does.
