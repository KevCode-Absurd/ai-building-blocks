# Ecommerce add-on: selling points

**Extra inputs:** product page specs (materials, dimensions, ingredients), reviews rated 2 to 4 stars (they hold the real objections), returns and warranty terms.

**Chain examples for physical products**
- Construction fact: "pocket springs" explains independent movement; what the sleeper feels is a separate claim needing its own support.
- Material fact: "5-ply steel" is a build fact; keeping heat under a cold steak is a performance claim.

**Point kinds that suit stores:** the easy-care point (what the routine really is), the size or fit point (a way to choose), the "it lasts" point stated as a build fact, never a lifetime promise, and the returns or warranty point last.

**Thresholds:** for an image, 3 points is a full list; add a fourth only for a high-consideration product. Put the guarantee last and word it exactly as the store states it, with its conditions.
