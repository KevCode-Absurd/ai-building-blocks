# For online stores: turn features into reasons to buy

## Extra things to give it
- The product page specs: materials, sizes, ingredients, with where each fact appears.
- Reviews rated two to four stars. They hold the real doubts.
- The returns and warranty terms, word for word.
- The price per item, per day or per use, if you've worked it out.

## How the rungs work for physical products
- "Pocket springs" is a fact. "Each spring moves on its own" is an effect, and needs support. "You don't feel your partner turn over" is a payoff, and needs its own support.
- "5-ply steel" is a fact about how it's made. "Keeps its heat when a cold steak goes in" is an effect, so it needs a test or a spec behind it.

## The five questions in a store
- **Will it work?** A spec with what it means, or a test result.
- **Is it easy?** The real care routine or set-up, step by step.
- **Why this one?** The build fact or routine a rival can't claim.
- **Is it worth it?** The price per year of use, only if the brand states how long it lasts.
- **What if it's not for me?** Returns, exchanges or warranty, last, worded exactly as the store states it, with its conditions.
