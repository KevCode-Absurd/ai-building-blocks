# Get a short film ad script with a twist: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Photos of the product from several sides, and the exact words on the pack.
- How the product is really used, step by step, and how big it is next to a hand.

## What changes
- **The plant:** the real pack, or the thing shoppers use before switching, sits in an early shot without comment, so the payoff has something to pay off.
- **The payoff shot:** the real pack or product in use, label to camera, held long enough to recognise. Same colour and size as on the product page.
- **The product's part:** it causes the turn (the gift opened, the pan lifting the egg cleanly). A logo card at the end doesn't count.
- **Offer text:** price, bundle, shipping or guarantee go on as text in editing, using today's terms only.
- **Customer results:** a made-up character never "reports" a result. A review quote appears as on-screen text with its source, never in a character's mouth.
