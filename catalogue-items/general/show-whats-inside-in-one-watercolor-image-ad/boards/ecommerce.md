# Watercolour image ad: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Sharp photos of the pack's front, side and top, and its label text word for word.
- The full ingredient or material list, copied from the pack or product page.
- The live offer, its terms and its end date.

## Good fits
- Ingredient stories: teas, skincare, supplements, food and drink. Build (glazing) for a long list, Reveal (lift-out) for one hero ingredient.
- Scent and warmth: candles, room sprays, bath goods. Spread.
- Gifts and seasonal ranges where the mood sells. Calm or Hold.

## Store rules
- Every painted ingredient must be on the list you gave.
- Paint scent, mood and contents. Never paint skin, hair or a body changing.
- Lay the real pack or label, cut out from its photo, over the painted one before the ad runs.
- Price and offer terms go on the image in editing, checked against the live offer that day.
