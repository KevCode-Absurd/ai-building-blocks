# Single Static Image Ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: variants, price, bundle options, review count and rating (real figures only), shipping and returns terms.
- Current offer with start and end dates.
- Product photos by job (pack, in use, scale).

## Where it fits
- Offer-led statics for warm shoppers: price or bundle as the third read, product as the image, one proof point as the second.
- Problem-led native statics for cold shoppers: the accommodation or the old way as the image, product small or absent.
- Comparison statics: old way beside the product, one contrast only.

## Store-specific rules
- A star rating or review count is shown only if it is real and current; never a default rating.
- Price, discount and shipping terms are copied from the live offer; if the offer has ended, drop the line.
- The product photo shows the true colour and size; a lifestyle crop must not hide the pack's label.
- Keep the first read about the buyer's problem or the benefit, not the product name.
