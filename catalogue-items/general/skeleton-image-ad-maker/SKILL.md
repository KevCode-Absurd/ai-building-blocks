# Skeleton and X-ray image ad maker

Use this for one still image with a non-graphic stylised skeleton or X-ray silhouette as a visual metaphor. Read the actual brand brain, real product photographs, buyer brief, approved claim evidence and relevant visual references first. The Studio source calls this world `skeleton-xray`; its image format is `skeleton`. It is a creative option, not a reason to make medical promises.

1. Pick one specific buyer problem and one supported proposition. Decide why this metaphor fits the brand and audience. Do not imply diagnosis, bodily change, medical results, clinical evidence or efficacy unless the source supports the exact claim. No injury, gore, misleading anatomy or invented timeline. If a product's visual facts matter, keep the real reference visible and avoid portraying generated details as verified.
2. Design a single static frame or a short static sequence of up to three panels if requested. Specify visual hierarchy, placement, lighting, type, logo, materials, text, real product and the metaphor. Use the brand's actual fonts, palette and logo rather than guessing. Write an image prompt and concise copy grounded in the source; a prompt is not an image.
3. Discover a real image-generation or editing capability, attach authorized reference images and execute it. Record actual tool/model where known, settings and output receipt. If unavailable, stop or clearly label a production brief; never quietly substitute a product-photo card. Add accurate readable typography and authentic logo in a design editor if generation distorts them.
4. Inspect the finished still against actual references and the job spec at output size. A non-maker rejects incorrect logo, font, color, product, claim, composition or legibility. Repair and recheck. Report unresolved problems and rights gaps. Visual reviews are host observations, not owner approval.

Deliver the finished image, editable copy, references, tool receipt, decision rationale and visual review. Do not substitute a video script or claim video was produced.

## Before you start
Use the material below to do this job. If facts or tool access are missing, explain what you can finish and what must wait.

- **Product photos** (yours): clear photos of the real product, from more than one angle. Attach them.

**Recommended steps before this one** (optional; do each once, then reuse what it makes):
1. **Brand file** with the [Brand file builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/website-brand-brain-builder). Bring your website, customer words (optional), your results (optional).

3. This one.

**From the brand file, read:** `index.md`, `look.json`, `offers.json`, `facts.json`. Take how the product looks, the colours, fonts and photo style, and the exact words allowed on the picture: approved claims and offer terms. Leave out review quotes and research notes. Only `live` lines count as facts: stale, `unconfirmed` and `banned` lines do not. If `worked.md` is there, keep what the client kept and leave out what they killed. Source notes and rejected ideas never go into the result.

How to start:
1. **Look first.** Use what they've already attached, pasted or put in their about-me note. In a coding assistant with file access, look in the project too: a brand file is a `context/brand-file/` folder with `index.md` in it. If it's there, read `index.md` and the files above. An older brand brain folder, with `00-start-here.md` in it, still works as material. Look for the other things by name.
2. **Check what's missing.** Look for your brand file, a buyer brief, product photos. They can attach files, paste the details, or share a file path your assistant can read. Offer the linked skills above when they help prepare that material. Ask only for missing facts that change the result. If they ask you to start with what they have, complete the parts you can and mark the gaps.
3. **Do the job.** Follow the method with the facts and tools you have. Mark each gap with `MISSING:` where it belongs and never invent a fact to fill it. Keep the answer tight: say each point once and merge steps that give the same thing. In CHECKS MISSED, list checks that failed or could not run. If every required check ran and passed, write “none”.
