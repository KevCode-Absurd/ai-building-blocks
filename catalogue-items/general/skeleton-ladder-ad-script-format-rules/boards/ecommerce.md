# Skeleton Ladder: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: pack, size, how long one unit lasts, what it replaces (the old way the shopper owns now).
- Review excerpts about the old way's failures, with source.
- Current offer with end date.

## Where it fits
- Consumables and replacements: a product that swaps out several old ones (one concentrate for nine sprays) suits a quantity ladder and a catastrophe payoff.
- Protect-and-maintain products (covers, filters, descalers) suit a time ladder: "what if you never ...".
- Durable goods suit a use-it-long-enough ladder only if the brand states durability numbers.

## Store-specific rules
- Time points and counts (months of wear, number of uses) are claims; take them from the product page, warranty or test data only.
- Never name a competitor's product as the failing old way. Use the category ("single-use gadgets", "old filters").
- Put price, bundle and guarantee in the last frame as on-screen text, with the current end date.
