# Skeleton video shots: for online stores

Extra things to give it: sharp photos of the pack's front, side and top (required), how it opens and how it's used, the old things it replaces, and a source for any rung with a number or time tied to a result.

- In stage 3, make the product sheet from the photos and attach it to every shot the product is in. Write its size against the skeleton's hand once, on the character sheet.
- The old things that pile up (tubs, bottles, gadgets) are plain and unbranded on the set sheet. Never a rival's pack.
- If you don't know how the product opens, it stays closed in every shot.
- Price, bundle and guarantee go on the end card as text in the edit, from the current offer, word for word.
