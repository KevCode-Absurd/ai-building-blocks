# Ecommerce add-on: skeleton "what happens if" video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo (required), how it is used, `Replaces`, `Core benefit`, sourced claims for any rung with a number or timed result.

- Product appears in the skeleton's hand from its tagged photo; size against the skeleton's hand stated once.
- Good fits: worn products (dressed look), anything bought to replace an old habit (catastrophe ladder: the pile of old gadgets grows each rung), anything with a stated benefit that can be felt through reactions.
- Where `How it is used` is missing, the product stands closed.
- Guarantee, price and offer are on-screen text on the final rung from the current offer.
