# Song Ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: name, benefit in one plain phrase, what it replaces.
- Review excerpts: the chorus line should echo the buyer's own words about the outcome.
- Current offer with end date for the last line's on-screen text.

## Where it fits
- Products with a clear emotional outcome and a short benefit phrase (a snack, a pet product, a sleep aid).
- Lower-consideration buys where a catchy line is enough to stay in memory; not for products whose sale rests on specifications.

## Store-specific rules
- Keep the chorus about the outcome, not the SKU. Product names, sizes and prices do not belong in sung lines.
- Check platform rules and music rights for the generated track before running it as a paid ad.
- Offer, price and shipping terms appear only as the last line's on-screen text, checked against the current offer.
