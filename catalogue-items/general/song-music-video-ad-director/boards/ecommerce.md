# Ecommerce add-on: song music video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, how it is used, `Customer language bank` (words that sing), `Core benefit`, offer line.

- Genre by audience: pick from the buyer profile (pop for gifting and beauty, country for outdoor goods, lo-fi for desk and home goods). State it in the style line as a sound description.
- Silent shots can be product moments the lyric names: the old gadgets swept off a shelf, one pack set down in their place.
- The lead may hold the real product (true shape and printing from its photo) but never sings a result as fact.
- Offer line goes on screen after the last chorus, from the current offer.
