# Starter offer list: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
Product prices and pack sizes, whether each product is used up or bought once (with how long one unit lasts), free-delivery threshold and delivery times, returns window and warranty, subscription terms (if sold).

## Store-shaped proposals worth trying
- **Used up:** a longer supply at a lower unit price; a trial size; a subscription (only if the store sells one); a reward on the second order.
- **Bought once:** a pair or one to give; a set that completes it; a care kit; a trial at home or an exchange for size; the warranty as the headline.
- **Either:** free delivery over a level just above the typical order; a gift with a named listed price; early access for the email list; a seasonal deal tied to real shipping cut-off dates.

## Extra checks
- Deliverable by the store: shipped, downloaded, access granted or done for the buyer.
- A first-order price states the ongoing price in the same sentence.
- A bundle shows the saving once, as money or as a share, never both.
