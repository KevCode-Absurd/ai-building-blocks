# Online store add-on: Static Ad and Storyboard Review

Extra inputs
- The product's real photos and truth sheet (`product-truth-sheet-from-photos`) to compare the render against, side by side.

Added rules for a store
- Product identity first: right variant, colour, printed words, closure, count and size against a hand. A wrong product fails the ad regardless of design.
- Check pack counts: items shown in a bundle or offer ad must match the order.
- Check prices, savings and dates against the live offer; a seasonal look does not supply a sale or delivery promise.
- A before and after of a body, or a generated reviewer, fails as evidence.
