# Ecommerce add-on: static ad copy

**Extra inputs:** product photos tagged by job, pack and how it is used, the visible result (only where the store records one), what must never be shown.

**Offer mechanics to check and say plainly**

| Mechanic | Show | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | Price, how often it ships, how to pause or cancel | If research shows fear of lock-in, put the real cancel steps in the primary text; never "cancel anytime" unless true |
| First-order discount | Price today and after | A surprising renewal price returns as refunds; show both |
| Bundle | Per-unit or per-use price and why that quantity | Tie quantity to how the product is used up |
| Sale | Sale price (not only the %), why it is worth it, real end date | "Was" and "lowest ever" only if confirmed |
| Guarantee or returns | Window, what the buyer does, what they get back | Every condition (unused, in box, first order); a warranty is not a lifespan |
| Free gift | What it is and what it is for | Only if it helps them use the product |
| Reason for now | Stock, batch, season, price change | Must be true |

**Price frames for products:** per use ("63 cents a morning", only if price and how long a bottle lasts both come from the store); against what they already spend; effort as the price ("wipe it, oil it, hang it up").

**Extra platform risks for physical goods:** green, safety and origin words ("sustainable", "non-toxic", "made in") only with proof; before-and-after of a person only from real, consented uploads and never for weight or health; the product drawn from its real photos, shown open or in use only as the store's facts say.

**Archetypes that suit products:** mechanism close-up of the material or part that does the work; old way vs new way with objects; sensory close-up (texture, pour, weave); price truth bomb for premium items ("Yes, it's $89. Here's what that buys." then up to three reasons).
