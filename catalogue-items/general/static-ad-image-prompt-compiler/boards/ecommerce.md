# Online store add-on: Static Ad Image Prompt Compiler

Extra inputs
- The product's truth sheet (`product-truth-sheet-from-photos`) as the source of the "few visible anchors": shape, colours, printed words, closure, size against a hand.
- The product photo by job (front, out of pack, in hand, in use), each attached with its job.

Added rules for a store
- State the exact variant and what it is not (the lookalike a model would draw).
- Say the size against a hand, body or furniture, never in units, unless the scene is a dimensions frame.
- A shiny or mirrored product reflects only the scene it stands in.
- One use is one real amount from the real opening.
- Keep the product's pack unchanged when a pack photo is attached; do not redraw the label.
