# Online store add-on: Static Ad Layout Adaptation Workflow

Extra inputs
- Product photos by job (front, out of pack, in hand, in use, close view) for every variant in the ad, plus the product's truth sheet if you have one (`product-truth-sheet-from-photos`).
- Live offer from the store (price, bundle, dates) and, for sale layouts, the catalogue range.

Added rules for a store
- The product must be exact: right colourway, pattern, material and silhouette. No invented variant, no near miss. If a layout shows more items than the store sells, repeat the real one or use fewer; several products in one frame are scaled to a common height, never cropped to fit.
- A studio shot does not say how the product looks worn, held or on a table. A scene of use needs a photo of use.
- Generated imagery is placed beside its reference photo and confirmed as the same product, at 3 to 7 times magnification, before it passes.
- Offer layouts use the live offer in its own words; with no offer on record, pick a non-offer layout.
- Press, "as seen in" and "award-winning" layouts need real placements on file; otherwise use a review or product-fact layout.

Thresholds
- Sale and promo layouts are for people who already know the brand; keep them to a small share of a cold-audience set (see `cold-audience-concept-static-ads`).
