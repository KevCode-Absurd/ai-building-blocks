# Stop image tools redrawing your product and words: for online stores

**Extra things to give it**
- The product's truth sheet (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`, "Stop AI pictures getting your product wrong"), and above all its lock lines. They go into the subject block and the keep line word for word.
- The product photos by role (front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use), each attached with its role.

**Extra rules for a store**
- Name the exact variant in the subject block, and describe the lookalike's opposite ("a stand-up foil pouch" rather than "not a jar").
- Give size against a hand, body or furniture, not in units, unless the image is a dimensions image.
- A shiny or mirrored product reflects only the scene it stands in.
- One use is one real amount, coming out of the real opening.
- With a pack photo attached, the keep line says the label stays as printed.
