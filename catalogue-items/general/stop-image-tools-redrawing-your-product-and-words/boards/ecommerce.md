# Stop image tools redrawing your product and words: for online stores

**Extra things to give it**
- The product's truth sheet (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`, "Stop AI pictures getting your product wrong"). It's the source of the few key visible details: shape, colours, printed words, closure, size against a hand.
- The product photos by job (front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use), each attached with its job.

**Extra rules for a store**
- Name the exact variant and what it is not (the lookalike an image tool would draw).
- Give the size against a hand, body or furniture, never in units, unless the image is a dimensions image.
- A shiny or mirrored product reflects only the scene it stands in.
- One use is one real amount, coming from the real opening.
- When a pack photo is attached, keep the pack unchanged and don't redraw the label.
