# Turn feedback on AI ads into rules: for online stores

## Use rules are product rules
For physical products, how a thing is opened, held and used causes the most repeat mistakes, and each answer belongs to one product. Put each one on that product's rule list and in its entry in `offers.md` (how it's used, what never to show). Some examples of the wording only:

- Prompt: "The candle is lit at its single centre wick." Check: a second wick, or a flame on the jar's rim, fails.
- Prompt: "The backpack's front buckle clips from left to right." Check: a buckle on the side or a zip in its place fails.
- Prompt: "The serum pump is pressed straight down with one finger." Check: a twisted cap or drops from the side fails.

Real entries come from your own feedback, not from these.

## Where the facts come from
For each product in `offers.md`: how it looks, the pack, how it's used and what never to show. If a product has no recorded way to open or use it, the first any-product rule applies: show it closed or at rest, and ask the brand.

## Extra checks
- A pack, part or label is described in the same words every time it appears in a set.
- A product photo beats a description written from a file name. Where they disagree, ask.
- Photos show the product as sold now. Its colour is its own, not the colour of the backdrop it was shot on.
