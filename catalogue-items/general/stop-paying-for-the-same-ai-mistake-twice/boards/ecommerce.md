# Turn feedback on AI ads into rules: for online stores

## Rules about using the product belong to the brand
For physical products, "how it's opened, held and used" is the biggest source of repeat mistakes, and every answer is specific to one product. Record each one in that brand's list and in the product's record (how it's used, never show). For example:

- "The sachet is torn straight across its top edge. Never from the side, the bottom or a corner."
- "The jacket zips from the hem up. Never shown zipped from the collar down."
- "The pan's non-stick face is the inside. Never shown cooking on the outer base."

These three only show the wording. Real entries come from the brand's own feedback.

## Where the facts come from
For each product in the brand file: how it's used, what never to show, what it looks like, the pack. If a product has no recorded way of opening or use, the first general rule applies: show it closed or at rest, and ask the brand.

## Extra checks
- A pack, part or label described in a prompt uses the same words every time it appears in a set.
- A product photo beats a description written from file names. Where they disagree, ask.
- Photos show the product as sold. The product's colour is its own colour, not the colour of the background it was photographed on.
