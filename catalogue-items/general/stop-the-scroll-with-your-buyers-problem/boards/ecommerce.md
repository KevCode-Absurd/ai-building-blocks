# For online stores: Stop the scroll with your buyer's problem

**Extra things to give it**
- Product photos sorted by what they show, so a scene of use is built from a photo of use. Add the product truth sheet if you have one (`stop-ai-pictures-getting-your-product-wrong`).
- Any visible result the brand has recorded, to decide whether the product may be shown working.

**Extra rules for a store**
- Show the product working only when that is the turn of the ad and a person would really see it (an egg sliding in a pan, rain beading on a sleeve). Otherwise show it closed and at rest.
- The product block names the product and the line on how it works. It carries an offer only when the store runs one, in the store's own words.
- Examples of problem scenes for physical goods: a phone charger cable fraying at the neck, with a tape-wrapped spare beside it; a "dishwasher safe" mug ring-stained on the inside.
- Honesty for physical products: draw no result the product facts do not state, and no cut-away of a part the product does not have.
