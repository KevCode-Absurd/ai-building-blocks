# For online stores: Find the story ad worth writing first

**Extra things to give it**
- Reviews with where each came from (your site, a retailer, a review site), the stars when you have them, and whether the customer agreed to ad use.
- A rough count of rivals telling the same story. Search the Meta Ad Library for your main problem phrase in quotes, set to active ads in your country.

**Fit examples**
- Used up (a cream, a supplement, pet food): look for the routine that kept slipping and the tubs left half full.
- Bought once (a frying pan, a rain jacket, a mattress): look for the last one that broke, and what buyers stopped putting up with.

**Where the click goes.** Use "Learn more" to an article-style page on your own site that opens on the same moment. Send strangers to the product page only from that article, never straight from the ad.

**The bar for results.** Use your own last month of ads for new customers as the bar, not figures from someone else's store. If you have no history yet, run the first set to set one, and judge rows against each other.
