## 4. CLAIM LIMITS

**The story may say:**
- "60+ ingredients, 21 vitamins & minerals, and 6g fiber" in one pack. Use 21, never "20+".
- The survey lines, always with "of surveyed users" and the brand-reported survey of 3,000+ customers.
- "Supports digestion and gut health", only as a side note in this story.
- Vegan, pectin base, free from gluten, dairy, nuts, synthetic dyes and sweeteners.
- Third-party tested (70 pesticides, 4 heavy metals, 16 contaminants, 9 microbial contaminants).
- Methylfolate, not folic acid.
- "Clinically tested for nutrient absorption", only with the study named and its 2 numbers (vitamin C +40.1%, folate +20.5%).
- Customer quotes from section 6, word for word, as "one customer wrote".

**The story may not:**
- Say or imply Sprout replaces a powder nutrient for nutrient. There is no evidence on file.
- Use "$1.26 vs $3.60", which is dated and on the risky list.
- Name the rival powder or show its tub branding.
- Say "works", "backed by 35,000+ studies", "revive whole body vitality", or anything about energy, immunity, skin, metabolism or weight.
- Claim 100% of daily needs "per gummy". It is per pack, and only for key vitamins.
- Mention GLP-1 or reuse #11 or #14.
- Say "4.8 stars from 100,000 reviews" without naming the source.
- Promise easy cancelling beyond the site's own words.
- Show $29.99 without saying it needs the text sign-up discount and renews at $49.99 every 4 weeks.
- Call the 30-day guarantee anything other than first order only.
