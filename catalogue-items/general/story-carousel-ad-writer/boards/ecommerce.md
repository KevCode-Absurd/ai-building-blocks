# For online stores: write a carousel ad people swipe through

## Extra things to give it
Bundle contents and the price of each item, sizes or variants, your returns window and your free-shipping threshold.

## Card ideas for stores
- **Range or bundle:** one card per item and its job in the set, then the price set against something real. Any price per item or per use must come from your own numbers.
- **Fixes that failed, for things that last:** the cheap one replaced twice, then the one that lasts, with a build fact (material, thickness, how it's joined) on the "how it works" card.
- **Before you buy:** a size or fit card, a care card (the real steps) and a returns card with its conditions.
- **What to expect:** first use, tenth use, first season, only where you can back each one.
- **Card headlines:** each card can link to its own product. Use the headline under a product card for its name and price.

## The last card for stores
The action, what ships when, and the guarantee in your own words. For example: "Tap Shop now. Ships Monday. 60 days to send it back."
