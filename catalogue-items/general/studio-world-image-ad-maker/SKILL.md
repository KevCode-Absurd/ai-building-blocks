# Studio visual world image ad maker

Read the complete org context, buyer brief, authorized product photos and `references/studio-worlds.json` before choosing a world. The source is the Studio example-app registry: its IDs, modes, look and aliases are design options, not evidence of performance or permission. Each world is a possible visual treatment, not a promise that every product fits it. Select from the whole relevant palette; do not confuse aspect ratios, headline changes or colors with materially different concepts.

## Make the work

1. State the buyer job, one truthful argument, product and placement. Extract the actual visual references, logo, fonts, colors, image permissions, claim limits and unresolved gaps. A site sample is not necessarily an approved campaign identity. Preserve real product geometry and do not present generated jewelry details or implied scale as verified facts.
2. Select the world by its real ID, inspect its `look`, and write a composition specific to this product: subject, hierarchy, spatial relationship, lighting/material, how the real product appears, exact intended copy and why it serves this buyer. Use the dedicated clay scene, clay town, comic strip or paper cutout image-ad skill when that exact method is a better fit; load it in full. Do not invent testimonials, claims, comparisons or medical evidence.
3. Discover the actual image-generation/editing or design capability and its reference-image support. Attach the authorized product and brand references. Name the real tool, model when exposed, parameters, reference hashes and output receipt. If required capability is absent, stop or explicitly label the result a production brief and ask for a meaningful alternative; do not silently replace generation with a photo-card or call a prompt a finished image. Add exact typography and the authentic logo in an editor when image text or marks are inaccurate.
4. Render the requested output. Inspect the full result, not merely output dimensions, browser layout or a contact sheet. Compare logo, typography, colors, product fidelity, composition and visual impact against actual references and the specific job. Have a non-maker reject mismatches and record observations per artifact. Repair, then recheck. Report what failed, missing capabilities and unknowns. A recorded PASS is the host's observation, not proof of quality or owner approval.

Deliver the finished image, editable copy and decisions, the selected world ID, source pins and visual review. Never claim a human approved it without evidence.

## Before you start
Use the material below to do this job. If facts or tool access are missing, explain what you can finish and what must wait.

**What you need** (use your own material or the linked skill):
- **Org context**, made by the [Org context builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/website-brand-brain-builder): who you are, your buyers, voice, look, markets, claims and offers, kept in one folder.
- **A buyer brief**, made by the [Buyer brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/buyer-brief-writer): who it's for, how much they already know, and what will move them.
- **Product photos** (yours): clear photos of the real product, from more than one angle. Attach them.

**Recommended steps before this one** (optional; do each once, then reuse what it makes):
1. **Org context** with the [Org context builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/website-brand-brain-builder). Bring your website, customer words (optional), your results (optional).
2. **A buyer brief** with the [Buyer brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/buyer-brief-writer). Bring customer words. It uses your org context.
3. This one.

**From the org context, read:** `index.md`, `look.json`, `foundation/design.md`, `offers.json`, `facts.json`. Take how the product looks, the colours, fonts and photo style, and the exact words allowed on the picture: approved claims and offer terms. Leave out review quotes and research notes. Only `live` lines count as facts: stale, `unconfirmed` and `banned` lines do not. If `worked.md` is there, keep what the client kept and leave out what they killed. Source notes and rejected ideas never go into the result.

How to start:
1. **Look first.** Use what they've already attached, pasted or put in their about-me note. In a coding assistant with file access, look in the project too: an org context is a `context/org-context/` folder with `index.md` in it. If it's there, read `index.md` and the files above. An older `context/brand-file/` folder, or a brand folder with `00-start-here.md` in it, still works as material. Look for the other things by name.
2. **Check what's missing.** Look for your org context, a buyer brief, product photos. They can attach files, paste the details, or share a file path your assistant can read. Offer the linked skills above when they help prepare that material. Ask only for missing facts that change the result. If they ask you to start with what they have, complete the parts you can and mark the gaps.
3. **Do the job.** Follow the method with the facts and tools you have. Mark each gap with `MISSING:` where it belongs and never invent a fact to fill it. Keep the answer tight: say each point once and merge steps that give the same thing. In CHECKS MISSED, list checks that failed or could not run. If every required check ran and passed, write “none”.

## Other static Studio variants

For `ugc`, use an authorized real person or an explicitly labelled fictional demonstration; never invent a customer, testimonial or endorsement. For `podcast`, make a clearly illustrative static composition; do not invent a real recording or quotation. For `satisfying`, show one frozen product action supported by actual product evidence; do not imply unverified performance. For `founder`, use an authorized real founder photo and their actual words, not a generated likeness or fabricated statement. For any of these, the same brand, rights, render and visual review gates apply. Use `references/studio-formats.json` for the source variant identifiers and descriptions. A video-format label does not authorize or imply a video.
