# Studio visual world image ad maker

Read the complete brand brain, buyer brief, authorized product photos and `references/studio-worlds.json` before choosing a world. The source is the Studio example-app registry: its IDs, modes, look and aliases are design options, not evidence of performance or permission. Each world is a possible visual treatment, not a promise that every product fits it. Select from the whole relevant palette; do not confuse aspect ratios, headline changes or colors with materially different concepts.

## Make the work

1. State the buyer job, one truthful argument, product and placement. Extract the actual visual references, logo, fonts, colors, image permissions, claim limits and unresolved gaps. A site sample is not necessarily an approved campaign identity. Preserve real product geometry and do not present generated jewelry details or implied scale as verified facts.
2. Select the world by its real ID, inspect its `look`, and write a composition specific to this product: subject, hierarchy, spatial relationship, lighting/material, how the real product appears, exact intended copy and why it serves this buyer. Use the dedicated clay scene, clay town, comic strip or paper cutout image-ad skill when that exact method is a better fit; load it in full. Do not invent testimonials, claims, comparisons or medical evidence.
3. Discover the actual image-generation/editing or design capability and its reference-image support. Attach the authorized product and brand references. Name the real tool, model when exposed, parameters, reference hashes and output receipt. If required capability is absent, stop or explicitly label the result a production brief and ask for a meaningful alternative; do not silently replace generation with a photo-card or call a prompt a finished image. Add exact typography and the authentic logo in an editor when image text or marks are inaccurate.
4. Render the requested output. Inspect the full result, not merely output dimensions, browser layout or a contact sheet. Compare logo, typography, colors, product fidelity, composition and visual impact against actual references and the specific job. Have a non-maker reject mismatches and record observations per artifact. Repair, then recheck. Report what failed, missing capabilities and unknowns. A recorded PASS is the host's observation, not proof of quality or owner approval.

Deliver the finished image, editable copy and decisions, the selected world ID, source pins and visual review. Never claim a human approved it without evidence.
## Other static Studio variants

For `ugc`, use an authorized real person or an explicitly labelled fictional demonstration; never invent a customer, testimonial or endorsement. For `podcast`, make a clearly illustrative static composition; do not invent a real recording or quotation. For `satisfying`, show one frozen product action supported by actual product evidence; do not imply unverified performance. For `founder`, use an authorized real founder photo and their actual words, not a generated likeness or fabricated statement. For any of these, the same brand, rights, render and visual review gates apply. Use `references/studio-formats.json` for the source variant identifiers and descriptions. A video-format label does not authorize or imply a video.
