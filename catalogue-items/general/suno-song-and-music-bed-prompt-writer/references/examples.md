# Suno Prompter — Quick Examples

## Cinematic instrumental

User direction:
> Dark anime fight scene. Starts restrained, then explodes. No vocals.

Target shape:
```text
[GENRE: Cinematic × Dark Orchestral (95 BPM)]
[VOCALS: instrumental only; 95% no vocals; never singing; no lyrics; no vocal chops]
[INSTRUMENTATION: tense low strings ostinato; thunderous taiko drums; distorted sub bass; sharp brass stabs; distant granular textures]
[STRUCTURE: restrained intro → tension build → impact transition → explosive peak → stripped breakdown → final climax]
[MIX: dark cinematic; deep controlled low end; wide strings; drums centered and punchy; strong dynamics; cohesive orchestral glue]
[MOOD/ATMOSPHERE: ruined city at night; danger closing in; controlled fear turning into violent resolve]
[STYLE NOTES: escalating anime battle score with a clean, brutal payoff]
```

## Voice-over bed

User direction:
> Futuristic background track for a 30-second AI tutorial. Needs space for narration.

Target shape:
```text
[GENRE: Ambient × Synthwave (100 BPM)]
[VOCALS: instrumental only; 95% no vocals; never singing; no lyrics; no vocal chops]
[INSTRUMENTATION: soft analog synth pulse; restrained electronic kick; airy pads; subtle plucked arpeggio]
[STRUCTURE: immediate hook → minimal groove → gentle lift → clean resolve]
[MIX: polished digital clarity; mid-scooped 1k–4kHz for VO clarity; mono low end; wide pads; controlled dynamics]
[MOOD/ATMOSPHERE: sleek creative studio at night; focused, futuristic, optimistic]
[STYLE NOTES: minimal modern AI-tech bed built to support spoken narration]
```
