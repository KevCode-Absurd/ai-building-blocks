# Talking object image ad: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Review lines that name an object ("a graveyard of tubs under my sink", "my wobbly suitcase"), each with where it came from.
- Sharp photos of the pack's front, side and top, its label text word for word, and its real size next to the narrator.
- The live offer, its terms and its end date.

## Good fits
- A product that replaces something people keep: the old item makes the best has-been (the scratched pan, the leaky bottle, the split zip bag).
- Home and kitchen problems with a witness in the room: a hook, a lamp, a spoon, a shelf.

## Store rules
- Show the general type of the old thing, never a rival's pack, shape or colours.
- The product stands at true scale beside the object, label readable, and never gets a face.
- Lay the real pack, cut out from its photo, over the one in the picture before the ad runs.
- Price and offer terms go on the image in editing, checked against the live offer that day. The object never says them.
