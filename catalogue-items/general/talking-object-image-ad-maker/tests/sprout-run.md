# Test run on Sprout, 2026-10-19

Result: **pass**. Ran on claude-opus-5-5 (effort medium), graded by claude-fable-5-1. Judge list: 5 of 5 met. Invented facts: 0.

A usable, well-built ad plan: the shaker from review 6 is a strong has-been, the bubble line is short and in character, and the offer is shown with its real renewal terms, which is more honest than the brand's own page. The owner can hand the prompt and edit steps straight to a designer with only pouch dimensions and the live-offer check left to confirm.

## Judge list

- Met: Shown for 2 seconds, someone can say what the object is fed up about and what the product is.
  Crusted shaker in the rack, sheepish face, "Turns out I was the problem.", sharp green pouch at right edge, name in bottom band.
- Met: The bubble has 7 words or fewer, says "I" or "me", and never says "you".
  "Turns out I was the problem." Six words, first person, no "you".
- Met: There are at most 3 other things to read, in type big and dark enough to read on a phone.
  Product name 44 px, offer line 30 px, button 34 px; cream on #002C17 "about 15:1", yellow button "about 11:1".
- Met: The object says nothing it couldn't have seen, and every fact matches your claims.
  Bubble is an admission only; primary text quotes "'60+ ingredients, 21 vitamins & minerals, and 6g fiber' in 1 pack" and the given offer with renewal terms.
- Met: The product matches your photos, has no face, and has your real pack laid over it.
  "Cut the pouch out of the front photo and lay it exactly over the generated pouch... No face on the pack."
