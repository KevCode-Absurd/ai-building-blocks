# Ecommerce add-on: talking object video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front or out-of-pack photo, `Replaces`, `Visible result`, `Tried before`, `Never show`.

Narrator choices by product (examples to adapt):

| Product | Witness | Thing it replaces | The problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bamboo bath mat | the towel rail | the soggy cotton mat | the puddle by the tub |
| Descaling tablets | the empty mug on the shelf | the old limescaled kettle | the white crust inside the kettle |
| Phone stand | the tangled charger cable | the stack of books it props the phone on | none (result not visible) |

- The product stands at the frame edge at true size against the object.
- The object never states a price or offer; offer text goes on the last shot from the current offer.
