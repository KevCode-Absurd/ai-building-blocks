# Talking object video ad script: for online stores

## Give it as well
- Review lines that name an object ("a graveyard of tubs under my sink", "my wobbly suitcase"), each with where it came from.
- The product's real size next to the narrator, the pack, and what it replaces.
- The live offer and its end date. It goes on screen as text, and the object never says it.

## Where it works best
- A product that replaces something people keep: the old item makes the best has-been (the scratched pan, the leaky bottle, the split zip bag).
- Home and kitchen problems with a witness in the room: a hook, a lamp, a spoon, a shelf.

## Store rules
- Start from an object your reviews mention. A kind of product buyers tried and gave up on is the richest has-been.
- Show the general type of the old thing, never a rival's pack, shape or colours.
- In a shelf shot the product stands at true scale beside the object, label readable.
- Price, offer and shipping terms stay in the on-screen text on the last shot, checked against the live offer.
