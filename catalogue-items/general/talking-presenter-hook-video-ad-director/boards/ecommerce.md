# Ecommerce add-on: talking presenter hook

Extra inputs: pack-front, in-hand and in-use photos; handling evidence (a front-only photo supports display, not in-hand scale or use); `Replaces`; `Visible result`; `Never show`; a real offer date with terms for the deadline lever.

Hook line patterns to rewrite in the brand's voice (illustrative, with the cue word last):

- Mirror: "If your sink has four half-empty sprays under it, look at *this*."
- Put it down: "Stop. Put the cheap sponge back. Check the back of *this* one."
- Open question: "Why does the grout go grey in the same corner every single *time*?"
- Number first: "Three hundred grams. Now weigh the one you already *own*." (only with the sourced weight)
- Replaces these: "These three can go. *This* does all of it."

- Hands: with display-only handling, the presenter points to or stands beside the product on a support; nobody opens or uses it.
- Offer and price are never spoken; the deadline lever uses the real date only.
