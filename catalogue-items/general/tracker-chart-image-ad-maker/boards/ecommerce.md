# For online stores: Get a second look with a test result photo

**Readings a store's buyers already check**
- Things people use up: a habit or streak calendar, a pill organiser count, a sleep chart, a water-intake app.
- Things people buy once: a water-test strip for a filter, a room humidity reading for a dehumidifier, a step count for shoes. Only when the brand's facts speak to that reading.

**Make the reading yourself when you can.** A real chart built from the story's real numbers (in a spreadsheet or a habit app) and photographed on a phone beats any generated screen, and every number in it is true.

**Product photos.** Send none unless the brief asks for the product.
