My habit app has a row called "vitamins". In March it had 4

I've tried a few green powders over the years. The issue was
never what was in them. It was sticking with it.
Sprout is one daily pack you tear open and eat: a small handful

**2. Scene**
Someone's phone on a kitchen counter showing a habit app where the "vitamins" row for March is almost entirely empty.

**3. Where and when**
- **Place and time:** A kitchen counter on a weekday morning, with cool daylight from a window to one side.
