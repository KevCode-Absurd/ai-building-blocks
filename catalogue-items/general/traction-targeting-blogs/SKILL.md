**When not to use:** to compare all 19 growth channels first (use the [Growth channel shortlist planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/traction-channel-ranker)), when you have no buyer evidence yet (talk to a few likely buyers first), or for free press coverage from journalists (ask a PR person; this plan covers paid or guest placements only).

## Targeting Blogs

Decision to resolve: Which trusted niche publication already reaches the precise buyers the business needs?

1. **Shortlist five to eight publications.** Blogs, newsletters or creators whose recent work fits your buyer. For each, record two or three recent pieces, who they seem written for, and the date you checked. Do not treat follower counts as buying intent.
2. **Compare three ways in.** A guest contribution, an early product preview and a disclosed sponsorship. State what the publisher and the reader get from each.
3. **Write the sponsorship brief.** For the chosen placement: the publication, the audience fit with evidence, the offer, the landing page, the tracking link or code, the cash cap and the review date. Treat current rates, availability and contacts as unknown until verified.

Cheap pilot: Prepare a small publisher shortlist and one contribution or sponsorship proposal, then test a limited approved placement. If no limits were given, propose a starting cash cap of one placement, up to 5 hours of work, and a review 30 days after it runs, all labelled as assumptions for the person to approve or change.

Produce: A publication-fit table, contribution pitch, sponsorship brief and tracked landing-page plan.

Channel measures: Qualified referred visitors, activated users, paid accounts and cost by publication; retain publisher and cohort identifiers.

Avoid this mistake: Do not merge paid placements with earned press. Current publication audiences and prices require fresh evidence.

General-business adaptation: Choose trade, local or interest publications with buyers for the real product. Replace activation with a qualified enquiry or order.
