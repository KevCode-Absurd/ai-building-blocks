# A real complaint that reads as praise: for online stores

Use this with the main skill when the story ad sells a physical product.

**Grumbles that pass the test for physical products** (made-up wording, to show the shape)
- Others take it: "bought one, my husband took it, had to order another".
- It made something pointless: "now I've given away my other pans".
- It gets used up because it's used: "the refills go faster than I planned, because I actually use it now".

**Where to find them:** return-reason notes, post-purchase emails and marketplace questions, as well as star reviews. Record the stars and the customer's permission with each one.
