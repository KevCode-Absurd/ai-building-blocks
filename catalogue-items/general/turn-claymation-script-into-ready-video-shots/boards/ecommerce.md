# Claymation video shots: for online stores

## Also give it
A photo of the pack front (required), the product out of its pack, how it's used, how it works, and your "never show" list.

## Ideas for the changing object

| What your product does better | Clay object that changes | What acts on it |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer steps in a routine (one cleanser replaces a shelf) | A leaning stack of grey bottles that shrinks to one | The product set down beside it |
| Even cooking (heavy-base skillet) | A pale clay patch under the pan that spreads warm orange edge to edge | The skillet itself, a real function shown as it is |
| Keeps feet dry (waterproof boots) | Blue clay droplets that bead and slide off | The boot's upper |

- Decide the pack's size against the character once (waist to shoulder high), then keep it identical in every clip.
- If the product has no visible result, show the routine or a plain metaphor. Never a result on someone's body.
- The offer and price go in as on-screen text over the last shot, from your current offer.
