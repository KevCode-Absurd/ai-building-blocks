# Comic panel video shots: for online stores

## Also give it
A photo of the pack front and of the product out of its pack, how it's used, any visible result, and your "never show" list.

## What changes
- The drawn product copies the photo's shape and colours: a pouch stands upright, a pan lies flat with its handle out, a shirt is folded. No letters on the drawn pack.
- Make the product the brightest thing in its panels. If the pack is black, white or grey, give it a thin outline in your brand colour so it still stands out.
- Example panel list for a lip balm: 1 a woman at a bus stop, wind in her hair (scene); 2 close on her lips pressed tight (action to action); 3 the balm in her pocket (subject to subject); 4 she applies it, slowed down (moment to moment, the reveal); 5 that evening, laughing with a friend (scene to scene); 6 photo panel.
- If the visible result is "nothing visible", the changed panel shows the routine going easier, not a measured outcome.
- Prices and offers are text on the photo panel, from your current offer.
