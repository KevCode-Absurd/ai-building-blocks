# Comic page video shots: for online stores

## Also give it
A photo of the pack front and of the product out of its pack (both attached to every still and clip), the pack colour, how it's used, any visible result, and your "never show" list.

## What changes
- The real product stands or lies on the desk beside the page the way its photo shows it: a pouch upright, a pan flat with the handle out, a garment folded.
- Accent colour: the pack colour. If the pack is black, white, grey or many colours, use your main brand colour.
- Example page plan for a lip balm: page 1, a woman at a desk wincing at cracked lips, then the same woman outdoors in wind, worse. Page 2, a cross-section of the stick (wax, oil, three empty label tags), then the balm arrives and the accent with it. Page 3, the same scenes, comfortable.
- If the visible result is "nothing visible", the turn page shows the routine going differently, not a measured outcome.
- Prices and offers: on-screen text on the final product frame, from your current offer.
