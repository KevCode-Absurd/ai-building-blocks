# Cutout explainer video shots: for online stores

## Also give it
Product photos by job (front, out of the pack, in use), how the product works in the brand's words, and any figures (sizes, weights, counts) with sources.

## What changes
- Cut the product out in full colour from its photo, front on. Make it larger in the frame if its label blurs.
- Example storyboard for a travel mug: "Most mugs leak at the lid." (cutout of a mug lid, ring round the seam) / "This one seals in two places." (diagram of two rings, an arrow from a blank strip to each) / "So it rides upside down in a bag." (the old mug slides off left, the product slides to the centre).
- Compare with "the old way" or "the category", never a named rival.
- Prices, offers and guarantee text are set in editing from your current offer, on the last scene.
