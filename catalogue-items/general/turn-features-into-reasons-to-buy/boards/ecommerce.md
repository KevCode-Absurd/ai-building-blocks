# For online stores: turn features into reasons to buy

## Extra things to give it
- The product page specs: materials, sizes, ingredients.
- Reviews rated two to four stars. They hold the real objections.
- The returns and warranty terms.

## How the chain works for physical products
- A fact about how it's built, such as "pocket springs", explains that each spring moves on its own. What the sleeper feels is a separate claim that needs its own support.
- A material fact, such as "5-ply steel", is a fact about how it's made. Keeping heat when a cold steak goes in is a performance claim.

## Kinds of point that suit stores
- The easy-care point: what the routine really is.
- The size or fit point: a way to choose.
- The "it lasts" point, stated as a fact about how it's built, never as a lifetime promise.
- The returns or warranty point, last.

## How many
On an image, three points is a full list. Add a fourth only for a product people think hard about before buying. Put the guarantee last, and word it exactly as the store states it, with its conditions.
