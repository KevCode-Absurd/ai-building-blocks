# Knitted wool video shots: for online stores

## Give it as well
Clean photos of the pack's front, side and top, its label text word for word with its colours, how big it is next to the doll, and how it's used.

## What changes for stores
- In stage 3, make the crocheted pack first and hold it next to the real one. Put large logo areas in intarsia (blocks of colour) and keep small print for the real label laid over in editing.
- For buyers who already know you, the pack can be the avatar, and stages 1 to 3 become the product and room sheets.
- Show use as your own steps, one gesture per clip (a crocheted hand tipping the pack).
- Pet products, home goods, gifts and awkward health subjects suit this warm world. Keep faces at full or medium shot and use close-ups for hands and the product.
- Price and offer go on the end card as text in the edit, from the offer that is live that day.
