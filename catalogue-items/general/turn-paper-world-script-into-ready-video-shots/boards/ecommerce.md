# Paper world video shots: for online stores

## Give it as well
Clean photos of the pack's front, side and top, its label text word for word with its colours, how big it is next to the paper figure, and how it's used.

## What changes for stores
- In stage 3, make the card pack first and hold it next to the real one. For a busy label, use a printed photo of the real pack cut out and set in the scene, so every word stays right.
- A gift or launch reveal often needs no figure: the pack is the avatar, and stages 1 to 3 become the product and set sheets.
- Show use as your own steps, one paper move per clip (a pinned hand tipping the pack).
- A pop-up of the product suits gifts and launches. Layered planes suit premium shelf goods. Flat-cut figures suit everyday problem-solvers.
- Price and offer go on the end card as text in the edit, from the offer that is live that day.
