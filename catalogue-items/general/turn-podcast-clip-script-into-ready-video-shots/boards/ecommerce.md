# Podcast clip video shots: for online stores

## Give it as well
A photo of the pack front, the product's height against a mug or glass, how it's held and opened, your never-show list, and the doubts buyers raise in reviews.

## Store rules
- The product stands on the table, front to camera, from frame 1. Only a small one-hand item with stated handling (a sachet, a tube) is picked up, in its own beat.
- Things people wear hang on a wall hook; anything large stands on the floor at the table's end.
- Speakers state product facts as facts about the product, never as their own use.
- Offer and price are on-screen text over the last two seconds, from the live offer, with the renewal terms beside the first-order price.
