# Podcast clip video shots: for online stores

## Also give it
A photo of the pack front, a height reference for the product (as tall as a glass, a mug), how it's held and opened, your "never show" list, and the doubts customers raise in reviews.

## What changes
- The Host's first line is a real buyer complaint in the buyer's own words (from reviews), turned into a question.
- Speakers state product facts as facts about the product, never as their own use.
- Example exchange for a desk cushion (made-up figures, to be replaced with the brand's real facts): Host: "Why does every seat cushion go flat by spring?" Guest: "Cheap foam gives up. This one is a gel grid, no foam in it." Host: "Wait. Nothing to squash?"
- Only small one-hand items with stated handling are picked up (a sachet, a tube). Big or worn items stay on display.
- Offer and price are on-screen text on the last clip only.
