# Skeleton video shots: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front (required), how it's used, what it replaces, its main benefit, and a source for any step with a number or timed result.

- The product appears in the skeleton's hand, made from its labelled photo. State its size against the skeleton's hand once.
- Good fits: things people wear (use the dressed look), anything bought to replace an old habit (a catastrophe ladder where the pile of old gadgets grows each step), and anything whose benefit can be shown through people's reactions.
- If you don't know how it's used, the product stands closed.
- The guarantee, price and offer go on screen as text on the final step, taken from the current offer.
