# Skeleton video shots: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a sharp photo of the pack front (required), how it opens and how it's used, the old things it replaces, and a source for any rung with a number or time tied to a result.

- Make the product its own reference image from the photo, and attach it to every shot it's in. Say its size against the skeleton's hand once, in the shot map.
- The old things that pile up (tubs, bottles, gadgets) are plain and unbranded. Never a rival's pack.
- If you don't know how the product opens, it stays closed in every shot.
- Price, bundle and guarantee go on the last shot as text in editing, from the current offer, word for word.
