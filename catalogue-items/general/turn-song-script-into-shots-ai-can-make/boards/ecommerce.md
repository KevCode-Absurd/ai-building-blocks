# Song video shots: for online stores

Extra things to give it: photos of the pack's front, side and top, how it's used, the main benefit, and the offer line.

- In stage 3, make the product sheet from the photos and attach it to every shot the product is in. Keep its true shape and printing.
- Show the pack in the first 5 seconds of the storyboard, even though the brand name is sung only in the tag.
- Silent shots can be product moments the lyric names: the old things pushed aside, one pack set down in their place.
- The lead may hold the real product but never sings a result as fact.
- The offer line goes on screen over the tag in the edit, from the current offer.
