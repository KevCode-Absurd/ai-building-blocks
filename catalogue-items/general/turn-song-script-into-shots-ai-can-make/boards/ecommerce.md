# Song video shots: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, how it's used, the main benefit, and the offer line.

- Make the product its own reference image from the photo and attach it to every shot it's in. Keep its true shape and printing.
- Show the pack in the first 5 seconds, even though the brand name is sung only in the tag.
- Silent shots can be product moments the lyric names: the old things pushed aside, one pack set down in their place.
- The lead may hold the real product but never sings a result as fact.
- The offer line goes on screen over the tag in editing, from the current offer.
