# Song video shots: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, how it's used, a list of your customers' words (phrases that sing well), the main benefit, and the offer line.

- Pick the music style from the buyer (pop for gifts and beauty, country for outdoor goods, lo-fi for desk and home goods). Put it in the style line as a description of the sound.
- Silent shots can be product moments the lyric names: old gadgets swept off a shelf, one pack set down in their place.
- The lead may hold the real product (true shape and printing from its photo) but never sings a result as fact.
- The offer line goes on screen after the last chorus, from the current offer.
