# Build your brand file: for online stores

## Extra pages to read
Collections, 5 to 10 product pages, the shipping and returns policies, the size guide or ingredients and materials page, and the subscription terms.

## Extra things to give it
- Store data: best sellers by revenue, repeat purchase rate, and products that get visits but don't sell.
- A review export (from any reviews app) or a paste of the reviews page.
- Email results: top subject lines, the automatic emails that are switched on, list size.
- Product photos. Take them from the store's own product pages or product feed, one folder per product, with file names in the order they show. Skip items that aren't products (shipping protection, gift cards). If the photos can't be fetched, ask the user to drop them in.

## Extra fields for each product
- **Kind:** how it's used (eaten, put on skin, worn, held, placed in a room) and how it's bought (once, again when it runs out, by subscription), with how long one unit lasts.
- **Made of, size and fit, what's in the box, how long it lasts and care.** For things that get used up: ingredients with amounts, serving, pack size. For things you keep: materials, dimensions, weight, sizes, care, warranty.
- **What the camera sees**, written only from looking at the photos (never from a product title or file name):
  - Looks like: shape, the product's own colour (not the background), finish, size next to a hand, what it must never be mistaken for.
  - Pack: what it's sold in, its colours, every large printed word and where it sits; versions that look different.
  - How it's used: the steps as hand actions.
  - Visible result: what a viewer can see change, or "Nothing a camera can show" and what can be shown instead.
  - Moves and sounds: 2 to 4 real movements with their sounds (mark sounds you haven't heard as "reasoned").
  - Never show: mistakes that are easy to make with this product.
  - Reference photos: one for each job below.

## Photo jobs
Sort plain photos (no text, collage or badges) by job: pack front, out of the pack, in a hand (for size), in use, close-up. Designed images stay in the folder but don't fill a job. Tell the user about any job with no photo, because those photos decide whether generated pictures and videos get the product right. A phone photo on a plain surface is enough.

## Extra file content
- `brand-guidelines.md`: **Brand world** (places, surfaces and props, light and time of day, who appears, what never appears) and **Motion and sound** (pace, camera, sound, words on screen, video formats in use), taken from lifestyle photos and 2 or 3 of the brand's videos. Write "Not captured" for anything you couldn't watch.
- `products-and-positioning.md`: delivery (cost, free-delivery threshold, time), returns (window, who pays), guarantee (exact terms and exclusions), warranty (kept goods only), subscription (pause, skip, cancel).
- Each customer profile also gets **On camera**: what this person would be doing if filmed.

## Extra checks
- The main product and every best seller have Looks like, Pack and How it's used written from photos, and at least two photo jobs filled.
- Every policy line quotes the site, or says "Not captured".
