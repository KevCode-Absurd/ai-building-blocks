# Build your brand file: for online stores

## Extra pages to read
Collections, 5 to 10 product pages, shipping and returns, the size guide or the ingredients and materials page, and the subscription terms.

## Extra things to give it
- Store data: best sellers by revenue, repeat purchase rate, and products that get visits but don't sell.
- A review export from any reviews app, or a paste of the reviews page.
- Email results: top subject lines, which automatic emails are on, list size.
- Product photos from the store's own product pages or product feed, one folder per product, named in the order they appear. Leave out non-products (gift cards, shipping protection). If the photos can't be fetched, ask the person to add them.

## A truth sheet for each product, in `offers.md`
- **Kind:** how it's used (eaten, put on skin, worn, held, kept in a room) and how it's bought (once, again when it runs out, on a plan), with how long one unit lasts.
- **Made of, size, what's in the box, how long it lasts, care.** Used-up goods: ingredients with amounts, serving, pack size. Kept goods: materials, dimensions, weight, sizes, care, warranty.
- **Camera facts**, written only from looking at the photos, never from a title or file name:
  - Looks like: shape, the product's own colour (not the backdrop), finish, size beside a hand, and what it must never be mistaken for.
  - Pack: what it comes in, its colours, every large printed word and where it sits, and any versions that look different.
  - How it's used: the steps, as things hands do.
  - Visible result: what a viewer could watch change. If nothing shows on camera, say so and name what can be shown instead.
  - Moves and sounds: two to four real movements and the sound each makes. Tag sounds you haven't heard `[inferred]`.
  - Never show: the easy mistakes for this product.
  - Reference photos: one per photo job, below.

## Photo jobs
Sort plain photos (no text, badges or collage) into five jobs: pack front, out of the pack, in a hand (for size), in use, close-up. Designed images stay in the folder but fill no job. Tell the person which jobs have no photo; generated pictures and video get the product right only when these exist. A phone photo on a plain table is enough.

## Extra file content
- `voice-and-look.md`: the **brand world** (places, surfaces and props, light and time of day, who appears, what never appears) and **motion and sound** (pace, camera, sound, words on screen, video formats in use), from lifestyle photos and two or three of the brand's videos. Tag anything you couldn't watch `[unknown]`.
- `offers.md`: delivery (cost, free-delivery level, time), returns (window, who pays), guarantee (exact terms and exclusions), warranty (kept goods), subscription (pause, skip, cancel).
- `customers.md`: each profile also gets **On camera**, what this person would be doing if filmed.

## Extra checks
- The main product and every best seller have Looks like, Pack and How it's used written from photos, and at least two photo jobs filled.
- Every policy line quotes the site, or is tagged `[unknown]`.
