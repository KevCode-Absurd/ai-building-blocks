# Turn your ad numbers into three next steps: for online stores

## Extra things to give it
Your store's own orders and revenue for both periods, refunds, how tax and shipping are counted, your best-selling products, and any stock-outs during the period.

## Extra lines for the numbers table
- **Average order value:** store revenue / orders.
- **Overall store return (MER):** the store's own revenue / total ad spend. Use this to judge the store as a whole.
- **Refund rate** by period, because refunds change what a good return on ad spend is.

## Extra checks
- Say whether revenue is before or after refunds, tax and shipping, and count it the same way in both periods.
- Note any stock-out or price change that could explain a move in revenue before crediting or blaming the ads.
