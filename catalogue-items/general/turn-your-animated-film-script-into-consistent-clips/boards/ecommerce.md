# Turn your animated film script into consistent clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, the product out of the pack, how it's used, the label text line by line, and your "never show" list.

- Attach the pack photo to every shot that shows the product, and write each label line with its colour (for example: a matte tube whose label reads LIP BALM in cream letters on teal).
- Put a hand, a countertop or a doorway in a product shot if you want it to look animated rather than like a product photo.
- A how-it-works shot, for example: a macro cross-section of a trainer sole, three stacked foam layers, a soft wave of pressure travelling through them. Use only the layers or ingredients the brand lists.
- For something you use up, show it used in the brand's own steps, one action per clip.
- Prices and offers are on-screen text added in editing, never made into the scene.
