# Turn your animated film script into consistent clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, the product out of the pack, how it's opened and used, the label text line by line, and your "never show" list.

- Attach the pack photo to every shot that shows the product, and type each label line with its colour (for example: a squat glass jar whose lid reads NIGHT BALM in white on navy).
- Show the product at true size next to something everyone knows: a hand, a mug, a doorway.
- An inside view, for example: a cutaway of a rain jacket showing the two layers the brand lists, with one raindrop beading and rolling off the outer layer. No people in the shot.
- For something you use up, show it used in your own steps, one step per shot.
- Prices and offers are on-screen text added in editing, never part of the scene.
