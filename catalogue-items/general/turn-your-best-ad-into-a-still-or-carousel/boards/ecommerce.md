# For online stores: Turn your best ad into a still or carousel

## Extra things to give it
- Product photos for each job (pack front, out of the pack, in a hand, in use) and the label text word for word.
- The current offer terms, if the original names an offer, so an expired one can be flagged.

## What changes
- Rebuild the product from its own photos, with shape, colours and label kept, and lay the real pack over the picture before the ad runs.
- Show it opening and being used as the store's facts say, frozen at the moment the card's line is about.
- If the original names an offer that has ended or changed, flag it and leave the words as they are. The owner decides.
- In a carousel, the price and offer go on the last card, with the action.
