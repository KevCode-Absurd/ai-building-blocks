# Turn your clay town script into handmade-looking clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, the label text line by line, how it's used, the pack's size next to a person, and your "never show" list.

- Sculpt the pack in clay at its true shape and colours, wrapped in a printed paper label with every line stated, so the label reads and no clay lettering appears.
- Clay hands handle it with chunky rounded fingers. Open it only in the brand's real steps.
- Pick the finish from the product: "crisp" for premium skincare, home and food; "handled" for most everyday goods; "rough" for snacks, gifts and silly brands.
- Crowd shots (a queue, a waiting room) suit "everyone has this problem" openings. Build them from a few base figures with distinct faces, and let only one figure move.
- Offers and prices go on screen in editing on the last shot, from the current offer.
