# Turn your clay town script into handmade-looking clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, the label text line by line, how it's used, the product's size next to a person, and your "never show" list.

- Sculpt the pack in clay at its true shape and colours, and print the label as real paper with every line stated, so no clay lettering appears.
- Handling uses clay hands with thick rounded fingers. Open the product only in the brand's real steps.
- The playful look suits snacks, adult products with a fun side, and gifts. The refined look suits premium skincare, home and food.
- Crowd shots (a queue, an audience) suit "everyone has this problem" openings. Describe each face as distinct.
- Offers and prices are on-screen text on the last scene, from the current offer.
