# For online stores: turn your idea into an article-style image ad

## Extra things to give it
- The product page link and the product's three strongest facts (a material, how it's made, a test, a count).
- The review count and average rating, with the platform they come from.

## What changes in a store
- **Comparisons that work:** coated against bare, clear against amber glass, a monthly pack against a quarterly one, refillable against single-use. Compare kinds of product, never a rival brand.
- **The image:** the real product photo, small, in a corner or beside the list. Don't restyle the pack or change its label.
- **List headlines:** "3 differences ..." works best when each item matches a section of the product page (material, how it's made, guarantee), so the page can repeat it.
- **Proof:** review counts and verified-buyer quotes only as the review platform shows them. No "bought today" counters.
- **The page check:** the page's first screen must make the same claim. If the first screen is a grid of products or a discount banner, send the ad to a page built for it.
- **Labels:** if that page is laid out like an article, put "Advertisement" in front of or just above its headline.
