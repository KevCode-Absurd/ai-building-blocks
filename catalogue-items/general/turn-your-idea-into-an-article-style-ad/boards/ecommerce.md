# For online stores: turn your idea into an article-style image ad

## Extra things to give it
- The product page link and the product's three strongest facts (a material, how it's made, a test, a count).
- The number of reviews and the average rating, if the page shows them.

## What changes
- **Comparisons that work for stores:** coated against bare, clear against amber glass, a monthly pack against a quarterly one, refillable against single-use. Compare kinds of product, never a rival brand.
- **Image:** the real product photo, small, in a corner or beside the list. Don't restyle the pack or change its label.
- **List headlines:** "3 reasons ..." works best when each reason matches a section of the product page (material, how it's made, guarantee), so the page can repeat it.
- **Proof:** review counts and verified-buyer quotes only as the review platform shows them. No "bought today" counters.
- **Page check:** the product page must show the same claim on its first screen. If the first screen is a generic grid of products, send the ad to a dedicated landing page instead.
