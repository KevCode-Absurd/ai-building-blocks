# Turn your product's hidden process into 3D clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a clean pack photo (front, and out of the pack), what it's made of, how it works, key features, any visible result (if there is one, think again about this format), and your approved and banned claims.

- Where to look for the hidden process, by product: a drink mix (what one ingredient does, only as far as the claim goes), a skin product (what it does at the surface), a fabric (what the weave does with heat or water), cookware (what the material does with heat or grease), luggage or shoes (how a joint or sole takes weight).
- Categories with claim rules (supplements, skincare, anything for a condition) need the brand to read the script before any pictures are made.
- Beats 6 and 7 show the pack in use the way the brand states. The product is named only from beat 6.
- Offer and price: caption-style on-screen text in the last picture, from the current offer. Never spoken.
