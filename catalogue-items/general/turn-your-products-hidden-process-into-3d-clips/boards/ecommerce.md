# Turn your product's hidden process into 3D clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: clean pack photos (front, and out of the pack), what it's made of, how it works with a source for each step, any visible result (if there is one, a filmed demo may suit you better), and your approved and banned claims.

- Where hidden processes live, by size: the tiny (an ingredient in a drink mix, a coating on a pan), the surface (what a cream does on skin, what a weave does with sweat) and the structure (how a sole's layers take a step, how a bag's seam takes weight).
- Products with claim rules (supplements, skincare, anything for a condition) need the brand to read the script before any pictures are made.
- The product enters at the link where it acts and is named there. The closing shot shows it used your way, with its real label.
- Offer and price: on-screen text over the closing shot, from the current offer. Never spoken.
