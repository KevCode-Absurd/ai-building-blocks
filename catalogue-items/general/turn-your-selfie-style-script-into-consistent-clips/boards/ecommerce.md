# Turn your selfie-style script into consistent clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: photos of the pack front, the pack in a hand and the product in use; how the pack opens, what comes out and its colour; any visible result; your "never show" list.

- How the product is handled decides the set-up. A stick you tear and tip needs two hands, so the phone is propped.
- Before a pack is opened on screen, the shape, colour and texture of what's inside must come from a real photo of it out of the pack or in use. A drawing on the label doesn't show what's inside.
- Anything worn is on the presenter from the first frame, and its photo is named as what they're wearing.
- Anything applied goes on the back of a hand or forearm unless the brand says more.
- If you only have a photo of the sealed front, keep that sealed pack on a surface. Nobody holds, opens or uses it.
- Offers and prices are on-screen text added afterwards from the current offer. The presenter doesn't say them unless the approved script does.
