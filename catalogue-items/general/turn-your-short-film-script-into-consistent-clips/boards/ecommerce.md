# Turn your short film script into consistent clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, the product out of the pack, how it's held and used, any visible result, your "never show" list, and the current offer.

- The pack photo fixes the shape, size and artwork. Characters hold the real object; show its size against a hand or a mug.
- A "negotiation" or "ceremony" film works well for small everyday products (a sachet, a lid, a cloth). Treat the inconvenience as serious, solve it with the product's real feature, and end on an understated reaction.
- Show a visible result only if the brand states one. If there's nothing visible, show the routine, not a result.
- Characters may doubt or react. They never claim a personal result or play a customer.
- Offers and prices appear as on-screen text in the closing shot, from the current offer.
