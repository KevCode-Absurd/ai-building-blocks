# Turn your short film script into consistent clips: for online stores

Extra things to give it: a photo of the pack front, the product out of the pack, how it's held and used, any visible result, your "never show" list, and the current offer.

- The pack photo goes into every setup D insert. Characters hold the real object, sized against a hand or a mug, so it reads true to scale.
- Put the product on the floor plan from the first shot (on the table, by the door), so it never appears from nowhere.
- Build the payoff on one small true detail the camera can see: the pack fits a pocket, the lid clicks shut, the bottle stands in a cup holder. The other person's reaction to that detail is the laugh.
- Show a visible result only if the brand states one. If there's nothing visible, show the routine getting easier.
- Characters may doubt, tease or react. They never claim a personal result or play a customer.
- Offers and prices go on screen in the closing shot, from the current offer.
