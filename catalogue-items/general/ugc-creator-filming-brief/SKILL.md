# UGC Creator Filming Brief

The brief a real person films from. The beats are the rail; the words are theirs. It never asks anyone to say a result they did not have, or to copy another channel's video.

## Inputs
- **Approved script** (required): the selected openings, body points, the one number, the verdict and the handoff.
- **Delivery requirements** (required): selected openings, duration, aspect ratios, extra shots wanted, raw-file needs, deadline, agreed usage and channels. Confirm anything missing; do not import another brief's clip count, deadline or testimonial claims.
- **Product handling facts** (required): how it opens, is held and used.
- **Never-say list** (required): banned words and claims.
- **Brand brain** (optional): voice.

## Steps
1. Write the **concept** in 3 to 5 sentences: who is talking, where, what happens, what the viewer is left thinking; then one line on why it should read as a person's post, not an advert.
2. List **openings to film**: only the approved, selected ones, each a different lever, each joining naturally to the body. Tell the creator: start at the approved opening, no greeting; show the product when the concept calls for it; film a first-person opening only if it is true of them, otherwise skip it and say so.
3. Write the **body points** as a table of beats (setup, shown use, the number, verdict, handoff), each with bracketed slots for their own true details. Say: use your own words; if a line is not something you would say, change it and keep the point; if you have no true detail for a slot, leave that beat out and tell us. List the words and claims they must not say. State the length.
4. List **extra shots** (no talking, clean, well lit, no seasonal decorations): the product where they use it, picking it up, opening it the way it really opens, using it close up, the result if there is one, the product held to the lens with its name readable, and one or two particular to this concept.
5. Add **filming rules** as do / do not: framing (agreed ratios, space for crop and captions, eye level); light (face a window or lamp, never a window behind); sound (quiet room, no music or fan); phone (clean lens, agreed capture settings, no filters or beauty mode); product (name to the lens, fingers off the name, no other brands in frame); clothes (plain, no logos or words, a different top for extra shots); editing (none: raw files only).
6. Write **sending instructions:** raw files in three folders (Hooks, Body, Extra shots); simple names ("Hook 1", "Body", "Extra 1 opening the jar"); with the files, one line on which opening felt most like them and anything they would not say; leave a beat of stillness before and after each take; list missing takes and unresolved factual lines and never fill them with invented experience; state agreed paid or organic use, duration and channels, noting that filming alone does not grant usage rights.

## Output
A brief in this order: Concept; Openings (table: opening, what to say, also do); Body (beat table with slots); Extra shots (checklist); Filming (do / do not table); Sending files.

## Checks
- Every spoken line in the brief is in the approved script or a bracketed slot.
- No result, health or money claim that is not in the approved script.
- Every opening the creator is asked to film is true of a creator who used the product, or marked skippable.
- Usage terms and deadline come from the requirements, not invented.

## If something is missing
Write `[CONFIRM: item]` for any missing requirement (duration, ratio, deadline, usage) and list them at the top for the sender to resolve before it goes out. The brief sends and publishes nothing.

## Avoid
- Asking the creator to copy another channel's video.
- Scripting their result for them.
- Treating filming or posting as proof of paid-use rights.
