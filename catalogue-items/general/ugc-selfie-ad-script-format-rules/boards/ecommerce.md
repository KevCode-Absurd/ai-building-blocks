# UGC Selfie Ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: how it is unboxed, opened and used; sizes and variants; what a first use looks like.
- Real customer clips or photos with permission to use in ads (if a real creator or customer is used).
- Review excerpts with source and date, for the doubt and for any quoted line.

## Where it fits
- Unboxing and first-use demonstrations for physical products.
- Doubt-led scripts: the common objection from reviews ("I thought it would be flimsy") named in the setup and answered in the demonstration.
- Size, fit or "which one" decisions, shown in hand.

## Store-specific rules
- The one number is often a time to first use, a size, a count or "week 3" taken from real customer data; take it from the product page, tests or reviews, not from invention.
- A creator talking about a product they have used may use their own result. Do not script one for them; use placeholders.
- A generated presenter never says they own or use the product. Offer and price go in the end text, checked against the current offer.
- Do not show a made-up star rating or purchase counter.
