# Ecommerce add-on: video ad edit plan

Extra inputs: pack photos for SKU and packaging checks, how the product is opened and used, current offer, price and destination, any `Never show` list.

- Verify packaging, logo, colours and SKU against the product photo in every shot where it appears.
- Show opening and use at the moment the line names them; hold a pack detail long enough to read.
- Keep a real review's wording, source and date; never lift a badge, counter or before-and-after from another brand.
- Close on one action using the live offer, price or destination exactly as approved.
- Hook swaps: keep the approved body; check the join for the product's state (opened or sealed), voice, light and music.
