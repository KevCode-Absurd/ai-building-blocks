# Video ad edit plan: for online stores

Extra things to give it: pack photos (to check the version and packaging), how the product is opened and used, the current offer, price and destination, and anything that must never be shown.

- Check the packaging, logo, colours and product version against the product photo in every shot where it appears.
- Show opening and use at the moment the line names them. Hold a pack detail long enough to read.
- Keep a real review's wording, source and date. Never lift a badge, counter or before-and-after from another brand.
- Close on one action, using the live offer, price or destination exactly as approved.
- Swapping openings: keep the approved body, and check the join for the product's state (opened or sealed), voice, light and music.
