# Ecommerce add-on: scripts from research

**Read from research:** reviews by star rating (2 to 4 stars hold objections), return reasons and support tickets (what not to promise), pre-purchase questions on product pages, post-purchase survey answers.

**Bought again vs bought once** (see step 3) maps to store data:
- Bought again: subscription and reorder rate, pack size, first-order price, pause and cancel steps.
- Bought once: size and fit data, returns policy, warranty, how long customers say theirs lasted.

**Mechanism sources in a store:** materials list, how it is made, what was rejected in development, test results the store holds.

**Proof sources:** review counts and the word customers use most (only if real), reorder numbers, third-party tests, a demonstration the store can film.

**Guardrail from refunds:** if refunds come from people who expected something the product does not do, no beat may imply it.
