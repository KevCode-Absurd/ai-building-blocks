# Ecommerce add-on: video ad scripts

**Bought again vs bought once** changes the doubts, proof, offer and action.
- Bought again (eaten, applied, used up): doubts are "will it work on me", "will I keep it up", "am I locked in". Proof: the first use shown, people who reordered. Offer: first order and cost after. Action: start.
- Bought once (worn, cooked on, carried): doubts are price, size or fit, whether it lasts, whether it can go back. Proof: the product put through something, how it is made, how long people have had theirs. Offer: one price against what it replaces or how long it lasts. Action: choose the right one.

**Offer mechanics to check and say plainly**
| Mechanic | Say | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | Price, shipping rhythm, how to pause or cancel | Never "cancel anytime" unless true; answer lock-in fear with the real steps |
| First-order discount | Price today and after | A surprising renewal price returns as refunds |
| Bundle | Per-unit or per-day price and why that quantity | "Save 20%" alone is weak |
| Guarantee | Window, what the buyer does, what they get back | First order only? opened product? say the limit |
| Returns and exchanges | How long, whether used or worn can go back, who pays postage | Answer "which size am I?" |
| Size or version | How they find theirs (quiz, guide, presenter's size) | Never guess a fit |
| Warranty | Period and coverage | A warranty period is not a lifespan |
| Shipping | A date the store can keep | |
| Reason for now | Stock, batch, season, price change | Must be true |

**Price frames:** per day suits what is used up; per use, per year or "against what it replaces" suits what is kept.

**Feed craft:** sound is often off at the start; end on a readable card (action, price frame, guarantee) held about two seconds; write vertical first.

**Format needs from the script**
| Format | The script must supply |
|---|---|
| Human creator | Lines they can honestly say, bracketed slots for their true details, delivery notes, b-roll list, hooks to record, what not to say |
| AI presenter | Who the person is, a shot per line, the exact spoken line per clip, short clips of one or two sentences each, the product's own reference photos, no invented label text, an adult |
| Claymation or animation | A character and world, one visual metaphor for the mechanism, a scene per beat, a voiceover and on-screen text per scene; fiction may dramatise the accommodation but not invent proof |
| VSL or faceless | A visual per sentence, the looped structure, on-screen proof items |
| Founder | The real founding story, what the founder shows on camera |
| Statics and carousels cut from it | Hook as overlay, mechanism as subline, proof as badge, offer as the final card |
