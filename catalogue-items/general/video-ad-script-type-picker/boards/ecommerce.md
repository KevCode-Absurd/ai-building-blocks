# Plan a batch of different video ad scripts: for online stores

Every type works for a product that's reordered (used up) and one that's kept (owned). The openings below are made up, for a toothpaste tablet and a hiking backpack. None goes into a real script.

| Group | Type | Reordered: toothpaste tablets | Kept: hiking backpack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Talk | Straight case | "You pack a travel tube, and it still leaks in the wash bag." | "You carry the heavy bag on one shoulder by mile three." |
| | Doubt first | "You think a tablet won't foam like paste. It foams less. Here's why that's fine." | "You're between sizes. Most people are, so measure your back, not your height." |
| | Flaw first | "The first morning feels strange. You'll chew too long." | "It costs more than the bag you have, and it weighs more empty." |
| | Offer up front | "Your first jar is half price, and refills come every 8 weeks." | "Free returns for 60 days, even after a muddy trail." |
| Show | Show it working | "One tablet, a wet brush, ten seconds." | "Twenty kilos in, a full flight of stairs, no strap slipping." |
| | Day in use | "6:40. Hotel sink. Nothing to squeeze." | "It hangs by the door, so it's the bag you grab." |
| | Line-up | "Three ways to pack toothpaste for a trip, one weekend each." | "Three packs, one hill, the same load." |
| | Making of | "We press each tablet dry, so there's no water to ship." | "We stitch the hip belt twice where most bags stitch once." |
| Story | Mirror | "There's a ziplock bag in your suitcase just for liquids." | "You check the weather app before you choose which bag." |
| | Turning point | "Security binned my toothpaste on the way to my sister's wedding." | "My shoulder gave out halfway up, and my friend carried both bags." |
| | Tries in a row | "First the mini tubes. Then the refillable ones. Both leaked." | "My last pack lasted two summers. The one before, one." |
| | Someone noticed | "My dentist asked what I'd switched to." | "The guide at the hut asked where I got the straps." |
| Teach | The real cause | "Most of a toothpaste tube is water." | "Back pain on a hike starts at the hips, not the shoulders." |
| | Time-lapse | "Day 1, it feels odd. Day 10, you forget it was ever paste." | "Trip 1, trip 20, trip 50. Here's what happens to the seams." |

**How it's bought changes the type**
- **Time-lapse:** for reordered goods, every point falls before the first pack runs out, so the offer is that pack. For kept goods, the points are the life of the thing (first use, a season, years) and the offer weighs the price against that life, with the warranty.
- **Show it working:** kept goods get put through something (water, load, a wash). Reordered goods show the use itself (the pour, the texture, one step), never an effect on a body.
- **Offer up front:** reordered, the first order, the price after, and how to pause. Kept, one price against what it replaces, and the returns.
- **Flaw first:** reordered, the flaw is taste, a step to prepare it, a getting-used-to-it period or the cost per use. Kept, it's weight, fit, breaking in, care or price.
- **Line-up:** for reordered goods, compare kinds of the product by what goes into each. For kept goods, put every option through one identical test.

**Extra facts that open more types:** how long a pack lasts or how long the product lasts (and the warranty), how it's made, what you can film it doing, the sounds and moves of using it, and the returns window.
