## What came out

```text
Batch: 4 scripts
1. Doubt first (Talk) | selling beat: doubt | opens on: the Last doubt, granted as fair: "A gummy can't hold enough to replace my powder." | tone: curious -> confident | needs met: Last doubt (brief, buyer doubt). It is answered with 1 pack = 60+ ingredients, 21 vitamins and minerals, 6 g fiber (business facts). The 30-day money-back on the first order is the matching guarantee (offer terms).
2. Show it working (Show) | selling beat: proof | opens on: the demo mid-action: tear, tip, eat, with no water and no shaker, in one continuous take | tone: matter of fact -> pleased | needs met: a result you can film (business facts: tear, tip, eat; no water, no shaker). It shows the use itself, never an effect on a body. The second proof from outside is the survey ("95% ... take [Sprout] at least 4-6x per week with 80% taking daily", always framed as "of surveyed users") and third-party testing for 70 pesticides (business facts).
3. Tries in a row (Story) | selling beat: how it works | opens on: the first try, the greens powder drink ("the drink was definitely gritty/chalky", review 3) | tone: fed up -> relieved | needs met: two or more Tries (brief: quit greens powders, pill stacks, multivitamins; review 3). How it works is the thing every try missed: one pack, no prep (business facts). Kinds of product only, never brands. Say what each try got right.
4. Straight case (Talk) | selling beat: offer | opens on: the Before point, the row of separate bottles ("separate probiotic gummies, b12 gummies, and multivitamin gummies", review 5) | tone: amused -> matter of fact | needs met: Before point (review 5; brief: quit pill stacks). Offer: $29.99 first order with text sign-up, renews at $49.99 every 4 weeks, 30-day money back on first order only (offer terms). All three terms are stated plainly.

Spread: groups used: Talk, Show, Story. Teach has no type that qualifies, so a second Talk type (Straight case) was taken with a selling beat not yet used (offer). Selling beats used: doubt, proof, how it works, offer (four). No shared openings: the Last doubt, the demo, the first Try (powder, review 3) and the Before point (bottle row, review 5) are four different points and facts.

Skipped types and why
- Offer up front: the buyer doesn't know Sprout yet, and this type is weak on buyers unaware of you.
- Flaw first: it qualifies (review 18 gives a real getting-used-to-it period on taste). It was not picked because its selling beat (doubt) is already carried by Doubt first.
- Day in use: it qualifies (review 15 "grab and eat on my way…"; survey). It was not picked because its selling beat (proof) is already carried by Show it working.
- Making of: partly met (pectin base, vegan, third-party tested for 70 pesticides). There is no fact on what the decision cost the maker, and its selling beat (how it works) is already carried by Tries in a row.
- Line-up: MISSING: a real test against rivals, or checkable facts about each rival kind.
- Mirror: the buyer is already aware of the problem and of fixes.
- Turning point: MISSING: a real Trigger with stakes from a customer.
- Someone noticed: MISSING: a real customer account of another person noticing. This can become filming notes for a real customer, with blanks for their details.
- The real cause: MISSING: one true cause backed by research.
- Time-lapse: MISSING: time points and a day count you can back. The length of 45 to 60 s also only reaches the 60 s minimum at the top end.
```
