# Ecommerce add-on: video ad shot list

Extra inputs: product photos by job (pack front, product out of pack, in hand, in use), how it is opened and held, "never show" list, current offer.

- **A photo beats the words.** A pack photo is the strongest thing in a request. Send a 28-serving pouch as "the product" and the clip may tear the pouch and pour from it. Send the photo of the object the shot is about (one stick, one capsule, the product out of its pack).
- **Single-serve products:** that serving's own pack is the object; the retail box stays out of the shot.
- **Open and use it the brand's way.** Write how it opens and what comes out in the brand's words, one step per shot.
- **A pack that stands on the counter in every shot is furniture.** Bring it in on the line that names it.
- **Worn products:** show on an adult cropped below the face, or flat, unless the brand has approved on-body footage.
- **Offer:** never spoken by a generated voice. Add it as on-screen text on the last shot, from the current offer only.
