# Ecommerce add-on: video ad text overlay planner

Extra inputs: product name as printed, current offer and guarantee wording, `Never say` list.

- First overlay: the product's name or plain description beside the pack, in the script's words, the first time the pack is clearly seen.
- Numbers worth a badge in a product ad: size, weight, count, ingredient amount, guarantee length, delivery or return terms; each only with its source.
- Offer or guarantee goes in the final scene as a badge, only as worded in the current offer; no added urgency.
- Demonstration labels name the step in 2 to 4 words ("tear the top", "stir 10 seconds") using the brand's own use steps.
- On a podcast or talking-head ad keep to labels and keep every overlay clear of the mouth.
