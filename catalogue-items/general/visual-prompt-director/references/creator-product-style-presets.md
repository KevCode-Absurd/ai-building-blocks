## Recreating Any Image

Being a great AI director is just as much about TASTE as it is about operating the tools.

How do we train taste?

Look at a lot of cool shit

Recreate the cool shit (and go "damn, I made some cool shit")

Repeat

Don't just be a cool shit observer, be a cool shit producer.

Let's break it down.

Step 1 — Find cool shit & look at them

I mainly use Pinterest for this.

What's nice is that as you click on images that resonate with you, the algorithm will start learning your taste and serve you even cooler shit. 

Here are some great search terms to get started:

High-Fashion Editorial Campaign

Avant-Garde Fashion Editorial

Y2K Aesthetic Chrome

Luxury Brand Campaign Aesthetic

Runway Backstage Photography

Street Style Editorial (Paris / Milan / Tokyo)

Minimalist Fashion Styling

Documentary Street Photography Emotion

Download any image that you love; we are going to recreate it.

Feel free to check out my personal growing Cool Shit moodboard

Step 2 — Place your character inside that scene

Go to Prompt Director, add your cool reference, then prompt this:

Give me a master image prompt that transforms any reference image of a subject into this exact style, composition, aesthetic, lens, distortion, lighting. The prompt should be detailed enough to recreate this exact scene using the referenced subject's identity features.

It should give you a pretty lengthy prompt that will recreate that image using any character. 

Now we go over to Nano Banana 2. I use it inside Higgsfield, but doesn't matter where you use it; you're using the same brain just different platform.

I've added my subject image as reference, then pasted in the prompt given to me by my trusty steed Prompt Director.
