# Reference library and coverage modes

## One image to camera coverage
Choose one approved source image and list identity, product, location, light and spatial layout as locks. Plan a small readable spread before making views: establishing wide, medium action, close reaction, product/detail insert, reverse or over-the-shoulder where physically plausible. A nine-view layout is an optional coverage preset, not a requirement or promised tool capability. For every view specify actual camera location, shot size, lens feel and what it reveals. Check axes, eyelines, product geometry, background architecture and object state. Approve stills before animation; do not silently animate nine inconsistent frames.

## Authorized personal reference library
For a user’s own or authorized adult digital character, collect a neutral front, left/right three-quarter, profile, full-body and selected expression images under consistent light. Add teeth/hand details only when needed and explicitly authorized. Label each image role. Keep identity photos separate from camera/style images. Build a stable identity description with observable distinguishing traits. Do not blend unrelated real people into a new claimed identity or infer traits from unavailable attachments.

## Starting-frame and animation handoff
Choose a frame matching the needed pose and camera. Define identity locks, one visible action, camera movement, sound/dialogue and final state. Preserve skin texture strength rather than restating excessive detail terms in every beat. A tool-dependent voice clone requires authorization and a separate supported service; this reference creates a plan and prompts.

## Review
Compare a new frame against the approved reference at equal size. Check facial proportions, age/skin tone, hairline, body/clothing, hands/accessories, product and lighting. Review clips normal speed, muted and paused at contact or occlusion. Report unperformed checks. Use the existing Video avatar and storyboard planner when the deliverable is a full production storyboard rather than a prompt fix.
