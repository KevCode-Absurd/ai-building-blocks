# For online stores: Stop the scroll with your buyer's problem

**Extra things to give it**
- Product photos sorted by what they show (closed, open, in use), so a scene of use is built from a photo of use. Add the product truth sheet if you have one (`product-image-spec-writer`).
- Any visible result the store has on record, to decide whether the product may be shown working.

**Extra rules for a store**
- Show the product working only when that is the twist and a person would really see it (water beading off a jacket, an egg sliding off a pan). Otherwise show it closed and at rest.
- Twists that suit physical goods: a phone cable frayed at the neck beside a tape-wrapped spare (side by side); a "dishwasher safe" mug stained inside, set in the rack with clean plates (plain, one exact detail).
- An offer goes on the ad only when the store runs one, in the store's own words, never as the headline.
- Draw no result the product facts don't state, and no cut-away of a part the product doesn't have.
