# Watercolor Painted Ad: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Product data: exact pack shape, colours, every label line, ingredient or material list, size.
- A product photo, so the painted pack keeps the true silhouette.
- Current offer with end date.

## Where it fits
- Scent, taste, ingredient or origin stories: candles, teas, skincare, food and drink, home fragrance.
- Gift and seasonal products where mood sells.
- A "what is inside" reveal for products with a clear ingredient list.

## Store-specific rules
- Paint only ingredients on the product's list; the brush reveal must match it.
- Skin, hair or body outcomes are never painted as results; keep to scent, mood or ingredient.
- The pack keeps true colours; label text is stated exactly in the prompt. A product with a very busy label gets the `pack-portrait` look or is left small.
- The offer, price and shipping terms go in the final overlay only, checked against the live offer.
