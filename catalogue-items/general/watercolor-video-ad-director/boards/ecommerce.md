# Ecommerce add-on: watercolour video ad

Extra inputs: pack-front photo, every label line with colour, `Made of` (ingredients or materials), `How it works`, `Never show`.

- Pigment trail from a painted ingredient (a lavender sprig) to the painted bottle works when `Made of` lists it; do not paint an ingredient the brand does not list.
- Use the product-illustration look for known products and launches, botanical for plant-based or ingredient-led goods.
- A painted scent or warmth is a metaphor; keep it plainly one and claim no effect.
- Offer and price are on-screen text after the held last frame, from the current offer.
