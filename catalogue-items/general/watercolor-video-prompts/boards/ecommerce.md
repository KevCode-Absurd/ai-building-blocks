# Watercolour video shots: for online stores

Give it as well: clean photos of the pack's front, side and top, its label text word for word with its colours, the ingredient or material list, and anything that must never be shown.

- In stage 3, make the painted product sheet first and hold it next to the real pack. If a label line is wrong, plan to overlay the real label in editing rather than prompting it again and again.
- Most store ads in this look need no figure: the pack is the avatar, and stages 1 to 3 become the product and set sheets.
- Ingredient shapes come only from your list. A glaze or a charge of colour can stand for an ingredient; it never shows what happens inside a body.
- Shelf products with a busy label: paint the pack smaller in the scene and save the large product shot for a held frame with the real label laid over it.
- Price and offer go on the end card as text in the edit, from the offer that is live that day.
