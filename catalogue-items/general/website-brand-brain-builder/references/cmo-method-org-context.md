# Product-use and channel-evidence records

Keep the org context's shape. Add an asset index to the notes in `look.json` and to the product's records in `facts.json`: exact variant, real photo name and source, date, view (front, back, detail, in use), use permission and what each image establishes. A packshot does not establish worn, opened, held or installed geometry.

Record shape, scale, material, finish, closures, label, real use sequence, hidden parts and excluded variants as `product` records, apart from marketing copy. Missing in-use views stay `unconfirmed` with `[unknown]`. Generated images stay marked generated and cannot establish physical product facts.

In `worked.md`, give each channel result its date range, source, metric definition, attribution basis and what is missing. Keep measured exports, reported results and guesses apart. Old successful claims are not automatically current. Settle price, stock and policy conflicts before writing live offers. Collect only the useful assets within the requested scope; do not store keys, passwords, private codes or customer-level personal data in the org context.
