# Weighted-average antidilution checker

Revised conversion price and protected-series conversion ratio.

## What you need

Actual charter formula, old conversion price, defined A shares, qualifying C shares, cash consideration and trigger/exemption confirmation.

## Method

1. Map actual A, B and C definitions including outstanding options and deemed issuances; verify exemptions and trigger.

2. Calculate B as qualifying consideration divided by old price, then old price times (A plus B)/(A plus C).

3. Retain the old price when there is no qualifying downward reset; document required contractual rounding separately.
