# Winning ad style: online store add-on

## Extra inputs
- Photos of the product by view (pack front, out of pack, in a hand, in use), stock level, any small print every ad must carry, visible results the product can honestly show.

## What changes
- **Product shape matters most:** re-proportion layouts for each product and show it as sold. If the winner showed the product open, held or in use, show this product the way it really works, or closed and at rest.
- **Offer:** only a current offer from the facts, with an exact name and dates. A winner's discount does not carry.
- **Not yet tried** often looks like: a proven bundle layout with a different product, or a customer-quote layout with a different buyer.
- **Results to record per ad:** spend, return on ad spend or cost per purchase, dates, comparison with your account's usual.
