# For online stores: write a story ad buyers see themselves in

**Used up or kept** changes the story.
- Used up and reordered: the past is a string of products started and given up. "Second try" and "the slow habit" fit well, because buyers reorder and report the weeks.
- Bought once and kept: the earlier versions they owned make the drops, each with its price and the way it failed. "Down and back up" fits, with the first use, then the tenth.

**Extra things to give it:** the page the story points to, your returns terms, and any customer stories you have written permission to use (with the name and permission on record).

**Narrators in a store:**
- A customer story needs written permission before it runs as their words. Without it, retell it as the brand and quote the review word for word.
- The founder suits why the product was made, or what they changed after complaints.
- Gift products suit "someone else's change", told by the brand or by a real customer who bought it for someone.

**Where the click goes:** a product page for a buyer who knows the problem and has tried a fix, or a page that teaches for a stranger. Never put a price or discount in a story aimed at strangers.
