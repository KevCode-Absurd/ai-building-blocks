# For online stores: Write a story ad buyers see themselves in

**Used up or kept** changes the story.
- Used up and reordered: the history is a run of products started and dropped. The first experience is the first week. "What changed" covers the period customers report.
- Bought once and kept: the history is the ones bought before, each with what it cost and how it ended. "First experience" becomes the first use, then the tenth. The "number then family story" form works when you have a cost or lifespan fact and a family story about the one that lasted.

**Extra things to give it:** the offer page or article the story points to, your returns terms, and reviews cleared for use (with the name and permission on record).

**Which form suits a store:** the short review suits a single product with real reviews. The letter about someone suits gifts. The forum post suits things that last and get long use. The expert voice only where the store has a real professional who agreed.

**Where the click goes:** a product page for a buyer who is ready, or an article-style page that teaches for the story aimed at strangers. Never put a price or discount in the story for strangers.
