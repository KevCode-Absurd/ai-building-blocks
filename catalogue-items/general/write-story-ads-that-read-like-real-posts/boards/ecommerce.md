# For online stores: write story ads that read like real posts

## Extra things to give it
- Real customer reviews with their source, whether the buyer was verified, and the date. The founder's story, if there is one.
- The product page terms: price, bundle, shipping, returns and the guarantee wording.

## What changes
- **Photo ideas for stores:** the hallway floor with the muddy boots, the kitchen bench after the fifth failed attempt, the bathroom shelf with too many bottles. The product appears as it would in a real home, unposed.
- **Proof:** the review count, a verified buyer's quote word for word, a retailer or press mention only if it's real. No "bought today" counters or default rating claims.
- **The offer at the end:** current terms only, in the store's own wording, with the link after the story, never inside the first three paragraphs.
- **Whose story:** with no real customer or founder account, use third person ("customers keep telling us...") and mark it inferred.
