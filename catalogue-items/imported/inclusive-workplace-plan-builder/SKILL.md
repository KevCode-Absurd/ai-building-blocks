# HR diversity, equity and inclusion

Comprehensive DEI support — from building diversity and inclusion strategies and training programs to measuring inclusion metrics, managing ERGs, addressing bias, and creating equitable HR processes.

## When to use it

Use it to turn your goals and team size into a written inclusion plan.

Skip it when:

- You need a legal review, a compliance check or a pay audit. Ask an employment lawyer or a specialist adviser.
- You only want a fair job post. Use the Inclusive job post writer.
- You only want survey questions. Use the Employee survey designer.

## What to gather first

Ask for anything missing once, in one message. Mark any answer you still lack as MISSING. Never invent your team numbers, survey results or progress figures.

1. Team size, industry and country.
2. How you hire, promote and set pay today.
3. Your goals for the next 1 year or 3 years.
4. Survey results or team data you already have (optional).
5. Who must approve the plan, and any leaders who may push back.

## Steps

1. Summarise the starting point in a short paragraph from what the user gave. Say what you do not know.
2. Set 3 to 5 goals that fit the team size. Each goal says what will change and how you will see it.
3. Draft the roadmap for the length the user chose, with milestones, an owner role and a measure for each.
4. Write fair hiring steps: how roles are written, how people are screened, who is on interview panels, how choices are recorded.
5. Add the people practices: bias training for managers, mentoring, employee resource groups (staff-run groups for a shared background or interest), and a pay review step.
6. Build the scorecard and a short survey plan. Do not fill in starting values you were not given.
7. Write short answers to the pushback the user expects from leaders.

## Output format

Return one plan with these headings, in this order:

1. Starting point
2. Goals
3. Roadmap (table: when, milestone, owner, measure)
4. Fair hiring steps (numbered list)
5. People practices (bias training, mentoring, groups, pay review)
6. Scorecard (table: measure, how to collect it, starting value, target)
7. Survey plan
8. Talking points for leaders

## Rules

- Do not give statistics or cite studies unless the user supplied them. Name a source the user can check, or say you have none.
- Say which data needs consent or is restricted by law in the user's country before suggesting you collect it.
- Treat the plan as a draft for HR and leaders to approve. It is not legal advice.

## After you run it

Share the plan with one leader and one employee for comment. Then use the Employee survey designer for the survey and the Mentoring program builder for the mentoring step.

## Supported tasks

- Developing comprehensive diversity and inclusion strategies
- Creating inclusive recruiting and job description practices
- Designing unconscious bias training for employees and managers
- Building employee resource groups (ERGs) and affinity networks
- Conducting diversity and inclusion surveys and measuring inclusion metrics
- Creating diversity scorecards and tracking progress
- Developing mentorship programs that promote equity
- Addressing systemic bias in talent processes
- Creating inclusive onboarding and leadership development
- Promoting pay equity and transparency

## Key prompts

### Diversity and inclusion strategy

1. "Describe the business case for diversity and inclusion. Cite sources the user can check, and give no figures without a source."
2. "Provide guidance on creating a comprehensive diversity and inclusion strategy aligned with [organization's] mission and goals."
3. "What measures can ensure that diversity and inclusion are embedded in our organization's core values?"
4. "How should I develop a diversity and inclusion roadmap with measurable milestones for [1 year/3 years]?"
5. "How can HR managers address resistance from senior leadership when introducing diversity and inclusion initiatives?"
6. "Share examples of diversity and inclusion initiatives and their outcomes. Name only examples you can source, and say where the user can check them."

### Inclusive recruiting

1. "How can I create an inclusive job description and recruitment process that attracts a diverse candidate pool?"
2. "What strategies can we employ to attract diverse talent and build a more diverse candidate pipeline?"
3. "How can I address unconscious bias in our talent acquisition and hiring decisions?"
4. "What measures help ensure that resume screening processes are free from bias?"
5. "How can I promote our company's commitment to diversity and inclusion in our employer brand?"
6. "What are best practices for implementing diverse interview panels that reduce hiring bias?"

### Training, education, and employee resource groups

1. "Explain the importance of unconscious bias training and how it promotes a bias-free workplace."
2. "Design a diversity and inclusion training program for [employees/managers/executives] covering [key topics]."
3. "How can I create diversity training that is engaging, evidence-based, and leads to behavior change?"
4. "What training approaches address microaggressions effectively and help employees respond constructively?"
5. "Describe the role of employee resource groups (ERGs) in promoting diversity and inclusion."
6. "What is best practice for launching and supporting an ERG for [specific community/identity group]?"
7. "How should ERGs be resourced and governed to ensure they are sustainable and effective?"

### Measuring inclusion

1. "Provide guidance on conducting diversity and inclusion surveys to assess employee perceptions and experiences of inclusion."
2. "How can I create a diversity and inclusion scorecard that tracks representation and inclusion over time?"
3. "What metrics should I include in a diversity, equity, and inclusion dashboard for executive leadership?"
4. "How can I use HR data to identify equity gaps in hiring, promotion, pay, and development?"
5. "Implementing a diversity and inclusion scorecard to track progress — what KPIs matter most?"

### Equity in HR processes and intersectionality

1. "How can HR managers ensure that performance evaluations are conducted without bias or stereotypes?"
2. "What measures help ensure pay equity and transparency across diverse employee groups?"
3. "How can I address biases in talent development and succession planning processes?"
4. "How should I design an inclusive employee recognition and rewards system?"
5. "What strategies address intersectionality and the unique challenges faced by employees with multiple identities?"
6. "What measures promote inclusivity for employees with disabilities?"
7. "How can I address ageism and promote effective intergenerational collaboration?"

### Communication and culture

1. "How can HR managers address conflicts related to diversity-related tensions or misunderstandings?"
2. "Describe the importance of building an inclusive feedback culture that values diverse perspectives."
3. "How can I promote a culture of allyship and advocacy for underrepresented groups?"
4. "What measures help ensure diversity, equity, and inclusion efforts are communicated effectively to all employees?"
5. "How can I address cultural misunderstandings and promote cultural intelligence in the workplace?"

## Tips

- Ground diversity, equity, and inclusion work in data — measure representation, equity gaps, and inclusion experience to focus efforts where they matter most.
- Make inclusion everyone's responsibility, not just HR's — engage managers and senior leaders as visible champions.
- Address bias in systems and processes, not just in individuals — systemic changes create more durable equity.
- Create psychological safety for diversity, equity, and inclusion conversations — employees need to feel safe to discuss these topics openly.
- Set specific, measurable diversity, equity, and inclusion goals with accountability — what gets measured gets done.
