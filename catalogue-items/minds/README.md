# Minds: how one is built

Spec and reasons: `docs/MINDS-PRD.md`. The 150 people and their suggested seats: `_roster.yaml`.

One folder per person: `minds/<slug>/` holding `mind.yaml`, `knowledge.md` and `personality.md`. `SKILL.md` is generated by `tools/build-minds.mjs`; never write it by hand.

## mind.yaml

```yaml
slug: jeff-bezos
name: "Jeff Bezos"
area: business                 # one of the ten areas in _roster.yaml
also: [leadership]             # other areas, optional
years: "1964–"                 # "1564–1616", "c. 544–496 BC"
living: true                   # checked, not guessed
known_for: "Founded Amazon and ran it on customer obsession, long-term bets and two-pizza teams."
summary: "Think through a business problem the way Jeff Bezos does: start from the customer, work backwards, take the long view."
use_cases:                     # 6 to 10, concrete, for anyone (not one industry)
  - "Work backwards from a press release for a new product idea"
  - "Decide if a choice is a one-way or a two-way door"
seats:                         # 2 or 3 suggested council seats; the first matches _roster.yaml
  - {seat: "Operations and the customer", asks: "What does the customer want that won't change in ten years?"}
pairs_with: [charlie-munger, steve-jobs, alex-hormozi]   # slugs from the roster
last_checked: 2026-10-20
sources:                       # numbered in order; knowledge.md cites them as [S1], [S2]
  - {n: 1, title: "1997 letter to shareholders", by: "Jeff Bezos", url: "https://...", kind: own-words}
  - {n: 2, title: "Jeff Bezos", by: "Encyclopaedia Britannica", url: "https://...", kind: reference}
tests:                         # five prompts: 2 use-alone, 2 advise-me, 1 trap
  - {kind: use, prompt: "..."}
  - {kind: use, prompt: "..."}
  - {kind: advise, prompt: "..."}
  - {kind: advise, prompt: "..."}
  - {kind: trap, prompt: "...", expect: "Says it has no record of that view and doesn't invent a quote."}
```

`kind` for sources is `own-words` (their letters, essays, books, talks, interviews), `reference` (encyclopedia, obituary, university or museum page) or `secondary` (biography, critical study). Aim for at least 5 sources and at least 2 `own-words`. Only list pages you actually opened. Use stable public URLs (Wikipedia, Britannica, Project Gutenberg, the person's own site, official letters, museum and university pages).

## knowledge.md (the data set, 1,500 to 2,500 words)

```markdown
# <Name>: what they knew and believed

Built from public sources, last checked <date>. Claims cite the sources in mind.yaml as [S1].

## In one paragraph
## Core ideas
### <idea name>
What it is, in two to five sentences, in our own words. [S1]
How they applied it: one real example. [S3]
(5 to 9 ideas)
## How they decide
Their rules of thumb, as a short list. [S2]
## Where they stood
The questions they kept coming back to, and their answer to each. [S4]
## Their work and record
The main works, companies, discoveries or campaigns, with dates. [S2]
## Where they were wrong or criticised
Fair, sourced, with no pile-on. [S5]
## Blind spots to watch
What this mind tends to miss, so a council balances it.
## Short phrases on the record
At most five, each under 15 words unless the work is in the public domain, each with its source. If you can't source a phrase, leave it out.
```

## personality.md (600 to 1,000 words)

```markdown
# <Name>: how they think and talk

One line on what this voice is drawn from (for example "the way he wrote his shareholder letters, 1997 to 2021; a public voice, not the private person").

## Voice
Sentence length, favourite words and images, formality, humour, pace.
## How they argue
How they open, what evidence they reach for, how they handle push-back.
## Questions they always ask
Five to eight, in their style.
## When they hear a weak idea
What they say and do.
## They'd never
Things out of character: words, positions, tones.
## Playing them well
How to sound like them without caricature: what to keep, what to overdo less. One short sample answer (60 to 100 words) to a neutral question, marked as a sample written by us.
```

## Rules

- **Our own words.** Summarise; don't copy. Quotes from copyrighted work stay under 15 words, at most five per mind. Public-domain work can be quoted briefly.
- **No invented facts.** Every claim in `knowledge.md` has a source tag. If you aren't sure, leave it out or mark it "(disputed)".
- **No private life or gossip** unless it shaped the work. No song lyrics.
- **Living people:** views can change, so date them ("as of 2024"). No speculation about plans, health or family.
- **Investors:** frameworks only, never buy or sell advice.
- **Political and religious figures:** their leadership and ideas from the record. Nothing on today's politics.
- **Plain English, grade 10 or below, no em dashes, no AI tells** (see `brand/COPYWRITING.md`).
- **General use cases:** "a small business", "a team", "a writer", not one industry.

## Avatars (`_avatars.yaml`)

Every mind on the roster, built or not, gets a small pixel portrait: a 16 by 16 face drawn by the site from a few traits in `_avatars.yaml` (renderer: `site/src/lib/face.ts`). One line per person, keyed by slug. Pick the traits from how the person is best known in public photos: the look people picture, not a likeness. Draw what's iconic (Jobs's black turtleneck and round glasses, Einstein's wild white hair, Shakespeare's ruff, Bezos bald with a grin). Never anything mocking.

```yaml
steve-jobs: {skin: 2, hair: receding, hairColor: grey, beard: stubble, glasses: round, top: turtleneck, color: black, mouth: neutral}
```

| Key | Values (default first) |
|---|---|
| `skin` | 1 to 6, light to deep |
| `hair` | `short`, `bald`, `receding`, `buzz`, `part`, `quiff`, `slick`, `spiky`, `curly`, `afro`, `wild`, `long`, `bob`, `bun`, `ponytail`, `locs` |
| `hairColor` | `black`, `dark`, `brown`, `auburn`, `red`, `blonde`, `sandy`, `grey`, `silver`, `white`, or a hex like `"#5a3a22"` |
| `beard` | `none`, `stubble`, `mustache`, `goatee`, `beard`, `longbeard` |
| `beardColor` | same as `hairColor`; defaults to it |
| `glasses` | `none`, `round`, `square`, `thick`, `sun` |
| `hat` | `none`, `cap`, `beanie`, `beret`, `fedora`, `tophat`, `laurel`, `headband`, `turban`, `wrap` |
| `hatColor` | a colour name or hex |
| `top` | `tee`, `shirt`, `suit` (with tie), `suit-open`, `turtleneck`, `hoodie`, `sweater`, `robe`, `ruff`, `blouse`, `kimono`, `labcoat`, `jacket` |
| `color` | main clothing colour: `black`, `white`, `navy`, `grey`, `charcoal`, `red`, `blue`, `green`, `brown`, `tan`, `purple`, `pink`, `yellow`, `orange`, `teal`, `cream`, `gold`, `maroon`, or a hex |
| `color2` | tie, bow tie or inner layer colour |
| `extra` | `none`, `pearls`, `earrings`, `chain`, `bowtie`, `scarf` |
| `mouth` | `neutral`, `smile`, `grin` |
