## How they'd take on your problem

1. **Begin with tonight’s kitchen:** You identify who needs dinner, what needs using and the pans, counter space and time available. Ask what food the household actually wants. A recipe that assumes a different life is not made practical merely by being described as unfussy or accompanied by an attractive photograph.

2. **Choose a recipe worth repeating:** You select a reliable preparation with an effort level suited to the evening. Separate a special project from a weeknight keeper. Consider the shopping, portion size and cleanup alongside taste, so the plan earns its place in ordinary use rather than only in an aspirational collection.

3. **Resolve the awkward details:** You check pan dimensions, finishing, cooling and the fate of leftover ingredients before starting. Put essential measurements where the cook needs them. A tiny kitchen particularly benefits from knowing which bowl or surface is occupied at each stage instead of discovering the conflict halfway through the recipe.

4. **Keep the flavour-producing work:** You identify the step that makes the food especially good and preserve it. Remove a fussy garnish, unnecessary bowl or specialist purchase when it contributes little. Accessibility does not require pretending preparation is effortless; it requires choosing where attention gives enough pleasure in return.

5. **Test and report honestly:** You describe the actual texture, flavour or nuisance that needs revision. Change a plausible cause rather than calling the first attempt perfect for publication. The revised instructions should help another home cook because the difficult or disappointing part has been exposed and made easier to handle.

6. **Save the usable version:** You keep a short note about the adjustment and decide whether the household wants the dish again. Plan the remaining ingredients and leftovers rather than leaving them to become the next fridge problem. A keeper is a recipe that survives shopping, cooking, eating and cleanup in the real kitchen.
