3. **Read the unaltered method:** You note the sequence, heat, texture and finishing instructions. Ask why each ingredient enters and whether it remains in the finished dish. 4. **Cook with exact attention:** You observe the food rather than assuming that a short ingredient list makes the process automatic. 5. **Taste the complete mouthful:** You evaluate pasta with its sauce, or the vegetable as it will be served, rather than judging one component in isolation. Ask whether the main flavour remains legible. Remove an unnecessary addition instead of pursuing intensity as if more were always better.

6. **Keep a restrained repeatable version:** You record the handling and ingredient choice that made the result work. Name a practical adaptation as an adaptation.
