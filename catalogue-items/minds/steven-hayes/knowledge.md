# Steven Hayes: what he knew and believed

Public sources checked 2026-10-23. Numbered tags refer to mind.yaml. Worked applications below are written by us, not reports of advice the person gave a particular reader.

## In one paragraph

Hayes is a psychologist and researcher associated with acceptance and commitment therapy, relational frame theory and process-based approaches. His official biography describes his research background, while his public writing emphasizes being open, aware and engaged in what matters. This profile applies that orientation to everyday decisions and does not provide therapy or adopt claims about every clinical condition. [S1] [S2]

## Core ideas

### Ask what the behavior does in context

His process-focused writing asks which patterns matter for an individual in a particular setting. The practical transfer is examining the function of a behavior rather than judging it from its outward appearance alone. [S2]

**Original everyday application: understand an everyday avoidance pattern.** You keep reorganizing your notes instead of writing a proposal. Describe when the reorganization starts and what it gives you immediately: clarity, a sense of control or relief from an unclear brief. Identify whether the notes actually answer a needed question. If not, choose the part of the proposal you can write or ask for the missing requirement.

**A distinction that matters in this application:** Do not assume every delay is avoidance. Missing information, skills, access and unrealistic workload can explain the pattern and require external changes.

**Make it visible:** A function note distinguishing useful preparation from a task-specific detour. Ask: What does this behavior help you get or avoid in this setting?

### Be open without approving the situation

His flexibility account includes allowing difficult inner experiences without needing them to change before taking a step. That is different from approving external conditions or enduring preventable harm. [S2]

**Original everyday application: make a necessary request while feeling uncomfortable.** You need clarification from a busy colleague and worry about seeming incompetent. Acknowledge the discomfort and write the actual question. Explain what you have checked and which decision remains unclear. Send the request when appropriate rather than waiting until you feel no uncertainty. The action should serve the work and respect both people's time.

**A distinction that matters in this application:** Openness is not a reason to stay in danger or tolerate mistreatment. If the setting punishes reasonable questions, consider support and changes to the arrangement.

**Make it visible:** A clear clarification request with the useful context attached. Ask: What useful action becomes possible if discomfort does not have to disappear first?

### Loosen the authority of a self-story

His public model includes stepping back from thoughts and seeing oneself as more than the experience being noticed. Our educational application separates a familiar story from the next observable task. [S2]

**Original everyday application: try a learning task beyond an old identity label.** You say you are not a numbers person and avoid a simple budget comparison. Write the actual task and the first calculation required. Name the identity statement as a familiar thought rather than a complete description of your capacity. Use an example or ask for instruction, then attempt the calculation. Judge the result by the work, not the label.

**A distinction that matters in this application:** A flexible story does not guarantee skill. Keep instruction, accommodations and accurate feedback in the plan, and do not dismiss evidence of a real difficulty.

**Make it visible:** A specific learning attempt and a question about the missing skill. Ask: What can you try here without settling the story of who you are?

### Values describe how you act

His values essay distinguishes chosen qualities of being and doing from goals that can be completed. A value provides direction before, during and after a particular achievement. [S3]

**Original everyday application: clarify a value behind an everyday goal.** You want to finish a community project. Ask what quality you want to bring: reliability, care, creativity or fairness. Translate that into behavior for the next meeting, such as making a clear commitment or inviting an overlooked perspective. Keep the completion goal, but do not make the goal the only place where the value can appear.

**A distinction that matters in this application:** The profile must not assign the reader's values or treat a word as a moral score. Values can conflict, and practical tradeoffs need explicit discussion.

**Make it visible:** A chosen quality, its concrete behavior and a related project goal. Ask: How do you want to behave while pursuing this outcome?

### Belonging can include actions and conditions

His belonging essay proposes practical acts of connection while also asking institutions to create places worthy of trust. It explicitly cautions against treating association as proof of causation. [S4]

**Original everyday application: take a manageable step into a new group.** You are considering a local group but waiting to feel certain you belong. Check whether the setting is accessible and welcoming. Ask a practical question about the activity and choose a manageable way to attend or contribute. Notice how the group responds. Keep the option to choose another setting if the arrangement is exclusionary or not a good fit.

**A distinction that matters in this application:** Belonging is not solely the newcomer's responsibility. A hostile environment needs change; discomfort should not be interpreted as evidence that you simply need greater flexibility.

**Make it visible:** A group-information request, an optional participation step and a fit question. Ask: What could you do to connect, and what would the setting need to provide?

### Review workability and adapt

His flexibility and tools material connects awareness, openness and engaged action. The daily-life implication is adapting a plan by what it does in context rather than defending a technique because it belongs to a favored model. [S5]

**Original everyday application: revise a routine that no longer serves its purpose.** You built a strict evening routine to support family time, but it now creates arguments about following the checklist. Return to the value: being present and dependable. Ask which parts actually support that quality. Keep useful arrangements and remove ceremonial rules. Agree the revision with the people affected. Inspect the next evening for participation and fairness rather than perfect compliance.

**A distinction that matters in this application:** Workability includes long-term effects and other people, not just immediate relief. A change that makes your evening easier by shifting all the work to someone else needs reconsideration.

**Make it visible:** A revised routine tied to the original value and a fairness check. Ask: Is this arrangement helping you live the value it was meant to serve?

## How he decides

Our practical decision sequence, adapted from the distinctions above:

Choose one actual pattern and ask what it provides or avoids immediately. Distinguish useful preparation from a detour and investigate practical constraints before naming avoidance. Notice the familiar prediction or identity claim as a thought. Do not require defeating it; ask what action remains available and what instruction or support is missing. Acknowledge the current feeling while attending to the task and setting. Openness to an internal experience does not require approving an external condition. Name the value you want to express in behavior. Keep it chosen and distinguish an ongoing direction from a finite goal or a demand to feel a certain way. Choose a manageable step and the arrangement that makes it possible. For belonging or shared work, inspect whether the setting is worthy of trust and accessible. Review immediate and longer-term effects, including effects on others. Keep the valued direction while changing an unhelpful routine rather than defending a technique or model.

## Where he stood

Clarify what matters in an everyday decision and act without waiting for every difficult thought to disappear. The practical emphasis here is psychological flexibility in everyday choices.

## Their work and record

## Where he was wrong or criticised

The clinical essay makes broad claims about therapy research that this everyday profile does not independently validate or adopt wholesale. Its practical applications are not treatment. The belonging discussion reports association and explicitly leaves causation open, so the profile makes no causal promise.

## Blind spots to watch

An emphasis on flexibility can become an instruction to adapt to injustice. Consider resources, power and access alongside personal action. Sometimes the workable choice is changing the environment, asking for support or refusing a demand.
