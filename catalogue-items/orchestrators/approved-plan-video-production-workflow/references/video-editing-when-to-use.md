# When to use `/video-editing`

**What it does:** Full pipeline. Raw footage in, published Reel/Short/TikTok out. Human approves the plan before anything renders.

**Runtime:** 5-15 minutes depending on length and b-roll needs.

## Run this when

1. **Daily Reel production.** Dropping one Reel a day and need a repeatable pipeline.
2. **Batch episode production.** 5-10 videos for a week, all from long-form source.
3. **Repurposing podcast/long-form.** Slice a 60-min podcast into 8 short-form cuts.
4. **Client-facing deliverables.** Paid video work where QA gates matter.
5. **Trending-rewrite output.** Converting a trending script into a polished edit.

## Before you run

- Raw video uploaded to Slack channel
- Target platform specified
- Target duration specified
- Caption style chosen
- Music vibe chosen (or "none")

## What you get back

- Full edit plan (scene list, captions, b-roll slots, music)
- Approval gate in Slack
- Produced MP4 at platform specs
- QA pass/fail report
- Optional: direct publish via scheduler

## Chains well with

- `/trending-rewrite` to feed the script/concept into the pipeline
- `/reel-cover` to produce the cover before publish
- `/hook-generator` to pre-sharpen the hook before recording
- Content scheduler for queued posting
