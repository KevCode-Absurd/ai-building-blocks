## Goal
Share due tasks and their threads. Get checked follow-up drafts in your voice, ready for review.

The result: one follow-up email draft for each due task, with To, CC, subject, body, the linked task ID and thread ID, and the send status. A second pass checks each draft against your voice and shows every fix as before and after. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

## When to use, and when not to

Use it when you have a task list with due dates and email threads that go with them, and each overdue or due task needs an email to someone.

Not for:
- Late invoices: use the Payment reminder letter writer.
- A stalled sales lead: use the Almost-buyer follow-up writer or the Follow-up email sequence writer.
- One reply to an upset customer: use the Angry customer reply writer.
- Starting from meeting notes, not from tasks that are already due: use Turn meetings into owned actions.

## Deliver
- **Follow-up drafts:** one per due task, with task ID, thread ID, To, CC, subject, body and send status.
- **Draft ledger:** each draft's version, state and message ID, so a rerun does not send twice.
- **Edited drafts:** the same drafts with off-voice lines fixed, each shown before and after.
