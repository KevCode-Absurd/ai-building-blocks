# Format taxonomy and disambiguation

Video starting buckets (expand if the corpus warrants): presenter problem/solution; founder story; genuine customer proof; product demonstration; comparison; explanation/education; street conversation; podcast conversation; list/reasons; documentary mechanism; animation/CGI; sensory/satisfying; montage/lifestyle; offer-led. Classify by the dominant communication mechanism, not incidental surface style.

Static starting buckets: product hero; problem metaphor; comparison; proof/quote; explanation/diagram; editorial/article; native note/chat; offer/catalogue. A paper texture alone does not make an editorial ad; the information structure does.

Store format and angle separately. A subcategory is worthwhile only when it changes the production brief. Multi-format ads get a primary label and secondary devices, not duplicate corpus rows.

Sampling ledger: cluster, all eligible IDs, advertisers represented, dates/age bands, chosen IDs, reason, available media, exclusions and analysis count. Include fresh tests and older references, avoid letting one prolific advertiser dominate. Coverage counts describe this sample only.
