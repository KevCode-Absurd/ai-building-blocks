## Deliver

Account map: each buyer role at the account (for example the economic buyer and your champion) with the person's name, what they win and risk personally, whether you have met them and who you still need to reach.

Deal actions and risks: each deal risk with its owner, the next action and its date, and the status of the mutual action plan.

Onboarding success plan: the onboarding stages, an owner on each side for every stage, and the check that shows first value has been reached.

Include source coverage, unresolved findings, missing facts and one next action.

What to do with the three results: give the account map and the deal actions to the account owner, and have a second person read the deal risks before any message goes to the customer. Share the onboarding success plan with the customer only once the account owner has approved it.
