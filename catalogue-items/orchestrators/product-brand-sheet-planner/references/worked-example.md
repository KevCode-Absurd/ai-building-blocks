# Fictional offline practice case

## Input

Plan a one-page brand sheet for a fictional teal mug. Product photo not attached; warm utilitarian direction, no customer claims.

## Example result

ASSETS: brief available; missing product photo, approved wordmark and font.
RULES: teal ceramic, warm oak, natural side light, generous space; fictional placeholder mug until photo supplied.
PAGE: header and one-line direction; large SKU hero; three smaller cells for morning-use context, editorial glaze detail and an original adult creator holding the mug. Bottom strip shows palette/type choices and missing approvals.
PROMPTS: hero isolates shape; use scene anchors scale; macro reveals glaze; creator holds handle naturally. Each states no added logo/text and no invented testimonial.
NOT RUN: reference inspection, image generation and sheet export.

## Checks

- Five distinct asset/page roles
- Shared visual system
- Missing real product reference exposed
- No invented testimonial or export

No external service was called in this practice case.
