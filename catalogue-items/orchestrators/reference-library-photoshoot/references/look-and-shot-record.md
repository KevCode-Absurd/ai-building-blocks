# Look and shot record

Truth: variant, size, material, finish, colour, shape, closures, label words, scale, how worn/used, what is hidden, anchor filenames, unknowns.

Fit tiers: EXACT = useful scene and geometry for this product; STRUCTURE = light/composition transfers but subject/use/props must change; LOOSE = only mood/palette, requiring an original shot design. None grants permission to reuse a reference photograph commercially.

Look row: id, reference, tier, reason, userPicked, assistantProposed, userDirection, keep/swap/drop, excludedRisk. A chosen reference with conflicting user direction must record the conflict and follow the requested direction while preserving product truth.

Shot row: shotId, lookId, productVariant, realAnchors, camera, light, setting, scale/use, quietRegion, requestedRatios, generationPrompt, negativeConstraints, outputVersion, QAresult, reviewerNotes.

Review deck: source look beside original rebuilt image where permitted, product truth evidence, full-size output link, requested ratio tabs and per-frame notes. Keep rejected exports in history and mark active replacement pending until inspected.
