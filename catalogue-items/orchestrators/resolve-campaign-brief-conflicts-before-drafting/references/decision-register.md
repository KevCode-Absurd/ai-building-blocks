# Campaign decision register

For each decision record: ID; exact conflicting statements; source IDs and dates; issue type (fact, preference, constraint or action authority); scope/variant/effective time; evidence; who can resolve it; explicit resolution or open question; affected deliverables; safe-independent work; status.

A newer filename or stronger wording does not itself prove authority or truth. The current user's explicit preference can settle style, but cannot make an unsupported testimonial genuine or turn a planned price into the current checkout price. Retain contradictions and their resolution rationale.

Checkpoint: checked facts, selected decisions, bounded job completed, output version, open issues, approvals and next trigger. It is an output record, not a promise of background memory or monitoring.
