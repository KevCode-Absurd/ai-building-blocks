OFFER AND REVENUE MODEL

Free prompts, starter skills, sample outputs and limited demonstrations support discovery. Proposed monthly plans are 99 dollars for an individual, 299 for a team and 899 for an agency. The model assumes a 60%/30%/10% mix, giving 239 dollars per paid organisation per month. Exact feature and usage allowances remain to be tested.

Paid access can cover maintained collections, current references, company configuration, team use, supported retrieval and bounded help. Do not include unlimited human review, unrestricted agent execution or every external model bill in a low subscription fee. Separate source-dependent usage and paid production where required.

Implementation is modeled as a 5,000-dollar completed bounded workflow project with 60 delivery hours. Consulting is a 1,500-dollar package with six hours including preparation and follow-up. Both are additional to standard two-hour onboarding. Larger applications, custom engineering and managed operations require their own scope and are not revenue assumed in the current projection.

Affiliate income uses a proposed average of 100 dollars per confirmed paid referral, after reversals. This is not a quoted provider commission or evidence of acceptance into a programme. Advertising uses a proposed 1,500-dollar completed sponsorship across relevant videos, boards or newsletters. Reach, buyers and sold volumes need validation. Mark paid placements and retain useful, evidence-based tool recommendations.

HOW CUSTOMERS ARE REACHED

Publish detailed YouTube and search content for exact tasks, with real labelled outputs and a usable next step. Send interested visitors to a customer-input trial and a supported small deployment, rather than asking them to assemble an entire generic automation platform.

Year-five content target: 60,000 qualified task-page visits, 10% activated trials and 20% of those becoming paid organisations, giving 1,200 new paid accounts. This requires 5,000 qualified visits per month, not simply video views.

Year-five partner target: enable 300 delivery partners, with 150 productive through the year and ten new paid organisations each, giving 1,500. HubSpot's reported partner population is context, not access secured by us.

Year-five sales target: 75,000 qualified account approaches, 2% held demos and 20% conversion of held demos, giving 300. Five sellers handle 25 demos per month each. Qualify outreach, respect permitted contact routes and test reach before scaling spend.

Deduplicate billing organisations across channels. An agency client counts as a subscriber only if it pays separately. Community invitations can support referral and feedback, but the current model does not assume a viral coefficient or extra paid-ad volume.

SERVICES THAT CAN SCALE

Use readiness forms, example outputs, setup videos, templates and standard acceptance criteria. Give each implementation project a named outcome, data boundary, tools, hours, review rounds, owner and handover. Quote new scope before doing it.

Train an India-based team for support, onboarding, product development and scoped delivery. Use specialists and appropriate review for advanced architecture, security and domain questions. Lower hiring costs do not make delivery effort free or justify assuming all roles can be junior.

Separate product work from customer project work in the budget and operating process. The five-year plan has a dedicated implementation/consulting team in addition to product staff and onboarding. Partners can extend delivery after they can pass the same acceptance and quality process.

Track human minutes per customer and project, accepted work, rework and contribution after all direct costs. The subscription library and reusable methods should create repeated value across customers; bespoke service volume should not become the only growth engine.

LIFECYCLE AND RETENTION

Discovery: show a task, honest sample and missing-data case. Trial: get a useful first output from selected customer inputs. Setup: configure private facts, source definitions, tools and review rules. Adoption: use one task in an existing meeting or production workflow.

Repeat use: save settings and accepted decisions; refresh only relevant evidence and references. Expansion: add one adjacent task, routine or application component when the customer wants it. Maintenance: publish tested updates and handle deprecations and incidents with an owner.

Measure retained paid use and outcomes accepted by the customer, not library size or download count alone. The model assumes 80% annual customer retention, applied monthly. That rate and the reasons for renewal are not yet observed.
