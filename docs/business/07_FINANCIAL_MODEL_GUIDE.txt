SUBSCRIPTION AND CUSTOMER FORMULAS

Annual new paid organisations are 200, 600, 1,200, 1,900 and 3,000. The content/partner/sales additions are respectively 80/40/80, 240/200/160, 480/480/240, 760/900/240 and 1,200/1,500/300. Existing balances carry into the next year.

Monthly retention r = 0.8^(1/12). Each month closing active = opening active x r + annual new adds / 12. New customers join at month end and begin billing the following month. Monthly subscription revenue = opening active x 239 dollars. This produces year-end active counts rounded to 181, 688, 1,636, 3,027 and 5,136.

The year-five subscription revenue is 11,565,447.66 dollars. Its exit ARR is about 14.73 million dollars, calculated from the ending active balance x 239 x 12; that is not revenue earned during the year. The 5,000 active-customer operating target implies 14.34 million dollars of subscription ARR.

ADDED REVENUE VOLUMES AND TIMING

Implementation: 12/36/80/150/240 completed jobs per year x 5,000 dollars. Year-five revenue is 1.20 million dollars. Each job carries 60 delivery hours, including testing and handover.

Consulting: 24/60/120/200/300 completed packages x 1,500 dollars. Year-five revenue is 450,000 dollars. Each package carries six delivery hours including preparation and follow-up.

Affiliate: 50/150/400/800/1,500 confirmed paid referrals x an assumed net accepted commission of 100 dollars. Year-five revenue is 150,000 dollars. No second-year recurring commission is assumed.

Advertising: 0/6/12/24/48 completed sold sponsorship campaigns x 1,500 dollars. Year-five revenue is 72,000 dollars. These are required paid campaign volumes, not assumed inventory that automatically sells.

Subscription additions are organisations; project, package, referral and campaign volumes are different units. An existing subscriber can purchase a service without becoming a second subscriber. Added revenue is recognized on completed accepted work, accepted commissions or delivered campaigns, not merely proposed sales. The calendar timing within each annual bucket remains a planning simplification.

DIRECT DELIVERY AND INDIA PAYROLL

Monthly subscription non-support cost per paid account is 62.27 dollars: 30 usage/licensing, 8.37 processing, 19.12 builder royalties and 4.78 refund allowance. Support/QA expense each month is the higher of ten dollars per opening paid account or actual support payroll. This pays for idle early capacity instead of assuming support scales perfectly from zero.

Support needs 0.5 hours per closing active account per month. Hire whole support staff each month at the ceiling of that workload / 120 productive hours. Standard onboarding needs two hours per new account; whole onboarding staff per year = ceiling(new adds x 2 / 1,440).

Fully loaded year-one monthly India budgets are 90,000 INR for support, 120,000 for onboarding, 270,000 for product/leadership and service delivery, and 180,000 for partner/media staff. Planning FX is 90 INR/USD; India wages rise 8% each year. These are role budgets to validate with hiring quotes, not claimed market averages.

Core product/leadership staff are 4/7/12/20/30 across years. Dedicated service staff = ceiling((implementation jobs x 60 + consulting packages x 6) / 1,440), giving 1/2/4/8/12. Year five has 16,200 service hours and 12 delivery staff. Their salary is included separately from core product payroll.

Service non-payroll cost is 10% of implementation plus consulting fees: 3.5% processing, 2% refund allowance and 4.5% tools/rework. Partner/media staff are 0.25/0.5/1/1/2 full-time equivalents, plus 10% of affiliate/advertising revenue for delivery costs. These are additional to commercial acquisition staff.

Year-five support peaks at 22 staff and onboarding uses five. Full-year onboarding payroll after raises is 108,839.12 dollars. Peak staff counts are capacity requirements; support payroll follows monthly hires, rather than paying the year-end peak for all twelve months.

The commercial pay budget is separate from India delivery wages and is held flat per role in this scenario. No additional paid-media acquisition uplift is assumed.

Administration is 90,000/150,000/240,000/360,000/540,000 dollars. Total operating cost includes subscription delivery, onboarding, core product/leadership, acquisition, administration, service payroll/other costs and partner/media payroll/other costs. Avoid adding those staff costs a second time when stress-testing the model.

MARKET SIZING AND VALIDATION

Reported June 2026 global customer accounts are HubSpot 306,446, Klaviyo 205,000+ and monday.com about 250,000. The reference account range uses the largest base to the sum: 306,446-761,446. At the unvalidated proposed 2,868-dollar annual subscription this is a 0.88-2.18 billion dollar revenue equivalent. Full global TAM is not established.

The initial marketing SAM ceiling uses HubSpot and Klaviyo: 306,446-511,446 account positions, or a 0.88-1.47 billion dollar subscription revenue equivalent. This is not an audited count of unique qualified companies; vendors may count brands or divisions and accounts overlap. Qualification by task, tools, language and budget can reduce it substantially.

Build a deduplicated permitted prospect list and test willingness to pay. The 5,000-customer SOM target comes from channel additions and retention, not an invented share of TAM. Services, affiliate and advertising revenue are not added to the subscription-based market-sizing equivalents.

Stress-test lower additions and retention, delayed onboarding, more human minutes, higher wages, larger projects, lower affiliate acceptance and unsold ad campaigns. Recalculate capacity and all associated costs, not only revenue. Keep the current case as a labelled baseline and give alternative cases their own assumption sets.
