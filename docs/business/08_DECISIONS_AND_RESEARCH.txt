DECISIONS AND RESEARCH AGENDA

As of 17 October 2026 | Version 2026-10-17-current-five-stream-plan

OPEN PRODUCT DECISIONS

Choose the first buyer and narrow task: agency reporting, brand/landing-page audit, Meta audit or BFCM production. Select one primary host and a manual fallback. Decide what evidence of useful output and willingness to pay will be required before a wider release.

Define the first reference collection, ownership/permissions, metadata and media handoff. Decide whether to start with owned/licensed assets and manual intake, or a licensed source integration. Confirm who may inspect, download, send assets to models or redistribute them.

Confirm tier entitlements and bounded help. Define a service project, included revision rounds, acceptance, change control and ongoing maintenance. The current prices and volumes are proposed, not final customer contracts.

Select the first data route and deployed-runner policy. Decide which information must remain in the customer environment and which services may receive it. Choose auth, storage, hosting and connector vendors only after that map is agreed.

RESEARCH NEEDED BEFORE PROMISES

Verify current Codex, ChatGPT and Claude packaging, available tools, connector authentication, media delivery, sharing, scheduling, account controls, costs and distribution rules. Test using a real supported customer profile before advertising plug-and-play support. Exact menus and product limits in the old plan are not a current support guarantee.

Compare direct integrations with a connector vendor such as Composio for exact needed fields, permissions, customer-owned credentials, logs, private deployment and costs. Clarify any intended reference to Composer before choosing a vendor.

Interview qualified customers and observe the current task being performed. Measure the actual correction and setup work, the alternative price and purchase authority. Generic statements that every other platform is useless are a hypothesis to challenge, not competitive evidence.

Obtain salary and specialist-delivery quotes, measure project hours, test support demand and validate affiliate approval and sponsor price with actual counterparties. Do not present the five-year case as evidence of market demand.

PLANNING SEQUENCE WITH DECISION GATES

First approve one buyer/task, sample output and test fixture. Then prove a manual/export-based workflow and its final artifact. Measure acceptance, missing inputs and human effort.

Next prove a versioned downloadable pack and one supported host adapter. Add authenticated reference retrieval only after the media and permission chain works. Introduce billing and service scope from observed economics.

Add durable routines after repeats, budgets, interruption recovery and an owner are tested. Add agents, partner builders, multiplayer and complete applications where customers need them and the same contracts can be reused. This is an order of proof, not an authorised implementation schedule or an arbitrary ninety-day plan.

SUGGESTED FIRST RESPONSE FROM THE RECEIVING AI

Restate the business and the prompt-to-application ladder. Separate founder decisions, proposed designs, external facts and unvalidated assumptions. Flag material contradictions or missing evidence. Rank the five most consequential validation questions and propose the smallest test for each.

Then recommend a first buyer/task and delivery route, including what can be downloaded, what needs a live tool connection and what needs deployed software or human work. Audit the financial model and show sensitivity to the main delivery and acquisition assumptions. Continue in planning mode until the founder authorizes a build.
