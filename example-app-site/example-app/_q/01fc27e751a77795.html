Static ads

Images made to run as ads, with or without words on them.
  • New
    Wide Face portrait

    An extreme overhead ultra-wide portrait with natural skin detail. Use your chosen character and product with the camera look kept consistent.

    Make this
  • New
    Scroll-stopping ads for cold audiences

    Each ad is 1 idea in 1 striking picture: a tiny diorama, a comic panel, a thermal image, a billboard. Made for people who have never heard of you.

    Make this
  • New
    Proof ads

    Each ad is 1 kind of proof: a customer's own words, 1 number, your product against the usual way, the product labelled. For people who are comparing.

    Make this
  • New
    Offer ads

    1 offer argued 3 ways, because the person who needs proof is not the person who needs the deal: the deal said plainly, a real review, and why the product is worth it at full price. The offer line is its own text on every ad, so it changes on all of them at once.

    Make this
  • Ad from a review

    Ads built on 1 thing buyers keep saying, in their own words: the phrase on the picture character for character, in formats made to carry a quote (a taped note, a strikethrough, a Q and A card). The feed headline is the phrase.

    Make this
  • Recreate a winning ad

    Pick ads you like in the reference library, say what each should do, and the app builds yours on exactly those.

    Make this
  • Static ad from a script

    A script is written first, the way an ad argues in words, then 1 image carries that argument.

    Make this
  • New
    Carousel

    2 to 6 cards read in order: a list, a before and after, how it works. Each card does 1 job.

    Make this
  • New
    Long-copy static

    A plain image with a long text beside it in the feed. For a buyer who needs the whole story before they click.

    Make this
  • New
    Advertorial static

    An image that looks like the top of an article, leading to a page that tells the story.

    Make this
  • Native ad

    A long first-person story with an image that looks like a person's own post, not an ad.
