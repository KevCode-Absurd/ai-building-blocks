Delivery, returns and guarantee

Delivery
Free shipping on subscription orders (the site's banner: "FREE SHIPPING + 30-DAY GUARANTEE"). A one-time purchase is shown as "No Free Shipping". How long delivery takes: not captured.
Returns
Not captured: the returns page has not been read.
Guarantee
30-day money-back guarantee on the first order only. It does not cover repeat orders. The brand says fewer than 1% of customers use it (brand-reported, unverified: do not quote).
Warranty
Does not apply: the product is eaten.
Subscription
The site says pause or cancel anytime. Third-party reviews say cancelling was hard (Trustpilot; an old BBB "F" rating reported by Innerbody in 2025), so do not promise an easy cancel beyond the site's own words. A limited flavour reverts to Original from month 2.

Questions buyers ask

  • How much do I take? 1 daily pack a day: 20 g of gummies for adults, 15 g (6 gummies) for children of 2 to 13.
  • What is it free from? Gluten, dairy, tree nuts, peanuts, gelatin, caffeine, synthetic sweeteners and synthetic dyes. It is vegan.
  • Low Sugar or Sugar-Free? Sugar-Free uses sugar alcohols, and some buyers report an upset stomach. A buyer with a sensitive gut is pointed to Low Sugar.
  • Can a gummy really replace my powder? In the brand's 2025 placebo-controlled study, vitamin C rose 40.1% and folate 20.5%.
  • Is it tested? Third-party tested for 70 pesticides, 4 heavy metals, 16 contaminants and 9 microbial contaminants, and made in NSF, GMP, FDA-registered facilities in the US and Canada.
  • What does the guarantee cover? The first order, for 30 days. Not later orders.
  • Can I pay with HSA or FSA money? Yes, through Truemed.
  • What happens after a limited flavour? The subscription goes back to Original from month 2.

Product notes for ad writing

  • Sugar-Free uses sugar alcohols. Some buyers report stomach upset (see the Customers tab objections).
  • Serving is "a small handful" of gummies per pack. Some reviewers call the serving large.
  • The 30-day guarantee covers the first order only.
  • Grüns claims "less than 1% of customers use our Money-Back Guarantee".
