Running a post from their handle
  1. 1The creator sends the clip to 1 shared place (a folder, a board).
  2. 2Pick the posts that did twice the creator's usual views.
  3. 3In the ad account, at the ad level, choose "Partnership ad" under identity and enter the creator's handle.
  4. 4Send the request. The creator approves it with 1 tap in their app.
  5. 5Once approved, the ad runs from their handle on the brand's spend. They do not need to post it again.
  6. 6Test it on its own for 7 to 14 days. What holds moves to the campaigns that scale.

You do these in the ad account. The app changes nothing there.
