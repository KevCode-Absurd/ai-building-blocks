Personas

Guide, chapter 14 of 20

A persona is 1 kind of buyer the brand writes for. The Personas screen (in the Brand group of the sidebar) is where you build them, edit them and decide which are in use.

What a persona holds

PartWhat it isWho reads it
The coreName, who they are in 1 sentence, what they are living with, what sets them looking, their own wordsEvery step that writes: concepts, scripts, image ads, the 50-ad sprint. Kept in brand.json
The profileAge, gender, what marks them out, what they want, what stops them, how the product is positioned for them, what they buy, the offer that fits, how their segment performed, their pictureYou. Kept in brand-brain/personas.json. Not sent to a model, except the parts named under Doubts and proofs below, which go to the writer of a script for that persona

A writer is sent the core of the buyers a piece is for, and only the name of the others.

Doubts and proofs (phase 47)

Each persona holds up to 8 doubts: what this buyer thinks just before they do not buy. Beside each doubt is the proof that answers it, or nothing where the brand holds no proof.

An example pair from the Grüns test personas. The doubt: "Will I actually take these every day, or quit like I did with the powder?" The proof: "80% of surveyed users take it daily, and 95% take it 4 to 6 times a week", with 2 customers' own words.

  • Fill doubts and proofs on a persona writes 3 to 5 pairs in 1 small call. It is sent the brand's own facts, the persona and the customer quotes closest to them. A proof that states a figure the brand has not stated is dropped by code.
  • Edit them. Each row is 2 text boxes. A pair you wrote or changed is marked Yours and is never rewritten by the app. The others are marked Written for you. Add a doubt adds a row.
  • Who reads them. The script writer is sent at most 3 pairs a script (none for an unaware buyer, 2 for a problem aware one), the ones closest to the idea. A doubt with no proof is never sent. The fact checker counts each proof as a fact the brand holds. The Offers screen asks which doubt an offer answers (chapter 15).
  • From the terminal: npx tsx src/scripts/personas.ts <brand id> doubts [--all] [persona id ...].

Measured on Grüns: 24 personas filled for 15 cents, about 0.6 cents each. The Grüns personas are test personas on simulated data, so their doubts are too.

Since phase 47 a script's writer and its fact checker are also sent 5 more things from the profile of the 1 persona the script is for: what they want, how they see themselves, what they tried, the notes, and these doubts and proofs. The age, gender, performance figures and picture are still never sent.

In use, or a draft

  • In use: the persona can be picked wherever a buyer is chosen (a concept, a set of ads, the sprint).
  • Draft: kept on this screen only. Nothing writes for it.

A persona stays in use while concepts are written for it.

Build from data

Press Build from data. 1 call reads:

  1. how each segment of buyers performed (spend, orders, cost an order, return on spend, what was sold, the hooks that worked),
  2. what buyers say in the research, for the themes closest to those segments,
  3. the brand's products and offers.

It matches the personas you already have to their segments, and proposes new ones for the segments nobody covers, best-performing first. Each arrives as a draft. Read it, change what is wrong, then put it in use.

Cost: about 2 cents for the personas, and about 1.5 cents a picture if you ask for them.

Their own words are kept only when the research holds them word for word. An ad may quote them as a customer's. Anything else goes under How they might put it, which is never quoted as a customer's.

Where the performance figures come from

For now, a file in the brand's folder: sample-data/performance/segments.json. The file says whether it is simulated, and the screen marks every simulated figure. The Grüns figures are simulated: they test the builder and are no record of how the brand performed.

When the brand's ad account and store are connected, the same builder reads the real figures.

The picture

Each persona gets a lifelike portrait: a candid photograph of 1 made-up adult, nobody real (it was a clay figure until 13 October 2026, I-58). The picture is drawn from How they look, in the place that line names. No children, no words, no logo, no pack. It is made at the picture quality chosen in Settings.

Press Draw again after you change how they look.

Avatars: the face and voice of this buyer's videos

An avatar is not the persona's portrait above. It is the person or character who appears on screen in videos: how they look, their pictures and their voice. It is saved once, on the persona's sheet, so every video for that buyer can show the same face and speak in the same voice. Before, a video's character lived inside 1 video job and was lost with it.

An avatar is always made up and always an adult. A description of a child is refused. A real person (the founder, a customer, an expert) is never an avatar: real people are kept under Real people on the Brand screen, with their consent.

KindWhat it is
A personLooks filmed, like someone holding a phone. Described in 7 fields: gender, age, skin, eyes, hair, what they wear, what marks them out
A made characterClay, paper, drawn, a skeleton, an object with a face. Described in 1 sentence, with the look it is made in. A character saved with a look is offered only to videos of that look

3 ways to add one.

  • Describe one and draw it. The form starts from the persona: age, gender and how they look. Change anything. Save and draw it makes 1 picture. Save without a picture makes none.
  • Upload a character you made before. A PNG, JPG or WebP picture of up to 20 MB. You tick a box to say it is a character your team made, not a real person. Without the tick the picture is not kept.
  • Save the character of a video. On a video job, once its character sheet is approved (see Production). Free: nothing is drawn.

Its voice. Up to 3 things:

  1. How the voice sounds, in words: age, pace, accent and mood. This is what a video is told.
  2. A voice from the list. 12 voices to start with, each with a sample you can play.
  3. A recording of its voice. MP3, WAV or M4A of up to 10 MB, once you tick that the voice is yours to use in ads. A recording is what a video is asked to match, ahead of a voice from the list.

The app does not make the voice. It has no connection to the voice service yet. The words go into the video's plan and every clip prompt. A voice from the list or a recording is a choice kept with the avatar, and it is written into the prompt pack of a video that uses it, for whoever makes the clips to pick or attach by hand.

Edit and Remove are on each avatar. A new picture drops the avatar's character sheets (its own, and any drawn in another format's look), which were sheets of the old face. Removing an avatar removes its pictures and its recording; a video already made with it keeps its own copies. If a persona is deleted, its avatars stay with the brand under Avatars on no persona's sheet, and a video can still use them.

To use one, pick it under Who is on screen when you make a video (chapter 8).

These screens were built on 2026-10-08. They have not been looked at in a browser yet.

Using a persona

  • A set of image ads: on the new-set screen, under "Who are these for?", pick 1 or more. Every ad in the set is written for them. Pick none and the writer chooses a buyer for each ad.
  • From the persona itself: open it and press Make ads for this persona.
  • A concept: pick the persona when you build the concept. Its scripts are then written for that buyer.
  • The 50-ad sprint spreads its ads across the personas in use.

Write one by hand

Press Write one by hand, fill in at least the name, who they are and what they are living with, and save it as a draft or put it in use.
