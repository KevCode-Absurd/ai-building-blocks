The chat, and the 2 views

Guide, chapter 16 of 20

2 things make the app simple to use: a chat where you say what you want and it is made, and a Normal view of every section that shows only what you need.

The chat

Chat is in the sidebar, under Dashboard. The list on the left is your earlier chats for this brand. New chat starts an empty one.

Type what you want, or press Speak and say it. Press Enter to send.

10 image ads for mothers aged 35 to 44

3 scripts, 30 seconds each, about the half-used tub

8 headlines for the first-order offer

5 comic book image ads for the kids pack

What stops The Busy Parent from buying?

Make the first script into a carousel

The reply says what is being made, and the result arrives under it: the images as each one is rendered, the scripts with their opening lines, the copy as a list you can copy from. You can leave the screen while it works. The bar at the bottom shows it running, and Pause and Stop work on it. A stopped reply has a Carry on button and picks up where it stopped: nothing is made twice.

A look. Name a look and every ad of the set is made in it: comic book, cartoon, clay, hand-drawn, pixel art, a billboard, a diorama, an infographic. The reply says the look back ("all in 1 look: vintage comic or retro print"). These ads follow no layout from the library: each is 1 idea drawn in that look. Say "video" if you want a video; "comic book ads" on its own means images.

Creative type. To choose exactly what is made, press Creative type under the box and pick from the list: static ads from winning layouts, scroll-stopping ads, proof ads, offer ads, a carousel, a photoshoot, an instant or a flash photoshoot, product page images, a UGC video, a podcast clip, a talking object, claymation, a comic video, an explainer, a creator brief; static ads of 1 kind (a testimonial, a comparison, a before and after, an offer: 15 kinds); or static ads in any 1 of 23 looks. Type in the list to find one: "comic" shows the comic look for static ads and the comic video, so you pick which. The type shows as a chip above your message. Then you only say what it is about and how many: "4 for the kids pack, for busy parents". The chip is for 1 message. Without it, the chat works out the type from your words, as before.

Calling a recipe by name. The app's skills are called recipes where a person meets them (Kevin, 2026-10-06). Press Recipe under the box, or type / and the first letters of its name, and pick 1 of the app's skills: Static Ad Copywriter, Concept Ad Director, Native Story Writer, Script from Customer Research, Podcast Clip Director, Photoshoot Looks Library, and so on (32 at the time of writing). The skill shows as a chip, and beside it the things that skill needs as dashed chips: + Product, + Who, + Stage, + Offer, + Reference. Press one to fill it, or just say it in words ("for people who gave up on powders, problem aware"). Then send. The message runs that skill's own engine, with the brand brain, the editors and the checks it always has: it is the same work as from the skill's screen, started from here. The reply says the skill back ("With Native Story Writer.") and the skill is recorded on what was made, so feedback on it reaches that skill.

Only skills that do a job are in the list. The skills the app reads as rules while it works (the principles, the copy pass, the type libraries) are not: they come along by themselves. A skill that works from a reference ad (Winning Ad DNA, Script on a Reference's Structure, Talking Hook Maker) starts nothing until one is tagged or attached, and says so. A skill wins over a creative type when both are set.

A skill that needs nothing can be sent with nothing typed: press Send, and the message is the skill's name. The result shows as a card of headed lines with a link to the screen it lives on, and buttons for what you may want next. The paid ones need live mode and say so in mock mode; nothing is spent.

The stage. Say where the buyer is and the scripts are written for it: "unaware" or "cold", "problem aware", "solution aware", "product aware" or "warm", "most aware" or "retargeting". Or press + Stage and choose. Without it, the persona's own stage decides.

The length of a script. Say a length and each script is held to it: "3 scripts of 30 seconds", "a 2 minute script", "a 10 minute script". The chat takes the nearest of the 6 lengths a script can have: 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 to 3 minutes, 5 to 10 minutes, 10 minutes. Say no length and each script runs as long as its buyer needs (chapter 4, "How long a script runs"). A native story keeps its own lengths.

The same Recipe and Stage choosers are on the box on the home screen. On a phone the choosers fold into 1 Choices button that opens a list.

If the chat can't start without an answer, it asks 1 question (which product, or what kind of thing) with the answers as buttons.

Changing what it made

  • An image: press Change under it. The next message is about that image ("About: Image 3" shows above the box). Say what is wrong: "the headline is too small", "swap the kitchen for a desk", "change the headline to Skip the chalky powder". You can also say "make the third one brighter" without pressing anything.
  • A script: press Change on it and say what should be different.
  • A carousel or a static ad made from a script: press Change it on its card and say what should be different. The plan is written again with your note and every card is rendered again.

Every note you give is kept as feedback and read for a lesson that holds next time. The lessons are on the Lessons screen.

A video in the chat

A video is made in stages, and each stage waits for you, as on the Production board. The card in the chat shows the stage and its buttons:

  1. The plan: read it, then Approve the plan, or Change it with a note.
  2. The character sheet, the look frame, the storyboard: each is a picture to approve. Where there are several options, press the one you want first.
  3. The video clips: after the storyboard is approved. Today this step writes the prompts and stops at Prompts ready: the chat says so, and the prompt pack is on the ad on the Production board, to paste into Seedance 2.5 by hand. No clip is made in the app until a provider of Seedance 2.5 is set up.
  4. Join the clips: 1 button. No captions are drawn in unless they are switched on, when the job is started or on the job later.

A video's storyboard has 4 frames for each clip unless you say a number from 1 to 8 when you ask for it ("a UGC video, 6 frames a clip"). The action then names the number, so you see it before anything is drawn. 5. Motion graphics: Add motion graphics, or Finish without them.

You can also type it: "approve it", "skip the motion graphics".

Checking claims

Ask "check the claims" after anything with words in it, or press the suggestion under a reply. With nothing named, the newest thing made in the chat is checked. You can also paste words and ask for them to be checked.

The result lists each line with a problem: Fix for a line the brand's approved claims do not support, Look for a line to read again. Checking a script turns its claim checks on from then on.

Tagging and attaching

Type @ and pick from the list, or press the Persona, Product, Offer or Reference button.

  • Persona: who it is written for. If you describe people without tagging ("35 to 44, mostly mothers"), the chat picks the persona that fits. If none fits, your words are passed to the writer as they are.
  • Product and Offer: what it sells and on what terms.
  • Reference: an ad from the References library to build on.

Attach uploads images or videos from your computer. Each is added to your references and attached to the message.

What the chat reads

Under each reply, "Read for this" names what was read: the brand brain, the personas you tagged, the customer research, the references. For every message the chat is sent:

  1. The brand brain cut to products, offers, approved claims, voice and personas.
  2. The personas you tagged, whole.
  3. The 5 research themes closest to your message, with 2 customer quotes each.
  4. What was made in this chat, with the words on the 2 newest sets of images.
  5. The last 10 messages, in short.

It never states a fact, a number or a price about the product that is not in the approved claims, the offers or the product facts.

In mock mode

Images are placeholders and copy is a stand-in. Scripts, script changes and videos need live mode, and the chat says so. A carousel is made from the brand's newest script.

Normal and Advanced

Every section has 2 views. The switch is in the bar across the top, and it belongs to the section you are in: Scripts can be in Advanced while everything else stays in Normal.

  • Normal shows what you need to make something and ship it: the script's lines, the images, approve, download.
  • Advanced shows everything: every control, every check, every id and what things cost.

Costs. In Normal, no cost of running the app is shown: no dollar amounts, no token counts, no spend meters. They are on the Costs screen, which is the same in both views, on the spend caps in Settings, and in Advanced. The caps still apply in both views.

A new installation opens every section in Normal.
