Ad-learning foundations (2026-10-08)

references/analysis.ts defines and reads ReferenceAnalysis for imported and newly analysed ads. references/repair.ts and scripts/repair-references.ts provide free, repeatable repairs, with a dry run and database backup. Exact media hashes link ad_references.analysis.library_ref_id to library_refs; both analyses stay in their original tables. Measured locally: 267 timed transcripts restored from Remix's native data, 590 image links, and 87 unused automatic script reference lists cleared.

references/choose.ts owns ranking and diversity for video examples and static choices. Stage, format, industry and product type come before brand-local usage and recency. studio/select.ts keeps the existing layout-type order and calls this chooser within each type. Uncalibrated craft scores are ignored. Migration 0026_reference_uses stores one (brand_id, reference_key, use_key) per example and writing request/work item. Retries count once; linked copies share history; browsing does not consume it. Usage starts with this change.

The default free-flowing script prompt now receives at most two automatic learning examples. references/learning.ts builds bounded source-labelled cards from real speech, timeline functions and strategy; missing speech stays missing and long excerpts preserve opening, middle and close. Explicit reference modes keep their existing path. The video planner reuses those saved source IDs for visual progression, preserving the approved script and cast, and includes the reference content in its resumable-plan key. Timelines are never called measured cut lists.

references/learning-context.ts joins selected static library rows to strategy belonging to the shared library or the current brand. The planner receives strategy; the words pass also receives source copy and text roles. Existing detailed/exact reference controls admit these examples; function-only and no-reference modes do not. Photo/PDP selection keeps suitability and craft ranking but rotates ties by per-brand usage. Photo briefs now include saved set construction and styling.

refs:learning writes a free, reproducible coverage inventory with complete source membership and video/static lesson cards under data/library-learning/. learning:image-test freezes paired briefs, plans and input hashes for the approved forty-image comparison. Its image calls explicitly pin the model with strictModel, use the normal cap/ledger path, and never fall back or automatically rerender. The general library-learning code contains no test-brand rules; the comparison fixture uses Grüns. See docs/LIBRARY-LEARNING-PLAYBOOK.md for scope and limitations.

copy:test --pick exercises automatic static selection and saves buyer stages in reusable briefs. --briefs supports any brand. One shot plan is saved before stopping at approval; no video is rendered. copy:report displays choices and plans. No Foreplay parser exists yet: it will use 5 real export records before the full import. Output quality has not been proven by a paid comparison. How the Creative Engine is built: every pathway from research to a finished ad, which model does each step, exactly what context it is given, how many tokens that is, and what it costs. Numbers are measured from this machine's call logs on 2026-10-02.

How to read the numbers

  • Every model call is logged in the database: the exact prompt sent, the tokens in and out, the time and the cost (llm_calls for text, media_calls for images and video). Nothing in this document is an estimate of tokens: they are averages of real calls. pnpm usage:report prints the current table.
  • Dollar figures for Claude and Gemini Flash use real price lists. Dollar figures for Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT-6 Sol are estimates: the app has no price entry for them and costs them at a fallback of $5 per million tokens in and $25 out, chosen to be on the safe side. The real bill for those 2 models is on the provider dashboards. Their token counts are exact.
  • Image dollars are computed from the usage the image API returns, at $5 per million text tokens in, $8 per million image tokens in, $30 per million out.
  • Video dollars are computed from a rate card (per second, by resolution), not returned by the provider.
  • The Costs screen shows the same logs per run: each set, job and script with its steps, models, tokens, images and every call in order.
  • Totals so far: $25.45 on text models (1,745 calls, 3.9 million tokens in, 1.1 million out), about $5.00 on 68 images, $5.09 on 7 video clips.

Where a person sees the starts (2026-10-08). On Settings a step with suggested models has a line "Also suggested: ..." under it, in both views, and those lines of models are marked "also suggested" in its list (src/app/settings/steps-card.tsx). The model guide in Learning (src/app/learning/models/page.tsx) has a table "Where each job starts" for 3 jobs (primary texts and headlines from chat_copy, video scripts from script, video plans from shot_plan), read from recommended() and suggested of the steps themselves, so it can't drift from Settings. It shows the recommended start, not a saved choice.

The order of a prompt. What never changes (method, rules), then what is the same for the brand (facts, voice, look, lessons), then the line === PER CALL ===, then what this call is about. Providers cache a repeated prefix: Claude on marked blocks, DeepSeek and OpenAI by themselves, Gemini through a stored prefix the app creates once a prefix repeats within 9 minutes.

What is sent. Only the sections of a skill the job uses, and the research themes that share the most with what is being written.

The order of the steps for an image ad. Write, copy pass, voice pass, claims read and ad review (once each), rewrite only if a claim fails, image prompts, read before rendering. The ad review advises the person; it no longer sends ads back.

Pause, stop, resume (src/lib/llm/run-control.ts). Every paid call checks for a stop before it is sent and a text request in flight is cut off. Work is saved after every batch. A job left running by a closed app is paused on the next start, never resumed.

Measured (real prices): the brief for an image ad went from 14.1 cents to 4.4 cents; a script from 28.1 cents to 11.2 cents.

What each step is sent, and who chooses

3 levels of context (src/lib/llm/context.ts). Focused: the product being sold, its offers, the brand's own claims, and with the piece its buyers and the claims closest to its subject. Full: the whole brand file and whole skills. Minimal: bare facts and rules. The brand block is in 2 parts: what is the same for every piece on a product (cached), and what 1 piece adds.

Every step is listed once (src/lib/llm/steps.ts): what it makes, what it is sent, its recommended model and effort, its context levels, its blind scores. The Settings screen shows the list with a dropdown per choice; a saved choice wins over the recommendation (routeFor and contextLevel in routing.ts).

Lines and versions (src/lib/llm/models.ts): a model belongs to a line (Claude Sonnet, GPT Sol) and a line lists its versions, newest first. A step's saved choice is a line's id (line:claude-sonnet: it runs on the newest version, resolved in routeFor) or a model's id (that version, held). A new version is 1 row in MODELS and its price in pricing.ts. The recommended model of a step stays an exact version: the one that was compared.

Model tiers (src/lib/llm/models.ts): frontier, mid, small. A step gets the cheapest model whose output held up when compared blind. The writer of ads and scripts is a small model; editing and compliance are on the mid Claude; prompts and reading are on the mid Gemini.

Scripts and image ads are written freely by default. Claim checks are a switch on each dialog, with a default per brand in Settings (checks). Off: no claim register rules in the prompt, no guardrails, no claims read, no rewrite. On: as before. The ad review is a second switch, off by default. Both checks can be run on a finished piece with 1 press.

A skill has a card (CARD.md beside SKILL.md): the rules with the explanation taken out. A step at the focused level is sent the card. Cards exist for the copy principles and the 2 voice skills.

An editor returns the words that change, not the whole field (scripts/edits.ts): the copy pass and the voice pass name the phrase that fails and what replaces it, and the app puts it in place and runs its guards on the result.

A piece is sent its own product's claims (claimsOfProduct), and the brand's voice with each point said once (oncePerPoint).

Claude's cache is written only where it will be read (worthCaching in providers.ts): a once-per-run step marks its prefix only if the same prefix was sent in the last 5 minutes.

A dry run (setTap) builds a step's prompt and sends nothing, which is how context is measured and tested.

Where the cost is (src/scripts/token-audit.ts): on the writer it is the model's own thinking (99% of its input is read from cache); on the Claude steps it is input. So the writer is given fewer rules to reason about, and the Claude steps are given less to read.

Measured now: the brief for an image ad 1.8 cents (4.5 before phase 21); a script written freely 4.3 cents (6.3). Judged blind, the ads scored 7.7 against 6.8.

Personas (src/lib/personas): the core of each persona in use is in brand.json and is what a writer is sent; the profile around it (who, what they want, what stops them, how their segment performed, the picture) is in brand-brain/personas.json and is never sent to a model. The builder (persona_build) reads performance by segment, the research and the products and offers. Performance is a file in the brand folder until the warehouse arrives, and says when it is simulated.

Offers (src/lib/offers, DECISIONS P22). brand.json's offers are the brand's price points. An offer designed here is built on one of them and has 5 parts (positioning, price architecture, value mechanic, risk reversal, urgency); it is kept in brand-brain/offers.json and is a draft until a person puts it in use.

  • The arithmetic is code (economics.ts, evaluate.ts; pure, shared by the server and the browser): the waterfall of an order, the return that breaks even, the most an order can cost to win and still leave what an order leaves today, the discount curve, where order values peak, which of the 7 mechanics fit, and the 7 failure tests. No model is asked and nothing is paid.
  • The 7 failures are a file (skills/offer-building/failures.md): the names, what each is and the fix are read from it; the test for each is code keyed by its id.
  • The builder is 1 call (build.ts, step offer_build, prompt offer-build.md, DeepSeek Flash): the method as a card, the 7 fixes in a line each, the brand in a dozen lines, its numbers already worked out, the buyers by name, 16 research themes as labels, and 1 industry's patterns when borrowing. About 4,000 tokens in, 12,000 to 20,000 out (mostly thinking), 1.5 to 2.4 cents, about a minute.
  • Other industries (skills/offer-building/industries.json, 46 of them): written from general knowledge and marked so. research.ts (step offer_research) searches the web once for an industry, with Anthropic's web search on Claude Sonnet, and keeps the result in skills/offer-building/research/<id>.json for every brand. Nothing about a brand is sent. Measured: 4 searches, 65,000 tokens in, 21 cents.
  • In use (store.ts): the offer's statement is written into brand.json's offers as off_x_<name>, so concepts, scripts and image ads find it where they already look. A writer is sent its full terms only for the piece that states it (brandPack); other pieces get its name and price. A repositioning in use also puts its buyer in use as a persona and files a draft concept, with the research themes that show the want. Add-ons (cart, checkout, post-purchase) are marked as running and sent to no writer.
  • The offer path (phase 47; path.ts, path-facts.ts, words.ts, screen src/app/offers/offer-path.tsx): an offer is built by 5 choices on the screen, and what each choice means is code, not a skill sent to a model: the 5 mechanics with an example each (the old upsell and post_purchase sit after the main offer), the 4 add-ons, the rule that marks 1 choice Recommended from the 4 questions and the kind of doubt (recommend), the sums (numbersTable: 1 order today beside 1 order with the offer, the break-even return and the return that keeps today's profit per order), the planner for cart, checkout and post-purchase add-ons, and the 7 failures as warnings (pathWarnings), which never block. The 1 model call (offer_words, prompt offer-words.md, the mid Gemini) is sent only the chosen path and 1 mechanic's snippet from skills/offer-building/snippets/ (about 1,050 tokens), plus the brand voice block every writer carries (about 900 for Grüns): measured 1,975 tokens in, 120 out, 0.19 cents and 2 to 3 seconds an offer, against about 4,700 in and 15,000 out (1.9 cents and about a minute, for 6 offers) for the bulk builder. It writes the name, the page line, the say line and why it answers the doubt, and judges whether the buyer would want a gift; code refuses a line with a figure it was not sent. The offer is saved through the offer book (book.ts) with its mechanic, 5 parts, buyer, doubt, sums and add-ons; matchOffers ranks the offers for a script's persona and angle; it is tested, and no writer calls it yet. An offer found on a site is sorted into the 5 parts by its own words (partsOfText), and a part no offer states is shown as a suggestion.
  • The brand's economics (data.ts): brand-brain/economics.json when a person has entered the costs, else sample-data/performance/economics.json, which says it is simulated. loadEconomics is the 1 function that changes when the warehouse arrives.

Any brand, any category (DECISIONS P23). Nothing in the code or the prompts is written for 1 brand. What used to be shaped around the first demo brand, and how it is general now:

  • Money: 1 helper (src/lib/money.ts) for every currency sign, and the words that mean a price is being said.
  • Fact rules (scripts/fact-rules.ts): the words that limit a figure are read off the brand's own claims ("per pack", "per use", "and minerals"), not a fixed list; a first-order price is matched to the offers of its own product.
  • Redaction (pipeline/redact.ts): phone numbers as the UK, Australia and Europe write them, and UK and Canadian postcodes, as well as the US forms. The same patterns guard every call to a model.
  • Products (brand-brain.ts): specs (material, size, capacity) beside ingredients, and variants (flavours, colours, sizes) that exclude each other, which the sprint uses when it picks a photo.
  • Image checks and the sprint (studio/inspect.ts, sprint/formats.ts, sprint/assets.ts): worded for a product that may have no packaging at all.
  • Reference matching (studio/select.ts): whole words, the narrow category before the broad one.
  • A brand with no brain yet: every screen says so and points to the Brand brain screen (components/needs-brain.tsx); an empty list says what comes first (components/empty-next.tsx); the dashboard shows the setup path.
  • Setting up from nothing (lib/brand-onboard.ts, lib/site-pages.ts): the brand's website read once, with no model call, into brand-brain/site/ (1 markdown file a page, after a boilerplate pass that moves a line standing on 3 or more pages into site/_shared.md once; a page is cut at 60,000 characters and its header says so) with website.md as the index (address, date, 1 line a page with its title, address, size, first heading and first paragraph, and its file). The brain build, the claims reader, the style card and the persona filter read that folder, never the browser; a competitor's site is kept the same way under competitors/<id>/site/. Pasted notes go into notes.md, the founder's answers into founder-interview.md; the brain is built from whichever exist.

Sources: the customer's voice and the brand's own (lib/taxonomy.ts, lib/brand-sources.ts, lib/customer-voice.ts). 5 sources carry what customers say. 2 carry what the brand says: its website (pages kept as items, read for claims by claim-sources.ts, never sent for insight extraction; each brand-brain claim is marked as stated on the site or not) and its founder (1 item for each answered section of the questionnaire, extracted like any item). A founder's words are left out of every query that treats a quote as a customer's (customerVoice), add nothing to a theme's source diversity (ranking.ts), and cannot be a concept's only evidence of desire.

A length the person chose (2026-10-08; src/components/length-choice.tsx, src/lib/copy/stage.ts, LENGTH_PRESETS and LENGTH_LABELS in taxonomy.ts). A length is seconds (LENGTH_SECONDS in taxonomy.ts: 15, 30, 60, 150, 450 and 600), and the words that fill it are those seconds at the speaker's pace (lengthWords(length, age); see "1 table of paces" below): 50, 99, 198, 450, 1,350 and 1,800 spoken words when the speaker's age is not known, the 3 lengths over a minute counted at 0.9 of the pace. When several scripts are asked for at once the read-out uses 1 age only where every speaker is in the same age band (sharedAge), else the usual pace. Where it is: the Generate scripts dialog (scripts/generation.tsx), the script studio's controls, the Script builder (builder-view.tsx, builder/start.ts) and the chat (lengthOf in chat/request.ts; 10 minutes or more is 10m). 1 table of paces (src/lib/pace.ts, 2026-10-08). For a plan of covered shots (cut.cover) 3 more things changed on 2026-10-08. The first line reads "4 shots with hard cuts at 2s, 5s and 7s; each cut is to a new camera set-up, a different size and angle, and the camera cuts only there", because a video model given several shots of 1 subject films them from 1 spot unless it is told the camera moved. Each shot line carries its size, angle, move and framing: "[2-5s] Shot 2 (close-up, low angle, push in: from the chest up, the camera at counter height; place: the car): ...". After the shot lines comes the motion line. At a calm pace it is "Every shot is moving from its first frame: the action is under way, and nothing waits to begin." At any other pace it is now "Every shot is moving from its first frame, at a quick real-life pace: the action is under way and is done before the cut. Nobody waits, drifts or moves in slow motion.", because the owner found the voice quick and the picture slow. The header of the spoken lines is "Spoken as 1 unbroken track across the cuts, from 0s, with no pause at a cut:". For a plan dealt at 3.2 words a second or more (plan.speech.rate) and not at a calm pace it ends "with no pause at a cut or between sentences:". The clip prompt of a made world (cut.montage) differs in 3 ways, so its room goes to the shots. What this audit of the architecture turned up

Things seen while measuring, not yet changed:

  1. The cost of Gemini Pro and GPT-6 Sol is an estimate. They are about half of text spend ($13.17 of $25.45). Their real rates should be entered in src/lib/llm/pricing.ts; until then the budget cap errs on the safe side but the dollars here are not the bill.
  2. The script-led image plan carries about 40,000 characters of skill text that does not apply: the style skills are loaded whole, including their install and setup phases ("Phase 0a: install check"). Cutting them to the sections that describe the look would take about 10,000 tokens off every image plan, about 5 cents each, and remove noise.
  3. The product's extra photos for Grüns are not product shots. The creative kit chose a "benefits lifestyle" image and a "what to expect" infographic as the second and third product references. They are sent to the image model as "the same product, another view". Choose plain shots on the set-up screen, or correct the kit.
  4. A planner answer that failed its shape was asked again in full, doubling a 25-cent call. A bare list is now accepted; other shape failures still cost a second call.
  5. Gemini calls are not cached. The 18,000-token skill block is identical for every ad plan of a kind. Provider-side caching would cut the input cost of repeat plans.
  6. The second look at an image costs 15 times the inspection (4 cents against a quarter of a cent) because it runs on Gemini Pro. It is the check that catches wrongly opened packs and bad hands, so it earns it, but it is the place to save if volume grows.
  7. The logo file is captured and never sent. By rule the pack carries the logo. An ad with no pack in it has no logo.
  8. Video prompts carry no brand colours or type. Captions are drawn by the app in a default style, not the brand's fonts.

Product by stage: unaware and problem aware none, solution aware scene, product aware clear, most aware hero; a native ad none; a product page image and a photoshoot frame hero. Styling: full, except none for a native ad and basics for an image ad, sprint ad or carousel for an unaware buyer. FIXED holds what a surface never changes (a product page image is always full; a cast picture has no product, words or styling).
  • 13 surfaces (SURFACES): script, hooks, caption, landing, image_ad, native_ad, sprint_ad, carousel, pdp, photoshoot, storyboard, shot_plan, cast. LEVERS_FOR says which levers each has.
  • Wiring. An engine calls decide() (or planFor / tasteBlock in apply.ts) and sends only what the plan says. Called from: studio/taste.ts (planFor, read by every step of studio/engine.ts), scripts/flow.ts (wordsPlan) and scripts/engine.ts, scripts/hooks.ts, references/hooks.ts and references/build.ts, production/taste.ts and production/engine.ts, sprint/engine.ts (1 plan a slot, at the slot's stage), chat/make.ts and chat/write.ts, concept-suggest.ts, coverage/start.ts, format-start.ts, landing/write.ts. wiring.ts is the list of points, printed as the appendix of docs/TASTE-DECISIONS.md. 2 bridges remain where an engine still takes the older fields (studioFields, scriptFields in apply.ts); compat.ts reads a stored variety, fidelity or product choice as the matching stop.
  • Snippets (skills/taste/). 1 file under 110 words for each stop that changes anything, by lever: look/picture-*.md and look/words-*.md (4 each), product/, styling/, sell/, words/, finish/, spread/, length/, pace/, people/, hooks/, and always.md (reads aloud, makes sense, 1 story, true facts). A step is sent only the files its plan names.
  • The screen. src/components/taste-bar.tsx, on every making screen. Normal view: the Look and the product. Advanced: styling and the smaller levers. The chat reads levers from plain words (ask.ts). The 3 CLIs take --look, --product, --styling and --taste='<json>' (cli.ts).
  • The table for a person is docs/TASTE-DECISIONS.md, printed by npx tsx src/lib/taste/write-doc.ts. A test fails when it and the table disagree.

Scripts. A script written from principles (not from a reference, not native, claim checks off) now runs: write with the story brief, code checks, then at most 2 loops of read, patch, read the patched lines again.

  • "Do not invent facts" is in prompts/script-rules-flow.md, script-hooks.md and script-primary-text.md: story, voice and persuasion are free; facts come only from what was sent.
  • The fact checker (scripts/fact-check.ts, step script_fact_check). factStore builds everything the brand holds, 1 fact a line: the product's facts, every claim about it, the offer in use and the guarantee, the buyer's record and proofs, the brief. The model lists each factual statement with the number of the fact it rests on. settleFacts decides in code: no citation is invented; stretched keeps the fact it stretches; scene (a picture the story paints) is listed and never sent back, unless it states a number, which makes it a claim.
  • The second reader (script_verify, GPT-6 Luna) reports against the 12 checks of CHECKS in edit-pass.ts. It is never the model that fixes.
  • Code checks (codeFaults): a sentence too long for 1 breath, 3 thoughts chained, a run of fragments, a figure said twice, a figure held nowhere, a second speaker, several prices in a line, more of the deal than the stage is told, a new figure after the after.
  • Patch-only fixes (step script_patch, prompts/script-patch.md). Sent: the story in a few lines (the spine), the faulty lines marked * with 1 neighbour each side (patchWindow), any earlier line a fault contradicts, the fault, and at most 6 held facts that bear on a fact fault (factsFor). Only changed lines come back. FIRST_STEP = "patch": no whole-script edit first.
  • The loop cap. LOOPS = 2 (SCRIPT_EDIT_LOOPS can lower it). What is left is written on the script (voice_check.remaining, log, facts).
  • The offer by stage (offerSaidAt, offerForStage in flow.ts): none for unaware and problem aware, 1 thing once for solution and product aware, the whole deal for most aware. The full terms go under the caption by code.
  • Doubts and proofs from the persona (pairDoubts, doubtsFor): at most 3, closest to the idea; a doubt with no proof is not sent. factBudget sets how many by stage.
  • The caption check (builder/kit.ts, step script_caption_check, prompts/script-caption-check.md): 1 reader holds the primary text, each headline and each further hook to the finished script on 7 faults, and fixes or cuts the sentence. A separate fact check of these words runs only with CAPTION_FACT_CHECK=on.
  • A hook's own headline and caption opening (hookTexts, hookPrimaryText in builder/kit.ts, step script_hook_texts): 1 call writes them for every further hook; the rest of the primary text is shared.
  • checkScriptFormat refuses a format the app does not make before anything is written.
