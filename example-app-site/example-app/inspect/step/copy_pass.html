Copy pass: sharpen the lines that do not sell

A few rewritten lines, each with a reason

What it is meant to be sent

The copy principles as a rule card; the facts of the product being sold (its offers, the guarantee, the brand's documented claims and the 8 others closest to the draft); who it is for; the draft. It returns only the words that change, not the whole field.

What it runs on, and why

claude-sonnet-5-5, medium effort, focused context is the recommended setting. 2 rounds, 10 editors. Editing is where the small models fail: 3 broke a fact, Haiku's 3 edits were all refused by the fact guard, Flash-Lite made 1 edit on 6 weak drafts, and Gemini Flash at high effort ran out of answer. Sonnet is half a point under Opus, inside the judges' disagreement, at 40% of the cost. Sent the facts of the product being sold, Sonnet scored 8.0; sent every product and offer, 6.3 (1 judge). Since 2026-10-02 it is sent the principles as a card (2,600 characters for 10,000) and returns phrases: 6,400 tokens in for 9,900, and the finished ads scored higher blind (see the ad writer).

What goes in, block by block

Each prompt is cut at its tags and its headings. Tokens are the provider's count for the whole call, shared out by length. A block that is not in every call is this step's own to 1 piece of work (a format, a reference, a note). A block that comes from a file is changed in that file; what the app builds is changed on the brand's own screens. Feedback is kept on the Notes tab.
