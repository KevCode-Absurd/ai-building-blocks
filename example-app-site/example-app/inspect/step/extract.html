What goes in, block by block

Each prompt is cut at its tags and its headings. Tokens are the provider's count for the whole call, shared out by length. A block that is not in every call is this step's own to 1 piece of work (a format, a reference, a note). A block that comes from a file is changed in that file; what the app builds is changed on the brand's own screens. Feedback is kept on the Notes tab.
