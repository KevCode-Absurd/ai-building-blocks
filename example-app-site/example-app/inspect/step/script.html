Write a script

A video ad script: lines, hooks, on-screen text, visual notes

What it is meant to be sent

The task skill, the principles and the winning patterns (cached); the product and the offer of this concept; the buyer, once, in the concept; the concept's own themes and quotes; 2 reference ads; how its sibling scripts open, prove and close; lessons about words. The claim register and the guardrails only when claim checks are on.

What it runs on, and why

gemini-3.1-pro-preview, low effort, focused context is the recommended setting. The owner's choice, 2026-10-08: he read the 10 video scripts each of 6 models wrote for 5 kinds of buyer and found the frontier Gemini's much the better, so it is where a script starts. The blind judges did not agree: in that test they scored its scripts 5.5 of 10 against 6.8 for Claude Opus and 6.1 for DeepSeek Flash, and the scores shown here are from the earlier comparison, which put DeepSeek Flash first. His read outranks a judge's; the model guide in Learning has every script side by side. The earlier comparison: 2 rounds, 2 briefs each, 2 blind judges, every provider. The small DeepSeek model beat every frontier model both times. Second round: low effort scored 7.5 against 7.4 at high for half the output and half the time, so low is recommended; focused, full and minimal context scored 7.4, 7.7 and 7.4, which is inside the judges' disagreement. The other small models did not hold up on scripts written freely: Haiku 5.0 to 5.6 (it ran long), Gemini Flash 4.3, Flash-Lite 4.8.
