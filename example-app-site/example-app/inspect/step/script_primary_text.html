Primary text and headlines for a script

The primary text and 3 or 4 feed headlines that run with 1 script: the same speaker, story and facts. For a buyer who is unaware or problem aware it is its own long telling of that story for a reader (the length follows the stage); lower down it stays close to the script

What it is meant to be sent

The ad principles (cached); the story the script tells (who speaks, the before, the turn, the after, each sentence that states a figure, how the offer is said); the script; the buyer; the stage brief; the length and the rule on technical terms for the stage; for a story stage, how a long story is told; the research finds and the fact sheet the script writer was sent; the offer as a person would say it and its facts; the brand's voice for a told story; lessons about words as advice.

What it runs on, and why

gemini-3.8-flash, low effort is the recommended setting. The owner's choice, 2026-10-08, after reading what 6 models wrote: for primary texts and headlines he suggests the mid Gemini and DeepSeek V4 Pro. The scores shown are the mean of 2 blind judges over the headlines and primary texts of that test, written on the chat's path and not this one; the judges put Claude Opus first, and his read outranks theirs. Before that it ran on DeepSeek Flash, the model that wrote the script it runs with, and earlier still unrouted on the frontier Gemini, at about 2.5 cents. Low effort, as the script writer: at medium a long caption cost 1.8 cents in thinking. No cap on the answer is set here: the step sizes it from the stage (a 2,000 word text is about 3,000 tokens, plus thinking), and a cap of 6,000 in this row overrode that and cut long texts short. When it comes back short of its range it is asked once more; what code still finds is noted on the script and blocks nothing.

What goes in, block by block

Each prompt is cut at its tags and its headings. Tokens are the provider's count for the whole call, shared out by length. A block that is not in every call is this step's own to 1 piece of work (a format, a reference, a note). A block that comes from a file is changed in that file; what the app builds is changed on the brand's own screens. Feedback is kept on the Notes tab.
