Write the ad: argument, every word, the picture

For each image: the argument, the words on it, the scene, the feed copy. For ads it is 2 calls: the picture and a first draft of the words, then the words again with the copy craft, in the same places

What it is meant to be sent

The method for a static ad (cached); the product being sold, once; its offers, the buyers the ads are for, the claims that bear on them; the brand's voice with each point said once; the 6 research themes closest to these ads; lessons; then each ad's reference and direction. The guardrails and the rule to cite a source for every fact only when claim checks are on.

What it runs on, and why

deepseek-flash, low effort, focused context is the recommended setting. 4 rounds, 2 blind judges, 6 ads, every provider. The frontier Gemini it replaced scored lowest of the big models and broke the most facts. Fourth round, on what it is sent: focused 7.3, full 7.5, minimal 7.1, inside the judges' disagreement, but focused kept every fact (0 drafts low on truth, 0 rule fails; full had 6 rule fails). High effort kept facts better than low (truth 8.7 against 6.9). The small models tried as writer: Haiku 5.9, Gemini Flash 5.5 to 5.8, GPT-6 Luna 5.7, Flash-Lite 4.5. Fifth comparison, of the whole pipeline: the same 6 ads written freely and edited once scored 7.7 blind, against 6.8 for the ads held to the claim register, read for claims and written again (2 judges, agreement 0.93). The guardrails had every ad carry a side-effect warning, which the judges marked down on both truth and copy.

What goes in, block by block

Each prompt is cut at its tags and its headings. Tokens are the provider's count for the whole call, shared out by length. A block that is not in every call is this step's own to 1 piece of work (a format, a reference, a note). A block that comes from a file is changed in that file; what the app builds is changed on the brand's own screens. Feedback is kept on the Notes tab.
