A store at 9% conversion on $50 orders can be a far worse business than one at 1% on $200 million. Conversion rate is gameable (turn off cold traffic and it jumps while revenue falls) and not comparable between brands. Track revenue per visitor, and contribution per visitor where the margin is known. What a better page is worth

CAC is ad spend over orders: $32.87 today. A 10% better conversion rate makes it $29.88, and adds $8,031 of revenue a period at the same spend. That headroom is what lets the brand spend more at the same CAC.

Shopify numbers that lie
  • Total sales include tax: use net sales plus shipping collected.
  • Shopify's average order value leaves out shipping collected, which is real revenue.
  • Shopify sessions include bots: use GA4's for any per-visitor figure.
  • Returning buyers convert higher and order more: a rising share of them flatters conversion rate.
