Build an offer

An offer is more than money off. It has 5 parts: who it is for, the price, what is added, what removes the risk, and a real reason to act now. Make 5 short choices below. 1 option in each is marked Recommended for Grüns; pick any you like.

1

Who is it for?

Start from the buyer and their biggest doubt, not from what you want to earn.

New buyers need a reason to try. Returning buyers need a reason to stay. They get different offers.

2

How does it work?

5 ways to build an offer. A discount is only 1 of them, and usually the worst to lead with.

Why spend more, get more is recommended: Your order sizes bunch in 2 groups, so 1 reward above each group moves both.
