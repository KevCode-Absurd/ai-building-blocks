Chosen when this script was set up. A problem-aware buyer is told no deal in the ad. The terms sit under the caption. Build an offer for this persona.

What it should be made as
The problem carries this script

The writer labeled this as offer, but the entire hook and problem line rely on exposing a painfully specific confession: 'If you buy the small size because you know you'll quit' and 'chalky powder turns every habit into a chore.' If that shared experience does not strike home instantly, the simple product-action line 'Rip. Eat. Done.' carries no contrast.

The writer meant the offer to carry it. As written, the problem does: either that is the ad, or the offer needs more of the script.

Recommended: 1 person to camera, Mixed video, Drawn, rendered or animated

Poor fit: Carousel, Designed static

Length: Fits its length

Unaware and problem-aware concepts need education. Prefer longer formats unless you are testing the angle with statics first.

Test plan

This script tests problem entry against its siblings: a purchase-behavior accommodation (buying the small size) in a 15s offer-led cut vs. the cabinet-tub and confession openings, so run its 5 hooks on this one body and compare hook rate and cost per purchase. If it wins, make a 30s cut that adds the 80% daily survey as a spoken proof beat and the first-order guarantee as risk reversal, plus a static using the winning overlay with the $29.99 end card.

Writer's notes

Spoken words: 39 (target 40, 98%), about 15s at 2.6 wps. Numbers traced: $29.99 first sub (off_first_sub), $49.99 per 28 packs (off_sub_28), 60+ ingredients and 6g fiber (clm_formula), 80% daily and 3,000+ surveyed (clm_adherence_survey), 30-day first-order guarantee (gtd_30day_first). No discount % quoted. No 'cancel anytime' or easy-cancel language. Guarantee stated as first order only. Adjustment-period disclosure and FDA disclaimer in end-card fine print. Taste is not promised. Warnings: 'chalky powder turns every habit into a chore' is a category-level statement drawn from persona language (gritty/chalky), not a measured comparison; judgement call that it reads as opinion, legal may want to soften. The hook is a conditional 2nd-person observation, so no personal-attribute assertion. No mechanism or risk-reversal beat at 15s by design; the survey stat sits on screen only. Confirm the renewal cadence wording ($49.99 per 28 packs) matches checkout. If the ad shows a Sugar-Free variant, add the stomach-upset warning; this cut shows Low Sugar only.
