Settings

The model for each step, the spend caps, the checks and what a finished piece is worth for this brand. The rest of the settings (how hard each step thinks, what it is sent, usage and naming) are in the Advanced view.

Model calls: mock · nothing is sent and nothing is spent

The model for each step
Each step of the work is done by a model, and each starts on the one that did best when they were compared. Pick another for any step. A step stays on the latest version of the model you pick, unless you hold it at 1 version. A change applies from the next run, for every brand.
Writer

Sets 1 model for every step that writes words a customer reads, so you can compare writers. Each step can still be set on its own below.

Spend caps
The most this installation may spend, across every brand, since it was set up. A step that would go over a cap is stopped before it is paid for. Raise a cap when you reach it; lower one to hold spending.
Checks for Grüns
Choose the default checks for this brand. You can also enable them on individual work. Failed checks pause automatic production; a human can override them, with the decision recorded.
What a finished piece is worth
The home screen adds up what Grüns's finished images, scripts and assets would have cost made by hand or hired out. Put in your own figures: what you would pay for 1 piece, and the hours 1 piece takes.
