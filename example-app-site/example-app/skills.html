Review how the app creates your work. Approve suggested improvements from feedback, or restore an earlier version.

Waiting for you

Each is 1 change to 1 skill. Read what the person said and what the change does, then the lines in green and red. Accept writes it as the skill's next version; reject keeps the note and drops the change.

Nothing waiting. A change arrives here when someone's feedback on a finished piece holds a rule true of any brand, or when you ask for a skill to be tightened below.

The skills

Version 1 is the skill as it shipped; every accepted change makes the next. Tighten asks a model for the same skill in fewer words, with every rule, heading, number and quoted phrase kept, and shows you the result above before anything changes.
