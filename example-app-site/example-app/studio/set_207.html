    Seen through the buyer's own eyes at eye level, arm's length from a white bench in a bright living room, hard morning sun through a window throwing sharp shadows of the window frame across the white surface, which is mostly bare. At the left of the frame a kid's backpack sits upright on the bench with its front pocket unzipped, a stoneware mug beside it, and a little right of that the real Grüns Kids pouch stands closed and upright, label to the camera and angled a few degrees left, big enough that its name reads.
