2. Choose the sound

Use a track cleared for this use; paid ads can require different rights from organic posts. For a silent export, turn off source audio and remove both voice and music tracks.

3. Add timed captions (optional)

Times refer to the edited timeline. Enter the exact spoken words or approved on-screen copy, using short phrases. Exports include a separate copy without these captions.

Transcription listens to the separate voice track when supplied; otherwise it listens to the trimmed source clips. Check the draft against the mix before export.
