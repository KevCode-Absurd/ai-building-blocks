# Sprout

Sprout is a real US greens-gummy brand. Name, people and some details changed. Built from its public website, ads and reviews, captured Sept to Oct 2026. Product facts, prices, discounts, claims, ad headlines and review quotes are as the brand showed them in public. Competitors and retailers are described, never named. The domain is sprout.example.

Files:
- `brand.md`: the brand file. Products, prices and the discount mess, claims (fine vs risky), buyers, voice, look, competitors, what seems to work.
- `reviews.md`: 20 real public review quotes, spelling kept, no names.
- `ads.md`: 15 real live ad headlines and what each one pushes.
- `product-page.md`: the main product page as text, top to bottom, with the issues you can see.
- `ad-results.csv`: practice results for 9 ads, 14 to 42 days each (one ad split into two 21-day windows). Opening lines are real headlines from ads.md. The numbers are made up, shaped on simulated cost and segment data, so analysis has something to find.
