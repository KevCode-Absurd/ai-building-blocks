## Two real main texts (the words above the ad)

> Big news: your first order of [Sprout] just got a lot more affordable. 💚 $29.99 for your first order, that's less than $1.10/day for 60+ whole-food ingredients, 21 vitamins & minerals, and gut-supporting probiotics in one daily gummy pack. ✅ $20 off your first order ✅ +$5 off for every extra person ✅ Third-party tested. Online-exclusive offer. Shop [Sprout].

> Feeling off in your gut lately? [Sprout] supports digestion with 6g of fiber, prebiotics, and daily vitamins to help keep your gut balanced without powders or prep.

Note: the first text says "probiotics". The ingredient list on the product page shows prebiotic fiber and no probiotics, so this looks like a real error to catch.

## The mix

- Of the 40 newest ads (made 30 Sept to 3 Oct): 19 offer-led, 7 perks, 6 hair, 3 habit or story, 2 gut, 2 limited flavour, 1 weight loss.
- Ads all say 61% off. The site says 52%, 55%, 49% or 58%, depending on the page.
- Missing from ads: the "I quit powders" story and the "I finally stuck with it" story, the two strongest themes in reviews.
