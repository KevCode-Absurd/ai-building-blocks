## Issues you can see

1. **Four discount numbers on one visit.** Bar says 52%, badge says "up to 55%", pop-up says 55%, the ad that sent you says 61%. Other pages show 49% and 58%.
2. **The $29.99 price needs the text sign-up.** Only the footer says so. "DISCOUNT AUTO-APPLIED" suggests otherwise.
3. **$1.07/day is the first box only.** It renews at $49.99 ($1.78/day). The renewal line is small, under the button.
4. **The guarantee covers the first order only.** The plan box just says "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee". The limit is in the last FAQ.
5. **Four different star ratings.** 4.8 from 100,000 (header), 4.75 from 109,639 (widget), 4.74 from 118,002 (reviews page), 4.0 from 2,411 (a public review site).
6. **Risky claims above the fold:** "revive whole body vitality", "Supports immunity + stress relief", "Promotes mental clarity". The study only measured 2 vitamins in the blood.
7. **The vitamin count changes:** "60+ ingredients ... 21 vitamins" in one place, "over 20" in the ingredients panel.
8. **Sugar-Free warning not seen.** The Low Sugar vs. Sugar-Free panel was not captured, so this is **(inferred)**. Sugar alcohols upset some stomachs, and the gut buyer is a core buyer.
9. **One-time purchase is made to look bad** ("No Free Shipping", "No First Order Discount"), which can feel like a push into a subscription for buyers who already fear one.
10. **Reviews sit at the very bottom.** The best proof (powder quitters, gut stories) is far from the button.
11. **Missing number** in the "don't meet daily nutrient intake" stat, and footnotes that only say "Source".
