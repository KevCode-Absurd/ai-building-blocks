# Sprout: customer reviews

20 real public reviews, copied as written (spelling kept). Reviewer names removed. The brand name and a competitor name are swapped in [brackets]. Captured Sept to Oct 2026.

Where they come from:
- **Brand site**: reviews the brand picked to show on its own pages. Expect them to be glowing.
- **Brand site widget**: the live review feed at the bottom of the product page (not hand-picked, but shown by the brand).
- **Retailer**: reviews on big-box retailer and warehouse club sites.
- **Review site**: public third-party review sites. These carry most of the complaints.

Star ratings were not captured for most quotes. The brand claims 4.8 stars from 100,000 reviews; a public review site showed 4.0 from 2,411.
