# LinkedIn commerce acquisition access

This framework does not install a LinkedIn connector. Discover the host's actual tools, account permissions and current supported schema. The [LinkedIn conversions overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/conversions/conversions-overview) and [Lead Gen Forms specifications](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/lead-gen-ads/specs), checked 2026-11-17, describe capabilities rather than this host's access. Recheck changing versions, fields and limits.

Start with authorized Campaign Manager, company/quote/order and merchant-policy exports. Keep account ID, currency, timezone, reporting period, attribution and cohort definitions explicit. Customer lists, contact details and conversion uploads need their own lawful, authorized scope; do not upload them to prove the tool works. Use the host secret store or owner-managed authentication, never pasted credentials.

Esmee owns paid acquisition of wholesale or corporate gifting buyers. Mei owns commercial qualification, quotes and sales. Map accepted companies through quotes and kept orders to contribution before optimizing for form volume. No approved wholesale/gifting terms means a readiness plan with proposals, not an invented active offer.

Finish local campaign/form/creative and handoff files without a connector. For an actual supported change, reuse valid account-scoped authority, verify exact object IDs, amount/units, budget kind, dates and limits, then retain returned IDs and fresh readback. Distinguish saved configuration from observed delivery and realized orders. Keep this account's history under `context/media-buying/linkedin/<account-id>/` and never borrow another platform's grant.
