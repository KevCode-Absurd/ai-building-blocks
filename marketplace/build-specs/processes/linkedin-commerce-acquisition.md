## Stage by stage

### 1. Confirm merchant and channel fit

Read brand/product/customer evidence and actual source definitions. Discover available LinkedIn account capabilities or use exports; do not invent channel data. A qualified-company to quote/order/contribution map and a commercial handoff to Mei.

- **Needs:** context/org-context/; The user brief, current authority and dated source exports
- **Makes:** linkedin/fit.md, linkedin/sources.json
- **Checks:** fit-review (The intended customer and offer fit the merchant evidence; unsupported account facts are marked missing.)
- **Done when:** The channel hypothesis, source limitations, offer qualifications and real account scope are explicit.

### 2. Make the campaign and measurement files

Follow workflows/commerce-wholesale-acquisition.md. Deliver editable campaign, creative and measurement files within the approved channel share, contribution/cash/stock constraints. Reuse existing authority and expose unsupported operations. A qualified-company to quote/order/contribution map and a commercial handoff to Mei.

- **Needs:** linkedin/fit.md; context/limits.json
- **Makes:** linkedin/build-sheet.csv, linkedin/creative.md, linkedin/measurement.md, linkedin/handoff.md
- **Checks:** package-review (The campaign stays inside channel allocation and business limits, and arithmetic, units and source qualifications are checked.)
- **Done when:** The actual files are reviewable, arithmetic is traceable and every unsupported provider step is labeled.

### 3. Apply only a supported authorized provider change

Only if the requested LinkedIn operation has an available verified tool and valid account-scoped authority, execute the concrete diff and read it back. Otherwise finish the local package without claiming provider completion.

- **Needs:** linkedin/build-sheet.csv; Valid exact-account operation and budget authority
- **Makes:** linkedin/provider-receipt.md
- **Goes live:** Only through a verified available LinkedIn interface within existing account-scoped authority; no connector is included or assumed.
- **Done when:** Returned object IDs and fresh readback support any claimed change; local-only work is explicitly distinguished.

### 4. Return the business decision and next observation

Recompute comparable metrics and preserve attribution and period differences. A qualified-company to quote/order/contribution map and a commercial handoff to Mei. Separate proposed tests, provider status, observed delivery and actual business outcomes.

- **Needs:** linkedin/measurement.md; linkedin/handoff.md
- **Makes:** linkedin/readout.md
- **Checks:** results-review (Material calculations are reproducible and the report does not confuse platform claims with kept orders or realized contribution.)
- **Done when:** The owner receives a specific supported decision, usable artifacts and the exact remaining gap or next observation.
