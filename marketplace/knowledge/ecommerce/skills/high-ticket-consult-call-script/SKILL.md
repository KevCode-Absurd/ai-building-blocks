# Consultation call for high-ticket products

**You get:** a consult kit ready to run: a money check, booking page copy, a pre-call form, confirmation and reminder messages, a one-page prep sheet, a full call script with show-on-camera lists, three closes, a follow-up rule, and a tracker of calls, closes and gross profit per call.

## When to use

- The store sells items where one order is a big decision (jewellery, furniture, mattresses, custom or made-to-order pieces).
- Buyers ask long chains of questions in chat and then go quiet.
- Buyers say they want to "see it" before paying.
- Checkout abandonment is high on the most expensive products.

## Not for

- Everyday pre-sale chat: use `live-chat-presale-playbook` and `write-chat-sales-scripts`.
- Price pushback on its own: use `handle-price-objections-in-chat`.
- Setting the price: use `set-prices-from-costs-and-competitors`.
- Research interviews with past buyers: use `customer-interview-script-and-summary`.

## What you need

Required:

1. **Products in scope** with price, gross profit per order (delivered costs), stock or lead time, and options (sizes, finishes, fabrics).
2. **Order export** for those products (last 6 to 12 months) and checkout abandonment counts.
3. **Who runs calls**, their hours and cost per hour.
4. **Policies**: returns, exchange, warranty, delivery and installation, payment and financing options.
5. **Booking tool**: any calendar with a booking page (free calendar booking pages work) and a video tool the buyer already has (WhatsApp video, Google Meet, Zoom, FaceTime).

Optional: chat transcripts with high-value buyers, reviews mentioning service, photos and videos of products in real homes or on real hands.

Access: reading orders is level 1. Publishing the booking button on product pages and turning on booking messages is level 2 in draft and goes live only with the client's yes. Taking deposits or sending payment links follows the store's checkout; the agent never takes card details on a call.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Consults pay.** Cost per call = staff minutes x cost per minute plus booking no-shows. Extra gross profit per call = gross profit per order x (close rate with a call minus the rate it would have had anyway). Pass: extra gross profit per call is at least 3 times cost per call (practitioner default). Higher-priced orders bring many more calls per order anyway [OPS-0006]. Fail: offer consults only above a price line where it passes. For furniture and other low-repeat goods the first order has to carry the cost, since repeat profit is near zero [MEA-0052].
2. **Gross profit per order on delivered costs** [MEA-0037]. Fail: compute it first.
3. **The team can deliver what it says.** Delivery dates, installation, warranty and customisation on the call must match policy. Fail: fix the policy line first.
4. **Recording and data.** Ask before recording any call; some places need every party's consent. Fail: do not record.

## Steps

### 1. Place the booking button where it belongs

Put "Book a 20-minute video consult" only on products above the price line from gate 1, near the price and add to cart, and in the chat hand-off. On cheaper pages a call-an-expert block draws very few clicks and adds clutter [LPG-0043]. Add one line on what happens: "See it on camera, ask anything, no obligation."

### 2. Use a 3-question pre-call form

1. What are you looking for, and what is it for? (occasion, room, who it's for)
2. What matters most? (budget range, size or space, delivery date, style)
3. Anything you've seen elsewhere or want to compare?

Add the phone number with a tick box to receive booking messages on WhatsApp or SMS, recorded as consent.

### 3. Confirm and remind

- On booking: date, time, agent's name, the video link, and "Have your room measurements ready" or "Tell us your ring size if you know it".
- 24 hours before and 1 hour before: one reminder each (practitioner default).
- About 5 minutes after the booking, a person sends a short hello by name and one open question ("Is this for an occasion?"), and a person answers from the first reply onward [EML-0031].

### 4. Prepare in 10 minutes

Read the form. Pick two products that fit and one runner-up. Check stock or lead time, delivery date to their postcode or ZIP, and current price. Have the proof ready: real customer photos with permission, reviews on the same concern, and certificates or specs.

### 5. Run the call (20 to 30 minutes)

| Minutes | Part | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Open | "Thanks for booking. I'll ask a few questions, show you two or three pieces, and you can ask anything. If it's not right, I'll tell you. Sound good?" |
| 2 to 8 | Discover | Ask about the occasion or room, what matters most, what they've tried. Use their words back to them. Let silences run. |
| 8 to 18 | Show | Show the product on camera (lists below). Show the runner-up only if the first is not right. |
| 18 to 22 | Recommend and price | "Based on what you said about [their words], I'd go with [product] at [price in the store's currency]. That includes [delivery, installation, warranty]." Then stop talking. |
| 22 to 27 | Answer | Questions and doubts. Price pushback: use `handle-price-objections-in-chat` replies, said aloud. |
| 27 to 30 | Ask | One of the three closes in step 6. |

**Show on camera, jewellery:** daylight by a window and indoor light; on a hand or neck next to a coin or ruler for scale; the clasp and back; the hallmark or certificate; weight on a scale if price depends on it.

**Show on camera, furniture:** the fabric or wood up close and in full; the piece next to something of known size; how it opens, folds or adjusts; the delivery path questions (door width, stairs, lift).

A short video of the founder or maker on the booking page can build trust before the call [LPG-0023]; make it about the people, not a pitch.

### 6. Close one of three ways

- **Clear yes:** "Great. I'll send the order link now with [options] already selected, and your delivery date will be [date]." Send it while on the call.
- **Not yet:** "What would you need to feel sure? And when will you decide?" Agree a date for one follow-up, or offer a reserve if the store allows one (a refundable hold on a one-off piece), with terms stated.
- **No:** "That's fine; this piece isn't right for you. I'll send the details in case anything changes." No chasing.

Never invent scarcity ("only one left" must be true) and never promise delivery dates the courier does not usually meet; a padded date the store beats is better [CXP-0010]. Warranty or guarantee lines must be the real terms; long warranties suit only items that truly last [OFF-0064].

### 7. Follow up once

Within 2 hours: a summary message with photos, the price, what's included, the order link and the agreed decision date. Then one message on that date. Nothing else unless they write back. Claims in the summary must be ones the store can prove [ADC-6146].

### 8. Track and adjust

Log every booking: source, product, show or no-show, outcome, order value, gross profit, call minutes. Monthly: close rate, no-show rate, gross profit per call, and the top 3 reasons for "not yet" and "no". Drop the booking button on products where gross profit per call stays below cost.

### Market notes

- **All markets:** gold jewellery prices move with the daily gold price, so state how long a quote holds. Show the hallmark details for gold (for example a UK assay office hallmark, or your country's hallmark scheme). State making charges and sales tax or VAT separately if that is how the invoice shows them. For furniture, confirm lift access and floor, and whether installation is included. Offer instalments or pay-later only through the store's checkout. Video calls on WhatsApp or FaceTime are often easiest for buyers.
- **UK and EU:** online buyers usually have 14 days to cancel; made-to-order or personalised pieces can be exempt, so say so before they pay.
- **US:** ask permission before recording; some states need consent from everyone on the call. Financing offers must show the real terms from the provider. Quote sales tax as "calculated at checkout" unless known.

## Checks before you hand it over

- The money check passes for every product with a booking button.
- The pre-call form has 3 questions and a recorded messaging consent.
- The call script fits 30 minutes and ends with one of three closes.
- Every promise in the script (delivery, warranty, reserve, price hold) matches policy.
- No scarcity or urgency line unless true and dated.
- Follow-up is one summary and one message on the agreed date.
- The tracker reads gross profit per call.

## Output

1. **One-screen summary:** products in scope, price line, expected calls a week, staff hours, and the gross profit per call needed to keep going.
2. **Booking kit:** button text, page line, pre-call form, confirmation and reminder messages.
3. **Prep sheet:** Buyer, Form answers, Picks (2 plus runner-up), Stock or lead time, Delivery date, Proof ready.
4. **Call script** (step 5) with show-on-camera lists and the three closes.
5. **Follow-up template.**
6. **Tracker:** Date, Source, Product, Showed, Outcome, Order value, Gross profit, Minutes, Reason if not yet or no.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking buttons on every product.** Gate 1 and step 1 keep them above the price line.
- **Talking the whole call.** Step 5 gives discovery its own 6 minutes and uses silence after the price.
- **Five options on camera.** Step 4 picks two and a runner-up.
- **Chasing after the call.** Step 7 sends one summary and one follow-up.
- **"Only one left" when it isn't.** Step 6 allows only true, dated scarcity.
- **Promising dates the courier can't meet.** Step 6 uses a padded date.

## Sources

- MEA-0052: furniture and homewares have near-zero repeat profit lift.
- MEA-0037: delivered gross margin.

- EML-0031: fast, personal first message to a new lead, then a person.
- LPG-0023: a short founder video builds trust on high-trust purchases.
- CXP-0010: specific, padded delivery dates.
- OFF-0064: long warranties only on items that truly last.

What makes this skill ours: consults are switched on only where gross profit per call beats the cost of the call; the booking button sits only above a price line; the camera lists are specific to jewellery and furniture; and the close allows a clean no with one follow-up, never a chase or fake scarcity.

## Selling in India

- Gold prices move with the daily gold rate; state how long a quote holds.
- Show the BIS hallmark with HUID for gold.
- State making charges and GST separately if the invoice shows them that way.
- Offer EMI only through the store's checkout. WhatsApp video calls are often easiest.
- Claims must be provable under ASCI's Code [ADC-6146].

Source: ADC-6146 (ASCI Code on provable claims).
