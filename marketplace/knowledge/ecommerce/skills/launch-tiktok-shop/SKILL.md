# Launch on TikTok Shop

**You get:** a launch checklist with each setting written out for approval, a per-product profit sheet after TikTok's fees, the first 3 to 10 products to list, a sample budget, a target list of 20 to 50 affiliate creators, a 30-day posting and outreach calendar, and a day-30 readout template.

## When to use

- A store with proven products in the US, UK or another TikTok Shop market wants to sell inside TikTok.
- A shop was opened but has few listings, no creators and no sales.
- The store already sells through TikTok ads to its website and wants in-app checkout.
- Creators keep asking for samples and a shop link.

## Not for

- Stores selling only in India: TikTok is not available there; use Instagram and YouTube routes such as `grow-instagram-with-collabs` and `tiktok-live-selling-plan`'s India note.
- Writing listings in depth: use `optimize-tiktok-shop-listings`.
- Recruiting creators in depth: use `tiktok-shop-creator-affiliate-outreach`.
- Paid campaigns for the shop: use `tiktok-shop-gmv-max-ads` after launch.

## What you need

Required:

1. **Market** and business details for the seller account. The client creates the account and signs in; never ask for passwords or ID documents in chat.
2. **Product list** with landed cost, weight, dimensions and the store's own price, from `build-sku-profit-sheet` and `calculate-landed-cost-per-unit`.
3. **TikTok Shop fees for the category**, read by the client or agent from Seller Center on the day (referral fee, any payment or transaction fee). Do not use remembered rates.
4. **Fulfilment**: who ships (own warehouse, 3PL, or the platform's fulfilment), cut-off times and carrier.
5. **Stock** per product and reorder lead time.

Optional: the store's best ads and creator videos; past TikTok ads data; Shopify order export to pick hero products.

Access: draft (level 2) for listings and settings saved unpublished where Seller Center allows; going live, accepting terms, sending samples and messaging creators each need the client's yes.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Market open.** TikTok Shop runs in its listed markets only; confirm the store's country and entity are eligible in Seller Center. Fail: stop.
2. **Profit after TikTok costs.** For each product: price minus landed cost, shipping, TikTok fees, affiliate commission, payment costs, returns, and sample cost spread over expected orders. Gross margin must use the full delivered cost, including marketplace fees [MEA-0037]. Fail: drop the product or raise price on TikTok only if price rules allow.
3. **Stock and restock.** Hero products need stock for a spike. A sell-out is fine only if restock takes about two weeks or less [ORG-0038]. Fail: list a product with deeper stock first.
4. **Shipping promise you can keep.** Seller Center sets handling-time and late-shipment rules; read them on the day and confirm the warehouse can meet them for 95% or more of orders (practitioner default). Fail: use platform fulfilment or delay launch.
5. **Claims and category rules.** Restricted categories (supplements, skincare claims, devices) need approval or have limits. Fail: get approval first or pick another hero.

## Steps

### 1. Pick the first products (3 to 10)

Score each candidate:

- Shows its benefit on camera in seconds (demo, reaction, before and after). Reaction and demo formats travel far on TikTok Shop but bring many non-buyers, so judge on sales per 1,000 views [MCR-0045] [MCR-0046].
- Price fits impulse buying on TikTok (practitioner observation: lower-priced items convert faster in-app; test it).
- Profit after all TikTok costs from gate 2 stays positive with commission at 15 to 20%.
- Ships cheaply and safely; heavy, fragile or bulky items lose money [PRD-0013].

Add one bundle or multi-pack per hero to lift order value, because TikTok Shop buyers can start with low order values and repeat less than full-price site buyers [CXP-0016].

### 2. Seller account and settings checklist

Write each item with its value for approval:

1. Account type and legal entity (client does the sign-up and verification).
2. Warehouse and return addresses.
3. Shipping templates: carrier, rates or free shipping line, handling time.
4. Return and refund policy matching the store's own policy where TikTok allows.
5. Payout bank account (client enters it; never typed by the agent).
6. Catalog sync: TikTok's Shopify app or the channel's sync, or manual listing; check that price and stock sync both ways.
7. Customer service hours and response owner; Seller Center tracks reply times.
8. Shop's TikTok account linked; display name and bio match the brand.

### 3. Listings

Draft titles, images, videos and prices with `optimize-tiktok-shop-listings`. Price must not be undercut by the store's own site in a way that breaks TikTok's pricing rules; read them on the day.

### 4. Sample budget

- Units for samples = number of creators to approve in the first 30 days x 1 to 2.
- Cost = units x (landed cost + shipping).
- Rule: approve sample requests from creators whose content fits and who have sold in the category; one agency targets 2 to 3 videos per sample sent [ORG-0009].

### 5. Line up the first creators

Build a list of 20 to 50 creators with `tiktok-shop-creator-affiliate-outreach`: open collaboration at a standard rate for all products, targeted invites at a higher rate for the best-fit 20. For the first 5 to 10 high-fit micro creators, consider a monthly floor or commission, whichever is higher, for a daily video [ORG-0036]. All creator posts carry TikTok's commercial content setting and plain disclosure [ORG-6405].

### 6. The shop's own content

Post 1 to 3 videos a day from the shop account for the first 30 days: product demos, creator reposts with permission, answers to comments. Reuse the store's winning ads re-cut for in-app shopping.

### 7. Paid support (after organic proof)

When 3 or more videos have sold at a profit organically, hand off to `tiktok-shop-gmv-max-ads`. Set the return target above TikTok's suggestion and avoid big promotion-day budget boosts until results are read [ADC-0062].

### 8. Day-30 readout

Per product: orders, net revenue, refunds, profit after all TikTok costs, samples sent, videos made, sales per 1,000 views, top 5 creators. Decide: scale, fix (listing, price, creators) or pull.

### 9. India and US notes

- **India:** TikTok is blocked in India, so an Indian brand can only use TikTok Shop through an eligible entity and warehouse in a market where it runs. For Indian buyers, use Instagram, YouTube and marketplaces instead.
- **US:** marketplace sales can fall under state marketplace facilitator rules for sales tax; confirm with the accountant who collects tax on TikTok Shop orders and how they show in the store's filings, and keep TikTok payouts as their own line in the monthly P&L.

## Checks before you hand it over

- Every product listed has a profit figure after TikTok fees, commission, samples and returns, using fees read on the day.
- Stock covers a spike and restock time is known.
- Shipping settings match what the warehouse can do.
- Restricted category approvals are done or the product is held.
- The first creator list has rates, sample rules and disclosure terms.
- Account sign-up, bank details and documents were handled by the client only.
- Nothing went live and no samples or messages were sent without the client's yes.

## Output

**One-screen summary:** market, products to launch and their profit per order after TikTok costs, sample budget, number of creators lined up, launch date, and what day 30 will decide.

**Profit sheet:** Product, TikTok price, Landed cost, Shipping, Referral fee (read on date), Other fees, Commission %, Sample cost per order, Return allowance, Profit per order, Bundle option.

**Settings checklist:** Item, Value proposed, Owner, Approved (Y/N, date).

**Creator line-up:** Creator, Followers, Category GMV or past sales if shown, Collaboration type, Commission, Sample (Y/N), Status.

**30-day calendar:** Day, Shop posts, Outreach batch, Samples to approve, Check.

**Day-30 readout:** the fields in step 8 per product, with the call.

## Common mistakes

- **Using last year's fee table.** Gate 2 reads fees in Seller Center on the day.
- **Launching with thin stock.** Gate 3 checks spike stock and restock time.
- **Products that do not demo.** Step 1 scores camera-readiness first.
- **Free samples to anyone.** Step 4 approves only fitting creators with sales history.
- **Judging videos by views.** Step 1 and the readout use sales per 1,000 views.
- **Ads before organic proof.** Step 7 waits for profitable organic sales.
