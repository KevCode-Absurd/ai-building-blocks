# TikTok Shop listing optimisation

**You get:** an upload sheet with new titles, image order, video, description, variants and prices for each listing, a before and after view, a profit check per price, and a creator-appeal checklist with the changes that make affiliates choose the product.

## When to use

- Listings get views from search or videos but convert poorly.
- Creators browse the affiliate center and pick rivals' products.
- The store is about to push a product with ads or a live show.
- Listings were synced from Shopify and never adapted for TikTok.

## Not for

- Titles for the store, Google Shopping, Amazon and Indian marketplaces: use `write-product-titles-for-store-and-marketplaces`.
- Setting commission and samples in depth: use `tiktok-shop-creator-affiliate-outreach`.
- Opening the shop: use `launch-tiktok-shop`.
- Full product photo shoots: use `art-direct-product-photoshoot`.

## What you need

Required:

1. **Current listings:** export or screenshots of title, images, video, description, variants and price per product.
2. **Listing performance** from Seller Center for the last 30 days: product page views, orders, conversion rate, search terms where shown, refunds and return reasons.
3. **Profit per order after TikTok costs**, before commission, from `launch-tiktok-shop`'s profit sheet.
4. **TikTok's listing rules on the day**: title length, banned words, image size and background rules, category attributes, pricing rules. Read them in Seller Center; do not rely on old guides.

Optional: rival listings in the same category; the store's top creator videos; reviews export.

Access: read (level 1) to audit; draft (level 2) to prepare edits; publishing changes needs the client's yes, and price changes need a separate yes.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Profit per order known.** Fail: compute it before touching price. Gross margin uses the full delivered cost, including marketplace fees [MEA-0037].
2. **Rules read on the day.** Title limits, image rules and price rules noted with the date. Fail: stop; a rejected or suppressed listing costs more than a slow one.
3. **Claims list.** Only approved claims go in titles and descriptions. Fail: run `check-ad-copy-for-policy-and-claims`.
4. **Enough data to judge.** Practitioner default: at least 500 product page views in 30 days before reading conversion. Fail: fix obvious issues only and wait.

## Steps

### 1. Diagnose each listing

| Symptom | Likely fix |
|---|---|
| Few page views from search | Title and category attributes |
| Many views, low add to cart | Images, video, price, proof |
| Add to cart but low orders | Shipping cost or time, variants confusing |
| High refunds | Description and images promise more than the product does |
| Creators ignore it | Commission, samples, demo clips, ratings |

Fix the biggest gap first; change one area per listing per week so the result can be read.

### 2. Rewrite the title

Order: what it is, the key attribute buyers search (size, count, material, flavour), who or what it is for, then pack size. Use the words buyers type, taken from TikTok's search suggestions and the search terms report. No promo words, prices, emojis or claims the rules ban, and stay under the limit read in gate 2. Write two versions; keep the one that reads naturally out loud.

### 3. Order the images

1. Main image: the product clearly, as the rules require.
2. In use, in the buyer's setting.
3. The key benefit shown, with a short label.
4. Size or scale (in hand, next to a common object).
5. What is in the box or the bundle.
6. Proof: rating and review count, or a real customer photo with permission. Two kinds of proof help more than one [LPG-0036]; never invented reviews [MCR-6045].

### 4. Add a product video

15 to 30 seconds, vertical, the product working in the first 3 seconds, no long logo intro. Reuse the store's best-selling creator clip if the contract allows reuse on listings. A product whose effect shows instantly can use a real reaction; slow-benefit products need a clear demo instead [MCR-0045].

### 5. Write the description

Five short blocks: what it does, who it is for, what is included, how to use it, care or sizing. Answer the top 3 questions from reviews and comments. Match return reasons: if buyers return for size, put the size chart first.

### 6. Set variants and bundles

Cut variants nobody buys. Add a multi-pack or bundle under the main option, with the saving in money in the option name ("3-pack, save $10") [LPG-0008]. For items people own several of, a 3-pack has added large monthly revenue in one brand's tests [OFF-0053]. A bundle also lifts order value, which matters because low-value TikTok Shop buyers tend to repeat less [CXP-0016].

### 7. Check the price

For each price: profit per order after fees, commission at the open rate, samples spread over orders, shipping and returns. If the price is under the floor, raise it, bundle, or drop the product from TikTok. Do not run two prices for the same product as a test; test bundles and framing instead [CRO-0009]. Keep the price consistent with the store's own site where TikTok's pricing rules require it.

### 8. Make creators pick it

In the affiliate center, creators compare commission, samples, ratings and sales. Check:

- Commission at or above category norms you can afford.
- Sample terms clear (free for proven creators).
- Rating and review count visible; fix the product issues behind low ratings first.
- 3 demo clips and hooks in the brief so creators know how to film it.
- Sales per 1,000 views of your top videos, shared with creators as the selling point; it is the number that predicts their earnings, and it varies more than 100 times between videos [MCR-0046].

### 9. India and US notes

- **India:** TikTok is not available in India; apply steps 2 to 7 to Instagram Shopping, Meesho, Flipkart and Amazon.in with `write-product-titles-for-store-and-marketplaces`.
- **US:** show prices in dollars, list sizes in US units first, and keep any "Made in" or material claims backed by documents.

## Checks before you hand it over

- Every title fits the length and wording rules read on the day.
- Every price clears the profit floor after fees, commission and samples.
- Image 6 uses only real, permitted proof.
- The video shows the product working in the first 3 seconds.
- Each listing changes one main area this week, with the date logged.
- No price test runs two prices for the same product.
- Nothing was published and no price changed without the client's yes.

## Common mistakes

- **Copying the Shopify title.** Step 2 rewrites it around TikTok search words.
- **Changing everything at once.** Step 1 changes one area per week.
- **Price under the floor.** Step 7 checks profit after commission and samples.
- **Fake proof.** Step 3 uses only real, permitted reviews.
- **Ignoring creators.** Step 8 treats affiliates as the main traffic source.
- **Testing two prices.** Step 7 tests bundles and framing instead.
