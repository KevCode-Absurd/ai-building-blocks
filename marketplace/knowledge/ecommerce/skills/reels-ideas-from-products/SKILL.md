# Reel ideas from your products

**You get:** a sheet of twenty Reel ideas, scored and ranked, each tied to one product with its hook, first frame, shot list, audio, caption opener and proof source, ready for a phone shoot and for the content calendar.

## When to use

- The team keeps posting the same product photo and needs new angles.
- A shoot day is booked and the creator needs a list they can film in a few hours.
- Reels get most views from existing followers and the hooks need to work on strangers.
- A new product launches and needs ten or more ways to show it.

## Not for

- Paid ad scripts with offers and claims for ads: use `write-tiktok-ad-scripts` or `product-demo-in-15-seconds`.
- A dated month of posts across all formats: use `plan-30-day-instagram-calendar` after this.
- Lighting, sound and b-roll setup for the shoot: use `plan-ugc-shoot-at-home`.
- Editing raw footage into cuts: use `short-video-edit-pipeline`.

## What you need

Required (read access, level 1):

1. **Product list** with gross profit per order and stock, from `build-sku-profit-sheet` or the Shopify export. Pick 4 to 8 products.
2. **Product pages and photos**: what each does, how it is used, sizes, materials.
3. **Reviews and questions**: the top 50 reviews (Shopify review app export or marketplace reviews) and the 20 most common pre-sale questions from DMs or support.
4. **Instagram Insights** for the last 90 days of Reels: views, non-follower share, average watch time, shares.

Optional: 5 to 10 category creators or competitors whose Reels do well, the store's existing video files, any audio the brand owns.

If reviews are thin (new store), use product questions and the product page, and mark proof as "to collect". This skill only drafts ideas, so level 1 is enough. Any filming of customers needs their consent, and posting needs the client's yes.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Gross profit per order** for each product is known, or the client confirms which products are worth pushing. Fail: work only on those and note it.
2. **Stock.** Products low on stock get at most two ideas. Fail: drop them.
3. **Claims.** Note any claim a Reel would make (results, health, durability). Each must have a source the store holds. Fail: reframe as a demo or drop it; health and before-and-after claims go through `check-ad-copy-for-policy-and-claims`.
4. **Audio rights.** Business accounts may see a limited music library. Use only audio the platform offers to the account, the brand's own voice, or licensed tracks. Fail: plan the Reel with voice or text instead.

## Steps

### 1. Pick products

Rank by gross profit per order, then by whether the benefit can be seen on camera in under 3 seconds. Take 4 to 8 products. Give the top 2 products five ideas each and spread the rest.

### 2. Mine each product for seeds

For each product, fill six seed lines from the inputs:

- **Visible change**: what looks different before and after use.
- **Mistake**: what buyers get wrong (from questions and 3-star reviews).
- **Objection**: the doubt that stops a purchase (size, price, will it last).
- **Ritual**: when and where it is used, in the buyer's own words.
- **Making or packing**: anything about how it is made or packed that is true and unusual.
- **Comparison**: the old way or the cheaper option it replaces.

Use buyers' exact words where possible. A seed with no source is dropped.

### 3. Check what already works in the category

Look at the top Reels of 5 to 10 category accounts. Drop high-view videos with engagement under about 2%, since those are likely paid boosts, and look at each creator's top 10 for the natural break in views [ORG-0010]. Note the structure of the winners, not their content. Never copy a creator's script or footage.

### 4. Turn seeds into ideas

Match each seed to one idea type:

| Idea type | Best seed | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Demo | Visible change | Product does the job in the first 2 seconds |
| Myth or mistake | Mistake | "Stop doing X" then the right way with the product |
| Ranking | Comparison | 3 options ranked, product last with the reason |
| Point of view | Ritual | Buyer's moment of use, filmed as they see it |
| Process | Making or packing | Packing an order, testing quality, making a batch |
| Objection answer | Objection | A real question shown on screen, answered on camera |
| Series episode | Any | Numbered episode that shows the product every time [ORG-0018] |
| Repeat visual | Visible change | One recurring visual test the account can repeat [ORG-0012] |

Aim for at least five idea types across the twenty, so the test teaches something.

### 5. Write the hooks

Treat each hook as three levers: spoken audio, on-screen text and the visual [MCR-0028].

- On-screen text of 5 to 8 words at a simple reading level; past 12 words performance drops [ORG-0016].
- The first frame shows the product in use or the result, not a logo or a slow pan [ORG-0017].
- Write for a stranger who has never heard of the store; if past Reels show 70 to 80% of views from followers, the hooks were written for insiders [ORG-0029].
- For the top 5 ideas, write 4 alternative openers each; the first 3 seconds decide whether people stop, and taste is not a reliable judge [MCR-0076].

### 6. Write shot notes

Three to six shots per idea, each one line: framing (close, hand, face, wide), action, and duration. Everything must be filmable on a phone in natural light. Add on-screen text per shot so the Reel works with the sound off.

### 7. Choose audio

Pick one: trending audio available to the account (good for reach, check the account can use it), original voiceover (good for demos and objection answers), or text with quiet background sound. Note the choice per idea. If any visual is AI-made, follow Meta's AI labelling and never show an AI person as a real customer [MCR-6063].

### 8. Score and rank

Score each idea 1 to 5 on four lines and add them up:

- **Profit**: gross profit per order of the product.
- **Ease**: hours to shoot and edit with the team's kit.
- **Proof**: how directly a stranger sees the benefit.
- **New**: how different it is from the account's last 30 posts.

Keep the top twenty. Make sure no single product holds more than five.

### 9. Set the test rule

For each idea, name the metric: 3-second hold and shares for reach ideas, comments and profile visits for selling ideas [ORG-0019]. An organic Reel with about 5 times the account's usual views is a candidate for ads, run as-is or with its hook on a product ending [ORG-0035]; hand those to `repurpose-ads-into-organic-posts` or the ad team.

### 10. India and US notes

- **India:** write a Hinglish or regional-language text hook for ideas aimed at a state where the product sells well; keep the price on screen in rupees and exact. Show COD or free delivery only if the store offers it. Festival and wedding-season gifting ideas work well 2 to 3 weeks before the date (practitioner default, test it).
- **US:** keep text hooks in plain American English; any gifted creator in the Reel needs a clear in-video disclosure [ORG-6405].

## Checks before you hand it over

- Exactly twenty ideas, each tied to one product that is in stock.
- Every idea has an on-screen hook of 12 words or fewer, a first frame showing the product or result, and 3 to 6 shot lines.
- Every claim has a named source (review, test, spec); none is invented.
- At least five idea types are used and no product has more than five ideas.
- Audio is something the account may use, or the idea works without music.
- The top 5 ideas carry 4 openers each.
- Each idea names the metric that will judge it.

## Output

1. **One-screen summary:** the products chosen and why, the five best ideas with their hooks, the shoot time needed, and the test rule.
2. **Ideas sheet** (`reel-ideas-<date>.csv`), one row per idea:

| Rank | Product | Gross profit per order | Idea type | Working title | On-screen hook | Spoken first line | First frame | Shots (3 to 6) | On-screen text per shot | Length (s) | Audio | Caption first line | Call to action | Proof source | Score (profit, ease, proof, new) | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

3. **Opener sheet** for the top 5: Idea, Opener 1 to 4 (text and visual).
4. **Shoot order**: ideas grouped by location and product so the shoot runs in one session.

## Common mistakes

- **Ideas about the brand, not the product in use.** Step 4 starts every idea from a product seed.
- **Long, clever hooks.** Step 5 holds text to 5 to 8 words.
- **Copying a viral Reel.** Step 3 takes structure only.
- **Unprovable claims in a casual video.** The claims gate applies to Reels as much as ads.
- **Music the account cannot use.** Step 7 checks audio first.
- **Twenty ideas for the bestseller.** Step 8 caps each product at five.

## Sources

- ORG-0010: drop low-engagement high-view videos when mining hooks.
- ORG-0012: a repeat visual vehicle for low-interest products.
- ORG-0016: on-screen hooks of 5 to 8 words.
- ORG-0017: first frame shows the topic being done.
- ORG-0018: numbered series that shows the product.
- ORG-0019: judge by goal: shares or comments.
- ORG-0029: follower-heavy views mean hooks fail strangers.
- ORG-0035: an organic post at about 5x usual views belongs in ads.
- ORG-6405: in-video disclosure for gifted creators.
- MCR-0028: hooks have three levers.
- MCR-0076: several openers per video because the first 3 seconds decide.
- MCR-6063: Meta's AI info label.

What makes this skill ours: every idea starts from a sourced product seed (reviews, questions, specs) instead of a trend list; ideas are scored on gross profit, ease and proof before anyone shoots; audio rights and claims are checked before the shoot; and winning Reels have a clear path into ads.
