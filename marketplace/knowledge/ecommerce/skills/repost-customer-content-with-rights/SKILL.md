# Repost customer content with permission

**You get:** a rights register sheet, ready permission messages for four scopes, a weekly find-ask-record routine, and a use checklist that blocks any customer photo or video without a recorded yes, ready for the social and ad teams once the client approves the wording.

## When to use

- Customers tag the store in photos and videos and the team wants to reshare them.
- The store is building a customer gallery for product pages or email.
- The ad team wants to use customer clips or photos in paid ads.
- Content has been reshared in the past without asking, and the store needs to clean it up.

## Not for

- Collecting new testimonial videos by asking customers to film: use `customer-testimonial-videos`.
- Review screenshot ads built from verified reviews: use `review-screenshot-ads`.
- Contracts and usage rights for paid creators: use `influencer-contract-and-usage-rights`.
- Asking for reviews and photos after delivery: use `build-review-collection-system`.

## What you need

Required (read access, level 1):

1. **Where customer content appears**: tagged posts and mentions on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok; branded hashtag; photo reviews in the review app; photos sent by DM, email or WhatsApp.
2. **Shopify order export** with Customer ID, email or phone and order date, to confirm a poster is a buyer. Read only; never share it.
3. **Planned uses**: which channels the store wants to use content on (organic social, website, email, paid ads, packaging).
4. **Incentive policy**: whether the store gives anything for permission (credit, product, feature), approved by the client.

Optional: any existing permission records, the store's review app terms, a branded hashtag.

If no records exist, treat every past repost as unpermitted until asked. Sending permission requests from the brand's accounts needs draft access (level 2) for drafts and the client's yes on the message wording before the first send. Reposting needs publish access (level 3) within the register's rules.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Ownership.** The person who took a photo or video owns it; being tagged does not give the store rights, and the platform's licence covers the platform, not the brand. Pass only with the owner's recorded yes. Fail: do not use it. No legal ruling here; for disputes, the client asks a lawyer.
2. **Real buyer and real experience.** Content used as proof must come from a real customer and show their honest experience; reviews and testimonials by people who do not exist, including AI-made ones, or that misrepresent the experience are banned in the US [MCR-6045].
3. **Other people in the shot.** Faces of others, especially children, need the consent of those people or a parent. Fail: crop, blur or skip.
4. **Incentive disclosure.** If anything of value is given for the content or permission, say so wherever it is used [MCR-6025]; in India this is a material connection that needs an ASCI label [ORG-6406].
5. **Personal data.** Store only what the register needs. In India the Digital Personal Data Protection Act expects clear consent and purpose; in the US follow the store's privacy policy. No legal ruling here.

## Steps

### 1. Find (weekly, 20 minutes)

Check tagged posts, mentions, the branded hashtag, photo reviews and media sent in DMs or WhatsApp. Shortlist content that is sharp, shows the product clearly, and shows real use. A photo sent to support about a broken product is not permission to post it.

### 2. Screen

For each item: is the poster a buyer (match handle or name to an order where possible, without exposing the order)? Does it show the current product? Any claims (health, results) that the store cannot back? Any minors or other people? Any music or other brands' logos? Anything unflattering to the poster? Drop or fix before asking.

### 3. Ask with a clear scope

Ask in the same channel the content was posted, privately by DM first. Say exactly what, where, how long and with what credit, and that no is fine. Four ready messages:

- **Organic repost only:** "Hi [name], we love this photo of [product]! Could we share it on our Instagram and Facebook, with credit to @[handle]? Reply YES if that's okay, or no worries if not."
- **Organic plus website and email:** adds "and in a customer gallery on our website and in our emails, for up to [12 months]".
- **Paid ads:** a separate ask, never bundled: "We'd also like to use it in paid ads on Meta and TikTok for [6 months], credited to you. We can [offer, if any]. Is that okay?"
- **Partnership ad from their handle:** use Meta's partnership ad permission so the customer approves in the app; once accepted, the brand can run ads from their handle and they can revoke it later [ADC-6081].

A public comment "reply #yes[brand]" only counts if the message states the scope and the person replies themselves; a DM reply with a screenshot is safer. Write the ask in the language the customer posted in.

### 4. Record the yes

Log in the register the same day: asset ID, link, owner handle, date asked, exact ask text, exact reply text, screenshot file, scope (organic, website, email, ads, partnership), term and expiry date, credit required, edit allowed (crop, captions, music), incentive given, other people's consent, status. No register row means no use.

### 5. Use within scope

- **Organic repost:** credit the owner as agreed; never edit their words or change the meaning. If an incentive was given, the post carries a plain label [ORG-6405], or an ASCI label in India [ORG-6406].
- **Website and email:** show it as a customer photo, not as a staged shoot.
- **Paid ads:** only with the paid-ads scope. A disclosure stays with the content when an incentive was given [MCR-6025]. An organic customer post with about 5 times the account's usual views is worth testing in ads, once rights cover it [ORG-0035].
- **AI edits:** do not change what the customer's product or result looks like; any AI edit beyond basic cropping and colour follows Meta's AI labelling [MCR-6063] and the store's accuracy rules in `keep-product-accurate-in-ai-media`.

### 6. Expire and remove

Each month, filter the register for expiry dates in the next 30 days. Remove content from ads and the website on expiry, or ask to renew. If a customer asks for removal at any time, remove it within the time set in the rule card (practitioner default: 2 working days) and log it.

### 7. Clean up the past

List content reshared before this system. Ask for permission now using the same messages; remove any that get a no or no reply within 14 days (practitioner default, test it).

### 8. India and US notes

- **India:** many buyers send photos on WhatsApp; ask there with the same scope wording and record the reply as a screenshot. Free product or store credit for a post makes it an ad under ASCI; use a permitted label such as "Free gift" or "Collaboration" [ORG-6406]. Keep regional-language captions as the customer wrote them.
- **US:** paying or gifting for content requires disclosure in the post itself, not just a platform tag [ORG-6405]. Fake or incentivised-for-positive reviews are banned under the FTC's rule [MCR-6045].

## Checks before you hand it over

- The register has every field from step 4 and every item in use has a row.
- Every ask states channels, term and credit, and paid ads are asked separately.
- No item in use lacks a recorded yes, a screenshot or a scope covering that use.
- Items with incentives carry the right disclosure for each market.
- Other people and minors in shots are consented, cropped or excluded.
- Expiry dates exist and a monthly expiry check is scheduled.
- Past reshares are listed with an ask-or-remove status.
- The client approved the message wording before the first send.

## Output

1. **One-screen summary:** how many items are in use, how many have full rights, how many need asking or removal, the scopes the store asks for, and the weekly routine owner.
2. **Rights register** (`ugc-rights-register.csv`):

| Asset ID | Link | Owner handle | Buyer confirmed | Date asked | Ask text | Reply text | Screenshot | Scope | Term | Expiry | Credit | Edits allowed | Incentive | Others' consent | Used where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

3. **Permission messages**: the four filled messages from step 3, plus a renewal message and a removal confirmation, in each language the store needs.
4. **Use checklist** (one line per channel): Organic, Website, Email, Paid ads, Partnership ads, with the scope and disclosure each needs.
5. **Clean-up list**: Past item, Where used, Asked on, Reply, Action (keep or remove), Done.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating a tag as permission.** Gate 1 needs a recorded yes from the owner.
- **One vague yes used for everything.** Step 3 states scope and asks for ads separately.
- **No record of the yes.** Step 4 logs exact text and a screenshot the same day.
- **Hidden incentives.** Step 5 labels any content tied to something of value.
- **Content that outlives its term.** Step 6 checks expiries monthly.
- **Editing a customer's words or result.** Step 5 keeps meaning and appearance intact.
