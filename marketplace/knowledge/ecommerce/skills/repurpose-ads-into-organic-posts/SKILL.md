# Repurpose ads into organic posts

**You get:** a one-hour weekly routine with a winners sheet, a rights check, and three ready organic posts per winning ad (Reel recut, carousel, story set) with captions, plus a return list of organic winners for the ad team, ready for the client to approve and post.

## When to use

- The ad account has proven hooks and angles that the organic feed has never used.
- Social posting stalls for lack of material while ad footage and alternate cuts sit unused.
- The ad and social teams work apart and want one shared weekly hand-off.
- A launch ad is working and the store wants the same idea in front of followers in a native form.

## Not for

- Refreshing a tired ad for paid use: use `refresh-a-tired-winning-ad`.
- Resizing an ad for paid placements: use `resize-ads-for-every-placement`.
- Deciding whether an ad test has a winner: use `call-creative-test-winners` first.
- Making new ads from the store's best-selling products: use `new-ads-from-store-winners`.

## What you need

Required (read access, level 1):

1. **Meta Ads Manager export, last 7 and 28 days, ad level**: ad name, spend, impressions, 3-second video plays, ThruPlays or average watch time, link clicks, purchases, cost per purchase, purchase value. TikTok Ads export works the same way if the store runs TikTok.
2. **Gross profit per order** by product, from `build-sku-profit-sheet`, to compute the break-even cost per purchase.
3. **Asset library**: raw footage, project files and alternate cuts for the winning ads.
4. **Rights record** for every creator or customer in the ads: what use was granted and until when.
5. **Instagram Insights, last 30 days**: views, non-follower share and shares per post, for the return list.

Optional: TikTok or YouTube Shorts analytics, the content calendar, brand voice samples.

If the rights record is missing for an asset, it is blocked until the client confirms the grant. Drafting posts needs draft access (level 2); publishing needs the client's yes on each post.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Gross profit per order is known**, so a "winner" means a profitable ad, not just high ROAS. Fail: compute break-even cost per purchase in `build-sku-profit-sheet` first.
2. **Organic rights.** A creator's grant for paid ads may not cover posting on the brand's own feed, and the reverse. Pass only when organic use is covered in writing. Fail: ask through `influencer-contract-and-usage-rights` or use only brand-made footage.
3. **Claims.** Ad copy went through review for ads; organic captions need the same truth. Fail: remove any claim without a source.
4. **Offer.** Any price or offer in the ad must still be live. Fail: cut it from the organic version.
5. **Disclosure.** Creator content with a paid or gifted relationship keeps a clear label in the organic post too [ORG-6405], using the label each market expects (for example "ad" in the UK).

## Steps

### 1. Monday: pull the winners (15 minutes)

From the export, keep ads that pass both lines:

- **Profit line:** cost per purchase below gross profit per order for the product, over at least 7 days.
- **Attention line:** 3-second plays divided by impressions above the account's median.

Take the top 3. Note the hook, angle, product and format of each. Skip near-duplicates of the same ad; Meta groups very similar ads together, so they show one idea, not three [MCR-0078].

### 2. Check rights and claims (10 minutes)

For each winner, fill: who appears, who made it, grant for organic (yes, no, unknown), expiry, claims in the ad and their source, offer still live. Any "no" or "unknown" on rights blocks that asset.

### 3. Strip the ad parts

Remove: price slashes and countdowns, "Shop now" end cards, ad-only overlays, and direct-response calls to action in the first seconds. Organic posts work best as pure value, with the ask carried by ads and stories [ORG-0003]. Keep: the hook idea, the demonstration, the proof, the buyer's words.

### 4. Rebuild as three native posts

For each winner make:

| Post | How | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Reel recut | Same hook idea with a native opener (face, hands, product in use in frame 1); on-screen text of 5 to 8 words [ORG-0016]; add a story or a "why we made this" beat; trending or original audio the account can use | Reach: shares and 3-second hold |
| Carousel | Hook as slide 1, the 3 to 5 proof points or steps as slides, last slide a save prompt | Saves and shares |
| Story set | 3 to 5 frames: question poll, demo clip, review screenshot (real, with permission), link sticker or keyword | Selling: replies and link taps [ORG-0019] |

Re-post a proven story more than once in new formats: a new hook, voiceover or text carousel each time [ORG-0026]. Post natively in the app; one practitioner programme found schedulers cut reach, so test it before trusting a scheduler [ORG-0013].

### 5. Write give-first captions

First line restates the hook for readers. Then 2 to 4 short lines that teach, show or tell the story. No price unless it is a selling story. Disclosure goes first when a creator was paid or gifted. Match the comment language of the audience (the languages your buyers use) and keep any price exact in the store's currency.

### 6. Slot into the calendar

Put the Reel and carousel in reach slots in `plan-30-day-instagram-calendar`, at least 3 days apart, and the story set in the nearest selling slot. Never post three versions of one ad on the same day.

### 7. Friday: read results and send back (15 minutes)

At 7 days, record views, non-follower share, shares, saves, story replies and link taps for each post. Build the return list for the ad team: any organic post with about 5 times the account's usual views is a strong ad candidate, run as-is through ad code or with its hook on a product ending [ORG-0035]. Also list hooks that did well organically but have not been tested in ads.

### 8. Keep the log

One row per winner: ad name, posts made, results, rights status, sent back to ads (yes or no). After 4 weeks, note which angles win in both paid and organic; those are the brand's strongest messages.

### 9. AI and market notes

- If any visual is AI-made or AI-edited, follow Meta's AI labelling and never present an AI person as a real customer [MCR-6063].
- **US, UK, EU and beyond:** a gifted creator's clip in an organic post keeps an "ad" or "sponsored" label in the video itself, not only in the caption [ORG-6405].
- **Every market:** ad copy in other languages may have been written for paid placements with tight claims; re-check it before organic use.

## Checks before you hand it over

- Each winner passes both the profit line and the attention line.
- Every asset used has organic rights confirmed in writing, with expiry noted.
- No price, offer or countdown appears unless it is live and in a selling story.
- Each winner has three posts (Reel recut, carousel, story set) with captions.
- Reel text hooks are 12 words or fewer and frame 1 shows the product or result.
- Disclosure lines are present wherever a creator was paid or gifted.
- The return list names posts at about 5x usual views.
- Nothing is posted without the client's yes.

## Output

1. **One-screen weekly summary:** this week's 3 winners, posts made, rights blocked, last week's organic results, and the return list for ads.
2. **Winners sheet**: Ad name, Product, Gross profit per order, Cost per purchase, 3-second rate, Account median, Hook, Angle, Rights for organic, Expiry, Claims and source, Offer live.
3. **Post pack per winner**: Reel recut (opener, on-screen text, shots, audio, caption), Carousel (slide 1 to N text, caption), Story set (frame 1 to 5 content, sticker).
4. **Results and return list**: Post, Date, Views, Non-follower share, Shares, Saves, Replies, Link taps, Views vs usual, Send to ads (yes or no), Note.
5. **Routine card**: the Monday and Friday steps with times and owners.

## Common mistakes

- **Posting the ad as-is.** Step 3 strips ad parts and step 4 rebuilds natively.
- **Picking winners by ROAS alone.** Step 1 uses cost per purchase against gross profit per order.
- **Assuming ad rights cover organic.** The rights gate checks each asset.
- **Stale offers in evergreen posts.** Step 3 removes them.
- **Three cuts of one ad in one day.** Step 6 spaces them.
- **A one-way pipe.** Step 7 sends organic winners back to ads.
