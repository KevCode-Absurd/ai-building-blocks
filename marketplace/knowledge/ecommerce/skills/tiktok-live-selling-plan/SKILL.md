# TikTok LIVE selling plan

**You get:** a show plan for one live (or a 4-week series): the product and offer table, a minute-by-minute run of show, the full host script, a promotion schedule, a staffing sheet with roles, a tech and stock checklist, and a post-show readout template.

## When to use

- The store has never sold live and wants a first show that does not flop.
- Lives get viewers but few orders.
- A launch, restock or sale should run as a live event.
- A creator or affiliate will host a live for the store.

## Not for

- Setting up the shop and listings: use `launch-tiktok-shop` and `optimize-tiktok-shop-listings`.
- Recruiting creators to host: use `tiktok-shop-creator-affiliate-outreach`.
- Writing short video scripts: use `write-tiktok-ad-scripts`.
- Planning the year's sale dates: use `plan-yearly-sale-calendar`.

## What you need

Required:

1. **Products for the show** (3 to 8) with price, stock and profit per order after TikTok costs, from `launch-tiktok-shop`'s profit sheet.
2. **Live eligibility and tools** read in Seller Center and the TikTok app on the day: who can go live from the shop, pinned products, live-only deals, any LIVE-specific rules.
3. **Host**: founder, staff member or creator; their on-camera comfort.
4. **Date and time** when the store's buyers are on (from TikTok analytics or past order times).

Optional: past live data (viewers, peak concurrent, orders); top-selling videos to reuse as hooks.

Access: draft (level 2) to set up products and deals unpublished; scheduling the live, publishing deals and going live need the client's yes.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Every live offer keeps a profit.** Work out profit per order at the live price, after fees, commission (if a creator hosts) and shipping. Fail: change the offer.
2. **Stock for the show.** Each featured product has stock for a strong show plus normal orders for a week. Fail: feature fewer products.
3. **Real urgency only.** A "live-only" price must be live-only and end when stated; false urgency and fake low-stock claims are misleading [ADC-6147]. Fail: rewrite.
4. **Disclosure.** A paid or commissioned host says so: announce it at the start and end, add it to the replay caption [ORG-6420], and repeat it during the show for late joiners [ORG-6405]. Fail: add the lines to the script.
5. **Fulfilment ready.** The warehouse knows the date and can ship the spike on time.

## Steps

### 1. Choose the show format

| Format | Length | When |
|---|---|---|
| Short frequent shows | 15 to 30 minutes, several a week | New shop, small following |
| Event show | 60 to 120 minutes, promoted | Launch, restock, sale |

New sellers do better with many short shows than with one long promoted show nobody comes to; one live-selling source saw four 15-minute shows beat one 1-hour show [ORG-0005]. Build to event shows once lives draw a steady crowd.

### 2. Pick products and offers

- **Hero:** the best seller, at the live offer.
- **Anchor:** one well-known, high-value item at a strong live price to pull people in; this works only when enough people watch at once (one source set the line at 300 to 500 concurrent viewers) [ORG-0007].
- **Add-ons:** 2 to 4 cheap items that ride along.
- **Bundle:** hero plus add-on at a set saving, shown in money.
- **Giveaway (optional):** for buyers only, if the platform and local rules allow. Break-even: prize cost / number of orders expected = the lift per order needed [ORG-0006].

### 3. Promote the show

Schedule 1 to 3 weeks ahead and promote three times: about a week before, 2 to 3 days before, and hours before; name the products and the offer [ORG-0008]. Use the shop's short videos, Stories on other platforms, email and SMS (with the client's approval of each send).

### 4. Staff the show

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Host | Talks, demos, sells |
| Producer | Runs the run of show, pins products, times segments |
| Moderator | Answers comments, flags questions to the host, removes spam |
| Ops | Watches stock and orders, warns before sell-outs |

A first short show can run with two people (host plus one doing the other three roles).

### 5. Write the run of show (60-minute example)

| Minute | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | Hello, who we are, disclosure if hosted for pay, what is on offer today, why now |
| 3 to 12 | Hero demo: problem, product in use, close-up details, price and live offer, pin |
| 12 to 15 | Recap for new joiners |
| 15 to 25 | Proof: real reviews read out with names as shown on the site, questions from comments |
| 25 to 35 | Anchor reveal, offer, pin |
| 35 to 38 | Recap |
| 38 to 50 | Add-ons and bundle, live Q and A |
| 50 to 55 | Last call on stated end time, stock left (true numbers only) |
| 55 to 60 | Thanks, next show date, disclosure again |

Recap every 10 to 15 minutes: viewers come and go, so each recap repeats who you are, what is on offer and how to buy.

### 6. Write the host script

For each segment, write the actual lines: the opening hook (first 10 seconds), the demo patter, the price line ("Today on this live only, X instead of Y, until 9 pm"), the call to tap the pinned product, three answers to the top questions from reviews, and the disclosure lines. Only claims on the approved list; reviews read out must be real and attributed as on the site.

### 7. Tech and stock checklist

Stable internet, phone on a tripod at eye level, light facing the host, clean background, products laid out in order, the live deals set up and tested, pinned product order set, stock confirmed one hour before.

### 8. Post-show readout

Peak concurrent viewers, average watch time, orders, revenue, profit after costs, conversion per product, top 5 questions asked, what to fix. Clip the best 3 moments into short videos with the host's rights in place.

### 9. India and US notes

- **India:** TikTok is not available in India. Run the same plan on Instagram Live or YouTube live. Instagram lets the brand send a private reply to a comment only during the live broadcast [ORG-6364]. Tagging the store's products in YouTube lives needs the YouTube Partner Program [ORG-6403]. Offer prepaid deals; say plainly whether COD is open for live orders. Host in Hindi or the buyers' regional language where that is how they shop.
- **US:** live giveaways may fall under state sweepstakes rules; keep "no purchase necessary" routes if prizes are big, and check with counsel.

## Checks before you hand it over

- Every live offer shows profit per order at the live price.
- Stock per featured product covers the show plus a week.
- No fake urgency or false stock claims; end times are real.
- Disclosure lines sit at the start, end and in recaps when the host is paid or commissioned.
- The run of show has recaps every 10 to 15 minutes.
- Each staffing role has a named person.
- Nothing was scheduled, published or announced without the client's yes.

## Output

**One-screen summary:** show date, format, host, products and live offers with profit per order, expected peak viewers from past shows (or "first show"), promotion dates and staffing.

**Product and offer table:** Product, Role (hero, anchor, add-on, bundle), Normal price, Live price, Profit per order at live price, Stock, Pin order.

**Run of show:** Minute, Segment, Host lines, Producer action, Product pinned.

**Host script:** full lines per segment, with disclosure lines marked.

**Promotion schedule:** Date, Channel, Message, Approved (Y/N).

**Staffing sheet:** Role, Name, Duties, Backup.

**Readout:** the fields in step 8.

## Common mistakes

- **One long first show.** Step 1 starts with short frequent shows.
- **Anchors with no crowd.** Step 2 uses big-value anchors only when enough people watch at once.
- **No promotion.** Step 3 promotes three times.
- **No recaps.** Step 5 repeats the offer every 10 to 15 minutes.
- **Fake countdowns or stock.** Gate 3 bans them.
- **Disclosure only at the start.** Gate 4 puts it at the start, end and in recaps.
