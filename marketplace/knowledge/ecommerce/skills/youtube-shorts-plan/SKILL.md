# YouTube Shorts plan

**You get:** a 60-day Shorts plan with channel setup done, three formats, a dated list of the first 24 Shorts with hooks and related video links, two to four long product videos that carry the store link, and a results sheet that tracks store visits and orders, ready for the client to approve and publish.

## When to use

- The store already makes vertical video for Instagram or TikTok and wants a second home for it.
- Buyers in the category research on YouTube (how-tos, reviews, comparisons).
- The YouTube channel exists but has random uploads and no route to the store.
- The store wants product videos that keep being found long after posting.

## Not for

- YouTube ads, video campaigns and budgets: use `set-up-youtube-ads-for-store`.
- Raw Reel and Short ideas from the product range: use `reels-ideas-from-products` first.
- Editing raw footage into vertical cuts: use `short-video-edit-pipeline`.
- Cover and thumbnail templates: use `short-video-cover-series`.

## What you need

Required (read access, level 1):

1. **YouTube Studio access** at a read or viewer level, or screenshots of channel settings and analytics if the channel exists.
2. **Product list** with gross profit per order and stock, from `build-sku-profit-sheet`.
3. **Existing vertical videos** (Reels, TikToks) with their results, and the raw files without other apps' watermarks.
4. **Team capacity**: hours a week for filming, editing and uploading.

Optional: Shopify store connected to Google (needed later for YouTube Shopping), the store's UTM rule, top product questions from support.

If the channel is new, plan setup first and set targets after 30 days. Changing channel settings and uploading need the client to grant a Studio role (editor or manager) through YouTube's own permissions; never ask for a password. Publishing each Short needs the client's yes on the batch.

## Before you start: the gates

1. **Gross profit per order** is known for the products you will feature. Fail: compute it first or feature only products the client confirms.
2. **Route to the store works.** Typed URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable [ORG-6402]. Pass when the plan uses the related video link, channel links or product tags. Fail: do not promise clicks from Shorts captions.
3. **Advanced features.** Clickable links in long video descriptions and the related video link need the channel's advanced features access, which YouTube Studio shows under its feature settings [ORG-6402]. Fail: the client enables it first.
4. **Audio rights.** Use YouTube's own Shorts sounds, the YouTube Audio Library, or original voice; Audio Library tracks can be used in monetised videos without claims, and Creative Commons tracks need credit [MCR-6065]. Fail: replace the audio.
5. **Disclosure.** Realistic AI-made people or events need YouTube's synthetic content disclosure [MCR-6064]; any paid or gifted creator needs the in-video label [ORG-6405], and the label local ad rules ask for in other markets (such as "#ad" in the UK).

## Steps

### 1. Set up the channel

Handle and name match the store. Channel description says what the store sells and for whom in two lines. Add the store link first among channel profile links (up to 14 are allowed) [ORG-6402]. Turn on advanced features. Connect the store to YouTube Shopping only if the channel meets the rules: in the YouTube Partner Program, not set as made for kids, and with no hate speech strikes [ORG-6403]. Until then, plan without product tags.

### 2. Build the long videos that carry the link

Make 2 to 4 longer videos (2 to 8 minutes, practitioner default) for the top products by gross profit: a full demo, a how-to, a comparison or a buyer's guide. Each description holds the clickable store link with UTM tags in the first line. In the first 30 seconds of each long video, state the promise, give a reason to trust it, and lay out what the video covers [ORG-0002]. These are the targets for every Short's related video link.

### 3. Pick three formats

Choose three recurring formats that show the product in every episode:

- **Demo**: the product does the job in the first 2 seconds.
- **Answer**: a real buyer question on screen, answered with the product.
- **Series**: a numbered episode ("Day 12 of...") that repeats the same test or use.

When one format keeps winning, name it as a series and give it more slots [ORG-0011]. Keep the whole channel on one buyer and one problem area for the first 30 to 50 Shorts, so YouTube learns who to show them to [ORG-0023].

### 4. Set the rhythm

Three to five Shorts a week (practitioner default, test it), batched in one filming session and one editing session a week. Upload clean files, not files carrying another app's watermark. Reuse proven Reels or TikToks only after re-editing the first second and the text for YouTube; re-posting a proven story in a new format is a sound habit [ORG-0026].

### 5. Write each Short

Per Short: on-screen hook of 5 to 8 words [ORG-0016]; first frame shows the product in use or the result [ORG-0017]; spoken first line; 3 to 6 shots; on-screen text so it works muted; title with the search phrase a buyer would type; related video set to the matching long video [ORG-6402]; product tag if the channel qualifies [ORG-6403]. No typed store URL in the description; use "full demo linked above" style wording pointing to the related video.

### 6. Plan the 60 days

Weeks 1 to 2: setup, long videos, first 6 Shorts. Weeks 3 to 8: rhythm from step 4. For every three or four Shorts aimed squarely at the buyer, one can go broader for reach [ORG-0001]. Put seasonal Shorts 2 to 4 weeks before the date.

### 7. Measure

Every two weeks in YouTube Studio: views, the share of viewers who viewed versus swiped away, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained, and related video clicks. In GA4: sessions and purchases from the long videos' UTM links. Add YouTube to the post-purchase "how did you hear about us" question; if a channel's share of answers is far off its share of effort, trust the survey trend over clicks [MEA-0045]. YouTube mentions also correlate with being named by AI assistants [AEO-0007], a side benefit, not a goal.

At 60 days: keep formats whose Shorts beat the channel median on viewed-versus-swiped and drive related video clicks; drop the weakest format; decide on YouTube Shopping if now eligible.

### 8. Market notes

- **Any market:** Shorts in the languages your buyers speak can reach buyers that English posts miss. Write titles in the language spoken in the Short. Own-store product tagging follows the same channel rules [ORG-6403]; check in YouTube Studio that Shopping is offered for the store's market. Show prices exactly as on the store, in its currency, and name payment options only if offered.
- **US:** product comparison and "is it worth it" Shorts match how US buyers search on YouTube. Gifted or paid creator Shorts need a spoken and on-screen disclosure early in the video [ORG-6405].

## Checks before you hand it over

- Store link is first among channel links and advanced features are on, or the gap is listed.
- 2 to 4 long videos are planned with the store link and UTM in the first description line.
- Every Short has a related video link to a matching long video.
- No Short relies on a typed URL in its description or comments.
- Three formats, each showing a product with known gross profit.
- Audio for each Short is from YouTube's sounds, the Audio Library or original voice.
- AI and creator disclosures are set where needed.
- Nothing is uploaded or published without the client's yes.

## Output

1. **One-screen summary:** channel status, the three formats, products and why, weekly rhythm and hours, the route to the store, YouTube Shopping eligibility, and the 60-day keep-or-drop rule.
2. **Setup checklist**: Item, Status, Owner (handle, description, channel links, advanced features, Shopping eligibility, Studio roles).
3. **Long video plan**: Title, Product, Type, Length, First 30 seconds outline, Description first line with UTM link.

5. **Results sheet**: Fortnight, Shorts posted, Views, Viewed vs swiped, Average % viewed, Subscribers, Related video clicks, GA4 sessions, Orders, Gross profit, Verdict.

## Common mistakes

- **Typing the store link in Shorts descriptions.** Step 2 and step 5 use the related video link instead.
- **No long video to send viewers to.** Step 2 builds them first.
- **Mixing many topics in the first months.** Step 3 keeps one buyer and problem.
- **Copyrighted music.** The audio gate sets the sources.
- **Uploading watermarked files from other apps.** Step 4 uploads clean files.
- **Judging on views alone.** Step 7 reads related video clicks, sessions and orders.
