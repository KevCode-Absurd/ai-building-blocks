# Partnerships and business development

Channel 12 of 19. Book chapter it updates: Business development. Checked 2026-11-06.

Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares, *Traction* (Portfolio, 2015) [1], separate business development from sales: sales sells to customers one by one, while business development makes deals with partners that reach many customers at once and help both sides. This file restates that idea in our own words and updates it for 2026. The authors have not reviewed or endorsed this kit.

## 1. What this channel is now

A partner already has the attention or trust of your buyer. You give the partner something it values (a better product for its customers, revenue, content, a gap filled), and it gives you access. The common forms in 2026:

- **Platform and integration partnerships.** You build on or connect to a platform your buyers use and get listed in its marketplace. Platform terms decide the economics: Shopify's partner programme pays for referrals, store builds and app and theme sales, says payout terms can change, and keeps the final decision on payouts at its discretion [2].
- **Account mapping and co-selling.** Two B2B firms compare which target companies each already sells to, then make warm introductions. Crossbeam, which merged with Reveal in 2024, said more than 25,000 companies used one of the two for this [3].
- **Co-marketing and content deals.** Two brands produce and share content or events for the same audience, as GoPro and Red Bull did from 2016 [4].
- **Referral swaps, bundles and distribution deals.** A complementary business recommends you, sells you alongside its own offer, or stocks you.

The 2026 risk is the same as ever, only faster: a partner can change its terms. Mailchimp left the Shopify App Store in 2019 after a dispute over partner terms and customer data [5], and in 2022 Shopify named Klaviyo its recommended email partner for Shopify Plus and invested in it [6].

## 2. When it works and when it doesn't

It works when a partner already serves your exact buyer, when your offer makes its customers happier or its product better, and when you can show a number it cares about. Some early traction helps: partners prefer proof.

It doesn't work when you pitch big names before you have results (large partners move slowly and your runway may not last), when incentives clash (you would compete for the same sale), or when one partner becomes most of your revenue. Start with smaller partners who answer their own email.

## 3. Cheap tests

Set the go and stop numbers before each test, from the client's own figures. Target cost per partner customer = the most the client can pay to acquire a customer. These are formulas, not industry averages.

**Test 1: Ten warm partner asks.** List partners who serve your buyer before or after you. Ask ten for one small joint action: a co-hosted session, a mention to their list, or a listing.
- Cost: no cash; two or three days of research and writing.
- Time: three weeks.
- Go: at least one partner agrees to a dated joint action.
- Stop: zero partners agree to a call after the full list and one follow-up.

**Test 2: One co-marketing pilot.** Run one joint action with the partner who said yes, with a tracked link or code.
- Cost: time, plus any incentive you agreed.
- Time: four weeks.
- Go: cost per partner customer at or below the client's target.
- Stop: zero customers from the partner's audience by the end.

**Test 3: Account mapping with one B2B partner.** Compare target company lists with a free account-mapping tool or under a written data-sharing agreement. Share company names, not personal data, unless both sides have a lawful basis.
- Cost: free plan or a spreadsheet; one day.
- Time: two weeks.
- Go: at least one shared account turns into a warm introduction and a meeting.
- Stop: zero overlap, or the partner will not share.

## 4. The numbers to watch

- Partner activation rate = partners who sent at least one lead ÷ partners signed.
- Partner-sourced customers and revenue, tagged by partner in the CRM.
- Cost per partner customer = (time cost + incentives + revenue share paid) ÷ partner-sourced customers.
- Partner concentration = revenue from the largest partner ÷ total revenue.
- Time to first deal = days from first contact to the first partner-sourced sale.
- Overlap rate = shared target accounts ÷ your target accounts.
- Partner-influenced pipeline = open deals where a partner introduced or helped ÷ all open deals.

## 5. Tools

- HubSpot CRM (free tools; paid hubs) or Attio (free plan; paid) to tag partners and partner-sourced deals.
- Crossbeam (free plan; paid) for account mapping.
- PartnerStack (paid) for partner tracking and payouts.
- Platform partner programmes such as Shopify Partners (free to join; payout terms vary) [2].
- Calendly (free plan; paid) and Zoom (free plan; paid) for partner calls and joint sessions.
- A short written partnership agreement, reviewed by a lawyer (paid).
- Access: the lead's brief sets a level per tool. Level 1 reads the CRM; level 2 drafts outreach and partner records in the CRM or mailbox, unsent; level 3 sends approved outreach within the client's caps, and every action is logged. Terms and contracts always go back to the client.

## 6. Common mistakes

- **Pitching what you want.** Lead with what the partner's customers get.
- **Biggest name first.** Start where a yes is possible this month.
- **No written terms.** Agree who owns the customer, the data, the money and the exit.
- **Sharing personal data casually.** Customer lists are personal data in the UK and EU; share company-level overlaps unless both sides have a lawful basis.
- **One partner, one point of failure** [5]. Watch partner concentration.
- **Hidden paid recommendations.** In the US the FTC expects people to disclose a business connection when they recommend something; a profile listing is not enough [7].
- **Paying for referrals in regulated fields.** US federal health programmes ban paying for patient referrals under the Anti-Kickback Statute [8]. Other countries and professions have their own rules, so check with a qualified adviser.

## 7. By business type

### General

Map the businesses your buyer deals with just before and just after you. A bookkeeper sits next to an accountant; a photographer next to a venue; a web designer next to a copywriter. Offer a referral swap or a co-hosted session first, because both cost little and show quickly whether the partner's audience buys. Agree in writing how you will track referrals (a link, a code or a "who sent you?" question) and how you thank the partner. Review partner-sourced customers monthly and drop partners who never send anyone.

### SaaS

Integrations are the main door. Build a useful integration with a platform your buyers already use, get listed in its marketplace, and co-market the launch. Klaviyo was part of the Shopify ecosystem from 2017 before the 2022 deal that made it the recommended email partner for Shopify Plus [6]. Mailchimp's 2019 exit shows the risk of building on one platform [5]. For B2B tools, account mapping with partners who sell to the same companies turns cold outreach into warm introductions [3]. Track partner-sourced trials and their activation.

### Ecommerce

Look for brands that sell to your customer without competing with you: co-branded products, bundles, gift-with-purchase swaps, shared giveaways, and stockists. GoPro's 2016 deal with Red Bull traded equity and an exclusive camera role for access to more than 1,800 Red Bull events in over 100 countries [4]; a small store can run the same trade at its own scale, such as a coffee brand and a ceramics maker. Check margins first: wholesale and revenue shares cut contribution margin, so work out break-even before you sign.

### Local

Local partners share a street, a customer or a moment: a dentist and an orthodontist, a gym and a physiotherapist, an estate agent and a removals firm, a venue and its preferred suppliers. Start with a printed or emailed referral card and a simple thank-you, and ask each new customer who sent them. Neighbouring shops can run joint offers or events. Health and legal services must check their regulator's rules before any payment or reward for referrals; in US federal health programmes it is a crime [8].

### Coaching

Coaches and solo experts partner with people who serve the same client at a different moment: a therapist and a career coach, an accountant and a business coach, a course creator and a community owner. Formats that test cheaply: a joint webinar, a guest session inside another expert's programme, a bundle, or a referral arrangement. Corporate learning teams are partners too. If a partner earns commission or any reward for recommending you, both of you should disclose it [7]. Keep each partner's scope clear so no one strays into therapy or legal advice.

## 8. Real examples

- **Klaviyo and Shopify.** On 2 Aug 2022 Klaviyo announced it had become the recommended email solution partner for Shopify Plus, with early access to new Shopify features and a strategic investment from Shopify; it had been in the Shopify ecosystem since 2017 [6].
- **Mailchimp and Shopify.** On 22 Mar 2019 Mailchimp's app left the Shopify App Store after Mailchimp objected to Shopify's new partner terms on sharing customer data [5].
- **GoPro and Red Bull.** On 24 May 2016 they announced a multi-year partnership: Red Bull took equity in GoPro, and GoPro became its exclusive point-of-view camera partner, with co-produced content shared across both companies' channels [4].
