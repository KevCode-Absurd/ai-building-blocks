# Interview: growth lead for first traction

For growth marketers, founders and advisers who have found traction for a business before. The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example, with the numbers?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work. Never ask for client names, passwords or private figures the expert cannot share; anonymised numbers are enough.

## Finding the channel

1. **Tell me about the first time you found a channel that really worked. What did you try before it, and how did you know it was the one?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert tells a real signal from noise.
2. **When you start with a new business, how do you decide which channels to test first? Walk me through your last ranking.**
   Why it matters: reveals the expert's own version of the Bullseye ranking.
3. **Which channel do you think founders dismiss too quickly, and which do they overrate?**
   Why it matters: captures blind spots the agent should push back on.
4. **How do you set a traction goal with a client who just says "more customers"?**
   Why it matters: turns step zero into the expert's actual words and method.
5. **How do you work out what a client can afford to pay for a customer when they do not know their own margins?**
   Why it matters: the allowable-cost maths is where most plans go wrong.

## Designing and reading tests

6. **Describe a cheap test you ran that told you a lot. What did it cost, how long did it run, and what number made you decide?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent real test recipes and real decision rules.
7. **How do you set the go and stop numbers before a test? Show me one you wrote.**
   Why it matters: separates disciplined testing from hindsight.
8. **When the numbers are too small to read, what do you do?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert handles uncertainty honestly.
9. **Tell me about a test that looked like a win and was not. What fooled you?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent which false positives to watch for.
10. **When did you keep going with a channel after a bad first test, and why? Was it right?**
    Why it matters: captures the judgement between "bad tactic" and "dead channel".

## Running the team

11. **How do you brief a specialist (an ads buyer, an SEO, a PR person) so you get back something you can use?**
    Why it matters: improves the agent's hand-off briefs to its channel specialists.
12. **Two specialists want the same budget or the same week of the founder's time. How do you decide?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert merges conflicting proposals.
13. **What does your weekly review look like? Which numbers do you look at first?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's weekly rhythm.
14. **When a client gives you access to their ad accounts, email tool or site, what do you do yourself, what do you always confirm first, and how do you keep a record?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's access levels, approval lines and action log in the expert's terms.

## By kind of business

15. **Which kinds of business have you grown (software, stores, local firms, coaches, others), and what was different about each?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty" and the variant the expert knows best.
16. **For the kind of business you know best, which numbers do you trust, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: sharpens the variant's numbers section.
17. **What worked five years ago that no longer works, and what replaced it?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent current on how channels change.
18. **How have AI answers, creators or communities changed where first customers come from in your work?**
    Why it matters: grounds the 2026 updates in practice.

## Mistakes, ethics and clients

19. **What is the most common mistake you see founders make in their first 90 days of marketing?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
20. **A client asks for something you think is wrong: fake reviews, a misleading claim, a list bought without consent. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests where the expert draws the line and how they hold it.
21. **What kind of client do you turn down, and why?**
    Why it matters: defines who the agent is not for.

## A hard job

22. **Tell me about the hardest traction problem you have worked on: the goal, what failed, what finally worked, and the numbers before and after.**
    Why it matters: hard stories show judgement better than any checklist.
23. **If you had one week to train a new growth hire, what would you teach first?**
    Why it matters: reveals what the expert thinks matters most.
