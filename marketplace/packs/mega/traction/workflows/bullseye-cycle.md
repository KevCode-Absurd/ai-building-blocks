# Bullseye cycle

**Trigger:** a new client, a new traction goal, or a focus channel that has stopped paying back.

**Time:** usually 8 to 13 weeks from intake to a focus decision; the variant playbook's "first 90 days" sets the week-by-week pace.

## Steps

1. **Intake (variant playbook, section 2; level 1).** Ask the intake questions for the client's business type. Ask which access level the client grants for each tool (1 read, 2 read and draft, 3 read, write and publish within limits) and write it down under "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1. Connect only through official connectors, MCP servers or the tool's own sign-in; never accept passwords or keys in chat.
2. **Set the goal and critical path (prompt: traction-goal-and-critical-path).** Agree one number and one date, the milestones to it, and the weekly hours for traction.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the goal, the critical path and the hours.
3. **Fill in the numbers (variant playbook, section 3).** Work out the allowable cost per unit of success (customer, order, booked job, booked call) from the client's own figures. Set up source tracking before any test.
4. **Brainstorm all 19 channels (prompt: bullseye-brainstorm).** One concrete idea per channel, with rough guesses for cost, ceiling and time to read. Compare with the variant's starting ranking (section 4) and note where this client differs.
5. **Rank and choose the inner three (prompt: bullseye-brainstorm, steps 4 and 5).** Check the three fit the hours and cash together and do not reach the same people at the same time.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the ranking and the inner three.
6. **Brief the specialists (prompt: specialist-brief-and-merge, part A; files agents/<channel-id>.md; level 2 to build drafts in the tools).** Pass on the access level for each tool. Each specialist returns a test card, drafts, tracking set-up, rules, its action list and approvals needed. Use the variant's cheap test menu (section 6) as the starting recipe.
7. **Merge and size the tests (prompt: specialist-brief-and-merge, part B; prompt: channel-test-card).** One board, one budget, one approval request.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves each test card, every draft, every contact list and every spend cap, item by item. For level 3 tools, this is the one confirmation of the action list and limits before anything goes live.
8. **Run round one (workflow: weekly-traction-rhythm; level 3 to launch).** At level 3 the lead and specialists launch the approved ads, outreach and posts within the limits and log every action; at level 2 the client publishes the drafts. The lead reviews weekly and changes at most one thing per test, asking again before anything outside the limits.
9. **Read the results (prompt: channel-post-mortem).** Judge each test on cost, ceiling and fit against the go and stop numbers set in advance.
10. **Round two if needed.** Rerun unclear tests with one change; replace stopped tests with middle-ring channels. Repeat steps 6 to 9.
11. **Focus decision (workflow: winning-channel-hand-off).** Pick the channel that can reach the goal, or say plainly that none has yet and what to test next.
    **Approval point 4:** the client approves the focus decision.

## Outputs

- Traction goal, critical path and weekly hours.
- Filled-in numbers sheet with allowable costs.
- Ranked list of all 19 channels with one test idea and one line of reasoning each.
- Approved test cards and drafts for the inner three.
- Action log, test log and post-mortems.
- Focus decision with reasons.

## Needs the client's approval

- The goal, critical path and hours (point 1).
- The ranking and the inner three (point 2).
- Every test card, draft, list, offer and spend cap before launch (point 3).
- The focus decision (point 4).
- The access level for each tool, which the client can lower at any time. Level 1 until the client says otherwise.
- Any action outside the level 3 limits (a new account or audience, spend above the cap, deleting data, anything that cannot be undone), each time.
- Publishing, sending and spending: done by the lead only with level 3 access and within the limits; otherwise by the client. Every action is logged and reported.
