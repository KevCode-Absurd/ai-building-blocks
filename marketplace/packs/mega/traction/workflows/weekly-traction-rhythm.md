# Weekly traction rhythm

**Cadence:** weekly while tests or a focus channel are live, with a client review every two weeks and a full re-rank every month.

**Trigger:** the first approved test goes live.

## Steps

1. **Monday: pull the numbers and read the action log (level 1; tools: analytics, ad accounts, store, CRM, booking system, and the front-desk or call tally).** Update the test board: spend, hours, units of success, cost per unit, days left. Check that tracking still works: links, codes, source fields. Read every logged action since last week (what, where, when, result) and flag errors, spend spikes or policy warnings.
2. **Tuesday: decide (prompt: weekly-traction-review, with the action log).** One decision per live test: keep, change one thing, or stop. Flag results too small to read. Check pace against the critical path.
3. **Wednesday: brief the specialists (prompt: specialist-brief-and-merge, part A; files agents/<channel-id>.md; level 2 to build drafts).** Send each specialist its next task and the access level for each tool: a new creative angle, the next outreach batch, a page update, a follow-up script.
4. **Thursday: one approval request.** Bundle the week's level 2 drafts for the client to publish, and every action outside the level 3 limits, into a numbered list the client can answer item by item.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves, edits or rejects each item.
5. **Friday: ship and log (level 3 to publish).** At level 3, the lead and specialists put approved work live within the limits and log each action; at levels 1 and 2, the client does. The lead adds one line per channel to the test log: date, result, decision, lesson.
6. **Every two weeks: client review.** Fifteen to thirty minutes against the critical path: are we on pace, what changed, what is the one most important decision?
   **Approval point 2:** the client confirms any change to a test's cap, time box, go or stop number.
7. **Every month: re-rank (prompt: bullseye-brainstorm).** Re-score all 19 channels with what the tests taught. Move channels between rings where the evidence says so.
8. **When a test ends: post-mortem (prompt: channel-post-mortem).** Record the decision and lessons before starting the next test.

## Outputs

- Updated test board and reviewed action log each Monday.
- Weekly decision note: keep, change or stop for each channel.
- Specialist task list.
- One bundled approval request per week.
- Test log, post-mortems and a monthly re-ranked channel list.

## Needs the client's approval

- Every item in the weekly approval request (point 1): level 2 drafts, and any copy, list, offer, creative or spend outside the level 3 limits.
- Any change to a live test's budget cap, time box, go or stop number (point 2).
- Starting or stopping any channel.
- Any new tool access or higher access level. Connections go through official connectors, MCP servers or the tool's own sign-in; passwords, API keys and card numbers are never shared in chat.
