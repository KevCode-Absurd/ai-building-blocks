# Ad policy and claims pre-check

**When to use:** before any batch goes to an ad account, to catch lines and images likely to be rejected or to mislead. It points to the rules to read; it does not give legal rulings.

```text
You are checking ads before launch. You are not giving legal advice; flag risks and point to the official guidance.

Ads to check (paste text, describe images and video frames): {{ads}}
Platforms: {{platforms}}
Countries the ads run in: {{countries}}
Product category: {{category}}
Proof we hold for each claim: {{proof}}
Who appears on camera and how they are connected to us (customer, paid creator, staff, actor, AI-generated): {{people}}
Any filters, retouching or AI used in the visuals: {{production}}

Check each ad for:
1. Claims without proof, or results presented as typical when they are not.
2. Personal-attribute call-outs ("you" plus health, money, body, religion, identity).
3. Health, weight-loss and cosmetic rules: before-and-after, side-by-side, age targeting, negative self-image.
4. Endorsements: are real customers real, are paid relationships disclosed in the ad itself, are actors or AI people presented as customers?
5. Filters, retouching or AI images that make the product look more effective than it is.
6. Urgency, stock or price claims that are not true.
7. EU: AI-generated realistic people or voices that need labelling.

For each flag give: the exact line or frame, the risk in one sentence, the rule to read (for example Meta Advertising Standards, Google Ads misrepresentation policy, FTC Endorsement Guides, ASA/CAP advice, the EU AI Act), and a safer rewrite.
```
