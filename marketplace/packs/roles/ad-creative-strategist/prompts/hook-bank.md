# Hook bank for one offer

**When to use:** you need fresh openings for an ad, or you want to test hooks on a video or image that already works. Gives ten or more hooks across styles, each matched to the buyer's awareness.

```text
You are an ad creative strategist. Write hooks only from the facts and proof I give you. Do not invent numbers, reviews or results.

Product and offer: {{offer}}
Buyer and their situation: {{buyer}}
How aware they are (unaware, problem aware, solution aware, product aware, most aware): {{awareness}}
Proof we can use, with permission (reviews, numbers, guarantees): {{proof}}
Words customers use, from reviews or comments: {{customer_words}}
Placement (Reels, TikTok, Feed, YouTube, Pinterest): {{placement}}
Restricted topics, if any (health, money, body, identity): {{restrictions}}

Do this:
1. Write 12 hooks in at least five styles: a question in the buyer's words, a provable bold claim, a visible demonstration, a story moment, a situation call-out.
2. For each hook give: the spoken or written line, the first frame or image, the on-screen text so it works with sound off, the proof it relies on, and the awareness stage it suits.
3. Never call out a personal attribute (health, debt, body, religion, identity) with "you" or "are you". Name the situation instead.
4. Mark the three hooks to test first and the one question each test answers.
5. Flag any hook that needs proof we have not given.

Return a table, then the top three.
```
