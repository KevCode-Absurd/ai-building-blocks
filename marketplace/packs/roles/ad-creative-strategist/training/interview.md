# Interview: ad creative strategist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new account, from the first call to the first ads going live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence, not the textbook one.
2. **Where do your ideas come from? Show me the notes or files you build before you write a hook.**
   Why it matters: research habits are what separate expert creative from generic AI ideas.
3. **How do you tell a new concept from a variation of an old one?**
   Why it matters: the line between concepts and variants decides whether a batch gives the platform anything new to work with.
4. **How do you decide the hook style for a buyer who has never heard of the brand, compared with one who has visited the site?**
   Why it matters: captures how they match hooks to awareness in practice.
5. **What is your process for a founder who will film themselves? What do you send them before the shoot?**
   Why it matters: most small clients produce their own footage, so the brief is the product.
6. **How do you write for the sound-off viewer without losing the sound-on one?**
   Why it matters: reveals their craft on on-screen text, first frames and audio.

## Testing and reading results

7. **Describe a test you designed that taught you something even though nothing won.**
   Why it matters: shows whether they test for learning or only for winners.
8. **How do you set a stop rule on a small budget? Give me a real example with the numbers you used.**
   Why it matters: small accounts are where most false winners come from.
9. **Which metrics do you look at first in an export, and in what order?**
   Why it matters: their reading order becomes the agent's default readout.
10. **Tell me about a time an ad looked like a winner and turned out not to be.**
    Why it matters: captures the checks they now run before believing a result.
11. **How do you know an ad is fatiguing rather than the market changing?**
    Why it matters: the fix differs, and experts have their own tells.

## Proof, policy and honesty

12. **What proof do you refuse to use, even when the client insists?**
    Why it matters: sets their personal standard on testimonials, numbers and visuals.
13. **Tell me about an ad that was rejected or restricted. What was the line, and what did you change?**
    Why it matters: real rejections teach the policy edges better than the policy pages.
14. **How do you handle creators or actors in ads, and how do you make sure nobody is presented as a customer when they are not?**
    Why it matters: endorsement rules and fake-review rules apply directly to creator-style ads.
15. **Where do you draw the line on filters, retouching and AI-made people or voices?**
    Why it matters: these are the fastest-moving risks in the role.

## Competitors and markets

16. **How do you study rivals' ads without copying them? Show me how you tag them.**
    Why it matters: the tagging scheme is a reusable skill.
17. **Describe a market where everyone made the same claim. What did you do instead?**
    Why it matters: shows how they work in sophisticated, tired markets.

## Clients and judgement

18. **A client wants ten versions of their favourite ad with different colours. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests how they push back while keeping the client.
19. **Tell me the story of your hardest account: what was wrong, what you tried, what finally worked.**
    Why it matters: a full story carries judgement that rules cannot.
20. **What does your weekly report to a client look like, and what do you leave out?**
    Why it matters: the report format becomes the agent's default handoff.
21. **Which tools do you actually use each week, and what access do you ask clients for?**
    Why it matters: grounds access.md in real practice and sets default access levels.
