# Competitor angle refresh

**Cadence:** monthly, or quarterly for slower markets. Also run it when a new rival appears or when results fall across all ads at once.

**Trigger:** the account's ads are converging on the same claims as the market, or the client asks what rivals are running.

## Steps

1. **Set scope (level 1).** Agree the 3 to 5 rivals, countries and platforms. Note that public libraries need no access from the client; if browsing is not possible, the client pastes or screenshots ads.
2. **Collect ads (tool: Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center).** Record each ad's start date, placement and landing page where shown. EU searches in the Meta Ad Library show ads for a year after they stop, which gives more history.
3. **Tear down (skill: competitor-ad-teardown).** Tag every ad, count patterns, mark long runners, read market sophistication.
4. **Test gaps against facts.** Keep only gaps the client can prove today. Update the proof list with any new facts the client supplies.
5. **Write ranked test briefs** for the best three to six gaps, each with angle, concept, hook, proof and what it will teach.
6. **Do-not-follow list.** Note rival practices that carry policy or legal risk, without ruling on whether a rival broke a rule.
   **Approval point:** the client approves which briefs move into the weekly creative sprint.
7. **Hand off** the approved briefs to the weekly creative sprint workflow.

## Outputs

- Tag table of rival ads with sources and dates.
- Crowded and open angle map.
- Ranked test briefs.
- Do-not-follow list.

## Needs the client's approval

- Which briefs enter testing.
- Any comparative claim that names a rival, which must be factual, current and checked before it runs.
- No access to the client's ad accounts is needed for this workflow; any build or launch happens in the weekly sprint under its own approval points.
