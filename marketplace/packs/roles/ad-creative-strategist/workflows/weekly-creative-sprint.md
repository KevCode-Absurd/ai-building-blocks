# Weekly creative sprint

**Cadence:** weekly, on a fixed day. Each sprint reads last week's tests, decides, and ships the next small batch. Review the learning log monthly.

**Trigger:** an account is running paid social ads and the client wants a steady supply of tested creative instead of one-off launches.

## Steps

1. **Confirm access (playbook intake).** Record the level granted per tool under "Access I have": level 1 read, level 2 read and draft, level 3 read, write and publish. Until the client answers, work at level 1. At level 3, record the accounts, campaigns, test-spend cap, allowed actions and end date.
2. **Pull results (level 1; tool: Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads Manager or Google Ads export, or the official MCP server where connected).** Ad-level results for the past week and the test period, plus quality data from the shop or CRM.
3. **Read the tests (skill: creative-test-plan-and-readout; prompt: creative-results-readout).** Check fairness, read from attention to result, decide winner, loser or no clear winner against the stop rule set in advance.
4. **Check fatigue.** Look for creative fatigue flags and rising cost per result on long-running ads. Mark ads to remix or retire (prompt: winner-remix-plan).
5. **Log the learning.** One line per test: date, question, result, what it teaches, next idea.
6. **Plan the next batch (skill: ad-concepts-and-hooks; prompt: hook-bank).** Mix remixes of winners with at least one new concept on a different angle. Every claim from the proof list.
7. **Write or brief production (skill: short-video-ad-script; prompt: static-ad-concepts).** Scripts, shot lists and static briefs, with safe-zone notes per placement.
8. **Policy pass (prompt: ad-policy-precheck).** Fix every flag before anything goes into the account.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the batch, the test plan and the stop rules.
9. **Build the tests.**
   - Level 2: create the new ads and tests as paused drafts in the named campaigns.
   - Level 3: launch them within the confirmed limits.
   - Below level 2: hand the client an upload sheet.
   **Approval point 2:** at level 3, the client confirms the action list and cap once before the first launch in the job; anything outside it needs a new yes.
10. **Report.** Send the readout, the learning log, the action log (what, where, when, result) and next week's plan.

## Outputs

- Test readouts and verdicts.
- Updated learning log.
- New concepts, hooks, scripts and static briefs.
- Policy check notes.
- Action log for any level 2 or level 3 work.

## Needs the client's approval

- The new batch, test plans and stop rules (point 1).
- At level 3, the action list and limits before the first live action (point 2), and a new yes before any new account or campaign, spend above the cap, deleting or overwriting ads, or a new audience.
- Any use of a customer's name, face, voice or review in an ad, which also needs that customer's permission.
