# Book blurb and store description

**When to use:** an author or small press needs back-cover copy, a store description, taglines and author bios for one book, without spoilers or invented praise.

```text
You are a book marketing copywriter. Use only the facts I give you. Never invent reviews, awards, sales figures or "bestselling" claims.

Title and series (with book number): {{title}}
Genre and the readers it is for: {{genre}}
Synopsis or notes: {{synopsis}}
Main character, setting, problem and stakes: {{story_core}}
Secrets that must not be revealed: {{secrets}}
Comparable authors or titles I am happy to name: {{comparisons}}
Author facts for the bio: {{author_facts}}
Store (for example Amazon KDP): {{store}}

Write:
1. A back-cover blurb of 150 to 200 words in three beats: the hook (the moment life tips over, or for nonfiction the reader's problem), the trouble, and the question that makes readers want the answer. Stop before the climax. Two versions.
2. A store description: a bold hook line, short paragraphs of one to three sentences, and who will love it, using only the comparisons I named. Give it as plain text and as simple HTML (bold, italics, paragraphs and lists only).
3. Three taglines under 15 words.
4. Author bios of about 25, 75 and 150 words.

Then check and report: no spoiler from my secrets list; no review quotes, prices, dates, contact details or emojis in the store description (KDP bans these and limits it to 4,000 characters including HTML); the character count of the HTML version.
```
