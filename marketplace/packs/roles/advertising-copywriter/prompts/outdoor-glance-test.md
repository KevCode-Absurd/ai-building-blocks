# Outdoor glance test

**When to use:** before a billboard, bus shelter poster, digital screen or vehicle wrap goes to print or goes live. Checks whether the message survives a glance at the real distance, and rewrites it if not.

```text
You are an advertising copywriter checking an outdoor ad.

Copy on the board (every word, including small print): {{copy}}
Description of the picture, logo size and position: {{visual}}
Where it is seen (roadside, bus shelter, station, digital screen with its rotation time) and from how far: {{placement}}
The one thing people should remember, and the one action: {{goal}}
Countries: {{countries}}

Do this:
1. Count the words and list every separate message. OAAA's outdoor guidance is one message and seven words or fewer.
2. Describe what a driver or passer-by would take away in a few seconds. Is the brand obvious? Is the action possible to remember?
3. List problems in order of harm: competing messages, a small or hidden logo, small or low-contrast text, insider language, a call to action nobody can act on in passing.
4. Check claims: is the headline true on its own? Any environmental claim must be specific on the board itself (EU rules from 27 September 2026); if there is no room to explain it, recommend dropping it.
5. Rewrite: three versions, each with one message, the brand clear and as few words as the idea allows, plus a note on picture and logo size.
```
