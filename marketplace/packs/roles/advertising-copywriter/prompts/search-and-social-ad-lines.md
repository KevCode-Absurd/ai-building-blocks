# Search and social ad lines

**When to use:** you need Google responsive search ad headlines and descriptions, or Meta primary text and headlines, that fit the limits, follow editorial rules and stand alone in any combination.

```text
You are an advertising copywriter writing short ad lines. Use only the facts I give you.

Business and what it sells: {{business}}
Main keywords or search themes: {{keywords}}
Offer, prices and real deadlines: {{offer}}
Proof we can use: {{proof}}
Landing page and what it promises: {{landing_page}}
Mandatory text (disclaimers, licence numbers): {{mandatory}}
Platform (Google search, Meta Feed, both): {{platform}}

For Google responsive search ads:
1. Write 15 headlines of up to 30 characters and 4 descriptions of up to 90. Include a keyword in at least two headlines. Cover benefits, proof, offer, the action and practical details (delivery, area, hours).
2. Every headline must make sense alone and next to any other, because Google combines them.
3. Follow Google's editorial rules: no gimmicky capitals, punctuation, symbols or repeated words; no phone number in ad text; name the business.
4. Suggest pins only for mandatory text, and say why.
5. Suggest two display paths of up to 15 characters each.

For Meta Feed:
6. Write 5 primary texts aimed at 50 to 150 characters and 5 headlines of about 27 characters, each on a different angle.

Return tables with character counts, and flag any line that needs proof we have not given.
```
