# Interview: advertising copywriter

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through a recent job from brief to final copy. What did you ask for first?**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you find the one idea when a client gives you ten things to say?**
   Why it matters: choosing the single message is the core skill of short-form copy.
3. **How many headlines or lines do you write before you choose, and how do you choose?**
   Why it matters: reveals their generation and selection habits.
4. **What changes in your writing between something held in the hand, something seen from a car and something heard on the radio?**
   Why it matters: captures how they write to the medium and the moment.
5. **How do you work with designers, voice artists, studios or composers? What do you hand them?**
   Why it matters: the handoff format becomes the agent's default output.

## Medium by medium

6. **How do you time a radio script, and what do you cut first when it runs long?**
   Why it matters: timing discipline separates broadcastable scripts from drafts.
7. **How do you handle phone numbers, web addresses and legal lines in audio?**
   Why it matters: these are the most common reasons radio ads fail.
8. **Tell me about a billboard or poster you are proud of. How many words, and why those?**
   Why it matters: shows how they judge brevity and distance.
9. **How do you write a jingle hook so the name sings naturally? Show me a syllable count you did.**
   Why it matters: the method is teachable and rarely written down.
10. **How do you set up direct mail so the client can tell which version paid?**
    Why it matters: tracking and break-even habits are what make mail accountable.
11. **What makes a book blurb work in your genre, and what do you refuse to put in one?**
    Why it matters: captures genre conventions and their honesty line.
12. **How do you write search ad headlines that make sense in any combination?**
    Why it matters: tests their grasp of modern responsive ad formats.

## Standards and honesty

13. **Tell me about copy that was rejected by a station, a platform, a clearance team or a regulator. What happened?**
    Why it matters: real rejections teach the rules better than the codes.
14. **A client wants "eco-friendly" on a poster. What do you ask and what do you write?**
    Why it matters: green claims rules are changing fastest, especially in the EU.
15. **How do you handle a host read when the host has never used the product?**
    Why it matters: tests their grasp of honest endorsement and disclosure.
16. **A client wants a jingle that sounds like a famous song. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests their line on copyright and imitation and how they hold it.
17. **Where do you put conditions and small print so they are both honest and readable?**
    Why it matters: qualifier placement is where many misleading ads go wrong.

## Clients, results and judgement

18. **How do you know whether a piece of copy worked? Give me a case with numbers the client shared.**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's results reading in practice.
19. **Tell me about the hardest client you have written for and how the work turned out.**
    Why it matters: a full story carries judgement that rules cannot.
20. **Which tools do you use, and what access do you ask clients for?**
    Why it matters: grounds access.md in real practice and sets default access levels.
