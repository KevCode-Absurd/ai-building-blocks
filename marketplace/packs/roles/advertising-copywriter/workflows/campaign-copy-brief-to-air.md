# Campaign copy: from brief to print or air

**Trigger:** a business has booked, or is about to book, print, outdoor, radio, podcast or mail space for one campaign and needs the copy written, checked, cleared where required and handed to production.

**Cadence:** once per campaign; for long-running campaigns, review the copy and results at the end of each flight.

## Steps

1. **Intake (playbook intake questions).** Medium, size or length, where and when it runs, audience, the one message, the one action, facts, mandatory lines, deadline for artwork or scripts, and who approves.
2. **Confirm access.** Record the level granted per tool under "Access I have": level 1 read, level 2 read and draft, level 3 read, write and publish. Work at level 1 until the client answers. At level 3, record which accounts or contacts, the spend or quantity cap, which actions and until when.
3. **Write the single proposition.** One sentence on what this business offers that rivals do not, and the proof for it.
4. **Draft by medium:**
   - Print and outdoor: skill print-and-outdoor-copy, then prompt outdoor-glance-test for boards and posters.
   - Radio and podcast: skill radio-and-podcast-script, or prompt podcast-host-read for host reads.
   - Jingle or sonic logo: skill jingle-and-sonic-logo-brief.
   - Direct mail: skill direct-mail-campaign.
   - Book listings: prompt book-blurb-and-store-description.
   - Search and social lines: prompt search-and-social-ad-lines.
5. **Standards check (prompt: ad-standards-claim-check).** Fix every flag. List claims that need the client's evidence and lines that need a qualified adviser's view.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the copy, the claims list and the response tracking.
6. **Clearance and pre-checks where needed.**
   - UK radio in a special category: prepare the Radiocentre clearance pack (level 2), or submit it at level 3 through the client's account.
   - UK non-broadcast ads in doubt: prepare a CAP copy advice question.
   - US stations: confirm sponsor identification is in the script.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves any submission before it is sent.
7. **Production handoff.**
   - Print: the copy deck plus image notes to the designer or printer.
   - Audio: the script, voice direction and pronunciation guide to the studio or station.
   - Jingles: the lyric sheet and melody guide to the composer.
   At level 3, send to the contacts the client named; otherwise hand the pack to the client.
8. **Proof check.** Check the final artwork, recording or listing against the approved copy, word for word, including legal lines, dates, prices and codes. Read audio against a timer.
   **Approval point 3:** the client signs off the final proof before print, broadcast or publishing.
9. **Go live.**
   - Level 3: publish or send only within the confirmed action list.
   - Otherwise: the client or their supplier does it.
   Log every action with time and result.
10. **Results.** After the flight, collect response counts by code, number or page, and note what to keep or change next time.

## Outputs

- Proposition line and claims list with evidence status.
- Copy decks, scripts, lyric sheets, mail packs or listings, by medium.
- Clearance or copy advice pack, where needed.
- Proof-check notes and the action log.
- Results summary by version.

## Needs the client's approval

- The copy, claims and tracking (point 1).
- Any clearance or copy advice submission (point 2).
- The final proof before print, broadcast or publishing (point 3).
- At level 3, the action list and limits before the first live action, and a new yes before anything outside them: a new account or contact, spend or quantity above the cap, overwriting live copy, or anything that cannot be undone.
