# Direct mail test cycle

**Cadence:** one cycle per mail drop. Most local businesses run it seasonally; online brands mailing their own customers may run it monthly.

**Trigger:** a business wants to mail postcards, letters or door drops and know which version paid for itself.

## Steps

1. **Intake.** Goal, areas or customer segments, costs per piece, average order or job value, margin, repeat rate, deadline, who logs responses. Record the access level granted per tool; level 1 until the client answers.
2. **Break-even (skill: direct-mail-campaign, step 1).** Show the sum with and without repeat value.
3. **List rules (client-owned).** The client sets who is in and out from their own data or chosen routes, and confirms their data rules and preference-service screening (for example MPS in the UK). The copywriter does not buy lists or handle personal data.
4. **Offer and versions (skill: direct-mail-campaign, steps 3 and 4).** Three versions that each change one thing, with a real deadline.
5. **Copy and layout spec (skill: direct-mail-campaign, steps 5 to 7; prompt: ad-standards-claim-check).**
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the offer, the versions, the copy and the tracking set-up.
6. **Tracking set-up.** A unique code, number or page per version; a simple log sheet for staff; a test call or scan to check each path works before printing.
7. **Print and post.**
   - Level 2: prepare files and the order for the client to place.
   - Level 3: send approved files to the named printer or mail house within the quantity and spend cap.
   - The client places and pays for postage orders such as USPS EDDM.
   **Approval point 2:** the client signs off the final proof and the quantities.
8. **Read results (skill: direct-mail-campaign, step 9).** After the response window the client sets, compute response rate, cost per response and revenue per version and area, against both break-even rates. Say "no clear winner" when the counts are too close to separate.
9. **Log and plan.** One line per version in the mail log: date, areas, version, what it tested, responses, verdict. Write the next test from it.

## Outputs

- Break-even table.
- Versions with copy and layout spec.
- Tracking set-up and staff log sheet.
- Results table, verdict and next test.

## Needs the client's approval

- The offer, versions, copy and tracking (point 1).
- The final proof and quantities before printing (point 2).
- The list and its data handling, which stay the client's responsibility.
- At level 3, the action list and caps before the first live action, and a new yes before anything outside them.
