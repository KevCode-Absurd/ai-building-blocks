# Interview: what makes you a good AEO and GEO specialist

The coach asks these one at a time and follows up on vague answers with "Show me an example" or "How did you measure that?". Each question has a line on why it matters.

1. **How do you explain to a business owner what AEO or GEO is, and how it differs from SEO?**
   Shows whether you can be clear and honest in a field full of hype.

2. **Walk me through your first AI visibility audit for a new client. Which questions, which engines, how many runs?**
   Captures your measurement method, the part plain AI handles worst.

3. **How do you choose the buyer questions you track, and how do you stop the list drifting?**
   A bad prompt set makes every later number meaningless.

4. **How do you deal with answers changing every time you ask? What counts as a real change for you?**
   Shows whether you understand run-to-run noise and sample sizes.

5. **Tell me about a time an AI tool said something wrong about a client. How did you find the source and fix it?**
   Tracing errors to sources is a core, hard-won skill.

6. **What do you tell a client who wants to block all AI bots? And one who wants to allow everything?**
   Shows whether you separate search, user-fetch and training agents.

7. **Which signals do you think matter most for being cited, and what is your evidence for each?**
   Separates evidence from belief and shows how you weigh studies.

8. **What makes a page easy for AI answers to quote? Show me a before and after you are proud of.**
   Captures your content method in concrete form.

9. **How do you get a brand onto the third-party pages AI engines cite, without faking anything?**
   Earned media is where much of the work is; ethics matter here.

10. **Which AEO or GEO tactics do you refuse to use, and why?**
    Defines your boundaries with clients and vendors.

11. **What is your view on llms.txt, and what do you tell clients who ask for one?**
    Tests whether you follow evidence or trends.

12. **Which tools do you use for tracking, and where do you not trust their numbers?**
    Maps your stack and your scepticism.

13. **How do you report AI visibility to a client in a way they understand and act on?**
    Captures your reporting format and language.

14. **How do you connect AI visibility to revenue? What do you track in analytics?**
    Shows whether you can prove business value, not just mentions.

15. **How has your approach changed in the last six months, and what triggered the change?**
    Shows how you keep up in a field that shifts every few weeks.

16. **Tell me about the hardest AEO job you have done. What went wrong and what did you learn?**
    Stories hold the lessons rules miss.

17. **How do you handle a client whose competitor dominates AI answers because of one big review site?**
    Tests strategy when the lever is outside the client's site.

18. **What do you check on a local business that wants to show up when people ask AI for recommendations nearby?**
    Local AEO has its own sources and facts to get right.

19. **How do you decide whether a drop in AI mentions is your fault, a model change or noise?**
    Diagnosis under uncertainty is a senior skill.

20. **What would you tell a client who has been promised "rank number one in ChatGPT" by an agency?**
    Shows how you protect clients from bad advice without sounding defensive.
