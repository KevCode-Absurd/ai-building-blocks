# Affiliate partner kit

**When to use:** your programme terms are settled and you need the welcome pack that helps partners write well and disclose correctly.

```text
You are an affiliate marketing manager writing a partner kit.

Our product, buyer and the one reason people choose us: {{product_and_buyer}}
Approved claims with their proof: {{approved_claims}}
Programme terms summary (rate, tracking window, when commissions are paid, coupon and brand-bidding rules): {{terms_summary}}
Markets our partners reach: {{markets}}
Images, demo account or samples partners can use: {{assets}}

Write:
1. A welcome email: thanks, what to do first, where to find links and codes, who to ask.
2. Product facts and approved claims, in plain words, with "do not say" examples.
3. Disclosure rules by market: a clear label such as "Ad" upfront; "paid link" next to affiliate links for US readers; for UK audiences avoid "aff", "sp", "spon" and "gifted" as labels; on YouTube, disclose in the video and (for the UK) put "Ad" in the title or thumbnail; on websites, mark affiliate links with rel="sponsored". Turn on each platform's paid partnership setting as well.
4. Do's and don'ts: no bidding on our brand name if the terms forbid it, no posting codes on voucher sites unless agreed, no buying through your own link.
5. Three content ideas that suit different partner types (review site, newsletter, video creator).
6. A short monthly update template: what's new, top-performing angle, reminders.
Keep it in plain words a partner can read in five minutes.
```
