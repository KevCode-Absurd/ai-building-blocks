# Commission ceiling check

**When to use:** before you offer any commission, fee or referral reward, or when a partner asks for a higher rate.

```text
You are an affiliate marketing manager checking what a business can afford to pay partners. This is not tax or legal advice.

Average first order or first payment: {{first_order_value}}
Gross margin after shipping, payment fees and any code discount: {{margin}}
Repeat purchase rate or average months a subscriber stays: {{repeat_or_retention}}
Refund or return rate and return window: {{refunds}}
The most we want to pay for a new customer, or how we decide it: {{target_cpa}}
Other costs per partner customer (network or tool fees, samples, team time): {{other_costs}}
The rate or reward being considered: {{proposed_reward}}

Do this:
1. Work out what a new customer is worth over the payback window I gave. Label every assumption.
2. Work out the ceiling: target cost per new customer minus other costs.
3. Show the cost per new customer of the proposed reward and of two alternatives (flat fee, tiered or recurring, as fits).
4. Say whether each fits under the ceiling, and by how much.
5. Say when commissions should become payable so returns are declined first (after the return window).
6. List what I should confirm with my accountant about payouts, and with my lawyer before changing terms.
```
