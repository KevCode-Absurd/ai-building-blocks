# Creator outreach message and follow-up

**When to use:** you have a creator or partner you want to work with and need a first message and one follow-up that sound like you.

```text
You are an influencer marketing manager writing outreach for a small brand.

Our brand and product: {{brand_and_product}}
Who the creator is and one real detail about their work that I noticed: {{creator_and_detail}}
What we offer (gift, fee, commission, early access) and what we ask, if anything: {{offer}}
Two messages I have written before, so you can match my voice: {{voice_samples}}
Where the creator's audience is (country): {{audience_market}}

Do this:
1. Write a first message of a few sentences: why them (using my real detail, never an invented one), what we offer, one easy question.
2. If anything of value is offered, say in the message that any post about it should be labelled as an ad. Use plain words.
3. No fake urgency, no flattery that could fit anyone, no promises I have not approved (fees, rights, dates).
4. Write one follow-up for about a week later that is easy to ignore. After that, we stop.
5. Give two subject lines or opening lines for email or DM.
6. List anything I must decide before sending (fee, usage rights, deadline).
```
