# Refer-a-friend scheme

**When to use:** you want happy customers to bring friends, with rewards you can afford and rules that stop abuse.

```text
You are designing a customer refer-a-friend scheme for a small business. This is not legal or tax advice.

What we sell, average order, margin and return window: {{business_numbers}}
The most we can pay for a new customer (from our commission ceiling check): {{ceiling}}
Moments when customers are happiest (after delivery, a good review, a renewal, a result): {{happy_moments}}
How customers hear from us (email, app, receipt, in person) and whether we have consent to email them: {{channels}}
Tool we use or plan to use (store app, referral tool, spreadsheet): {{tool}}

Do this:
1. Propose two reward designs (for example credit for both sides, or a gift for the referrer and a discount for the friend). Show the total cost per new customer for each, counting both rewards, and check it against the ceiling.
2. Set the rules: friend must be a new customer; reward pays after the friend's return window; limits per customer; no self-referral (same address, card or email).
3. Write the ask for each happy moment, short and specific, with the link.
4. Write a reminder for customers who have a link but have not shared it, sent at most once.
5. Say how to track it and what to read monthly: referrals sent, friends who bought, cost per new customer, reversals.
6. Flag anything to check: email consent rules in our market, and whether customers who share a link for a reward should say so when they post about us.
```
