# Programme review and payout check

A programme that is not checked pays for refunds, existing customers and fraud. Networks give a window to approve or decline each sale before it is paid: on Awin, unreviewed sales are approved automatically when the validation period ends and amounts already paid cannot be reversed; on impact.com, changes must happen before the action locks (as of 6 Nov 2026). This skill reads the data, prepares the decisions and the payout file, and leaves the money with the client. Tax forms and withholding on payouts are for a qualified accountant.

## When to use

- Monthly, before the validation or lock date.
- When partner costs rise faster than new customers.
- When a code appears on coupon sites or a partner's sales look odd.
- Before renewing or ending partners.

## What you need

- **Network or tool export** for the period: per transaction, partner, date, order value, commission, status, and whether the customer is new (if tracked).
- **Store or billing export:** refunds, returns, cancellations and chargebacks by order, and new versus returning customer.
- **Programme terms:** tracking window, what counts as a valid sale, coupon and brand-bidding rules, self-referral rules.
- **Deadlines:** the validation period or lock date for the pending sales.
- **Creator deals:** fees and gifts paid, posts delivered, for creator ROI.

**Access:** level 1 to read the network, tool and store. Level 2 to prepare the validation list and payout file. Applying the validation list and paying stay with the client: the client confirms every validation batch, and the client funds and sends payouts. Changing payout or bank details is never done by the agent.

## Steps

1. **Match orders.** Join partner transactions to store orders by order ID. Flag any transaction with no matching order.
2. **Mark returns and cancellations.** Any refunded, returned, cancelled or charged-back order: propose decline, reason "returned" or "cancelled".
3. **Check the terms.** Propose decline, with the rule quoted, for: self-referrals or own purchases (same name, address, email or card as the partner); sales from codes used outside the agreed channel; sales from brand-bidding ads if the terms forbid it; duplicate orders.
4. **Find coupon leakage.** Codes used far more than the partner's audience could explain, or showing up on voucher sites. Propose a fix (single-use or personalised codes, or ending the code), not only declines.
5. **Read partner quality.** For each partner: clicks, sales, new-customer share, reversal rate, commission paid, cost per new customer against the target. Group partners: keep and grow, keep, watch, end.
6. **Read creator results.** For each creator: fee and gift cost, link and code sales, new customers, content delivered and labelled. Note content worth reusing (if rights allow).
7. **Prepare the validation list** before the deadline: every pending transaction with propose-approve or propose-decline and the reason. Declines need a clear, legitimate reason; Awin lists returns, cancellations, wrong partner, terms breaches, unfulfillable orders and fraud.
8. **Prepare the payout file and statements.** Approved commissions and fees per partner, period, and a short statement each partner can read (clicks, sales, approved, declined with reasons). The client checks and pays. Any new partner receiving money for the first time is flagged for the client's yes.
9. **Write the review.** Three to five changes: rates, partners to recruit or end, code fixes, rules to tighten, briefs to change.

## Checks

- Every proposed decline has a reason tied to an order record or a written rule.
- No transaction is left unreviewed before the deadline.
- Partner tables show new-customer share and reversals, not just sales.
- The payout file matches the approved list to the cent.
- The client approves the validation batch and makes the payments.
- Tax questions are flagged for a qualified accountant.

## Output format

1. **Summary:** spend, new customers, cost per new customer against target, reversal rate.
2. **Partner table** with group (grow, keep, watch, end).
3. **Creator table.**
4. **Validation list:** transaction, partner, order, proposed status, reason.
5. **Payout file and partner statements** for the client to approve and pay.
6. **Changes** and **issues found** (leaked codes, self-referrals, unlabelled posts).

## Example of good output (short)

*Pebble & Post is a made-up business used only to show the format: an online stationery shop on an affiliate network, with a 30-day return window. All numbers are invented.*

**Summary (October):** 214 partner orders; 141 from new customers (66%); 19 proposed declines (12 returns, 4 cancellations, 3 self-referrals); cost per new customer $9.80 against a $12 target.

| Partner | Orders | New-customer share | Reversals | Cost per new customer | Group |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planner review blog | 62 | 81% | 3 | $7.40 | Grow |
| Voucher site | 71 | 28% | 9 | $21.10 | Watch: separate terms |
| Student creator | 18 | 89% | 1 | $8.90 | Keep |

**Validation list (excerpt):** order 10482, Voucher site, propose decline, "returned on 9 Oct"; order 10511, Student creator, propose decline, "partner's own address and card (terms 4.2)".

**Payout file:** ready for your approval; total matches approved commissions. One new partner (a stationery newsletter) is due a first payment: needs your yes.

*Why this works:* each decline has a reason, partners are judged by new customers rather than sales, and the money stays with the client.
