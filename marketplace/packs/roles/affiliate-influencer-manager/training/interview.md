# Interview: affiliate and influencer marketing manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the manager's work (for example, network questions for someone who only runs creator gifting).

## How you approach a programme

1. **Walk me through the last programme you set up, from the first numbers to the first payout.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you work out what a business can pay a partner? Show me the sum from a real case.**
   Why it matters: the reward maths decides whether a programme pays for itself.
3. **When do you choose a percentage, a flat fee, tiers, recurring or a two-sided reward?**
   Why it matters: the model choice is a judgement call that the agent should copy.
4. **What goes into your programme terms, and which rule has saved a client the most money?**
   Why it matters: terms stop the expensive problems before they start.
5. **How do you treat coupon and cashback sites, and brand bidding?**
   Why it matters: the most common source of paying for sales the client would have made anyway.

## Creators and partners

6. **How do you decide a creator fits? Show me a profile you said no to and why.**
   Why it matters: fit judgement is the core skill and hardest to write down.
7. **What do you ask creators for before you pay them, and how do you check it?**
   Why it matters: separates real audience data from follower counts.
8. **Read me an outreach message that worked, and one that did not.**
   Why it matters: captures voice, length and what earns a reply.
9. **What goes into your creator brief, and what do you leave to the creator?**
   Why it matters: the balance between control and creative freedom drives results.
10. **How do you handle gifting: who gets what, and what do you ask in return?**
    Why it matters: gifting rules touch both results and disclosure law.
11. **How do you agree usage rights when a brand wants to run creator content as ads?**
    Why it matters: unclear rights cause disputes and wasted content.

## Disclosure, money and risk

12. **What disclosure wording do you require in the US, the UK and the EU, and how do you check it?**
    Why it matters: the agent's wording must match the FTC, ASA, CMA and EU guidance.
13. **Tell me about a partner who would not label their posts. What did you do?**
    Why it matters: brands share responsibility, so the response matters.
14. **How do you run the monthly validation: what do you decline, and how do you explain it to partners?**
    Why it matters: validation is where money is saved and partner trust is kept or lost.
15. **What fraud or leakage have you found, and how did you spot it?**
    Why it matters: real patterns make the agent's checks sharper.
16. **Who signs contracts and who pays partners in the businesses you work with? What do you never do yourself?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's habits into the agent's access rules for contracts and payouts.

## Results and clients

17. **Which numbers do you report each month, and which one tells you a partner is worth keeping?**
    Why it matters: reveals what the expert believes drives value.
18. **Tell me about a client who wanted the wrong thing: positive reviews for free product, hidden ads, or paying for fake followers.**
    Why it matters: captures how the expert says no and what they offer instead.
19. **Which networks and tools do you use, and what access do you ask for in each?**
    Why it matters: grounds access.md in real practice.
20. **What is the hardest programme you have run, and what would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: hard stories hold the lessons the rest of the interview misses.
