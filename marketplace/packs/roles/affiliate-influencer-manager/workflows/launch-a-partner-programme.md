# Launch a partner programme

**Trigger:** a business wants to start an affiliate programme, a creator programme or both, or restart one that stalled. For a cheap first test before committing, use the First traction kit's affiliate and creators channel files.

**Time:** about four to six weeks from numbers to first posts, depending on how fast partners reply.

## Steps

Steps 1 and 2 need level 1. Steps 3 to 5 use level 2. Step 6 can use level 3 within the client's limits; steps 4 and 7 always need the client's fresh yes.

1. **Get the numbers and markets (level 1).** Order or contract value, margin, repeat rate, return window, target cost per new customer, and where customers and partners are. Read any existing exports.
2. **Design the reward and rules (skill: reward-maths-and-programme-terms; prompt: commission-ceiling-check).** Model, rate, tracking window, when commissions are payable, coupon, brand-bidding and self-referral rules, disclosure rules.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the economics, the model and the rules.
3. **Draft the terms and kit (level 2; prompt: affiliate-partner-kit).** One-page terms summary, partner kit, and, for creators, the brief template (skill: creator-brief-and-disclosure). Set up the programme in the network or tool as a draft: commission groups, tracking window, terms not yet published.
4. **Legal and setup check.** The client's lawyer reviews the terms and any creator agreement; a qualified accountant answers payout tax questions. The client funds the network or tool account and sets payout details.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves final terms and switches the programme live, or gives me a yes to publish it at level 3.
5. **Build the shortlist (skill: creator-shortlist-and-outreach).** Right-partner profile, fit checklist, scored shortlist, outreach and one follow-up, gifting plan if used.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the named list, the messages and the gifting budget. Approving the list is the yes for each new paid partner on it.
6. **Send outreach and gifts (level 3 within limits).** Send the approved messages to the approved names only, create links and codes for partners who accept, log every send and gift. At level 1 or 2, the client sends from the drafts. Anyone not on the list, any new fee or any change to terms needs a new yes.
7. **Agreements.** For each paid creator or partner, the agreement is sent as binding only after the client's fresh yes. Briefs go out with disclosure wording for each market.
8. **First posts check (prompt: partner-post-disclosure-check).** Check labels, claims and links on the first posts; ask for fixes before any payment.

## Outputs

- Economics and reward design with the maths shown.
- Terms summary, partner kit and creator brief template.
- Programme set up as a draft, then live once approved.
- Scored shortlist, outreach, gifting plan and tracking sheet.
- First-post disclosure check.
- Action log.

## Approval

- Economics, model and rules (point 1).
- Final terms after the lawyer's review, and going live (point 2).
- The named partner list, messages and gifting budget (point 3).
- Always a fresh yes: each binding agreement, any partner not on the list, rate or term changes, and deleting data. Funding, payouts and payout details stay with the client.
