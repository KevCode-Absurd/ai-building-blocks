# Monthly programme run

**Cadence:** monthly, timed to finish before the network's validation or lock date. A quick weekly pass covers steps 2 and 6 only.

**Trigger:** a live affiliate, referral or creator programme.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 3 need level 1. Steps 4 and 5 use level 2. Step 6 can use level 3 within the client's limits. Step 5's approvals and all payments stay with the client.

1. **Pull the data (level 1).** Network or tool export, store orders with refunds and new versus returning customers, creator posts delivered, and the validation or lock dates.
2. **Check partner content (prompt: partner-post-disclosure-check).** New posts and videos: labels, platform settings, claims, links and codes. Send fixes; flag repeat offenders.
3. **Review the programme (skill: programme-review-and-payout-check).** Match orders, mark returns and terms breaches, find coupon leakage and self-referrals, read partners by new-customer share and cost per new customer, and read creator results.
4. **Prepare the validation list (level 2).** Every pending transaction with propose-approve or propose-decline and a reason, ready before the deadline.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms the validation batch and applies it, or gives a fresh yes for me to apply exactly that batch where the tool allows.
5. **Prepare payouts (level 2).** Payout file and partner statements matching the approved list. New partners due a first payment are flagged.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the payout file, any first payment to a new partner, and pays. I never change bank or payout details.
6. **Partner updates (level 3 within limits).** Send the approved monthly update and statements to partners, refresh the partner kit, create codes for approved partners, and replace leaked codes as agreed. At level 1 or 2, the client sends from drafts.
7. **Changes.** Recommend three to five changes: rates, partners to grow or end, code fixes, rule changes, briefs.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves any change to rates, terms or partners; binding changes go out only after that yes.
8. **Re-check the rules each refresh.** Note any change to network validation or locking pages, the FTC, ASA, CMA and EU guidance, or platform disclosure settings, with the date.

## Outputs

- Disclosure check of the month's partner content.
- Programme review: summary, partner and creator tables, issues found.
- Validation list ready before the deadline.
- Payout file and partner statements for the client to approve and pay.
- Partner update sent within limits, or drafted.
- Recommended changes and the action log.

## Approval

- The validation batch (point 1).
- Payouts, including any first payment to a new partner (point 2). Paying, refunds above the client's limit, and bank or payout details always stay with the client.
- Rate, term and partner changes (point 3); binding changes only after a fresh yes.
- At level 3, routine partner updates, statements and approved codes go out within the agreed limits and are logged.
