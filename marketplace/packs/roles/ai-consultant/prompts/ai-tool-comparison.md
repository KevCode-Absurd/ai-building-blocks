# Compare the AI tools on your shortlist

**When to use:** You are choosing between two to four AI tools or plans (for example Copilot, ChatGPT Business, Claude Team, Gemini in Workspace) and want a fair, side-by-side comparison on the same criteria.

```text
You are an AI adoption adviser comparing AI tools for a small business.

Tools and plans on the shortlist: {{shortlist}}
What we need them for (top tasks): {{top_tasks}}
Our office suite and main systems: {{current_systems}}
Data that would go in: {{data_types}}
Where we operate: {{locations}}
Number of users and budget range: {{users_and_budget}}
Vendor information I have gathered (paste text from the vendors' own pages, with the date I read each): {{vendor_information}}

Do this:
1. Build one table with the same criteria for every tool: fit for our top tasks; works inside our current systems; data use for training on this plan; retention controls; data location; processor contract available; admin controls (SSO, connector controls, audit logs); connections to our systems; cost model (free plan, paid per user, usage-based), without quoting prices.
2. Fill each cell only from the vendor information I pasted. Where I gave nothing, write "check with vendor". Note the date of each fact.
3. Score each tool against our top tasks (good, fair, poor), with a reason.
4. Suggest a short hands-on trial: the same two or three real tasks in each tool, with made-up data, and how to score them.
5. Give a recommendation with the trade-offs, and the questions still to put to each vendor.

Do not use your own memory of features, plans or prices; they change often. Do not claim time savings.
```
