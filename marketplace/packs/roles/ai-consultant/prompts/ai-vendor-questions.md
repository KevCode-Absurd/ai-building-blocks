# Questions to ask before buying an AI tool

**When to use:** Before you sign up a team to an AI tool, renew one, or let it connect to company email, files or a CRM.

```text
You are an AI governance adviser preparing vendor questions for a small business.

Tool and plan we are considering: {{tool_and_plan}}
What we want to use it for: {{use_cases}}
Data that would go into it (public, internal, confidential, personal or sensitive; give examples): {{data_types}}
Where we and our clients are (countries or states): {{locations}}
Systems it would connect to: {{connections_or_none}}

Write a list of questions to send the vendor, grouped under:
1. Training and data use: Are our inputs and outputs used to train or improve models by default? Can we turn that off for the whole organisation? Is feedback (thumbs up or down) treated differently?
2. Retention and deletion: How long are prompts, outputs, files and logs kept? Can we set retention? How do we delete data, and what happens at the end of the contract?
3. Location and transfers: Where is data stored and processed? Are some models or features outside that region?
4. Contract: Is a data processing agreement (processor contract) available on this plan? Which sub-processors are used, and how are we told about changes?
5. Access and controls: Single sign-on, admin controls, role-based access, audit logs, ability to turn connectors or web search on and off.
6. Connected data: When it searches our files or email, does it respect existing permissions? What can it do (read, draft, send, delete)?
7. Security: Certifications (for example ISO/IEC 27001 or 42001, SOC 2), incident notification, protection against prompt injection.
8. Accuracy and limits: What the vendor says the tool should not be used for; how it shows sources.
9. Regulation: How the vendor supports customers with the EU AI Act (for example AI literacy materials, transparency labels) if we operate in the EU.

For each question, add one line on what a good answer looks like and a red flag answer.
Then list the vendor's own public pages I should read first (privacy, trust or security centre, data processing terms) and tell me to record the date I read each one.
Do not state what this vendor's answers are unless I paste them. End with: "This is a checklist, not legal advice. Ask a qualified adviser to review the contract."
```
