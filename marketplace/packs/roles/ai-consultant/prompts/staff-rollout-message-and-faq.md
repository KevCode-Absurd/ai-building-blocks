# Staff message and FAQ for new AI rules

**When to use:** You have new AI rules or a newly approved AI tool and need to tell staff in a way that is clear, calm and easy to act on.

```text
You are helping a small business announce its AI rules to staff.

Business and team size: {{business_and_team}}
What is changing (new rules, approved tool, banned uses): {{what_is_changing}}
The rules (paste the policy or the key points): {{rules}}
Approved tools and how to sign in: {{approved_tools}}
Who staff ask, and how: {{contact}}
Date it starts and any training planned: {{start_date_and_training}}
Known worries or habits (for example staff using personal ChatGPT accounts): {{worries_or_habits}}

Write:
1. A message from the owner, under 200 words: what is changing and why, the three rules that matter most, what staff should do this week, where the full rules live, who to ask. Warm and plain, no hype, no threats.
2. An FAQ of 8 to 12 questions staff are likely to ask, with short answers, including: "Can I still use my personal account?", "What if I already pasted client data into a free tool?", "Do I have to say when I used AI?", "Will AI be used to monitor or judge my work?", "What if the AI gets something wrong?", "Who checks AI-written messages to clients?".
3. A short note for managers on how to answer questions and report mistakes without blame.

Use only the rules I gave you. Where my rules do not answer a likely question, write [DECISION NEEDED] instead of inventing an answer. Grade 8 to 9 reading level.
```
