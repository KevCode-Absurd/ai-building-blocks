# Training interview: AI consultant

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, staff details, contract terms and anything under a confidentiality agreement.

1. **What kinds of teams do you help with AI, and which jobs do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of what it should take on.

2. **Walk me through your last engagement from the first call to the hand-over.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the expert spends time.

3. **What do you ask in the first meeting, before anyone mentions a tool?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions that keep the work grounded in tasks and data.

4. **How do you find out what staff are already doing with AI, including on personal accounts?**
   Why it matters: records how the expert surfaces shadow use without scaring people.

5. **How do you decide which tasks go into a first pilot and which stay out?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the expert's scoring of fit, time, risk and data sensitivity.

6. **How do you measure a pilot, and how do you agree the go or stop rule in advance?**
   Why it matters: captures measurement habits that resist wishful thinking.

7. **Tell me about a pilot that failed or that you stopped. What did you learn?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real case of saying no with evidence.

8. **What goes into your staff AI policy every time, and what do you leave out?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's policy template and keeps it short.

9. **How do you decide what data may go into which tool?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's data classes and how plan and contract change the answer.

10. **Which questions do you always put to a vendor, and which answers make you walk away?**
    Why it matters: builds the vendor checklist and red flags.

11. **Where do you insist on a person checking AI output, and how do you write the reject rule?**
    Why it matters: captures the human-check design that keeps workflows safe.

12. **Have you seen an AI tool connected to email or files do something unexpected? What happened?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's caution about connectors, prompt injection and excess permissions.

13. **How do you handle a client who wants AI to screen job applicants or score customers?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert flags high-risk uses and brings in legal advice.

14. **How do you keep up with the EU AI Act, UK data protection guidance and US rules, and how do you avoid giving legal advice?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's line between pointing to official sources and ruling on the law.

15. **What does a good AI training session look like for non-technical staff?**
    Why it matters: captures the practice-first format and the habits the expert teaches.

16. **How do you handle a sceptical or anxious team?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the tone and arguments that work with worried staff.

17. **Which tools and access do you ask clients for, and what do you never touch?**
    Why it matters: grounds access.md and the access levels the agent should ask for.

18. **When a client asks "how many hours will this save?", what do you say?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent from promising savings and teaches it to offer measurement instead.

19. **What do you review every quarter once AI is in use?**
    Why it matters: builds the register and rules review habit.

20. **What would you tell a new AI consultant to stop doing?**
    Why it matters: captures common mistakes the agent should avoid and spot in others' plans.
