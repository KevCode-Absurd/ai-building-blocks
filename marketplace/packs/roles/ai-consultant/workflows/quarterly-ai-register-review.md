# Quarterly AI register and rules review

**Cadence:** every three months, and straight after any AI incident, a new tool request that changes how data is used, or a change in a vendor's data terms. Usually half a day of the owner's time.

## Steps

1. **Confirm access.** Check the access level recorded for each tool is still right ("Access I have"). Until the client confirms, work at level 1.
2. **Gather the facts.** Register, policy, admin usage reports (for example the Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot usage reports, or your AI tool's own admin reports), new tool requests, incident notes and any staff questions since last review. Level 1.
3. **Check each tool in the register.**
   - Is it still used, by whom and for what?
   - Has the vendor changed its plans, data-use terms, retention, data location or sub-processors? Re-read the vendor's own pages and record the date.
   - Are the settings still as approved (training opt-outs, connectors, web search, retention)?
   - Is the processor contract still in place?
   Mark each tool: keep, change or remove.
4. **Check the rules against the outside world.** Re-read the official sources listed in knowledge.md "What changes fast": EU AI Act dates and guidance, UK ICO and GOV.UK updates, US FTC and state rules for the client's locations. Note anything that might affect the client and send legal questions to their adviser; do not rule on them.
5. **Review incidents and near misses.** What happened, what the human check caught, what changed. Update rules or training where the same problem appears twice.
6. **Check training.** New starters trained? Skills check results? Topics for the next micro-course (skill: ai-skills-workshop, step 6). For EU clients, update the internal record of AI literacy steps.
7. **Decide on new tool requests** with the ai-vendor-questions and ai-risk-check-one-use prompts.
8. **Write the review note:** changes to the register, rule updates, training actions, open legal questions, next review date.
   **Approval point:** the owner approves the note and any rule or setting changes.
9. **Make the changes at the right level.**
   - Level 1: deliver the note and updated drafts.
   - Level 2: update the register and policy pages as drafts for review.
   - Level 3: after the owner confirms the action list and limits, publish the updated register and policy, change the listed settings, remove access for retired tools from the named accounts, and send the update to staff. Log each action. Ask again before deleting any data or removing users not on the list.

## Outputs

- Updated register with keep, change or remove for each tool and dated vendor checks.
- Review note: rule changes, training actions, incidents, open legal questions, next review date.
- Updated policy and staff update (if rules changed).
- Action log of every change made in the client's tools (level 2 or 3).

## Needs the client's approval

- The review note and every rule or setting change.
- Adding or removing tools, users or connectors.
- Any staff message.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits, asked again each time.
