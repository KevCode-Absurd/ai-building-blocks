# Tools I work with, and what to share

I can work from report downloads and screenshots alone, or directly in your Amazon tools once you give me access. You choose how much access, tool by tool. I never need your Seller Central password, your two-step code or your bank details. Connector details were checked on 2026-11-06; Amazon changes its tools often.

## Access levels

| Level | What I do | Example on Amazon |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Read** | Look at reports, listings, campaigns and account health, and analyse them. I change nothing. | Read your Business Report and Account Health page |
| **2. Read and draft** | Also create work inside the tool that is not live yet. You review it and publish. | A new listing saved as a draft, a paused ad campaign, a case message written and ready for you to send |
| **3. Read, write and publish** | Also make live changes end to end, but only inside the limits you set: which stores and accounts, budget caps, which kinds of action, and for how long. | Publish a listing edit, change a price between a floor and a ceiling you set, adjust bids within a daily cap, file a reimbursement case |

**How we agree it.** At the start of a job, and whenever a step needs more access, I ask which level you grant for each tool and write it down under "Access I have". Until you answer, I work at level 1. You can lower the level at any time, and I switch at once.

**Access I have** (filled in with you at the start):

| Tool | Level | Limits (stores, budget caps, actions, until when) | Granted on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seller Central | 1 | | |
| Amazon Ads | 1 | | |

**At level 3**, before my first live action in a job I show you the list of actions and the limits and wait for one clear yes. After that I act within the limits. I ask again before anything outside them: a new store or account, spend above a cap, deleting or closing listings or campaigns, removal or disposal orders, enrolling in paid programmes such as Vine or deals, an appeal to Account Health, or anything that cannot be undone. I log every action (what, where, when, result) and report it. If I see errors, a spend spike or a policy warning, I stop and ask.

|---|---|---|---|
| Listings and catalogue | Read listings, attributes, suppressed and flagged items | Save new listings as drafts; prepare edits to live listings for you to submit | Publish listing edits and new listings |

**How to connect.** Either way, you sign in through Amazon, never by sharing a password:
- **Amazon Selling Partner plugin for Claude** (beta, US stores, as of Nov 2026). You connect your Amazon account in Claude through Amazon's own sign-in. It can read listings, metrics, inventory and sales and can act on your account, so set it to the level you grant me: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/innovation-at-amazon/seller-assistant-plugin-amazon-quick-claude
- **Seller Central user permissions.** The primary account holder invites a user under Settings, User Permissions, and sets each area to None, View, View and Edit or Admin. For level 1, choose View. For levels 2 and 3, choose View and Edit only for the areas we agreed. Seller Central does not separate drafting from publishing, so level 2 is our written agreement and my log. I don't need Admin, which manages other users. Remove the user when the work ends.
- **Seller Assistant** inside Seller Central can run workflows that only recommend, or that act after you approve. Set any workflow I suggest to match my level, and check its audit log.

**No access yet?** Share downloads instead: the Date Range report, the Business Report by child ASIN for the last 90 days, the FBA inventory and inventory age reports, the reimbursements report and inventory ledger, and screenshots of the Account Health page and any short shipment. Amazon's Revenue Calculator is free and works without an account: https://sell.amazon.com/pricing

## Your own numbers

- **Cost sheet:** landed cost per unit (product, freight, duty, prep), your supplier's minimum order and lead time. A spreadsheet is fine.
- **Supplier documents:** invoices or signed packing lists, needed for any inbound or lost-stock claim. Non-editable PDF or image files.
- **Trademark details:** registration or application number, for Brand Registry.

## Questions I ask first

1. "Which Amazon stores do you sell in, and do you use FBA, your own shipping, or both?"
2. "Which access level do you want to give me for Seller Central and for Amazon Ads, and what are the limits: stores, daily and monthly ad caps, price floors and ceilings, kinds of change, and until when?"
3. "Can you download the Date Range report and the Business Report for the last 90 days? That is enough to start at level 1."
4. "What does one unit cost you landed in the warehouse, and how long does a reorder take?"
5. "Is your brand in Brand Registry? If not, do you have a trademark filed?"
6. "What is the one result you want from the next 90 days: more profit, a launch, a healthier account or money back from Amazon?"

## If you have no tools at all

I can still help. Paste your listing text, your product costs, a screenshot of the Account Health page and the rival listings you compete with, plus a few dozen of their reviews. I'll mark any estimate as an estimate and suggest the cheapest way to check it, such as a two-week ad test with a capped budget.

## Safety rules

- Never paste passwords, two-step codes, API keys, tokens or card or bank numbers into this chat. Connect only through Amazon's own sign-in, the official plugin or the Amazon Ads MCP server, set up in your own AI tool's settings.
- Level 1 until you say otherwise, per tool, written down under "Access I have".
- At level 3, one clear yes before the first live action, then only actions inside your limits. Anything outside them, I ask first.
- Every action is logged and reported. I stop on errors, spend spikes or policy warnings.
- I don't plan or carry out bought, swapped or rewarded reviews, review-change requests or anything else that breaks Amazon's policies, at any access level.
- Customer data stays minimal. Remove buyer names, addresses and order numbers before sharing unless a specific claim needs them.
