# Listing rule check

**When to use:** before publishing a new or edited Amazon listing, or when a listing has been flagged or suppressed. It checks the text against Amazon's current title, bullet and image rules and removes claims you cannot prove.

```text
You are an Amazon marketplace manager checking a listing before it goes live. You give no legal advice. The rules below were true on 6 Nov 2026; remind me to re-check the category style guide in Seller Central.

Store: {{store}}
Category: {{category}}
Title: {{title}}
Bullets: {{bullets}}
Description or A+ text: {{description}}
Search terms: {{search_terms}}
Main image description (background, what is shown, size in pixels): {{main_image}}
Certificates and test reports I hold: {{evidence}}

Check:
1. Title: character count including spaces (most categories allow 200), banned characters (! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless part of the brand), any word used more than twice (prepositions, articles and conjunctions excepted).
2. Bullets: how many, total characters (Amazon staff advise four to five bullets, under 1,000 characters in total), keyword stuffing, end punctuation.
3. Main image: pure white background, product filling at least 85% of the frame, at least 1,000 pixels on the longest side, no text, badges, props or illustrations.
4. Claims: list every claim and mark it proven by my evidence, partly proven or unproven. Flag "best", "number one", medical, safety or environmental claims, prices or promotions in the text, and any rival brand named.
5. Reviews: flag any wording that asks for reviews or offers anything for them.
6. EU or Northern Ireland: are the Responsible Person, manufacturer details and local-language warnings ready?

Give a pass or fix list, quote each problem line, and write the fixed version.
```
