# Amazon launch plan

A launch spends money to buy early sales and data. The plan's job is to spend it on purpose: know the break-even before the first click, buy the data that matters (which searches convert), and stop or change course on rules set in advance. Fee and policy facts checked on 2026-11-06; re-check them in Seller Central before launch.

## When to use

- A product is listed, or about to be, and stock is on its way to Amazon.
- A relaunch after a listing rebuild or a new version.
- A launch that has run for a few weeks with no clear picture of profit.

Not for: plans that rely on bought, swapped or rewarded reviews, or on promises of rankings.

## What you need

- **Unit profit sheet:** price, every fee, landed cost, margin before ads (from the product-opportunity-research skill or the client's own sheet).
- **Budget:** total cash for ads and promotions over 90 days, and the most the client will lose before stopping.
- **Stock:** units arriving, arrival date, supplier lead time, reorder quantity.
- **Listing status:** live or not, Brand Registry or not, A+ done or not, Vine eligible or not.
- **Search terms:** the main and secondary phrases from the listing work.
- **Calendar:** the store's big sale events in the window and their inbound deadlines.
- **Access:** steps 1, 2, 4, 8 and 9 need level 1 (read). Building campaigns paused needs level 2. Launching campaigns, changing bids, budgets or prices, and creating shipments need level 3 within the client's caps. Paid enrolments (Vine, deals) are asked for separately even at level 3.

## Steps

1. **State the goal and the limit.** For example: "Reach steady organic sales on the main phrase within 90 days without losing more than [amount] in total." The limit is the client's number, not yours.
2. **Set the break-even.** Margin before ads = break-even ACOS. Write it at the top of the plan. Any ad that costs more than this per sale loses money on that sale.
3. **Pre-launch checklist (weeks -4 to 0).** Listing passes title, bullet and image rules; every attribute filled; A+ live if registered; Vine enrolled if eligible, with its fee in the budget (ask before enrolling, even at level 3, because it carries a fee), and stock in Amazon's network early (Amazon suggests at least three weeks before launch for Vine); inbound shipment booked with the split option and placement fee chosen on purpose; EU safety details present if selling there.
4. **Price and promotion.** Start at the price the margin sheet supports. A launch coupon or deal is optional: show its cost per unit and its fee (Amazon's 2026 holiday deal fee was $100 upfront per promotion plus 1.5% of promotional sales, capped at $5,000). Never show a fake "was" price.
5. **Ad structure (level 2 to build paused, level 3 to launch within the caps).** Week 1: one automatic Sponsored Products campaign and one manual exact-match campaign on the main and secondary phrases, each with a daily cap. Add Sponsored Brands once registered and the product has some reviews. Name campaigns so reports are readable.
6. **Weekly search-term routine (level 1 to read; level 3 to apply within the caps, otherwise a change table).** Each week, read the search term report: move converting terms into exact match, add terms with spend and no sales as negatives once they have had a fair number of clicks for the price point (agree the rule with the client), lower bids on terms above break-even ACOS.
7. **Review plan.** Use the Request a Review button on eligible orders, and Vine if enrolled. Nothing else: no inserts asking for reviews, no rewards, no friends or staff. Answer product questions and fix listing gaps the early reviews reveal.
8. **Stock plan.** Weekly sales rate, weeks of cover, the reorder date (lead time plus shipping plus a buffer), and the low-inventory-level fee risk if cover falls under four weeks of sales.
9. **Checks and stop rules.** Weekly: sessions, conversion, units, ad spend, ACOS against break-even, TACOS trend, rating, stock cover. At day 30, 60 and 90: scale, hold, fix or stop. Write the stop rule now, for example: "If margin after ads is negative at day 60 and conversion is below the client's own baseline, stop ads and review the listing and price."
10. **Request or apply changes.** At levels 1 and 2, list every bid, budget, price or promotion change for the client to approve. At level 3, show the action list and limits, get one yes before the first live change, act within the caps, log each action (what, where, when, result), and stop and ask on errors, spend spikes or policy warnings.

## Checks

- Break-even ACOS stated and every ad budget line checked against it.
- The total 90-day spend fits the client's limit, including Vine and deal fees.
- Review plan contains only Request a Review and Vine.
- Reorder date set from real lead times.
- Stop rules written before launch, with the client's numbers.
- Promotions show their unit cost and fee.
- Holiday or event deadlines checked against Amazon's current dates.

## Output format

1. **Goal and loss limit.**
2. **Break-even box:** price, margin before ads, break-even ACOS.
3. **Pre-launch checklist** with owner and date.
4. **Week-by-week table** for 13 weeks: price, promotion, ad budget per campaign, review actions, stock action, check.
5. **Search-term routine** in five lines.
6. **Stop and scale rules.**
7. **Change requests or action log**, by access level.

## Example of good output (short)

*Illustrative structure, not a real business: a pet brand launching a no-pull dog harness on Amazon. Copy the shape, never the content: every fact comes from the client's own context. Figures only show the shape.*

**Access I have:** Seller Central level 1, Amazon Ads level 2 (campaigns built paused).

**Goal:** steady organic sales of the no-pull dog harness on "no pull dog harness" within 90 days; total loss limit $3,000.

**Break-even box:** price $29.99, margin before ads $9.60 (32%), break-even ACOS 32%.

| Week | Price | Promotion | Ads (daily cap) | Reviews | Stock | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| -3 | - | - | - | Vine enrolled, 20 units | 600 units inbound, minimal split chosen | Listing rule check |
| 1 | $29.99 | 10% coupon (cost $3.00 a unit) | Auto $25, exact $25 | Request a Review on every eligible order | 600 in stock | Daily spend, first search terms |
| 2-4 | $29.99 | Coupon on | Move converting terms to exact; negatives agreed | Same | Cover 11 weeks | ACOS against 32% |
| 5-8 | $29.99 | Coupon off | Add Sponsored Brands if 15+ reviews | Same | Reorder by week 6 (lead time 7 weeks) | Day 60 decision |
| 9-13 | $29.99 | None | Scale terms under 32% ACOS | Same | New stock lands week 13 | Day 90 decision |

**Stop rule:** if margin after ads is negative at day 60 and conversion is below 8% (the brand's own rate on its website), stop ads and review the listing and price.

**Change requests:** coupon start and end dates; launching the paused campaigns; each weekly bid change list; Sponsored Brands launch.
