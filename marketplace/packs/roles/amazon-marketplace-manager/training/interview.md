# Interview: Amazon marketplace manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new Amazon client: what you ask for, what you look at first, and what you deliver.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you decide whether a product idea is worth selling? Show me the last scorecard or margin sheet you used.**
   Why it matters: product choice is where most money is won or lost, and the format is reusable.
3. **How do you read rival reviews? What do you count, and what do you ignore?**
   Why it matters: review mining is the core research skill for both product choice and listings.
4. **Which fees do new sellers forget, and how do you make sure none are missed?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's fee checklist.
5. **How do you write a title and bullets? Where do the words come from, and in what order do you put them?**
   Why it matters: captures their listing method beyond the published rules.

## Launch and ads

6. **Describe a launch you ran: the budget, the ad structure in week one, and what you changed in weeks two to eight.**
   Why it matters: real launch sequences are hard to find written down.
7. **How do you decide when a search term has had enough clicks to judge? Does it change with price?**
   Why it matters: turns a judgement call into a rule the agent can state and agree with clients.
8. **When do you use coupons, deals or Vine, and when do you skip them?**
   Why it matters: shows how they weigh promotion cost against margin.
9. **What is your stop rule for a launch that is not working, and have you ever had to use it?**
   Why it matters: experts stop losing launches early; plain AI rarely does.
10. **How do you use ACOS and TACOS together, and what do you tell a client who only looks at sales?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent explains profit, not just volume.

## Account health and money back

11. **Tell me about an account health problem you fixed. What was the violation, and what did you send Amazon?**
    Why it matters: account rescues are high-stakes and rarely documented.
12. **How do you find money Amazon owes a client? Which reports do you open, in what order?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's reimbursement routine.
13. **What evidence wins an inbound shipment claim, and what gets one rejected?**
    Why it matters: claim packs succeed or fail on evidence detail.

## Quality bar, tools and rules

14. **What do you check before a listing or a campaign goes live? Give me your actual checklist.**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality checks.
15. **Which tools do you pay for, which Amazon tools do you rely on, and what do you ask clients to export?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.
16. **How do you keep up with Amazon's rule and fee changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: feeds the refresh list and keeps the agent current.
17. **A client asks for something against Amazon's review rules. What exactly do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests their ethical line and how they hold it.
18. **Which changes do you make yourself in a client's account, which do you always send back for a yes, and what limits do you agree first?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's level 3 limits and the actions it always asks about.
19. **What differs when you sell in the UK or the EU compared with the US?**
    Why it matters: regional rules are where generic advice goes wrong.

## Mistakes, clients and specialty

20. **What are the three mistakes you see most in accounts you take over, or in plans written by AI?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
21. **Which categories or kinds of seller do you know best, and what is different about them?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty".
22. **What kind of client or product do you turn down, and why?**
    Why it matters: defines what the agent is not for.

## A hard job

23. **Tell me about the hardest Amazon job you have done: what went wrong, what you tried, and how it ended.**
    Why it matters: hard stories show judgement under pressure better than any checklist.
24. **If you had one week to train a new Amazon assistant, what would you teach first?**
    Why it matters: reveals what the expert thinks matters most.
