# Idea to launch

**Trigger:** a client wants to sell a new product on Amazon, or to choose between several ideas, and has not yet ordered stock.

**Time:** research and listing usually take two to four weeks of working time; supplier samples, trademark filing and shipping add their own lead times. The launch itself runs 90 days.

## Steps

1. **Intake (playbook intake questions).** Ask which access level the client grants for Seller Central and Amazon Ads, with the limits, and write it under "Access I have" (see access.md). Until they answer, work at level 1. Confirm the stores, fulfilment method, budget for a first order and for launch ads, the loss limit, Brand Registry status and the decision date. Ask for the cost sheet and any supplier quotes.
2. **Gather evidence (level 1; tools: Product Opportunity Explorer, Jungle Scout or Helium 10 exports, rival reviews).** See access.md for what to share and how.
3. **Mine reviews (prompt: review-complaint-mining).** Ranked complaint themes, jobs, and buyer phrases.
4. **Judge the idea (skill: product-opportunity-research).** Gap check, unit profit with every fee (prompt: unit-profit-and-break-even), risks by store, scorecard and verdict.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves go, test small or stop, and the first order size. No stock is ordered on the agent's say-so.
5. **Supplier and compliance.** The client asks the supplier for samples and test certificates; for EU and Northern Ireland, appoints an EU Responsible Person; files a trademark if needed. Flag anything that needs a qualified adviser.
6. **Build the listing (skill: amazon-listing-builder; level 1 to write, level 2 to save it as a draft in Seller Central).** Fact sheet, term map, title, bullets, A+ outline, search terms, image brief.
7. **Check the listing (prompt: listing-rule-check).** Fix every flag.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the listing text, claims and image brief. At level 3 the agent then publishes it and logs the action; otherwise the client publishes.
8. **Plan the launch (skill: amazon-launch-plan; level 2 to build campaigns paused).** Break-even ACOS, pre-launch checklist, 13-week table, review plan (Request a Review and Vine only), stock plan, stop rules.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the 90-day budget, any coupon or deal and its fee, Vine enrolment and its fee, and the stop rules.
9. **Run the launch.** Weekly search-term review (prompt: search-term-report-review) produces a change table each week. At level 3, the agent shows the action list and limits, gets one yes before the first live change, then launches campaigns and applies bid, budget and negative changes within the caps, logging each action and stopping on spend spikes or policy warnings. At levels 1 and 2, the client applies approved changes.
10. **Day 30, 60 and 90 decisions.** Scale, hold, fix or stop, against the written rules. At day 90, hand over to the monthly account review.

## Outputs

- Review mining table and research report with a scorecard and verdict.
- Unit profit sheet with break-even ACOS, dated.
- Listing pack and claims log.
- 90-day launch plan with stop rules.
- Weekly change tables and a day 30, 60 and 90 decision note.

## Needs the client's approval

- The go, test small or stop decision and the first order (point 1).
- The listing text, claims and images (point 2).
- The launch budget, promotions, Vine and stop rules (point 3).
- Every weekly bid, budget, price or promotion change, unless the client has granted level 3 with limits that cover it.
- The access level and limits for each tool, and one yes before the first live action at level 3.
- Anything outside the limits, even at level 3: a new store, spend above a cap, deleting or closing listings or campaigns, paid enrolments, removal or disposal orders.
