# Monthly account review

**Cadence:** monthly, in the first week after the month closes. Also run it after Amazon's yearly fee changes, at the start and end of the holiday peak period, and whenever the Account Health page shows a new warning.

## Steps

1. **Check "Access I have" and collect the exports (access.md; level 1).** Date Range transaction report, Business Report by child ASIN, campaign and search term reports, FBA inventory and inventory age reports, inventory ledger, reimbursements report, shipment screenshots for any short shipment, Account Health screenshot, unit cost sheet.
2. **Run the check (skill: amazon-account-check).** Profit per SKU, fee changes and causes, ads against break-even, stock cover and age, reimbursements, inbound shipments, account health.
3. **Review ad search terms (prompt: search-term-report-review).** One change table per campaign.
4. **Build claim packs (prompt: reimbursement-claim-pack; level 2 to draft cases in Seller Central, level 3 to file them).** One per claim, inside its window, with evidence attached.
5. **Re-check rules that changed.** Read Amazon's News and Announcements for fee, policy and review rule changes since last month, and note anything that affects the client's listings or costs.
6. **Write the summary.** One-line verdict, the tables, and the top three actions as change requests with cost, expected effect and how to check next month.
   **Approval point:** the client approves or declines each change request and each claim. At level 3, actions inside the agreed limits can go ahead after the client's one yes for this month's action list.
7. **Apply or hand over.** At level 3, the agent carries out the approved actions within the limits and logs what, where, when and the result, stopping on errors, spend spikes or policy warnings. At levels 1 and 2, the client or their team applies approved changes and files approved claims. Either way, record what was approved, by whom and when.
8. **Follow up next month.** Check each approved action against its expected effect, and each claim's outcome.

## Outputs

- Monthly account report: profit by SKU, fees, ads, stock, reimbursements, account health.
- Change tables for ads.
- Claim packs ready to file.
- Top three actions as change requests.
- A log of approvals and outcomes.

## Needs the client's approval

- Every change request (bids, budgets, prices, promotions, reorders, listing edits), unless it falls inside level 3 limits the client set.
- Every reimbursement claim before it is filed.
- Any reply to an Account Health warning or appeal; for policy or legal questions, the client should ask a qualified adviser.
- Live changes only with level 3 access, inside the limits written under "Access I have"; anything outside them is asked first.
