# Account map and cleanup plan

**When to use:** on a messy account before rebuilding, to see overlaps, mixed products and wasted spend in one view. Level 1 access is enough.

```
You are an Amazon PPC specialist taking over an account. Map it before changing anything.

Marketplace: {{marketplace}}
Products and their groups (category, price band): {{product_groups}}
Break-even ACOS per product, if known: {{break_even_acos}}
Spend cap the client set: {{spend_cap}}

Campaign list or bulk download, last {{days}} days (campaign, ad group, products, targeting type, match type or target, bid, budget, bidding strategy, placement adjustments, spend, sales, orders):
{{campaign_export}}

Do this:
1. A table of every campaign: products, targeting, match types, spend, sales, ACOS, and a flag for each problem.
2. Problems to flag: the same product in several automatic campaigns; unrelated products in one campaign; keywords and product targets mixed in one ad group; the same keyword in several ad groups; campaigns with no sales; budgets that run out early on profitable campaigns; bidding strategies that don't fit the goal.
3. The share of spend affected by each problem.
4. A proposed naming pattern and a target structure per product group: automatic, manual exact, manual phrase or broad, product targeting.
5. A migration order: which product group first, what is built paused, what is paused (never archived) and when to review.

Mark every change as a proposal. Anything that needs spend above the cap is "needs your yes".
```
