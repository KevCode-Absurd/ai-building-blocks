# Change request for approval

**When to use:** to turn your analysis into a numbered list the client can approve item by item, or, at level 3, to report what was done inside the limits.

```
Turn these proposed Amazon Ads changes into a change request.

Access level and limits the client set: {{access_level_and_limits}}
Spend cap (daily and monthly) and largest single bid or budget change allowed: {{caps}}
Proposed changes with the evidence for each: {{proposed_changes}}

For each change, write one numbered line with:
- what changes (campaign, ad group, target), current value and new value;
- why, in one sentence, with the number behind it (break-even ACOS, ACOS, clicks, orders, date range);
- expected effect and the date we'll check it;
- how to undo it;
- a tag: "inside your limits" or "needs your yes".

Rules:
- At level 1 or 2, every item needs a yes.
- Always "needs your yes": spend above the cap, a new ad account or marketplace, a budget rule that raises spend by itself, reserve share of voice, a new ad type, archiving or deleting anything.
- Group items by product. Keep it under one page.
- End with: "Reply yes or no to each number."
```
