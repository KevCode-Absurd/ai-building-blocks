# Sponsored Brands claims check

**When to use:** before submitting a Sponsored Brands headline, image or video script, to catch claims that need proof under advertising law. Read Amazon's ad policy pages in a browser as well; this check doesn't replace them.

```
You are an Amazon PPC specialist checking ad copy before it goes to Amazon's ad review.

Marketplace(s): {{marketplaces}}
Product and what it really does (facts only): {{product_facts}}
Proof we hold (tests, certificates, awards with dates, review counts): {{evidence}}
Draft headline(s), image text and video script: {{draft_creative}}

Do this:
1. List every claim in the draft: objective claims (numbers, "lasts 2x longer"), superlatives ("best", "#1"), comparisons with rivals, price or discount claims, "free", green or eco claims, health or safety claims.
2. For each, say whether our evidence supports it, and mark it keep, soften or cut.
3. Flag rules by region: US (ads must be truthful and claims proved before they run), UK (CAP Code: evidence before publishing; unqualified superlatives count as comparisons), EU (misleading practices banned; stricter rules on green claims since 27 September 2026).
4. Rewrite the headline in plain words with only claims we can prove.
5. List what to check on Amazon's own ad policy pages before submitting.

This is a general check, not legal advice. For a health, safety or environmental claim you're unsure about, ask a qualified adviser.
```
