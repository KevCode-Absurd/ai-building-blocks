# Interview: pulling out an Amazon PPC specialist's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("show me a real example from an account"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour in an Amazon Ads account you've never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you work out break-even ACOS, and which costs do sellers most often forget?**
   Why: every judgement in this role rests on that number.
3. **Tell me about an account where the blended ACOS looked fine but the seller was losing money. How did you find it?**
   Why: real stories teach the per-product habit better than rules.
4. **How do you structure campaigns: by product, by match type, by phase? What made you settle on it?**
   Why: captures your structure rules and the reasons behind them.
5. **What's your naming pattern, and what goes wrong without one?**
   Why: naming is how other people read your work.
6. **What are your exact rules for harvesting a search term to exact match?**
   Why: harvesting is the core weekly skill.
7. **When do you make a term negative, and what's the worst negative keyword mistake you've seen?**
   Why: teaches the collateral-damage check.
8. **How do you choose between dynamic down only, up and down, and fixed bids?**
   Why: bidding judgement changes results more than any single keyword.
9. **When do placement adjustments pay off, and how do you read the placement report?**
   Why: placement settings can multiply bids, so mistakes are expensive.
10. **How do you set the budget, end date and ACOS ceiling for a launch, and when have you stopped one early?**
    Why: launch spend without limits is where most money is lost.
11. **What tells you a product should move from growth to defend?**
    Why: records your phase thresholds in your own words.
12. **Have you tested cutting spend on brand terms? What happened?**
    Why: shows how you think about sales the ads didn't really cause.
13. **How do you use TACOS, and when has it told you something ACOS didn't?**
    Why: separates real growth from ad-funded sales.
14. **How do you use Sponsored Brands and display ads alongside Sponsored Products?**
    Why: captures your view of the other ad types after Amazon's recent platform changes.
15. **What do you check in the Prompts report and Amazon Ads Agent that didn't exist two years ago?**
    Why: keeps the kit current on fast-changing tools.
16. **What limits do you ask for before taking level 3 access, and what do you always ask about again?**
    Why: writes down your safety habits for live changes.
17. **Which claims have you had to cut from Sponsored Brands creative, and why?**
    Why: ad claims carry legal risk in the US, UK and EU.
18. **How do you report to a client who only wants to know "is it working?"**
    Why: shows how you turn tables into decisions.
19. **What's a common piece of Amazon PPC advice you think is wrong?**
    Why: your disagreements are your expertise.
20. **Tell me about the hardest account you've cleaned up. What did you change first, and what would you do differently?**
    Why: a full story shows judgement under pressure.
