# Product launch ads

**Trigger:** a new product will go live on Amazon within the next four to eight weeks, or has just gone live with no ads.

**Goal:** a launch with a budget, an end date and a stop rule, built so the first search data turns into a clean manual structure.

## Steps

1. **Margin first** (skill: `break-even-acos-review`; level 1). Price, fees, landed cost, profit before ads and break-even ACOS for the new product. If profit before ads is thin, say so before any spend.
2. **Listing gate** (level 1). In stock, able to win the Featured Offer, a complete detail page. Amazon's guidance points to reviews and ratings as part of ad readiness; plan honest review routes with the marketplace manager, never paid reviews.
3. **Launch rule card** (skill: `phase-bid-rules`). The client sets the total launch budget, the end date and the highest ACOS they'll accept during launch. Write the exit-to-growth and stop conditions.
4. **Build the campaigns paused** (skill: `sponsored-products-structure`; level 2). Automatic campaign, core exact and phrase keywords from research and rival listings, product targeting on close rivals. Names follow the pattern. Bidding strategy and any top-of-search adjustment as on the rule card.
5. **Approval to launch.** Send the build, budget and rule card. Launch spend always needs the client's yes, even at level 3.
6. **Go live** (level 3, or the client switches campaigns on at level 2). Log the time each campaign went live.
7. **First two weeks.** Check twice in the first week (spend pace, impressions, clicks, alarms), then weekly. Don't add negatives on thin data. Amazon suggests letting the automatic campaign run about two weeks before building manual campaigns from it.
8. **First harvest** (skill: `search-term-harvest`). Move converting terms to exact, add clear negatives, start the watch list.
9. **Sponsored Brands** (only with Brand Registry). Once Sponsored Products is running, propose a Sponsored Brands campaign on brand and category terms, as Amazon's launch playbook suggests. New ad type, so it needs a yes.
10. **Exit review.** At the end date or when an exit condition is met: move to growth, extend (with a new yes and budget), or stop and review the listing or price.

## Outputs

- Break-even line for the product.
- Launch rule card.
- Campaign build sheet (paused) and launch log.
- Week-two harvest lists.
- Exit review note.

## Approval

**Always needs the client's yes:** the launch budget and end date, the launch ACOS ceiling, switching launch spend on, extending the launch, adding Sponsored Brands or display ads, any spend above the cap.

**At level 3, inside the rule card:** bid and budget moves within the step size, harvested keywords and negatives on the agreed rules, pausing targets that meet the stop rule.
