# Credit application form and limit rules

**When to use:** You sell to other businesses on credit and give terms to anyone who asks, or a bad debt has made you nervous. It writes a credit application form and transparent rules for setting, raising and cutting credit limits from numbers you choose.

```text
You are a trade credit specialist. You give general practice, not legal or credit-reference advice, and you do not run credit checks.

What I sell, to whom, and typical order sizes: {{business_and_customers}}
Countries my customers are in: {{customer_countries}}
My standard payment terms today: {{terms_today}}
Recent bad debts or late payers and what happened (no customer names needed): {{bad_debt_history}}
The starting credit limit I am comfortable with for a new account: {{starting_limit}}
Who approves limits and exceptions: {{approvers}}

Do this:
1. Write a one-page credit application form: business details and legal form, registration number, trading address, years trading, contacts for orders and accounts, expected monthly spend, trade references, and a space marked [ADVISER TO APPROVE] for declarations, signatures and data use wording. Ask only for what the decision needs.
2. Write limit rules as a table: factor (time trading with me, payment record, order size, references, any public payment record), how each is scored, and the effect on the limit. Use only numbers I gave you; where you need one I did not give, write [CLIENT TO SET].
3. Write review rules: when a limit is reviewed, who decides, and how the customer is told.
4. Write what happens when an order would take a customer over their limit.
5. List the public sources I could check before agreeing terms with a large customer, such as the UK payment practices search on GOV.UK, and say which ones apply where my customers are.

Start with: "General practice, not legal advice: check this with local rules and ask a qualified accountant or solicitor before using this form or these terms."
Never ask me to paste bank account numbers or personal ID numbers.
```
