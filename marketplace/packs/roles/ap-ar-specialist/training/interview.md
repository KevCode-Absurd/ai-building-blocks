# Training interview: Accounts payable and receivable specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share customer or supplier names, account numbers or amounts that could identify a client; anonymise every story.

1. **Which side do you know best (billing, credit, collections or payables), and for what kinds of businesses and countries?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the rules it must know, and marks where it should refer work on.

2. **Walk me through your week: what happens on which day, from invoices out to the payment run.**
   Why it matters: captures the real rhythm of the work, which the weekly workflow should copy.

3. **What do you check on an invoice before it goes out, and what has bounced back most often?**
   Why it matters: the expert's pre-send checks prevent the most avoidable late payments.

4. **How do you decide what terms and credit limit a new customer gets?**
   Why it matters: limit-setting is judgement; the agent needs the factors and the order the expert weighs them in.

5. **Show me your reminder ladder. What do you say at each step, and when?**
   Why it matters: tone and timing are the core of credit control, and they should sound like the expert, not a template.

6. **What do you say on the first call to a customer who has gone silent?**
   Why it matters: call openings and replies to excuses are hard to write well and easy to learn from a real script.

7. **When do you offer a payment plan, and what makes a plan work?**
   Why it matters: plans keep customers and cash; the expert knows which terms customers actually keep.

8. **When have you put an account on stop, and how did you tell the customer?**
   Why it matters: stops can save money or lose a customer; the agent must learn how to do them without burning the relationship.

9. **Have you ever claimed statutory interest or compensation? What happened to the relationship?**
   Why it matters: shows when the expert uses legal rights and when they hold back, which the agent should flag rather than decide.

10. **How do you handle a dispute that holds up a large invoice?**
    Why it matters: splitting disputed and undisputed amounts is a skill that speeds cash.

11. **What do you check on a supplier bill before you let it into a payment run?**
    Why it matters: the expert's payables checks become the agent's batch checklist.

12. **Tell me about a duplicate or wrong payment you caught, or one that got through. How?**
    Why it matters: real stories teach the agent which patterns to scan for.

13. **What is your process when a supplier asks to change bank details?**
    Why it matters: this is where the largest single losses happen; the agent must follow the expert's exact verification steps.

14. **How do you decide the order of payments when cash is short?**
    Why it matters: priority rules are business judgement the agent should ask for, not guess.

15. **Which early payment discounts do you take, and how do you decide?**
    Why it matters: tests whether the expert works out the yearly cost, and how they explain it to owners.

16. **Which tools do you use for invoicing, chasing and paying, and what do you never let a tool do on its own?**
    Why it matters: maps tools to the access levels and shows where the expert keeps a human check.

17. **What do you split between people so no one person can add a supplier, approve a bill and pay it?**
    Why it matters: separation of duties is the cheapest fraud control; the agent should suggest the expert's version.

18. **How do you report to the owner each week and each month?**
    Why it matters: the format and numbers the expert uses become the agent's weekly and month-end notes.

19. **What changed in your work in the last two years: e-invoicing, bank checks, AI tools, new rules?**
    Why it matters: shows what the expert watches, which feeds the "what changes fast" list.

20. **Tell me about the hardest collection you ever made. What worked in the end?**
    Why it matters: a hard story reveals the expert's judgement under pressure and the lines they would not cross.

21. **What do you never put in writing to a customer or supplier?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's limits on threats, accusations and legal wording.

22. **When do you hand a case to an accountant, solicitor or collection agency?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent exactly where its work stops and a qualified professional takes over.
