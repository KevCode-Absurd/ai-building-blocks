# Training interview: application security engineer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, real findings under confidentiality, secrets and anything that would help someone attack a real system; describe systems in general terms.

1. **Which stacks, frameworks and kinds of apps do you know best, and what work do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and its limits.

2. **How do you confirm a client owns a system and agree scope before you start?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's real ownership and authorisation routine.

3. **What goes into the written authorisation you ask for before any scan or test?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a practical scope checklist.

4. **When you open a codebase for a security review, where do you look first, and why?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's order of value, not a textbook list.

5. **Tell me about an access control bug you found. How did you spot it, and how did you prove the fix?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's habit of testing one user against another's data.

6. **How do you tell a real static analysis finding from a false positive?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent to treat tools as leads.

7. **How do you rank a long list of dependency findings? What do you look at besides the CVSS score?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert uses KEV, EPSS and reachability in practice.

8. **Tell me about an update that broke something. What would you do differently?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent concrete safe-update habits.

9. **Walk me through how you handle a leaked key, from the moment it is found.**
   Why it matters: records the order of revoke, rotate, check logs and prevent.

10. **How do you make sure you never see or repeat a secret while helping?**
    Why it matters: aligns the agent with the never-in-chat rule.

11. **How do you roll out a Content Security Policy or rate limits without locking out real users?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's report-only and soft-mode routine.

12. **What password storage and session settings do you set by default, and how do you upgrade old hashes?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent current, practical defaults.

13. **How do you run a threat model with a small team that has never done one?**
    Why it matters: records a lightweight method that works with non-specialists.

14. **What do you check differently in code written largely by AI, or in apps that call an AI model?**
    Why it matters: prepares the agent for the most common new review requests.

15. **How do you help a small company answer an enterprise customer's security questionnaire honestly?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to answer from evidence and avoid overclaiming.

16. **What do you put in a security.txt and disclosure policy, and who should read the reports?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent practical disclosure set-up.

17. **When a client asks you to test a partner's or competitor's site, what do you say?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert refuses while staying helpful.

18. **What do you never change in production without asking again, even with full access?**
    Why it matters: aligns the agent with the fresh-yes rule for production changes.

19. **How do you explain severity to a founder who is not technical?**
    Why it matters: captures plain-language habits for reports.

20. **What do clients misunderstand most about application security?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent plain explanations for common misbeliefs.

21. **Tell me about a hard engagement. What went wrong, and what did you learn?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a realistic story and lessons.

22. **What do you check before you hand over any report or fix?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's personal quality bar on top of the role's.
