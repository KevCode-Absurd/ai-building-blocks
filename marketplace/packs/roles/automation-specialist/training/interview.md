# Training interview: automation specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, keys and anything under a confidentiality agreement.

1. **Which tools do you build in most (Zapier, Make, n8n, Power Automate, Airtable, Notion, scripts), and for what kinds of businesses?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the stacks it knows well.

2. **Walk me through the last automation you built, from the first call to the day it went live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where time goes.

3. **How do you decide whether a task should be automated at all?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's filter for judgement, risk and waste.

4. **How do you choose between Zapier, Make, n8n and Power Automate for a client?**
   Why it matters: records real tool-choice reasons beyond price.

5. **What is in your field map, and what do you always check about dates, phone numbers and blanks?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's data-shape habits.

6. **How do you stop duplicates?**
   Why it matters: makes dedupe keys and find-before-create routine.

7. **Tell me about an automation that failed silently. How was it found, and what did you change?**
   Why it matters: adds a real failure pattern and its alerting fix.

8. **How do you set up alerts so a real person notices problems the same day?**
   Why it matters: shapes the agent's error paths and alert routing.

9. **Where do you insist on a human approval step?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's line on money, deletions and customer messages.

10. **When do you write a code step, and what do you never put in one?**
    Why it matters: captures code discipline and secret handling.

11. **How do you estimate task, credit or run usage before go-live? Tell me about a time it went over.**
    Why it matters: makes plan limits part of every design.

12. **How do you test an automation before switching it on?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a real test list.

13. **How much access do you ask for in a client's tools, and what limits do you agree before making live changes?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's three access levels in practice.

14. **How do you hand over an automation so it survives you or the client's staff leaving?**
    Why it matters: covers ownership, connections and documentation.

15. **What do you do when a client wants to message all their contacts automatically?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert handles consent and regional rules.

16. **How do you use AI steps or AI agents in automations, and where do they go wrong?**
    Why it matters: sets rules for AI actions, review and limits.

17. **Tell me about fixing an automation someone else built. What did you look at first?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a diagnosis order.

18. **What does your monthly check of a client's automations look like?**
    Why it matters: shapes the health-check workflow around real practice.

19. **Tell me about a job where the client wanted the wrong thing. How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to push back with care.

20. **What do you wish every small business knew before they automate?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's core advice in plain words.
