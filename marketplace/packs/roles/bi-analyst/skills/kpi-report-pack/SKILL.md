# KPI report pack

## When to use

- A monthly or weekly management report is due and the numbers are in the client's files or BI tool.
- A board, investor or partner meeting needs a pack a week ahead.
- An existing report has grown too long and nobody reads past page two.

## What you need

1. The agreed metric cards (or use the `metric-definition-sheet` skill first).
2. The latest figures and at least six previous periods, from the BI tool (level 1) or exports.
3. Targets the owner has set, if any. Never set targets yourself.
4. Known events in the period: closures, price changes, a new system, a big client lost or won.
5. Who reads it and what decisions are on the agenda.

## Steps

### 1. Choose the five to seven numbers

Start from the goal and the KPI tree. Keep at least one lagging result (revenue, profit, attended appointments) and one or two leading drivers (bookings on the books, leads, pipeline). Everything else goes to the appendix.

### 2. Pull and tie out

Pull each number for the current period and six earlier periods. Tie the headline revenue or volume figure to a source total the owner trusts. If a number cannot be tied out, mark it "provisional" on the page.

### 3. Give each number context

For each: value, unit, change from last period, change from the same period last year where seasonal, and target if the owner set one. Show a six-period trend as a small line. Never present a number alone.

### 4. Set the status

For each number choose one status and give the reason in one sentence:
- **Good:** on or ahead of target or trend, no concern.
- **Watch:** moving the wrong way, or close to a limit; no action yet.
- **Act:** a decision or action is needed now.

Status needs a word or symbol, not only colour.

### 5. Write the comments

One plain sentence per number: what happened and the most likely reason, with the evidence. "Utilisation fell to 61% because Room 2 was closed for nine days for repairs" beats "Utilisation decreased". If the reason is a guess, say so.

### 6. Decisions and risks

List the top three decisions the readers need to make, each with options and a recommendation where the data supports one. List the risks that could move the numbers next period. For a board pack, add an agenda and send-by date.

### 7. Forecast only when there is enough history

Add a simple forecast with a range only if there are enough periods to show a pattern; otherwise say there is not enough history yet. Hand anything more complex to the data analyst kit's forecasting skill.

### 8. Deliver (level 2 or 3)

At level 2, save the pack as a draft in the agreed folder or as an unsent scheduled delivery. At level 3, send or schedule it to the agreed readers only. Anyone new on the list needs the owner's yes first.

## Checks

- One page for the main report; detail in the appendix.
- Every number has a unit, a comparison and a status with a reason.
- Headline figures tie to a trusted source, or are marked provisional.
- Every estimate is marked; no target or benchmark was invented.
- Comments say what happened and why, not just "up" or "down".
- Definitions are the agreed ones; any change since last period is footnoted.
- The pack is readable in black and white.

## Output format

1. Title, period, date sent, data as of
2. Headline table: metric, value, change, trend, target, status, comment
3. Top three decisions
4. Risks for next period
5. Appendix: detail tables, definitions changed, data notes
6. For board packs: agenda and send-by date

## Example of good output

*Made-up business, for illustration only: "Brightwell Dental", two practices.*

> **October 2026 — data as of 3 Nov, 07:00**
>
> | Metric | October | vs Sep | vs Oct 2025 | Target | Status | Comment |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | Revenue, net of refunds | £182,400 | +3.1% | +6.8% | £180,000 | Good | Hygienist hours added in Practice B filled within two weeks. |
> | Appointments, attended | 2,214 | −1.2% | +4.0% | 2,250 | Watch | Two dentist sick days (40 slots); no sign of lower demand. |
> | New patient bookings | 118 | −19% | −11% | owner to set | Act | Website booking form broke 9–16 Oct (24 form errors logged). |
> | Utilisation, chair hours | 81% | −2 pts | +3 pts | 85% | Watch | Practice A Tuesdays at 64%. |
>
> **Decision 1:** Fund a form-monitoring check for the booking page (recommended) or accept manual checks.
