# Training interview: business intelligence (BI) analyst

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names and any personal data; describe businesses and datasets in general terms.

1. **Which BI tools have you built in for clients, and which do you reach for first for a small business?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's tool defaults and where its advice stops.

2. **Walk me through the last dashboard you delivered, from the first conversation to the day it went live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the time goes.

3. **When a client says "I want a dashboard", what are the first three questions you ask?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert turns a vague request into decisions and questions.

4. **How do you decide which five to seven numbers make the cut, and what do you do with the rest?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a selection method rather than a long list.

5. **Tell me about a metric two teams counted differently. How did you settle it?**
   Why it matters: records a real conflict-resolution habit, including who decides.

6. **What goes on your metric card, and which field do clients most often leave blank?**
   Why it matters: shows the expert's definition standard and the usual gaps.

7. **How do you check the grain of a table you have never seen?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the modelling habit that prevents double counting.

8. **When do you break the star schema on purpose?**
   Why it matters: teaches judgement, not just rules.

9. **Which DAX, calculated-field or SQL patterns do you reuse on almost every job?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent tested building blocks in the tool's own syntax.

10. **How do you prove a dashboard number is right before anyone sees it?**
    Why it matters: captures the tie-out routine and tolerances.

11. **Tell me about the worst mismatch you have chased. What was the cause and how long did it take to find?**
    Why it matters: adds real causes to the agent's checklist, in the order they usually appear.

12. **How do you set up who sees what, and how do you test it?**
    Why it matters: covers row-level security, credentials and the mistakes that leak data.

13. **What refresh and freshness settings do you use, and who gets told when a refresh fails?**
    Why it matters: makes the agent plan for stale data instead of discovering it.

14. **How do you lay out a page? What sits top left and why?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's design rules and how they handle colour and accessibility.

15. **When has a metric turned into a target and started to mislead? What did you add to catch it?**
    Why it matters: teaches guardrail metrics from experience.

16. **How do you handle a client who wants a number shown in a way that flatters it?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's honesty line and the words to use.

17. **How do you use AI features such as Copilot or Gemini in your BI work, and what do you never trust them with?**
    Why it matters: sets the boundary between drafting and checked output.

18. **What do you hand over at the end so the client can run it without you?**
    Why it matters: defines the handover pack and the owner's responsibilities.

19. **What access do you ask for on day one, and when do you ask for more?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's level 1, 2 and 3 requests in practice.

20. **What does a dashboard that is actually used look like six months later?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a picture of success beyond go-live.
