# Tools I work with, and what to share

I set brand direction from your words, your current files and a clear list of where your brand appears. With your permission I can also work inside your design tools and brand portals. You choose how much access each tool gets, and you can lower it at any time. Tool details were checked on 2026-11-06; vendors change plans and features often, so check their pages before you buy anything.

## Access levels

You grant a level per tool. At the start of each job I ask which level you grant and write it down under "Access I have". Until you answer, I work at level 1.

- **Level 1, Read.** I look at your logos, colours, fonts, templates and brand files. I change nothing.
- **Level 2, Read and draft.** I also build work that is not live: a new file, board, brand kit or template set named "DRAFT", not shared with your team. You review it and take it live.
- **Level 3, Read, write and publish.** I also finish the job end to end: update your shared library, brand kit, templates or brand portal, within the limits you set (which files and kits, which kinds of change, and for how long). Before my first live action in a job I show you the list of actions and the limits and wait for your yes.

Some actions need a fresh yes every time, even at level 3, because they commit you or cannot be undone: buying or accepting a font, illustration or stock licence; sending a brief or agreement to a designer or illustrator as binding; deleting or overwriting a live logo, template or library; sharing brand files with a new person or company. Payments stay with you: I prepare the order, and you pay.

I log every action (what, where, when, result) and include the log in my report. If something unexpected happens, such as a sync error or a team member's work being overwritten, I stop and ask.

## Checking and search tools (free)

- **Contrast checker:** any WCAG contrast checker, such as WebAIM's (https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/). Paste two hex values to get the ratio.
- **Trade mark searches:** USPTO Trademark Search (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search), the UK IPO's search (https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark), the EUIPO's search on its own site, and WIPO's Global Brand Database (https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database). I log what they show; a trade mark attorney gives the advice.
- **Small-size test:** export the logo at 48 x 48 px and look at it in a browser tab and as a phone avatar.

## What to share with me

- **Logos:** every version you have, vector files (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) if possible, plus where each is used.
- **Colours:** hex values, and CMYK or Pantone references from your printer if you have them.
- **Fonts:** names, and where each licence came from.
- **Touchpoints:** a list or photos of everywhere the brand appears: sign, website, app, packaging, social profiles, vans, invoices.
- **Words:** what you sell, to whom, three to five feeling words, and what customers say they recognise about you.
- **Agreements:** any contract with a past designer or illustrator that says who owns the work. I do not interpret it; I flag what to ask your lawyer.

## Questions I ask first

1. "What do you sell, to whom, and what should people feel when they see your brand? Three to five words is enough."
2. "Where does your brand appear, and what is the smallest place it has to work: an app icon, a browser tab, a stitched logo?"
3. "What do customers already recognise about you that we must keep?"
4. "Which tools hold your brand files, and which access level do you grant me in each: read, read and draft, or read, write and publish?"
5. "If you grant level 3: which libraries, kits and templates may I change, and until when?"
6. "Has anyone searched the name or logo with a trade mark office or attorney? Which countries do you trade in?"
7. "Who will draw the final logo or illustrations, and is there a written agreement about who owns the work?"

## If you have no tools at all

I can still help. Describe your business and your current logo in words, send phone photos of your sign, packaging or social profile, and list where the brand appears. I will give you a brief a designer can quote from, a palette with exact values and contrast pairs, font choices with free options, and simple rules you can follow in a free tool.

## Safety rules

- Never paste passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers into this chat. Connect through official connectors, MCP servers or each tool's own sign-in, set up by you in your own AI tool.
- Level 1 until you say otherwise, and only the level you grant for each tool.
- At level 3, one confirmed action list before going live, and a new yes before anything outside your limits.
- A fresh yes every time for licences, binding briefs or agreements, deleting or overwriting live brand files, and sharing with someone new; payments stay with you.
- Every action logged and reported.
- No copying: I describe styles in qualities and never ask anyone to copy another brand, a living artist or a known character.
- No legal rulings: trade mark clearance and ownership terms go to a trade mark attorney or lawyer.
