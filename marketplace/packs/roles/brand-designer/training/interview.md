# Interview: brand identity designer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the designer's work (for example, mascot questions for someone who only writes brand books).

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your last identity project from the first call to the files being handed over.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and where the time actually goes.
2. **What do you insist on knowing before you start, and what do you do when the client cannot answer?**
   Why it matters: discovery is where most wrong turns start, and how an expert fills gaps is reusable.
3. **How do you turn a client's feeling words into design choices? Give me an example where the words pointed one way and you went another.**
   Why it matters: captures the reasoning that links strategy to shapes and colours.
4. **How do you decide what to keep in a refresh?**
   Why it matters: protecting recognition is one of the role's highest-value judgements.

## Logos and systems

5. **What checks do you put logo drafts through, and which one kills the most drafts?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's draft checklist, in the order that matters.
6. **How do you test a mark at small sizes? Tell me about one that failed late.**
   Why it matters: small-size failures are common and expensive to fix after launch.
7. **How do you set clear space and minimum sizes, and how do you explain them so people follow them?**
   Why it matters: rules that people ignore are useless; their wording habits matter.
8. **How do you build a palette, and what do you do when the brand colour fails contrast?**
   Why it matters: shows how they balance brand and accessibility with clients.
9. **How do you choose and license fonts? Has a licence ever caused a problem?**
   Why it matters: font licences are a quiet source of risk in logos and apps.
10. **Do you hand developers design tokens? In what format, and what goes wrong?**
   Why it matters: tokens are how brand decisions reach products, and formats are changing.

## Illustration, mascots and icons

11. **How do you brief an illustrator so the style is clear without naming another artist?**
   Why it matters: describing style in qualities is a core skill and a copyright safeguard.
12. **What goes into your mascot or character sheets, and how do you keep a character consistent over years?**
   Why it matters: characters are long-lived assets that drift without rules.
13. **How do you specify an icon set so it looks like one family?**
   Why it matters: system rules are what make sets consistent across many hands.

## Rights, names and AI

14. **When do you check names and marks with the trade mark offices, and when do you hand over to an attorney?**
   Why it matters: sets the line between a designer's first look and legal clearance.
15. **How do you handle ownership of the work with clients, designers and illustrators?**
   Why it matters: missing ownership terms can leave a client without their own logo.
16. **Where do you use AI image tools in brand work, and where do you refuse to?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's boundaries given the ownership limits on AI-made art.

## Clients and judgement

17. **Tell me about a client who wanted something that would hurt the brand, such as copying a competitor or adding every colour they liked. What did you do?**
   Why it matters: teaches how to hold the line and keep the client.
18. **How do you get a team to actually use the brand book?**
   Why it matters: the deliverable only works if people follow it.
19. **How do you know an identity worked a year later?**
   Why it matters: links the craft to outcomes the client can see.
20. **What is the hardest brand job you have done, and what would you do differently now?**
   Why it matters: a real story carries judgement that no checklist holds.
