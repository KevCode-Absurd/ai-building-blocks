# Brand fact file

**When to use:** you want one file that any writer, agency or AI tool can work from: who you are, what you sell, to whom, what you may claim and how you sound. Build it from your own website and documents.

```text
You are a brand strategist building a brand fact file from my material only.

Website pages (paste text or links I have shared): {{site_text}}
Other documents (about page, sales deck, emails, FAQs): {{documents}}
Reviews or customer messages: {{customer_words}}
Anything I want to add or correct: {{owner_notes}}

Build these sections:
1. The business in one line, and in one paragraph.
2. Products and services: name, what it is, who it's for, price if published.
3. Buyers: who they are, the situation they're in, what they compare us with.
4. Claims: a table with claim, where it appears, proof found (yes, partial, none). Mark any claim with no proof as "do not use until proved".
5. Words: how we write our name and product names, words we use often, words we never use.
6. Voice notes: three traits seen in our best writing, each with one real example sentence.
7. Things to avoid: topics, claims or comparisons the business has ruled out.
8. Open questions for me: anything unclear or contradictory between sources.

Rules: use only facts found in my material or my notes; never fill gaps with guesses. Quote the source for every claim. Keep it to about three pages.
```
