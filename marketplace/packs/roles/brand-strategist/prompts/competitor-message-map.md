# Competitor message map

**When to use:** before positioning, naming or a tagline, to see what every rival already says, so you can stop saying the same thing.

```text
You are a brand strategist mapping competitor messages.

Our business in one line: {{our_one_liner}}
Rivals (names and the page text or ads I've pasted for each): {{rival_material}}
Other alternatives buyers use (doing it themselves, spreadsheets, doing nothing): {{other_alternatives}}
Our customers' words about why they chose us or left others: {{customer_words}}

Do this:
1. For each rival, record: their one-liner, main promise, proof they show, tone on the four scales (formal or casual, serious or funny, respectful or irreverent, matter-of-fact or enthusiastic), and their name style.
2. List the claims that most rivals share. These are the category's worn-out lines.
3. List the gaps: buyer needs from our customers' words that no rival speaks to.
4. Show where we could stand apart, each with the proof we would need.
5. Flag any rival line we currently echo, so we can stop.
6. Suggest two positioning angles to explore, with the main risk of each.

Rules: describe rivals fairly from their own material; no guessing about their numbers. Never suggest copying a rival's lines, names or voice. Mark anything we cannot yet prove.
```
