# Interview: brand strategist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work: a naming specialist may skip voice questions, and a voice specialist may skip naming ones.

## Positioning and messaging

1. **Walk me through a recent positioning job, from the first conversation to the final one-liner.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **What evidence do you insist on before you write a positioning statement, and what do you do when the client has none?**
   Why it matters: evidence is the gap between expert work and generic AI work.
3. **How do you find a business's real alternative? Tell me about a time it surprised the client.**
   Why it matters: the alternative usually decides the positioning.
4. **How do you stop a client from claiming a difference rivals also have?**
   Why it matters: tests their honesty and their method for proof.
5. **Show me a messaging hierarchy you're proud of. How did teams use it?**
   Why it matters: becomes the output format and the rollout advice.

## Naming

6. **How do you write a naming brief, and what is always in it?**
   Why it matters: a good brief is half the naming job.
7. **How many names do you generate, from which kinds, and how do you cut them?**
   Why it matters: captures the generation and cutting method.
8. **What screens do you run before a client sees a shortlist?**
   Why it matters: becomes the checklist in the naming skill.
9. **Tell me about a name a client loved that you had to talk them out of.**
   Why it matters: shows how they hold a line with clients.
10. **How do you handle trade mark and domain checks, and where does your job stop and the attorney's start?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent inside safe limits.
11. **How do you decide whether a business should rename at all?**
    Why it matters: renames are costly and often wrong; the judgement matters.

## Voice

12. **How do you find a brand's voice in its existing writing? What do you look for first?**
    Why it matters: the extraction method is the core of the voice skill.
13. **What makes a voice guide get used rather than ignored?**
    Why it matters: sets the length and format rules.
14. **How should tone change for a complaint, a refund or an outage? Give me real examples.**
    Why it matters: hard moments are where voices break.
15. **How do you check a new writer's or an AI tool's copy against the guide?**
    Why it matters: becomes the voice-and-message-check method.

## Claims, tools and testing

16. **Which claims do you push back on most often, and how do you word the honest version?**
    Why it matters: claims rules differ by country and the agent must catch risky ones.
17. **How do you test names, taglines or one-liners with real buyers? What do you ask?**
    Why it matters: replaces office votes with evidence.
18. **Which tools do you use for research, writing rules and testing, and what do you ask clients to share?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.
19. **What do you never do without the client's explicit go-ahead?**
    Why it matters: sets the approval points and the fresh-yes habits.

## A hard job

20. **Tell me about the hardest brand job you've done: the brief, the conflict, what you recommended and what happened.**
    Why it matters: stories show judgement that checklists miss.
21. **Which piece of your work aged badly, and why?**
    Why it matters: shows what the expert has learned about lasting choices.
