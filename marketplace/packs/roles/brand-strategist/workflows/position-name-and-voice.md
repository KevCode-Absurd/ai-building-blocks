# Position, name and voice

**Trigger:** a new business or product needs a name and a voice, or an existing brand is considering a rename or a full message reset.

**Time:** usually two to six weeks, most of it waiting for customer evidence, test results and trade mark advice.

## Steps

1. **Intake (playbook intake questions; access.md "Questions I ask first").** Confirm what is in scope (positioning, name, tagline, voice), who decides, the deadline, the markets and languages, and the access level for each tool. Until the client answers, work at level 1.
2. **Gather evidence (level 1).** Reviews, five to ten customer conversations or call notes recorded with consent, lost-deal reasons, the client's best and worst writing, rivals' material.
3. **Map rivals (prompt: competitor-message-map).** What every rival says, the worn-out lines, and the gaps.
4. **Position (skill: positioning-and-messaging).** Alternatives, differences with proof, best-fit buyers, frame, positioning statement, one-liner, messaging hierarchy.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the positioning and one-liner. Do not start naming until this is agreed, because the name has to carry it.
5. **For a rename only: weigh what the current name earns.** Recognition, search traffic, reviews, signage, contracts. Present keep, adjust or change, with reasons.
   **Approval point 2:** the client decides whether to rename.
6. **Name (skill: name-generation-and-screening).** Brief, long list, cuts, screens, first-look searches, shortlist and recommendation.
7. **Test (prompt: message-test-plan).** Test the top two or three names or one-liners with real buyers for understanding.
   **Approval point 3:** the client picks the name to send for trade mark clearance.
8. **Clearance (client and their trade mark attorney).** The attorney clears the name in each market. Filing, company or business name registration, domains and handles each need a fresh yes, and the client pays.
9. **Tagline (prompt: tagline-set).** Optional, once the name is cleared.
10. **Voice (skill: voice-guide).** Traits, examples, word lists, tone by situation, fact sheet, rewrites.
    **Approval point 4:** the client approves the voice guide.
11. **Roll out.** At level 2, draft updated page copy, email templates and bios as unpublished drafts. At level 3, publish approved copy on the pages and channels the client named. The public launch of a new name or tagline, and any handle change, needs a fresh yes every time.
12. **Hand over the visual side.** Brief the brand identity designer (kit id brand-designer) with the positioning, name, voice and where the brand appears.

## Outputs

- Competitor message map.
- Positioning statement, one-liner and messaging hierarchy.
- Rename recommendation (if relevant).
- Naming brief, long list, shortlist with screens, test results and recommendation.
- Tagline set (optional).
- Voice guide and brand fact file.
- Rollout log of drafts and published changes.
- Brief for the visual identity work.

## Needs the client's approval

- Positioning and one-liner (point 1).
- Whether to rename (point 2).
- The name sent for clearance (point 3).
- The voice guide (point 4).
- A fresh yes every time for trade mark filing, company or business name registration, domain purchases, handle changes, the public launch of a name or tagline, and any binding brief to an agency. Money stays with the client.
