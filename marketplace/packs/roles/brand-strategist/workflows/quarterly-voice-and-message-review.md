# Quarterly voice and message review

**Cadence:** every quarter, and after any big launch, new agency or new AI writing tool.

## Steps

1. **Collect a sample (level 1).** Homepage and two key pages, the last three marketing emails, ten recent social posts, five support replies, and the current sales deck or proposal template.
2. **Check the touchpoints (skill: voice-and-message-check).** Mark lines pass, fix or fail; build the drift table showing how each touchpoint describes the business; list every one-liner variant found.
3. **Check claims.** List every objective claim seen, with the proof held. Flag unproven superlatives, out-of-date numbers and environmental words for review against current rules.
4. **Find patterns.** Group repeated slips by channel or writer, and note any new product names that break the naming rules.
5. **Propose fixes.** Rewrites for failing lines, guide updates (new examples, new avoid words), and template changes.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the fixes and guide updates.
6. **Apply (level 2 or 3).** At level 2, leave suggested edits and draft revised copy. At level 3, publish the approved fixes on the pages and channels named, and update the guide and any writing-tool style rules, within the client's limits. Any change to the brand name, tagline or a public claim needs a fresh yes every time.
7. **Refresh the brief for AI tools.** Update the brand fact file (prompt: brand-fact-file) so writers and AI tools work from current facts.
8. **Report.** One page: what drifted, what was fixed, what changed in the guide, and what to watch next quarter.

## Outputs

- Line-by-line check and drift table.
- Claims list with proof status.
- Pattern summary.
- Approved rewrites and guide updates.
- Updated brand fact file.
- Quarterly one-page report and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Fixes and guide updates before they are applied (point 1).
- Publishing at level 3: only the approved fixes, on the pages and channels named.
- A fresh yes every time for changes to the brand name, tagline or a public claim.
- Claims in regulated areas: checked by a qualified adviser first.
