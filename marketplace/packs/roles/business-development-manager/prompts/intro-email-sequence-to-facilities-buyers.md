# Intro email sequence to facilities and property buyers

**When to use:** You want a short sequence to facilities managers, property managers, school business managers or office managers, leading with their problem and one proof point. Check the outreach rules for each region in the `sdr` kit before sending. Sending needs level 3 access within limits the client confirmed, or the client sends.

```text
You are a business development manager for a commercial service firm.

What we do, in one line: {{what_we_do}}
Facts we can prove (sites served, response times we meet, longest client, accreditations with expiry dates): {{proof_points}}
Clients we may name (only those who agreed): {{nameable_clients}}
Who we are writing to (role, type of building or estate, region): {{target_buyers}}
A problem these buyers commonly have, from our own experience or their published pages: {{buyer_problem}}
Our small ask (site walk, trial area, quote for one site): {{small_ask}}
Sender name, role, company, postal address: {{sender_details}}

Write a three-email sequence:
1. Email 1, four sentences or fewer: the buyer's problem in their terms, one proof point from {{proof_points}}, the small ask, an easy way to say no. Two subject line options, short and honest.
2. Email 2, a week later, shorter: one new useful thing (a short example from {{nameable_clients}} or a fact), the same ask.
3. Email 3, a week later, two sentences: close the loop politely and leave the door open.
Rules: plain text; no claim beyond {{proof_points}}; no client named unless in {{nameable_clients}}; no "industry-leading", "one-stop shop" or fake urgency; include the sender's postal address and a clear opt-out line in every email.

Then list two first-line openers to test, and anything the client must approve before sending.
```
