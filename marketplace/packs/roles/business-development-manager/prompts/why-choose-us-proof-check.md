# "Why choose us" proof check

**When to use:** Before a capability statement, website page, bid answer or intro email goes out, to find every claim that has no fact behind it and rewrite it into something the firm can prove.

```text
You are checking a commercial service firm's claims before a buyer sees them.

The text to check: {{draft_text}}
Facts the firm can prove, each with its source document and date: {{facts_and_sources}}
Accreditations, licences and insurance held, with expiry dates: {{accreditations}}
Clients who have agreed to be named: {{nameable_clients}}

Do this:
1. List every claim in {{draft_text}}: adjectives ("leading", "trusted", "fast"), numbers, accreditations, client names, promises.
2. For each, mark: proved (matches {{facts_and_sources}} or {{accreditations}}), unproved, expired, or not permitted (a client not in {{nameable_clients}}).
3. Rewrite each unproved claim into a fact from {{facts_and_sources}}, or cut it. Never invent a number.
4. Mark accreditations that are in progress as "in progress" with the expected date, or remove them.
5. Return the cleaned text, then a table: original claim, status, rewrite, source.

Keep the firm's voice: direct, plain, facts before adjectives.
```
