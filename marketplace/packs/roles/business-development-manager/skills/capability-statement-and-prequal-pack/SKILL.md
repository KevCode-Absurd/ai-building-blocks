# Capability statement and pre-qualification pack

Buyers want three answers fast: can you do it, have you done it, and will you turn up. A capability statement answers them on one page. The pre-qualification pack proves the paperwork behind it. This skill writes both from the firm's own facts and shows the gaps plainly.

## When to use

- A firm is moving from homeowners or one-off jobs to commercial contracts.
- A buyer, housing provider or council has sent a supplier questionnaire or document list.
- The firm wants to join an approved supplier list or framework.
- The current statement is full of adjectives and short on facts.

## What you need

**Access:** level 1 to read the firm's records, certificates and the buyer's list. Level 2 to write the statement and checklist in a shared folder. Level 3 to file final versions and keep the expiry list current within the client's limits. Submitting any questionnaire, registration or declaration on a buyer's portal needs a fresh yes every time and the named account holder's sign-in.

- Services, team size, equipment, areas covered, working hours and response times the firm actually meets.
- Legal name, registration number, address, contact.
- Insurance schedules and certificates, accreditations, licences and policies, each with its expiry date.
- Past commercial work, with which clients agreed to be named.
- The buyer's document list or questionnaire, if there is one.

## Steps

### Capability statement

1. **Collect the facts.** List everything the firm can prove, with its source document. Separate facts from hopes. Anything in progress gets its expected date.
2. **Write one line on what you do.** Who you serve and the result, in plain words: "Grounds maintenance for schools, offices and housing estates across the West Midlands."
3. **List core services.** Short list, in the buyer's terms (a facilities manager says "reactive repairs", not "handyman services").
4. **Write "why choose us".** Each point is a fact: years trading, sites served each week, response times you meet, how long your longest client has stayed, staff vetting you actually do. Cut any line with no fact behind it.
5. **Add past work.** Named clients only with recorded permission. Otherwise describe them: "a 12-site housing provider, three years running". Give scope and result in one line each.
6. **Add company facts.** Registration number, insurance cover and levels, accreditations with expiry dates, areas covered, contact. For US federal buyers, add the UEI from SAM registration and the NAICS codes that match the services. For public buyers anywhere, use their category names where they publish them.
7. **Fit it on one page.** Labelled blocks, readable on a phone, printable. Contact details at the bottom.

### Pre-qualification pack

8. **Build the list.** Start from the buyer's own list. If there is none, use the common set: company registration, insurance certificates (in the UK, employers' liability at the legal minimum of £5 million, plus public liability and professional indemnity at the buyer's levels), health and safety policy, risk assessments and method statements, any accreditation the buyer names, data protection and security (for IT firms in the UK, often Cyber Essentials), quality, equality and environmental policies, accounts, references and staff vetting.
9. **Fill the table.** Document, who holds it, expiry date, status (held, expiring soon, missing), action, owner.
10. **Order the gaps.** Gaps that would stop the application go first, then those that would lose marks, then nice-to-haves. Add the lead time to close each one.
11. **Check expiry dates.** Anything expiring before the buyer's decision date is flagged now.
12. **Prepare, don't submit.** Fill drafts where the portal allows. [Fresh yes] Submitting, registering or signing any declaration needs the client's yes for that submission, from their named user.

## Checks

- Every "why choose us" line has a fact and a source.
- Clients are named only with recorded permission.
- No accreditation, licence or insurance is listed that the documents do not show; in-progress items say "in progress" with a date.
- Insurance levels in the pack match the buyer's stated levels, not a general guide.
- Every certificate shows its expiry date.
- No legal reading of the buyer's terms; questions go to the client's lawyer or the buyer's named contact.
- Words to cut: "industry-leading", "one-stop shop", "unrivalled", "turnkey solutions".

## Output format

1. Capability statement (one page): what we do, core services, why choose us, past work, company facts, contact.
2. Pre-qualification table: document, holder, expiry, status, action, owner.
3. Gaps in order, with lead times.
4. Questions for the client, marked `[NEED: ...]`.

## Example of good output

*Illustrative structure, not a real business: an 11-person grounds maintenance firm. Copy the shape, never the content: every fact comes from the client's own context.*

**[Business] Ltd: capability statement**

**What we do.** Grounds maintenance for schools, offices and housing estates within 25 miles of Stafford.

**Core services.** Grass cutting and edging; hedge and shrub care; litter picks; winter gritting; small landscaping works.

**Why choose us**
- 34 sites maintained every week (rota, October).
- Gritting call-outs attended within 3 hours last winter (job log).
- Longest client: a secondary school, 6 years and renewed twice.
- All staff hold a current DBS check (HR file).

**Past work.** A 9-block housing estate: weekly grounds care since 2023. [School name] Primary School (named with written permission): grounds and gritting since 2020.

**Company facts.** Company no. [NEED]. Employers' liability £5m; public liability £5m (schedule to 31 March). Cyber Essentials: not held. ISO 14001: in progress, audit booked for February.

**Pre-qualification table (extract)**

| Document | Holder | Expiry | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employers' liability certificate | Office manager | 31 Mar | Held | Display electronically |
| Public liability (buyer asks £10m) | Office manager | 31 Mar | Gap: cover is £5m | Ask broker for a quote; stops the application |
| Health and safety policy | Director | Reviewed yearly | Held, last review 14 months ago | Review and re-sign |
| Safety scheme named by buyer | Director | n/a | Missing | Check lead time with the scheme |
