# Training interview: business development manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share buyers' names, contract prices, partner terms or portal logins; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of firms do you open accounts for, and which buyers do they sell to?**
   Why it matters: sets the trades, buyers and paperwork the agent must know.

2. **How do you decide which accounts are worth chasing? Show me the rules, written or unwritten.**
   Why it matters: the agent needs the expert's account rules, not a generic list.

3. **Walk me through the first month of opening a new market, week by week.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where time goes.

4. **Show me a capability statement that worked. What made a buyer keep it?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a model of the one page buyers value.

5. **Which claims do you cut from a capability statement, and what do you replace them with?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent the expert's proof standard.

6. **Which documents do buyers in your market ask for before a quote? Which gaps stop applications most often?**
   Why it matters: builds the agent's pre-qualification checklist from experience.

7. **Tell me about an approved supplier list you got onto, or failed to. What made the difference?**
   Why it matters: real stories show which paperwork and relationships count.

8. **How do you research a target account before the first contact? Which sources do you trust?**
   Why it matters: the agent must research from facts and date them.

9. **What does your first email to a facilities or property manager look like, and what gets replies?**
   Why it matters: sets the tone and structure the agent copies.

10. **What do you ask on a site walk, and what have you learned to ask after a quote went wrong?**
    Why it matters: site walk questions protect the quote and the margin.

11. **How do you use MEDDICC, or another checklist, on a live deal? Which letter do teams most often fake?**
    Why it matters: the agent must use qualification as questions, not theatre.

12. **How do you find the person who actually signs, when your contact is friendly but not senior?**
    Why it matters: an unknown economic buyer is a common reason deals stall.

13. **Tell me about a partnership that worked and one that did not. What was written down in each?**
    Why it matters: shows the terms and habits that make partnerships produce work.

14. **When you team with another firm on a bid, what do you agree first, and what do you never discuss?**
    Why it matters: the agent must keep prices and bids away from competitor-partners.

15. **How do you handle hospitality, gifts and referral fees with buyers and partners?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's line on bribery risk and the client's policy.

16. **Which CRM fields, stages and notes do you keep current, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent what to log at level 3.

17. **When would you walk away from an account or a tender?**
    Why it matters: good business development drops weak opportunities early.

18. **What would you check in an AI-written capability statement or account plan before trusting it?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's quality bar into the agent's self-check.
