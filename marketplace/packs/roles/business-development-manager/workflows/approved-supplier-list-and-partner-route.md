# Getting on an approved supplier list, alone or with a partner

**Trigger:** A target buyer (a housing provider, council, school group, property manager or large company) buys only from an approved list or framework, or has published a supplier questionnaire; or the work is too big for the firm alone and a partner or prime is needed.

**Cadence:** Per application, working back from the buyer's deadline; partner reviews at the dates agreed in the partnership proposal; expiry checks monthly.

**Access:** At the start, ask which level the client grants for documents, the CRM, email and any buyer portal, and record it under "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1. Steps marked [L2] need read and draft access; [L3] needs read, write and publish access within the client's limits. Steps marked [Fresh yes] need the client's yes every time, even at level 3. Buyer portal logins belong to the client's named user.

## Steps

1. **Read the buyer's requirements.** The document list, questionnaire, scoring and deadline. Note the clarification deadline. For public tenders and portals, follow the `proposal-writer` kit.
2. **Sort what you hold.** Run the `prequal-gap-list` prompt. *Approval: the client confirms the documents and their dates.*
3. **Decide: alone, with a partner or as a subcontractor.** If gaps cannot be closed by the deadline, or the work is too big, consider teaming or subcontracting. Run the `partnership-proposal` skill to score candidates and pick a type. Flag any candidate that could compete for the same work. *Approval: the client chooses the route.*
4. **Approach the partner, if any.** Run the `partner-intro-email` prompt. [L3] Send the approved email to the named contact and log it. Keep prices, bids and territories out of talks with any competitor-partner until the client's lawyer has set boundaries.
5. **Agree terms.** Write the one-page proposal and the terms checklist. *Approval: the client's lawyer reviews.* [Fresh yes] Sending, signing or accepting any partnership, teaming, NDA or referral agreement.
6. **For US federal bids,** check whether the team or joint venture must be disclosed in the offer and whether an SBA programme applies; point the client to SBA and their lawyer for eligibility.
7. **Close the gaps.** Track each gap with its owner and lead time. [L3] Keep the expiry list current in the documents folder.
8. **Prepare the application.** Update the capability statement for this buyer's categories. [L2] Fill drafts where the portal allows saving. Draft answers to any written questions.
9. **Final check with the client.** Show the completed pack, the declarations and anything still missing. [Fresh yes] Submitting the application or any declaration, from the client's named user.
10. **Log and follow up.** [L3] Record the submission, date and contents in the CRM; set a follow-up date. Draft answers to the buyer's clarification questions for approval.
11. **Keep the place.** Monthly, check certificate and insurance expiry dates against the list's renewal rules, and draft renewals. *Approval: the client approves renewals.*

## Outputs

- Requirements table and ordered gap list.
- Route decision: alone, partner, or subcontract, with reasons.
- Partnership proposal and terms checklist, if a partner is used.
- Updated capability statement and completed pack.
- Submission log and follow-up dates.
- Monthly expiry report.

## What needs the client's approval

- The documents and dates listed in the pack.
- The route and any partner approached.
- Every agreement, NDA and referral fee: a fresh yes every time, after the lawyer's review.
- Every submission and declaration on a buyer's portal: a fresh yes every time, from the named user.
