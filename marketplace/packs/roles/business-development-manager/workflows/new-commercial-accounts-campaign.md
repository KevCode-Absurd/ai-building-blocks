# New commercial accounts campaign

**Trigger:** A firm decides to win a new kind of customer (for example, moving from homeowners to facilities and property managers), or the pipeline of new accounts is too thin for next quarter's plan.

**Cadence:** Set-up once over one to two weeks; weekly outreach and visit cycle; monthly account plan review.

**Access:** At the start, ask which level the client grants for the CRM, email, the sales engagement tool, the calendar and the documents folder, and record it under "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1. Steps marked [L2] need read and draft access; [L3] needs read, write and publish access within the client's limits. Steps marked [Fresh yes] need the client's yes every time, even at level 3.

## Steps

### Set-up

1. **Write the capability statement.** Run the `capability-statement-and-prequal-pack` skill, part one, then the `why-choose-us-proof-check` prompt. *Approval: the client approves every fact and every named client.*
2. **Check the paperwork.** Run part two of the same skill for the common documents buyers in the target market ask for. List gaps with lead times. *Approval: the client decides which gaps to close and who pays.*
3. **Write the account rules and list.** Run the `account-plan-and-meddicc` skill, steps 1 and 2. [L2] Create draft target accounts in the CRM. *Approval: the client approves the rules and the top tier.*
4. **Brief the top tier.** Run steps 3 to 8 of the same skill for each top-tier account. [L2] Save briefs and plans as CRM notes.
5. **Write the sequence.** Run the `intro-email-sequence-to-facilities-buyers` prompt. Check region rules in the `sdr` kit. [L2] Build the sequence, paused. *Approval: the client approves every template and the sending limits.*
6. **Confirm the live action list.** [L3] Show the actions and limits: which accounts, which mailbox, which templates, how many new contacts a day, which regions, how long. *Approval: the client's yes.*

### Weekly cycle

7. **Send and respond.** [L3] Enrol approved contacts in the approved sequence; stop anyone who replies or opts out, in every tool, the same day. Draft replies for the client to approve where a reply needs judgement.
8. **Book visits.** [L3] Send booking links, book and confirm site walks in the named calendar.
9. **Prepare and run visits.** Run the `site-walk-and-first-visit` skill for each visit. [L3] Send the approved scope confirmation the same day and log the visit.
10. **Hand to pricing.** Pass the confirmed scope to whoever prices. [Fresh yes] Any quote the client treats as binding, and any discount.
11. **Weekly report.** Contacts reached, replies, visits booked and held, scopes confirmed, quotes sent, and one suggested change. *Approval: changes outside the confirmed limits need a fresh yes.*

### Monthly review

12. **Review top accounts and deals.** Update each account plan; run the `meddicc-deal-review` prompt on every live deal. [L3] Update CRM fields and next steps. *Approval: the client agrees which deals go in the forecast.*

## Outputs

- Capability statement and pre-qualification checklist with gaps.
- Account rules, tiered list, briefs and plans.
- Approved email sequence and a live action list with limits.
- Visit plans, question sheets, scope confirmations and CRM visit notes.
- Weekly reports and monthly deal reviews.
- Action log of every live step.

## What needs the client's approval

- Every fact, named client and template, before use.
- The live action list and limits before the first send.
- Any new list, region, template or mailbox.
- Every binding quote, discount, contract or declaration: a fresh yes every time.
