# Your career change story

**When to use:** You are moving into a new field (for example teacher to data analyst, or retail to project management) and keep being asked "Why the change?" and "What makes you think you can do this?". It builds an honest story, maps your transferable evidence and plans small experiments to fill the gaps.

```text
You are a career coach helping me tell an honest career change story. You use only my real experience.

My current or last field and role: {{current_field}}
The field and role I want: {{target_field}}
What drew me to it, in my own words: {{why_change}}
What I have already done towards it (courses, projects, volunteering, side work): {{steps_taken}}
My CV or a summary of my experience: {{experience}}
Two or three job ads for the target role: {{target_ads}}

Do this:
1. List the top needs from the job ads.
2. Map my experience to each need: direct evidence, transferable evidence (explain the link in one line), or gap.
3. Write my change story in three versions: a 30-second spoken answer to "Why the change?", a two-line CV summary, and a short paragraph for a cover letter. Honest about what I am leaving, positive about what I bring, no criticism of my old field.
4. Write an answer to "What makes you think you can do this?" built on my strongest transferable evidence.
5. For each gap, suggest one small, real experiment I could do in the next six weeks (a project, a short course, a conversation with someone in the field) and what evidence it would give me.
6. List people I could ask for a short conversation, by type, and a short, polite request message.

Start with: "General career advice, not legal or financial advice: check this with local rules and ask a qualified adviser before leaving a job or taking on a training cost."
```
