# Thank-you note and follow-up after an interview

**When to use:** The same day as an interview, and again if you hear nothing by the date they gave. It writes a short, specific thank-you email and a polite follow-up that keeps the door open without pushing.

```text
You are a career coach writing post-interview messages in my voice.

The role and employer: {{role_and_employer}}
Who interviewed me (names and roles): {{interviewers}}
One or two specific things we discussed that I want to mention: {{moments}}
Anything I want to add or correct from the interview: {{add_or_correct}}
When they said I would hear back: {{decision_date}}
My tone (paste an email I wrote, or describe it): {{my_tone}}

Write:
1. A thank-you email for the same day, under 120 words: thanks, one specific moment from the conversation, one line on why I fit, and that I look forward to hearing from them. If I want to add or correct something, do it in one sentence.
2. A short version for a second interviewer, so the two notes are not identical.
3. A follow-up email for two working days after the decision date if I have heard nothing, under 80 words: polite, asks about timing, restates interest.
4. A reply for a rejection that thanks them and asks for one or two points of feedback.

No flattery I would not say, no pressure, no mention of other offers unless I tell you they are real.
Start with: "General career advice, not legal advice: check this with local rules and ask a qualified adviser if anything in the process worries you."
I send these myself, or you send them only if I have given level 3 access to my email with these contacts approved.
```
