# Training interview: Career coach

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, employers, pay figures or stories that could identify a client; anonymise every example.

1. **Who do you coach most: which careers, levels and countries, and what do they usually come to you with?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty, the norms it must know, and where it should refer people on.

2. **Walk me through your first session with a new client. What do you ask, and in what order?**
   Why it matters: the expert's intake order becomes the agent's intake questions.

3. **How do you decide whether someone is ready to apply, or needs to work out their target first?**
   Why it matters: stops the agent from polishing a CV for a search with no direction.

4. **How do you pull good stories out of someone who says they "just did their job"?**
   Why it matters: drawing out evidence is the core skill; the agent needs the expert's questions.

5. **Show me a CV bullet before and after you worked on it. What did you change and why?**
   Why it matters: concrete edits teach the agent the expert's standard better than rules.

6. **What do you refuse to put on a CV, even if the client asks?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's honesty line and how to explain it kindly.

7. **How do you handle a gap, a dismissal or a short stay in a job?**
   Why it matters: honest, brief answers to worries are where clients most need help.

8. **How do CV norms differ between the countries you work with?**
   Why it matters: country norms (photos, dates of birth, length) change what a good CV looks like.

9. **How do you tailor an application without keyword stuffing?**
   Why it matters: shows the expert's line between matching an ad and gaming it.

10. **How do you prepare someone for a competency or values-based interview?**
    Why it matters: structured interviews score evidence against criteria; the expert's prep method becomes the skill.

11. **What do you tell clients about AI-led or recorded video interviews?**
    Why it matters: a fast-changing format the agent must handle with current, practical advice.

12. **How do you run a mock interview, and what feedback do you give?**
    Why it matters: the agent's mock scripts and feedback should follow the expert's style.

13. **What is your advice when an employer asks about current salary?**
    Why it matters: rules differ by place, and the expert's wording helps clients answer calmly.

14. **Walk me through how you prepare someone for an offer call.**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's method for facts, numbers and scripts.

15. **Tell me about a negotiation that went well, and one that went badly. What made the difference?**
    Why it matters: real stories teach the agent what works and what costs offers.

16. **How do you write a LinkedIn headline and About section for someone who sounds like everyone else?**
    Why it matters: the expert's method for finding the one line that sets a person apart.

17. **What do you advise about Open to Work and keeping a search private?**
    Why it matters: privacy settings carry real risk with a current employer.

18. **How do you use AI in your own coaching, and where do you keep it out?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert respects employers' AI rules and keeps the client's voice.

19. **Which job scams or bad actors have your clients met, and how did you spot them?**
    Why it matters: adds real warning signs to the agent's safety checks.

20. **When do you refer a client to someone else, such as an employment lawyer, immigration adviser, counsellor or financial adviser?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent exactly where its work stops.

21. **How do you keep a client going when the rejections pile up?**
    Why it matters: the person's pace and confidence matter as much as the documents.

22. **What does success look like for your clients, and how do you measure it?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's measures beyond the number of applications sent.
