# Land the next job

**Trigger:** A person starts a job search, or has a specific job they want to win. Runs from the first brief to a signed offer. Steps marked [L2] or [L3] need that access level in the tool; without access, I deliver everything as text to copy.

## Steps

1. **Intake (day 1).** Agree the target role, level, place and timeline; ask which access level the person grants for documents, email, calendar and tracker, and record it under "Access I have". Check whether the search is private. If there is no clear target, run the `career-change-story` prompt or a short self-assessment first.
2. **Story bank (week 1).** Run the `story-bank-builder` prompt to collect ten or more real stories in STAR form, tagged by skill.
3. **Master CV and profile (week 1).** Run the `tailored-cv-and-cover-letter` skill against two or three typical ads to build a master CV, and the `linkedin-profile-and-bios` skill for the profile text. [L2] create the documents. *Approval: the person reads and approves every fact; they paste and publish the LinkedIn text from their own account.*
4. **Search plan (week 1).** Run the `job-search-plan-and-scam-check` prompt: weekly plan, tracker and outreach messages. [L3] keep the tracker current.
5. **Each application (ongoing).** Tailor the CV and letter for each job with the `tailored-cv-and-cover-letter` skill, and use the `selection-criteria-answers` prompt where forms ask for statements. Check the employer's AI rules first. *Approval: the person submits every application through their own account.*
6. **Outreach (ongoing).** Draft short messages to people in the field. [L2] save in drafts; [L3] send approved templates to contacts the person named. *Approval: any new contact needs a fresh yes.*
7. **Interview (each invite).** Run the `interview-prep-from-your-stories` skill: criteria, likely questions, story map, openers, worry answer, questions to ask, mock script. [L3] accept slots inside the person's windows. Practise with the mock script.
8. **After each interview (same day).** Use the `thank-you-and-follow-up` prompt. [L3] send the approved thank-you notes. Update the tracker.
9. **Offer (when it comes).** Run the `offer-and-pay-talk` skill: get it in writing, gather facts, set the numbers, script the call. *Approval: every message about pay, and accepting or declining, needs the person's fresh yes each time.* Mark contract terms for a qualified adviser.
10. **Resign and start (after a written offer).** Use the `resignation-letter` prompt. *Approval: the person sends the resignation.* Include: *General career advice, not legal or immigration advice: check this with local rules and ask a qualified adviser where the law decides.*

## Outputs

- Story bank in STAR form
- Master CV, tailored CVs and cover letters, criteria answers
- LinkedIn profile text and bios
- Weekly plan and job tracker
- Interview prep sheets and mock scripts
- Thank-you and follow-up messages
- Offer brief, pay script and follow-up email
- Resignation letter and handover list
- Action log for every level 2 and level 3 action

## Needs the client's approval

Every fact on every document; every application (submitted by the person); messages to new contacts; anything about pay; accepting or declining an offer; the resignation; and the first live action under any level 3 limit.
