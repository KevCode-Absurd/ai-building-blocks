# Weekly job search review

**Cadence:** Once a week during an active search, on the same day, taking about 30 minutes with the person. Steps marked [L3] need level 3 access to the tracker or email within the person's limits.

## Steps

1. **Update the numbers.** From the tracker: applications sent, replies, first interviews, later interviews, offers, and rejections, this week and in total. [L3] update the tracker from the person's email and calendar.
2. **Find where it is stuck.** Few applications: time or targeting. Applications but no replies: tailoring or fit; review two recent applications with the `tailored-cv-and-cover-letter` skill. Interviews but no offers: run a mock interview from the `interview-prep-from-your-stories` skill and ask for feedback from employers. Offers below hopes: revisit the `offer-and-pay-talk` facts.
3. **Check follow-ups.** List every application or interview past its decision date and draft polite follow-ups with the `thank-you-and-follow-up` prompt. [L3] send approved follow-ups to named contacts. *Approval: messages to new contacts need a fresh yes.*
4. **Scam and safety check.** Review any new recruiter or offer against the scam signs in the `job-search-plan-and-scam-check` prompt. Anything that asks for money, ID numbers or bank details before a real offer stops until checked.
5. **Plan next week.** Choose the jobs to apply for, the people to contact and the practice to do, within the person's hours. Add calendar holds. [L2] tentative holds.
6. **Check in on the person.** Ask how they are finding the search and adjust the pace. A coach respects the person's own decisions and pace. If they raise money worries, health or legal problems, point them to the right qualified help and official services.
7. **Weekly note.** One page: numbers, what changed, where it is stuck, next week's plan, decisions for the person. Include: *General career advice, not legal or immigration advice: check this with local rules and ask a qualified adviser where the law decides.*

## Outputs

- Updated tracker and numbers
- Diagnosis of where the search is stuck
- Follow-up messages (sent or drafts)
- Scam and safety notes
- Next week's plan and calendar holds
- Weekly note and action log

## Needs the client's approval

Messages to new contacts, anything about pay, any application submission (done by the person), and any change to the agreed level 3 limits.
