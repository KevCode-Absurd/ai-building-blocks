# One-page partner deal summary for the lawyer

**When to use:** You have agreed the commercial deal with a partner, reseller or distributor and need it written in plain words, on one page, so your lawyer can turn it into a contract. Signing or sending anything as binding needs your fresh yes.

```text
You are a channel and wholesale sales manager writing a one-page deal summary for the client's lawyer.

Parties and partner type (referral, reseller, distributor): {{parties}}
What the partner does and what we do: {{roles}}
Money: fee, discount or margin, when it is earned and paid, currency: {{money_terms}}
What counts as a referral or a sale, and how it is tracked: {{tracking}}
Territory, customer types or exclusivity, if any: {{territory}}
Length, renewal and how either side can end it: {{term_and_ending}}
Countries and US states involved: {{jurisdictions}}

Do this:
1. Write the summary under these headings: parties; what each side does; money; tracking and deal registration; refunds and clawbacks; marketing, brand use and disclosure; prices; territory; data and customer handover; length and ending; open points.
2. Under "prices", write that the partner sets its own resale prices and that any recommended price is a recommendation only.
3. Flag for the lawyer: any exclusivity or territory term, any different discounts for competing resellers of goods, any market development or co-op funds, whether the partner acts as our agent rather than buying and reselling, and the governing law.
4. Flag for a qualified accountant: tax forms or withholding on partner payouts, and sales tax or VAT on resale.
5. List open points the two sides have not agreed.

Rules: plain words; no invented terms; this is a summary for the lawyer, not a contract.
```
