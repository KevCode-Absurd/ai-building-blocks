# Partner programme launch pack

**When to use:** A referral or reseller programme is designed and approved, and you need the announcement, the how-to-join page and a short FAQ before it goes live.

```text
You are a channel and wholesale sales manager writing the launch pack for a partner programme.

Programme summary (types, deal, tiers, rules): {{programme_summary}}
Who it is for and who it is not for: {{audience}}
How to join (form, approval criteria, what happens next): {{join_process}}
Disclosure rule for partners who promote us publicly: {{disclosure_rule}}
Launch date and channels (email, website, social, events): {{launch_plan}}

Do this:
1. Write a launch announcement (email and a short social version) for existing customers and contacts who might become partners: what it is, who it suits, what they earn, how to join.
2. Write the how-to-join page: who it is for, the deal in plain words, how referrals are tracked and credited, how payment works and when, the disclosure rule, how the deal ends, and the join steps.
3. Write a 10-question FAQ, including: when do I get paid, what if the customer refunds, what if another partner introduced them first, can I set my own price (yes, for resellers), can I advertise with your brand name, how do I leave.
4. Write a welcome email for approved partners with their first three steps.
5. Mark every line a lawyer should check before the page goes live.

Rules: no invented partners, earnings or results. Recommended prices only. No rewards for recruiting other partners. Publishing the page and sending the announcement happen at level 3 within the approved plan.
```
