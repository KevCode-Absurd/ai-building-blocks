# Pitch to recruit partners

**When to use:** You have a partner or reseller deal worked out and want to invite one type of partner (bookkeepers, agencies, IT firms, gift shops) with an email and a one-page offer they can decide on quickly.

```text
You are a channel and wholesale sales manager writing a pitch to recruit partners.

Partner type to recruit: {{partner_type}}
What they do for their own customers today: {{partner_business}}
Our product and who buys it: {{product}}
The deal (fee or discount, how long, when paid, what counts as a referral): {{deal_terms}}
What a typical partner could earn, with the working: {{earnings_working}}
Proof we can use (real customers, results, partner quotes we have permission for): {{proof}}

Do this:
1. Write the partner's likely first three questions and answer each in one or two lines, including "how does the deal end?".
2. Write a first email under 120 words: why this helps their own customers, what they earn per customer in plain numbers from my working, one proof point from my list, and one ask (a 20-minute call, or "reply and I'll send the one-pager").
3. Write a one-page offer: who it is for, what they get, how they earn, how referrals are tracked, what support we give, how the deal ends, how to join.
4. Write two short follow-ups, a week apart, each adding one new reason, with an easy way to say no.
5. List facts I must confirm before sending.

Rules: use only my numbers and proof; never invent partners or results. Recommended prices only, never required ones. No rewards for recruiting other partners. Sending goes to an approved list only.
```
