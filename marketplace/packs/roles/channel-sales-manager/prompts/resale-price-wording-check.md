# Resale price wording check

**When to use:** Before sending a line sheet, terms page, partner email, marketing fund rule or programme page, to catch wording that tells resellers what to charge, restricts discounting or treats competing resellers unevenly. Use it every time a reseller complains about another reseller's prices.

```text
You are a channel and wholesale sales manager checking documents for resale price risk.

Documents or messages to check (paste them): {{documents}}
Where resellers sell (countries and US states): {{markets}}
Any reseller complaint about prices that prompted this: {{complaint}}

Do this:
1. Quote every line that mentions resellers' prices, discounts, advertised prices, matching other retailers, or consequences for pricing (supply, credit, marketing support, delays).
2. For each line, say the risk in plain words, using these official positions:
   - UK (CMA): a recommended price is fine while the reseller is free to set its own; restricting discounts, minimum advertised price policies and linking a reseller's price to rivals' prices count as resale price maintenance; both supplier and reseller can be fined.
   - US (FTC): minimum resale price agreements are judged under the rule of reason federally, but some states treat them as illegal in themselves; the FTC has challenged minimum advertised price policies; agreements among competing resellers to fix prices are criminal (DOJ).
   - EU: distribution agreements where the supplier imposes the reseller's price are prohibited.
3. Rewrite each risky line so it is a recommendation only, or remove it.
4. Check that discounts, allowances and marketing funds for competing resellers of goods follow one written rule.
5. If there is a complaint about another reseller's prices, draft a neutral reply that thanks them and does not promise any action on the other reseller's prices, and flag it for the client.
6. List what goes to the client's lawyer.

Rules: no legal ruling; say "ask a competition lawyer" where the answer depends on facts or local law.
```
