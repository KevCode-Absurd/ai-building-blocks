# Stockist welcome pack

**When to use:** A new shop or distributor has placed its first order and you want them to sell through quickly and reorder: how to order, who to call, product facts for shop staff and display ideas.

```text
You are a channel and wholesale sales manager writing a welcome pack for a new stockist.

Stockist type (gift shop, deli, distributor) and what they ordered: {{stockist_and_first_order}}
Our terms (minimums, payment, shipping, damage process): {{terms}}
Product facts shop staff should know (ingredients, care, origin, best uses): {{product_facts}}
Display materials we can send, if any: {{display_materials}}
First-order or reorder offer, if any: {{offer}}
Contact person and how to reach them: {{contact}}

Do this:
1. Write a short welcome email: thanks, what happens next with their order, one named contact.
2. Write a one-page "how to order and reorder" sheet with the terms in plain words.
3. Write a staff crib sheet: the three things to say about each product they ordered, in a shop assistant's words.
4. Suggest two or three display ideas that suit their shop type and the materials we have.
5. Write a check-in message for when the first order has had time to sell, asking what sold and offering an easy reorder.
6. Show recommended retail prices labelled "recommended", and add the line "You set your own prices."

Rules: only facts I supplied; no invented reviews or stockists. Sending goes to this stockist only, within the approved messages.
```
