# Training interview: channel and wholesale sales manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share partner, buyer or customer names, contract figures that are confidential, or personal details; anonymise every story.

1. **Which channels have you run: shops, distributors, referral partners, resellers, solution partners? In which industries and countries?**
   Why it matters: sets the range the agent should claim and the rules it should point to.

2. **How do you work out what a partner or shop can earn before you offer a deal?**
   Why it matters: the margin working is the base of every programme; the agent needs the expert's method.

3. **Tell me about a deal that looked generous and turned out to lose money. What did you miss?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent the costs people forget (support, refunds, delivery).

4. **How do you choose which partner types to recruit first?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's tests for fit and reach.

5. **What does your first email to a shop or partner look like, and why does it work?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a proven model for tone and length.

6. **What goes on your line sheet, and what have you taken off it?**
   Why it matters: shows what busy buyers actually read.

7. **How do you set minimum orders and payment terms for a new account?**
   Why it matters: protects the client's cash; the agent should copy the expert's defaults and exceptions.

8. **How do you talk about retail prices with stockists without crossing the line on resale price rules?**
   Why it matters: the most serious legal risk in the role; the agent needs safe, natural wording.

9. **A shop complains another shop is undercutting them. What do you say and do?**
   Why it matters: a common trigger for illegal pressure; the agent needs the expert's response.

10. **How do you write deal registration and conflict rules, and when did one fail you?**
    Why it matters: conflicts damage partner trust; the agent should write rules that hold.

11. **How do you design tiers so partners care about them without costing too much?**
    Why it matters: captures benefits that matter to partners and cost the client little.

12. **What do you require before paying market development or co-op funds?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's default rules for proof and fairness between resellers.

13. **What happens in a new partner's first 30 days in your best programme?**
    Why it matters: the onboarding plan is the agent's main lever on time to first sale.

14. **Which numbers do you track per partner and per account, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's scorecard and monthly report.

15. **How do you time reorder messages, and what do you say to a buyer who has gone quiet?**
    Why it matters: reorders are most of wholesale revenue; the agent needs the expert's timing and words.

16. **How do you end a partner relationship that is not working?**
    Why it matters: endings are where disputes start; the agent should write them in from day one.

17. **Have you been asked to design something that turned out to be a pyramid or multi-level scheme? How did you spot it?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent the warning signs and how to say no.

18. **What do partners who promote you online need to disclose, and how do you check?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's disclosure rules in practice.

19. **Which parts of a partner deal do you always send to a lawyer or accountant?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's flags for professional review.

20. **Tell me about the hardest channel job you have done, start to finish.**
    Why it matters: a full story shows the judgement the agent should learn from.
