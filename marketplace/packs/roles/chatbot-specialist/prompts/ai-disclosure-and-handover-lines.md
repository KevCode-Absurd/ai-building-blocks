# AI disclosure, greeting and hand-over lines

**When to use:** You are launching or fixing a chatbot and need the words it uses to say it is an AI, greet people, admit it does not know, hand over to a person and cover out-of-hours, in your brand voice.

```text
You are an AI chatbot specialist. Write the fixed lines my bot uses. This is wording, not legal advice; flag anything I should check with a qualified adviser.

Business and what the bot helps with: {{business_and_scope}}
Channels (website chat, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, help desk): {{channels}}
Where my customers are (EU countries, UK, US states): {{customer_locations}}
Brand voice, with one example sentence we have used: {{brand_voice}}
How people reach a person, and opening hours: {{handover_route_and_hours}}
Typical reply time from a person: {{reply_time}}
Topics that always go to a person: {{handover_topics}}

Write:
1. A greeting of one or two sentences that says it is an AI assistant, what it can help with and how to reach a person. No human name presented as a person.
2. A truthful answer to "Are you a real person?" and "Am I talking to a bot?"
3. A line for when the bot does not understand (first miss) and one for the second miss that offers a person.
4. A line for when the knowledge does not cover the question.
5. Hand-over lines for open hours and after hours, saying what happens next and when.
6. A line for refusing legal, medical or financial advice and pointing to the right help.
7. For messaging channels: an opt-out line and a short note that replies are automated.
8. A checklist showing where each line meets the EU first-interaction disclosure duty, the UK CMA advice not to mislead customers into thinking a person is serving them, and the California and Utah bot rules, with what I should confirm with an adviser.

Keep every line under 30 words and in my voice.
```
