# Training interview: AI chatbot specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, customer details and any transcript text that could identify a person.

1. **Which bots and assistants have you set up: website chat, help-desk AI agents, messaging flows, custom GPTs, Claude Projects, Gems, Copilot Studio?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's tool defaults and where its advice stops.

2. **Walk me through the last bot you launched, from the first call to the first week live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the time goes.

3. **How do you decide what a bot should answer and what it should hand to a person?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's scope and hand-over judgement.

4. **What do you ask for on day one, and what do you refuse to accept as source material?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent an intake list and a standard for approved sources.

5. **How do you write a knowledge entry? Show me a good one and a bad one.**
   Why it matters: records the expert's entry format and the faults they look for.

6. **Tell me about two documents that contradicted each other. How did you resolve it?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent to surface clashes rather than pick a winner.

7. **How do you write instructions so the rules that matter are actually followed?**
   Why it matters: pulls out practical prompt structure, not theory.

8. **What does your bot say in its first message, and why those words?**
   Why it matters: grounds the AI disclosure and greeting in real practice.

9. **What happens in your flows when the bot does not understand?**
   Why it matters: captures fallback design and when to offer a person.

10. **How do you make a hand-over work so the customer never repeats themselves?**
    Why it matters: records what context to pass and how in the tools the expert knows.

11. **What is in your test set before launch, and which case has caught the most problems?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent tested categories and a real high-value case.

12. **Tell me about a bot answer that went badly wrong. What caused it and what did you change?**
    Why it matters: adds real failure causes and fixes to the agent's checklist.

13. **How do you test for prompt injection, and where do you draw the line on testing?**
    Why it matters: sets safe, owner-approved testing habits.

14. **How do you read a vendor's resolution or containment figures?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to question metrics that count customers giving up as success.

15. **Which rules on AI disclosure and data protection come up with your clients in the EU, UK and US, and how do you handle them without giving legal advice?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent practical and inside its limits on law.

16. **What do you set up for transcripts: who can read them, how long they are kept, whether the vendor can train on them?**
    Why it matters: makes privacy settings part of every launch.

17. **How do you keep a bot's knowledge current after launch?**
    Why it matters: captures the upkeep routine, owners and triggers.

18. **When have you told a client their bot should be paused or not launched? How did you say it?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's honesty line and the words to use.

19. **What access do you ask for, and when do you ask for more?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's level 1, 2 and 3 requests in practice.

20. **What does a bot that is working well look like three months after launch?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a picture of success beyond go-live.
