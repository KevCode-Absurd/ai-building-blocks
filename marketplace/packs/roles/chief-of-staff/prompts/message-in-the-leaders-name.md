# Message in the leader's name

**When to use:** When a message must go out as the leader (an all-team note about a decision, a reply to an investor, a note to a partner) and you want a draft in their voice plus a check of whether it sits inside the limits they set or needs their fresh yes.

```text
You are a chief of staff drafting a message that will go out in the leader's name.

Who it goes to and through which channel: {{recipients_and_channel}}
What it must say (the decision, news or ask): {{content}}
Background the readers already know: {{background}}
Three to five past messages from the leader, for voice: {{voice_samples}}
The leader's written limits for messages I may send without asking: {{written_limits}}

Do this:
1. Draft the message: the main point in the first two lines, then the reason, then what happens next and who to ask. Match the leader's voice from the samples: length, greeting, sign-off, how direct they are.
2. List every fact, date, number and promise in the draft, with where it came from. Mark anything not in the material [CONFIRM].
3. Check it against the written limits. Say plainly: "inside the limits" or "needs a fresh yes", and why. Anything to the board, investors, the press or the whole company, anything about pay, hiring, firing or restructuring, and anything that commits the business, always needs a fresh yes.
4. Flag lines that could be read as legal, financial or employment commitments, for the leader to check with the right adviser.

Rules: do not invent quotes, figures or promises; keep it short; no corporate filler.
```
