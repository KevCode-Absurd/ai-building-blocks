# Monthly review and updates

**Cadence:** Monthly, after the books close (the bookkeeper's close date), with the board pack added in months the board meets.

**Trigger:** Month-end numbers are available, or a board meeting is scheduled within the next few weeks.

**Access:** Level 1 (read) on the accounts, scorecard sources, CRM, goals tool and last month's papers. Steps marked [L2] need read and draft access; steps marked [L3] need read, write and publish access within the leader's written limits. Anything sent to investors or the board needs a fresh yes every time.

## Steps

1. **Work back from the dates.** Put on the calendar: numbers locked, first draft, leader review, send date. For a board month, add the pack deadline and the date papers go to directors.
2. **Lock the numbers.** Take each figure from its source with the same definitions as last month. List anything that does not reconcile for the bookkeeper or a qualified accountant to check. Do not publish figures still under question.
3. **Write the monthly report.** Run the `board-and-investor-update` skill (monthly report format): what changed, numbers against plan, cash and runway, lessons, decisions needed. [L2] Save it as a draft in the leadership folder.
4. **Leader review.** Walk the leader through bad news first. *Approval: the leader approves the report before it is shared.* [L3] Publish the approved report to the leadership folder and post the link in the agreed channel.
5. **Draft the investor update** (if investors get monthly updates). Same numbers and definitions, shorter, with specific asks. [L2] Save as an unsent draft. *Approval: a fresh yes from the leader every time before it is sent.* The leader sends it, or I send it after that yes.
6. **Board month: build the pack.** Leader's letter, dashboard, "for decision" items each with a one-page brief (run `decision-brief-and-log`), risks, appendices. Leave resolution wording [FOR COMPANY SECRETARY]. *Approval: the leader approves the pack; the company secretary or lawyer checks resolutions and minutes. Check this with local rules.* [L3] After a fresh yes, share the pack with the named directors only.
7. **Prepare for questions.** List the questions the readers are likely to ask and draft short answers for the leader.
8. **After the meeting.** Turn board decisions into decision-log entries and actions. Formal minutes stay with the company secretary.
9. **Review the month's rhythm.** Did the weekly meetings decide things? Are actions closing? Note one change for next month.

## Outputs

- Monthly report
- Investor update draft
- Board pack (board months)
- Likely questions with draft answers
- Decision-log entries and actions from the board
- List of figures to check, with sources

## Needs the leader's approval

The monthly report before sharing; every investor update and board pack before sending (a fresh yes each time, even at level 3); sharing board or data-room folders with anyone new; any resolution or minute (with the company secretary or lawyer); and any figure the board relies on (confirmed with a qualified accountant). Signing, voting, filings and moving money stay with the leader.
