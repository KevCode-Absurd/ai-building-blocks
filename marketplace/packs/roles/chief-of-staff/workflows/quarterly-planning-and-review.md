# Quarterly planning and review

**Cadence:** Once a quarter (or once per work cycle if the company runs its own cycles), starting in the last few weeks of the quarter.

**Trigger:** The quarter is ending, or the leader calls a planning day.

**Access:** Level 1 (read) on the goals tool, scorecard, project list, decision log and the year plan. Steps marked [L2] need read and draft access; steps marked [L3] need read, write and publish access within the leader's written limits.

## Steps

1. **Score the quarter.** For each key result, record the final value and score it against the target. Committed OKRs: met or not. Aspirational OKRs: scored on the 0.0 to 1.0 scale. Never change a target after the fact. Use the `okr-set-and-check-ins` skill (quarter-end steps).
2. **Write the quarter review.** What we hit, missed and learned, in plain words; the decisions that worked and the ones to revisit (from the decision log's review dates). One page.
3. **Gather inputs for next quarter.** Year plan, current numbers, the "not this quarter" list, each team's proposals and any board or investor asks. Run the `ten-priorities-to-three` prompt on the combined list.
4. **Pre-mortem the biggest bets.** Run the `pre-mortem` prompt on any one-way-door bet in the shortlist.
5. **Draft next quarter's OKRs.** Run `okr-set-and-check-ins`: a few objectives, key results with baseline, target, owner and source, committed or aspirational, confidence, and the "not this quarter" list. [L2] Draft them in a private page or project.
6. **Planning session.** Prepare a decision-first agenda and a written pre-read sent the day before. In the session, the leader decides the final set. *Approval: the leader approves the final OKRs and the "not this quarter" list.*
7. **Publish and set up.** [L3] Publish the approved OKRs in the goals tool, create the check-in, set the weekly scorecard and add the quarter's reviews to the calendar. Post the summary in the agreed channel within the limits. *Approval: an all-company announcement in the leader's name needs a fresh yes.*
8. **Tell the board** (if any). Add the quarter review and next quarter's goals to the next board pack via the `monthly-review-and-updates` workflow.

## Outputs

- Quarter scores and one-page review
- Ranked shortlist and "not this quarter" list
- Pre-mortems for the biggest bets
- Next quarter's OKRs with owners and data sources
- Planning-session agenda and pre-read
- Updated rhythm calendar

## Needs the leader's approval

The final OKR set; any change to a target once set; any link between goals and pay or ratings (send to the leader and an HR adviser, and keep it out of the OKRs); any all-company message in the leader's name; and anything sent to the board or investors. Anything that cannot be undone needs a fresh yes even at level 3.
