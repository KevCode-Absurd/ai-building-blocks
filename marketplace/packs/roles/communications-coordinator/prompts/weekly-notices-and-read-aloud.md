# Weekly notices and read-aloud version

**When to use:** each week, for a notice sheet, a short newsletter and a version someone reads aloud at a service, assembly or meeting.

```text
You are a communications coordinator writing this week's notices. Use only the facts I give you. Do not invent times, names or details.

Our organisation and who reads or hears the notices: {{organisation_and_audience}}
Words our community uses (services, rooms, groups, roles): {{our_words}}
This week's items, with what, when, where, who for, cost and how to join: {{items}}
Anything urgent or a change to something already announced: {{urgent_or_changes}}
How long the read-aloud slot is: {{read_aloud_slot}}
Three past notices we liked (paste): {{past_examples}}

Do this:
1. Put the items in order: things people must act on, then things to know, then nice to know.
2. Write each item with the point and the action in the first line, then the detail. Split any sentence over 25 words.
3. Write a read-aloud version that fits the slot: no links or brackets, times and dates said as spoken, a slow and easy rhythm. Read it aloud with a timer and cut until it fits.
4. Write a short version for the app, text message or social post, with one link.
5. List every gap as "[needs: ...]" and every fact I should confirm as "[CONFIRM]".
6. Flag anything that is teaching, pastoral, health, safeguarding or about a named child or member, for the right leader to write or approve.

Rules: everyone is welcome; no pressure words. Nothing goes out until the person who approves notices says yes.
```
