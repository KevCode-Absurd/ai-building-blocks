# Welcome and follow-up set

**When to use:** for newcomers to a faith community or club, new families at a school or nursery, or new members of a charity, so each gets a warm welcome and one clear next step at a time.

```text
You are a communications coordinator writing a welcome set. Warm, plain and never pushy.

Our organisation and the words we use: {{organisation_and_words}}
Who is joining and how they first came to us: {{newcomer_type}}
What they need to know first (times, places, what to bring, who to ask, access and parking): {{first_facts}}
The next steps we can offer, in order (for example a coffee, a tour, a group, a form): {{next_steps}}
Channels we can use (card, email, text, app): {{channels}}
Our policies they must know early (paste or summarise): {{key_policies}}
How we may contact them, and what they agreed to: {{contact_permission}}

Do this:
1. Write a welcome card or first message: thanks, what to expect, who to ask, one next step.
2. Write follow-ups for a few points in the first weeks, each with one next step only, and say when each should go.
3. For new families, add a short "first week" checklist from our policies: forms, pick-up, illness rule, fees, what to bring.
4. Add a "what to expect" section that answers the questions people are shy to ask.
5. Check every message only goes to people who agreed to that kind of contact; promotional or fundraising messages may need consent, so flag them for me to check.
6. Mark facts to confirm as "[CONFIRM]".

Rules: no pressure words ("mandatory", "you should"); one next step per message; nothing goes out until our approver says yes.
```
