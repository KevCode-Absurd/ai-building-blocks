# Term or season plan

**Cadence:** once per school term, church season (such as Advent, Lent or Ramadan), programme year or campaign, before it starts.

**Trigger:** also run it when a new coordinator starts or the organisation changes its channels.

## Steps

1. **Collect the dates (level 1).** Term or season dates, services and festivals, events, closures, deadlines, report and funder dates, and anything carried over. With web search connected, check public dates such as bank holidays for the client's country; without it, ask the client for them.
2. **Review last period.** Which messages got action, which caused repeat questions, which went out late and why.
3. **Build the calendar and sign-off chain (skill: comms-calendar-and-approvals).** Every message with channel, audience, owner and approver; load and gap notes; fresh-yes messages marked.
   **Approval point 1:** the head, minister, manager or owner approves the calendar and the sign-off chain.
4. **Refresh the house style (skill: house-style-guide).** Add new terms, names and roles; fix slips seen last period.
5. **Prepare the big pieces early.** Welcome sets for new families or members (prompt: welcome-and-follow-up-set), festival or event invitation packs, and any report (prompt: impact-report-numbers-and-stories).
6. **Check templates (skill: accessible-documents-and-emails).** Newsletter and email templates, letterheads and poster templates pass the accessibility checks.
7. **Set up drafts.** At level 2, create the season's draft campaigns, pages and posts; at level 3, schedule the approved ones within the limits.
   **Approval point 2:** each message still needs its approver's yes on the final version before it goes out.
8. **Report at the end of the period.** What went out, what worked, what to change.

## Outputs

- Calendar for the period, with owners and approvers.
- Sign-off matrix with fresh-yes messages marked.
- Updated house style guide.
- Welcome sets, invitation packs and report plans prepared early.
- Accessible templates.
- End-of-period report.

## Needs the client's approval

- The calendar and sign-off chain (point 1).
- Each message's final version (point 2).
- Always a fresh yes: formal statements, all-staff and all-customer, member or parent messages, new audiences, deleting published content, and sensitive topics.
- Any spend on tools, printing or promotion stays with the client.
