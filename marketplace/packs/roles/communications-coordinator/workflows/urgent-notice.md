# Urgent notice

**Trigger:** something people must know now: a closure (weather, heating, illness), a change of time or place, a cancelled event, a lost-property or safety reminder, or a service outage.

**Cadence:** as needed. For a crisis that affects safety, reputation or the law (an incident, an allegation, a data breach, press interest), hand over to the decider and the PR specialist kit's crisis workflow; this workflow covers the practical notice only.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the facts with the decider (level 1).** What has happened, what is confirmed, what is not, who is affected, what people should do, when the next update comes. Write nothing as fact that is not confirmed.
2. **Decide the audience.** Only the people affected, or everyone? A notice to all staff or all parents, members or customers needs the decider's fresh yes, even at level 3.
3. **Write the notice (skill: newsletter-and-notices).** First line: what is happening and what to do ("The nursery is closed today, Tuesday 14 January, because the heating has failed. Please do not bring children in."). Then why in one line, what happens next, when the next update comes, and who to contact. Plain words, no blame, no guessing.
4. **Write the channel versions.** Text or app message, email, website banner or page, social post, phone message or sign on the door, all with identical facts. Accessible: real text, not a photo of a sign.
5. **Approval.**
   **Approval point:** the decider approves the exact wording and the audience. Anything touching safeguarding, health, an incident or legal matters also goes to the person who owns it.
6. **Send at the level granted.**
   - Level 1: hand over the texts for the client to send.
   - Level 2: load them as drafts on each channel so the client can send in one pass.
   - Level 3: send to the approved audience on the approved channels, then confirm what was sent and when.
7. **Update.** Send the next update when promised, even if there is no news yet. Correct any wrong fact on every channel at once, with the decider's yes.
8. **Close and review.** Send the all-clear, take down the website banner, log everything, and note what to prepare in advance next time (for example a ready-to-use closure template).

## Outputs

- Confirmed facts sheet.
- Notice and channel versions with identical facts.
- Approval record with the audience.
- Sends or drafts by access level, with times.
- Updates, all-clear and a short review.

## Needs the client's approval

- The facts, wording and audience of every notice and update.
- A fresh yes every time for anything sent to all staff or all customers, members or parents, and for any formal statement.
- Anything about safeguarding, health, an incident or legal matters, from the person who owns it.
- Removing or changing anything already published.
