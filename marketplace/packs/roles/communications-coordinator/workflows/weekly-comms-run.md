# Weekly comms run

**Cadence:** weekly, on the day the client sets for gathering news (for example Monday morning for a Friday newsletter and Sunday notices).

**Trigger:** also run it whenever a regular message is due.

## Steps

1. **Gather (level 1).** Read the calendar, last week's messages, replies and repeat questions, and the items people sent in. With no access, ask for the week's items pasted in.
2. **Check the calendar (skill: comms-calendar-and-approvals).** Confirm what is due this week on each channel, who approves it, and any clashes or gaps.
3. **Write (skill: newsletter-and-notices; prompt: weekly-notices-and-read-aloud).** Newsletter, notices, read-aloud version, short app or social versions, website updates. Facts checked; gaps marked.
4. **Check style and access (skills: house-style-guide and accessible-documents-and-emails).** House words and mechanics; headings, alt text, contrast, link text, real text not images; photos against the consent list.
5. **Get approval.** Send each item to its approver with gaps listed.
   **Approval point:** the named approver says yes to each item's final version. Formal statements and anything to all staff or all customers, members or parents need the decider's fresh yes, even at level 3.
6. **Schedule or send.**
   - Level 1: hand over the final text and a send checklist.
   - Level 2: save as unsent campaigns, draft pages and draft posts.
   - Level 3: show the week's action list (channels, audiences, times) once, get a yes, then send or schedule within the limits. Ask again before a new audience, a deletion or anything outside the limits.
7. **Watch the replies.** Note questions that show a message was unclear; correct facts on every channel at once if something was wrong, with the approver's yes.
8. **Log and report.** What went where and when, approvals, results that matter (replies, forms back, attendance), and changes for next week.

## Outputs

- This week's newsletter, notices, read-aloud version, short versions and website updates.
- Style and accessibility check notes.
- Approval record for each item.
- Drafts (level 2) or sends and schedules (level 3), with an action log.
- A short weekly note: what went out, what people asked, what to change.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool and the level 3 limits (channels, audiences, message types, dates).
- Each item's final version, from its named approver.
- A fresh yes every time for formal statements and anything to all staff or all customers, members or parents, and for anything about complaints, incidents, safeguarding, money owed or someone's job.
- Corrections to something already sent.
