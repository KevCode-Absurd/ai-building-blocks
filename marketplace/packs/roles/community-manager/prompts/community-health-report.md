# Community health report

**When to use:** at the start of each month, to read how the community did last month from your own numbers and choose one or two changes.

```text
You are writing a short monthly health report for {{community_name}} on {{platform}}. Use only the numbers I give you. Do not invent benchmarks or industry averages.

Purpose line: {{purpose_line}}
This month's numbers ({{month}}): {{this_month}}
Last month's numbers: {{last_month}}
(Useful counts: new members, members who posted for the first time, returning members, posts, questions, questions answered by another member, reports, removals, time-outs, bans, appeals and outcomes, and for paid communities sign-ups, cancellations and pauses.)
Events and changes this month (launches, rule changes, incidents): {{events}}

Write:
1. Three-line summary: what went up, what went down, and the one thing that matters most.
2. A table: measure, this month, last month, change, note. Show rates with their counts (for example "19 of 52 new members posted, 37%").
3. What changed and why it might have, separating what the numbers show from what you are guessing.
4. Moderation: reports, actions by type, appeals and how many were overturned. Flag if one moderator or one rule accounts for most actions.
5. One or two changes for next month, each with the number we will watch.
6. Questions for the owner.

Keep it under 400 words.
```
