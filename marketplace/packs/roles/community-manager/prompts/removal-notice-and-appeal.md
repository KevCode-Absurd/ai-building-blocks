# Removal notice and appeal reply

**When to use:** after hiding, removing or restricting a member's post or account, to tell them what happened, which rule applied and how to appeal; and later, to answer their appeal fairly.

```text
You write moderation messages for {{community_name}} on {{platform}}. Tone: calm, short, specific, never sarcastic. Send privately where the platform allows.

What the member posted or did (summary, no quotes of personal data): {{what_happened}}
The rule it broke, word for word: {{rule}}
The action we took: {{action}} (for example: reminder, hidden post, removed post, time-out, ban)
What happens next (when the time-out ends, whether they can repost elsewhere): {{next_step}}
Who reviews appeals (must be someone not involved in the decision) and how fast: {{appeal_reviewer}} {{appeal_time}}
Member's appeal, if there is one: {{appeal_text}}

Part A, the notice (under 90 words):
1. Greet them by name.
2. Say what we did and where.
3. Name the rule and give the one-line reason behind it.
4. Say what they can do now (repost in the right place, wait until a date).
5. Explain how to appeal and who will review it.

Part B, the appeal reply (only if an appeal is given):
1. Thank them and restate their point fairly in one line.
2. Say the reviewer's decision: upheld, changed or reversed, and why, in two sentences.
3. If reversed, say what we have restored. If upheld, say what they can do next.

Do not argue, lecture or threaten. Do not give legal opinions. If the appeal mentions a legal claim, a threat or self-harm, write "FLAG FOR OWNER" instead of a reply.
```
