# Interview: community manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you build and run a community

1. **Tell me about a community you built or rescued. What was it for, in one line, and how did you find that line?**
   Why it matters: shows whether the expert starts from purpose or from channels and features.
2. **Walk me through your welcome flow. What does a new member see, do and get in their first week?**
   Why it matters: the first week decides who stays; the details are a reusable method.
3. **Which weekly formats have worked best for you, and which did you drop?**
   Why it matters: real formats beat generic "engagement ideas".
4. **How do you get members answering each other instead of waiting for you?**
   Why it matters: hosting versus broadcasting is the core skill of the role.
5. **How do you treat your most active members without letting them take over?**
   Why it matters: participation is always uneven; how they shape it is judgement.
6. **Which numbers do you read each month, and which do you ignore?**
   Why it matters: separates useful health measures from vanity counts.

## Moderation

7. **Show me a set of house rules you wrote. Why is each one there?**
   Why it matters: captures their rule-writing style and reasons.
8. **Describe your moderation ladder for one common problem, step by step.**
   Why it matters: reveals how consistent and proportionate they are.
9. **Tell me about the hardest moderation call you made. What did you do, and would you do it again?**
   Why it matters: a story of judgement under pressure, the heart of the role.
10. **When do you hide, remove, ban or delete? What do you never do without the owner?**
    Why it matters: sets the line for level 3 actions and fresh-yes cases.
11. **How do members appeal in your communities, and who reviews appeals?**
    Why it matters: fairness and trust depend on a real appeal route.
12. **What do you do in the first hour after a threat, self-harm post or possible illegal content?**
    Why it matters: checks they act fast, use official routes and escalate to the owner.
13. **How do you keep moderators consistent across shifts and time zones?**
    Why it matters: rotas and handover logs are where communities break.

## Platforms and tools

14. **Which platforms have you run communities on, and what does each do badly?**
    Why it matters: platform limits shape the plan, such as history limits or permanent bans.
15. **What do you automate, and what do you insist a person does?**
    Why it matters: sets their stance on AutoMod, AI sorting and AI replies.

## Memberships

16. **For a paid community, what is the first win, and how fast do members reach it?**
    Why it matters: early wins are the main lever against cancellations.
17. **How have you designed a cancel flow? What did members say about it?**
    Why it matters: tests honesty and current practice on easy cancellation.
18. **Tell me about a membership that lost members after month two. What did you find and change?**
    Why it matters: shows how they read cancellations by joining month.

## Outside communities

19. **How do you decide whether a business should post in a community it does not own?**
    Why it matters: captures their community-mapping method.
20. **What is your disclosure line, and has a moderator ever pushed back on it?**
    Why it matters: checks honesty and how they handle moderators.
21. **A client asks for "a few accounts to get the conversation going". What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests their line on fake activity and how they hold it.

## Clients and craft

22. **What do clients most often get wrong about community, and how do you reset their expectations?**
    Why it matters: helps the agent handle clients who want the wrong thing.
23. **Show me a reply you are proud of and one you would rewrite.**
    Why it matters: teaches the voice and the quality bar by example.
