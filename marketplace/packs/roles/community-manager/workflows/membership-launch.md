# Membership launch

**Trigger:** the client wants to start charging for a community, or turn a free group into a paid one.

**Cadence:** a launch over several weeks, on the dates the client sets, then a monthly cancellation read.

## Steps

1. **Design the offer (skill: membership-offer-and-retention, steps 1 to 4).** Promise, benefits with owners and hours, levels, and the break-even sum from the client's costs.
2. **Map the member path (same skill, steps 5 and 6).** Joining, first win in week one, monthly rhythm, renewal, and quiet-member actions.
3. **Write the welcome flow and first eight weeks (skill: community-launch-and-weekly-rhythm).** Level 2 drafts; level 3 publishes on the agreed dates.
4. **Set the rules (skill: house-rules-and-moderation-ladder).** A paid community needs clear rules on refunds and removals, agreed by the owner.
5. **Build the cancel flow (membership-offer-and-retention, step 7).** As easy as joining, with a pause option. Check current rules for the client's countries (FTC negative option rulemaking in the US; the UK subscription contracts regime) and suggest the client asks a qualified adviser for anything binding.
6. **Plan the launch.** Waitlist, founding-member offer with an honest, honoured deadline, and launch messages. Level 2 drafts the emails and posts; the client or, at level 3, the agent sends them to the lists the client approved.
7. **Prepare the payment set-up (level 2).** Prices, levels and checkout copy are prepared for the client, who sets up payments, prices and payouts in their own tools.
8. **First 30 days.** Run the weekly community cycle; watch first wins and first-week posts.
9. **Monthly cancellation read (membership-offer-and-retention, step 9).** By joining month, active after one, two and three months; pick one change.

## Outputs

- Offer, levels and break-even working.
- Member path with messages at each step.
- Welcome flow and eight weeks of posts.
- Rules, including refunds and removals.
- Cancel flow.
- Launch plan and messages.
- Monthly cancellation read.

## Needs the client's approval

- Prices, levels, refund terms and the cancel flow before launch.
- The founding-member deadline (and confirmation that it will be honoured).
- At level 3, the one-time confirmation of which messages go to which lists, and when.
- Payments, price changes, refunds above the client's limit and payout details: the client does these.
