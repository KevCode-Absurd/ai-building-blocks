# Moderation incident

**Trigger:** a report, a flagged post or something the agent sees that may break the rules: spam wave, raid, harassment, a pile-on, personal data posted, a threat, or possible illegal content.

**Cadence:** as it happens; reviewed in the Friday handover and the monthly health report.

## Steps

1. **Triage (skill: house-rules-and-moderation-ladder).** Sort it: routine rule break, heated argument, raid or spam wave, personal data, threat or self-harm, possible illegal content (including child sexual abuse material), legal claim.
2. **Stop the harm first, for serious cases.** Hide or remove the content as the rules allow, or ask a moderator to. For a raid on Discord, pause invites (Discord added Pause Invites in 2022; check current settings). Never download, forward or re-share suspected child sexual abuse material.
3. **Save evidence (level 1 or above).** Link, date and time, usernames and a screenshot, stored where the client says. Do not copy personal data beyond what the rules allow.
4. **Act under the ladder (level 3 within limits).** Take the gentlest listed step that fits. Bans and deletions not listed in the rules: prepare them and ask for a fresh yes. Erasing a member's account or data: always a fresh yes.
5. **Tell the member (prompt: removal-notice-and-appeal).** What happened, which rule, what happens next, how to appeal.
6. **Report through official routes, for serious cases.** The platform's report tools; NCMEC's CyberTipline (US) or the Internet Watch Foundation (UK) for child sexual abuse material. The owner decides on police contact or legal advice.
7. **Tell the owner.** Same day for serious cases; in the Friday handover for routine ones.
8. **Handle the appeal.** Someone not involved in the first decision reviews it, and the member hears back with reasons.
9. **Log and learn.** Add the action to the log. If the same problem keeps coming, propose a rule or ladder change.

## Outputs

- Action log entry with rule, step, level, notice sent and outcome.
- Evidence record, stored where the client chose.
- Owner alert for serious cases.
- Appeal decision with reasons.
- Proposed rule or ladder change, if needed.

## Needs the client's approval

- Any ban or deletion the written rules do not list for this case.
- Erasing any member's account or data.
- Contact with police, lawyers or the press: always the owner's decision.
- Any formal reply to a legal claim or complaint.
- Changes to the house rules or ladder.
