# Weekly community cycle

**Cadence:** weekly, with a short daily reply pass and a monthly health read.

**Trigger:** the community is live and the client wants a steady rhythm of posts, replies and moderation.

## Steps

1. **Monday: set the week (skill: community-launch-and-weekly-rhythm).** Pick this week's posts from the eight-week plan, adjust for events or news, and check the facts with the client. Level 2 puts the posts in a staff space or document; level 3 publishes them on the agreed days in the agreed spaces.
2. **Daily: reply and flag pass (prompt: reply-and-flag-batch; tool: the community platform, through its connector or MCP server where one exists).** Draft or post replies, welcome every new member by name, invite members to answer each other, and send anything serious or about money to the owner.
3. **Daily: moderation queue (skill: house-rules-and-moderation-ladder).** Work through reports and flagged content. At level 3, take only the steps the written rules list, send the notice (prompt: removal-notice-and-appeal) and log each action. Prepare bans and deletions the rules do not list, and ask for a fresh yes.
4. **When a thread heats up (prompt: heated-thread-cool-down).** Post the cool-down note, message rule-breakers privately, and flag anything serious.
5. **Friday: handover log.** Open issues, decisions made, appeals waiting, members to welcome or thank, and anything the next moderator should know.
6. **First working day of the month: health read (prompt: community-health-report).** Compare with last month from the client's counts and pick one or two changes.
7. **Monthly: quiet members (prompt: quiet-member-nudge).** For paid communities, message quiet members with one useful thing each.

## Outputs

- Published or drafted weekly posts.
- Replies and welcomes, with a flag list for the owner.
- Moderation action log with notices sent and appeals.
- Friday handover log.
- Monthly health report with one or two changes.

## Needs the client's approval

- The eight-week plan and any change to the house rules.
- At level 3, the one-time confirmation of the action list and limits (which spaces, which actions, until when).
- A fresh yes for any ban or deletion not listed in the written rules, any erasing of member data, any post to a new audience, and anything involving money or a formal complaint.
- Serious cases (threats, self-harm, child safety, legal claims): the owner decides, after urgent safety steps.
