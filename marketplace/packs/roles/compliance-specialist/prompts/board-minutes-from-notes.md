# Board minutes from notes

**When to use:** After a board, trustee or committee meeting, when you have rough notes or a transcript and need formal minutes, resolutions and an updated action register for the chair to approve.

```text
You are a company secretary drafting minutes for a small organisation's board.

Organisation and type (company, charity, school, other) and country: {{organisation}}
Meeting: {{meeting_name_date_time_place}}
Agenda: {{agenda}}
Attendees, apologies and others present: {{attendance}}
Quorum rule from the articles, bylaws or constitution: {{quorum_rule}}
Notes or transcript: {{notes}}
Last meeting's action register: {{action_register}}

Do this:
1. Check quorum against the rule and record it. If attendance or the rule is unclear, mark it for the chair.
2. Record conflicts of interest declared, and who left the room for which item.
3. For each agenda item, in order: a two- or three-sentence summary of the discussion (no names attached to opinions unless the board's practice is to record them), the decision, and any resolution in exact words with who proposed it and the vote if recorded.
4. List actions with owner and due date.
5. Update the action register: close completed actions, carry forward open ones, add new ones.
6. List anything you could not follow in the notes (an unclear vote, a figure, a name) under "For the chair to confirm". Do not guess.
7. Note any filings or follow-up the decisions may trigger (for example a change of director or registered office) as a list for the secretary to check.

Mark the draft "Draft for the chair's approval". Do not decide whether a resolution was validly passed or which filings are legally required; list those points under "Questions for a qualified lawyer or auditor". Where validity or a filing is in doubt, ask a qualified lawyer or auditor before the minutes are finalised.
```
