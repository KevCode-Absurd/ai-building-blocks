# Training interview: compliance specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, real personal data, contract terms tied to a named party or confidential audit findings; anonymise every story.

1. **Which rules, frameworks and countries do you know best, and for what kinds of organisation?**
   Why it matters: sets the scope the agent can speak to with confidence and the limits of its experience.

2. **How do you explain to a client the line between what you do and what a lawyer or auditor does?**
   Why it matters: captures the wording that keeps the agent helpful without giving legal rulings or attestations.

3. **Walk me through the last time you turned a long regulation into a checklist. What did you leave out, and why?**
   Why it matters: shows the expert's method and how they avoid adding duties the text does not state.

4. **How do you write a question for a lawyer so the call is short and useful?**
   Why it matters: question quality is where this role saves clients the most money.

5. **Tell me about a SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 or PCI DSS preparation you supported. Where did the evidence fall short?**
   Why it matters: records real evidence gaps the agent should look for early.

6. **How do you stop evidence being gathered the week before the audit?**
   Why it matters: captures habits for controls that must run all year.

7. **What do compliance automation platforms do well, and where have you seen teams rely on them too much?**
   Why it matters: helps the agent use these tools without mistaking a dashboard for an audit.

8. **How do you build records of processing for a small business that has never done it?**
   Why it matters: turns a dull register into a practical method the agent can repeat.

9. **What is your routine when a rights request arrives?**
   Why it matters: records the steps and deadlines the agent should follow.

10. **Describe a data incident you helped log. What did you do in the first hours, and who decided on notification?**
    Why it matters: shows the expert's line between logging facts and making legal decisions.

11. **What do you look for first when reading a supplier contract?**
    Why it matters: captures the reading order and the clauses that matter most to small firms.

12. **Which contract clauses do small businesses most often miss until it is too late?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a priority list for flags and renewal trackers.

13. **How do you take minutes from messy notes without changing what the board decided?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's habits for accuracy and for points to confirm with the chair.

14. **How do you build and keep a governance calendar so nothing is missed?**
    Why it matters: turns deadlines into a system the agent can maintain.

15. **How do you screen a new AI tool a team wants to use?**
    Why it matters: captures practical AI Act and privacy screening for small firms.

16. **How do you keep policies short enough that people read them, but complete enough for an auditor?**
    Why it matters: records the balance between usable and evidential documents.

17. **Which official sources do you check, and how often, to stay current?**
    Why it matters: builds the agent's refresh routine from a working expert's habits.

18. **When did a client want you to say "we're compliant", and how did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert keeps the line under pressure.

19. **Tell me about the hardest compliance job you have done and what you would do differently.**
    Why it matters: records judgement in a hard case, which plain AI lacks.
