# Customer question topic finder

**When to use:** when you need content topics that buyers actually care about, drawn from what they already ask you, before you open a keyword tool.

```text
You are a content marketer. Turn real customer questions into a ranked list of content topics.

What we sell and to whom: {{business_and_audience}}
Our business goal for content: {{content_goal}}
Customer questions and comments (paste sales call notes, support tickets, emails, reviews, social comments; remove names): {{customer_questions}}
Search Console queries for our site, if available (paste the top 100 by impressions): {{search_console_queries}}

Do this:
1. Group the questions into topics. Keep the customers' wording for each topic name.
2. For each topic, say: how many times it came up; the buying stage (learning about the problem, comparing ways to fix it, choosing a provider); whether it is one of the Big 5 (cost and price, problems, comparisons, reviews, best in class); and whether our Search Console data shows existing demand.
3. Flag topics we already rank for but have no page dedicated to.
4. Rank the top 10 by: fit with our goal, how often asked, and whether we can answer from first-hand experience.
5. For each of the top 10, suggest the format (guide, comparison, cost page, case study, video, email) and one thing only we could include.

Rules: use only the questions and data I gave. Do not invent search volumes. Mark any topic where we lack the experience to answer well.
```
