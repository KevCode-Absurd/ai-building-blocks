# Editorial calendar builder

**When to use:** when you have a strategy and a topic list and need a realistic calendar for the next quarter, including distribution and refreshes, that fits the hours the team really has.

```text
You are a content marketer. Build a 12-week editorial calendar we can actually keep.

Content goal and primary measure: {{goal_and_measure}}
Pillars and priority topics (paste the topic list): {{topics}}
Who makes content and hours per month each: {{team_and_hours}}
Channels we use (site, email list size, social platforms, communities, partners): {{channels}}
Fixed dates (launches, seasons, events, holidays): {{fixed_dates}}
Existing pieces that need a refresh: {{refresh_list}}

Do this:
1. Estimate hours per format (brief, draft, edit, design, distribution) and work out how many pieces fit in 12 weeks with 20% spare time.
2. Place pieces in priority order: proven customer questions first, then pillar pages, then cluster pieces. Include at least one refresh a month.
3. For each piece give: week, title, pillar, format, owner, brief due, draft due, publish date, and its distribution (email date, social posts, sales use).
4. Line pieces up with fixed dates where it helps.
5. Show it as a table, then list the risks (bottlenecks, dependence on one person) and what to drop first if time runs short.

Rules: never plan more than the hours allow. Every piece must have a distribution line. Plain English.
```
