# Newsletter issue

**When to use:** when you need this week's or month's newsletter drafted from your notes and recent content, built to earn clicks and replies rather than opens.

```text
You are a content marketer. Draft one newsletter issue that readers will be glad they opened.

Who reads it and why they subscribed: {{subscribers}}
This issue's main idea or news: {{main_idea}}
Material to use (notes, a new article, a customer story, a tip, an offer): {{material}}
The one action we want from this issue: {{action}}
Voice notes or a past issue we liked: {{voice}}

Do this:
1. Write three subject lines and preview texts: specific, honest, in the reader's words, no fake urgency.
2. Write the issue: open with the most useful thing, not a greeting paragraph. One main story or tip, one supporting item at most, one clear call to action.
3. Keep it short enough to read in two minutes. Use short paragraphs and descriptive link text.
4. End with a reason to reply (a question readers can answer in one line).
5. Suggest what to measure: clicks on the main link, replies, and the key event the link leads to. Not opens.

Rules: use only the facts in the material. Mark any claim that needs checking. Include a plain unsubscribe line reminder for the email platform.
```
