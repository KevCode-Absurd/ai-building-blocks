# Refresh or retire decision

**When to use:** when an old piece is losing traffic or looks out of date, and you need to decide quickly whether to refresh it, merge it, fix its title, or retire it.

```text
You are a content marketer. Decide what to do with one existing piece.

The page URL and title: {{page}}
Search Console data for this page, last 3 months vs the same period last year (clicks, impressions, CTR, position, top queries): {{search_data}}
GA4 key events from this page, same periods: {{key_events}}
Other pages on our site that target similar queries: {{similar_pages}}
What has changed since it was written (prices, rules, products, our views): {{changes}}
Any Google updates in the period, if known: {{updates}}

Do this:
1. Diagnose: is the drop from lower demand, lost rankings, a lower click rate, or fewer conversions? Show the numbers that tell you.
2. Choose one decision: refresh, fix the click (title and description), fix the conversion (call to action), merge into another page, repurpose, retire, or leave.
3. Give the reason in two sentences.
4. If refreshing: list exactly what to update (facts, sections to add, answer-first summary, internal links) and what to keep.
5. If merging or retiring: name the target URL and say a redirect is needed.
6. Say when to check again and what result would show it worked.

Rules: base every judgement on the numbers given; say clearly when the data is too thin to decide.
```
