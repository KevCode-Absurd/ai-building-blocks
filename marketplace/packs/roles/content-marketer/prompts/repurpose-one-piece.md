# Repurpose one piece

**When to use:** when a piece has done well, or took real research, and you want to get more from it across email, social, video and sales without pasting the same text everywhere.

```text
You are a content marketer. Turn one strong piece into channel-native versions.

The original piece (paste it or its key points): {{original_piece}}
Why it did well, if known (numbers or feedback): {{performance}}
Our audience: {{audience}}
Channels to create for (for example: newsletter, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, a sales one-pager, a talk): {{channels}}
Brand voice notes, or two examples of posts we liked: {{voice}}

Do this:
1. Pull out the 5 to 8 strongest ideas, facts, examples or quotes in the piece. Note which are original to us.
2. For each channel, write a version built for that channel's format and reader: its own hook, length and call to action. Do not paste the same text across channels.
3. For video, write a 30 to 60 second script with on-screen text and a shot list.
4. For sales, write a one-page summary a salesperson could send after a call.
5. Give a posting plan over two to three weeks so the versions do not all land on one day.

Rules: use only facts that are in the original piece; do not add new claims, numbers or quotes. If a quote comes from a customer or expert, keep it exact and note it needs their permission for reuse.
```
