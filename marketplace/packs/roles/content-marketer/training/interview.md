# Interview: content marketer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Strategy

1. **Walk me through how you build a content strategy for a new client, from the first call to the first piece.**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence and what they refuse to skip.
2. **How do you tie content to a business goal when the client only says "we need more traffic"?**
   Why it matters: goal-setting is where most content plans fail.
3. **How do you choose the few topics a business should own, and what makes you drop a topic?**
   Why it matters: topic selection is the expert's core judgement.
4. **Where do your best topic ideas come from: sales calls, support, search data, communities? Give an example.**
   Why it matters: reveals their research habits and sources.

## Making the work

5. **Show me a brief you are proud of. What makes it good enough for a stranger to write from?**
   Why it matters: the brief format becomes the agent's.
6. **How do you get real experience and expertise into content when the client is busy?**
   Why it matters: original material is what separates good content from slop.
7. **How do you use AI in your process today, and where do you never let it near the work?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's rules for AI drafting.
8. **What does your editing pass look like? What do you cut first?**
   Why it matters: their edit becomes the agent's quality checks.
9. **How do you decide the format: article, video, email, case study, webinar?**
   Why it matters: format choices depend on audience and team, and experts have rules of thumb.

## Distribution and measurement

10. **What happens after a piece is published? Walk me through the two weeks after.**
    Why it matters: distribution is where most content loses its value.
11. **How do you repurpose one piece across channels without it feeling copied?**
    Why it matters: captures their repurposing method.
12. **Which numbers do you report to clients, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: separates real measurement from vanity metrics.
13. **How do you show content's effect on sales when attribution is messy?**
    Why it matters: every client asks this, and good answers are rare.
14. **How do you decide to refresh, merge or delete an old piece?**
    Why it matters: maintenance judgement keeps a site healthy.

## Search and platforms

15. **How has your approach changed since AI answers started appearing in search results?**
    Why it matters: shows how current their practice is.
16. **Which tools do you use for research, analytics, planning and publishing, and what do you ask clients to share?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.

## Mistakes, clients and stories

17. **What are the three most common content mistakes you see small businesses make?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
18. **A client wants 50 blog posts next month written by AI. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests their line on scaled, low-value content.
19. **What kind of client or project do you turn down?**
    Why it matters: defines what the agent is not for.
20. **Which industries do you know best, and what is different about content for them?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty".
21. **Tell me about the hardest content project you have done: what went wrong and how you fixed it.**
    Why it matters: hard stories show judgement under pressure.
22. **Paste two pieces you are proud of and one that flopped. Why did each go the way it did?**
    Why it matters: gives real examples of their standard and their lessons.
