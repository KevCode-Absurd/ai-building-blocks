# Monthly editorial cycle

**Cadence:** monthly, starting in the last week of the previous month. Assumes a strategy already exists (if not, run the content-strategy skill first).

**Trigger:** the start of each month, or when the calendar runs dry.

## Steps

1. **Review last month (skill: content-performance-review; level 1).** Pull Search Console, GA4 key events and email clicks. Sort pieces into grow, refresh, fix, merge, repurpose, retire or leave.
2. **Refill the topic list (prompt: customer-question-topic-finder).** Add the new questions from sales calls, support tickets and comments this month.
3. **Plan the month (prompt: editorial-calendar-builder).** Pick the new pieces and refreshes that fit the team's hours, with distribution for each.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the month's plan: topics, refreshes, dates and owners.
4. **Brief each piece (skill: content-brief).** One page per piece, with the angle, must-includes, expert questions and the call to action.
5. **Gather original material.** Interview the expert, collect the business's data, photos and examples named in each brief.
6. **Draft and edit (skill: ai-assisted-drafting; level 2 saves drafts in the client's CMS).** Draft with placeholders, fill them from real sources, run the fact, originality, slop, voice and structure passes.
   **Approval point 2:** the subject expert or owner checks facts, claims and anything in a regulated area; the client approves the final piece.
7. **Publish (tool: the client's CMS; level 3 within the client's limits, logged; at level 1 or 2 the client publishes).** Add author byline, date, internal links, search title and description, and the AI note where needed. Check the GA4 key event fires on the call to action.
8. **Distribute (workflow: repurpose-and-distribute; level 3 to send and schedule, level 2 to save as drafts).** Send the email, schedule the social versions, give sales their one-pager.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the email and posts. At level 3 this is the one-time confirmation of the month's send and post list; I then send and schedule within it and log each one.
9. **Log.** Record what shipped, when, and the review date for each piece.

## Outputs

- Last month's review and decisions.
- This month's calendar.
- Briefs, edited drafts, fact logs.
- Published pieces with tracking checked.
- Distribution pack and schedule.

## Needs the client's approval

- The month's plan (point 1).
- Each final piece, including facts and any claims (point 2).
- The month's send and post list (point 3). At level 3, after this one-time yes, I publish, send and schedule within the agreed sites, lists, channels and dates, and log each action.
- Always a new yes: any redirect or removal of an existing page, a new list or audience, paid promotion, or anything outside the agreed limits.
- At level 1 or 2, the client or their team publishes and sends.
