# Quarterly content review

**Cadence:** every three months, and after any Google core or spam update that hits the site.

**Trigger:** the end of a quarter, a traffic drop, or a change in business goals.

## Steps

1. **Gather data (level 1; tools: Search Console export, GA4 key events, email platform reports, form answers to "How did you hear about us?").** Three months, plus the same three months last year.
2. **Check the context.** Google's Search Status Dashboard for updates in the period; any tracking, site or product changes; seasonality.
3. **Review every piece (skill: content-performance-review).** Sort into grow, refresh, fix the click, fix the conversion, merge, repurpose, retire or leave.
4. **Decide on old pages (prompt: refresh-or-retire-decision).** For each page marked refresh, merge or retire, write the specific action.
5. **Check the strategy (skill: content-strategy).** Are the pillars still right? Has the business goal changed? Do new customer questions point to a new topic? Is the cadence realistic?
6. **Write the quarterly report.** What content earned against the goal, what worked by pillar and format, the decisions table, and next quarter's three priorities.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the decisions, especially any merges, redirects or removals, and any change to the strategy.
7. **Feed the plan.** Put approved refreshes and new pieces into the next monthly editorial cycle.

## Outputs

- Quarterly report with decisions table.
- Refresh, merge and retire actions with owners and dates.
- Updated strategy one-pager if anything changed.

## Needs the client's approval

- Any merge, redirect or removal of a page (point 1).
- Any change to goals, pillars or cadence (point 1).
- Changes to tracking setup in GA4 or Search Console: the client's admin makes them, or I make the agreed changes at level 3 and log them.

Reading data and writing the report need level 1 only.
