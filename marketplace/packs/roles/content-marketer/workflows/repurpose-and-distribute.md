# Repurpose and distribute

**Trigger:** a new piece is published, or a review finds a strong piece that has not been used beyond the site.

**Time:** two to three weeks of scheduled posts and emails from one source piece.

## Steps

1. **Pick the piece.** New pieces always qualify. For older pieces, use the content-performance-review skill to find the ones that earn key events or took real research.
2. **Extract the core (prompt: repurpose-one-piece, step 1).** List the 5 to 8 strongest ideas, facts, examples and quotes, and mark which are original to the business.
3. **Check permissions.** Any customer or expert quote, photo or name reused in new places needs that person's permission. Any creator or partner who is paid or given free product must disclose it in their post.
4. **Write channel versions (prompt: repurpose-one-piece).** Newsletter feature, two to four social posts written for each platform, a short video script, a sales one-pager. Each gets its own hook and one call to action.
5. **Write the email (prompt: newsletter-issue).** Judge it later by clicks and replies, not opens.
6. **Schedule.** Spread versions over two to three weeks. Note which version goes where and when.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the versions and the schedule. At level 3 this is the one-time confirmation of the action list; after it I send and schedule within the agreed channels, lists and dates.
7. **Send and post (tools: the client's email platform and social scheduler).** Level 3: send and schedule within the agreed limits, and log each one. Level 2: save everything as drafts or unscheduled posts for the client to send. Level 1: hand over the pack for the client to post.
8. **Watch and reply** (level 3 to reply from the brand's accounts, within the kinds of reply agreed; otherwise draft the replies). Answer comments and replies within a day where possible; note questions that could become new pieces.
9. **Measure after two weeks.** Clicks and key events by channel. Feed results into the next monthly review.

## Outputs

- Core ideas list with sources.
- Channel versions: email, social posts, video script, sales one-pager.
- Posting schedule.
- Two-week results note and new topic ideas.

## Needs the client's approval

- The versions and schedule (point 1). At level 3, after this one-time yes, I send and schedule within the agreed channels, lists and dates, and log each one.
- Always a new yes: a new channel, list or audience, or any reply about a complaint, refund or legal matter.
- Reuse of any customer or expert quote, image or name (permission from that person).
- Any paid promotion or creator partnership, including how it will be disclosed.
