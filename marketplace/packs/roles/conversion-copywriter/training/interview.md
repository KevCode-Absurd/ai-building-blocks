# Interview: conversion copywriter

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first three days of a recent copy job, from brief to first draft.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **What research do you insist on before you write a word, and what do you do when the client has none?**
   Why it matters: research discipline is the biggest gap between expert copy and generic AI copy.
3. **How do you decide where a page should start: the offer, the product, the result or the problem?**
   Why it matters: reveals how the expert reads awareness in practice.
4. **Tell me about a market where every competitor made the same claim. How did you stand out?**
   Why it matters: captures how they handle sophisticated, tired markets.
5. **How do you turn a pile of reviews or call transcripts into a message? Show me your notes format.**
   Why it matters: the coding and sorting method is a reusable skill.
6. **How do you write headlines: how many, from what, and how do you pick?**
   Why it matters: shows their generation and selection process.

## Offers, proof and honesty

7. **When have you changed the offer itself rather than the words? What happened?**
   Why it matters: experts know when copy is not the real problem.
8. **What proof do you trust most, and what proof do you refuse to use?**
   Why it matters: sets their personal standard for claims and testimonials.
9. **A client wants a countdown timer or "only 3 left" when it isn't true. What do you say?**
   Why it matters: tests their ethical line and how they hold it with clients.
10. **How do you handle a testimonial that shows an unusually good result?**
    Why it matters: checks they know typical-results disclosure and permission.

## Quality bar and tools

11. **What do you check before you send copy to a client? Give me your actual checklist.**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality checks.
12. **Which tools do you use for research, analytics, heatmaps and testing, and what do you ask clients to share?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.
13. **How do you decide whether something is worth A/B testing, and how long you run it?**
    Why it matters: separates real testing practice from guesswork.
14. **Tell me about a test you were sure would win and lost. What did you learn?**
    Why it matters: captures humility and the lessons that change future work.

## Mistakes and clients

15. **What are the three mistakes you see most in copy written by non-copywriters, or by AI?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
16. **What feedback from clients do you push back on, and what do you always accept?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent handles disagreement.
17. **What kind of client or project do you turn down, and why?**
    Why it matters: defines what the agent is not for.
18. **Which industries or offers do you know best, and what is different about writing for them?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty".

## Voice and craft

19. **Paste three pieces of copy you are proud of. What makes each one work?**
    Why it matters: gives real examples of their standard and their voice.
20. **How do you edit your own first draft? What do you cut first?**
    Why it matters: their editing pass becomes the agent's.
21. **What words or phrases do you never use in copy?**
    Why it matters: builds the "words I avoid" list in personality.md.

## A hard job

22. **Tell me about the hardest copy job you have done: the brief, what went wrong, and how you got it over the line.**
    Why it matters: hard stories show judgement under pressure better than any checklist.
23. **If you had to train a junior copywriter in one week, what would you teach first?**
    Why it matters: reveals what the expert thinks matters most.
