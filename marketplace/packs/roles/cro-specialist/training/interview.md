# Interview: CRO specialist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Research and prioritising

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new client, from access to your first ranked list.**
   Why it matters: shows the real research order, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you find the step that matters most in a funnel? Show me a recent example.**
   Why it matters: separates absolute-loss thinking from chasing the worst-looking rate.
3. **What do you look for in session recordings, and how many do you watch before you trust a pattern?**
   Why it matters: captures their behaviour-reading method and evidence bar.
4. **How do you score and rank ideas? What do you do with ideas that have no evidence?**
   Why it matters: prioritisation is where most CRO time is won or lost.
5. **When do you fix something without testing it?**
   Why it matters: knowing when not to test is a mark of expertise.

## Test design

6. **How do you choose the smallest change worth detecting with a client?**
   Why it matters: ties statistics to the client's economics.
7. **Tell me about a time you told a client their traffic was too low to test. What did you do instead?**
   Why it matters: honesty under pressure and practical alternatives.
8. **Which statistics method does your usual testing tool use, and how does that change when you can look at results?**
   Why it matters: checks they understand fixed-horizon, sequential and tool-specific methods.
9. **How do you pick primary metrics and guardrails for a store, a lead form and a booking flow?**
   Why it matters: metric choice decides whether a "win" is real.
10. **What is your QA checklist before a test goes live?**
    Why it matters: most broken tests fail at launch, not at analysis.

## Reading results

11. **Tell me about a test where the split was off. How did you spot it, and what was the cause?**
    Why it matters: sample ratio mismatch is the most common silent failure.
12. **A client wants to stop a test early because it "hit 95%". What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests their stance on peeking and how they explain it.
13. **How do you handle a test with four variants?**
    Why it matters: checks multiple-comparison corrections in practice.
14. **What do you do when a segment wins but the overall test does not?**
    Why it matters: separates honest readers from segment fishers.
15. **Tell me about a result that looked too good. What was really going on?**
    Why it matters: a real Twyman's law story is gold for training.
16. **How do you write a readout so the owner can decide in one minute?**
    Why it matters: the format of the call is a reusable skill.

## Consent, privacy and tools

17. **How do you check what fires before consent on a site? What do you usually find?**
    Why it matters: consent errors are common and costly.
18. **How do you set masking on recordings for forms, accounts and checkout?**
    Why it matters: protects visitors and the client.
19. **Which testing and analytics tools do you trust, and what does each do badly?**
    Why it matters: real tool limits shape plans.
20. **What do you do when consent limits mean your data covers only some visitors?**
    Why it matters: tests honesty about what the data can show.

## Clients and craft

21. **What do clients most often get wrong about CRO, and how do you reset their expectations?**
    Why it matters: helps the agent handle clients who want the wrong thing.
22. **Tell me about the hardest engagement you've had. What went wrong and what did you learn?**
    Why it matters: a story of judgement under pressure, the heart of training.
