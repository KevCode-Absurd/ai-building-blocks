# Research sprint

**Trigger:** a new client, a conversion drop, a redesign or platform move, or a list of test ideas with no evidence behind them.

**Cadence:** once at the start of an engagement, then a lighter repeat each quarter or after any big site change.

## Steps

1. **Agree the goal and access.** The one goal action and its value; which access level the client grants for analytics, behaviour, testing and site tools; the regions visitors come from.
2. **Consent first (skill: tracking-and-consent-check; prompt: consent-and-recording-check).** Check what fires before consent and how recordings are masked. Fixes to consent settings need the client's fresh yes; until they are live, use only data collected lawfully.
3. **Funnel (level 1; tool: Google Analytics 4 or the store's own analytics).** Steps from landing to goal by device and source, for recent whole weeks.
4. **Behaviour (level 1; tool: Microsoft Clarity, Hotjar or Contentsquare, where consent rules are met).** Patterns at the leaking step, with counts of sessions showing each.
5. **Checkout, forms and speed (skill: conversion-research-audit, steps 5 and 6).** Compare with Baymard's public findings and Core Web Vitals thresholds.
6. **Customer words (conversion copywriter kit: voice-of-customer-mining).** Doubts and questions that match the leak.
7. **Usability round, if possible.** Five people try the main task; note where they stumble.
8. **Rank (prompt: ranked-fix-list).** Fix now, test, judgement call, drop.
9. **Ship fixes (level 2 or 3).** Write clear bug fixes into a draft or duplicate page (level 2), or publish the ones the client approved (level 3), and log each.
10. **Hand over the test plan.** The first test goes into the experiment cycle.

## Outputs

- Consent and tracking findings, with fixes by region.
- Funnel table and biggest leak.
- Behaviour, checkout, speed and customer-word findings with evidence.
- Ranked fix list.
- Log of fixes shipped.
- First test brief.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access levels and limits for each tool.
- Any change to consent settings or tracking before consent (always a fresh yes).
- Publishing fixes at level 3: the one-time confirmation of the action list; a fresh yes for prices, offers, checkout payment steps and anything outside the list.
