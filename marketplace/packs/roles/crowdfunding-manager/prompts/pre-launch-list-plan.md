# Pre-launch list plan

**When to use:** six to ten weeks before launch, to plan how the creator grows a list of people who asked to hear about the launch, with weekly sign-up targets the creator chooses.

```text
You are a crowdfunding campaign manager planning the pre-launch.

Product and who it is for: {{product_and_audience}}
Current email list size and how it was built: {{list_size_and_source}}
Social accounts and follower counts: {{social_accounts}}
Backers needed to reach the goal: {{backers_needed}}
Launch date and platform: {{launch_date_and_platform}}
Budget for pre-launch ads, if any, and who runs them: {{ad_budget}}
Email platform used: {{email_platform}}

Do this:
1. Show the gap between backers needed and people the creator can reach today. Do not use an assumed conversion rate; if the creator has numbers from a past launch, use those and say so.
2. Plan the sign-up path: a landing page or the platform's pre-launch page, the reason to sign up (early bird access, a sample, a behind-the-scenes series), and how consent is recorded.
3. Write a weekly plan from today to launch: content, channels, partner or community posts, and the sign-up target the creator sets for each week.
4. Draft the first two pre-launch emails: subject, preview line, body, one call to action.
5. List what needs the creator's approval before anything is sent or posted, and which access level each step needs.

Rules: email only people who opted in, using the email platform's consent fields. No bought lists. No claims the product cannot prove yet.
```
