# Interview: crowdfunding campaign manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the manager's work (for example, hardware questions for someone who only runs book campaigns).

## How you approach a campaign

1. **Walk me through your last campaign from the first call to the last reward shipped.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and the real order of work, not the textbook one.
2. **What do you ask a creator in the first conversation, and what answer makes you say "not yet"?**
   Why it matters: an expert's intake questions and red lines are the most reusable part of the craft.
3. **How do you set a funding goal? Show me a budget table you used.**
   Why it matters: the goal is where most campaigns are won or lost, and the table shows which costs an expert never forgets.
4. **How do you decide between Kickstarter and Indiegogo now? What changed for you after Indiegogo's 2025 upgrade?**
   Why it matters: platform choice depends on current rules, and old habits can mislead.
5. **How do you judge whether a creator's list is big enough for their goal?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert sizes the pre-launch gap without made-up conversion rates.

## Rewards, page and video

6. **How do you design a reward ladder? Show me one that worked and one that did not.**
   Why it matters: real ladders teach what backers compare and what confuses them.
7. **How do you check that every tier makes money? What cost do creators forget most often?**
   Why it matters: the forgotten cost becomes a check the agent always runs.
8. **How do you handle shipping, VAT and duties for overseas backers?**
   Why it matters: shipping and import charges cause many losses and angry backers.
9. **What goes on the page above the first reward, and why?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert builds trust before asking for money.
10. **How do you write the risks section so it is honest without scaring people off?**
    Why it matters: honest risk writing is a skill plain AI usually gets wrong.
11. **What makes a campaign video work, in your experience? What do you cut?**
    Why it matters: turns taste into rules the agent can apply to scripts.

## Running the campaign

12. **What does your launch day look like, hour by hour?**
    Why it matters: launch-day timing is specific and hard to guess.
13. **What do you do when pledges stall in the middle?**
    Why it matters: real rescue moves beat generic advice to "post more".
14. **How do you spot a fake large pledge or a "guaranteed backers" scam?**
    Why it matters: protects clients from scams platforms warn about.
15. **What do you write in the final 48 hours, and what do you refuse to write?**
    Why it matters: shows the line between urgency and false scarcity.

## After funding

16. **How do you plan the pledge manager and the factory order after funding?**
    Why it matters: the order of these steps decides whether money runs short.
17. **Tell me about a delay you had to announce. What did the update say, and how did backers react?**
    Why it matters: the hardest message in the role, best learned from a real example.
18. **How do you handle refund requests and chargebacks with the creator?**
    Why it matters: sets the line between what the agent drafts and what the creator decides.
19. **What do you track between funding and delivery, and how often do you update backers?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's post-campaign routine.

## Judgement and limits

20. **When has a creator wanted something you would not do? What happened?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's red lines on honesty, dates and investment language.
21. **When does a crowdfunding question become a legal, tax or investment question for you?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent when to say "ask a qualified adviser".
22. **What access do you ask for in a client's Kickstarter, Indiegogo and email tools, and what do you never touch?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's access levels in real practice.
23. **Tell me about the hardest campaign you have run.**
    Why it matters: a full story shows judgement under pressure better than any rule.
