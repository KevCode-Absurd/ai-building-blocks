# After funding: pledge manager to delivery

**Trigger:** the campaign ends funded. **Cadence:** a backer update at least monthly until every reward ships (Kickstarter's stated minimum after funding, checked 6 Nov 2026), plus updates whenever a date moves.

## Steps

1. **Thank-you update (skill: backer-updates-and-emails).** Within a day of the end: thanks, the final numbers, and the next dates (survey or pledge manager, production steps, shipping window), each labelled firm or hopeful.
2. **Money check (level 1).** Read the final totals, dropped pledges and fees once the payout statement is available. Re-run the goal table with real numbers. Kickstarter advises against binding manufacturing contracts until the project has funded and the payout has arrived, so the factory order waits for the payout. Signing it needs the client's fresh yes; paying it stays with the client.
3. **Pledge manager set-up (level 2).** Build the survey, add-ons, shipping rates by zone, tax settings and, on Kickstarter, any tariff surcharge the client asked for. Check VAT handling for UK and EU backers and the EU duty plan (EUR 3 per item type from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028; DDP or DDU) with the client's accountant and fulfilment partner. Check EU product safety information (manufacturer and EU responsible person details, product identification, warnings in the buyer's language).
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the pledge manager settings and tax wording. Opening it charges backers, so it needs the client's fresh yes.
4. **Production tracking.** Keep a dated tracker: final files, samples, sample approval, mass production, quality check, freight booking, customs, fulfilment. Ask the factory and forwarder for written dates.
5. **Monthly update.** What was done, photos, what is next, the delivery date restated as firm or hopeful. Level 2: draft. Level 3: post the approved update within the agreed limits and log it.
6. **Delays.** If a date slips, post a delay update that week: the news first, why, the new date, what backers can do, when the next update comes. Mark delayed rewards in the backer report status (Kickstarter) at level 3.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves every delay update before it goes out.
7. **Refund requests.** Collect requests, draft replies, and prepare a refund list with amounts. The client decides and issues refunds; Kickstarter collaborators cannot issue them. Formal complaints and chargeback responses need the client's fresh yes each time.
8. **Shipping.** Share tracking through the pledge manager or fulfilment partner; mark rewards shipped. Watch comments for damage and loss and log replacements against the damage allowance.
9. **Close-out.** Final update when all rewards have shipped; report on budget versus actual, delays and lessons for the next campaign.

## Outputs

- Thank-you update and dated next steps.
- Updated budget with real totals.
- Pledge manager configuration, tested.
- Production tracker.
- Monthly and delay updates, with the action log.
- Refund list for the client.
- Close-out report.

## Needs the client's approval

- Factory, freight and fulfilment contracts, every time; payment stays with the client.
- Pledge manager settings, tax wording, and opening the pledge manager.
- Every delay update before it is posted.
- Refunds (the client issues them), formal complaints and chargeback responses.
- Any change to reward contents, shipping terms or dates.
- Changing payout or bank details: the client only.
