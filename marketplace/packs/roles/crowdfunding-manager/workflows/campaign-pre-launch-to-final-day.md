# Campaign plan: pre-launch to final day

**Trigger:** a creator decides to crowdfund a product and has, or can get, written quotes. Start at least eight weeks before the hoped-for launch. Kickstarter recommends campaigns of 30 days or less and advises sharing the pre-launch page at least a week before launch; Indiegogo needs a launch date at least 7 days after the review request (all checked 6 Nov 2026).

## Steps

1. **Intake (level 1).** Ask what is being made, real costs, audience size, platform and launch window, and which access level the client grants in each tool. Write it under "Access I have". Use the questions in access.md.
2. **Platform check (prompt: platform-choice-check).** Confirm the platform's current funding model, length limits, fees and launch timing on its own help pages, with dates.
3. **Goal and budget (skill: funding-goal-and-budget).** Build the goal from quotes, run the 100% and 90% checks, compare backers needed with the list. If the goal is out of reach, change scope or the date before anything else.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the goal and budget. Tax and duty lines are confirmed by a qualified accountant.
4. **Rewards (skill: reward-tier-pricing).** Five to eight tiers and add-ons, each costed after fees, making, packing, damage allowance and shipping to every zone. Decide Late Pledges and Pledge Over Time now.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the reward table, limits and delivery dates labelled firm or hopeful.
5. **Pre-launch plan (prompt: pre-launch-list-plan).** Weekly plan to launch, sign-up targets the client sets, first emails. Level 2: emails saved as drafts. Level 3: approved pre-launch emails sent to the client's own consented list on the agreed dates.
6. **Page and video (skill: campaign-page-and-video-script; prompt: risks-and-challenges-section).** Page copy in backer reading order, risks section, FAQ, video script. The video is built with the video producer kit's methods. Level 2: build the draft page and rewards in the platform, not launched.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the page, video, rewards as entered, and every claim and disclosure.
7. **Launch-week task list.** Hour-by-hour for launch day, then daily tasks for days 2 to 7: emails, platform updates, comment replies, partner posts, any ads within the cap (Meta and Google Ads kits).
8. **Launch.** The client presses launch on Kickstarter, or the approved Indiegogo date arrives. Launching locks the goal and deadline, so it needs the client's fresh yes even at level 3.
9. **Run the campaign (skill: backer-updates-and-emails).** Level 3: post approved updates and reply to comments with approved answers, within the agreed limits. Daily check of pledges and referral sources; stop and ask on suspicious large pledges, cancellation spikes or platform warnings. If it stalls, use the stalled-campaign-rescue prompt.
10. **Final 48 hours (prompt: final-48-hours-push).** Reminder email, two updates, social posts.
11. **Close.** Report results, list follow-ups for the after-funding workflow, and hand over the action log.

## Outputs

- Platform check with dated facts.
- Goal table and checks.
- Reward table with costs and margins.
- Pre-launch plan and email drafts.
- Page copy, risks section, FAQ and video script.
- Launch-week task list.
- Update and email calendar, drafts, and the action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access level and limits for each tool.
- Goal and budget (point 1), rewards (point 2), page and video (point 3).
- Launching the campaign, every time.
- Any new or changed reward, price, add-on, date or shipping rule after launch.
- Ad spend, within a cap the client sets.
- Anything that offers a financial return: stop and ask a qualified adviser.
- Refunds, payouts and bank details stay with the client.
