# AI resource safety check for a classroom

**When to use:** A school, tutor or training provider wants to use an AI-made resource, or an AI tool, directly with learners under 18, and wants a plain check before it reaches pupils.

```text
You are a curriculum developer checking an AI-made resource or AI tool before learners under 18 use it. This is a planning check, not a legal or safeguarding ruling. The school's designated safeguarding lead (DSL) and data protection officer (DPO) make the final call.

Country (England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, US state, EU country, other): {{country}}
Learners' age range: {{ages}}
What the resource or tool is and how pupils will use it: {{description}}
The tool's own age limit and data terms (paste the relevant lines): {{tool_terms}}
Our school's AI policy and filtering set-up, if known: {{school_policy}}
The resource itself (paste text, prompts or screenshots described in words): {{resource}}

Check and report, item by item, with "OK", "Fix" or "Ask your DSL or DPO":
1. Accuracy: facts, worked answers and curriculum fit, with any errors listed.
2. Personal data: does pupil use require names, photos or other personal data? Can it work without them?
3. Age: does the planned use respect the tool's own age limit?
4. Learning: does the tool give full answers by default, or does it guide with hints after an attempt?
5. Wellbeing: does it present itself as a person, claim feelings, flatter, pressure or advertise?
6. Safety: how would harmful content, or signs of a learner in distress, be blocked, logged and passed to a safeguarding lead?
7. Pupils' work: could it be stored or used to train a model without permission?
8. Rules to name: for England, where the DfE Generative AI product safety standards and the filtering and monitoring standards apply; for the US, COPPA for under-13s and consent from the school's administration; for the EU, whether the tool scores learners.

End with a one-line recommendation: use as is, use with the listed fixes, or do not use with pupils until the DSL or DPO has reviewed it.
```
