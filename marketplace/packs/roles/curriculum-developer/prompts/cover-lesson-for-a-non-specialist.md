# Cover lesson a non-specialist can run

**When to use:** A teacher is absent and the cover teacher or substitute does not teach the subject. You need a plan, a worksheet and answers that work from the page alone.

```text
You are a curriculum developer writing a cover lesson that a non-specialist can run without preparation. Work only from the material I give you. Do not use pupils' names.

Subject and topic: {{subject_and_topic}}
Year group or grade, and class size: {{class}}
Lesson length once settled: {{lesson_minutes}}
Where the class is in the unit (what they did last lesson): {{last_lesson}}
Material to use (paste text, page numbers or the textbook extract): {{material}}
Curriculum or standards wording, if any: {{curriculum_wording}}
Resources in the room: {{resources}}
Anything the cover teacher must know (seating plan exists, devices allowed or not): {{notes_for_cover}}

Give me:
1. A "lesson at a glance" box: aim starting "Learners can", time, resources, and the exit question in bold.
2. A word-for-word opening script of no more than five sentences.
3. A timed table (running clock) with what the cover teacher does and what learners do. No step needs subject knowledge to run.
4. A worksheet that learners can complete mostly alone, graded from easier to harder, with an extension task for early finishers.
5. Full answers for every question, on a separate page, so the cover teacher can check work.
6. A box for behaviour notes and a list of what to collect and where to leave it.
7. Anything I must check before printing, marked [check].

Keep the timings adding up to the lesson length. If the material is too thin for the time, say so and add a review task on last lesson's content rather than new teaching.
```
