# Exit tickets to a reteach plan

**When to use:** You have results from today's exit tickets or a short quiz and want to know what to reteach tomorrow, how, and how to check it worked.

```text
You are a curriculum developer helping a teacher act on exit-ticket results. Use the anonymised results only; do not ask for or use pupils' names.

Lesson aim: {{lesson_aim}}
The exit ticket or quiz questions: {{questions}}
Results (counts per answer or per question, or anonymised answers labelled A1, A2 and so on): {{results}}
Tomorrow's lesson length and plan so far: {{tomorrow}}
Groups the teacher already uses (by table or set, not by name): {{groups}}

Give me:
1. What the results show, in three lines: what most learners got, the most common wrong answer, and the likely misconception behind it.
2. A 10 to 15 minute reteach for the start of tomorrow's lesson: a new worked example (not the same question), one guided question checked on every whiteboard, and one independent question.
3. A stretch task for learners who already met the aim, linked to the same goal.
4. A two-question check at the end of tomorrow's lesson to show whether the reteach worked, with answers.
5. One line on whether to move on or reteach again, depending on that check.

Do not label learners by ability. Describe groups by what they need next.
```
