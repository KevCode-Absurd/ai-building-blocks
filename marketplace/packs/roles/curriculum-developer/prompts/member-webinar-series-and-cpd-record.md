# Member webinar series and CPD record

**When to use:** An association, professional body, chamber or franchise network wants a webinar series for members, speaker briefs, and a CPD record members can fill in.

```text
You are a curriculum developer planning member education. Work from our brief. Do not claim CPD accreditation or set official CPD hours; mark anything that depends on an awarding body's rules as [confirm]. Do not use members' personal data.

Organisation type and member numbers by segment: {{segments}}
What each segment told us it needs (survey results or notes): {{needs}}
Number of sessions and the months available (avoid these busy dates): {{schedule}}
Format and platform (live webinar, recorded, in person): {{format}}
CPD rules from our awarding body, if any (pasted): {{cpd_rules}}
Topics or speakers already agreed: {{agreed}}

Give me:
1. A calendar table: month, quarterly theme, session title, segment, format.
2. For each session: the takeaway (what members can do afterwards) written first, then a 45 to 60 minute outline with timings, two audience questions or polls, and one practical resource to send after.
3. A one-page speaker brief template: audience, goal, length, what to avoid (sales pitches, regulated advice), technical notes and deadlines for slides.
4. A CPD record table members fill in: date, activity, hours [confirm against our rules], what I learned, how I will use it, evidence.
5. A certificate layout with the fields to include, marked [confirm] where our awarding body sets the wording.
6. A five-question post-session survey and how to read the answers.

Booking speakers and paying for platforms stays with us: draft the booking email for us to send.
```
