# Member education year

**Trigger:** Once a year, two to three months before the programme year starts (often tied to renewals); a lighter review each quarter. For associations, professional bodies, chambers of commerce and franchise networks.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Gather (level 1).** Member segments and counts, last year's sessions with attendance and feedback, renewal dates and busy seasons, conference dates, the awarding body's CPD rules if members log hours, and any topics or speakers already agreed. No member personal data is needed.
2. **Read the needs.** Summarise what each segment asked for and what last year's feedback says. With web search connected, I also check the awarding body's current CPD rules page and note the date; without it, I use the rules you paste and mark hours [confirm].
3. **Shape the year.** Set four quarterly themes that serve more than one segment. Use the `scope-and-sequence-with-alignment` skill's member variant to place sessions month by month, away from busy dates.
4. **Outline sessions.** Run the `member-webinar-series-and-cpd-record` prompt: takeaway first, timed outline, audience questions, a practical resource to send after.
5. **Brief speakers.** One-page brief per speaker with a "please avoid" list (sales pitches, regulated advice). Booking speakers, agreeing fees and paying for platforms stay with the client; I draft the booking emails.
6. **CPD record and certificate.** Build the record and certificate template, with hours and wording marked [confirm] against the awarding body's rules.
7. **Check.** No accreditation claims; segments and needs traced to the brief; no member named; sessions accessible (captions on recordings, slides readable).
8. **Draft (level 2).** Create draft session pages and unsent invites and reminders in the webinar platform or email tool. *Approval: the education lead approves the calendar, outlines and invite copy.*
9. **Schedule and send (level 3).** After the yes, schedule the agreed sessions and send invites and reminders to the agreed member list on the agreed dates. Log each action.
10. **After each session.** Send the post-session survey; each quarter, summarise attendance and feedback and propose changes to the next quarter.

## Outputs

- 12-month calendar by theme and segment
- Session outlines and speaker briefs
- CPD record and certificate templates
- Draft or scheduled sessions, invites and reminders, with a log
- Quarterly feedback summary

## Needs the client's approval

**Approval points.** The calendar, outlines and invite copy before anything is scheduled or sent.

**Runs at level 3 after one confirmation.** Scheduling the agreed sessions and sending invites and reminders to the agreed member list.

**Always needs a fresh yes.** Sending to a new list or non-members; publishing a public event page for the first time; changing CPD hours or certificate wording; cancelling a session people registered for; anything that commits money, which stays with the client.
