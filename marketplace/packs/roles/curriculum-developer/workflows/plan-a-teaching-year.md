# Plan a teaching year

**Trigger:** Before a new school year, course intake or syllabus change; also when a school finds coverage gaps after a test or inspection. Plan about three to six weeks before teaching starts.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Most of this runs at level 1 and level 2.

## Steps

1. **Gather (level 1).** The syllabus, programme of study or state standards with exact codes, the version and adoption year; term dates, holidays, exam weeks and events; hours per week; the learners' starting point; existing schemes of work and resources.
2. **Check the version.** For England, confirm which programmes of study apply to the year; any plan running past summer 2028 carries the note that the revised national curriculum is due by spring 2027 for first teaching in September 2028. For US states, check the state's page for new standards scheduled to take effect. With web search connected, I check the official page live and note the date; without it, I work from the documents you paste and mark the version [confirm].
3. **Map the year.** Run the `scope-and-sequence-with-alignment` skill: count teaching weeks, group outcomes into units, order them, place assessment points and buffer weeks.
4. **Find the gaps.** Run the `standards-alignment-gap-check` prompt on the draft. Every outcome must be taught and checked at least once.
5. **Decide what does not fit.** If outcomes do not fit the weeks, show the count and options. *Approval: the head of department or owner chooses what to cut, combine or move.*
6. **Write unit plans.** One per unit: outcomes, key knowledge and skills, lesson-by-lesson outline, assessment and resources.
7. **Plan assessments.** For each assessment point, a blueprint using the `assessment-blueprint-and-mark-scheme` skill. Write the first unit's test in full; the rest follow during the year.
8. **Inclusion and access.** List the adaptations the year needs (reading range, learners of English, access needs described without names) and plan when to run the `adapted-materials-same-goal` skill. Check that digital materials meet the WCAG 2.2 basics.
9. **Save (level 2).** Save the year plan, unit plans and grid to the agreed folder; build hidden course sections in the LMS if used. *Approval: the head of department signs off the plan.*
10. **Publish (level 3).** After sign-off, make the agreed sections visible to the named courses and share the plan with named staff. Log each action.
11. **Review points.** Put a review in the calendar at the end of each term: compare test results with the plan and adjust the next term.

## Outputs

- Teaching week count and year-at-a-glance table
- Unit plans for every unit
- Alignment grid with no unexplained gaps
- Assessment calendar and the first unit test with mark scheme
- Adaptation plan and accessibility notes
- Decision list for the client

## Needs the client's approval

**Approval points.** What to cut or combine when outcomes do not fit; sign-off of the year plan before anything is shared or published.

**Runs at level 3 after one confirmation.** Making the agreed course sections visible and sharing the plan with named staff.

**Always needs a fresh yes.** Replacing a live course structure that holds learner work, deleting old courses or sections, and sharing outside the named staff.
