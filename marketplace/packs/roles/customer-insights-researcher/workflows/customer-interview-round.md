# Customer interview round

**Trigger:** A decision that needs to know why customers act as they do: why they cancel, why they chose a rival, why a feature goes unused, what to say on a product page. Typical length is two to four weeks for 8 to 15 interviews, set by how fast people can be recruited. The client or their moderator runs the interviews; I prepare, recruit and analyse.

**Access:** Ask at the start which level the client grants for each tool (CRM or email, survey tool for the screener, scheduling, call and transcription tool, research repository) and write it down as "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1 (read). Log every action: what, where, when, result.

## Steps

1. **Frame (day 1).** Write the decision, the three to five things to learn, who matters and what result would change the plan. *Approval: the client confirms the decision and the budget for incentives.*
2. **Privacy and ethics check (day 1).** Read the client's privacy notice; confirm research contact is allowed; list sensitive topics, children or vulnerable people; note questions for a qualified adviser. *Approval: the client confirms, with their adviser where needed. Check this with local rules.*
3. **Screener and outreach (days 1 to 2).** Use the `screener-and-outreach` prompt. *Level 2: build the screener unpublished and draft invitations unsent. Approval: the client approves every message and the list.*
4. **Consent (days 1 to 2).** Use the `consent-form-and-privacy-notice` prompt. *Approval: the client (and adviser if needed) approves the final text.*
5. **Recruit (days 3 to 10).** Run the `interview-recruiting-and-consent` skill. *Level 3 within agreed limits (which list, how many invitations, which dates, panel budget cap): publish the screener, send invitations and booking links after the client confirms the action list. A new audience or list needs a fresh yes. Paying incentives stays with the client; I keep the list of who is owed what.*
6. **Guide (days 3 to 6).** Use the `discussion-guide` prompt; pilot it with one person if possible. *Approval: the client approves the guide.*
7. **Interviews (days 6 to 15).** The client or their moderator runs each session, reads the consent script and records only with consent. After every three to four interviews, I read the notes and suggest guide tweaks. *Level 1: read transcripts as they arrive.*
8. **Synthesis (days 12 to 18).** Run the `interview-and-feedback-synthesis` skill; check AI-made transcripts against audio for every quote used. *Level 2: draft tags, themes and highlight reels in the research repository. Sharing clips or names needs each participant's consent and a fresh yes.*
9. **Words and fixes (days 16 to 19).** Use the `customer-words-and-fix-list` prompt. *Level 1.*
10. **Readout and hand-off (final days).** Answer first, theme cards, fix list, customer words, method box. *Level 3 within limits: share the readout with the named people. Then delete or anonymise contact details and recordings on the agreed date and log it.*

## Outputs

- Decision sentence and ethics check with adviser questions
- Screener, invitations, consent text and moderator script
- Recruitment log (invited, screened, booked, attended, incentives owed)
- Discussion guide
- Synthesis: theme cards, decision map, customer words, ranked fix list
- Method box and action log

## Needs the client's approval

The decision and incentive budget; privacy and consent wording (with a qualified adviser where needed); every recruiting message and list; the guide; any new audience; any sharing of names, clips or recordings; paying participants; deletion dates.
