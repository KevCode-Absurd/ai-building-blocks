# Product trial or group study

**Trigger:** A choice between versions (recipe, pack, name, concept, message) or a need to hear customers react together. Typical length is two to five weeks: one to two weeks to recruit, the sessions or trial period, then one week to analyse. The client or their moderator runs the groups and serves the samples.

**Access:** Ask which level the client grants for each tool (survey tool for screeners and score sheets, scheduling, email or CRM, shared drive) and write it down as "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1 (read). Log every action: what, where, when, result.

## Steps

1. **Frame (day 1).** Write the decision and the decision rule ("switch to B unless..."), who matters, and the budget. *Approval: the client confirms the rule before any data is collected.*
2. **Design (days 1 to 4).** Run the `group-sessions-and-product-trials` skill: pick the format, write the guide or diary tasks, the stimulus brief, or the trial plan (coded samples, balanced order, scoring sheet). *Level 2: build score sheets and diary forms unpublished. Approval: the client approves the design; for food, drink or cosmetics, a qualified person confirms safety and allergen handling.*
3. **Recruit and consent (days 3 to 12).** Use the `interview-recruiting-and-consent` skill with allergy and dietary questions first for any tasting. *Level 3 within agreed limits: publish the screener and send invitations after the client confirms the action list. Paying participants stays with the client. Children or vulnerable participants need a fresh yes and the permissions the codes and law require; check this with local rules.*
4. **Run (client).** The client or their moderator runs groups or serves samples. For diaries, I send scheduled prompts and reminders. *Level 3 within limits: send the agreed prompts and reminders to enrolled participants. Level 1: read entries as they arrive and suggest follow-ups.*
5. **Analyse (week after fieldwork).** Groups and diaries: code and theme as in the `interview-and-feedback-synthesis` skill. Trials: scores by version, just-about-right results, differences against chance with every sum shown, and comment themes. *Level 1.*
6. **Call (final days).** Launch, change or test again, against the rule written in step 1, with the main risk and the next test. *Approval: the client decides.*
7. **Hand-off.** Readout, data files, and deletion or anonymisation of personal data on the agreed date. *Level 3 within limits: share with named readers.*

## Outputs

- Decision rule
- Design pack: screener, guide or diary plan, stimulus brief or trial plan, scoring sheet
- Recruitment log
- Readout with themes or results tables and the call
- Method box and action log

## Needs the client's approval

The decision rule; the design; safety and allergen handling (qualified person); every recruiting message and list; anything involving children or vulnerable people; paying participants; the final call; sharing outside the team.
