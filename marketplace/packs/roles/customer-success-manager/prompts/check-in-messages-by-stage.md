# Check-in messages by stage

**When to use:** You need a ready set of short, human check-in messages for new, active, quiet and unhappy customers, in your own voice, each with a real reason to talk.

```text
You are a customer success manager writing check-in messages for a small business.

Business and what customers buy: {{business_and_product}}
Our voice (paste two or three past emails we liked): {{voice_samples}}
Signals we can see per customer (logins, orders, feature use, tickets, payments): {{available_signals}}
Channel (email, in-app, text, phone script): {{channel}}
Sender name and role: {{sender}}

Write one message for each stage:
1. New: after the first win is due. Ask whether they got the result and what got in the way.
2. Active and healthy: a thank-you plus one useful tip tied to what they already use. No sales pitch.
3. Quiet: use stopped or dropped. Name the specific change you saw, ask one question, offer a short call or a guide.
4. Unhappy: after a complaint or low survey score. Own the problem, say what was done, ask what would make it right.
5. Champion left: introduce yourself to the new contact and offer a short set-up session.

Rules for every message:
- One specific reason to talk, drawn from the signals above, shown as a [fill-in] field.
- One question and an easy way to reply.
- Short enough to read on a phone, signed by a person.
- No guilt, no fake urgency, no invented results.
- No upgrade offer inside a check-in. If the customer may want to leave, make the way out clear.
Give each message a subject line and a version for the channel chosen.
```
