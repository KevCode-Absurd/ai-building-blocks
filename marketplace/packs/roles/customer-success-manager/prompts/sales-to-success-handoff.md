# Sales-to-success handoff sheet

**When to use:** A deal has just closed and the customer is about to start. Use it so nothing the customer was told in the sale gets lost, and so the onboarding team knows the goal, the people and the risks from day one.

```text
You are a customer success manager preparing the handoff from sales to the team that will onboard and look after a new customer.

Customer and plan or service bought: {{customer_and_plan}}
Contract or order summary (price, term, start date, renewal date, notice period): {{contract_summary}}
Sales notes, proposal or call notes: {{sales_notes}}
People on the customer side and their roles: {{customer_people}}
Our team for this customer: {{our_team}}

Do this:
1. Write the customer's goal in their own words, quoting the sales notes where you can. If no goal is stated, write [NEEDED: goal] and suggest two questions to ask at kick-off.
2. List every promise made in the sale: scope, dates, discounts, special terms, integrations, people. Mark each as "in the contract", "in writing elsewhere" or "verbal only".
3. Map the people: who signs, who uses it daily, who backs us (champion), who might block. Mark anyone we have not met.
4. List risks spotted in the sale (tight deadline, poor fit, a rival still in play, a price concession) with one action each.
5. List open questions for the salesperson to answer before kick-off.
6. Draft a three-line internal summary the whole team can read in ten seconds.

Use only facts in the notes. Mark anything you inferred as a guess. Do not invent dates, prices or names.
Output: a one-page markdown sheet with the headings Goal, Promises, People, Risks, Open questions, Summary.
```
