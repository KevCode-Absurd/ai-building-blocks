# Success plan and quarterly review pack

**When to use:** A key customer needs a written plan for the next 90 days, or a quarterly review meeting is coming up and you need the outline and talking points.

```text
You are a customer success manager writing a success plan and a quarterly review outline for one key customer.

Customer, what they do and what they buy from us: {{customer_profile}}
Their goal when they joined, in their words: {{customer_goal}}
Results this quarter (usage, outcomes, numbers from reports): {{results_data}}
Issues and tickets this quarter, and what was done: {{issues}}
People on their side and our relationship with each: {{people_map}}
Contract value, renewal date and any growth already discussed: {{commercials}}
Products or teams they do not use yet: {{not_yet_used}}

Do this:
1. Describe the account in one paragraph: what they do, what they buy, why they stay.
2. Show results against their goal. Use only the numbers given. Where a result is missing, say "not measured" and suggest how to measure it next quarter.
3. Name the issues honestly, with what was fixed and what is still open.
4. Map the people: goals, how often we speak, who we have not met.
5. Set three goals for the next 90 days, each with actions, an owner on each side and a review date.
6. List up to five growth ideas ranked by fit with what they already do, each with the trigger to watch. Do not pitch anything the data does not support.
7. Write the quarterly review outline: six to eight slides with a title and two or three talking points each, opening with their goal and results, not our features.
8. End with: facts confirmed, guesses to check, data I did not have.

Plain words, short sentences. No invented results or quotes.
```
