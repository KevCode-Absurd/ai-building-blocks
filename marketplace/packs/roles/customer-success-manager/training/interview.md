# Training interview: customer success manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share customer names, contract values or personal details; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of businesses, products and customer counts do you know best, and are your customers businesses or consumers?**
   Why it matters: B2B and consumer subscriptions follow different rules and rhythms, and this sets the limits of the agent's advice.

2. **How do you define a customer's first win, and how fast should a good customer reach it?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's idea of time to value for their kind of product.

3. **Walk me through the onboarding of your last new customer, from signed to first result.**
   Why it matters: shows the real steps, owners and shortcuts, not the slide version.

4. **Where do new customers stall most often, and what fixed it?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent the stuck points and the fixes that actually worked.

5. **What do you need from sales before a kick-off, and what goes wrong when you don't get it?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the handoff fields and the promises that cause churn later.

6. **Which signals tell you a customer is about to leave, and which ones turned out to be noise?**
   Why it matters: builds a health score from evidence instead of vendor defaults.

7. **Tell me about a customer you saved. What did you see, what did you say and what did you offer?**
   Why it matters: captures a real save play and the words that worked.

8. **Tell me about one you lost that you think you could have kept. What did you miss?**
   Why it matters: reveals the checks the expert now adds and the early warnings they watch.

9. **How do you handle a failed payment differently from a cancel request?**
   Why it matters: separates billing fixes from save offers, a common source of wasted effort.

10. **What does your cancel process look like, and where do you draw the line between saving and trapping?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's ethics and the fair-exit rules it must follow.

11. **Which save offers have you used (pause, downgrade, discount, call), and which ones only delayed the cancel?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which offers keep customers and which just postpone churn.

12. **How do you run a renewal, and how far ahead do you start?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's cadence and how they tie it to notice periods.

13. **How have you told customers about a price rise, and how did they react?**
    Why it matters: gives wording, timing and reasons that customers accepted.

14. **When do you offer an upgrade, and when do you hold back?**
    Why it matters: keeps expansion tied to real need rather than quotas.

15. **What goes into your quarterly review with a key customer, and what do you leave out?**
    Why it matters: shapes the review pack around the customer's goal, not features.

16. **Which tools do you use for health, billing and messages, and what access do you usually get from a client?**
    Why it matters: sets realistic access levels and connectors for the agent.

17. **Which rules on renewals, cancellation or email have tripped you up, and in which places?**
    Why it matters: flags the rules the agent must check by place, without giving legal advice.

18. **How do you write a check-in that a busy customer actually answers?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's voice, length and the specific reason to talk.

19. **Which numbers do you report to the owner each week or month, and which ones do they act on?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which metrics matter and how to present them.

20. **What would you never do to keep a customer?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's hard lines, which become the agent's own.
