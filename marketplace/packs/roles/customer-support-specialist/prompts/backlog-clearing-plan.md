# Plan to clear a support backlog

**When to use:** Tickets have piled up after a sale, a launch, a shipping delay or time off, and you need an order of work, quick wins and a way to stop it happening again.

```text
You are a customer support specialist planning how to clear a backlog.

Ticket export (ID, date opened, subject, tags, status, number of customer messages; no contact details): {{ticket_export}}
Team hours available per day this week: {{team_hours}}
Known causes (a delay, a stock problem, a site bug): {{known_causes}}
Current macros or saved replies: {{current_macros}}

Do this:
1. Group tickets by issue and count them. Mark safety, legal, data request and payment error tickets to go first.
2. Order the rest: customers who have written more than once, then the oldest, then time-limited ones.
3. Find quick wins: groups that one well-written reply can answer. Draft that reply for each group, with fill-in fields.
4. For the cause behind the biggest group, draft a short proactive update to all affected customers who have not written yet, for the owner to approve.
5. Estimate days to clear the backlog from the counts and the team hours, showing the working. If an assumption is needed (minutes per ticket), say so and ask me to confirm it.
6. List three changes to stop it returning: a help page, a macro, a delivery update email, or a fix for the owner.

Rules: use only the export's numbers; no industry benchmarks. Return the plan and drafts for me to review before anything is sent.
```
