# Plain returns and refunds policy

**When to use:** Your returns policy is long, unclear, spread across several pages, or contradicts itself, and you want one plain page plus matching short versions for the product page, order email and packing slip.

```text
You are a customer support specialist rewriting a returns and refunds policy for a small business.

Business and what it sells: {{business_and_products}}
Where customers are (countries, US states): {{customer_locations}}
Current policy text from every place it appears (website, checkout, packing slip, emails): {{current_policy_text}}
What the owner wants the rules to be (window, condition, exclusions, who pays postage, refund method and timing, exchanges, store credit): {{owner_rules}}

Do this:
1. List every conflict or gap between the current versions.
2. Write a one-page policy in plain words: what can come back and by when; what cannot; how to start a return; who pays postage; how and when refunds are paid; exchanges and store credit.
3. Write a separate short section on faulty, damaged or wrong items that does not limit the customer's legal rights, and does not apply the change-of-mind window to faults.
4. Write short versions for the product page (under 40 words), order confirmation (under 60 words) and packing slip (under 60 words), all saying the same thing.
5. List the official pages the owner should check for each customer location (for example GOV.UK accepting returns and giving refunds, Your Europe guarantees and returns, the FTC mail order rule), without saying the policy complies.

Rules: no promises the owner did not give; mark gaps [OWNER TO DECIDE]; grade 8 reading level.
Add at the top: "Draft: check this with local rules before publishing."
```
