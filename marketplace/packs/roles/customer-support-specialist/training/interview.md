# Training interview: customer support specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share customer names, order numbers, emails or other personal details; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of businesses and products have you supported, in which countries, and through which channels (email, chat, phone, social)?**
   Why it matters: sets the limits of the agent's advice and which consumer rules it should point to.

2. **Walk me through the first hour of a normal support day.**
   Why it matters: shows the real routine and order of work the agent should copy.

3. **How do you decide which tickets come first?**
   Why it matters: captures triage rules beyond "oldest first".

4. **Show me a reply you are proud of. Why does it work?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a model of tone, structure and length.

5. **How do you calm a customer who is angry, on their third email?**
   Why it matters: the expert's wording for hard moments becomes the agent's default.

6. **When has a reply promised something the business could not deliver? What happened next?**
   Why it matters: over-promising is the costliest support mistake; the agent needs the warning signs.

7. **How do you tell a faulty-item claim from a change-of-mind return, and what changes when it is faulty?**
   Why it matters: mixing the two breaks consumer rules; the agent needs the expert's tests.

8. **Which cases do you always send to the owner or a manager, and how do you write that handover?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's escalation list and the shape of a good escalation note.

9. **How do you write a template that does not sound like a template?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert keeps macros personal and accurate.

10. **How do you keep templates and help pages up to date when the policy changes?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent an upkeep routine, not just a one-off library.

11. **Tell me about a bad batch or product problem you spotted in tickets before anyone else did.**
    Why it matters: shows how support data feeds product and supplier fixes.

12. **How do you handle a chargeback or a customer threatening one?**
    Why it matters: real dispute habits, including when to refund instead of fight.

13. **What do you let a chatbot or AI agent answer, and where must a person step in?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's own limits and how it labels itself to customers.

14. **What access to the help desk, store and payments would you give a new assistant in week one, and what later?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent realistic access levels and refund caps to propose.

15. **How do you measure whether support is getting better?**
    Why it matters: decides which numbers the agent reports and which it ignores.

16. **What have you learned about apologising well?**
    Why it matters: an apology that owns the mistake once is a skill the agent should copy.

17. **When have you pushed back on an owner's policy because it was causing complaints? What did you say?**
    Why it matters: the agent should be able to suggest policy fixes with evidence and tact.

18. **What do new support people get wrong most often?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's list of mistakes to avoid.
