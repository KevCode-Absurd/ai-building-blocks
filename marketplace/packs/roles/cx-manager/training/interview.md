# Training interview: Customer experience (CX) manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, customers' or staff names, quotes that could identify anyone, or confidential scores; anonymise every story.

1. **Which part of customer experience do clients bring you in for most: measurement, maps and blueprints, mystery shopping, complaints, or behaviour change?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and which skill it reaches for first.

2. **How do you find out which decisions a client's scores should feed?**
   Why it matters: decisions drive what to measure; the expert's questions become the agent's intake.

3. **How do you choose between NPS, CSAT and an effort question for a given moment?**
   Why it matters: captures practical rules for matching measures to moments.

4. **What sampling and frequency rules do you set, and how have you dealt with survey fatigue?**
   Why it matters: protects response quality and customer goodwill.

5. **Walk me through how you read a quarter's results, from the raw export to the first chart.**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a tested reading routine.

6. **Tell me about a score change that turned out to be noise, or a real change everyone missed.**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent when to call a trend and when not to.

7. **How do you code open comments into themes, and how do you keep the coding consistent?**
   Why it matters: comment themes are where fixes come from.

8. **How do you close the loop with customers in practice: who calls, how fast, what they say?**
   Why it matters: captures a follow-up routine that customers value.

9. **How do you make sure recurring causes get fixed, not just logged?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert drives owners and measures.

10. **How do you build a customer path map when evidence is thin, and how do you show what is a guess?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent's maps honest.

11. **When do you draw a service blueprint, and what is the most useful fail point you ever found with one?**
    Why it matters: shows where blueprints earn their time.

12. **How do you turn a vague service standard into something a mystery shopper can score fairly?**
    Why it matters: the core skill of a fair mystery shopping programme.

13. **How do you keep mystery shopping fair to staff, and what do you refuse to do?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's ethical line and the pushback wording.

14. **Tell me about a behaviour change you tested. What did you change, how did you measure it, and what happened?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a real example of a test done well.

15. **Which persuasion tactics do you refuse, and how do you explain that to a client who wants them?**
    Why it matters: trains the agent's ethics check and pushback.

16. **How do you handle complaints with legal time limits, and where does your work stop and the compliance team's start?**
    Why it matters: sets the boundary for regulated complaint work.

17. **What makes a complaint reply fair and complete? Show me a sentence you always rewrite.**
    Why it matters: improves the agent's reply drafts.

18. **How do you check that a chatbot or AI agent treats customers well, and how do you measure it against human channels?**
    Why it matters: AI channels are new and need the same standards.

19. **What goes into your quarterly report, and what do leaders actually act on?**
    Why it matters: makes the agent's reports useful, not just complete.

20. **Tell me about the hardest customer experience job you have done, and what you would do differently.**
    Why it matters: a story of judgement under pressure teaches more than a checklist.
