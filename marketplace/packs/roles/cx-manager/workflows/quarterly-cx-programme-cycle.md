# Quarterly customer experience programme cycle

**Cadence:** Every quarter. Week 1 reads the last quarter and plans fixes; weeks 2 to 12 run surveys, follow-ups and fixes; the last week writes the report. For a business with an active survey programme or one being set up.

**Access:** Ask which level the client grants for each tool (survey tool, help desk, CRM, analytics, whiteboard, tracker) and write it down as "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1. Log every action: what, where, when, result.

## Steps

1. **Check the programme (week 1).** Review the touchpoint table from the `survey-programme-and-closing-the-loop` skill: moments, measures, frequency caps, questions. Add or drop surveys only against the decision list. *Level 1. Level 2: draft survey changes, unpublished. Approval: the client approves any new survey or question.*
2. **Read last quarter (week 1).** Results with bases, themes with counts, drivers and behaviour measures, using the same skill. *Level 1.*
3. **Plan fixes (week 1).** Top causes with owners, fixes, dates and measures; behaviour-change ideas go through the `behaviour-barrier-diagnosis` skill and become tests. *Level 2: draft entries in the tracker. Level 3 within limits: update owners and dates.*
4. **Run surveys (weeks 2 to 12).** Surveys go out under the sampling rules. *Level 3 within limits: publish and send to the agreed lists and channels. Approval: the client confirms the action list before the first send of the quarter; a new audience needs a fresh yes.*
5. **Close the loop weekly (weeks 2 to 12).** Follow up customers who asked for contact with the `close-the-loop-follow-up` prompt; route anything that reads like a formal complaint to the complaint workflow. *Level 2: drafts. Level 3 within limits: send approved follow-ups. Approval: formal complaint replies need a fresh yes each time.*
6. **Check AI channels (once a quarter).** Run the `ai-disclosure-check` prompt on any chatbot or AI agent and compare its satisfaction, repeat contacts and complaints with human channels. *Level 1.*
7. **Maps and mystery shopping (as planned).** Refresh the customer path map or blueprint for the moment with the most complaints (`customer-path-map-and-service-blueprint` skill); run a mystery shop round where the client has one (`mystery-shopping-programme` skill). *Level 2: draft boards and forms. Level 3 within limits: publish forms to named shoppers.*
8. **Report (last week).** Write the one-page report with the `quarterly-cx-report` prompt. *Level 3 within limits: share with the named readers. Approval: the client approves before anything is shared outside the team.*

## Outputs

- Updated touchpoint and survey table
- Quarter readout: bases, scores, themes, drivers
- Fix list with owners, dates and measures, and test plans for behaviour changes
- Loop log of follow-ups
- AI channel check
- Refreshed map or blueprint, mystery shop results (if run)
- One-page quarterly report and action log

## Needs the client's approval

New surveys or questions; the first send each quarter and any new audience; formal complaint replies (fresh yes each time); sharing reports outside the team; anything outside the agreed level 3 limits; any refund or credit above the limit set.
