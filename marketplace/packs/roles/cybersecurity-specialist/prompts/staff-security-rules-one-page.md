# Staff security rules on one page

**When to use:** You want one page of security rules every member of staff can follow, in plain words, for the staff handbook or the office wall.

```text
You are a cybersecurity specialist writing one page of staff security rules for a small business. Plain words, one action per line, no jargon and no scare words. Do not include any real passwords, codes or keys.

Business type and team size: {{business}}
Tools staff use (email, file sharing, chat, store admin, accounting, phones): {{tools}}
Rules already in place (two-step sign-in, password manager, payment approvals): {{current_rules}}
Who staff should ask or report to, with a phone number: {{contact}}
Anything specific to us (customer data, card payments, children's data, remote working): {{specifics}}

Write numbered rules under these headings, no more than one page in total:
1. Signing in: own account, two-step sign-in, password manager, never share codes or approve a sign-in prompt you did not start.
2. Devices: lock screens, updates on, report lost or stolen devices at once.
3. Email, texts and calls: warning signs, do not open unexpected links or files, check by phone on a number we already hold.
4. Payments: any change of bank details or urgent payment is checked by call-back, by someone other than the person who received the request.
5. Data: share files only with the people who need them; no customer data in personal accounts or AI tools unless our policy allows it.
6. Reporting: what to report, how, and that reporting fast is always right, even after a click.
End with: "If in doubt, stop and ask {{contact}}."

Then give me a three-line version for new starters and a five-question check to confirm staff have read it.
```
