# Training interview: Cybersecurity specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, domains, IP addresses, passwords, keys and anything under a confidentiality agreement or incident non-disclosure; describe cases in general terms.

1. **What kinds and sizes of business do you protect, and on which platforms?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **Walk me through your first meeting with a small business that has never thought about security.**
   Why it matters: reveals the real order of questions and the first fixes the expert trusts.

3. **What are the three fixes you would make in almost every small business, and why those?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's priorities for the 90-day plan.

4. **How do you explain risk to an owner who is not technical, without frightening them?**
   Why it matters: shapes the agent's tone and how it scores likelihood and impact.

5. **How do you keep a risk register alive after the first meeting?**
   Why it matters: shows the review habits that stop registers going stale.

6. **Tell me about a payment-change or fake-invoice scam you dealt with. What stopped it, or what would have?**
   Why it matters: grounds the call-back rule in a real case.

7. **How do you run awareness training that changes behaviour? What do you measure?**
   Why it matters: separates real programmes from yearly videos.

8. **What is your view on phishing simulations, and what conditions do you set before running one?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's line on authorisation, HR agreement and no-blame reporting.

9. **How do you handle a help desk or IT firm that resets passwords over the phone?**
   Why it matters: captures defences against help desk social engineering.

10. **Walk me through the first hour of the worst incident you have handled.**
    Why it matters: teaches containment order, evidence and who decides.

11. **When do you tell a client to call a specialist incident response firm instead of handling it themselves?**
    Why it matters: draws the agent's boundary for live incidents.

12. **How do you handle reporting deadlines (GDPR, state laws, sector rules) without giving legal advice?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent fast on facts and careful on rulings.

13. **How do you run a tabletop exercise for a team of five who have never done one?**
    Why it matters: makes the exercise practical for small firms.

14. **For online stores, what are the signs that tell you it is card testing rather than a good sales night?**
    Why it matters: records the pattern-reading the store agent relies on.

15. **How do you decide which store apps to keep, limit or cut?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's app review method.

16. **What is your rule if a client pastes a password or an API key into a message?**
    Why it matters: aligns the agent with the expert's secrets handling.

17. **When has a client asked you to scan or test a system they did not own, or without permission? What did you do?**
    Why it matters: teaches the written authorisation line in a real situation.

18. **Which free government resources do you actually use with small clients, and which do you skip?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's trusted sources.

19. **Tell me about a client who wanted the wrong thing, such as an expensive tool before the basics. How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: teaches pushback that keeps the client on side.

20. **What do you check before you hand over a plan or a rule sheet?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality bar.
