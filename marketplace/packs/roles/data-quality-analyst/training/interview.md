# Training interview: data quality and governance analyst

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, real records and credentials; describe datasets in general terms.

1. **Which kinds of data do you know best (CRM, orders, finance, bookings, members), and which tools do you check them in?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the tools it suggests first.

2. **When you first get a dataset, what do you look at in the first half hour?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's real profiling routine, not a textbook one.

3. **How do you decide which rules matter most for a client?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent to rank rules by what the data feeds and what has gone wrong.

4. **How do you set severity, and who decides that a failure stops a report?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's stop, fix this week and note levels.

5. **Tell me about a rule you wrote that caught something important. What was it, and how was it found?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real example of a high-value check.

6. **How do you tell the difference between valid data and accurate data in practice? What reference do you use?**
   Why it matters: keeps the agent from calling well-formatted data correct.

7. **Where do you put checks: sheets, SQL, dbt, a data quality tool, database constraints? How do you choose?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's tool choices for different clients.

8. **How do you find duplicates, and where do you stop loosening the match?**
   Why it matters: captures the line between useful fuzzy matching and false merges.

9. **Tell me about a merge that went wrong, or nearly did. What would you do differently?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent concrete traps around irreversible merges.

10. **How do you write survivorship rules, and who signs them off?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert decides which value wins.

11. **What goes into your data dictionary, and how do you handle a column nobody can explain?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's dictionary format and its habit of marking guesses.

12. **How do you settle an argument between two teams about what a term means?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to surface definitions without taking sides.

13. **Walk me through a system move you ran. Where did the record counts not match, and why?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's migration routine and the usual gaps.

14. **What is in your cut-over checklist, and when have you used the rollback?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's rollback-first planning.

15. **How do you make test data, and what edge cases do you always include?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's favourite rows that break things.

16. **How do you handle a client who wants to "just delete the bad rows" or "clean it later"?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert pushes back while keeping the client on side.

17. **When a person asks for their data to be corrected, how do you find every copy?**
    Why it matters: records the practical side of correction requests across systems.

18. **What do you put in an issue log, and how do you get to a root cause?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to fix at the source rather than repeat clean-ups.

19. **How do you work with access to a client's systems? What do you never do without asking again?**
    Why it matters: aligns the agent with the client's limits and the fresh-yes rule for merges, deletions and bulk updates.

20. **What do clients misunderstand most about data quality?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent plain explanations for common misbeliefs.

21. **How do you show progress to a client over months?**
    Why it matters: records which measures the expert reports and how.

22. **What do you check before handing over any rules, dictionary or plan?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's personal quality bar on top of the role's.
