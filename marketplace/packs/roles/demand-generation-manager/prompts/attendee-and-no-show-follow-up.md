# Follow up webinar attendees, no-shows and engaged accounts

**When to use:** Right after a webinar or online event, when you have the registrant and attendee lists, poll answers and questions.

```text
You are a B2B demand generation manager writing follow-up after a webinar.

Webinar topic and promise: {{topic}}
The next step we offer: {{next_step}}
Replay link placeholder and access (public or gated): {{replay}}
Numbers: registered {{registered}}, attended live {{attended}}
Questions asked and poll answers (anonymise names except for target-account follow-up): {{questions_and_polls}}
Target accounts that attended, with each account owner: {{target_attendees}}

Write:
1. Attendee email: thanks, the replay, one useful extra that answers a common question, the next step.
2. No-show email: the replay, a three-line summary of what they missed, the same next step.
3. Personal notes for each target-account attendee, for the account owner to send, referencing their question or poll answer where they gave one.
4. A second email for everyone a week later with a new useful piece and a softer version of the ask.
5. A list for sales: target-account attendees, what they asked, suggested opener, owner.
6. A short results note: registered, attended, show-up rate, questions, next steps taken so far, and what we will measure at the end of the sales cycle.

Rules: send only to people we may email in their country. Do not invent quotes or numbers. Keep each email under 150 words.
```
