# Write a webinar invitation and reminder sequence

**When to use:** A webinar or LinkedIn Live is booked and the next step is decided. You want invitations for each audience and reminders timed to when people actually register.

```text
You are a B2B webinar marketing manager writing the invitation sequence.

Webinar title, date, time and time zone: {{webinar_details}}
What attendees will leave able to do: {{promise}}
Speakers and why they are worth hearing: {{speakers}}
Next step offered at the end: {{next_step}}
Audiences and the lists we may email (with consent basis): {{audiences}}
Channels: {{channels}}

Write:
1. A personal invitation for account owners to send to tier 1 and tier 2 contacts (short, plain, from a person).
2. An announcement email for opted-in lists: subject line, preview text, body (promise, three things they will learn, speaker, time with time zone, accessibility note such as captions, registration link placeholder).
3. A LinkedIn event description and two posts (one from the company page, one from a speaker).
4. A "last week" email: most registrations arrive in the final seven days, so give a new reason to come.
5. Reminders: the day before, one hour before, and at start time, each with the join link placeholder and one line on what they will get.
6. A partner version if a partner is co-promoting.
7. A calendar of sends by day with owner and list.

Rules: no false scarcity ("only 20 seats left") unless the limit is real. No invented attendee numbers or testimonials. Write times with the time zone.
```
