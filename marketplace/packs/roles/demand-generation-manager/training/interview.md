# Training interview: B2B demand generation manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert to leave out client names, account names, contact details and deal values that could identify anyone; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of B2B companies, deal sizes and sales cycles do you know best?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **Walk me through your first two weeks with a new client. What do you ask for, and what do you look at first?**
   Why it matters: captures the real intake order and the shortcuts an expert takes.

3. **How do you build an ideal customer profile when the client has only a handful of won deals?**
   Why it matters: most small B2B firms have thin data, and this is where judgement shows.

4. **How do you decide how many accounts go in tier 1? Tell me about a time it was too many.**
   Why it matters: turns capacity planning into a rule the agent can apply.

5. **How do you get a sales leader to agree a target list when they already have their own?**
   Why it matters: alignment with sales is the hardest part of the job and rarely written down.

6. **Show me a buying-group map you made. How did you find out who could block the deal?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real format and the questions that uncover hidden roles.

7. **Which signals have actually predicted a purchase for your clients, and which turned out to be noise?**
   Why it matters: separates useful signals from popular ones.

8. **How do you run LinkedIn for a list of a few hundred accounts without falling below audience minimums?**
   Why it matters: captures practical audience-building tricks that stay within platform rules.

9. **What makes a B2B webinar produce meetings rather than just registrations?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's view on topic choice, next steps and follow-up.

10. **How do you write follow-up for attendees, no-shows and target accounts differently? Show me one of each.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent real examples of segmented follow-up.

11. **How did you build the last lead scoring rule you were proud of, and how did you test it?**
    Why it matters: turns scoring from guesswork into a repeatable method.

12. **What does your handoff agreement with sales say, and what happens when sales doesn't follow it?**
    Why it matters: captures how agreements survive real behaviour.

13. **When the board asks how much pipeline marketing created, what do you show and what do you refuse to show?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's honesty rules on attribution.

14. **Tell me about a time attribution data pointed the wrong way. How did you find out?**
    Why it matters: real cases teach which numbers to distrust.

15. **How do you check whether outreach is allowed in each country before a campaign? Which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent careful on consent without giving legal rulings.

16. **What do you do when a client wants to buy a contact list or scrape LinkedIn?**
    Why it matters: captures how the expert says no and offers a better route.

17. **Which tools do you use daily, and what access do you ask clients for at the start?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's default tool list and access requests.

18. **What do you check before any campaign or email goes live?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's pre-launch checklist.

19. **What are the most common mistakes you see in B2B demand programmes, and how do you spot each quickly?**
    Why it matters: turns experience into fast diagnostics.

20. **Tell me about your hardest account programme: what went wrong, and what did you change?**
    Why it matters: a full story shows judgement under pressure better than any rule.

21. **How do you write for a sales team: plans, reports, messages? Show me a monthly report you sent.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's format and tone.

22. **How do you work with AI tools today, and where do you not trust them?**
    Why it matters: sets honest limits for the agent's own work.
