# Webinar countdown

**Trigger:** a webinar, online workshop or LinkedIn Live is agreed, ideally six weeks before the date. Works with less time; the steps compress.

**Access:** set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Level 1 to read past reports and lists. Level 2 to set up the event unpublished and draft every email and post. Level 3 to publish registration, launch approved promotion within the cap and send approved emails to approved lists. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Week -6: decide the next step.** What attendees should do after. Confirm audience tiers, speakers, platform and date (check time zones).
2. **Week -6: follow-up first.** Draft the four follow-ups with the `webinar-programme` skill (attendees, no-shows, engaged target accounts, one week later).
3. **Week -5: outline and run sheet.** Session outline, then a minute-by-minute run sheet with fallbacks. Book the rehearsal.
4. **Week -5: set up (level 2).** Create the webinar unpublished, the registration page with only the questions you will use, privacy notice, consent where needed, and an optional accessibility question.
5. **Week -4: invitations.** Write the sequence with the `webinar-invitation-sequence` prompt. Check each list's outreach rules with the `b2b-outreach-consent-check` prompt.
6. **Week -4: approve and publish (level 3).** After your yes to the send calendar and promotion cap, publish registration and the LinkedIn event, and start account-owner invitations.
7. **Weeks -3 to -1: promote.** Announcement to opted-in lists, partner sends, posts from speakers, paid promotion within the cap. Retarget event engagers if the audience reaches 300 members.
8. **Final week: push.** "Last week" email, then reminders the day before, an hour before and at start. Most registrations arrive in this window.
9. **Day: run.** Rehearsal check an hour before; chat and Q&A owners in place; follow the run sheet; note questions and poll answers by company.
10. **Same day: follow up.** Send approved follow-ups to attendees and no-shows; pass engaged target-account attendees to account owners by name with the `attendee-and-no-show-follow-up` prompt.
11. **Week +1: second follow-up and report.** Send the second email; report registrants, attendees, show-up rate, replay views, next steps taken and meetings. Pipeline and revenue are reported at the end of the sales cycle.

## Outputs

- Follow-up emails, session outline and run sheet with fallbacks
- Registration page and questions
- Invitation and reminder sequence with send calendar
- Attendee lists by company and a sales handover list
- Results report

## Needs the client's approval

**Approved once, then run at level 3 within your limits.** The send calendar (which lists, which days), the promotion budget cap, publishing the registration page and LinkedIn event.

**Always needs a new yes.** Sending to a new list or audience, spend above the cap, paying a platform or partner, adding a speaker's name or quote to promotion, and gating or ungating the replay after launch.
