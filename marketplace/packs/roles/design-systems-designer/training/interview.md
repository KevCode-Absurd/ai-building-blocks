# Training interview: design systems designer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, internal file links, screenshots under a confidentiality agreement and anything that identifies a colleague.

1. **Which systems work do you do most: audits, tokens, component specs, documentation, releases or migrations? Which do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of what it takes on.

2. **Walk me through the last system you started or rescued, from the first message to the first release.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the time goes.

3. **What do you ask before you open a single file?**
   Why it matters: captures intake questions that stop the work going in the wrong direction.

4. **How do you run an audit or inventory? What do you count, and how do you show it to people who do not care about consistency?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's method for turning drift into agreement.

5. **How do you decide whether a near-duplicate value survives or merges?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the judgement behind "cut 14 greys to 3".

6. **How do you name tokens? Show me a naming pattern you use and one you stopped using.**
   Why it matters: naming is where systems last or break; the agent needs a pattern and its reasons.

7. **How do you build dark mode or a second brand, and what goes wrong most often?**
   Why it matters: captures theme methods and the traps the expert has hit.

8. **Which token format and tools do your developers read, and how do you hand tokens over?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent in real tool chains, not just the standard.

9. **What must a component spec include before you call it done? What do people usually forget?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's handoff bar and the states it must never skip.

10. **How do you decide when one-off work should become a system component?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's contribution bar and how they say no.

11. **How do you version and announce changes? Tell me about a release that broke something.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent real deprecation habits and a story of what happens without them.

12. **How do you measure whether teams use the system?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which numbers the expert trusts (insertions, detaches, raw values, build time) and which they ignore.

13. **Tell me about a migration you planned. How did you order it, and what was your rollback?**
    Why it matters: captures staging and risk habits for live products.

14. **How do you check accessibility at the system level, and where do you hand off to someone else?**
    Why it matters: sets what the agent checks itself and when it refers to an auditor or lawyer.

15. **What do you document, where, and how do you keep docs from going stale?**
    Why it matters: records documentation habits that survive real teams.

16. **When a senior stakeholder wants a one-off style that breaks the system, what do you do?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert holds the line without losing the relationship.

17. **Which tasks do you let AI do, and what do you always check yourself?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's review points for AI-drafted tokens, specs and code.

18. **What access do clients usually give you in Figma and the repo, and what do you never do even with full access?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's level 2 and 3 behaviour in the expert's real limits.

19. **What would make you refuse or pause a job?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's red lines.

20. **What do you wish every client knew before hiring a design systems designer?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a plain explanation it can use at intake.
