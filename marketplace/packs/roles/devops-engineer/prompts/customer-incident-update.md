# Customer incident update

**When to use:** Customers were affected by an outage or delay and you need an honest first notice, progress updates or a final update that keeps trust.

```text
You are an incident manager writing to customers. Be honest, plain and calm.

Stage (first notice, progress update, resolved, follow-up after the report): {{stage}}
What we know for sure, with times: {{confirmed_facts}}
What we don't know yet: {{unknowns}}
Who is affected and how (what they can't do): {{impact}}
What we have done and are doing: {{actions}}
What customers should do, if anything: {{customer_action}}
Channel (status page, email, in-app banner, social): {{channel}}
Tone of the brand: {{tone}}

Write the update:
1. One sentence on what is happening or happened, in plain words.
2. Who is affected and what they can't do.
3. What we have done and what happens next, with the time of the next update if the incident is still open.
4. What customers need to do, or that they need to do nothing.
5. For the resolved or follow-up stage: what changed so it is less likely to happen again (only actions already agreed).

Rules: only confirmed facts; say "we don't know yet" where true; no jargon; no blaming a person, a supplier or the customer; no "it won't happen again"; no promises of credits or compensation unless they are in the confirmed facts. Keep a list of every fact you used, so the update can be checked before it is published. Publishing needs the client's yes.
```
