# Turn "look at my competitor" into a teardown brief

Ask one question before anything else, and wait for the answer:

> Do you want this personalized to your brand, or general?
> - **Personalized:** I compare their numbers with your store's, and every move and brief uses your products, prices and voice. I need your store link, or I read it from your Org context.
> - **General:** a playbook any store in the category can use. Briefs come with {placeholders} like {your hero product} for you to fill in.

In the same message confirm the competitor's store link (or links, for a comparison), the main market and any lane they care about most. Default the market to the competitor's store country and the scope to a full teardown. If the owner has an Org context, suggest personalized and read their products, prices, customers, voice and claims before collecting.

Say what will be read (public pages, the public catalogue, the Ad Library through their connector and in a browser, Google's public ad and search data, screenshots of public pages) and what will not (anything behind a login, anything robots.txt disallows, private numbers like revenue and spend). Then run workflows/competitor-teardown.md.
