# Brief the owner's agent team from a teardown
Use the lane map at the end of evidence.md: each lane lists the agents who act on it and the evidence they need.

1. Pick the lanes where the competitor is strong or weak and the owner can act this month. Six is the floor; eight to twelve is normal for a full teardown.
2. One hand-off per agent in the report's "Hand-offs to your agents" section, headed `### Name · lane (agent-id)`, with four bullets: What they do (cited), Take, Beat them, Brief.
3. A Brief is a job the agent can start tomorrow: the deliverable, how many (ads, emails, pages), the price or offer, the deadline, the evidence ids to read and the owner's constraint (budget, voice, claims). Use the owner's org context when shared; otherwise write for a store in the same category and say so once.
4. Send each agent only its lane. Run `teardown-report.mjs handoffs` to write handoffs/<agent-id>.md files.
5. Never ask an agent to copy the competitor's words, images or claims. Ask for the owner's own version of the strategy.

Common routes: Meta winners and launch rate to lindsey-meta-media-buyer; angles and hooks to tendai-ad-creative (with devon-video-ugc, ayaka-ad-design, kofi-ad-copy); Google formats and brand-bidding affiliates to raymond-google-ads; price ladder, bundles and subscription to brendan-offers; product page anatomy to annabel-landing-pages or gretchen-store-testing; email, SMS and loyalty stack to morag-email-sms or imogen-retention; affiliate and creator tools to pilar-creators or ritika-influencer-outreach; comparison pages, autocomplete objections and llms.txt to stuart-store-seo and rustam-ai-answers; review counts to corinne-reviews; attribution stack to emre-tracking; positioning history to philippa-brand; launches and collabs to revathi-product-research.
