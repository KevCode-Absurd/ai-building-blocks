# Decode how a competitor prices and packages
Read ../../knowledge.md section 4 first.

1. From the catalogue evidence: the median and quartile prices, the bands with most products, the entry product and the premium anchor.
2. AOV levers: free-shipping threshold divided by median price; bundles, kits and multi-packs and their price per unit against singles; quantity breaks on product pages; cart and thank-you upsell tools.
3. Subscription: selling plans on hero products, the discount, whether any product requires a plan, and where "subscribe" appears (announcement bar, ads, product page).
4. Discount posture: share of products with a compare-at price and the median depth. Always-on anchoring and real promotions are different strategies.
5. Hidden and offer-only products: list them with price per unit and compare with the public versions. These are usually the real ad landing offers.
6. Offer history: compare the Wayback snapshots' announcement bars and offers.

Output a short table (offer element, what they do, evidence id, what the owner should test) and one sentence naming the true entry offer.
