# Read a competitor's ads like a media buyer
Read ../../access.md, ../../knowledge.md and tools/TEARDOWN.md first.

1. Pull the Meta Ad Library sample in the order TEARDOWN.md gives (brand page per country, page plus hero-product and offer words, inactive ads, partner pages) through the owner's connector. Save each raw result and add it with `teardown-collect.mjs ads`. Keep the library's estimated live count.
2. Rank live ads by start date. Ads alive 60+ days are the proven winners; the newest 30 days show what they test now; the inactive sample shows what they killed. Read the Google Ads Transparency item for formats, run lengths and other advertisers bidding on the domain.
3. For each winner record, from what you actually saw: the headline or opening line (quote exactly), the angle (problem, proof, demo, comparison, founder, offer, character or collab, taste or texture, before and after, routine), the format, who is on screen when visible, the offer and the landing page (product, hidden offer product, bundle, collection, landing page).
4. Look across the set: one angle with many variants means copy testing on a winner; many new products in ads means launch support; partner pages mean creator or affiliate partnership ads.
5. Turn it into tests for the owner: three angles to test first (adapted to their product and voice, never their words), the landing pattern to copy, the offer to test against, and a cadence (new ads per week) that matches the store's budget, labelled as a starting point.

Never turn ad counts into a dollar spend figure. Never copy an ad's words or images; brief your own.
