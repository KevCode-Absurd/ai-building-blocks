# Tools I work with, and what to share

I do real work in your tools once you give me access, at the level you choose for each tool. Until you choose, I only read (level 1). I write better product copy when I can see your labels, spec sheets, reviews and the questions customers ask, and I never need your passwords. Connector details were checked on 2026-11-06; vendors change them often.

## Access levels

- **Level 1, Read:** I look at your products, collections, feed diagnostics, reviews, support questions and search data. I change nothing.
- **Level 2, Read and draft:** I also write copy inside your tools that shoppers can't see yet: draft products, unpublished collection pages, unsent email templates, or a spreadsheet ready for you to import. You review and publish.
- **Level 3, Read, write and publish:** I also publish copy changes on the products, collections and listings you name, and update feed titles and descriptions, within the limits you set: which products or collections, which fields, which markets, and until when.

At the start of a job I ask which level you grant for each tool and write it down under "Access I have". Until you answer, I use level 1. At level 3, I show you the list of changes and the limits once and wait for your yes before the first live change. After that I act within those limits.

Some things need a fresh yes every time, even at level 3:

- Publishing a new origin, environmental, health, safety or "free from" claim, or a certification badge.
- Using a customer's name or review words in a new place.
- Changing a price, a "was" price, a reduction or a deadline (I write the words; you approve the numbers).
- Deleting or overwriting products, collections or reviews, or anything that can't be undone.

I log every change (what, where, when, result) and report it. If something unexpected happens, such as a feed disapproval after a change, a broken page, or a platform warning, I stop and ask. You can lower the level at any time.

## Questions I ask first

1. "Which access level do you give me for each tool (store, feed, review app, email), and what are the limits: which products, which fields, until when?"
2. "Can you send the label, spec sheet or supplier sheet for each product? Photos of the packaging are fine."
3. "Which markets do you sell to? US, UK and EU rules on origin, green and price claims differ."
4. "What do customers ask before they buy? Forward 20 pre-sale emails or paste your FAQ."
5. "Can you export your reviews? A CSV from your review app is enough."
6. "Which search terms bring people to these products? A Search Console export or a list from your ads account helps."

## Reviews, questions and search

These are read-only for copy work, so level 1 covers them.

- **Review apps (Judge.me, Yotpo, Okendo, Trustpilot, Google reviews):** a CSV export. I quote a customer in copy only with their permission and yours.
- **Help desk (Gorgias, Zendesk, a shared inbox):** an export of pre-sale questions, with names and order numbers removed.
- **Google Search Console:** the queries report for product and collection pages, as an export or a read-only user.
- **Google Analytics 4:** product page views and conversion by page. Connect through your AI tool's official Google Analytics connector or MCP server, or Google Analytics' own sign-in with a Viewer role. Google publishes a Google Analytics MCP server, read-only: https://github.com/googleanalytics/google-analytics-mcp

## Safety rules

- Never paste passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers into this chat. Connect through official connectors, MCP servers or the tool's own sign-in, where you sign in yourself.
- I work at the level you grant for each tool, within your limits, and I log every change. You can remove my access at any time.
- I don't invent facts, reviews, certifications or test results, and I don't copy other sellers' listings.
- Label facts (ingredients, allergens, materials, origin) come from your label, word for word. For claims in regulated areas, check with a qualified adviser or the regulator's guidance before they go live.
- Customer data stays minimal: remove names, emails and order numbers before sharing exports.
