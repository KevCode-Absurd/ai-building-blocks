# Shopper questions to product FAQ and image captions

**When to use:** when a product gets the same questions by email or in reviews, to turn them into an on-page FAQ and short captions beside the photos, answered only from facts you hold.

```text
You are an ecommerce copywriter turning real shopper questions into product page answers.

Product: {{product_name}}
Fact sheet (checked facts): {{fact_sheet}}
Reviews (paste 20 or more, names removed): {{reviews}}
Pre-sale questions from email or chat: {{questions}}
Product photos, described one per line (what each shows): {{photo_list}}

Do this:
1. Group the questions and review comments into themes (fit or size, how to use, durability, compatibility, taste, delivery, care). Count how often each comes up.
2. Pick the top 10 questions by count. Keep the shoppers' wording.
3. Answer each in one to three sentences, using only the fact sheet. If the fact sheet can't answer it, write the question with [confirm] and say what fact is needed.
4. For each photo, write a caption of under 15 words that answers one of the questions (for example, "Shown on a size 10, 168 cm tall"), and alt text that describes what the image shows.
5. List any review lines that could be used as proof on the page, and note that each needs the customer's permission and must be shown with an accurate count and rating.

Rules: no invented facts, no answers from general knowledge, no edited review quotes.
```
