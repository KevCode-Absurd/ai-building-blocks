# New product copy, from label to live page and feed

**Trigger:** new products arrive (a launch, a seasonal range, a supplier drop), or a client sends a batch of products with no copy.

**Cadence:** per batch. A batch of 10 to 20 products usually takes one review round.

## Steps

1. **Agree access and scope (level 1 to start).** Ask which level the client grants for the store, the feed and the review app, and write it under "Access I have". Agree the products, surfaces (store, Google feed, marketplace) and markets.
2. **Collect the facts (prompt: product-fact-sheet).** Labels, spec sheets, supplier text and client notes become one fact sheet per product. Every gap is marked [confirm].
3. **Collect shopper language (prompt: shopper-questions-to-faq; tools: review app export, help desk export, Search Console queries).** For new products with no reviews, use reviews of the closest existing product.
4. **Send the open questions.** One message to the client listing every [confirm] item and every claim that needs evidence. Copy for products with open label facts waits.
5. **Write the copy (skill: product-page-copy; prompt: titles-by-surface; prompt: food-product-page for food).** Titles per surface with counts, bullets, description, specs, FAQ, alt text.
6. **Check the claims (skill: claims-and-label-check).** Every origin, green, health, free-from, review and price claim gets a status and evidence. Produce the claims log.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the copy and the claims log. Any new origin, green, health, safety or free-from claim needs the client's explicit yes, even at level 3.
7. **Load as drafts (level 2; tool: Shopify or the store's import, Merchant Center supplemental sheet).** Products stay hidden, or the copy sits in an import sheet the client loads.
8. **Publish (level 3, within the limits; or the client publishes).** Before the first live change, show the list of products and fields to be changed and get a yes. Publish only those. For Google feeds, send AI-drafted titles and descriptions in the attributes Google asks for.
9. **Check after publishing.** Look at each live page on a phone, check the feed for new disapprovals after the next update, and fix any mismatch between page, feed and filters. If disapprovals or errors appear, stop and report.
10. **Log and report.** List every change: product, field, before, after, date, result.

## Outputs

- Fact sheets with sources and open questions.
- Product copy sheet and import file.
- Claims log.
- Change log and a short report of what went live.

## Needs the client's approval

- The copy and claims log before anything goes live (point 1).
- Each new origin, green, health, safety or free-from claim, every time.
- The list of live changes before the first publish at level 3.
- Any price, "was" price, reduction or deadline wording.
- Using any customer's review words in new copy (the customer's permission too).
