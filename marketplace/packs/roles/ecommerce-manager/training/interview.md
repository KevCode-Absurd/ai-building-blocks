# Interview: ecommerce manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you work

1. **Walk me through your Monday: which reports you open, in what order, and what you decide.**
   Why it matters: shows the real trading routine behind the job.
2. **Which numbers do you use to decide what goes on the home page, and how do you work them out?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's merchandising formulas and rules.
3. **How do you decide when to start marking something down, and how deep each step goes?**
   Why it matters: markdown timing is where most margin is won or lost.
4. **How do you build a category tree for a store you don't know yet?**
   Why it matters: reveals the method for turning shopper behaviour into structure.
5. **When is something a variant, and when is it a separate product? Give me an edge case.**
   Why it matters: variant rules affect filters, feeds, reviews and search.
6. **How do you choose which attributes become filters, and in what order?**
   Why it matters: filter design decides whether shoppers can narrow down.

## Feeds and data

7. **Tell me about the worst feed you've fixed. What was wrong, and where did you fix it?**
   Why it matters: real feed stories hold the cause-and-fix patterns.
8. **What do you do when a product has no GTIN, or the supplier's codes are wrong?**
   Why it matters: tests their line on never inventing identifiers.
9. **When do you fix data in the store, and when in the feed tool?**
   Why it matters: the answer shows whether fixes last or keep breaking.

## Order value and pricing

10. **How do you find bundles that customers actually want?**
    Why it matters: separates basket analysis from guesswork.
11. **How did you set your last free delivery threshold, and what happened to margin?**
    Why it matters: thresholds are often set by feel; the expert's method is reusable.
12. **A client wants a "was" price from last year and a 48-hour sale that will run all month. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests their honesty line and knowledge of EU, UK and US price rules.
13. **How do you show delivery costs to UK shoppers?**
    Why it matters: checks they know mandatory delivery belongs in the total price.

## Tools, people and results

14. **Which tools do you work in every week, and what access do you need in each?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent what to ask for and at which level.
15. **What do you hand to buyers, designers, copywriters and ad managers, and in what format?**
    Why it matters: the role sits between teams; handovers decide whether its work is used.
16. **How do you judge whether a restructure or a bundle worked?**
    Why it matters: shows whether results are measured with margin, not just revenue.
17. **For digital products, how do you plan a range from one product to a full ladder?**
    Why it matters: captures the method for creators who sell downloads.
18. **Tell me about a decision that went wrong. What did you learn?**
    Why it matters: mistakes carry the lessons a checklist misses.
19. **What do clients most often want that you advise against?**
    Why it matters: prepares the agent for common pushback.
20. **What do you check before any change goes live in a store?**
    Why it matters: defines the expert's personal quality bar.
