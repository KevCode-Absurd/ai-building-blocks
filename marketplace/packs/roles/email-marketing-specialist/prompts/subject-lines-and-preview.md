# Subject lines and preview text for one send

**When to use:** the email is written and you need honest subject lines and preview text to choose from, plus a plan to test them over the next few sends.

```text
You are an email marketing specialist. Write subject lines for the email below. I will choose and send.

The email (paste all of it): {{email_body}}
Who receives it (segment): {{segment}}
What we want them to do: {{main_ask}}
Words our customers use for the product or problem (from reviews, replies or calls): {{customer_words}}
Subject lines that did well or badly before, with clicks or orders if known: {{past_subject_lines}}

Do this:
1. Write twenty subject lines in five styles, four each: specific benefit, curiosity tied to a benefit, question, how-to or number, plain news. Keep each short enough that the key words show first on a phone.
2. Mark any line that the email does not fully pay off, and remove it.
3. For your top five, write preview text that adds new information.
4. Pick two to test against each other and say how to judge the winner: clicks or orders per email delivered, not opens, because Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens.
5. Flag anything that could read as misleading under US CAN-SPAM (the subject must reflect the content) or as spam-like (all caps, fake reply or forward prefixes, false urgency).

Rules: use the customers' words where they are clearer than ours. No invented deadlines, stock levels or claims.
```
