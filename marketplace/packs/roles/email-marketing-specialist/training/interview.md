# Interview: email marketing specialist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work (for example, HTML questions for a newsletter writer).

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new client, from the first call to the first email you hand over.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work, not the textbook one.
2. **What do you ask to see before you plan anything: which exports, which numbers, which past emails?**
   Why it matters: the intake list becomes the agent's first questions.
3. **How do you decide which automated flow to build first?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's sense of where revenue and risk sit.
4. **How do you segment a small list when the data is thin?**
   Why it matters: most small businesses have only a few usable fields.
5. **What makes a newsletter worth opening for months, not just once?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the expert's editorial standard.
6. **How do you write a subject line? Show me your process and a few you are proud of, with the results.**
   Why it matters: subject lines are where generic AI copy is easiest to spot.

## Consent and deliverability

7. **How do you check that a client's list was collected properly, and what do you do with contacts of unknown origin?**
   Why it matters: sets the expert's line on permission, which protects clients and their sender reputation.
8. **Tell me about a time mail went to spam. How did you find the cause and how long did recovery take?**
   Why it matters: real recovery stories teach diagnosis better than rules.
9. **How do you read SPF, DKIM and DMARC records and headers? What mistakes do you see most?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's checklist for pasted records.
10. **How do you warm up a new domain or platform in practice? What makes you slow down?**
    Why it matters: captures judgement about pace that no rule spells out.
11. **What is your sunset rule for quiet contacts, and how do you explain it to a client who hates losing subscribers?**
    Why it matters: list hygiene is where experts and clients most often disagree.
12. **How do you handle consent differences between US, UK and EU subscribers on one list?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert applies regional rules without giving legal rulings.

## Measuring and testing

13. **Since Apple's privacy changes, what do you measure instead of opens, and how do you report it to clients?**
    Why it matters: reporting habits shape what clients ask for next.
14. **How do you decide whether a test is worth running on a small list?**
    Why it matters: separates real testing practice from noise.
15. **Tell me about a result that surprised you. What did you learn?**
    Why it matters: captures lessons that change future work.

## Build and tools

16. **Which email platforms and tools do you use, and what do you ask clients to share from each?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.
17. **How do you make sure an email works in Outlook, Gmail, dark mode and with a screen reader?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's HTML test list.

## Clients and judgement

18. **A client wants to buy a list, or email everyone who ever enquired. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests how the expert holds the line kindly and offers an alternative.
19. **A client wants a "last chance" email every week. How do you handle it?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert deals with fake urgency and fatigue.
20. **What do you check before you hand over any email? Give me your actual list.**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality checks.
21. **Tell me about your hardest email job. What made it hard and what would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: hard cases reveal judgement and values for soul.md.
