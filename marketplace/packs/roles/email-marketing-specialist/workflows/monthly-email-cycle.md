# Monthly email cycle

**Cadence:** once a month, in the last week of the month, for the month ahead. A quarterly version re-runs the full plan.

**Trigger:** the client has an agreed email plan (or wants one) and sends at least one newsletter or campaign a month.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 3 and 6 need level 1 (read). Steps 4 and 5 can use level 2, and step 7 needs level 3.

1. **Read last month (prompt: results-read-clicks-first).** Read the results in the platform, or ask the client for an export of last month's sends and flow results: clicks, orders or bookings, unsubscribes, complaints, bounces. Rank by the goal action, check guardrails, and note what to repeat and stop.
2. **Check list health (skill: deliverability-and-consent-check, steps 3 to 5).** Look at complaint and bounce trends, new sign-up sources, and how many contacts have gone quiet. Ask for a Gmail Postmaster Tools screenshot if the client has access. If anything looks wrong, switch to the inbox-placement-recovery workflow.
3. **Update the calendar (skill: email-program-plan, steps 4 to 6).** Add next month's real dates and offers from the client, the segments for each send, exclusions, and one main action per email.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the calendar, segments and offers before any writing.
4. **Write the emails (prompt: newsletter-issue for newsletters; prompt: subject-lines-and-preview for every send; skill: lifecycle-flow-builder for any flow change).** Each email comes with subject options, preview text, body, button text, sign-off, segment, exclusions and send time. Every fact to confirm is marked. At level 2 or 3, I build each email as an unsent draft in the platform and send tests to the client's internal addresses.
5. **Build or update the template (skill: html-email-build-and-qa)** if the layout changes, saved as a draft template at level 2. Hand over the test checklist.
6. **Run the pre-send check (skill: deliverability-and-consent-check, Checks section; knowledge.md Quality bar).** Consent basis per segment, unsubscribe link and footer, honest subject lines, real offers, one main action, images-off reading.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves final copy, segments and send times.
7. **Schedule and send** (level 3). I schedule exactly the approved emails, to the approved segments, at the approved times, and log each send. At level 1 or 2, the client schedules and sends in their own email platform.
8. **Log the month.** One line per send in a results log: date, segment, goal, subject used, clicks, goal actions, unsubscribes, complaints, lesson. The log feeds step 1 next month.

## Outputs

- Last month's results read with five recommendations.
- Updated calendar for the month ahead.
- Copy for every send, with facts to confirm, ready to paste or built as drafts in the platform.
- Template changes and test checklist, if any.
- Pre-send checklist, completed.
- Updated results log.

## Approval

- The calendar, segments and offers (point 1).
- Final copy, segments and send times (point 2). At level 3, this is the action list: I send exactly what was approved, to whom and when.
- Always a new yes: any change to flows, sign-up forms or live templates that is not on the list, any DNS record, a send to a segment or at a time that was not approved, and importing or deleting contacts.
- At level 1 or 2, the client or their team applies changes and presses send.
