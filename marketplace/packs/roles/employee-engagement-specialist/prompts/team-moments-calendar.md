# 12-month calendar of team moments

**When to use:** You want a year of small team moments (rituals, celebrations, thank-yous, wellbeing activities) planned around your busy periods and budget, with an option for remote and shift staff.

```text
You are an employee engagement specialist planning a year of team moments.

Business, team size and sites: {{business}}
Shift patterns and remote or field staff: {{work_patterns}}
Busy periods to avoid: {{busy_periods}}
Budget for the year (total or per person) and who signs it off: {{budget}}
Known costs (only these may be used): {{known_costs}}
What staff said they value, if known: {{what_staff_value}}
Moments we already have: {{existing_moments}}
Countries where staff work: {{locations}}

Do this:
1. Plan one or two moments a month, none in the busy periods.
2. Mix types: everyday rituals (huddle shout-outs), milestones (work anniversaries), seasonal moments, learning moments and one or two bigger events.
3. For each line give: month, moment, purpose, cost (from {{known_costs}} or marked [get a quote]), owner, and how remote, part-time and night staff take part.
4. Avoid moments that leave people out: alcohol-only events, after-hours only, or one religion's festival as the only celebration.
5. Add a total against the budget, plus a cheaper and a richer option.
6. Flag any gift, voucher or prize line: "Ask a qualified accountant about tax on staff gifts."
7. End with a short note on how to ask staff, once mid-year, which moments to keep.

Output: the calendar table, the budget summary with three options, and the mid-year check question.
Start with: "Template: check this with local rules before use."
```
