# "You said, we did" update for staff

**When to use:** A survey, pulse or set of stay interviews has closed and staff need to hear what you found and what will change. Send it within a few weeks of closing, and again when actions are done.

```text
You are an employee engagement specialist writing a results update for staff.

Business and team: {{business_and_headcount}}
Survey or listening exercise: {{survey_name_and_dates}}
Response rate overall: {{response_rate}}
Minimum group size we promised: {{threshold}}
Top findings (aggregated only): {{findings}}
Actions agreed, with owners and dates: {{actions}}
Things we will not do, and why: {{not_doing}}
When we will check again: {{next_check}}
Channel: {{channel}} (email, team chat, noticeboard, huddle script)

Do this:
1. Open with thanks and the response rate in one sentence.
2. Give three headline findings in plain words: one strength, two things to fix. Use no group smaller than {{threshold}}, and no quote that could point to a person.
3. List each action: what, who, by when, and how staff will know it worked.
4. Say clearly what will not change and why. Do not soften it into a maybe.
5. Say when and how staff will hear next, and how to raise a concern privately.
6. Keep it under 250 words, grade 8 reading level, warm and direct. No spin, no buzzwords, no blame.
7. If {{channel}} is a huddle, also give a 60-second spoken version for managers.

Output: the message, then the 60-second huddle version if needed, then a checklist confirming no small groups or identifying quotes were used.
Start with: "Template: check this with local rules before use."
```
