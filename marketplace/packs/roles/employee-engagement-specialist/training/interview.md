# Training interview: Employee engagement specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, survey answers or personal details of real staff; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of businesses, team sizes and countries do you know best?**
   Why it matters: size and location decide which survey rules, privacy laws and help lines apply.

2. **Walk me through the last staff survey you ran, from the first conversation to the update back to staff.**
   Why it matters: shows the expert's real sequence and where they add the most value.

3. **How do you decide whether a survey is confidential or anonymous, and how do you explain that to staff?**
   Why it matters: this is where trust is won or lost, and where many surveys mislead people.

4. **What minimum group size do you use, and when have you merged or hidden a group?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's privacy rules and how they handle small teams and differencing.

5. **Show me a question you rewrote because it was leading or asked two things at once.**
   Why it matters: pulls out real before-and-after wording the agent can learn from.

6. **How do you read open comments, and what do you do when one describes harassment or danger?**
   Why it matters: tests the route for serious concerns and the refusal to identify authors.

7. **Tell me about a survey that led to real change. What made the actions stick?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert turns findings into owned, measurable actions.

8. **Tell me about one that led to nothing. What went wrong?**
   Why it matters: failures show the traps the agent must warn clients about.

9. **How do you write "what we won't do" without losing staff trust?**
   Why it matters: honest limits are what make the rest of the update credible.

10. **How do you design recognition that reaches shift, remote and part-time staff?**
    Why it matters: most programmes reward the visible few; the expert's fixes are the specialty.

11. **What award criteria and selection rules have you used to avoid favourites?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent tested criteria, forms and panel rules.

12. **How do you handle tax questions on staff gifts and awards?**
    Why it matters: shows where the expert draws the line and hands over to an accountant.

13. **When an owner asks for a wellbeing programme, what do you look at first?**
    Why it matters: tests whether the expert fixes work conditions before adding perks.

14. **What do you tell managers to say, and not say, when someone is struggling?**
    Why it matters: real phrases make the manager guide usable.

15. **Where do you signpost people, and how do you keep those details current?**
    Why it matters: the help lines must be right for each country and checked often.

16. **How do you work out turnover and early leaving, and what traps have you seen in HR exports?**
    Why it matters: captures the formulas, data checks and small-team cautions.

17. **What questions do you use in stay interviews, and how do you record the answers?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a tested guide and a fair way to record.

18. **How do you write a staff message about hard news, such as job losses or a change of direction?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert balances honesty, care and legal review.

19. **Which survey, chat and HR tools have you set up, and what access do you ask for?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which tools to expect and what each access level should cover.

20. **What would you never do with staff survey data, whoever asks?**
    Why it matters: sets the expert's hard limits, which the agent must hold even under pressure.
