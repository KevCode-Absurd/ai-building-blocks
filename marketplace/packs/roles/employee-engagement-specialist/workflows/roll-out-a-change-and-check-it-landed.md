# Roll out a change and check it landed

**Trigger:** The business has decided a change that affects staff: a new tool, rota, policy, office move, structure or direction. Runs from the decision to a 30-day check, usually five to six weeks.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Until you choose, I work at level 1. Steps marked level 2 or 3 need that level in the named tool.

## Steps

1. **Pin down the change (day 1).** From what, to what, by when; who is affected; what is fixed and what is still open. If it touches legal rights (redundancy, pay, contracts, hours, monitoring), flag it for an employment adviser now. *Approval: owner confirms the facts and the legal check.*
2. **Draft the pack (days 2 to 4).** Use the `change-announcement-and-manager-pack` prompt: announcement, impact table, manager talking points, FAQ and rollout plan (level 2: drafts in your documents and chat tool). *Approval: owner approves every message before it is sent.*
3. **Brief managers first (week 1).** At level 3, I send the approved manager pack to the named managers and book a 30-minute briefing. Managers bring back questions staff are likely to ask; I add answers to the FAQ (new answers need the owner's yes).
4. **Tell staff (week 1 to 2).** At level 3, I post the approved announcement to the agreed channels and send it to the agreed list. Shift and field staff hear it in a huddle the same day, using the 60-second version.
5. **Show, practise, support (weeks 2 to 4).** A show-and-practise session, a one-page quick guide and a place to ask questions. I log questions as themes, not names, and draft answers for the owner.
6. **Count use (weekly).** Two or three measures you can count by hand (people using the new tool, rotas published on time, forms returned). I read them at level 1 and report the trend.
7. **30-day check (week 5).** A three-question pulse or huddle check: Do you understand what changed? Can you do what's needed? What is getting in the way? Report only groups at or above your threshold. Use the `staff-survey-design-and-readout` skill for privacy rules.
8. **Fix what isn't sticking (week 5 to 6).** Misunderstandings get a short correction; blockers get an owner and a date. Send a short follow-up: what we heard, what we're fixing. At level 3, I send it once approved.

## Outputs

- Announcement, impact table, manager talking points, FAQ
- Rollout plan with owners and dates
- Questions log by theme
- Weekly use measures
- 30-day check read-out and follow-up message
- Action log (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Before the first live message, I show the list (manager pack to named managers, announcement to the agreed channels and list, follow-up message) and its limits: channels, lists, dates. After your yes, I send and log each one.

**Always needs a new yes.** Any change to the approved wording, a message to a new group, anything touching legal rights, any spending, and anything that names or singles out a person. Legal wording goes to an employment adviser. Check this with local rules.
