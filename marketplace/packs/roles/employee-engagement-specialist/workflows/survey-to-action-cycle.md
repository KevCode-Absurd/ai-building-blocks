# Survey to action cycle

**Cadence:** A full survey once or twice a year, a short pulse each quarter in between, and onboarding check-ins all year. One full cycle takes about eight weeks from design to the first "you said, we did" update.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Steps marked level 2 or level 3 need that level in the named tool; at a lower level I hand you the work to enter or send yourself. Until you choose, I work at level 1. Identifying results stay inside your survey tool at every level.

## Steps

1. **Agree the decisions and privacy rules (week 1).** Use the `staff-survey-design-and-readout` skill, step 1: survey type, minimum group size, comment handling, named person for serious concerns. *Approval: owner signs off the privacy rules and threshold before anything is built.*
2. **Build the survey (week 1 to 2).** Questions, invitation and two reminders (level 2: saved unsent in the survey tool). Pretest with three to five staff from different groups. *Approval: owner approves the wording.*
3. **Launch and run (weeks 3 to 4).** At level 3, I send the approved survey to the approved list, send the reminders and close it on the agreed date. Managers announce it in huddles so frontline staff have time to answer.
4. **Read the results inside the tool (week 5).** Scores, group gaps above the threshold, counted comment themes. Serious concerns go as themes to the named person. Nothing identifying leaves the tool.
5. **Choose actions (week 5 to 6).** A short workshop with managers: three to five actions with owners, dates and measures, plus what you won't do and why. Add wellbeing work fixes with the `wellbeing-plan-and-signposting` skill and recognition fixes with the `recognition-programme` skill where the themes point there. *Approval: owner approves actions and any spending.*
6. **Report back (week 6 to 8).** Draft the update with the `you-said-we-did-update` prompt. At level 3, I post or send the approved update to the agreed channels.
7. **Track actions (monthly).** Check each action's status with its owner and update the tracker (level 2 draft notes; level 3 updates the live tracker you named).
8. **Pulse and close the loop (next quarter).** Run a short pulse on the topics you acted on, using the same wording. Report what moved and what didn't.
9. **Onboarding check-ins (all year).** Run the `onboarding-check-in-questions` prompt's schedule; review the cohort view each quarter when enough starters have answered.
10. **Rules watch (each cycle).** Check `knowledge.md` section 10: tool privacy defaults, data rules where staff work, help lines. With web search connected I check the official pages; without it I list what to check.

## Outputs

- Privacy rules box and approved survey
- Read-out with counted themes, at or above the threshold
- Action plan with owners, dates and measures
- "You said, we did" update and a 60-second huddle version
- Pulse comparison next quarter
- Action log of everything done in the survey tool, chat and documents (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Before the first live action of the cycle, I show the list (send the approved survey and reminders, close it on the date, share the approved dashboard with named managers, post the approved update) and its limits: which tool, which staff list, which channels and until when. After your yes, I do these and log each one.

**Always needs a new yes.** Privacy type or threshold changes, survey wording, any message to a new group, any spending, raw-data export, deleting results, and anything touching a named person. Legal or health matters go to an employment lawyer, a privacy adviser or a qualified professional. Check this with local rules.
