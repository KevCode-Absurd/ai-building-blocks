# Etsy shop manager: what the role knows

Checked 2026-11-06. Sources are numbered [n] and listed in sources.md with the date each was checked. Etsy changes its search, ads, fees and seller tools often, so every platform fact below says when it was true.

**A note on sources.** Etsy's policy pages at etsy.com/legal and its Seller Handbook blocked automated reading on 6 November 2026 (tested on the Creativity Standards page and a Seller Handbook article). The Seller Policy, Prohibited Items, Intellectual Property, Fees and Payments and Reviews policies live there too. Every Etsy fact here comes from Etsy's Help Center, which did load. Where a question turns on the policy wording itself, the agent reads the live policy page with the client or, with web search connected, checks it before advising. Without web search, it works from the pages and exports the client supplies and says what it could not check.

Related kits: the product photographer kit covers Etsy photo specs and AI edits in depth; the ecommerce copywriter kit covers origin, green and safety claims; the reputation manager kit covers review replies and policy flags; the bookkeeper kit covers Etsy payout reconciliation.

## 1. What this role is for

Makers, vintage dealers, print-on-demand designers and digital-download sellers hire this role when the shop gets few visits, visits do not turn into orders, or the owner has no time to keep 10 to 300 listings tidy. Typical clients: a potter with 40 mugs and no tags plan, a print shop whose titles are keyword piles, a candle maker selling into the EU without the safety details, a shop that lost its Star Seller badge.

What good work looks like:

- Every listing says plainly what the item is, fills all 13 tags with different buyer phrases, sets every attribute that applies, and shows a clear first photo.
- Shop sections group items the way buyers shop: by product type, occasion or recipient.
- Shipping profiles, processing times and the return policy are set, and the shop meets Etsy's customer service standards.
- Every item fits Etsy's Creativity Standards, with production partners and AI use disclosed.
- Items sold into the EU, Northern Ireland, Great Britain or the US carry the safety information those markets require.
- Etsy Ads, if used, run inside a budget the owner set and are judged on return, not clicks.

How it is judged: visits from Etsy search, conversion rate, orders and revenue in Etsy Stats [10]; the search visibility checks passed [8]; customer service standards met or Star Seller held [19][20]; ad return on spend [15]; and zero listing removals or policy strikes.

## 2. The canon

**Etsy's own rulebook.** The Creativity Standards (summarised in the Help Center [1]), the drop-shipping and reselling rules [3], the production partner rules [2], the review system [21] and the customer service standards [19] decide what a shop may sell and how it is ranked. For this role they matter more than any book.

**Etsy's explanations of its search.** Etsy does not publish a ranking formula, but its Help Center says a good deal: listings are matched to searches through titles, tags, attributes and categories [5][6][13]; a set of listing-quality checks decides "priority placement" [8]; customer service standards affect visibility [8][19]; and results are personalised, so a seller's own searches do not show true rank [11]. Treat these pages as the field's primary text.

**Product safety law.** The EU General Product Safety Regulation [23][27], Great Britain's General Product Safety Regulations 2005 [28], CE marking rules for toys and other covered products [29], and the US Consumer Product Safety Commission's rules for children's products and small batch makers [26][30]. These apply to handmade goods as much as factory goods.

**Further reading (credited only, no claims drawn from them):** Meg Mateo Ilasco, *Craft, Inc.*; Kari Chapin, *The Handmade Marketplace*. Both cover pricing, production and running a small craft business.

## 3. How the work is done now

### 3.1 What may be sold (as of November 2026)

Everything listed must be made, designed, handpicked or sourced by the seller [1]:

- **Made by the seller:** physical items made by hand or with tools such as 3D printers, CNC machines or cutting machines, shown in the seller's own photos [1].
- **Designed by the seller:** original designs sold as digital downloads or produced by a partner, including the seller's own prompted AI creations [1].
- **Handpicked by the seller:** vintage items at least 20 years old, items from nature, curated collections [1].
- **Sourced by the seller:** craft supplies, party supplies, and items personalised with the buyer's own text or image [1].

Drop shipping is not allowed except for craft and party supplies, and reselling is not allowed except for supplies, vintage and gift baskets the seller selects and packs. Adding a card to a bought-in item does not make it the seller's own [3].

**Production partners.** A partner, such as a printer, engraver or print-on-demand service, makes items from the seller's own designs. The seller discloses partners on the listing and in the About section. Suppliers, white-label, OEM and ODM manufacturers and wholesalers are not partners; using them to source items for resale breaks the reselling rules [2].

**AI.** AI tools are allowed when the seller adds their own creative input, and AI use must be disclosed in the listing description. Bot-generated content, AI-written functional instructions (patterns, recipes, instructions) and AI prompt bundles are not allowed [1]. For photos, AI edits to light, colour, crop and background are allowed, but every listing needs at least one unedited photo of the actual item on a real background, edits may not hide flaws, and patterns and other guides need a photo of a real result [12].

**"How it's made."** The listing form asks who made the item, what it is and when it was made, and where production partners are added [4]. Answer truthfully; these answers are the shop's claim under the Creativity Standards.

### 3.2 How Etsy search finds listings (as of November 2026)

- **Matching.** Titles and tags decide relevance for ads [13], and Etsy's Help Center tells sellers to fill both attributes and tags because each helps buyers find items [5][6]. The category sets which attributes exist [6].
- **Listing quality checks for priority placement** [8]: at least one image; a primary photo of 2000 pixels or more, showing a single finished product (no collage), clear and well lit; a category as specific as possible; a return policy, even "no returns"; and for US listings, domestic shipping under $6 is prioritised. A complete shop with a logo also helps.
- **Customer service standards.** Shops that fall below the minimums can lose search visibility [8][19].
- **Personalisation.** Results differ by shopper, so a seller searching for their own items learns little [11].
- **Timing.** Changes can take up to a day to show in search [8].
- **Buyer filters.** Filters such as ship-to country and price remove listings from view [13], and custom variations cannot be used as search filters [4].

### 3.3 The listing (as of November 2026)

- **Title:** up to 140 characters; Etsy suggests fewer than 15 words, saying what the item is first, without repetition, subjective words, price or shipping; holidays or recipients only if essential [4]. Search engines show roughly the first 50 to 60 characters [7].
- **Tags:** 13 per listing, each up to 20 characters, letters, numbers and spaces; phrases are fine; most useful where no attribute covers the term; include buyer-focused phrases such as recipient or occasion [5].
- **Attributes:** set every one that applies; if the exact colour is missing, choose the closest and put the exact shade in a tag [6].
- **Description:** first sentence says what the item is; then size, materials, variations, personalisation, care and shipping; buyer words without repeating phrases [7].
- **Photos and video:** up to 20 photos and 2 videos of 5 to 15 seconds [4]; alt text on photos [7]. See the product photographer kit for shot lists.
- **Web address:** set when the listing is first published and does not change with later title edits [7].
- **Shop sections and featured listings:** chosen in the listing settings [4].

### 3.4 Keyword research

- **Etsy Stats** shows the search terms that brought shoppers, plus visits, conversion rate and listing-level views, favourites and orders [10]. Etsy Stats has no export in the Help Center's description [10], so the client shares screenshots or the agent reads it with access.
- **Marketplace Insights** (Stats, web only) shows popular and related search terms, search and listing counts and trends for the last 30 days; 15 keyword searches a week on the standard tier, unlimited with Etsy Plus (as of Nov 2026) [9].
- **Year over year.** Compare long periods; a single month misleads [11].
- **Third-party tools** that use Etsy's API exist (see access.md). Never scrape Etsy: the API terms forbid screen-scraping [25].

### 3.5 Shipping and processing (as of November 2026)

- **Shipping profiles** hold rates, processing time, service, origin postcode and destinations. A listing uses one profile, and editing a profile changes every listing on it [17].
- **Fixed or calculated shipping.** Calculated shipping is only for US and Canada sellers using USPS or Canada Post [17].
- **Destinations.** "Everywhere Else" means the listing ships worldwide; remove it to limit sales [17].
- **US imports.** Etsy says orders shipping into the US must include duties and import fees upfront to stay eligible for Purchase Protection (from 9 July; the page does not state the year) [17].
- **Processing time** sets the ship-by date and the point when buyers can open a case or review. Shorter times can shorten the delivery estimate; if a shop ships faster than it says, buyers may see its actual times. A ship-by date can be extended once, by up to 21 days, after confirming with the buyer [18].

### 3.6 Service standards, Star Seller and reviews (as of November 2026)

- **Minimum standards:** reply to 80% of first messages within 48 hours; ship 80% of orders on time; keep low reviews and refunded cases under set limits [19]. Missing them can lower search visibility and Purchase Protection eligibility [19].
- **Star Seller:** reply to 95% of first messages within 24 hours; ship 95% on time with tracking; average rating 4.8 or higher; at least 5 orders and $300 in sales in the past 3 months; checked on the 1st of each month, from 90 days after the first sale [20].
- **Reviews:** buyers have 100 days from delivery; the rating is weighted to recent reviews [21]. Message an unhappy buyer before replying in public, because a public response stops the buyer editing the review; one response per review; no tracking numbers, links or private details [22]. The reputation manager kit covers reply craft.

### 3.7 Etsy Ads and Offsite Ads (as of November 2026)

- **Etsy Ads** are bought by the click in an auction. Placement depends on listing quality (likelihood of a click, history, titles and tags) and the bid [13]. The owner sets a daily budget, which spend never exceeds; Etsy sets a maximum daily budget it recalculates weekly; strategies are Greater visibility, Efficient spending and Lower click cost; all listings are advertised by default unless removed [14].
- **Measure on return.** Etsy credits an order to an ad if the shopper buys within 30 days of clicking, reports return on ad spend (ROAS), and suggests running an ad for 30 days before turning it off [15]. Compare ROAS with the listing's margin, not with a general target.
- **Offsite Ads** are run by Etsy on other sites. The fee applies only to attributed orders within 30 days of a click: 15% for shops that have never made $10,000 in any 365 days, 12% for shops that have; the fee is capped at $100 per order. Once a shop reaches $10,000 in any 365 days, Offsite Ads are compulsory for the life of the shop; below that, the owner can opt out [16]. Price with this fee in mind.

### 3.8 Product safety duties (as of November 2026)

This is general practice, not legal advice. Sellers should confirm their own duties with Trading Standards, a product safety adviser or a lawyer.

- **EU and Northern Ireland.** The GPSR applies from 13 December 2024 [27]. Etsy says it may apply to traders selling into the EEA and Northern Ireland wherever they are based, and asks them to add an economic operator and/or the manufacturer's details and to fill in the Product safety info field; sellers can instead stop selling to those countries [23]. A responsible economic operator must be established in the EU or Northern Ireland; its details and the manufacturer's must appear on the product, packaging, parcel or an accompanying document, with a type, batch or serial number and safety information consumers can understand [27]. Etsy cannot decide whether a seller is a "trader"; the seller should take advice [23].
- **CE marking** applies only to products covered by EU rules that require it, such as toys, electrical goods, batteries and personal protective equipment; the maker affixes it [29].
- **Great Britain.** The General Product Safety Regulations 2005 require safe products; producers give risk information, mark name, address and a batch reference where reasonable, keep a complaints register and tell Trading Standards about unsafe products [28].
- **United States.** Children's products need a Children's Product Certificate. Registered small batch makers get relief from third-party testing for some rules only; small parts, lead in paint and other listed rules always need an accepted lab, and tracking labels are always required [30]. From 8 July 2026, importers of products under CPSC rules must file certificate data electronically, and postal imports must use the CPSC Product Registry route [26].

### 3.9 Working in the client's shop

Etsy's **Shared access** lets the owner invite up to 5 team members with their own Etsy accounts and two-factor sign-in. Team members can manage orders, create, edit and publish listings, and reply to messages; they cannot cancel or refund orders, delete or deactivate listings, see finances, or open marketing, stats, settings or apps (as of Nov 2026) [24]. So with Shared access an agent can do listing work at level 2 (drafts) or level 3 (publish), but Etsy Ads, Stats and shop settings stay with the owner, who shares screenshots or makes those changes from the agent's plan. Etsy's Open API lets a seller's own app act on their own shop [25]; Etsy's developer MCP server gives AI coding tools API documentation, not shop access [25]. Never ask for the owner's password. access.md has the details.

## 4. Benchmarks and numbers

These are Etsy's own thresholds, not market averages. Recheck them each refresh.

- **Search visibility checks:** primary photo 2000 pixels or more; US domestic shipping under $6 prioritised; changes take up to a day [8].
- **Customer service minimums:** 80% of first messages answered within 48 hours; 80% of orders shipped on time [19].
- **Star Seller:** 95% within 24 hours; 95% on time with tracking; 4.8 average rating; 5 orders and $300 in 3 months [20].
- **Ads:** 30-day attribution for Etsy Ads [15] and Offsite Ads [16]; Offsite Ads fee 15% or 12%, capped at $100 per order, compulsory from $10,000 in any 365 days [16].
- **Listing limits:** 140-character titles, 13 tags of up to 20 characters, 20 photos, 2 videos [4][5].
- **Reviews:** 100-day review window [21].

*Caveat:* Etsy publishes no average conversion rate, click rate or ROAS for sellers that I could find. Use the shop's own last 90 days and the same period last year as the baseline [11].

## 5. Decision rules

- If a title is a pile of keywords, rewrite it to say what the item is in the first few words, because Etsy suggests fewer than 15 words and search engines show only the start [4][7].
- If an attribute exists for a term, set the attribute and use the tag slot for a different phrase, because tags are most useful where no attribute exists [5][6].
- If an item is bought in and unaltered, do not list it unless it is a craft or party supply, vintage, or part of a gift basket the seller assembles, because Etsy bans reselling [3].
- If a print-on-demand or other partner makes the item, add the production partner before publishing [2].
- If AI helped create the design or the description, disclose it in the listing description; if the item is an AI-written pattern, recipe or set of instructions, do not list it [1].
- If a listing's first photo is a collage, dark or under 2000 pixels, fix the photo before touching keywords [8][11].
- If a US listing's domestic shipping is $6 or more, test folding part of the cost into the price, because cheaper domestic shipping is prioritised [8]; check the margin first.
- If a shop sells into the EU or Northern Ireland and has no EU or Northern Ireland responsible operator, either set one up with advice or stop selling to those countries in Etsy's settings, because the GPSR requires one [23][27].
- If the shop sells toys or other children's items, check CE marking for the EU, the GB safety rules and US children's product certification before listing [26][28][29][30].
- If the owner wants to judge rank by searching Etsy, use Stats search terms instead, because results are personalised [10][11].
- If an Etsy Ads listing has run 30 days and its return does not cover its margin, remove it from ads [15].
- If the shop is near $10,000 in sales over 365 days, tell the owner that Offsite Ads will become compulsory and check prices against the fee [16].
- If a buyer complains, message them before replying to the review [22].
- If the client asks to copy a top seller's listing or photos, say no and work from the shop's own stats and photos [1].

## 6. Common mistakes, and how to spot them

- **Keyword-stuffed titles.** Spot it: the same word three times, or a title that does not read as a sentence [4].
- **Wasted tags.** Spot it: fewer than 13 tags, single words, or tags that repeat an attribute such as the colour already set [5][6].
- **Collage or dark first photo.** Spot it: the Listings manager flags it [8][11].
- **No return policy.** Spot it: the search visibility page lists it, even though "no returns" would pass [8].
- **Reselling by mistake.** Spot it: supplier photos, wholesale listings or "custom" items that arrive unchanged [2][3].
- **Undisclosed partners or AI.** Spot it: print-on-demand products or AI designs with no partner set and no note in the description [1][2].
- **Processing time set too short.** Spot it: on-time shipping below the 80% minimum [18][19].
- **"Everywhere Else" left on by accident.** Spot it: orders from countries the seller cannot ship to [17].
- **Selling to the EU with no safety details.** Spot it: empty manufacturer and product safety fields on EU-visible listings [23].
- **Ads left on everything.** Spot it: all listings in the campaign, and ROAS never checked [14][15].
- **Replying to a bad review before messaging.** Spot it: the buyer could no longer update the review [22].
- **Judging rank by searching.** Spot it: "we've dropped off page one" based on the owner's own phone [11].

## 7. Quality bar: what an expert checks before shipping

1. Does every item fit a Creativity Standards category, with partners and AI disclosed [1][2]?
2. Does each title say what the item is first, in fewer than 15 words, with no repetition or price talk [4]?
3. Are all 13 tags used, all different, within 20 characters, and not repeating attributes [5][6]?
4. Are category and attributes as specific as possible [6][8]?
5. Does the first description sentence say what the item is, and are size, materials, care and shipping covered [7]?
6. Does the first photo pass the search visibility checks, and is there an unedited real photo [8][12]?
7. Are shipping profile, processing time and return policy set, and is "Everywhere Else" deliberate [8][17][18]?
8. Are EU, GB and US safety details complete for the markets the listing ships to [23][27][28][30]?
9. For ads, is there a written daily budget and a ROAS rule tied to margin [14][15]?
10. Is the access level written down, and was every live change logged?

## 8. How this role writes

**Deliverables.**

- *Listing rewrite:* per listing: new title, 13 tags, attributes to set, the first two lines of the description, photo order notes, and a note on anything that needs the owner's facts.
- *Tag and keyword plan:* buyer phrases grouped by product type and buyer need (gift, colour, occasion), with which listings use which phrases.
- *Shop sections plan:* section names, the listings in each, and which four to feature.
- *Shop audit:* a ranked fix list across search visibility, listings, photos, shipping, policies, service standards, rules and safety, each with effort and access level.
- *Ads plan:* which listings to advertise, daily budget, strategy, 30-day review date and the ROAS needed to keep each listing.

**Tone.** Maker to maker: warm, plain and practical. Titles and tags are listed, not explained at length. No "unique", "best quality" or "handmade with love" as filler. Facts about materials, sizes, safety and origin come only from the owner.

## 9. Glossary

- **Creativity Standards:** Etsy's rules on what may be sold: made, designed, handpicked or sourced by the seller [1].
- **Production partner:** an outside business that makes items from the seller's designs, disclosed on listings [2].
- **Drop shipping:** selling items a supplier ships unchanged; banned except supplies [3].
- **Tag:** a phrase of up to 20 characters used to match searches; 13 per listing [5].
- **Attribute:** a set field such as colour or material that buyers can filter by [6].
- **Search visibility page:** Etsy's page of checks and insights for priority placement [8].
- **Marketplace Insights:** Etsy's search-term research tool inside Stats [9].
- **Conversion rate:** orders divided by visits, shown in Etsy Stats [10].
- **Shipping profile:** a saved set of shipping settings shared by many listings [17].
- **Processing time:** the days needed to make or prepare an order before shipping [18].
- **Ship-by date:** the date an order must be marked shipped [18].
- **Customer service standards:** Etsy's minimums for messages, shipping, reviews and cases [19].
- **Star Seller:** Etsy's badge for shops that beat set service levels [20].
- **Etsy Ads:** Etsy's pay-per-click ads inside Etsy [13][14].
- **ROAS:** return on ad spend, ad revenue divided by ad cost [15].
- **Offsite Ads:** ads Etsy runs on other sites, paid only on attributed sales [16].
- **GPSR:** the EU General Product Safety Regulation [23][27].
- **Responsible economic operator:** the EU or Northern Ireland business responsible for a product's safety tasks [27].
- **Children's Product Certificate:** the US certificate that a children's product meets CPSC rules [26][30].
- **Shared access:** Etsy's way to give team members their own access to a shop [24].
