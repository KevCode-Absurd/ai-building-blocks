# Creativity Standards and AI disclosure check

**When to use:** before listing a new product type, after a policy notice, or when you use a printer, print-on-demand service, supplier or AI tools, to check each item fits Etsy's rules and is disclosed properly.

```text
Check my Etsy products against Etsy's Creativity Standards and disclosure rules.

For each product type: what it is, who makes it and how, any production partner (printer, print-on-demand, engraver), any supplier of finished goods, and any AI use (design, images, description): {{product_types}}
Current listing text and "How it's made" answers for one example of each: {{example_listings}}
My About section: {{about_section}}

For each product type, tell me:
1. Which category it fits: made by me, designed by me, handpicked by me (vintage 20+ years, nature, curated), or sourced by me (craft or party supplies, items personalised with the buyer's own text or image). Or "may not fit", with why.
2. Whether a production partner must be added, and where (listing and About section).
3. Whether AI use must be disclosed in the description, and suggested plain wording.
4. Any red flag: reselling unchanged bought-in items, drop shipping outside supplies, AI-written patterns, recipes or instructions, AI prompt bundles, photos that are all AI with no unedited real photo, or other sellers' photos or text.
5. What to read on Etsy's live policy pages before deciding, if the case is unclear.

Do not give a legal ruling. If something may break Etsy's rules, say so plainly and suggest how to fix or remove it.
```
