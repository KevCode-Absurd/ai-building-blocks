# Product safety readiness (EU, Northern Ireland, GB, US)

**When to use:** before selling a product type into a new country, or when Etsy asks for manufacturer and product safety details, to list what to prepare and what to ask an adviser.

```text
Help me prepare product safety information for my Etsy shop {{shop_name}}.

Where I am based, and whether I sell as a registered business or regularly for profit: {{base_and_status}}
Countries I sell into now or plan to: {{markets}}
Product types (what they are, materials, who uses them, any for children, any electrical, candles, cosmetics, toys): {{product_types}}
Who makes each, and any responsible person, importer or partner in the EU or Northern Ireland: {{operators}}
Labels, warnings, test reports or certificates I already have: {{documents}}

Write:
1. For EU and Northern Ireland sales: the details Etsy asks for (economic operator or manufacturer, product safety info field), what a responsible economic operator is, the product information to have (type, batch or serial number, manufacturer and responsible person details, safety information in the buyer's language), and the option to stop selling to those countries until ready.
2. For Great Britain: the basic producer duties (safe products, risk information, name, address and batch reference where reasonable, complaints record, telling Trading Standards about unsafe products).
3. For the US, if relevant: whether any item looks like a children's product needing a Children's Product Certificate, the small batch route and its limits, and eFiling for imports.
4. Which product types may need CE marking or sector rules, as questions to check.
5. A to-do list ranked by risk, and the exact questions to take to Trading Standards, a product safety adviser or a lawyer.
This is general guidance, not legal advice. Do not tell me a product is compliant.
```
