# Shipping and processing review

**When to use:** when on-time shipping is slipping, buyers complain about delivery, shipping costs look high, or orders arrive from countries you cannot serve.

```text
Review the shipping set-up of my Etsy shop {{shop_name}}.

Shipping profiles (name, origin postcode, rates or calculated, services, destinations, "Everywhere Else" on or off, upgrades): {{shipping_profiles}}
Processing profiles and times by listing (made to order or ready to ship): {{processing}}
What it really takes to make and send an order, by product type: {{real_times}}
Real postage costs for typical parcels: {{postage_costs}}
Customer service stats (on-time shipping, tracking, message replies): {{service_stats}}
Where I am based and which countries I want to sell to: {{markets}}

Tell me:
1. Processing times that are shorter than what I really achieve, and new times to set.
2. Profiles where the price charged is far from the real postage cost (losing money or putting buyers off).
3. For US listings, whether domestic shipping is $6 or more, and whether folding part of it into the price would work for my margin.
4. Whether "Everywhere Else" should be on, and which countries to remove.
5. If I ship into the US from abroad, what Etsy says about duties and import fees for Purchase Protection.
6. Which listings should share a profile, so one change updates them all.
7. A short change list in order, marking which changes I make in settings and which can be made on listings.
```
