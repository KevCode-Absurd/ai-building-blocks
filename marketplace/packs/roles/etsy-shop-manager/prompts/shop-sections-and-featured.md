# Shop sections and featured listings

**When to use:** to group a shop's listings into sections the way buyers shop, and to choose the four listings to feature.

```text
Plan shop sections and featured listings for {{shop_name}}.

All active listings (title, price, category, views, favourites, orders for the last 90 days): {{listings}}
Best sellers and new items: {{best_and_new}}
Seasons or occasions coming up in the next three months: {{upcoming_occasions}}
How buyers describe what they want in messages and reviews: {{buyer_words}}

Write:
1. A section list (names of 1 to 3 plain words a buyer would use, such as product type, occasion or recipient), with the listings in each. Every listing in exactly one section.
2. The four listings to feature at the top of the shop, with one line each on why (best seller, seasonal, strong photo, good margin).
3. Listings that do not fit anywhere, which may need retiring, combining or rewriting.
4. A note on when to change the featured listings for the coming season.
Keep section names short and avoid internal names such as "Collection B".
```
