# Tag audit

**When to use:** to find wasted tags across the shop: empty slots, single words, repeats of attributes, copied competitor tags and phrases that never bring orders.

```text
Audit the tags in my Etsy shop {{shop_name}}.

Listings with current tags and attributes (CSV or pasted): {{listings_with_tags}}
Etsy Stats search terms for the last 90 days, with visits and orders where shown: {{search_terms}}
Same period last year, if I have it: {{last_year_terms}}
What I sell, and what I do not sell: {{product_scope}}

For each listing:
1. Count tags used out of 13.
2. Mark each tag: keep, replace or drop, with a reason: single generic word, repeats an attribute or the category, over 20 characters, describes something I do not sell, another brand's name, or never brought visits.
3. Suggest replacements only from my search terms or plain buyer phrases for the item, each 20 characters or fewer.

Then for the whole shop:
- Tags used on almost every listing (too much overlap).
- The ten search terms that bring visits but appear in few tags.
- A short note on anything you could not judge because data was missing.
Quote my numbers exactly; do not estimate search volumes.
```
