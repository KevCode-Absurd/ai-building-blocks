# Etsy shop audit

Most Etsy shops lose sales to a handful of fixable things: a weak first photo, a missing return policy, a vague category, slow replies, or a rule problem waiting to remove listings. This skill checks the whole shop against Etsy's own pages and ranks the fixes, so the owner does the few that matter first.

Access levels for this skill: the audit itself is level 1, from screenshots, the listings CSV and public shop pages. The fix list and drafts are level 2. Fixes inside listings can be made at level 3 within limits through Shared access; settings, ads and stats changes stay with the owner as of November 2026, because Shared access does not reach them.

## When to use

- A new client's first job.
- Visits are fine but orders are low.
- The Star Seller badge was lost, or a customer service warning arrived.
- A listing was removed or a policy notice arrived.
- The shop is about to sell into the EU, Northern Ireland, Great Britain or the US for the first time.

## What you need

- **Access I have:** level and limits.
- Screenshots: the search visibility page, Customer service stats, Stats (90 days and the same period last year), the shop home page, three best-selling and three weak listings, policies, shipping settings and the Policy violations page if anything was removed.
- The listings CSV.
- The owner's answers on: who makes each item, partners, AI use, countries shipped to, trader status, any responsible person in the EU or Northern Ireland, and children's or other regulated items.
- **Research mode.** With web search connected, open Etsy's help pages on search visibility, customer service standards and GPSR before scoring, and note any change. Without it, score against the kit's dated facts and list what you could not recheck.

## Steps

1. **Search visibility checks.** For each listing: at least one image; primary photo 2000 pixels or more, a single finished product, clear and well lit; specific category; return policy set; for US listings, domestic shipping under $6. Count failures per check. Is the shop complete, with a logo?
2. **Listings.** Sample at least ten listings, plus all best sellers. Titles: what the item is first, fewer than 15 words, no repetition. Tags: 13, different, within 20 characters, not repeating attributes. Attributes: all that apply. Description: first sentence says what it is. Send detailed rewrites to the etsy-listing-rewrite skill.
3. **Photos.** First photo, scale shot, detail, an unedited real photo, alt text. Send deeper work to the product photographer kit.
4. **Shop structure.** Sections by how buyers shop; four featured listings chosen for best sellers or seasonal items; About section with how items are made and any partners.
5. **Shipping and processing.** Profiles match real costs and carriers; processing times match what the shop actually achieves; "Everywhere Else" is deliberate; US-bound orders follow Etsy's duties rule if the shop ships into the US from abroad.
6. **Service standards.** Message replies, on-time shipping, review score and cases against Etsy's minimums and Star Seller levels. Find the cause of any miss (processing time too short, no tracking, slow replies).
7. **Reviews.** Recent themes and how complaints were handled; any public reply made before messaging the buyer. Pass reply work to the reputation manager kit.
8. **Rules.** Each product group fits a Creativity Standards category; partners disclosed; AI use disclosed; no reselling outside the exceptions; no AI-written patterns or instructions; no other sellers' photos or wording. Any Policy violations entries with reasons.
9. **Product safety.** EU and Northern Ireland: economic operator or manufacturer details and product safety info filled, or sales to those countries switched off. Toys and children's items: CE marking (EU), GB rules, and US children's product certificate and tracking labels flagged for the owner's adviser. GB sellers: name, address and batch reference on products where reasonable.
10. **Ads.** If Etsy Ads run: budget, strategy, which listings, 30-day ROAS by listing against margin. Offsite Ads status and whether the shop is near the compulsory threshold.
11. **Rank the fixes.** For each: effect (high, medium, low), effort (minutes, hours, days), who does it (agent at level 2 or 3, or owner), and the source of the rule. Put rule and safety risks first, then search visibility checks, then listing quality, then everything else.

## Checks

- Every finding points to a screenshot, a listing or an owner answer.
- Every rule or threshold cited is dated and comes from Etsy's help pages or an official source.
- Rule and safety risks are at the top, not buried.
- No promise of a ranking.
- Safety and trader-status points are framed as questions for the owner's adviser, not rulings.
- Each fix names the access level and who does it.

## Output format

1. **Summary:** three biggest problems and the first three fixes.
2. **Ranked fix list:** fix, effect, effort, who, access level, source.
3. **Search visibility table:** check, listings failing, fix.
4. **Service standards table:** metric, current, minimum, Star Seller level, cause, fix.
5. **Rules and safety notes:** what to confirm and with whom.
6. **What I could not check**, and what I need next.
