# Interview: Etsy shop manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Search and listings

1. **Walk me through how you rewrite one listing, from the stats you open first to the moment you publish.**
   Why it matters: their order of work becomes the agent's method.
2. **Show me a title you changed and what happened to the listing's visits and orders.**
   Why it matters: real before-and-after cases show what moves results.
3. **How do you choose 13 tags? What makes you drop a tag?**
   Why it matters: tag judgement is the craft most owners get wrong.
4. **How do you use attributes, and when does an attribute replace a tag?**
   Why it matters: saves tag slots and shows how well they know the listing form.
5. **Where do you find the phrases buyers really use, beyond Etsy Stats?**
   Why it matters: reveals research habits the agent can copy, such as reading messages and reviews.
6. **When do you advise a new listing instead of editing an old one?**
   Why it matters: a judgement call with real risk to a best seller.

## Photos, shop and service

7. **What do you check first on a listing's photos?**
   Why it matters: the first photo is part of Etsy's own quality checks.
8. **How do you plan shop sections and featured listings, and how often do you change them?**
   Why it matters: shop structure shapes how buyers browse.
9. **Tell me about a shop that lost its Star Seller badge or fell below Etsy's service standards. What was the cause and the fix?**
   Why it matters: service metrics affect search, and causes are often hidden in processing times.
10. **How do you set processing times and shipping prices for made-to-order items?**
    Why it matters: wrong settings cause late orders and lost visibility.

## Rules and safety

11. **How do you decide whether an item fits Etsy's rules: made, designed, handpicked or sourced?**
    Why it matters: rule mistakes can remove listings or shops.
12. **How do you handle print-on-demand partners and AI-assisted designs in listings?**
    Why it matters: disclosure rules here are recent and often missed.
13. **What do you tell a seller who wants to start selling into the EU or Northern Ireland?**
    Why it matters: product safety duties now reach small sellers.
14. **Have you worked with toys, children's items, candles or cosmetics? What did you check, and who did you send the seller to?**
    Why it matters: these categories carry the most safety risk and need advisers.
15. **Tell me about a listing removal or intellectual property notice you helped with.**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent where its work stops and the owner's adviser starts.

## Ads, tools and clients

16. **When do Etsy Ads make sense, and how do you judge them?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's ads rules in terms of margin, not clicks.
17. **What do you tell sellers about Offsite Ads?**
    Why it matters: a fee many sellers do not understand until it is compulsory.
18. **Which tools do you use, and what can you do with only a CSV and screenshots?**
    Why it matters: shapes access.md and the no-tools fallback.
19. **How do clients give you access, and what limits do they usually set?**
    Why it matters: real limits make the agent's level 3 defaults sensible.
20. **What is the hardest shop you have worked on, and what did you learn?**
    Why it matters: hard stories teach judgement that rules do not.
21. **What do new Etsy helpers get wrong in their first month?**
    Why it matters: turns into the agent's common mistakes list.
