# Monthly shop health

**Cadence:** monthly, in the first week, after Etsy's Star Seller check on the 1st. About an hour of the agent's work and 20 minutes of the owner's.

**Trigger:** the shop has at least one month of sales, and the owner sends Stats and service screenshots (or grants a route that reaches them).

**Access:** L1 (read), L2 (read and draft), L3 (read, write and publish within limits).

## Steps

1. **Collect, L1.** Screenshots from the owner: Stats (last 30 and 90 days, same period last year), Etsy search terms, listing stats, Customer service stats, search visibility page, Etsy Ads stats, Policy violations page. Plus the listings CSV.
2. **Read the trend, L1.** Visits, conversion rate, orders and revenue against last month and last year. Do not judge rank by searching Etsy; results are personalised.
3. **Service standards, L1.** Message replies, on-time shipping, review score, cases, against the minimums and Star Seller levels. Find the cause of any slip and suggest the fix (processing time, tracking, reply routine).
4. **Search visibility, L1 then L3.** New insights on the page; fix listing-level issues within limits; list settings issues for the owner.
5. **Search terms, L1 and L2 (skill: etsy-tag-and-keyword-plan).** New terms bringing visits; terms that faded. Update the plan; apply tag changes to up to the agreed number of listings at L3.
6. **Reviews, L1.** New reviews and themes. Pass replies to negative reviews to the owner for a fresh yes (see the reputation manager kit). Message unhappy buyers before any public reply.
7. **Ads, L1 and L2 (skill: etsy-ads-plan).** ROAS by listing against break-even; keep, fix or stop list; Offsite Ads status and 365-day sales against the threshold.
8. **Rules and safety, L1.** Any Policy violations entry; any new product type needing a rules or safety check; any change on Etsy's help pages that the kit tracks.
9. **Report, L2.** One page: trend, three wins, three problems, fixes made (with the log), fixes the owner must make, ads results, and decisions needed.

## Outputs

- One-page monthly shop report.
- Updated tag plan and listing changes made within limits.
- Service standards fixes.
- Ads keep, fix or stop list.
- Action log (what, where, when, result).

## Needs the client's approval

- Settings changes: shipping profiles, return policy, processing times set in settings, and markets.
- Ads budget changes and the keep, fix or stop list.
- Replies to negative reviews, cases and disputes.
- Price changes, sales and coupons outside the limits.
- Any new claim about materials, safety or origin.
