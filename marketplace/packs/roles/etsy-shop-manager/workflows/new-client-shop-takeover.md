# New client shop takeover

**Trigger:** a new client hands over an existing Etsy shop.

**Cadence:** once, over the first 30 days, then the monthly shop health workflow takes over.

**Access:** L1 (read), L2 (read and draft), L3 (read, write and publish within limits). The agent starts at L1 until the owner writes down levels and limits.

## Steps

1. **Access and limits, L1.** Ask which level the owner grants for the shop, and the limits (listings, fields, prices, until when). If listing work is at L2 or L3, the owner invites the agent through Etsy's Shared access. Never ask for the password. Write "Access I have".
2. **Collect, L1.** Listings CSV, Stats and service screenshots, policies, shipping settings, Etsy Ads stats, Policy violations page, the owner's answers on makers, partners, AI use, markets and trader status.
3. **Audit, L1 (skill: etsy-shop-audit).** Ranked fix list with effect, effort, who and level.
4. **Agree the first month, L1.** Walk the owner through the top ten fixes; agree which the agent makes and which the owner makes.
   **Approval point:** the action list and limits, once, before the first live change.
5. **Rule and safety risks first, L2 and owner.** Disclosure gaps, reselling risks, EU and Northern Ireland safety details, children's product questions. The owner decides with their adviser; the agent drafts the listing changes.
6. **Search visibility fixes, L3 within limits and owner.** Photos, categories, return policy, shipping prices.
7. **Best sellers first, L2 then L3 (skill: etsy-listing-rewrite).** Rewrite similar listings before touching the top best sellers; change a best seller only with the owner's yes.
8. **Tag plan, L2 (skill: etsy-tag-and-keyword-plan).** Apply in batches and log each.
9. **Sections and featured, L2 then L3 (prompt: shop-sections-and-featured).**
10. **Ads, L1 and L2 (skill: etsy-ads-plan).** Recommend keep, pause or start with a budget the owner sets. The owner applies it.
11. **Day 30 report, L2.** What changed, what moved, what is next; then switch to the monthly shop health workflow.

## Outputs

- "Access I have" record.
- Shop audit and agreed action list.
- Rule and safety question list for the owner's adviser.
- Rewritten listings, tag plan, sections plan.
- Ads plan.
- Day 30 report and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access levels, limits and first action list.
- Changes to best sellers.
- Settings, ads budgets, prices and markets.
- Any declaration about trader status, economic operators or product safety.
- Any reply to a negative review, case, dispute or intellectual property notice.
