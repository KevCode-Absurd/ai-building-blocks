# New listing launch

**Trigger:** a new product or product range is ready to list, with photos taken and the owner's facts written down.

**Cadence:** per launch; review at 7 and 30 days after publishing.

**Access:** L1 (read), L2 (read and draft), L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Settings, Stats and Etsy Ads stay with the owner while Shared access does not reach them (as of November 2026).

## Steps

1. **Rules check, L1 (prompt: creativity-and-ai-disclosure-check).** Confirm the Creativity Standards category, any production partner, and any AI use to disclose. Stop and ask the owner if the item may be reselling.
2. **Safety check, L1 (prompt: product-safety-readiness).** For each country the listing will ship to: EU and Northern Ireland details, GB producer duties, US children's product rules. Anything unresolved goes to the owner's adviser, and the listing does not ship to that market until it is resolved.
3. **Keywords, L1 (skill: etsy-tag-and-keyword-plan).** Pull phrases for this product type from Stats and Marketplace Insights; pick the lead buyer phrase and 13 tags.
4. **Write the listing, L2 (skill: etsy-listing-rewrite).** Title, category, attributes, tags, description, photo order and alt text, partner and AI notes, safety fields. Save as a draft in the shop through Shared access, or deliver a sheet.
5. **Shipping and processing, L1 then owner.** Choose or create the right shipping and processing profiles (prompt: shipping-and-processing-review). Settings changes are made by the owner.
6. **Section and feature, L2.** Choose the shop section; decide whether to feature it (prompt: shop-sections-and-featured).
7. **Owner review.** The owner checks facts, price and photos and approves.
   **Approval point:** price, claims and any market switched on.
8. **Publish, L3.** Publish within limits. Log the change. Check the listing page on a phone.
9. **Day 7 check, L1.** Search visibility page: any new insight for this listing? Fix and log.
10. **Day 30 review, L1.** Views, favourites, orders and search terms for the listing. Adjust tags or title once, based on data, and log the change. Decide with the owner whether to add it to Etsy Ads (skill: etsy-ads-plan).

## Outputs

- A checked, published listing (or a draft at L2).
- Rules and safety notes, with open questions for the owner's adviser.
- Change log entries.
- Day 7 and day 30 notes with any changes made.

## Needs the client's approval

- Price, materials, origin, safety and "handmade" claims.
- Selling into a new country.
- Publishing, unless the listing is inside the written level 3 limits.
- Adding the listing to Etsy Ads, and the daily budget.
