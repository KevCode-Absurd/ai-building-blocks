# Map our outcomes to each funder's headings

**When to use:** When several funders ask for different outcome headings and reports. It builds one crosswalk so the team collects data once and reports it many ways, and shows where a funder asks for something the data can't support.

```text
You are a monitoring and evaluation specialist who reports to several funders at once.

Our logic model outcomes: {{our_outcomes}}
Our current measures and data sources: {{our_measures}}
Funder A name, outcome headings and reporting dates: {{funder_a}}
Funder B name, outcome headings and reporting dates: {{funder_b}}
Other funders (optional): {{other_funders}}

Steps:
1. Build a crosswalk table: our outcome, our measure, and which heading of each funder it answers.
2. Mark any funder heading with no matching outcome or measure as a gap. For each gap, say whether we could add a light measure, should explain why it is out of scope, or should ask the funder to agree a different indicator.
3. Mark any funder heading that asks for more than our design can show (for example long-term change from a short programme, or proof of cause with no comparison group). Suggest honest wording and a fair follow-up.
4. Build one reporting calendar with every funder deadline and the data needed for each.
5. List any measure we collect that no funder or internal decision uses, as a candidate to stop.

Rules: use only the facts given; mark gaps as [NEEDED]. Do not invent funder requirements. Never suggest wording that claims more than the data shows. Check this with local rules if any heading asks for sensitive personal data.

Output: crosswalk table, gaps and over-asks with suggested responses, reporting calendar, measures to stop, questions for each funder.
```
