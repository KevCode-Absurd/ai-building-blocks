# Survey consent and privacy page

**When to use:** Before any programme survey, interview or focus group goes out. It writes the plain-language page participants read first, and a short version for paper or phone.

```text
You are a monitoring and evaluation specialist writing a consent and privacy page for programme participants.

Organisation and programme: {{organisation_and_programme}}
What the survey or interview is for: {{purpose}}
Who takes part (ages, reading level, languages, any vulnerable groups): {{participants}}
Who gives consent (adult, parent or carer, young person with parent informed): {{who_consents}}
How they will answer (link, paper, phone, in person): {{mode}}
Who will see individual answers: {{who_sees}}
How long answers are kept, and where: {{retention_and_storage}}
How answers are matched (participant code): {{matching}}
Contact for questions or to withdraw: {{contact}}
Our privacy notice link or text: {{privacy_notice}}

Write:
1. A consent page under 200 words at a reading age the youngest participant can manage: purpose; that taking part is voluntary and saying no changes nothing about the help they get; they can skip any question and stop at any time; who sees answers and that reports show groups only; how long answers are kept and how to ask for removal; who to contact.
2. A clear opt-in (an unticked box or a spoken yes recorded by staff). No pre-ticked boxes.
3. A 60-second spoken version for phone or in-person use.
4. If participants are children: a version for the young person and a separate one for the parent or carer.
5. A checklist for the client: points to confirm with their privacy lead (legal basis for using the data, retention period, any sensitive questions, any school or funder rules).

Rules: plain words only. Do not state a legal basis yourself; flag it for the client's privacy lead. Do not promise anonymity if answers are matched by code; say "confidential" and explain. Check this with local rules.

Output: consent page, opt-in wording, spoken version, child and parent versions if needed, checklist for the privacy lead.
```
