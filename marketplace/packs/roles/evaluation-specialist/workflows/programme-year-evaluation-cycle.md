# Programme year evaluation cycle

**Trigger or cadence:** A new programme, a new funding year, or a funder asking for outcomes. One cycle runs from planning before the first session to the funder reports at the end of the year, with a monthly monitoring check in between.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Steps marked level 2 or level 3 need that level in the named tool; at a lower level I hand you the work to enter or send yourself. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Scope (before the programme starts).** Run the `evaluation-scope-and-questions` prompt: purpose, users, proportionality and questions. Map funders' headings with the `funder-outcome-headings-map` prompt. *Approval: the programme lead agrees the purpose, design and questions.*
2. **Logic model (week 1 of planning).** Use the `logic-model-and-theory-of-change` skill with staff. Draft on a board or in a document (level 2). *Approval: the team agrees the model reflects what really happens.*
3. **Measures and data plan (weeks 1 to 2).** Use the `outcome-measures-and-data-plan` skill. Draft new fields and codes (level 2); add the approved ones to the named records (level 3). *Approval: the programme lead signs off staff time and the data handling table; the privacy lead confirms consent and retention.*
4. **Baseline survey (first session).** Run the `survey-round` workflow for the "before" point.
5. **Monthly monitoring.** Read attendance and delivery data (level 1). Flag drop-out spikes, sessions missed and groups not reached. Write a short monitoring note for the programme lead.
6. **Mid-point check (long programmes only).** Look at process data and any interim outcome measure. Suggest fixes to delivery; do not change survey wording.
7. **End survey and follow-up (last session, plus any follow-up point).** Run the `survey-round` workflow again with the identical survey.
8. **Analysis and report (two to four weeks after the end).** Use the `evaluation-report-and-funder-summary` skill. Check claims with the `what-this-data-cant-show` prompt. Add stories with the `participant-story-from-notes` prompt, only with consent. *Approval: the programme lead and chief executive approve the report and every number in it.*
9. **Publish and submit.** At level 3, publish the approved report to the folder or page you chose. Submitting to a funder needs your fresh yes for each submission; usually you submit it yourself.
10. **Learn.** A short meeting to agree changes to the programme and to next year's data plan. Update the logic model.

## Outputs

- Scope note and funder crosswalk
- One-page logic model and assumptions table
- Measures plan, data handling table and data dictionary
- Monthly monitoring notes
- Evaluation report with the "what this data can't show" box on page one
- Funder summaries under each funder's headings
- Action log of everything done in the client's tools (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Before the first live action of the cycle, I show the list (add approved fields and codes to named records, send approved surveys and reminders to the approved lists, close surveys on the dates, publish the approved report to the chosen folder) and its limits: which tools, which lists and records, which dates. After your yes, I do these and log each one.

**Always needs a new yes.** Any new group of participants, changed survey wording, surveys for children or on sensitive topics, sharing anything that could identify a participant, deleting or overwriting data, and every submission to a funder. Questions on data law or ethics approval go to your privacy lead or an ethics committee. Check this with local rules.
