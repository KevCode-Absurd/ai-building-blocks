# Survey round

**Trigger or cadence:** Each data collection point in the plan: the "before" survey at the first session, the "after" survey at the last, and any follow-up. One round takes one to three weeks from final check to close.

**Access:** Set per tool (see `access.md`). Building the survey needs level 2 in the survey tool; sending it needs level 3. At a lower level I hand you the finished survey and messages to send yourself.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the list and consent route.** Check who should receive the survey, who gives consent (adult, parent or carer, young person) and any school, funder or organisation rules. *Approval: the programme lead confirms the list.*
2. **Build or copy the survey (level 2).** For the first round, use the `before-and-after-survey` skill. For later rounds, copy the first survey exactly; change only dates and the "end only" block. Add the consent page from the `survey-consent-and-privacy-page` prompt.
3. **Check settings.** Email and IP collection off unless needed; participant code field required; close date and response limit set; reminders scheduled but unsent.
4. **Pretest (first round only).** A few people like the participants answer aloud. Fix wording that confuses them, before the baseline goes out.
5. **Final approval.** Show the client the survey, consent page, list, dates and reminders. *Approval: the programme lead approves wording, list and dates.*
6. **Send (level 3).** Send the approved survey to the approved list, or give staff the paper or phone script. Log the send.
7. **Watch replies.** Track response by group (not by person). Flag anything odd: replies from outside the list, many identical answers, a complaint. Stop and ask if something looks wrong.
8. **Remind and close (level 3).** Send the agreed reminders and close on the date. Log each action.
9. **Export and secure.** Export responses with codes only. Store them where the data plan says. Report the response count by group, above the minimum group size.
10. **Hand over.** Pass the clean export to the analysis step in the `programme-year-evaluation-cycle` workflow.

## Outputs

- The approved survey, consent page and messages
- Send log and reminder log
- Response counts by group
- A clean, coded export stored as the data plan says

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Sending the approved survey to the approved list, sending the agreed reminders and closing on the date. Before the first send I show the list of actions and limits and wait for your yes; then I log each action.

**Always needs a new yes.** Adding people outside the approved list, any wording change after approval, any survey for children or on sensitive topics, exporting data with names, and deleting responses. Check this with local rules.
