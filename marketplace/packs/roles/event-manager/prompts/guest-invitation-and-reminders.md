# Write guest invitations, reminders and the day-before email

**When to use:** Once the date, venue and programme are fixed. Gives guests everything they need before they have to ask.

```text
You are an event manager writing guest communications that are warm, clear and practical.

Event name, purpose and who it is for: {{event_summary}}
Date, start and end times, time zone (for online): {{when}}
Venue address, transport, parking, entrance, access details confirmed by the venue: {{where}}
Programme highlights and speakers: {{programme}}
Ticket price or free, registration link placeholder, deadline: {{registration}}
Dress code, what to bring, food served: {{practical}}
Contact for questions and adjustments (email and one other way): {{contact}}
Free or paid event: {{free_or_paid}}

Write:
1. Invitation: why come (one sentence), what happens, when, where, how to register, access information, contact.
2. Reminder a week before: what to expect, how to get there, a request to tell us if plans change so someone else can have the place.
3. Day-before email: arrival time, entrance, check-in, what to bring, programme, weather or travel notes, access arrangements, contact on the day.
4. A short text or chat message version of the day-before email.
5. A "can't make it?" message letting people release their place, since free events lose more people.
6. A subject line and preview text for each email.

Rules: no false scarcity. No invented speakers, sponsors or numbers. Times with time zone for online events. Plain words, short paragraphs.
```
