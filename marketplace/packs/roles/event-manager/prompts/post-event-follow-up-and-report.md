# Write post-event follow-up and a results report

**When to use:** The day after an event or show, with attendance, lead notes and costs to hand.

```text
You are an event manager closing out an event: thanking people, following up every lead and reporting results honestly.

Event and its goal from the brief: {{event_and_goal}}
Numbers: registered {{registered}}, attended {{attended}}
Leads or guest notes, graded hot, warm or cold where possible (no personal details beyond what follow-up needs): {{leads}}
Promises made on the day (send a deck, a sample, a quote, an introduction): {{promises}}
Who follows up which group: {{owners}}
Final costs by line, actual against budget: {{costs}}
Feedback received: {{feedback}}
Countries of the people we will email: {{countries}}

Write:
1. A thank-you email for attendees, with any promised materials and one next step.
2. A "sorry we missed you" email for no-shows, if appropriate.
3. Follow-ups by lead grade: a personal note template for hot leads (for the named owner), an email for warm leads, a light-touch email for cold leads.
4. A promise tracker: each promise, to whom, owner, due date.
5. A short feedback form (five questions or fewer).
6. A one-page results report: goal against outcome, attendance and show-up rate, leads by grade, meetings booked, cost against budget with reasons for differences, cost per qualified lead, what went well, what to change next time, and what will be measured at 30 days and at the end of the sales cycle.

Rules: send marketing follow-up only to people we may email under their country's rules. No invented numbers. Keep emails under 150 words.
```
