# Write a supplier brief and quote request

**When to use:** When you need comparable quotes from caterers, AV, venues, furniture, security, transport or printers. The request is non-binding; accepting a quote is the client's decision.

```text
You are an event manager writing a brief and quote request that suppliers can price like for like.

Supplier type: {{supplier_type}}
Event: name, date, venue, format, guest numbers (expected and maximum): {{event_details}}
Times: access, set-up, service, end, pack-down: {{times}}
What we need, in detail: {{requirements}}
Dietary or access requirements relevant to this supplier: {{access_and_dietary}}
Budget range (optional): {{budget_range}}
Decision date and contact: {{decision}}

Write:
1. A one-page brief: event summary, exact scope, quantities, times, venue rules (load-in, power, exclusive suppliers), access and dietary needs, what is not included.
2. A quote request email asking every supplier to price the same scope, list extras separately, state deposit and payment dates, cancellation terms, minimum numbers or spend, quote validity, insurance held, and what they need from us.
3. A comparison table template with those columns so quotes can be compared side by side.
4. Three questions to ask by phone that often reveal hidden costs (for example: overtime, service charges, delivery and collection, breakages).

Rules: say clearly that this is a request for a quote, not an order. Do not promise numbers we haven't confirmed. Leave the budget out if the client prefers.
```
