# Training interview: Event manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert to leave out client names, venue contract figures that could identify a deal, and any attendee details; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of events and trade shows do you know best, and at what size and budget?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **When a client says "we're doing an event", what are the first five questions you ask?**
   Why it matters: captures the real intake order.

3. **How do you get a client to name the one result that would make the event worth it?**
   Why it matters: purpose drives every later choice, and clients rarely start with it.

4. **Show me a budget you built. Which lines do first-timers always forget?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real format and a list of hidden costs.

5. **How do you decide how much contingency to hold, and when do you release it?**
   Why it matters: turns judgement about uncertainty into a method the agent can explain.

6. **Which outside deadlines have caught you out: licences, road closures, interpreters, catering numbers?**
   Why it matters: real lead times prevent the worst surprises.

7. **What do you look for first in a venue contract? Tell me about a clause that cost a client money.**
   Why it matters: captures which terms matter most in practice.

8. **How do you compare quotes when suppliers price different things?**
   Why it matters: like-for-like comparison is a skill that saves money.

9. **Walk me through a run sheet you're proud of. What makes it work without you there?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a model of ownership, cues and backups.

10. **Tell me about the worst thing that went wrong on an event day. What did your backup look like?**
    Why it matters: a real story teaches contingency better than a list.

11. **How do you plan access for disabled guests from the start, not at the end?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's practical access routine.

12. **How do you handle dietary requirements and allergens with caterers?**
    Why it matters: captures a process where mistakes can cause harm.

13. **What do you ask on registration forms, and what have you stopped asking?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent's forms short and careful with data.

14. **How do you plan for no-shows on free and paid events?**
    Why it matters: turns attendance experience into numbers the client can plan with.

15. **How do you decide whether a trade show or sponsorship is worth it?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's cost and fit tests.

16. **What do you put in a booth staff brief, and how do you stop staff losing leads?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a proven staff kit.

17. **How do you make sure every lead or guest is followed up after the event?**
    Why it matters: follow-up is where most event value is lost.

18. **What do you never do without the client's explicit yes?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's boundaries on contracts, payments and data.

19. **Which tools do you use for registration, planning and follow-up, and what access do you ask for?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's default tools and access requests.

20. **How do you report results so the client decides whether to run it again?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's report format.

21. **What do you check the week before every event?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's final checklist.

22. **Where do you find AI helpful in event work, and where don't you trust it?**
    Why it matters: sets honest limits for the agent's own work.
