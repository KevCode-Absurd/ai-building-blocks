# Post-event wrap-up

**Trigger:** the day after any event, show or webinar. Runs for 30 days, with a final read at the end of the client's normal sales cycle when leads are involved.

**Access:** set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Level 1 to read attendance, leads and costs. Level 2 to draft emails, the feedback form and the report. Level 3 to send approved follow-ups to approved lists, update the CRM and share the report with the agreed people. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Day 1: gather.** Attendance and check-ins, lead notes and grades, promises made on the day, issues logged during the event, supplier notes, actual costs so far.
2. **Day 1: thank and follow up.** Use the `post-event-follow-up-and-report` prompt to write the thank-you, no-show and graded follow-up emails and the promise tracker. Send approved emails to approved lists at level 3; hot leads go to their named owners for a personal call.
3. **Day 2: feedback.** Send the short feedback form to attendees, speakers, crew and key suppliers.
4. **Week 1: close the books.** Check supplier invoices against quotes and the contract summaries; list differences and their reasons. Prepare the payment list for the client to approve and pay. Disputes with suppliers, and any claim against a deposit, need the client's fresh yes.
5. **Week 1: tidy the data.** Delete dietary and access answers on the date set in the data plan; remove attendee data shared only for the event from supplier and staff copies; keep marketing contacts only where the person agreed or the law allows.
6. **Day 30: report.** One page: goal against outcome, attendance and show-up rate, leads by grade, meetings and opportunities, cost against budget, cost per qualified lead or per attendee, feedback themes, what to change.
7. **End of the sales cycle: final read.** Revenue or other outcomes linked to the event against full cost. Feed into the next event's brief.

## Outputs

- Thank-you and graded follow-up emails, sent or ready
- Promise tracker with owners and dates
- Feedback form and summary
- Invoice check and payment list for the client
- Data deletion record
- Day-30 report and end-of-cycle read

## Needs the client's approval

**Approved once, then run at level 3 within your limits.** Follow-up emails to approved lists, CRM updates for event leads, sharing the report with the agreed people, deleting event-only data on the dates set in the data plan.

**Always needs a new yes, every time.** Paying any invoice (the client pays), disputing an invoice or claiming against a deposit, sending a formal complaint to a supplier, sending to a new list, and sharing attendee data with anyone new.
