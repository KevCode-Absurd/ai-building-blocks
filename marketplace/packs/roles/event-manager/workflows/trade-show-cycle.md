# Trade show cycle

**Cadence:** once a year to choose shows and sponsorships, then for each show from about three months before to 30 days after. Results feed next year's choice.

**Access:** set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Level 1 to read packs, past results and the CRM. Level 2 to draft the shortlist, booth kit, lead form and emails, and prepare exhibitor portal orders for the client to submit. Level 3 to publish the lead form, send approved pre-show meeting requests and post-show follow-ups to lawful lists, and log leads in the CRM. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Yearly choice.** Use the `trade-show-plan` skill to compare shows on audience fit, full cost and cost per qualified lead. Run the `sponsorship-decision-brief` prompt on any package offered. The client decides; booking a stand or signing a sponsorship needs the client's fresh yes, and the client pays.
2. **Exhibitor manual (about 12 weeks before).** Pull every deadline from the organiser's manual into the timeline: stand design, furniture and power orders, badge names, shipping, lead-retrieval app. Prepare orders for the client to submit; anything that commits money needs a fresh yes.
3. **Follow-up first (about 8 weeks before).** Write lead grades, follow-up emails, owners and the 30-day plan. Check post-show email rules for each region with the knowledge section 3.8 notes.
4. **Booth kit (about 6 weeks before).** Goal, layout notes, opener, qualifying questions, demo, lead fields with consent, rota with breaks, staff brief.
5. **Pre-booked meetings (from 4 weeks before).** Send approved meeting requests to target accounts and existing contacts who may attend, using lawful lists.
6. **Logistics (2 weeks before).** Use the `run-sheet-and-contingency` skill for build-up day, show days and breakdown: arrivals, shipping, stand checks, rota, backups for a missing staff member or failed demo.
7. **Show days.** Staff capture leads with grade, need, timing, consent and owner. The agent logs leads into the CRM at level 3 each evening and flags hot leads to their owners.
8. **The Monday after.** Owners call hot leads; approved emails go to warm and cold leads by grade.
9. **Day 30.** Use the `post-event-follow-up-and-report` prompt: conversations, qualified leads, meetings, opportunities, orders, full cost and cost per qualified lead.
10. **End of the sales cycle.** Revenue from show leads against full cost. Update next year's shortlist.

## Outputs

- Show and sponsorship comparison with calls
- Timeline of exhibitor deadlines
- Follow-up plan and emails
- Booth kit and staff brief
- Build and show-day run sheet
- Leads in the CRM with grades and owners
- Day-30 and end-of-cycle results

## Needs the client's approval

**Approved once, then run at level 3 within your limits.** The lead form, pre-show meeting requests to approved lists, logging leads in the CRM, and post-show follow-ups by grade to lawful lists.

**Always needs a new yes, every time.** Booking a stand, signing a sponsorship or exhibitor contract, ordering paid services, paying anything (the client pays), cancelling or changing a booking, using an organiser's attendee list for marketing, and sending to any new list.
