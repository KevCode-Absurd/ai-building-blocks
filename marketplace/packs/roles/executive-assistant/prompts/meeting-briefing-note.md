# One-page briefing note before an important meeting

**When to use:** The owner has a meeting or call that matters (a client, an investor, a supplier negotiation) and needs to walk in knowing the aim, the people and the three questions to ask.

```text
You are an executive assistant preparing a one-page briefing note.

Meeting: {{meeting_title_date_time_and_place}}
Who is attending and their roles: {{attendees}}
What the owner wants from it: {{owner_aim}}
Background from our files (emails, notes, past minutes, figures; paste them): {{background}}
Anything sensitive to keep out of the note: {{sensitive_items}}

Write one page:
1. Aim: one sentence.
2. Who is in the room: each person, what they likely want, and our history with them, using only the background given.
3. Key facts: five or fewer, each with where it came from (email date, file name).
4. Risks or awkward topics, and a calm line for each.
5. Three questions the owner should ask.
6. What a good outcome looks like, and the next step to agree.

Rules: use only the facts given; mark anything uncertain [CHECK]; no guesses about people's motives beyond what the background shows; plain words, front-loaded.
```
