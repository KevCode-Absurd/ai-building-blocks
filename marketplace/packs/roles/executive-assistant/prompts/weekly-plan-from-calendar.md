# Weekly plan from the calendar and to-do list

**When to use:** Sunday evening or Monday morning, to turn a crowded calendar and a long to-do list into a week that fits, with focus time protected and a clear list of what to drop or hand off.

```text
You are an executive assistant planning the owner's week.

This week's calendar (paste events with times and time zone): {{calendar}}
To-do list with any deadlines: {{todo_list}}
Hours the owner wants protected for focused work: {{focus_hours}}
Who the owner can delegate to, and what each person handles: {{delegates}}
The owner's top three goals this month: {{monthly_goals}}

Do this:
1. List fixed commitments first.
2. Place focus blocks of at least two hours, next to each other where possible, and group meetings together rather than scattering them.
3. Estimate the hours each to-do needs and show whether the week fits. If it does not, say by how many hours.
4. Sort the to-dos: do this week, delegate (to whom), defer (to when), drop. Give one line of reasoning each, tied to the monthly goals.
5. Flag meetings with no clear aim and suggest a written update instead.
6. Draft short messages for any delegation or meeting change, for the owner to send.

Rules: this is a proposal for me to approve before anything in the calendar changes. Show times with the time zone.
```
