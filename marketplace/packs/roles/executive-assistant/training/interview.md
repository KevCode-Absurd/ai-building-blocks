# Training interview: executive assistant

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, email contents or personal details of real employers, clients or contacts; anonymise every story.

1. **Who have you supported (role, size of business, industry), and how many people at once?**
   Why it matters: sets the kind of owner the agent is built for and the pace it should expect.

2. **Walk me through your first hour of a normal day with an owner's inbox and calendar.**
   Why it matters: shows the real routine the agent should copy, in order.

3. **How do you decide, email by email, whether to reply, delegate, decline, wait or file?**
   Why it matters: captures the sorting rules that save the owner's time.

4. **How do you learn to write like the owner? What did you get wrong at first?**
   Why it matters: voice matching is the difference between drafts that get sent and drafts that get rewritten.

5. **What do you never reply to without the owner, and how did you agree that list?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent its "owner only" rules and how to set them with a new client.

6. **Tell me about a time you caught a fake payment request or a phishing email.**
   Why it matters: real fraud patterns and the checks that stopped them become the agent's red flags.

7. **How do you protect an owner's focus time when everyone wants a meeting?**
   Why it matters: calendar judgement is hard to write down; the expert's rules make it concrete.

8. **What goes into a briefing note you would be proud of?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a model and the sources the expert trusts.

9. **How do you take minutes so actions actually get done?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert handles missing owners, vague decisions and follow-up.

10. **Have you used AI note-takers in meetings? What did they get wrong, and how did you check them?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent where AI notes fail and what to verify.

11. **Show me a complaint or dispute letter that worked. What made the other side act?**
    Why it matters: the structure and tone of letters that get results.

12. **How do you set up filing so a new person can find things on day one?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's folder and naming habits into the agent's defaults.

13. **What renewals, filings or deadlines have you seen businesses miss, and how do you track them now?**
    Why it matters: builds the agent's admin calendar checklist from real misses.

14. **What access do you ask for when you start with a new owner, and what do you refuse?**
    Why it matters: sets safe access habits: an access level per tool, delegation or official connectors, no shared passwords.

15. **Describe a trip you planned that went wrong. What do you check now?**
    Why it matters: itinerary checks (time zones, connections, entry rules) come from real failures.

16. **How do you handle confidential matters, such as staff issues or a sale of the business?**
    Why it matters: discretion rules the agent must follow.

17. **When have you pushed back on an owner's request, and how did you say it?**
    Why it matters: the agent needs a respectful way to say "this is risky" or "this will not work".

18. **What does a great week look like for an owner you support, and how do you measure it?**
    Why it matters: defines what success means for the agent and how to report it.
