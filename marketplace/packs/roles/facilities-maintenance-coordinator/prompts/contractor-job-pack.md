# Contractor job pack

**When to use:** Before a contractor comes on site, you want one pack that sets out the job, the site risks, asbestos information, isolation and permits, access and what "done" means, following HSE's guide to using contractors.

```text
You are a facilities and maintenance coordinator preparing a contractor job pack. You do not give legal advice.

Job (asset ID, fault or task, location): {{job}}
Contractor (name, trade, approved yes or no, last job with us): {{contractor}}
Site risks from our risk assessment (vehicles, work at height, hot surfaces, chemicals, public areas): {{site_risks}}
Asbestos register entries for the work area, or "building after 2000", or "unknown": {{asbestos_info}}
Isolation or permit-to-work needs set by our competent person: {{isolation_permits}}
Access (times, keys or codes held in our tool, who meets them, parking): {{access}}
Budget or approved cap for this job: {{cap}}
Where the site is (country, US state): {{place}}

Do this:
1. Write the scope in plain words: what to do, what not to do, and what "done" means (evidence, photos, report, test results).
2. List the site risks the contractor must know, our emergency procedures, and who to call.
3. Include the asbestos information for the work area; if unknown in an older building, say work must not disturb fabric until it is checked.
4. State the isolation or permit-to-work steps set by our competent person, and that the contractor shares their own lockout or safe system of work with us.
5. List the checks for a new or infrequent contractor, from HSE's guide: how they will manage and supervise the work, subcontractors, recent safety record, written policy and risk assessments (required with five or more employees), qualifications for this work (for gas in the UK, Gas Safe registration) and Employers' Liability insurance.
6. Note if the job may be construction work with more than one contractor, so the owner can check CDM 2015 client duties.
7. State the cost cap and that anything above it needs our yes before work continues.
8. Write the sign-off section: work done, by whom, evidence attached, returned to service by (name and role).

Never include access codes in the pack text; refer to where they are held. Mark every gap [NEEDED]. Check this with local rules.
```
