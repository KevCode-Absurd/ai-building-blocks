# Training interview: facilities and maintenance coordinator

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share door codes, key safe numbers, staff names or client addresses; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of assets have you looked after (vehicles, gym kit, kitchen equipment, plant, buildings), how many, and in which countries or states?**
   Why it matters: rules and good practice differ by asset type and place, and this sets the limits of the agent's advice.

2. **When you start somewhere new, what do you do in the first week?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's first-week checklist.

3. **How do you number assets, and what goes in your register?**
   Why it matters: captures the ID scheme and fields that work in practice.

4. **Where do your service intervals come from, and what do you do when the manual is missing?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert avoids guessed intervals.

5. **Which jobs do you schedule on fixed dates and which on hours, mileage or from the last job?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent when to use fixed, floating and meter triggers.

6. **Show me a daily check sheet that actually gets done. Why does it work?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a tested format and length.

7. **How do you know whether checks are really being done and not just ticked?**
   Why it matters: captures the signals the agent looks for in completion data.

8. **What happens in your system when a check fails on a safety item?**
   Why it matters: pins down the out-of-use and return-to-service rules.

9. **How do you set work order priorities, and who decides?**
   Why it matters: records priority rules the agent can propose without borrowing targets.

10. **Which maintenance software have you used, and what did you wish you had set up differently?**
    Why it matters: captures real CMMS lessons for set-up.

11. **How do you choose and check a new contractor?**
    Why it matters: turns into the agent's contractor checks.

12. **Tell me about a contractor job that went wrong. What would have prevented it?**
    Why it matters: reveals the briefing and supervision steps that matter most.

13. **Who are the competent persons on your sites for water, fire, gas, electrical, lifting and vehicles, and how do you keep their dates?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert runs a compliance calendar without signing off safety.

14. **How do you handle legionella checks day to day, and who do failures go to?**
    Why it matters: captures the routine behind the written scheme.

15. **How do you decide whether to repair or replace something?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the factors and figures the expert trusts.

16. **How do you cost downtime, and who gives you the figure?**
    Why it matters: downtime is the cost most often left out of repair decisions.

17. **What spending can you approve alone, and what goes to the owner?**
    Why it matters: maps to the agent's caps and fresh-yes list.

18. **Tell me about the worst breakdown you dealt with. What was behind it?**
    Why it matters: shows the expert's root-cause habits.

19. **What do you put in your weekly report to the owner?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's weekly review.

20. **What do new maintenance coordinators most often get wrong?**
    Why it matters: turns into common mistakes and test briefs.
