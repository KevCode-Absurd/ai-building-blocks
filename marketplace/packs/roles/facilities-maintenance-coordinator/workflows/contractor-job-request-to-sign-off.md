# Contractor job: request to sign-off

**Trigger:** A work order needs an outside contractor: a repair beyond in-house skills, a statutory service, a gas, electrical, lifting or water job, or building work.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the job (level 1).** Asset ID, fault or task, priority, and whether the asset is out of use. Check the asset's history for the last contractor visit.
2. **Choose the contractor.** Use an approved contractor for this trade if there is one. For a new one, run the checks in the `contractor-job-pack` prompt (management of the work, subcontractors, safety record, policy and risk assessments, qualifications such as Gas Safe registration for UK gas work, Employers' Liability insurance). *Approval: a new contractor needs the owner's yes.*
3. **Get the price.** Ask for a quote for anything not covered by an agreed rate. *Approval: accepting a quote as binding, or any job above the cap, needs a fresh yes every time.*
4. **Prepare the job pack.** Use the `contractor-job-pack` prompt: scope, site risks, asbestos information for the work area, isolation and permits set by the competent person, access, emergency procedures and what "done" means. If the job may be construction work with more than one contractor, flag the CDM 2015 client duties for the owner. *Approval: the competent person issues any permit-to-work.*
5. **Raise and book (level 3 within the cap).** Send the approved work order to the contractor and book the visit; tell the site who is coming and when. Record it on the work order.
6. **On the day.** The contractor signs in, receives the pack, and confirms isolation and permits with the competent person before starting. If they report asbestos, a gas fault or another serious finding, stop and tell the owner and competent person.
7. **Check the work.** Collect the evidence: report, photos, test results, parts and time. Compare the cost with the quote; anything over goes to the owner before the invoice is approved.
8. **Return to service.** Only the competent person or qualified contractor returns the asset to service, recorded on the work order. *Approval: competent person.*
9. **Close and review.** Close the work order with cost and downtime, update the register and next-due dates, file certificates against the compliance calendar, and pass the invoice to the owner for payment. Note how the contractor performed for the approved list.

## Outputs

- Work order with contractor, quote and job pack
- Booked visit and site notice
- Evidence and certificates filed
- Return-to-service record
- Closed work order with cost and downtime
- Invoice passed to the owner for payment
- Contractor performance note

## Needs the client's approval

New contractors, binding quotes, jobs above the cap, permits-to-work (competent person), cost overruns, returns to service, and every payment.
