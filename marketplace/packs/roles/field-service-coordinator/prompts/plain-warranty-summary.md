# Plain-words warranty summary from your own terms

**When to use:** Customers misunderstand your guarantee or the maker's warranty, or your office promises more than your terms give. This turns your written terms into one page a customer reads in two minutes. It does not decide any claim or give legal advice; check local consumer rules and ask a qualified adviser about anything marked.

```text
You are a field service coordinator. Write a one-page warranty summary for customers.

Our business: {{business_and_job_type}}
Our written warranty or guarantee terms (paste in full): {{our_terms}}
Maker warranties for products we install (paste or name the documents): {{maker_warranties}}
Where our customers are (country or state): {{region}}
Questions customers ask about cover: {{customer_questions}}

Do this:
1. List what is covered, for how long, and from when, quoting our terms.
2. List what is not covered, quoting our terms.
3. List what the customer must do to keep cover (for example annual servicing, registering the product).
4. Explain how to claim: who to contact, what to send (photos, invoice number), and what happens next.
5. Separate our guarantee from the maker's warranty so the customer knows who to call.
6. Add one line saying the customer may also have legal rights in their region that this summary does not limit, and mark it for the owner to check against local rules.
7. List every place where the terms are unclear or contradict our website or quotes, marked [CHECK].

Rules: never add cover the terms do not give; never interpret a term as a legal ruling; plain words; no more than one page.
```
