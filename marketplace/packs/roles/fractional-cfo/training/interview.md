# Training interview: Fractional CFO

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, figures that could identify a client, or personal data; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of businesses do you work with (size, sector, country), and how many at once?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's defaults, its regional rules and the depth it can give each client.

2. **When an owner first calls you, what do they usually say is wrong, and what is usually really wrong?**
   Why it matters: the gap between the stated and the real problem is where a CFO earns their fee.

3. **Walk me through your first two weeks with a new client, step by step.**
   Why it matters: captures the real order of work and which files the expert asks for first.

4. **How do you check that the books can be trusted before you rely on them?**
   Why it matters: the tie-out habits stop the agent building on wrong numbers.

5. **Which five or six numbers do you look at first in a health check, and why those?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a tested starting set instead of a long list of ratios.

6. **How do you decide what counts as red, amber or green for a client, without industry averages you can't trust?**
   Why it matters: the expert's way of judging against the client's own history and plans is reusable.

7. **Tell me about a time profit looked fine but cash ran out. What did you find and what did you do first?**
   Why it matters: real cash crises teach the agent which checks matter most.

8. **How do you build a 90-day plan the owner actually follows?**
   Why it matters: captures how many actions, what owners and what measures make a plan stick.

9. **How do you compare two loan offers, and what clauses do you always read twice?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's checklist for fees, covenants, security and guarantees.

10. **Describe a conversation with a lender that went well, and one that went badly. What was different?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent how to prepare an owner, while the owner does the talking.

11. **How do you decide whether an add-back is strong, medium or weak?**
    Why it matters: the expert's proof standard keeps the agent from inflating adjusted profit.

12. **What do you look for first when someone is buying a small business?**
    Why it matters: shows the buyer-side red flags the expert has learned to spot quickly.

13. **Tell me about a deal where diligence changed the price or stopped the deal. What gave it away?**
    Why it matters: real stories teach the agent where profit that isn't real tends to hide.

14. **How far ahead should an owner start preparing to sell, and what do you fix first?**
    Why it matters: captures the order of sale preparation and which fixes need a long track record.

15. **How do you reduce a business's reliance on its owner without upsetting key clients?**
    Why it matters: owner dependence is the hardest fix; the expert's method is valuable.

16. **Where is your line between finance advice and tax, legal, valuation or insolvency advice? Give an example of handing something on.**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent inside its role and shows how to hand over cleanly.

17. **What do you do when you think a client may not be able to pay its debts as they fall due?**
    Why it matters: the agent must know when to stop optimising and point to a licensed professional.

18. **Which tools do you use, and what access do you ask each client for?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's default tools and access levels.

19. **How do you write for an owner who is not a finance person?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's plain-language habits and layout.

20. **Tell me about a recommendation you got wrong. What did you change afterwards?**
    Why it matters: honest mistakes become checks in the agent's quality bar.

21. **What do clients most often ask you for that you refuse, and how do you say no?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent tested wording for out-of-scope requests.

22. **What does a great month look like for one of your clients?**
    Why it matters: defines the outcome the agent works toward.
