# Sale preparation programme

**Trigger:** An owner plans to sell in one to three years, or has had an approach and wants to be ready. **Cadence:** a full review at the start, then monthly progress checks and a re-score every six months until the business goes to market.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Data room steps need level 2 (private folders) or level 3 (filing documents in named folders). Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Set the goal.** Timing, what the owner wants after the sale, and the advisers in place (accountant, lawyer, broker). If there are none yet, list which ones the owner will need and when.
2. **Score readiness.** Run the `sale-readiness-review` skill: seven areas scored with evidence, the owner dependence map and the ranked fixes.
3. **Build the document index first (level 2).** Create the folder structure in a private area and mark each document present, missing or out of date, with an owner and a date. *Approval: nobody outside sees the folders until the owner says yes.*
4. **Prove the profit.** Run the `quality-of-earnings-review` skill on the seller's own numbers: tie three years to the bank, list add-backs with proof and strength, show upkeep spending, concentration and working capital by month. *Approval: the owner's accountant reviews the add-back schedule before anyone else sees it.*
5. **Start the slow fixes now.** Moving client relationships to the team, signing contracts with the largest customers, writing processes, renewing the lease. Each goes into the 12-month plan with an owner, cost and the evidence it will produce.
6. **Clean up the records.** Filings up to date (for example Companies House identity checks in the UK), share register checked, old disputes closed, personal costs out of the business. *Approval: legal and filing items go to the owner's lawyer or accountant.*
7. **Monthly progress check.** Plan actions done or not, documents added to the index, and any new risk (a big customer leaving, a key person resigning).
8. **Re-score every six months.** Run the readiness review again and show the change since the last score.
9. **Hand over to the broker.** When the owner is ready: the one-page profile with no confidential names, the adjusted profit schedule with proof, and the document index. *Approval: the owner approves every document and every person given access, each time.*
10. **Tax and structure questions.** Keep a running list for the accountant and lawyer: timing, share or asset sale, reliefs such as UK Business Asset Disposal Relief, and US asset allocation forms. I list questions; they advise.

## Outputs

- Readiness scorecard, re-scored every six months
- Document index and private folder structure
- Adjusted profit schedule with proof and strength ratings
- Owner dependence map and handover plan
- 12-month preparation plan with progress notes
- One-page business profile for the broker
- Questions for the accountant, lawyer and broker
- Action log of everything done in the tools

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Filing and renaming documents in the named private folders and updating the plan tracker.

**Always needs a new yes.** Giving any broker, buyer or lender access to the folders, sending the profile or schedules to anyone outside, any letter of intent or offer, and deleting documents. Valuation, price, tax and legal structure are for the broker, accountant and lawyer: check this with local rules and ask a qualified accountant or lawyer.
