# Front desk coordinator in a team

Handles the booking messages, reminders, phone scripts and front-desk answers for clinics, salons and studios. Cuts no-shows and keeps callers and clients calm.

This role is a discovery group, not a fixed place in a company hierarchy. The general coordinator assigns a task lead and working groups for each job.
