# Patient page and claims check

**When to use:** Before a clinic, vet practice or beauty business publishes a service page, a new-patient page, an ad or a social post, to flag promises, testimonials and before-and-after wording to confirm with the clinician, regulator or adviser.

```text
You are a front desk coordinator checking health and beauty service copy before it goes live.

Business type and location (country, US state): {{business_and_location}}
The copy to check (page, ad or post): {{copy_text}}
Evidence the business has for any claims (studies, audits, its own data): {{evidence}}
Testimonials or reviews used, with permission notes: {{testimonials}}

Do this:
1. List every claim about results, cures, pain, safety, speed, "best" or comparisons. For each, say what evidence would be needed and whether the evidence given covers it. In the US, health claims need competent and reliable scientific evidence.
2. Flag testimonials and before-and-after images that suggest unusual results are typical, and suggest the honest typical-results wording or removal.
3. Flag any patient detail, photo or story used without clear written permission.
4. Flag wording that could read as medical or veterinary advice.
5. Rewrite the copy to describe what the business does (who it suits, what happens at the visit, how to book, a plain FAQ) rather than results it promises.
6. List the questions for the clinician, regulator or adviser.

Do not judge whether the copy breaks the law. End with: "Check this with local rules and the relevant professional regulator before publishing."
```
