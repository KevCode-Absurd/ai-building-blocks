# Training interview: front desk coordinator

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client, patient or pet names, health details or contact details; anonymise every story.

1. **Which kinds of front desks do you know best (clinic, vet, salon, studio, trades), and in which countries or states?**
   Why it matters: privacy, texting and fee rules differ by sector and place, and this sets the limits of the agent's advice.

2. **Walk me through a busy hour on the desk: phone, walk-ins, messages. What do you do first?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert triages competing demands, which the agent's scripts must respect.

3. **What do your booking confirmations and reminders say, and when do they go out?**
   Why it matters: captures real timing and wording that the expert has seen work.

4. **Where do no-shows happen most in your experience, and what reduced them?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent tested fixes and the patterns to look for in an export.

5. **How do you explain a cancellation fee or deposit to an upset client?**
   Why it matters: captures firm but kind wording that keeps the client and the policy.

6. **When have you waived a fee, and who decided?**
   Why it matters: records where discretion sits so the agent never promises an exception it cannot give.

7. **What are the top reasons people call, and which could a recorded message answer?**
   Why it matters: shapes the phone menu and hold messages around real demand.

8. **What happens when a caller describes symptoms or says it's an emergency?**
   Why it matters: pins down the emergency script and the line the desk never crosses.

9. **What must the desk never say, and what must only the clinician or vet say?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's hard limits on clinical and policy answers.

10. **What do you leave on a voicemail for a patient or client, and what do you leave out?**
    Why it matters: captures privacy habits for messages that others may hear.

11. **Tell me about a privacy slip you saw or nearly made. What changed afterwards?**
    Why it matters: reveals the checks the expert now runs before anything is sent or said.

12. **How do you handle a caller who is angry, crying or frightened?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent calm, human words for the hardest calls.

13. **How do you reply to a bad public review that mentions care or a staff member?**
    Why it matters: captures safe reply habits and when the owner must decide.

14. **How do you ask clients for reviews?**
    Why it matters: checks the expert's method against review rules and keeps it fair.

15. **What do new clients get wrong on intake forms, and how would you fix the forms?**
    Why it matters: improves form wording and the intake flow from real friction.

16. **Which booking and phone systems have you used, and what did they do well or badly?**
    Why it matters: sets realistic tool advice and access levels for the agent.

17. **How do you fill a slot that frees up at short notice?**
    Why it matters: captures waitlist practice the agent can automate within limits.

18. **What does the desk check at the end of each day and week?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's routine into the agent's monthly review.

19. **What would you change first in a front desk you had never seen before?**
    Why it matters: shows the expert's priorities, which shape the agent's first recommendations.

20. **What makes a client leave the desk feeling looked after, even when the answer was no?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's sense of hospitality, the tone the agent should keep.
