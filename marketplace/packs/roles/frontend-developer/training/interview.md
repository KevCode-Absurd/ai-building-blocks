# Training interview: front-end developer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, private code and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe projects in general terms.

1. **Which frameworks, versions and kinds of projects do you know best, and which do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **Walk me through the last component or page you built, from brief to merged pull request.**
   Why it matters: reveals the real order of work and where the expert spends time.

3. **What do you ask for before you write a line of code?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions that prevent rework.

4. **How do you make sure every state is handled when the design only shows the happy path?**
   Why it matters: records how the expert finds and proposes missing states.

5. **When do you reach for a library, and when do you build it yourself or use a native element?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's threshold for adding dependencies.

6. **Tell me about a React or Vue bug that took you longest to find. What was it?**
   Why it matters: surfaces real failure patterns such as effect loops or stale state.

7. **How do you write component tests that survive refactoring?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's testing style in practice.

8. **What is your keyboard and screen reader routine before you hand over?**
   Why it matters: turns accessibility from a checklist into a habit the agent copies.

9. **How do you handle a brand colour that fails contrast when the client loves it?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert balances brand wishes with accessibility.

10. **A client says the site is slow. What do you look at first, second and third?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's diagnosis order for speed problems.

11. **How do you explain Core Web Vitals or a speed report to a non-technical owner?**
    Why it matters: captures plain-language explanations the agent can reuse.

12. **Tell me about a speed fix that did not work, or made something else worse.**
    Why it matters: adds risk notes the agent should attach to common fixes.

13. **How do you decide which browsers and devices to test on, and how do you test Safari on iPhone?**
    Why it matters: sets realistic test lists for small teams.

14. **What makes a good pull request description in your eyes, and what makes you reject one?**
    Why it matters: defines the hand-over format and quality bar.

15. **How do you fit new code into an old codebase with its own odd conventions?**
    Why it matters: captures how the expert matches existing style without spreading bad patterns.

16. **When have you pushed back on a design or feature request, and how?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert disagrees and records the disagreement.

17. **If you build browser extensions: how do you choose permissions, and what will you refuse to build?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's least-privilege habits and ethical limits.

18. **How do you handle user-written HTML, third-party embeds and secrets in front-end code?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's security habits.

19. **What access do you ask a client for, and what do you refuse to accept?**
    Why it matters: feeds access.md and the safety rules in the expert's own words.

20. **How do you check AI-generated front-end code before you trust it?**
    Why it matters: lists the made-up APIs, missing states and accessibility gaps the agent must catch.

21. **How do you keep up with browser and framework changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: sets refresh habits and trusted sources for this agent.
