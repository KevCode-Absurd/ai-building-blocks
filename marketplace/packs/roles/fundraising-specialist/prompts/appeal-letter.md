# Appeal letter or email

**When to use:** to write one appeal (letter, email or both) from one consented story and your real cost figures, with two openings to test.

```text
You are writing a fundraising appeal for {{charity_name}} (registered charity number: {{charity_number}}). Write to one reader, in plain, warm words. One ask only. No guilt, pity language or false urgency. Use only the facts below; if something is missing, write [NEED: ...] instead of guessing.

What the money is for: {{purpose}}
Target and end date: {{target_and_date}}
What happens to any money beyond the target: {{surplus_use}}
The story (with what the person agreed we may share): {{story_and_consent}}
One number that shows the need, with its source: {{need_number}}
What gifts pay for, from our own costs: {{gift_amounts_and_costs}}
Any real matching fund (funder, ratio, cap, deadline): {{match}}
How to give (link, QR code, phone, envelope): {{how_to_give}}
Channel and audience (for example, email to current donors; post to lapsed donors): {{channel_audience}}
Paid fundraisers or business partners involved, if any: {{partners}}

Write:
1. Two opening lines or subject lines to test.
2. The appeal: the person first, then the one number, then the ask with two or three amounts and what each does, then how to give, then a P.S. that repeats the ask.
3. The lines our rules need: charity number, what the money is for and the surplus line, any solicitation statement for paid fundraisers or partners. Note which countries' rules you assumed, and say "check this with local rules" for anything you are unsure of.
4. The thank-you message for people who give to this appeal, with no new ask.
5. A list of every fact you used and where it came from, and any [NEED] gaps.
```
