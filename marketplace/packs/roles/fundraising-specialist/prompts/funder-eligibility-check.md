# Funder eligibility check

**When to use:** before writing any bid, to check every rule in one or more funders' guidelines against your facts and get a verdict for each.

```text
Check our eligibility for each funder below, rule by rule. Read the exclusions first. Quote each rule exactly. Mark each as met, not met or unclear, using only our facts. One "not met" on a must-have rule means the verdict is skip, with the reason. Unclear rules become questions for the funder's staff. Do not assume anything about a funder that its guidelines don't say.

Our facts: legal form {{legal_form}}; registration {{registration}}; where we work {{area}}; who we serve {{who_we_serve}}; yearly income {{income}}; years of accounts {{years_of_accounts}}; bank account in our name {{bank_account}}; governing document {{governing_document}}; board or committee (number, and how many are unrelated) {{board}}.
What we need money for, how much and when: {{project_and_amount}}
Funder guidelines (paste each funder's full guidelines, exclusions and deadlines; or, if web search is connected, the funder's guideline page addresses): {{funder_guidelines}}

If web search is connected, read each funder's current guidelines on its own site and note the date checked. If not, use only what is pasted.

For each funder return:
1. Eligibility table: rule (quoted), met / not met / unclear, evidence from our facts, fix if possible.
2. Verdict: apply now, prepare first (and what to fix by when), or skip (and why).
3. Up to three questions to ask the funder's staff.

Then a ranked summary table across all funders: funder, verdict, grant range, deadline, prep start date.

Do not rule on our legal form, charity status or tax position: for those, say "check this with local rules, and ask a qualified adviser".
```
