# Progress or final report to a funder

**When to use:** when a funder report is due, to turn your records into a clear, honest report in the funder's template.

```text
Write a {{report_type}} report for {{funder_name}} on the grant for {{project_name}}, in the funder's template below. Use only our records. Report what did not go to plan as plainly as what did. If a figure is missing, write [NEED: ...]. Do not round results up.

Funder's template or questions (paste): {{template}}
What the grant was for, the amount and the dates: {{grant_terms}}
Outcomes and measures we promised (paste from the application): {{promised_outcomes}}
What we did (activities, dates, numbers from our records): {{activities}}
What changed, and how we measured it: {{results}}
Feedback from the people we work with, with consent to quote: {{feedback}}
What we learned and what we will change: {{learning}}
Budget against actual spend, line by line: {{budget_vs_actual}}
Any changes the funder agreed during the grant: {{agreed_changes}}

Return:
1. The report in the funder's order and word limits.
2. A budget table (budget, actual, difference, reason for any difference).
3. A list of statements that need evidence attached, and any [NEED] gaps.
4. A note on anything that might need the funder's approval (an underspend, a change of activity), so we raise it before submitting.

Submitting the report needs our fresh yes on the final version. Questions about restricted funds or accounting treatment: check this with local rules, and ask a qualified accountant.
```
