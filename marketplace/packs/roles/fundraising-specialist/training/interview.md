# Interview: fundraising and grants specialist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work. Names of donors, beneficiaries and funders stay out of the answers unless they are public.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your first fortnight with a new charity client, from the first call to the first money in.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and the order of work.
2. **What do you ask for before you write a single word of an appeal?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's intake list.
3. **How do you choose the one story for an appeal, and how do you check the person agreed to it?**
   Why it matters: captures their consent standard and story judgement.
4. **How do you turn a charity's costs into ask amounts a donor can picture?**
   Why it matters: shows how they size asks honestly.

## Donors and stewardship

5. **What do you do in the first 90 days after someone gives for the first time?**
   Why it matters: first gifts are where most donors are lost.
6. **When have you held back an ask, and what did you do instead?**
   Why it matters: tests patience and judgement on timing.
7. **How do you decide who is a major gift prospect when a charity has no research budget?**
   Why it matters: shows how they work from the charity's own records.
8. **Describe an ask meeting that went well, and one that didn't. What was different?**
   Why it matters: real practice for the ask prep sheet.
9. **What thank-you practice do you believe in that you have actually measured? What did you find?**
   Why it matters: separates habit from evidence.

## Grants

10. **How do you cut a long list of funders to the few worth a bid?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's fit and verdict rules.
11. **Tell me about a bid you advised the client not to write. Were you right?**
    Why it matters: shows how they judge odds and effort.
12. **How do you make a budget narrative that a funder trusts?**
    Why it matters: budgets are where weak bids are caught.
13. **What do you put in a report when the project didn't go to plan?**
    Why it matters: captures their honesty standard with funders.

## Rules and safety

14. **Which receipt, acknowledgment or Gift Aid mistakes have you seen most, and how did you catch them?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's compliance checks.
15. **How do you handle consent for email and text, and what changed for charities in the UK in 2026?**
    Why it matters: tests current knowledge of consent and the soft opt-in.
16. **Where do you draw the line between a strong appeal and a manipulative one?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's tone limits.
17. **What would you never do with donor data, even if a trustee asked?**
    Why it matters: sets data limits for major donor work.

## Tools, quality and clients

18. **Which donor database, giving platform and email tool do you prefer for a small charity, and why?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's tool advice.
19. **What do you check on the last read of an appeal before it goes out?**
    Why it matters: becomes the quality bar.
20. **What do you measure after a campaign, and what would make you change the next one?**
    Why it matters: shows how they learn from results.
21. **Tell me about the hardest fundraising job you have done. What went wrong, and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: the story the coach turns into test briefs and pushback lines.
