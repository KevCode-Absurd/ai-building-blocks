# Appeal campaign cycle

**Trigger:** the client sets an appeal date (winter, year-end, a project or an emergency). Start about eight weeks before launch for a planned appeal; for an emergency, compress steps 1 to 4 into two days and keep every check.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits). Until the client grants more, everything runs at L1 and the client sends and publishes.

## Steps

1. **Brief and facts, L1.** Purpose, target, end date, surplus line, the story and its consent record, cost figures, donor groups and counts, consent basis for each list, registration facts, past results. Write down "Access I have" for each tool.
2. **Plan and write (skill: appeal-campaign; prompt: appeal-letter), L1 then L2.** The campaign summary, the messages, the compliance lines, the thank-you set for this appeal and the tracker. At L2, build the emails unsent in the email tool and the campaign page as a draft.
3. **Thank-you and receipts ready (skill: gift-thanks-and-acknowledgement; prompt: receipt-and-declaration-check), L2.** Thank-you automations built but switched off; IRS acknowledgment, quid pro quo or Gift Aid wording added and flagged for the accountant where unsure.
4. **Client review.** **Approval point:** the client (and trustees, for a major campaign) approve the story, the ask, the compliance lines and the go-live list: each send with its list, date and message.
5. **Launch, L3 within limits.** Publish the page and send the launch to the approved lists on the agreed date; switch on thank-you automations. Log each action.
6. **Watch daily during the campaign, L1.** Gifts, new and returning donors, average gift, unsubscribes and complaints. Stop and tell the client about a spike in unsubscribes or complaints, a payment error or a platform warning.
7. **Mid-point update and last call, L3 within limits.** Real progress figures only. A deadline only if it is real.
8. **Thank everyone, L3 within limits.** Thank-yous within the client's target time; a progress note to people who did not give, with no new ask.
9. **Close and report, L1.** Total raised, cost, net, donors by group, new donors, average gift, what worked, what to change. Restricted funds and any surplus recorded against the stated purpose.

## Outputs

- Campaign summary, messages, page and thank-you set.
- Compliance lines and receipt or declaration wording.
- Tracker with daily and final figures.
- Action log (what, where, when, result).
- Close-out report with lessons for the next appeal.

## Needs the client's approval

- The story, the ask amounts, the compliance lines and the go-live list, before any send (one yes at L3 covers the agreed sends).
- A fresh yes for any send to a list or group not in the limits, any change to the target or deadline, and any formal reply to a complaint about the appeal.
- Refunds above the client's limit, and any change to payout or bank details: these stay with the client.
