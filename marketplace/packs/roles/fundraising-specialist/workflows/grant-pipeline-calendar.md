# Grant pipeline and calendar

**Cadence:** a full funder review once a year (or when the client's needs change), a monthly calendar check, and the per-bid steps below for each application.

**Trigger:** the client wants grant income planned, a new project needs funding, or a funder deadline appears.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits). With web search connected, funder facts are checked live and dated; without it, the client pastes guidelines.

## Steps

**Yearly review**

1. **Needs list, L1.** Projects and core costs that need funding over the next 12 months, with amounts and dates.
2. **Prospect list, L1.** Funders the client already knows, plus new ones from GrantNav (UK), Form 990-PF filings (US) or Candid, live if web search is connected, or from the client's research.
3. **Eligibility and fit (skill: funder-fit-and-grant-writing; prompt: funder-eligibility-check), L1.** Verdict per funder: apply now, prepare first or skip.
4. **Calendar, L2 or L3.** Prep start, internal sign-off, deadline, decision and reporting dates for each shortlisted funder, in the client's shared calendar or tracker.
   **Approval point:** the client confirms the shortlist and the calendar.

**Per bid**

5. **Approach, L1 then L2.** Questions for the funder's staff, drafted for the client to send or, at L3, sent from the agreed mailbox.
6. **Draft (skill: funder-fit-and-grant-writing), L2.** Answers, logic model, budget and narrative in a shared document or the funder's portal if the account holder added the agent as a user. For public portals and registrations, use the proposal writer kit's `grant-application` skill.
7. **Review, L1.** The client checks facts, figures and budget; trustees or the head sign where needed.
8. **Submit.** **Approval point:** a fresh yes for this exact version, every time, even at L3. The account holder presses submit, or the agent submits at L3 from an account the client has allowed, after that yes. Confirm receipt and log it.

**Monthly**

9. **Calendar check, L1.** Prep starts due this month, deadlines in the next 60 days, decisions due, reports due.
10. **Reports (prompt: funder-report), L2.** Draft reports from records; raise any underspend or change with the funder before submitting. Submitting a report needs a fresh yes.
11. **Results log, L1.** Applications sent, won, lost, with feedback, to improve next year's verdicts.

## Outputs

- Ranked funder list with verdicts and eligibility tables.
- 12-month funding calendar.
- Approach questions, draft applications and still-missing lists.
- Funder reports.
- Results log and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- The shortlist and calendar.
- Any message to a funder sent in the client's name.
- A fresh yes for every submission of an application, report or declaration.
- Any grant agreement or acceptance terms: signed by the client, never by the agent.
