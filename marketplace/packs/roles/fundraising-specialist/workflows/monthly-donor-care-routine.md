# Monthly donor care routine

**Cadence:** a short weekly check (about 20 minutes of the client's time) and a monthly review in the first week of each month.

**Trigger:** the thank-you set and the donor database fields (thank-you date, acknowledgment date, Gift Aid status, consent per channel) are in place.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits).

## Steps

**Weekly**

1. **New gifts, L1.** List gifts since last week by donor group. Flag first gifts, major gifts, gifts in memory and any gift with a note from the donor.
2. **Thank-yous, L2 or L3 (prompt: thank-you-set).** Check every gift has a thank-you sent within the client's target. At L3, send approved templates and log the date; personal notes for major and first-time gifts go to the named signer as drafts.
3. **Acknowledgments and declarations, L2.** US gifts of $250 or more and payments over $75 that bought something have the right wording; UK taxpayers without a Gift Aid declaration get a short invitation. Anything unclear goes to the accountant.
4. **Consent and preferences, L3 within limits.** Apply opt-outs and preference changes the same week. Erasure requests and record deletions need a fresh yes.
5. **Weekly note, L1.** Five lines: gifts and donors, thank-yous on time, anything waiting for the client.

**Monthly**

6. **First-time donor plan, L1 then L2 (skill: gift-thanks-and-acknowledgement).** Who is in the 90-day plan, who got the impact update, who is due an invitation, and who is ready for a second ask.
7. **Retention and lapsed donors, L1.** Donors who gave in the same month last year but not this year; draft a win-back message that reports impact first.
8. **Major donors, L1 then L2 (skill: major-gift-cultivation-and-ask).** Every top prospect has a dated next step; overdue steps go to their lead.
9. **Gift Aid claim schedule, L2.** Prepare the schedule from valid declarations for the client's authorised person to check and submit through Charities Online. The agent does not submit the claim.
10. **Monthly report, L1.** Donors, gifts, retention against the same month last year, thank-you timing, complaints, and next month's plan.

## Outputs

- Thank-yous sent or drafted, with dates logged.
- Acknowledgment and declaration gaps, with questions for the accountant.
- Updated consent and preference records.
- First-time donor and major donor next steps.
- Gift Aid claim schedule for the client to submit.
- Weekly note, monthly report and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- New thank-you templates and win-back messages before first use.
- A fresh yes for record deletions, erasure requests, formal complaint replies and any send to a new audience.
- Gift Aid claims, refunds above the limit and any bank or payout change stay with the client.
