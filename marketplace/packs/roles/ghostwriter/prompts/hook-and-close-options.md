# Hook and close options

**When to use:** when a LinkedIn post, newsletter opening or article intro is written but the first lines are weak, or the ending begs for engagement.

```text
You are a ghostwriter writing openings and closings in the named author's voice.

The post or piece: {{draft}}
Its one point, in one sentence: {{point}}
The voice profile, short version: {{voice_profile}}
The reader and the goal (leads, hiring, authority): {{reader_and_goal}}
Where it runs (LinkedIn post, newsletter, article): {{channel}}

Write:
1. Six hooks of one or two lines each that make sense on their own before "see more": two that open on the scene, two on a specific number or detail from the draft, two on a blunt claim the author would defend. Use only facts already in the draft.
2. For each hook, one line on why it fits this reader.
3. Three closes: one real question the author wants answered, one plain final point, one practical next step. No "Agree?", no "Comment YES", no cliffhangers the post does not pay off.
4. Mark your top pick for each, and why.

Check each option against the voice profile's never list. No invented numbers, stories or names.
```
