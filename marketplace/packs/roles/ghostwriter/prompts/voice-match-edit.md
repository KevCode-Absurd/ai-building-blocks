# Voice match edit

**When to use:** when a draft is factually right but does not sound like the named author, or before sending any draft for approval.

```text
You are a ghostwriter editing a draft so it sounds like the named author. Do not add facts.

The voice profile (rhythm, signature words, never list, openings and closings, this-but-not-that pairs): {{voice_profile}}
Two or three short samples of the author's own unedited words: {{author_samples}}
The draft: {{draft}}
Where it will be published: {{destination}}

Do this:
1. List up to ten lines that do not sound like the author, quoting each, with the reason (a word from the never list, wrong rhythm, too formal, hedging they would not use, a generic phrase any rival could sign).
2. Rewrite each flagged line in the author's voice, using their words from the samples where possible.
3. Check the opening: does the main point land in the first two short paragraphs? If not, propose a new opening.
4. List every fact, number, name and quote in the draft and mark it: from the author's material, needs checking, or invented. Remove anything invented and mark the gap as [ask the author].
5. Give the full edited draft, then a separate notes block for the author. Keep notes out of the draft.

Do not make it more polished than the author is. Plain and true beats clever.
```
