# Interview: ghostwriter

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you capture a voice

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new client, from the first call to the first draft they approved.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **What do you need from a client before you can sound like them, and what do you do when they give you almost nothing?**
   Why it matters: voice capture is the biggest gap between a ghostwriter and plain AI.
3. **Show me a voice profile or notes you keep on a client. What goes in it, and what have you learned to leave out?**
   Why it matters: the profile format is a reusable tool.
4. **How do you tell when a client's past posts were written by someone else?**
   Why it matters: copying the wrong samples is a common silent failure.
5. **What do client edits teach you, and how do you use them on the next draft?**
   Why it matters: reveals how fast the expert improves the match.

## Getting the material

6. **What questions get you real stories instead of opinions? Give me your three best.**
   Why it matters: interview craft decides the quality of everything after it.
7. **Tell me about a client who was hard to interview. What finally worked?**
   Why it matters: captures tactics for guarded or busy clients.
8. **How do you log stories and permissions so nothing private slips out?**
   Why it matters: confidentiality is part of the job, and the system matters more than memory.

## Writing and editing

9. **How do you find the one point of a piece before you draft? What do you do if the client has three?**
   Why it matters: one-point pieces are the core of the craft.
10. **How do you write a LinkedIn opening that works before "see more" without resorting to bait?**
    Why it matters: separates honest hooks from engagement tricks.
11. **What do you cut from a client's draft every time?**
    Why it matters: shows their editing instincts and their "never" list.
12. **How do you handle a client who wants to sound more impressive than the facts support?**
    Why it matters: tests their line on boasting and invented results.

## Books

13. **How do you shape a pile of posts or talks into a book's big idea and chapter plan?**
    Why it matters: book structure is a distinct, high-value skill.
14. **What does a proposal you're proud of contain, and how do you handle comparable titles and the author's platform?**
    Why it matters: reveals what agents and publishers actually respond to in their experience.

## Ethics, disclosure and terms

15. **What jobs do you turn down, and how do you say no?**
    Why it matters: their boundaries (academic work, fake reviews, invented stories) must be explicit in the trained agent.
16. **Has a student or parent ever asked you for help with an essay, a dissertation or an admissions essay? What did you say?**
    Why it matters: the agent must refuse graded and assessed work in a kind, firm way.
17. **How do you handle AI in your process, and what do you tell clients about disclosure to platforms and publishers?**
    Why it matters: AI disclosure rules for books and public-interest text now apply to drafts like these.
18. **What do your contracts say about credit, copyright and confidentiality? What has gone wrong without them?**
    Why it matters: ownership and credit disputes are the main business risk of the role.
19. **How do you handle sponsored or gifted mentions in a client's posts?**
    Why it matters: disclosure rules for endorsements apply to ghostwritten posts too.

## Access and publishing

20. **Do you post for clients? Through which tools, and what approval do you insist on first?**
    Why it matters: sets the trained agent's level 3 habits.
21. **Tell me about a post that went wrong after it was published. What did you do?**
    Why it matters: shows how they stop, report and recover.

## Judgement and results

22. **How do you know the writing is working for the client? What do you look at, and what do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: separates real outcomes (the right conversations, enquiries, invitations) from vanity numbers.
23. **Tell me about your hardest ghostwriting job: the brief, what went wrong, and what you would do differently.**
    Why it matters: the richest source of judgement for the trained agent.
