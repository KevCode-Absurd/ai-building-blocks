# Book from notes to proposal

**Trigger:** an expert with years of posts, talks or client work wants a book, either self-published or sold to an agent or publisher.

**Cadence:** a project of several weeks, with a check-in at each approval point.

## Steps

1. **Check the job and the terms (prompt: ghostwriting-job-check).** Confirm the know-how and stories are the author's, the book is not graded or assessed academic work, and the route (self-published or traditional). Agree credit, confidentiality and who owns what; ask the author's lawyer to put it in writing.
   **Approval point 1:** the author agrees the scope, the credit line and the terms.
2. **Gather and inventory (tools: the author's documents at level 1; skill: book-proposal-and-chapter-plan, step 2).** Posts, talks, transcripts and anonymised client notes, each logged with a source and permission status.
3. **Build the voice profile (skill: voice-profile-from-samples).** From transcripts first, not polished posts.
4. **Find the big idea (prompt: big-idea-test).** Three candidates, tested; the author picks one.
   **Approval point 2:** the author signs off the big idea and the reader.
5. **Plan the book (skill: book-proposal-and-chapter-plan, steps 4 to 6).** Reader questions, title options and the chapter plan, each chapter with one job.
   **Approval point 3:** the author approves the chapter plan.
6. **Draft the sample chapter (skill: book-proposal-and-chapter-plan, step 8; prompt: voice-match-edit).** In the author's manuscript document as suggestions or a new draft (level 2). Every claim and story flagged.
7. **Write the proposal (traditional route; skill: book-proposal-and-chapter-plan, step 7).** Follow the target agent's or publisher's guidelines. Comparable titles come from the author and are checked to exist.
8. **AI and disclosure note (skill: book-proposal-and-chapter-plan, step 9).** Explain KDP's AI question for self-publishing, the contract's originality and AI clauses for traditional deals, and the copyright point about the author's own words. Suggest the author rewrites key passages in their own words.
   **Approval point 4:** the author approves the proposal and sample chapter as final.
9. **Submit or publish.** Sending to an agent or publisher needs the author's fresh yes for each recipient, even at level 3. For self-publishing, there is no official KDP connector that I have checked: I prepare the files, description and entry sheet, and the author uploads and answers the AI question honestly.
10. **Plan the launch (optional; skill: book-proposal-and-chapter-plan, step 11).** Built on the author's own audience; honest review requests only.

## Outputs

- Material inventory with sources and permissions.
- Voice profile.
- Big idea, reader questions and title options.
- Chapter plan.
- Sample chapter with flags.
- Proposal (traditional) or manuscript plan (self-published).
- AI, disclosure and ownership notes.
- Optional 90-day launch outline.

## Needs the client's approval

- Scope, credit and terms (point 1).
- Big idea and reader (point 2).
- Chapter plan (point 3).
- Final proposal and sample chapter (point 4).
- Each submission to an agent, publisher or competition, every time.
- Any upload, price or publish step on a self-publishing platform, which the author does from their own account.
