# Monthly founder content cycle

**Cadence:** monthly, starting in the last week of the month before. Voice profile refreshed every cycle.

**Trigger:** a founder, consultant or specialist wants a steady run of LinkedIn posts (and, if wanted, one longer article or newsletter) in their own voice without writing them.

## Steps

1. **Set or confirm access (tool: access.md questions).** Confirm the access level for each tool: the document where drafts live, the scheduler, the blog or newsletter. Write it under "Access I have". Level 1 until the client answers. Confirm the "never" list and any brand links.
2. **Check the job (prompt: ghostwriting-job-check).** First cycle only, or when the destination changes: in bounds, disclosure needs, credit and confidentiality agreed.
3. **Capture the month (prompt: story-mining-questions).** A 20 to 30 minute recorded call or voice memo. Get consent before recording. Log each story, number and opinion with a source ID and permission status.
4. **Refresh the voice (skill: voice-profile-from-samples, step 12).** Add what last month's client edits taught you.
5. **Draft the batch (skill: linkedin-post-batch).** Plan the mix, write each post, label any paid, gifted or connected brand mention, remove private details.
6. **Draft the long piece, if any (skill: story-interview-to-article).** One point, one scene, flags in a notes block.
7. **Tighten (prompts: hook-and-close-options, voice-match-edit).** Fix weak hooks and any line that does not sound like the client.
8. **Place drafts for review (tool: Google Docs suggesting mode or the scheduler's drafts; level 2).** At level 1, send the drafts in a document.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves each piece word for word. Record who approved and when in the approval table.
9. **Schedule and publish (tool: the scheduler or CMS the client connected; level 3 only).** Before the first live action of the cycle, show the list (which posts, which accounts, which dates) and the limits, and get a yes. Then schedule only approved pieces. At level 2 or below, the client schedules from the approved drafts.
   **Approval point 2:** a fresh yes, even at level 3, for anything outside the limits: a new account or audience, a piece answering a complaint or crisis, a claim about a named person or company, or deleting or editing a live post.
10. **Watch and stop if needed.** If a post fails, a platform warning appears, or a thread turns hostile, stop and ask. Comments and direct messages are answered by the client.
11. **Report (tool: screenshots or exports of post analytics from the client).** The action log (what, where, when, result), which posts started the right conversations, and three ideas for next month. Keep the story bank updated: used, unused, retired.

## Outputs

- Story bank and month's source log.
- Updated voice profile.
- Approved batch of posts and, if agreed, one article or newsletter.
- Approval table with dates.
- Action log and a one-page monthly report.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits per tool (step 1).
- Every piece, word for word, before it is scheduled or published (point 1).
- The level 3 action list before the first live action in each cycle (step 9).
- Anything outside the limits, and any piece that answers a complaint or crisis or names a third party critically (point 2).
