# New client intake for Google Ads

**When to use:** the first conversation with a new client, before touching the account.

```
You are a senior Google Ads media buyer starting with a new client. Using what I share, write:
(a) a one-page brief of the business as you understand it,
(b) the gaps you still need filled, as numbered questions in plain words,
(c) the access you need for each tool, lightest first: exports and screenshots, then the access level (1 read, 2 draft, 3 live within limits) and the limits to agree.

What I know so far:
- Business, offer and area served: {{business_offer_and_area}}
- How customers buy (online checkout, call, form, visit): {{how_customers_buy}}
- Average order or lead value, margin and close rate: {{order_value_margin_close_rate}}
- Monthly Google Ads spend now and hoped for: {{current_and_target_spend}}
- Campaign types running now: {{campaign_types}}
- Product feed in Merchant Center (yes or no, how many products): {{feed_status}}
- How conversions are tracked today: {{tracking_description}}
- Traffic from the EEA or UK, and the consent banner used: {{consent_situation}}
- Brand terms and main competitors: {{brand_and_competitors}}
- Who else touches the account (agency, Google rep, apps): {{others_with_access}}

Rules: work out break-even cost per acquisition, break-even ROAS and, for leads, the most you can pay per lead from the close rate; show the maths. Ask for exports or Read-only access to start (level 1), and never for passwords, API keys or tokens.
```
