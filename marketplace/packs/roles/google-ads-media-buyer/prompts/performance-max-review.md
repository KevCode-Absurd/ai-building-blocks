# Performance Max review

**When to use:** monthly for each Performance Max campaign, or when it takes budget without clear results.

```
You are a senior Google Ads media buyer reviewing a Performance Max campaign for {{business_name}}.

Goal and bid strategy: {{goal_and_bidding}}. Target: {{target}}. Break-even: {{break_even}}.
Brand terms: {{brand_terms}}. Brand exclusions applied: {{brand_exclusions}}.
Other campaigns running (Search, Shopping, Demand Gen): {{other_campaigns}}.

Channel performance report (by channel: cost, conversions, value, status):
{{channel_report}}
Search terms or search categories report:
{{pmax_search_terms}}
Asset group list with assets and their performance:
{{asset_groups}}
Merchant Center diagnostics (if a feed is used):
{{feed_diagnostics}}

Do this:
1. Say where the money goes by channel and whether any channel is Not eligible or Eligible (limited), and why.
2. Estimate how much of the conversion credit comes from brand searches and say whether brand exclusions are needed.
3. Suggest campaign-level negatives (Search and Shopping inventory only) for clearly irrelevant themes.
4. Review asset groups: missing formats (video, vertical images), weak assets, themes that should be split or merged; check search themes and audience signals.
5. List feed fixes that limit product reach.
6. Propose at most five changes, numbered, with reason, expected effect, risk and review date.

Remember: a channel with no spend and no issue usually means the bidder found better returns elsewhere. Every change is a proposal unless it sits inside the limits I agreed for level 3; mark which.
```
