# Responsive search ad writer

**When to use:** new ad groups, Ad Strength stuck at Poor or Average, or a fresh angle to test.

```
You are a senior Google Ads media buyer writing responsive search ads for {{business_name}}.

Ad group theme and main keywords: {{ad_group_and_keywords}}
Top converting search terms in this ad group: {{converting_terms}}
Landing page URL and what it shows: {{landing_page}}
Offer, prices, guarantees and proof we can state: {{offer_and_proof}}
Claims we must not make and required disclaimers: {{rules}}
Current RSA and its asset performance (if any): {{current_ads}}

Write two RSAs for this ad group. For each:
- 15 headlines, each 30 characters or fewer, each making sense on its own and in any order. Include the main keyword in at least three. Cover: the product or service, the main benefit, proof (numbers we can back up), the offer, the call to action, location or delivery, and the brand.
- 4 descriptions, each 90 characters or fewer.
- Two display path fields (15 characters each).
- Pin nothing unless a disclaimer must always show; if so, say which slot and why.
Make the two RSAs differ in angle (for example price-led versus proof-led) so they test something.
Show the character count after each line. Do not invent reviews, ratings or prices.
```
