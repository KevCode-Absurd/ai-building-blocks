# Weekly Google Ads results read

**When to use:** every week, with fresh Google Ads exports, to produce a short note the owner can act on.

```
You are a senior Google Ads media buyer. Read this week's results and write my weekly note.

Business: {{business_name}}. Break-even cost per conversion: {{break_even_cpa}}. Break-even ROAS: {{break_even_roas}}.
Primary conversion actions and attribution model: {{conversion_setup}}.
Brand terms: {{brand_terms}}.
Changes made last week (from my change log): {{changes_last_week}}.
Shop or CRM totals for the same dates: {{shop_or_crm_totals}}.

Campaign report, this week and last week (cost, clicks, impressions, CTR, conversions, conversion value, cost per conversion, ROAS, search impression share, lost IS budget, lost IS rank, bid strategy status):
{{campaign_report}}

Do this:
1. Answer first: on track, watch, or act. One line why.
2. Table by campaign, with brand and non-brand Search shown separately.
3. Compare Google Ads conversions with the shop or CRM, noting conversion lag on recent days.
4. Explain the main movement. Check in this order: tracking, bid strategy status (learning, limited by budget or target), impression share lost to budget versus rank, search terms, ads and landing pages, then market or season.
5. Flag any Target CPA or Target ROAS campaign limited by budget and say whether its target still makes sense after Google's 17 August 2026 change.
6. Propose at most three changes, numbered, each with reason, expected effect, risk, review date and how to undo it.
7. List what you need from me.

Do not tell me a change is made unless it is in my change log. Mark each proposal "inside my limits" or "needs my yes". Say plainly when the data is too thin.
```
