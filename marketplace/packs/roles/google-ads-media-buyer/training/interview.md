# Interview: pulling out a Google Ads media buyer's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("show me a real example"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour in an account you have never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you decide which conversion actions should be primary?**
   Why: goal setup drives every Smart Bidding decision.
3. **Tell me about a time Google Ads and the client's CRM disagreed badly. How did you find the cause?**
   Why: real debugging stories beat checklists.
4. **How do you structure Search campaigns for a small local business, and when do you split brand from non-brand?**
   Why: captures your structure rules and reporting habits.
5. **How do you use match types today, and when do you switch on broad match or AI Max?**
   Why: shows how you balance reach against control.
6. **What does your search terms routine look like, and how do you decide the level and match type of a negative?**
   Why: negatives management separates careful buyers from careless ones.
7. **What is the worst negative keyword mistake you have seen?**
   Why: teaches the collateral-damage check.
8. **How do you choose between Maximize conversions, Target CPA and Target ROAS, and how do you set the first target?**
   Why: bidding judgement is the core of the craft.
9. **What changed in your accounts after Google's August 2026 change to budget-limited target campaigns, and what did you do?**
   Why: records current platform experience that changes fast.
10. **How do you run Performance Max alongside Search and Shopping without them cannibalising each other?**
    Why: overlap management is a common failure point.
11. **How do you judge whether Performance Max is finding new customers or just taking credit for brand searches?**
    Why: shows your incrementality thinking.
12. **What makes a product feed good, and which feed fixes have paid off most for you?**
    Why: feed quality is the targeting for Shopping.
13. **How do you write responsive search ads, and do you ever pin?**
    Why: captures your copy method and testing habits.
14. **How do you get offline sales back into Google Ads for lead businesses?**
    Why: lead quality depends on it.
15. **How do you handle consent mode and enhanced conversions for clients with EEA or UK traffic?**
    Why: measurement gaps from consent are now normal.
16. **When do you raise a budget, and how do you forecast what it will buy?**
    Why: budget conversations make or break client trust.
17. **Tell me about an experiment you ran in Google Ads. How long, what split, what did you learn?**
    Why: shows your testing discipline.
18. **How do you explain a bad month to an owner who is not a marketer?**
    Why: shapes the agent's voice.
19. **Which Google recommendations do you usually accept, and which do you ignore?**
    Why: separates judgement from button-pushing.
20. **What do you refuse to do for a client, even if they ask?**
    Why: defines limits and ethics.
21. **Which tools outside Google Ads do you rely on, and what for?**
    Why: fills access.md with your real stack.
22. **Tell me about your hardest account and what finally worked.**
    Why: judgement under pressure.
23. **How do you keep up with Google's changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why: tells the refresh process where to look.
