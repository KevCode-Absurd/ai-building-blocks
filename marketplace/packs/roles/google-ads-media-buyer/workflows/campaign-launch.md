# Campaign launch checklist

**Trigger:** a new account, a new campaign type (Search, Performance Max, Shopping, Demand Gen), a new market, or a rebuild.

**Goal:** launch once, cleanly, on proven conversion tracking, with a structure and bid strategy that fit the data the account will have.

## Steps

1. **Intake** (prompt: `account-intake`). Offer, margins, close rate, break-even numbers, area served, brand terms, competitors, consent situation.
2. **Prove tracking** (skill: `google-conversion-tracking-check`). Only business outcomes primary, counted once, with values; enhanced conversions on; consent mode working for regions that need it; offline stages planned for lead businesses. Place a real test conversion.
3. **Choose the campaign mix.**
   - Brand Search: protects the name, cheap, reported apart.
   - Non-brand Search: themed ad groups by intent, two or more RSAs each, landing pages that match.
   - Shopping or Performance Max with a feed (retail): decide which owns product traffic; apply brand exclusions in Performance Max if brand Search runs.
   - Demand Gen: only once there is budget beyond capturing existing demand and creative to support it; Google recommends at least ten times the target CPA as daily budget when using Target CPA.
4. **Keywords and match types.** Start with exact and phrase on proven terms, broad match or AI Max only alongside Smart Bidding and a negatives list. Build account-level negatives (jobs, free, irrelevant products) before launch.
5. **Feed** (retail). Merchant Center diagnostics clean for top sellers; titles lead with what people search; GTINs present; price and availability match the site.
6. **Bid strategy** (skill: `smart-bidding-strategy`). New campaigns with no history: Maximize conversions or clicks; move to targets once volume allows.
7. **Budgets.** Explain to the client that daily budgets are averages (up to twice on a given day, capped at 30.4 times monthly).
8. **Ads and assets** (prompt: `responsive-search-ad-writer`). Sitelinks and other assets in place; Performance Max asset groups with images, logos, text and video (vertical included).
9. **Build it paused, then review settings** (level 2). Build the campaigns paused, or prepare them in Ads Editor unposted, or hand over a build sheet at level 1. Check locations (presence versus interest), languages, networks (search partners on or off by choice), schedule, final URL expansion and text customisation choices, conversion goals used by each campaign, tracking templates tested.
10. **Client sign-off.** Launch plan with structure, budget, bidding, what we judge and when, and the level 3 limits (accounts, budget cap, step size, kinds of change, end date). Written approval. At level 3 this is the one-time confirmation before going live.
11. **Launch and hold** (level 3, or the client publishes at level 2). Check within 24 hours: ads approved, spend starting, conversions recording. Then avoid changes for one to two weeks except to fix errors or add obvious negatives (inside the agreed limits, or with a yes).
12. **First read** at day 7 (workflow: `weekly-optimisation-routine`).

## Outputs

- Launch plan with the client's approval.
- Tracking test record.
- Keyword, negative and ad copy sheets.
- Change log started.

## Approval

- The launch plan, budget, dates and level 3 limits. At level 3, this yes covers publishing the campaigns exactly as planned.
- Bidding on competitor terms, every time.
- AI Max, final URL expansion and text customisation choices.
- Anything that differs from the approved plan, such as a new audience, more spend than the cap or a different conversion goal, needs a new yes.
