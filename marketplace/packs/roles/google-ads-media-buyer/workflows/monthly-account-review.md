# Monthly account review

**Cadence:** once a month, plus after any big change on the site, tracking or Google's platform.

**Goal:** step back from weekly tuning, check the account against the business, and plan the next month's tests.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 7 need level 1 (read). Step 8 needs level 3, or level 2 if the client publishes.

1. **Light audit** (skill: `google-ads-account-audit`, sections 2 to 9). Full audit every quarter.
2. **Reconcile the month.** Google Ads conversions and value against shop or CRM; brand versus non-brand profit; revenue against total marketing spend alongside platform ROAS.
3. **Performance Max and feed** (prompt: `performance-max-review`). Channel mix, brand share, asset groups, feed fixes.
4. **Bidding review** (skill: `smart-bidding-strategy`). Targets against the last 30 days and break-even; budget-limited target campaigns; candidates for Smart Bidding Exploration ahead of peak seasons.
5. **Check what changed at Google** (knowledge.md section 10). AI Max, Performance Max controls, bidding changes, Data Manager, consent mode, Demand Gen and Display migration, Merchant Center rules. Note anything that affects this account.
6. **Plan experiments.** One question for next month, for example "does AI Max add profitable conversions on non-brand?", run as a custom experiment with a written decision rule.
7. **Monthly report.** What worked, what did not, what we learned, next month's plan and decisions needed, plus the budget cap and limits for next month.
8. **Make the agreed changes** (level 3 within limits; at level 2, built paused or left unposted in Ads Editor for the client). Set up an approved experiment the same way. Log each change.

## Outputs

- Monthly report.
- Experiment plan.
- Change request for approval.
- Updated change log.

## Approval

- Every item on the change request that falls outside the current level 3 limits. Items inside them are made and logged.
- Starting any experiment, and applying its result, every time.
- Next month's budget cap, targets and limits.
