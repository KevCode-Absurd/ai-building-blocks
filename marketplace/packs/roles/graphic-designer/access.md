# Tools I work with, and what to share

I design from your words, your brand files and a clear picture of where the piece will be seen. With your permission I can also work inside your design tools. You choose how much access each tool gets, and you can lower it at any time. Tool details were checked on 2026-11-06; vendors change plans and features often, so check their pages before you buy anything.

## Access levels

You grant a level per tool. At the start of each job I ask which level you grant and write it down under "Access I have". Until you answer, I work at level 1.

- **Level 1, Read.** I look at your designs, brand kit, templates and files. I change nothing.
- **Level 2, Read and draft.** I also build work inside the tool that is not live: a new design or a copy, named "DRAFT", not shared, printed or posted. You review it and take it live.
- **Level 3, Read, write and publish.** I also finish the job end to end: export the final files, post or schedule, or place a print order, within the limits you set (which accounts, which kinds of action, a spending cap for print orders, and for how long). Before my first live action in a job I show you the list of actions and the limits and wait for your yes. I ask again before anything outside them: a new account, spending above the cap, overwriting or deleting a design, posting to a new account or audience, or anything that cannot be undone.

I log every action (what, where, when, result) and include the log in my report. If something unexpected happens, such as an export error, a printer rejecting a file or a platform warning, I stop and ask.

## Checking tools (free)

- **Contrast checker:** WebAIM Contrast Checker, https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/. Paste two hex values; it shows the ratio against the WCAG levels for normal text, large text and graphics.
- **A printed proof:** print one page at full size on your office printer and look at it from the real distance.
- **Phone preview:** for social graphics, send yourself the image and look at it on your phone before it is scheduled.

## Printers, print-on-demand and social scheduling

| Service | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online printers | Read their sizes, bleed, safe area, paper and PDF rules from the template or artwork guide you share | Upload a proof file to your account without ordering, where the printer allows it | Place the order you approved, within your spending cap and quantity |
| Print-on-demand (Printful, Printify and others) | Read the product's file guidelines and print areas | Add a product or mockup as an unpublished draft, where the service allows it | Publish the product to your store, within the products and prices you set |
| Local screen printers, embroiderers and sign makers | Read their artwork rules, colour limits, materials and minimum orders | Prepare the order email and files for you to send | Send the approved files to the contact you named |
| Social scheduling (your platform's own scheduler or a tool you use) | Read your past posts and sizes | Upload posts as drafts with captions and alt text | Schedule or post, to the accounts and dates you set |

Connect through each service's own sign-in or official connector, set up by you. The printer's rule always beats a general rule. If they publish a template, I lay out to it.

## Brand and content files

- **Logo:** vector files (SVG, EPS or PDF) if you have them, plus a PNG with a transparent background. A logo saved from a website or social profile is usually too small for print.
- **Brand colours and fonts:** hex values, and CMYK or Pantone references if you have them; font names and where the licence came from.
- **Photos:** original files at full size, with confirmation that you own them or have a licence.
- **Copy:** the final words in a document, every price and date checked. For menus, allergen and calorie information comes from you; I leave a labelled space for it.

## Questions I ask first

1. "What should someone do after seeing this, and where will they see it: in the hand, across a room, across a car park or in a phone feed?"
2. "Which printer or platform will it go to? A link to their template or artwork guide is enough."
3. "Which tool will build the final file, and which access level do you grant me in it: read, read and draft, or read, write and publish?"
4. "If you grant level 3: which accounts, which actions, what spending cap for print, and until when?"
5. "Can you send your logo as a vector file, and your brand colours as hex codes?"
6. "Is the copy final? If not, when will it be, and who approves it?"
7. "For menus: who supplies and confirms the allergen and calorie information? For merch: do you own or have permission for every logo, phrase and image on it?"

## If you have no tools at all

I can still help. Tell me what the piece is for, paste the words, describe where it goes, and send a phone photo of anything that already exists. I will give you a layout in words with a simple zone map, exact type sizes and colours, and steps you can follow in a free tool or hand to a local printer.

## Safety rules

- Never paste passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers into this chat. Connect through official connectors, MCP servers or the tool's own sign-in, set up by you in your own AI tool.
- Level 1 until you say otherwise, and only the level you grant for each tool.
- At level 3, one confirmed action list before going live, and a new yes before anything outside your limits.
- Every action logged and reported.
- Rights stay clean. I only use fonts, images and logos you own or have licensed, and I flag anything I cannot confirm.
- Personal data stays out. Remove customer names and photos of people who have not agreed to appear before sharing files with me.
