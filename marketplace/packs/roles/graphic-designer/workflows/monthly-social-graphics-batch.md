# Monthly social graphics batch

**Cadence:** once a month, about two weeks before the month starts, for a client who posts regularly and wants a consistent feed.

## Steps

1. **Access check.** Confirm the access level in the design tool (for example Canva) and the scheduler or social account: level 1 (read), level 2 (read and draft) or level 3 (read, write and publish, with limits on accounts, dates and post types). Record it as "Access I have". Until the client answers, level 1.
2. **Review last month (level 1).** Read last month's posts and results the client shares or that I can see at level 1: which posts were saved, shared or clicked most, and any comments saying something was hard to read.
3. **Collect the content.** The client's topics, offers, dates and any posts or notes to turn into graphics, in their words. List anything missing with a default.
4. **Check sizes (skill: social-graphics-spec, step 1).** Open each platform's help page and record the current sizes and safe zones with the date checked.
5. **Spec the batch (skill: social-graphics-spec).** Each post: job, format, slide-by-slide text with character counts, layout, colours, alt text and caption. Reuse the client's template kit; flag any template that no longer fits the current sizes.
6. **Check legibility (skill: contrast-and-legibility-check).** Every text and background pair measured; colour never the only cue; words inside safe zones; previewed on a phone.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the batch: posts, words, captions and dates.
7. **Build.**
   - Level 1: deliver build notes (prompt: handoff-build-notes); the client's team builds.
   - Level 2: build every post in the client's design tool as "DRAFT" designs, and upload them to the scheduler as drafts with captions and alt text. Nothing is published. Log each item.
8. **Schedule.**
   - Level 1 or 2: the client schedules or approves the drafts in their tool.
   - Level 3: show the action list (each post, account, date and time) and the limits, and get a yes; then schedule within them. Ask again before posting to a new account, moving a post outside the agreed dates, deleting a post or replying to comments if that was not in the limits. Log each action.
9. **Watch and report.** After posting, stop and tell the client if a post is removed, flagged or gets complaints about readability. At month end, report what was posted, the action log, and the three posts that worked best, with what to repeat.

## Outputs

- Current size and safe-zone notes with the date checked.
- The monthly spec: every post with text, layout, colours, alt text and caption.
- Contrast table for the batch.
- Draft designs and scheduler drafts (level 2) or scheduled posts (level 3).
- Action log and month-end report.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level per tool and, for level 3, the accounts, dates, post types and time window.
- The batch content, words and dates (point 1).
- Any claim, number, review or before-and-after image, which the client must supply and be able to prove.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits, asked again each time.
