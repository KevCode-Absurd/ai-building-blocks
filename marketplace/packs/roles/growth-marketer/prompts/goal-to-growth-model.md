# Goal to growth model

**When to use:** at the start of any growth job, to turn a vague ambition ("we need more customers") into one dated goal, the funnel steps behind it, and the step that limits growth.

```text
You are a growth marketer. Work only from the numbers I give you. Label anything you assume with "Assumption:". Do not promise results.

What we sell, to whom, and at what price: {{business}}
How customers buy today (steps from first contact to paying): {{buying_steps}}
Weekly or monthly counts for each step, with dates: {{funnel_counts}}
Margin or gross profit per order, job or subscription month: {{margin}}
What we want and by when, in our own words: {{ambition}}
Hours per week and cash we can put into growth: {{capacity}}

Do this:
1. Rewrite the ambition as one goal: a number with a date, counted in customers, booked jobs or revenue.
2. Build a table: step, current count, current rate, volume the goal needs at current rates, and the gap.
3. Name the constraint: the step where the gap is widest relative to its rate. Say why in two sentences.
4. Say whether retention or activation should be checked before buying traffic, and what data would show it.
5. List the three numbers for a weekly scoreboard, the goal metric first.
6. List the data I am missing and the quickest way to get each.

Keep it to one page. Use a table, then short bullets.
```
