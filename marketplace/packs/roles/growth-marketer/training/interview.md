# Interview: growth marketer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example, with the numbers?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first two weeks with your most recent client, from the first call to the first test going live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you turn "we need more customers" into a goal? Give me the last goal you set and how you picked the number and date.**
   Why it matters: goal-setting is where most plans go wrong.
3. **How do you decide what limits growth right now: traffic, activation, conversion or retention? What data do you look at first?**
   Why it matters: captures their diagnosis order.
4. **How have you found an activation moment in practice? What did the analysis look like, and was it right?**
   Why it matters: activation work separates growth practice from campaign management.
5. **How do you choose between channels when the client wants to do everything?**
   Why it matters: shows how they cut scope and handle pressure.

## Numbers and judgement

6. **Show me how you work out what a client can afford to pay for a customer. Which inputs do clients usually get wrong?**
   Why it matters: unit economics set every pass and stop mark.
7. **When do you refuse to A/B test something, and what do you do instead?**
   Why it matters: low-volume judgement is the norm for small businesses.
8. **Tell me about a test result that looked great and turned out to be wrong. How did you find out?**
   Why it matters: captures their trust checks (bugs, sample ratio, tracking).
9. **How do you decide whether a channel really added customers, rather than taking credit for them?**
   Why it matters: incrementality thinking is rare and valuable.
10. **What do you do when the ad platforms, analytics and the bank account all disagree?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's reconciliation habit.

## Tools and access

11. **Which tools do you use for analytics, product data, testing and reporting, and what access do you ask clients for at the start?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what works.
12. **When a client gives you access to make live changes, what limits do you agree, and how do you log what you did?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent behaves at level 3.
13. **How do you handle consent banners and privacy rules for clients in the EU, UK or California?**
    Why it matters: shows where they draw the line and when they send the client to an adviser.

## Rhythm and reporting

14. **What does your weekly growth review look like? Paste a real one with the numbers changed.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a real format and standard.
15. **How do you keep a learning log, and when have you used an old test to stop a bad idea?**
    Why it matters: learning memory is what makes a growth team faster over time.
16. **How do you tell a client a channel they love is not working?**
    Why it matters: captures their voice in hard conversations.

## Mistakes, clients and stories

17. **What are the three mistakes you see most in growth plans written by founders, agencies or AI?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
18. **What kind of client or project do you turn down, and why?**
    Why it matters: defines what the agent is not for.
19. **Tell me about the hardest growth job you have done. What was stuck, what did you try, what finally worked?**
    Why it matters: a real story shows judgement better than any rule.
20. **Which kinds of business do you know best (software, shops, services, local, coaches), and what is different about growing each?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty".
21. **What words or claims do you never put in a growth plan or report?**
    Why it matters: builds the "words I avoid" list in personality.md.
