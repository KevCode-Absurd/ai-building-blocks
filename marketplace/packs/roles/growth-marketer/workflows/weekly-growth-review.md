# Weekly growth review

**Cadence:** every week, same day. Thirty to sixty minutes of meeting, plus about two hours of preparation. Growth leads using the Traction pack spread the same loop across the week with `workflows/weekly-traction-rhythm.md`.

**Trigger:** at least one growth test is live, or the scoreboard has been agreed.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits). Until the client grants more, everything runs at L1 and the client does the L2 and L3 steps.

## Steps

1. **Pull the numbers (tools: GA4, product analytics, CRM, store or billing, ad accounts), L1.** Export the scoreboard metrics and each live test's results for the week and the four weeks before. Pull backend totals (orders, bookings, new paid customers) as the anchor.
2. **Check the action log, L1.** Read last week's logged actions and their results. Anything unexpected (errors, spend spikes, disapprovals, policy warnings) goes to the top of the memo, and any live action involved is paused only within level 3 limits or flagged to the client.
3. **Reconcile (prompt: attribution-reconciliation, light version), L1.** Compare what channels claim with backend totals. Note gaps; do not adjust the scoreboard to match a platform.
4. **Call each test (skill: growth-experiment-design, step 10; Traction test card for channel bets), L1.** For each live test: result against pass and stop marks; keep, change one thing, or stop; "unclear" when volume is too low, with the volume that would settle it. Check sample ratio and tracking before reading any controlled experiment.
5. **Update the learning log, L1.** One line per closed test: what we believed, what happened, what we now think.
6. **Pick next tests (prompt: experiment-card-and-sizing), L1.** Take the top of the ICE-ranked backlog, size it, and write the card.
7. **Write the memo (prompt: weekly-growth-review-memo), L1.** One page: scoreboard, test calls, reconciliation note, learning log lines, next tests, approvals.
8. **Prepare the work, L2.** Build approved changes and new tests in the client's tools without starting them: paused campaigns, unsent emails, draft pages, unstarted experiments.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the memo's calls, the new test cards and the drafts.
9. **Go live, L3 or the client.** At L3, show the week's action list and limits, get one yes, then start, stop or change tests within the limits and log every action. Anything outside the limits (a new audience, spend above the cap, a price or offer change customers will see, deleting anything) needs a fresh yes. At L1 and L2, the client publishes.
10. **Every fourth week:** re-check unit economics with the latest cohort data (skill: unit-economics-and-cac-limit) and re-rank the backlog. Growth leads also re-rank the 19 channels in the Traction pack.

## Outputs

- One-page weekly growth review memo.
- Updated scoreboard and test board.
- Learning log lines.
- New test cards with sizing.
- Drafts ready in the client's tools (L2), or the list of changes for the client.
- Action log entries for every action taken (what, where, when, result).

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits for each tool, at the start and whenever they change.
- Each week's test calls and new test cards (approval point 1).
- Every draft before it goes live: at L2 the client publishes it; at L3 the client confirms the action list once before the first live action of the week.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits, before it happens.
- Any change to prices, offers, guarantees or deadlines customers will see.
