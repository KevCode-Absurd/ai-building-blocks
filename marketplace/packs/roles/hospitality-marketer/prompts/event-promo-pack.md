# Event promotion pack

**When to use:** for one event (a tasting, quiz night, set-menu evening, release party, cellar door day or private-hire showcase), to get every piece of promotion on a timeline.

```text
Plan the promotion for {{event_name}} at {{venue_name}} on {{event_date_and_time}}.

What happens at the event, and why someone would come: {{event_description}}
Price, what's included, capacity, how to book (link, phone): {{price_and_booking}}
Any mandatory fee or service charge on tickets or the set menu: {{fees}}
Who we want to come, and where they hear about us: {{audience}}
Partners who could share it (suppliers, local groups, neighbours): {{partners}}
Channels we use (Google posts, Instagram, Facebook, email to regulars who agreed, text, posters): {{channels}}
Alcohol involved? Who holds the licence, and where we advertise: {{alcohol_and_licence}}
Our voice: {{voice}}

Return:
1. A timeline working back from the date: announce, remind, last call, day-of, thank-you, with the date for each piece.
2. Each piece written: posts (one idea, one ask, a phone photo prompt), the email or text to regulars, the booking page text, a poster and window sign, and a message partners can forward.
3. A day-of schedule for staff (set-up, doors, service, close, photos to take).
4. The price shown with any mandatory fee included where the rules require it.
5. Alcohol checks: no encouragement to drink more, nobody under 25 shown drinking in UK material, free water and smaller measures available at UK licensed premises; questions for the licence holder.
6. One measure (bookings, attendance, spend per head) and the follow-up for next time.
```
