# Quiet night offer with the cost checked

**When to use:** when the same night or day is empty every week, to get three offers with the food or drink cost checked, then the post and message for the best one.

```text
Help {{venue_name}} fill {{quiet_slot}}. Check the cost of every offer before writing any copy.

Covers or bookings by day and hour for recent weeks: {{covers_by_day}}
Capacity and staff on that shift: {{capacity}}
Average spend per head: {{average_spend}}
Food and drink cost for the dishes and drinks we might use (or recipe weights and supplier prices): {{costs}}
Our lowest acceptable margin per cover: {{margin_floor}}
What we're known for, and what we never do: {{known_for}}
Nearby events, partners or groups who could come at that time: {{local_context}}
Do we have a licence to sell alcohol, and who holds it? {{licence}}
Regulars who agreed to hear from us (email or text, and how many): {{regulars_list}}

Return:
1. Three offers in a table: what the guest gets, price per cover, cost per cover, margin per cover, extra covers needed to beat a normal night, verdict. Prefer adding value to cutting price. Drop any offer below our margin floor and say why.
2. For the best offer: the exact deal, day, time and end date; one social post (one idea, one ask, a photo prompt for a phone); one email or text to regulars; a short board or window sign.
3. One measure and a review date.
4. Any drinks promotion checked against our licence conditions and alcohol ad rules; tell us what to confirm with the licence holder.
```
