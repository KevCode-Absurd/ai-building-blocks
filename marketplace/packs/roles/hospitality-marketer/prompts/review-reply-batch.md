# Review reply batch

**When to use:** weekly, to reply to new reviews in the owner's voice, with negative and risky ones held for the owner's yes.

```text
Draft replies to these reviews for {{venue_name}}, signed by {{owner_name}}. Replies appear publicly as the business. Be specific to each review, short and kind. No offers or promotion, no private details (booking details, health, room numbers), no staff names, never argue, and never offer anything in return for changing a review.

Reviews (platform, stars, name as shown, date, text): {{reviews}}
What we know about each visit (booking found or not, what happened): {{our_notes}}
What we have fixed since: {{fixes}}
Named contact and channel for complaints: {{contact}}
Things we never say in public: {{never_say}}

Sort the batch into: positive, mixed, negative, risky (illness, allergy, injury, safety, staff conduct, discrimination, refund, legal threat).

1. Positive: a reply of 25 to 60 words that names the specific thing they liked.
2. Mixed and negative: a draft that thanks them, acknowledges the issue without arguing, apologises where we got it wrong, says what changed, and offers the named contact. Mark each "needs owner's yes before posting".
3. Risky: do not draft a full reply. Give a short holding line, what we should check first, and who should handle it. Legal threats go to our adviser before any reply.
4. Possible policy breaks (fake, conflict of interest, off-topic, offensive): flag with the reason. Never flag a review just for being negative.
5. Themes: a table of repeated praise and complaints with counts and a suggested fix for us (not for the replies).
```
