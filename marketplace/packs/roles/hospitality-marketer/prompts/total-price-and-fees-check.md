# Total price and fees check

**When to use:** before publishing room rates, menus, event tickets or ads, to check that mandatory fees, service charges and resort fees are shown the way the rules expect.

```text
Check how {{venue_name}} shows prices. This is general practice, not legal advice; mark anything uncertain for our adviser.

Where we sell and advertise (countries, US states): {{markets}}
What we sell: {{products}} (for example hotel rooms, restaurant meals, event tickets, private hire)
Every fee or charge we add, and whether each is optional or mandatory: {{fees}}
How prices appear now (paste menu lines, room rate pages, ads, posts, checkout screens): {{current_price_displays}}
Do we sell rooms through Google free booking links or hotel ads? {{google_hotel}}

Use these rules as of November 2026, and tell me to re-check the official pages before relying on them:
- US short-term lodging and event tickets (FTC fees rule, from 12 May 2025): total price including mandatory fees such as resort fees shown upfront and most prominently; only government charges, shipping and optional add-ons left out; ads with a price show the total.
- California restaurants and bars: a mandatory fee must be clearly shown wherever prices appear.
- UK (CMA price transparency guidance): prices in ads, posts, emails and web pages include mandatory charges, including mandatory cover or service charges at a restaurant, cleaning, booking and resort fees and local taxes payable at a hotel; optional charges disclosed but may be shown separately; amounts paid on arrival shown separately.
- EU: total price including all taxes and charges; no pre-ticked extras.
- Google hotel prices: the booking page total must match Google's total, including mandatory fees.

Return a table: where the price appears, what it shows now, the rule that applies, the problem if any, suggested wording, question for our adviser.
```
