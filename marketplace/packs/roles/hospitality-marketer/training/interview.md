# Interview: hospitality marketing manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your first week with a new restaurant, bar, taproom or hotel client.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and order of work.
2. **What three numbers do you ask an owner for before you write anything?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's intake list.
3. **How do you find a place's voice when the owner can't describe it?**
   Why it matters: captures how they make copy sound like the venue.
4. **How do you write a menu line that sells a simple dish without overselling it?**
   Why it matters: the core writing skill of the role.

## Offers and events

5. **How do you decide which quiet night to fix first?**
   Why it matters: shows how they read the week's rhythm.
6. **Tell me about an offer that filled the room but lost money. What did you change?**
   Why it matters: captures their cost-check habit.
7. **What makes an event worth repeating, and how do you measure it?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's measure for events.
8. **How do you sell a taproom, cellar door or tasting on a weekday?**
   Why it matters: drinks makers' biggest marketing problem.

## Listings, prices and bookings

9. **How do you keep menus, hours and prices the same on Google, booking sites and delivery apps?**
   Why it matters: becomes the change routine.
10. **How do you handle service charges, cover charges or resort fees in marketing copy?**
    Why it matters: tests current knowledge of price display rules.
11. **For hotels: how do you balance booking sites against direct bookings?**
    Why it matters: shows how they think about commission and contracts.

## Reviews

12. **What do you say in public to a one-star review you think is unfair?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's tone for hard replies.
13. **Which reviews do you never answer without the owner, and why?**
    Why it matters: becomes the fresh-yes list.
14. **How do you get more reviews without breaking platform rules?**
    Why it matters: tests honest-asking practice.

## Rules and safety

15. **How do you handle allergen information when you write a menu?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's allergen standard.
16. **Which alcohol marketing rules have tripped up a client, and how did you fix it?**
    Why it matters: real cases for the alcohol checks.
17. **What would you refuse to write, even if the owner insisted?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's pushback lines.

## Tools, quality and clients

18. **Which booking, till and email tools do you prefer, and what do you need from each?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's tool advice.
19. **What do you check before any menu, post or offer goes out?**
    Why it matters: becomes the quality bar.
20. **Tell me about the hardest hospitality job you've done. What went wrong, and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: the story the coach turns into test briefs.
