# Event or launch countdown

**Trigger:** the owner sets a date for an event, a new menu, a drinks release or a private-hire showcase. Start about four weeks before for an event, six for a seasonal menu or release.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits).

## Steps

1. **Brief, L1.** What happens, why someone would come, price, capacity, booking route, fees, partners, alcohol and licence holder, and the measure. Write down "Access I have".
2. **Cost check (skill: quiet-slot-offers-and-events; prompt: quiet-night-offer), L1.** Price, cost per cover or per ticket, margin and break-even attendance. Drop or change anything that loses money unless the owner chooses it.
3. **Copy (skill: menu-and-drinks-copy; prompts: menu-descriptions or tasting-notes-and-trade-sheet), L1 then L2.** Menu lines or tasting notes with allergen lines from the kitchen's records, and alcohol copy checked for where it runs.
4. **Promotion pack (prompt: event-promo-pack), L2.** Timeline, posts, email or text to regulars, booking page, poster, partner message, day-of schedule. Prices include any mandatory fee where required (prompt: total-price-and-fees-check).
5. **Owner review.** **Approval point:** the owner approves the price, the copy, the allergen and alcohol lines and the go-live list (each post, send and page with its date). One yes covers the agreed list at L3.
6. **Listings update, L3 within limits (skill: listings-prices-and-bookings).** Event or new menu on Google, the website, booking sites and delivery apps the same day; booking link live.
7. **Announce, remind, last call, L3 within limits.** On the agreed dates. Watch bookings after each; tell the owner if bookings lag the plan so they can decide on a change.
8. **Day-of, L3 within limits.** The day-of post and staff schedule; photos for the thank-you (with guests' agreement if they are identifiable).
9. **Thank-you and review, L2 or L3.** Thank-you post and message to attendees who agreed to hear from the venue, with the next date. Results against the measure: bookings, attendance, spend per head, new names on the list, reviews mentioning the event.

## Outputs

- Cost check and verdict.
- Copy, promotion pack and day-of schedule.
- Listing updates and booking link.
- Action log.
- Results note with what to repeat or change.

## Needs the client's approval

- The price, copy and go-live list before anything goes out.
- A fresh yes for any change to price, fees or offer after launch, any send to a new audience, and any private-hire or group contract sent as binding.
- Replies to negative reviews about the event, each with a fresh yes.
- Deposits, refunds above the owner's limit and payouts stay with the owner.
