# Menu or price change rollout

**Trigger:** a new or seasonal menu, a price rise, a new fee or service charge, or new opening hours.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits).

## Steps

1. **Collect the change, L1.** The new menu with ingredients and prices, the kitchen's updated allergen records, calorie figures where required, any fee or service charge (optional or mandatory), the start date and new hours.
2. **Write the copy (skill: menu-and-drinks-copy; prompt: menu-descriptions), L2.** Dish lines, header line, allergen notice and lines from the records; gaps marked for the kitchen.
3. **Check prices and fees (prompt: total-price-and-fees-check), L1.** Mandatory charges shown where the rules require, for each country or state.
4. **Owner and kitchen review.** **Approval point:** the owner approves prices, fees and copy; the person responsible for food safety confirms every allergen line. A new price or fee guests will see needs a fresh yes.
5. **List every place to update (skill: listings-prices-and-bookings), L1.** Till, website, Google menu editor or menu link, Reserve with Google or booking-tool experiences, booking sites, delivery apps, printed menus and boards, social profile links.
6. **Update in order on the start date, L3 within limits.** Till first, then website, Google, booking tools, booking sites and delivery apps the same day; remove or flag outdated menu photos. Log each change.
7. **Check after 48 hours, L1.** Google menu changes can take 24 to 48 hours to show; confirm every listing shows the new menu, prices and hours. For hotels, check the price accuracy score after any rate or fee change.
8. **Tell guests, L2 or L3.** A post and a message to regulars who agreed, if the change is worth announcing.

## Outputs

- New menu copy with allergen and calorie lines from the records.
- Price and fee check.
- Update checklist with a log of each change.
- 48-hour verification note.
- Announcement post and message, if wanted.

## Needs the client's approval

- New prices, fees and service charges (fresh yes).
- Every allergen line, confirmed by the person responsible for food safety.
- The announcement before it goes out, unless already in the limits.
- Delivery-app prices, commissions and payouts stay with the owner.
