# Weekly venue marketing routine

**Cadence:** weekly, about 20 to 30 minutes of the owner's time, plus a monthly look-back in the first week of each month.

**Trigger:** listings are fixed (skill: listings-prices-and-bookings) and the owner has granted access levels for each tool.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Until the owner grants more, everything runs at L1 and the owner posts and replies.

## Steps

1. **Read last week, L1 (Monday).** Covers, bookings or room nights by day against the same week last year; offer redemptions; event bookings. Note the quiet slots for next week.
2. **Read the action log, L1.** Last week's posts, sends, replies and listing changes with results. Errors, rejected posts, price accuracy warnings or booking-site notices go to the top of the note.
3. **Reviews (skill: guest-review-replies; prompt: review-reply-batch), L2 or L3.** Reply to positive reviews within limits; put negative and risky reviews in the owner's queue with drafts. **Approval point:** each negative or risky reply needs the owner's fresh yes.
4. **Listings check, L1 then L3 within limits.** Hours (especially holidays and private-hire closures), menu, prices and booking links on Google, booking sites and delivery apps. Fix within limits; anything new that guests will pay goes to the owner.
5. **This week's posts and messages (skill: quiet-slot-offers-and-events), L2 or L3.** Two or three posts built around the week (specials, events, behind the scenes, people) and one email or text to regulars who agreed, if planned. At L3, publish on the agreed days.
6. **Weekly note, L1.** Five lines: covers or bookings against last year, reviews and rating, what was posted and sent, what needs the owner's yes, next week's plan.

**Monthly (first week):**

7. **Offer review, L1.** Keep, change or drop each running offer by its measure.
8. **Review themes, L1.** Repeated praise and complaints across platforms, passed to the owner as fixes.
9. **Next month's plan, L1 then L2.** Quiet slots, events, launches and holidays; drafts for approval.

## Outputs

- Weekly five-line note.
- Review replies posted (positives) and the owner's queue (negative and risky).
- Listing fixes logged.
- Posts and messages published or drafted.
- Monthly offer review, review themes and next month's plan.
- Action log entries (what, where, when, result).

## Needs the client's approval

- Each reply to a negative or risky review (fresh yes every time).
- Any new price, fee, service charge or offer guests will see, unless already in the limits.
- Messages to any guest group outside the limits, and any spend above the cap.
- Payouts, refunds above the owner's limit and bank details stay with the owner.
