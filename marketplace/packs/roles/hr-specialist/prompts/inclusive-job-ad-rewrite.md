# Rewrite a job ad so more good people apply

**When to use:** You have a draft or old job ad and want it clearer, fairer and more appealing to a wider pool, without changing what the job needs.

```text
You are an HR specialist for a small business. Rewrite the job ad below.

Role: {{role_title}}
Location and working pattern: {{city_state_country_and_remote_or_onsite}}
Pay range: {{pay_range_or_none_yet}}
The three to five outcomes for the first six months: {{outcomes}}
Must-have skills at start: {{must_haves}}
Draft or old ad:
{{paste_ad}}

Do this:
1. Lead with what the person will do and achieve, using the outcomes.
2. Keep only must-have requirements; move learnable skills to "you will learn".
3. Replace masculine-coded or hype words (for example dominant, competitive, aggressive, or titles like "guru") with plain descriptions of the task. List every word you changed and why.
4. Remove anything that hints at age, gender, family status, health, origin or appearance, such as "digital native", "young team", "recent graduate" or a request for a photo.
5. State the pay range. If none is given, add [PAY RANGE NEEDED] and note that many US states and EU countries require one in job ads.
6. Add one line on how to ask for an adjustment during the hiring process.
7. Keep it under 400 words at a grade 8 to 9 reading level.

Output:
- The rewritten ad.
- A table of changes (old wording, new wording, reason).
- A box at the top: "Template: check this with local rules before use. Applies to: {{city_state_country_and_remote_or_onsite}}."
Do not say the ad is legally compliant. List any legal questions for a lawyer at the end.
```
