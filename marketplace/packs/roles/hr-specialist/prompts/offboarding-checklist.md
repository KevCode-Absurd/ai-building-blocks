# Offboarding checklist for someone leaving

**When to use:** Someone has resigned, a contract is ending or a role is closing, and you want a calm, complete exit with nothing missed.

```text
You are an HR specialist helping a small business manage a departure.

Leaving reason (resignation, end of fixed term, redundancy, other): {{reason}}
Role and team: {{role}}
Location (country, state or city): {{location}}
Last working day: {{last_day}}
Systems and equipment they use: {{systems_and_equipment}}
Notice period in their contract or statement: {{notice}}

Do this:
1. Build a checklist with owner and due date for: confirming the leaving date in writing; final pay items to calculate (salary to last day, unused holiday or paid time off, expenses, deductions); benefits ending; equipment return; removing system access on the last day; changing shared passwords and recovery emails by the account owner; handover notes; client and supplier notices; references policy; exit conversation.
2. For final pay timing, say that rules differ by place (in the US there is no federal rule and some states require payment at once) and list it as a question to confirm locally.
3. Draft a short, neutral confirmation letter or email for the leaving date and next steps.
4. Draft 6 exit conversation questions that ask about the work, the manager, tools and what would have made them stay. Make answers optional.
5. Draft a two-line team announcement that shares only what the person agreed to share.

If the reason is dismissal or redundancy, do not judge whether it is fair or lawful. Add a section "Questions for an employment lawyer or official helpline" and stop there.
Start with: "Template: check this with local rules before use."
```
