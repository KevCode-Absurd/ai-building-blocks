# Training interview: HR specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names or personal details of real staff or candidates; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of businesses and team sizes do you know best, and in which countries or states?**
   Why it matters: location and size decide which rules and habits apply, and set the limits of the agent's advice.

2. **Walk me through the last hire you ran, from "we need someone" to their first month.**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence, the shortcuts taken and where the expert adds the most value.

3. **How do you turn a vague "we need help" into a job description?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's method for defining outcomes before writing anything.

4. **What are your go-to interview questions, and how do you score the answers?**
   Why it matters: pulls out real questions and scoring guides the agent can reuse.

5. **When have you used a work sample or trial task, and how did you keep it fair to candidates?**
   Why it matters: work samples predict well but are easy to make unfair; this captures the expert's limits.

6. **Tell me about a hire that went wrong. What did the process miss?**
   Why it matters: reveals the checks the expert now adds and the warning signs they look for.

7. **What do you put in the first week for a new starter, and what do you leave out?**
   Why it matters: turns onboarding theory into the expert's practical plan.

8. **How do you write a policy so people actually read and follow it?**
   Why it matters: captures structure, tone and roll-out habits for handbooks.

9. **Which policies do small businesses most often get wrong or forget?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a priority list for policy reviews.

10. **How do you help a manager give feedback that is hard to hear?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's script and how they keep feedback about the work.

11. **How do you stop reviews from being written from memory the night before?**
    Why it matters: captures evidence habits (logs, check-ins) the agent can build into templates.

12. **How do you set or check pay ranges with little budget for salary surveys?**
    Why it matters: shows which data sources the expert trusts and how they explain pay.

13. **How do you handle a request for pay transparency, or a law that requires ranges in job ads?**
    Why it matters: tests how the expert stays current and where they draw the line between practice and law.

14. **What do you ask in a staff survey, and what do you do with the results?**
    Why it matters: separates surveys that lead to action from ones that just measure.

15. **What records do you keep, where, and for how long? How do you decide?**
    Why it matters: captures data minimisation and retention habits, and which official sources the expert checks.

16. **When do you tell a client to call an employment lawyer or an official helpline instead of you?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's safety line in the expert's own words.

17. **Describe a hard exit you handled. What did you do before, during and after?**
    Why it matters: captures calm, respectful offboarding steps without legal judgements.

18. **How do you check your own documents for biased or exclusive language?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's inclusive-language checklist.

19. **Which HR tools have you set up or used, and what would you tell a small business to start with?**
    Why it matters: feeds access.md with the expert's real tool advice.

20. **What does a client say when they think HR is going well? What do they say when it is not?**
    Why it matters: defines the expert's quality bar in the client's words.

21. **What HR advice do you hear often that you think is wrong?**
    Why it matters: captures the myths the agent should correct.

22. **How do you keep up with law and practice changes, and how often?**
    Why it matters: sets the refresh habits and trusted sources for this agent.

23. **Tell me about a client who wanted something you would not do. How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert pushes back and what they offer instead.
