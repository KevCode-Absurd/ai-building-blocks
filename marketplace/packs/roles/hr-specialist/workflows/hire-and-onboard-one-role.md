# Hire and onboard one role

**Trigger:** The owner decides to fill a role (new or replacement). Runs once per role, usually over 4 to 10 weeks.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Steps marked level 2 or level 3 need that level in the named tool; at a lower level I hand you the work to enter yourself. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Define the job.** Agree three to five six-month outcomes with the hiring manager. Use the `structured-hiring-kit` skill, steps 1 and 2. *Approval: owner confirms outcomes and budget.*
2. **Set the pay range.** If no band exists, run the `pay-band-builder` prompt with named, dated market data. *Approval: owner signs off the range.*
3. **Write the job description and ad.** Use `structured-hiring-kit` step 3, then the `inclusive-job-ad-rewrite` prompt. Check which pay transparency and advertising rules apply to the location. At level 2 I save the job, scorecards and interview plan as drafts in the applicant tracker. *Approval: owner approves the final ad before it goes live.*
4. **Post and collect applications (level 3 in the ATS).** At level 3, once the owner has approved the ad, I publish it in the applicant tracker (for example Workable or Greenhouse) on the agreed boards. At a lower level, the owner or recruiter posts it.
5. **Screen on must-haves only.** Score each application against the must-have criteria with a short reason. Flag any AI screening tool in use and check local rules for it. *Approval: hiring manager confirms the shortlist.* At level 3 I then move the shortlisted candidates to the next stage.
6. **Interview and work sample.** Use the interview guide, scoring guides and work sample from the kit. Each interviewer scores alone before the debrief. At level 3 I can send the agreed invites and booking links to shortlisted candidates.
7. **Decide.** Run the decision meeting agenda from the kit. Record one paragraph of reasons per finalist. *Approval: hiring manager and owner make the decision; I only prepare the evidence.*
8. **Checks before offer.** List the steps the location requires (references, background check notices, right to work or eligibility steps), each linked to the official source. I do not give immigration advice. *Approval: owner confirms which checks to run.* Anything filed with a government body always needs a new yes.
9. **Offer and replies.** Draft the offer letter from the business's template and the replies to unsuccessful candidates (level 2: saved unsent in the ATS). *Approval: the offer always needs a new yes, and a lawyer reviews any new contract template.* At level 3, once the decision is made, I send the agreed replies to unsuccessful candidates.
10. **Onboard (level 2 or 3 in the HR system).** Run the `onboarding-plan` skill as soon as the offer is accepted. At level 2 I set up the onboarding tasks unassigned; at level 3 I assign them and book the 30, 60 and 90 day check-ins.
11. **Look back at 90 days.** Compare the hire's progress against the outcomes; update the kit for next time.

## Outputs

- Job description, job ad, interview guide, scoring guides, work sample, scorecards
- Shortlist with reasons, decision record
- Offer letter draft and candidate replies
- 90-day onboarding plan and check-in notes
- Action log of everything done in the ATS and HR system (what, where, when, result)
- Lessons for the next hire

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Before the first live action, I show the list (publish the approved ad, move candidates after each decision, send the agreed replies and invites, assign onboarding tasks, book check-ins) with its limits: which tools, which candidates or staff, which templates and until when. After your yes, I do these without asking each time and log each one.

**Always needs a new yes.** Outcomes and budget, the pay range, the final ad, the shortlist, the hiring decision, which checks to run, the offer, any pay detail, any message to a new group of people, deleting candidate records, and anything filed with a government body. People make every hiring decision. Every template carries: *Check this with local rules before use.*
