# Quarterly people review

**Cadence:** Every three months, plus a light monthly check. Takes about half a day of the owner's time per quarter for a team under 30.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Steps marked level 2 or level 3 need that level in the named tool; at a lower level I hand you the work to enter or send yourself. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Pulse survey (week 1).** Build the short survey from the `pulse-survey-design` prompt (level 2: saved unsent in the survey tool). At level 3, once the wording is approved, I send it to the agreed staff and send the reminders. Report only groups at or above the minimum size you set.
2. **Performance check-ins (weeks 1 to 3).** Managers use the `performance-review` skill: gather evidence, draft from evidence, hold growth conversations. Pay is not discussed in these meetings.
3. **Bias and consistency check (week 3).** Compare ratings and evidence across the team. Flag gaps for the owner.
4. **Policy questions log (week 3).** List the questions staff asked this quarter. Each repeat question is a missing or unclear rule; draft fixes with the `policy-template` skill (level 2: a draft copy or suggested edits). *Approval: owner approves any policy change before it is shared.* At level 3 I then publish the approved version.
5. **Records tidy (week 4).** Run the `hr-records-retention-schedule` prompt once a year and check the deletion routine each quarter. I list the records due for deletion and the accounts of people who left; deleting records and removing access always need a new yes, unless the owner has named them as a limit.
6. **Rules watch (week 4).** Check the "What changes fast" table in `knowledge.md` for the business's locations: new pay transparency laws, leave changes, AI hiring rules, record rules. List what changed and what the business may need to do, with the official link. *Approval: owner decides on actions; legal questions go to a lawyer.*
7. **Report back to staff (week 4).** Draft a "you said, we did" note with three actions, owners and dates. At level 3, once the owner approves it, I send it to the agreed staff list.
8. **Pay round (separate, once or twice a year).** Use the `pay-band-builder` prompt to check pay against bands. *Approval: owner sets any pay changes; each one needs a new yes before it is entered.*

## Outputs

- Survey results with themes and three actions
- Reviews written from evidence, next-quarter goals
- Consistency check notes for the owner
- Draft policy fixes
- Rules-watch note with official links
- Staff update message
- Action log of everything done in the survey tool, HR system and documents (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Before the first live action of the quarter, I show the list (send the approved survey and reminders, publish approved policy versions, send the approved staff update) with its limits: which tools, which staff lists, which documents and until when. After your yes, I do these and log each one.

**Always needs a new yes.** Survey wording and privacy rules, any policy change, any message to a new group, any pay change, deleting records, and any formal process. I do not decide on warnings or dismissals; those follow the disciplinary procedure with advice from an employment lawyer or official helpline.
