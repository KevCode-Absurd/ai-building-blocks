# A year of window themes

**When to use:** a shop wants twelve months of window themes tied to its sales dates, with props it can reuse and dates to change each window. Pairs with the window-and-display-plan skill for each month's build, and with the `retail-manager` kit's promotion-calendar-and-signs skill for offers.

```text
You are a visual merchandiser planning a year of shop window themes for a small independent shop.

Shop, what it sells and its best-selling ranges by season: {{shop_and_ranges}}
Window size (width, depth, height) and any step or back panel: {{window_size}}
The shop's promotions calendar or key sales dates, if any: {{sales_dates}}
Local moments (festivals, school terms, tourist season, sports, markets): {{local_moments}}
Props the shop owns, and storage space for props: {{owned_props}}
Yearly props budget: {{props_budget}}
Time and people available to change the window: {{change_time}}

Do this:
1. A table by month: theme in one line, the range it features, the goal (draw people in, launch, clear stock, gifts), change date, take-down date.
2. For each theme: the hero item or group, two or three colours, and the props, marking which are reused across months.
3. A props budget by quarter that stays within the yearly budget, buying reusable bases (risers, crates, plinths, neutral fabrics) early and small seasonal props late.
4. A change routine: how long each change takes and who does it, with a quick mid-month refresh (move the hero, swap one group) for long themes.
5. Safety notes that apply every month: nothing blocks the door or an exit, lights checked for heat on products and props, fabrics near lights fire-safety labelled, stacks stable.
6. Flag any month where a theme depends on an offer or a claim (a sale, "eco", "local", "handmade") so the shop checks it is true before the window goes in.

Rules: use only the shop's ranges and dates I gave; do not invent events or offers; mark gaps [CHECK].
```
