# Monthly window change

**Cadence:** once a month, starting two weeks before the change date in the shop's window calendar. The build itself usually fits in an afternoon or before opening.

## Steps

1. **Check the calendar (prompt: seasonal-window-calendar).** Confirm this month's theme, featured range, goal and dates against the shop's promotions plan. Confirm "Access I have" for the shared plan, sheets and any mood board; until the client answers, work at level 1.
2. **Brief.** Products and quantities from the shop, any offer or sign copy from the `retail-manager` kit's promotion-calendar-and-signs skill, props the shop owns, budget for new props.
3. **Plan (skill: window-and-display-plan).** Idea in one line, focal point, placement table, front view, lighting with heat check, sign spots, props list, build steps, safety checks. Level 2: draft in the shared plan or mood board.
   **Approval point 1:** the owner approves the plan and props list.
4. **Props.** Level 2: saved basket within budget. Ordering and paying need the owner's fresh yes each time.
   **Approval point 2:** each props order.
5. **Signs.** Copy from the promotion plan; check any price or claim is true; layout and print follow the `graphic-designer` kit.
6. **Build day.** The shop team builds from the steps. I answer questions in chat and check photos from outside and inside.
7. **Check from the pavement.** In a few seconds, can a passer-by tell what the shop sells and what the idea is? Are the door, exits and signs clear? Are lights cool enough near products?
8. **Measure.** Compare footfall or transactions and sales of the featured range with the weeks before, using the shop's till data.
9. **Take-down and store.** Label reusable props by theme; note what to reuse next month.
10. **Log and report.** What was built, what it cost, what it did, and one change for next month.

## Outputs

- Window plan: idea, zone map, front view, placement table, lighting, sign spots, props list, build steps.
- Props basket and receipts list.
- Before and after photos.
- One-paragraph result against the goal, with the till figures used.
- Action log of every change made in the client's tools.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool and the level 3 limits.
- The window plan and props list (point 1).
- Every props order and payment (point 2); paying stays with the client.
- Any offer, price or claim on window signs.
- Anything electrical beyond plug-in lights: a registered electrician decides.
