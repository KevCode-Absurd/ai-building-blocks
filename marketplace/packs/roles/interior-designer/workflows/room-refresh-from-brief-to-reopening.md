# Room refresh: from brief to reopening

**Trigger:** a cafe, salon, studio, office or holiday let owner wants a refresh with a budget and a date. Typical run: one to three weeks of planning, then a few days of work, most of it waiting for measurements, quotes and deliveries.

## Steps

1. **Intake and access (access.md questions; prompt: measure-and-brief).** Measuring checklist, goal in one line, users, limits. Ask which access level the client grants for each tool (plan files, shared sheets, booking listing); until they answer, work at level 1.
2. **Who to ask (prompt: professional-check-triage).** Sort planned changes into owner, trade, and building control or another professional. Changes that need approval are raised now, before design time is spent on them.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms the brief and which changes stay in scope.
3. **Layout (skill: space-plan-and-clearances).** Zone map, placement table, route, counter, door and exit checks against the region's guide. Level 2: draw it in the client's tool or a "DRAFT" copy.
4. **Light and colour (skill: lighting-and-colour-plan).** Layers, colour scheme, finishes, contrast, electrician points.
5. **Preview (prompt: concept-image-prompts).** Optional concept images, labelled "concept only".
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the layout, lighting and colour plans.
6. **Budget and timetable (skill: refresh-budget-and-order-of-work).** Shopping list with safety checks, trades and sign-offs, order of work, trade briefs.
7. **Quotes and orders.** Level 2: saved baskets and draft orders. Level 3: send approved briefs to the trades the client named for quotes.
   **Approval point 3:** each order, each accepted quote and every payment. The client places orders and pays, or gives a fresh yes for each one.
8. **Before work starts.** Remind the responsible person to review the fire risk assessment if exits, seating or sleeping arrangements change; confirm building control or trade bookings; ask for asbestos records (UK) or lead-safe needs (US) where older buildings are disturbed.
9. **During the work.** Keep the shared list current (ordered, delivered, fitted) at level 3 within limits; log every action; flag delays and any change from the plan.
10. **Reopening check.** Walk the routes, check exits and signs are clear, test lights, compare with the placement table, photograph. For holiday lets: update listing photos and room descriptions at level 3 within limits, or hand them to the client.
11. **Hand over.** Final plans, shopping list with what was bought, care notes for finishes, the action log, and a list of anything still open with the trade or authority.

## Outputs

- Brief and measuring checklist.
- Professional check list (who to ask for each change).
- Zone map and placement table; lighting and colour plan; optional concept images.
- Costed shopping list with safety checks; trade briefs; order of work.
- Reopening checklist and photos; updated listing (level 3) or listing content for the client.
- Action log of every change made in the client's tools.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool, and level 3 limits (files, listings, actions, end date).
- The brief and scope (point 1), and the design plans (point 2).
- Every order, accepted quote and payment, each time (point 3); paying stays with the client.
- Sending plans or photos to anyone new for the first time.
- Any change that needs building control, a registered trade or a fire risk assessment review: the professional or authority decides, not me.
