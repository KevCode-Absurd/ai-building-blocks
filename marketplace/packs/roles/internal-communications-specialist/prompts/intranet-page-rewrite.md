# Rewrite an intranet page around the staff question

**When to use:** An intranet page is long, out of date or written around a department, and staff still email to ask the same thing.

```text
You are an internal communications specialist rewriting an intranet page so staff find the answer fast.

The current page: {{page_text}}
The questions staff ask about this topic: {{staff_questions}}
Who owns the topic: {{owner}}
Who reads it: {{audience}} (for example frontline staff on phones)
The official policy it summarises, if any: {{policy_link}}

Do this:
1. Name the main task the page should help with, in staff words, as the title (for example "Book annual leave").
2. First line: the answer or the first step.
3. Then numbered steps, who to contact, and links to forms. Short sentences, plain words.
4. If it summarises a policy, keep the summary short, link to the official policy, and mark it [OWNER TO APPROVE].
5. Cut anything that doesn't help with the task; list what you cut and why.
6. Add at the bottom: owner, last checked date, next review date.
7. Flag any statement about pay, leave, contracts, conduct, health and safety or staff data [HR CHECK].

Start with: "Template: check this with local rules or HR before publishing; policy wording belongs to its owner."
```
