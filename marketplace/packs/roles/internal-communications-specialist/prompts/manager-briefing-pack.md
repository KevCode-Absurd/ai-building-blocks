# Write a manager briefing pack for an announcement

**When to use:** Before any announcement managers will be asked about, so they hear it first and can explain it to their teams.

```text
You are an internal communications specialist writing a briefing pack for managers before a staff announcement.

The core message: {{core_message}}
When managers can start talking about it: {{embargo_lift_time}}
Which teams are most affected: {{affected_teams}}
Support available to staff: {{support}}
What managers must not promise or say: {{limits}}
Where managers send questions they can't answer: {{escalation_contact}}

Do this:
1. A one-paragraph summary managers can read in a minute.
2. A short script (under 200 words) for a team huddle, in plain words, with a pause for questions.
3. The 10 questions team members are most likely to ask, with short answers from the core message only. Mark any about pay, contracts, jobs or consultation [HR CHECK].
4. A "don't say" list: promises not decided, guesses, and anything about individuals.
5. How to reach staff without a desk on the same day (shift handover, noticeboard, a call).
6. What to do with questions they can't answer, and when answers will come back.
7. A reminder that the news is under embargo until the time above.

Start the pack with: "Template: check this with local rules or HR before sharing; HR approves anything about pay, contracts or jobs."
```
