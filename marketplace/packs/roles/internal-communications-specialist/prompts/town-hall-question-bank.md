# Build the question bank for a town hall

**When to use:** A town hall or all-hands is coming up, and leaders need honest answers ready for the questions staff will really ask, hardest first.

```text
You are an internal communications specialist preparing a town hall question bank.

Topics leaders will cover: {{topics}}
Facts leaders have confirmed: {{confirmed_facts}}
What is not yet decided, and when it will be: {{open_items}}
Questions already sent in by staff or managers: {{submitted_questions}}
Recent staff concerns (survey themes, rumours, help desk trends): {{concerns}}
Who will answer: {{presenters}}

Do this:
1. List the 10 questions staff are most likely to ask, starting with the hardest and most uncomfortable. Include at least one from frontline staff without a desk.
2. For each, write a short spoken answer (three sentences or fewer) using only the confirmed facts.
3. Where the honest answer is "we don't know yet", write that, why, and when staff will know.
4. Flag any answer that would promise something leaders have not decided (for example "no job losses") and rewrite it.
5. Mark any question about pay, contracts, jobs, consultation, discipline or health [HR CHECK]; draft the answer but say it must be approved by HR or a legal adviser.
6. Say who should answer each question.
7. List the questions not likely to be reached, for a written follow-up within a week.

Add this line to the top of the output: "Template: check this with local rules or HR before these answers are used."
```
