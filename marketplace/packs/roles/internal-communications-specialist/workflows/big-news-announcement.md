# Big news announcement

**Trigger:** a decision staff will feel: a restructure, redundancies, new leadership, a merger, a site opening or closure, a new system, a major policy change.

**Cadence:** as needed. If the news may reach the press, regulators or customers, bring in the PR specialist kit's news announcement workflow at step 1 so staff hear first.

## Steps

1. **Confirm and check (level 1).** What is decided, why, who is affected, what is not yet known. HR or a legal adviser confirms whether consultation, representatives or notice rules apply and what can be said now. Check this with local rules or HR.
2. **Core message (skill: change-announcement-sequence).** One page in the leader's voice: what, why, who, what's not decided, support, next steps, where to ask.
   **Approval point:** the leader and HR approve the core message.
3. **Timeline.** Managers briefed, affected people told first, everyone else told, FAQ page live, Q&A, pulse check, follow-up, with dates, channels, owners and approvers. Plan each country separately where local law differs.
4. **Manager pack (prompt: manager-briefing-pack).** Script, likely questions, what not to promise, where to send questions, embargo time.
5. **Affected people.** Personal messages or meeting scripts, approved by HR. Where possible, told one to one or in small groups by their manager or a leader, with HR present if needed.
6. **All-staff message and channel versions (prompt: chat-channel-announcement).** Identical facts on email, chat, intranet news, noticeboards and huddles, released at the same time.
   **Approval point:** a fresh yes from the leader for the all-staff message, every time, even at level 3.
7. **FAQ page (prompt: intranet-page-rewrite).** Live at the same moment; dated; updated as answers come.
8. **Q&A (skill: leadership-update-and-town-hall; prompt: town-hall-question-bank).** Within days. Hardest question first. Captions on; recording or summary for shift workers.
9. **Send at the level granted.** Level 1: hand over texts. Level 2: drafts in each tool. Level 3: post and send approved pieces to the set audiences at the set times; log each action with the time.
10. **Pulse check (prompt: three-question-pulse-check).** One to two weeks later. Small-group rules agreed before launch.
11. **Follow up.** What we heard, what changes, what doesn't, and the next dates. Update the FAQ page.
12. **Watch for problems.** If news leaks early, a fact is wrong, or a message reaches the wrong people, stop, tell the leader and HR, and correct on every channel with their yes.

## Outputs

- Core message and approved timeline.
- Manager pack.
- Messages for affected people (HR-approved).
- All-staff message and channel versions.
- FAQ page.
- Q&A run of show and answers.
- Pulse check, results and follow-up.
- Action log with times.

## Needs the client's approval

- HR or legal adviser sign-off on the plan, the core message and anything about jobs, pay or contracts.
- The leader's approval of the core message and the timeline.
- A fresh yes every time for the all-staff message, any formal statement, and each message to affected individuals.
- Any change to the timeline or audience.
- Corrections to anything already sent or posted.
