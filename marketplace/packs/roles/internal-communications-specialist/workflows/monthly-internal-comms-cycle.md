# Monthly internal comms cycle

**Trigger:** the start of each month, or the first month of a new internal comms programme.

**Cadence:** monthly, with weekly chat updates in between.

## Steps

1. **Access check (level 1).** Confirm the access level the client grants for each tool (chat, email, intranet, survey, events) and the limits for level 3. Write them under "Access I have".
2. **Gather.** Leaders' notes, site and team news, HR and IT updates, key dates, people stories with consent, and last month's questions and results.
3. **Update the calendar.** Use the communications coordinator kit's calendar and approvals skill: newsletter, leadership update, weekly chat posts, manager briefings, town hall if due, with owners and approvers. Flag weeks with too many asks of the same staff.
4. **People-topic check.** List anything about pay, contracts, jobs, policies, health or staff data and send it to HR or the owner before writing. Check this with local rules or HR.
5. **Leadership update (skill: leadership-update-and-town-hall; prompt: leadership-update-from-notes).** In the leader's voice, with the why and what is not yet known.
   **Approval point:** the leader approves the exact text.
6. **Newsletter (skill: staff-newsletter-edition).** Phone, print and chat versions.
   **Approval point:** the named approver signs off the edition.
7. **Weekly chat posts (prompt: chat-channel-announcement).** Short, with the action and date in the first line.
8. **Intranet upkeep (skill: intranet-page-map-and-copy).** Update pages touched by this month's news; review pages past their review date.
9. **Send and post at the level granted.**
   - Level 1: hand over the final texts for the client to send.
   - Level 2: load them as drafts in each tool.
   - Level 3: post and send approved items to the channels, lists and sites set. Anything to all staff needs a fresh yes every time, even at level 3.
10. **Listen.** Collect replies, manager feedback, help desk trends and questions. Report themes, not names.
11. **Monthly report.** What went out, what landed, repeat questions, and what to change next month.

## Outputs

- Updated comms calendar.
- Leadership update.
- Newsletter edition (three versions).
- Weekly chat posts.
- Updated intranet pages.
- Send and post log.
- Monthly report with themes and next steps.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access levels and level 3 limits at the start.
- The leader's update text, by the leader.
- The newsletter edition, by the named approver.
- A fresh yes every time for any all-staff send or formal statement.
- HR or the owner's approval for anything about pay, contracts, jobs, policies, health or staff data.
- Retiring or deleting intranet pages.
