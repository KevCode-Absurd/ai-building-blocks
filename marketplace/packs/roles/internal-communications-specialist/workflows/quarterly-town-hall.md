# Quarterly town hall

**Trigger:** a town hall or all-hands is on the calendar, usually quarterly.

**Cadence:** quarterly. Start three to four weeks before the event.

## Steps

1. **Set up (level 1).** Date, time, length, format (in person, Teams event, other platform, hybrid), presenters, whether it is recorded, and how shift workers will see it. Confirm the access level for the event tool, chat and intranet.
2. **Topics.** Results, decisions, people news and the hard topic staff are already talking about. Check anything about pay, contracts, jobs or consultation with HR first. Check this with local rules or HR.
3. **Collect questions early.** Open an anonymous form or a thread in the announcement channel two weeks before; ask managers to gather questions from staff without a desk.
4. **Run of show and script (skill: leadership-update-and-town-hall).** Timings, presenters, slide cues, the hard topic addressed directly, and the largest block of time you can protect for questions. For delivery coaching, use the speechwriter kit's timing and rehearsal skill.
5. **Question bank (prompt: town-hall-question-bank).** Ten likely questions, hardest first, honest answers, [HR CHECK] where needed.
   **Approval point:** presenters approve the script and answers.
6. **Event setup.** At level 2, draft the event description, Q&A settings and invitation; at level 3, create or update the event within the limits set. Turn on Q&A and captions. For large Teams events (over 1,000 attendees, run as "optimised for large audience"), attendees interact through Q&A and captions are AI-generated; consider a human captioner for important events.
7. **Invitation.** Sent to all staff with the agenda and how to ask questions.
   **Approval point:** the invitation goes to all staff, so it needs a fresh yes.
8. **Dry run.** On the platform, with presenters, slides, Q&A and captions.
9. **On the day.** Moderate questions; pick the most-asked, not the easiest; note any promised follow-up.
10. **Follow up within a week.** Answers to questions not reached (prompt: chat-channel-announcement for the post), the recording or a summary (large Teams event recordings expire after 30 days by default unless extended), and any promised decisions.
11. **Review.** Attendance, questions asked and answered, and a short pulse question in the next newsletter or survey if useful.

## Outputs

- Run of show and script.
- Question bank with answers.
- Event setup and invitation.
- Follow-up answers, recording or summary.
- Review notes and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Topics, script and answers, by the presenters.
- HR approval for anything about pay, contracts, jobs or consultation.
- A fresh yes for the all-staff invitation and the all-staff follow-up.
- Live changes to the event at level 3, within the limits set at the start.
- Publishing the recording.
