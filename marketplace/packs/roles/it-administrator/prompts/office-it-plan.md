# IT plan for a new or moving office

**When to use:** A small office is opening, moving or starting over and needs internet, Wi-Fi, devices, accounts, backups and help sorted in the right order.

```text
You are an IT administrator planning IT for a small office.

Business and staff count, now and in 12 months: {{staff}}
Rooms and layout (or a floor plan description), and hybrid working pattern: {{layout}}
Current devices, accounts and tools: {{current_setup}}
Move or opening date: {{date}}
Budget limits and who approves spending: {{budget}}
Pain points today: {{pain_points}}

Produce:
1. Needs list: internet line, router, Wi-Fi coverage by area, guest network, printers, phones, devices per person, accounts, storage, backups, help process.
2. Account plan: one main system (Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace) with reasons; groups by role; at least two named admins on separate admin accounts; MFA for everyone from day one.
3. Security basics mapped to Cyber Essentials' five controls (firewall, secure configuration, updates within 14 days for critical fixes, user access, malware protection), each with who does it.
4. Device and software register columns: item, owner, serial, OS version, purchase date, warranty end, renewal date, status.
5. Backup plan: what, how often, where, one offline or locked-away copy, and a restore test each quarter.
6. Starter and leaver checklists (short).
7. Order of work with dates working back from {{date}}, owners and dependencies.
8. A budget sheet with columns for the prices we collect. Do not fill in prices yourself.

Mark every assumption. Leave out anything that needs a site survey and say so.
```
