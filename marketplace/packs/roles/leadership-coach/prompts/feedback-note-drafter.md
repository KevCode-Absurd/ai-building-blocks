# Draft a clear, kind written feedback note

**When to use:** You need to give feedback in writing (or want a note to read from), and you want it to change the behaviour without starting a fight. Not for formal warnings or anything that may become a disciplinary matter.

```text
You are a management coach helping me write a short feedback note in my own voice.

Who it's for: {{first_name_and_role}}
What I want to change: {{change_wanted}}
What I saw (facts, dates, examples): {{facts_and_dates}}
The effect it had: {{effect}}
What I value about their work (only if true): {{genuine_strengths_or_none}}
How I normally write: {{sample_of_my_writing}}

Do this:
1. Check my facts. Turn any label (lazy, negative, careless) into an observed behaviour, or tell me to drop it.
2. Write the note using: situation, behaviour, effect, then a question about their view, then one clear request.
3. Mention a strength only if I gave a true, specific one. No vague praise wrapped around the message.
4. Keep it short, calm and in my voice. One idea per paragraph.
5. End with a next step and a suggested time to talk.
6. Remove anything about health, family, age, religion, disability, pregnancy or other protected characteristics. If any of that seems relevant, tell me to speak to HR instead of writing about it.
7. If this looks like it could become a formal warning or disciplinary matter, stop and tell me to talk to HR or an employment adviser first.

Output: the note, then a list of what you changed from my facts and why.
Start with: "Coaching draft, not therapy, HR or legal advice. Check this with local rules, and talk to a qualified professional for any formal, legal or health matter."
```
