# Training interview: Executive and leadership coach

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share clients' names, employers or personal details; anonymise every story, and leave out health details entirely.

1. **Who do you coach most: new managers, senior leaders, founders, coaches building programmes, or mentoring schemes?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the kind of client it should expect.

2. **How do you decide, in the first conversation, whether coaching is the right help at all?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's screen for when to coach and when to refer on.

3. **Walk me through a coaching programme you ran from first session to last.**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real arc, timings and the tools that worked.

4. **Which coaching model or questions do you rely on, and when do you drop them?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's method and their judgment about when a model gets in the way.

5. **What goes in your coaching agreement, especially when an employer pays?**
   Why it matters: confidentiality and three-way contracting are where trust breaks; this captures the expert's terms.

6. **Tell me about a time you referred a client to a therapist, doctor or lawyer. What did you notice, and what did you say?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent the signs and the words for referring on kindly.

7. **What tasks between sessions actually get done, and which never do?**
   Why it matters: helps the agent set small, linked tasks that clients complete.

8. **How do you help a manager prepare for a conversation they dread?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's script method and rehearsal habits.

9. **What do managers get wrong most often in feedback, and how do you fix it?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a list of common errors and corrections.

10. **How do you set up one-on-ones for a first-time manager?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's agenda, rhythm and question bank.

11. **What does a new manager need in the first 30 days that they usually skip?**
    Why it matters: turns experience into a practical first-month plan.

12. **How do you design a mentoring scheme so pairs keep meeting?**
    Why it matters: captures matching rules, check-ins and the expert's fix for schemes that fizzle.

13. **How do you handle a client who wants you to tell them what to do?**
    Why it matters: sets the line between coaching, mentoring and consulting.

14. **How do you measure whether coaching worked, without promising results?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent honest ways to review progress.

15. **What do you write in session notes, and what do you never write?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's note-taking and privacy habits.

16. **When a manager mentions possible harassment, discrimination or a health issue in their team, what do you do?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's boundary with HR and legal matters.

17. **Do you use AI or recording tools in coaching? What rules do you follow?**
    Why it matters: consent, privacy and EU limits on emotion recognition shape what the agent may do.

18. **Tell me about a coaching engagement that went badly. What would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: reveals the checks the expert now adds.

19. **How do you run group coaching differently from one to one?**
    Why it matters: captures timings, peer coaching rounds and group ground rules.

20. **Which books, codes or training shaped how you coach?**
    Why it matters: builds the agent's reading list and the ethics code it follows.
