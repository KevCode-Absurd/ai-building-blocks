# Coaching engagement cycle

**Trigger:** A new coaching client or a sponsor (employer) asks for a coaching programme. Runs from the first conversation to the final review; the cadence between sessions is agreed in step 2.

The coaching limits in access.md apply to every session and document in this cycle.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Steps marked level 2 or level 3 need that level in the named tool; at a lower level I hand you the drafts to send or book yourself. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Chemistry and scope.** A short first conversation: what the client wants to change, why now, who else is involved, and whether coaching is the right help. If the need is clinical, legal or HR-formal, say so and point to the right professional instead.
2. **Contract.** Draft the coaching agreement with the `coaching-programme-plan` skill (step 2): goals, sessions, confidentiality, what the sponsor hears, what must be passed on, notes and how to end. *Approval: the client (and the sponsor, if any) agree it. Sending it as a binding agreement always needs a new yes.*
3. **Access I have.** Record the level granted for calendar, documents, email and any platform, with limits (which clients, which messages, until when).
4. **Programme plan.** Build the arc, session plans, worksheets and tasks with `coaching-programme-plan` (level 2: drafts in the coach's documents). *Approval: the coach reviews before the first session.*
5. **Book and schedule.** At level 3, within the limits, book the sessions in the named calendar and schedule the messages from the `between-session-messages` prompt. Otherwise hand over the list to book.
6. **Each session.** Use the plan or the `coaching-session-plan` prompt. After the session, write notes with facts and agreed actions only, in the agreed place.
7. **Referral check, every session.** If distress, health, trauma, harassment or a legal matter comes up, pause the goal and refer on: urgent help if anyone is at risk (999 or NHS 111 in the UK, 911 or 988 in the US, 9-8-8 in Canada, 000 or Lifeline 13 11 14 in Australia, Tele-MANAS 14416 in India, or the local emergency number), otherwise a doctor, counsellor, employee support service, HR or a lawyer. *Approval: the client decides whether coaching pauses or continues.*
8. **Midpoint review.** Compare progress with "done". Adjust the plan. If a sponsor is involved, share only what the contract allows. *Approval: the client agrees what is shared.*
9. **Final review.** Review against "done", name what helped, and write a three-month plan the client owns. Ask for feedback on the coaching.
10. **Close.** Store or delete notes as the contract says. Deleting records always needs a new yes. Send the closing message.

## Outputs

- Coaching agreement (template, agreed version)
- "Access I have" note
- Programme plan, session plans, worksheets and tasks
- Messages between sessions
- Session notes (facts and actions only)
- Referral log (date, what was suggested, no clinical detail)
- Midpoint and final reviews, and the client's three-month plan
- Action log of everything done in the coach's tools (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Booking sessions and sending the agreed messages between sessions to the agreed clients. Before the first live action, I show the list and limits and wait for your yes.

**Always needs a new yes.** The coaching agreement sent as binding, anything shared with a sponsor, any message to a new person, any change to session notes, deleting records, and any step touching a formal HR, legal or health matter.
