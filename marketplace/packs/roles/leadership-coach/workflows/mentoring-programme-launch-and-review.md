# Mentoring programme launch and review

**Trigger:** An employer or professional body decides to run a mentoring scheme, or to fix one that fizzled. Cadence: design, launch, early check-in, midpoint review, end review.

Mentors are not counsellors, and this is not HR or legal advice. Check this with local rules, including privacy rules for sign-up and survey data, and talk to a qualified professional for HR, legal or mental-health matters.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Steps marked level 2 or level 3 need that level in the named tool; at a lower level I hand you the drafts to publish or send yourself. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Purpose and design.** Run the `mentoring-programme-design` skill steps 1 to 3: one purpose, the shape, length, rhythm, matching rules and sign-up questions. *Approval: the sponsor agrees the purpose and design.*
2. **Privacy check.** List what sign-up and survey data is collected, why, who sees it and how long it's kept. *Approval: the sponsor's data protection lead or adviser checks the privacy notice before launch.*
3. **Guides and templates.** Write the mentor and mentee guides and session templates (skill steps 4 and 5) as drafts (level 2).
4. **Launch.** At level 3, within the limits, publish the approved sign-up form and send the announcement to the agreed groups. Otherwise hand over the drafts.
5. **Matching.** Prepare a matching table from sign-up answers against the rules. *Approval: the programme lead makes every match.* Send matching confirmations (level 3, agreed list only).
6. **Mentor briefing.** A short session or note on roles, boundaries, confidentiality, and when and how to refer on, including local urgent help lines.
7. **Early check-in.** After the first few meetings, send the agreed check-in messages. Flag pairs who haven't met; the programme lead follows up without blame.
8. **Midpoint review.** Send the midpoint surveys from the `mentoring-review-survey` prompt (level 3: approved wording, agreed groups). Report only groups at or above the minimum size. Offer a no-blame re-match to stalled pairs.
9. **End review.** Send the end surveys, run a closing message, and write a short report: what worked, what to change, three actions with owners. *Approval: the sponsor decides on changes for the next round.*
10. **Records.** Keep or delete sign-up and survey data as the privacy notice says. Deleting records always needs a new yes.

## Outputs

- Programme design on a page
- Privacy notice and data list
- Sign-up form, guides and session templates
- Matching table (for the programme lead's decisions)
- Launch, confirmation, check-in and closing messages
- Midpoint and end survey results with actions
- End-of-round report
- Action log of everything done in the sponsor's tools (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Publishing the approved sign-up form, sending the agreed announcement, confirmations, check-ins and surveys to the agreed groups. Before the first live action, I show the list and limits and wait for your yes.

**Always needs a new yes.** Every match, the privacy notice, survey wording, any message to a new group, any report that names individuals, deleting records, and anything a participant raises about health, harassment, discrimination or a legal matter (which goes to HR or a qualified professional).
