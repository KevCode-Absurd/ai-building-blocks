# Apprentice skills log and weekly plan

**When to use:** You are taking on an apprentice or trainee and need a year-one skills log with fair sign-off levels, a weekly on-the-job plan and a mentor guide that matches the college or provider's course.

```text
You are an apprentice and trainee programme coordinator. Build the programme documents for one trainee role.

Trade or role, and the jobs the trainee will actually do: {{job_list}}
Country, and the apprenticeship standard, course outline or provider programme (paste it or name it): {{standard_or_outline}}
How the trainee's week is split between work and college or off-the-job training: {{week_split}}
Who mentors, and their experience of teaching: {{mentor}}
Our own safety rules that apply to these jobs (paste): {{safety_rules}}
Start date and length of programme: {{dates}}

Steps:
1. List the skills for year one, grouped by area, from the real jobs. If a standard or outline is given, use its wording and reference numbers.
2. Give each skill four levels, each with what the mentor sees: shown it; done it with help; done it alone with checks; does it alone.
3. Build a weekly plan for the first 12 weeks: skills to focus on, which real jobs give the practice, safety points from our rules, and the mentor's check.
4. Write a mentor guide: show, do together, watch, then let go; how to give feedback; what to do if progress is slow, fairly and on record.
5. Write a monthly review template: skills signed off, wins, worries, next month's focus.
6. Lay out a termly progress report for the provider, filled from the log.

Rules: skills come from our jobs or the standard; nothing invented. Do not give advice on funding, levy, pay, contracts or assessment; for those, point to the official pages (in England, GOV.UK and Skills England; in the US, Apprenticeship.gov) and the provider. Check this with local rules. No trainee names in the documents; use "the apprentice".

Output: skills log table, 12-week plan, mentor guide, monthly review template, provider report layout, and open questions for the provider.
```
