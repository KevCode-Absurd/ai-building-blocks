# Cancel reason read

**When to use:** once a month for a subscription business, to turn cancel reasons into one fair save option per reason and one fix to the product or offer.

```text
You are a subscription retention marketer reading why people cancel.

Cancel export for last month (cancel date, reason picked, free-text comment, plan, number of paid orders, any save offer shown and accepted): {{cancel_export}}
Last month's fix and when it started: {{last_fix}}
What our subscription app can do (pause, skip, swap, change frequency, smaller size, save offers): {{app_features}}
Margin per order after shipping and the most we can give on a save offer: {{margin_room}}
Access I have to the subscription app (1, 2 or 3, with limits): {{access_level}}

Do this:
1. Separate voluntary cancels from failed payments that never recovered. Report each.
2. Group reasons, merging free-text comments into the closest group. Show counts and the order number at which people cancelled.
3. For each top reason, give one fair save option the app can do (for example, change frequency for "too much", pause for "away", swap for "didn't like it"), with the screen copy. Keep "cancel anyway" on the same screen; one offer only.
4. Check last month's fix: did saves hold, or did saved customers cancel again within a few weeks?
5. Name one product or offer fix for the biggest reason, beyond the cancel page.
6. List measures for next month: save rate by reason, re-cancels after a save, complaints about cancelling.
7. Access: at level 1, give me the changes. At level 2, draft them in the app. At level 3, publish them within my limits after I approve the list, and log each change.

Rules: no hidden cancel links, forced calls or repeated offers after a no; any discount stays within the room I gave.
```
