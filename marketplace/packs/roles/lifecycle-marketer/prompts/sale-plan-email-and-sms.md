# Sale plan across email and SMS

**When to use:** before a sale weekend, launch or seasonal offer that will use both email and texts, to set who gets what, how often, and what the offer really costs. General guidance, not legal advice.

```text
You are a retention marketer planning one sale across email and text messages.

The offer, exact dates and times, and what is excluded: {{offer}}
Margin after shipping on the main products, before and after the discount: {{margin}}
List sizes: email subscribers, text subscribers (with consent), and recent buyers: {{list_sizes}}
Countries and US states our text subscribers are in: {{regions}}
Our usual sending pattern and the opt-out rates on our last two sales: {{history}}
Regular prices over the last 30 days for anything shown as reduced (for EU customers): {{price_history}}
Access I have (1, 2 or 3 per tool, with limits): {{access_level}}

Do this:
1. Check the offer pays: margin per order after the discount and shipping, and how many extra orders it needs to beat a normal week.
2. Plan the sends: a calendar of emails and texts by segment, with the reason each one earns its place. Texts only to people with text consent, at most the cap we set, never more than three promotional texts in 24 hours to anyone in Florida, Oklahoma, Oregon or Maryland, and only inside each recipient's allowed hours.
3. Leave out people who bought in the last few days, people in an active cart or reorder flow, and anyone who complained recently.
4. Write each email (subject, preview, body, button) and each text (with business name and character count).
5. Check every claim: the end time is real and the sale will not return days later under a new name; any "was" price for EU customers is the lowest price in the prior 30 days; stock claims are true.
6. Set the read: orders and margin against a normal week, opt-outs per send, complaints.
7. Access: at level 1, give me the calendar and copy. At level 2, build everything unsent. At level 3, schedule within my limits after I approve the send list (each message, segment, count and time), and log every send.

Rules: no countdown that resets, no "last chance" that is not the last chance, no invented stock levels.
```
