# Interview: retention and lifecycle marketer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the marketer's work (for example, subscription questions for someone who only runs shop flows).

## How you read a business

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new client, from the first message to the first change you made.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and what they look at before touching anything.
2. **What data do you ask for first, and what do you do when the client cannot export it?**
   Why it matters: retention work lives or dies on the order export; their workarounds are reusable.
3. **How do you define a repeat customer, a lapsed customer and a cancel? Have those definitions ever caused an argument?**
   Why it matters: consistent definitions are what make month-to-month comparisons honest.
4. **Show me a cohort or drop-out table you built. What did it tell you that the client did not know?**
   Why it matters: captures how they turn a table into a decision.
5. **How do you decide when a customer has lapsed? Does it differ by product?**
   Why it matters: one fixed number for every product is the most common timing mistake.

## Flows, timing and channels

6. **Which flows do you build first for a new shop, in what order, and why?**
   Why it matters: their order of work becomes the agent's default plan.
7. **How do you time a reorder message? Tell me about one you re-timed and what happened.**
   Why it matters: timing from real usage is the core skill of the role.
8. **What earns a text instead of an email? What have you stopped texting?**
   Why it matters: channel judgement protects opt-out rates and the client's goodwill.
9. **How do you check consent before a client starts texting? Where have you found problems?**
   Why it matters: consent mistakes are the costliest in this role; their checks become the agent's checks.
10. **How do you handle quiet hours and frequency for customers spread across time zones and countries?**
   Why it matters: real set-up habits beat rules recited from memory.

## Offers, subscriptions and measurement

11. **When do you agree to a discount, and how do you check it pays?**
   Why it matters: shows how they protect margin and avoid training customers to wait.
12. **Walk me through a subscription offer you designed or fixed. What did the monthly maths show?**
   Why it matters: subscription discounts repeat; their maths method is reusable.
13. **What does a fair cancel flow look like to you? Have you ever pushed back on a client who wanted it harder?**
   Why it matters: captures how they hold the line on honest cancelling.
14. **How do you prove a flow or programme adds sales, rather than taking credit for orders that would have happened?**
   Why it matters: holdouts and cohort comparisons separate real experts from report-readers.
15. **Which numbers do you report every month, and which do you refuse to lead with?**
   Why it matters: fills the agent's monthly report format.

## Tools, clients and hard jobs

16. **Which email, SMS and subscription tools do you use, and what does each get wrong?**
   Why it matters: fills access.md with what really happens in each tool.
17. **What do you need the client to approve, and what will you change on your own once you have access?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's level 2 and level 3 habits and limits.
18. **Tell me about a retention job that went badly. What did you miss, and what do you check now?**
   Why it matters: failures become checks in the quality bar.
19. **Which client asked for something you would not do, and how did you handle it?**
   Why it matters: captures their pushback and the words they use.
20. **What do you know about this work that most people in the role get wrong?**
   Why it matters: becomes "My specialty" in knowledge.md.
