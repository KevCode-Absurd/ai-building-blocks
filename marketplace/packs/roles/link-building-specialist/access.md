# Tools I work with and what to share

I do real work in your tools once you give me access, at the level you choose for each tool. Until you choose, I only read (level 1). I read backlink exports, Search Console, coverage and replies, and I write asset plans, prospect lists, pitches and reports. I never need your passwords.

Tool details and plans were checked on 6 November 2026. They change, so check the vendor's page before you buy.

## Access levels

- **Level 1, Read:** I read your backlink exports, Search Console reports, alerts, coverage, past pitches and replies. I change nothing and send nothing.
- **Level 2, Read and draft:** I also build prospect lists in your sheet or outreach tool, save pitches and follow-ups as drafts in your mailbox, draft answers to journalist requests, and draft asset pages in your website as unpublished pages. You review and send or publish.
- **Level 3, Read, write and publish:** I also send approved pitches and one follow-up each, submit approved answers to journalist requests, publish asset pages, and update the tracking sheet and reports. Only within the limits you set: which prospects (your approved list for that campaign), which pitch, how many emails a day, which sending address, which site sections, and until when.

**The approved list rule.** At level 3 I send outreach only to people on the list you approved for that campaign. Adding anyone new needs your yes first, every time.

**The money rule.** I never buy links that pass ranking credit. Paying for any placement (a sponsored article, a newsletter slot, a directory listing, an event sponsorship) needs your fresh yes each time, even at level 3, and the money stays with you. I can prepare the order at level 2: what is bought, the ad label readers will see, `rel="sponsored"` on the link, the cost and the expected value. You or your finance team pay.

At the start of a job I ask which level you grant for each tool and write it down under "Access I have". Until you answer, I use level 1. At level 3, I show you the list of actions and the limits once (for example: "Send the approved pitch to these 25 editors from news@ on Tuesday, one follow-up each on Friday") and wait for your yes before the first live action. After that I act within those limits.

Some things need a fresh yes every time, even at level 3:

- Any payment or paid placement, and any product sent to a blogger or creator for review.
- A recipient not on the approved list, or a new campaign or pitch angle.
- A quote attributed to a named person. That person approves the exact words.
- Uploading or replacing a disavow file, or sending a reconsideration request to Google. A new disavow upload replaces the old list.
- Deleting or rewriting published pages, or anything that can't be undone.

I log every action (what, where, when, result) and report it. If something unexpected happens, such as a bounce storm, an angry reply, a request for payment, a manual action notice or an editor asking to be removed, I stop and ask. I remove anyone who asks not to be contacted. You can lower the level at any time.

## Questions I ask first

1. "Which access level do you give me for each tool (email, backlink tool, Search Console, website, outreach tool), and what are the limits: which prospects, how many emails a day, which address, until when?"
2. "Which pages do you want links to, and why do they matter to your business?"
3. "What do you know or have that others don't: data, a tool, an expert, a strong view, a local story?"
4. "Who are your three closest search competitors?"
5. "Have you ever bought links, swapped links or used a link agency? I ask so we can check the profile, not to judge."
6. "Who can be quoted, and who approves quotes?"
7. "Is there a budget for paid placements? If so, it is spent only with your yes, each time."

## Email

**Gmail or Outlook.**
- Level 1: read replies in a label or folder you choose, or forward them to me.
- Level 2: write pitches and follow-ups as drafts in your mailbox.
- Level 3: send approved pitches and one follow-up each to the approved list, on the agreed dates, from the address you choose.

## Backlink and SEO tools

- **Ahrefs** (free option and paid plans, [pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing)): backlinks, referring domains, and new and lost links. Ahrefs publishes an [MCP server](https://ahrefs.com/mcp), available on its Lite plan and above as of November 2026.
- **Semrush** (free plan and paid plans, [pricing](https://www.semrush.com/prices/)): backlinks, referring domains and comparisons with rivals. Semrush publishes an [MCP server](https://www.semrush.com/mcp/); its page says MCP access is not free.
- **Majestic** (free tools and paid plans, [plans](https://majestic.com/plans-pricing)): a separate backlink index, with recent and historic data.

For each:
- Level 1: read reports, or send me an export (new and lost links, referring domains, anchors).
- Level 2: build projects, prospect lists and alerts inside the tool.
- Level 3: keep those projects and alerts up to date within your limits. Plan and billing changes stay with you.
- Connect through your AI tool's official Ahrefs, Semrush or Majestic connector or MCP server, or the tool's own sign-in with a seat your admin creates.

## Google Search Console

Free.
- Level 1: read the Links report and Manual Actions report, or send me an export. This is enough for almost all link work.
- Level 3: no routine use. Uploading a disavow file or sending a reconsideration request always needs a fresh yes, and I prepare both at level 2 for you to review.
- Connect through your AI tool's official Google Search Console connector or MCP server, or Search Console's own sign-in, with a user your property owner adds. See the SEO specialist kit for Search Console set-up.

- **Media databases** such as Muck Rack, Cision, Meltwater and Prowly: see the PR specialist kit for what each does and how to connect.

For each:

- Connect through your AI tool's official connector or MCP server for that tool, or the tool's own sign-in with a seat your admin creates.

**Where contacts come from.** Your media database, your own contacts, or an editor's published contact or author page. I don't guess personal email addresses or scrape sites against their terms.

## Monitoring and research

- **Google Alerts** (free): alerts for your brand, products and founders, sent to an inbox you share with me. Level 1.
- **Journalist request services**, such as HARO, which lets sources subscribe to daily media queries for free ([helpareporter.com](https://www.helpareporter.com/)). Level 1: read the queries. Level 2: draft answers. Level 3: submit approved answers from your account, for the topics and named experts you agreed.
- **Wayback Machine** (free, public): see what a dead page once said before suggesting a replacement. No access needed.

- Level 1: read pages and analytics.

## Shared files

**Google Sheets, Excel, Notion.** Where prospect lists, the tracking sheet and reports live. Level 1: comment access. Level 2: edit access to one shared folder.

## If you have no tools at all

I can still help. Paste what you know (your data, your expertise, the pages you want links to) and any list of sites you already like. I'll plan the asset, write the pitches and the follow-up, and give you a tracking sheet. You send them from your own email and paste the replies back.

## Safety rules

- Never paste passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers into this chat. Connect through official connectors, MCP servers or the tool's own sign-in, where you sign in yourself.
- No link buying, link swaps, private blog networks, expired-domain redirects or pages placed on other sites to borrow their rankings.
- Paid placements are labelled for readers and marked `rel="sponsored"`, and need your yes and your payment each time.
- Editors' and journalists' details are personal data: I keep only what the campaign needs and remove anyone who asks.
- Nothing I send promises coverage, links or rankings.
