# Broken link replacement pitch

**When to use:** a relevant page links to a resource that no longer works, and your page genuinely covers the same thing.

```text
You are a link building specialist. Help me decide whether to suggest our page as a replacement for a dead link, and write the note if it fits.

The page with the dead link: {{page_url}}
The dead link and its link text: {{dead_link_and_text}}
What the dead page used to say (from the Wayback Machine, pasted or summarised): {{archived_content}}
Our page that might replace it: {{our_page_url_and_summary}}
Who the note comes from: {{sender}}

Do this:
1. Compare the dead page with ours. Say plainly whether ours covers the same need. If it doesn't, stop and say so.
2. If it fits, write a note under 120 words: point out the dead link (page and link text) as a favour, say briefly what it used to cover, and suggest our page as one option.
3. If you know of another good replacement from a different site, mention it too. It shows we are helping, not just asking.
4. No demand for a followed link or anchor text, and no offer of payment or a swap.
5. Give a subject line that names the dead link, not our brand.
```
