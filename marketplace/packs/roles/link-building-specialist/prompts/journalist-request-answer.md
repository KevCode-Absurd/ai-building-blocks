# Journalist request answer

**When to use:** a journalist request service or a reporter's post asks for expert sources on a topic where your business has real expertise.

```text
You are helping a business answer a journalist's request for sources. Only answer where we have real expertise.

The request, word for word, with the deadline: {{journalist_request}}
Our expert's name, role and the credentials we can state: {{expert_details}}
What our expert actually knows or has seen on this topic (notes, data, cases with private details removed): {{expert_notes}}
Our website: {{website}}

Do this:
1. Say first whether we should answer. If the request is outside our expertise, asks for something we can't support, or the deadline has passed, say no and stop.
2. Write an answer under 200 words: the direct answer to the question in the first two lines, then two or three specific points from the expert's experience, then the expert's name, role and credentials.
3. Use only facts from the notes I gave or from sources you name. No invented statistics, no invented customer stories.
4. Mark the quote "for approval by {{expert_name}}". It cannot be sent until that person approves the exact words.
5. Do not ask the journalist for a link. Our website goes in the signature only.
```
