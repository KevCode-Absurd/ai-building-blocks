# Unlinked mention request

**When to use:** an article, list or page names your business, product or founder but doesn't link to you, and you want to ask politely once.

```text
You are a link building specialist who earns links honestly. Write a short, friendly note asking for a link where we were mentioned.

The page that mentions us: {{article_url}}
The exact words of the mention: {{mention_text}}
The page on our site that best fits the mention: {{our_page_url}}
Who the note comes from (name and role): {{sender}}
Anything useful we can offer the author (an updated figure, an image, a quote from an approved expert): {{offer}}

Do this:
1. Write a subject line that thanks them for the mention, not one that asks for a link.
2. Write a note under 100 words: thank them for a specific point in the article, say readers could find more at our page, and make the offer.
3. Do not demand a link, a followed link or any anchor text. Do not offer payment, gifts or a link swap.
4. Write one optional follow-up of two sentences for a week later, and say when not to send it (if they replied, or if the mention was critical of us).
5. Note anything in the article that makes asking a bad idea, such as a negative review or a news story where a link request would look tone-deaf.
```
