# Training interview: link building specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, journalists' names and contact details, and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe campaigns in general terms.

1. **What kinds of businesses and niches do you build links for, and which do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and its limits.

2. **Tell me about the campaign that earned the most good links. What was the story?**
   Why it matters: captures what a winning angle looks like in practice.

3. **Tell me about a campaign that earned nothing. What do you think went wrong?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent the failure patterns to avoid.

4. **How do you find the story in a business that seems dull?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's method for turning ordinary data into news.

5. **What makes you drop an asset idea before building it?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a kill test before effort is spent.

6. **How do you decide a site is worth pitching? What makes you skip one?**
   Why it matters: turns the expert's prospect judgement into rules.

7. **What signs tell you a site sells links?**
   Why it matters: sharpens the agent's seller and spam detection.

8. **Walk me through a pitch that got a yes. Why did it work?**
   Why it matters: captures structure, length and tone that editors respond to.

9. **How do you follow up without annoying people?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's follow-up rules.

10. **How do you handle a journalist who asks for payment or a "contribution" for a link?**
    Why it matters: records how to decline and what to offer instead.

11. **When, if ever, have you used paid placements? How were they labelled and marked?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's approach to disclosure and rel="sponsored".

12. **How do you work with expert comment and journalist request services?**
    Why it matters: captures speed, selection and quote approval habits.

13. **How do you approach unlinked mentions and broken link outreach? What reply rates have you actually seen?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent realistic expectations from real experience.

14. **Which backlink tools do you trust for what, and where do their numbers mislead?**
    Why it matters: tunes how the agent reads third-party metrics.

15. **How do you report results to a client who only wants a link count?**
    Why it matters: sets the honesty and format of monthly reports.

16. **Have you cleaned up a profile after a manual action? What did you do, in order?**
    Why it matters: records the removal, disavow and reconsideration sequence from real work.

17. **When do you tell a client not to disavow anything?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent from over-using the disavow tool.

18. **How do you keep journalists' and editors' personal data safe and up to date?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's data handling across regions.

19. **What changed most in link building in the last two years, and what do you check at each Google spam update?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent what to recheck at each refresh.

20. **What would you never do for a client, whatever they pay?**
    Why it matters: fixes the agent's hard lines in the expert's own words.
