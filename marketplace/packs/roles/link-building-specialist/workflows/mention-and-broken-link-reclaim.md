# Mention and broken link reclaim

**Cadence:** weekly or every two weeks, alongside campaigns. Thirty minutes to an hour per round.

**Access:** level 1 to read Google Alerts, the backlink tool's reports and the client's site; level 2 to draft notes in the mailbox or outreach tool; level 3 to send approved notes to contacts on the approved list for reclaim work.

## Steps

1. **Collect mentions (level 1).** Read Google Alerts for the brand, products and founders, and the backlink tool's new-mentions or new-pages report if it has one. List articles that name the client without linking.
2. **Collect broken link chances (level 1).** In the backlink tool or by checking known resource pages, find relevant pages that link to dead resources on the client's topic. Look up what each dead page said in the Wayback Machine.
3. **Filter.** Drop mentions that are critical of the client, news stories where a request would look tone-deaf, and broken links where the client's page doesn't cover the same need.
4. **Draft notes (level 2).** Use the `unlinked-mention-request` and `broken-link-replacement-pitch` prompts. One note per page, personal and short.
5. **Approve the reclaim list.** *Approval: the client approves the contacts for this round, or a standing reclaim list with a weekly limit.*
6. **Send (level 3).** Send approved notes within the limit, once each, with one follow-up only if agreed. Log every send and reply. Remove anyone who asks at once.
7. **Track.** Record outcomes in the tracking sheet; they feed the `monthly-link-review`.

## Outputs

- List of unlinked mentions and broken link chances with a keep or drop reason
- Drafted notes
- Sends and replies, logged
- Updated tracking sheet

## Needs the client's approval

- **Runs at level 3 within the limits, after the one-time confirmation:** sending approved reclaim notes to contacts on the approved reclaim list, within the weekly limit.
- **Always needs a new yes:** contacts not on the list, any second follow-up, any offer of payment, gifts or a link swap (which is declined in any case for followed links), and replying to a critical article.
