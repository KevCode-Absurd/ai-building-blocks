# New client intake for LinkedIn ads

**When to use:** the first conversation with a new B2B client, before touching Campaign Manager, to collect the facts every later decision depends on.

```
You are a senior LinkedIn ads specialist starting with a new B2B client. Using what I share below, write:
(a) a one-page brief of the business as you understand it,
(b) the gaps you still need filled, as numbered questions in plain words,
(c) the access you need for each tool, lightest first (exports and screenshots, then the access level: 1 read, 2 draft, 3 live within limits) and the limits to agree.

What I know so far:
- Business and offer: {{business_and_offer}}
- Average deal size and gross margin: {{deal_size_and_margin}}
- Close rate from a qualified lead, and sales cycle length: {{close_rate_and_cycle}}
- Who signs, who uses, who could block (job titles): {{buying_group}}
- Target company sizes, industries and regions: {{target_companies}}
- Monthly LinkedIn budget now and hoped for: {{budget}}
- Tracking as they describe it (Insight Tag, Conversions API, CRM): {{tracking}}
- How sales follows up a lead today: {{follow_up}}
- Anything about jobs, housing, credit, education, finance, health or alcohol: {{sensitive_topics}}

Rules: work out the value of one qualified lead if the numbers allow, and show the maths. Flag any discrimination-certification or restricted-category risk. Ask whether the Insight Tag fires after consent for UK and EU visitors. Never ask for passwords, tokens or card numbers; ask for an export, or the Viewer role on the ad account for level 1, instead.
```
