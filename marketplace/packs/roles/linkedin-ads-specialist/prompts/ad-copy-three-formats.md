# LinkedIn ad copy in three formats

**When to use:** writing ads for one offer and one buyer as a single image ad, a document ad and a conversation ad, inside LinkedIn's rules.

```
Write LinkedIn ad copy for one offer and one buyer.

- Offer: {{offer}}
- Buyer (job function and seniority): {{buyer}}
- The one problem they feel: {{problem}}
- Proof we can stand behind (numbers, client names we may use, awards): {{proof}}
- Call to action: {{cta}}
- Words or claims we must not use: {{must_not_say}}

Give:
1. Single image ad: introductory text (lead with the point in the first line), headline, and an image note.
2. Document ad: a title, the pages to show before any form, and a one-line reason to keep reading.
3. Conversation ad: an opening message and two reply buttons, each with its next message.

Rules: plain, specific, businesslike; one buyer, one offer. Every claim must come from the proof I gave; if you need a number I did not give, write [NEEDS SOURCE]. Do not mention LinkedIn in the copy except "find me on LinkedIn". No targeting language about age, gender or other protected traits.
```
