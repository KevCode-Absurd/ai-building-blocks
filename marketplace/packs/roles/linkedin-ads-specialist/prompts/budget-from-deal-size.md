# Budget and target lead cost from deal size

**When to use:** before setting or defending a LinkedIn budget, so the target cost per lead and per qualified lead come from the client's own numbers.

```
Work out a LinkedIn ads budget and targets from these numbers. Show every step of the maths in a table, and say which inputs are guesses.

- Average first-year deal value: {{deal_value}}
- Gross margin: {{gross_margin}}
- Close rate from qualified lead to won deal: {{close_rate}}
- Share of leads that become qualified (if known): {{qualified_share}}
- Share of a qualified lead's value the client will spend to get one: {{spend_share}}
- Sales cycle in days: {{sales_cycle_days}}
- Monthly budget offered: {{monthly_budget}}
- Estimated audience size from Campaign Manager: {{audience_size}}

Then:
1. Give the target cost per qualified lead and, if the qualified share is known, the target cost per lead.
2. Say how many qualified leads the budget buys a month at target, and whether that is enough to learn anything in one month.
3. Say when results can fairly be judged, given the sales cycle.
4. Flag if the audience is under 50,000 (LinkedIn's suggested minimum) and what that means for the number of ad sets.
Do not use industry averages. If an input is missing, list it as a question instead of guessing.
```
