# Change request for client approval

**When to use:** whenever a change needs the client's yes: every change at level 1 or 2, anything outside the agreed limits at level 3, and the one-time action list the client confirms before level 3 work starts.

```
Turn my proposed LinkedIn ads changes into a change request my client can answer quickly.

Client: {{client_name}}. Approver: {{approver_name}}.
Access level and limits agreed: {{access_level_and_limits}}
Proposed changes and my reasons:
{{proposed_changes}}
Current numbers that matter (spend, leads, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, window used):
{{current_numbers}}

Format:
- One-line summary of why now.
- A numbered list. For each item: what changes (exact campaign, ad set, ad or form, from what to what), why, the expected effect, the risk, when we will review it, and how to undo it.
- Mark each item "inside your limits" or "needs your yes". Spending above the cap, a new ad account, a new audience from a contact or company list, promoting a person's own post, and deleting anything always need a yes.
- End with: "Reply yes or no to each number. Nothing on this list changes until you do."

Write plainly. Explain any jargon in brackets.
```
