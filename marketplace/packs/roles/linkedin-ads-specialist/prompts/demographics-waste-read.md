# Find waste in a demographics export

**When to use:** after pasting a Campaign Manager demographics export, to see which job titles, seniorities and companies take money without producing qualified leads.

```
Here is my LinkedIn demographics export and my CRM summary. Find the waste.

Demographics export (job title, function, seniority, company, company size, industry, spend, impressions, clicks, leads):
{{demographics_export}}

CRM summary of LinkedIn leads by stage (names removed):
{{crm_summary}}

Date range and conversion window used: {{date_range_and_window}}
Who we sell to: {{target_buyer}}

Do this:
1. Group spend by seniority, function and company size; show a table with spend, leads and qualified leads for each group.
2. List the ten job titles with most spend and the ten with most qualified leads, side by side.
3. Name the groups to exclude or down-weight, with the money at stake over the period.
4. Give the three changes most likely to pay back, ranked, each with the expected effect, risk and how to undo it.
State what the data cannot tell us. Do not guess at numbers that are not in the export.
```
