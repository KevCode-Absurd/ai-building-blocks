# Interview: pulling out a LinkedIn ads specialist's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("can you give me a real example?"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour in a LinkedIn account you have never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you turn deal size, close rate and sales cycle into a budget and a target lead cost?**
   Why: captures the maths behind every plan.
3. **How do you map a buying group to LinkedIn targeting? Give a real example.**
   Why: separates job-title guessing from a method.
4. **When do you target by job title, and when by job function and seniority?**
   Why: a core judgement that shapes audience size and waste.
5. **What is the smallest audience you will run, and what do you do when the client's market is tiny?**
   Why: records how you handle the 300-member floor and narrow niches.
6. **Which exclusions do you add by default, and which have saved a client the most money?**
   Why: exclusions are where expert habits hide.
7. **How do you design a Lead Gen Form that filters out junk without killing volume?**
   Why: captures your field and question choices.
8. **What follow-up do you insist on from sales, and what happens when they don't do it?**
   Why: lead quality is half sales speed; this shows how you manage it.
9. **Have you used the qualified leads goal or fed CRM stages back to LinkedIn? What changed?**
   Why: records hands-on experience with the newest optimisation.
10. **How do you set up the Insight Tag and Conversions API, and how do you check they are not double counting?**
    Why: tracking habits protect every later decision.
11. **How do you handle cookie consent for the Insight Tag on UK and EU sites?**
    Why: shows whether you treat consent as part of the set-up.
12. **Which formats do you reach for first (single image, document, video, conversation, Thought Leader), and why?**
    Why: format judgement differs a lot between specialists.
13. **When do you choose maximum delivery, cost cap or manual bidding?**
    Why: bid strategy choices are a mark of experience.
14. **How do you read a demographics report? What do you look at first?**
    Why: your reading order becomes the agent's.
15. **How do you report LinkedIn results to a sales director who only cares about pipeline?**
    Why: shapes the voice and format of reports.
16. **How do you measure brand campaigns aimed at people who won't buy this year?**
    Why: separates long-term thinking from last-click habits.
17. **Tell me about a client who wanted the wrong thing (cheap leads, a tiny list, a vanity metric). How did you handle it?**
    Why: pushback skills keep clients on track.
18. **Tell me about an ad that got rejected or a targeting choice LinkedIn would not allow. What did you change?**
    Why: policy experience protects clients.
19. **What is the hardest LinkedIn job you have done, and what would you do differently?**
    Why: a real story is worth more than any checklist.
20. **What do you check before you press launch?**
    Why: becomes the agent's quality bar.
