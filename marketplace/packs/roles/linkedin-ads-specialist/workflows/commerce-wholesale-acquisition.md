# LinkedIn wholesale and corporate gifting acquisition

Use when a commerce brand explicitly wants LinkedIn acquisition of stockists, company buyers or corporate gifting orders. This is not a generic SaaS demo funnel. Esmee owns paid acquisition; Mei (mei-wholesale-sales) owns commercial qualification, quotes and wholesale selling. A consumer-only ad request stays with its channel buyer. Jared keeps cross-channel allocation.

## Ground the commercial offer

Read real products, brand proof, available stock, wholesale/gifting eligibility, minimum quantities, personalization limits, production/shipping lead times, payment terms and returns. Label unapproved terms as proposals. Do not promote bulk discounts, gifting services or fulfillment promises merely because the brand sells retail products.

Choose a company/customer hypothesis from evidence: buyer function, organization type, need, order scale, geography, purchasing timing and exclusions. A professional profile is not proof of purchasing authority or a qualified company. Do not buy, scrape or upload contact lists without appropriate scope and data rights.

## Trace acquisition to kept orders

Define a qualified company with Mei before buying leads. Keep separate counts for inquiries, deduplicated companies, accepted opportunities, quotes, accepted orders, delivered/kept orders and contribution. Each stage needs a timestamp, source, owner and reason for loss. Do not present all contacts as distinct companies or all quoted revenue as booked revenue.

Calculate cost per accepted opportunity and contribution after acquisition and servicing costs when the actual data supports it. Derive scenario ceilings from close probability, landed contribution, payment timing and stock; unknown close rates are assumptions with sensitivity ranges, not evidence of profit. Keep quoted, invoiced, collected and refunded cash distinct. Low cost per lead is not the success criterion.

## Make the campaign and sales handoff

Deliver a test plan, audience rationale, editable ad concepts/copy, a native lead-form or landing-page specification and an explicit handoff record to Mei. Collect only the fields needed to decide fit and route a response, such as company, role, order use, approximate quantity and timing, subject to current supported fields and privacy requirements. Form minimization must preserve commercially useful qualification.

Assign response ownership and an achievable service target agreed with the sales team. Include what Mei receives, how accepted/rejected opportunities are returned, what happens when capacity or stock is exhausted, and how the owner sees recurring objections. Follow-up messages remain drafts unless sending is authorized. A saved form is not an installed CRM integration or a delivered customer message.

## Inspect actual platform capabilities

Discover available LinkedIn tools or use dated Campaign Manager/CRM/order exports. Inspect exact account identity, currency/timezone, permissions, current fields, API version, reporting definitions and format limits. Do not substitute a Meta connector or assume that a listed feature is connected. Verify online/offline event support, event identifiers and deduplication from actual settings before designing uploads. Never send private customer data to a provider merely to test this workflow.

Maintain operating history under `context/media-buying/linkedin/<account-id>/`; the shared brand brain is read-only evidence for this account's work, not another account's approval. Record intended operation, exact objects, budget units/kind, cap, end date and valid current authority. Reuse unchanged scoped grants. Complete local build files while capability or authority is missing; do not repeatedly request already supplied approval.

For supported authorized writes, show a concrete diff, execute only within scope, retain returned IDs and verify fresh readback. Reconcile partial success before retrying. Distinguish local plan, provider draft, review/active status, delivery, accepted opportunity and realized order contribution. Keep the original four consumer media buyers and their grants separate.

Keep canonical intervention records and provider receipts in `jobs/<run-id>/`; platform context indexes those job links instead of duplicating active state. For a material executed change, use the shared `intervention/v1` record and `org-context.mjs intervention-check` where available. Keep proposed lessons separate from approved operating rules.

## Completion and review

The founder gets a channel-fit decision, an editable campaign/form/creative package, commercial scenario arithmetic, a measurement map and a usable Mei handoff. If real results are supplied, include a source-backed cohort readout and next decision; otherwise report a test design, not a measured winning channel. Recheck stock, cash and sales capacity before recommending expansion.
