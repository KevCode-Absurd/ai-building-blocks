# Weekly optimisation routine

**Cadence:** once a week, same day, plus a deeper monthly look at pipeline. About 45 to 75 minutes for a small account.

**Goal:** keep spend on the people who can buy, catch tracking and follow-up problems early, and make a few reasoned changes within the access level and limits.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 6 need level 1. Step 7 can use level 2. Step 9 needs level 3.

1. **Pull the data** (tool: Campaign Manager export and demographics export, last 7 days and last 28 days). Pull CRM leads by stage for the same dates.
2. **Check tracking first.** Conversion counts by source in the breakdown, any sudden drop, form test still landing in the CRM. If something broke, stop and raise it.
3. **Who saw the ads** (prompt: `demographics-waste-read`). Spend by seniority, function and company size. New waste?
4. **Lead quality.** Leads, qualified leads and cost per qualified lead by ad set. Did sales call every lead within one working day? If not, raise it before changing ads.
5. **Ads and formats.** Click-through, form open and completion rates by ad. Is any ad tiring (falling click-through at steady reach)? Add a new concept to the next brief.
6. **Pacing.** Month-to-date spend against plan; cost caps that under-spend for several days are too tight.
7. **Draft changes** (prompt: `change-request-for-approval`; level 2 if built as drafts). At most three, each with reason, effect, risk, review date and undo.
8. **Send the weekly note and change list.** Mark each item "inside your limits" or "needs your yes".
9. **Make the changes** (level 3; tool: Campaign Manager). Only items inside the limits or with a yes. Log each.
10. **Monthly:** read pipeline and revenue on the Measurement Insights page or the Revenue Attribution Report if CRM Sync is on; compare with the CRM; review windows against the sales cycle.

## Outputs

- Weekly note (one page) and monthly pipeline read.
- Change request with numbered items.
- Updated change log.

## Approval

**At level 3, inside the limits confirmed at the start:** budget moves within the cap and step size, pausing ads that hit the agreed stop rule, adding exclusions from the agreed list, and adding ads from the approved copy.

**Always needs a new yes:** spend above the cap, a new campaign objective or ad account, a new audience from a contact or company list, a bid strategy change not on the list, form wording changes, deleting anything, and any rejected ad or policy warning.

Reading data, writing notes and drafting changes need no approval.
