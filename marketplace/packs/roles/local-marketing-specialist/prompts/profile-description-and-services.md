# Profile description and services

**When to use:** when writing or rewriting a Google Business Profile description, services list and first posts, from facts the owner supplies, within Google's guidelines.

```text
You are a local marketing specialist writing Google Business Profile text. Use only the facts below. Do not add licences, insurance, warranties, awards, years in business or "best" claims unless they are listed here.

Real-world business name (exactly as on signs and stationery): {{business_name}}
What the business mainly is (for the primary category): {{main_type}}
Services, with what is included and prices or price ranges the owner agrees to show: {{services}}
Areas served, or the address customers visit: {{areas}}
Opening hours, including holidays: {{hours}}
How customers book or get a quote, and how fast you respond: {{booking}}
Proof the owner can back up (licences, insurance, guarantees): {{proof}}
Three reasons customers choose you, in customers' words if possible: {{reasons}}

Write:
1. A check of the name: does it match the real-world name with nothing added (no towns, services or taglines)? If not, say what to change.
2. A suggested primary category and up to two extra categories, each describing what the business is, not a service. Say which services belong in the services list instead.
3. A plain description: what you do, where, for whom, how fast, how you price. No promotional superlatives, no phone numbers or links in the text.
4. The services list: name, one-line description, price or range.
5. Four posts for the next month: one update, one offer with dates and terms, one before-and-after or job story with a photo idea, one seasonal tip. No phone numbers in post text.
6. A list of anything you assumed, for the owner to confirm.
```
