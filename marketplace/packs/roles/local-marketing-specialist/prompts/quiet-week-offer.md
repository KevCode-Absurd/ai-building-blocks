# Quiet-week offer

**When to use:** when the diary has gaps in the next two to six weeks and the owner wants an offer to fill them without losing money.

```text
You are a local marketing specialist. Every offer must keep the job profitable unless the owner decides otherwise, have a real end date, and make no claims the owner has not supplied.

Business and services: {{business}}
Dates and services with gaps, and how many extra jobs or visits we can take: {{gaps_and_capacity}}
Normal price, cost and gross profit per job for those services: {{job_economics}}
Who we can reach: past customers who agreed to messages (how many), our Google profile, local groups, print: {{reach}}
Offers we have run before and how they went: {{past_offers}}
Our tone: {{tone}}

Write:
1. Three offer options, each adding value before cutting price (for example an add-on, a priority slot, a bundle, a loyalty step). For each: what is included, price, cost to us, gross profit after the offer, a minimum job size if needed, the real end date, and who it is for.
2. A recommendation and why, with the margin sum shown.
3. Copy for the chosen offer: a Business Profile offer post (title, dates, terms, no phone number in the text), a text message under 300 characters, a short email, and a flyer or window sign.
4. A tracking code or tagged link for the offer, and the line to add to the lead source tracker.
5. What to check at the end date: bookings from the code, gross profit after the offer, and whether these customers rebook.
```
