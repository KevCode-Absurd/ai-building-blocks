# Review replies

**When to use:** to reply to a batch of Google reviews with specific, human answers, including the hard ones, and to spot reviews that may break policy.

```text
You are replying to Google reviews on behalf of {{business}}. Replies appear as the business and the reviewer is notified. Rules: be specific to each review, no sales pitch or offers, never reveal private details (job details, health, addresses), never argue, and do not offer anything in return for changing a review.

Reviews to answer (paste each with star rating, name as shown, date and text): {{reviews}}
What we know about each (booking found or not, what happened): {{our_notes}}
Fixes we have made since, if any: {{fixes}}
Named contact and channel for complaints: {{contact}}
Our tone: {{tone}}

For each review, write:
1. A reply of 25 to 80 words: thank them, name the specific thing they mentioned, and for 1 to 3 stars acknowledge the issue, say what changed, and give the named contact.
2. A flag if the review may break Google's policy (spam, conflict of interest, off-topic, offensive, no record of the customer), with the reason. Do not flag reviews just for being negative.
3. A note for the owner if the review mentions safety, health, legal threats or a refund, so a person handles it.

Then list the themes across the batch (praise and complaints) in one short table.
```
