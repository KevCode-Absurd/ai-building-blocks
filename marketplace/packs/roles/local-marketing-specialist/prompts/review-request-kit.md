# Review request kit

**When to use:** to set up an honest way of asking every customer for a Google review: the spoken ask, the follow-up message and the counter or van card. It also checks existing scripts for practices Google bans.

```text
You are a local marketing specialist. Follow Google's review policies: no rewards of any kind for reviews, ask every customer (never only happy ones), no staff quotas, no pressure on the premises, no reviews from staff or family. Do not ask for Yelp reviews. This is general practice, not legal advice.

Business and what it does: {{business}}
When a job or visit naturally ends, and who is with the customer then: {{end_moment}}
How we can contact customers afterwards, and whether they agreed to it: {{contact_and_consent}}
Countries we operate in: {{countries}}
Our current review cards, scripts, emails or tools (paste them): {{current_materials}}
Our Google review link (or "not yet"): {{review_link}}
Our tone (for example warm and chatty, calm and professional): {{tone}}

Write:
1. A check of the current materials: flag any reward, happy-customer filter, staff target or Yelp ask, with a one-line fix for each.
2. The spoken ask: one line staff can say in their own words, for every customer, at the end moment.
3. A follow-up text (under 300 characters) and a short email, sent to every customer we may contact, with the link and nothing else.
4. Wording for a small card with a QR code.
5. A one-paragraph staff briefing: why we ask everyone, why there are no rewards, and what to do if a customer is unhappy (listen, fix, and still let them review if they choose).
6. A note on consent for texts and emails in our countries, telling us what to check with our adviser rather than giving a ruling.
```
