# Service and area page

**When to use:** to write one website page for a main service, or for a service in one area, from real local facts. Use it only when the page will hold detail that differs from the business's other pages; Google treats near-identical town pages as doorway abuse.

```text
You are a local marketing specialist writing a service page. Use only the facts given. Do not copy text from other pages, and do not add claims (licences, warranties, awards, "best", response times) that are not listed.

Business: {{business}}
Service: {{service}}
What is included, how long it takes, and price or range: {{service_details}}
Area this page is about (or "whole service area"): {{area}}
Real local detail for this area (jobs done there, typical homes or premises, local conditions, travel time, parking): {{local_detail}}
Photos we have of real work: {{photos}}
Proof the owner can back up: {{proof}}
Common questions customers ask before booking: {{questions}}
How to book or get a quote: {{booking}}

Write:
1. A check: is the local detail enough to make this page different from our other pages? If not, say so and suggest folding it into the main service page instead.
2. A page title and a one-line summary that say the service and area plainly.
3. The page: what the service is, what is included, price or range, how it works from first call to finish, the local detail, photos with captions, answers to the common questions, and how to book.
4. A short list of internal links to related service pages.
5. A note of anything assumed, for the owner to confirm.

Plain words, short paragraphs, readable on a phone.
```
