# Interview: local marketing specialist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your first week with a new local client, from the first call to the first change on their listing.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and order of work.
2. **What do you check first on a Google Business Profile, and what problem do you find most often?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's audit order.
3. **How do you choose the primary category when a business does several things?**
   Why it matters: category choice is a high-impact judgement call.
4. **How do you handle a service-area business that wants to rank in towns far from its base?**
   Why it matters: tests honesty about distance and the doorway-page line.
5. **When is an area page worth writing, and when do you refuse?**
   Why it matters: captures their standard for local content.

## Reviews and reputation

6. **What exact words do you give staff to ask for reviews, and when in the visit do they say them?**
   Why it matters: real scripts beat generic advice.
7. **A client wants to give a discount for reviews, or send unhappy customers to a private form. What do you say?**
   Why it matters: tests how they hold the line on Google's rules and the law.
8. **Show me a reply you wrote to a harsh one-star review. Why did you write it that way?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real standard for hard replies.
9. **When do you report a review, and what happened the last time you did?**
   Why it matters: separates real policy breaks from wishful removal requests.

## Leads, ads and tracking

10. **How do you find out which marketing actually makes a local firm's phone ring?**
    Why it matters: becomes the lead-tracking habit.
11. **How do you set up call tracking without upsetting the listing?**
    Why it matters: captures their practice on a grey area in Google's guidelines.
12. **When do you recommend Local Services Ads, and when do you tell a client to wait?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's paid-ads judgement.
13. **What have you learned from Local Services Ads lead quality and disputes in your country?**
    Why it matters: rules on credits and recordings differ by region.

## Seasons, offers and access

14. **Pick a trade you know well. Walk me through its year: busy months, quiet months, and what you do in each.**
    Why it matters: seasonal knowledge is the core of this role.
15. **How do you design an offer for a quiet month without training customers to wait for discounts?**
    Why it matters: protects the client's margin.
16. **When a client lets you edit their listing or post for them, what limits do you agree, and what do you always ask first?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent behaves at level 3.

## Mistakes, clients and stories

17. **What are the three mistakes you see most on local listings set up by owners, agencies or AI tools?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
18. **Tell me about a profile suspension or verification problem you fixed. What caused it and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: real recovery stories teach caution.
19. **Tell me about the local client whose results changed the most. What made the difference?**
    Why it matters: a real story shows which levers matter.
20. **Which kinds of local business do you know best, and what is different about marketing each?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty".
21. **What words or claims do you never put on a listing, a post or a flyer?**
    Why it matters: builds the "words I avoid" list in personality.md.
