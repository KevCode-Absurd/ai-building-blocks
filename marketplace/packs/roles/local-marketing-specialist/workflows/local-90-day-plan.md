# Local 90-day plan

**Trigger:** a new local client, or an owner who says "I don't know what's working". A local growth lead with the Traction pack runs the 19-channel Bullseye from `variants/local.md` alongside this; this workflow covers the local basics every plan needs.

**Cadence:** one 13-week cycle, then repeat with a fresh audit.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Until the owner grants more, everything runs at L1 and the owner makes the changes.

## Steps

1. **Intake, L1 (week 1).** Services, prices, areas, hours, capacity, gross profit per job, busy and quiet months, how the last 20 customers found the business, who answers the phone. Ask which access level the owner grants for the Business Profile, the website, the booking system, any ad account and the email or text tool, with limits, and write it down as "Access I have".
2. **Start the lead source tracker (skill: local-lead-tracking), L1 then L2 (week 1).** Draft the sheet or booking field and the "How did you hear about us?" list; the owner briefs staff.
3. **Audit the profile (skill: business-profile-audit), L1 (week 2).** Rule check, rival comparison, fix list with risk and access level.
   **Approval point 1:** the owner approves the fix list. Name, address, primary category and phone changes get a separate, explicit yes.
4. **Fix the profile, L3 or the owner (weeks 2 to 3).** At L3 with a manager seat, make the approved edits within the limits, one group at a time, and log each. Nothing changes on name, address or category while verification is pending.
5. **Start the review habit (skill: review-request-and-reply-system), L2 then L3 (week 3).** Kit drafted at L2; replies posted at L3 within the agreed star levels, or by the owner.
6. **Website basics (prompt: service-area-page), L2 (weeks 3 to 6).** One page per main service; area pages only where there is real local detail. Drafts in the website's draft state or a document.
   **Approval point 2:** the owner approves each page before it is published.
7. **Plan the season (skill: seasonal-offer-calendar), L1 (week 4).** The next three months in detail, the rest of the year in outline.
8. **Decide on paid (prompt: local-services-ads-check), L1 (week 5).** Go, wait or stop on Local Services Ads or search ads, based on phone handling, category availability and the allowable cost per booked job.
   **Approval point 3:** the owner approves any paid test: budget cap, dates, and the access level for the ad account.
9. **Run the weeks (workflow: weekly-local-routine), weeks 5 to 13.** Posts, replies, tracker check, offer delivery.
10. **Review at week 13, L1.** Booked jobs by source, cost per booked job, new reviews and rating, profile actions against the first month, what to keep, change or stop.

## Outputs

- "Access I have" record.
- Lead source tracker and weekly sheet.
- Profile audit, fix list and action log.
- Review kit and reply templates.
- Service and area page drafts.
- Seasonal calendar and first offers.
- Paid test decision.
- Week-13 review.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access levels and limits for each tool, at the start and whenever they change.
- The profile fix list (point 1), with a separate yes for name, address, primary category and phone.
- Every page before it goes live (point 2).
- Every offer, price, discount and deadline customers will see.
- Any paid test and its budget (point 3); at L3, one yes to the action list before the first live action, and a fresh yes for anything outside the limits.
- Which customers may receive texts or emails.
