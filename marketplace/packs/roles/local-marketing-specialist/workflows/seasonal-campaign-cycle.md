# Seasonal campaign cycle

**Trigger:** six to eight weeks before a season the calendar marks as busy or quiet, or when the owner sees gaps in the diary.

**Cadence:** once per season, typically four times a year; a quiet-week offer can run inside it when gaps appear.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Until the owner grants more, everything runs at L1.

## Steps

1. **Look back, L1.** Last year's bookings for the same season, last season's offer results (bookings from the code, gross profit after the offer, rebooking), and current capacity.
2. **Set the season's job (skill: seasonal-offer-calendar), L1.** Book ahead before a peak, protect capacity during it, fill gaps after it.
3. **Design the offer, L1.** Value before discount, margin check, minimum job size, real end date, tracking code. For a quiet patch inside the season, use the quiet-week-offer prompt.
   **Approval point 1:** the owner approves the offer, price, terms and end date.
4. **Write the pieces, L2.** Business Profile offer post and two or three updates; an email and a text to past customers who agreed to messages; a flyer, window sign or postcard for one area with its own code; any website page update. Photo checklist for the team.
5. **Check the pieces, L1.** No claims the owner has not supplied; prices and dates match everywhere; no phone numbers in profile post text; consent checked for every message list; tracking codes in place.
   **Approval point 2:** the owner approves the final pieces and the send and post dates.
6. **Go live, L3 or the owner.** At L3, after one yes to the action list (posts, sends, dates, audiences), publish on schedule and log each action. A new audience, a bigger list or a changed price needs a fresh yes. At L1 and L2, the owner publishes and sends.
7. **Ask for reviews in the busy weeks (skill: review-request-and-reply-system), L1.** Remind staff of the ask; check the card stock.
8. **Watch weekly (workflow: weekly-local-routine), L1.** Bookings from the code, enquiries by source, capacity.
9. **Close the season, L1.** Bookings, gross profit after the offer, cost per booked job, new reviews, what to repeat next year. Add the lesson to the calendar.

## Outputs

- Season brief with the job, the offer and its margin check.
- Profile posts, email, text and print pieces.
- Tracking codes and tracker lines.
- Action log for everything published or sent.
- End-of-season summary and calendar notes.

## Needs the client's approval

- The offer, price, terms and end date (point 1).
- The final pieces and dates (point 2).
- Which customers receive messages, based on the consent the business holds.
- At L3, one yes to the action list before the first post or send, and a fresh yes for anything outside the limits.
