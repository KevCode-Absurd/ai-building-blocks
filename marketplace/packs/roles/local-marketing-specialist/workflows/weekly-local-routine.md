# Weekly local routine

**Cadence:** weekly, about 15 to 30 minutes of the owner's time, plus a longer monthly check in the first week of each month.

**Trigger:** the profile basics are fixed and the lead source tracker is running.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Until the owner grants more, everything runs at L1 and the owner posts and replies.

## Steps

1. **Read the tracker, L1 (Monday).** New enquiries by source, booked jobs, "don't know" answers. If "don't know" is growing, flag a staff refresher.
2. **Read the action log, L1.** Last week's posts, replies and any live changes, with results. Errors, rejected posts or a profile warning go to the top of the note.
3. **Reply to reviews (prompt: review-replies), L2 or L3.** Draft replies to every new review. At L3, post replies within the agreed star levels; send anything outside them (low stars, safety, legal, refund) to the owner. Flag reviews that may break policy for reporting.
4. **Post on the profile (skill: seasonal-offer-calendar), L2 or L3.** One update or offer from the calendar. At L2 the owner posts the draft; at L3 post it on the agreed day and check its status (live, pending, not approved).
5. **Check the profile, L1.** Hours (especially holidays coming up), any edits suggested by Google or the public, new photos from customers.
6. **Check Local Services Ads if running, L1.** New leads, status, which booked, missed calls. Lead disputes or budget changes at L3 within limits, or by the owner.
7. **Send the weekly note, L1.** Five lines: enquiries and booked jobs by source, new reviews, what was posted, what needs the owner's yes, next week's plan.
   **Approval point:** the owner approves next week's drafts and anything outside the limits.

**Monthly (first week):**

8. **Download profile performance, L1.** Calls, directions, website clicks, bookings; compare with last month and the same month last year if available.
9. **Cost per booked job by source (skill: local-lead-tracking), L1.** Against the owner's allowable figure.
10. **Review count and themes, L1.** New reviews, rating, reply rate, repeated complaints to pass to the owner.
11. **Next month's offers (prompt: quiet-week-offer if gaps), L1 then L2.** Margin checks and drafts.

## Outputs

- Weekly five-line note and drafts.
- Replies posted or drafted, and policy flags.
- One profile post a week, live or drafted.
- Action log entries (what, where, when, result).
- Monthly lead and review summary with cost per booked job.

## Needs the client's approval

- The weekly drafts (at L2 the owner posts them; at L3 the owner confirms the month's action list once, then posting follows the calendar).
- Replies outside the agreed star levels, and any reply that touches safety, health, legal threats or refunds.
- Any new offer, price, discount or deadline.
- Any LSA budget, bid or lead-type change outside the limits.
