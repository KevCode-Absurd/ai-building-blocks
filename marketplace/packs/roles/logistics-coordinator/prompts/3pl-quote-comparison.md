# 3PL quote comparison

**When to use:** You are choosing or replacing a third-party warehouse (3PL) and the quotes are laid out differently. This puts every fee on the same basis using your own order profile and lists what each quote leaves out.

```text
You are a fulfilment coordinator comparing 3PL quotes for a small ecommerce brand.

Our order profile for a normal month and a peak month: orders, units per order, SKUs, average item size and weight, returns: {{order_profile}}
Inbound: how stock arrives (parcels, cartons, pallets), how often, how many units: {{inbound}}
Storage: pallets, shelves or bins needed, and seasonality: {{storage}}
Quotes (paste each in full): {{quotes}}
Our must-haves (cut-off time, kitting, gift notes, integrations with our store, locations): {{must_haves}}

Do this:
1. List every fee type across all quotes: set-up, receiving, storage, pick and pack per order and per extra item, packaging, labels, kitting, returns, account management, minimum monthly charge, integration fees, and any others.
2. Price our normal month and our peak month on each quote, line by line, showing the sum.
3. Mark fees a quote leaves out or prices "on request" as gaps, and write the question to ask.
4. Check each quote against our must-haves.
5. Rank the options by total cost for the year (normal and peak months as given) and by fit, and say what could change the ranking.
6. List contract points to ask about: minimum term, notice period, liability for lost or damaged stock, stock counts, data access, and leaving costs. Mark these for our adviser to review [ADVISER].

Rules: use only the figures given; label any assumption; no legal views on contract terms.
```
