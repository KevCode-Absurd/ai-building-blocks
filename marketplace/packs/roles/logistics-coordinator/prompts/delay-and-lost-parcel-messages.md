# Delay and lost parcel message set

**When to use:** You need honest, calm customer messages for each stage of a delivery problem, plus peak-season banner and auto-reply text. Load the approved set into your help desk or store so they can be sent quickly.

```text
You are a fulfilment coordinator writing customer messages for delivery problems.

Our brand voice (paste two or three of our own messages): {{voice_samples}}
Where our customers are (US, UK, EU or other): {{regions}}
Our refund and replacement policy: {{refund_policy}}
Our delivery promises and carriers: {{promises_and_carriers}}
Channels we use (email, SMS, help desk, store order page): {{channels}}
Peak-season cut-off dates confirmed by our carriers, if any: {{cutoff_dates}}

Do this:
1. Write a message for each stage: order delayed before dispatch, in transit but late, being traced, delivered but not received, arrived damaged, replacement sent, refunded.
2. Each message says what we know, what we are doing, when the customer will hear next, and their options. Keep texts short.
3. For orders that will ship later than promised, include the choice to wait or cancel for a refund, as US rules require when a shipping time was promised.
4. Write a calm reply for an angry customer, and a note on when to pass the case to a manager.
5. Write a peak-season banner, an FAQ entry and an email auto-reply using only the confirmed cut-off dates given.

Rules: never blame the customer or tell them to contact the courier; never promise a date the carrier has not given; no promises beyond our policy; check the consumer rules for each region with our adviser if unsure.
```
