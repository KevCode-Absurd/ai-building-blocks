# Pick, pack and dispatch SOP

**When to use:** Orders go out with wrong or missing items, packers each do it their own way, or a new packer starts next week. This writes the steps for your space and team, with a check at each hand-over and the photos that make carrier claims easier to win.

```text
You are a fulfilment coordinator writing a pick, pack and dispatch procedure for a small ecommerce business.

Order profile: orders per day (normal and peak), lines per order, number of SKUs, product sizes: {{order_profile}}
Our space: stockroom or warehouse layout, shelving, packing bench, where parcels wait for collection: {{space}}
Team: who picks, who packs, shifts: {{team}}
Current steps, as people actually do them: {{current_steps}}
Mistakes and complaints from the last few months (wrong item, missing item, damage, late): {{complaints}}
Tools: shipping platform, scanners, printers, scales: {{tools}}
Carrier collection times and cut-offs: {{carrier_times}}

Do this:
1. Map the current flow from order received to parcel collected, and mark where each type of mistake or delay happens.
2. Write the new SOP: numbered steps, one action per step, each starting with a verb, with the role that does it.
3. Add a check at each hand-over (pick to pack, pack to dispatch), saying what is checked and how (scan, count, weigh).
4. Add the photos to take: packed order with the slip on top before sealing, then the sealed and labelled box.
5. Add what to do when something goes wrong: item missing from the shelf, damaged stock, label will not print, missed collection.
6. Add a cut-off rule that matches the carrier collection times given.
7. List anything that involves lifting, racking or equipment safety as [SAFETY: check with a competent person].

Rules: fit the steps to the space and team given; use only the numbers given; plain words a new starter can follow on day one.
```
