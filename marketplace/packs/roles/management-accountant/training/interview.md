# Training interview: Management accountant

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, staff pay by name, account numbers or figures that could identify a client; anonymise every story.

1. **Which kinds of businesses do you produce management accounts for, in which countries, and on which software?**
   Why it matters: sector, region and software decide which costs matter, which reports exist and which rules the agent must point to.

2. **Walk me through your monthly cycle, from the day the books are reconciled to the meeting with the owner.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work, the checks the expert never skips and where time goes.

3. **What is on page one of your management pack, and what did you remove over the years?**
   Why it matters: page one is what owners read; the expert's choices teach the agent what earns its place.

4. **How do you work out a true labour cost per hour, and what do owners usually leave out?**
   Why it matters: the labour rate drives every job cost and quote; the expert's method is the most reusable thing they know.

5. **How do you choose an overhead driver, and when have you changed one?**
   Why it matters: captures when a single rate is good enough and when it hides loss-making work.

6. **Tell me about a job, client or product everyone thought was profitable that was not. How did you find out?**
   Why it matters: real stories teach the agent which patterns to look for first.

7. **How do you flex a budget, and how do you explain the activity and spending split to an owner who has never heard of it?**
   Why it matters: the explanation is as important as the maths; the expert's words make the agent clear.

8. **Which variance causes do you check first when a cost line is over budget?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a search order that finds causes in the data instead of guessing.

9. **How do you build a product cost stack for an online seller, and which costs do platform reports miss?**
   Why it matters: records the cost lines the expert insists on and where to find each one.

10. **How do you check whether a planned discount or sale will pay?**
    Why it matters: owners ask this often; the expert's method and fallback offers make the agent useful in one answer.

11. **For builders: how do you build the WIP schedule, and how do you handle estimates the project managers have not updated?**
    Why it matters: WIP figures depend on judgement about estimates; the expert's checks keep the schedule honest.

12. **For service firms: how do you report utilisation and effective rates without turning them into a staff ranking?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's line between pricing problems and people decisions.

13. **For charities: how do you keep restricted money apart and allocate shared costs in a way funders accept?**
    Why it matters: captures the allocation rules and grant tracking the expert has seen work.

14. **Which money controls do you check in a small business, and which cheap fix do you recommend most?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which controls matter most when there is no finance team.

15. **Tell me about a time a control check found something odd. How did you raise it without accusing anyone?**
    Why it matters: teaches tone and escalation, which matter as much as the finding.

16. **Which KPIs and ratios do you give lenders, and how do you define them?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent tested definitions and the lender questions to prepare for.

17. **How have recent reporting changes (for example lease accounting, new subtotals) changed the numbers you report?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert bridges old and new figures so covenants and trends stay comparable.

18. **Where do you draw the line between your work and the external accountant's or auditor's?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's handoff points for tax, standards and filed accounts.

19. **What do you do when an owner wants the numbers to look better for the bank?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert holds the line kindly and offers honest alternatives.

20. **Which tools, connectors and spreadsheet habits save you the most time, and what do you never let software do unchecked?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's tool use and its review rules for automated or AI changes.

21. **What does a great month-end look like for a client one year after you started, and how do you measure it?**
    Why it matters: defines success in the expert's terms, which the agent uses to judge its own work.
