# Write a customer interview guide that gets the truth

**When to use:** You are about to talk to potential or past buyers and want questions about what they actually did, not polite opinions about your idea, plus a way to recruit them and record what you learn.

```text
You are a market research analyst writing a customer interview guide. The goal is honest learning about real past behaviour, not a sales pitch.

The idea or product, in one sentence: {{idea}}
Who we want to talk to (role, situation, recent experience of the problem): {{target_people}}
What we most need to learn (the riskiest assumptions): {{learning_goals}}
How we can reach them: {{recruiting_channels}}
Interview length and format (call, video, in person): {{format}}

Do this:
1. Write a screener of three to five questions that confirms the person has faced the problem recently.
2. Write a short, honest recruiting message: who we are, that this is research and not a sales call, how long it takes, any thank-you gift, and that they can say no.
3. Write the consent script: purpose, recording (only with permission), how notes are stored and for how long, and that they can stop at any time.
4. Write the guide in sections: warm-up; the last time the problem happened (what triggered it, what they did, who was involved, what it cost in time or money); what they use now and what they dislike about it; what they have tried and paid for; how they decide and who signs off.
5. For each section, give one main question and two follow-ups that ask for specific past examples ("Tell me about the last time..."). No hypotheticals like "Would you use...?" and no describing our idea until the final minutes, if at all.
6. List the signals of real demand to note: money or time already spent on the problem, workarounds built, urgency, and a commitment at the end (an intro, a pilot, a pre-order).
7. Give a note-taking template: facts (what they did), quotes (short, exact), pains, commitments, and our open questions.
8. Say how to know when to stop: when new interviews stop adding new themes for this group.

Output:
- Screener.
- Recruiting message.
- Consent script.
- Interview guide with follow-ups.
- Demand signals checklist.
- Note template.

Rules: never record without permission; collect only the personal details needed; keep research and selling apart, and ask before any sales follow-up. If interviewing anyone under 18 or in a sensitive area (health, money troubles), ask a qualified adviser about consent and safeguards first.
```
