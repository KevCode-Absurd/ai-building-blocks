# Training interview: Market research analyst

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, participants' personal details or confidential figures; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of research questions do clients bring you most, in which sectors and countries?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the data sources and rules it must know first.

2. **How do you turn a vague request ("is there a market for this?") into a decision you can research?**
   Why it matters: framing is where most research succeeds or fails; the expert's questions become the agent's intake.

3. **Walk me through how you size a market, step by step, for a recent project.**
   Why it matters: captures the real method, the sources trusted and the checks that catch bad totals.

4. **Which official or public data sources do you rely on, and which do you avoid?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a tested source list and the expert's warnings about weak ones.

5. **When top-down and bottom-up sizes disagree, what do you do?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent how to find the input that breaks the model instead of averaging.

6. **How do you keep facts, estimates and guesses apart in a report the client will skim?**
   Why it matters: records the formats that make uncertainty visible without burying the answer.

7. **How do you decide between interviews, a survey and a live test?**
   Why it matters: method choice decides cost and truth; the expert's rules guide the agent's first recommendation.

8. **What makes a survey question bad, and what is your favourite fix?**
   Why it matters: captures the wording habits that prevent biased results.

9. **How do you recruit survey respondents or interviewees, and how do you spot fake or careless ones?**
   Why it matters: sample quality is now a major risk; the expert's screening tricks are directly reusable.

10. **How do you run a pricing study, and how far do you trust what people say they would pay?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert links stated-preference methods to live tests.

11. **Tell me about an interview that changed your mind about a market. What did the person say or do?**
    Why it matters: real stories teach the agent which signals of demand are worth more than praise.

12. **How do you research competitors, and where do you draw the ethical line?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's boundaries on sources, deception and confidential information.

13. **How do you segment a market so the segments actually change what the client does?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's test for a useful segment versus a decorative persona.

14. **How do you design a smoke test or pre-order test, and what pass line do you set?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert sets thresholds from the business model and handles taking money.

15. **What do you do when results are bad news for the client's favourite idea?**
    Why it matters: teaches tone and firmness, so the agent does not soften weak evidence.

16. **How do you use AI or synthetic respondents today, and what do you refuse to use them for?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's disclosure habits and limits on AI-generated data.

17. **What consent, privacy and data-retention steps do you always take?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a practical checklist that keeps participants safe.

18. **For location or export work: which factors and weights do you start with, and what do you check on the ground?**
    Why it matters: captures specialist know-how for site and country choices.

19. **For property work: how do you pick comparables and set adjustments, and what do you never say about an area?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's adjustment logic and fair housing habits.

20. **How do you present a recommendation so the client acts on it?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's report structure and the next-test habit.

21. **Which mistakes did you make early in your career that you now check for every time?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's hard lessons into the agent's quality checks.
