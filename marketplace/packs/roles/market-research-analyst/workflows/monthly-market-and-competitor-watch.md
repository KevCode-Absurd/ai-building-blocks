# Monthly market and competitor watch

**Cadence:** Monthly, in the first week of the month. Also triggered when a rival launches, cuts prices or starts a big campaign, or when an official data release changes the market size used in plans.

**Access:** Confirm the access level for each tool at the start ("Access I have"); default to level 1 (read). Log every action.

## Steps

1. **Collect rivals' changes (day 1 to 2).** For each tracked rival: pricing and offer pages, new products, terms, public ad library entries, emails received as a subscriber, new public reviews. Capture and date each item. *Level 1; public sources and ordinary-customer access only.*
2. **Update the change log (day 2).** Use step 8 of the `competitor-research` skill: date, rival, what changed, source, needs a response or not. *Level 2: update the shared log as a draft for review.*
3. **Review mining (day 2).** New reviews for the client and each rival: praise and complaint themes with counts and short labelled quotes. Note any new complaint no rival answers. *Level 1.*
4. **Market data check (day 3).** Check the "What changes fast" table in `knowledge.md`: new official releases (Census, BLS, ONS, Eurostat), code updates (ESOMAR, MRS, Insights Association), and rule changes on pricing or pre-orders. If a release changes an input in the client's market size, rerun that part of the `market-sizing` skill and show the change. *Level 1.*
5. **Sales and support signals (day 3).** Won and lost deals naming rivals, support questions about rivals, and search or enquiry trends the client shares. *Level 1.*
6. **So what (day 4).** Pick the one or two changes worth answering this month and propose a response for each (a page change, a price display, an offer, a sales answer), or "watch only". *Approval: the client decides which responses to make.*
7. **Act on approved responses (day 4 to 5).** *Level 2: draft the page text, battle card or offer in the client's tools for review. Level 3 within agreed limits: publish the approved change after the client confirms the action list. Approval: every change to the client's live pages, prices or ads needs the client's yes; changes outside the limits need a fresh yes.*
8. **Short report (day 5).** One page: what changed, why it matters, what we did, what we are watching, and any updated market size. *Level 2: save it as a draft in the shared drive; level 3: share it with the named readers.*

## Outputs

- Dated competitor change log
- Review themes for the client and rivals
- Market data and rules update, with any change to the market size
- One or two recommended responses, or "watch only"
- One-page monthly report
- Action log of everything done in the client's tools

## Needs the client's approval

Which responses to make; every change to the client's live pages, prices, offers or ads; sharing the report beyond the named readers; and anything outside the agreed level 3 limits. No fake accounts, scraping against site terms or confidential information are used, and comparative advertising claims go to a qualified adviser before publishing.
