# Keep opportunity decisions consistent with the model

Use when recommending a product, accessory or new offer using contribution and cash thresholds.

1. Define the candidate, currency, unit, selling channel and scenario. Keep a separate-order cost model apart from an incremental item in an existing parcel. Identify every cost and exclusion, its source and whether it is observed, quoted or assumed. Missing costs block a verified pass; they are not free.
2. Write the proposed decision thresholds and who authorized them. A research assumption is not the owner's financial policy. Record both a minimum contribution amount and ratio where relevant, including the denominator. Keep a cheap learning test distinct from approval to buy stock.
3. Use `node tools/opportunity-economics.mjs scenario.json` for a conditional per-unit contribution screen. Supply every required field explicitly: `currency`, `price`, `priceMultiplier`, `landedCost`, `landedMultiplier`, `handling`, `feeRate`, `returnLossRate`, `acquisition`, `otherCosts`, `minimumAmount`, `minimumRatio`. Rates are fractions; percentage fee and loss allowances use revenue after the price multiplier. The model does not estimate missing costs or account for complex tax/refund populations; use a suitable sourced ledger for those cases.
4. Generate the table, gate labels, percentages and numeric statements from the same saved result. Compare unrounded values to each threshold before formatting. Require both amount and ratio gates when both were specified. Do not copy a base-case sentence into a stress-case recommendation. A rounded display on the threshold is not evidence of passing.
5. Recompute material scenarios independently, then check the rendered recommendation against the result in both directions. Every claim that a candidate passes must identify the scenario and all required gates. A correct JSON or table does not excuse a contradictory headline, sentence or green badge. Keep this final consistency check with the deliverable.
6. Join contribution, demand, supplier and cash decisions only after each has its own evidence. A contribution pass does not clear an MOQ or reserve shortfall. A hold on buying can coexist with a recommendation for a low-cost research test; state exactly which action is being recommended.
