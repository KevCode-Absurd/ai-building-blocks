# Training interview: Marketing analyst

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert to leave out client names, account IDs and any customer data; anonymise every story.

1. **What kinds of businesses, channels and budgets do you know best?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **Walk me through the first week with a new client: what do you ask for, and what do you look at first?**
   Why it matters: captures the real intake order and the shortcuts an expert takes.

3. **How do you decide what becomes a key event, and what stays a plain event?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the judgement behind lean tracking plans.

4. **Show me a tracking plan you are proud of. What makes it buildable?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real format and level of detail to copy.

5. **What are the tracking bugs you find most often, and how do you spot each in under five minutes?**
   Why it matters: turns experience into a fast diagnostic checklist.

6. **How do you write UTM rules that a busy team actually follows?**
   Why it matters: captures the practical side of naming rules, not just the theory.

7. **When an owner asks "which number do I trust?", what do you say first?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's plain-language framing of attribution.

8. **Tell me about a time the ad platforms and the backend disagreed badly. What was behind it?**
   Why it matters: real cases teach which gap reasons matter most in practice.

9. **How do you explain view-through credit to someone who has never heard of it?**
   Why it matters: captures analogies and wording that land with owners.

10. **What makes you decide a channel needs a lift test rather than another report?**
    Why it matters: defines the trigger for spending money on testing.

11. **Walk me through a holdout or geo test you designed: the regions, the length, what went wrong.**
    Why it matters: pulls out design choices and the failure modes that books skip.

12. **How do you check, before launch, that a test can actually detect the effect?**
    Why it matters: separates experts who check power from those who hope.

13. **How do you report a result that is "unclear" without losing the client's trust?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent wording for honest, useful null results.

14. **Have you built or reviewed a media mix model? What did you check first, and what made you doubt it?**
    Why it matters: captures practical review steps for MMM output.

15. **Which numbers go in your weekly report, and which do you refuse to include?**
    Why it matters: shows the expert's sense of signal and vanity metrics.

16. **How do you handle consent and privacy rules in the EU, UK and US when they reduce what you can track?**
    Why it matters: records safe habits and where the expert stops and points to official guidance.

17. **What access do you ask for, and what do you never touch even when you could?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's defaults for access levels and limits.

18. **Tell me about the hardest client you have had on measurement. What did they want, and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: a hard story shows how the expert holds a line kindly.

19. **Which tools, templates or queries do you reuse on every job?**
    Why it matters: lists assets the agent can rebuild or point to.

20. **What do junior analysts most often get wrong, and how do you coach them?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's corrections into the agent's quality checks.
