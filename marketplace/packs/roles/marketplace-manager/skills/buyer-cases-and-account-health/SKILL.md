# Buyer cases and account health

On a marketplace, how you treat buyers is measured, and the measures decide whether you keep selling. The job is to answer fast, stay inside each marketplace's rules, attach proof, and fix the cause behind repeat problems.

## When to use

- "Our inbox is out of control across eBay, Walmart and Etsy."
- Open claims or cases with deadlines.
- Negative feedback or reviews to answer.
- A rising defect, late shipment or cancellation rate.
- A performance warning, listing suppression or account suspension.

## What you need

**Access:** level 1 to read messages, cases and performance pages. Level 2 to save draft replies, case responses and appeals. Level 3 to send buyer replies using approved templates, accept returns and issue refunds up to the client's limit. Claim, case and dispute evidence, refunds above the limit, and any reply to a suspension, appeal, policy warning or formal complaint need the client's fresh yes every time.

- The marketplaces, order volume and the issues buyers raise most.
- Open claims and cases with their deadlines; paste these first.
- Order details for each case: order number, dates, tracking, delivery scans, photos, listing text, messages.
- Performance pages from each marketplace (eBay seller dashboard, Walmart performance standards, bol service norms).
- The client's refund limit and returns policy.

## Steps

1. **Deadlines first.** List every open case with its deadline. On eBay, sellers have 3 business days to respond to a not-received report or a return request, and refunds are due within 2 business days of receiving a return; eBay may step in after that. On Walmart, the response standard is 48 hours.
2. **Templates by marketplace and issue:** where's my order, damaged, wrong item, not as described, return, refund, cancellation. Short, polite, order facts as fill-ins, one next step. Keep the conversation on the marketplace where its rules require it.
3. **Case responses.** Facts in order, the evidence to attach (tracking with a delivery scan, signature for high-value orders, photos, the listing text, messages), and a "refund or defend" note with the reason. Hold risky cases for the client.
4. **Feedback and review replies.** Calm and factual; never argue; offer to fix the problem through messages. Never offer a refund or anything else in return for changing feedback. On eBay, request removal only for feedback that breaks eBay's rules, within 90 days of the transaction.
5. **Account health report.** Each metric against the marketplace's published standard, trend and cause:
   - *eBay:* transaction defect rate (standard: 2% or below), cases closed without seller resolution (0.3% or 2), late shipments and tracking for Top Rated; evaluated on the 20th of each month.
   - *Walmart:* cancellation, on-time delivery, valid tracking, response rate, negative feedback, return rate, item not received, late shipment, over 30 or 60 days.
   - *bol:* the nine service norms in the seller account.
6. **Fix causes.** When one issue drives many cases, name the listing line, packing step, carrier or handling time behind it and the fix.
7. **Appeals and warnings.** Draft a short appeal: what happened, the root cause, what has changed (with proof), and how it's prevented. No blame, no promises the client hasn't approved. The client approves before it is sent.
8. **Send and log (level 3).** Send approved-template replies and refunds within limits; log each with order number, time and result.

## Checks

- No message asks buyers to change or remove feedback for a reward.
- No promise of a refund, replacement or date the client hasn't approved.
- Every case response has its evidence list and deadline.
- Appeals, warnings and formal complaints went to the client for a yes.
- Health report uses the marketplace's own published standards.

## Output format

1. **Deadline list.**
2. **Template table:** marketplace, issue, template.
3. **Case drafts:** deadline, facts, evidence, refund-or-defend note.
4. **Feedback replies.**
5. **Account health report:** metric, standard, current, trend, cause, fix.
6. **Appeal draft,** marked "for your approval".

## Example of good output (short)

*Ember & Wick is a made-up candle shop on eBay and Etsy used only to show the format. Figures are invented.*

**Deadline:** eBay return request #2 (item not as described: "melted"), opened Monday; respond by Thursday (3 business days).

**Case draft:** Order 12-34567. Shipped 2 September, delivered 5 September; heatwave in the buyer's area that week. Photos show melted tops. Listing says soy wax, no heat warning. Recommend: refund ($24) and no return; it's within the $30 refund limit. Evidence: tracking with delivery scan, buyer photos.

**Account health (eBay, as of 20 September):** defect rate 1.4% (standard 2% or below), rising for 3 months; 70% of defects are "melted in transit" cases from June to September.

**Fix:** add a heat warning to listings and switch to insulated mailers for June to August deliveries; pause shipping to the hottest regions in heat alerts.
