# Interview: online marketplace manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Marketplaces and listings

1. **Which marketplaces do you work on most, and what is different about each one's rules?**
   Why it matters: shows where the expert's specialist knowledge lies.
2. **Walk me through taking a store's products onto a new marketplace: what do you do before writing a single title?**
   Why it matters: captures the mapping-first method.
3. **What do you do when the product data is missing a required field or an identifier?**
   Why it matters: tests the line on never inventing details.
4. **Tell me about a bulk upload that went wrong. What did you change afterwards?**
   Why it matters: real failures hold the checks that matter.
5. **How do you handle used, refurbished or for-parts items?**
   Why it matters: condition mistakes are a common route to account trouble.

## Prices and profit

6. **How do you work out what a seller keeps on each marketplace? Which costs do sellers forget?**
   Why it matters: fee bases and hidden costs decide whether a channel pays.
7. **How do you set repricer floors and limits, and what went wrong when someone didn't?**
   Why it matters: bulk pricing errors are the fastest way to lose money.
8. **When a marketplace says your price isn't competitive, what do you check before cutting?**
   Why it matters: shows judgement between margin and visibility.
9. **For delivery apps, how do you price a menu after commission?**
   Why it matters: captures the method for restaurant clients.

## Buyers, cases and account health

10. **What do you do in the first hour after a performance warning or suspension notice?**
    Why it matters: the order of actions protects the account.
11. **Show me an appeal or plan of action that worked. What was in it?**
    Why it matters: appeals that work share a shape worth copying.
12. **How do you decide whether to refund or defend a case?**
    Why it matters: the rule behind the call is reusable.
13. **A seller wants to offer refunds for changed feedback. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests the expert's line on review rules.
14. **Which account health numbers do you watch weekly, and what early signs do you look for?**
    Why it matters: prevention beats appeals.

## Rules, countries and tools

15. **How do you prepare a seller for identity checks (INFORM, EU trader checks) and tax reporting?**
    Why it matters: paperwork stops more launches than demand does.
16. **What do you check before taking a brand into a new country?**
    Why it matters: captures the landed-cost and rules method.
17. **Which tools do you use (seller portals, multichannel tools, spreadsheets), and what access do you need in each?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent what to ask for and at which level.
18. **What do you do yourself in a client's account, and what do you always hand back to the client?**
    Why it matters: maps habits onto access levels and the fresh-yes list.
19. **What do sellers most often ask for that you advise against?**
    Why it matters: prepares the agent for common pushback.
20. **What do you check before any change goes live on a marketplace?**
    Why it matters: defines the expert's personal quality bar.
