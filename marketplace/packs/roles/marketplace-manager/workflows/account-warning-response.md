# Account warning response

**Trigger:** a marketplace sends a performance warning, suppresses listings, limits selling, holds funds or suspends the account, or asks for identity or product safety information with a deadline.

**Cadence:** immediately on the notice; then daily until it's resolved.

## Steps

1. **Stop and read (level 1).** Pause any bulk or automated changes on that marketplace. Read the notice in full; note the deadline, the metric or policy named, and what the marketplace asks for.
2. **Tell the client the same day.** What the notice says, the deadline, the likely cause, and what I'll prepare. No reply is sent yet.
3. **Find the root cause (skill: buyer-cases-and-account-health).** Pull the metrics and the orders or listings behind them; find the pattern (carrier, handling time, stock sync, a listing line, packaging, a policy breach).
4. **Fix what's inside the limits (level 3 within the client's limits).** For example: correct handling times, fix stock sync, add missing listing details, end listings that break a rule (ending in bulk needs a fresh yes). Log each fix with proof.
5. **Information requests (prompt: seller-readiness-check).** If the marketplace asks for identity, tax or product safety details, list exactly what's needed; the client provides identity, tax and bank details in the marketplace's own sign-in.
6. **Draft the appeal or plan of action (prompt: appeal-draft; level 2).** Root cause, changes made with proof, prevention steps, attachments.
   **Approval point:** the client approves the appeal before it is sent, every time, even at level 3. If the notice involves legal claims, intellectual property or safety, the client checks with a qualified adviser first.
7. **Send and track (after the yes).** Submit through the marketplace's own process; log the date; check daily for a reply; answer follow-up questions with the client's yes.
8. **Afterwards.** Add the cause and fix to the weekly health report; adjust templates, listings or processes so it doesn't recur.

## Outputs

- Same-day note to the client.
- Root-cause summary with the metrics.
- Fix log with proof.
- Appeal or plan of action, approved before sending.
- Follow-up log until resolved.

## Needs the client's approval

- Every appeal, plan of action, or reply to a warning, suspension or formal complaint.
- Ending listings in bulk or deleting data.
- Identity, tax and bank details (the client provides these directly).
