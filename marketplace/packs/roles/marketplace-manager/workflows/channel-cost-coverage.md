# Compare the cost of a marketplace order

Use this before recommending a new channel or calling a channel profitable.

## Retrieve the cost evidence

Read the relevant product, inventory, finance, supplier, carrier, packaging and returns records before declaring a cost unavailable. A fee page alone cannot establish what an order keeps. Keep a coverage table with source, date, currency, country, SKU or parcel, fulfillment method, amount, fee base and status: observed, quoted, assumed or unknown.

Match the quote to the proposed order. A shipping quote for another country, weight, carrier service or batch is not an actual cost for this order. When useful, show it as an explicitly conditional scenario and name the mismatch. Do not silently discard available assumptions or promote them into verified facts.

## Calculate comparable scenarios

- Use the same item, selling price, currency, destination, order quantity and fulfillment assumptions across channels. Separate scenarios when these differ.
- Show platform commission, its actual fee base, fixed order charges, payment charges and subscription allocation separately. Check whether shipping and tax enter each fee base. Avoid adding a payment charge already included in a platform fee.
- Include product cost, packaging, pick and pack, outbound delivery, applicable duties and nonrecoverable taxes, expected returns or failed deliveries, and acquisition cost where supplied. Distinguish missing costs from zero costs.
- Do not count inbound freight twice when a landed product cost already includes it. Explain recoverable tax separately from economic cost and cash timing.
- Explain the return model: return probability, unrecovered revenue, refund of platform fees, reverse shipping, handling, and salvage or restocking value. Do not subtract a full refund twice or treat a return rate as a monetary cost without a defined base.
- Calculate a source-backed base case and meaningful sensitivities from the available assumptions. A conditional scenario may support comparison even when actual costs remain unverified. Label the result as conditional contribution, and list exclusions beside it.

Keep calculations in a portable table or executable file. Recompute each row and the recommendation from the same saved inputs. Fee-only proceeds are not total cost or profit. If unknown costs could change the ranking, give a break-even allowance and the specific quote that would resolve the choice.

## Check whether the seller can launch

Confirm current country and category availability from the marketplace's own sources. Seller eligibility, invitation, product approval and operational readiness are separate from marketplace availability. Compare delivery promises, cancellations, tracking, returns handling, stock allocation and cash requirements.

Reserve existing commitments before allocating test inventory. Respect receipt dates and payment dates; incoming stock cannot fulfill an earlier promise. Recommend a conditional test or a hold when stock, cash or eligibility blocks launch. A research recommendation does not authorize account creation, publication or spend.

## Deliver

Include the ranked comparison, cost coverage, reproducible scenarios, operational and inventory constraints, and the next decision. Use bundle-relative links to files included in the delivered folder. Check every local link from that folder; include required scripts there rather than linking to temporary working files.
