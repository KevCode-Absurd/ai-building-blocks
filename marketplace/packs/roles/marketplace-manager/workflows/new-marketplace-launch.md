# New marketplace launch

**Trigger:** the client wants to sell on a new marketplace (eBay, Walmart, bol, Zalando, Etsy, a delivery app) or in a new country.

**Cadence:** one-off, about two to six weeks from decision to first listings, then a 90-day test.

## Steps

1. **Plan the entry (skill: new-marketplace-entry-plan).** Compare options, work out landed cost and margin, list the rules to verify, and recommend one test market. Level 1.
2. **Check readiness (prompt: seller-readiness-check).** Seller identity, product safety and tax reporting items, each with an owner. The client's accountant or adviser confirms tax and legal items.
   **Approval point:** the client decides to open the account. Opening a seller account, signing the marketplace agreement and entering identity, tax and bank details are the client's, every time, in the marketplace's own sign-in.
3. **Work out prices (skill: channel-profit-sheet; prompt: what-you-keep-sheet).** Floors and target prices per item on the new channel.
   **Approval point:** the client approves the price sheet and the limits for any repricer.
4. **Build listings (skill: marketplace-listing-mapper).** Field map, bulk sheet, titles and attributes from real data, EU safety details where needed. Save as drafts or inactive (level 2).
5. **Publish the test range (level 3, after the client's yes for this first launch).** Upload, read the processing report, fix rejections, log every upload.
6. **Set up buyer handling (skill: buyer-cases-and-account-health).** Templates for the new marketplace, response deadlines, refund limit.
7. **Watch the first 30 days.** Daily for the first week: errors, orders, messages, cases. Then weekly: keep per order, returns and account health. Stop and ask if anything unexpected happens (listing blocks, policy notices, price errors, a spike in cases).
8. **Day 90 review.** Against the stop rule: continue, change or stop.

## Outputs

- Entry plan with landed cost and the rules checklist.
- Price sheet with floors.
- Bulk sheet and upload log.
- Buyer templates for the new marketplace.
- 30-day and 90-day reviews.

## Needs the client's approval

- Opening the account and every identity, tax and bank step (the client does these).
- The first listing on the new marketplace or in the new country.
- The price sheet, floors and repricer limits.
- Continuing past the 90-day stop rule.
