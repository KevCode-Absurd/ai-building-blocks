# Weekly marketplace operations

**Cadence:** daily inbox and case check (15 minutes per marketplace); weekly review on Monday; monthly fee and price check.

**Trigger:** the client sells on one or more marketplaces other than Amazon and wants them run, not just set up.

## Steps

1. **Daily: deadlines first (skill: buyer-cases-and-account-health; prompt: claim-response-draft).** Open cases and messages, sorted by deadline. Answer with approved templates and refund up to the client's limit at level 3. Case evidence, refunds above the limit and formal complaints go to the client for a fresh yes.
2. **Daily: listing errors.** Read each marketplace's error or suppression list; fix data problems from the source data within limits (level 3), and list anything that needs the client.
3. **Weekly: account health.** Each metric against its standard (eBay, Walmart, bol); trend; cause; fix. Raise any metric heading toward its limit before it gets there.
4. **Weekly: listing health (prompt: listing-gap-check).** New gaps, Walmart listing quality, items unpublished for pricing; fix in order of sales at stake.
5. **Weekly: stock and handling times.** Check stock sync across channels so items don't oversell; set handling times the warehouse can meet.
6. **Monthly: fees and prices (skill: channel-profit-sheet).** Compare the month's fee invoices with the sheet; flag fee changes; review items below the floor; propose price moves.
   **Approval point:** price moves inside the approved sheet and limits run at level 3 without a new yes; any change beyond the client's limit (more items or a bigger move) needs a fresh yes.
7. **Monthly: rules watch.** With web search connected, check each marketplace's fee and policy pages and any regulator updates (INFORM, DSA, GPSR, tax reporting); without it, review notices the client forwards. Note changes in the report.
8. **Log and report.** Every action: what, where, when, result. A weekly note: sales and keep by marketplace, cases opened and closed, account health, fixes made, decisions needed.

## Outputs

- Daily case and message log.
- Weekly account health and listing health report.
- Monthly fee check and price proposal.
- Change log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Claim, case, dispute or chargeback evidence; refunds above the limit.
- Any reply to a warning, suspension, appeal or formal complaint.
- Price changes beyond the approved sheet or limits.
- Ending or deleting listings in bulk.
