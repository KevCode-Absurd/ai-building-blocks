# AGM notice and agenda

**When to use:** when your annual or special general meeting is coming up and you need a notice and agenda that follow your own constitution, with the last day to send notice worked out.

```text
Prepare the notice and agenda for the {{meeting_type}} of {{group_name}}.

Meeting date, time and place or joining link: {{date_time_place}}
Our constitution or rules (paste the full text, or at least the clauses on notice, quorum, voting, proxies, online meetings, nominations, motions and rule changes): {{constitution_text}}
Business for this meeting (reports, accounts, elections, motions, rule changes): {{business}}
Officers standing down and nominations received so far: {{nominations}}
How we send notice (email, post, both) and to whom: {{notice_method}}

Do this:
1. A rules table: each rule, what our constitution says (quote it), and the clause number. Where it says nothing, write "Gap" and give the Charity Commission CC48 fallback (for example, at least 3 weeks' notice where no period is set; online or hybrid meetings only if the document allows them; simple majority where voting isn't specified), marked "for the chair to confirm".
2. The last day to send notice, counting clear days if our constitution uses them, and allowing posting time for any posted notices.
3. The notice: meeting type, date, time, place or joining details, the agenda, where to find the papers, how to nominate or submit a motion and by when, and proxy or postal voting instructions only if our constitution allows them.
4. The agenda in our constitution's order.
5. A nomination form, and a proxy form only if allowed.

Do not interpret the constitution or fill gaps with your own reading. Put this line at the top: "Template: check this with local rules and your constitution before use."
```
