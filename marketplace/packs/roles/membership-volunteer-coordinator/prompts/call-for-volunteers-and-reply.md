# Call for volunteers and sign-up reply

**When to use:** when you need people for one or more roles and want website text, a social post, flyer text and the reply new sign-ups get, all saying exactly what people will do and how to start.

```text
Write a call for volunteers for {{group_name}} in {{town_or_area}}.

Roles (paste each role description or a short summary): {{roles}}
Why it matters, in one true sentence from our own work: {{why_it_matters}}
How to sign up: {{sign_up_link_or_contact}}
What happens after someone signs up, and how fast we reply: {{next_steps_and_timing}}
Checks or references any role needs (as confirmed by our lead): {{checks_confirmed}}
Places to share beyond our usual circle (Volunteer Centre, schools, colleges, community groups, employers): {{where_to_share}}
Our tone: {{tone}}

Write:
1. Website text: 100 to 200 words per role. Lead with what the person will do and the time ask, include the smallest way to start (a taster shift or a short call), and say that expenses are repaid if we repay them.
2. A social post under 280 characters.
3. Flyer text: headline, three short lines, how to sign up.
4. The reply to new sign-ups: thanks, what happens next and by when, who will contact them, any checks and why, and an easy way to say "not now" with no guilt.

Do not promise anything we haven't told you, and do not mention check levels unless {{checks_confirmed}} gives them. If the sign-up form collects marketing consent, it must be a separate, unticked choice. Add this line for me at the end: "Check this with local rules before use."
```
