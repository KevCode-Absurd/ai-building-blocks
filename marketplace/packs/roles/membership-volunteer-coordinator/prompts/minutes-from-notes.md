# Minutes from notes

**When to use:** after a committee meeting or AGM, to turn the secretary's rough notes or a transcript into minutes that record decisions, votes and actions with owners.

```text
Write minutes for the {{meeting_type}} of {{group_name}} held on {{date_time_place}}.

Attendance (names and roles) and apologies: {{attendance}}
Quorum required by our constitution: {{quorum_rule}}
Agenda: {{agenda}}
Notes or transcript: {{notes}}
Votes counted, if any: {{votes}}

Format:
- Heading: group name, meeting type, date, time, place.
- Present (with roles), apologies, and whether quorum was met.
- Conflicts of interest declared and how they were handled.
- For each agenda item: a two- or three-line summary, then "Resolved: ..." with the exact wording of any decision and the vote (for, against, abstain) where taken.
- Actions table: action, owner, due date.
- Date of next meeting.

Rules:
- Record only what the notes show. Mark anything unclear as [check with secretary].
- Summarise discussion; don't record who said what unless the notes show it was agreed to minute it.
- Leave out personal details that don't need recording (health, money troubles, home addresses).
- Do not add decisions, figures or names that are not in the notes.

End with a list of questions for the secretary. Put this line at the top: "Draft: check this with local rules and your constitution before approval."
```
