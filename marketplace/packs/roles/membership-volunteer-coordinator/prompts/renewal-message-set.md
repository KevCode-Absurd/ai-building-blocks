# Renewal message set

**When to use:** at renewal season, to get every member message (invitation, reminders, failed payment, last call and lapsed note) with timing, channel and a stop rule, matched to your membership platform.

```text
Write a renewal message set for {{club_name}}, which has about {{member_count}} members.

Membership types and fees (from our committee): {{fees}}
Renewal deadline and any grace period: {{deadline_and_grace}}
How members pay: {{payment_methods}}
Membership platform and any automatic emails or payment retries it sends: {{platform_and_automations}}
Channels members agreed to, and who has opted out: {{channels_and_opt_outs}}
Hardship option (concession, payment plan, bursary), if any: {{hardship_option}}
What membership gives this year (bookings, coaching, events): {{this_years_benefits}}
Who signs the messages: {{signer}}

Plan and write:
1. A message map table: message, member group, channel, send date, does the platform already send it (yes or no), stop rule.
2. Each message. The first line says what to do and by when. Then the fee, how to pay, one line on this year's benefits, the hardship option where we have one, and who to contact. Texts under 160 characters where possible, with an opt-out.
3. A "payment didn't go through" message timed to the platform's retry schedule.
4. One lapsed-member note that keeps the door open and asks one optional question about why.

Stop rule: after the last call, no more renewal messages this year. Do not include sponsor offers, appeals or event promotions in these messages. Do not include card or bank details. Put this line at the top: "Template: check this with local rules before use, including consent for each channel."
```
