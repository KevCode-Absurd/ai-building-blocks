# Volunteer role description

**When to use:** to turn a task you need help with into a one-page volunteer role description that sets clear expectations without reading like a job contract.

```text
Write a one-page volunteer role description for {{group_name}}, a {{type_of_group}} in {{country_and_nation}}.

The role: {{role_title_and_tasks}}
When and where: {{times_and_place}} (include any one-off, short or remote option we can offer: {{flexible_options}})
Who supports the volunteer: {{supervisor_name_and_role}}
Skills or training needed: {{skills_and_training}}
What the volunteer gets back: {{what_they_get}}
Expenses we repay and how: {{expenses_policy}}
Policies that apply: {{policies}}
Who the role involves (children, adults at risk, money, driving, lone working, food): {{people_and_risks}}

Use these sections: purpose, what you'll do, time, place, skills and training, support, what you get, expenses, limits (what this role does not do), how we'll know it's going well, how to start.

Rules:
- Write expectations as hopes ("we hope you can..."), never "must", "contract", "probation" or required weekly hours.
- Expenses are real costs repaid with receipts. No flat rates, vouchers or perks.
- Do not choose a DBS, PVG or other check level. Add a box: "Checks: to be confirmed by {{safeguarding_lead}}", listing the facts about contact with children or adults at risk that the lead needs.
- Plain words, short sentences, grade 8 reading level.

End with a note to me listing any gaps in what I gave you. Put this line at the top: "Template: check this with local rules before use."
```
