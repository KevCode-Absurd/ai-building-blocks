# Interview: membership and volunteer coordinator

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work. Names of members, volunteers and anyone involved in a safeguarding matter stay out of the answers.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your first fortnight with a new club or charity, from the first call to the first message sent.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and the order of work.
2. **What do you ask for before you write a single volunteer role or member message?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's intake list.
3. **How do you turn a messy list of tasks into volunteer roles people actually want?**
   Why it matters: role design is where most volunteer problems start.

## Volunteers

4. **Where do you find volunteers beyond the usual circle? What has worked, and what hasn't?**
   Why it matters: captures real recruitment channels, not generic advice.
5. **What happens in a volunteer's first day and first month when it goes well?**
   Why it matters: becomes the welcome and induction standard.
6. **When volunteers drift away, what are the signs, and what do you do before they go?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent when to offer a lighter role.
7. **How do you thank volunteers so it lands, without creating favourites or anything that looks like pay?**
   Why it matters: shows judgement on recognition and the line with expenses.
8. **Tell me about a volunteer problem you had to handle. What did you do, and what would you change?**
   Why it matters: real practice for the problem-solving route.

## Checks and safeguarding

9. **How does a check level get decided for a role in groups you've worked with? Who decides, and what do you prepare for them?**
   Why it matters: keeps the agent in its lane on checks.
10. **How do you keep check records without keeping what you shouldn't?**
    Why it matters: certificate handling is a common slip.
11. **What would you do in the first hour if a volunteer told you about a safeguarding worry?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's stop-and-pass-on rule.

## Members

12. **What does your renewal season look like, week by week?**
    Why it matters: becomes the renewal workflow and timing.
13. **How many reminders do you send, and when do you stop? Why there?**
    Why it matters: captures their stop rule.
14. **How do you handle members who can't afford the fee this year?**
    Why it matters: shows how they balance income and inclusion.
15. **What do lapsed members tell you when you ask why they left?**
    Why it matters: real reasons improve the lapsed note.

## Meetings and paperwork

16. **How do you prepare for an AGM so nobody can say it wasn't valid?**
    Why it matters: becomes the AGM checklist.
17. **What makes minutes useful a year later? What do you leave out?**
    Why it matters: sets the minutes standard.
18. **Describe a time the constitution didn't cover something. What did you do?**
    Why it matters: tests the habit of flagging gaps instead of guessing.

## Rules, tools and judgement

19. **Which tools do you use for members, volunteers and messages, and what would you never let an assistant do in them?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md and the limits for level 3.
20. **When has someone asked you to message people who hadn't agreed to hear from you? What did you say?**
    Why it matters: captures their consent standard.
21. **What does a great volunteer report to a funder look like? What makes you distrust one?**
    Why it matters: becomes the impact report quality bar.
22. **Tell me about the hardest job you've done in this role, start to finish.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a real story and the judgement behind it.
