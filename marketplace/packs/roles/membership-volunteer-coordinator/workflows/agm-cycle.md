# AGM cycle

**Trigger:** the committee sets a date for the AGM or a special general meeting.

**Cadence:** from the date being set to the minutes being filed, usually two to three months.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits).

## Steps

1. **Rules table, L1 (skill: agm-papers-minutes-and-club-rules).** Pull notice, quorum, voting, proxy, online meeting, nomination and motion rules from the constitution, with clause numbers. Flag gaps for the chair.
2. **Timeline, L2.** Work back from the meeting date: last day for notice (counting clear days if the constitution uses them), nominations, motions, papers. Add it to the committee's calendar.
3. **Call for nominations and motions, L2 then L3.** Draft the call; at L3, send the approved version to members entitled to it.
4. **Notice and papers, L2 (prompt: agm-notice-and-agenda).** Notice, agenda, papers list, nomination form, and a proxy form only if allowed. Reports come from the officers; accounts from the treasurer.
5. **Send notice, L3 within limits.** Confirm the member list and method with the client, get one yes, send by the deadline, and log what went to whom and when.
6. **On the day, client.** The chair runs the meeting. If asked, the agent prepares a quorum checklist, a running order and ballot papers.
7. **Minutes, L2 (prompt: minutes-from-notes).** Draft from the secretary's notes within a week. Mark unclear points for the secretary.
8. **Approval and filing, client then L3.** The committee or next meeting approves the minutes. At L3, file the approved minutes where the group keeps records, and update officer details in the member platform.
9. **Handover, L2.** A short handover note for new officers: what's in the folder, open actions with owners, key dates for next year.

## Outputs

- Rules table and timeline.
- Notice, agenda, forms and papers sent and logged.
- Draft and approved minutes filed.
- Officer records updated and a handover note.

## Needs the client's approval

- The rules table and any flagged gap, by the chair, before notice goes out.
- Notice and papers before sending; the member list and method.
- Minutes are approved by the committee or the next meeting, not by the agent.
- A fresh yes for any message to people outside the member list and any formal reply to a complaint or appeal.
- Template: check this with local rules and your constitution before use.
