# Renewal season

**Trigger:** six to eight weeks before the club's renewal deadline, or when the client says renewal season starts.

**Cadence:** a set-up week, then a weekly check until two weeks after the deadline.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits).

## Steps

1. **Read last year, L1.** From the member export: members due, renewed, lapsed, and when people renewed. Note complaints or questions from last year.
2. **Confirm the facts, client.** Fees, deadline, grace period, hardship option and payment methods, as decided by the committee. Nothing is guessed.
3. **Message set, L2 (skill: member-welcome-and-renewal-messages; prompt: renewal-message-set).** Build the message map and messages, matched to the platform's own automatic emails and payment retries.
4. **Platform set-up, L2.** Set up the messages as drafts or paused automations in the membership platform or email tool. Switch off duplicate platform emails only if the client agrees.
5. **Go live, L3 within limits.** Show the send list (messages, dates, member groups, channels) and get one yes. Then switch on the automations or send on schedule.
6. **Weekly check, L1 then L3.** Count renewals against last year. Apply opt-outs and updated contact details the same week. Answer routine questions with approved wording; pass fee disputes, hardship requests and complaints to the client.
7. **Failed payments, L3 within limits.** Send the approved "payment didn't go through" message. Payment settings, refunds and any change to bank or payout details stay with the treasurer.
8. **Deadline and lapsed, L3 within limits.** After the last call, stop renewal messages, mark non-renewers as lapsed if the client's rules say so, and send the one lapsed note.
9. **Wrap-up, L1.** A one-page report: renewed, lapsed, new joiners, reasons people gave for leaving, what to change next year.

## Outputs

- Message map and messages, live in the platform.
- Updated member statuses and preferences, with an action log.
- Weekly renewal counts and a wrap-up report.

## Needs the client's approval

- Fees, dates, hardship wording and every template before first use.
- The send list before the first live send.
- A fresh yes for deleting member records, ending a membership for any reason other than non-renewal, formal complaint replies, and any send to a group outside the approved lists.
- Refunds above the client's limit, payment settings and bank or payout details stay with the treasurer.
- Template: check this with local rules before use, including consent for each channel.
