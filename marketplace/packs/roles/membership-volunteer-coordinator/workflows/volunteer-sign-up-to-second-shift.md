# Volunteer sign-up to second shift

**Trigger:** a new person signs up to volunteer, or a recruitment drive opens.

**Cadence:** each new sign-up moves through the steps below; a weekly 15-minute review catches anyone stuck.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits).

## Steps

1. **Roles ready, L1 then L2 (skill: volunteer-roles-recruitment-and-induction; prompt: volunteer-role-description).** Each open role has a description and a check level confirmed by the safeguarding lead. Roles waiting for the lead are not advertised.
2. **Call for volunteers, L2 or L3 (prompt: call-for-volunteers-and-reply).** At L3, publish the approved call on the group's website or volunteer platform and share it where the client listed.
3. **Reply to the sign-up, L3 within limits.** Send the approved reply within the time the group promised. Log the date in the volunteer record.
4. **Checks and references, client.** Where a role needs a DBS, PVG or other check, the group's lead or Registered Body starts it. The agent records only the permitted fields (date of issue, check type, role, reference number, decision) and never stores a copy of a certificate.
5. **First shift booked, L3 within limits.** Book the shift in the sign-up sheet or volunteer tool, send the welcome pack and first-day details, and tell the person who will meet them.
6. **First-shift check-in, L3 within limits.** A short message the day after: how did it go, anything to change, and the date of their next shift.
7. **Second shift, L1 then L3.** If no second shift is booked within the time the client set, send one friendly nudge offering a different day, a shorter shift or a remote task. Then stop.
8. **Weekly review, L1.** List sign-ups waiting for a reply, checks waiting for the lead, people with no second shift, and any concern raised.

## Outputs

- Role descriptions and a published call for volunteers.
- Replies, welcome packs and check-ins sent and logged.
- A volunteer record per person with permitted check fields only.
- A weekly list of who is stuck and why.

## Needs the client's approval

- New role descriptions, the call for volunteers and the message templates before first use.
- Check levels and every decision to accept someone for a role that needs a check: the safeguarding lead decides.
- A fresh yes before declining or removing a volunteer, replying formally to a complaint, deleting records, or messaging anyone outside the approved lists.
- Any safeguarding concern goes to the named lead the same day; the agent stops and does not investigate.
- Template: check this with local rules before use.
