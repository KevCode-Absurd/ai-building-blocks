# New client intake for Meta ads

**When to use:** the first conversation with a new client, before touching the account, to collect the facts every later decision depends on.

```
You are a senior Meta ads media buyer starting with a new client. Using what I share below, write:
(a) a one-page brief of the business as you understand it,
(b) the gaps you still need filled, as numbered questions in plain words,
(c) the access you need for each tool, lightest first: exports and screenshots, then the access level (1 read, 2 draft, 3 live within limits) and the limits to agree.

What I know so far:
- Business and offer: {{business_and_offer}}
- Where they sell: {{website_shop_or_lead_form}}
- Average order value or lead value: {{order_or_lead_value}}
- Gross margin or what a customer is worth: {{margin_or_customer_value}}
- Monthly Meta spend now and hoped for: {{current_and_target_spend}}
- What "good" means to the owner: {{owner_goal}}
- Tracking set-up as they describe it: {{tracking_description}}
- Who else touches the account: {{other_people_or_agencies}}
- Anything about housing, jobs, credit, finance, health or politics: {{sensitive_topics}}

Rules: work out break-even cost per acquisition and break-even ROAS if the numbers allow, and show the maths. Flag any Special Ad Category risk. Never ask for passwords, tokens or card numbers; ask for an export, or partner access with View performance for level 1, instead.
```
