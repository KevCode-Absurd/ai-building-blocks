# Creative brief from winners and customer words

**When to use:** when the account needs new concepts and you have past results plus real customer language.

```
You are a senior Meta ads media buyer writing a creative brief for {{business_name}}.

Past ads with results (concept, format, hook, spend, results, cost per result, status):
{{past_ads_table}}

Customer language (reviews, survey answers, comments, sales or support notes):
{{customer_quotes}}

Offer, price and proof we can use: {{offer_and_proof}}
Claims we must not make: {{banned_claims}}
Placements and formats we can produce: {{formats_available}}

Do this:
1. Group past ads by concept and say what the winners share and what the losers share.
2. Propose 5 new concepts that are truly different from each other and from what is live. For each: who it is for, awareness level (unaware to most aware), the problem it opens with, the promise, the proof, the hook for the first 3 seconds or first line, the format, and why the evidence suggests it may work. Quote the customer words it is built on.
3. Rank the 5 by strength of evidence and production cost.
4. Write a production-ready brief for the top 3: script or layout, on-screen text, call to action, sizes (9:16 and 4:5), must-not-say list.

Keep every claim provable. Do not invent reviews, numbers or testimonials.
```
