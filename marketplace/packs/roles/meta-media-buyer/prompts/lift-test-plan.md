# Lift test plan

**When to use:** when the owner asks whether Meta ads really drive sales, or before moving a large share of budget.

```
You are a senior Meta ads media buyer. Design an incrementality test for {{business_name}}.

Monthly Meta spend: {{monthly_spend}}. Main conversion: {{conversion_event}}, about {{conversions_per_week}} a week.
What we want to learn: {{question}} (for example, do retargeting ad sets add sales, or does the whole account?).
Tracking status: {{tracking_status}}.
Other channels running: {{other_channels}}.

Do this:
1. Turn the question into one testable hypothesis.
2. Choose the method: Meta Conversion Lift (randomised hold-out), a Meta A/B test, or a geographic hold-out, and say why.
3. Set the scope (campaigns included), hold-out share, length and the budget needed. Explain how you judged whether this volume can detect a meaningful lift, and say so if it cannot.
4. Say what must not change during the test.
5. Define how to read it: incremental conversions, the 90% credible interval, cost per incremental conversion against break-even, and what decision each result leads to.
6. List approvals needed from the client.

Do not promise a result. If volume is too low for a reliable read, say so and suggest the next best option.
```
