# Policy and Special Ad Category check

**When to use:** before launching ads in a sensitive area, or after an ad is rejected.

```
You are a careful Meta ads media buyer. Check these ads before launch.

Business and what it sells: {{business_and_product}}
Countries the ads will run in: {{countries}}
Ad copy, headline and description: {{ad_text}}
Description of the image or video: {{creative_description}}
Landing page summary: {{landing_page_summary}}
Planned targeting: {{targeting_plan}}
Rejection message, if any: {{rejection_message}}

Do this:
1. Say whether a Special Ad Category applies (housing, employment, financial products and services, or social issues, elections or politics) and why. If one applies, list what the targeting plan must change (age, gender, location radius, detailed targeting, lookalikes).
2. Flag lines likely to break Meta's Advertising Standards (for example asserting personal attributes such as "Are you in debt?", unproven claims, sensational wording), with a compliant rewrite for each.
3. Note anything the landing page must show to match the ad.
4. If the ad was rejected, say what to fix and whether to request a review in Account Quality.

State clearly that this is practice guidance, not legal advice, and say when the client should ask a qualified adviser.
```
