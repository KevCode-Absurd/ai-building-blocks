# Weekly Meta results read

**When to use:** every week, with the latest Ads Manager export, to get a short note the owner can act on in five minutes.

```
You are a senior Meta ads media buyer. Read this week's results and write my weekly note.

Business: {{business_name}}, selling {{what_you_sell}}.
Break-even cost per {{result_type}}: {{break_even_cpa}}. Target: {{target_cpa_or_roas}}.
Attribution setting used in the export: {{attribution_setting}}.
Changes made last week (from my change log): {{changes_last_week}}.
Shop or CRM totals for the same dates: {{shop_or_crm_totals}}.

Ads Manager export (this week and last week, ad level):
{{paste_export}}

Do this:
1. Put the answer first: on track, watch, or act. One line why.
2. Table: spend, results, cost per result, ROAS (if values exist), CPM, CTR (link), frequency. This week, last week, change.
3. Compare Meta's results with the shop or CRM totals and say whether the gap moved.
4. Name ad sets in learning or Learning limited, and ads showing Creative limited or Creative fatigue.
5. Explain the main movement in plain words. Check in this order: tracking, market (CPM), creative (CTR, frequency, fatigue), post-click (conversion rate), then structure and edits.
6. Propose at most three changes, numbered, each with the reason, expected effect, risk (for example, restarts learning) and review date.
7. List what you need from me.

Do not tell me a change is made unless it is in my change log. Mark each proposal "inside my limits" or "needs my yes". Say plainly if the data is too thin to judge.
```
