# Interview: pulling out a Meta media buyer's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("can you give me a real example?"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour you spend in an account you have never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you decide whether the tracking is good enough to make decisions on?**
   Why: separates buyers who check signal health from those who trust the dashboard.
3. **What is your default account structure for a small shop spending under 10,000 a month, and when do you break from it?**
   Why: captures your consolidation rules and the exceptions you have learned.
4. **How do you work out what a client can afford to pay for a customer or lead?**
   Why: pulls out your unit-economics method (margin, lifetime value, payback).
5. **Tell me about a time Meta's numbers and the shop's numbers disagreed badly. What was it and how did you find it?**
   Why: a real debugging story is worth more than any checklist.
6. **How do you come up with new creative concepts? Where do the ideas come from?**
   Why: reveals your research sources and how you avoid "same ad, new colour".
7. **How do you test creative, and how do you decide a winner?**
   Why: captures your test method, sample sizes and stop rules.
8. **When do you change a budget, and by how much at a time?**
   Why: shows how you balance learning-phase resets against speed.
9. **When do you use cost caps, bid caps or ROAS goals instead of highest volume?**
   Why: bid strategy judgement is a mark of experience.
10. **How do you spot creative fatigue before it shows in cost per result?**
    Why: early-warning habits (frequency, hook rate, CTR trend) differ between experts.
11. **What do you look at first when cost per result jumps overnight?**
    Why: your triage order is a reusable diagnostic.
12. **How do you report results to an owner who is not a marketer?**
    Why: shapes the agent's voice and report format.
13. **How do you handle a client who wants to judge ads after one day?**
    Why: tests how you manage expectations without losing the client.
14. **Have you run a Conversion Lift or other incrementality test? What did it teach you?**
    Why: shows whether you separate correlation from cause.
15. **How do you treat retargeting and existing-customer campaigns?**
    Why: reveals your view on incrementality versus reported ROAS.
16. **What is your approach for lead-gen clients where lead quality varies a lot?**
    Why: captures CRM feedback loops and lead verification habits.
17. **Which Advantage+ features do you switch on by default, and which do you switch off, and why?**
    Why: records current platform judgement, which changes fast.
18. **Tell me about an ad that got rejected or an account that got restricted. What happened and what did you change?**
    Why: policy experience protects clients.
19. **What is the most expensive mistake you have seen in a Meta account?**
    Why: mistakes teach the quality bar.
20. **What do you refuse to do for a client, even if they ask?**
    Why: defines the agent's ethics and limits.
21. **Which tools outside Meta do you rely on, and what for?**
    Why: fills access.md with your real stack.
22. **Tell me about your hardest account. What made it hard and what finally worked?**
    Why: the story shows judgement under pressure.
23. **How do you keep up with Meta's changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why: tells the refresh process where to look.
24. **What does a great week of work look like for you on one account?**
    Why: sets the agent's routine and pace.
