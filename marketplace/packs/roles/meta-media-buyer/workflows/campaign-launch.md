# Campaign launch checklist

**Trigger:** a new campaign, a new product or offer, a new market, or rebuilding a messy account.

**Goal:** launch once, cleanly, with tracking proven, structure simple enough to leave learning, and every setting chosen on purpose.

## Steps

1. **Intake** (prompt: `account-intake`). Confirm the offer, margin, break-even cost per acquisition and ROAS, the real goal, and any Special Ad Category risk.
2. **Prove tracking** (skill: `meta-signal-health-check`). The optimisation event fires once per action, from browser and server, deduplicated, with value and currency. Place a real test order or lead and see it in Test Events. Do not launch on unproven tracking.
3. **Policy and category** (prompt: `policy-and-special-category-check`). Set the Special Ad Category if needed. Check claims and landing page.
4. **Choose structure.** Default for a small account: one Sales or Leads campaign with Advantage+ campaign budget, Advantage+ audience and Advantage+ placements, and one or two ad sets. Add a separate existing-customer split only if the business has a reason and accepts the trade-off. Each ad set should be able to reach about 50 results a week at the planned budget (weekly spend of roughly 50 times target cost per result). If not, combine or optimise for a higher-volume event.
5. **Choose bidding.** Highest volume or highest value by default. Use a cost per result goal, bid cap or ROAS goal only when the client needs a ceiling and accepts lower volume.
6. **Attribution model.** Pick standard (and the windows), incremental or custom, and write the choice in the plan. Keep it the same across ad sets you will compare.
7. **Creative** (skill: `meta-creative-test-plan`; prompt: `creative-brief-from-winners`). At least three distinct concepts, in 9:16 and 4:5, with names that encode concept and format. Review Advantage+ creative enhancements with the client.
8. **Naming and logs.** Campaign, ad set and ad names follow the convention. Start the change log and test log.
9. **Build it paused, then pre-flight review** (level 2). Build the campaign unpublished or paused, or hand over a build sheet at level 1. Take a second look at budget (daily versus lifetime, currency), schedule, locations, languages, placements, links and UTM tags, pixel and dataset selected, attribution model, Special Ad Category.
10. **Client sign-off.** Send the launch plan: structure, budget, bidding, creative, measurement, what we will judge and when, and the level 3 limits (accounts, budget cap, step size, kinds of change, end date). Wait for written approval. At level 3 this is the one-time confirmation before going live.
11. **Launch and leave it** (level 3, or the client publishes at level 2). After launch, check delivery within 24 hours (ads approved, spend starting, events arriving). Then hands off for about a week unless something is broken.
12. **First read at day 7** (workflow: `weekly-optimisation-routine`).

## Outputs

- Launch plan (one page) with the client's approval.
- Tracking test record.
- Creative brief and asset list.
- Change log started.

## Approval

- The launch plan as a whole, including budget, dates and the level 3 limits. At level 3, this yes covers publishing the campaign exactly as planned.
- Any Special Ad Category choice and targeting limits, every time.
- Advantage+ creative enhancements.
- Anything that differs from the approved plan, such as a new audience, more spend than the cap or a different goal, needs a new yes.
