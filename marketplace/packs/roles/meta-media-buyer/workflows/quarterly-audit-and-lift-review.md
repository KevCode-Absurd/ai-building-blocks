# Quarterly audit and incrementality review

**Cadence:** every three months, and after any big change in strategy, site or tracking.

**Goal:** step back from weekly tuning, check the whole account against the business, and find out what the ads actually cause.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 7 need level 1 (read). Step 8 needs level 3, or level 2 if the client publishes.

1. **Full audit** (skill: `meta-account-audit`). Tracking, goal and value, structure, stability, creative, bidding, measurement and policy, ranked by money at stake.
2. **Reconcile the quarter.** Meta-reported results against shop or CRM totals and total marketing spend. Work out revenue against total spend (sometimes called MER) alongside platform ROAS, and say how they moved.
3. **Re-check what changed on Meta** (knowledge.md section 10). Advantage+ rules, attribution options, AEM, Conversions API guidance, bid strategy names, Special Ad Categories. Note anything that affects this account.
4. **Decide on a lift test** (prompt: `lift-test-plan`). If spend and volume allow, plan a Conversion Lift or A/B test on the question that matters most this quarter, often "do retargeting and existing-customer ad sets add sales?" If volume is too low, say so and suggest a geographic hold-out or a post-purchase survey instead.
5. **Creative review** (skill: `meta-creative-test-plan`). Hit rate for the quarter, concepts that won, concepts that tired, gaps by awareness level and format.
6. **Write the quarterly report.** What worked, what did not, what we learned, what we will do next quarter, and what needs the client's decision.
7. **Client meeting.** Walk through the report and the change list. Agree next quarter's targets, test plan, budget cap and level 3 limits.
8. **Make the agreed changes** (level 3 within limits; at level 2, built paused for the client to publish). Set up an approved lift test the same way. Log each change.

## Outputs

- Audit with scorecard and ranked findings.
- Quarter reconciliation table.
- Lift test plan (or a reasoned "not yet").
- Quarterly report and change list.
- Updated change log.

## Approval

- Every item on the change list that falls outside the current level 3 limits. Items inside them are made and logged.
- Starting a lift test, every time (it holds back some people from ads, which can cost sales during the test).
- Next quarter's targets, budget cap and limits.
