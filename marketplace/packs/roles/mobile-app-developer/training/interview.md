# Training interview: mobile app developer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, private code, keys, customer data and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe apps in general terms.

1. **Which frameworks and versions do you work in most (React Native with Expo, bare React Native, Flutter, native), and what work do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **When a client describes a new screen, what do you write down before you open the editor?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's planning habit and its format.

3. **Which screen state do clients forget most often, and how do you get them to decide what it shows?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert turns vague briefs into complete states.

4. **How do you decide what an app shows and does with no signal?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's offline choices in real trade-offs.

5. **Which phones and OS versions do you test on, and how did an older or cheaper phone catch you out?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's real device list and the bugs it finds.

6. **Tell me about the hardest layout bug you fixed: notches, keyboards, edge-to-edge, foldables or large text.**
   Why it matters: captures platform knowledge that documentation misses.

7. **How do you test a screen with VoiceOver and TalkBack, step by step?**
   Why it matters: turns accessibility from a checklist into a working method.

8. **How do you choose libraries, and what makes you say no to one?**
   Why it matters: shapes the agent's dependency choices and its caution about native modules.

9. **Walk me through your last React Native, Expo or Flutter upgrade. What broke, and in what order did you fix it?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a realistic upgrade sequence.

10. **What tests do you write for a screen, and which do you skip on a small budget?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's testing floor for small clients.

11. **Tell me about an App Store or Google Play rejection you dealt with. What was the reason, and what did you change?**
    Why it matters: records real review lessons the guidelines don't spell out.

12. **How do you fill in Apple's privacy label and Play's Data safety form, and who signs off the answers?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's inventory method and approval habit.

13. **How do you plan a rollout: test tracks, phased release, staged rollout? What is your halt rule?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent concrete rollout steps and stop signals.

14. **Tell me about a release that went wrong after launch. How did you find out, and what did you do in the first hour?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's incident instincts for apps people already installed.

15. **Which crash and performance numbers do you watch after a release, and where?**
    Why it matters: tunes what the agent monitors and how it reads crash tools against Android vitals.

16. **How do you handle signing keys, certificates and store accounts with a client, so nobody pastes secrets into chat?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's safe-access advice in practice.

17. **What do you ask a client before you agree to touch their App Store Connect or Play Console?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's access questions and limits.

18. **What does a hand-over look like when you finish a job?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's final deliverable and documentation habit.

19. **What do small businesses most often want from an app that you talk them out of, and how?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to push back with reasons.

20. **What would you check first in a screen another developer wrote, if you had ten minutes?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's fastest quality signals.
