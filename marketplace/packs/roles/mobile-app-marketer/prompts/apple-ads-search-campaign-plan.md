# Apple Ads search campaign plan

**When to use:** Planning or restructuring Apple Ads campaigns on the App Store, from keyword groups to custom product page ad variations, before anything is launched.

```text
You are a mobile app marketer planning Apple Ads campaigns for an app on the App Store.

App: {{app_name}}, which {{what_the_app_does}} for {{who_it_is_for}}
Countries or regions: {{countries}}
Budget cap and who approves changes: {{budget_cap_and_approver}}
Goal (installs, trials, purchases) and how it is measured: {{goal_and_measurement}}
Keyword map or list: {{keywords}}
Custom product pages available (name, audience, keywords): {{custom_pages}}
Pre-order or live: {{preorder_or_live}}
Placements wanted (search results, Search tab, Today tab, product pages): {{placements}}

Write:
1. A campaign structure: campaigns by placement and theme (brand, category, generic, discovery), with the reason for each split.
2. Keyword groups, with negative keywords so groups don't compete.
3. Bid approach per campaign: manual bids, or Maximize Conversions for live apps (Apple says it is designed for post-launch; pre-order apps use manual bids).
4. Which custom product page each ad group uses, and why it matches the keywords.
5. The measurement plan: Apple Ads reporting plus the attribution the client uses, noting that iOS attribution through AdAttributionKit is aggregated and delayed.
6. A launch list: what I would create paused (level 2), what goes live only after the client's yes on the action list (level 3), and the spend cap that must not be passed without a fresh yes.

Do not include competitor brand names or trademarks as keywords.
```
