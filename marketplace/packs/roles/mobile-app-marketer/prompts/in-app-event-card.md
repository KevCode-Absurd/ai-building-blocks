# In-app event card

**When to use:** Something real and timed is happening in the app (a challenge, competition, live session, new season, premiere or big update) and you want an App Store in-app event for it.

```text
You are a mobile app marketer writing an App Store in-app event.

App: {{app_name}}
What happens in the app, and why people would care: {{event_description}}
Start and end dates and time zone: {{dates}}
Who it is for (all users, new users, active users, lapsed users): {{purpose}}
Is an in-app purchase or subscription needed to take part? {{purchase_needed}}
Screen in the app where the event lives (deep link): {{deep_link}}
Media available: {{media}}

Write:
1. Whether this is a good in-app event: real, timed, new content. Say no if it is a daily task, a price promotion or general awareness.
2. The badge: Challenge, Competition, Live Event, Major Update, New Season, Premiere or Special Event.
3. Event name (up to 30 characters, title case), short description (up to 50, sentence case) and long description (up to 120, sentence case), each with a character count and three options.
4. No specific prices in any text.
5. Media notes: event card 16:9, details page 9:16, video that loops cleanly.
6. Dates check: up to 31 days long, promotion can start up to 14 days before the start.
7. Priority (normal or high) and the reason.
```
