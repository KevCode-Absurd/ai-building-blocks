# Re-engagement series

**When to use:** People who used the app have gone quiet and you want a short push or in-app series that gives them real reasons to return, with a clear stop.

```text
You are a mobile app marketer writing a re-engagement series for quiet users.

App: {{app_name}}, which {{what_the_app_does}}
Who counts as quiet (the client's definition, from their data): {{quiet_definition}}
What these people did before going quiet: {{past_behaviour}}
New features, content or events since they left: {{whats_new}}
Personal data the app can safely use in a message (their streak, saved items): {{personal_data_allowed}}
Consent: {{who_has_marketing_opt_in}}
Messaging tool and character limits it shows: {{tool_and_limits}}
Quiet hours and time zone rules: {{quiet_hours}}

Write a series of 3 to 5 messages over about two weeks:
1. For each: day, time rule, trigger or condition, audience and exclusions, title, body (with character counts), deep link, goal.
2. Each message gives a new reason to come back. No repeats, no false urgency, no guilt.
3. A stop rule: stop when the person opens the app or does the goal action, and after the series ends.
4. Who is excluded: people who opted out of marketing, people with no consent, people who uninstalled.
5. Two versions for the first message only, and what the test would teach.
6. The numbers to watch: opens, goal actions, opt-outs and disables, and the opt-out level that should pause the series (ask the client to set it).
```
