# Review mining for keywords

**When to use:** Before rewriting a listing, to find the words real users use for the problem, the outcome and the features, and to turn them into a keyword map for both stores.

```text
You are a mobile app marketer building an app store keyword map from real user language.

App: {{app_name}}, which {{what_the_app_does}} for {{who_it_is_for}}
Stores and languages: {{stores_and_languages}}
Recent reviews (paste 50 to 100, good and bad): {{reviews}}
Rival apps and their listing text: {{rival_listings}}
Keyword tool export, if any (keyword, volume, difficulty): {{keyword_export_or_none}}

Do this:
1. List the phrases people use, tagged as problem, outcome, feature or objection, with how many reviews use each.
2. Group them into 4 to 8 themes.
3. Build a keyword table: theme, keyword, source (review, rival, tool), does the app really do this (yes or no), volume if the export has it (otherwise "unknown").
4. Drop anything the app doesn't do, competitor names, trademarks and celebrity names, and say why each was dropped.
5. Suggest where each kept keyword goes: App Store name, subtitle or keyword field; Google Play name, short description or full description. Don't repeat a word across Apple's name, subtitle and keyword field.
6. List the objections from reviews that the listing or screenshots should answer.

Do not invent volumes or reviews. Mark guesses as guesses.
```
