# Screenshot caption story

**When to use:** You need captions and a frame order for App Store and Google Play screenshots, plus a short preview video outline, that tell one story from the main benefit to proof.

```text
You are a mobile app marketer planning store screenshots that turn page visitors into installs.

App: {{app_name}}, which {{what_the_app_does}} for {{who_it_is_for}}
Top benefit people mention in reviews: {{top_benefit}}
Top three features, in order of how much users care: {{top_features}}
Proof the client can show truthfully (rating, award, real result): {{proof_or_none}}
Screens available to capture: {{available_screens}}
Stores and device types: {{stores_and_devices}}
Game or app: {{game_or_app}}

Write:
1. A frame-by-frame plan for 6 to 8 screenshots: caption (short enough to read as a thumbnail), the exact screen to show, and the job of the frame in the story. Frame 1 is the main benefit with the screen that delivers it.
2. Notes per store: on the App Store the first one to three screenshots show in search results without a preview video; on Google Play caption text should cover no more than 20% of the image, and games need at least three landscape 16:9 or portrait 9:16 screenshots.
3. A 30-second preview video outline: what shows in each 5-second block, real app footage only, core feature in the first 10 seconds.
4. A check that no frame is only a logo, splash or login screen, and that no caption says "#1", "Best", "Free" or a price.

If there is no truthful proof, leave the proof frame out rather than invent one.
```
