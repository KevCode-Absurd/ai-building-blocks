# Training interview: mobile app marketer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, private numbers, logins and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe apps in general terms.

1. **Which kinds of apps do you market most (games, subscriptions, local business apps, marketplaces), and which work do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **When a client hands you an app, what do you look at in the first hour?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's triage order.

3. **How do you find keywords when the client has no paid tool?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a method that works on a small budget.

4. **Tell me about a time reviews gave you a better word than the client's own copy. What changed?**
   Why it matters: grounds review mining in a real result.

5. **How do you decide what goes in the App Store name, subtitle and keyword field?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's placement rules beyond the official limits.

6. **How do you write a Google Play description differently from an App Store one?**
   Why it matters: captures store-specific writing habits.

7. **Walk me through how you plan screenshots. What goes in frame one, and why?**
   Why it matters: turns the screenshot story into a repeatable method.

8. **Tell me about a listing test that surprised you. What did you expect, and what happened?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent humility about hunches and how to read results.

9. **How do you decide when a test is finished?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's stopping discipline.

10. **When do you use a custom product page or custom store listing, and when is it not worth it?**
    Why it matters: captures judgement about audience pages.

11. **What makes a good in-app event, and what have you seen rejected or ignored?**
    Why it matters: records real event lessons the guidelines don't spell out.

12. **How do you set up Apple Ads for a small budget? What do you never do?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a safe starting structure and red lines.

13. **How do you explain iOS attribution limits to a client who wants per-person data?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's plain-words explanation.

14. **When do you ask for notification permission, and what does your pre-prompt screen say?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's permission method.

15. **How do you build a message map? What columns are in your sheet?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's planning format into the agent's default.

16. **How do you set frequency caps and stop rules, and what told you a cap was too high?**
    Why it matters: grounds caps in real signals, not guesses.

17. **Tell me about a push or in-app message that backfired. What did you change?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent what annoys users.

18. **A client asks for more five-star reviews fast. What do you tell them, and what do you do instead?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert holds the line on fake or rewarded reviews.

19. **Which numbers do you report weekly and monthly, and which ones do clients misread?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's reports.

20. **What access do you ask for at the start, and what do you never take on?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's access habits and limits.

21. **Tell me the story of your hardest app marketing job, start to finish.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a worked example of judgement under pressure.
