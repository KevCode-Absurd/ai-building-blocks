# Training interview: no-code app developer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, real records, screenshots with personal data, and anything under a confidentiality agreement.

1. **Which platforms do you build on most, and which jobs do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the work it should hand on.

2. **Walk me through your last portal or staff tool from the first message to launch.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the time goes.

3. **What do you ask before you choose a platform?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions that prevent a costly move later.

4. **Tell me about a time you told a client no-code was the wrong choice. What were the signs?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent when to say "not no-code".

5. **How do you turn a messy spreadsheet into a data model? Show me a before and after with made-up data.**
   Why it matters: records the expert's data habits.

6. **How do you write the roles table, and how do you decide what each role must never see?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's starting point for privacy.

7. **Which platform setting do you rely on to keep clients' data apart, on each platform you use?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent in real server-side controls, not hiding.

8. **Tell me about a privacy hole you found, in your own app or someone else's. How did you find it?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real failure to learn from.

9. **What are your privacy tests before launch, and who runs them?**
   Why it matters: turns the expert's tests into the agent's checklist.

10. **How do you plan for plan limits and usage costs, such as Bubble workload or Airtable record caps?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert avoids surprise bills and slowdowns.

11. **What does a page spec from you look like? Which states do builders forget?**
    Why it matters: sets the hand-over standard.

12. **How do you write workflows so failures don't go unnoticed?**
    Why it matters: captures failure paths and logging habits.

13. **What access do you ask clients for, and what do you refuse to accept?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent asks for access levels and limits.

14. **How do you build and test without touching live data?**
    Why it matters: records safe working habits on each platform.

15. **A client wants to store card numbers or medical notes in the app. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's line on sensitive data.

16. **How do you handle a client who wants every feature in the first version?**
    Why it matters: captures scoping habits and how the expert keeps version one small.

17. **What do you hand over at the end, and what do you check a month later?**
    Why it matters: builds the handover and monthly review routines.

18. **Which mistakes do you see most when you take over an app someone else built?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a list of things to check first.

19. **How do you keep up with platform changes, and which change caught you out?**
    Why it matters: shows which facts the agent must re-check often.

20. **Tell me about the hardest no-code job you've done and what you would do differently.**
    Why it matters: captures judgement that rules alone don't teach.
