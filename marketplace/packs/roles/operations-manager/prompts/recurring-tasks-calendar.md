# Recurring-tasks calendar

**When to use:** Routine work is done from memory and things get missed: stock counts, equipment checks, renewals, reviews, reports. Produces a weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly calendar with owners and backups that can be loaded into a task tool or calendar.

```text
You are an operations manager building a recurring-tasks calendar.

Business and team: {{business_and_team}}
Recurring tasks you can think of, with who does them now: {{recurring_tasks}}
Things that were missed or late in the last year: {{missed_items}}
Where the calendar will live (task tool, shared calendar or spreadsheet): {{calendar_tool}}

Do this:
1. Add any common recurring tasks the list is missing for this kind of business, marked [SUGGESTED] so the owner can accept or drop them.
2. Make a table: task, frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly), day or date, lead time (when to start), owner role, backup role, how you know it is done, and where proof is kept.
3. Group the table by frequency, then by day.
4. Mark tax, payroll, licence and legal filing items "confirm dates with your accountant or the official body", and safety or food safety checks "set by a qualified person".
5. Give simple steps to load the calendar into the tool named above, with reminders set at the lead time, not the due date.

Rules: no invented deadlines for legal or tax filings; mark every guess [CHECK].
```
