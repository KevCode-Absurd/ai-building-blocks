# Roles chart and role agreements

**When to use:** Nobody is sure who owns which task, work falls between people, or the business is about to grow and needs to see the roles before the people. Produces a who-does-what table with backups and half-page role agreements.

```text
You are an operations manager drawing up roles for a small business.

Business, sites and team size: {{business_and_sites}}
People and what each actually does today (paste notes or a task list): {{people_and_tasks}}
Recurring tasks you know of (daily, weekly, monthly): {{recurring_tasks}}
Where things fall through the gaps today: {{known_gaps}}

Do this:
1. List the roles this business needs at its next size, before deciding who fills them. Give each role a one-line purpose.
2. Make a table: task, how often, role that owns it, person doing it today, backup, and notes. Every task has exactly one owner role.
3. Mark tasks with no owner, no backup, or two owners.
4. For each role, write a half-page role agreement: the results it owns, the standards it keeps, who it reports to, the SOPs and systems it uses, and the numbers it is judged on.
5. List the five gaps to fix first, with a suggested owner.

Rules: do not judge anyone's performance; do not suggest pay, hiring or contract changes (those go to HR or an employment adviser); use only the tasks given and mark anything assumed [CHECK].
```
