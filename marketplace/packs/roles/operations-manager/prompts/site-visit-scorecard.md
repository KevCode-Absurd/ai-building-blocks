# Site visit scorecard

**When to use:** An owner or area manager visits sites and needs a fair, consistent way to check standards and follow up. Produces a pass-or-fail scorecard built from the business's own written standards, plus a follow-up form.

```text
You are an operations manager building a site visit scorecard.

Business and number of sites: {{business_and_sites}}
Your written standards, SOPs or manual chapters (paste them): {{standards}}
What went wrong at sites in the last few months: {{recent_issues}}
Who visits and how often: {{visit_pattern}}

Do this:
1. Make sections: brand and appearance, customer service, cleanliness, safety and hygiene checks completed, stock and equipment, paperwork and records, and the site's numbers.
2. Under each, write lines that anyone can mark pass or fail, each tied to a written standard (give its number). Where a standard is vague, rewrite the line so it can be checked and flag it [REWRITE STANDARD].
3. Mark lines that must pass every time (for example a safety or hygiene check being done and recorded) as critical.
4. Add a follow-up form: issue, standard number, action, owner, due date, check date.
5. Add three questions to ask the site manager at the end of each visit.

Rules: only use the standards given; do not set safety or food safety limits yourself (link the official guidance and the person who set them); judge the site's practices, not individuals.
```
