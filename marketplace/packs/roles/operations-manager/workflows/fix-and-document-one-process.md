# Fix and document one process

**Trigger:** One process keeps causing delays, redos or complaints, or the owner wants it out of their head and written down. Runs over a few weeks, depending on how long the trial needs.

**Cadence:** One working session to map it, a dated trial, then one session to standardise.

**Access:** Level 1 (read) on the task tool, job logs and documents is enough to map and diagnose. Steps marked [L2] need read and draft access; steps marked [L3] need read, write and publish access within the client's limits.

## Steps

1. **Gather the facts.** Collect the client's counts (items started, finished, waiting, redone), two or three real examples with dates, and any recordings or notes of the task. Use the `process-map-and-bottleneck` skill, steps 1 to 3.
2. **Map and diagnose.** Same skill, steps 4 to 7: find the constraint with its evidence, the causes of delay, and how to get the most from the constraint. [L2] Draw the current and future maps on a new board. *Approval: the owner agrees the map matches reality.*
3. **Design the trial.** Same skill, steps 8 to 10: the leaner flow, the measure, the guard measure and the stop rule. *Approval: the owner agrees the trial, its dates and who is involved.*
4. **Run the trial.** [L3] Set up the trial in the live board or tracker (columns, work-in-progress limits, task owners) after the owner's yes, and log each change. Otherwise the owner's team sets it up from the written steps.
5. **Check in mid-trial.** Read the measure and the guard. If the guard is breached, stop and tell the owner at once.
6. **Decide.** At the end, compare against the baseline. *Approval: the owner decides to keep, change or drop the new way.*
7. **Write the SOP.** If kept, run the `sop-writing` skill on the new way, with a one-page checklist. Someone who does the task confirms the steps. [L2] Save it as a draft page.
8. **Publish.** *Approval: the owner signs off the SOP; a qualified person signs any control step.* [L3] Publish it, archive the old version, assign it to the role and put the review date on the recurring-task calendar.
9. **Watch it.** Add the measure to the weekly ops review for the next few weeks, so the fix does not fade.

## Outputs

- Current and future process maps
- The constraint, its evidence and the ranked causes of delay
- Trial plan and trial results against the baseline
- SOP and one-page checklist, with owner and review date
- Action log of every live change (level 3)

## Needs the client's approval

The map, the trial and who takes part, any spend on capacity, keeping or dropping the change, publishing the SOP, and any step that touches safety, food, money or personal data (signed by a qualified person where the law or a regulator sets the rule).
