# Open a second site

**Trigger:** The owner has decided to open a second (or third) site, or to prepare the business for franchising. Runs from the decision to the end of the new site's first month open.

**Cadence:** Weekly check-ins against the opening checklist; site visits with the scorecard after opening.

**Access:** Level 1 (read) on SOPs, notes and photos is enough to plan and write. Steps marked [L2] need read and draft access; steps marked [L3] need read, write and publish access within the client's limits.

## Steps

1. **Roles first.** Run the `operations-manual` skill, steps 1 and 2: org chart of roles for one site and role agreements. Use the `owner-time-audit` prompt to see which roles the owner still fills. *Approval: the owner agrees the roles.*
2. **Agree the chapter plan.** Same skill, step 3. *Approval: the owner signs off the chapter plan before writing starts. For a planned US franchise, the franchise lawyer also sees it, because the table of contents goes into the disclosure document.*
3. **Fill the SOP gaps.** For each missing procedure, run the `sop-writing` skill with the best site's staff. [L2] Save drafts in the manual space.
4. **Write the chapters and standards.** Same skill, steps 4 to 6, with pass and fail examples and photo places. Link official health, safety and food guidance for the new site's country, and have a qualified person set the controls. *Approval: the owner signs off each chapter; a qualified person signs safety and hygiene controls.*
5. **Build the opening checklist.** Same skill, step 7. [L3] Set it up as tasks with owners, dates and sign-offs in the task tool, after the owner's yes.
6. **Test the manual.** Someone who has never worked at the first site runs a shift from the manual. Fix every place they got stuck.
7. **Publish.** [L3] Publish the approved chapters and archive drafts. Otherwise hand over the files.
8. **Open and watch.** Weekly check-ins against the checklist. Use the `site-visit-scorecard` prompt for the first visits, and add the new site's numbers to the `weekly-operations-rhythm`.
9. **Close the gaps.** After the first month, list what the manual missed and run `fix-and-document-one-process` on the worst gap.

## Outputs

- Org chart of roles and role agreements
- Approved chapter plan and written manual
- New SOPs for the gaps
- Opening checklist with owners and sign-offs
- Site visit scorecard and follow-up forms
- First-month gap list
- Action log (level 3)

## Needs the client's approval

Roles, the chapter plan, every chapter and standard, safety and hygiene controls (signed by a qualified person), the opening dates, any spend, publishing to the team, and anything shared with franchisees or their lawyers. Franchise agreements and disclosure documents are for a franchise lawyer, not this workflow.
