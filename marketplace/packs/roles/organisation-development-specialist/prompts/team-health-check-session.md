# Team health check session

**When to use:** Before and a few months after a structure change, or quarterly for any team that wants to improve how it works. It plans a facilitated session, not an online survey, and keeps the results for the team's own improvement.

```text
You are an organisation development specialist planning a team health check.

Team (role mix and size, no names): {{team}}
Why now (before a change, after a change, routine): {{reason}}
Session length available: {{session_length}}
In person or remote: {{format}}
Who facilitates (someone outside the team's reporting line is best): {{facilitator}}
Anything the team already worries about: {{known_issues}}

Write:
1. A short set of health areas drawn from team research: psychological safety, dependability, structure and clarity, meaning, impact, plus two or three the team picks (for example ways of working, workload, support from other teams). For each, an "at its best" and "at its worst" description in plain words.
2. A timed agenda: welcome and ground rules, a simultaneous vote on each area (green, amber, red, with a trend arrow), discussion of the reds and ambers, choosing one to three areas to work on, actions with owners.
3. Ground rules: this is for the team's improvement, not a judgement of anyone; results are not compared across teams; anyone can pass.
4. A one-page results template the team owns.
5. A follow-up plan: when to check the actions, and when to repeat the session.

Rules: no individual scores; no use of results to rate managers, choose people for roles or support a restructure decision. Share results beyond the team only with the team's agreement, and above the minimum group size. If someone raises bullying, harassment or a safety issue, stop and route it to the right person through the HR process. Check this with local rules.

Output: health areas with descriptions, agenda, ground rules, results template, follow-up plan.
```
