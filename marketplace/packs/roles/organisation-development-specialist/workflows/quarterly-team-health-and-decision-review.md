# Quarterly team health and decision review

**Trigger or cadence:** Every quarter after a redesign, or as a routine for growing firms. Each cycle takes about two to three weeks from scheduling sessions to the leadership summary.

**Access:** Set per tool (see `access.md`). Building surveys and drafting pages need level 2; sending approved surveys, booking approved sessions and publishing approved pages need level 3. Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

1. **Schedule the sessions.** Agree which teams take part and who facilitates each (ideally someone outside the team's reporting line). At level 3, book the approved sessions in the agreed calendars. *Approval: the sponsor agrees the teams and dates.*
2. **Run the team health checks.** Use the `team-health-check-session` prompt, with the same health areas as last quarter so trends are comparable. Teams own their results and choose one to three areas to improve.
3. **Optional short pulse.** If teams want it, send a short anonymous pulse on the same areas. Build it unsent (level 2); at level 3, send the approved survey to the approved teams, remind and close on the date. Report only above the minimum group size.
4. **Collect themes, not scores.** With each team's agreement, gather the themes they're happy to share (for example "unclear who decides priorities between product and sales"). No team league tables.
5. **Re-test decision rights.** Ask managers for decisions that got stuck this quarter, with names removed. Walk them through the `decision-rights-table` and note gaps or rows that no longer fit.
6. **Check spans.** Re-run the `span-and-layer-check` prompt on a fresh export to spot managers whose teams have grown or shrunk.
7. **Write the leadership summary.** Shared themes, decision rights fixes, span flags, and actions with owners. *Approval: leaders approve any changes to the decision rights table or structure.*
8. **Publish approved updates.** At level 3, publish the approved decision rights changes to the agreed page. Any change that affects a named person's role, manager or team needs a fresh yes for each one, after HR review. Check this with local rules.
9. **Close the loop.** Each team reviews its own actions at the next session.

## Outputs

- Team-owned health results and actions
- Shared themes (with team agreement)
- Updated decision rights table
- Span and layer flags
- Leadership summary with owners
- Action log (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Booking approved sessions, sending the approved pulse to approved teams, reminders and closing, and publishing approved decision rights updates to the agreed page.

**Always needs a fresh yes.** Sharing any team's results beyond the team, any message to a new group, any change to a named person's role, manager or team, and any use of results in people decisions (which I decline). People and legal questions go to HR or an employment lawyer.
