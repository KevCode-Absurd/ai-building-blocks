# Insert card and unboxing plan

**When to use:** for products shipped in a box or mailer, to plan what the buyer sees, in order, when they open it, with insert card layouts and copy.

```text
You are a packaging designer planning an unboxing for an online brand.

Product and brand: {{product_and_brand}}
Shipping box or mailer, with inside dimensions and whether the inside can be printed: {{box_details}}
What goes in the box now (product, protection, paperwork): {{current_contents}}
What I want the buyer to do after opening (use it right, review, reorder, follow, share): {{goals}}
Budget per order for extras, if any: {{budget_per_order}}
Brand voice, with two lines I like: {{brand_voice}}
Access I have to the design tool (1, 2 or 3, with limits): {{access_level}}

Do this:
1. Write the opening sequence in order: outside of box, first thing seen on opening, product, card, anything underneath.
2. Plan up to two insert cards with a size that fits the box, one job each (for example how to use, and a review or reorder ask). Give a zone map and copy for each, with character counts.
3. Write three short sticker or inside-lid lines in the brand voice.
4. Keep any review ask neutral: ask for honest reviews, never only good ones, and offer no reward that depends on a positive review.
5. Flag anything that adds weight or a new material, since it may change packaging data.
6. Access: at level 1, give me the plan and copy. At level 2, build the cards in a copy named "DRAFT". Sending files to a printer needs my fresh yes.

Rules: no claims I cannot back; keep personal data out of printed cards unless I confirm how it is handled.
```
