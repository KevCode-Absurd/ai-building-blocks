# Label change and reprint

**Trigger:** a recipe, supplier, size, address or material change; a new market or channel; a rule change found at a kit refresh; or a printer or retailer query about a printed pack.

**Cadence:** also run a light check every 30 days on live packs against the "What changes fast" list in knowledge.md.

## Steps

1. **Log the change.** What changed, which products and variants, which markets, and the date stock with the old label runs out. Record "Access I have" for each tool (level 1 until the client answers).
2. **Find what the change touches (skill: required-label-details-check).** For each affected variant, list every required detail that changes: ingredients, allergens and their emphasis, net quantity, address, warnings, nutrition, barcode (a new size needs a new GTIN), recycling label, component list.
   - **Research mode:** with web search connected, open the current official pages for each market and note the date. Without it, use knowledge.md and mark rules to confirm.
3. **Mark the risk.** Allergen and safety changes first: the client decides with their adviser whether old stock can be sold, relabelled or held. I do not decide that.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms the new required details and wording.
4. **Update copy and layout (skills: on-pack-copy-by-panel, panel-map-and-layout-spec).** Change only what the change needs; keep the range grid. Show a before and after for every changed line.
5. **Update packaging data (prompt: packaging-component-list)** if materials or weights changed. Level 2 at most.
6. **Draft.** Level 1: give the designer the change list. Level 2: update a copy of the artwork named "DRAFT", and log it.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the changed artwork.
7. **Preflight and proof (skill: artwork-preflight-and-print-release).** Pay extra attention to the changed lines on the proof.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the proof and reprint quantity with a fresh yes, every time, even at level 3.
8. **Close out.** Update the spec sheet, the copy deck, the change log (old and new wording, date, who approved) and the action log. Remind the client to update online listings so the website matches the pack.

## Outputs

- Change log per variant with old and new wording.
- Updated required details list, copy deck and layout.
- Updated component list where materials changed.
- Preflight report, proof check and release record.
- Action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- New required details and wording, with their adviser where needed.
- What happens to old stock.
- Changed artwork.
- Proof approval and reprint release, with a fresh yes each time.
- Any spend, new supplier or new GTIN licence.
