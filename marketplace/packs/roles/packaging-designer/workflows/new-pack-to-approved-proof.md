# New pack: brief to approved proof

**Trigger:** a new product, a new pack format, or a maker moving from home-printed labels to printed packs, with a launch or shop date.

**Time:** usually two to four weeks, most of it waiting for facts, the dieline, approvals and the printer's proof.

## Steps

1. **Intake and access (access.md questions).** Product, markets, channels, pack type, printer, deadline. Ask which access level the client grants in each tool (design tool, printer portal, email, shared drive) and record it as "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1.
2. **Printer spec (prompt: printer-spec-request).** Get the current dieline, artwork guide, proof terms and lead time. At level 2 the email is saved as a draft; at level 3 it goes to the named contact only.
3. **Required details (skill: required-label-details-check).** Build the list for each market. The client supplies and confirms every item.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms the required details and their exact wording, with their adviser where needed.
4. **Copy (skill: on-pack-copy-by-panel).** Front and back copy to the panel sizes, with a claims log and two front-claim options.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the copy deck and the claims log.
5. **Layout (skill: panel-map-and-layout-spec).** Panel map A to F, one table per panel, barcode and required detail spaces, print notes. For several variants, the range system (prompt: range-layout-system).
6. **Front panel test (prompt: front-panel-thumbnail-test).** Shelf and thumbnail checks; fix before the client sees the layout.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the layout.
7. **Build.**
   - Level 1: the client's designer builds the artwork from the spec.
   - Level 2: I build a draft in the connected tool, named "DRAFT", and log it.
8. **Packaging data (prompt: packaging-component-list).** Component list with materials and weights; recycling label from the client's scheme. Level 2 at most: the client's approved person submits any data.
9. **Preflight (skill: artwork-preflight-and-print-release, steps 1 to 8).** Check the file against the printer's guide and the approved copy.
10. **Send for proof.**
    - Level 2: upload for a proof without ordering, where the printer allows it.
    - Level 3: send final files to the named printer contact within the limits.
11. **Proof check (same skill, step 11).** Check the proof and record the reply deadline.
    **Approval point 4:** the client approves the proof, quantity and price with a fresh yes. Only then is the proof approved or the job released. Payment stays with the client.
12. **Hand over.** Panel map, copy deck, required details list, claims log, print notes, component list, preflight report, the client's release record and the action log.

## Outputs

- Printer spec sheet with dieline version and proof terms.
- Required details list per market, confirmed by the client.
- Copy deck by panel with claims log.
- Panel map and layout spec, or a draft design (level 2).
- Component list for recycling labels and EPR data.
- Preflight report and proof check.
- Release record (who approved which proof, when, what quantity).
- Action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access levels per tool, and the limits for level 3.
- Required details and their wording (point 1).
- Copy and claims (point 2).
- Layout (point 3).
- Each proof approval and print release, every time, even at level 3 (point 4).
- Any new printer or supplier, any order above the agreed quantity or spend, and all payments.
