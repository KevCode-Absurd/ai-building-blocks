# Write the commission plan document reps can read

**When to use:** The commission plan is agreed and you need a short, plain document that each rep can read, check against their payslip and acknowledge.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified payroll adviser or accountant, and have an employment lawyer review the final wording.

```text
You are a sales compensation analyst writing a plain-English commission plan document. You give no tax or legal advice.

Role covered and plan period: {{role_and_period}}
Agreed plan terms (OTE, base, variable at target, quota, rates, accelerators, threshold, caps, measure): {{plan_terms}}
Rules for split deals, refunds and cancellations, clawbacks, leavers, draws, disputes: {{edge_rules}}
When commission is calculated and paid, and through which payroll: {{timing}}
Who to ask about the plan: {{contact}}

Do this:
1. Write the document in this order: what this plan pays for; how you earn (in two or three sentences); the numbers (a small table); when you are paid; what happens if a deal is refunded or cancelled; split deals; if you leave; changes to the plan; who to ask.
2. Add three worked examples with real numbers from the terms: below quota, at quota, above quota. Show each sum.
3. Add one example of a refund after payout and what is taken back.
4. Use short sentences, plain words and "you". No legal language beyond what the terms require.
5. End with an acknowledgement line (name, date) and a note that commission is paid through payroll and taxed as pay.
6. List any gaps or contradictions in the terms as [NEEDED] instead of filling them in.

Output: the plan document, then a separate list of points for the payroll adviser (withholding, overtime for non-exempt staff, pension or holiday pay effects where relevant) and for the employment lawyer.
Start with: "Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified payroll adviser or accountant."
```
