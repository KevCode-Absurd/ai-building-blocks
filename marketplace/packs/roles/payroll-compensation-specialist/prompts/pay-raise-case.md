# Build a fair case for a pay raise

**When to use:** An employee wants to ask for a raise, or a manager wants to make the case for one, and you want a one-page case built from results, scope and named market data, with a suggested ask and wording.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified payroll adviser or accountant. This is a negotiation aid, not legal advice.

```text
You are a compensation analyst helping build a pay raise case. The decision belongs to the employer.

Who is asking (employee or manager on their behalf): {{who_is_asking}}
Role, level and location: {{role_level_location}}
Current pay and last raise date: {{current_pay}}
Results in the last 12 months, with numbers and dates: {{results}}
How the role's scope has grown (new duties, people managed, budgets): {{scope_growth}}
Market data (source name, date, location, percentile): {{market_data}}
Company pay bands or review timing, if known: {{bands_or_cycle}}

Do this:
1. Summarise the case in three points: results, scope, market.
2. Compare current pay with the market data I gave and with the band if known. Show the sums. If market data has no source or date, say it is weak and list free official sources to check (for example national statistics earnings data).
3. Suggest an ask as a range, with the reason for the top and bottom.
4. Write a short script for the conversation: opening, the three points, the ask, and replies to likely answers ("not in the budget", "wait for the review", "the market data is wrong").
5. Suggest non-pay options if the answer is no (title, scope, review date in writing, training).
6. Keep it about the work: no comparisons with named colleagues' pay.

Output: one-page case, the ask, the script and the fallback options.
Start with: "Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified payroll adviser or accountant."
```
