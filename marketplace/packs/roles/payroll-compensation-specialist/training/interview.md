# Training interview: Payroll and compensation specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, pay, bank details or ID numbers of real staff; anonymise every story.

1. **Which countries, states and business sizes have you run or checked payroll for?**
   Why it matters: location and size decide which rules the agent points to and where its advice stops.

2. **Walk me through your checks between the change cut-off and payday, in order.**
   Why it matters: captures the real sequence and the checks an expert never skips.

3. **What are the five payroll input errors you catch most often, and how do you spot each fast?**
   Why it matters: turns experience into the agent's first-pass checklist.

4. **How do you set the threshold for "this change is big enough to question"?**
   Why it matters: pulls out judgement the agent needs when the client gives no threshold.

5. **Tell me about a payroll mistake that reached payslips. What did the process miss?**
   Why it matters: real failures show which checks matter most.

6. **How do you word a question to a manager so it gets answered before the deadline?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent tone and format for question lists.

7. **Where do you draw the line between checking payroll and giving tax or legal advice?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's safety boundary in the expert's own words.

8. **How do you handle overtime, bonuses and commission for hourly staff?**
   Why it matters: captures the practical steps where variable pay meets overtime and pension rules.

9. **How do you group roles into levels when a small company has no job structure?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the expert's levelling method for first pay structures.

10. **Which pay data sources do you trust, and which do you refuse to use?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a source hierarchy for market data.

11. **How do you decide how wide a pay band should be?**
    Why it matters: band width is a judgement call the agent must explain, not guess.

12. **Tell me about a pay gap you found. How did you check whether there was a fair reason?**
    Why it matters: shows how an expert handles a sensitive finding with evidence.

13. **How do you explain to a manager why two people in the same role are paid differently?**
    Why it matters: captures wording that keeps trust when pay differs.

14. **How do you set quota for a new sales role with no history?**
    Why it matters: records the capacity method behind fair quotas.

15. **What commission plan features have backfired for you, and why?**
    Why it matters: lists failure modes such as caps, booked-revenue pay and unclear splits.

16. **How do you model a plan's cost so the owner isn't surprised in a great year?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the scenarios and checks to run before launch.

17. **How do you handle a rep who disputes a commission payment?**
    Why it matters: captures the dispute process and the documents needed.

18. **What access do you ask for in payroll and HR systems, and what do you refuse to touch?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's defaults for access levels and limits.

19. **How do you keep up with rate and rule changes each year?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which official pages to recheck and when.

20. **Tell me about your hardest pay review. What did the owner want, and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: a hard story shows how the expert holds a line kindly.
