# Annual pay review

**Cadence:** once a year, eight to ten weeks before the date new pay takes effect; also triggered by a new country, a pay transparency or reporting rule starting, or a new sales plan year.

**Access:** set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Most steps need level 1 (read) and level 2 (build models and documents in copies). Publishing the approved band document is level 3. Entering any pay change in payroll needs your yes to that exact change; running payroll stays with you.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified payroll adviser or accountant.

## Steps

1. **Scope and rules watch (week 1).** List where staff work. Check the "What changes fast" table in `knowledge.md` for those places: minimum wage rates, overtime thresholds, sick pay, pension minimums, pay transparency and pay gap reporting. List what changed, with the official link. *Approval: you decide what to act on; legal questions go to an employment lawyer.*
2. **Market data (week 1 to 2).** Refresh market data from named, dated sources (official statistics and any survey you license). Record source, date and sample for each role.
3. **Bands and placement (week 2 to 3).** Run the `pay-bands-and-fair-pay-check` skill: update levels and bands, place every person, compa-ratios, flags.
4. **Fair-pay check (week 3).** In the same skill, compare similar roles and levels, list gaps with factors and whether a written reason exists. Prepare any reporting your size and location require (for example gender pay gap figures), for your adviser to check.
5. **Benefits (week 3 to 4).** Cost options with the `benefits-options-three-budgets` prompt. *Approval: you choose the option after speaking to your broker or adviser.*
6. **Sales plans (week 4 to 5, if you have sales roles).** Review last year's commission plan and build next year's with the `sales-commission-plan` skill, including the 50%, 100% and 150% model. Draft rep documents with the `commission-plan-document` prompt. *Approval: you approve the plan; an employment lawyer reviews the wording.*
7. **Budget and proposals (week 5 to 6).** Cost raises within bands, moves to minimum, and benefit and plan changes against the budget. Managers propose individual changes using the band and movement rules. *Approval: you decide every person's pay.*
8. **Letters and documents (week 6 to 7).** Draft pay letters and the band summary for managers. At level 3, publish the approved band document to the agreed folder. Pay letters sent as binding need a fresh yes for each.
9. **Into payroll (before the effective date).** Build the change log of approved pay changes with the `payroll-change-log` prompt. At level 2, enter them in the unsubmitted pay run for the effective period, each with your approval on file. *Approval: you review and run payroll.*

## Outputs

- Rules-watch note with official links
- Updated bands, placement and fair-pay check
- Benefits options and chosen option
- Sales plan, payout model and rep documents
- Budget summary of approved changes
- Pay letters and band summary for managers
- Change log entered in the prepared pay run (level 2)
- Action log

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs within your limits after one confirmation.** Publishing the approved band document to the agreed folder (level 3) and entering approved pay changes in the unsubmitted pay run (level 2).

**Always stays with you or needs a new yes.** Every person's pay decision, each pay change entered, pay letters or offers sent as binding, benefit contracts, the final commission plan, running and paying payroll, and any tax, pension or legal question, which goes to your payroll adviser, accountant or employment lawyer.
