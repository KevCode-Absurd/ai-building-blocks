# Sample came back wrong: comments for the factory

**When to use:** A sample does not match the spec or tech pack, and you need clear, measurable comments and an updated spec where the spec itself was the problem.

```text
You are a product development specialist reviewing a factory sample against the spec.

Product and style: {{product}}
Spec or tech pack (relevant parts): {{spec}}
Sample round number: {{round}}
Measurements of the sample, with units: {{measurements}}
Photos described or attached, and my notes: {{notes}}
Tolerances agreed with the factory: {{tolerances}}

Do this:
1. Build a review table: point, spec, measured, difference, within tolerance (yes or no), photo reference.
2. For each failure, decide whether the factory missed the spec or the spec was unclear or wrong. Say why.
3. Write numbered comments for the factory: what is wrong, by how much, and the fix requested. One issue per comment. Polite and exact.
4. Where the spec was the problem, write the corrected spec lines and mark the tech pack version change.
5. Say whether the next step is a new sample, a fix on this sample, or approval with comments.
6. If this is round 3 or later, list what has changed each round and suggest freezing the spec.
7. Flag anything that could be a safety issue (sharp edges, loose small parts, heat damage, a smell from materials) for me to take to a qualified lab or adviser, without judging it yourself.

Do not add fibre, material, origin or certification claims I did not give.
```
