# Reconcile demand and stock before a sourcing decision

Use for product selection, a sourcing recommendation or a first-order/reorder decision. Identify the exact product and variant before deciding whether demand exists. A new concept, an existing SKU and a product family are different populations.

1. Make a small evidence table for the named SKU: sales and returns, paid commitments or reservations, available and committed stock, open POs, forecast assumptions, customer research and concept tests. Record the source file/field, entity, unit, period, status and missing definition for each. Search the supplied inventory and order sources as well as the opportunity report; a role's first retrieved passages are a starting point, not proof that other relevant evidence is absent.
2. Match records by explicit identifiers and variant attributes. A zero-preorder result for another concept cannot erase an existing SKU's paid reservations or sales. A missing field means unknown in that source, not zero across the business. State the precise sources inspected before asserting that evidence is unavailable; inspect likely omitted files or qualify the statement to what was actually searched.
3. Keep units distinct. Units reserved for paid orders are committed stock, not automatically a count of orders or customers. Historical revenue is not a paid-order quantity or a forecast. Do not add reservations to fulfilled sales without proving that they are disjoint. Reconcile available stock to on-hand less commitments; existing stock may already cover the paid commitments.
4. Separate what each signal proves. Paid reservations support an existing commitment or demand signal. They do not alone prove repeatable acquisition, future sell-through, product-market fit, available cash, margin, product quality or a safe order size. An undated SKU revenue summary cannot establish a current sales velocity. Preserve the positive evidence and specify the missing denominator or time window instead of declaring that no demand exists.
5. Distinguish a new launch from replenishment or a revision of an existing product. Protect current customer commitments and account for separate open POs before sizing another order. Link the demand decision to the actual lead time, stock chronology, cash, sample and supplier gates. A conditional hold can still be right even when paid demand exists; explain the actual blocking gate.
6. Check the final decision against the evidence table in both directions: every material assertion has a matching source, and every material contradictory source changes or qualifies the assertion. A technical illustration is an asset but is not a product photograph. Missing usable photographs do not justify saying that no assets folder exists. Save a concise source/decision reconciliation with the deliverable using portable links.

The table is a trace of evidence, not a certificate that the records are complete or authentic. Customer and account records remain private. Where sources disagree, retain both scoped observations and the specific resolution needed; do not silently join different products or treat a retrieval miss as proof of absence.
