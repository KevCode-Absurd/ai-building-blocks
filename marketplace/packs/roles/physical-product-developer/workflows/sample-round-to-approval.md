# Sample round to approval

**Trigger:** A factory sample arrives (prototype, salesman sample, pre-production or top-of-production). Run it once per round.

## Steps

1. **Check access and limits.** Confirm the level per tool and which supplier contacts I may write to at level 3. Until the client answers, use level 1.
2. **Log the sample (level 1).** Sample ID, round number, date received, what the factory said it changed since the last round.
3. **Measure and test.** The client (or their team) measures the points in the spec or tech pack and runs the owner tests from the requirements document, with photos. Lab tests go to the lab, not to me.
4. **Review against spec.** Run the `factory-sample-comments` prompt: review table, within-tolerance checks, which failures are the factory's and which are the spec's.
5. **Flag safety concerns.** Anything that could be a safety issue (sharp edges, loose small parts, heat damage, smells, battery swelling) goes to the client for a qualified lab or adviser the same day. I do not judge it.
6. **Update the spec.** Where the spec was wrong or unclear, update the tech pack or spec sheet and bump the version (level 2 in a copy; level 3 in the live file within limits).
7. **Send comments (level 3, within limits).** Send the numbered comments and the updated spec to the existing factory contact, as a routine message. At level 2, draft them for the client to send. Anything that reads as a claim, a penalty, a price change or a cancellation is held for the client's yes.
8. **Decide the next step with the client.** New sample, fix on this sample, or approval with comments. From round 3 on, show what changed each round and recommend freezing the spec. *Approval: the client approves the sample and decides the next round.*
9. **On approval.** Record the approved sample as the golden sample (photos, measurements, version), and prepare the production purchase order and any inspection brief for the client. *Approval: the purchase order needs the client's fresh yes, and deposits are paid by the client.*
10. **Report.** Round summary, comments sent (from the action log), spec version, open risks, and the next date.

## Outputs

- Sample log entry and review table
- Factory comments, numbered and measurable
- Updated tech pack or spec with a version number
- Safety flags for the lab or adviser, if any
- Next-step recommendation and, on approval, a draft purchase order and inspection brief
- Action log of messages sent

## Needs the client's approval

The access level per tool and level 3 limits; each approval or rejection of a sample; any message that is a claim, a penalty, a price change or a cancellation; sample fees and new sample orders; the production purchase order and any contract (a fresh yes every time); deposits, balances and inspection fees, which the client pays.
