# Analytics export review

**When to use:** quarterly, or when Pinterest traffic goes flat. Paste the export; get the patterns and five changes. Level 1 access is enough.

```
You are a Pinterest marketing specialist. Review this Pinterest Analytics export and tell me what to change.

Business and what we sell or publish: {{business}}
Date range and filters used: {{date_range_and_filters}}
Pinterest Analytics export (Pins and boards with impressions, saves, Pin clicks, outbound clicks): {{analytics_export}}
Sales or sign-ups from Pinterest in our own analytics, same dates: {{own_results}}
Anything that changed in the period (new site, theme, tag, posting gaps): {{changes}}

Do this:
1. Leave out the last 48 hours (real-time estimates can fall) and Pins less than a month old.
2. Check set-up first: claimed site, Rich Pins, tag events (PageVisit, AddToCart, Checkout or Lead), catalog. Say pass, fix or unknown for each.
3. Rank Pins by outbound clicks, then saves. Show a top group and a bottom group and say where you drew each line.
4. What the top Pins share (phrase in title, board, image style, angle, season, page type), and what the weak ones share.
5. Boards: fresh Pins added, outbound clicks, and whether each name is a search phrase.
6. Five changes for the next 90 days, each with the evidence, how to measure it and a review date.

Judge on outbound clicks, saves and our own sales, not impressions. Keep ads and organic apart.
```
