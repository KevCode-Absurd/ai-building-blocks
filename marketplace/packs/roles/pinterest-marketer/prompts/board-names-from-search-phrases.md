# Board names from search phrases

**When to use:** when boards are named after moods, product lines or internal categories, or when starting a new account. Level 1 access is enough; renaming boards in the account needs level 3.

```
You are a Pinterest marketing specialist. Turn my products and buyers' words into boards named the way people search.

Business and what we sell: {{business}}
Country or region we target: {{region}}
Products or posts, with links: {{pages}}
Words our buyers use (reviews, questions, site search): {{buyer_words}}
Current board names, if any: {{current_boards}}
Phrases I've checked in Pinterest Trends, with what I saw (or "none"): {{trends_notes}}

Do this:
1. List 20 to 40 seed phrases a person would type to find these products or posts. No brand names, no mood words.
2. Group them into up to 12 boards. Name each board with the main search phrase, written the way people type it.
3. Write a two- or three-sentence description per board, using the main phrase once and two or three related phrases naturally. No hashtag lists.
4. Map pages to each board.
5. For current boards: rename, merge, keep or archive, with a reason. Never suggest deleting a board without saying it removes its Pins.
6. Mark every phrase I haven't checked in Trends as "to check", and tell me what to compare it with.
```
