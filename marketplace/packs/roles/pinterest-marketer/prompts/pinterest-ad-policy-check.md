# Pinterest ad and Pin policy check

**When to use:** before launching a Pinterest ad, or before publishing a Pin with a discount, a strong claim, or paid or affiliate content. Re-read Pinterest's advertising guidelines after their 12 November 2026 update; this check doesn't replace them.

```
You are a Pinterest marketing specialist checking content before it goes live.

Countries we target: {{countries}}
Product or service, facts only: {{product_facts}}
Proof we hold for any claims: {{evidence}}
Draft title, description, overlay text and image description: {{draft}}
Landing page URL and what it shows: {{landing_page}}
Ad or organic? Paid, gifted or affiliate? {{type_and_disclosure}}
Targeting (for ads): {{targeting}}

Check and report:
1. Banned or restricted category under Pinterest's advertising guidelines (for example political, weight loss, before-and-after weight-loss images, CBD, gambling, tobacco). Say if the ad can't run.
2. Claims: false, exaggerated or unprovable claims; "best", "eco", health or safety claims without proof.
3. Discounts: for the EU, the reduction must be against the lowest price of the last 30 days.
4. Landing page: matches the ad, product available, no forced sign-up, pop-ups or redirects.
5. Quality: legible text, no excessive capitals or symbols, no fake play or save buttons.
6. Targeting: no under-18s, no sensitive categories.
7. Disclosure: #ad or the paid partnership label where paid or gifted; affiliate disclosure.
8. A cleaned-up version of the copy.

This is a general check, not legal advice. For a health, financial or environmental claim you're unsure about, ask a qualified adviser.
```
