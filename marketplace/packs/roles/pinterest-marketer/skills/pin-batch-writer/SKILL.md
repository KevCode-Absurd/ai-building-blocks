# Pin batch writer

## When to use

- Once the keyword and board map exists.
- For a seasonal push, timed from the seasonal calendar.
- When a blog or shop has many good pages but few or old Pins.
- When a client can give Pinterest one afternoon a week or a month and wants it batched.

## What you need

- The board map with phrases per board (from the pinterest-keyword-board-map skill).
- The pages to pin: URL, what's on the page, the best photos available.
- Publish window and how many Pins per week the client can keep up.
- Brand rules: logo use, fonts, colours, words to avoid.
- Any paid partnerships, gifted products or affiliate links involved.
- Countries the Pins are aimed at (for discount and claim rules).

**Access:** level 1 to write the batch; level 2 to load drafts into a scheduler or design tool; level 3 to publish or schedule approved Pins within the agreed limits (Pinterest's own scheduler holds up to 10 Pins, up to 30 days ahead).

## Steps

### 1. Allocate Pins to pages and boards
Spread the batch across boards in proportion to what each board needs, with seasonal boards weighted by the calendar. Give each strong page two to four distinct Pins rather than one.

### 2. Choose an angle for each Pin
Vary the angle on a page: the finished result, a close-up detail, a how-to step, a list ("7 ways to..."), a gift idea, a before-and-after of a room or table (never weight-loss before-and-after, which Pinterest bans in ads). Different angles reach different searches and count as fresh Pins.

### 3. Write the title
Up to 100 characters, with the search phrase and the benefit in the first 40, because that's usually all that shows in feeds. Sentence case. No clickbait, no all-caps.

### 4. Write the description
Two to four natural sentences, up to 800 characters: what it is, who it's for, the main phrase once and one or two close variants, and what they'll find on the page. No hashtag walls. Pinterest uses the description for relevance even when it's hidden.

### 5. Write the overlay text
Three to seven words at the top of the image, matching the search phrase or the promise. Legible on a phone. No fake buttons.

### 6. Write the image brief
One line a designer can follow after reading once: 2:3 vertical (1000 x 1500), the subject, the shot, the background, where the overlay sits, small logo placement. If the client has only square photos, say how to extend them.

### 7. Set the link and date
The exact page URL (not the home page). Publish date from the calendar, spread so the board gets steady fresh Pins.

### 8. Check claims and disclosure
- A discount on a Pin aimed at the EU must be measured from the lowest price in the previous 30 days.
- "Eco", "best", health or safety claims need proof.
- Paid or gifted content needs #ad or Pinterest's paid partnership label; affiliate Pins need disclosure too.

## Checks

- Every title has its phrase in the first 40 characters.
- No two Pins for the same page share an image idea or title.
- Descriptions are sentences, not keyword lists.
- Every link goes to the exact page and works.
- Overlay text is short and legible; no fake buttons.
- Claims are provable; disclosure is present where needed.
- The batch fits the client's weekly rhythm.

## Output format

A table (or CSV for bulk upload) with: number, board, title, description, overlay text, image brief, link, publish date, disclosure needed (yes or no). Then a one-paragraph note on the spread across boards and dates, and any pages that need better photos.

## Example of good output (short)

*Illustrative structure, not a real business: a vegetarian recipe blog in the US. Copy the shape, never the content: every fact comes from the client's own context.*

> **Pin 4** | Board: Vegetarian Christmas dinner ideas | Publish: 18 Nov
> - **Title:** Vegetarian Christmas dinner ideas: mushroom wellington with crispy sage
> - **Description:** A vegetarian Christmas dinner centrepiece that slices like a roast. This mushroom wellington uses chestnuts, spinach and shop-bought puff pastry, and can be made a day ahead. Serves 6, with tips for a vegan version.
> - **Overlay:** Make-ahead veggie Christmas main
> - **Image brief:** 2:3, the wellington sliced on a wooden board, overhead, dark green linen, sage leaves; overlay top third on a cream band; logo bottom right, small.
> - **Link:** [domain]/mushroom-wellington
> - **Disclosure:** no
>
> **Pin 5** (same page, new angle) | Board: Make ahead Christmas recipes | Publish: 2 Dec
> - **Title:** Make ahead Christmas recipes: assemble this wellington the day before
> - **Overlay:** Prep it tomorrow, bake on the day
> - **Image brief:** 2:3, step shot of hands rolling pastry around the filling, natural light from the left.
