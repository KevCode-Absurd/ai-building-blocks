# Seasonal Pin calendar

## When to use

- Every quarter, for the next 90 days.
- Before a big season for the business.
- When last year's seasonal Pins went live after searches had already peaked.
- When the client asks "when should we post our Christmas content?"

## Why it matters

People use Pinterest to plan, so searches for a moment rise well before the day. Pinterest's own festive-season advice is to plan early and stay always-on, with the catalog and tag ready before the season (see knowledge.md). Rather than a fixed rule of thumb, this skill reads each moment's curve in Pinterest Trends, which shows up to two years of history by region and flags seasonal patterns, and sets dates from it.

## What you need

- The keyword and board map (pinterest-keyword-board-map skill).
- The business's moments: public holidays, its own launches and sales, and seasons, by country.
- Pinterest Trends access for each region, or the client's help pulling the curves.
- How many hours a week the client (or I, at level 3) can give Pinterest.
- Stock or content readiness: which products and posts exist, and which need making.

**Access:** level 1 to read Trends and plan. Level 3 only if I'm to schedule Pins or start ads within agreed limits; ads always need a cap and a yes for new campaigns.

## Steps

### 1. List the moments in the window
Everything in the next 90 days plus anything whose climb starts inside the window, even if the day itself falls later. A December moment can belong in an October plan.

### 2. Read the curve for each moment
For each moment's main phrases, in each region: the week the line starts to climb from its base, the peak week, and the drop. Use last year's curve as the guide and check this year's line as it forms. Compare two or three phrase variants and keep the one with the clearer pattern. If you have no Trends access, say so and mark dates as estimates for the client to confirm.

### 3. Set the publish-by date
Pins for a moment should be live before the climb starts, so they're there when people begin searching. Set publish-by a little before the climb week; for a new account with few followers, earlier still.

### 4. Choose boards and pages
For each moment, the boards (existing or new seasonal ones) and the pages that will carry Pins. Note content gaps: a moment with demand but no page to link to needs a page first.

### 5. Set the weekly rhythm
Pinterest asks for new original Pins at least once a week. Plan a number the client can keep every week, then shift the mix towards each moment as its climb approaches. A steady small rhythm beats a burst followed by silence.

### 6. Decide where ads help
Organic Pins take time to be found. For moments that matter to sales, propose ads around the climb and peak: consideration or sales objectives with keyword targeting on the moment's phrases, or shopping ads from the catalog. Ads need a cap, an objective and working tracking. Note them as proposals for the client's yes.

### 7. Lay out the calendar
Week by week: moments climbing, Pins to publish (count and boards), pages to create, ad proposals, and the check-in date.

## Checks

- Every moment has a curve read from Trends for the right region, or is marked as an estimate.
- Every publish-by date sits before the climb starts.
- The weekly Pin count matches the time the client has.
- Content gaps are listed with the date a page must exist.
- Ads are proposals with an objective, cap and tracking check.
- Discount and claim rules for each country are noted for sale moments.

## Output format

1. **Moments table:** moment, region, main phrase, climb starts, peak, publish-by, boards.
2. **Weekly calendar:** week, Pins to publish (by board), pages to create, ads, notes.
3. **Content gaps:** page needed, for which moment, needed by.
4. **Ad proposals:** objective, targeting, suggested cap, dates (for approval).
5. **What to measure:** saves and outbound clicks per moment, and sales or sign-ups from Pinterest.

## Example of good output (short)

*Made-up business: Petal & Twine, a wedding stationery shop in the US. Curves invented for illustration.*

> | Moment | Main phrase | Climb starts | Peak | Publish by | Boards |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | Spring weddings | spring wedding invitations | early Dec | Feb | late Nov | Spring wedding invitations |
> | Valentine's Day | valentines day card ideas | early Jan | early Feb | late Dec | Valentines day card ideas |
> | Engagement season | engagement party ideas | mid Dec | Jan | early Dec | Engagement party ideas |
>
> **Weekly rhythm:** 5 new Pins a week (your 2 hours). Weeks of 17 and 24 Nov: 3 spring wedding, 2 evergreen. From 8 Dec: 2 spring wedding, 2 engagement, 1 Valentine's.
>
> **Gap:** no page for "engagement party ideas"; a blog post is needed by 1 Dec.
>
> **Ad proposal (needs your yes):** consideration campaign on spring wedding phrases, 6 Jan to 15 Feb, cap 15 a day.
