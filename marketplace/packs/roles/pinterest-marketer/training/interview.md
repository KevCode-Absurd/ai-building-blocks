# Interview: pulling out a Pinterest marketing specialist's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("show me a real Pin or board"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour on a Pinterest account you've never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you find the phrases people actually search for, and how do you check them?**
   Why: keyword work is the base of every board and Pin.
3. **Show me a board you renamed. What was it called before, what did you call it, and what happened?**
   Why: a concrete before-and-after teaches the naming habit.
4. **How do you write a Pin title and description? What goes in the first 40 characters?**
   Why: captures your copy method in your own words.
5. **How many distinct Pins do you make for one page, and how do you keep them from being repeats?**
   Why: freshness without spam is a core judgement.
6. **How do you read a Pinterest Trends curve to set seasonal dates? Give me a real example.**
   Why: seasonal timing is where most accounts win or lose.
7. **What weekly rhythm do you set for a client with two hours a week, and what happens when they miss weeks?**
   Why: shows how you plan for real capacity.
8. **What image styles have worked best for you, by industry?**
   Why: records pattern knowledge a plain AI doesn't have.
9. **How do you brief a designer so they get it right first time?**
   Why: the brief is the hand-off that most often fails.
10. **Which metrics do you report, and which do you ignore?**
    Why: shows whether you judge on saves, clicks and sales or on vanity numbers.
11. **Tell me about an account where traffic dropped. How did you find the cause?**
    Why: real debugging stories beat checklists.
12. **How do you set up the tag, events and catalog for a shop, and what breaks most often?**
    Why: shopping results depend on the plumbing.
13. **When do you recommend Pinterest ads, and which objective do you start with?**
    Why: captures your judgement on paid versus organic.
14. **How do you use keyword targeting and negatives in Pinterest ads?**
    Why: ad targeting choices drive cost.
15. **What content have you had to refuse or rewrite because of Pinterest's policies?**
    Why: policy knowledge protects the client's account.
16. **How do you handle paid partnerships, gifted products and affiliate links on Pinterest?**
    Why: disclosure rules differ by country and change often.
17. **What limits do you ask for before taking level 3 access, and what do you always ask about again?**
    Why: writes down your safety habits for publishing live.
18. **Which scheduling or design tools do you use, and which do you avoid?**
    Why: Pinterest allows automation only through approved routes.
19. **What's a common piece of Pinterest advice you think is wrong?**
    Why: your disagreements are your expertise.
20. **Tell me about your hardest Pinterest job, from start to finish. What would you do differently?**
    Why: a full story shows judgement under pressure.
