# Quarterly Pinterest plan

**Cadence:** every 90 days, about three weeks before the quarter starts (earlier before the year-end season).

**Goal:** a 90-day plan built on what worked last quarter, the search curves for the coming moments, and the time the client really has.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 5 need level 1. Step 7 needs level 3 for board changes.

1. **Review last quarter** (skill: `pinterest-analytics-review`; prompt: `analytics-export-review`). Set-up check, top and weak Pins, what winners share, five changes.
2. **Refresh the keyword and board map** (skill: `pinterest-keyword-board-map`; prompt: `board-names-from-search-phrases`). New phrases, boards to rename or add, phrases that faded.
3. **Read the curves** (skill: `seasonal-pin-calendar`; prompt: `trends-curve-reader`). Moments in the window and any whose climb starts inside it; publish-by dates.
4. **Re-read the rules.** Pinterest's advertising, merchant and community guidelines (updated 12 Nov 2026) and any new disclosure or price rules for the client's countries.
5. **Write the plan.** Board changes, weekly Pin rhythm, seasonal calendar, content gaps with dates, what to measure, and ad proposals with objectives and caps.
6. **Agree it with the client.** Time per week, content they'll create, access levels and limits for the quarter, and any ad budget.
7. **Apply board changes** (level 3: create and rename boards as agreed; deletions always need a yes).
8. **Run the weekly batch** (workflow: `weekly-pin-batch`) and check in mid-quarter.

## Outputs

- Last-quarter review with five changes.
- Updated keyword and board map.
- 90-day seasonal calendar and weekly rhythm.
- Content gap list with dates.
- Ad proposals for approval.
- Access levels and limits for the quarter, written down.

## Approval

**Needs the client's approval each quarter:** the plan itself, the weekly Pin number, board renames and merges, any board deletion, content the client must create, any ad budget, new campaigns and caps.

**At level 3, inside the agreed plan:** creating and renaming boards as listed, and the weekly publishing within the agreed number.

Analysis and drafting need no approval.
