# Weekly Pin batch

**Cadence:** once a week, same day, in one sitting of one to two hours (or one longer sitting a month, scheduled ahead).

**Goal:** a steady flow of fresh, search-led Pins on the boards and moments in the plan, published at a rhythm the client can keep.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 4 need level 1. Step 5 uses level 2. Step 6 needs level 3.

1. **Look at the calendar** (skill: `seasonal-pin-calendar`). Which moments are climbing or about to; which boards need fresh Pins this week.
2. **Quick pulse** (tool: Pinterest Analytics, last 7 days, excluding the last 48 hours). New Pins from last week getting saves or outbound clicks; anything with a policy notice; broken links.
3. **Pick the pages.** The week's pages from the board map, favouring pages for climbing moments and pages whose Pins have done well before.
4. **Write the batch** (skill: `pin-batch-writer`; prompt: `pin-batch-from-one-page`). Titles with the phrase in the first 40 characters, natural descriptions, overlay, image brief, board, link, date. Run the claims and disclosure check (prompt: `pinterest-ad-policy-check` for anything with a discount, a strong claim or paid content).
5. **Prepare** (level 2). Image briefs to the designer or draft designs in Canva marked "DRAFT"; Pins loaded into the scheduler as drafts. The client reviews.
6. **Publish or schedule** (level 3, inside the limits: which boards, how many Pins a week). Use Pinterest's scheduler (up to 10 Pins, up to 30 days ahead) or the approved scheduler the client uses. Log each Pin: title, board, link, date.
7. **Note for the client.** What went out, what's scheduled, anything that needs a decision.

## Outputs

- The week's Pin batch (table or CSV).
- Image briefs or draft designs.
- Publishing log.
- A short weekly note.

## Approval

**At level 3, inside the limits confirmed at the start (no new yes each week):** publishing or scheduling Pins on the agreed boards at the agreed weekly number, from the agreed content.

**Always needs a new yes:** a new board theme outside the map, deleting Pins or boards, any paid or gifted partnership content, any Pin with a discount or a health, green or "best" claim, and anything that touches ads or spend.

At levels 1 and 2, the client publishes; I prepare everything.
