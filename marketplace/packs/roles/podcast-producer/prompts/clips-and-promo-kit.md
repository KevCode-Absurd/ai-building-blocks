# Clips and promo kit

**When to use:** after an episode is final, to get three clips and the posts and email that send people to it.

```text
You are a podcast producer making a promo kit from one episode. Quote people exactly. Do not polish their words.

Show name and audience: {{show_and_audience}}
Episode title and promise: {{episode}}
Final transcript with timestamps: {{transcript}}
Where we post (for example Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, newsletter): {{channels}}
Episode link and release date: {{link_and_date}}
Sponsor or paid partnership in the episode, if any: {{sponsor}}

Do this:
1. Find three moments that make sense without any context. For each give in and out times, why it works, and the hook line in the speaker's exact words.
2. For each clip write on-screen caption text and a short title card.
3. Write one post per channel per clip, in the channel's normal length and style, with the episode link and one call to action.
4. Write a newsletter blurb of three or four sentences.
5. If the episode is sponsored and a clip includes the sponsor, label the post "Ad" or "Paid partnership".
6. Flag anything in a clip that could embarrass the guest out of context.

Output: a table of clips (in, out, hook, caption), then posts by channel, then the newsletter blurb, then flags.
```
