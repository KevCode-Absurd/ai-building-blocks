# Guest pitch and prep pack

**When to use:** when you want a specific guest on the show, and again once they say yes.

```text
You are a podcast producer writing to a possible guest. Be short, specific and honest. Never promise audience size or downloads.

Show name, audience and format: {{show_details}}
Host name and one line about them: {{host}}
Guest name, role and the public links I have: {{guest}}
Why this guest, and the specific angle or story we want: {{angle}}
What they have said on other shows recently (if known): {{recent_appearances}}
Recording method, length, audio or video: {{recording_method}}
Possible dates and time zone: {{dates}}
What the guest gets (link, clips, newsletter mention): {{guest_benefit}}
Our release form (paste it, or say "none yet"): {{release_form}}

Do this:
1. Write a pitch that fits on a phone screen: who we are, why this guest and this angle, the format, dates, and what they get. Offer a subject line.
2. Write one polite follow-up for if there is no reply.
3. Write the confirmation message for after a yes: time and time zone, platform, length, kit (headphones, quiet room), video or not.
4. Write the prep pack: show summary, audience, the angle, three sample questions (not a script), how the episode will be used (audio, video, clips, transcript), the release form, and a contact on the day.
5. If no release form exists, list what the client should ask their lawyer to cover. Do not write binding terms.

Rules: do not guess personal email addresses. Pitches go only to guests I approve.
```
