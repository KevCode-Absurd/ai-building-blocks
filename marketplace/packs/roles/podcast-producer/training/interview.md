# Interview: Podcast producer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Shows and formats

1. **Which kinds of shows do you produce most, and which do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the work it should refuse.
2. **How do you work out what a show is for before the first episode? Tell me about a show where that was hard.**
   Why it matters: the show's promise drives every later choice, and agreeing it is a skill.
3. **When do you recommend interview, solo, co-hosted or narrative, and when have you talked a client out of a format?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent real rules for matching format to host and budget.

## Guests

4. **How do you find guests who are right for the audience rather than just well known?**
   Why it matters: guest choice decides most interview shows.
5. **Read me a guest pitch that got a yes. What made it work?**
   Why it matters: real pitches teach structure and tone.
6. **What goes in your guest prep pack, and what do you leave out on purpose?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert balances preparation with spontaneity.
7. **What does your release form cover, and what happened the last time a guest wanted something removed?**
   Why it matters: reveals how the expert protects the client and the guest.

## Recording

8. **How do you write questions that get stories rather than short answers? Give me a before and after.**
   Why it matters: question design is the core craft of interview shows.
9. **What is on your run sheet that most producers forget?**
   Why it matters: surfaces the checks that save a session.
10. **How do you handle a guest who rambles, dodges or gets upset on tape?**
    Why it matters: real recovery moves the agent can suggest to hosts.

## After the recording

11. **Walk me through what you do with a transcript in the first ten minutes.**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert finds the best moments fast.
12. **How do you write a title that gets clicked without overselling?**
    Why it matters: titles carry discovery, and honesty protects trust.
13. **What do you check in a transcript before it goes public?**
    Why it matters: transcript errors misquote guests and fail accessibility.
14. **How do you pick clips, and which kinds of clips have never worked for you?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent tested clip rules.

## Release, rights and sponsors

15. **What is on your checklist before you press publish, and what went wrong when you skipped something?**
    Why it matters: the expert's release check becomes the agent's.
16. **How do you deal with music? Tell me about a rights problem you caught or missed.**
    Why it matters: music is the most common rights failure in podcasts.
17. **How do you write and place sponsor reads so they are disclosed and still sound like the host?**
    Why it matters: balances sponsor value with honesty and the rules.
18. **Have you used AI voices or voice clones? What consent and disclosure did you insist on?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's line on synthetic voices.

## Growth and clients

19. **Which numbers do you trust when judging whether a show is working, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent's reports honest and useful.
20. **Tell me about a show that grew. What actually made the difference?**
    Why it matters: real growth stories beat generic advice.
21. **What does a client do that makes your job harder, and how do you handle it?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's intake and boundaries.
22. **Tell me about your hardest production job. What would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: the hard story shows judgement under pressure.
