# Episode from booking to release

**Trigger:** a guest or topic is chosen for an episode.

**Cadence:** once per episode, starting as early before the release date as the guest's diary allows.

## Steps

1. **Book the guest (skill: guest-booking-and-release; prompt: guest-pitch-and-prep-pack).** Level 2 saves pitches and confirmations as drafts and adds tentative holds; level 3 sends them to guests on the approved list.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the guest list and the angle for each guest.
2. **Plan the episode (skill: episode-plan-and-run-sheet).** Promise, outline, questions with follow-ups, run sheet with consent line and sponsor slots. If sponsored, write the read (prompt: sponsor-read-with-disclosure).
   **Approval point 2:** the host approves the outline; the sponsor and client approve the read's final words.
3. **Send the prep pack** with the release form (level 3 sends to the confirmed guest; levels 1 and 2 hand it to the client).
4. **Record.** The host records from the run sheet and gets consent on tape at the start. The client or their editor edits; with level 2 access to Descript or a similar tool I can run agreed edits, such as filler-word removal, in a project named "DRAFT".
5. **Package the episode (skill: show-notes-and-transcript; prompt: clips-and-promo-kit).** Best moments, titles, notes, chapters, corrected transcript, clips and posts, all from the final edit.
6. **Check before release (skill: episode-release-check; prompt: rights-and-consent-log).** Format, loudness, feed basics, signed release, music, sponsor disclosure, AI voice consent and disclosure.
   **Approval point 3:** the client says yes to this episode's final audio, title and notes, and to the release date, time and platforms.
7. **Publish.**
   - Level 1: hand over files and a checklist for the client to upload.
   - Level 2: upload as a draft or private with everything attached.
   - Level 3: publish or schedule within the client's limits, then check each platform after the next feed refresh.
8. **Promote.** Send the guest the link, clips and suggested posts. Schedule promo posts at level 3 within the client's limits, or hand them over.
9. **Log and report.** Every action with what, where, when and result; open items.

## Outputs

- Approved guest list, pitches, confirmations and prep pack.
- Outline, questions and run sheet; approved sponsor read.
- Show notes, titles, chapters, transcript file, clip list and posts.
- Release checklist and rights and consent log.
- Draft, scheduled or published episode, by access level.
- Action log and a short release report.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool and the level 3 limits (shows, feeds, dates, actions).
- The guest list and angles (point 1). At level 3 I contact only these guests.
- The outline and the sponsor read (point 2).
- The final audio, title, notes and release slot of each episode (point 3). I do not publish an episode without this yes.
- Always a fresh yes: deleting, unpublishing or replacing a published episode; changing the feed URL, host or GUIDs; publishing a cloned or synthetic voice of a real person; buying a licence; sending any agreement as binding.
- Payments, including sponsor invoices and licences, stay with the client.
