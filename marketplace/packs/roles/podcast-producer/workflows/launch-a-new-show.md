# Launch a new show

**Trigger:** a client wants to start a podcast, or relaunch a show that stopped.

**Cadence:** once per show, over several weeks from first brief to launch day, depending on how fast guests and artwork arrive.

## Steps

1. **Agree the show (level 1).** Who it is for, what each episode gives them, the format, length and release rhythm the host can keep. Read the client's audience questions; with web search connected, check similar shows' names and formats so the new one stands apart, and without it, ask the client which shows their audience already hears.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the show name, promise, format and rhythm.
2. **Plan the first season (prompt: season-plan).** Episode ideas, guests to pitch, working titles and a calendar with recording dates.
3. **Set up the tools.** The client chooses a host and creates the accounts in their own name; I never ask for passwords. At level 2 I fill in show details, categories, artwork and the explicit setting as drafts. Choose episodic or serial, and put season and episode numbers in their fields.
4. **Make the trailer.** Two minutes or less: who the show is for, a highlight, when episodes arrive, and a reminder to follow (Apple trailer guidance, checked 6 Nov 2026).
5. **Record the first episodes (workflow: episode-from-booking-to-release).** Aim to have more than one episode ready for launch day so new listeners have something to keep listening to.
6. **Check everything (skill: episode-release-check).** Format, loudness, feed basics, releases, music, sponsor and AI disclosures, chapters and transcripts.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the trailer, the launch episodes, their notes and the launch date.
7. **Submit and launch.** The client, or I at level 3 within agreed limits, submits the feed to Apple Podcasts and Spotify and, if wanted, sets up YouTube delivery by RSS after checking YouTube's rules on ads and sponsorship. Publish the trailer and launch episodes on the agreed date.
8. **Promote at launch.** Apple's search guidance says engagement from new listeners helps a show rank for related searches, so line up the guest posts, newsletter and the client's own channels for launch week.
9. **Review after the first month (workflow: monthly-show-review).**

## Outputs

- Show brief: audience, promise, format, rhythm, name.
- Season plan and recording calendar.
- Host and platform setup checklist, with drafts at level 2.
- Trailer script and launch episodes, checked.
- Launch-week promo plan and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- The show name, promise, format and rhythm (point 1).
- Accounts are created by the client in the client's name.
- The trailer, launch episodes, notes and launch date (point 2).
- Submitting the feed to each directory and connecting YouTube delivery, unless already inside the agreed level 3 limits.
- Any spend on hosting plans, artwork or promotion stays with the client.
