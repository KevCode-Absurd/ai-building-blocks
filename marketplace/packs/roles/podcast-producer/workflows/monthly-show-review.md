# Monthly show review

**Cadence:** monthly, after the last release of the month.

**Trigger:** also run it when the client asks "is the show working?" or before planning a new season.

## Steps

1. **Collect the numbers (level 1).** From Apple Podcasts Connect: listeners, engaged listeners (at least 20 minutes or 40% of an episode), plays, average consumption and followers; Apple shows data only once at least five unique listeners have played in the period. From Spotify for Creators and YouTube Studio: their equivalent audience and engagement figures. From the host: downloads by episode. With no access, ask for exports or screenshots.
2. **Compare like with like.** Each platform measures differently and covers only its own listeners. Compare an episode with other episodes on the same platform and over the same days since release. Downloads are counted from server logs under IAB Tech Lab's guidelines, and a download is not a listen.
3. **Read the drop-off.** Where the platform shows it, note where listeners leave. Check those timestamps in the transcript: a long intro, a slow segment, an ad break, a topic change.
4. **Find patterns.** Which promises, guests, titles and formats held listeners; which did not. Note anything that changed (release day, length, artwork, new promo channel).
5. **Check the housekeeping.** Feed warnings, missing transcripts, chapters that did not show, rights or policy notices, and episodes missing a signed release.
6. **Recommend.** Three changes for next month, each with the reason from the data and how we will know if it worked. Keep the season plan updated (prompt: season-plan).
   **Approval point:** the client approves the changes before they are applied.
7. **Apply.** At level 2 I draft changed titles, notes or chapters for old episodes; at level 3 I update them within the client's limits and log each change. Changing a published episode's audio, its GUID or the feed URL always needs a fresh yes.

## Outputs

- A one-page monthly report: what happened, per platform, with dates.
- Drop-off notes with timestamps.
- Three recommended changes with reasons and a test for each.
- Housekeeping list and action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- The recommended changes before they are made.
- Edits to published episodes' notes or chapters, unless already inside the level 3 limits.
- Always a fresh yes: replacing published audio, changing GUIDs or the feed URL, or deleting an episode.
- No promise of download numbers is made in any report.
