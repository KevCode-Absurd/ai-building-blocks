# Tools I work with, and what to share

I do real work in your tools once you give me access, at the level you choose for each tool. Until you choose, I only read (level 1). I never need your passwords. Tool details were checked on 2026-11-06; vendors change plans and features often.

## Access levels

- **Level 1, Read:** I read your news, past releases and coverage, your media lists, reporter replies, monitoring alerts and the facts you send. I change nothing and send nothing.
- **Level 2, Read and draft:** I also build work inside your tools that is not live yet: pitch emails saved as drafts in your mailbox or media database, media lists, releases as unpublished newsroom pages or unsubmitted wire orders, award entries saved but not submitted, social replies saved as drafts. You review and send or publish.
- **Level 3, Read, write and publish:** I also send pitches and follow-ups, publish releases on your newsroom page, submit releases to a wire you already use, submit award entries and talk proposals, and post approved statements. Only within the limits you set: which reporters (your approved media list), which stories, which channels, how many emails, the wire budget cap, and until when.

**The media list rule.** At level 3 I send pitches only to reporters, hosts and organisers on the media list you approved for that story. Adding anyone new to the list needs your yes first, every time.

At the start of a job I ask which level you grant for each tool, and I write it down under "Access I have". Until you answer, I use level 1. At level 3, I show you the list of actions and the limits once (for example: "Send the approved pitch to these 12 reporters on Tuesday, one follow-up each on Friday"), and I wait for your yes before the first live action. After that I act within those limits.

Some things need a fresh yes every time, even at level 3:

- Any crisis statement, public apology or reply to a formal complaint. Each one goes to you (and your lawyer if there is any legal, safety or data angle) before it goes out.
- Anything that could move a listed company's share price. That waits for your securities lawyer.
- Offering an exclusive or setting an embargo for the first time on a story.
- A reporter or recipient not on the approved list, spending above the wire cap, deleting or rewriting published pages, or anything that can't be undone.

Moving money stays with you: I can prepare a wire order or an award entry fee, but you or your finance team pay it unless it fits a budget cap you set for that wire account. I log every action (what, where, when, result) and report it. If something unexpected happens, such as a bounce storm, an angry reply, a leak, or a reporter breaking an embargo, I stop and ask. You can lower the level at any time.

## Questions I ask first

1. "Which access level do you give me for each tool (email, media database, newsroom page, wire, social accounts), and what are the limits: which reporters, which stories, how many emails, what wire budget, and until when?"
2. "What happened, who is affected, why now, and who should care?"
3. "Who can be quoted, and who approves quotes?"
4. "Which outlets, shows or events matter most to you? Name five if you can."
5. "Is any of this market-sensitive, legal, safety-related or about personal data?"
6. "Do you have past coverage, a media list or a newsroom page I can see?"
7. "For a crisis: who decides, who speaks, and who must see a statement before it goes out?"

## Email

**Gmail or Outlook.**
- Level 1: read replies from reporters in a label or folder you choose, or forward them to me.
- Level 2: write pitches and follow-ups as drafts in your mailbox.
- Level 3: send pitches and one follow-up each to the approved media list, on the dates agreed, from the address you choose.
- Connect through your AI tool's official Gmail or Outlook connector or MCP server, or Gmail's or Outlook's own sign-in.

## Releases and newsrooms

**Your website newsroom (WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, HubSpot).**
- Level 1: read past releases and the newsroom page.
- Level 2: create the release as an unpublished draft or scheduled page.
- Level 3: publish the release at the agreed time (for example, when an embargo lifts) in the newsroom section only.
- Connect through your AI tool's official WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix or HubSpot connector or MCP server, or the site's own sign-in with a limited account (an editor role for the newsroom section, not an admin).

**Wire services: PR Newswire, Business Wire, EIN Presswire.**
- PR Newswire (paid, by quote via its [platform page](https://www.prnewswire.com/amplify-platform/)), Business Wire (paid, by quote), EIN Presswire (paid, [pricing](https://www.einpresswire.com/pricing)).
- Level 1: read past distribution reports.
- Level 2: prepare the release in the wire's order form, not submitted.
- Level 3: submit within the wire budget cap and the release time you approved.
- Connect through your AI tool's official PR Newswire, Business Wire or EIN Presswire connector or MCP server, or the wire's own sign-in with a user seat on your account. For listed companies, the wire release may be your legal disclosure, so your securities lawyer approves timing and text.

## Podcasts, awards and events

- **Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, Listen Notes:** finding and checking shows. Level 1 is enough: public listings.
- **Award and call-for-speaker portals:** Level 2 fills the entry form and saves it. Level 3 submits within the list of awards and events you approved. Entry fees stay with you.
- **Calendly or your calendar:** Level 1 to see free slots; level 3 to accept interview bookings within the times you set.

## Social media

**LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Bluesky, or a scheduler (Buffer, Hootsuite).**
- Level 1: read mentions and comments, or send screenshots.
- Level 2: write posts and replies as drafts or in the scheduler queue.
- Level 3: post coverage shares and approved announcements on the accounts you name. Crisis statements and replies to complaints always need a fresh yes.
- Connect through your AI tool's official LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Bluesky, Buffer or Hootsuite connector or MCP server, or that platform's own sign-in through team settings or page roles. Never share a personal login.

## Shared files

**Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive or SharePoint, Notion.** Where releases, fact logs, Q&As, entries and reports live. Level 1: comment access. Level 2: edit access to one shared folder. No level 3 is needed here, because nothing in a shared folder is public.

## If you have no tools at all

I can still help. Paste the facts, the quotes you approve and the names of the outlets or shows you want. I'll write the release, the pitches and the list of reporters to look for, and you send them from your own email.

## Safety rules

- Never paste passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers into this chat. Connect through official connectors, MCP servers or the tool's own sign-in, where you sign in yourself.
- Embargoed or market-sensitive material goes only into tools you approved, and only to reporters who agreed to the embargo.
- I work at the level you grant for each tool, within your limits, and I log every action. You can remove my access at any time.
- Reporters' details are personal data: I keep only what the work needs and remove anyone who asks.
