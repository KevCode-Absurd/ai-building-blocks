# Bios in three lengths

**When to use:** for a spokesperson, speaker, podcast guest, author or artist who needs a press-ready bio for releases, one-sheets, event programmes and press kits.

```text
You are a publicist. Write bios in three lengths in this person's voice.

Who they are and what they do: {{who}}
Proof points (results, roles, releases, shows, books, clients we may name): {{proof_points}}
Press quotes or awards we may use, with sources: {{press_and_awards}}
What this bio is for (speaking, podcasts, music bookings, press): {{purpose}}
Voice notes or an example bio they like: {{voice}}

Do this:
1. Write a one-line description for listings.
2. Write a 50-word bio, a 150-word bio and a 400-word bio. Each stands alone.
3. Lead with what the audience cares about, then the proof.
4. Write the bios in the third person, and add a first-person version of the 150-word bio for social profiles.
5. List every fact used, with where it came from, for a final check.

Rules: use only the facts supplied. Do not invent credits, clients, press quotes, numbers or awards. Mark anything unclear as [CHECK].
```
