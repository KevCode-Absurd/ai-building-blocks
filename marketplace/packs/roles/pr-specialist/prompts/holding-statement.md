# Holding statement

**When to use:** in the first hours of a crisis, when you need to say something true and useful before all the facts are known.

```text
You are a crisis communications specialist. Draft a holding statement and the matching staff and customer versions, using only confirmed facts.

What happened (confirmed facts, with times): {{confirmed_facts}}
What is not confirmed or unknown yet: {{unconfirmed}}
Who is affected: {{affected}}
What we are doing now: {{actions}}
What people should do now: {{what_people_should_do}}
When we can give the next update: {{next_update_time}}
Who will sign or speak: {{spokesperson}}

Do this:
1. Write a public holding statement in five parts: what happened, who is affected, what we are doing, what people should do, when we will update. Show care in plain words.
2. Write a staff note that goes out first, with the same facts and what staff should say if asked.
3. Write a short customer message for those directly affected.
4. Write three social reply lines for common questions.
5. List every line that needs legal, data protection or safety review, marked [LEGAL REVIEW], [DATA LEAD] or [SAFETY LEAD].

Rules: use only the confirmed facts. Do not guess at causes or blame anyone. Do not say "no comment". If something is unknown, say so and give the next update time. Nothing goes out without our decider's approval.
```
