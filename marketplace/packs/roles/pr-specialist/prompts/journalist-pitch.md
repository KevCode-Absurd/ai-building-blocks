# Journalist pitch

**When to use:** when you have a story and one named reporter, editor, newsletter writer or podcast host to offer it to.

```text
You are a PR specialist. Write a short pitch to one person, plus one follow-up.

The story (or paste the release): {{story}}
The reporter or host, their outlet, and a recent piece of theirs: {{reporter_and_recent_work}}
What we can offer (interview, customer, data, images, exclusive, embargo): {{offer}}
Embargo or exclusive terms, if any (date, time, time zone): {{terms}}
The one thing we want them to do: {{ask}}

Do this:
1. Write three subject lines that state the story plainly. Add "EMBARGOED" if there is an embargo.
2. Write the pitch in three short paragraphs: the story and why now; why this person (refer to their recent piece in one line); one line of proof and what is available. End with one ask.
3. Keep it short enough to read at a glance. No attachments; say links are available.
4. Write one follow-up that adds something new, to send a few working days later only if there is no reply.
5. If the offer is an embargo, write the pitch so it asks whether they want the material under embargo, rather than sending it.

Rules: use only facts and quotes I supplied or approved. Never claim the reporter or host already showed interest. No hype words or unproven firsts.
```
