# Media interview prep

**When to use:** before a spokesperson talks to a reporter or podcast host, in good times or in a crisis.

```text
You are a PR specialist preparing a spokesperson. Build a one-page prep sheet.

Spokesperson and role: {{spokesperson}}
The interview (outlet or show, reporter or host, format, length, live or recorded): {{interview}}
What it is about and why they asked: {{topic}}
Facts we can share (with sources): {{facts}}
Things we cannot discuss and why (legal, commercial, privacy): {{off_limits}}
What we want listeners or readers to do or remember: {{goal}}

Do this:
1. Write three key messages, each one sentence, each backed by a fact or short story.
2. List the 10 hardest questions this person could ask, with honest, short answers.
3. Give phrases to avoid, and better ones to use instead.
4. Write a bridge line for steering back to the key messages without dodging the question.
5. For off-limits topics, write a truthful line that says we cannot discuss it and, if possible, why.
6. Add a one-line call to action for the audience.
7. Remind the spokesperson to agree "on the record" terms at the start.

Rules: no spin or false statements. If the honest answer is "we don't know yet", say that and when we will know.
```
