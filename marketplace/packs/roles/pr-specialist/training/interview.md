# Interview: PR specialist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Stories and judgement

1. **How do you decide whether a client's news is a story? Walk me through the last time you told a client "this isn't news yet".**
   Why it matters: the newsworthiness call is the expert's core judgement, and saying no well is rare.
2. **What do you do to turn a weak story into one a reporter will run?**
   Why it matters: reveals their angle-finding moves (data, customers, timing, local hooks).
3. **Which kinds of stories have worked best for small businesses you have helped, and which never work?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent patterns grounded in real results.

## Releases and pitches

4. **Show me a release you are proud of. What makes it easy for a reporter to use?**
   Why it matters: their release format becomes the agent's.
5. **How do you build a media list for one story? Where do you look, and what makes you cut a name?**
   Why it matters: list quality decides most outcomes.
6. **Read me a pitch that got a yes. What did it do that others did not?**
   Why it matters: real winning pitches teach structure and tone.
7. **How do you follow up, and when do you stop?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's follow-up rules and protects relationships.
8. **How do you handle quotes when the spokesperson is busy or the words are bland?**
   Why it matters: approved, meaningful quotes are a frequent bottleneck.

## Embargoes, exclusives and sensitive news

9. **How do you set up an embargo or exclusive, and what has gone wrong with one?**
   Why it matters: embargo mistakes burn relationships; their safeguards become the agent's.
10. **When have you had to bring in a lawyer before announcing something? What triggered it?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent where PR's job stops.

## Crisis

11. **Tell me about the hardest crisis you have handled. What did you say in the first hour?**
    Why it matters: a real story shows sequence, tone and trade-offs under pressure.
12. **How do you keep messages consistent across staff, customers, press and social when facts keep changing?**
    Why it matters: consistency is where most crisis responses fail.
13. **What do you never say in a crisis, and what do you say instead?**
    Why it matters: their phrase rules become the agent's.

## Awards, talks, podcasts and bookings

14. **How do you choose which awards, events or podcasts are worth the effort?**
    Why it matters: their scoring criteria shape the agent's shortlists.
15. **What makes an award entry or talk proposal stand out to judges and organisers?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent concrete criteria beyond "tell a story".
16. **How do you prepare someone for a podcast or live interview?**
    Why it matters: prep is where coverage turns into outcomes.
17. **If you work with artists: what does a booker need to see in the first minute?**
    Why it matters: music publicity has its own conventions the agent must follow.

## Tools, measurement and clients

18. **Which tools do you use for lists, monitoring and sending, and what would you do without them?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's tool choices and no-tool fallback.
19. **How do you report results to a client who asks for "ad value" of coverage?**
    Why it matters: teaches outcome-based reporting in the expert's words.
20. **Which clients are a poor fit for PR, and how do you tell them?**
    Why it matters: helps the agent set honest expectations.
21. **What do you check before anything goes out under your name?**
    Why it matters: their checklist becomes the agent's quality bar.
22. **How do you use AI in your work today, and where do you never use it?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's limits on drafting, data and embargoed material.
