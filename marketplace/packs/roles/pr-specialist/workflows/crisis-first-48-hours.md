# Crisis: the first 48 hours

**Trigger:** something has gone wrong that customers, staff, reporters or the public know about, or soon will: a fault, outage, data incident, accident, complaint storm or damaging story.

**Cadence:** start at once; run updates on the schedule promised in the holding statement; review after the issue settles.

## Steps

1. **Open the fact log (skill: crisis-response; level 1).** Confirmed, not confirmed, unknown, with source and time for each fact.
2. **Check duties that come first.** Ask about personal data, harm, product safety, listed-company status and legal claims. The lawyer and the data or safety lead decide on any reporting duties (for example a data breach report or a product recall notice) and their deadlines. Record their decisions.
   **Approval point 1:** the decider confirms severity, roles and who approves statements.
3. **Draft the holding statement and staff note (prompt: holding-statement; level 2 saves drafts in the client's tools).**
   **Approval point 2:** the decider (and the lawyer where needed) approves the holding statement and staff note. This is a fresh yes for each statement, even at level 3.
4. **Tell staff first (level 3 sends the approved staff note to the internal list; otherwise the client sends).**
5. **Tell affected customers, then the public (level 3 publishes the approved versions on the named channels).** Website notice or FAQ, customer email, social post.
6. **Prepare the spokesperson (prompt: media-interview-prep).** Key messages, hardest questions, honest answers.
7. **Handle media and social questions.** Answer with confirmed facts and the next update time. Draft replies at level 2; posting a reply to a formal complaint needs a fresh yes.
8. **Keep the update schedule.** Every promised update goes out on time, even if it says "no new facts yet". Each new statement goes through approval point 2 again.
9. **Correct openly.** If an earlier statement proves wrong, say so and correct it.
10. **Review (after the issue settles).** What happened, how the response went, what people said, what to fix, and a 60-day plan of follow-up messages that show what changed.

## Outputs

- Fact log with times and sources.
- Severity, roles and approvals record.
- Holding statement, staff note, customer message, website notice or FAQ, social replies.
- Media Q&A and spokesperson prep.
- Update log and monitoring notes.
- Post-crisis review and recovery plan.

## Needs the client's approval

- Severity and roles (point 1).
- Every public statement, apology, customer message and reply to a formal complaint (point 2), each time, even at level 3, with legal review where there is a legal, safety or data angle.
- Any regulatory report or notice is decided and made by the client's lawyer or responsible lead, not by me.
- At level 3, after each approval, I publish or send that approved message on the named channels and log it. At level 1 or 2, the client publishes and sends.
