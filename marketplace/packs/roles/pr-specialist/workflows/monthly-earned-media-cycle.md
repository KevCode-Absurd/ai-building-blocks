# Monthly earned media cycle

**Cadence:** monthly, in the last week of the previous month.

**Trigger:** the start of each month, or when the pipeline of stories, awards, talks and podcast pitches runs low.

## Steps

1. **Review last month (level 1).** Coverage, interviews, podcast appearances, award results, talk bookings, and what each led to (visits, enquiries, invitations). Note which reporters and hosts replied.
2. **Refill the story list (prompt: newsworthiness-check).** Collect possible stories from the business: results, customers, hires, data, events, opinions the founder can defend. Rate each one.
3. **Update the stages pipeline (skill: awards-talks-and-podcasts).** Scored shortlist of awards, calls for speakers, podcasts and, for artists, venues and festivals, with deadlines.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the month's plan: which stories to pitch, which awards to enter, which talks and shows to pitch, and the approved list of reporters, hosts and organisers for each.
4. **Write and send (skills: press-release, media-pitching, awards-talks-and-podcasts; level 2 saves drafts, level 3 sends and submits within the approved lists and dates).**
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves each release, entry and proposal before it goes out. Entry fees are paid by the client.
5. **Prepare people for what is booked (prompts: media-interview-prep, bios-in-three-lengths).**
6. **Share coverage (level 3 posts on the client's named social accounts and newsroom; level 2 drafts the posts).** Add coverage to the website "in the news" page where the outlet's terms allow.
7. **Report.** What ran, where, message pull-through, outcomes, relationships built, and next month's best bets. No advertising value equivalents.

## Outputs

- Last month's coverage and outcomes report.
- Rated story list.
- Scored awards, talks and podcast pipeline with deadlines.
- Releases, pitches, entries and proposals, with send and submission logs.
- Prep sheets and bios.

## Needs the client's approval

- The month's plan and the approved lists (point 1). At level 3, after this one-time yes, I send pitches and submit entries only to the approved names and events, on the agreed dates, and log each action.
- Each release, entry and proposal (point 2).
- Always a fresh yes: anyone not on the approved lists, exclusives, embargoes set for the first time, any fee or spend, and anything that touches legal, safety or market-sensitive matters.
- At level 1 or 2, the client sends and submits.
