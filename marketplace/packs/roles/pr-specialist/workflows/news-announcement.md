# News announcement: from story to coverage

**Trigger:** the business has news (a launch, hire, partnership, result, award or event), usually two to four weeks before it can be made public.

**Cadence:** once per announcement.

## Steps

1. **Test the story (prompt: newsworthiness-check; level 1).** Decide story, weak story or no story yet. Strengthen the angle or move the news to owned channels.
   **Approval point 1:** the client agrees the angle, the target outlets and the date. If the news is market-sensitive, the securities lawyer approves timing and text before anything else happens.
2. **Write the release and fact sheet (skill: press-release; level 2 saves it as an unpublished newsroom page).** Collect approved quotes, images and the boilerplate.
   **Approval point 2:** the client and every quoted person approve the final text.
3. **Build the media list (skill: media-pitching; level 2 saves it in the media database or a sheet).** Name each reporter or host, their recent piece and the angle for them. Decide exclusive, embargo or open.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the media list. At level 3 this is the boundary: I pitch only these names.
4. **Write the pitches and follow-ups (prompt: journalist-pitch).** One per person.
5. **Offer the embargo or exclusive (level 3 sends within the approved list; levels 1 and 2 hand the drafts to the client).** Ask first; send material only after a yes. Record who agreed to what.
6. **Prepare the spokesperson (prompt: media-interview-prep).** One prep sheet per booked interview.
7. **Go live at the release time (tool: the client's newsroom or CMS; level 3 publishes at the agreed time; wire submission only within the wire budget cap).** Send to remaining reporters on the approved list once the news is public. Post on owned channels.
8. **Follow up once (level 3, approved list only).** Add something new.
9. **Track and report.** Log sends, replies, interviews, coverage links, message pull-through, and what followed (visits, enquiries, invitations). Report outcomes, not advertising value equivalents.

## Outputs

- Newsworthiness verdict and angle.
- Release, fact sheet and newsroom page.
- Approved media list with reasons.
- Pitches, follow-ups and send log.
- Spokesperson prep sheets.
- Coverage report with outcomes and next steps.

## Needs the client's approval

- The angle, outlets and date (point 1), with securities-lawyer sign-off for market-sensitive news.
- The final release and every quote (point 2).
- The media list (point 3). At level 3, after this one-time yes, I send pitches and one follow-up each to the names on it, on the agreed dates, and log every send.
- Always a fresh yes: anyone not on the approved list, a new exclusive offer, spend above the wire cap, or changing a published release.
- At level 1 or 2, the client sends the pitches and publishes the release.
