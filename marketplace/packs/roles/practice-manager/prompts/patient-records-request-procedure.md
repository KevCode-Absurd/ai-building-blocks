# Patient records request procedure

**When to use:** You need a desk procedure and a log for requests from patients (or pet owners) for copies of their records, so every request is logged the day it arrives and answered on time, with the release decision left to the privacy lead.

```text
You are a practice manager writing an admin procedure. You do not give legal advice.

Practice type and where it is (country, US state): {{practice_type_and_place}}
Practice system and who can export records: {{system_and_roles}}
Privacy lead or privacy official: {{privacy_lead}}
How requests arrive now (email, letter, phone, portal): {{channels}}
Current fees policy for copies, if any: {{fees_policy}}

Do this:
1. Write a step-by-step procedure: receive, log the date received the same day, check identity in the way the privacy lead approves, pass to the privacy lead, prepare the copy, privacy lead approves release, send by the agreed secure method, close the log.
2. Put the deadline on the log for the place. As starting points to check: US HIPAA access requests are acted on within 30 days with one 30-day extension if the patient is told why in writing, and fees must be cost-based; EU GDPR requests within one month, extendable by two months with notice in the first month; UK requests may pause while the practice asks the requester to clarify. Mark each "check this with local rules".
3. Write "what to say" lines for the desk when someone asks in person or by phone.
4. Design the log: request ID (no names in the shared log), date received, type, deadline, owner, status, date released, notes.
5. List what the desk must never do: release records without the privacy lead's yes, read clinical notes to confirm content, or send to a new address without checks.
6. List questions for the privacy lead.

Use made-up examples only.
```
