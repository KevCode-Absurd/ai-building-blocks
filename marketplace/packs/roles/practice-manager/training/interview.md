# Training interview: practice manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share patient, client or pet names, health details or contact details; anonymise every story.

1. **Which kinds of practices have you run (dental, physio, chiro, optometry, vet, therapy), how big, and in which countries or states?**
   Why it matters: privacy, fee and working time rules differ by profession and place, and this sets the limits of the agent's advice.

2. **Walk me through the busiest ten minutes of your typical day. Who does what?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert splits the phone and the door, which shapes every desk procedure.

3. **Which practice systems have you used, and which features did you actually rely on?**
   Why it matters: captures real system know-how (recall tools, templates, reports) the agent can use.

4. **How do you build an appointment template with clinicians who all want different things?**
   Why it matters: reveals how the expert gets clinicians to agree slot rules without overstepping into clinical decisions.

5. **How do you protect admin time and emergency slots so they survive a busy week?**
   Why it matters: protected time is easy to design and hard to keep; the expert's habits make it stick.

6. **Describe your recall process from check-out to the last attempt. Where did it leak before?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a tested contact sequence and the failure points to check.

7. **When has someone at the desk tried to set a recall interval or give clinical advice, and what did you do?**
   Why it matters: records how the expert holds the clinical line kindly but firmly.

8. **Which numbers do you report to the owner each month, and how do you define utilisation?**
   Why it matters: definitions stop arguments; the agent needs the expert's exact ones.

9. **Tell me about a month when the numbers dropped. How did you find the cause?**
   Why it matters: shows the expert's diagnostic method, which the agent repeats in KPI packs.

10. **How do you build a rota and decide who covers whom?**
    Why it matters: captures cover rules that work with real staff and real sickness.

11. **How do you handle a request from a patient for their records? Who decides what is released?**
    Why it matters: pins down the log, the clock and the privacy lead's role.

12. **Tell me about a privacy slip you saw or nearly made. What changed afterwards?**
    Why it matters: reveals the checks the expert now runs, which become the agent's habits.

13. **How do you give staff, locums and outside services access to the practice system?**
    Why it matters: shows the expert's approach to own logins, roles and leavers.

14. **What do you check before switching on a new vendor, such as a texting or AI tool?**
    Why it matters: captures contract and privacy checks (business associate or processor agreements) before data flows.

15. **How do patients learn what things cost before treatment, and who writes the estimates?**
    Why it matters: fee information rules are changing (US self-pay estimates, UK vets); the agent needs the expert's practice.

16. **How do you handle a formal complaint from first contact to closure?**
    Why it matters: records the steps, timings and who signs the reply.

17. **What do you never let the desk say or promise?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's hard limits.

18. **Tell me about the hardest change you led in a practice. What made it stick, or fail?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a realistic sense of pace and how to bring a team along.

19. **What would you check first if you walked into a practice you had never seen?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's first-day checklist.

20. **What do new practice managers most often get wrong?**
    Why it matters: turns into common mistakes and test briefs for the agent.
