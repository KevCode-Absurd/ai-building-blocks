# Monthly practice review

**Cadence:** Once a month, in the first week. About two hours once the setup is in place.

## Steps

1. **Pull the exports (level 1).** Appointments by status and type, available clinical hours, recall report, new patients, wait-list activity, the month's rota, and the records request, breach and complaint logs.
2. **Build the KPI pack.** Use the `practice-kpi-pack` skill with last month's definitions. Show every sum. Break down the two biggest movers.
3. **Check recalls.** Patients due, contacted, booked and still overdue by recall type. Check the workflow sent what it should (failed messages, opt-outs). At level 3, fix template typos within approved wording; anything that changes meaning goes to the owner. *Approval: owner approves new wording or new stages.*
4. **Check the diary and template.** Unused emergency slots, protected time overwritten, overruns by appointment type. Propose one template change if the pattern holds for two months. *Approval: the clinician whose template changes.*
5. **Check the rota.** Uncovered sessions, overtime, leave coming up next month. Draft next month's rota with the `staff-rota-with-cover-rules` prompt. *Approval: owner approves the rota; check this with local rules for breaks and hours.*
6. **Check the legal clocks.** Every open records request, breach and complaint against its deadline. Escalate anything due within a week to the privacy lead or owner. For UK vet practices, check the CMA compliance calendar. *Approval: releases, notices and formal complaint replies need a fresh yes each time.*
7. **Check access.** Run the monthly access check: leavers made inactive, no shared logins, roles still right. *Approval: owner approves user changes.*
8. **Pick one test.** One change for next month, its measure and owner, in a Plan-Do-Study-Act cycle.
9. **Report.** Send the one-page pack, the open-clock list, the test for next month and the action log to the agreed people (level 3) or to the owner to send (level 2).

## Outputs

- Monthly KPI pack with definitions and arithmetic
- Recall check and fixes
- Template change proposal (if any)
- Next month's rota with cover rules
- Open legal clocks list
- Access check result
- One test for next month
- Action log

## Needs the client's approval

New message wording, template changes, the rota, user changes, and every record release, breach notice and formal complaint reply.
