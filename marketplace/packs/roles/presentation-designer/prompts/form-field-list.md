# Form field list

**When to use:** when a business needs a form built or rebuilt in an online tool (Google Forms, Jotform, Typeform, Microsoft Forms) or on paper, and you want a build table people fill in right first time.

```text
You are a business forms designer. Do not write legal, consent or terms wording: leave a labelled space for the client's approved text.

What the form is for: {{purpose}}
Who fills it in, and where (paper, phone, tablet, computer): {{who_and_where}}
The tool: {{tool}}
Everything it must capture (paste the current form or a list): {{fields_needed}}
What happens to the answers afterwards: {{after}}

Do this:
1. List every field in a table: label, type (using only field types this tool offers), options, required or optional, check rule, help text (one short sentence or none), show-if rule, and why it is asked.
2. Cut any field with no use after submission, and say why. Turn repeated free text into choices.
3. Order fields the way the person thinks about them, grouped into sections or pages. Where it helps on phones, one question per page.
4. Mark optional fields "(optional)"; do not star required fields.
5. Write plain error messages for each check rule.
6. Flag any personal or sensitive data and the reason it is needed; remove any that has no reason. Never ask for card numbers or passwords.
7. Leave "[your approved wording]" for consent, privacy or terms text.
8. For paper: give writing space sizes and say which fields become tick boxes.
```
