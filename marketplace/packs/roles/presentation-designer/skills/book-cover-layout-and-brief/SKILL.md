# Book cover layout and brief

A cover has to work twice: as a thumbnail in an online store, and as a printed object whose spine depends on page count and paper. KDP and IngramSpark publish exact rules for both. This skill turns the author's details into a sized layout and a brief an artist can work from.

## When to use

- A self-published paperback or hardback needs a cover planned.
- A series needs a shared look.
- An author has art (or AI art) and needs it laid out for print and ebook.
- A cover was rejected by the printer and needs fixing.

## What you need

- Title, subtitle, author name (exactly as printed), series name and number if any.
- Genre and the reader.
- Printer or platform (KDP, IngramSpark or another), binding, trim size, page count, paper (white, cream, colour).
- Back cover blurb (final text), author bio and photo if wanted, imprint logo if any, ISBN.
- Review quotes only if the author supplies them with their source and permission.
- Existing art or the artist's details; whether any art is AI-generated.
- Access: level 1 is usual. The author uploads to their own KDP or IngramSpark account; there is no official connector that I have checked.

## Steps

1. **Check the facts.** If the page count is missing, stop and ask: the spine depends on it.
2. **Get the exact size from the printer.** Use KDP's cover calculator or IngramSpark's cover template generator for this trim, page count and paper, and lay out to their template. As a cross-check for KDP paperbacks, spine width = page count x 0.002252 in (white paper), x 0.0025 in (cream), x 0.002347 in (premium colour) or x 0.002252 in (standard colour). Full width = bleed + back + spine + front + bleed; full height = bleed + trim height + bleed. KDP bleed is 0.125 in (3.2 mm); IngramSpark uses 0.125 in (3 mm).
3. **Spine rules.** On KDP, spine text only for books over 79 pages. Keep title, author and imprint logo inside the printer's spine safe area from the template. Agree the spine text direction with the author, following the convention of the market where the book will mostly sell.
4. **Read the genre.** Describe in words what this genre's readers expect at a glance: type style, image type, colour mood, where the title sits. Never point to a specific real cover to copy.
5. **Lay out the front.** Zones for title, subtitle, author name and image. Give type sizes and weights. The title must read when the cover is shown small in a store: shrink the front to thumbnail size and check that the title and the main shape still read.
6. **Lay out the back.** Blurb zone, author photo spot if any, review quotes only if supplied with permission, imprint logo, and the barcode area. KDP adds a barcode if you do not supply one; IngramSpark's template places a barcode that must not be resized, only moved.
7. **Ebook cover.** For KDP: ideal 2,560 x 1,600 px (height to width 1.6:1), minimum 1,000 x 625 px, JPEG or TIFF, RGB, under 50 MB; add a thin grey border if the cover is very light. Simplify the front for small sizes if needed.
8. **Write the artist brief or AI prompts.** Scene, mood, colours, the space to keep clear for title and author, the full wrap size and resolution, what continues around the spine and back. For an artist, add rights questions (exclusive or not, uses allowed, credit) and the number of revision rounds.
9. **AI art.** If any image is AI-generated, tell the author that KDP asks them to disclose AI-generated images when publishing, even if edited afterwards, and that in the US prompts alone do not make AI output protectable; the hand-made layout and changes are where human authorship sits.
10. **Print file checklist.** KDP: one PDF with back, spine and front; images at 300 DPI; CMYK; no colour profiles or spot colours. IngramSpark: PDF/X-1a:2001 or PDF/X-3:2002, 300 ppi, CMYK, total ink no higher than 240%, built on its template. Remove template guide layers before export.
11. **Series look (if needed).** A shared grid, type and colour rules, and the spine pattern that will line up on a shelf.

## Checks

- Size and spine come from the printer's current calculator or template for this exact book.
- Spine text only if the page count allows it.
- Every word on the cover is from the author's brief; no invented quotes, awards or bestseller claims.
- The title reads at thumbnail size.
- No real cover is copied or closely imitated.
- AI art disclosure and rights explained if relevant.
- The print checklist matches the named printer.

## Output format

1. **Book facts:** title, author, printer, binding, trim, pages, paper.
2. **Sizes:** spine width with the formula or calculator used, full wrap width and height with bleed, ebook size.
3. **Zone map:** front, spine and back, with contents and type sizes.
4. **Genre notes** in words.
5. **Artist brief or AI prompts.**
6. **Print and ebook file checklists.**
7. **Open questions.**

## Example of good output (short)

*Illustrative structure, not a real book or author: a paperback novel with a lighthouse on the cover, published through KDP. Copy the shape, never the content: every fact comes from the client's own context.*

**Book facts:** paperback, KDP, 6 x 9 in, 312 pages, white paper, black and white interior.

**Sizes:** spine = 312 x 0.002252 in = 0.703 in (confirm with KDP's cover calculator, which gives the template). Full wrap = 0.125 + 6 + 0.703 + 6 + 0.125 = 12.953 in wide; 0.125 + 9 + 0.125 = 9.25 in high. Ebook: 2,560 x 1,600 px, RGB JPEG.

**Front:** title in a condensed serif, two lines, top third, about a quarter of the cover's height; author name across the bottom band; image zone fills the middle (a lighthouse at dusk seen from a harbour wall, ledger pages blowing in the foreground). Thumbnail check: at small size the lighthouse shape and the title still read; the subtitle does not, so it moves to the back.

**Spine:** 312 pages is over 79, so spine text is allowed: title and author, top to bottom, inside the template's spine safe area; imprint logo at the foot.

**Back:** blurb (from the brief, 140 words) in the top half; no review quotes (none supplied); barcode area bottom right, left free for KDP's barcode.

**AI prompt (one of three):** "Painted illustration, lighthouse at dusk from a stone harbour wall, warm lamp light, cool blue sky, loose ledger pages lifting in the wind in the foreground, clear empty sky in the top third for the title, portrait 2:3, no text, no lettering." Note for the author: if you use this AI art, tell KDP it is AI-generated when you publish.
