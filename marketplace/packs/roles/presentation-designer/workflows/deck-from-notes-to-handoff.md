# Deck from notes to handoff

**Trigger:** a talk, pitch, sales or training deck is due, starting from notes or a crowded existing deck.

**Cadence:** per deck; for a team template, review it every six months or when the brand changes.

## Steps

1. **Intake and access (tool: access.md questions).** Audience, room or screen, time slot, tool, template, whether the deck will be sent afterwards, and the access level for the deck file (and the team template if it exists). Write it under "Access I have". Level 1 until the client answers.
2. **Template first, if there is none (skill: template-and-style-system).** Canvas size set before any slide is built, type scale, colour roles with contrast, named layouts.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the style system, or confirms the existing template.
3. **Story and headlines (skill: deck-layout-plan, steps 1 to 5).** Main point, supporting points, a sentence headline per slide, and a slide count that fits the slot.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the headline list. Changing the story later costs the most.
4. **Layouts and evidence (skill: deck-layout-plan, steps 6 to 8; prompts: slide-crowding-fix, chart-choice).** Layout per slide, chart specs from the client's numbers only, detail moved to speaker notes, [add] wherever a number, logo or quote is missing.
5. **Build (tools: Canva or Figma MCP servers, Google Slides, PowerPoint or Keynote).** At level 1, the client's team builds from the plan and build notes. At level 2, build in a copy marked "DRAFT".
6. **Accessibility pass (prompt: accessibility-check; tool: the app's own checker).** Contrast, unique titles, alt text, reading order, fonts embedded or fallbacks named, captions.
7. **Review.** The client presents or reads it through; note slides that fail the 3-second glance and fix them.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the final deck.
8. **Finish and share (level 3 only).** Before the first live action, show the action list (update which file, export which formats, share with whom) and the limits, and get a yes. Export PDF or PPTX, share with the people named, and log each action. Overwriting a team template, or sharing beyond the named people, needs a fresh yes.
9. **Report.** The action log, what was cut and why, open [add] items, and a short "how to edit this deck" note for the team.

## Outputs

- Style system and layout list (if new).
- Headline list and layout plan.
- Built deck (draft or final, depending on access).
- Speaker notes and, if needed, a read-alone version.
- Accessibility fix list and results.
- Action log and handoff note.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access level and limits per tool (step 1).
- The style system or template (point 1).
- The headline list (point 2).
- The final deck (point 3).
- The level 3 action list before the first live action, and a fresh yes before overwriting a shared template or sharing outside the named people (step 8).
