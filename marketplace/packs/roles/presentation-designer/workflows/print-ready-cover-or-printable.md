# Print-ready cover or printable

**Trigger:** a self-published book needs a cover, or a workbook, form or lead magnet needs to go to a printer, print-on-demand service or home printers.

**Cadence:** per project; re-check printer specs at the start of every project, because they change.

## Steps

1. **Intake and access (tool: access.md questions).** What it is, who uses it, the printer or platform, trim or page size, page count and paper, the tool, and the access level for each tool and service. Level 1 until the client answers.
2. **Get the current spec.** Download the printer's template or guide for this exact job: KDP's cover calculator, IngramSpark's cover template generator and File Creation Guide, or the printer's own artwork guide. The printer's rule beats any general rule.
3. **Plan the layout.**
   - Covers (skill: book-cover-layout-and-brief; prompt: cover-size-check): spine width, full wrap, zones, genre notes, ebook size.
   - Forms, workbooks and lead magnets (skill: form-and-workbook-layout): field table or page plan, writing spaces, print settings.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the layout plan and all text on it. Nothing invented; gaps marked [add].
4. **Brief the art (skill: book-cover-layout-and-brief, step 8).** Artist brief with rights questions, or AI image prompts. If AI art is used, the client knows KDP asks for disclosure of AI-generated images.
5. **Build.** At level 1, the client's designer builds from the spec. At level 2, build in a copy marked "DRAFT" in Canva or another tool the client connected.
6. **Preflight (prompt: accessibility-check for PDFs; the printer's checklist).** Size against the template, bleed, colour mode (CMYK for print), resolution, PDF standard, fonts embedded, barcode area, writing spaces tested on a printed page.
   **Approval point 2:** the client signs off a proof (a printed page at full size or the printer's digital proof).
7. **Send or upload.**
   - Books on KDP or IngramSpark: the client uploads from their own account, answers the AI content question, sets the price and publishes. There is no official connector that I have checked; I provide the files and an entry sheet.
   - Printers and print-on-demand (level 3 only): after the confirmed action list, send the approved files or place the order within the spending cap and quantity set. A fresh yes before any order above the cap or any change after proof sign-off.
8. **Log and report.** What was sent, where, when and the result. If the printer rejects a file, stop, read the message, fix and ask before resending.

## Outputs

- Printer spec used, with its date.
- Layout plan, zone map or field table.
- Artist brief or AI prompts.
- Print-ready file checklist and proof.
- Entry sheet for KDP or IngramSpark.
- Action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access level and limits per tool and service (step 1).
- The layout plan and every word on it (point 1).
- The proof (point 2).
- Any print order (within the cap at level 3; a fresh yes above it).
- Upload, AI disclosure, pricing and publishing, which the client does in their own account.
