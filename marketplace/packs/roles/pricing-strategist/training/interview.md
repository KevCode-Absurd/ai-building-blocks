# Interview: pricing strategist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the strategist's work (for example, subscription questions for someone who only prices physical products).

## How you set a price

1. **Walk me through the last price you set, from the first number you looked at to the price you recommended.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you build a cost floor? Which costs do owners forget?**
   Why it matters: a floor with missing costs lets a business price below cost.
3. **How do you put rival prices on the same basis? Show me a comparison you adjusted.**
   Why it matters: headline-to-headline comparisons mislead.
4. **What buyer evidence do you trust, and how do you tell "too expensive" from "not worth it"?**
   Why it matters: the answer changes whether to cut the price or fix the offer.
5. **When have you told a client to charge more, and what happened?**
   Why it matters: captures your judgement and your evidence for a rise.

## How you shape packages and models

6. **How do you choose what to charge by: seats, usage, projects or a flat fee?**
   Why it matters: the value metric decides whether bills feel fair as customers grow.
7. **Show me a three-tier design you are proud of. Why is the middle tier the default?**
   Why it matters: good tiers are a craft the agent should copy.
8. **When do you say no to a fourth tier or another add-on?**
   Why it matters: reveals where simplicity beats choice.
9. **How do you price an annual plan against monthly?**
   Why it matters: cash, churn and margin trade off here.

## Discounts, tests and readouts

10. **What does a good discount policy look like? What does it make a buyer give in return?**
    Why it matters: unmanaged discounts leak margin quietly.
11. **Walk me through a price test you ran: the design, the rule you set beforehand and the result.**
    Why it matters: shows how you keep a test honest and undoable.
12. **How do you read a past price change when the season also changed?**
    Why it matters: the comparison group is what separates a price effect from noise.
13. **Tell me about a promotion that sold a lot and lost money. How did you show it?**
    Why it matters: profit, not units, has to lead the readout.
14. **How small can a business be before a price test cannot tell you anything? What do you do then?**
    Why it matters: small firms need a different method, not a fake test.

## Price displays, rises and rules

15. **How do you check a "was" price before it goes live, and in which markets?**
    Why it matters: false reference prices are where businesses get fined.
16. **How do you handle fees: booking, service, delivery, joining?**
    Why it matters: drip pricing rules differ by market and are enforced.
17. **Walk me through a price rise for existing customers you handled. What did the letter say?**
    Why it matters: notice, options and tone decide who stays.
18. **What do you never do in pricing, even if a client asks?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's red lines, such as talking to rivals or hiding fees.

## Clients and the hard job

19. **How do you explain a pricing recommendation to an owner who prices by gut?**
    Why it matters: captures your voice and how you show the maths.
20. **What access do you ask for, and what do you never change without the client's yes?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's approval habits.
21. **Tell me about your hardest pricing job. What made it hard and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: the story holds the judgement calls no checklist covers.
