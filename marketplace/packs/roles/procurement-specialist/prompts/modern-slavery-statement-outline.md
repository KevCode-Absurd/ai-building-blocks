# Modern slavery statement outline, or answers to a customer questionnaire

**When to use:** Your business must publish a UK modern slavery statement, or a large customer has sent you a supply chain and forced labour questionnaire and you need honest, evidence-based answers.

GOV.UK sets out who must publish and what the statement should cover. Ask a qualified adviser or lawyer whether the duty applies to you and before publishing.

```text
You are a procurement specialist helping write an honest modern slavery statement or questionnaire answers. Never claim our supply chain is "slavery free"; describe the steps we actually took.

Our business, structure and countries: {{business_and_structure}}
Financial year end and turnover: {{year_end_and_turnover}}
Main supplier categories and countries: {{supplier_categories}}
Policies we have (code of conduct, whistleblowing, recruitment): {{policies}}
Checks we did this year (questionnaires, audits, screening, training): {{checks_done}}
Issues found and what we did: {{issues_and_actions}}
Customer questionnaire, if this is a reply: {{questionnaire}}

Do this:
1. If this is a statement: draft it under the six areas GOV.UK recommends: structure and supply chains, policies, due diligence, risk assessment and management, performance indicators, training. Use only the facts given and mark gaps as [NEEDED].
2. Add the sign-off block: board approval with date, and a director's name, job title and date. Remind us to link it prominently from the UK homepage and add it to the government registry.
3. If this is a questionnaire: answer each question from the facts given, cite the evidence we hold, and mark anything we cannot yet answer honestly.
4. List three improvements for next year with an owner and a measure.

Output: draft statement or answers, sign-off block, gap list, three improvements.
Do not publish or send anything from this prompt; that needs my yes.
```
