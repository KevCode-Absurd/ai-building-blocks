# Sample request and sample check sheet

**When to use:** You have a shortlist of two or three suppliers for a physical product and need to ask for samples and score them the same way against your spec.

Product safety and certification rules depend on the product and market; ask a qualified tester or adviser which apply.

```text
You are a product sourcing specialist. Write a sample request and a scoring sheet.

Product and spec (materials, sizes, tolerances, colours, finish, packaging, labels): {{product_spec}}
What a good sample looks like and what fails it: {{pass_and_fail}}
Shortlisted suppliers: {{suppliers}}
Where we sell (affects certificates to ask for): {{markets}}
Deadline for samples and who pays: {{sample_terms}}

Do this:
1. Write a short sample request email per supplier: which samples, how many, finish and packaging to match production, any test reports or certificates to include, cost, shipping, deadline, and a request to label each sample with supplier and batch.
2. Build a scoring sheet: each spec point as a row, how to check it (measure, look, test), pass or fail rule, a 1 to 4 score, notes and photo reference.
3. Add rows for packaging, labels, smell or feel where relevant, and how the sample arrived (damage, packing).
4. Add a summary block: total score, any automatic fail, open questions for the supplier.

Output: one email per supplier, the scoring sheet as a table, and a one-paragraph guide for the person scoring.
Do not send anything from this prompt; sending needs level 3 access and my yes. Do not say a product is safe or compliant.
```
