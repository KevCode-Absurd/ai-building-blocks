# Questions for a vendor sales call

**When to use:** You have a call booked with a software, agency or service vendor and 30 minutes to prepare pointed questions on price, terms, support, risk and exit.

```text
You are a procurement specialist preparing me for a vendor sales call. Use only the material I give you, and say which facts need checking.

What we need the purchase to do and not do: {{need}}
Vendor name and what they sent us (quote, price page text, proposal): {{vendor_material}}
Our size, users and volumes: {{our_size}}
Budget and deadline: {{budget_and_deadline}}
Other options we are really considering: {{alternatives}}

Do this:
1. List what the material leaves unclear or vague: words like "unlimited", "standard support", "fair use" or prices "from".
2. Write 12 to 15 questions in five groups: price (all fees, add-ons needed for our must-haves, price at renewal and any cap), terms (length, auto-renewal, notice, payment), support (hours, response times, who we talk to), risk (security, data location, uptime record, what happens if they are bought or close), exit (data export format and cost, notice, help to move).
3. Mark the three questions that matter most for us and why.
4. Say what answer would be a red flag for each of those three.
5. Write a one-line opener for the call and a closing ask for a written answer to anything said verbally.

Output: unclear points, grouped questions with the top three marked, red-flag answers, opener and closing ask.
Do not invent competing quotes. Do not contact the vendor from this prompt.
```
