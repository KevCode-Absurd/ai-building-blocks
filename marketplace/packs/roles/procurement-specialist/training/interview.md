# Training interview: Procurement specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share supplier prices, contract terms or names that are confidential; anonymise every story.

1. **What do you buy most often, for what kinds of businesses, and in which countries?**
   Why it matters: category and location decide which methods, rules and sanctions lists the agent points to.

2. **Walk me through your first hour with a new buying request.**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions an expert never skips.

3. **How do you write a need or specification so quotes come back comparable?**
   Why it matters: the quality of quotes depends on the question, and this is where beginners fail.

4. **When do you use an RFI, an RFQ or an RFP, and when do you skip formal requests altogether?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent judgement on how much process a purchase deserves.

5. **How do you choose criteria and weights, and how do you stop them shifting once a favourite appears?**
   Why it matters: protects the agent's scoring from bias.

6. **Show me how you put two very different quotes on one basis.**
   Why it matters: turns the expert's levelling habits into steps the agent can repeat.

7. **Which hidden costs catch clients out most often in your categories?**
   Why it matters: feeds the agent's checklist of terms and fees to look for.

8. **What due diligence do you do for a low-risk supplier, and what changes for a high-risk one?**
   Why it matters: sets proportionate depth instead of one heavy checklist for everything.

9. **How do you run sanctions screening, and what do you do with a possible match?**
   Why it matters: the agent must stop at the right moment and record the right evidence.

10. **Tell me about a supplier that looked fine on paper and failed. What would have caught it?**
    Why it matters: real failures show which checks matter most.

11. **How do you handle forced labour or modern slavery questions from customers or for your own statement?**
    Why it matters: captures honest practice where overclaiming is the common mistake.

12. **How do you find a client's real alternative before a negotiation, and what do you do when there is none?**
    Why it matters: the agent must never bluff, so it needs the expert's way of building options.

13. **What do you trade instead of price, and which trades have worked best?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a list of concessions with a track record.

14. **Which contract terms do you always read first, and which do you send to a lawyer?**
    Why it matters: sets the line between the agent's checklist and legal advice.

15. **How do you prepare for a renewal, and how early do you start?**
    Why it matters: most savings are lost at renewals that roll over unnoticed.

16. **How do you prove a saving actually reached the accounts?**
    Why it matters: makes the agent report real savings, not quoted ones.

17. **What do you look for before telling a small supplier to bid for a public contract?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a sound bid or no-bid judgement.

18. **How do you check utility bills and compare energy quotes?**
    Why it matters: captures a specialist method for a common cost-cutting job.

19. **Which tools do you use for spend, sourcing and screening, and what access do you give an assistant in each?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's access levels in real practice.

20. **Tell me about the hardest buying job you have done. What made it hard and what did you decide?**
    Why it matters: a full story shows trade-offs and judgement the checklists miss.

21. **What does a great procurement file look like when an auditor or partner reads it?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's standard for records and decision memos.
