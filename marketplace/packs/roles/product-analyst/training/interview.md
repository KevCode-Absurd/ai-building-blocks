# Training interview: product analyst

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names and any personal data; describe products and datasets in general terms.

1. **What kinds of products do you know best: apps, SaaS, online shops, subscriptions, memberships?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and where its advice stops.

2. **When a client says "set up tracking for us", what do you ask before writing a single event?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert turns a vague request into questions and flows.

3. **Which naming convention do you use for events and properties, and why that one?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a convention the expert can defend, not just copy.

4. **Tell me about an event list you inherited that was a mess. What was wrong, and how did you fix it without breaking reports?**
   Why it matters: records real clean-up and migration habits.

5. **How do you decide whether an event should fire from the browser, the app or the server?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the expert's rules for accurate counts.

6. **What goes wrong with user identity, and how do you set up identify calls?**
   Why it matters: identity errors break funnels and retention more than anything else.

7. **Walk me through the last funnel you built, from the first question to the change you recommended.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where time goes.

8. **How do you choose the conversion window, step order and counting method?**
   Why it matters: makes the agent state settings the way the expert does.

9. **What is the most misleading funnel or retention chart you have seen, and why did it mislead?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent concrete traps to spot.

10. **How do you choose the return event for retention? When is "any event" wrong?**
    Why it matters: the return event decides whether retention means anything.

11. **When do you use "on" versus "on or after" retention, and how do you explain the difference to an owner?**
    Why it matters: captures how the expert handles differing tool defaults.

12. **How small is too small for a cohort or group, and how do you show that?**
    Why it matters: lets the expert set the small-group rule the kit leaves open.

13. **How do you pick which drop-off to fix first?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's ranking of size against fixability.

14. **A client wants to test a change but has little traffic. What do you tell them?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert handles tests that cannot reach their size.

15. **Have you seen a test stopped early or a traffic split go wrong? What happened?**
    Why it matters: real stories teach peeking and split checks better than rules.

16. **How do you keep personal data out of events, and what do you do when a client wants emails in their analytics?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's privacy line in the expert's words.

17. **Which tools do you trust for which job (GA4, Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog, a warehouse), and when do you switch?**
    Why it matters: grounds tool advice in practice.

18. **What do you check before you send any analysis to a client?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's personal quality bar.

19. **Tell me about a time the data said something the client did not want to hear. How did you say it?**
    Why it matters: captures tone and how the expert handles pushback.

20. **What would you never do in a client's analytics account, even with full access?**
    Why it matters: sets the expert's own limits on top of the access levels.
