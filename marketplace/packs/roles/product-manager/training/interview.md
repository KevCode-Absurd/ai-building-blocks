# Interview: product manager

The training coach asks these questions one at a time and writes down the answers in the expert's own words. Ask for a real example whenever an answer stays general.

1. **Tell me about the last feature you took from idea to launch. What happened at each step?**
   Why it matters: shows the expert's real process and where they spend time.

2. **How do you know a problem is worth solving? What evidence is enough for you?**
   Why it matters: separates evidence-led judgment from opinion and sets the agent's bar.

3. **How do you run customer conversations so people tell you what they do, not what they think you want to hear?**
   Why it matters: captures interview habits that stop false positives.

4. **When a founder brings a big idea, how do you cut it to a first version? Give a real example of what you cut.**
   Why it matters: scope cutting is the core skill of this role.

5. **How do you decide the one thing a first version must prove, and the result that means stop?**
   Why it matters: shows whether the expert sets go and stop rules before building.

6. **What does a good PRD look like to you, and what do you leave out?**
   Why it matters: defines the spec format the agent should produce.

7. **How do you write acceptance criteria that a tester can pass or fail? Show me one you are proud of.**
   Why it matters: testable criteria prevent rework.

8. **How do you prioritise a long backlog? Which scoring method do you trust, and when do you ignore the score?**
   Why it matters: reveals the expert's judgment beyond the formula.

9. **How do you say no to a senior stakeholder or a big customer?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent language for hard conversations.

10. **How do you build a roadmap without promising dates the team has not agreed?**
    Why it matters: protects the team and the client's credibility.

11. **Which metrics do you set before a launch, and how do you choose targets?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert ties work to outcomes.

12. **Tell me about an experiment whose result you did not trust. What was wrong?**
    Why it matters: data-quality instincts (sample mismatch, peeking, novelty) are hard to learn from books.

13. **What do you do when traffic is too low for an A/B test?**
    Why it matters: most small businesses face this; the expert's alternatives are valuable.

14. **How do you plan a rollout and a rollback? Has a rollback ever saved you?**
    Why it matters: captures release safety habits.

15. **What extra checks do you run before shipping an AI feature?**
    Why it matters: AI features fail differently; the expert's checks become the agent's checklist.

16. **How do you handle disclosure and data questions for AI or personal data, and when do you call a lawyer?**
    Why it matters: sets the line between product judgment and legal advice.

17. **Which tools do you work in every day, and what would you let an assistant change in them?**
    Why it matters: maps tools and comfortable access levels.

18. **What does your one-page stakeholder update look like?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a proven reporting format.

19. **Tell me about a launch that went wrong. What did you change afterwards?**
    Why it matters: real failures produce the best rules.

20. **What do clients most often misunderstand about product management?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent ready explanations in the expert's voice.
