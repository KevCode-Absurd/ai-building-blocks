# Idea to build-ready spec

**Trigger:** a new product or feature idea, a cluster of customer requests, or a founder asking what to build first.

**Time:** usually one to three weeks, depending on how much evidence exists and how fast the client can recruit people to talk to.

## Steps

1. **Intake and access (access.md questions).** Goal for the period, the idea, evidence on hand, budget and people. Ask which access level the client grants in each tool (issue tracker, docs, analytics) and record it as "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1.
2. **Problem brief (prompt: problem-brief-from-notes).** Group the evidence into problems, with sources. With web search connected, check competitors live and date findings; without it, list what to check.
3. **Discovery and scope (skill: discovery-and-first-version-scope).** Four risks, the one thing to prove with go and stop results, must, later and never, the cheapest way to deliver each must, the appetite and the test plan. If evidence is thin, run the cheapest test first; recruiting and consent follow the customer-insights-researcher kit.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the problem, the one thing to prove, the go and stop results, and the scope.
4. **Metrics (prompt: success-metrics-plan).** Success and guardrail metrics, tracking events, and how targets will be set from a baseline.
5. **Spec (skill: prd-and-user-stories).** PRD, stories with numbered acceptance criteria, edge cases, risks, rollout and rollback.
6. **AI and data check (skill: ai-feature-responsible-launch)** if the feature uses AI or new personal data. Open legal questions go to the client's adviser.
   **Approval point 2:** the client and the lead engineer approve the PRD and stories; engineering confirms feasibility and effort.
7. **File the work.**
   - Level 1: stories as text to paste.
   - Level 2: PRD as a draft page; stories as unassigned backlog issues under one epic; log each.
   - Level 3: create and assign issues in the agreed project and cycle, within the limits; post a short note in the agreed channel. Ask again before notifying anyone outside the agreed list.
8. **Hand over.** Brief, scope, metrics plan, PRD, story list, AI and data notes, open questions with owners, and the action log.

## Outputs

- One-page problem brief with sourced evidence.
- First-version scope with the one thing to prove and go or stop results.
- Success metrics plan.
- PRD and stories with acceptance criteria.
- AI and data readiness notes (when relevant).
- Issues in the tracker (level 2 or 3) and the action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access levels per tool, and the limits for level 3.
- The problem, the thing to prove, and the scope (point 1).
- The PRD and stories (point 2).
- Any date shared outside the team, any message to customers, and anything outside the level 3 limits.
- Legal questions on AI or data, answered by the client's adviser.
