# Quarterly roadmap cycle

**Cadence:** a planning round before each quarter or cycle, a request screen as requests arrive, and a monthly stakeholder update.

## Steps

1. **Before the quarter: goals and access.** Agree two or three goals with their measures and latest values. Record "Access I have" for the backlog tool, the roadmap tool and the update channel (level 1 until the client answers).
2. **Collect the list.** Export the backlog, open requests and ideas. With level 1 access to Jira, Linear, GitHub or Jira Product Discovery, read them directly.
3. **Score and fit (skill: prioritise-and-roadmap).** Bets, scoring table, dependencies, capacity with room held back, the "not now" list with reasons, and the now, next, later roadmap.
   **Approval point 1:** the client and engineering lead approve the goals, the committed list and the "not now" list.
4. **Publish the plan.**
   - Level 1: give the roadmap and table as text.
   - Level 2: add scores and a draft roadmap view visible only to the client.
   - Level 3: update priority fields, reorder the backlog and publish the roadmap view to the agreed audience, within the limits.
5. **During the quarter: screen requests (prompt: feature-request-screen).** Accept, defer or decline each new request with a reason. Customer-facing replies go out only with the client's yes.
6. **Monthly: update (prompt: roadmap-stakeholder-update).** Done, next, changed and why, risks, decisions needed. At level 3, post to the agreed channel only.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves any change to committed work or to a date promised to a customer.
7. **After each release: read the results (prompt: experiment-readout, or the success metrics plan).** Feed the result into the next round's scores.
8. **End of quarter: review.** What moved each goal, what did not, what the "not now" list taught us, and what changes in the next round.

## Outputs

- Goals with measures.
- Scoring table and the "not now" list with reasons.
- Now, next, later roadmap (draft or published).
- Request decisions and reply texts.
- Monthly one-page updates.
- End-of-quarter review and the action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access levels and level 3 limits per tool.
- Goals, the committed list and the "not now" list (point 1).
- Changes to committed work, and any date shared with customers (point 2).
- Replies to customers and anything posted outside the agreed audience.
