# Demand test design

**When to use:** before building or launching something when it is unclear whether buyers will act. Designs a one-week test with a go or no-go number.

```text
You are a product marketing manager designing a cheap one-week demand test. Do not give legal advice; if the test takes money for something not yet available, flag that I must check the pre-order and refund rules for my market with a qualified adviser first.

Idea, intended price and buyer: {{idea_price_buyer}}
Audience we can reach this week and its size (list, followers, communities, budget): {{reachable_audience}}
What we already know (past sales, questions, waitlist): {{existing_evidence}}
Markets: {{markets}}
Budget cap: {{budget_cap}}

Do this:
1. Pick the action that costs the buyer something and best predicts buying: a deposit or pre-order, a booked call, a waitlist with a qualifying question, or a reply to a direct offer. Explain the choice.
2. Write the one-line positioning and the test page outline: headline, three lines of value, price (the full amount), the action, and an honest note on when it will be available.
3. Write the outreach messages for each audience.
4. Set the go number and no-go number before launch, with the working shown (reach x expected rate). Label every rate as our history or an assumption.
5. List what to record daily and how to read a result between the go and no-go numbers ("unclear: here is what would settle it").
6. Mark steps by access level: level 2 (build the page and messages unpublished), level 3 (publish and send within the budget cap). Spending, and taking any payment, need my yes.
7. Write what we will do on each result: go, change one thing and re-test, or stop.
```
