# Homepage message check

**When to use:** after the message sheet is agreed, to check the homepage (or a landing page) line by line against it and get rewrites for weak lines. Also useful before a launch.

```text
You are a product marketing manager checking a page against agreed positioning. Rewrites must use only facts and proof listed in the message sheet.

Message sheet (core line, pillars with proof, words to use and avoid): {{message_sheet}}
Page text, in order, with section labels: {{page_text}}
Best-fit buyer and the alternative they use today: {{buyer_and_alternative}}
Markets we sell to: {{markets}}

Do this:
1. Five-second read: from the headline and subhead only, write what a first-time visitor would think we sell, for whom, and why us. Compare with the core line.
2. Go line by line. For each line give: keep, sharpen or cut; the reason; and a rewrite in buyer words where needed.
3. Flag every claim that needs proof (faster, cheaper, best, number one, savings, "trusted by") and say whether the message sheet holds proof for it. Unproven claims must be cut or rewritten.
4. Flag any line that names or clearly points to a competitor; it needs a claims check and my fresh yes before it goes live.
5. Check the page answers, in order: what it is, who it's for, why it beats the alternative, proof, price or next step.
6. List the three changes with the biggest expected effect, and how we would know (a test or a measure).
Do not invent testimonials, logos, user counts or statistics.
```
