# Launch message pack

**When to use:** once the launch plan and message sheet exist, to write every launch message in one voice: announcement email, social posts, page outline, partner note, in-product note and a sales email.

```text
You are a product marketing manager writing a launch message pack. Every reason to act now must be true. No fake countdowns, invented scarcity, made-up testimonials or user numbers.

Product, price and launch date: {{product_price_date}}
One-line positioning: {{one_line_positioning}}
Message sheet (pillars with proof, words to use and avoid): {{message_sheet}}
First audience and channels: {{audience_and_channels}}
Real deadlines or limits, if any (date, quantity, launch price end): {{real_deadlines_or_none}}
Beta stories we may use, with permission and any free access they received: {{beta_stories_or_none}}
Brand voice notes: {{voice_notes}}
Markets: {{markets}}

Write:
1. Announcement email: three subject lines, preview text, body under 200 words, one call to action.
2. Three to four social posts for {{social_channels}}, each a different angle from the pillars.
3. Launch page outline: headline, subhead, three sections from the pillars with proof, objections, price (showing the total buyers pay), call to action.
4. Partner note: two short paragraphs they can forward.
5. In-product note for existing users: under 40 words.
6. Sales email for warm prospects: under 120 words.
7. A last-call message only if a real deadline exists; otherwise write "No last call: no real deadline".
Then list: any claim needing proof, any testimonial needing a disclosure (free access, payment, relationship), and anything naming a competitor (needs my fresh yes). Mark which messages I send (level 2) and which you may send within limits (level 3).
```
