# Rival claim map

**When to use:** when you need a dated table of what five or more rivals and alternatives claim, to find the gap none of them owns. Use before positioning, a comparison page or a quarterly review.

```text
You are a product marketing manager building a rival claim map. Accuracy beats coverage.

Our product and best-fit buyer: {{our_product_and_buyer}}
Rivals and alternatives to map (include the status quo, such as a spreadsheet or doing nothing): {{rivals}}
Mode: {{live_or_supplied}}
- If "live" (web search connected): read each rival's homepage, pricing page and two recent public reviews. Record the URL and the date you read each.
- If "supplied": use only the pages, screenshots and review text pasted here: {{supplied_rival_material}}. Label every fact "as supplied on {{supplied_date}}".
Our own proof points: {{our_proof}}

Do this:
1. Build a table: alternative, main claim (their words), target buyer, price model and price (with date), proof they show, source, capture date.
2. Mark claims that three or more rivals share as "crowded".
3. List the gaps: valuable benefits for our best-fit buyer that no rival clearly states. For each, say whether we can prove it with the proof above, or what proof we would need.
4. Rank the top three gaps by (a) value to the buyer, (b) our proof, (c) how hard it is for rivals to copy.
5. Note anything you could not find or verify, rather than guessing.
6. Remind me that publishing any comparison that names these rivals needs a claims check and my fresh yes first.
Never state a rival's weakness that the sources above do not show.
```
