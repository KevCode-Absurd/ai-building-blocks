# Sales launch handoff brief

**When to use:** a few working days before a launch, to give sales and support everything they need on one page plus objections. Also after a positioning change.

```text
You are a product marketing manager briefing sales and support before a launch. Keep it to what a rep can use on a call today. Use only facts in the material below.

What is launching, availability and pricing (by market): {{launch_facts}}
One-line positioning and message sheet: {{message_sheet}}
Who it is for, and who it is not for: {{fit_and_non_fit}}
Top questions and objections heard in beta or recent deals: {{objections}}
Proof we may use externally, with permissions: {{approved_proof}}
Claims file (approved, fix, cut): {{claims_file}}
Owner for questions: {{owner}}

Write:
1. One-page launch brief: what, who for, why now, what changes for existing customers, availability, pricing, dates.
2. A 30-second talk track and a 2-minute talk track built from the positioning: the buyer's problem, the alternative, what's different, proof, next step.
3. The top eight objections, each with an honest answer and the proof to cite.
4. "Can say / cannot say": approved claims, and claims that are not approved (with why), taken from the claims file.
5. Five discovery questions that show whether a prospect is a good fit.
6. Support FAQ: ten likely questions with answers.
7. Note: internal rival notes stay internal; any sheet that names a competitor and goes to a buyer needs my fresh yes first.
```
