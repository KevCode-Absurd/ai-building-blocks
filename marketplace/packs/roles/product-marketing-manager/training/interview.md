# Interview: product marketing manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example, with what you had to work with?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first week of your most recent positioning project, from the first call to the first draft.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **What raw material do you ask for first, and what do you do when the client has almost none?**
   Why it matters: most small clients arrive with thin evidence; this becomes the agent's intake habit.
3. **How do you find the real competitive alternative? Tell me about a time it turned out to be something the team never considered.**
   Why it matters: positioning starts from the alternative, and experts find it in surprising places.
4. **How do you run a switching or win/loss interview so people tell you what happened rather than what they think you want to hear?**
   Why it matters: captures interview craft the agent can teach.
5. **How do you decide between an existing category, a sub-segment and a new category?**
   Why it matters: category choice is one of the riskiest calls in the role.

## Messages and proof

6. **Show me a message sheet you are proud of. What made the core line work?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real quality bar for its main deliverable.
7. **How do you get a team that wants to say five things to agree on one?**
   Why it matters: the hardest part of messaging is often internal.
8. **What counts as proof for you, and what do you do with a strength the client cannot prove yet?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's evidence threshold.
9. **How do you test whether a message lands, on a small budget?**
   Why it matters: small businesses need cheap, honest tests.

## Competitors and claims

10. **Walk me through how you build and keep a rival map current. How do you date and source rival facts?**
    Why it matters: stale rival facts cause most comparison problems.
11. **Tell me about a comparison page or claim that got challenged, or nearly did. What happened and what changed?**
    Why it matters: real near-misses teach the claims check better than rules alone.
12. **Where do you draw the line between a sharp comparison and denigrating a rival?**
    Why it matters: the line differs by market and needs judgement.
13. **When do you send a claim to a lawyer, and what do you hand them?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's lawyer-flag list.

## Launches and pricing

14. **How do you decide the size of a launch, and when do you talk a client out of a big one?**
    Why it matters: small teams often over-launch.
15. **Describe a demand test you ran. What was the go number and how did you set it?**
    Why it matters: shows how they turn uncertainty into a decision.
16. **What goes into your sales and support handoff, and how do you know reps actually use it?**
    Why it matters: the handoff is where launches often fail quietly.
17. **How do you approach a pricing page: tiers, the table, and the claims on it?**
    Why it matters: pricing pages mix positioning, design and rules.
18. **What do you look at on day 7 and day 14 after a launch, and what decisions have those reviews led to?**
    Why it matters: captures how they learn from results.

## Clients, tools and judgement

19. **Tell me about a client who wanted the wrong thing (a fake countdown, a claim they could not prove, a launch to everyone). How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: the agent needs real pushback language.
20. **Which tools do you work in, and what access do you ask for at the start?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with real practice.
21. **What do you do differently for business buyers compared with consumers?**
    Why it matters: rules, proof and messages differ.
22. **Tell me about your hardest positioning or launch job. What made it hard, and what would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: captures judgement under pressure, the best material for the soul and playbook.
23. **What do new product marketers most often get wrong?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's common-mistakes list in your voice.
