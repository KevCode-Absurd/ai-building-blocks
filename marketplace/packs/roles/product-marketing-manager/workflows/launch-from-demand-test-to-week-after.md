# Launch: from demand test to the week after

**Trigger:** a product, major feature or relaunch has a target date, or the owner asks "how do we launch this?"

**Cadence:** about six to eight weeks for a major launch, three to four for a medium one; then reviews at day 7 and day 14.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits). Until the client grants more, everything runs at L1 and the client does the L2 and L3 steps.

## Steps

1. **Intake, L1.** Get the product, price, buyer, date, budget, audience sizes, past launch numbers and the markets. Ask which access level the client grants for each tool and write it under "Access I have".
2. **Positioning, L1 (skill: product-positioning-and-message-sheet; prompt: positioning-from-reviews-and-calls).** Write the one-line positioning and message sheet, or check the existing one is current.
3. **Demand check, L1 then L2 (prompt: demand-test-design).** If no buyer has paid or committed yet, design a one-week test with go and no-go numbers. Build the page and messages unpublished (L2).
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the test, its budget cap and any payment taking (pre-orders need the client's own check of local rules).
4. **Run and read the test, L3 or the client.** Publish and send within limits, log each action, then call it go, change one thing, or stop. Stop here if the answer is stop.
5. **Size and plan the launch, L1 (skill: product-launch-plan).** Launch size, first audience, the first-100 sum, beta plan if useful, calendar backwards from the date, owners, access level per row.
6. **Beta, L1 and L2.** Recruit 10 to 30 testers who match the best-fit buyer, collect feedback weekly, and apply the written launch rule. Record any free access given, for testimonial disclosures.
7. **Claims and pricing, L1 (skills: competitor-comparison-and-claims-check, pricing-page-and-packaging-review).** Build the claims file; check prices, fees and offers per market. Anything naming a competitor is flagged for a fresh yes.
8. **Messages, L2 (prompt: launch-message-pack).** Draft every message in the client's tools: unsent emails, unpublished posts and pages, paused campaigns.
9. **Sales and support handoff, L2 (prompt: sales-launch-handoff-brief).** Deliver the brief, talk tracks, objections, "can say / cannot say" and FAQ at least a few working days before launch.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the calendar, the drafts, the claims file and the handoff. Separate fresh yes for each item naming a competitor and for any price customers will see.
10. **Launch days, L3 or the client.** At L3, show the launch-week action list and limits, get one yes, then publish and send within limits and log each action (what, where, when, result). Accounts tied to one person (a personal Product Hunt maker profile, a founder's LinkedIn) are posted by their holder. Stop and ask on errors, complaints or policy warnings.
11. **Plan B check, day 4, L1.** Compare measures with targets; if below half, propose the plan B change for approval.
12. **Reviews, day 7 and day 14, L1 (library: SaaS launch decision agent for software).** Plan against result, what the launch caused versus the weeks before, feedback themes, and a dated decision log: repeat, change, stop.

## Outputs

- Message sheet and one-line positioning.
- Demand test page, messages and result (if run).
- Launch plan calendar with owners and access levels.
- Claims file and pricing check.
- Message pack and drafts in the client's tools.
- Sales and support handoff pack.
- Action log for every live step.
- Day-7 and day-14 launch reviews with decisions.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits for each tool, at the start and whenever they change.
- The demand test, its budget and any payment taking (approval point 1).
- The calendar, drafts, claims file and handoff (approval point 2).
- A fresh yes for anything that names a competitor, every time, and for prices, fees or offers customers will see.
- The level 3 action list once before the first live action of launch week; anything outside the limits before it happens.
- Plan B changes.
