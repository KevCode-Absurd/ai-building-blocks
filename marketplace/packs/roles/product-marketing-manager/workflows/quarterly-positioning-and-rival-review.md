# Quarterly positioning and rival review

**Cadence:** every quarter, plus whenever a major rival launches, prices change sharply, or win rates move.

**Trigger:** the quarter ends, or a rival or win-rate change is spotted.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits). Until the client grants more, everything runs at L1 and the client does the L2 and L3 steps.

## Steps

1. **Gather the quarter's evidence, L1.** New reviews, win and loss reasons, five or more call notes, support themes, and any churn reasons. Pull CRM fields if connected.
2. **Refresh the rival map, L1 (prompt: rival-claim-map; library: Monthly competitor watcher).** Live with web search, or as supplied by the client, with dates. Note what changed since last quarter.
3. **Test the positioning, L1 (skill: product-positioning-and-message-sheet).** Is the main alternative still the same? Do best-fit buyers still match the best customers? Are pillars still true and proven? Mark each part: hold, adjust, rethink.
4. **Check live claims, L1 (skill: competitor-comparison-and-claims-check).** Re-check every live comparative, savings and superiority claim against current rival facts and evidence. List anything now stale.
5. **Check pricing pages, L1 (skill: pricing-page-and-packaging-review).** Look for new fees, new markets, discount display and plan confusion from support tickets.
6. **Homepage check, L1 (prompt: homepage-message-check).** Line-by-line against the current message sheet.
7. **Write the review, L1.** One page: what changed in the market, what buyers said, positioning status, stale claims, recommended changes ranked by risk then effect.
8. **Draft changes, L2.** Update the message sheet draft, page drafts and the sales handoff in the client's tools, unpublished.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the review and the drafts.
9. **Apply changes, L3 or the client.** Within limits, update the live message sheet and sales hub and publish approved page changes, logging each action. Anything naming a competitor, and any price customers see, needs its own fresh yes. Stale comparative claims are taken down only with the client's yes, and the action is logged.
10. **Brief the team, L2 or L3.** Send the updated message sheet and "can say / cannot say" list to sales and support, within the client's limits.

## Outputs

- Quarterly review memo.
- Dated rival map with changes highlighted.
- Positioning status (hold, adjust, rethink) with evidence.
- List of stale or risky claims and the fix for each.
- Updated drafts of the message sheet, pages and handoff.
- Action log for every live change.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access levels and limits for each tool.
- The review and drafts (approval point 1).
- A fresh yes for any change that names a competitor or changes prices customers see.
- Taking down or deleting live content.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits.
