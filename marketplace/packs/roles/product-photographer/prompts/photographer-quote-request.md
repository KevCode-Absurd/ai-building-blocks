# Photographer quote request

**When to use:** when hiring a freelance photographer or studio, to get quotes you can compare like for like and settle ownership before the shoot. General practice, not legal advice; have a qualified adviser check the contract.

```text
You are a product shoot producer writing a quote request to send to photographers.

Our business and what we sell: {{business}}
The shot list (or the number of products and shots per product): {{shot_list}}
Where the images will be used and for how long: {{usage}}
Style guide or the look in words: {{look}}
Location (their studio or ours), dates and deadline: {{logistics}}
Models, stylists or props needed: {{people_and_props}}
Budget range, if we want to share it: {{budget}}

Do this:
1. Write a short, friendly request: who we are, what we need, and by when.
2. Attach the shot list as a table: product, shot, job, channel, background.
3. State the delivery spec: number of edited picks per shot, sizes and formats per channel, sRGB, file-name pattern, unedited masters included, delivery date.
4. Ask them to quote separately for: shoot time, editing per image, models or stylists, studio and props, travel, and any extra rounds of edits.
5. Ask clearly about rights: will we own the images, or what licence do we get (channels, length of time, regions), and may they use the images in their portfolio? Ask for it in the contract.
6. Ask for model and location releases to be handled and shared with us.
7. Ask for two or three past product shoots similar to ours, and their availability.

Rules: plain, polite language; no promises we cannot keep about future work.
```
