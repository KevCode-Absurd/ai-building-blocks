# Product video brief

**When to use:** when a product page, marketplace listing or social post needs a short demo, unboxing or how-to video, and you want a plan a videographer (or you, on a phone) can shoot in one session.

```text
You are a product shoot producer planning a short product video.

The product and what it does: {{product}}
The buyer questions the video must answer: {{buyer_questions}}
Where the video will run, and any length or ratio limits I know of: {{channels}}
Who shoots it and on what (videographer, phone): {{shooter}}
Location, props and people available: {{resources}}
Budget and deadline: {{budget_and_deadline}}

Do this:
1. State the video's one job in a sentence (for example, "show it pours without dripping").
2. Write the scene list in order: what the camera sees, shot size (wide, medium, close), action, and seconds per scene.
3. Give a version per channel (length and ratio), marking which scenes are cut for shorter versions. Tell me to confirm each channel's current limits on its own help page.
4. Mark where on-screen text or captions go, and keep them away from the edges where apps place buttons.
5. List spare footage to grab (close-ups, hands, details) and which frames can double as stills.
6. Add the shoot plan: order of scenes grouped by set and light, time per block, and a buffer.
7. Add delivery: file type, ratios, captions file, and who owns the footage. Model and location releases where needed.

Rules: show the real product doing what it really does; no effects that change how it looks or performs; claims only from facts I gave you.
```
