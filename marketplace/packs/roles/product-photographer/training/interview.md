# Interview: product photographer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the photographer's work (for example, studio questions for someone who only coaches sellers shooting on phones).

## How you plan

1. **Walk me through your last product shoot, from the first message to the images going live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **What do you ask before you write a shot list, and what do you do when the client cannot answer?**
   Why it matters: the intake decides what gets shot; their defaults are reusable.
3. **How do you decide which shots make the list and which get cut?**
   Why it matters: "one job per shot" only works with real judgement about what earns a place.
4. **Show me a brief you wrote that a photographer could quote from. What did you learn to add over time?**
   Why it matters: fills the brief template with what really prevents callbacks.
5. **How do you plan a shoot day so you finish on time?**
   Why it matters: scheduling by set and light is a skill the agent should copy.

## Light, colour and editing

6. **What is the first thing you change when a seller's photos look grey or yellow?**
   Why it matters: the fastest fix becomes the agent's first question.
7. **How do you light shiny, clear or very dark products? Show me a before and after.**
   Why it matters: reflective products are where most home set-ups fail.
8. **How do you keep colour true from shoot to screen? What has gone wrong?**
   Why it matters: colour complaints drive returns; their checks become the agent's checks.
9. **Walk me through your edit recipe for a typical product, slider by slider.**
   Why it matters: exact values make the recipe reusable.
10. **Where is your line on retouching? What will you refuse to change?**
   Why it matters: captures the truth-to-product rule in their own words.

## Channels and AI

11. **Which marketplace image rules trip up your clients most, and how do you check them?**
   Why it matters: fills the spec sheet with real failure points.
12. **Which AI tools do you use on product photos, for what, and what do you never let them do?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's AI habits and limits.
13. **How do you check an AI-edited image before it goes live?**
   Why it matters: becomes the AI check list.
14. **How do you name, size and deliver files so the client can use them without asking?**
   Why it matters: fills the delivery spec.

## Clients, rights and hard jobs

15. **How do you handle ownership and licences with clients and with photographers you hire?**
   Why it matters: rights problems surface late and cost a lot; their process prevents them.
16. **What will you change in a client's store or listings yourself once you have access, and what do you always send back for approval?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's level 2 and level 3 habits.
17. **Tell me about a shoot that went wrong. What did you miss, and what do you check now?**
   Why it matters: failures become checks in the quality bar.
18. **Which client wanted something you would not do, and how did you handle it?**
   Why it matters: captures their pushback and the words they use.
19. **What do most sellers get wrong about their product photos that you could fix in ten minutes?**
   Why it matters: becomes "My specialty" in knowledge.md.
