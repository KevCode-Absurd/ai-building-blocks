# Finish feasible product images and preserve product identity

Use for product-photo execution or an image sequence. Inspect available originals before planning: product/variant identity, exact source file, angle, native pixels, visible geometry, finish, lighting and rights reference. Specifications can establish dimensions; a photograph cannot by itself establish alloy, gemstone identity, certification or an unseen feature.

Choose the buyer question for every slot: recognition, detail, scale, use, included parts or material evidence. Plan distinct scenes rather than repeated specification cards. Preserve real product geometry, colour and visible features when preparing crops or compositions. Record cropping/upscaling and distinguish an actual photo, edited photo, generated concept and technical illustration. Use the host image tool for requested raster edits/generation when available, or an explicitly permitted local composition route below; no generated product detail becomes evidence for the actual SKU.

Different layouts or crops alone do not establish distinct buyer concepts. A detail slot must show the relevant detail larger and more readable than in the overview at the intended display size, using sufficient source pixels; three filler layouts from one photograph do not fulfill three distinct buyer questions. A square and portrait export of the same argument are variants of one concept. Independently review the promised detail or proof in the actual image; a manifest's concept label cannot certify it.

Complete the feasible requested outputs with the supplied evidence. If a required angle, genuine product photo, ruler/scale reference or packaging photo is absent, list that exact dependency and deliver the shoot setup and feasible outputs without claiming the missing shot exists. Do not infer cross-SKU matching from similar-looking jewellery or filenames.

## Product fidelity is a delivery gate

Before generation, make a source card for the exact supplied photograph: relative path, SHA-256, native dimensions, visible angle, product region and identifying features. Separate observed appearance from merchant-confirmed SKU facts and rights evidence. A filename, resemblance, source hash or plausible generated image does not authenticate a SKU, materials or public-use rights. Use an uncalibrated photo to compare visible appearance, not to certify physical colour.

Choose the least destructive route that answers the brief:

1. **Preserve the source first.** Deliver useful editable HTML/SVG/layout compositions referencing the untouched local photograph, with its aspect ratio intact. A square presentation, portrait layout with surrounding space or a supported close-up can be finished private-review layouts. Keep original pixels in the source asset; ordinary display scaling is not new captured detail. Record the displayed product size, crop and effective source resolution. Do not count ratio changes as new angles or buyer concepts.
2. **Use generation only for a justified scene need.** Follow the host's authorized image tools and their instructions, including the installed imagegen skill where available; a native-subscription-only or no-paid-API boundary remains in force. Prefer a separate product-free background with matching perspective and light, then retain the real photograph in an editable composition. Generate no jewellery, labels or implied scale reference. If a cutout/mask would alter product edges or is unavailable under the allowed tools, keep the complete source photo in a designed panel instead. Do not route restricted raster edits through another tool as a workaround. If the user explicitly allows local compositing and the host permits it, record that job-scoped permission and combine the unchanged photograph with the separate generated background using local tools. This permits composition, not product retouching, invented angles/materials or publication. Keep both inputs and editable layers/render source; log input/output hashes, crop coordinates, scaling/resampling, mask if any and export dimensions. Inspect edge masks for lost settings or altered silhouette; use the full-photo panel if preservation cannot be shown. Compare exported product regions with the corresponding source under the declared transforms. Report actual differences and any decoding tolerance; scaling is not new detail, and unchanged source bytes do not prove unchanged exported pixels.
3. **Treat a reference-image edit as an attempt, never a fidelity guarantee.** A prompt saying "keep the product unchanged" does not lock pixels, even for a crop-only request. Inspect the actual returned image before calling it a finished product variant. Stop repeating a failed approach when it redraws the product; use the source-preserving route and name the remaining photo need.

Open each candidate export beside the exact source at native size and inspect matched crops of the critical product regions. For jewellery, compare the outer and inner silhouettes, perspective, bezel/prong thickness and settings, connectors, visible stone count and arrangement, facet boundaries, metal edges, colour/reflections and finish. Record which features are clear, obscured or too soft to compare; do not infer a total stone count from a partly hidden arc. Then check shadows/contact, crop, text, actual dimensions and phone-size readability. A similar overall ring outline or a plausible shadow cannot compensate for altered settings or facets. Phone-size inspection alone cannot establish fine-detail fidelity. Never stretch a schematic to pretend its geometry has been measured.

Use an evidence table per file: `source path/hash | output path/hash | feature/region | observed comparison | pass/fail/blocked | disposition`. A visible identity change is **fail**, not merely "unverified". An unreadable or unseen feature is **blocked**, not a pass. No numerical similarity score, intact source hash or delivery-integrity result overrides an observed mismatch. Record comparison crops/screenshots where useful without claiming checks that were not performed.

**Discard identity-drifting attempts from the finished product set.** Disclosures such as "private draft", "AI-assisted" or "not catalogue approved" do not repair changed product features or fulfill a preservation request. Retain failed attempts only as clearly separated rejected evidence if useful, never as the lead deliverables, approved exports or completed requested variants. Replace them with source-preserving layouts, or mark the exact requested treatment unavailable. Do not label a visually faithful source layout commercially release-ready while SKU, rights, channel or physical-colour evidence is still missing; those are separate checks.

Deliver the best feasible source-preserving artifacts now, plus a concrete remaining-shot brief: each missing buyer question, required real angle/detail, lighting and neutral colour target, focus/resolution need, merchant fact or rights document, and the slot it unblocks. A new angle, underside, worn-scale shot, facet macro or material proof needs actual source photography and, for material claims, merchant records; generation cannot supply that evidence. Missing business-performance data does not prevent a faithful local layout. If only the original and one honest layout are feasible, deliver those and explain the unmet variants instead of filling the set with drifted images.

Include editable composition/render files and permitted originals in the handoff, with all dependencies local. Give actual dimensions, source references, change log and shot gap list. Run the delivery check below for finished media, not for a planning-only request.

When these images form a static ad set checked by `check-creative-set.mjs`, derive the requested concepts and exact dimensions from the brief in a separate plan, for example `{"brief":"brief.md#deliverables","concepts":[{"id":"detail","sizes":["1080x1080","1080x1350"]}]}`. Add the matching `concept_id` to each creative-manifest entry and keep its product, angle, format, segment and funnel consistent across variants. Run `node check-creative-set.mjs <exports> --concept-plan <brief-plan.json>` for explicit concept mode. Every requested size must exist, with one export per aspect; `--sizes` alone only permits dimensions. Each concept's first requested size represents it for diversity, while all cross-concept exports remain subject to near-copy checks and every export retains its quality checks. Keep a common first size when the brief allows it, and inspect every variant independently. Preserve missing brief requirements as gaps instead of editing the plan to pass. This ad-set check is separate from marketplace listing rules and the media-delivery integrity manifest below.

For concept-mode type checks, probe each export at its actual render size and key `type-report.json` by the exact export filename. Shared HTML keys, numeric prefixes and inline manifest type declarations cannot cover multiple ratio variants. Do not copy one probe under multiple export keys; the checker verifies report coverage, not the authenticity of the inspection.

## Delivery integrity check

Create `manifest.json` beside the handoff's sources, editable project and exports; run `node tools/media-delivery.mjs path/to/manifest.json`. The optional local dependency `ffprobe` measures media metadata; missing support is reported, never counted as a pass. SHA-256 values must come from the actual files after the final edit.

The manifest uses `schema: "media-delivery/1"` and these arrays:

- `requiredSourceKinds`: the actual brief's requirements, such as `["photograph"]` or `["footage"]`. Do not remove a required kind to turn an incomplete deliverable into a pass.
- `sources`: `id, path, sha256, kind, provenance, rightsReference`. Kind is `photograph`, `footage`, `audio`, `illustration`, `generated` or `reference`. Record the evidence for each declaration; the checker cannot authenticate it.
- `editableFiles`: `path, sha256` for the project/render source and its local dependencies.
- `outputs`: `path, sha256, kind` (`image` or `video`), `placement, sourceIds, width, height, review`. Video also has `durationSeconds` and `audioExpected`.
- Each output's `review`: its current `sha256`, `reviewer`, concrete `notes`, and `"reviewed"` only for checks actually completed. Images require `phoneSize, productFidelity, textAndCrop`. Video requires `phoneSize, productFidelity, captions, fullPlayback, sound`. For a deliberately silent export, explain the checked absence of sound; do not claim listening to nonexistent dialogue.

All paths are relative to the manifest directory. An `INTEGRITY_PASS` checks containment, bytes, metadata and the presence of current review declarations. It does **not** certify actual visual quality, product identity, rights, marketplace compliance, full playback or permission to publish. Keep useful completed work moving while the specific missing dependency is held.
