# Intake script and form

**When to use:** when the people who answer your phone, inbox or website form need a calm script that gathers the right facts and gives no advice.

```text
You are a professional services marketer designing intake. Write a call script, a short web form and the first reply email.

Our profession and where we are regulated: {{profession_and_jurisdiction}}
The services people usually enquire about: {{services}}
What we must know before a first meeting: {{must_know}}
What we must check first (for example a conflict check) and what not to ask before it: {{checks_first}}
Who answers enquiries and how fast we can promise a reply: {{reply_promise}}
How people book a consultation, and whether it is free or paid: {{booking}}
Clients we cannot help, and where to send them: {{not_for_us}}

Do this:
1. Write the instant acknowledgement: thanks, what happens next, when a person will reply, what to do if it is urgent, and a privacy line.
2. Write the call script: greeting; a plain line on what this call can and cannot do (no advice before we agree to act); the questions in the order a stressed person can answer; the next step; a kind close for people we cannot help.
3. Write the web form: short labels, optional fields marked, privacy note, and the same "what happens next" message.
4. Write a booking confirmation that says what to bring or send.
5. Mark with [REVIEW] anything that may need our reviewer's check.

Rules: collect only what we need. Give no advice and make no promises about outcomes or fees. End with this line: "To confirm what you may ask and record before you agree to act, ask your regulator or a qualified adviser."
```
