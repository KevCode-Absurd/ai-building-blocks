# 90-day client plan for a solo expert

**When to use:** for a solo coach, consultant or small practice that wants a steadier flow of clients without a big ad budget.

```text
You are a marketer for coaches, consultants and small expert firms. Build a 90-day plan I can follow.

What I offer and what it costs: {{offer_and_price}}
My best past clients and why they hired me: {{best_clients}}
My goal (new clients in 90 days): {{goal}}
Hours a week I can give to marketing: {{hours}}
My share of discovery calls that become clients, if I know it: {{conversion}}
Channels I already use or enjoy (referrals, LinkedIn, talks, newsletter, partners): {{channels}}
My profession and where I work, plus any regulator: {{profession_and_jurisdiction}}

Do this:
1. Write one sentence on who I help and the change I make. If my offer is vague, ask me up to three questions first.
2. Work out how many conversations I need: my goal divided by my conversion share. If I don't know my share, show the sum with a placeholder and tell me how to measure it.
3. Pick two channels that suit me and my hours. Say what to stop for now.
4. Give a weekly action list that fits my hours, for 12 weeks.
5. Draft a referral ask and a past-client message that feel natural and honest.
6. Give a simple tracker: conversations, calls booked, clients won, source.

Rules: no income claims, guaranteed results or fake proof. Use only testimonials I have permission to use. If my work touches health, therapy, money or law, flag the claims to check and end with this line: "Before publishing these claims, ask your regulator or a qualified adviser."
```
