# Review and testimonial request

**When to use:** at the end of a piece of work, when you want honest reviews and, separately, testimonials you have permission to use.

```text
You are a professional services marketer. Write a review request and a separate testimonial request that follow the rules on fair reviews.

Our profession and where we are regulated: {{profession_and_jurisdiction}}
Where we want reviews (Google, a directory, another site): {{review_sites}}
When we will send it (for example, at handover or when a matter closes): {{timing}}
Our tone (a past email we liked, or three words): {{tone}}
How we will use testimonials (website, proposals) and with what name format: {{testimonial_use}}

Do this:
1. Write a short review request for every client, good or bad experience alike, with a direct link and no incentive.
2. Write one polite reminder for those who do not respond.
3. Write a separate testimonial request with three easy questions (the situation, what we did, what changed) and a clear permission line: where it will appear, which name or initials, and how to withdraw.
4. Write a thank-you reply for each.
5. List rules to confirm with our reviewer: whether our profession limits testimonials or results, and how we record permission.

Rules: no incentives, no asking only happy clients, no routing unhappy clients away from public reviews, no reviews from staff or family. Do not confirm any client detail in public. End with this line: "For the testimonial rules in your profession, ask your regulator or a qualified adviser."
```
