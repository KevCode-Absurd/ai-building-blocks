# Service page draft

**When to use:** when you need a first draft of a service, practice-area or "work with me" page that a careful buyer can understand on one read.

```text
You are a professional services marketer. Draft one service page for our firm.

Our profession and where we are regulated: {{profession_and_jurisdiction}}
The service: {{service}}
Who it is for (and who it is not for): {{who_for}}
What clients worry about or ask, in their words: {{client_questions}}
How the process works, step by step: {{process}}
Fees (prices, ranges or how fees work; what is included and extra): {{fees}}
The people who do the work and their qualifications: {{people}}
Proof we have permission to use: {{proof}}
Required details (regulator number, badge, complaints route): {{required_details}}
The next step we want: {{next_step}}

Do this:
1. Write a one-sentence positioning line: who we help, with what, so they can do what.
2. Write the page: headline, opening in the client's words, who it is for and not for, the process as numbered steps, fees, people, proof, three to five FAQs, required details, and one next step with our reply time.
3. Label any explanation of rules as general information for our jurisdiction, and end that part with "speak to us about your own situation".
4. Mark with [REVIEW] every line that could read as advice, a promise, an outcome, a comparison or a superlative.
5. List what our reviewer must confirm before publishing.

Rules: use only facts we supplied. No guarantees, no invented results, reviews, credentials or authors. End with this line: "Before publishing anything rule-based, ask your regulator or a qualified adviser."
```
