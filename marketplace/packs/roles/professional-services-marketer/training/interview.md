# Interview: professional services marketer

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## Clients and positioning

1. **Which kinds of firms do you work with (law, accounting, insurance, coaching, consulting, design), and in which countries or states?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the rule books it must point to.
2. **How do you help a firm say who it helps and with what, when the partners all describe it differently?**
   Why it matters: positioning is the first fix in almost every job.
3. **Tell me about a firm whose enquiries changed after your work. What did you change first?**
   Why it matters: a real story shows the expert's order of work.

## Rules and sign-off

4. **How do you find out which advertising rules apply to a firm, and who do you ask when unsure?**
   Why it matters: the agent must point to rules, not rule on them; the expert's habits show how.
5. **Which claims do you cut on sight, and what do you write instead?**
   Why it matters: their rewrite patterns become the agent's claims check.
6. **How do you work with a firm's compliance lead or partner who signs off marketing?**
   Why it matters: sign-off is the bottleneck; the expert's process keeps work moving.
7. **Have you ever had a regulator or professional body question a firm's marketing? What happened?**
   Why it matters: real near-misses teach the agent where the lines are.

## Pages, intake and proof

8. **Show me a service page you are proud of. What makes a careful buyer trust it?**
   Why it matters: their page structure becomes the agent's.
9. **How do you handle fees on a website when the firm does not want to publish prices?**
   Why it matters: price is what buyers compare first, and some rules require it.
10. **Walk me through a good intake call. What does the person answering say in the first minute?**
    Why it matters: intake wording is where advice and confidentiality risks sit.
11. **How fast should a firm reply to an enquiry, and how do you make that happen in a busy practice?**
    Why it matters: response speed is often the cheapest fix.
12. **How do you get more reviews without breaking platform or profession rules?**
    Why it matters: the agent needs a clean, repeatable method.
13. **How do you write a case or project story without revealing a client or implying a promise?**
    Why it matters: case stories are powerful and risky in equal measure.

## Keeping clients

14. **What client updates or reminders cut the most "what happens next?" calls for your firms?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent proven message types.
15. **How do you write renewal or deadline reminders that people act on?**
    Why it matters: timing and wording decide whether clients stay.
16. **How do you ask for referrals in a way that partners and past clients don't find awkward?**
    Why it matters: referrals bring much of the work in these professions.
17. **What goes into a client newsletter that people actually read?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's newsletter standard.

## Tools, AI and judgement

18. **Which tools do your firms use for intake, email and reviews, and what do you do when they have none?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's tool choices and fallback.
19. **How do you use AI in this work, and what do you never let it do?**
    Why it matters: sets limits on advice, confidentiality and invented proof.
20. **What do you check before anything goes out under a firm's name?**
    Why it matters: their checklist becomes the agent's quality bar.
21. **Which firms are not ready for marketing help, and how do you tell them?**
    Why it matters: helps the agent set honest expectations.
