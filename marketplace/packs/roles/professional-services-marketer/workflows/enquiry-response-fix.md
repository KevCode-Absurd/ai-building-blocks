# Enquiry response fix

**Trigger:** enquiries go unanswered for too long, many never book, or the firm cannot say where its clients come from.

**Cadence:** once, then a short check each month as part of the monthly cycle.

## Steps

1. **Measure today (level 1).** For the last three months: enquiries by source, time to first reply, booked, attended, became a client, reason lost. Use an export with client names and matter details removed.
2. **Find the leaks.** Where do enquiries wait, drop or get the wrong answer? Note out-of-hours gaps, long forms, missing booking links, and replies that say too much or too little.
3. **Redesign the path (skill: enquiry-to-consultation; prompt: intake-script).** Instant acknowledgement, short form, call script, booking step, reminders, follow-up and a kind close for poor fits.
4. **Check the rules.** What staff may ask and say before the firm agrees to act, conflict checks where they apply, privacy notices, and any limits on following up people who did not book. Ask your regulator or a qualified adviser where unsure.
   **Approval point 1:** the firm's reviewer signs off scripts, forms and templates.
5. **Build it (level 2 drafts in the intake tool or CRM, switched off).** Set up tracking fields for source, reply time and outcome.
6. **Switch on (level 3 turns on the approved sequences and booking flow within the agreed lists; at level 1 or 2 the firm does this).** Test every path with a dummy enquiry before going live.
   **Approval point 2:** the firm confirms the test results and the go-live date.
7. **Train the people who answer.** A one-page guide: what to say, what not to say, where to send urgent or unsafe situations.
8. **Check after 30 days.** Compare reply time, bookings and clients won with the starting numbers. Fix the next biggest leak.

## Outputs

- Starting numbers and leak map.
- Acknowledgement, script, form, booking confirmation, reminders and follow-ups.
- Rules notes and sign-off record.
- Configured, tested intake path with tracking.
- One-page guide for staff.
- 30-day comparison report.

## Needs the client's approval

- Scripts, forms and templates (point 1), signed off by the firm's reviewer.
- Test results and go-live (point 2). At level 3, after this yes, I switch on the approved flow and log each change.
- Always a fresh yes: any message to people outside the agreed lists, deleting records, binding fee quotes, and changes to what staff are allowed to say.
