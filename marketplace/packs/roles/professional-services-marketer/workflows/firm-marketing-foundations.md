# Firm marketing foundations: the first 90 days

**Trigger:** a new client firm or solo expert, or a firm whose website, intake and proof have grown up piece by piece.

**Cadence:** once, over about 90 days, then hand over to the monthly cycle.

## Steps

1. **Set the rules frame (level 1).** Record the profession, the country or state of regulation, the official rule books to check (see knowledge.md, "Rules by profession"), and the firm's named reviewer for marketing. Ask your regulator or a qualified adviser about anything the firm is unsure of.
   **Approval point 1:** the firm confirms the reviewer, the jurisdictions and any house rules (banned phrases, required wording).
2. **Positioning and numbers (prompt: ninety-day-client-plan; level 1).** One sentence on who the firm helps and the change it makes. Work out the conversations needed to reach the client goal from the firm's own conversion share. Pick two main channels.
   **Approval point 2:** the firm approves the positioning, goal and channels.
3. **Fix the enquiry path first (skill: enquiry-to-consultation; level 2 drafts forms and templates, level 3 switches on approved versions).** Acknowledgement, intake script and form, booking, reminders, follow-up, tracking.
4. **Rewrite the main service pages (skill: regulated-service-page; prompt: claims-check; level 2 saves drafts).** Problem first, process, fees, people, proof, required information, next step.
   **Approval point 3:** the firm's reviewer signs off each page. At level 3, I publish only signed-off pages, in the sections agreed.
5. **Start honest proof (skill: proof-and-reviews; prompt: review-request).** Review requests to every recent client on the agreed list; testimonial requests with permission; one or two case or project stories.
   **Approval point 4:** the firm approves the client list for requests and each story before use.
6. **Set up client communications (skill: client-updates-and-reminders).** Onboarding sequence; deadline, stage or renewal messages using only dates and wording the firm supplies.
7. **Referral and past-client messages (skill: client-updates-and-reminders).** A thank-you and referral ask for partners and past clients who opted in, within the profession's contact rules.
8. **Measure and hand over.** Enquiries by source, reply time, consultations booked, clients won, reviews gained. Hand over to the monthly cycle.

## Outputs

- Rules frame: profession, jurisdictions, rule books, reviewer, house rules.
- Positioning line, goal, conversation maths and channel choice.
- Enquiry path: templates, script, form, booking flow, tracking.
- Signed-off service pages.
- Review and testimonial requests, permission log, first stories.
- Onboarding and reminder sequences; referral messages.
- 90-day results report.

## Needs the client's approval

- The reviewer, jurisdictions and house rules (point 1).
- Positioning, goal and channels (point 2).
- Every page, ad, story and template that counts as marketing, signed off by the firm's reviewer (point 3).
- The client list for review requests and every story (point 4).
- At level 3, after sign-off, I publish pages, switch on sequences and send requests within the agreed lists and sections, and log each action.
- Always a fresh yes: fee proposals or quotes sent as binding, replies to formal complaints, any new audience or cold approach, deleting pages or records.
