# Monthly client communications

**Cadence:** monthly, in the last week of the previous month.

**Trigger:** the start of each month, or a deadline season (tax year end, renewal peaks, project handovers).

## Steps

1. **Review last month (level 1).** Enquiries by source, reply times, consultations, new clients, reviews, newsletter clicks and replies, unsubscribes, and any complaints about a message.
2. **Pick the month's topics (prompt: plain-english-explainer).** One or two questions clients asked most, plus any seasonal deadline the firm wants to cover, using only dates and sources the firm supplies.
3. **Draft the newsletter and explainer (skill: client-updates-and-reminders; level 2 builds them as unsent drafts).** General information, jurisdiction and date checked, "speak to us about your own situation".
4. **Draft reminders and updates (skill: client-updates-and-reminders).** Deadline, document, stage or renewal messages due this month, each with one action.
5. **Run the claims check (prompt: claims-check).** Mark anything that could read as advice, a promise, an outcome or a comparison.
   **Approval point 1:** the firm's reviewer signs off the newsletter, explainer and every reminder. Ask your regulator or a qualified adviser about anything rule-based.
6. **Send (level 3 sends and schedules to the approved client lists, after sign-off; at level 1 or 2 the firm sends).** Log each send.
7. **Reviews and replies (skill: proof-and-reviews; level 3 posts replies that follow the approved reply rules).** Send review requests to clients whose work finished this month. Draft replies to new reviews; hold any that mention a matter, complaint or legal issue for the firm.
   **Approval point 2:** the firm approves replies to critical reviews and anything held.
8. **Report.** What went out, what it led to (replies, bookings, documents received on time, renewals kept, referrals), what to change next month.

## Outputs

- Last month's results.
- Newsletter and explainer, signed off.
- Reminders, stage updates and renewal messages due this month.
- Review requests and replies, with held items listed.
- Send log and monthly report.

## Needs the client's approval

- Every message that counts as marketing or a financial promotion, signed off by the firm's reviewer (point 1). At level 3, after sign-off, I send to the approved lists and log each send.
- Replies to critical reviews and anything held (point 2).
- Always a fresh yes: replies to formal complaints, any new audience, fee quotes sent as binding, deleting records.
- At level 1 or 2, the firm sends and posts.
