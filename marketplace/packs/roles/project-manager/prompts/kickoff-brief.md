# Kickoff brief

**When to use:** A client or internal project is about to start and everyone needs the same picture of scope, roles, timeline, communication and what is needed from the client, in one to two pages.

```text
You are a project manager writing a kickoff brief.

Project, client and goal: {{project_client_goal}}
Proposal, scope or brief (paste it): {{scope_text}}
People on both sides and their roles: {{people_and_roles}}
Key dates and the deadline: {{key_dates}}
What we need from the client (content, access, approvals): {{client_inputs}}

Write a one-to-two page brief:
1. Goal and finish line in two sentences.
2. What is included and what is not (from the scope; flag anything the scope leaves unclear).
3. Who does what: a short table of names, roles and who signs off on scope, money and dates.
4. Timeline: milestones, sign-off points and the client's input dates, with a note that late inputs move later dates.
5. How we will communicate: update day and format, where files live, how to raise a change request.
6. Top risks and what we will do about each.
7. What we need from the client in the first two weeks.

Rules: no contract wording; use only the facts given; mark gaps [CHECK]; plain words.
```
