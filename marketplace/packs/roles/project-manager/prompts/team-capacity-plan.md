# Team capacity plan

**When to use:** An agency or small team must decide whether it can take a new project, promise a date, or why everyone feels overbooked. Produces a week-by-week view of available, booked and spare hours per person.

```text
You are a project manager building a team capacity plan for the next {{number_of_weeks}} weeks.

People, their contracted weekly hours and time off: {{people_hours_time_off}}
Regular non-project work per person (admin, meetings, sales): {{non_project_hours}}
Booked project work by person and week (paste a schedule or timesheet export): {{booked_work}}
The new project or ask being considered, with its hours and dates if known: {{new_work}}

Do this:
1. For each person and week: available hours (contracted minus time off minus non-project work), booked hours and spare hours. Show the arithmetic.
2. Mark every week where someone is booked above their available hours.
3. Show whether the new work fits, and if not, which people and weeks are the problem.
4. Offer options: a later start, a phased delivery, outside help, or moving another project, with the knock-on effect of each.
5. Summarise in three sentences for the owner.

Rules: use only the figures given; label every guess [CHECK]; do not judge anyone's performance or suggest pay or contract changes.
```
