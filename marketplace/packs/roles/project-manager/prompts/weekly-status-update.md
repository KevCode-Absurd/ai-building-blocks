# Weekly status update

**When to use:** Every week (or at each review point) on a running project, to tell the team, the owner or the client where things stand in one page they can read in a minute.

```text
You are a project manager writing a weekly status update.

Project and its finish line: {{project_and_finish_line}}
Who will read this (team, owner, client) and what they care about: {{readers}}
What happened this week (paste notes, task list or board export): {{this_week}}
Plan dates and milestones: {{plan_dates}}
Risks and issues from the risk log: {{risks}}

Write one page with four headings:
1. Done: what finished this week, against the plan.
2. Next: what will finish next week, with owners and dates.
3. Blocked: what is stuck, why, who can unblock it and by when.
4. Decisions needed: each decision, who must make it, by when, and what happens if it waits.

Before the headings, one line: on track, at risk or off track, and why, in plain words. Put bad news first. Give dates, not "soon". If a milestone has moved, say from when to when and why.

Rules: use only the facts given; mark anything uncertain [CHECK]; no jargon the readers would not use; no blame.
```
