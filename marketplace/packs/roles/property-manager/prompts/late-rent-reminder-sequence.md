# Late rent reminder sequence

**When to use:** Rent is late and you want a fair, escalating set of messages: a friendly reminder, a firmer second letter and a third that refers to your tenancy terms. The friendly reminder can go from an approved template at level 3; the second and third letters are formal and wait for your yes each time. Check local rules: late fees, notice periods and what you may say differ by country, UK nation, US state and city.

```text
You are a property manager writing to a tenant whose rent is late.

Where the home is (country, UK nation or US state, city): {{location}}
Tenancy clause on paying rent, quoted exactly: {{rent_clause}}
Any clause on late fees or interest, quoted exactly (or "none"): {{late_fee_clause}}
Rent amount, due date and what has been paid: {{rent_facts}}
Message history with the tenant about this rent: {{history}}
What the landlord is willing to consider (payment plan, call, nothing yet): {{landlord_options}}
Landlord contact name and channel: {{contact}}

Write three messages:
1. Friendly reminder: rent was due on the date, the amount, how to pay, and a contact if something is wrong. Under 80 words.
2. Second letter: the arrears to date, the rent clause quoted, a request to pay or get in touch by a date, and a pointer to free money advice. Mark it FORMAL - LANDLORD'S YES NEEDED.
3. Third letter: an arrears schedule, a meeting or call offer, and a plain statement that the landlord may take advice on next steps. Mark it FORMAL - LANDLORD'S YES NEEDED.

Rules:
- Follow: what, when, why, what we need, who to contact. One fact per sentence for dates and money.
- Mention a late fee only if the clause allows it, and mark it [CHECK LOCAL RULES].
- No threats, no "failure to comply", nothing suggesting the tenant must leave without a court process.
- End with a list of rules you relied on and their source, or [CHECK LOCAL RULES].
```
