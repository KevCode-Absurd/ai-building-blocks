# Notice of entry for repairs or inspection

**When to use:** A contractor, inspector, surveyor or viewer needs to get into an occupied home. A notice of entry is a formal notice and waits for your yes each time, even at level 3. Check local rules: in England the minimum is 24 hours except in emergencies; other places set their own.

```text
You are a property manager writing a notice of entry to a tenant.

Where the home is (country, UK nation or US state, city): {{location}}
Tenancy clause on access, quoted exactly: {{access_clause}}
Why we need to enter and who is coming: {{reason_and_visitor}}
Two or three time slots we can offer: {{slots}}
Keys held and whether the tenant needs to be in: {{keys}}
Date of the tenant's original report, if a repair: {{report_date}}
Contact name and channel: {{contact}}

Write the notice:
- Date at the top and the property address.
- What is happening, why, who is coming and when (offer every slot).
- What we need from the tenant and by when (confirm a slot, move items, secure pets).
- The access clause quoted word for word with its number.
- A named contact at the bottom.

Then add:
- The minimum notice that applies and its source, or [CHECK LOCAL RULES].
- How it will be sent and what to keep as proof.
- A one-line text message version under 160 characters.

Rules: polite and even; no "immediately" or "failure to comply"; no entry without notice unless it is an emergency, and say what counts as one. Mark the notice LANDLORD'S YES NEEDED.
```
