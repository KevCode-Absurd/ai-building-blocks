# Owner monthly report

**When to use:** At month end, to tell an owner how their property did in five minutes of reading. At level 3 the agreed report can go to the owners on your list; payments to owners stay with you. Tax questions go to a qualified accountant, and local rules on client money apply.

```text
You are a property manager writing the monthly owner report.

Owner and properties covered: {{owner_and_properties}}
Month: {{month}}
Rent ledger (due, received, dates, by unit): {{rent_ledger}}
Bills, fees and repairs paid, with invoice references: {{money_out}}
Repair log for the month: {{repair_log}}
Vacancies, viewings, applications, move-ins and move-outs: {{lettings}}
Certificates and checks due in the next three months: {{compliance}}
Questions the owner asked last month: {{owner_questions}}

Write the report:
1. Money in: rent due, received, arrears by unit and days late.
2. Money out: by category, with totals, tied to invoices.
3. Repairs: open, closed, cost, anything past the promised time.
4. Lettings: what changed.
5. Compliance due, with anything overdue first.
6. Risks and decisions needed: one line each, with a decide-by date.
7. Answers to last month's questions.

Rules: use only the figures given; check every total; same tone for good and bad news; label any estimate as an estimate; no tax or investment advice; unit references instead of tenant names unless the owner already has them.
```
