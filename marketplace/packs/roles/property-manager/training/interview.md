# Training interview: property manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share tenant names, addresses, ID documents, screening reports, bank details or access codes; anonymise every story.

1. **Where are the homes you manage or have managed (country, UK nation, state, city), how many units, and what kind (flats, houses, HMOs, student lets)?**
   Why it matters: local rules decide notices, deposits and safety duties, so the agent must know where it works.

2. **Who were your clients: your own portfolio, accidental landlords, investors, an agency's landlords? What did they want most from you?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's default tone and what it reports on first.

3. **Walk me through a month in your job, from rent day to the owner report. Where does time go?**
   Why it matters: the expert's real month becomes the agent's monthly workflow.

4. **How do you sort repair requests by urgency? Give me three real examples and how you classed them.**
   Why it matters: urgency rules are judgement that rarely gets written down.

5. **Tell me about the worst repair you handled: what was reported, what you did first, who you called, how it ended.**
   Why it matters: shows priorities under pressure, especially for hazards like damp, gas or leaks.

6. **How do you keep track of gas, electrical and other certificates? What has slipped before, and why?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a working compliance calendar and the failure points to watch.

7. **Show me a notice you are proud of, anonymised. What makes it hard to argue with?**
   Why it matters: a model of tone and structure for formal letters.

8. **How do you handle late rent? What do you send on day one, day seven and later, and when do you involve a solicitor or attorney?**
   Why it matters: the expert's arrears ladder becomes the agent's sequence, with the right approval points.

9. **What changed in your work after the Renters' Rights Act (or the latest change in your local law)? What did you have to redo?**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert keeps up with rule changes and which documents changed.

10. **How do you write an advert, and what words have you taken out of other people's adverts?**
    Why it matters: real fair housing edits make the agent's advert check specific.

11. **What are your written screening criteria, and how do you make sure every applicant is treated the same way?**
    Why it matters: consistent criteria are the defence against discrimination claims.

12. **What goes in your move-in pack, in what order, and what has been missing when a dispute came up later?**
    Why it matters: the pack is the evidence base for every later disagreement.

13. **Tell me about a deposit dispute you won and one you lost. What evidence made the difference?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent what a deduction proposal must contain.

14. **What do you put in an owner report, and what do owners actually read?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent's report short and useful.

15. **How do you judge a rental deal? Which inputs do sellers' agents get wrong most often?**
    Why it matters: the expert's stress tests and doubts sharpen the deal skill.

16. **Which property software, accounting and messaging tools do you use, and what would you let an assistant do in each without asking you?**
    Why it matters: sets realistic access levels and limits for each tool.

17. **What must never happen without your yes, even if an assistant had full access?**
    Why it matters: confirms the fresh-yes list in the expert's own words.

18. **When did a tenant or owner complain about how you communicated, and what did you change?**
    Why it matters: real complaints shape the agent's tone rules.

19. **Which official websites, bodies or advisers do you check when you are unsure of a rule?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent trusted places to check live when web search is connected.

20. **What do new property managers get wrong in their first year?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's list of common mistakes to catch.
