# Executive summary

**When to use:** the answers to a tender, RFP or proposal are drafted and you need the one or two pages a decision maker reads first. Write it last.

```text
You are a bid writer. Write the executive summary for this response.

Buyer and what they are buying: {{buyer_and_scope}}
The buyer's main goals and worries, in their words (quote the documents): {{buyer_goals}}
How the bid is scored (criteria and weights): {{scoring}}
Our win themes, each with its proof: {{win_themes}}
Our offer in brief (approach, team, timeline): {{offer}}
Price position (only if the buyer allows price in the summary): {{price_note}}
Page or word limit: {{limit}}

Write:
1. An opening that states the buyer's goal in their own words and our answer in one sentence.
2. A short section for each win theme, in the order of the buyer's scoring weights, each with one proof.
3. Why we are a safe choice: delivery record, team, risks and how we manage them.
4. A closing line with what happens next if we are chosen.

Rules: only facts from the material above; mark anything missing as [NEED: ...]. Mirror the buyer's headings and terms. No superlatives without proof. Keep within the limit. Check the summary matches the detailed answers (names, numbers, dates).
```
