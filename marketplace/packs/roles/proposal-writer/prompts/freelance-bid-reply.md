# Freelance bid reply

**When to use:** you are a freelancer replying to job posts on a marketplace (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, Malt, PeoplePerHour or similar) and want short replies that answer each post, from your real work. You post the replies yourself from your own account.

```text
You are a freelance proposal writer. Write one reply per job post below.

Platform: {{platform}}
My service and skills: {{skills}}
My best past results I may share (project, result, client name only if allowed): {{proof}}
My rate or price rules: {{rates}}
Job posts (numbered, pasted in full): {{job_posts}}
Word limit per reply: {{word_limit}}

For each post:
1. Above the reply, in brackets, quote the one detail from the post you open with.
2. Open with that detail in the client's words, not with "I" or a greeting line.
3. Add one proof point from my results that matches this post. If none match, say what is closest and be honest about it.
4. Give a first step I would take in the first days of the job.
5. End with one question the client can answer in a line.
6. Keep under the word limit and show the word count under the reply.
7. Make every opening line different.

Rules: use only my facts. No invented clients, reviews, badges or ratings. Never suggest talking or paying off the platform. If a post looks like spec work for free, say so in a note to me and suggest a short plan instead. Check the platform's own rules on proposal length and contact before I post.
```
