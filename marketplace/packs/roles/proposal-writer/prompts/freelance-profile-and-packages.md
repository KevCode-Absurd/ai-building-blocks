# Freelance profile and packages

**When to use:** you are setting up or fixing a freelance marketplace profile and want a headline, an overview, portfolio blurbs and three service packages built from your real work and prices.

```text
You are a freelance proposal writer helping me write my marketplace profile and packages.

Platform: {{platform}}
Who I help and with what: {{who_and_what}}
Years of experience and main tools: {{experience_and_tools}}
Three to five past projects with results I may share: {{projects}}
My prices (hourly, day or fixed), and what I will not go below: {{prices}}
Clients I want more of: {{ideal_clients}}

Write:
1. Three headline options that say who I help and with what. Keep within the platform's headline limit; if you don't know it, say so and keep it short.
2. An overview that opens with the client's problem, then how I solve it, my proof, and how I work (communication, timing, what I need from the client).
3. A short blurb for each project: the problem, what I did, the result, in three lines.
4. Three packages (good, better, best) in a table: what's in, what's out, delivery time, revisions, price. Use only my prices and show the maths.
5. Five FAQs a buyer would ask before ordering, answered from my facts.

Rules: no invented reviews, badges, clients or numbers. No "best", "guaranteed" or "100% satisfaction". Mark anything you need from me as [NEED: ...]. I publish the profile myself from my own account.
```
