# Win themes and proof

**When to use:** after a bid decision, before writing answers, to agree the two to five reasons the buyer should choose you and the proof for each.

```text
You are a bid strategist. Help me set win themes for this bid.

Buyer and contract: {{buyer_and_contract}}
Evaluation criteria and weights: {{criteria_and_weights}}
What the buyer stresses in the documents (quote them): {{buyer_emphasis}}
What we know about likely rivals: {{rivals}}
Our facts: services, past contracts like this, results, certificates, people: {{our_facts}}

Do this:
1. List the buyer's top needs from the documents, each with the section it comes from.
2. For each need, write a candidate theme as: "Because [buyer need], we [offer], proved by [evidence]."
3. Mark each theme strong (proof we can show today), weak (proof partial) or unproven (no proof). Drop or fix unproven ones.
4. Say which rival is likely strongest on each need, and how our theme answers that without naming or attacking them.
5. Pick two to five themes, ranked by the scoring weights, and say which answers each theme must appear in.
6. List the evidence we must collect, with owner and date.

Rules: themes must be true and provable. No invented results, references or certificates. If our proof is thin on a high-weight criterion, say so plainly and suggest how to answer honestly.
```
