# Tender from notice to submission

**Trigger:** a tender, RFP, solicitation or grant call arrives that the client may want to bid for.

**Time:** set by the buyer's deadline. Work backward from it; the clarification deadline usually comes first.

## Steps

1. **Intake (playbook intake questions; access.md "Questions I ask first").** Confirm the access level for each tool, the deadline, the clarification deadline, who signs off price and technical answers, and who holds the portal account. Until the client answers, work at level 1.
2. **Bid or no-bid (skill: bid-no-bid-and-compliance-matrix).** Check gates, score the opportunity and give the call in one line.
   **Approval point 1:** the client says bid or no-bid. If no-bid, write a two-line note on why and stop.
3. **Registrations and lead times (level 1).** Check the organisation is registered where this buyer requires: SAM.gov and Grants.gov roles for US federal work, the central digital platform (Find a Tender) for UK public work, the Funding and Tenders Portal for EU calls, or the buyer's own e-tendering system. The client's account holders register; I list what each form needs.
4. **Compliance matrix and calendar (skill: bid-no-bid-and-compliance-matrix).** At level 2, build them in the client's RFP tool or shared drive. At level 3, assign owners and dates within the client's limits.
5. **Clarification questions.** Draft only where the documents are unclear or conflict.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the questions; the client or the agent (with a fresh yes for that message) sends them through the buyer's portal.
6. **Win themes (prompt: win-themes-and-proof).**
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves themes and the outline (the Pink team stage) before full drafting.
7. **Draft answers (skill: tender-and-rfp-answers; for grants, skill: grant-application).** Write in the buyer's order, mark gaps as NEED, and chase owners for missing facts.
8. **Price (client).** The client sets the price. I check that it matches the scope, staffing and timeline in the answers.
9. **Executive summary (prompt: executive-summary).** Written last.
10. **Review (Red, Green and Gold).** Someone outside the writing team scores the draft against the buyer's scheme; the price is checked against the offer; a named person signs off security, legal, privacy and uptime claims.
    **Approval point 4:** final sign-off of the full response and price.
11. **Submission checklist.** File names, formats, signatures, declarations, portal steps and a target time well before the deadline.
    **Approval point 5, a fresh yes every time:** the client approves submission of this exact version. At level 3 I upload and submit only after that yes; where the portal needs a personal seat (a Grants.gov AOR, a named portal user), that person submits. Declarations, certifications and bank details are completed by the client.
12. **Confirm receipt.** Save the portal receipt or confirmation and log the action.
13. **Lessons (prompt: debrief-and-lessons).** After the result, update the answer bank and the scorecard.

## Outputs

- Bid or no-bid scorecard and the call.
- Compliance matrix and bid calendar.
- Clarification questions.
- Win themes, outline and full draft response.
- Executive summary.
- Review notes and sign-off record.
- Submission checklist, receipt and action log.
- Lessons note and answer bank updates.

## Needs the client's approval

- Bid or no-bid (point 1).
- Clarification questions before they are sent (point 2).
- Themes and outline (point 3).
- Price, and the final response (point 4).
- Submission (point 5): a fresh yes every time, even at level 3.
- Any declaration, certification, signature or bank detail: completed by the client.
- Naming any client or reference: their permission first.
