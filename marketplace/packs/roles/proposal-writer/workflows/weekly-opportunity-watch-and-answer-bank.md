# Weekly opportunity watch and answer bank

**Cadence:** weekly for the opportunity watch; monthly for the answer bank; after every result for lessons.

## Steps

1. **Run saved searches (level 1).** Check the portals the client chose: SAM.gov Contract Opportunities, Grants.gov, Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Find a grant, TED, the EU Funding and Tenders Portal, and any buyer or framework lists. Use the client's keywords, codes (such as NAICS) and regions.
2. **Quick filter.** For each new notice, note buyer, scope, value, deadline, procedure and any obvious gate (certificate, turnover, registration). Drop clear misfits with a one-line reason.
3. **Shortlist (skill: bid-no-bid-and-compliance-matrix, gates only).** For notices that pass the quick filter, check gates and give a one-line early view: worth a full bid or no-bid review, or not.
   **Approval point 1:** the client picks which notices get a full review.
4. **Update the pipeline (level 2 or 3).** At level 2, draft entries in the client's CRM or tracker. At level 3, add or update entries within the client's limits: notice, deadline, owner, status.
5. **Answer bank check (monthly).** List answers older than the client's review period, answers that cite expired certificates, insurance or staff who have left, and answers marked partial or gap. Propose rewrites.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves rewrites before they replace library answers. At level 3, I update only the entries approved.
6. **Registration check (monthly).** Flag registrations that will lapse soon, such as the SAM.gov renewal every 365 days, and certificates with renewal dates (for example Cyber Essentials, renewed yearly where a contract needs it). The client renews.
7. **Lessons (after each result; prompt: debrief-and-lessons).** Feed changes into the answer bank and the scorecard weights.

## Outputs

- Weekly shortlist with one-line reasons and deadlines.
- Pipeline entries (draft or live, by level).
- Monthly answer bank report with proposed rewrites.
- Registration and certificate renewal alerts.
- Lessons notes after each result.

## Needs the client's approval

- Which notices get a full review (point 1).
- Any rewrite to approved library answers (point 2).
- Any renewal, registration or payment: done by the client.
- Any bid, quote or grant submission: a fresh yes every time (see the tender-from-notice-to-submission workflow).
