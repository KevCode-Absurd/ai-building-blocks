# Training interview: QA engineer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, private code, customer data and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe products in general terms.

1. **What kinds of products and teams do you test best, and what do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **When a new feature lands on your desk, what do you do in the first hour?**
   Why it matters: captures the real order of work, before any test is written.

3. **How do you decide what to test first when there isn't time to test everything?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's risk ranking in practice, not in theory.

4. **Show me a test case you're proud of. What makes its expected result good?**
   Why it matters: sets the bar for concrete, checkable expected results.

5. **Which edge cases do you try on every form, and which one has caught the most bugs?**
   Why it matters: turns instinct into a checklist the agent can reuse.

6. **How do you decide whether a check should be a unit, API or browser test?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's choice of test level.

7. **Playwright or Cypress: which do you reach for, and when would you pick the other?**
   Why it matters: captures tool judgement beyond the docs.

8. **Tell me about the worst flaky test you fixed. What was the real cause?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent real patterns for diagnosing flakiness.

9. **When do you allow retries in CI, and when do you refuse?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's line between evidence-gathering and hiding problems.

10. **How do you handle test data and accounts so tests never touch real customers?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's data and environment safety habits.

11. **Have you ever been asked to test on production? What did you agree to, and what did you refuse?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's rules for production checks and fresh approvals.

12. **Walk me through a bug report that got fixed fast. What did it contain?**
    Why it matters: captures the format developers actually act on.

13. **How do you chase a bug you can't reproduce?**
    Why it matters: records experiments and the reasoning behind them.

14. **How do you set severity, and who decides priority on your teams?**
    Why it matters: tunes the agent's severity scale and when to defer to the owner.

15. **What does your accessibility pass include beyond an automated scan?**
    Why it matters: makes sure the agent does the manual checks tools miss.

16. **Tell me about a release you said should not ship. What happened next?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert holds a line and words a hold decision.

17. **What exit criteria have you seen bent under deadline pressure, and how do you record it when that happens?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to record risk acceptance by a named person.

18. **How do you review AI-written tests? What mistakes do you see most?**
    Why it matters: tunes the agent's checks on generated test code.

19. **How do you explain test coverage to an owner who isn't technical?**
    Why it matters: sets the tone and words for sign-off notes.

20. **What did you get wrong early in your career that you now check every time?**
    Why it matters: surfaces hard-won checks to add to the quality bar.
