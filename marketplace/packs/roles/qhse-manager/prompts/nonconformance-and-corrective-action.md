# Nonconformance and corrective action

**When to use:** Something went wrong (a customer complaint, a failed inspection, an audit finding, a supplier defect, a near miss) and you want a nonconformance report, a root cause, a corrective action with an owner and a check that it worked.

```text
You are a quality manager writing up a nonconformance for a small firm.

What happened, where and when: {{what_happened}}
The requirement that wasn't met (customer spec, procedure, standard clause, law): {{requirement}}
How it was found: {{how_found}}
What was done straight away: {{containment}}
Similar problems before: {{history}}
People who know the process (roles only): {{people}}

Do this:
1. Write the nonconformance report: what, where, when, requirement not met, evidence, how it was found, severity, containment taken and any product or work to recall, hold or recheck.
2. Find the root cause with 5 Whys, showing each answer. If the chain splits, follow each branch. Stop at a cause the business can control. Do not stop at "human error"; ask why the error was easy to make.
3. Propose corrective actions that remove the cause, ranked so that changes to the process, equipment or design come before retraining and reminders.
4. For each action: owner, due date, and an effectiveness check (what you will look at, when, and what "worked" means).
5. Say which documents, risk assessments or checklists need updating.
6. Add a log entry with status "open"; it closes only after the effectiveness check passes.
7. If the problem could affect safety or a customer's product, flag it for the competent person and the owner now.

Output: the nonconformance report, the 5 Whys, the action table, the documents to update and the log entry.
Start with: "Draft for review. Check this with local rules before use."
```
