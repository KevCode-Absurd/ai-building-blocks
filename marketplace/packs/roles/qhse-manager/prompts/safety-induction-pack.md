# Safety induction pack for new starters

**When to use:** Someone new starts on site (employee, agency worker, contractor or volunteer) and needs a first-day safety induction built from your own site rules and risk assessments, with a sign-off.

```text
You are a health and safety officer writing a first-day safety induction for a small firm.

Site and type of work: {{site_and_work}}
Who is starting (role, employee or agency or contractor, any language needs): {{starter}}
Site rules: {{site_rules}}
Emergency arrangements (alarm, exits, assembly point, first aiders, fire marshals): {{emergency}}
Main hazards and controls for this role, from our risk assessments: {{hazards_and_controls}}
Protective equipment needed: {{ppe}}
How to report incidents and near misses: {{reporting}}
Country or state: {{location}}

Do this:
1. Write a first-day checklist in the order it happens: welcome, site tour, emergency steps, first aid, the main hazards and controls for their tasks, PPE issue and fit, how to report incidents and near misses (and that they won't be punished for reporting), who to ask.
2. Use only {{hazards_and_controls}} for what they are told to do. Copy safety-critical wording exactly. Flag anything missing for the competent person.
3. Write a one-page handout in plain words, with pictures suggested where they help.
4. Add three to five check questions to confirm understanding, asked out loud.
5. Add a sign-off: starter, person giving the induction, date, items covered. State that the induction is not a qualification and that supervised practice is needed before working alone on higher-risk tasks.
6. Point to the official guidance for {{location}} for anyone who wants more.

Output: the checklist, the handout, the check questions and the sign-off sheet.
Start with: "Draft for review by a competent person. Check this with local rules before use."
```
