# ISO gap review and document set

## When to use

- A customer, tender or insurer asks for ISO 9001, 14001 or 45001, or for "a quality system".
- A certified firm needs to move to ISO 9001:2026 or ISO 14001:2026.
- The firm has documents nobody follows and wants a system that matches the work.
- Before a certification or surveillance audit, to find gaps early.

Not for: certifying the firm or acting as its auditor. A certification body decides whether the firm passes.

## What you need

1. What the firm makes or does, its sites, headcount and the reason for certification.
2. Which standard and edition, and the client's licensed copy if they want clause wording quoted. I don't reproduce the standard's text; iso.org blocks automated reading, so the client buys it from BSI or their national standards body.
3. Current procedures, forms, records, org chart and any audit reports.
4. The certification body, if chosen, and the target audit date.
5. For ISO 45001: the safety risk assessments and incident records. For ISO 14001: the energy, waste and permit records.

**Access:** level 1 to read the document folders and registers; level 2 to write drafts and a register copy with changes marked; level 3 to publish approved documents, update the live register and archive old versions as replaced (never deleted without a fresh yes), within your limits.

## Steps

### 1. Map the processes

List the main processes from enquiry to delivery (quote, order, plan, make or deliver, inspect, invoice, after-sales) and the support processes (buying, training, maintenance, calibration, document control). For each, note the owner, the inputs and outputs, and the records it already makes. Use the firm's words.

### 2. Review clauses 4 to 10

The ISO management system standards share the same requirement clauses: context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement. For each clause, ask what the firm does now, what proves it, and what's missing.

| Clause | Ask | Typical evidence | Status | Gap to close |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 Context | What issues and interested parties affect us? What is in scope? | Scope statement, issues list | Covered / partly / missing | |
| 5 Leadership | Who owns the system? Is there a policy people know? | Policy, roles | | |
| 6 Planning | What risks and opportunities, objectives and plans? | Risk and opportunity list, objectives | | |
| 7 Support | People, competence, awareness, communication, documented information | Training matrix, document register | | |
| 8 Operation | How is the work planned, controlled and checked? | Procedures, inspection records, supplier checks | | |
| 9 Performance evaluation | What is measured, audited and reviewed? | KPIs, audit reports, management review minutes | | |
| 10 Improvement | How are problems fixed and prevented? | Nonconformance and corrective action log | | |

For the 2026 editions, add the changes BSI lists. ISO 9001:2026: climate and sustainability in context, leaders' commitment to ethics and quality culture, risks and opportunities handled separately, better use of data. ISO 14001:2026: a life cycle perspective, planning for change, all potential emergencies, objectives for each internal audit. Check transition dates with the certification body; BSI's pages gave none on 6 November 2026.

### 3. Rank the gaps

- **Must have before audit:** missing records the auditor will sample (internal audit, management review, training, calibration, corrective actions).
- **Must write:** missing documented information the standard requires.
- **Should improve:** things that work but aren't written or measured.

### 4. Write the documents in order of need

1. Scope and policy (one page each).
2. Process map with owners.
3. Document and record control procedure and register: title, owner, version, review date, where kept, who can change it.
4. Procedures and forms, one per process that needs one. Short, in the firm's words, steps starting with a verb, records named.
5. Objectives with measures and owners.

### 5. Build the internal audit plan and checklists

- A 12-month plan covering every process and clause at least once, more often for high-risk or problem areas.
- A checklist per process: questions, the evidence to sample, and a findings form.
- Auditors don't audit their own work.

### 6. Set up nonconformance and corrective action

- Nonconformance report: what, where, when, requirement not met, containment.
- Corrective action: root cause (5 Whys or a fishbone; see the operations manager kit), action, owner, due date, and an effectiveness check date.
- Log: every case to closure; close only after the check shows it worked.

### 7. List what must happen, not just be written

Before a certification audit: records built up over time, at least one round of internal audits, a management review, corrective actions raised and closed. Say this plainly to the client with dates.

## Checks

- Every clause 4 to 10 has a status and named evidence.
- No document describes a process nobody follows.
- The register lists every document with owner, version and review date.
- Audit plan covers every process; auditors aren't auditing their own work.
- The edition and transition position are stated with the date checked.
- No promise that the firm will pass.
- The document starts with: *Draft for review by a competent person. Check this with local rules before use.*

## Output format

1. Process map (table or simple diagram).
2. Gap table by clause with status, evidence and gap.
3. Ranked gap list with owners and dates.
4. Document set: scope, policy, document register, procedures and forms.
5. Internal audit plan and checklists.
6. Nonconformance form, corrective action form and log.
7. "Before the audit" list with dates.

## Example of good output (short)

*Made-up business: Calder Precision, 22 people, CNC machining for food equipment makers, Yorkshire. Goal: ISO 9001:2026 certification.*

> **Draft for review by a competent person. Check this with local rules before use.**
>
> | Clause | What you do now | Evidence | Status | Gap to close |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 7 Support: measuring equipment | Micrometers checked "when someone remembers" | None | Missing | Calibration list with due dates; label each gauge; Owner: QC lead; by 31 Jan |
> | 8 Operation: outside suppliers | Two heat-treat suppliers, chosen by price | Purchase orders only | Partly | Supplier approval and yearly review form; Owner: buyer; by 28 Feb |
> | 9 Performance evaluation: management review | Monthly owner meeting, no minutes | None | Missing | Add a quarterly QMS agenda and minutes template; first review by 31 Mar |
>
> **Before the audit:** two months of calibration records, one full round of internal audits, one management review with minutes, and at least the open corrective actions from those audits tracked.
