# Training interview: QHSE manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, injury details or client secrets; anonymise every story.

1. **Which sectors, firm sizes and countries do you know best, and which standards have you worked to?**
   Why it matters: sector and location decide which regulators, standards and hazards apply.

2. **Walk me through the last ISO certification project you ran, from first meeting to certificate.**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence, timings and where small firms get stuck.

3. **How do you run a gap review without drowning a small firm in documents?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's method for keeping systems short and used.

4. **What has a certification auditor picked up that surprised your client?**
   Why it matters: real findings teach the agent what auditors sample.

5. **How do you write a risk assessment when you can't visit the site?**
   Why it matters: the agent works remotely, so it needs the expert's questions and limits.

6. **Tell me about a control that looked fine on paper but wasn't in place.**
   Why it matters: the paper-versus-practice gap is the most common failure.

7. **Which tasks do you always send to a specialist or competent person?**
   Why it matters: sets the high-risk list the agent must flag.

8. **How do you decide whether an incident is reportable, and what do you do when you're not sure?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's reportability habits and how they avoid guessing.

9. **Tell me about a time a client didn't want to report or stop work. What did you do?**
   Why it matters: tests how the expert handles pressure while leaving the decision with the client.

10. **How do you investigate an incident without blaming the person?**
    Why it matters: pulls out questions that find the conditions behind errors.

11. **What makes a corrective action actually stick?**
    Why it matters: most actions are closed too early; the expert's checks are valuable.

12. **How do you write a toolbox talk crews listen to?**
    Why it matters: real examples of openings, stories and questions make the talks usable.

13. **What do you include in a first-day safety induction, and what do you leave for later?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert balances essentials with overload.

14. **How do you set up internal audits in a firm with only a handful of staff?**
    Why it matters: independence is hard in small firms; the expert's workarounds matter.

15. **How do you build a carbon footprint from bills, and which factor sets do you use?**
    Why it matters: captures method, data traps and how the expert records factor versions.

16. **What do you do when a customer questionnaire asks for something the firm doesn't have?**
    Why it matters: honest "not yet" answers protect the client; the expert's wording helps.

17. **Which green claims have you talked a client out of, and what did you suggest instead?**
    Why it matters: real before-and-after claims teach safer wording.

18. **How do you run a continuity exercise that people take seriously?**
    Why it matters: untested plans fail; the expert's scenarios and injects are reusable.

19. **Which tools (inspection apps, document systems, trackers) have you set up, and what access do you ask for?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which tools to expect and what each access level covers.

20. **What would you never sign or put your name to, whoever asks?**
    Why it matters: sets the expert's hard limits on certification, competence and claims.
