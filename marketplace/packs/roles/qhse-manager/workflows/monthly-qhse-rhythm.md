# Monthly QHSE rhythm and yearly calendar

**Cadence:** A monthly review of about an hour, plus fixed points in a yearly calendar. Suits firms of 5 to 250 people with or without certification.

**Access:** Set at the start, per tool (see `access.md`). Until you choose, I work at level 1.

## Steps

### Monthly

1. **Incident and near-miss review.** Read the log (level 1). Count by type, task, site and month; flag repeats and open reportability decisions. Check every recordable US case is on the 300 log within seven days.
2. **Inspections.** Read inspection results from the inspection app. List fails without an action. At level 3, I raise issues for fails and assign them within your limits.
3. **Corrective actions.** List overdue actions and those due for an effectiveness check. At level 3, I close actions whose checks passed and remind owners of overdue ones in the agreed channel.
4. **Toolbox talks.** Confirm last month's talks happened and were signed for; prepare next month's from the calendar with the `toolbox-talk-and-sign-in-sheet` prompt.
5. **Documents due for review.** List documents past their review date from the register; draft updates (level 2).
6. **Rules watch.** Check `knowledge.md` section 10 for the client's locations: standard editions and transition dates, RIDDOR and OSHA pages, emission factor updates, reporting rules. With web search connected I check the official pages; without it I list what to check.
7. **One-page report** to the owner: what happened, what's overdue, what changed, decisions needed.

### Yearly calendar

| When | What | Tool or skill |
|---|---|---|
| January | US: prepare the 300A summary for certification by a company executive; post from 1 February to 30 April; electronic submission by the deadline if covered | `incident-log-and-reportability-check` |
| Quarterly | Internal audits per the audit plan | `iso-gap-review-and-document-set` |
| Quarterly | Footprint workbook update | `carbon-footprint-and-green-claims` |
| Twice a year | Continuity exercise | `continuity-exercise-script` prompt |
| Yearly | Risk assessment review for every site and task, plus after any change | `risk-assessment-and-checklists` |
| Yearly | Management review with minutes | `iso-gap-review-and-document-set` |
| When factors are published | Switch the footprint to the new factor set and note it | `carbon-footprint-and-green-claims` |

## Outputs

- Monthly one-page QHSE report
- Updated incident log, action list and document register
- Next month's toolbox talks
- Yearly calendar with owners
- Action log of everything done in your tools (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs at level 3 within your limits, after one confirmation.** Before the first live action, I show the list (raise and assign issues from failed inspections, close actions after passed checks, post approved reminders, update the register) and its limits. After your yes, I act and log each step.

**Always needs a new yes.** Certifying the 300A (a company executive does this), submitting anything to a regulator, publishing a changed procedure, spending, deleting records, and any decision to stop work. Check this with local rules.
