# Closing day plan

## When to use

- A closing or completion date is set and within about a week.
- Last time, closing day ran late because something was missing.
- A chain of linked sales completes on the same day.
- An agent wants a standard day-of checklist.

## What you need

1. The tracker, checklist and parties from the earlier skills.
2. The time and place of signing or completion, and how parties will sign (in person, separately with an escrow agent, or electronically where allowed). Check local rules: closing practice differs by state, and some states require an attorney for each side.
3. The closing agent's, escrow officer's or conveyancer's name and published phone number, given by the agent.
4. Key arrangements, access codes kept in the agent's system (never in chat), and utility and meter details to hand over.
5. Access: level 1 to draft; level 2 for tasks and tentative holds; level 3 for approved day-of reminders, checklist updates and filing within limits.

## Steps

### 1. Work back from the signing or completion time

List each task with a time, an owner and a status. Typical lines to check against the client's own process:

**US closing (one week out to the day after):**
- One week out: ask the lender or closing agent how and when the Closing Disclosure will reach the buyer; request the note, mortgage or deed of trust and deed for early review. The buyer must receive the Closing Disclosure at least three business days before closing.
- When the Closing Disclosure arrives: the buyer compares it with the Loan Estimate (loan terms, amount, rate, monthly payment, closing costs, cash to close) and takes questions to the lender. A change to the APR beyond tolerance, the loan product, or an added prepayment penalty brings a new three-business-day wait.
- Day before: confirm time, place, who signs, ID needed; confirm with the closing agent that everything they need is in.
- Day of, before signing: final walk-through against the contract (agreed repairs done, agreed items present).
- Signing: nobody signs blank documents or anything that differs from the deal.
- After signing: funding and recording confirmed by the closing agent; keys released per the contract.
- Day after: final documents to the file; broker compliance file closed.

**England and Wales completion:**
- Exchange done, completion date fixed in the contract; buildings insurance in place from exchange (buyer).
- Morning of completion: the buyer's conveyancer sends funds; the seller's conveyancer confirms receipt.
- On confirmation: the agent releases keys (often around lunchtime in practice); meter readings taken.
- After: SDLT return and payment within 14 days and Land Registry registration by the buyer's conveyancer.
- In a chain, list every link's conveyancer and the order money moves.

### 2. Plan for what goes wrong

| If this happens | Who acts | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Funds late | Closing agent or conveyancer | Agent informs all parties with a revised time from the professional; no keys until they confirm |
| Document missing | Owner on the tracker | Request immediately; tell the other side the new time |
| Walk-through finds a problem | Buyer's agent | Stop and call the agent; any credit or holdback is agreed by the parties and their professionals |
| Anyone receives new or changed bank details, or an urgent request to send money elsewhere | Nobody sends anything | Treat as fraud until confirmed by phone with the closing agent on a known number; alert the agent at once |
| Money may have gone to the wrong account | The sender | Call their bank immediately to ask for a recall; report at ic3.gov (US) or to Report Fraud (UK) |
| A link in the chain is delayed | Conveyancers | Agent updates everyone with the professionals' revised plan |

### 3. Write the day-of messages

Short reminders for each party: time, place, what to bring, who to call, and the wire-safety line. At level 3, send only approved templates to parties on the file at the agreed times. Delay messages and anything about missed deadlines go to the agent for a fresh yes.

### 4. Close the file

Final documents uploaded, checklist complete, keys and codes handed over through the agent's system, lessons for next time. Deleting any record or document needs a fresh yes.

## Checks

- Every line has a time, an owner and a status.
- The Closing Disclosure timing is on the plan for financed US buyers.
- Funding is confirmed by the professional, never assumed or relayed from an email.
- No wiring instructions, account numbers or access codes in the plan or messages.
- Every problem line has an owner and an action.
- Local rules on who attends and signs are confirmed or marked [CHECK LOCAL RULES].
- At level 3, sends and file updates are logged and within limits.

## Output format

1. **Timed checklist** from one week out to the day after.
2. **What if** table.
3. **Day-of messages** per party.
4. **File close list.**

## Example of good output (short)

*Made-up deal for illustration: 14 Maple Court, US, closing Tuesday 15 December 2026 at 10:00 with a title company.*

> | When | Task | Owner | Status |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 8 Dec | Ask lender when the Closing Disclosure goes out | TC | Done: emailing it 9 Dec |
> | 9 Dec | Buyers compare Closing Disclosure with Loan Estimate | Buyers, lender | Waiting |
> | 14 Dec, 16:00 | Confirm signers, ID and time with the title company | TC | Not started |
> | 15 Dec, 08:30 | Final walk-through (roof repair receipt, fridge stays) | Buyer's agent, buyers | Booked |
> | 15 Dec, 10:00 | Signing | Title company | Booked |
> | After funding | Keys released when the title company confirms funding and recording | Listing agent | Waiting |
>
> Reminder to buyers: "Wiring instructions will only come from the title company. Call them on the number on your contract paperwork before sending anything."
