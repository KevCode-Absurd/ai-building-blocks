# Training interview: real estate transaction coordinator

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share client names, addresses, account numbers, wiring details, ID documents or loan files; anonymise every story.

1. **Where have you coordinated sales (states and counties, or UK nations), on which side, and how many files at once?**
   Why it matters: local rules and forms decide every deadline, and file volume shapes the agent's routines.

2. **Walk me through a file from accepted offer to closed. What do you do on day one?**
   Why it matters: the expert's opening routine becomes the agent's intake and tracker steps.

3. **How do you build your deadline tracker? Forwards from the contract date or backwards from closing, and why?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert keeps the critical dates visible.

4. **Which deadlines have you seen sink a deal, and what warning signs came first?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent its red flags and lead times.

5. **When a deadline has to be counted from another date, how do you handle it, and who confirms it?**
   Why it matters: keeps the agent from making legal calls on day counting.

6. **What is on your document checklist for each stage, and what does your broker's compliance review reject most often?**
   Why it matters: turns real review failures into checklist items.

7. **Which disclosures and forms are specific to your state or region, and where do you check them?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent the official sources to name for local rules.

8. **Show me an update you sent to an anxious first-time buyer, anonymised. What made it work?**
   Why it matters: a model of tone and structure for party updates.

9. **How often do you update each party, and what happens when you go quiet?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's default update cadence.

10. **Tell me about a deal where the loan, appraisal or mortgage offer came late. What did you tell each party, and when?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert handles bad news early without promising dates.

11. **Have you ever seen a fake wiring email or a "changed bank details" message? What gave it away and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: real fraud attempts make the agent's warning signs specific.

12. **What do you say to buyers about wiring and deposits, and when do you first say it?**
    Why it matters: the expert's wording becomes the agent's wire-safety line.

13. **What is your rule for questions about what a clause means or whether someone can walk away?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent on the right side of legal advice.

14. **Which tools do you use (transaction management, e-signature, calendar, CRM), and what do you let assistants do in them?**
    Why it matters: maps the agent's access levels to real tool permissions.

15. **When you route documents for signature, how do you check the signers, order and version before sending?**
    Why it matters: prevents wrong-signer and wrong-version mistakes at level 3.

16. **What does your closing or completion day look like, hour by hour, and what has gone wrong on the day?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's closing day plan and what-if table.

17. **For UK sales: how do you chase a stalled chain, and who do you call first?**
    Why it matters: sale progression habits that GOV.UK pages do not teach.

18. **How do you work with the lender, the title company or escrow, and the conveyancer? What do they need from you, and what do you never ask them for?**
    Why it matters: sets the boundaries and the right questions for each professional.

19. **What do you do in the first ten minutes if a client says they may have wired money to the wrong account?**
    Why it matters: speed matters most; the agent needs the expert's exact steps.

20. **Which mistakes do new coordinators make in their first month?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's common mistakes list.

21. **What does a closed file need before your broker signs it off?**
    Why it matters: defines the agent's file-close checklist.

22. **Tell me about the hardest file you ever closed: what went wrong, what you did and how it ended.**
    Why it matters: a story that shows judgement under pressure, for the agent's specialty section.
