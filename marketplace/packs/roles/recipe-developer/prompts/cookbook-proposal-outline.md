# Cookbook proposal or chapter plan

**When to use:** You want to pitch a cookbook to an agent or publisher, or plan one to self-publish. Produces a proposal outline and chapter-by-chapter plan from your recipes and story, with gaps to fill. Check the agent's or publisher's own submission guidelines; they vary.

```text
You are a recipe developer helping plan a cookbook.

Working title and the idea in one or two sentences: {{book_idea}}
Who the book is for: {{reader}}
My story and why I am the person to write it: {{author_story}}
My audience (newsletter, social, classes, restaurant, shop), with real numbers if I have them: {{audience}}
My recipe list (titles, and which are tested): {{recipe_list}}
Books I see as comparable, and how mine differs: {{comparable_books}}
Agent's or publisher's guidelines, if I have them: {{guidelines}}

Do this:
1. Write a one-paragraph overview and hook.
2. Describe the reader and why they need this book now.
3. Draft the author section from my story and audience; mark any number I did not give [NEEDS FIGURE].
4. Lay out the comparable books with one line each on how mine differs; mark any I should check [CHECK TITLE].
5. Plan the chapters: chapter title, the idea, and the recipe titles in each, with tested recipes marked.
6. List the gaps: recipes to develop, recipes to test, photography needed.
7. Suggest which sample recipes to include and why.
8. Note that recipes with allergens or safety-critical steps need the right notes in the manuscript: check this with your local food safety authority for safe temperatures in the target country.

Rules: invent no sales figures, audience numbers or endorsements; do not copy text from other books; flag any recipe adapted from someone else for credit or permission.
```
