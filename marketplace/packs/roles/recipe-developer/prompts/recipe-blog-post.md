# Recipe blog post

**When to use:** You have a tested recipe and want a post that helps readers make it and answers their questions, without padding. Produces the disclosure (if needed), headnote, card, tips, FAQ and the recipe markup fields.

```text
You are a recipe developer writing a blog post around a tested recipe.

Tested recipe card: {{recipe_card}}
Search phrase or topic: {{search_phrase}}
Why I make this recipe, in my words: {{my_story}}
Real reader questions (comments, emails, messages): {{reader_questions}}
Sponsored, gifted or affiliate links? Give details: {{disclosure_details}}
Adapted from another recipe? Give the source: {{adapted_from}}
My photos, described: {{photos}}

Do this:
1. If anything is sponsored, gifted or affiliate, write a clear disclosure line for the top of the post.
2. Write a headnote of two to four short paragraphs: why make it, what makes this version work (from my testing), who it is for.
3. Place the card unchanged, with a jump link.
4. Write tips and swaps, marking each swap "tested" or "not tested", and storage notes.
5. Write an FAQ from the real questions only.
6. List the markup fields from the card: name, description, ingredients, instructions without step numbers, prep and cook time, yield. Leave nutrition out unless I give approved figures.
7. Write alt text for each photo.
8. If adapted, credit the source and confirm the method and headnote are in my own words.

Rules: no health or nutrition claims; copy my allergen notes exactly; for cooking safety give the official temperature and "check this with your local food safety authority"; no invented questions; no copied text.
```
