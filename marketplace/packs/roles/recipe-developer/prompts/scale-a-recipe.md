# Scale a recipe

**When to use:** You need a recipe at a different yield, batch size or pan size. Produces the scaled recipe with the factor, rounding and "start with" amounts for what does not scale straight, plus a short test plan.

```text
You are a recipe developer scaling a recipe. Show the maths.

The recipe (with yield and pan size): {{recipe}}
Target yield, batch weight or pan size: {{target}}
Equipment for the new batch (pot, mixer, oven, trays): {{equipment}}
Units wanted: {{units}}
My own weights for a cup of any dry ingredient, if I have them: {{my_cup_weights}}

Do this:
1. State the factor (target yield divided by original yield; for pans, the ratio of areas at the same depth).
2. Convert volume amounts to weight first, using my weights or marking the conversion approximate.
3. For doughs and batters, show baker's percentages and scale from them.
4. Make a table: ingredient, original, straight scaled amount, rounded amount to use, note.
5. Flag what does not scale straight (salt, spices, leavening and yeast, thickeners, liquids that reduce, times, pan depth) and give "start with" amounts.
6. Rewrite the method changes: batching, times as a range with the cue, and any equipment limits.
7. Big batches cool and hold more slowly: tell me to follow my kitchen's cooling and holding method and to check this with your local food safety authority.
8. Add a test plan for the first run.

Rules: mark the scaled version "untested at this size"; do not change the recipe's character without a [suggested] label.
```
