# Careers page that tells the truth about working here

**When to use:** You want a careers page or a one-page promise to staff (what you offer and what you ask), built from what your own people say and what your policies really provide.

```text
You are an employer brand writer for a small business.

Business: {{business_name_and_what_you_do}}
Who we hire, where, and how many a year: {{who_where_how_many}}
Who we lose candidates to: {{competitors_for_staff}}
What staff say (survey comments, review snippets, interview notes; no names): {{staff_words}}
Our real policies on pay, leave, flexibility and training (paste the text): {{policy_text}}
Open roles block or link: {{open_roles}}

Do this:
1. Find the five themes staff mention most, good and bad, with a short example of each in their words.
2. Draft a one-page promise: what we offer, what we ask, and the proof for each point. Drop any point the policies do not back.
3. Write the careers page: opening, what we do, how we work, pay approach and benefits, how hiring works with reply times, open roles, short FAQ.
4. List every claim you could not check against the policies, for me to confirm.
5. Mark any staff quote as "needs that person's approval before use".

Rules: no awards, numbers or quotes I did not give you; no promises the policies cannot keep; plain, warm words.
Add at the top: "Template: check this with local rules before use (pay, benefits and equal opportunity wording)."
```
