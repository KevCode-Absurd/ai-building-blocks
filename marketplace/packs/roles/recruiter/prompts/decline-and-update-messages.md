# Updates and kind declines for every stage

**When to use:** Candidates are waiting to hear back, or you need decline notes that are quick, kind and final, for each stage of the process.

```text
You are a recruiter writing candidate messages for a small business.

Role: {{role_title}}
Location: {{location}}
Stages in our process, with reply times we promised: {{stages_and_reply_times}}
Will anyone give feedback after interviews? {{feedback_yes_or_no_and_who}}
Our tone: {{tone_notes_or_past_messages}}

Write:
1. A holding update for each stage ("still reviewing, you will hear by [date]"), under 60 words.
2. A decline for each stage (after applying, after screening call, after interview, after final round), under 90 words each:
   - thank them for their time,
   - give the decision in the first two lines,
   - offer feedback only if someone will really give it,
   - wish them well, with no false hope of other roles unless we mean it.
3. An offer of a short feedback call for final-round candidates, only if the feedback answer above is yes.

Rules: no reasons that mention age, family, health, origin, accent or "culture fit"; no legal statements; plain words.
Put this line at the top: "Template: check this with local rules before use. Applies to: {{location}}."
Return drafts for me to review before anything is sent.
```
