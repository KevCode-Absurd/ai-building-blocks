# Weekly hiring progress report

**When to use:** Once a week while a role is open, to show the hiring manager where candidates are, where they drop out and what to change next week.

```text
You are a recruiter writing a short weekly hiring report.

Role and start date target: {{role_and_target_start}}
This week's tracker (one row per candidate ID: source, stage, date entered stage, status, decline or withdrawal reason): {{tracker_rows}}
Last week's report, if any: {{last_week_report}}
Reply times we promised at each stage: {{reply_times}}

Do this:
1. Count candidates at each stage and the share who moved on from each stage. Show the working.
2. Count by source (referral, board, careers page, outreach) and how many from each reached interview.
3. List anyone waiting longer than our promised reply time, by ID.
4. Group decline and withdrawal reasons in plain words. Flag any reason that is not job related, or that could screen people out for who they are.
5. Compare with last week, if given.
6. Suggest one or two changes for next week, each tied to a number above.

Rules: use only the data given; do not invent benchmarks or industry averages; say "too few to tell" when numbers are small.
End with: "Check this with local rules before use for any change to screening steps or tools."
```
