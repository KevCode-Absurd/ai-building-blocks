# Training interview: recruiter

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names or personal details of real candidates, clients or staff; anonymise every story.

1. **Which roles, levels, industries and places do you recruit for most, and in-house or agency?**
   Why it matters: sets the limits of the agent's advice and which rules and channels it should know.

2. **Walk me through your last search, from the first call with the hiring manager to the accepted offer.**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence, the shortcuts and where the expert adds the most value.

3. **What do you ask a hiring manager in the first meeting, and what do you push back on?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions and how the expert turns a wish list into outcomes.

4. **How do you decide which channels to use for a role? Give one role where the obvious channel failed.**
   Why it matters: channel choice is judgement; the agent needs the expert's rules of thumb and their reasons.

5. **Show me a job post you are proud of. What did you cut, and why?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real model and the editing choices behind it.

6. **What does a first message look like when it works? And one that got no replies?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the outreach patterns, length and tone that actually get answers.

7. **How do you handle pay when the client will not put a range in the ad?**
   Why it matters: a common conflict; the agent needs the expert's argument and their fallback.

8. **How do you screen a large pile of applications without falling back on gut feel?**
   Why it matters: captures the criteria, evidence rules and time limits the expert uses.

9. **Which interview questions do you trust most, and how do you score the answers?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent reusable questions and scoring guides grounded in practice.

10. **Tell me about a hire that went wrong. What did the process miss?**
    Why it matters: reveals the checks and warning signs the expert now adds.

11. **Have you used an AI tool to source, screen or rank people? What did you check first, and what went wrong?**
    Why it matters: AI hiring tools carry legal and fairness risk; the agent needs real limits, not vendor claims.

12. **How do you keep candidates informed, and what is your rule for reply times?**
    Why it matters: candidate experience lives in these habits; the agent should copy them.

13. **How do you write a decline after a final interview?**
    Why it matters: a hard message to get right; the expert's wording becomes the agent's template.

14. **What do you check in an offer letter before it goes out?**
    Why it matters: offer errors cost hires; this gives the agent its checklist.

15. **How do you run a reference call so it tells you something useful?**
    Why it matters: captures questions and listening cues beyond "would you rehire them?".

16. **Describe a counter-offer you handled. What could move and what could not?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert prepares the client and keeps the offer honest.

17. **When have you had to tell a client their request was unfair or risky? What did you say?**
    Why it matters: the agent must push back kindly; real wording helps it do that.

18. **Which local rules do you check before every search, and where do you check them?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which official sources the expert trusts for their regions.

19. **What numbers do you report to clients each week, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's report format and its limits on unsourced benchmarks.

20. **What do new recruiters get wrong most often?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's list of mistakes to avoid and to coach clients away from.
