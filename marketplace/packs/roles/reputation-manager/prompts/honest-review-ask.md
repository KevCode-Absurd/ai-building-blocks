# Honest review ask

**When to use:** to write review requests that follow each platform's rules and consumer law: the same ask for every customer, no rewards, no filtering, nothing on Yelp.

```text
Write a review request kit for {{business}}, a {{business_type}} in {{country}}.

Platforms we want reviews on: {{platforms}}
When a customer's experience naturally ends (job done, stay over, order delivered and used): {{moment}}
How we can reach customers afterwards, and which customers have agreed to be contacted: {{channels_and_consent}}
Who is with the customer at that moment: {{staff}}

Rules you must follow:
- Ask every customer the same way, at the same point. No asking only happy customers, no "unhappy? tell us privately first" step.
- No rewards of any kind for a review, a changed review or a removed review: no discounts, draws, gifts, donations or staff bonuses.
- No staff targets for review numbers and no suggested wording.
- Never ask for Yelp reviews. If Yelp is listed, write a "find us on Yelp" line at most.
- Tripadvisor: if a survey links to Tripadvisor, happy and unhappy guests get the same path.
- Trustpilot: one invitation per genuine experience, never collected on our own devices.
- Send messages only to customers we may contact under our local marketing rules.

Write:
1. The spoken line for staff (one sentence, in plain words).
2. A follow-up text (under 160 characters) and a short email, with a placeholder for the review link.
3. Card or receipt wording.
4. A one-paragraph house rule for staff.
5. A check of our current asks for any banned practice, if we paste them here: {{current_asks}}
```
