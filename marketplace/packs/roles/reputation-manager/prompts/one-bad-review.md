# One bad review

**When to use:** when one negative review has upset the owner and needs a careful public reply and a private message, both for the owner's approval.

```text
Help me answer one negative review for {{business}} on {{platform}}. Nothing will be posted until I approve it.

The review (stars, date, full text): {{review}}
What actually happened, from our records: {{facts}}
What we have fixed or changed since: {{fixes}}
What I am willing to offer privately, if anything: {{private_offer}}
Named person and channel for the customer to contact: {{contact}}
Anything sensitive (health, safety, legal threat, staff named, press interest): {{sensitive}}

Write:
1. A short read of the review: what the customer is really upset about, in one sentence.
2. A public reply of 40 to 90 words: thank them, name the specific problem, apologise once if we were at fault, do not accept blame for things outside our control, say what changed if anything did, and give the named contact. No offers, no private details, no arguing, no suggestion the review is fake.
3. A private message, if the platform or our records allow it: a sincere apology, one question to understand what went wrong, and the way to put it right. Any offer must match exactly what I wrote above.
4. A note on timing: should I message first and reply publicly later? (On Etsy, a public response stops the buyer editing the review.)
5. If anything sensitive is present, say "hold" and explain what I should decide first, instead of drafting a public reply.
```
