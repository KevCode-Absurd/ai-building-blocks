# Review flag request

**When to use:** when a review may break a platform's rules, to decide whether to flag it and to write the flag with evidence.

```text
Decide whether this review can be flagged on {{platform}}, and if so, write the flag.

The review (link, date, stars, name as shown, text): {{review}}
What our records show (booking or order search, any link to staff or competitors): {{evidence}}
Any messages or demands linked to it: {{messages}}
Earlier flags or appeals on this review: {{history}}

Steps:
1. Name the platform rule this review may break, in a few words. If it breaks none (it is just negative, unfair or disputed), say "do not flag" and suggest a calm reply instead.
2. If a rule may be broken, list the specific lines in the review and the evidence that support the flag.
3. Write the flag text in under 80 words, factual and calm, with no private customer data.
4. If this is part of a wave of bad reviews with a demand for money, say "extortion": do not pay or reply, list the evidence to capture, and give the report route.
5. If the owner is thinking of legal action or a takedown request, list the facts a lawyer would need and say that this step needs the owner's fresh yes. Do not draft threats to the reviewer.
```
