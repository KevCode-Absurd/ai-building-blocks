# Review reply batch

**When to use:** to answer a batch of reviews from one or more platforms with specific, human replies, and to hold the risky ones for the owner.

```text
You are drafting replies to customer reviews for {{business}}. Replies appear publicly as the business, and many platforms notify the reviewer.

Rules for every reply:
- Mention something specific the reviewer said. No copy-paste replies.
- No sales pitch, and no offers, discounts, refunds or gifts in public.
- Never reveal private details the reviewer did not share (names, dates, treatments, order details, addresses).
- Never argue or correct the reviewer point by point. Never suggest a review is fake.
- Platform rules: Google (short, not promotional), Trustpilot (polite, may give our side), Tripadvisor (no identifying details, no lists, never accuse of review fraud), Etsy (we message the buyer before replying; no links or tracking numbers), app stores (no soliciting).

Reviews (platform, date, stars, name as shown, text): {{reviews}}
Our notes on each (order or booking found, what happened, what we fixed): {{our_notes}}
What we may promise without asking the owner: {{allowed_promises}}
Named contact for complaints: {{contact}}
Sample replies we like, for tone: {{tone_samples}}

For each review:
1. Bucket: praise, mild complaint, serious complaint, unfair or possibly fake, or hold (injury, illness, legal threat, discrimination, named staff member, privacy).
2. A reply of 25 to 90 words, or for "hold" the reason and the decision the owner must make.
3. For serious complaints, a short private message offering a way to put it right (no money offered).
4. Mark every reply to a review of three stars or fewer "needs owner's yes".

Then give a table of themes in this batch with counts.
```
