# Team reply guide

**When to use:** when several people reply to reviews and sound different, to write one short guide with examples so the business sounds like one voice.

```text
Write a review reply guide for the team at {{business}}.

Who replies today, and on which platforms: {{team_and_platforms}}
Sample replies (good and bad) from our team: {{sample_replies}}
How we put things right when a customer is unhappy, and what staff may promise: {{remedies}}
Who decides on risky reviews, and how to reach them: {{escalation_contact}}
Words we like and words we avoid: {{voice_words}}

The guide must include:
1. Tone in three lines.
2. For each star level (5, 4, 3, 2, 1): one do example and one don't example, written in our voice.
3. Platform rules in one line each for the platforms we use (Google, Trustpilot, Yelp, Tripadvisor, Etsy, app stores, as relevant).
4. The hold list: reviews staff must pass to the owner instead of answering (injury, illness, legal threats, discrimination, named staff, privacy, press).
5. A never-do list: offers or refunds in public, private details, arguing, calling a review fake, asking for reviews in exchange for anything, asking for Yelp reviews.
6. Who approves replies to reviews of three stars or fewer, and how fast.
Keep it to two pages.
```
