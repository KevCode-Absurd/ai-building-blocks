# Testimonial request

**When to use:** to ask one happy customer for a testimonial with three questions that draw out specifics, and a clear note on how their words will be used.

```text
Write a testimonial request from {{business}} to {{customer_name}}.

What we did for them, and the recent good moment: {{what_we_did}}
Where we want to use their words (website, ads, sales deck, case study): {{planned_use}}
How long we would like to use them: {{duration}}
Channel (email, text, in person): {{channel}}
Our tone: {{tone}}

The request must:
- Sound like a thank-you, not a form.
- Say plainly where their words may appear and that we will send the final wording for approval before using anything.
- Say they can say no, change their words or withdraw later.
- Offer no reward tied to what they say.
- Ask exactly three questions: what was going on before, what changed and roughly when, and a number, moment or detail that sums it up.

Also write:
1. A short follow-up for one week later if there is no reply.
2. The approval message we will send with the edited quote, asking them to confirm the words, name or photo use, where and until when.
3. A note for the owner on any claim in the likely answer that needs checking, and a reminder that consent wording should be checked by our lawyer.
```
