# Repeat complaint report

One angry review is a story; the same complaint five times is a fact about the business. This skill counts what customers keep saying, so the owner fixes causes instead of answering symptoms, and so the best praise is ready to use as proof once permission is in place.

Access levels for this skill: everything here is level 1 (reading reviews and exports) plus level 2 for the report and the proof bank in a shared document. Nothing goes live. Quotes in the proof bank are not used in marketing until the testimonial and consent library skill records permission.

## When to use

- Monthly, in the first week.
- The rating has dropped, or a run of bad reviews has landed.
- Before a change to menu, prices, opening hours, delivery or staffing.
- The owner asks what customers keep saying.

## What you need

- **Access I have:** read access or exports for each platform.
- All reviews for the period: platform, date, stars, text. Include replies, so you can see which complaints were already answered.
- Last month's report, for the trend.
- Anything the owner changed during the month (new supplier, new staff, new booking system).
- Optional: support tickets, survey comments and refund reasons, which often show the same themes earlier.

## Steps

1. **Collect.** Put every review from every platform into one sheet: platform, date, stars, text, reply status.
2. **Set the theme list.** Start from last month's themes, then add new ones. Keep themes concrete: "wait for mains", "delivery late", "price for portion size", "staff friendly", "booking app failed". Avoid vague themes such as "service".
3. **Tag.** Give each review one or two themes and a sentiment for each (praise or complaint). Mark severity for complaints: annoyance, lost sale, harm or safety.
4. **Count and rank.** Count complaints and praise by theme. Rank complaints by count, then severity. Note the platforms where each theme appears, since some platforms attract certain customers.
5. **Compare.** Show each theme's count against last month. Flag a theme that rose sharply, and one that fell after a fix.
6. **Pick example quotes.** For each top theme, two short quotes in the customer's own words, with platform and date. Keep names out of the internal report unless needed.
7. **Find the likely cause.** Ask the owner what was happening: a staff gap, a supplier change, a new process. Do not guess at causes the data cannot show.
8. **Recommend one fix.** The single change that would remove the most complaints, with what it would cost the owner in effort, and how you will know it worked (the theme count next month).
9. **Update the proof bank.** Copy the strongest specific praise into the proof bank, tagged by theme, product and buyer type, with platform, date and link. Mark each "permission needed" until the consent library records a yes.
10. **Send the report** as a one-page summary with the full sheet attached.

## Checks

- Every review in the period is counted, from every platform.
- Themes are concrete enough that the owner knows what to change.
- Counts and quotes match the sheet.
- Trend uses the same theme names as last month, or notes renamed themes.
- Causes are the owner's facts, not the agent's guesses.
- Safety, health or legal complaints are raised at the top, even if few.
- Proof bank quotes are copied word for word, with source and date, and marked "permission needed".

## Output format

1. **Headline:** three lines: the biggest repeat complaint, the biggest repeat praise, and the one fix.
2. **Complaint table:** theme, count this month, last month, severity, platforms, two quotes.
3. **Praise table:** theme, count, two quotes.
4. **The one fix:** what, effort, how we will measure it.
5. **Safety, health or legal mentions:** listed separately, with what was done.
6. **Proof bank additions:** quote, theme, platform, date, link, permission status.

## Example of good output (short)

*Kestrel Cycle Repairs is a made-up bike shop, used only to show the format.*

**Headline:** Repairs promised "by Friday" missed the date 7 times this month (3 last month). Customers keep praising clear explanations of what was wrong (11). One fix: text customers when a part is delayed, the day you know.

| Complaint theme | This month | Last month | Severity | Platforms | Quotes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missed collection date | 7 | 3 | Lost trust | Google 5, Yelp 2 | "Said Friday, ready Wednesday, nobody called" (Google, 9 Oct); "Had to chase twice" (Yelp, 21 Oct) |
| Price higher than quote | 2 | 2 | Annoyance | Google 2 | "Final bill £18 more than quoted" (Google, 14 Oct) |

**The one fix:** a same-day text when a part is late, with a new date. Effort: one template and a rule for the workshop. Measure: "missed collection date" count in next month's report.

**Proof bank addition:** "He showed me the worn chain next to a new one, so I knew exactly why" (Google, 3 Oct, link). Theme: clear explanations. Permission needed.
