# Monthly reputation review

**Cadence:** monthly, in the first week, about one hour of the agent's work and 20 minutes of the owner's.

**Trigger:** at least one month of reviews in the review log, or a rating drop the owner wants explained.

**Access:** L1 (read), L2 (read and draft), L3 (read, write and publish within limits).

## Steps

1. **Pull the month, L1.** All reviews from all platforms, replies, flags and outcomes, plus the action log.
2. **Count and rank (skill: repeat-complaint-report), L1 and L2.** Themes, counts, trend against last month, two quotes per top theme, safety or legal mentions at the top.
3. **Agree the one fix, L1.** Ask the owner what was happening behind the top complaint; write the one fix and how next month's count will show whether it worked.
4. **Check the reply desk, L1.** Reply rate and median reply time by platform; any replies removed by a platform; held reviews still waiting.
5. **Check the asks, L1.** Are review asks going to every customer the same way? Any rewards, filters, quotas or Yelp asks crept in? (prompt: honest-review-ask, check section.)
6. **Refresh the proof bank and testimonials (skill: testimonial-and-consent-library), L2 or L3.** Add the month's best quotes marked "permission needed". Send testimonial requests to the customers the owner approves, at L3 within limits. Re-confirm or retire quotes whose consent date has passed.
7. **Check rules that change, L1.** Note any platform guideline or law change in the past month (knowledge section 10) that affects how replies or asks are done.
8. **Write the report, L2.** One page: headline, complaint and praise tables, the one fix, reply desk numbers, asks check, new testimonials, rule changes, decisions needed.
9. **Agree next month, L1.** The owner confirms the fix, any limit changes and which customers may be asked for testimonials.

## Outputs

- A one-page monthly reputation report with the theme sheet.
- The one fix, with its measure.
- Reply desk numbers by platform.
- An updated proof bank and consent log.
- A list of decisions for the owner.

## Needs the client's approval

- The one fix and any change to how the business operates.
- Which customers receive testimonial requests, and any use of a customer's words in a new place.
- Any change to how review asks are sent, or to whom.
- Changes to the standing level 3 limits.
