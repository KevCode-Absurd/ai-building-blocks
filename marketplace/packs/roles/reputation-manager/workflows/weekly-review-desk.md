# Weekly review desk

**Cadence:** weekly (daily for busy restaurants, hotels and apps), about 15 to 30 minutes of the owner's time.

**Trigger:** the platforms are listed, "Access I have" is written down, and the owner has said who decides on risky reviews.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Until the owner grants more, everything runs at L1 and the owner posts.

## Steps

1. **Collect new reviews, L1.** From every platform in the list (Google, Trustpilot, Yelp, Tripadvisor, Etsy, app stores, others), into the review log: date, platform, stars, text, reply status.
2. **Read the action log, L1.** Last week's replies and flags, with results. Removed replies, rejected flags, platform warnings or a review spike go to the top of the note.
3. **Sort and draft (skill: review-reply-desk; prompt: review-reply-batch), L2.** Five buckets; replies for praise and complaints; private messages for serious complaints; holds with reasons.
4. **Post inside limits, L3.** Post replies that sit inside the written limits (for example, four and five stars on named platforms). Check each went live. On Etsy, message the buyer before any public reply to a complaint.
5. **Send for a fresh yes, L2.** Every reply to a review of three stars or fewer, every private message that mentions a remedy, and every held review goes to the owner. Once approved, post at L3 or the owner posts.
6. **Flag what breaks policy (skill: policy-flags-and-takedowns; prompt: review-flag-request), L2 or L3.** File flags with evidence through each platform's tool within limits. Anything legal, and any contact with a reviewer about their review, waits for a fresh yes.
7. **Watch for attacks, L1.** A burst of one-star reviews, or a demand for money, moves straight to the review attack response workflow.
8. **Tag themes, L1.** Add one or two themes per review to the log for the monthly report.
9. **Send the weekly note, L1.** Five lines: new reviews by platform and rating, replies posted, replies waiting for the owner, flags and outcomes, anything unusual.

## Outputs

- Replies posted inside limits, and drafts waiting for approval.
- A held list with reasons and decisions needed.
- Flags filed or drafted, with evidence.
- An updated review log with themes.
- Action log entries (what, where, when, result).
- The five-line weekly note.

## Needs the client's approval

- Every reply to a negative review (three stars or fewer, or alleging harm), every time.
- Any promise of a refund, credit or fix, and any message to a reviewer about their review.
- Any legal or takedown request, or a flag resting on legal grounds.
- Any change to the standing limits (new platforms, new star levels).
