# Monthly source and retraction watch

**Cadence:** Monthly, for a client who publishes content that cites studies, statistics or reports (guides, white papers, product pages, a knowledge base). Needs web search on. Takes a few hours a month for a library of up to a hundred cited sources.

**Access:** Ask which level the client grants for each tool (content management system, documents, reference library) and write it down as "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1 (read). Log every action: what, where, when, result.

## Steps

1. **Keep the source register.** A table of every source the client's published pages cite: page, claim, source, DOI or link, date checked. Build it once from the pages. *Level 1. Level 2: keep it as a draft sheet or database in the client's tools.*
2. **Retraction and correction check.** For every journal article in the register, check Crossref (the `update-to` field or the Retraction Watch data) or the journal page for new retractions, corrections or expressions of concern. *Level 1.*
3. **Link and change check.** Open every web source. Note broken links and pages whose numbers or wording changed; compare with an archived capture where needed. *Level 1.*
4. **New evidence check.** Rerun the saved searches for the client's main topics (from the `literature-search-and-review` search log) for the past month. Flag studies that strengthen, weaken or overturn a published claim. *Level 1.*
5. **Triage.** Sort findings: fix now (retracted source, contradicted claim, broken source for a key number), update soon (newer data), no action. *Level 1.*
6. **Fixes.** For each fix-now item, run the `claim-and-source-check` skill on the affected page and write the new wording and source. *Level 2: suggested edits or draft page updates. Level 3, within agreed limits (which pages, which kinds of fix): publish the updates after the client confirms the action list; a correction that names a person or company as wrong needs a fresh yes every time. Approval: the client approves every change to a claim about health, money, law or a named party.*
7. **Report.** One page: what was checked, what changed, what was fixed, what waits for approval. *Level 1.*

## Outputs

- Updated source register with check dates
- List of retractions, corrections and changed or broken sources
- New evidence relevant to published claims
- Suggested or published fixes
- Monthly one-page report
- Action log of everything done in the client's tools

## Needs the client's approval

The action list before the first live update; every change to a claim about health, money, law or a named person or company; corrections that name someone as wrong; deleting any page or source.
