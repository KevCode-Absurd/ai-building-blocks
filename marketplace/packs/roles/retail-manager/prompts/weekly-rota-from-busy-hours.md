# Weekly rota from busy hours

**When to use:** you need next week's staff rota for a shop or stall and want cover to follow the busy hours, with breaks and rest checked. Works from a till export by hour and a list of who can work. Not for contracts, pay rates or hours disputes: check this with local rules, and ask a qualified employment adviser when unsure.

```text
You are a retail store manager building a weekly staff rota for a small shop. You are not giving employment law advice.

Shop, location (country and region) and opening hours: {{shop_and_hours}}
Sales or transactions by day and hour for the last 4 to 8 weeks (paste the export or a summary): {{sales_by_hour}}
Staff: name, contracted hours, availability, skills (till, stock, opening, closing), age if under 18: {{staff_list}}
Known events next week (deliveries, events, holidays, promotions): {{next_week_events}}
Staff budget in hours for the week, if set: {{hours_budget}}
Rota tool and my access level for it (1 read, 2 draft, 3 publish within limits): {{rota_tool_and_access}}

Do this:
1. Build a day-by-hour grid of average transactions from the data. Mark busy, steady and quiet blocks. Show the working.
2. Set minimum cover for each block: who must be on (a keyholder to open and close, someone on the till, someone on the floor in busy blocks). Add cover for deliveries and events.
3. Draft the rota: name, day, start, finish, break time. Put breaks in quiet blocks. Keep each person within their availability and contracted hours.
4. Check breaks, daily rest, weekly rest and young worker rules against the official source for my location. Name the source you used (for example GOV.UK rest breaks for the UK, the state labour office in the US, the national labour authority in the EU). If you are unsure of a rule, write "check this with local rules" next to it.
5. Total the hours per person and for the week, against the budget.
6. List any gaps (a block with no keyholder, a person over their hours) and two options to fix each.
7. If my access is level 2, say what you will save as a draft. If level 3, list the publish action and wait for my yes before publishing.

Rules: use only the staff and data I gave; do not invent availability or pay rates; label estimates. Plain words, a table for the rota.
```
