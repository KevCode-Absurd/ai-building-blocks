# Promotion calendar and signs

A promotion is a short, planned change to what people see and pay, aimed at one result. This skill plans the year so promotions fall where they help most, then writes signs that can be read in a glance and are true in every word.

## When to use

- The owner wants a plan for the next 12 months, or for one quiet season.
- A promotion is needed for a specific moment: a local festival, school holidays, a new line, a slow month, stock to clear.
- Sign copy is needed for the window, A-board, shelf edge, till point or counter.

Not for: online store promotions or email campaigns (see the `ecommerce-manager` kit), full window display plans (see the `interior-designer` kit), or the print layout of the signs (see the `graphic-designer` kit).

## What you need

- **Till readout** for the last 12 months (see the till-report-readout skill): busy and quiet weeks, best and slow lines, ATV.
- **Local calendar:** school terms, bank and public holidays, local festivals, markets, sports fixtures, paydays, tourist season, and what neighbouring shops plan.
- **Margins and floors:** cost prices, minimum margins, lines that can't be discounted.
- **Price history** for any line you might reduce, to work out a genuine "was" price.
- **Where the shop trades:** the country, and for the EU the member state, because price claim rules differ.
- **Access I have:** level 1 to read; level 2 to save discounts in the till switched off and draft sign files; level 3 to switch on an approved promotion for its dates. Any new "was" price, reduction or deadline needs a fresh yes even at level 3.

## Steps

1. **Map the year.** One row per month: busy or quiet (from the till), local moments, seasons and stock arriving. Mark the three quietest periods; they are where promotions earn the most.
2. **Give each promotion one goal.**
   - **More visits:** an event, a window theme, a reason to come in (workshop, signing, late opening, tasting).
   - **Bigger baskets:** add-ons at the till, bundles, "complete the set", gift wrapping.
   - **Clearing stock:** markdown in steps, starting with moves and bundles.
3. **Pick the offer.** Prefer value over discount for lines that sell anyway (free gift wrap, a bundle, an event). Discount slow lines. Check every offer against floors and margins.
4. **Check the claim.**
   - **UK:** shelf prices and posts show the total price including any mandatory fee; no false "today only" or "last few"; don't present legal rights (such as refunds for faulty goods) as a special perk; "free" means free.
   - **EU:** an announced reduction ("20% off", "was €40") is worked out from the lowest price in at least the 30 days before; generic green claims ("eco-friendly") need evidence, and new EU rules on green claims apply from 27 September 2026.
   - **US:** "Made in USA" only with supplier proof; no fake or bought reviews on signs or posts; for former-price comparisons, ask a qualified adviser about federal guidance and state law.
   - Mark the claim "check this with local rules" when unsure, and send it to a qualified adviser.
5. **Plan placement.** Featured line where people pause: a table a few steps inside the door, the till point, an end bay. Keep the doorway, aisles and fire exits clear. For a full window or display layout, hand over to the `interior-designer` kit.
6. **Write the signs.** For each sign: where it goes, the reading distance, the headline (product or offer first), the price, one reason to care, and the action. Short words, one idea per sign. Window and A-board headlines must be readable from the pavement; shelf talkers from arm's length. Leave layout and type size to the print layout (see `graphic-designer`).
7. **Set the measure.** One number per promotion, compared with the same weeks last year or the weeks before: footfall or transactions for visits, ATV or UPT for baskets, units sold and stock left for clearance.
8. **Write the calendar** with prep dates: order stock, brief staff, change the window, switch on the discount, take signs down.
9. **Set up in the till.**
   - Level 2: save each discount in the till system switched off, with its dates.
   - Level 3: switch on the approved promotions for their dates, within the agreed lines and floors; log each change.

## Checks

- Every promotion has one goal, one offer, one measure, a start and an end date.
- Every reduction has a genuine reference price (the 30-day low in the EU), and every deadline is real.
- Prices on signs match the till and include mandatory fees.
- No claim about origin, eco credentials, reviews or "best seller" status lacks evidence.
- No sign blocks an exit, aisle or doorway.
- Signs come down when the promotion ends.
- Level 3 actions stayed inside the approved list and were logged.

## Output format

1. **Year map:** month, busy or quiet, local moments, stock arriving.
2. **Promotions table:** dates, goal, offer, lines, placement, measure, prep tasks and owners.
3. **Claim checks:** each price or claim, the rule checked, status (OK, needs evidence, check with an adviser).
4. **Sign copy:** sign ID, location, reading distance, headline, price, support line, action.
5. **Till set-up notes** and the access level used, with a log of any changes.

## Example of good output (short)

*Fern and Fable is a made-up gift shop in a UK market town, used only to show the format. Access I have: Square level 2.*

**February (quiet; half-term week 3; no local events).** Goal: more visits on weekdays.

| Dates | Goal | Offer | Placement | Measure | Prep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 to 20 Feb | Visits | Free kids' craft table, 10:00 to 12:00, with any purchase from the craft range | Table by the door, craft range beside it | Weekday morning transactions vs the two weeks before | Order craft kits by 30 Jan (owner); brief staff 13 Feb |

**Claim check:** "Free with any craft kit" is conditional, so the sign must say so, and kit prices must not rise to cover it. OK.

**Sign copy:**
- **W1, window, read from the pavement:** "Half-term craft mornings" / "Mon to Fri, 10 to 12" / "Free with any craft kit".
- **S1, shelf edge, craft range:** "Make it here this half-term" / "Kits from £6".
- **T1, till point:** "Add a gift card for the craft fan in your life".

**Till:** no discount needed. A hidden £0 "craft morning" item drafted in Square so staff can count uptake; waiting for your yes to make it visible on 16 Feb.
