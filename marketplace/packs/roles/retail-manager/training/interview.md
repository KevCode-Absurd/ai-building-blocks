# Interview: retail store manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the person's work (for example, market questions for someone who only runs a shop).

## How you approach a shop

1. **Walk me through the first week with a new shop or stall: what do you look at first, and what do you ask the owner?**
   Why it matters: shows the real intake order, not the textbook one.
2. **When you open a till report, what do you read first, and what makes you stop and dig?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's scanning habit, which becomes the readout skill's first steps.
3. **How do you decide a promotion's goal? Tell me about one that brought people in but lost money, or the reverse.**
   Why it matters: a real trade-off teaches the agent when footfall and margin pull apart.
4. **How do you plan around local moments: school terms, festivals, paydays, weather? What's on your calendar that an outsider would miss?**
   Why it matters: local timing is where retail experience beats generic advice.
5. **What do you put a few steps inside the door, at the till and in the window, and why?**
   Why it matters: placement habits become decision rules.

## Stock and shrink

6. **How do you run a stock count? Who counts, when, and what do you do with the gaps?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's count plan and reconcile checks.
7. **When the count is short, how do you tell theft from paperwork errors or supplier short deliveries?**
   Why it matters: the cause decides the fix, and blaming the wrong cause wastes money.
8. **How do you decide when to reorder a best seller and how many, and when to stop buying a line?**
   Why it matters: reorder judgement is the core of cash and stock control.
9. **How do you mark down slow stock? What do you try before cutting the price?**
   Why it matters: markdown order protects margin.

## People and rotas

10. **How do you build a rota? What do you check before you publish it?**
    Why it matters: fills the rota prompt with real checks, including local rules.
11. **What do you teach a new starter on day one about selling, and what do you never let staff say?**
    Why it matters: becomes the staff selling guide and its limits.
12. **How do you handle a refund request the owner's policy says no to but the customer insists on?**
    Why it matters: tests whether they put legal rights first.

## Markets and pop-ups

13. **How do you decide whether a market is worth the pitch fee? Give me a real example with the numbers you used.**
    Why it matters: the break-even habit, in the expert's own words.
14. **What goes wrong on market days, and what is always in your kit now because of it?**
    Why it matters: real failures become checklist items.
15. **How do you set the float and handle card payments with no signal?**
    Why it matters: practical payment habits prevent lost sales.

## Tools, access and claims

16. **Which till systems have you used, and which reports do you trust? Which do you rebuild in a spreadsheet?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.
17. **Which changes would you make in a client's till system on your own, and which do you always check first? What limits do you agree?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's level 2 and level 3 habits.
18. **Have you seen a sign or offer that got a shop into trouble, or nearly did? What do you check now?**
    Why it matters: price and claim checks are where retail advice can cause harm.

## Mistakes and specialty

19. **What are the three mistakes you see most in independent shops?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's "spot it" list.
20. **Tell me about the hardest shop or event you worked on. What happened, and what would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: a hard story shows judgement under pressure.
21. **Which kinds of shops or products do you know best, and what is different about them?**
    Why it matters: feeds "My specialty".
22. **What work do you turn down, and why?**
    Why it matters: defines what the agent is not for.
