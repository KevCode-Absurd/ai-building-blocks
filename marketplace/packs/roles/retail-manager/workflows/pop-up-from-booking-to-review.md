# Pop-up from booking to review

**Trigger:** a maker or shop is offered, or is considering, a market pitch, craft fair or pop-up space. Start at least three weeks before the event where possible.

## Steps

1. **Intake and access (access.md questions).** Event details, products and past sales, people, payments, location. Ask which access level the client grants for the till or card reader app and the shared sheets; until they answer, work at level 1.
2. **Go or think again (skill: pop-up-and-market-plan, step 1).** Break-even against past sales. If it doesn't stack up, suggest what to change (fee, staff, hours, stock) before anything is paid.
   **Approval point 1:** the client decides to go ahead. The client books and pays.
3. **Application (prompt: market-application).** Draft answers, product descriptions and a photo shot list from the client's facts. The client reviews and submits.
4. **Plan the day (skill: pop-up-and-market-plan, steps 2 to 8).** Stock list, layout, rota with breaks checked against local rules, payments with backup and offline cap, float by note and coin, packing and set-up checklist.
5. **Signs (skill: promotion-calendar-and-signs, step 6; prompt: price-and-returns-sign-check).** Stall sign, price tags, any offer sign, checked for true claims.
6. **Till set-up.** Level 2: draft an event location or price list, or a stock transfer to the event, in the till. Level 3: apply it within the client's limits and log it.
   **Approval point 2:** event prices and any offer.
7. **Start count (skill: stock-count-and-shrink).** Count every line before leaving.
8. **The day.** The client runs the stall. I can answer questions in chat.
9. **Review (skill: till-report-readout, event review).** End count, reader export and cash log; sales, profit, hourly pattern, best and worst lines; three changes for next time.
10. **Stock back in.** Level 3: transfer remaining stock back to the shop location within the limits and log it; otherwise list the moves for the client.

## Outputs

- Break-even and go or think-again note.
- Draft application, product descriptions and photo shot list.
- Event plan: stock list, layout, rota, payments, float, checklist.
- Sign copy with claim checks.
- Start and end count sheets.
- After-event review with profit and three changes.
- Action log of every change made in the client's tools.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool, and level 3 limits.
- Going ahead, booking and paying for the pitch (client only).
- Submitting the application (client only).
- Event prices and offers.
- Any spend on kit or stock, and any order to a supplier, each time.
- Food, licence, insurance and safety questions: check this with local rules and the organiser.
