# Stock count cycle

**Cadence:** a full count once or twice a year (before the busy season, or at the date the accountant needs), with rolling cycle counts in between. Trigger an extra count when best sellers vanish, shrink rises or a delivery goes wrong.

## Steps

1. **Access and plan (access.md; skill: stock-count-and-shrink).** Confirm the till system, its plan (some count features need a paid tier) and "Access I have". Rank lines A, B and C from the till readout. Set the cycle: A lines most often, then B, then C, so every line is counted within the gap the client chooses (Square suggests at least every 90 days per variation).
2. **Before each count.** Book in all deliveries, transfers and returns. Set aside special orders and layaways. Level 2: set up the count in the system with the right location and categories.
3. **Count.** Staff count by zone with scanners, the POS app or count sheets. Recount large variances.
4. **Reconcile.**
   - Cycle count: level 3 approves within the agreed categories; level 2 leaves it for the owner.
   - Full count: show the variance summary and the list of uncounted items, and get a fresh yes before approving, because uncounted items may be set to zero and approval can't be undone.
   **Approval point 1:** full count approval, every time.
5. **Shrink report.** Shrink at cost and at retail, rate against net sales since the last count, by category and top lines. Split by likely cause using reason codes and patterns.
6. **Fixes.** Receiving checks, reason codes required on every adjustment, moving high-risk lines, till routines. Supplier claims for short deliveries are drafted at level 2.
   **Approval point 2:** any supplier claim or message before it is sent.
7. **Report and schedule.** Send the shrink report, the fixes with owners and dates, and the next counts.

## Outputs

- Count plan and count sheets (or counts set up in the till).
- Variance summary with recounts and paperwork fixes.
- Shrink table by category and line, with causes.
- Fix list with owners and dates; draft supplier claims.
- Count schedule for the next cycle.
- Action log of every change made in the client's tools.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits for the till system.
- Approval of any full count, every time.
- Supplier claims and messages, every time.
- Deleting or archiving items found during the count.
- Valuing stock for accounts or tax is for the client's accountant; ask a qualified accountant.
