# Weekly trading review

**Cadence:** every Monday morning, before the week's deliveries and rota are fixed. Takes about an hour once the exports arrive.

## Steps

1. **Access check (access.md questions).** Confirm "Access I have" for the till system, the rota tool and the shared sheets. Until the client answers, work at level 1.
2. **Pull last week's numbers.** Item sales, sales by hour, stock on hand and the adjustment history from Square, Shopify POS or Lightspeed (level 1), or the client's export.
3. **Readout (skill: till-report-readout).** Headline numbers against the same week last year or the four weeks before; best and worst lines; busy and quiet blocks; slow stock and stock-outs; three actions. Level 1.
4. **Stock follow-up (skill: stock-count-and-shrink).** If adjustments without reasons, negative stock or unexplained gaps appear, schedule a cycle count of those lines for the week. Level 2 to set up the count in the till; level 3 to approve a cycle count within the agreed categories.
5. **Reorder list.** Best sellers at risk of running out, with quantity from sales rate and supplier lead time. Level 2: draft purchase orders in the till or a sheet.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves each purchase order. Sending it to the supplier needs a fresh yes each time, even at level 3; paying stays with the client.
6. **Promotions check (skill: promotion-calendar-and-signs).** Is this week's promotion on track against its measure? Do the next two weeks' signs, discounts and stock need preparing? Run any new sign through the price-and-returns-sign-check prompt.
   **Approval point 2:** new prices, reductions, "was" prices and deadlines.
7. **Price changes.** Level 2: save the approved changes in the till, inactive. Level 3: apply them on the named lines, never below the floor sheet; log each change.
8. **Rota (prompt: weekly-rota-from-busy-hours).** Draft next week's rota against the busy blocks and planned events; check breaks and rest against local rules. Level 2: save as a draft in the rota tool. Level 3: publish after the client's yes on the draft.
   **Approval point 3:** the rota before it is published.
9. **Report.** One page to the owner: the readout, actions taken (with the log), approvals waiting, and the one number to watch.

## Outputs

- One-page weekly readout with three actions.
- Reorder list and draft purchase orders.
- Promotion status and sign copy checks.
- Draft or published rota.
- Action log of every change made in the client's tools.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool, and the limits for level 3 (categories, price floors, actions, end date).
- Every purchase order and supplier message, each time.
- New prices, reductions, "was" prices and deadlines.
- The rota before publishing.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits, asked again each time.
