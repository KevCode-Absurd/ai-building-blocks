# Training interview: RevOps specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share customer names, deal values tied to named clients, or personal data; anonymise every story.

1. **Which CRMs, marketing tools and billing systems do you know best, and for what size of team?**
   Why it matters: sets the tools the agent can speak about with confidence and the limits of its experience.

2. **Walk me through the last CRM you set up or rebuilt, from the first conversation to the first month live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real sequence, the shortcuts taken and where the expert adds most value.

3. **How do you get marketing, sales and finance to agree on one definition of a qualified lead, a won deal and churn?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's method for the hardest part of the role, which is agreement, not configuration.

4. **What makes a good entry or exit rule for a stage? Give one you are proud of and one that failed.**
   Why it matters: turns stage design from theory into checkable rules the agent can reuse.

5. **How do you decide whether a business needs a second pipeline?**
   Why it matters: records a judgement call that often goes wrong.

6. **Tell me about a lead scoring model you built. How did you test it, and did sales trust it?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert builds from real outcomes and earns trust.

7. **What is your routine for cleaning a messy CRM without breaking anything?**
   Why it matters: captures batch sizes, checks and undo habits the agent should copy.

8. **When have you refused to merge or delete records, and why?**
   Why it matters: reveals the expert's safety lines for irreversible changes.

9. **How do you build a forecast for a team with few deals?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert handles uncertainty honestly.

10. **When sales dropped, how did you find out which number moved?**
    Why it matters: captures the diagnostic order the agent should follow.

11. **How do you run win-loss reviews so sellers don't feel blamed?**
    Why it matters: records tone and method for a sensitive analysis.

12. **What have you learned from buyer interviews that the CRM notes never showed?**
    Why it matters: shows the gap between stated and real reasons, with examples.

13. **How do you set territories and quotas so reps accept them?**
    Why it matters: captures the balance rules and explanation style that make plans stick.

14. **How do you connect the CRM to billing, and where do the numbers usually stop matching?**
    Why it matters: builds the agent's map of common CRM-to-billing gaps.

15. **How do you handle refunds, credits and plan changes for a client, and where do you stop and ask?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's money limits and approval habits.

16. **Which automation went wrong on your watch, and what did you change afterwards?**
    Why it matters: turns a failure into checks the agent can run before switching anything on.

17. **What do you check about consent and email rules before loading contacts into marketing automation?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent within email and privacy rules for each market.

18. **Which reports or dashboards do owners actually use, and which do they ignore?**
    Why it matters: helps the agent build less and build what gets used.

19. **What do you hand over at the end of a job so the team can keep the system clean?**
    Why it matters: captures the handoff documents and habits that make work last.

20. **Tell me about the hardest client you had in this role and what you would do differently.**
    Why it matters: records judgement under pressure, which plain AI lacks.
