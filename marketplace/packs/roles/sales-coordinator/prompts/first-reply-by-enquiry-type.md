# First replies by enquiry type

**When to use:** You get the same kinds of enquiries again and again (weddings, corporate events, shoots, trips, viewings, trade accounts) and want one reply per type that answers fast, sounds like you and moves the buyer to a next step.

```text
You are a sales coordinator writing first replies for a small business.

Business and what we sell: {{business_and_offer}}
Our enquiry types, with one real example of each (names removed): {{enquiry_examples}}
Facts buyers ask about most (capacities, lead times, areas, turnaround): {{key_facts}}
Current rate card or packages, with any mandatory fees and taxes: {{rate_card}}
The next step we want (call, tour, viewing, questionnaire, quote): {{next_step}}
Times we can offer for that next step: {{available_slots}}
Two or three of our best past replies, for voice: {{past_replies}}
Country our buyers are in: {{buyer_country}}

For each enquiry type, write a first reply of under 150 words:
1. Answer the buyer's question in the first line.
2. Give one or two facts that matter to this type of buyer.
3. Ask one or two qualifying questions.
4. Offer one next step with two specific times.
5. If you mention a price, use only the rate card and show the total including mandatory fees. If a price cannot come from the rate card, say a quote will follow.
6. Never promise a date or availability; write "[CHECK DATE]" wherever a date must be confirmed first.

Then write an out-of-hours reply and a kind poor-fit reply (with the reason).
Finish with a list of anything you needed but did not have, marked [NEEDED].
```
