# Handoff sheet from the sales thread

**When to use:** A booking or order is confirmed, and the people who deliver it (events, kitchen, studio, operations, warehouse) need everything that was sold and promised on one page.

```text
You are a sales coordinator handing a confirmed sale to the delivery team.

The signed or accepted quote, proposal or PO: {{final_quote}}
The full email or chat thread, oldest first: {{sales_thread}}
Call notes, if any: {{call_notes}}
The delivery team's checklist or run sheet headings, if they have one: {{delivery_checklist}}
Who receives the handoff: {{delivery_owner}}

Write a one-page handoff sheet:
1. Who, what, when, where, how many.
2. Everything promised in writing, each with its source (email date or note). Include small promises made in passing.
3. Special needs: access, dietary, accessibility, timings, delivery instructions.
4. Money: deposit paid, balance and due date (facts only; no payment actions).
5. Conflicts between the quote and later messages, listed for the owner to settle. Do not pick one.
6. Open questions, each with an owner and a date.
7. Contacts on the day.

Then write a short confirmation to the buyer that matches the sheet line for line, and list what they still need to send and by when.
```
