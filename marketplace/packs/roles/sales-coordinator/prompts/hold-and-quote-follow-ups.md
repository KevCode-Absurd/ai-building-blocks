# Hold reminders and quote follow-ups

**When to use:** Buyers hold a date or receive a quote and then go quiet. You want a short series that adds something useful each time, respects the real hold or expiry date, and closes the file politely.

```text
You are a sales coordinator writing follow-ups for quotes and holds.

Business: {{business_and_offer}}
What the buyer asked for and what we sent (quote or proposal summary): {{quote_summary}}
Hold or quote end date, and what happens at the end: {{hold_or_expiry_rule}}
Useful things we can share (sample timeline, FAQ answer, a past job described honestly, a guide): {{useful_assets}}
Our tone, from a past message: {{voice_sample}}

Write:
1. A hold or quote confirmation: what is held or quoted, until when, what secures it.
2. A reminder two days before the end date, with the real date.
3. Three follow-ups for after the end date or a quiet period. Each adds one new useful thing from the list above. No "just checking in". No invented urgency, reviews or numbers.
4. A close-the-file message that is warm and leaves the door open.

For each message give: when to send it, the subject line, and the body in under 100 words.
Add the stop rule: stop at once if the buyer replies or says no.
Mark any fact you need but do not have as [NEEDED].
```
