# Quote and proposal builder

## When to use

- A buyer has given enough detail to price: date, numbers, scope, location or order lines.
- Quotes go out with missing fees, wrong totals or no expiry, and have to be re-issued.
- Buyers reply to quotes with "what does this include?" or "what happens next?".
- The client wants a standard layout that anyone on the team can fill in.

Do not use it to set prices, discounts or terms. Those come from the client.

## Access needed

- **Level 1 (read):** the rate card, packages, supplier quotes, product list, past proposals, the buyer's thread and CRM record.
- **Level 2 (read and draft):** build the quote or proposal as a draft in the CRM, client platform or accounting tool; prepare an e-signature envelope, unsent.
- **Level 3 (read, write and publish):** send quotes and proposals that are clearly non-binding (an estimate or "subject to confirmation"), update the deal stage and log the send, within the confirmed limits.
- **Fresh yes every time, at any level:** a quote that binds the client once the buyer accepts it, a contract, and any discount or price not on the rate card.

## What you need

1. The buyer's brief in their own words: what, when, where, how many, budget if given, what matters most.
2. The rate card or supplier quotes, with the date they are valid to.
3. Mandatory fees and taxes: service charges, booking fees, cleaning, resort fees, VAT or sales tax.
4. Deposit, payment schedule, cancellation and change terms, as the client has them.
5. Whether this quote is binding once accepted, and how long prices hold.
6. Which country the buyer is in (pricing, cancellation and package travel rules differ).

## Steps

### 1. Restate the brief

Open with two or three lines that restate what the buyer asked for in their words. If anything is missing (guest count, delivery address, travel dates, usage rights), list it as a question rather than guessing.

### 2. Pick the options

Offer one recommended option and at most two others. Each option is something the client already sells, matched to the brief. Explain in one line why the recommended option fits this buyer.

### 3. Price from the card

Every line comes from the rate card or a supplier quote that is still valid. Show quantities, unit prices and line totals. Add mandatory fees and taxes into the total. Optional extras sit in their own clearly marked list.

- **UK consumers:** the headline price is the total with mandatory fees; "from" prices must include unavoidable charges such as a flat booking fee; charges paid locally are shown as pay now and pay locally, with the overall total.
- **US hotels, short-term lodging and ticketed events:** the total with mandatory fees comes first and most prominently; only government charges, shipping and optional extras may sit outside it, disclosed before payment.
- **Travel:** if the trip combines a flight with accommodation or other services, follow the client's package process (EU package information and form; in the UK, the client's ATOL position) and point to official sources for entry rules.

If a line cannot be priced from the card, leave it blank and flag it.

### 4. State what is and is not included

A short "Included" list and a short "Not included" list. The "Not included" list prevents more disputes than any other part of the quote.

### 5. Add dates and money

- Quote date and expiry date.
- Deposit amount, what it secures, and when the balance is due.
- For trade quotes: lead time "to confirm on order", delivery terms (the Incoterms rule and place, if the client uses them), and the purchase order details needed.

### 6. Mark the terms

Summarise cancellation, change and usage terms in plain words from the client's own terms, and mark each one [CLIENT TO CONFIRM]. Do not interpret them. If the buyer is a consumer in the EU or UK, note that cancellation rights may apply and the client's lawyer decides how they apply to this booking.

### 7. Write the cover note

Four to six sentences: why this option fits, the total, what happens next, what the buyer needs to do and by when.

### 8. Decide how it goes out

- Non-binding estimate within the confirmed limits: send at level 3, log it, set a follow-up date.
- Binding quote, contract or anything off the rate card: save as a draft, send the client a one-line summary (buyer, option, total, expiry, anything unusual) and wait for a yes for this send.

## Checks

- Every price traces to the rate card or a valid supplier quote; no line is estimated.
- The total includes mandatory fees and taxes; extras are separate and marked optional.
- Inclusions, exclusions, deposit, balance date and expiry are all present.
- Terms are the client's own words, summarised and marked to confirm.
- Country rules considered: UK total price, US fees rule, EU and UK cancellation rights, package travel.
- Binding quotes and contracts are held for a fresh yes.
- The maths adds up: line totals, tax and the grand total.

## Output format

1. Cover note
2. The brief in the buyer's words, plus open questions
3. Options table: option, what it includes, total price
4. Recommended option in detail: lines, fees, taxes, total
5. Included / Not included
6. Dates and money: quote date, expiry, deposit, balance, delivery terms
7. Terms summary marked [CLIENT TO CONFIRM]
8. How to confirm
9. Send note: binding or non-binding, and what needs the client's yes

## Example of good output (short)

*Illustrative structure, not a real business: a two-person product photography studio quoting a skincare brand. Copy the shape, never the content: every fact comes from the client's own context.*

> **Cover note:** Thanks for the brief, Dana. For 24 product shots on white plus 6 styled scenes, I'd recommend the Launch package below: it covers everything you listed for the March site relaunch in one studio day. The total is £3,180 including VAT. If it works for you, the £950 deposit holds 4 March; the quote is valid until 4 December 2026.
>
> | Option | Includes | Total (incl. VAT) |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Launch (recommended)** | 24 white-background shots, 6 styled scenes, 2 rounds of retouching, web licence | £3,180 |
> | Essentials | 24 white-background shots, 1 round of retouching, web licence | £1,980 |
>
> **Not included:** props over £150 (charged at cost with your OK), hair and make-up, print licence.
>
> **Terms to confirm:** usage licence length, reshoot policy, cancellation within 14 days of the shoot [CLIENT TO CONFIRM].
>
> **Send note:** non-binding estimate from the 2026 rate card, within limits; ready to send. The contract will come to you for a yes before it goes.
