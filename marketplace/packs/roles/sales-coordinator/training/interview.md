# Training interview: sales coordinator

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, emails, phone numbers or payment details of real buyers; anonymise every story.

1. **What kind of business do you coordinate sales for, what do buyers book or order, and in which countries?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's market, its enquiry types and the pricing and cancellation rules it must point to.

2. **Walk me through what happens from the moment an enquiry lands to the moment the booking or order is confirmed.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work, the tools and the hand-offs, not the textbook version.

3. **What are your five most common enquiries, and what do you say first to each?**
   Why it matters: the agent's reply set should start from replies that already work.

4. **Show me a first reply that booked and one that went nowhere. What was different?**
   Why it matters: real examples teach length, tone and order better than rules.

5. **Which questions tell you fastest whether an enquiry is a good fit?**
   Why it matters: qualifying questions are where coordinators save the most time, and they are rarely written down.

6. **How do you check a date, room, stock level or lead time before you offer it? What happens when you can't?**
   Why it matters: the agent must copy the expert's checks so it never promises what is not there.

7. **How do holds or options work in your business: how long, who can extend, what happens at the end?**
   Why it matters: hold rules are business-specific and decide most follow-up timing.

8. **Take me through how you build a quote or proposal. Where do the prices, fees and terms come from?**
   Why it matters: the agent must work from the same sources and never price from memory.

9. **Which quotes are binding once the buyer accepts them, and who signs off before they go?**
   Why it matters: binding quotes need a fresh yes every time; the agent needs the exact boundary.

10. **What mistakes have you seen in quotes: missing fees, wrong totals, no expiry? How do you catch them now?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's own error history into the agent's checklist.

11. **How do you follow up a quote without nagging? What do you send, and when do you stop?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's follow-up content and its stop rule from experience.

12. **What does your delivery team (events, kitchen, studio, warehouse) need from you when a sale is confirmed?**
    Why it matters: the handoff sheet should be built in the delivery team's language.

13. **Tell me about a handoff that went wrong. What was missed, and what would have caught it?**
    Why it matters: a real failure shows which promises slip through and where the checklist must pause.

14. **How do you handle a buyer who changes things after they have confirmed?**
    Why it matters: change control is where errors and disputes start; the agent needs the expert's rule.

15. **For trade orders: how do you handle out-of-stock lines, part shipments and substitutes?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the options and wording trade buyers accept.

16. **What does a clean CRM look like to you? Which fields, stages and rules matter most?**
    Why it matters: the hygiene sweep should check what this expert relies on, not a generic list.

17. **Where do your duplicate records come from, and how do you decide which record to keep?**
    Why it matters: merge rules are judgement calls the agent should learn before it proposes any.

18. **Which rules do you watch in your field: total pricing, cancellation rights, package travel, property listing information, fair housing?**
    Why it matters: shows which official sources the agent should point to, and when the expert calls a lawyer or trade body.

19. **When do you hand a conversation to the owner instead of answering it yourself?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's escalation rules for complaints, discounts, legal questions and payments.

20. **Which access would you give an assistant in your inbox, calendar and CRM, and what would you never let it do alone?**
    Why it matters: sets the default access levels and the actions that always need a fresh yes.

21. **Tell me about the hardest booking you ever got over the line. What did you do that others would not have?**
    Why it matters: brings out the expert's specialty and the judgement no template captures.

22. **What do you measure each week to know you are doing well?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's own scorecard for its weekly report.
