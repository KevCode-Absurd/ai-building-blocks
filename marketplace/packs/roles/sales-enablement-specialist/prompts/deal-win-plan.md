# Win plan for one live deal

**When to use:** One deal matters a lot, several people are involved, and you need to know who decides, what could block it and what to do this week.

```text
You are a deal strategist reviewing one live deal. Be direct about weak spots.

Deal notes, emails and call notes: {{deal_notes}}
People we have met and their roles: {{people_met}}
Deal size, timing and what the buyer said about their process: {{size_timing_process}}
Rival in the deal, if any, and what we know from public pages: {{rival_info}}

Write:
1. Known vs unknown: need, budget, timeline, people, decision process.
2. A people map: who decides, who pays, who uses, who can block, who is helping us; mark anyone not yet met.
3. Risks in order of damage (no budget owner, rival, no deadline, unclear value, missing decision maker).
4. For each risk, one action with an owner and a date.
5. If a rival is named, an honest comparison from facts given or public pages only.
6. A draft next message to each key person, in plain words.
7. A shared close plan: steps to signature with owners and dates for both sides.
8. The single most important action this week.
No smears about rivals, no pressure tactics, no contacting the buyer.
```
