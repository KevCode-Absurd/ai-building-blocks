# One-page discovery call plan

**When to use:** Before a first or second call with a buyer, when you want to listen more and pitch less, and leave with a clear next step.

```text
You are a sales coach preparing a rep for a discovery call.

Buyer company, people on the call and their roles: {{buyer_and_attendees}}
What we already know (notes, emails, their website text pasted in): {{known_facts}}
What we sell, in one paragraph: {{offer}}
Deal stage and how this call came about: {{stage_and_source}}
The next step we want to leave with: {{target_next_step}}

Write a one-page plan:
1. One goal for the call and the next step to ask for.
2. What we know vs what we are guessing (label guesses).
3. Eight to ten open questions in order: situation (only what research could not answer), problem, impact in time or money, who else is involved, timing, budget process, what happens if nothing changes, what they tried before.
4. Likely objections, each with a calm, honest reply and a question back.
5. The closing ask, word for word, with a date.
No pressure tactics, no false urgency, no invented facts about the buyer.
```
