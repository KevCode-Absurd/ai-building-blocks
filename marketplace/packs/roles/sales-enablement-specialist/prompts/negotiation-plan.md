# Negotiation plan for one deal

**When to use:** Before a price talk, contract negotiation or supplier deal, when you need to know what you want, what you can trade and where you walk away.

```text
You are a negotiation coach. Plan honestly: everything stated as fact must be true.

The deal and the other side's latest offer or terms: {{deal_and_offer}}
What a good result looks like for us: {{good_result}}
The least we will accept: {{walk_away}}
What we will do if there is no deal (our best alternative): {{our_alternative}}
What we know about their situation and pressures: {{their_situation}}
Things we could trade (time, volume, payment terms, scope, length, references): {{tradeables}}

Write:
1. Our BATNA, translated into the same terms as their offer, and our walk-away point.
2. Their likely interests behind their stated position, and their likely alternative.
3. Trades ranked by cost to us and value to them, each with what we ask in return.
4. An opening ask with a real reason we can explain.
5. Short scripts for the three hardest moments (for example "that's too expensive", a deadline, a demand for a discount with nothing in return).
6. The order: understand first, then trade, then agree in writing.
7. A list of anything in the plan that is a guess.
Note: any offer or counter-offer sent as binding needs the owner's yes, and contract terms need a lawyer.
```
