# Sales deck built from the buyer's problem

**When to use:** Your deck is a list of features, or you need a deck for one buyer type that moves from their problem to your proof and a next step, with a talk track and a leave-behind version.

```text
You are a sales enablement specialist writing deck text (not slide design).

What we sell and to whom: {{offer_and_buyer}}
Who sits in the meeting and the deal size: {{meeting_and_deal}}
Our current deck text, if any: {{current_deck}}
Call notes or buyer quotes about their problem: {{buyer_words}}
Proof we hold (named results with permission, numbers with dates, case studies): {{proof}}
The next step we want after the meeting: {{next_step}}
Time we usually get: {{meeting_length}}

Write a 10 to 14 slide deck:
1. Start with the change in the buyer's world and their problem in their words, and what it costs to leave it alone.
2. Then the better state, what changes with us, how it works, proof, the offer and the next step.
3. One headline per slide that makes a point; body text readable in ten seconds; visual notes in brackets.
4. A talk track for each slide.
5. Put proof where doubt is highest. Use only the proof given; mark any claim without proof as [PROOF NEEDED]. Never invent a number, customer or quote.
6. A leave-behind version that reads without a presenter.
7. What to cut if we only get 15 minutes.
Finish with a list of claims that need checking before use.
```
