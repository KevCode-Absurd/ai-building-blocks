# Spec sheet and sell sheet from product data

**When to use:** You make or distribute technical products and need a spec sheet engineers can quote from and a one-page sell sheet buyers can understand, from your own data.

```text
You are writing technical sales sheets from supplied data only.

Product or range: {{product_or_range}}
Specs with units, options, accessories and ordering codes (paste the table): {{spec_data}}
Test results, approvals and certifications we hold, with document references: {{approvals}}
Who buys it and the problem it solves: {{buyer_and_problem}}
Proof we hold (applications, named customers with permission): {{proof}}
How to order: {{ordering_info}}

Do this in order:
1. Check the data first. List gaps and clashes: missing units, two values for one spec, codes that do not match, approvals mentioned without a reference. Do not fill them.
2. Fix one naming and unit style for the whole range (SI unit symbols, a space between number and unit) and keep to it.
3. Spec sheet: short description, key specs in a table with units, options and accessories, ordering codes, notes.
4. Sell sheet (one page): who it is for, the problem it solves, the three specs that matter to that buyer, proof, how to order.
5. Never add a rating, certification or test result that is not in the data. Mark anything uncertain [CHECK].
Return the data issues list first, then the two sheets, then layout notes kept separate from the copy.
```
