# New rep onboarding in 30 days

**Trigger:** The client hires one or more sales reps, or a playbook is ready but nobody practises it.

**Cadence:** Set-up in the week before the reps start; daily sessions and practice for four weeks; certification in week four; a behaviour check on real calls in the month after.

**Access:** At the start, ask which level the client grants for the training tool, drive, CRM, calendar and call recorder, and record it under "Access I have". Until they answer, work at level 1. [L2] needs read and draft; [L3] needs read, write and publish within limits; [YES] needs a fresh yes every time.

## Steps

### Set-up

1. **Gather.** At level 1, read the playbook, product sheets, CRM stages and a few anonymised or approved call examples. If there is no playbook, run `sales-playbook-builder` first.
2. **Set goals.** Run `rep-onboarding-and-certification`, steps 1 and 2: four to six certification goals and the month plan. *Approval: the client approves the goals and who certifies.*
3. **Build the material.** Steps 3 to 6 of the skill: role-plays, cheat sheets, quizzes, certification checklist. [L2] Save as unpublished modules. *Approval: the client checks every claim on the cheat sheets.*
4. **Check call examples.** Use only recordings the client confirms were made with the consent its policy requires, anonymised where possible.
5. **Confirm the live action list.** [L3] Which reps, which modules, due dates, which calendars for role-plays and certification, who sees scores, for how long. *Approval: the client's yes.*

### Weeks 1 to 4

6. **Publish and assign.** [L3] Publish modules and assign them to the named reps on schedule.
7. **Quiz and repeat.** Send short quizzes after each session and repeat key questions in later weeks.
8. **Role-play.** Book role-plays [L3] and score them on the sheet; send feedback to the rep and coach as agreed.
9. **Certify.** Week 4: the manager runs certification calls with the checklist. Reps who miss get the retry plan.

### After

10. **Evaluate.** Four levels: reaction survey, quiz and role-play scores, behaviour on real calls in the next month, and the business result the client chose. Suggest one change to the plan. *Approval: changes outside the confirmed limits need a fresh yes.*

## Outputs

- Certification goals and 30-day plan
- Role-plays, cheat sheets, quizzes and certification checklist
- Assignment and progress log
- Evaluation report with one suggested change

## Needs the client's approval

Goals and certifiers, every claim on the cheat sheets, the level 3 action list and limits, who sees scores, and anything outside the limits. Certification results are for development; pay, review and hiring decisions stay with the client.
