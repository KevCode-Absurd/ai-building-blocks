# Playbook and battlecard refresh

**Trigger:** A quarter ends, win rates against a rival drop, a rival changes prices or product, the offer changes, or reps stop using the playbook.

**Cadence:** Quarterly for the playbook; monthly check of each battlecard's sources; at once when a rival or the offer changes.

**Access:** Record the level granted for the CRM, content or wiki tool and any competitive intelligence tool under "Access I have". Until the client answers, work at level 1. [L2] needs read and draft; [L3] needs read, write and publish within limits; [YES] needs a fresh yes every time.

## Steps

1. **Read the quarter.** At level 1, read won, lost and stalled deals since the last refresh, with notes and any win-loss interviews. Use the `four-forces-deal-review` prompt on lost and "no decision" deals.
2. **Check usage.** Which plays, sheets and cards reps used (from the content tool or CRM), and which they ignored.
3. **Update the playbook.** Run `sales-playbook-builder` on what changed: new plays, retired plays, updated objections and proof. [L2] Save as a draft with a change list. *Approval: the client approves the changes.*
4. **Refresh battlecards.** Run `competitive-battlecard` for each active rival: re-check every public source and date, update where we win and lose from the quarter's deals. [L2] Save drafts. *Approval: the client approves each card.*
5. **Check claims.** Every claim about us has proof on file; every claim about a rival has a dated public source. [YES] Any rival claim going into public material needs a fresh yes and the client's legal review.
6. **Publish.** [L3] Publish approved versions to the agreed space, retire old versions with the client's OK, and log the change.
7. **Tell the team.** Draft a short "what changed" note and, if needed, a 10-minute huddle with one role-play on the new play. [L3] Post the note to the agreed channel.
8. **Report.** What changed, why, and what to watch next quarter.

## Outputs

- Updated playbook and play cards with a change list
- Refreshed battlecards with dated sources
- "What changed" note and huddle
- Action log and quarterly report

## Needs the client's approval

Every playbook change, every battlecard, any public claim about a rival (fresh yes and legal review), retiring old versions, and anything outside the confirmed limits.
