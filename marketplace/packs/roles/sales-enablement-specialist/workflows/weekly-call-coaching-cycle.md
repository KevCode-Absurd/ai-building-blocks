# Weekly call coaching cycle

**Trigger:** A team records sales calls with consent and wants regular coaching, or managers score calls differently.

**Cadence:** Weekly: each rep gets coaching on one or two calls they choose or their manager tags. Monthly: a calibration session for managers.

**Access:** Record the level granted for the call recorder, CRM and the place where coaching notes go under "Access I have". Until the client answers, work at level 1. [L2] needs read and draft; [L3] needs read, write and publish within limits; [YES] needs a fresh yes every time.

## Steps

### Set-up

1. **Consent and notice.** Ask the client how recording consent is obtained for buyers and how reps are told their calls may be reviewed, for every country and US state involved. Point to the official sources in the `call-coaching-from-recordings` skill. Recording settings, consent messages and notices stay with the client. *Approval: the client confirms in writing before any recording is analysed.*
2. **Switch off emotion scoring.** If the call tool offers sentiment or emotion scores about employees, ask the client to switch them off for coaching, and always for teams in the EU.
3. **Agree the scorecard.** Build or adopt a scorecard with example lines per level (skill step 7). *Approval: managers agree the scorecard.*
4. **Agree who sees what.** Rep and manager, or manager only; how long notes are kept; that notes are for development only.
5. **Confirm the live action list.** [L3] Which reps, which call tags, where notes are posted, for how long. *Approval: the client's yes.*

### Weekly

6. **Pick calls.** Read only the tagged calls, within the agreed limits.
7. **Coach.** Run `call-coaching-from-recordings`: call map, scorecard with evidence, three things done well, three habits with rewrites and drills.
8. **Post.** [L3] Post notes and drills to the agreed people. At level 2, notes wait for the manager.
9. **Practise.** Add each drill to the next role-play session.
10. **Escalate.** Stop and tell the client if a recording shows no clear consent, a buyer objects to recording, or anything suggests a legal or HR issue.

### Monthly

11. **Calibrate.** Two managers score the same call; compare and adjust the scorecard examples.
12. **Report.** Behaviours improving across the team, common gaps, best example calls for the library (with consent and anonymised). *Approval: adding clips to a shared library needs the client's yes.*

## Outputs

- Consent and notice confirmation on file
- Agreed scorecard
- Weekly coaching notes and drills per rep
- Monthly calibration notes and team report
- Action log of every note posted

## Needs the client's approval

Consent and notice confirmation before any analysis, the scorecard, who sees notes, the level 3 action list, clips in a shared library, and anything outside the limits. Coaching notes are never used by this agent for pay, review or dismissal decisions.
