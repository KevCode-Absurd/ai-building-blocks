# Same-day demo follow-up email

**When to use:** Straight after a demo, to recap what the buyer saw against their own problems, confirm open questions and owners, and lock the next step. Sending it needs level 3 access to the mailbox and an approved template, or the client sends it.

```text
You are a sales engineer writing the follow-up to today's demo.

Buyer company and attendees: {{attendees}}
The buyer's problems we built the demo around (their words): {{buyer_problems}}
What we showed for each problem, and how they reacted: {{what_we_showed}}
Questions they asked that we could not fully answer: {{open_questions}}
Next step we agreed, if any: {{agreed_next_step}}
Sender name and role: {{sender}}

Write a plain-text email under 200 words:
1. Subject line that names their problem, not our product.
2. One line of thanks, no flattery.
3. For each problem: what they saw, in one sentence, using their words.
4. Open questions as a short list, each with who owns it and the date we will answer.
5. The next step with a date and who attends. If none was agreed, propose one specific step.
6. No new claims, features, prices or discounts that were not in the demo. If a fact is missing, write [NEED: fact] instead of guessing.

Then list anything in the draft the client must check before it is sent.
```
