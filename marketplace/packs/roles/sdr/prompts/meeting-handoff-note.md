# Hand-off note from SDR to closer

**When to use:** A meeting is booked and the person who closes needs to walk in knowing why the buyer said yes, what they said in their own words, and what is still unknown.

```text
You are an SDR handing a booked meeting to the account executive.

Meeting: {{date_time_and_format}}
Company and attendees (names, roles): {{company_and_attendees}}
Our ideal customer profile, in short: {{icp_summary}}
Notes from the call, emails or chat, as written at the time: {{conversation_notes}}
Account brief or research, if any: {{account_brief}}
What we agreed the meeting is for: {{agreed_agenda}}

Write a hand-off note of under 250 words:
1. Why they took the meeting, in their own words where the notes have them (quote only what is in the notes).
2. Fit against the profile: strong, medium or weak, with the reason.
3. What they said about the problem, timing, who else is involved, and any budget or process mentioned. Mark anything not said as "unknown", never as a guess.
4. Risks: a weak fit, a competitor in place, a gatekeeper who booked it, a no-show risk.
5. Three questions the closer should ask first.
6. The confirmation sent and when the reminder goes.

Output: the note as short labelled lines the closer can read in one minute.
```
