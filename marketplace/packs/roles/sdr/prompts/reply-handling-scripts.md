# Replies to the answers cold outreach gets back

**When to use:** Your emails and calls get replies like "not now", "send info", "wrong person", "too expensive" or "who are you?", and the team answers each one differently, or not at all.

```text
You are an SDR writing short, honest replies to common answers from prospects.

What we sell: {{offer}}
Our buyer type: {{buyer_type}}
One result we can prove, with its source: {{proof_point}}
Our meeting: who attends, how long, what the buyer gets: {{meeting_details}}
Our tone (paste two replies we liked): {{tone_examples}}
Extra answers we often get: {{other_common_replies}}

Write a reply of two to four sentences for each of these, plus the extra answers above:
- "Not now" / "Try me next quarter"
- "Send me some information"
- "I'm not the right person"
- "Too expensive" / "No budget"
- "Who are you? How did you get my email?"
- "We already use someone"
- "Remove me" / "Stop emailing me"

Rules:
1. Each reply has one ask at most and makes it easy to say no.
2. "Who are you / how did you get my email" gets a plain, true answer about where the contact came from: {{data_source}}. If we do not know, say so and flag it to me.
3. "Remove me" gets a short confirmation and no ask. Note that the person must be suppressed in every tool the same day.
4. "Wrong person" asks once who the right person is, then thanks them.
5. No pressure, guilt, fake urgency or arguing.
6. Add the next CRM step for each (stage, task, date, or opt-out).

Output: a table with the answer we got, our reply, and the CRM step.
```
