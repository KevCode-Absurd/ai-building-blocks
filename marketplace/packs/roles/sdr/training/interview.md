# Training interview: sales development representative (SDR)

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, emails or phone numbers of real prospects or clients; anonymise every story.

1. **What do you sell, to whom, and in which countries? Who closes the deals you book?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's market, the rules it must know and the hand-off it serves.

2. **Walk me through how you would start a new segment on Monday morning, step by step.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the expert spends time.

3. **How do you decide who your best customers are? Show me a profile you built and the deals behind it.**
   Why it matters: the agent needs the expert's way of turning deal history into a profile, not a generic one.

4. **What makes a lead go straight to the bottom of your list?**
   Why it matters: red flags save the most time; they are rarely written down.

5. **Where do your lists come from, and what do you check before you use one?**
   Why it matters: list history decides both results and legal risk; the agent should copy the expert's checks.

6. **Show me a cold email that got good replies and one that flopped. What was different?**
   Why it matters: real examples teach length, tone and structure better than rules.

7. **How do you find a true reason to contact someone now? Give an example of a trigger that worked.**
   Why it matters: the reason to write is the hardest part to fake well; the agent needs real patterns.

8. **Read me your opening line on a cold call. What do you do in the first ten seconds?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's spoken style and their tested opener.

9. **What are the five brush-offs you hear most, and what do you say to each?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent reply scripts grounded in what works for this market.

10. **How do you handle a gatekeeper? Tell me about one who helped you.**
    Why it matters: honest gatekeeper lines are a craft skill the agent should learn.

11. **What do you put in a voicemail, and how long is it?**
    Why it matters: voicemail habits vary widely; the agent should follow the expert's.

12. **How many touches, over how long, across which channels, before you stop?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's cadence and its stop rule from experience, not folklore.

13. **How fast do you answer inbound enquiries, and what do you say first?**
    Why it matters: speed to lead depends on habits and templates the agent can copy.

14. **What do you do to make sure a booked meeting actually happens?**
    Why it matters: show rate is where many SDRs lose their work; this gives the confirmation routine.

15. **What goes in your hand-off note to the closer? Show me a good one.**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's hand-off format and what the closer values.

16. **Which rules do you check before emailing or calling a new country, and where do you check them?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which official sources the expert trusts and how they apply them.

17. **How do you keep your sending domain healthy? What made you pause a campaign?**
    Why it matters: deliverability failures are costly; the agent needs real warning signs.

18. **Have you used AI or automation tools for prospecting? What went wrong, and what do you now refuse to do?**
    Why it matters: the agent needs the expert's limits on automation, not vendor claims.

19. **Tell me about a campaign that failed. What did you change?**
    Why it matters: reveals the checks and judgement the expert now adds.

20. **Which numbers do you track each week, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's report format and stops it chasing vanity numbers.
