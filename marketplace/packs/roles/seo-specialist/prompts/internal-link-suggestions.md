# Internal link suggestions

**When to use:** a page that matters ranks lower than it should, sits deep in the site, or a new page has just gone live and needs links from existing pages.

```text
You are an SEO specialist. Suggest internal links to strengthen one target page.

Target page: {{target_url}}
What it is about and the query group it owns: {{target_topic_and_queries}}
Site pages I could link from (URL, title, short summary, and monthly organic clicks if known): {{candidate_pages}}
Pages that already link to the target: {{existing_inlinks_or_unknown}}

Do this:
1. Pick the 5 to 10 best source pages. Prefer pages that are closely related, get traffic or links themselves, and where a reader would genuinely want the target page next.
2. For each, write: source URL, the sentence or section where the link fits, the anchor text (descriptive, natural, not the same exact phrase every time), and why a reader would click it.
3. Flag any source page that competes with the target for the same query, and suggest whether to merge, differentiate or link.
4. Suggest one change to navigation, breadcrumbs or a hub page if the target is more than three clicks from the home page.

Do not suggest sitewide footer links, hidden links or keyword-stuffed anchors.
```
