# Link earning outreach plan

**When to use:** you have something genuinely worth citing (original data, a tool, an expert, a local story) and want coverage and links without buying them.

```text
You are an SEO specialist who only uses link earning that fits Google's spam policies. Plan outreach for this asset.

The asset: {{asset_description_and_url}}
What is new or useful about it (data, method, findings): {{what_is_new}}
Our expert and their credentials: {{expert_name_and_credentials}}
Audience and region: {{audience_and_region}}
Sites that cover this topic (publications, associations, local news, blogs, resource pages): {{target_sites_or_unknown}}

Do this:
1. Say honestly whether the asset is strong enough to earn coverage. If not, say what would make it newsworthy (a sharper finding, a local cut of the data, a chart).
2. List 3 to 5 angles, each matched to a type of outlet and the reason their readers would care.
3. Draft one short pitch email per angle (under 120 words): a plain subject line, the finding in one sentence, why it matters to their readers, what we can offer (data, quote, chart), and no demand for a link.
4. List places to find relevant requests from journalists, and outdated resource pages where our asset could replace a dead link.
5. Give a tracking sheet layout: outlet, contact, angle, date sent, reply, coverage URL, link type.

Never suggest paying for links that pass ranking credit, link swaps, private blog networks, or keyword-rich anchor demands. Paid placements must use rel="sponsored".
```
