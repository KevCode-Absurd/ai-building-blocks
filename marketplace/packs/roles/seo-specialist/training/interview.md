# Interview: what makes you a good SEO specialist

The coach asks these one at a time and follows up on vague answers with "Show me an example" or "What did the data say?". Each question has a line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first two weeks with a new client. What do you look at first, and what do you ignore?**
   Shows your triage order and whether you start from business value or from tool scores.

2. **Which five numbers do you check every month for a client, and why those five?**
   Reveals what you think success is and whether you separate branded, non-branded and conversions.

3. **Tell me about a traffic drop you diagnosed. How did you rule causes in and out?**
   Captures your diagnosis method, the part plain AI gets most wrong.

4. **How do you decide whether two keywords belong on one page or two?**
   Tests whether you group by search results and intent rather than by similar words.

5. **What makes a content brief good enough that a writer you have never met produces a page you are happy with?**
   Pulls out the parts of your brief that carry the most weight.

6. **When a client asks for 200 location pages or AI-written articles at scale, what do you say?**
   Shows how you handle scaled content risk and say no without losing the client.

7. **How do you earn links today? Give a recent example that worked and one that did not.**
   Separates real link earning from link buying in disguise.

8. **What is on your migration checklist that most people forget?**
   Migration mistakes are expensive; your extra checks are valuable know-how.

9. **How do you explain a core update hit to a business owner who is panicking?**
   Captures your tone and how you set expectations without false promises.

10. **Which technical issues do you fix first, and which do you leave even though tools flag them?**
    Shows judgement about impact, effort and risk.

11. **How has your work changed since AI Overviews and AI Mode spread? What do you report now that you did not before?**
    Shows whether your measurement and advice have kept up.

12. **What do you look for when you judge whether a page shows real experience and expertise?**
    Turns E-E-A-T from a buzzword into specific checks.

13. **Which tools do you use daily, and what do you use each one for? What do you never trust a tool to do?**
    Maps your tool stack and where you rely on your own judgement.

14. **Tell me about the hardest client or project you have had. What went wrong and what did you change afterwards?**
    Stories carry the lessons rules miss.

15. **What is the most common bad SEO advice your clients bring to you?**
    Collects the myths you correct, useful for every client conversation.

16. **How do you handle a client who wants to rank for one vanity keyword that will not bring customers?**
    Shows how you redirect effort to value while keeping trust.

17. **What does a "done" audit look like when you send it? What must be in it before it leaves your hands?**
    Captures your personal quality bar.

18. **How do you work with developers who push back on SEO changes?**
    SEO fails without implementation; this shows how you get things shipped.

19. **For local businesses, what moves results most in your experience, and what is overrated?**
    Local work has its own rules and myths.

20. **When do you tell a client that SEO is not the right channel for them right now?**
    Honest advisers know when not to sell their own service.

21. **What do you check before you trust a study or a "ranking factors" article?**
    Shows how you separate evidence from noise in a field full of opinion.
