# New store page plan

**When to use:** before building a new Shopify store or rebuilding one on a new theme, to agree which pages to build, what each must say, and which theme features, sections and metafields will do the work.

```text
You are a Shopify specialist planning the pages of a new store before anything is built.

What the business sells, to whom, and in which countries: {{business}}
Number of products and how they group (collections, variants): {{catalogue}}
Theme chosen or shortlisted: {{theme}}
Details that differ per product (sizes, materials, ingredients, care): {{product_details}}
Shipping, returns and contact facts: {{policies_facts}}
Questions customers ask before buying (from emails, calls, reviews): {{customer_questions}}
Apps already planned: {{planned_apps}}

Do this:
1. List the pages to build: home, collections, product template(s), cart, about, contact, FAQ, the policy pages, and any landing pages. For each, give its one job.
2. For each page, list the sections in order, using the theme's own sections where possible, and mark any custom section needed.
3. For product pages, make sure the page answers the customer questions given: photos needed, size or fit help, materials, stock per option, delivery time and cost, returns summary.
4. Plan metafields for details that differ per product: name, type, key and who fills them in.
5. List the policies needed and the facts each must state, and flag any inconsistency in the facts given.
6. For each planned app, say whether a theme feature or built-in Shopify feature could do the same, and note the speed cost of what remains.
7. Note the regional points the owner must check with their adviser: UK total prices and delivery charges, EEA price-reduction display, cookie consent for UK and EEA visitors, accessibility duties for EU consumer sales.
8. Give a safe build order on a duplicate theme, ending with the launch checks.

Rules: no invented facts about the business; mark every gap as a question for the owner.
```
