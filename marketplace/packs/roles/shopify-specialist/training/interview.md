# Interview: Shopify specialist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your last small theme change, from the owner's first message to the moment it went live.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, including where approvals and previews happen.
2. **How do you decide between a theme setting, an existing section, a new section, a snippet and an app?**
   Why it matters: choosing the smallest change is the core judgement of this role.
3. **What do you ask for before you touch any code, and what do you refuse to accept, such as a password?**
   Why it matters: fills the intake list and the access rules with what really works.
4. **How do you get a working copy of the theme and keep the live store safe while you work?**
   Why it matters: captures their safe-editing routine and undo habits.
5. **How do you design section settings so the owner can maintain the change without you?**
   Why it matters: editable settings separate a lasting fix from a support burden.
6. **Which themes do you know best, and what is different about working on Horizon or other theme-block themes compared with older themes?**
   Why it matters: feeds "My specialty" and the theme-specific traps.

## Speed and apps

7. **When a store says it is slow, what do you look at first, and why?**
   Why it matters: shows whether they start from real-visitor data or a lab score.
8. **Tell me about an app that was hurting a store. How did you prove it and what did you do?**
   Why it matters: app bloat is the most common speed cause on Shopify.
9. **How do you find code left behind by uninstalled apps?**
   Why it matters: their search routine becomes the agent's clean-up method.
10. **How do you rank speed fixes when the owner has a small budget?**
    Why it matters: payback ranking is what owners pay for.

## Launches and sales

11. **What is on your pre-launch checklist that most people forget?**
    Why it matters: their hard-won checks make the launch skill better than a generic list.
12. **How do you prepare a store for Black Friday or a product drop, week by week?**
    Why it matters: captures the freeze, scheduling and day-of routine.
13. **Tell me about a launch or sale day that went wrong. What happened and what do you do differently now?**
    Why it matters: failure stories carry the most transferable lessons.
14. **How do you handle checkout requests now that Scripts and the old checkout files no longer work?**
    Why it matters: shows whether their checkout knowledge is current.

## Quality bar and tools

15. **What do you check before you hand a change to the owner? Give me your actual checklist.**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality checks.
16. **Which tools do you use daily: Shopify CLI, Theme Check, an AI coding tool, browser dev tools, accessibility checkers?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works and how it is set up safely.
17. **How do you check accessibility and contrast on a theme change?**
    Why it matters: accessibility duties now apply to many stores selling to EU consumers, and to US businesses under the ADA.
18. **How do you write install, test and undo steps for an owner who is not a developer? Show me one.**
    Why it matters: their handover format becomes the agent's.

## Clients and boundaries

19. **What requests do you push back on, and how do you say no?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent handles fake urgency, risky edits and "just publish it".
20. **What kind of Shopify job do you turn down or hand to someone else?**
    Why it matters: defines what the agent is not for.
21. **When a client lets you publish changes yourself, what limits, checks and records do you agree first? And what do you say if they offer their password instead?**
    Why it matters: sets how the agent works at level 3 and where it stops to ask again.

## A hard job

22. **Tell me about the hardest Shopify job you have done: the brief, what went wrong, and how you got it over the line.**
    Why it matters: hard stories show judgement under pressure better than any checklist.
23. **If you had one week to train a junior Shopify developer, what would you teach first?**
    Why it matters: reveals what the expert thinks matters most.
