# Launch and sale readiness

**Trigger:** a new store is about to go live, a store is moving platform or theme, or a big sale or product drop is four or more weeks away.

**Cadence for sales:** start four weeks out; checks at four weeks, one week and the day before; review the week after.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish). Until the owner grants more, everything runs at L1 and the owner does the L2 and L3 steps.

## Steps

1. **Intake (playbook intake questions), L1.** Launch or sale date and time zone, markets sold to, offers and codes, hero products and stock, apps, team and hours, who can publish. Ask which level the owner grants per tool and the limits (store, kinds of change, price or discount caps, dates); write it down as "Access I have".
2. **Page plan, for new stores (prompt: new-store-page-plan), L1.** Agree the pages, sections, metafields and policies before building.
   **Approval point 1:** the owner approves the page plan.
3. **Full check (skill: store-launch-check), L1.** Walk the buyer path on a phone, check product pages, prices and delivery costs, policies, notifications, tracking, cookie banner, accessibility basics and apps. Produce the fix list ranked by sales at risk, with the level each fix needs.
4. **Speed baseline (skill: speed-fix-list), L1.** Record field Core Web Vitals per page type and list what puts sales at risk before the date.
5. **App clean-up (prompt: leftover-app-code-finder), L2.** Remove leftovers of uninstalled apps on a duplicate theme.
6. **Fix round, L2 to build, L3 to publish.** Each fix goes through the safe-theme-change workflow or is handed to the owner with steps. Draft pages and draft policy text at L2.
   **Approval point 2:** the owner approves each change request.
7. **Owner tests.** The owner places the listed test orders (test mode, real card refunded, each discount code, each shipping zone), then turns test mode off. Payment settings and card details stay with the owner.
8. **Sale plan (prompt: sale-day-runbook), L1 to plan, L2 to build.** Freeze date, sale changes built on a duplicate theme, day-of timeline, rollback plan.
   **Approval point 3:** the owner approves the runbook and the action list for any L3 steps.
9. **Go live, L3 or the owner.** Schedule the sale changes with Rollouts with an end date, publish approved fixes, and for a launch remove the password page. At L3, act only on the approved action list and within the caps, keep a backup of the live theme, log each action and check the live store after each one.
10. **Day of, L1 to watch, L3 within limits.** Follow the runbook. Watch for reported problems; at L3, apply fixes that fall inside the approved limits and log them; anything outside the limits, such as a discount above the cap or a rollback to an untested theme, needs a fresh yes. Stop and ask on any error or warning.
11. **Review, L1.** Confirm the store reverted, compare speed and orders with the baseline, send the action log, and record what to change next time.

## Outputs

- "Access I have" record and action log.
- Page plan (new stores).
- Pass or fix list with owners and levels.
- Speed baseline and fix list.
- Owner test list.
- Sale-day runbook and post-sale review.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits per tool, and any change to them.
- The page plan (point 1), every change request (point 2) and the runbook with its action list (point 3).
- At L3, one yes to the action list before the first live action, and a fresh yes for anything outside the limits.
- Test orders and payment test mode: the owner does these.
- Any price, policy, cookie or accessibility wording: the owner confirms it with their adviser; this agent gives no legal ruling.
