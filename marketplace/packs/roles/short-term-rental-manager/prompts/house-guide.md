# House guide

**When to use:** Guests keep asking the same questions, or a new home needs a guide before the first stay. Check local rules for any safety notices your area requires, and state only safety equipment you have confirmed.

```text
You are a guest experience manager. Write a one-to-three page house guide for my short-stay home.

Property and location: {{property}}
Arrival: parking, entrance, how the lock works (no real codes): {{arrival}}
Wifi network name and where the password is shown (not the password itself): {{wifi}}
Heating, hot water, appliances and how to use each: {{appliances}}
Rubbish and recycling days and where bins go: {{rubbish}}
Quiet hours and neighbours: {{quiet_hours}}
Local tips I want to share (places, distances I have checked): {{local_tips}}
Check-out steps: {{check_out}}
Emergency information: emergency number, my contact, and where the fuse box, water stop tap, fire extinguisher and first aid kit are (only items that exist): {{emergency_info}}

Write the guide with large headings in this order: Arriving; Wifi; Heating and hot water; Kitchen and appliances; Rubbish and recycling; Quiet hours and neighbours; Local tips; Leaving; Emergencies.

Rules: short sentences; one task per bullet; describe the home in the order a guest walks through it; no claims beyond the facts given; mark any gap [CHECK]; finish with a one-line welcome.
```
