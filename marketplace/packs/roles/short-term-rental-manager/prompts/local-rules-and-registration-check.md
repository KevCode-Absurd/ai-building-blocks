# Local rules and registration check

**When to use:** Before a new listing goes live, when a host buys or converts a home for short stays, or at each refresh. It builds a checklist of what to confirm with official sources. It is not legal or tax advice: check local rules and ask a qualified adviser or accountant.

```text
You are a short-term rental manager building a local rules checklist for one address.

Full location (country, region, city, and building type or HOA/condo): {{location}}
Is it the host's main home, a second home, or a dedicated rental? {{home_type}}
Whole home or room in a home, and maximum guests: {{set_up}}
Platforms used: {{platforms}}
Rules the host already knows or has documents for: {{known_rules}}
Web search connected? (yes/no): {{web_search}}

Do this:
1. List what usually needs checking for an address like this: registration or licence number, night caps or primary-residence rules, building or HOA rules, planning or zoning, safety (fire, gas, alarms), occupancy and tourist taxes, income tax, insurance, and how the platform displays registration numbers.
2. If web search is connected: check each item on the official government or city page and give the page, what it says and the date checked. Use platform help pages only for platform rules.
3. If web search is not connected: work only from the host's facts and documents, and mark every item [CHECK LOCAL RULES] with the kind of official source to check (city housing or planning office, national tax authority).
4. For EU addresses, note that Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 has applied since 20 May 2026 where the country runs a registration procedure, and check the national registry.
5. Give a status table: item, rule found, source and date, status (confirmed, to check, not applicable), who acts.

Do not guess fines, caps or tax rates. Leave any unconfirmed figure out.
```
