# Training interview: short-term rental manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share guest names, booking references, door or lockbox codes, ID documents or payout details; anonymise every story.

1. **What kind of places have you run (city flats, holiday cottages, B&Bs, small hotels), where, how many units, and on which platforms?**
   Why it matters: location, property type and channel mix decide which rules and tactics apply.

2. **Did you work as the owner, a co-host, a manager for owners, or hotel staff? What were you paid for?**
   Why it matters: sets whose goals the agent serves and how it reports.

3. **Walk me through how you set a price for a night six months out, and again for the same night three days out.**
   Why it matters: the expert's pricing logic becomes the agent's rule layers.

4. **How do you work out a base rate, and what costs go into your break-even?**
   Why it matters: a sound floor is what stops discounting below cost.

5. **Tell me about a season you priced wrong. What did the data show, and what did you change?**
   Why it matters: real mistakes teach the agent what to watch in pace checks.

6. **What are your minimum stay and gap-night rules, and how did you arrive at them?**
   Why it matters: stay rules are where small hosts lose or win the most nights.

7. **Which pricing tools or platform features do you use, and when do you override them?**
   Why it matters: shows when the agent should trust a tool and when to step in.

8. **How do you read a booking export? What do you clean out before you look at any rate?**
   Why it matters: clean data is the base of every revenue review.

9. **How do you compare channels? Which one pays best after fees for you, and why?**
   Why it matters: net-of-fees thinking keeps the agent from chasing gross revenue.

10. **Show me your best listing, anonymised. What did you add after a bad review?**
    Why it matters: real fixes show which facts prevent complaints.

11. **Which guest messages do you send, when, and which one cut the most questions?**
    Why it matters: timing and wording become the agent's message set.

12. **What is in your house guide, and what do guests still ask after reading it?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent where guides fail.

13. **Tell me about the worst guest problem you handled during a stay. What did you do in the first ten minutes?**
    Why it matters: shows priorities under pressure: safety, fix, then money.

14. **How do you decide on refunds? What limit would you give an assistant, and what must always come to you?**
    Why it matters: sets the refund limit and the fresh-yes line in the expert's words.

15. **When have you cancelled on a guest, and what did it cost you?**
    Why it matters: makes the agent's caution about cancellations concrete.

16. **How do you handle damage? What evidence won or lost a claim?**
    Why it matters: teaches the damage pack and the timing.

17. **How do you run turnovers: who cleans, how do they hear about bookings, and what goes on the checklist?**
    Why it matters: most bad reviews start with a missed turnover.

18. **How do you reply to a review you think is unfair? Show me one.**
    Why it matters: models a calm public reply.

19. **What local rules have you had to follow (registration, night caps, taxes, safety), and where do you check them?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent trusted official sources to check live.

20. **What would you let an assistant change without asking, and what would worry you?**
    Why it matters: sets realistic access levels and limits per tool.

21. **What do new hosts get wrong in their first year?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's list of common mistakes to catch.
