# Interview: small business advisor

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example, with the numbers?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your first meeting with a new owner. What do you ask, and what do you ask them to bring next time?**
   Why it matters: shows the real intake, which shapes everything after it.
2. **Which kinds of business and which countries do you know best, and where do you stop and refer?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and its limits.
3. **How do you find out who will read a plan and what they need, before writing a word?**
   Why it matters: the reader decides the format; experts ask early.

## Plans, lenders and grants

4. **Show me how you build an assumptions table. Which assumptions do lenders or panels question most?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's habit for labelling estimates.
5. **Tell me about a loan or grant application that succeeded, and one that failed. What made the difference?**
   Why it matters: real outcomes beat textbook advice.
6. **How do you handle an owner who wants a market figure or a growth rate they cannot source?**
   Why it matters: captures pushback language for invented numbers.
7. **What do you check in a draft plan before it goes out?**
   Why it matters: becomes the send-ready checklist in the expert's words.

## Cash and profit

8. **How do you explain to an owner why they are profitable but out of cash?**
   Why it matters: the most common conversation in the role.
9. **How do you build a cash forecast with an owner who has messy records?**
   Why it matters: small businesses rarely have clean data.
10. **If you use a profit-first style allocation, how do you pick the starting shares, and when do you slow the step-up?**
    Why it matters: the judgement behind the percentages.
11. **How do you work with the owner's accountant on the tax set-aside?**
    Why it matters: defines the handoff on tax.

## Freelancers and service firms

12. **Walk me through a rate calculation you did recently. What did the person get wrong about their billable hours?**
    Why it matters: billable-hours errors drive most underpricing.
13. **How do you turn custom quoting into packages without losing good clients?**
    Why it matters: captures packaging craft.
14. **What contract topics do freelancers most often miss, and what happened as a result?**
    Why it matters: gives the checklist real stories.

## Set-up, hiring and referrals

15. **How do you help an owner choose a structure without giving tax or legal advice?**
    Why it matters: the line between laying out options and ruling.
16. **Which registrations or deadlines do owners in your country miss most often?**
    Why it matters: becomes a local warning list.
17. **How do you check whether an owner is ready for a first employee?**
    Why it matters: the readiness test before an offer goes out.
18. **When do you say "this needs a lawyer" or "this needs an accountant", and how do you prepare the owner for that meeting?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's referral rules.
19. **How do you keep up with rule changes, and which official sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: feeds the refresh list.

## Clients and judgement

20. **Tell me about an owner who wanted the wrong thing (a rosy forecast, a cheaper structure without advice, hiring too soon). How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: the agent needs real pushback language.
21. **Which tools do you use with clients, and what access do you ask for?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with real practice.
22. **Tell me about your hardest client job. What made it hard, and what would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: captures judgement under pressure, the best material for the soul and playbook.
23. **What do new advisors most often get wrong?**
    Why it matters: becomes the common-mistakes list in your voice.
