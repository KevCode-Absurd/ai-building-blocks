# Allocation days and the quarterly check

**Cadence:** twice a month on the owner's two allocation days (for example the 10th and 25th), a weekly forecast update, and a quarterly check.

**Trigger:** the allocation set-up from the skill `cash-flow-forecast-and-profit-allocation` is agreed.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Moving money between accounts, paying tax and taking payouts are always the owner's own actions.

## Steps

### Each week

1. **Update the forecast, L1 then L3 within limits.** Pull the bank export; replace last week's forecast with actuals; roll forward to a new week 13; highlight the lowest week. At L3, update the live forecast file; otherwise share the updated version.
2. **Flag risks, L1.** If the lowest week falls below the next payroll or rent payment, tell the owner the same day with the ranked fixes (collect sooner, delay spend, talk to suppliers, then an adviser).

### Each allocation day

3. **Work out the amounts, L1 then L2.** Income received since the last allocation day; times each share; the amount for each purpose account (profit, owner's pay, tax, running costs). Save the filled worksheet.
4. **Check against the forecast, L1.** Make sure the running-costs account still covers the payments due before the next allocation day. If not, suggest a smaller transfer this time and say why.
5. **The owner moves the money.** I send the worksheet (L3 within limits, or the owner opens it). The owner makes the transfers and ticks them off.

### Each quarter

6. **Quarterly review, L1.** Today's shares against targets; how the running-costs account coped; tax set aside against the accountant's estimate; the profit account balance.
7. **Payout and reserve, L2.** Draft the quarterly note: the share of the profit account to take as a payout and the share to keep as a reserve, with reasons.
8. **Step-up decision, L2.** Recommend whether to step up the shares next quarter, hold, or step back, based on the forecast.
9. **Accountant check, L2.** Draft questions for the accountant: is the tax share still right, and how should the payout be taken?
   **Approval point:** the owner approves the payout, reserve and new shares after the accountant's answer.
10. **Update the plan, L3 within limits.** Update the live allocation sheet with the approved shares and add next quarter's allocation days to the calendar. Log each action.

## Outputs

- Weekly updated 13-week forecast with the lowest week highlighted.
- Filled allocation worksheet for each allocation day.
- Quarterly note: shares against targets, payout and reserve recommendation, step-up decision, accountant questions.
- Updated allocation sheet and calendar reminders.
- Action log for every update made at level 3.

## Needs the owner's approval

- Access levels and limits for each tool.
- Every transfer, payout and tax payment, which the owner makes personally.
- The quarterly payout, reserve and share changes, after the accountant's answer on tax.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits, such as sharing figures with someone new.
- Check this with local rules and ask a qualified accountant about the tax share and how payouts are taken.
