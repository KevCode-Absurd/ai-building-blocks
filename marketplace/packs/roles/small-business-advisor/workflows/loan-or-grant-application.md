# Loan or grant application

**Trigger:** the owner wants to apply for a bank loan, a microloan, a government-backed loan, or a council, government or foundation grant.

**Cadence:** usually two to six weeks, set by the funder's deadline; then follow-up questions from the funder.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Submitting an application to any lender or funder, and any filing with a government body, needs the owner's fresh yes; the owner or their adviser submits through their own login.

## Steps

1. **Intake, L1.** The funder, the amount, the purpose, the deadline, and the funder's guidance notes, eligibility rules, headings, page limits and scoring criteria. Record access levels.
2. **Eligibility check, L1.** Match the business against the funder's published eligibility rules. Anything unclear goes to the funder as a question, drafted at L2 for the owner to send.
   **Approval point 1:** the owner decides whether to apply.
3. **Facts and gaps, L1.** Accounts, bank statements, quotes, forecasts, CVs, licences, letters of support. List gaps and owners for each.
4. **Forecast, L1 then L2 (skill: cash-flow-forecast-and-profit-allocation; prompt: startup-costs-and-break-even).** Monthly or weekly cash forecast covering the loan term or grant period, with the lowest point shown.
5. **Draft the plan, L2 (skill: business-plan-for-lenders-and-grants).** Full plan, then the lender or grant version in the funder's headings and limit, with the assumptions table.
6. **Send-ready check, L1 (prompt: business-plan-send-ready-check).** Mismatches, unsourced figures, missing documents, ranked fixes.
7. **Adviser review, L1 (prompt: accountant-or-lawyer-triage).** Questions on tax treatment of the grant or loan, personal guarantees, security, lease or loan terms. The owner takes them to an accountant or lawyer.
8. **Fix and finalise, L2.** Apply fixes; build the submission pack (plan version, forms, attachments, portal or address, deadline).
   **Approval point 2:** the owner reviews the final pack.
9. **Submit, the owner.** A fresh yes is recorded; the owner or their adviser submits through their own login. I log what was prepared and when.
10. **Follow-up, L1 and L2.** Track the funder's questions and deadlines; draft answers for the owner's yes; prepare for any interview or site visit.
11. **After a decision, L1.** If approved: a checklist of conditions, reporting dates and spending evidence (library: Prepare a grant spending evidence pack). If declined: ask for feedback and note what to change.

## Outputs

- Eligibility note.
- Facts and gaps list.
- Forecast with the lowest point shown.
- Full plan, funder version and one-page summary, with the assumptions table.
- Send-ready check and fix log.
- Adviser questions.
- Submission pack and approval record.
- Follow-up tracker, and a conditions checklist if approved.

## Needs the owner's approval

- Access levels and limits for each tool.
- The decision to apply (approval point 1).
- The final pack (approval point 2).
- A fresh yes before any submission, and before each answer sent to the funder.
- Any personal guarantee, security or signed declaration, which is the owner's own act after advice.
