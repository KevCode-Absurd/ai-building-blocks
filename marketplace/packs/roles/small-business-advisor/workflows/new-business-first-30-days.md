# New business: the first 30 days

**Trigger:** someone is starting a business or going freelance, or has just started and wants the set-up done properly.

**Cadence:** four weeks, with a short check-in each week; then a review at day 30.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the owner's limits). Filing with any government body needs the owner's fresh yes, every time; identity-linked logins stay with the owner or their authorised accountant. Moving money and signing stay with the owner.

## Steps

### Week 1: decide and plan

1. **Intake, L1.** Country and region, what is sold, who buys, expected first-year turnover, income needed, savings available, accountant or lawyer if any. Ask which access level the owner grants for each tool and write it under "Access I have".
2. **One-page plan, L1 (prompt: one-page-business-plan).** What we do, who buys, the numbers that matter, what we need, the next 12 months.
3. **Start-up costs and break-even, L1 (prompt: startup-costs-and-break-even).** One-time and ongoing costs, break-even, cash needed before break-even.
4. **Structure options, L1 (skill: business-setup-and-first-hire-checklist, step 2).** Trade-offs laid out; questions for the accountant or lawyer.
   **Approval point 1:** the owner chooses a structure after speaking to an accountant or lawyer, and approves the plan.

### Week 2: register and set up

5. **Set-up checklist, L1 (skill: business-setup-and-first-hire-checklist).** Registrations, tax IDs, licences, insurance, deadlines, each linked to the official page with the date checked.
6. **Prepare filings, L2.** Completed answer sheets and document lists for each registration, saved for the owner.
   **Approval point 2:** a fresh yes for each filing. The owner, or their accountant as authorised agent, submits through their own login.
7. **Bank and records, the owner and L2.** The owner opens a business account. I draft the record-keeping set-up with the bookkeeper kit's chart of accounts.

### Week 3: money routines

8. **Rates or prices, L1 (skill: freelance-rate-and-service-packages for freelancers).** Rate floor, target rate and packages; for product businesses, price against the break-even.
9. **Cash forecast and allocation, L1 then L2 (skill: cash-flow-forecast-and-profit-allocation).** A first 13-week forecast; allocation shares with the tax share marked for the accountant; the twice-monthly worksheet.
10. **Calendar, L3 within limits.** Add allocation days, tax dates and licence renewals to the owner's calendar.

### Week 4: clients and terms

11. **Contract checklist, L1 (prompt: contract-checklist-and-lawyer-questions).** Topics and lawyer questions; the owner takes them to a lawyer.
12. **Client process, L2 (prompt: client-process-message-templates).** Process map and message templates saved in the owner's tools.
13. **Adviser triage, L1 (prompt: accountant-or-lawyer-triage).** One agenda each for the accountant and the lawyer.

### Day 30 review

14. **Review, L1.** What is registered, what is pending, the first actuals against the forecast, and the next 90 days.

## Outputs

- One-page plan, start-up costs and break-even.
- Structure options with adviser questions.
- Dated set-up checklist and prepared filings.
- Rate worksheet and packages, or a pricing check.
- 13-week cash forecast, allocation sheet and calendar reminders.
- Contract checklist, client process and message templates.
- Adviser agendas.
- Action log of everything done at level 2 and level 3.

## Needs the owner's approval

- Access levels and limits for each tool.
- The structure choice (after advice) and the plan (approval point 1).
- A fresh yes for every government filing (approval point 2); the owner or their authorised accountant submits.
- Bank accounts, money moves and signing, which are the owner's own actions.
- Any quote, proposal or contract sent as binding.
- Anything outside the level 3 limits.
