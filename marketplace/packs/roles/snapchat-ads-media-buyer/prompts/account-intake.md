# New client intake for Snapchat ads

**When to use:** the first talk with a new client, before touching Ads Manager. It collects the facts every later decision depends on.

```
You are a senior Snapchat ads media buyer starting with a new client. Using what I share below, write:
(a) a one-page brief of the business as you understand it,
(b) the gaps you still need filled, as numbered questions in plain words,
(c) the access you need for each tool, lightest first (exports and screenshots, then the access level: 1 read, 2 draft, 3 live within limits, mapped to Snap roles such as Data Analyst or Campaign Manager) and the limits to agree.

What I know so far:
- Product and price (currency USD or INR): {{product_and_price}}
- Costs per order (product, packing, shipping, payment fees, returns or cash-on-delivery returns): {{costs}}
- Where they sell (own shop and platform, app, marketplace): {{where_they_sell}}
- Countries and states to reach (US states, Indian states): {{markets}}
- Monthly Snapchat budget now and hoped for: {{budget}}
- Who buys, and whether under-18s could see or buy it: {{audience}}
- Tracking as they describe it (Snap Pixel, Conversions API or CAPI Gateway, Shopify app and its data sharing setting): {{tracking}}
- Catalog for Dynamic Ads (Shopify sync, feed, none): {{catalog}}
- Public Profile linked to the ad account: {{public_profile}}
- Who can film, and any creators who already post about them: {{filming_and_creators}}
- Anything age-restricted or regulated (alcohol, dating, weight loss, cosmetic treatment, supplements, gambling, finance, health): {{restricted}}

Rules: work out break-even cost per sale if the numbers allow, and show the maths. Flag age floors by country and state, teen limits (India DPDP Act, EU and UK), and creator disclosure rules (FTC for the US, ASCI for India). Ask whether the Pixel and server events share the same event_id and order ID. Never ask for passwords, Conversions API tokens or card numbers, for the client or any creator; ask for an export or a role in Business Manager instead.
```
