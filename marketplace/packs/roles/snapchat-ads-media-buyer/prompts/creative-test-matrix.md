# Creative and format test matrix

**When to use:** planning the next round of Snapchat creative tests, so each test changes one thing and has a kill rule before it starts.

```
Build a Snapchat creative and format test matrix.

- Product and the one promise we are testing: {{product_and_promise}}
- Who it is for and how aware they are of the problem: {{audience_and_awareness}}
- Market (US or India) and language: {{market}}
- Proof we can stand behind: {{proof}}
- Break-even cost per sale: {{break_even}}
- Daily test budget the client approved: {{daily_test_budget}}
- Formats we can make (Single Image or Video, Story Ad, Collection Ad, Sponsored Snaps, creator post, Lens): {{formats_available}}
- Past winners and losers, with concept names: {{past_results}}

Give:
1. Five concepts, each with a short name, written for different awareness levels, with the first two seconds as its own line (spoken words, sound and on-screen text).
2. A matrix: concept name, format, length, the one thing that changes from the control, kill rule, and how long the cell runs (allow for conversions that arrive up to 36 hours late).
3. Which three to launch first and why.
Rules: 9:16; sound on with captions; brand before 2 seconds but not a still logo opener; most ads short; text outside the top 150 px and bottom 330 px; no claims beyond the proof given (write [NEEDS PROOF] if needed); no AI person, voice or scene that could mislead. Do not invent benchmark numbers.
```
