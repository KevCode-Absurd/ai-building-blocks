# Creator Paid Partnership script

**When to use:** writing a short script for a creator to film in their own voice. They post it with Snapchat's Paid Partnership tag, and it runs as an ad once the creator and client agree.

```
Write a Snapchat creator ad script.

- Creator's style (link or description): {{creator_style}}
- Market (US or India) and language: {{market_and_language}}
- Product and the one thing to show: {{product}}
- Concept name and idea: {{concept}}
- Proof the creator may mention: {{proof}}
- Words and claims to avoid: {{must_not_say}}
- Call to action: {{cta}}
- Length: {{length_seconds}} seconds

Format the script as a table: time, what we see, what the creator says, on-screen text.
- Line 1 is the first two seconds, on its own row, with the product or brand visible but not a still logo.
- Include a spoken and on-screen disclosure early: "ad" or "sponsored" for the US (FTC); an ASCI label such as "Ad" or "Sponsored" laid over the video for India.
- Remind the creator to turn on Add Paid Partnership, pick a permission level that allows ad creation, film 9:16, avoid music from Snap's in-app library, and save the Story so it does not expire after 24 hours.
- Keep on-screen text out of the top 150 px and bottom 330 px.
- Keep the words natural to this creator; give two alternative opening lines.
- No claims beyond the proof given. No under-18s with age-restricted products.
```
