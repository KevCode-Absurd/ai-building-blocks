# Interview: pulling out a Snapchat ads media buyer's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("can you give me a real example?"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour in a Snapchat ad account you have never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you work out what a store can afford per sale, in the US and in India?**
   Why: captures the maths behind every budget call, including returns and cash on delivery.
3. **How do you check the Snap Pixel and Conversions API, and how have you caught double counting?**
   Why: tracking habits protect every later decision.
4. **What have you learned about Shopify stores on Snapchat that the help pages don't say?**
   Why: most store clients run Shopify, and its settings trip people up.
5. **When do you add Dynamic Ads, and what do you fix in the catalog first?**
   Why: records your judgement on catalog-driven ads.
6. **When do you pick Auto-Bid, Target Cost or Max Bid? What made you change your mind last time?**
   Why: bid choice shapes delivery and cost.
7. **How long do you wait before judging a new ad, and why?**
   Why: late conversions make early calls risky on Snapchat.
8. **How do you open a Snapchat ad? Show me three openings you are proud of and why they worked.**
   Why: the first two seconds are the core craft of this role.
9. **When do you use Story Ads, Collection Ads, Sponsored Snaps or Lenses instead of a single video?**
   Why: format choice is a Snapchat-specific skill.
10. **How do you name concepts and keep a test log?**
    Why: a good log is how an account learns.
11. **How do you set kill rules before a test, and what do you do when a client wants to break them?**
    Why: separates disciplined testing from gut calls.
12. **What do you look at first when cost per sale doubles overnight?**
    Why: your triage order becomes the agent's.
13. **How do you explain Snapchat's 28-day window to an owner who compares it with Meta?**
    Why: shapes honest reporting.
14. **How do you find and brief creators, and how do you handle Paid Partnership and disclosure in the US and India?**
    Why: protects clients and creators.
15. **How do you plan ads for age-restricted products, or products teens might buy?**
    Why: age floors and teen rules are where accounts get into trouble.
16. **Tell me about an ad or account that got rejected or restricted. What changed?**
    Why: policy experience protects clients.
17. **How do you report to an owner who only looks at sales in their shop?**
    Why: shapes the agent's report voice.
18. **Tell me about the hardest Snapchat account you have worked on.**
    Why: a real story is worth more than any checklist.
19. **What do you check before you press launch?**
    Why: becomes the agent's quality bar.
