# Weekly review routine

**Cadence:** once a week, same day, plus a quick mid-week look at spend, rejections and catalog sync. About 45 to 60 minutes for a small account.

**Goal:** keep the account learning, stop losers by the agreed rules, move budget to winners within the limits, and keep the next concepts coming.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 4 need level 1. Step 5 can use level 2. Step 7 needs level 3.

1. **Pull the data** (tool: Ads Manager export, last 14 days by day and ad, attribution window named). Pull shop orders for the same dates.
2. **Trust check** (skill: `snapchat-creative-test-and-weekly-review`, step 1). Snap against the shop by day, leaving out the last two days. If tracking broke, stop and raise it, and run `snap-pixel-capi-and-catalog-check`.
3. **Read and call each ad** (same skill, steps 2 to 4). Apply kill rules as written; diagnose with the funnel.
4. **Rules pulse.** Any rejected ads, account warnings, catalog errors or out-of-stock winners, creator posts that need saving or renewing, and age floors on any new targeting.
5. **Plan the next test** (prompt: `creative-test-matrix`; level 2 to build paused ads). One change per test, named concepts, kill rules.
6. **Send the weekly note and action list** (prompt: `change-request-for-approval`). Lead with the one ad to scale and the one to stop.
7. **Make the changes** (level 3; tool: Ads Manager). Only items inside the limits or with a yes. Log each with date, item number, result, and the yes or limit that covered it.
8. **Update the test log.** Every result and decision, dated.

## Outputs

- Weekly note (one page) led by the ad to scale and the ad to stop.
- Updated test log and change log.
- Next test plan and any creator scripts.

## Approval

**At level 3, inside the limits confirmed at the start (no new yes each week):** pausing ads that hit their kill rule, budget steps within the cap and step size, launching concepts from the approved test plan, and swapping in approved creative.

**Always needs a new yes:** spend above the cap, a new campaign objective or ad account, a new audience from a customer list, a creator's post as an ad, creator fees, a new country or state for a restricted product, deleting anything, and any rejected ad or account warning.

Reading data, writing notes and drafting tests need no approval.
