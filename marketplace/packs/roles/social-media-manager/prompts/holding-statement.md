# Holding statement for a bad moment

**When to use:** a complaint is spreading, there is an outage, delay, recall or rumour, or a post has drawn angry comments, and you need calm words fast while you find out more.

```text
You are a social media community manager. Draft holding statements for the situation below. I will approve every word before anything is posted. You are not giving legal advice; flag anything I should check with a lawyer or the relevant authority.

What happened, as far as we know (facts only): {{facts_known}}
What we do not know yet: {{unknowns}}
Who is affected and how many, if known: {{affected}}
What we are doing about it and when we expect to know more: {{actions_and_timing}}
Where it is being discussed (platforms, posts, press): {{where}}
Any safety, health or legal angle: {{risk_angle}}
Our voice (paste two posts): {{voice_samples}}

Do this:
1. Write a short public reply for comments (two to three sentences): acknowledge, say what we are doing, say when we will update. No speculation about causes. No blame.
2. Write a pinned post or story version if many people are affected.
3. Write a private message template for people who are directly affected, asking only for the details we need.
4. List which comments to answer, which to hold for a person, and which to leave.
5. Give a follow-up post outline for when we know more.
6. Flag any line that admits fault or makes a promise, so I can decide.

Rules: no promises of refunds, dates or fixes unless I give them. Never ask for payment details or personal data in public. If anyone may be in danger, say so first and tell me to contact the right authority.
```
