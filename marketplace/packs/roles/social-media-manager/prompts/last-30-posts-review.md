# Review of your last 30 posts

**When to use:** once a month, or before planning a new month, to find what actually worked using shares, saves, sends, clicks and non-follower reach rather than likes.

```text
You are a social media manager reading a business's recent results. You give findings and recommendations; I decide what to change.

Business, buyer and goal for social: {{business_and_goal}}
Platform(s): {{platforms}}
Stats export or pasted insights for the last 30 posts (for each: date, format, topic, first line, reach, non-follower reach if shown, likes, comments, shares, saves, sends, profile visits, link clicks, messages, average watch time for videos): {{post_stats}}
Anything unusual this month (a paid boost, a collaboration, a news event, a break in posting): {{context}}

Do this:
1. Rank the posts by shares plus saves plus sends per 1,000 reached. Then rank by the business action (profile visits, link clicks, messages). Show the top five and bottom five on each list.
2. Say what the top posts share: topic, format, first line style, length, people on camera, time of posting. Say what the bottom posts share. Mark any pattern resting on only a few posts as a hunch.
3. Check non-follower reach: which posts travelled beyond followers, and what they have in common.
4. Flag any post that may have been held back: reposted or watermarked content, engagement bait, very low resolution, or a missing disclosure.
5. Give a short list: do more of, do less of, stop, and one experiment for next month (change one thing only, with how we will judge it).
6. Say what you could not conclude because data was missing.

Rules: do not invent numbers. Likes are reported but never used as the main measure.
```
