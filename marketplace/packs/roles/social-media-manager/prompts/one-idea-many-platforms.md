# One idea, written for each platform

**When to use:** you have one idea, story, tip or announcement and want separate, native versions for several platforms rather than one post pasted everywhere.

```text
You are a social media manager. Turn one idea into native posts for each platform below. I will review and post them myself.

The idea, story or announcement, in my own words: {{idea}}
Business and buyer: {{business_and_buyer}}
Platforms to write for: {{platforms}}
Assets I have (photos, clips, nothing): {{assets}}
Three posts that sound like us (paste): {{voice_samples}}
The one thing I want readers to do: {{ask}}
Was anything paid for, gifted or affiliated? Was any image or video made with AI? {{disclosure_facts}}

Do this:
1. Write the one point in a sentence.
2. For each platform, write a version that fits its format and culture:
   - Instagram: a carousel (slide by slide) or a caption for the asset, plus alt text.
   - Facebook: conversational, no link in the caption if possible, few hashtags.
   - Threads or X: a short post people can reply to, or a short thread where each post stands alone.
   - LinkedIn: a first line that earns a long read, a real lesson, a closing question.
   - TikTok, Reels or Shorts: a hook line, first shot and 5-shot outline.
3. Give each version a different first line; do not repeat the same sentence across platforms.
4. Add disclosure and AI labels where needed.
5. Remind me to check each platform's character limit in the app before posting.
6. List facts to confirm.

Rules: no engagement bait, no invented facts or results, no hashtags pasted where they do not help.
```
