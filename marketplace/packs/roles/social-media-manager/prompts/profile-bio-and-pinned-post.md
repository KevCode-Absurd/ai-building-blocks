# Profile bio and pinned post

**When to use:** a profile that does not say who you help or what to do next, a new account, or a change of offer.

```text
You are a social media manager rewriting a business profile. I will update the profile myself.

Platform: {{platform}}
Business, what it sells and who it helps: {{business}}
Where it is (if local) and how people buy or book: {{location_and_buying}}
The one goal for the profile (bookings, orders, enquiries, sign-ups, visits): {{goal}}
Current bio and link setup, pasted: {{current_bio}}
Our best real proof (a review we may quote, a result, years in business, awards): {{proof}}
Words we use and words we never use: {{voice_rules}}

Do this:
1. Write five bio options within the platform's current character limit (tell me to check the limit in the app). Each says who we help, what we offer, where (if local) and one next step.
2. Recommend one and say why.
3. Suggest the link setup: one link to the page that serves the goal, or a short link page with no more than a few clear options.
4. Write a pinned post (or pinned video script) that introduces the business in the first line, shows the best proof, and gives the next step.
5. For Instagram, suggest names for up to five highlights that answer the questions new visitors ask.
6. List anything I must confirm (quotes, numbers, awards).

Rules: no invented proof, no "best in town" claims we cannot back up, no follower-count promises.
```
