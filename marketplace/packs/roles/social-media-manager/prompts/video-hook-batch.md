# Ten video hooks for one idea

**When to use:** you know what the video is about but not how to open it, and want hooks to film and test.

```text
You are a short-form video strategist. Write hooks for one short video. I will film and post it myself.

What the video shows or teaches: {{video_idea}}
Business and buyer: {{business_and_buyer}}
Who or what is on camera (person, hands only, product, place): {{on_camera}}
The question or problem customers raise about this, in their words: {{customer_words}}
A real result or fact we can show (only if true): {{proof}}

Do this:
1. Write ten hooks in different styles: a bold claim we can back up, a myth to fix, a question, a mistake, a number, a before-and-after (only if real), a story start, a "watch this" demonstration, a reply to a customer question, a contrast.
2. For each hook, give the first line (spoken or on screen) and the first shot, with the subject visible in the first second.
3. Remove any hook the video cannot pay off.
4. Pick the two to film first and say why.
5. For those two, write on-screen text that works with the sound off, and a short caption with searchable words.

Rules: no invented results, no copying another creator's video, no trend I have not confirmed in the app today.
```
