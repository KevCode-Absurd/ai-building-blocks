# Platform-native posts

The same idea needs a different shape on each platform. A carousel teaches step by step; a LinkedIn post earns a long read; a Threads post invites replies; an X post has to work in a few lines. This skill writes posts that fit the platform, sound like the business and get the reader to do one thing. The client posts them, or the agent does at level 3 within the agreed limits.

## When to use

- A batch of captions for photos or clips the client has.
- A carousel from a tip, process or list.
- A week of short text posts or a thread from one idea or a blog post.
- A profile bio and pinned post.
- One announcement written separately for several platforms.

## What you need

- **The material:** photos or clips described in words (or the client's own description), notes, a blog post, a voice memo transcript.
- **Voice:** three posts the client likes and any words to avoid.
- **The goal and the ask** for each post or batch.
- **Facts:** prices, dates, product details, results. Only what the client confirms.
- **Relationships:** whether any product was gifted, paid for or has an affiliate link.
- **AI use:** whether any image or video was made or changed with AI in a way that looks real.
- **Platforms** and any brand rules (emoji use, hashtags, words never used).

**Access:** level 1 to read past posts and insights; level 2 to save the posts as drafts in your scheduler; level 3 to schedule or publish the approved posts, within your limits.

## Steps

1. **Find the one point.** Write it in one sentence before drafting. If there are two points, make two posts.
2. **Write the first line.** It must work alone, because most people see only that line. Options: a specific claim ("Most bread isn't stale. It's thirsty."), a question the reader is already asking, a surprising number the client can back up, or the start of a story. Write three options and pick one.
3. **Shape it for the platform.**
   - *Instagram caption:* the first line adds to the image, then a short tip or story, then one ask (save, send, comment with a real question, tap the link in bio). Words people would search for, used naturally. Few, specific hashtags if any; check the current limit in the app.
   - *Instagram carousel:* slide 1 promises a clear gain; one idea per slide in large text; the last slide gives one action; the caption adds context rather than repeating the slides.
   - *Facebook:* conversational, no link in the caption where possible, minimal capitals, few hashtags (Meta's own advice is no more than five).
   - *Threads and X:* short, one thought, something people can answer or argue with in good faith; for a thread, each post works alone and the first earns the second. Check the character limit in the app.
   - *LinkedIn:* a first line that earns "see more", short paragraphs, a real lesson from the business's work, a closing question worth answering; 3 to 5 relevant hashtags at most on Page posts.
   - *TikTok and YouTube Shorts captions:* short, searchable words for what the video shows; the video does the work.
   - *Bio and pinned post:* who you help, what you offer, where you are (if local), and one next step; the pinned post repeats the best proof and the next step.
4. **Write the ask.** One per post. Prefer asks that reflect real value (save, send to a friend who needs it, ask a question, book) over bait ("comment YES").
5. **Add the extras.** Alt text describing what matters in each image. Captions on video. Disclosure for any paid, gifted or affiliate post, in the first lines and with the platform's label. AI disclosure where realistic AI content is used.
6. **Vary the batch.** Mix tip, story, opinion, question, proof and offer, so the feed does not repeat itself.
7. **Mark every fact to confirm** and anything that needs permission (a customer's photo or words).

## Checks

- One point and one ask per post.
- First line stands alone and is specific to this business.
- Platform format respected; limits checked in the app on the day.
- No engagement bait, no fake urgency, no invented results, reviews or quotes.
- Disclosure and AI labels where needed.
- Alt text and video captions included.
- Hashtags few, specific and relevant; none pasted onto platforms where they do not help.
- Sounds like the samples, not like an ad.
- Facts and permissions marked for the client.

## Example of good output (short)

*Illustrative structure, not a real business: a family-run garden centre with a Saturday plant clinic; one idea, written for Instagram and Threads. Copy the shape, never the content: every fact comes from the client's own context.*

**Point:** most houseplants that die indoors get too much water, not too little.

**Instagram carousel**

- Slide 1: "Your houseplant isn't thirsty. It's drowning."
- Slide 2: "Push a finger into the soil. Damp at your first knuckle? Don't water yet."
- Slide 3: "No hole in the bottom of the pot? Water has nowhere to go."
- Slide 4: "Winter means slower growth. Water less than you did in summer."
- Slide 5: "Bring a leaf to our Saturday plant clinic and we'll take a look (client to confirm times)."

Caption first line: "Save this before you reach for the watering can again." Alt text, slide 1: "Text slide on a green background: 'Your houseplant isn't thirsty. It's drowning.'"

**Threads post**

"Unpopular opinion from a garden centre: the kindest thing you can do for most houseplants this winter is forget to water them for a week.

What's the plant you've killed with kindness?"

*Why this works:* one point, a first line only this business would write, a real reason to save or reply, and the one fact that needs confirming is marked.
