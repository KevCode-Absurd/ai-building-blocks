# Social content plan

A plan decides what to post, where, how often and why, before anyone writes a caption. Its real test is whether the business is still posting in month three. This skill builds the plan from the client's goals, time and past results. Who posts depends on the access level the client grants: the client, or the agent at level 3 within the agreed limits.

## When to use

- The business posts in bursts, then goes quiet.
- The owner does not know what to post, or only posts offers.
- Starting on a new platform, or dropping one that is not working.
- A month or quarter ahead, using the last 30 posts' results.

## What you need

- **Goal for the next 90 days:** bookings, enquiries, orders, sign-ups, event attendance, or local awareness.
- **The buyer:** who they are and where they already spend time online. If unsure, ask three recent customers.
- **Time and people:** hours a week the owner or team can give, who is comfortable on camera, and what can be filmed or photographed in about 30 minutes a week.
- **Current accounts:** platforms, follower counts, and a stats export or screenshots of the last 30 posts (reach, non-follower reach, shares, saves, sends, profile visits, link clicks, messages).
- **Voice:** three posts the owner likes (theirs or others') and words they never want to sound like.
- **Real dates and offers:** launches, events, seasons, closures. Only real ones.
- **Rules:** anything that cannot be shown (customers' faces, prices, medical claims), and who approves posts.

**Access:** level 1 to read insights and past posts; level 2 to load the calendar into your scheduler as drafts; level 3 to schedule approved posts, within your limits.

## Steps

1. **Pick the platforms.** One to three, chosen by where the buyers are and what the business can make. A café with a photogenic counter and a chatty owner fits Instagram and TikTok; a B2B consultant fits LinkedIn, where posts are matched to members by topic and can reach people outside the network. Say why each platform is in or out. Steady posting on fewer platforms beats thin posting on many.
2. **Set two to four pillars, each with a job.**
   - *Attract:* useful or entertaining for strangers (tips, how-tos, answers to common questions).
   - *Teach:* deeper help for people already following.
   - *Prove:* real results, reviews used with permission, behind the scenes, the people and process.
   - *Sell:* offers, launches, how to buy. Keep these a minority of posts.
   Give each pillar three example topics from the business's own work.
3. **Fit formats to the platform and the owner.** Short video for Reels, TikTok and Shorts if someone will appear or film hands and process; carousels for step-by-step and lists; text posts on LinkedIn, Threads and X; photo posts for products and places. Original content only: what the business films, photographs or writes.
4. **Set the rhythm.** A weekly pattern the owner can keep, for example two short videos and one carousel a week plus a few stories, or two LinkedIn posts a week. Base it on the hours available, not on a generic "post daily" rule. Plan one batch session for video.
5. **Use past results.** From the stats export, rank the last 30 posts by shares, saves, sends, profile visits, clicks and non-follower reach, not likes. Note what the top posts share (topic, format, first line, length) and plan more of it.
6. **Lay out 30 days.** For each post: date, platform, pillar, format, hook (first line or first seconds), the one point, the ask, the asset needed (photo, clip, slide design), and who makes it.
7. **Plan the community time.** When replies will be done each day or week, and who handles risky messages.
8. **Add checks.** Disclosure for any gifted, paid or affiliate post; AI disclosure for realistic AI-made visuals; no engagement bait; offers and dates confirmed.
9. **Set measures.** One main measure per pillar (attract: non-follower reach and shares; teach: saves; prove: profile visits and messages; sell: clicks and enquiries) and a monthly review date.

## Checks

- Platforms chosen for the buyer, not for fashion, with a reason each.
- Every pillar has a job and real example topics.
- The rhythm fits the hours the client said they have.
- Every post has one point and one ask.
- Offers are a minority and every offer is marked "client to confirm".
- No reposted or watermarked content planned; any third-party material has permission and real added value.
- Disclosure and AI labelling are planned where needed.
- Measures go beyond likes.
- The plan says clearly who posts or schedules: the client, or the agent at level 3 within the agreed limits.

## Output format

1. **Summary:** goal, platforms and why, pillars, weekly rhythm, first batch session date.
2. **Pillar table:** pillar, job, example topics, formats, main measure.
3. **What worked before:** top and bottom posts and the pattern.
4. **30-day calendar:** date, platform, pillar, format, hook, point, ask, asset, owner.
5. **Batch shoot list** for the month's video.
6. **Community plan:** reply times, escalation contact.
7. **Questions and facts to confirm.**

## Example of good output (short)

*Copper Kettle Bakery is a made-up business used only to show the format: a neighbourhood bakery with one owner who will film hands and ovens but not her face. All details are invented.*

**Summary:** goal is more pre-orders for weekend loaves. Platforms: Instagram (local customers already tag the shop) and TikTok (process videos suit the oven); Facebook kept for the events group only. Pillars: process (attract), baking tips (teach), regulars and reviews (prove), pre-orders (sell). Rhythm: two short process videos, one carousel and daily stories a week, filmed in one Thursday-afternoon session.

**Pillars (extract)**

| Pillar | Job | Example topics | Format | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Process | Attract | Shaping 40 loaves at 5am; the first cut of a sourdough | Short video | Non-follower reach, shares |
| Tips | Teach | How to revive a day-old loaf; storing bread without plastic | Carousel | Saves |
| Pre-orders | Sell | This weekend's loaves and how to order (client to confirm) | Story and post | Link clicks, orders |

**Calendar (extract)**

| Date | Platform | Pillar | Format | Hook | Point | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 3 | Instagram, TikTok | Process | Short video | "This dough has been resting since Sunday." | Why slow loaves taste different | Follow for Thursday's bake |
| Thu 5 | Instagram | Tips | Carousel | "Your bread isn't stale. It's thirsty." | Three ways to revive a loaf | Save for the weekend |
| Fri 6 | Instagram | Pre-orders | Story | "Saturday loaves: 3 left to pre-order" (only if true) | What is baking and how to order | Tap to pre-order |

*Why this works:* every post is something only this bakery can make, the rhythm fits one filming session a week, and only one post in four asks for a sale.
