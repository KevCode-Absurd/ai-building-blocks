# Interview: social media manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work (for example, video questions for a community manager).

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through the first two weeks with a new client, from the first call to the first post you hand over.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you decide which platforms a business should be on, and which to drop?**
   Why it matters: platform choice is the biggest decision a small business gets wrong.
3. **How do you set content pillars? Show me a real set and the job each one does.**
   Why it matters: becomes the planning method in the agent's playbook.
4. **How do you size a posting rhythm to an owner's real week?**
   Why it matters: plans that do not fit the owner's time stop within weeks.
5. **How do you learn a brand's voice, and how do you check you have it right?**
   Why it matters: voice is what separates the expert from generic AI captions.

## Writing and video

6. **How do you write a first line or a hook? Show me three you are proud of and why they worked.**
   Why it matters: hooks are where generic AI writing is easiest to spot.
7. **How do you turn one idea into posts for different platforms without repeating yourself?**
   Why it matters: captures platform-native judgement.
8. **How do you plan a batch shoot so one session covers weeks of video?**
   Why it matters: the batching method is a reusable skill for small teams.
9. **What do you do with an owner who will not appear on camera?**
   Why it matters: a common constraint with many good answers.
10. **How do you keep up with platform changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent where to check facts at each refresh.

## Measuring

11. **Which numbers do you report to clients, and which do you ignore?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's reporting standard beyond likes.
12. **Tell me about a post that did far better or worse than you expected. What did you learn?**
    Why it matters: captures lessons that change future work.
13. **How do you tell whether a drop in reach is the platform, the content or something wrong with the account?**
    Why it matters: diagnosis skill that most owners lack.

## Community and honesty

14. **How do you decide which comments to answer, which to take private and which to hold for the owner?**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's triage rules.
15. **Tell me about the worst comment thread or bad moment you have handled. What did you post first?**
    Why it matters: real crisis stories teach calm, safe wording.
16. **A client wants to buy followers or join an engagement pod. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: tests the expert's ethical line and how they hold it kindly.
17. **How do you handle gifted products, paid creators and affiliate links in posts?**
    Why it matters: checks disclosure practice in the regions the expert works in.
18. **Where do you draw the line with AI-made images or video in a client's posts?**
    Why it matters: AI labelling rules are new and practice varies.

## Tools and clients

19. **Which tools do you use for planning, scheduling, design and insights, and what do you ask clients to share?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md with what really works.
20. **What do you check before you hand a post or reply to a client? Give me your actual list.**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality checks.
21. **Tell me about a client who wanted the wrong thing. How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: reveals judgement and values for soul.md.
