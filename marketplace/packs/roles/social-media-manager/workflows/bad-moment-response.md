# Bad moment response

**Trigger:** a complaint or angry thread is spreading, a product problem or outage affects customers, a rumour or false claim is circulating, a post draws a pile-on, or press or an influencer picks up a story.

**Cadence:** starts the hour the client asks; check-ins as often as the situation needs until comments return to normal.

## Steps

A bad moment is outside any routine limits, so every public word needs a fresh yes, even at level 3.

1. **Stop the scheduled posts.** Pause scheduled promotional posts and ads on the affected accounts, so cheerful posts do not land in the middle of a problem. At level 3, I pause the scheduled posts as soon as the client says yes, and log it. Otherwise the client pauses them. Ads stay with whoever runs them.
2. **Get the facts (prompt: holding-statement, inputs).** What happened, what is known and not known, who is affected, what the business is doing and when it will know more. If anyone may be in danger, the client contacts the right authority first.
3. **Draft holding statements (prompt: holding-statement).** A short public reply, a pinned post or story if many people are affected, and a private message template. No speculation, no blame, no promises the client has not made.
   **Approval point 1:** the client (and their adviser, if legal, safety or health issues are involved) approves every word.
4. **Sort the comments (skill: comment-and-message-replies, step 1).** Which to answer with the holding reply, which to take private, which to hold for the owner, which to hide or report under the community policy (spam, abuse, personal data). Do not delete honest criticism.
5. **Post and reply.** At level 3, I post the approved words and send the approved replies, and log each one. At level 1 or 2, the client posts and replies.
6. **Update when facts change.** Draft follow-up posts as the business learns more: what happened, what was done, what changes. The client approves each one; it is then posted by the client, or by me at level 3.
   **Approval point 2:** each update is approved before posting.
7. **Close and learn.** When comments are back to normal, write a short review: what happened, how fast replies went out, what worked, what to change in the policy, fact sheet and escalation list.

## Outputs

- Holding statements (public, pinned, private).
- A comment triage table.
- Follow-up post drafts.
- A short after-action review with policy updates.

## Approval

- Every public word (points 1 and 2) and every private message, even at level 3.
- Pausing or resuming scheduled posts and ads.
- Any hiding, deleting or reporting of comments.
- Any statement involving legal, safety, health or press matters, which should also go to a qualified adviser.
