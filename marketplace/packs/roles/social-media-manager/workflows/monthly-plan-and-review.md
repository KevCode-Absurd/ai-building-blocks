# Monthly plan and review

**Cadence:** once a month, in the last week of the month. Every third month, re-check platform choice and pillars.

**Trigger:** the month is ending, or the client wants to start posting steadily.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 4, 6 and 7 need level 1 (read) or none. Step 5 needs level 3 if I make the change.

1. **Read the month (prompt: last-30-posts-review).** Rank the last 30 posts by shares, saves and sends per 1,000 reached and by business actions. Name the patterns, the posts that travelled to non-followers, and anything that may have been held back (reposts, watermarks, bait, missing disclosure).
2. **Check the platforms' own news.** Before planning, re-read the official pages listed in knowledge.md "What changes fast" for any change to formats, limits, originality rules or disclosure tools since the last refresh. Note anything that changes the plan, with its date and source.
3. **Re-plan (skill: social-content-plan).** Keep what worked, drop what did not, and change one thing to test. Lay out the next 30 days with real dates and offers from the client.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves platforms, pillars, rhythm and the calendar.
4. **Plan the batch shoot (skill: short-video-scripts, step 7).** One session for the month's video, grouped by location and props.
5. **Update the profile if needed (prompt: profile-bio-and-pinned-post)** when the offer, goal or best proof changes.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves any bio, link or pinned post change. At level 3, I make the approved change and log it; otherwise the client makes it.
6. **Update the reply fact sheet and policy (skill: comment-and-message-replies, steps 7 and 8)** from the month's repeat questions and complaints.
7. **Pass on business signals.** Repeat complaints and questions go to the owner as a short list, because some problems are not social media problems.

## Outputs

- Monthly results read with five recommendations.
- Notes on platform changes since last month, with sources and dates.
- Next month's calendar and batch shoot plan.
- Profile changes, if any.
- Updated fact sheet and community policy.
- A short list of business issues raised by customers.

## Approval

- The plan and calendar (point 1).
- Any profile, link or pinned post change (point 2), every time.
- Posts and replies follow the weekly cycle: within the level 3 limits once approved, otherwise the client posts.
