# Weekly social cycle

**Cadence:** every week, on a fixed day (for example Thursday for the following week). Replies run daily or on the client's chosen schedule.

**Trigger:** the client has an agreed content plan (or wants one) and posts at least once a week on one or more platforms.

## Steps

Steps 1 and 2 need level 1 (read). Steps 3 and 7 can use level 2, and step 6 and sending replies need level 3.

1. **Check last week (prompt: last-30-posts-review, run on last week's posts).** Read insights at level 1, or the client shares screenshots or an export. Note the best and worst posts by shares, saves, sends, clicks and non-follower reach, and any comment themes.
2. **Confirm next week's slots (skill: social-content-plan, step 6).** Pull next week's rows from the monthly calendar. Swap in anything the client has learned: news, stock, events, a question customers kept asking.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms topics, offers and dates for the week.
3. **Draft the posts (skill: platform-native-posts; prompt: one-idea-many-platforms for cross-platform ideas).** First line, full text, slide copy, alt text, hashtags if useful, disclosure, ask, facts to confirm. At level 2 or 3, I save them as drafts in the scheduler.
4. **Script the videos (skill: short-video-scripts; prompt: video-hook-batch).** Hooks, shot lists, on-screen text and captions. If footage already exists, write edit notes from the client's description or transcript.
5. **Run the honesty check (prompt: disclosure-and-claims-check)** on anything paid, gifted, affiliated, AI-made, or making a claim.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves final copy and scripts.
6. **Post or schedule** (level 3) in the platform or a scheduling tool the client controls (for example Meta Business Suite, Buffer, Hootsuite or Later). I schedule exactly the approved posts on the approved dates and log each one. At level 1 or 2, the client posts or schedules.
7. **Replies (skill: comment-and-message-replies).** I read new comments and messages at level 1, or the client pastes them, and return a reply table with risky items held. At level 2, replies wait as drafts. At level 3, I send the routine replies the limits cover and log them; held items go to the client.
8. **Log the week.** One line per post: date, platform, pillar, format, first line, shares, saves, sends, clicks, messages, lesson.

## Outputs

- A short read of last week.
- The week's posts and video scripts, with facts to confirm, ready to post or saved as drafts.
- Completed honesty check.
- Reply drafts with held items and holding replies.
- Updated post log and action log.

## Approval

- Topics, offers and dates for the week (point 1).
- Final copy, scripts and disclosures (point 2). At level 3, this is the action list: I post exactly what was approved, where and when.
- Replies: at level 3, routine replies from the approved fact sheet go out within the limits; every held item, complaint and refund question needs the client.
- Always a new yes: any post not on the list, any change to profiles, links, ads or account settings, deleting posts or comments, and paid boosts. At level 1 or 2, the client posts, schedules and sends every reply.
