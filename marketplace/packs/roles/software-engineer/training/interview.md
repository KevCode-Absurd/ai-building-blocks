# Training interview: software engineer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, private code and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe systems in general terms.

1. **Which languages, frameworks and kinds of codebases do you know best, and what do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **Walk me through the hardest bug you have found. How did you find it?**
   Why it matters: reveals the expert's real debugging sequence and instincts.

3. **What is the first thing you do with a stack trace or error log?**
   Why it matters: captures the reading order the agent should copy.

4. **How do you reproduce a bug that only happens sometimes?**
   Why it matters: records techniques for timing, data and environment bugs.

5. **When do you reach for `git bisect`, a debugger, or more logging?**
   Why it matters: grounds tool choice in practice.

6. **Tell me about a fix that made things worse. What did you learn?**
   Why it matters: surfaces checks the expert now always runs.

7. **What do you look at first in a code review, and what do you deliberately ignore?**
   Why it matters: sets the review priorities and avoids style noise.

8. **How do you word a blocking comment so the author does not get defensive?**
   Why it matters: captures tone and phrasing for reviews.

9. **What mistakes do you see most in AI-written code?**
   Why it matters: tunes the agent's AI-code checks to real failures.

10. **How do you get untested legacy code under test before changing it?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's seams and characterisation test habits.

11. **Tell me about a refactor that changed behaviour by accident. How was it caught, or not?**
    Why it matters: shows where safety nets fail.

12. **How do you plan a framework upgrade that is several major versions behind?**
    Why it matters: grounds the upgrade plan in real steps and blockers.

13. **What do you do when a key package has no release for the version you need?**
    Why it matters: records options such as replacing, forking or waiting.

14. **How do you write a regex you trust, and when do you refuse to use one?**
    Why it matters: sets testing habits and the limits of patterns for validation.

15. **How do you explain an unfamiliar codebase to a new developer, and to a non-technical owner?**
    Why it matters: captures the map and onboarding style for two audiences.

16. **What signs tell you a codebase is in trouble?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a risk checklist for health checks.

17. **How small should a pull request be, and how do you split a big one?**
    Why it matters: sets practical size and splitting rules.

18. **What access do you accept from clients, and what do you refuse?**
    Why it matters: feeds access.md and the safety rules in the expert's own words.

19. **Tell me about a time you disagreed with a client or tech lead about a technical choice. What did you do?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert disagrees and records decisions.

20. **How do you decide when a quick fix is acceptable and when the real fix must happen now?**
    Why it matters: captures the judgement between speed and safety.

21. **How do you keep up with language and framework changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: sets refresh habits and trusted sources for this agent.
