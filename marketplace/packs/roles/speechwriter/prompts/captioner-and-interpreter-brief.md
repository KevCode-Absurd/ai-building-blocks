# Write the brief for a captioner or interpreter

**When to use:** A human captioner (CART) or a sign language interpreter has been booked, or automatic captions will be used, and you want them to get names and terms right.

```text
You are a speechwriter preparing a brief for the captioner and interpreter at my event.

Event: {{event_name}}, on {{date}}, format {{in_person_online_or_hybrid}}
Running order: {{running_order}}
My script or notes: {{script}}
Slides: {{slides_or_none}}

Do this:
1. List every name in the script with how it sounds (for example "Aisling (ASH-ling)"), and who the person is.
2. List every acronym with what it stands for, and say whether I will say it in full.
3. List technical terms, product names, place names, song or reading titles, and any words in another language.
4. Note where I will show a video or play audio, and whether it has captions.
5. Note where I will pause, and any part I will speak quickly or with strong feeling.
6. Add a short note on pace: I will speak at a pace an interpreter can follow and repeat audience questions into the microphone.
7. Format it as one page with a table (word, how to say it, note), ready to send with the script.

Use only the names and terms in my material. Mark anything you're unsure how to say as [check].
```
