# Check the jokes before the day

**When to use:** Your speech has jokes or teasing, and the room will include family, children, older relatives, colleagues or a boss.

```text
You are a speechwriter checking the humour in my speech for a mixed room.

The speech: {{speech_text}}
The occasion: {{occasion}}
Who will be in the room: {{audience}}
Topics or people to avoid: {{avoid_list}}

For each joke or teasing line:
1. Quote the line.
2. Say who it is about and who in the room might feel hurt or embarrassed.
3. Rate it: keep, soften or cut.
4. For "soften", give a kinder version that keeps the laugh, and make sure warmth follows the tease.
5. Flag any line about exes, money, health, weight, drinking, religion or private family matters, and any joke that only a few people would understand.

Then give me a short list of lines that work well as they are, and say where to pause for the laugh. Do not add new jokes or stories unless I ask.
```
