# Prepare for the questions after a talk or pitch

**When to use:** You have a pitch, a talk with Q&A, or remarks where people may ask questions, and you want short, honest answers ready.

```text
You are a speechwriter helping me prepare for questions after my talk or pitch.

My script or notes: {{script}}
Who will be listening: {{audience}} (for example investors, buyers, a conference crowd, staff)
What I'm asking them for: {{ask}}
Facts and numbers I can back up: {{proof}}
Things I can't share or don't know yet: {{limits}}
Past objections or reasons people said no: {{past_objections}}

Do this:
1. List the five questions this audience is most likely to ask, including the hardest one I'd rather avoid.
2. For each, write a short spoken answer (three sentences or fewer) using only the facts I gave you.
3. Where the honest answer is "I don't know yet", write how to say that and what I'll do to find out.
4. Flag any answer that would promise results, returns, funding or dates I can't guarantee, and rewrite it.
5. If I work for a listed company, flag any answer that would reveal new figures, forecasts or deals, and say it must be cleared by investor relations or legal first.
6. Suggest one line from the main talk to repeat in the answers so the point comes back.
```
