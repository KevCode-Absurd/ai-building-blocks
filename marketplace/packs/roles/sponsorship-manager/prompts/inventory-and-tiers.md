# Inventory and tiers

**When to use:** before you pitch anyone, to list everything you can offer sponsors, attach a real audience number to each asset, and package it into a few tiers with limits.

```text
Build a sponsorship inventory and two to four tiers for {{property_name}}, a {{type_of_property}} in {{location}}.

Dates, format and what's new this year: {{dates_and_format}}
Audience numbers, each with its source and date (attendance or scans, members, podcast downloads and whether the host follows the IAB Tech Lab standard, newsletter subscribers and clicks, social followers): {{audience_numbers}}
Who the audience is (if known): {{audience_profile}}
Assets we could offer, including ones we've never sold: {{assets}}
Past deals and prices: {{past_deals}}
Categories we won't take, and whether our audience includes under-18s: {{category_rules}}
Are we a US nonprofit, a UK charity, or neither? {{legal_status}}

Return:
1. An inventory sheet: asset, quantity, audience reached (with source and date), value basis, suggested value, notes. If a number is missing, write "no data" rather than estimating.
2. A tier table: tier name, what's included, price, how many are for sale, category exclusivity, term. Put the scarce assets in the top tier and keep a small local supporter tier.
3. Flags: any restricted category (gambling, alcohol, others) against an under-18 audience; and, for US nonprofits or UK charities, tax or VAT questions to ask a qualified accountant. Give no tax ruling.
4. A list of missing data that would change the prices.

Never invent audience numbers, market rates or past sponsors.
```
