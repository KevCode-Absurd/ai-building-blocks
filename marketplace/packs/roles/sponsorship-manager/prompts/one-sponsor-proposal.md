# Proposal for one sponsor

**When to use:** when a prospect says "send me something", to get a two-page proposal built around their goals and a deliverables schedule your lawyer can drop into the contract.

```text
Write a sponsorship proposal from {{property_name}} to {{sponsor_name}}.

Their goals, in their words if possible (from our call notes): {{sponsor_goals}}
Our audience facts, each with source and date: {{audience_facts}}
The package we're offering (tier or custom), with every item: {{package_items}}
Price, payment terms and dates: {{price_and_terms}}
Activation ideas we discussed: {{activation_ideas}}
Category exclusivity, if any: {{exclusivity}}
Who signs on our side: {{signer}}

Proposal, two pages:
1. Your goals (their words).
2. Why our audience fits (sourced facts).
3. What you get, item by item.
4. How you could use it (one or two activation ideas).
5. Investment, payment terms and dates.
6. Next steps and a decision date.

Then a deliverables schedule table for the contract: benefit, quantity, date, owner, proof, creative approval needed (yes or no), disclosure wording for paid mentions ("Ad" up front in the UK; disclosed in the same channel as the endorsement in the US), logo use and end date, make-good if missed.

Mark the whole proposal "Draft: not an offer until approved by {{signer}}". Promise deliverables, not sales or results. If we are a US nonprofit or UK charity, add a note listing questions for our accountant about how the benefits affect tax or VAT; give no ruling.
```
