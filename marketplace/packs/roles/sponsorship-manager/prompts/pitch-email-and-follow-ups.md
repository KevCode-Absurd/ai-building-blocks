# Pitch email and follow-ups

**When to use:** for each top prospect, to get a short pitch written for that company, two follow-ups with a new reason each, and a note they can forward to their boss.

```text
Write a sponsor pitch for {{company_name}}, to {{contact_name_and_role}}, from {{sender_name_and_role}} at {{property_name}}.

How we know them: {{relationship}}
What we know about their goals right now (launch, new site, hiring, community profile): {{their_goals}}
One audience fact that matters to them, with its source and date: {{audience_fact}}
The tier or package that fits: {{package}}
One activation idea for them: {{activation_idea}}
A real deadline, if we have one: {{deadline}}
Our postal address and opt-out line (needed in the US): {{address_and_opt_out}}

Write:
1. The pitch: under 150 words. A subject line about their goal or something specific to them. One line on why our audience fits them, the audience fact, the activation idea, and one ask: a 15-minute call. No hype, no promised sales or results.
2. Follow-up 1 (after {{gap_1}} days): a new reason, such as a new fact or a relevant result. Under 80 words.
3. Follow-up 2 (after {{gap_2}} days): a short, polite last note. Then stop.
4. A forwardable note (under 120 words) the contact could send to their manager: what we are, the audience, the package and price range, the idea.

Use only the facts above. Mark anything you'd need to check as [CHECK].
```
