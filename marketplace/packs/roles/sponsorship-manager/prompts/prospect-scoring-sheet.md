# Prospect scoring sheet

**When to use:** when you have a list of businesses you know or have met and want to see who to call first, with a reason for every score.

```text
Score our sponsor prospects for {{property_name}}.

Our audience in one line, with its source: {{audience_summary}}
Our tiers and prices: {{tiers}}
Our contact list (company, contact, how we know them, past sponsorships or ads, notes, how we got their details): {{contact_list}}
How many prospects we can follow up personally this month: {{capacity}}

For each company, score 1 to 3 on:
- Fit: does this company want our audience, and does our audience suit the brand?
- Budget signs: past sponsorships or advertising, growth, size, local presence, from our notes only.
- Warmth: who knows them, and how well.
Give one line of reason per score. If you don't know, write "unknown", not a low score.

Then:
1. A ranked table: company, fit, budget, warmth, total, reason, which tier fits, next step.
2. A first wave sized to {{capacity}}, past sponsors and warm contacts first, with two talking points each.
3. A "basis for contact" column from how we got each contact's details, and a list of contacts we should approach by phone or in person instead of email until we confirm the rules (CAN-SPAM in the US; permission for individuals in the UK; prior consent in the EU).

Do not look up, guess or add contact details we haven't given you.
```
