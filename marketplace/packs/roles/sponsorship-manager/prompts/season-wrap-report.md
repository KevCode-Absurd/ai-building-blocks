# Season wrap-up report

**When to use:** at the end of a season, event or campaign, to show a sponsor every promise with its proof before you ask them to renew.

```text
Write an end-of-season report for {{sponsor_name}} from {{property_name}}, covering {{period}}.

The deliverables schedule from their contract: {{deliverables_schedule}}
Our fulfilment tracker (deliverable, date, status, proof link): {{tracker}}
Audience numbers for the period, each with source and date: {{audience_numbers}}
Anything missed, why, and the make-good we offered or delivered: {{misses_and_make_goods}}
Standout moments, quotes or photos we have permission to use: {{highlights}}
Ideas for next season: {{next_season_ideas}}

Format:
1. One-page summary: what they bought, what was delivered, three headline numbers with sources, one standout moment.
2. Delivery table: each promise, date, status, proof link.
3. Audience numbers with footnotes.
4. What we missed and how we made it good. Be plain and honest.
5. Two ideas for next season, tied to their goals.
6. A short closing line proposing a date to talk about next year.

Do not claim results for the sponsor's sales or brand unless the sponsor gave us those figures to share. Label any estimate. Keep notes to us separate from the report.
```
