# Sponsored content label check

**When to use:** before a paid podcast read, video, social post or newsletter slot goes out, to check the ad label, its placement and the sponsor's claims.

```text
Check this sponsored content before it goes out.

Channel (podcast read, YouTube video, Instagram or TikTok post, newsletter slot, event page): {{channel}}
Where our audience mostly lives (UK, US, EU country): {{audience_regions}}
The content: script, caption, title, thumbnail text or slot copy: {{content}}
What the sponsor gave us (payment, free product, affiliate link or code): {{what_we_received}}
Claims in the content that the sponsor wants made: {{sponsor_claims}}
Platform label we plan to use, if any (for example YouTube's paid promotion setting or Instagram's Paid partnership): {{platform_label}}

Check against:
- UK (ASA/CAP advice, August 2026): a clear "Ad", "Advert", "Advertising" or "Ad feature" label up front; not "Sponsored", "Supported by", "Gifted", "In association with" or "Thanks to X" alone; on YouTube, in the title or thumbnail; advertorials labelled, for example "advertisement feature".
- US (FTC): disclosed in the same channel as the endorsement (said out loud in audio, shown in video), close to it, not only in a description or comments; "Sponsored by [brand]" rather than "Sponsored"; repeated in live streams.
- EU: made clear by images, words or sound.

Return:
1. Pass or fix for each region, with the reason.
2. Rewritten label wording and placement for this channel.
3. A list of claims the sponsor must confirm they can back up before it airs.
4. Anything that presents a paid message as independent opinion, with a fix.

This is a practical check, not legal advice; for anything unclear, ask the sponsor's or our lawyer.
```
