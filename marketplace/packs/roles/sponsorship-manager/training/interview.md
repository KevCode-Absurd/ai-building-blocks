# Interview: sponsorship manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you give me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the expert's work. Sponsor names and deal values stay out of the answers unless they are public.

## How you approach a job

1. **Walk me through your first month with a new property, from the first call to the first signed sponsor.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method and the order of work.
2. **What do you ask for before you price anything?**
   Why it matters: becomes the agent's intake list.
3. **How do you find assets a property didn't know it could sell?**
   Why it matters: captures their inventory habits.

## Pricing and packages

4. **How do you put a price on an asset when there's no market data you trust?**
   Why it matters: teaches pricing from the buyer's numbers, not guesses.
5. **How many tiers do you build, and how do you decide what goes in the top one?**
   Why it matters: becomes the tier rules.
6. **When have you turned down a sponsor or a category? Why?**
   Why it matters: shows fit and audience judgement.

## Selling

7. **How do you decide who to call first from a long contact list?**
   Why it matters: becomes the scoring method.
8. **Show me a pitch email that worked, and tell me why you think it did.**
   Why it matters: a real model for the agent's pitches.
9. **What do you put in a proposal that most people leave out?**
   Why it matters: sets the proposal standard.
10. **Tell me about a deal you lost. What would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: real objections and how to answer them.
11. **How do you handle a sponsor who asks for audience data you don't have?**
    Why it matters: tests honesty about numbers.

## Delivery and renewal

12. **How do you track what you've promised each sponsor, and what counts as proof?**
    Why it matters: becomes the tracker and proof rules.
13. **What do you do when you miss a deliverable?**
    Why it matters: captures their make-good habit.
14. **What goes in your end-of-season report, and when do you send it?**
    Why it matters: sets the renewal sequence.
15. **What makes a sponsor renew, in your experience? Give an example.**
    Why it matters: real renewal judgement.

## Rules, tools and money

16. **How do you label host reads, sponsored posts and newsletter slots? Has a label ever been challenged?**
    Why it matters: teaches disclosure in practice.
17. **What do you check in a sponsor's script before it airs?**
    Why it matters: catches unsupported claims.
18. **For charities or nonprofits, which tax or VAT questions do you always send to the accountant?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent flagging, not ruling.
19. **Which tools do you use, and what would you never let an assistant do in them?**
    Why it matters: fills access.md and the level 3 limits.
20. **Tell me about the hardest sponsorship you've delivered, start to finish.**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a real story and the judgement behind it.
