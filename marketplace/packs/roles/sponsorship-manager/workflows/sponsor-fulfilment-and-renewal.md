# Sponsor fulfilment and renewal

**Trigger:** a sponsor signs a contract with a deliverables schedule.

**Cadence:** weekly tracker check during the term; the wrap-up report in the last month; the renewal talk after the report.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits).

## Steps

1. **Tracker, L2 (skill: sponsor-fulfilment-disclosure-and-renewal).** One row per promise from the deliverables schedule: due date, owner, proof. Vague promises go back to the client to define with the sponsor.
2. **Kick-off note, L2 then L3.** Draft a welcome note to the sponsor contact: what happens when, who to contact, what we need from them (logos, creative, scripts) and by when. At L3, send the approved note.
3. **Before each sponsored item, L2 (prompt: sponsored-content-label-check).** Check the ad label and its placement for each region, and list claims the sponsor must confirm.
4. **Publish, L3 within limits.** Publish approved sponsored posts, newsletter slots or show notes with the agreed label. Podcast reads and videos are recorded by the client or host with the label in the read or on screen.
5. **Proof, L3.** File proof as each item goes out (screenshots with the label visible, timestamps, reports, photos, scan counts) and update the tracker.
6. **Weekly check, L1.** Items due in the next fortnight, anything late, anything missing from the sponsor. Flag misses to the client with a draft make-good.
7. **Money steps, L2.** Draft invoices on the contract's payment dates and any credit or refund requests for the client to send or issue. Bank and payout details are never touched.
8. **Partnership end, L3 within limits.** When a term ends without renewal, remove logos, "partner" wording and sponsor pages on the channels in the limits, and list any others for the client.
9. **Wrap-up and renewal, L2 (prompt: season-wrap-report).** Write the report and send it (at L3, the approved version) before the renewal conversation; then draft the renewal proposal.

## Outputs

- Fulfilment tracker and proof folder.
- Labelled sponsored content published and logged.
- Make-good, invoice and credit drafts for the client.
- Wrap-up report and renewal proposal.

## Needs the client's approval

- Each label wording set and each sponsored item before it airs or publishes, unless the client has approved a standing format within the level 3 limits.
- Make-goods offered to sponsors, and any change to the deliverables.
- Invoices, credits and refunds: the client sends or issues them.
- A fresh yes for a formal reply to a sponsor's complaint or claim, for renewal quotes sent as binding, and for any new audience.
