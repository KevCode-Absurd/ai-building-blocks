# Sponsorship sales season

**Trigger:** the client sets a date by which sponsors must be signed (usually several months before an event, a season or a new podcast or newsletter run).

**Cadence:** a set-up fortnight, then a weekly pipeline review until the deadline.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish within the client's limits).

## Steps

1. **Facts first, L1.** Gather audience numbers with sources and dates, past deals, permissions to name past sponsors, category rules, and the client's legal status (US nonprofit, UK charity, business).
2. **Inventory and tiers, L2 (skill: sponsorship-inventory-tiers-and-deck; prompt: inventory-and-tiers).** Build the inventory sheet and tier table. Flag restricted categories and any tax or VAT questions for the client's accountant.
3. **Deck and media kit, L2.** Draft the deck and one-page media kit in the client's deck tool, every number footnoted. At L3, publish an approved sponsorship page.
4. **Prospect scoring, L1 then L2 (skill: sponsor-prospecting-pitch-and-proposal; prompt: prospect-scoring-sheet).** Score the client's own contacts and pick a first wave. Record the basis for contact for each.
5. **Pitches, L2 then L3 (prompt: pitch-email-and-follow-ups).** Draft each pitch and follow-ups. At L3, show the send list, get one yes, then send to the approved prospects and log every send. Hand replies to the client the same day.
6. **Proposals, L2 (prompt: one-sponsor-proposal).** Draft a proposal and deliverables schedule for each interested sponsor, marked draft.
7. **Contract, client.** The client or its lawyer finalises the contract with the deliverables schedule. Sending a quote or contract as binding needs the client's fresh yes; signing is the client's.
8. **Weekly pipeline review, L1 then L3.** Update CRM stages, list next steps and overdue follow-ups, count tiers sold against limits, and flag any tier that should be repackaged.
9. **Hand over to fulfilment.** For each signed sponsor, start the fulfilment tracker (workflow: sponsor-fulfilment-and-renewal).

## Outputs

- Inventory sheet, tier table, deck and media kit.
- Scored prospect list and first-wave call list.
- Pitches and follow-ups sent and logged; proposals and deliverables schedules.
- Weekly pipeline note: signed, in talks, lost (with reasons), tiers left.

## Needs the client's approval

- Prices, tiers, the deck and any published sponsorship page before use.
- Naming any past sponsor: written permission first.
- The send list before the first live send; a fresh yes for any new audience.
- A fresh yes for every quote or contract sent as binding; the client signs.
- Tax and VAT questions go to the client's accountant; the agent gives no ruling.
