# Head-injury steps for parents and coaches

**When to use:** At the start of a season, or when a club adopts a concussion policy. Use it to write a short, plain message and a pitch-side card that explain the club's steps and point to official guidance. It is not for a named child's injury and never clears anyone to play.

```text
You are helping a sports club explain its head-injury steps. This is not medical advice. Every question about a real injury, symptoms or return to play goes to a qualified medical professional, and young players need extra care.

Club and sport: {{club_and_sport}}
Country (UK nation, US state, or EU country): {{country}}
Age groups: {{age_groups}}
Our club's concussion policy or governing body rules (paste, or say none): {{club_policy}}
Who parents contact at the club: {{club_contact}}
Channel and length limit: {{channel_and_limit}}

Write two things:
A. A message to parents (under 200 words).
B. A pitch-side card for coaches (under 100 words).

Rules:
1. Use the official steps for the country, in plain words, and link the official page:
   - UK grassroots: "If in doubt, sit them out"; recognise, remove, return; no return to sport within 24 hours; get checked via NHS 111 or a healthcare professional within 24 hours; 999 for danger signs; return only through the graduated return to activity and sport, and not to competition before day 21.
   - US: CDC HEADS UP; remove the athlete, keep them out that day and until a healthcare provider clears them; 911 or the emergency department for danger signs; follow state law and the league's policy.
   - EU and elsewhere: the national federation's and health service's guidance; ask me for it if I did not paste it.
2. The coach's job: spot, remove, tell the parent, record. Coaches never decide when a player returns.
3. No dates, symptoms checklists or advice for a named child. No treatment advice.
4. Say clearly: ask a qualified medical professional about any injury or symptoms.
5. Warm, calm, short sentences. No scare words.

Output: the parent message, the coach card, and the official link used.
```
