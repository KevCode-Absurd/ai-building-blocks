# Training interview: sports coach

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share names, photos, health details or stories that could identify real players, especially children; anonymise every story. Medical and safeguarding details stay out of training material.

1. **Which sports, age groups and levels do you coach, in which country, and is your club under a governing body?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's sport range, development stages and the safeguarding and concussion rules that apply.

2. **Walk me through how you plan a session from scratch. What do you need to know before you choose a single drill?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's intake, the part plain AI skips when it invents drills for the wrong numbers.

3. **Show me a session plan that worked really well. Why did it work?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real model and the reasons behind each choice.

4. **How do you spot that a drill is failing, and what do you change first?**
   Why it matters: the agent needs the expert's signals and the order they change space, task, equipment or people.

5. **How do you keep every player busy when numbers, space or kit are short?**
   Why it matters: anti-queue tricks are the most useful thing a volunteer coach can borrow.

6. **How do you decide the order of skills across a season?**
   Why it matters: the agent's season plans should follow the expert's progressions, not a generic list.

7. **How do you coach a group with a big ability spread, including players with additional needs?**
   Why it matters: inclusion methods differ by sport and need real examples, without medical detail.

8. **What do you say to a player who keeps making the same mistake? Give me your exact words.**
   Why it matters: gives the agent the expert's coaching points and tone.

9. **How do you track progress, and what do you never write down about a child?**
   Why it matters: shapes the progress sheet and the line on personal and health data.

10. **How do you use competition and results with young players, and when do you start keeping score?**
    Why it matters: matches the agent's advice to the expert's view of long-term development.

11. **A parent wants their child to train more or specialise early. What do you say?**
    Why it matters: a common, tricky conversation; the agent needs a respectful answer that defers load questions to medical professionals.

12. **What are your rules for messaging players and parents, and for photos and video?**
    Why it matters: the agent must follow the expert's club rules, which may be stricter than general guidance.

13. **Tell me about a time a player had a suspected head injury. What did you do, step by step?**
    Why it matters: checks the expert follows recognise, remove, return and never clears a player; the agent copies that discipline.

14. **When something worried you about a child's welfare, how did you handle it, without names or details that identify anyone?**
    Why it matters: shows the expert's safeguarding route so the agent sends concerns to the welfare officer, not into messages.

15. **How do you handle a parent shouting from the touchline or a row with another coach?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent practical scripts for conduct issues.

16. **What goes in your volunteer handbook, and what do new coaches get wrong first?**
    Why it matters: the agent's handbook should answer the real first-week problems.

17. **Which tools do you use (club app, planning tool, spreadsheet, video), and which jobs would you hand to an agent?**
    Why it matters: sets realistic access levels and the jobs worth automating.

18. **What would you never let an AI do for your club?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's limits, which become fresh-yes rules.

19. **Which coaching books, courses or people shaped how you coach?**
    Why it matters: adds the expert's own canon, credited without copying.

20. **What makes a player come back next week? What makes one quit?**
    Why it matters: retention is the measure clubs care about most; the agent should plan for it.

21. **Tell me about your hardest season. What went wrong and what did you change?**
    Why it matters: a real story shows judgement under pressure that plain AI cannot fake.
