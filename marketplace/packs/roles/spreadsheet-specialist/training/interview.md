# Training interview: Excel and Google Sheets specialist

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names and real figures; describe workbooks in general terms.

1. **Which kinds of spreadsheets do you build or fix most: calculators, budgets, trackers, reports, macros, scripts?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and its limits.

2. **Walk me through the last workbook you built, from the first conversation to handover.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where time goes.

3. **How do you lay out a new workbook, and why that way?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's structure for inputs, workings and outputs.

4. **When you open a workbook you did not build, what do you check first?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a fast, real review sequence.

5. **Tell me about the worst spreadsheet error you have found. How was it hidden, and what did it cost?**
   Why it matters: adds a real failure pattern to the agent's checks.

6. **How do you decide between a formula, Power Query, a macro, Office Scripts and Apps Script?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's tool choice rules.

7. **Which functions do you reach for most, and which do you avoid?**
   Why it matters: shapes the agent's formula style and what it treats as fragile.

8. **How do you handle clients on different Excel versions or a mix of Excel and Google Sheets?**
   Why it matters: makes version checks part of every answer.

9. **How do you make a sheet safe for someone who is not good with spreadsheets?**
   Why it matters: pulls out validation, protection and layout habits.

10. **What does a good worked example look like, and how do you choose the test cases?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's standard for proving formulas are right.

11. **How do you clean up a recorded macro? What do you always change?**
    Why it matters: captures concrete VBA rewrite rules.

12. **What safety parts do you build into every macro or script?**
    Why it matters: makes backups, test modes, logs and safe stops habitual.

13. **How do you deal with macro security when you send a workbook to a client?**
    Why it matters: records safe trust practice without lowering security.

14. **What Apps Script limits or permission problems have you hit, and how did you design around them?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent real quota and scope lessons.

15. **When a client asks you to make the totals match, what do you do?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert holds the line on honest numbers.

16. **How do you hand over a workbook so the next owner can run it?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's notes tab and handover output.

17. **Do you use Copilot or Gemini in your spreadsheet work? Where do they help, and where do they go wrong?**
    Why it matters: sets rules for checking AI-written formulas and macros.

18. **How much access do you ask for in a client's files, and how do you decide when to make live changes yourself?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's access levels in real practice.

19. **Tell me about a job where the client wanted the wrong thing. How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: teaches the agent to push back with care.

20. **What would you check before a workbook goes to a bank, board or customer?**
    Why it matters: sets the final quality bar for high-stakes sheets.
