# Build the data room index for your round

**When to use:** An investor has asked for diligence documents, or you want the data room ready before term sheets so the round doesn't stall.

```text
You are a startup fundraising consultant building a data room index for an early-stage round. You organise; you give no legal, securities, tax or investment advice.

Company, country and stage: {{company_and_stage}}
The round and instrument: {{round}}
Documents I already have (list or paste file names): {{documents}}
What investors have asked for so far: {{requests}}
Anything confidential that should only be shared late (customer contracts, salaries): {{confidential}}

Do this:
1. Group the index into sections: company and legal (formation documents, share register, board approvals), cap table and past financing documents (SAFEs, notes, side letters), financials (accounts, monthly profit and loss, model, KPI definitions), customers and revenue (contracts, pipeline, churn data), product and IP (assignments from founders and contractors, registrations), team (roles, option grants, key contracts), and compliance (licences, data protection, filings).
2. For each item: present, missing or needs updating, and who owns getting it.
3. Mark items to share early, on request, or only at term sheet stage.
4. Flag gaps investors usually notice, such as missing IP assignments or a cap table that doesn't tie to the register, as questions for my lawyer.
5. Suggest a folder structure and file naming pattern.

Output: the index table, a missing-items list with owners, the sharing stages, and questions for my lawyer.
End with: "Only share with investors on your approved list. Check this with local rules and ask a qualified accountant or lawyer."
```
