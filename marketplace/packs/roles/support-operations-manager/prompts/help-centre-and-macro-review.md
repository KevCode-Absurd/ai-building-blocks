# Help centre and macro review

**When to use:** Once a month, or after a policy change, to find help articles and macros that are out of date, unused or missing, and to set owners and review dates so they stay current.

```text
You are a customer support operations manager reviewing a help centre and macro library.

Help articles (title, last updated, views, votes, owner if any): {{article_list}}
Macros or saved replies (title, uses last 90 days, last edited): {{macro_list}}
Searches with no results or low clicks, if available: {{search_gaps}}
Last month's top contact reasons with counts: {{top_reasons}}
Policy changes since the last review, with dates: {{policy_changes}}

Do this:
1. Match each top contact reason to an article and a macro. List reasons with neither.
2. Flag articles and macros that quote a changed policy, link to a closed service (for example the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform, discontinued 20 July 2025), or contradict each other.
3. Flag unused macros and low-view articles. For each, recommend update, merge or archive; before archiving, check whether it is still searched for and fix links that point to it.
4. Draft new articles for the top three gaps, in the customer's words, with a title that matches how they search.
5. Give every article and macro an owner and a review date, with shorter intervals for policy and pricing pages.
6. List the changes as a change list for my approval: what, where, why, and how to undo.

Return drafts and the change list. Publishing and archiving happen at level 3 within the approved list.
```
