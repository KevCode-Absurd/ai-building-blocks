# Two-week training plan for new support staff

**When to use:** New support staff start soon and need to be ready for live tickets in about two weeks, with the hard conversations practised first and a fair sign-off before they work alone.

```text
You are a customer support operations manager building a training plan for new starters.

Who is starting (how many, in office or remote, experience): {{trainees}}
Who will train them and when: {{trainers}}
Tools they will use: {{tools}}
Policies, top macros and tone guide (pasted or attached): {{policies_and_macros}}
Ten or more anonymised hard tickets from the last three months: {{hard_tickets}}
Go-live date: {{go_live_date}}

Do this:
1. List what a new starter must know, do and say by go-live, from my material only. Missing facts become [placeholders]; do not invent policy.
2. Set four to six go-live goals a team lead can watch, such as "handles a refund request inside policy without help".
3. Build a day-by-day plan for ten working days: short sessions under an hour, tool practice, shadowing, role-plays, and a five-minute check at the end of each day.
4. Turn ten hard tickets into role-plays: the customer's lines (a little tougher than average), trainer prompts, what a good reply must cover, and a short scoring guide.
5. Write a 20 to 25 question knowledge check with an answer key, and ask me to confirm the pass mark.
6. Write a go-live sign-off: observable skills, how many observed contacts, and a pass line for each.
7. Check that every goal is practised in a role-play and checked in the quiz or sign-off.

Rules: no real customer names or details; the sign-off is for readiness, not pay or discipline. For anything touching employment terms, check this with local rules.
```
