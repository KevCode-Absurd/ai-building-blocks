# Training interview: customer support operations manager

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers, and asks for a real example each time. Answers fill in the expert's knowledge, playbook and specialty. Remind the expert never to share customer or staff names, ticket numbers or other personal details; anonymise every story.

1. **What size of support teams have you run or set up, in which industries, and on which help desks?**
   Why it matters: sets the range of teams and tools the agent should claim to handle.

2. **When you take over a messy help desk, what do you look at in the first hour?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's diagnostic order, which the agent should copy.

3. **How do you decide how many queues a team needs, and who owns each one?**
   Why it matters: queue design is the core of the set-up; the agent needs the expert's tests.

4. **Show me a tag list you are proud of. What did you remove to get there?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent to keep tags short and single-purpose.

5. **How do you set a response target for a team that has never had one?**
   Why it matters: shows how the expert turns volume data into targets rather than wishes.

6. **Tell me about a target that failed. What was wrong with it, and what did you change?**
   Why it matters: the agent needs the warning signs of a target the team cannot keep.

7. **How do you work out staffing by hour for chat compared with email?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's real method, including any rules of thumb and their limits.

8. **What do you put in a shift handover note?**
   Why it matters: handovers are where tickets get lost; the agent should use the expert's template.

9. **Walk me through the last automation or routing rule that misfired. How did you find it and fix it?**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent the checks to run before and after a live change.

10. **How do you test a change before it goes live, with and without a sandbox?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's default release routine.

11. **What lines are on your quality scorecard, and which ones are critical?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a proven scorecard to start from.

12. **How do you run calibration when two reviewers disagree?**
    Why it matters: fair grading depends on it; the agent needs the expert's process.

13. **How do you write coaching notes that agents act on rather than resent?**
    Why it matters: captures tone and structure for the most sensitive output.

14. **Which numbers go in your weekly pack, and how do you define each one?**
    Why it matters: the agent's reports must use definitions that hold week to week.

15. **Tell me about a time ticket data led to a fix outside support. How did you get the other team to act?**
    Why it matters: shows how insight becomes change, which is the role's main value.

16. **What are your escalation levels, and what goes straight to the owner?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's escalation defaults and risk triggers.

17. **How do you handle a serious complaint that reaches the owner or managing director?**
    Why it matters: captures careful wording and the facts-first habit.

18. **Have you introduced an AI agent or bot into a support team? What went wrong, and how did you measure it?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's advice on AI rollout in real experience.

19. **How do you keep help articles and macros current, and who owns them?**
    Why it matters: teaches the upkeep routine and ownership model.

20. **When has a client asked you to use quality scores for pay or discipline, and what did you say?**
    Why it matters: the agent needs the expert's words for holding that line.

21. **What is the hardest support operations job you have done, start to finish?**
    Why it matters: a full story shows judgement under pressure that the agent can learn from.
