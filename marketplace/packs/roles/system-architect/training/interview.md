# Training interview: system architect

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, secrets and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe systems in general terms.

1. **What kinds of systems and team sizes do you know best, and on which clouds?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of its advice.

2. **Walk me through the last design you led, from the first conversation to the team building it.**
   Why it matters: reveals the real sequence of work and where the expert spends time.

3. **How do you get clear quality goals out of a founder who says "it just needs to scale"?**
   Why it matters: captures the questions that turn vague wishes into numbers.

4. **Which diagrams do you actually draw, at what level, and in which tool?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's diagram habits in practice, not theory.

5. **When do you write an ADR, and when do you skip it?**
   Why it matters: sets the threshold for decision records so they stay useful.

6. **Tell me about a time you recommended a monolith, or talked a team out of microservices. What happened?**
   Why it matters: records a real structural trade-off and its outcome.

7. **Tell me about a design of yours that did not hold up. What did you miss?**
   Why it matters: surfaces the checks the expert now adds to every review.

8. **How do you choose a database for a new product?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's data trade-off reasoning (consistency, query shape, operations, cost).

9. **How do you design for failure in a small system with no ops team?**
   Why it matters: pulls out timeouts, retries, idempotency and queue habits.

10. **What is your approach to multi-tenancy, and when have you moved a tenant to dedicated infrastructure?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent real triggers and migration lessons.

11. **How do you pick an availability target, and how do you explain the cost of each extra nine to a client?**
    Why it matters: captures how the expert links SLOs to business impact.

12. **How do you run a threat model with a team that has no security specialist?**
    Why it matters: makes the agent's threat modelling practical for small teams.

13. **What is the first thing you look at in a cloud bill?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's cost review order and common waste.

14. **Which well-architected framework questions do you find most useful, and which do you skip?**
    Why it matters: turns long checklists into a short, useful review.

15. **How do you review a system you did not design, without making the team defensive?**
    Why it matters: captures tone and method for reviews.

16. **How do you plan a migration off a legacy system?**
    Why it matters: records strangler fig steps and transitional architecture choices from real work.

17. **What do you need from a client before you can start, and what do you refuse to accept (for example admin keys)?**
    Why it matters: feeds access.md and the safety rules in the expert's own words.

18. **How do you know a design doc is good enough to build from?**
    Why it matters: defines the expert's quality bar.

19. **How do team structure and system structure interact in your experience?**
    Why it matters: captures Conway's law in practice.

20. **Which advice in architecture blogs and talks do you think is wrong for small teams?**
    Why it matters: lists myths the agent should push back on.

21. **Tell me about a client who wanted a design you thought was wrong. What did you do?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert disagrees and records the disagreement.

22. **How do you keep up with cloud changes, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: sets refresh habits and trusted sources for this agent.
