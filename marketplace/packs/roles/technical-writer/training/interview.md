# Training interview: technical writer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, confidential code and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe products in general terms.

1. **What do you document most: help centres, developer docs, API references, manuals for physical products, or internal procedures?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the skills it leans on.

2. **Walk me through the last documentation project you finished, from the first conversation to publication.**
   Why it matters: reveals the real sequence of work and where time goes.

3. **How do you find out who the reader is and what they need to do?**
   Why it matters: captures the questions that shape every page.

4. **Where do your facts come from, and what do you do when the expert is too busy to answer?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's habits for sourcing and marking unknowns.

5. **How do you decide what kind of page something should be: tutorial, how-to, reference or explanation?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's structure choices in practice.

6. **Show me a procedure you rewrote. What was wrong with the old one?**
   Why it matters: records concrete before-and-after editing moves.

7. **How do you test that instructions work?**
   Why it matters: becomes part of the agent's quality bar.

8. **Which style guide do you follow, and where do you break from it?**
   Why it matters: captures house style and the reasons behind exceptions.

9. **How do you keep docs in step with releases? Who owns what?**
   Why it matters: shapes the agent's docs-as-code and release habits.

10. **What tools do you use to write, review, lint and publish, and what do you think of each?**
    Why it matters: grounds tool advice in real use.

11. **For API docs: what do you check in a spec before writing, and what do developers complain about most?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent its API gap checklist.

12. **How do you turn support tickets into articles? How do you know an article worked?**
    Why it matters: captures the ticket-to-article method and how success is measured.

13. **For physical products: how do you work with illustrators and the person who checks safety warnings?**
    Why it matters: records the handoffs the agent must plan for.

14. **Tell me about documentation of yours that caused a problem: a wrong step, a stale screenshot, a confused customer. What did you change afterwards?**
    Why it matters: surfaces the checks the expert now never skips.

15. **When has a client wanted something in the docs you thought was wrong, such as hiding a limitation or a misleading claim? What did you do?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert pushes back.

16. **How do you write for readers whose first language is not English, or for content that will be translated?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's global writing habits.

17. **How do you make docs accessible?**
    Why it matters: grounds alt text, headings and plain language in practice.

18. **How do you use AI in your documentation work, and where do you not trust it?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's limits on drafting and verification.

19. **What do you hand over at the end of a project so the docs stay alive?**
    Why it matters: shapes the agent's handoff: owners, review dates, templates.

20. **What is the hardest documentation job you have done, and how did you get it over the line?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a real story of judgement under pressure.
