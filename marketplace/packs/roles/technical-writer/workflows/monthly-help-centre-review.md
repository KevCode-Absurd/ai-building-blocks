# Monthly help centre review

**Cadence:** monthly, in the first week, for any business with a help centre or FAQ page.

**Trigger:** also run it after a product launch, a pricing or policy change, or a jump in tickets on one topic.

## Steps

1. **Collect the month's evidence.** Ticket export (personal data removed), help centre search terms with no results, article feedback ("was this helpful?"), and the list of product and policy changes. Level 1 access is enough.
2. **Group and count questions (skill: help-centre-articles-from-tickets, steps 1 and 2).** Compare with last month: which topics grew, which shrank after last month's fixes.
3. **Check articles against reality.** For the top topics, follow each article on the live product. Mark current, stale, confusing or missing. Check policy pages against the latest approved policy.
4. **Draft fixes and new articles (skill: help-centre-articles-from-tickets, steps 4 to 9).** Answer first, steps with exact labels, expected results, one common mistake, a way to reach a person.
5. **Troubleshooting updates (prompt: troubleshooting-chart)** for problems that keep returning.
6. **Owner review.** Product, support and policy owners answer [confirm] items and check accuracy.
   **Approval point:** the owner approves the batch of new and changed articles.
7. **Publish.**
   - Level 2: create drafts in the help desk for the client to publish.
   - Level 3: publish and update only the approved articles in the named sections, within the client's limits, and log each action.
8. **Close the loop.** Ask support to link solved tickets to articles and flag wrong ones. Record articles retired or merged (with the owner's yes).
9. **Monthly note.** Top five questions and their articles, what changed, ticket trend by topic, gaps the docs cannot fix (product or policy issues), and next month's top five.

## Outputs

- Question map with counts and trend.
- New and updated articles, FAQ and troubleshooting rows.
- Policy clash list for owners.
- Action log.
- Monthly note with next month's priorities.

## Needs the client's approval

- Each batch of new or changed articles before it goes live.
- Any change to policy wording, which the policy owner (and, where needed, their qualified adviser) approves.
- Retiring, merging or redirecting articles.
- At level 3, the action list and limits before the first live action, and a new yes before anything outside them.
