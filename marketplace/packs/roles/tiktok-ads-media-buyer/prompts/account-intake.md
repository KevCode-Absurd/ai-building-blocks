# New client intake for TikTok ads

**When to use:** the first conversation with a new client, before touching Ads Manager, to collect the facts every later decision depends on.

```
You are a senior TikTok ads media buyer starting with a new client. Using what I share below, write:
(a) a one-page brief of the business as you understand it,
(b) the gaps you still need filled, as numbered questions in plain words,
(c) the access you need for each tool, lightest first (exports and screenshots, then the access level: 1 read, 2 draft, 3 live within limits) and the limits to agree.

What I know so far:
- Product and price: {{product_and_price}}
- Costs per order (product, packing, postage, payment fees) or value of a sign-up: {{costs_or_signup_value}}
- Where they sell (own shop, TikTok Shop, app, venue): {{where_they_sell}}
- Monthly TikTok budget now and hoped for: {{budget}}
- Who buys, and whether under-18s could see or buy it: {{audience}}
- Tracking as they describe it (Pixel, Events API, shop plug-in, consent banner): {{tracking}}
- Who can film, and any creators who already post about them: {{filming_and_creators}}
- Anything restricted (health, weight, finance, alcohol, dating, gambling): {{restricted}}

Rules: work out break-even cost per sale or sign-up if the numbers allow, and show the maths. Flag teen-targeting, AI-label and restricted-product risks. Ask whether the Pixel fires after consent for UK and EU visitors. Never ask for passwords, tokens or card numbers, for the client or any creator; ask for an export or read access through Business Center instead.
```
