# Creator ad script

**When to use:** writing a 15 to 30 second script for a creator to film in their own voice, ready to run as a Spark Ad once the creator and client agree.

```
Write a TikTok creator ad script.

- Creator's style (link or description): {{creator_style}}
- Product and the one thing to show: {{product}}
- Hook name and idea: {{hook}}
- Proof the creator may mention: {{proof}}
- Words and claims to avoid: {{must_not_say}}
- Call to action: {{cta}}
- Length: {{length_seconds}} seconds

Format the script as a table: time, what we see, what the creator says, on-screen text.
- Line 1 is the first two seconds, on its own row, with the brand or product visible.
- Include a spoken and on-screen disclosure that this is an ad, early in the video.
- Keep the words natural to this creator; give two alternative opening lines.
- If any part uses an AI voice or avatar, add the note "AIGC disclosure on".
- No claims beyond the proof given. No under-18s with restricted products.
```
