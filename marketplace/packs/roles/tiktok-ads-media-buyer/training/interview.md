# Interview: pulling out a TikTok ads media buyer's real know-how

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on vague answers ("can you give me a real example?"), and writes the answers into knowledge.md ("My specialty") and playbook.md. Each question has one line on why it matters.

1. **Walk me through the first hour in a TikTok ad account you have never seen.**
   Why: shows your real audit order and what you trust first.
2. **How do you work out what a client can afford per sale or sign-up?**
   Why: captures the maths behind every budget call.
3. **How do you check the Pixel and Events API, and how have you caught double counting?**
   Why: tracking habits protect every later decision.
4. **When do you choose Smart+ and when a manual set-up? What made you change your mind last time?**
   Why: records current judgement on TikTok's automation.
5. **How do you write a hook? Show me three you are proud of and why they worked.**
   Why: hooks are the core craft of this role.
6. **How do you name hooks and keep a test log?**
   Why: a good log is how an account learns.
7. **How do you set kill rules before a test, and what do you do when a client wants to break them?**
   Why: separates disciplined testing from gut calls.
8. **What do you look at first when cost per result doubles overnight?**
   Why: your triage order becomes the agent's.
9. **How do you spot a tired ad before costs rise?**
   Why: early-warning habits differ between experts.
10. **How do you find and brief creators, and what goes in every brief?**
    Why: captures your creator method.
11. **How do you handle disclosure and the creator's agreement for Spark Ads?**
    Why: protects clients and creators.
12. **How do you agree creator fees, and who signs them off?**
    Why: money moves stay with the client; this shows how you prepare them.
13. **Which AI tools do you use for creative, and when do you label an ad as AI-generated?**
    Why: AI labels are a fast-changing rule.
14. **How do you plan ads for products that may reach under-18s, or restricted products?**
    Why: teen and policy rules are where accounts get into trouble.
15. **How do you judge premium formats like TopView against auction ads?**
    Why: shows whether you judge on effect or on price.
16. **How do you report to an owner who only looks at sales in their shop?**
    Why: shapes the agent's report voice.
17. **Tell me about an ad or account that got rejected or restricted. What changed?**
    Why: policy experience protects clients.
18. **Tell me about the hardest TikTok account you have worked on.**
    Why: a real story is worth more than any checklist.
19. **What do you check before you press launch?**
    Why: becomes the agent's quality bar.
