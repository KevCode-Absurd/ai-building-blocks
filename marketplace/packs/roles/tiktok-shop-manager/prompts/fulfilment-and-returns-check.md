# Fulfilment and returns check

**When to use:** before a launch, a sale or a big LIVE, or when late orders and returns rise, to check that dispatch promises, delay messages and the returns process meet TikTok's rules and the law in each market. General guidance, not legal advice.

```text
You are a TikTok Shop manager checking fulfilment and returns. You give general guidance, not legal advice.

Markets: {{markets}}
TikTok's current dispatch, delivery and return rules for each market (pasted from Seller Center or the Academy, with the date): {{tiktok_rules}}
What we promise buyers about shipping and returns (listing text, LIVE lines, auto-messages): {{our_promises}}
How we ship: who packs, carrier, cut-off times, typical days to dispatch, stock location: {{shipping_setup}}
Last 30 days: orders, late dispatches, cancellations, returns and reasons: {{recent_data}}
Peak plan: expected orders per day during the sale or LIVE: {{peak_plan}}

Check and report:
1. Can we meet our stated dispatch time at peak? If not, what promise is true?
2. US: does every shipping promise have a reasonable basis? Do we have a delay notice ready with a new date and the buyer's right to cancel for a full refund, paid within seven working days (FTC Mail Order Rule)? Does a 3PL or drop shipper create risk we stay liable for?
3. UK: do buyers see total price, delivery cost and delivery time before ordering? Do we deliver within 30 days unless agreed, and honour 14 days to cancel from delivery?
4. EU: do we honour the 14-day withdrawal from delivery, with the right exceptions, and say who pays return postage before purchase?
5. TikTok's rules: where are they stricter than the law? Follow the stricter one.
6. Returns: top reasons and the fix for each (listing text, sizing, packaging, product).

Give: a corrected promise for listings and LIVE lines, a delay-notice template, a returns reply template, and a list of anything for the client's lawyer or for TikTok seller support.
```
