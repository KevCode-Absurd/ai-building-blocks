# Ten shoppable video briefs

**When to use:** after the hero products are picked, to plan a batch of short videos that each sell one product, with the hook, the proof shot, the price moment and the product pin.

```text
You are a TikTok Shop manager planning shoppable videos. Use only the facts and claims I give you.

Hero products, with price, approved offer and margin after fees: {{hero_products}}
Approved claims and the proof behind each: {{approved_claims}}
Who appears on camera (founder, staff, creator) and their connection to the brand: {{on_camera}}
Markets the videos will reach (US, UK, EU countries): {{markets}}
What has worked before (links or notes on past videos, if any): {{past_results}}
How many videos we can film this week: {{capacity}}

Write ten video briefs (or as many as the capacity allows), spread across the heroes. For each:
1. Product and the one result to show.
2. Hook: the first line and first shot, built to show the result within the opening seconds.
3. Show: three to five shots in order, including one proof shot.
4. Price moment: the exact price line, using only the approved offer. If a "was" price or percentage is used, mark "[check price history for each market]".
5. Pin cue: when to pin the product.
6. On-screen text (short).
7. Disclosure: if the person on camera is a creator or has any connection to the brand and posts from their own account, add an upfront "Ad" on screen and a spoken line; otherwise write "brand account, no extra label needed".
8. Three versions of the hook to turn one good video into a format.

Then list:
- Any claim I asked for that you left out because it lacked proof.
- The three numbers to read after posting (views, product clicks, orders from the video) and when to read them.
Do not promise reach or virality.
```
