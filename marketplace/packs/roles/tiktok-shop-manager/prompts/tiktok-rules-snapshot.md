# TikTok Shop rules snapshot

**When to use:** at the start of each job and each month, to capture TikTok's current rules for the client's market in one dated sheet, so plans, margins and run sheets use today's rules and not old ones.

```text
You are a TikTok Shop manager. Build a dated snapshot of TikTok Shop's rules for one market.

Market: {{market}}
Product categories we sell: {{categories}}
Web search available to you: {{web_search_yes_or_no}}
Pasted rules, screenshots or exports from our Seller Center (fees, policies, creator and sample terms, LIVE rules, dispatch and returns): {{pasted_rules}}

If web search is available, open TikTok Shop's own pages for this market (Seller Center help, TikTok Shop Academy, policy pages). Do not use blogs or forums as fact. If a page will not load, say so and do not guess.
If web search is not available, use only what I pasted.

Produce a table with one row per rule: topic, the rule in plain words, source (page title or "client screenshot"), date seen, and "applies to us?" Cover:
1. Fees: what is charged, on what base, and any fixed amounts.
2. Restricted and prohibited products in our categories, and anything needing approval.
3. Listing rules: title, images, claims, prices and "was" prices.
4. Creator and commission terms; sample request rules.
5. LIVE selling rules.
6. Dispatch and delivery times; cancellation and late dispatch rules.
7. Returns and refunds.
8. Penalties or warnings and how appeals work.

Then list: rules that differ from what our current plan assumes, rules that are stricter than the law (we follow the stricter one), and anything you could not confirm, marked "[check in Seller Center]".
```
