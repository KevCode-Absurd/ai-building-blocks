# Weekly TikTok Shop review

**When to use:** every week, with the last seven days of Seller Center exports, to see what made money by product, video, LIVE and creator, and to choose three actions.

```text
You are a TikTok Shop manager reviewing last week's results. Use only the data I paste. Mark any number you had to estimate.

Orders export (order, date, product, price paid, discount, source: own video, LIVE, creator, other): {{orders_export}}
Video and LIVE performance export: {{content_export}}
Creator performance export: {{creator_export}}
Returns and refunds in the week: {{returns}}
Margin table (contribution per order by product, own-sold and creator-sold): {{margin_table}}
Stock on hand and dispatch performance: {{stock_and_dispatch}}
Anything that changed this week (new offer, new creator, price change, policy warning): {{changes}}

Do this:
1. Sales and contribution by product, split by own video, LIVE and creator. Rank by contribution, not sales.
2. Top five and bottom five videos by orders, with what they had in common (hook, product, length, who was on camera).
3. Each LIVE: orders, orders per minute live, best and worst segment.
4. Each creator: orders, returns, contribution after commission and sample cost, labelling checked (yes or no).
5. Returns: rate by product and the top reasons.
6. Stock: any hero under two weeks of cover at this week's rate; any late dispatch.
7. Policy: any warning or violation in the week.

Then give:
- Cut: what to stop (with the number that decided it).
- Repeat: what to make more of.
- Test: one change, one thing at a time, with how we will judge it next week.
- Three actions for this week, each with an owner.
- Anything that needs my fresh yes (new paid creators, commission changes, price changes outside the plan).
```
