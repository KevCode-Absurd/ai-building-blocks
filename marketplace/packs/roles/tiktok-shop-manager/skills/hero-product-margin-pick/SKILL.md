# Hero product and margin pick

## When to use

- Before launching a TikTok Shop, to choose what to lead with.
- Before a sale, a LIVE event or a creator push, to check each product can carry the discount and commission.
- When a product sells a lot but the bank balance does not move.
- When TikTok changes its fees, or a supplier or carrier changes prices.

## What you need

1. Product list: name, selling price, unit cost, packaging cost, shipping cost the seller pays, weight or size band if shipping varies.
2. The fee terms for each market from the client's Seller Center fee page, with the date they were read. With web search connected I also open TikTok Shop's fee pages for the market and note the date; if they disagree with the client's screen, the client's screen wins and I flag it.
3. Planned discount for each product (percent or amount) and any free shipping offer.
4. Creator commission rate the client is willing to offer, if creators will sell it.
5. Return or refund rate per product (or the category average from their own store), and the cost of one return (lost shipping, damaged stock).
6. Stock on hand and restock lead time.
7. Any results so far: past TikTok videos, best sellers on other channels.

**Access:** level 1 is enough (exports or screenshots). Level 2 lets me save the margin table in the client's shared sheet; level 3 lets me update it each week from Seller Center exports.

## Steps

### 1. Confirm the fee basis

Write down exactly how the client's fees apply: as a percentage of what (price before or after discount, with or without shipping), any fixed amount per order, and any extra fees for programmes the client uses. If anything is unclear, mark it "[check in Seller Center]" and show the result both ways.

### 2. Build the contribution per order

For each product, for each market:

- Price after discount = price − discount.
- TikTok fees = the fee terms applied to the right base.
- Creator commission = commission rate × the base the client's terms use (only on creator-sold orders).
- Returns allowance = return rate × cost of one return.
- Contribution per order = price after discount − TikTok fees − creator commission − product cost − packaging − seller-paid shipping − returns allowance.
- Contribution margin = contribution per order ÷ price after discount.

Show two rows per product: sold by the shop's own videos (no commission) and sold by a creator (with commission).

### 3. Find the discount ceiling

For each product, find the largest discount that keeps contribution at the client's minimum (or at zero if they have none). Show it as a price floor. Any planned offer below the floor is a red flag.

### 4. Score the camera test

For each product, answer yes or no:

- Does it show a clear result on camera in the first seconds (before and after, texture, a gadget doing its job)?
- Can it be shown honestly without a claim the client cannot prove?
- Is it allowed in this category and market under TikTok's rules [check in Seller Center]?
- Is there stock for the plan, with a restock date?
- Does it suit the age of TikTok's main audience? (Pew's 2026 survey found TikTok used far more by adults under 30 than over 65.)

### 5. Pick the heroes

Rank products by contribution per creator-sold order at the planned discount, then apply the camera test. Pick two or three heroes. A hero needs: positive contribution on creator-sold orders at the planned discount, a yes on all camera questions, and enough stock. Name one backup.

Products that fail on margin are not dropped from the shop; they are dropped from discounts, LIVE offers and creator pushes until costs or prices change. Suggest a fix for each: a bundle, a higher price, a cheaper pack, or no discount.

### 6. Write the decision

One line per hero on why, one line per dropped product on what would change the answer.

## Checks

- Every fee line cites the Seller Center page and date, or is marked "[check in Seller Center]".
- Commission sits on its own line and only on creator-sold orders.
- The returns line is filled in, even as an estimate labelled "estimate".
- The discount ceiling is shown for every product.
- No hero has negative contribution on creator-sold orders.
- Stock covers at least the first planned LIVE and a week of videos.
- Figures add up; spot-check one row by hand.
- No claim in the "why" line that the client cannot prove.

## Output format

1. **Fee basis** (three lines, with source and date).
2. **Margin table:** product, price, discount, price after discount, TikTok fees, commission, cost, packaging, shipping, returns allowance, contribution (own video), contribution (creator), margin %, discount ceiling.
3. **Camera test:** yes or no per question per product.
4. **Heroes:** two or three, each with a one-line why, plus one backup.
5. **Not now:** products held back from discounts and creator pushes, with the fix for each.
6. **Open questions** for the client.

## Example of good output

*Brightwick Home (a made-up business). The fee terms below are made-up example figures, not TikTok's: 9% of the price after discount plus $0.30 per order. Creator commission 15%. Planned discount 20%.*

**Fee basis:** 9% of price after discount + $0.30 per order, from the client's Seller Center screenshot dated 6 Nov [example]. Commission on price after discount.

| Product | Price | After 20% off | Fees | Commission | Cost | Pack | Ship | Returns | Contribution (creator) | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reed diffuser | $34.00 | $27.20 | $2.75 | $4.08 | $7.50 | $1.20 | $5.80 | $0.36 | **$5.51** | 20% |
| Room spray | $18.00 | $14.40 | $1.60 | $2.16 | $3.20 | $0.60 | $4.10 | $0.15 | **$2.59** | 18% |
| Votive candle | $16.00 | $12.80 | $1.45 | $1.92 | $4.10 | $0.90 | $4.60 | $0.20 | **−$0.37** | −3% |

**Heroes:**
1. Reed diffuser: best contribution, and the reeds darkening as they soak up oil is a clear shot.
2. Room spray: smaller margin but a fast, visible spray-and-react hook; good add-on in LIVE bundles.

**Backup:** none yet; test a diffuser refill pack.

**Not now:** Votive candle loses $0.37 per creator-sold order at 20% off. Fix: sell it only as a bundle add-on at full price, or raise to $19 and test.

**Open questions:** Does your fee apply before or after the discount? Is the 4% return rate on diffusers from your store data?
