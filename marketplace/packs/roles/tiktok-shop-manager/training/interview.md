# Interview: TikTok Shop manager

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the manager's work (for example, LIVE questions for someone who only runs creator selling).

## How you pick and price

1. **Walk me through the last time you chose hero products for a TikTok Shop. What did you have on the table, and what decided it?**
   Why it matters: shows the real picking method, not the textbook one.
2. **Show me a margin table you trust. Which cost lines do new sellers forget?**
   Why it matters: missed fee, commission or returns lines turn best sellers into losses.
3. **Where do you read TikTok's fees and rules, and how do you keep up when they change?**
   Why it matters: the agent must know which sources to trust and how often to check them.
4. **When have you dropped a product that was selling well? What told you to?**
   Why it matters: reveals the line between volume and profit in your judgement.
5. **How do you set a launch offer without training buyers to wait for discounts?**
   Why it matters: offer depth and timing decide whether the shop makes money after week one.

## How you make content and run LIVE

6. **What makes the first seconds of a shoppable video work for a product? Show me one that worked and one that flopped.**
   Why it matters: hooks are the craft the agent has to copy.
7. **How do you turn one video that sold into a format with versions?**
   Why it matters: repeatable formats beat one-off hits.
8. **Walk me through a LIVE run sheet you wrote. How did you choose the product order and the offer timing?**
   Why it matters: LIVE structure is hard to learn from outside.
9. **What do you never let a host say on a LIVE, and why?**
   Why it matters: shows how you handle claims, urgency and price lines under pressure.
10. **How do you make sure countdowns, stock calls and "was" prices are true?**
    Why it matters: false urgency and fake reductions are where sellers get fined.

## How you work with creators

11. **How do you decide which sample requests to approve? Give me a recent yes and a recent no.**
    Why it matters: sample spend is easy to waste.
12. **How do you set a commission rate, and when do you raise it for a creator?**
    Why it matters: the rate must sit under the product's margin.
13. **What do you put in a creator brief about disclosure, and what do you do when a creator skips the label?**
    Why it matters: brands share responsibility for creators' posts.
14. **Tell me about a creator partnership that went wrong. What would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: real failures teach the agent the warning signs.

## Fulfilment, returns and rules

15. **What dispatch promise do you make, and how do you check you can keep it during a big LIVE?**
    Why it matters: late orders cost refunds and shop standing.
16. **What are the top return reasons you see, and which listing or product fixes cut them?**
    Why it matters: returns quietly eat margin.
17. **Have you had a listing or video taken down or a policy warning? What happened and how did you fix it?**
    Why it matters: shows how you read and appeal TikTok's rules.
18. **Which products or claims do you refuse to sell on TikTok Shop, even when a client asks?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's red lines.

## Clients and reporting

19. **What does your weekly review look like? What three numbers do you look at first?**
    Why it matters: the agent's report should match how an expert reads results.
20. **How do you explain to a client that their favourite product cannot be a hero?**
    Why it matters: captures your voice when giving bad news.
21. **What access do you ask for, and what do you never do without the client's yes?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's approval habits.
22. **Tell me about your hardest TikTok Shop job. What made it hard and what did you do?**
    Why it matters: the story holds the judgement calls no checklist covers.
