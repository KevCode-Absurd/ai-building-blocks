# 30-day TikTok Shop launch

**Trigger:** a client is opening a TikTok Shop, or relaunching one that stalled. Runs over 30 days, then hands over to the weekly shop cycle.

## Steps

### Days 1 to 3: rules, access and numbers

1. Ask which access level the client grants for Seller Center, the store, the TikTok account and email, and the limits (products, prices, sample budget, dates). Write "Access I have". Level 1 until they answer.
2. Run the **tiktok-rules-snapshot** prompt for each market: fees, restricted products, listing, creator, sample, LIVE, dispatch and returns rules, dated. With web search, from TikTok's own pages; without it, from the client's screenshots.
3. Check the seller basics: in the US, whether INFORM Act verification applies; in the EU, the seller's contact details and product safety checks.

### Days 3 to 6: heroes and offers

4. Run the **hero-product-margin-pick** skill. Pick two or three heroes and one backup, with discount ceilings.
5. Agree the launch offer for each hero, inside its ceiling. If any "was" price is planned, check price history for each market (pricing strategist kit).

### Days 5 to 10: listings

6. Run the **tiktok-shop-listing** skill for the heroes first, then the rest. Level 2: upload sheet or drafts. Level 3: publish once the client approves the sheet.

### Days 7 to 14: videos

7. Run the **shoppable-video-briefs** prompt for the first two weeks of videos. Film the strongest hooks first.
8. Run the **claims-price-and-disclosure-check** prompt on every script.
9. Level 3: post approved videos to the agreed calendar. Level 2: save drafts for the client to post.

### Days 10 to 20: creators

10. Run the **creator-affiliates-and-samples** skill: commission ceiling, sample rule, creator brief.
11. Send samples to approved creators within the budget (level 3) or prepare the list (level 2). Invitations into paid commission offers go to the client for a fresh yes.

### Days 12 to 25: first LIVE sessions

12. Run the **fulfilment-and-returns-check** prompt against the peak plan.
13. Run the **live-selling-run-sheet** skill for the first LIVE: short, one or two heroes, a true offer.
14. After each LIVE, pull the numbers and change one thing for the next.

### Days 26 to 30: review and hand over

15. Run the **weekly-shop-review** prompt on the full month.
16. Write the launch report: what sold, what made money, what to repeat, what to cut, and the weekly rhythm going forward.

## Outputs

- Access table and dated TikTok rules snapshot.
- Margin table, heroes and offers.
- Live listings (or an upload sheet).
- Video briefs and a posting calendar; posted videos at level 3.
- Sample rule, creator brief, request log and post check log.
- LIVE run sheets and after-LIVE notes.
- Delay-notice and returns templates.
- Launch report with three next actions.

## What needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits for each tool, before any work.
- Heroes, prices and offers, before listings or videos use them.
- The listing sheet before publishing (level 3) or uploading (level 2).
- The sample rule and budget, once.
- Each new creator invited into a paid commission offer, and any commission change: a fresh yes every time.
- Any "was" price, deadline or price change outside the approved plan.
- At level 3, the action list and limits before the first live action.
- The host always goes live themselves.
