# Training interview: translator

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, confidential text and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe jobs in general terms.

1. **Which language pairs and directions do you work in, and which countries' variants do you know well?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's real range and stops it claiming pairs it cannot judge.

2. **What kinds of content do you translate most: websites, product listings, help articles, apps, subtitles, marketing, documents?**
   Why it matters: shapes the specialty and which skills to lean on.

3. **Walk me through your last job from the client's first message to delivery.**
   Why it matters: reveals the real sequence of work and where the time goes.

4. **What do you ask a client before you start, and what do you do when they cannot answer?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions that turn a thin request into a brief.

5. **How do you build and maintain a glossary? Show me one entry you argued about.**
   Why it matters: records how terms are chosen and defended.

6. **When do you use machine translation, and when do you refuse to?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's threshold for MT by risk and content type.

7. **What errors do engines make in your language pair that a client would never spot?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent pair-specific traps to hunt during post-editing.

8. **How do you decide between staying close to the source and rewriting for the reader?**
   Why it matters: captures the expert's fidelity judgement, section by section.

9. **Tell me about a slogan, joke or product name that did not survive translation. What did you do?**
   Why it matters: records a real transcreation method and how the client chose.

10. **Which locale details catch people out in your target market: formats, address, names, units, references?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a market-specific checklist.

11. **How do you handle UI strings with placeholders, plurals and length limits?**
    Why it matters: grounds software localisation habits in practice.

12. **If you do subtitles: what limits do you work to, and how do you condense a line?**
    Why it matters: captures the condensing judgement that makes subtitles readable.

13. **How do you check your own work before delivery? Show me your checklist.**
    Why it matters: becomes the agent's quality bar.

14. **What do you put in your notes to the client or the reviewer?**
    Why it matters: sets the format and tone of the agent's notes.

15. **Tell me about a translation of yours that caused a problem after publication. What did you change afterwards?**
    Why it matters: surfaces the checks the expert now never skips.

16. **When has a client asked for something you thought was wrong, such as a literal translation or a misleading claim? How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert pushes back without losing the client.

17. **Which documents do you refer to certified or sworn translators, and how do you tell the client?**
    Why it matters: keeps the agent clear about its limits and referrals.

18. **Which tools do you use (engines, translation memory, platforms, subtitle editors), and what does each do well or badly?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's tool advice in real use.

19. **How do you handle confidential or personal data in source files?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's data-handling habits.

20. **What does a native reviewer most often change in your work, and why?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent where its own output is weakest.

21. **How do you price and time a job, and what makes you say a deadline is unrealistic?**
    Why it matters: helps the agent set honest expectations about time and review.
