# Monthly translation upkeep

**Cadence:** monthly, or at each product release, for a business that already publishes in more than one language.

**Trigger:** new or changed source content (product pages, help articles, app strings, emails, video), or a complaint that a translated page is wrong or out of date.

## Steps

1. **Collect what changed.** From the CMS, localisation platform or repository, list new and edited source items since the last cycle, and translated pages that are now older than their source. Work at level 1 until the client grants more.
2. **Check the glossary first.** Add new product and feature names, retire old ones, and get native sign-off on new entries (skill: glossary-and-style-sheet). Update the engine's glossary and keep the master file.
3. **Pre-translate from memory.** Reuse 100% and exact-context matches from the translation memory; treat them as suggestions and read them in context.
4. **Translate the rest by risk.** Machine translation with full post-editing for help and product text (skill: machine-translation-post-editing); human translation for anything that sells or carries legal weight (skill: translate-and-localise-content); subtitles for new videos (skill: captions-and-subtitles).
5. **Quality sample.** Log errors by type and severity for a sample of each content type and compare with last month. Rising terminology errors usually mean the glossary is behind.
6. **Native review** of anything public, from the bilingual table.
   **Approval point:** the client or their reviewer signs off the batch.
7. **Publish or hand over.**
   - Level 2: save unpublished in the tool and send previews.
   - Level 3: publish the confirmed items within the client's limits, log each action and report it.
8. **Fix the reported problems.** Trace each customer complaint to its cause (source unclear, glossary gap, engine error, layout) and fix the cause, not only the sentence.
9. **Monthly note.** Volumes, methods used, error trend, glossary changes, pages still out of date, and anything to re-check (for example new AI labelling guidance in the EU, or a platform's subtitle spec).

## Outputs

- Change list and out-of-date page list.
- Updated glossary and translation memory.
- Translated, reviewed and published items, with an action log.
- Error trend table.
- Monthly note with next month's priorities.

## Needs the client's approval

- New glossary entries and any change to approved terms.
- Each batch of public translations before it goes live.
- The level 3 action list and limits, and a new yes before anything outside them: a new language, overwriting human-approved translations, or deleting memory or glossary entries.
