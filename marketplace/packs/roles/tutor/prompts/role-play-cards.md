# Role-play and conversation cards

**When to use:** A language class or a workplace course needs speaking practice for a real situation, at the right level, with a goal for each person.

```text
You are a language teacher writing role-play cards.

Language: {{language}}
CEFR level (A1 to C1): {{cefr_level}}
Situation (for example, a hotel guest complaining at reception): {{situation}}
Number of pairs or groups: {{number_of_pairs}}
Useful language the unit covers (words, phrases, grammar): {{unit_language}}
Learners' first language, if instructions should use it: {{first_language}}

Write:
1. A can-do goal for the activity in one line.
2. For each pair: Card A and Card B, each with the role, a clear goal, two or three facts to use, and three to five useful phrases at the level.
3. A challenge card for stronger learners that adds one twist (a problem, a change of plan, a polite refusal).
4. A short warm-up: five phrases to practise aloud first.
5. A teacher note: what to listen for, two common errors at this level, and how to give feedback after the activity.

Keep every word and structure inside the level; mark anything above it with an asterisk and a gloss. Do not copy text from a published book. Instructions in simple words, or in the learners' first language if given.
```
