# Training interview: UI designer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, unreleased screens under a confidentiality agreement and anything that identifies a user.

1. **Which UI work do you do most: foundations, screen specs, component sheets, reviews or dark mode? Which do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of what it takes on.

2. **Walk me through your last screen design job, from the brief to the developer's first build.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the time goes.

3. **What do you ask before you design anything?**
   Why it matters: captures intake questions that stop the work going in the wrong direction.

4. **How do you set a type scale? What do you start from, and how many styles do you usually end with?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's method and their sense of "enough".

5. **How do you choose spacing values, and how do you keep them consistent across a team?**
   Why it matters: spacing drift is the most common sign of unfinished UI; the agent needs a method.

6. **How do you decide what the eye should see first on a screen, and how do you check it worked?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the expert's hierarchy judgement and their test for it.

7. **How do you handle a brand colour that fails contrast?**
   Why it matters: a frequent conflict between brand and accessibility; the agent needs the expert's way through it.

8. **How do you build dark mode? Tell me about one that went wrong.**
   Why it matters: captures dark mode habits and real failure stories.

9. **Which states do developers most often forget, and how do you make sure they are specified?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's state checklist from experience, not theory.

10. **How do you adapt one design for iOS, Android and the web? What do you keep the same and what do you change?**
    Why it matters: records platform judgement, including Liquid Glass and Material 3 Expressive habits.

11. **What does a spec from you look like? What do developers thank you for including?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's handoff standard.

12. **How do you review someone else's screens without making it personal?**
    Why it matters: shapes the tone of the agent's before-and-after notes.

13. **Tell me about a time a client wanted something you thought looked wrong. What happened?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert balances taste, evidence and the client's call.

14. **Which tools do you use for contrast, specs and handoff, and which have you dropped?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent in real tools and the reasons behind choices.

15. **What do you check on the built screen that you cannot check in the design file?**
    Why it matters: captures post-build review habits.

16. **How do you keep up with Apple and Google guideline changes?**
    Why it matters: tells the agent which change logs and sources the expert trusts.

17. **Which tasks do you let AI do, and what do you always check yourself?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's review points for AI-drafted specs and values.

18. **What access do clients usually give you in Figma, the repo or the site builder, and what do you never do even with full access?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent's level 2 and 3 behaviour in the expert's real limits.

19. **What would make you refuse or pause a job?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's red lines, such as deceptive designs or unlicensed fonts.

20. **What do you wish clients understood about UI design before hiring you?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a plain explanation it can use at intake.
