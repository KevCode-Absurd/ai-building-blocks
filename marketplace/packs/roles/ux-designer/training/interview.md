# Training interview: UX designer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, participant details, screenshots under a confidentiality agreement and anything that could identify a research participant.

1. **Which kinds of UX work do you do most: structure and menus, screens and flows, interface words, or research and testing? Which do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the limits of what it should take on.

2. **Walk me through your last project from the first message to the hand-over.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the expert spends time.

3. **What do you ask a client before you look at a single screen?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions that stop work going in the wrong direction.

4. **How do you work out what users actually come to do, when the client only has opinions?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's sources of evidence (search terms, tickets, analytics, interviews) and how they rank tasks.

5. **When you review a flow without testing, how do you decide how bad each problem is?**
   Why it matters: pulls out the expert's severity judgement and the reasons behind it.

6. **Tell me about a test where users did something you did not expect. What changed afterwards?**
   Why it matters: gives the agent a real example of why watching beats guessing.

7. **How do you write test tasks that do not lead people to the answer?**
   Why it matters: captures task-writing habits that keep findings honest.

8. **How many people do you test with, how many rounds, and when do you stop?**
   Why it matters: grounds the agent's research planning in real budgets and timelines.

9. **What does a wireframe or spec from you look like, and what must it include before a developer gets it?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's hand-over standard and the states it must never skip.

10. **How do you settle an argument about the main menu?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert uses evidence (card sorts, tree tests) to move a team.

11. **Show me an error message you rewrote. What was wrong with the old one?**
    Why it matters: gives concrete before-and-after microcopy in the expert's own voice.

12. **How do you keep the same thing named the same way across a product?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's term list and voice guide habits.

13. **Which accessibility checks do you do on every screen, and which do you hand to a specialist?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's accessibility floor and when it should refer out.

14. **Has a client asked you for a dark pattern, such as a hidden cancel button? What did you do?**
    Why it matters: records how the expert pushes back and what honest alternative they offered.

15. **Which tools do you use for design, research and analytics, and what access do you ask clients for?**
    Why it matters: grounds access.md and the levels the agent should ask for.

16. **How do you use AI in your work today, and where do you not trust it?**
    Why it matters: sets the line between AI drafts and real user evidence.

17. **Tell me about a design of yours that failed after launch. What did you miss?**
    Why it matters: surfaces the checks the expert now adds to every project.

18. **How do you present findings to a founder who is attached to their design?**
    Why it matters: captures the expert's tone and how they frame hard news.

19. **What do you check after a change goes live, and when?**
    Why it matters: makes sure the agent closes the loop with measures, not just designs.

20. **What would you tell a new UX designer to stop doing in their first year?**
    Why it matters: captures the common mistakes the agent should avoid and spot in others' work.
