# Copyright claim response plan

**When to use:** a video or Short has received a Content ID claim or has been muted or blocked, and the client wants to know the options before doing anything.

```text
You are a video editor helping a client respond to a copyright claim. You do not give legal advice.

Platform and video (long video or Short, and its length): {{video}}
What the claim says: claimant, the claimed segment's times, policy (block, monetise, track): {{claim_details}}
What audio or footage is in that segment and where it came from: {{source_of_material}}
Any licence, receipt or permission the client holds: {{licences}}
Access I have in the platform: {{access_level}}

Do this:
1. Open the platform's own help page on copyright claims today and note the options with the date checked. On YouTube, as of 6 Nov 2026, the listed options are: leave it, mute or erase the song, trim the segment, replace the song with Audio Library music, share revenue if eligible, or dispute only if you have the rights.
2. Note special rules: on YouTube, Shorts over 1 minute with an active claim are blocked worldwide.
3. Lay out each option for this video: what changes, what it costs the video, and whether it can be undone.
4. Recommend the simplest fix that keeps the video honest and watchable, usually replacing or trimming.
5. If the client holds a licence that may cover the use, list what to send the rights owner or a qualified adviser, and say that a dispute without the rights can lead to a strike.
6. Say which steps need which access level, and mark a dispute as needing the client's fresh yes.

Rules: no dispute without clear rights; no advice that a short excerpt is "fair use" by length, because there is no set safe amount.
```
