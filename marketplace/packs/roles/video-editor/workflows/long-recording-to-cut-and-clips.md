# Long recording to a finished cut and clips

**Trigger:** the client has a long recording (podcast episode, webinar, interview, talk) and wants a tightened version, short clips, captions and chapters. Usually one to five working days depending on length and review speed.

## Steps

1. **Intake (level 1).** Ask for the timed transcript, shot notes, platforms, target lengths, clip count, caption style, editing tool and music licences. Ask which access level the client grants in each tool and write it under "Access I have". Use the questions in access.md.
2. **Tighten (skill: edit-decision-list-from-transcript; prompt: tighten-to-target-length).** Build the EDL to the target length, flag meaning risks and plan cover for jump cuts. For multi-camera shoots, add the angle plan (prompt: multicam-angle-plan).
3. **Meaning check (prompt: meaning-check-on-cuts).** Rebuild the kept text and check every cut against the source.
   **Approval point 1:** the client (and the speaker, if the client wants) approves the EDL and every flagged cut.
4. **Clips (skill: short-clip-finder).** Pick and plan clips from the approved cut, with hook text from the speaker's words, reframe notes, platform limits (checked that day on each platform's help page) and music status.
5. **Cue notes and chapters (skill: cue-notes-and-chapters).** B-roll, pacing, graphics and music cues for the long cut; chapters and description.
6. **Captions (skill: clip-captions-srt).** One caption file for the long cut and one clip-relative file per clip, from the final audio.
7. **Build.**
   - Level 1: hand over the EDL, clip table, cue sheet and caption files, with tool steps (prompt: editor-handoff-steps).
   - Level 2: apply the edits in a draft composition or sequence named "DRAFT" in a connected tool (for example through Descript's connector), and export exchange files for other editors.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the rough cut, clips, captions, chapters and every music track.
8. **Deliver.**
   - Level 2: upload as private or unlisted with captions, chapters and description.
   - Level 3: show the action list (channels, videos, dates and times) and get a yes, then publish or schedule within those limits. Log each upload.
9. **Watch for problems.** In the first days after posting, check for copyright claims, blocked Shorts and caption errors. If a claim arrives, stop and use the copyright-claim-response prompt; a dispute needs the client's fresh yes.

## Outputs

- Edit decision list with meaning-risk flags and cover plan.
- Angle plan for multi-camera shoots.
- Clip table with hooks, lengths, ratios and music status.
- Cue sheet, chapters and description.
- Caption files for the long cut and each clip.
- Tool handover steps or exchange files; drafts at level 2.
- Uploads (level 2) or scheduled posts (level 3), with the action log.

## Needs the client's approval

- Access level and limits for each tool.
- The EDL and every cut flagged as a meaning risk (point 1).
- Rough cut, clips, captions, chapters and music (point 2).
- Any licence bought or accepted, each time; payment stays with the client.
- Disputing a copyright claim, deleting or replacing a live video, or posting to a new account.
- Anything outside the agreed limits.
