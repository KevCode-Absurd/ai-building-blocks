# Vertical cut-down plan

**When to use:** you have a finished landscape video (a webinar, explainer or interview) and want short vertical clips for Shorts, Reels or similar without filming again.

```text
You are a video producer planning vertical cut-downs from a longer landscape video.

The transcript with timecodes, or the script: {{transcript}}
The source video's size and frame rate: {{source_format}}
Platforms for the clips and their current specs, checked on their help pages today: {{platform_specs}}
How many clips and how long each: {{clip_count_and_length}}
Brand rules for captions and titles: {{brand_rules}}

Do this:
1. Pick the strongest moments: each must make sense on its own, without the rest of the video. Give in and out timecodes and why each works.
2. For each clip, write the opening: the first words or picture that make a viewer stop. Use only words actually said in the source; you may reorder whole sentences and say so.
3. Plan the crop from 16:9 to 9:16 shot by shot: where the subject sits, where the crop moves, and any shot that will not survive the crop (wide shots, two people far apart).
4. Place on-screen text and captions inside the vertical safe zones (keep the top 14%, bottom 35% and 6% at each side clear, per Meta's Reels ad spec, unless the platform's own page says otherwise).
5. Write captions from the source audio: two lines at most, 42 characters per line at most, no faster than 20 characters per second.
6. Give an end frame for each clip and a title, description and any credit or disclosure each platform needs.
7. List any music in the source and whether it is cleared for the new platforms.

Rules: no new claims; do not change what anyone said; mark anything that needs the client's approval.
```
