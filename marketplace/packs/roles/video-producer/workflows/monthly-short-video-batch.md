# Monthly short video batch

**Cadence:** monthly. A business wants a steady run of short vertical videos (Shorts, Reels and similar) from one filming session or from longer videos it already has.

**Time:** about one week of work spread across the month: planning in week 1, filming or cutting in week 2, review and scheduling in week 3, results in week 4.

## Steps

1. **Review last month (level 1).** Read the client's analytics for last month's clips: views, audience retention, average view duration, follows and clicks. Note which openings held viewers and which lost them. With no access, ask for screenshots or an export.
2. **Pick the ideas.** From the client's product facts, questions customers ask and last month's results, choose the month's ideas, one idea per clip. Re-use winners as new versions; drop formats that lost viewers early.
3. **Check specs and music.** Open each platform's help page and write the current spec with the date (for example Shorts up to 3 minutes, square or vertical, as checked on 6 Nov 2026). Confirm which music is cleared for each platform and for business use (prompt: music-and-footage-rights-log).
4. **Plan the shots (skill: storyboard-and-shoot-plan).** A short shot table per clip, text inside the vertical safe zones, and one shoot plan that films every clip in one session (prompt: shoot-day-plan). If cutting from long videos instead, use the vertical-cutdown-plan prompt.
   **Approval point 1:** the client approves the month's ideas, scripts and any claims.
5. **Film or cut.** The client or their filmer shoots from the plan; consent forms are signed before filming. At level 2, assemble rough cuts and captions in a connected editor as drafts.
6. **Check every clip (skill: delivery-and-rights-check).** Length, safe areas, captions from the final audio, nothing flashing more than three times a second, every song and clip cleared, any realistic AI content disclosed.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the final clips, captions and posting calendar.
7. **Schedule.**
   - Level 1: deliver files, captions and a posting calendar for the client to post.
   - Level 2: upload as drafts or private with captions and descriptions.
   - Level 3: show the action list (accounts, clips, dates and times) and get a yes, then schedule within those limits. Ask again before posting to a new account, changing the agreed dates or deleting a live clip. Log each post.
8. **Report.** At month end, report results against last month and the plan for next month.

## Outputs

- Monthly idea list with one idea per clip.
- Shot tables and one shoot plan, or a cut-down plan.
- Rights log for the month's music and clips.
- Final clips with captions and a posting calendar.
- Drafts uploaded (level 2) or scheduled posts within limits (level 3).
- Action log and a monthly results note.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level for each tool and the level 3 limits (accounts, number of posts, dates).
- The month's ideas, scripts and claims (point 1).
- Final clips, captions and calendar (point 2).
- Any licence bought or accepted, each time; payment stays with the client.
- Written consent from anyone recognisable on camera.
- Anything outside the agreed limits.
