# Training interview: website designer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, logins, unpublished client content and anything under a confidentiality agreement.

1. **Which builders do you work in most, and which do you turn down? Why?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and the platforms it should hand on.

2. **Walk me through your last site from the first message to launch day.**
   Why it matters: shows the real order of work and where the time goes.

3. **What do you ask a client before you open the builder?**
   Why it matters: captures the intake questions that stop rework.

4. **How do you decide which pages a small business needs, and when do you merge or cut pages?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's judgement on page lists and menus.

5. **What does your build sheet or plan look like? Show me one, with client details removed.**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's hand-over format.

6. **How do you choose between Squarespace, Wix, Webflow and Framer for a client?**
   Why it matters: pulls out real trade-offs beyond feature lists.

7. **When do you plan a CMS, and how do you name collections and fields so the client can use them?**
   Why it matters: captures CMS habits that keep sites editable.

8. **What is your rule for custom code? Tell me about a snippet that broke after an update.**
   Why it matters: teaches the agent when code is worth the risk.

9. **How do you handle images: sizes, focal points, alt text and file weight?**
   Why it matters: images drive both speed and accessibility.

10. **What do you check on a phone that clients usually miss?**
    Why it matters: records the expert's phone-first checks.

11. **How do you check accessibility on a builder site, and what do you tell clients about "compliance"?**
    Why it matters: sets honest wording and a repeatable check.

12. **How do you set up cookie consent for clients with EU or UK visitors, and what goes wrong?**
    Why it matters: grounds the agent in real banner set-ups and failure modes.

13. **Tell me about a domain move that went wrong, or nearly did.**
    Why it matters: DNS and email are where launches fail; the story teaches caution.

14. **What access do you ask clients for, and what do you never accept?**
    Why it matters: shapes how the agent asks for access levels and limits.

15. **What does your launch checklist include, and what has it caught?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's checklist into the agent's quality bar.

16. **How do you write page copy when the client sends almost nothing?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert avoids filler and invented claims.

17. **A client wants a feature the builder can't do. How do you handle that conversation?**
    Why it matters: captures plain pushback and the simplest workaround.

18. **How do you hand over so the client can edit next year without you?**
    Why it matters: sets the editor guide standard.

19. **What do you check a month after launch?**
    Why it matters: builds the monthly care routine on real habits.

20. **Which mistakes do you see most when you take over someone else's builder site?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a list of things to look for first.
