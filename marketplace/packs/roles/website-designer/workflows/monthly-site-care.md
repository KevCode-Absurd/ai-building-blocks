# Monthly site care

**Cadence:** once a month, plus after any builder or template update, a new tag or tool, or a complaint from a visitor.

**Time:** about one to two hours a month for a 5 to 10 page site.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish). Most of this runs at L1; fixes follow the level the client granted.

## Steps

1. **Confirm access, L1.** Check "Access I have" is still right: the site, the level and the limits. Note any seats that should be removed.
2. **Forms and contact, L1.** Submit one test entry per form and confirm it arrives. Check phone numbers, opening hours and addresses against the client's latest facts.
3. **Speed, L1 (tool: PageSpeed Insights or the builder's speed dashboard).** Record field Core Web Vitals for the home page and one inner page against last month. If LCP, INP or CLS moved out of "good", find the change that caused it: a new image, embed, font or script.
4. **Accessibility spot check, L1 (prompt: accessibility-quick-pass).** Check any page added or changed this month: contrast, alt text, labels, link text, tap targets, keyboard focus.
5. **Consent and tags, L1 (prompt: cookie-banner-check).** In a private window, list cookies before any click and after reject. Any new tag gets a consent category or is removed.
6. **Content, L1.** Out-of-date offers, past events still listed, broken links, CMS items with empty alt text or missing descriptions.
7. **Builder changes, L1.** Read the builder's release notes or help pages for changes to editors, code, banner or plans that affect this site, and check plan limits (pages, CMS items) against current use.
8. **Fix list.** Each issue with page, fix, level needed and how to verify. Upgrading a plan or buying an app stays with the client; I list what and why.
   **Approval point:** the client approves the fix list, or the fixes fall inside the level 3 limits already agreed.
9. **Fix, L2 then L3.** Duplicate a page before a large change, fix, check on a phone and desktop, then publish within the limits. Deleting pages or CMS items needs a fresh yes. Log every action.
10. **Report, L1.** One page: what was checked, what changed, the numbers against last month, what is waiting on the client.

## Outputs

- Monthly report with Core Web Vitals against last month.
- Fix list and action log.
- Notes on builder changes and plan limits.
- Updated "Access I have" record.

## Needs the client's approval

- The fix list, unless the fixes fall inside level 3 limits already agreed.
- A fresh yes for deleting content, DNS changes, or inviting people.
- Any purchase: plan upgrades, apps, domains.
