# Plan, build and launch a small site

**Trigger:** a client wants a new 5 to 10 page site in Squarespace, Wix, Webflow or Framer, or a full rebuild of a template site.

**Time:** usually two to four weeks, most of it waiting for text, photos and approvals.

**Access:** steps are marked L1 (read), L2 (read and draft) or L3 (read, write and publish). Until the client grants more, everything runs at L1 and the client does the L2 and L3 steps from my build sheet.

## Steps

1. **Intake, L1.** Business, visitors, the one main action, pages wanted, must-have features, who edits after launch, where visitors live, who controls the domain and email. Ask which level the client grants for each tool and the limits (which site, which kinds of change, until when), and write it down as "Access I have".
2. **Builder check (prompt: choose-a-site-builder), L1.** Only if the builder isn't chosen. Buying the plan is the client's step.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms the builder and plan.
3. **Page list and build sheet (skill: site-build-sheet), L1.** Page jobs, sections in the builder's block names, image slots, style settings, open questions.
4. **CMS plan (skill: cms-collection-plan), L1.** Only if content repeats: collections, fields, template pages, sample items, limits.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the page list, build sheet and CMS plan.
5. **Words and images (prompts: page-copy-for-blocks, image-spec-and-alt-text), L1.** Copy from the client's facts with [add] for gaps; image sizes, focal points and alt text.
   **Approval point 3:** the client approves the copy and fills every [add].
6. **Build, L2.** Set global styles, header and footer, then pages as hidden or unpublished pages, CMS collections with draft items, and any snippet switched off (skill: builder-code-snippet). Log every action. At L1, the client or a helper builds from the sheet.
7. **Launch check (skill: site-launch-check; prompts: accessibility-quick-pass, cookie-banner-check), L1.** Phone and desktop walk, forms, titles, accessibility basics, speed baseline, consent by region, DNS plan.
8. **Fixes, L2.** Fix on the hidden pages or staging version and re-check.
   **Approval point 4:** the client reviews the preview on their own phone and approves the launch action list.
9. **Domain, client or fresh yes.** The client buys or renews the domain. Changing DNS records needs a fresh yes for that change, even at L3: save the current records, add the builder's records, keep MX records, and expect up to 48 hours.
10. **Go live, L3 or the client.** At L3: show the action list and limits, get one yes, publish, remove the site password, check every page live, submit a test form, and log each action. Stop on any error.
11. **Two weeks later, L1.** Re-check forms, analytics after consent and Core Web Vitals, and hand over the editor guide and action log.

## Outputs

- "Access I have" record.
- Page list, build sheet, style settings and, if needed, a CMS plan.
- Page copy, image list and alt text.
- Launch check with fix list and DNS plan.
- Action log (what, where, when, result).
- Editor guide: how to edit pages, add CMS items and publish.

## Needs the client's approval

- The access level and limits for each tool at the start, and any change to them.
- The builder and plan (point 1), the plans (point 2), the copy (point 3) and the launch list (point 4).
- A fresh yes for each DNS change or domain connection, for deleting anything, and for inviting new people.
- Buying a domain, plan, app or template is always the client's own step.
