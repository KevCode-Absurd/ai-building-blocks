# Training interview: WordPress developer

The coach asks these one at a time, follows up on short answers and asks for a real example each time. Remind the expert to leave out client names, site addresses, private code and anything under a confidentiality agreement; describe sites in general terms.

1. **What kinds of WordPress sites and clients do you know best, and what work do you turn down?**
   Why it matters: sets the agent's specialty and its limits, such as checkout or payment logic.

2. **Walk me through the last broken site you fixed, from the first message to the all-clear.**
   Why it matters: reveals the real order of diagnosis and where time goes.

3. **What do you ask an owner for before you look at anything?**
   Why it matters: captures intake questions that save hours of guessing.

4. **How do you read a debug log to find the real error under the noise?**
   Why it matters: turns log reading into steps the agent can copy.

5. **How do you run a plugin conflict test without taking the live site down?**
   Why it matters: records the expert's staging and half-split habits.

6. **Tell me about a fix that made things worse. What did you miss?**
   Why it matters: surfaces the checks the expert now always does.

7. **How do you decide whether to fix a plugin problem, replace the plugin or write a small plugin of your own?**
   Why it matters: captures the build-or-replace judgement.

8. **What is your update routine for a small business site, and which plugins do you never auto-update?**
   Why it matters: grounds the maintenance workflow in practice.

9. **How have you handled a host forcing a PHP upgrade on an old site?**
   Why it matters: records real PHP migration lessons.

10. **What security mistakes do you see most in custom plugins and theme `functions.php` files?**
    Why it matters: tunes the hardening skill to real-world findings.

11. **How do you check AI-written WordPress code before it goes near a site?**
    Why it matters: lists the made-up functions, missing nonces and escaping gaps the agent must catch.

12. **How do you build a block today, and what changed for you with the iframed editor?**
    Why it matters: keeps block advice current and practical.

13. **What do you do when forms stop sending email?**
    Why it matters: captures the order of checks for a very common complaint.

14. **What have you learned cleaning up a site after a hack?**
    Why it matters: records safe clean-up and prevention steps without attack tooling.

15. **How do you work with page builders such as Elementor when something breaks inside them?**
    Why it matters: covers a frequent real-world setup the agent must handle.

16. **What access do you accept from clients, and what do you refuse?**
    Why it matters: feeds access.md and the safety rules in the expert's own words.

17. **How do you explain a fix and its risks to an owner who is not technical?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's plain-language style for reports.

18. **Tell me about a client who wanted something you thought was a bad idea, such as editing a plugin's files directly. What did you do?**
    Why it matters: shows how the expert disagrees and records the decision.

19. **How do you work on WooCommerce sites without touching checkout or payments?**
    Why it matters: defines safe boundaries for shop work.

20. **What makes a site you inherited easy or hard to look after?**
    Why it matters: gives the agent a list of risk signals for inherited sites.

21. **How do you keep up with WordPress, PHP and plugin security news, and which sources do you trust?**
    Why it matters: sets refresh habits and trusted sources for this agent.
