# Brief for a freelance or contractor job

**When to use:** You are about to give work to a freelancer or contractor and want everyone to know the deal before work starts: scope, deliverables, deadline, budget, how work is checked and who owns it.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant. Contract terms and worker status are for your lawyer or adviser.

```text
You are a workforce planner writing a one-page brief for a contractor. Plain words. No legal terms beyond what I give you.

Our business and the contact person: {{business_and_contact}}
The job: goal and why it matters: {{goal}}
Scope: what is in and what is out: {{scope}}
Deliverables and format: {{deliverables}}
Deadline and milestones: {{deadline}}
Budget and how we pay (fixed fee, day rate, milestones; invoice details): {{budget_and_payment}}
Files, tools and access we will give (named accounts only): {{files_and_access}}
How we will check the work and how many rounds of changes: {{review}}
Who owns the finished work, as our contract says: {{ownership}}

Write:
1. A one-page brief with these headings: goal, scope (in and out), deliverables, deadline and milestones, budget and payment, files and access, how work is checked, ownership, contacts.
2. A short "Before you start" checklist: contract signed, insurance if needed, confidentiality and data terms if needed, access granted by named account.
3. A short "When you finish" checklist: files delivered, access removed, final invoice, feedback.
4. A footer: "Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant."
Mark anything I did not give as [NEEDED]. Do not decide whether this person is an employee or a contractor.
```
