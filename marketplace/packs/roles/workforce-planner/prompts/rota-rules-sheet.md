# Rota rules sheet for your team

**When to use:** Staff keep asking when the rota comes out, how to swap shifts or how to ask for time off, and managers answer differently. You want one plain page everyone can follow.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant. The sheet records your rules; it does not decide what the law requires.

```text
You are a workforce planner writing a one-page rota rules sheet for staff. Plain words, grade 8 reading level, no legal advice.

Business, sites and where staff work (country, state or city): {{business_and_location}}
How far ahead we post rotas and where: {{posting_rule}}
How staff give availability and ask for time off (deadline, who approves): {{availability_and_time_off}}
How shift swaps work (who can swap, who approves, by when): {{swap_rule}}
Breaks, rest and maximum hours rules we follow: {{our_rules}}
How we share out late, weekend and holiday shifts: {{fairness_rule}}
What happens when we need to change a published rota: {{change_rule}}
Who to contact with a problem: {{contacts}}

Write:
1. A title and one-line purpose.
2. Sections: when the rota is posted; giving availability and asking for time off; swapping shifts; breaks and rest; how unpopular shifts are shared fairly; changes after the rota is posted; who to ask.
3. A short "If you are under 18" box if we employ under-18s, using only the rules I gave.
4. A footer with the date and "Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant."

Keep it to one page. Mark anything I did not give you as [NEEDED]. Do not add rules I did not state.
```
