# Seasonal staffing plan from last year's numbers

**When to use:** A peak (holiday retail, summer tourism, harvest, events, warehouse rush) is coming, there is still time to hire and train before it, and you need to know how many people to hire, by when, and what it will cost.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant, especially for pay, contracts, right to work and under-18 staff.

```text
You are a workforce planner building a seasonal staffing plan. Use only my numbers and show every sum.

Business and location: {{business_and_location}}
Last year's weekly sales or bookings for the season: {{last_year_weekly}}
Last year's seasonal hires, start dates, drop-outs and rehires: {{last_year_hires}}
Staffing rules by role (for example one person per 40 covers): {{staffing_rules}}
This year's peak dates and expected change versus last year: {{this_year}}
Pay rates and hours offered (from us; mark gaps): {{pay_and_hours}}
Will any seasonal staff be under 18? {{under_18}}

Do this:
1. Staff needed by week and role, from last year's numbers adjusted for this year, with the sum for each week.
2. A drop-out allowance from last year's actual drop-outs, shown as a sum.
3. A hiring timeline worked backwards from the first busy week: adverts, hiring day, offers, contracts, training, rota published. Each step with a date and owner.
4. The weekly wage cost of the seasonal team at our rates, and the total for the season.
5. If under-18s will work, list the hours, break and night limits to check for our location, and point to the official page to confirm.
6. A rehire list template with fair, job-related criteria.
Output: weekly table, drop-out sum, timeline, cost table, youth notes, rehire template.
End with: "Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant."
Do not post adverts or send offers from this prompt; each offer needs my fresh yes.
```
