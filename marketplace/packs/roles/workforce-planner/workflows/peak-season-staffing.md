# Peak season staffing

**Trigger:** as soon as the dates of a seasonal peak are known (holiday retail, summer tourism, harvest, events, a warehouse rush), working back from the first busy week with last year's actual lead times for advertising, hiring and training. Runs until two weeks after the season ends.

**Access:** level 1 is enough to plan. Level 2 lets me draft job posts, the hiring day plan and rotas in your tools. Level 3 lets me publish approved job posts and send agreed scheduling messages to candidates within your limits. Offers, contracts and publishing rotas need your fresh yes.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant.

## Steps

1. **Size the season (week 1).** Run the `seasonal-staffing-plan` prompt or the seasonal steps of the `contractors-and-seasonal-staff` skill: staff by week and role from last year, drop-out allowance, wage cost for the season. *Approval: you confirm headcount and budget.*
2. **Check the rules (week 1).** List the hours, breaks, night and under-18 limits for your location from the official pages, and any scheduling law that covers you. Confirm pay rates and contract type with your adviser.
3. **Plan the timeline (week 1).** Work back from the first busy week: rota posted, training, contracts, offers, hiring day, adverts. Each step with a date and owner.
4. **Hiring kit (week 2).** Use the `structured-hiring-kit` skill in the `hr-specialist` kit for job posts written around the tasks, a group hiring day plan and a fair scorecard. Level 2: drafts in your job board or careers page. Level 3: publish the approved posts.
5. **Hiring day (as planned).** Prepare stations, timings and score sheets; collect scores in one sheet. *Approval: you decide who gets an offer. Each offer and contract needs your fresh yes.*
6. **Onboarding (two to three weeks before peak).** Use the `onboarding-plan` skill in the `hr-specialist` kit for a short training plan and buddy list.
7. **First rotas (by your posting rule).** Run the `weekly-rota-cycle` workflow with seasonal staff added, under-18 limits checked first. *Approval: publishing each rota needs your yes.*
8. **During the season (weekly).** Track hours, overtime, drop-outs and cost against plan; adjust hiring if drop-outs run above the allowance.
9. **After the season (within two weeks).** Rehire list with fair criteria, a short survey, and the numbers to keep for next year (hires, drop-outs, hours, cost). Remove access for leavers on their last day (level 3, on the dates you set).

## Outputs

- Seasonal headcount and cost by week
- Rules checklist for the location
- Dated hiring timeline with owners
- Job posts, hiring day plan and scorecards (drafts, or published at level 3)
- Onboarding plan
- Seasonal rotas (published after your yes)
- Weekly tracking against plan
- Rehire list, survey and next-year numbers
- Action log (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs within your limits after one confirmation.** Publishing approved job posts, sending agreed interview scheduling messages to candidates, and removing leavers' access on the dates set (level 3).

**Always needs a fresh yes, or stays with you.** Headcount and budget, every job offer and contract, choosing who to hire, publishing each rota, changing anyone's hours, pay rates, running payroll and paying staff, and any legal question, which goes to your employment adviser.
