# Quarterly headcount and contractor review

**Cadence:** every quarter, in the first two weeks after the quarter closes. About half a day for a team of up to 100 people and contractors.

**Access:** level 1 in the HR system, accounting and the plan file is enough to review. Level 3 on the agreed tab of the headcount plan and the contractor tracker lets me keep them current within your limits. People decisions stay with you.

Check this with local rules, or ask a qualified employment adviser or accountant.

## Steps

1. **Actuals against plan (day 1).** Hires made and their start dates, leavers, open roles, wage and contractor cost against the plan, by month.
2. **Re-test the plan (day 1).** Update the `headcount-plan-and-cost` skill with the latest revenue, cash and confirmed rates. Show the normal and slow cases for the next four quarters, the cash low point and any hire to pause or bring forward.
3. **Capacity check (day 2).** Hours of work needed against hours available by team, overtime trends from timesheets, and any team where overtime keeps recurring in the same slots (a staffing gap, not a scheduling quirk).
4. **Contractor roster (day 2).** Refresh the roster with the `contractors-and-seasonal-staff` skill: missing contracts, end dates passed, spend against agreed amounts, access still live for people who have finished, and any contractor whose working pattern now looks like a job (facts listed for your adviser).
5. **Rules check (day 2).** Note any change on the official pages you rely on (minimum wage, employer tax rates, working time, scheduling laws) and update the rates in the plan once your payroll provider or accountant confirms them.
6. **Report (day 3).** One page: plan versus actual, cash low point, roles to start, pause or drop, contractor gaps by risk, status facts for the adviser, and three actions with owners. *Approval: you decide hiring changes and contractor actions.*
7. **Act (after your yes).** Level 3: update the plan tab and contractor tracker, and remove access for finished contractors on the dates you set. Ending a contractor's engagement, offers and contract changes need your fresh yes each.

## Outputs

- Plan versus actual by month
- Updated headcount plan with normal and slow cases
- Capacity and overtime view by team
- Refreshed contractor roster and ranked gaps
- Status facts for the adviser
- Rates change note
- One-page quarterly report with three actions
- Action log (what, where, when, result)

## Needs the client's approval

**Runs within your limits after one confirmation.** Updating the headcount plan tab and contractor tracker, and removing finished contractors' access on the dates you set (level 3).

**Always needs a fresh yes, or stays with you.** Hiring, pausing or cutting roles, choosing people, ending a contractor's engagement, offers and contracts, pay decisions, running payroll and paying anyone, and any decision on worker status or what the law requires, which goes to your adviser.
