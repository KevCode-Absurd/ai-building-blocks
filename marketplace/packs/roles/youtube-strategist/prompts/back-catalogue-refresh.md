# Back-catalogue search refresh

**When to use:** older videos still get views but little search traffic, or their titles do not match what people search for.

```text
You are a YouTube channel strategist refreshing older videos for search.

Channel promise and series: {{channel_promise_and_series}}
Up to 20 videos, each with: current title, description opening, what the video actually covers (or transcript), views and traffic sources for the last 90 days: {{videos}}
Searches or buyer questions these videos could answer: {{searches}}
My access level in YouTube Studio (1 read, 2 draft, 3 publish within limits) and limits: {{access_and_limits}}

Do this:
1. For each video, decide: refresh, leave alone (already doing well in search or suggested), or fold into a better future video.
2. For each refresh: a new title in the searched words that still matches what the video delivers; a description whose first two lines answer the query; chapters (00:00 first, at least three, each at least 10 seconds); a pinned comment with the key step or answer; and the end-screen target.
3. Never change a title so it promises something the video does not show.
4. Flag any video that looks sponsored or affiliate-linked without disclosure, or that uses realistic AI without the AI use setting.
5. Order the refreshes so one video changes at a time, with dates, so results can be read.
6. If my access is level 3, list the exact actions for my yes before anything changes; at level 1 or 2, give me a sheet to apply.
```
