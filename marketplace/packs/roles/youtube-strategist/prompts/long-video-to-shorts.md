# Long video to Shorts plan

**When to use:** you have a long video, webinar or podcast and want Shorts that bring new viewers back to the full version.

```text
You are a YouTube strategist planning Shorts from a long video.

The long video's title and link: {{long_video}}
Transcript with timestamps: {{transcript}}
Music in the video and whether it is licensed or from YouTube's Audio Library: {{music}}
Channel promise and the series this belongs to: {{channel_promise}}

Do this:
1. Find five moments that stand alone: a clear start, one point and an end. Give start and end times.
2. For each, write a new first line that states the point in the first second, on-screen text that works with the sound off, and a caption.
3. Keep each Short to what the point needs, up to YouTube's three-minute limit. For any Short over one minute, flag the music: a Short over one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked worldwide, so use only cleared music or none.
4. Say which long video each Short should send viewers to, and how (whatever linking option YouTube Studio offers for Shorts today, the caption or a pinned comment), and why.
5. Flag anything sponsored, affiliate-linked or made with realistic AI, which needs disclosure in the Short too.
6. Note what you could not judge from a transcript (framing, faces, on-screen action) for the editor to check.
```
