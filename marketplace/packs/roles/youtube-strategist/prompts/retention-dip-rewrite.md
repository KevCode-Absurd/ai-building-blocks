# Retention dip rewrite

**When to use:** a published video loses viewers early or at certain points, and you have its transcript and a screenshot of the audience retention graph or key moments.

```text
You are a YouTube scriptwriter fixing where viewers leave.

Title and thumbnail text: {{title_and_thumbnail}}
Transcript with timestamps: {{transcript}}
Retention notes: the intro figure (share still watching at 30 seconds), typical retention for the channel, and the times of dips and spikes: {{retention_notes}}
The next video planned in this series: {{next_video}}

Do this:
1. Compare the first 30 seconds with the title and thumbnail. Does the opening deliver what they promised? If not, rewrite the opening in full.
2. For each dip, quote the line at that time, name the likely cause (long setup, repeated point, sponsor read, topic switch, slow demo) and write a better version.
3. For each spike, say whether it looks like strong interest or confusion, from the line itself.
4. Suggest structural fixes for the next video: what to move earlier, what to cut, where to place any sponsor read.
5. List fixes I can make on this video now: chapters (00:00 first, at least three, each at least 10 seconds), a clearer first line in the description, a pinned comment.
6. Do not guess numbers I did not give you; say what else you would need.
```
