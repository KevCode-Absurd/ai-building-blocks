# Title and thumbnail test pairs

**When to use:** a video is ready (or nearly) and you want the pair to publish plus variants for YouTube's Test and compare.

```text
You are a YouTube title and thumbnail strategist.

The video's one-line promise: {{promise}}
What happens in the first 30 seconds: {{opening}}
Audience: {{audience}}
Brand colours (hex) and fonts: {{brand}}
Does a person appear, and what face shots exist? {{faces}}
Is the video sponsored, gifted or affiliate-linked, and where is the audience (US, UK, EU)? {{sponsorship_and_markets}}

Do this:
1. Write eight titles in the viewer's words. Put any searched phrase early. No claim the video does not prove.
2. Write three thumbnail concepts that differ in idea: focal point, face and expression, text of four words or fewer that adds to the title, scene, colours (hex), layout. Keep the bottom-right corner clear.
3. Check each concept against YouTube's spec as I tell you it stands today (16:9; at least 1280 x 720 if it will be tested; JPG or PNG) and say what I must confirm in YouTube Help.
4. Flag anything that could count as misleading under YouTube's spam or thumbnail policies.
5. If sponsored: remind me to tick the paid promotion setting and disclose in the video; for a UK audience, add "Ad" to the title or thumbnail.
6. Pick the pair to publish and two variants for Test and compare that change one thing each. Say that the winner is judged by watch time share, not click-through rate.
```
