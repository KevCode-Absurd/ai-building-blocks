# Interview: YouTube channel strategist

The training coach asks these questions one or two at a time, follows up on vague answers with "Can you show me a real example?", and writes what it learns into playbook.md, knowledge.md ("My specialty") and soul.md. Skip any question that does not fit the strategist's work (for example, thumbnail questions for someone who only writes scripts).

## How you approach a channel

1. **Walk me through the last channel you planned, from the first call to the first month of results.**
   Why it matters: shows the real method, not the textbook one.
2. **How do you write a channel promise, and how do you know when a client's promise is too vague?**
   Why it matters: the promise sorts every later decision, and how an expert tests it is reusable.
3. **How do you choose which series to run, and when have you killed a series?**
   Why it matters: series choices decide whether viewers come back; the kill rule shows judgement.
4. **Where do your topic ideas come from? Show me a topic map you built and the evidence behind three ideas.**
   Why it matters: separates real demand research from guessing.
5. **How do you decide the mix of Shorts and long videos for a business channel?**
   Why it matters: a common, costly question with no official answer; the expert's reasoning is the value.

## Titles, thumbnails and scripts

6. **Show me a title and thumbnail pair you are proud of, and the alternatives you rejected.**
   Why it matters: captures how the expert turns a promise into a picture and words.
7. **How do you run and read thumbnail or title tests, and what has a test taught you that surprised you?**
   Why it matters: test design and reading are where most people fool themselves.
8. **What does the first 30 seconds of one of your scripts look like? Read it to me.**
   Why it matters: the opening is the core retention skill.
9. **Tell me about a retention graph you fixed. What did the dip show, and what did you change?**
   Why it matters: a worked example of reading analytics into script changes.
10. **How do you work with a client who is nervous on camera or has very little time?**
    Why it matters: shows how plans fit real capacity.

## Analytics and results

11. **Which three numbers do you put in front of a client each month, and why those?**
    Why it matters: reveals what the expert believes drives growth.
12. **How do you tell clickbait from a strong promise in the data?**
    Why it matters: a judgement call that protects the channel.
13. **How do you connect YouTube results to leads or sales for a business?**
    Why it matters: business clients judge the role by this, not by views.
14. **Tell me about a back-catalogue refresh. What did you change and how did you read the result?**
    Why it matters: a high-value, low-cost job with its own method.

## Rules, risks and clients

15. **How do you handle sponsored videos, affiliate links and disclosure across the US, UK and EU?**
    Why it matters: the expert's practice must match the FTC, ASA and EU guidance.
16. **Have you dealt with a strike, a copyright claim, a lost monetisation review or a made-for-kids question? What happened?**
    Why it matters: policy incidents are where clients most need calm, correct advice.
17. **Where do you draw the line on AI: scripts, voices, faces, thumbnails?**
    Why it matters: sets the agent's own rules beyond YouTube's disclosure page.
18. **Tell me about a client who wanted the wrong thing (fast subscribers, viral hits, misleading thumbnails). How did you handle it?**
    Why it matters: captures how the expert says no and what they offer instead.
19. **What access do you ask for in YouTube Studio, and what do you never do without a fresh yes?**
    Why it matters: turns the expert's habits into the agent's access rules.
20. **What is the hardest channel you have worked on, and what would you do differently?**
    Why it matters: hard stories hold the lessons the rest of the interview misses.
