# Quarterly channel review and refresh

**Cadence:** every 90 days, or when growth stalls. A shorter monthly check uses steps 1 to 3 only.

**Trigger:** the channel has at least one quarter of uploads, and the client wants to know what is working and what to change.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 4 need level 1. Step 6 uses level 2 or 3. Step 7 needs level 3, within the client's limits.

1. **Pull the data (level 1).** 90-day exports or screenshots from YouTube Studio for this quarter and the last: per video reach, engagement, traffic sources, audience (new, casual, regular), retention for top and bottom videos, and any policy, strike or copyright notices. Ask the client for clicks, enquiries or sales tied to videos.
2. **Review (skill: channel-analytics-review).** Read by series, traffic source and audience; check policy health; pick three to five changes.
3. **Re-check the rules.** Open YouTube's help pages for the Partner Program, monetisation policies, Shorts, Test and compare and AI disclosure, and note any change with the date. The updated Partner Program terms take effect on 1 February 2027 (checked 6 Nov 2026).
4. **Update the plan (skill: channel-plan-and-topic-map).** Adjust series, rhythm and the topic map for next quarter.
   **Approval point 1:** the client agrees the findings, the changes and the updated plan.
5. **Choose the refresh list (prompt: back-catalogue-refresh).** Up to 20 older videos with new titles, description openings, chapters, pinned comments and end-screen targets.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the refresh sheet, video by video.
6. **Prepare tests (level 2).** For videos with steady impressions, set up Test and compare variants that change one thing each. At level 2, hand the client the variants to start.
7. **Apply the refresh (level 3).** Show the action list (videos, changes, dates) and get a yes, then change one video at a time on the listed dates, start the approved tests and log each action. Ask again before touching any video not on the list, changing settings or deleting anything. At level 1 or 2, the client applies the sheet.
8. **Read results next quarter.** Compare refreshed videos' search traffic and test outcomes with their own past.

## Outputs

- A 90-day review: headline, series table, findings and three to five changes.
- Notes on any YouTube rule changes, with dates.
- An updated channel plan and topic map.
- A back-catalogue refresh sheet and test plan, applied within limits at level 3.
- An action log.

## Approval

- Findings, changes and the updated plan (point 1).
- The refresh sheet, video by video (point 2). At level 3, the action list is the yes for live edits.
- Always a new yes: any video not on the list, deleting or hiding videos, made-for-kids or disclosure changes, channel settings and permissions. Payments, AdSense and payout details stay with the client.
