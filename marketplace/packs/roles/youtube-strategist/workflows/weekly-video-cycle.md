# Weekly video cycle

**Cadence:** every week, on a fixed day, for each long video in the plan (Shorts follow the long video they come from).

**Trigger:** the client has a channel plan and topic map (skill: channel-plan-and-topic-map) and publishes on a regular rhythm.

## Steps

Steps 1, 2 and 9 need level 1 (read). Step 7 uses level 2. Step 8 needs level 3, within the client's limits.

1. **Check last week's video (level 1).** In YouTube Studio, or from the client's screenshots: impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, the intro figure against typical retention, and traffic sources. Note one lesson.
2. **Confirm the next topic.** Take the next idea from the topic map; swap in anything new the client heard from buyers this week.
   **Approval point 1:** the client confirms the topic and the promise.
3. **Title and thumbnail first (skill: title-and-thumbnail-concepts; prompt: title-and-thumbnail-test-pairs).** Promise, titles, three thumbnail concepts, the pair to publish and a test note.
4. **Script (skill: retention-script).** Opening written in full, timed sections, B-roll and on-screen notes, chapters, end-screen target, facts to check, disclosures.
5. **Policy check.** Sponsored, gifted or affiliate-linked? Plan the in-video disclosure and the paid promotion setting (and "Ad" in the title or thumbnail for a UK audience). Realistic AI? Plan the AI use setting. Could it appeal to children? Raise the made-for-kids decision with the client.
   **Approval point 2:** the client approves the script, title, thumbnail and disclosures.
6. **Film and edit.** The client or their editor films and cuts from the script. Production specs and captions follow the video producer kit.
7. **Prepare the upload (level 2).** Upload as private with title, description, chapters, thumbnail, end screen, playlist, paid promotion and AI use settings, and the made-for-kids setting the client chose. At level 1, hand the client a filled-in upload sheet instead.
8. **Publish (level 3).** Show the action list (video, date, time, settings) and get a yes for this job, then publish or schedule as agreed and log it. Start a Test and compare test if the plan calls for one. Ask again before anything outside the limits: a different date, a new playlist, changing settings on other videos, or deleting anything. At level 1 or 2, the client publishes.
9. **First-week read (level 1).** After seven days, note impressions, click-through rate by source, the intro figure and the first dips. Feed one lesson into next week's script (prompt: retention-dip-rewrite).
10. **Shorts (prompt: long-video-to-shorts).** Plan Shorts from the new video; they follow the same approval and access steps.

## Outputs

- The week's title and thumbnail sheet, script, chapters and upload sheet.
- A private upload ready to publish (level 2), or a published or scheduled video within limits (level 3).
- A Shorts plan from the long video.
- A one-line lesson from last week and the action log.

## Approval

- Topic and promise (point 1).
- Script, title, thumbnail and disclosures (point 2). At level 3, this plus the action list is the yes for publishing.
- Always a new yes: deleting or hiding a public video, changing a made-for-kids setting, turning off a disclosure, channel settings or permissions, and anything binding with a sponsor. Payments and payout details stay with the client.
