# AI default patterns

The choices a model makes when nothing pushes it elsewhere. Each one is fine when chosen on purpose, for a reason you can name. Used without a reason, it makes the work look like every other AI output. Name the ones a job could slip into in the taste brief, and check for them before you hand the work over.

This is the pack's fallback copy. The library's ai-default-patterns skill holds the maintained list, and the build uses it when it is available.

## Web

- **web-template-stack**: centred hero, then 3 benefit cards, "How it works in 3 steps", testimonials, FAQ and a final call to action, whatever the category. Why it reads as default: it is the average landing page.
- **web-purple-gradient**: purple or blue gradients. Why: the stock look of AI-made sites.
- **web-font-swap**: Inter, Geist or system fonts in place of the brand font. Why: the brand's own type is its strongest code.
- **web-pill-buttons**: pill buttons where the brand uses square ones.
- **web-emoji-icons**: emoji used as icons.
- **web-glass-cards**: glassmorphism cards.
- **web-empty-trust-row**: a "Trusted by" row with no real logos.
- **web-stock-hero**: a generic stock-style hero image.
- **web-even-rhythm**: every section the same height.
- **web-fade-up**: a fade-up animation on every block.
- **web-rounded-everything**: rounded cards everywhere.
- **web-dropped-shell**: the client's nav and footer dropped.
- **web-cream-premium**: a cream background swapped in to look "premium".

## Copy

- **copy-ai-verbs**: "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world".
- **copy-rule-of-three**: lists of three everywhere.
- **copy-headline-formula**: headline formula clichés.
- **copy-fake-urgency**: urgency the offer does not have.
- **copy-generic-benefit**: benefits any competitor could claim.

## Ads

- **ads-one-look**: every concept in one look or style.
- **ads-hook-formula**: the same hook formula reused across variants.
- **ads-product-on-gradient**: the product floating on a gradient.

## Audits

- **audit-checklist-score**: a fixed checklist score with no account-specific diagnosis of why a metric moved.

Add a pattern when a blind test or a buyer report shows it.
