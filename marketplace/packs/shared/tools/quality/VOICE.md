# Voice match (v1)

## Writer
1. Read `context/brand-file/voice.md` first; if missing, use voice notes in `facts.md`, else write plain and calm.
2. Pick the channel voice that fits the deliverable (site, email, social, ads, docs, support, blog, B2B). Copy its person, formality, sentence length, punctuation and the brand's own terms.
3. Customer-facing copy must sound like the brand wrote it. For internal or analytical work, clarity beats house style: keep only the brand's terms and no-go words.
4. Never put in a phrase from the voice file's "words not seen" or "don't" list.

## Checker
1. Quote two lines from the draft. Compare each with the voice file's closest example: could it sit on the brand's own page?
2. Flag:
   - generic AI phrasing the brand never uses (list below)
   - wrong formality (too stiff, too chummy, hype where the brand is calm)
   - wrong person ("we" where the brand says "you", or the reverse)
   - wrong punctuation habits (exclamation marks, emoji, em dashes, title case, full stops on bullets, apostrophe style)
   - wrong sentence length or reading level for the channel
3. Score voice inside specificity for customer-facing work. Each flag costs a point; a no-go word costs two. For internal work, flag only lines that hurt clarity.

## Generic AI phrases to strip in any brand voice
- "In today's fast-paced world", "In an ever-evolving landscape"
- "Unlock", "unleash", "elevate", "supercharge", "empower"
- "Seamless", "game-changing", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge"
- "Delve into", "dive deep", "navigate the complexities"
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "Whether you're X or Y"
- "Look no further", "Say goodbye to"
- "We're excited to announce", "We're thrilled"
- "At the end of the day", "Here's the thing"
- Stacked triplets of adjectives, rhetorical-question openers, closing "Ready to...?" lines
