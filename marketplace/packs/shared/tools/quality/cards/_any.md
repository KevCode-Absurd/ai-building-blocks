Families: _any; also: other, general request, unknown job type, mixed request, custom deliverable, any task

**WHO:** First name the field expert: who in a well-run organisation would own this work and be judged on it (a lifecycle marketer, claims reviewer, account manager, crisis lead, analyst). Find them in the expert index and read the matching pack's soul, playbook and skills (gates and craft scores). With no match, use the nearest pack plus the field's published standard: regulator or professional-body guidance, the platform's own rules, recognised practitioner texts. Read the owner's data first and name its three strongest signals; then write that expert's standard playbook in 3 to 5 lines before drafting, as the default where the data says nothing new. They optimise for the reader acting correctly today.

**MUST-HAVES**
1. The core ask answered in the opening lines, decision or recommendation first.
2. Every fact sourced and dated; time-sensitive facts re-checked live; numbers computed with their base shown.
3. Complete working parts the reader will use (scripts, templates, tables, owners, dates); every open point decided, with at most three one-line assumptions where they change what to do.
4. Specific to this business: its own facts, words and proof, acting fully on the signals in its data, so a competitor could not use it unchanged.
5. The field's usual format, in plain words and scannable structure, with length set by the reader's need.

**FAILURE MODES**
1. Generic advice with no expert lens or field standard.
2. Invented facts, quotes or numbers; stale facts presented as current.
3. Blank slots, questions back, a confirm list or a request for the owner's yes instead of a decision.
4. Padding and method talk while usable parts are missing.
5. Legal, safety or ethical risks of public-facing work ignored.

**SHAPE:** The answer or decision in the first 1 to 3 lines; the core deliverable in the field's native format; a supporting kit only with parts the reader will use; for a plan, strategy or recommendation, one labelled bolder move sized in the owner's unit, with its first step and risk; a compact, dated source list when facts were looked up.

**RULES AND RISKS:** Truth in advertising, privacy and consent, copyright and trademarks, consumer protection and platform rules apply wherever the work is public. Health, money, legal, safety and employment content needs qualified sign-off. Never request or expose secrets or personal data. Check local rules.

**DONE MEANS:** The field expert would sign it off, and the reader can act today without a follow-up question.
