Families: creator-brief; also: influencer brief, UGC brief, creator content brief, sponsored post brief, ambassador brief, gifting brief, creator campaign brief, partnership content brief

**WHO:** A senior creator partnerships manager. Optimises for native content carrying one true message, clearly disclosed, with usable rights and no over-scripting.

**MUST-HAVES**
1. One key message and one proof point, both provable, plus the audience insight it lands on.
2. Must-haves split from creative freedom: required beats (product in real use, exact claim wording, call to action, link or code) and explicit room for the creator's own hook, format and voice.
3. Dos and don'ts, including claims creators must not make: no results beyond evidence, no health or money promises, only real personal experience.
4. Exact disclosure wording and placement: upfront, on screen and spoken in video, repeated in live streams, plus the platform's paid-partnership tool.
5. Deliverables and terms: format, count, length, aspect ratio, post dates, review rounds, usage rights (organic or paid, duration), payment, metrics to report.

**FAILURE MODES**
1. Scripts that make every post read like an ad; one hook for every creator.
2. Weak or hidden disclosure ("gifted", "collab", description only).
3. Generic benefits any rival could claim, or unprovable claims handed over.
4. No rights, review process or timeline; terms left verbal.
5. One brief for every platform, ignoring audience and style.

**SHAPE:** One page, 400 to 700 words: campaign line, product facts, audience and insight, message and proof, must-haves, freedom with two or three example angles (not scripts), dos and don'ts, disclosure, deliverables table (platform, format, length, date), review steps, rights and payment, contact.

**RULES AND RISKS:** Any material connection (fee, free product, commission, discount, relationship) must be disclosed clearly, before the content, in plain words such as "Ad". Brands share responsibility and must monitor and correct posts. Incentives cannot depend on positive sentiment; no fake engagement. Regulated categories (health, finance, alcohol, children) carry extra limits. Use a written contract. Check local rules.

**DONE MEANS:** A creator could make compliant, on-message content from this page alone, every claim provable and rights agreed.
