Families: seo-article; also: blog post, SEO blog post, article, how-to article, long-form guide, pillar page, search article, content piece

**WHO:** A search content writer and editor. Optimises for fully answering one search intent for one reader, with first-hand substance a summary cannot replace, and a clear next step.

**MUST-HAVES**
1. One main query and intent named; the direct answer in the first two or three sentences.
2. Something only this business can add: experience, its own data, a worked example, expert input, real photos.
3. Headings that work as a skimmable answer path; short paragraphs; steps, a table or checklist where they help.
4. Every fact, number and quote sourced or marked for checking; a real author; a published or updated date.
5. A search title of about 50 to 60 characters, a meta description under 160, three or more internal links, one call to action matched to intent.

**FAILURE MODES**
1. A generic summary of what already ranks.
2. Throat-clearing intros and padding to a word count.
3. Invented statistics, quotes, studies or experience.
4. Keyword stuffing, or the wrong intent (a sales page for a how-to query).
5. No internal links, no call to action, real follow-up questions unanswered.

**SHAPE:** Title, meta description, slug; short answer box; body under clear H2 and H3 headings; FAQ of three to six real questions; call to action; internal link ideas; sources. Length follows the query and the pages that already rank for it, never padded to a word count. Reading grade at or below the audience ceiling in CLARITY.md.

**RULES AND RISKS:** Search spam policies penalise mass-produced low-value pages, near-duplicates per place or keyword, and new dates without real updates. Disclose AI use where readers would expect it; never invent authors. Disclose affiliate links and paid placements. Health, money and safety topics need expert review. Quote briefly and link sources. Check local rules.

**DONE MEANS:** A reader with that query leaves with the problem solved and a reason to trust and contact the business, and nothing is made up.
