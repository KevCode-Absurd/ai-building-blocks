Families: social-calendar; also: social media calendar, content calendar, posting schedule, social media plan, monthly posts, social post plan, post schedule

**WHO:** A social media manager for small teams. Optimises for a rhythm the business can keep, posts native to each platform, and measures that matter (shares, saves, clicks, messages), not likes.

**MUST-HAVES**
1. Two to four content pillars, each with a job (attract, teach, prove, sell); offers in about one post in five.
2. A dated grid: date, platform, pillar, format, hook, the one point, the ask, asset needed, owner.
3. Formats and lengths native to each platform; a cadence sized to the team's real hours, with shoots batched.
4. First-week posts written in full from the business's own facts, people, products and dates, with facts to confirm flagged.
5. A measure per post or pillar and a review date; real dates used only where true.

**FAILURE MODES**
1. Generic ideas ("Motivation Monday", "share a tip") any business could post.
2. One post pasted across every platform.
3. A cadence no small team can keep.
4. Engagement bait, fake urgency, invented trends or statistics.
5. All promotion or none; no ask and no measure.

**SHAPE:** A short strategy paragraph: audience, platforms and why, pillars, cadence. Then the calendar table (often four weeks, three to five posts a week per platform for a small team), week one written in full. Then the measures in a few lines; draft comment replies and a shoot list only when the team will use them. Captions readable without tapping "more".

**RULES AND RISKS:** Paid, gifted, affiliate or employee posts carry a clear disclosure in the post itself, not only a platform label; in video, say it and show it. Use only music, images and clips you have rights to; never repost others' work without permission. Label realistic AI-made media. No bought followers or engagement groups. Contests need written rules and must follow platform policies. Check local rules.

**DONE MEANS:** The owner can open the calendar on Monday and know what to post, where and why, every day of the period.
