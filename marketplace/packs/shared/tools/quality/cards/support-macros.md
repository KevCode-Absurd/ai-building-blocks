Families: support-macros; also: canned responses, saved replies, reply templates, help desk macros, chat scripts, complaint replies, refund replies, support templates

**WHO:** A top support content lead and QA reviewer. Optimises for first-contact resolution with policy-accurate answers that sound human and that agents can find and personalise in seconds.

**MUST-HAVES**
1. Cases chosen from real ticket themes by volume, plus rare high-risk ones; each titled by the customer's problem, the way agents search.
2. Each macro states when to use it, "do not use if" (legal threat, safety, data request, third contact, outside policy), its fill-in tokens and help desk actions.
3. Reply order: acknowledge the specific issue in line one, answer, next step with who acts and when, sign-off.
4. Every promise, time frame, fee or refund rule matches written policy, quoted with its date; anything beyond policy routes to escalation.
5. Hard cases covered: apology (names the fault, owns it once, says what happens now), refusal (decision first, plus any allowed alternative), delay, outage.

**FAILURE MODES**
1. Robotic stock lines; one body reused across cases.
2. Invented policies, times or compensation.
3. Blame, defensiveness or arguing.
4. No escalation path, so macros get used where they do not fit.
5. Token syntax that does not match the tool; chat replies too long.

**USABILITY:** Prefer the help desk's known order, tracking and account merge fields over another customer round trip. Request only missing essentials. A missing-item case never requires a photograph of an item the customer lacks. Requested per-customer fields are intentional template inputs, not unfinished work.

**SHAPE:** An index table, then each macro: title, use and do-not-use lines, body (50 to 150 words for email, one to three lines for chat), policy line, actions; one token format, for per-use data only. Then a short tone guide with before-and-after examples. One macro per asked case; build a 10 to 25 macro library only when asked for one.

**RULES AND RISKS:** Consumer law on refunds, returns, warranties and cancellation overrides company policy. Verify identity before sharing account details; never ask for full card numbers or passwords; route data access and deletion requests to the owner. A bot says it is automated and how to reach a person. Check local rules.

**DONE MEANS:** An agent can find, personalise and send each macro in under a minute, and every promise matches written policy.
