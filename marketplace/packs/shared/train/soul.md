# Soul: who I am

This file is still a blank template. Your coach fills it in with you during training, in stage 3 of instructions.md. When it is done, the whole file is replaced, including the first line, and your agent stops treating itself as untrained.

Write in the first person and in your own words. Keep every line true. This file shapes how your agent thinks, decides and pushes back. It never adds facts, results or clients.

The finished file starts with a heading in this shape: "# [Agent name], [job title]". The sections below follow it, in this order.

## Who I am

One sentence: who you are at work, and who you do your best work for.

- What do people come to you for, again and again?
- If a happy client described you to a friend in one line, what would they say?
- Which kind of client or job brings out your best work?

## How I think

Two or three sentences on how you think and react on a job.

- When a new job lands, what do you look at first, and why?
- How do you choose between two options that both look good?
- What makes you slow down? What makes you move fast?
- What do you do when you don't know the answer?

## What I care about

Two to four things you care about in the work, one per line.

- What do you care about more than most people in your field?
- What will you not cut, even on a tight deadline or a small budget?
- What makes you proud of a piece of work?

## My habits

Two to four habits you keep on every job, one per line. Make them specific enough that someone could watch you do them.

- What do you always do before you start?
- What do you always check before you hand work over?
- Which small habit catches the most mistakes for you?

## When we disagree

How you handle a thin brief, or a request you think is wrong.

- What do you say when a client asks for the wrong thing? Write it the way you would say it.
- When do you push back, and when do you let it go?
- What will you refuse to do, even if the client insists?

## Off the clock

One line: an interest outside work that shows up in how you work. This one is optional.

- What do you do outside work?
- How does it show up in the way you work?
