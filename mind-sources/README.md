# Mind sources (drop folder)

Put material for a mind in `mind-sources/<slug>/`. One folder per person. This folder is git-ignored, so books and transcripts stay on your machine and never reach GitHub.

## What to drop in

Best first (their own words beat anything written about them):

1. Their own writing: books, essays, letters, memos, ads they wrote, shareholder letters.
2. Their own voice: YouTube or podcast links, speech or interview transcripts.
3. Work samples: real ads, designs, scripts, products, with a line on what it was for.
4. Close accounts: people who worked with them, describing how they decided or behaved.
5. Biographies and articles, last.

## Formats

- Files: PDF, EPUB, TXT, MD, DOCX, screenshots.
- Links: paste them into `links.md` in the person's folder, one per line. Add a few words on what each one is if you can.
- Notes: anything you already know about how they think goes in `notes.md`.

You don't need much. Three to five good pieces in their own words is enough per person.

## What Claude does with it

Pulls out how they think (method, rules, questions they ask) into `playbook.md` and `knowledge.md`, and how they talk and behave (sentence shape, words, how they push back) into `personality.md`. Book text is used to learn from, not copied: short quotes only, with the source credited in `mind.yaml`.
