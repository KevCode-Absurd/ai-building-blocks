# One source per mind

One thing to find for each of the 303 minds. Each is in their own words, and picked because it shows how they think and how they talk better than their best-known work. Find it, put it in `mind-sources/<folder>/` (or put the link in `links.md` there), and I'll work it into that mind.

The list runs from the thinnest minds (fewest sources in their own words today) to the best covered, so the top rows help most. You don't need all of them.

- 146 of 303 are free to find online (archive.org, Gutenberg, YouTube, the person's own site). The other 157 are books from a shop or library.
- By kind: 163 book, 51 interview, 19 primary text, 17 talk, 17 letters, 14 essay, 9 lectures, 8 speech, 5 film.
- 59 are marked *(check)*: the exact interview or edition wasn't confirmed, so pick the best match you can find, or swap in something similar.
- Books in this folder stay on your machine (git-ignored). Only this list is pushed.
