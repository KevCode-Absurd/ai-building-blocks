# Thin sources, 2026-10-20

These minds were built from too little of the person's own words. Own-words sources are their books, letters, talks, interviews and essays. The goal is at least 5, plus a "Real decisions" section built from real choices they made. A thin mind still works, but it leans on summaries by other people and sounds more generic.

**How to read the lists:** the number after each name is their own-words source count. "No decisions" means knowledge.md has no "Real decisions" section yet. "Stub" means the saved source folder is almost empty, often only a short excerpt and a Wikipedia page.

**What would help most:** legal copies of their own work.
- Books you own, as notes or PDFs.
- Public-domain texts, such as Project Gutenberg for anyone published before 1930.
- Full transcripts of talks and interviews.

Each one goes through the intake process in docs/INTAKE.md: skeleton, then skills, then a workflow.
