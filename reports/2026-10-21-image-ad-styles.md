# Image ad styles in AI Blocks

47 image ad skills: 32 styles you can make, 10 planners and checkers, and 5 workflows that chain them. Each style skill writes the words, the picture plan and an image prompt. Claude can't draw, so you paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Higgsfield to make the picture. ★ marks a style that has a finished example picture on its page.

Pages live at `useaiblocks.com/skills/<slug>`.

## 1. Real-life photo styles (looks like a buyer took it)

| Style | What you get |
|---|---|

| Feared outcome ★ | A quiet photo of what your buyer is scared of, and the step to offer |
| Empty room ★ | An empty-room photo prompt and its caption |
| Leftover mess ★ | A close-up of the mess the problem leaves, with a caption |
| Tried-before products ★ | The pile of things your buyer gave up on, and its caption |
| Odd-one-out | One thing out of place in a photo, and a caption |
| Circled flaw | A photo of the flaw with one rough mark drawn on it |
| Visible symptom | A body-safety check, then a close-up photo prompt or a safer idea |
| Tracker chart | A photo of a tracker or chart, one mark and the line under it |
| Overhead portrait ★ | One top-down portrait with the camera and light set |

## Planners and checkers (10)

Use these before or after a style:

- **Image style picker for story ads ★:** picks the right style for your ad, with a runner-up to test.
- **Winning image ad style finder:** finds what your best ads share and plans new ones that test one change.
- **Single image ad planner:** layout, image words, feed copy and prompt from your facts.
- **Three-way offer image ad planner:** three offer ads in two sizes.
- **Repeated image campaign planner:** one flat image repeated in new colours.
- **Image ad remake planner:** turns your best ad into a carousel or still, every word kept.
- **Image ad settings picker:** a fixed brief, a conflict check and what's missing.
- **Image ad maker with exact text ★:** word-perfect text in the picture, tool settings and fixes.
- **Accurate product image ad maker:** keeps your real product in the picture, then checks it.
- **Image upscale checker:** upscale settings, a change check and a score out of 100.

## Workflows (5)

These run several of the skills above in a row:

1. **Turn reviews into image ads buyers believe ★:** from your site and reviews to finished, checked ad plans.
2. **Image ads that stop strangers scrolling ★:** a set of ads, each with a different reason to buy.
3. **Turn customer quotes into image ads that quote them:** quote ads, a review card, a rules check and the gaps.
4. **Make new image ads in the style your winners share:** your winners' style, new plans and prompts.
5. **Get three offer image ads ready to launch:** new offers, three ads on one and a rules check.
