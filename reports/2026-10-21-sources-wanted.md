## How to hand them over
Put each file in `mind-sources/<slug>/`, or paste the text, and tell me who it's for. Each one then goes through the intake: the mind's knowledge, personality and playbook are rebuilt from it, and any method it teaches becomes a skeleton, then skills, then a workflow. Audiobooks and podcasts need a transcript; a text export or the publisher's ebook works best.
