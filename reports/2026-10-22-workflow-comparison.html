The free service improved after judging

The initial free planner scored 59.7/100 and passed 1/10. Its main gaps were missing deliverables, generic handoffs and missing repair loops. Versioned recipes now cover landing pages, hiring, n8n, support, reports, email, SEO and video ads, while general catalogue search remains available. The separate follow-up on the same tasks scored 85.3/100 and passed 9/10.

This is an in-sample improvement, not an independent generalization result. A later holdout set should include paraphrases, combined goals, unusual industries and incomplete requests.

How to improve the free service

  • Implemented: better retrieval. Weighted rare terms, title/alias matches, phrase synonyms, stable tie-breaking and rejection of greeting-only requests.
  • Implemented: assemble from parts. Skills, prompts and preserved workflow/agent coordinators can be combined. Explicit “then” and “plus” connectors can join supported methods.
  • Implemented: reviewed domain rules. Landing pages, hiring, support, n8n, reports, email, SEO and video ads get concrete deliverables, checks, bounded fixes and handoff instructions.
  • Implemented: explicit input conversions. Generated code must be rendered before a page audit; email bodies and spoken scripts can be extracted as copy drafts. Unrelated blocks must not be inserted merely because they output a broad type.
  • Implemented: relevant questions and reusable context. Approved choices control existing/new, reference, delivery and recurrence. Context is optional and travels in the pack.
  • Implemented: editable and portable results. Reorder, add, remove and edit steps; persist locally; round-trip editable JSON; preserve skill assets, versions and licenses in ZIP exports; open in the canvas.
  • Next: richer contracts. Distinguish draft/final, source/rendered, data/report and reviewed/unreviewed artifact types. Add named input bindings, tool capabilities, preconditions and explicit check gates to every catalogue item.
  • Next: coverage and ambiguity. Use typo-tolerant matching, domain synonym dictionaries, exclusions and confidence thresholds. Offer two or three concrete interpretations when several methods match.
  • Next: stronger evidence. Add a holdout set of paraphrases, mixed goals, unsupported deliverables and incomplete inputs. Record edits, missing steps, export failures and actual execution outcomes with user consent.
  • Next: convenience. Asset slots, readiness checks, user-controlled tool filters, branch previews, offline caching and tool-specific launch instructions can improve usability without model calls.

What would justify charging for AI composition?

The free final planner already passed nine of ten tasks in its in-sample recheck. The cheap AI route is not demonstrated to be ten times better. Charge for understanding new combinations, using detailed business constraints, tailoring each handoff and reducing the edits needed for unfamiliar requests. Keep basic editing and complete pack downloads useful for free users. Validate any 10× claim using measured time-to-ready-pack and completion rates on new tasks. Hosted execution, progress tracking and automatic verification are a separate future upgrade and are not implemented by this composer.

Method and limitations

  • Judges: Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6.1 Sol. Candidates have random opaque IDs. Writer names, provider, price, latency and source labels are removed from judge input. Each judge sees the task, expected outcomes, plan and selected catalogue contracts.
  • Six dimensions scored 0–5: coverage, order, inputs, checks, specificity and safety. Passing requires score ≥75, coverage ≥4 and safety ≥4. Every assigned judge must pass the plan.
  • Judges do not grade their own outputs. Fable and Sol therefore have one judge; other models have two. This asymmetry and model-judge bias limit cross-model ranking confidence.
  • Ten tasks are a smoke benchmark, not a production failure-rate estimate. Scores are not evidence that the landing pages or other deliverables were executed successfully.
  • Valid generated plans are checked for allowed blocks, bounded steps, supported questions and input mapping. The selected default also receives browser pack-export checks.
  • Each provider's usage is priced using the standard rates captured in composer-models.ts. DeepSeek uses UTC peak/off-peak timing, excluding holiday inference. These are calculated usage costs, not reconciled invoices. Transport failures without returned usage can have unknown billing.
  • Raw local runs retain their prompt/code fingerprint. The main comparison and free follow-up are separate snapshots. No claim that later free rules change the already-measured model scores.

Cost and product decisions

Ship free assembly with reviewed recipes and transparent missing-input lists. Default AI composition to Haiku 5.5; offer Sonnet 5.5 for a higher-acceptance premium route. The updated service keeps model choice on the backend; see the new20-request follow-up. Do not advertise “10× better” from this pilot. Measure fewer edits, completeness, first-use success and completion time on a holdout set before making a numerical benefit claim.

Illustrative pricing hypothesis: free deterministic packs; $19/month for 50 AI packs; $49/month for 150 and team features. Budget $0.10 per delivered pack provisionally for inference and supporting variable services, then replace that allowance with telemetry. Hosted execution is a separate product with its own quoted limits.
