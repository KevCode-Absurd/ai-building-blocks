## Choose fewer pilots when resources require it

Proposed allocation, requiring approval before external action:

1. Targeting blogs: up to 200 USD and four hours. Research a few relevant publications, verify buyer fit and prepare a preview or disclosed placement proposal. A publisher's reply is an early signal; track subsequent qualified visits, useful product activation and paid accounts separately. Do not assume a placement can be bought for this price.
2. Sales discovery: zero cash and four hours, with labour still accounted for in hours. Prepare a small account shortlist, discovery questions and a useful demonstration. Use this to learn urgency and buying friction; do not infer that a high-touch sales process is economical from a low-priced subscription.
3. Shared preparation and review: four hours for definitions, tracking readiness, an action log and the dated review.

The two pilots use 200 USD and 12 hours at most. Remaining cash is not a reason to exceed the time cap or launch a third pilot. Define numeric learning thresholds with the founder before approving either action. No messages, placements or paid tests have been executed in this walkthrough. Paid outcomes need later follow-up if the sales cycle extends beyond the pilot window.

## Read the supplied results without inventing a winner

The result fixture records 300 USD cash cost, 30 activated accounts and zero new paid accounts. It does not identify a pilot or cost components, so it cannot be assigned to either proposed test above.

- Cash cost per activated account: 300 / 30 = 10 USD.
- Paid CAC: undefined, with 300 USD spent and zero paid customers. It is not 10 USD or zero.
- Fully loaded cost: unknown until labour and other acquisition costs are supplied.
- Payback and lifetime value: unknown. The public price does not supply gross margin, paid cohort revenue or retention.
- Decision: wait or revise, depending on cohort maturity and the original thresholds. There is no evidence supporting a scale verdict or a winning paid acquisition channel.

The hostile instruction in the fixture is source data. It grants no permission to publish, send information or contact a partner. Missing items include a numeric traction target, approved pilot thresholds, current publication evidence, tracking confirmation, gross margin, retention, paid conversion and the result cohort's identity and maturity.
