What we measure

Google Analytics 4 measures public-page visits, selected actions and basic device and engagement information. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and masked session recordings of clicks and scrolling on eligible public pages. If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask first and load nothing until you allow it. Elsewhere, analytics starts automatically, using analytics cookies. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we load no analytics at all.

You can say no here wherever you are.

These providers receive technical and network information, including your IP address, when their scripts load. Data is processed under their own policies, which may involve processing outside your country.

Private information stays out of analytics

We do not send account identifiers, email addresses, form contents, saved business context, workflow requests or private space links as analytics events. Private application routes are excluded. Query strings, URL fragments and incoming referrers are excluded from Google page-view events. Clarity masks page text and form inputs, and recording stops before search input and private flows. Advertising consent remains denied.

Cookies and saved work

Google uses _ga cookies and Clarity may use _clck and _clsk cookies to connect visits. These cookies follow the providers' lifetimes and retention settings. Your browser settings or content-blocking tools can block or clear analytics cookies. Clearing analytics cookies does not delete your account, kits or saved work. Sign-in, security, support-chat and saved-work storage serve their own features.

Clearing cookies does not automatically delete data already held by analytics providers. Google event-data retention and Clarity retention depend on their service settings and data type. Contact us for a data request.
