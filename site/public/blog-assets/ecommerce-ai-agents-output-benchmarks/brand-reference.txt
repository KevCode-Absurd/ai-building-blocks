## Voice writers can reproduce

**Voice:** Calm, specific and useful. Name the object, give the fact, then offer one clear action. Source: brand record `voice`.

Only two approved lines exist; channels were not supplied. They are anchors, not evidence of five approved examples:

- “Room for the everyday.”
- “Choose the size that fits your routine.”

The first is calm and economical; the second addresses the reader directly and offers a useful choice. It does not authorize invented sizes. B500 has one documented capacity, so use “See the bottle details” when there is no genuine size choice.

| Rule | New draft example | Avoid | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calm: state facts without hype | “500 mL for the everyday.” | “The ultimate bottle.” | No superiority claim |
| Specific: units beat adjectives | “280 g empty.” | “Effortlessly lightweight.” | No comparative weight inference |
| Useful: give a truthful next step | “See dimensions and care.” | “Get yours before it’s gone.” | No artificial shortage |

Use: bottle, twist cap, matte blue, capacity, empty weight, hand wash. Write `500 mL`, `280 g`, `70 mm × 220 mm`, `B500`, and `500 mL twist-cap bottle` consistently. Use sentence case. Prefer plain verbs; avoid empty “premium,” “ultimate” and “revolutionary” language as editorial guidance. Those are proposed writing rules, not additional owner bans.

### Channel and hard-moment rules

Product pages: lead with capacity and cap type, then weight, dimensions and care. Email and social: one useful point and one action, without urgency unsupported by availability. Support: acknowledge the issue, explain the next review step, and avoid humour or premature promises. Proposed damage response: “I’m sorry the cap arrived damaged. Please share a photo of the damage so support can review it.” Source: [support record](../evidence/support-and-consent.json), `current_policy.damage_escalation`. In an actual case use known order details rather than asking again. No refund decision or response time is promised.
