## Contribution screen

Base assumes 3% payment fees, 5% return-loss allowance, $1 incremental parcel handling or $7 separate-order dispatch/handling. Incremental acquisition is $0 only for the owned-customer research route. Other costs are $0 solely as an incomplete preliminary screen; packaging overlap, incremental postage, duties completeness, taxes, defects and development still require reconciliation. ## Ranking for research only

Weights: request evidence 40% (closest supplied buyer signal), economics robustness 25%, MOQ exposure 25% (cash scarcity), interest index 10% (weak purchase evidence). Scores run 1–5. Request scores: ≥8 = 5; 4–7 = 3; ≤3 = 1. Economics: all routes/cases pass = 5; add-on both cases only = 3; add-on fails = 1. All supplied candidates receive 3 for economics because every candidate passes both add-on cases but fails at least one separate-order case. Exposure: ≤$200 = 5; ≤$1,000 = 3; above = 1. Index: ≥75 = 5; 40–74 = 3; below 40 = 1.

Weighted total = sum(score ÷ 5 × weight). Cap: 5/3/5/1 = 82; sleeve: 3/3/3/3 = 60; larger bottle: 1/3/1/5 = 38. ≥70 prioritizes research, 50–69 is secondary, below 50 stops. Every hard gate still overrides the score. Editable [candidate scorecard](candidates.csv). Examples: eight requests earns 5, three earns 1; $200 exposure earns 5, $15,000 earns 1. No extra candidates were invented to fill a list.

## Cheapest useful test

Idea: existing B500 owners needing a replacement cap may value a compatible $8 cap added to an already planned parcel. Riskiest assumptions are exact compatibility (wrong-likelihood 3 × consequence 5 = 15) and willingness to pay/attach to an order (4 × 4 = 16); channel reach (3 × 3 = 9), full costs (4 × 4 = 16) and reliable supply (4 × 5 = 20) also remain unresolved. Scores are analyst judgments, not observed probabilities. Begin with the supplier/compatibility evidence request, because paying demand cannot rescue an unsafe or unavailable cap.

Then prepare a 7-day, no-payment concept study with 20 eligible B500 owners, recruited only through an authorized research channel. Ask what happened to their last cap, what replacement they used, actual spend, and whether an $8 compatible cap would be selected alongside a planned parcel. Show a clearly labeled concept; make no delivery promise. Proposed learning line: at least 5 of 20 choose the priced add-on and describe a recent replacement problem; 2–4 means revise the problem/offer, 0–1 means stop. This is a research filter, not a population estimate or inventory demand forecast; no stock order follows from it alone. Cash spend cap: $0, using existing staff time; recruitment access and staff capacity remain unverified. If recruitment is unavailable, the test is blocked rather than replaced by invented results.

**Bolder move:** inspect whether the 200 existing CAP units can serve the repair need before creating another SKU ([inventory.json](../evidence/inventory.json), sku_table CAP). First match the actual CAP item to B500 fit and ownership records; do not assume CAP equals P1. This could avoid the proposed 100-unit new MOQ, but misidentification or incompatible parts would create refunds and safety risk. No stock is reserved or sold by this report.
