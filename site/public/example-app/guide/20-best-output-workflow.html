Using Mercatura for better creative outputs

Guide, chapter 20 of 20

Use this handbook to turn existing brand resources into image ads, video scripts, landing pages and substantial SEO articles. Start with a narrow buyer decision, give it exact product evidence, review a small pilot, then expand the accepted direction. This is the most useful operating habit across the app.

This edition uses the Grüns review batch made on 16 October 2026. Its screenshots show real local app screens and saved drafts. Recommendations in this handbook are operating suggestions; they are not measured advertising or search results. No connected Meta account, Google Ads account or Search Console feed is needed for these workflows. Paid generation still needs the app's configured model providers.

The sequence to follow

  1. Check the product, variant, market and source material in Brand.
  2. Select one buyer situation and the decision the output should help them make.
  3. Write the brief and review the plan before rendering media.
  4. Inspect the actual output against the source, copy and visual instructions.
  5. Accept, hold or reject each output deliberately. Export the reviewed version.
  6. Save a precise correction so the next batch starts with better instructions.

How to use this handbook

The first half covers setup, image ads and video scripts. The middle covers landing pages and SEO. The final sections explain variety, speed, spending, review and troubleshooting. Copy the example briefs into the appropriate direction or notes fields, then replace the product and evidence details for your own brand.

The complete 40 image ads, 10 scripts, landing page and two articles remain in the organized Grüns review package. They are drafts for judging output quality, with their review notes retained.

Begin with one product and one purpose. The home prompt is convenient when you already know the brief. Use the dedicated creation screen when you need to choose references, layouts or detailed evidence.

Example request: Make four image ad plans for Grüns Original Adults, for a buyer who dislikes preparation. Explain the gummy format without water or mixing. Give each plan a different visual structure. Use no price, medical outcome or testimonial. Review the plans before rendering.

2 Prepare the brand before making a batch

The product workspace showing missing information and controls for existing product resources

In Brand, inspect Products, Guidelines, Customers and Facts and claims. A polished brand summary can still contain old commercial terms or an overbroad claim. Check the original record that supports the specific product you will create for.

For each product, record its physical form, packaging, how it opens, how it is used, relevant ingredients or specifications, and the exact benefits the evidence supports. Separate Adults, Kids, Low Sugar and Sugar-Free material. Keep the market and capture date with commercial facts.

Name reference photos by their job: pack front, daily unit, out of pack, in hand, in use and close-up. Include a clean front pack and a daily-unit photo. Add an opening demonstration when the product is difficult to depict correctly.

Grüns example: The outer pouch and daily sachet are different objects. The sachet is small, flat and square; it opens across its top edge. The gummies are small opaque dark-green bears. These details are more useful to an image model than a vague instruction to make it look premium.

Pulling or loading resources supplies material for review. It does not establish that every statement is current, applicable or suitable for an advertisement.

3 Keep every fact within its actual scope

Evidence and sources with product facts source revisions and explicit limits

Open Creative quality, select the product and market, then use Evidence & sources. Inspect the excerpt, product applicability, qualifications and revision before relying on a record. Use Review typed evidence in the production queue for quantities and competing source values.

A usable fact includesGrüns example
Subject and propertyDietary fiber in Original Adults Low Sugar
Value and unit6 g
Serving basisPer one daily pack of 20 g
Source and limitsSaved Low Sugar nutrition panel; current formula not confirmed

Never detach a nutrient amount from its serving basis or copy it into a neighboring variant. Ingredient counts do not establish ingredient quantities or health outcomes.

The Grüns material contained a conflict about the Sugar-Free sweetener description. The final sample copy withheld the disputed classification and asked readers to inspect the actual selected label. Resolve conflicting sources explicitly; more fluent wording cannot resolve a factual conflict.

A reviewer should approve only material they inspected. A source record marked approved still records a human judgment about supplied evidence. The app does not independently certify the underlying science or the current product formulation.

A filled Grüns example

Product: Grüns Original Adults. Use the supplied Low Sugar pack.
Market: US. Buyer: comparing gummy and powder preparation.
Question: What preparation does this gummy format need?
Argument: Open the daily pack and eat; no water or mixing.
Evidence: reviewed preparation passage and actual sachet photos.
Action: Explore Original Adults.
Visual: 4:5 editorial still life with one outer pouch and sachet.
Fixed: accurate pack artwork, plausible scale, exact approved copy.
Vary: setting, camera, light and visual structure across four plans.
Avoid: health outcomes, current price, superiority, testimonials.

“Make 40 premium ads” leaves the selling argument and evidence boundaries unresolved. Write four clear plans first. Expand only after inspecting whether they solve the intended buyer question.

5 Make image ads in two review stages

A real Grüns image opened at full size inside its saved Studio set

In Create, choose an image-ad route, the product, relevant photos, layout and aspect ratio. Put the buyer, argument and exclusions in the direction box. Use a specific direction for each ad rather than repeating the same set-wide note.

  1. Write the plan. Read the headline, supporting text, feed copy and scene together.
  2. Check that one argument connects the picture and words. Remove unsupported quantities and extra promises before rendering.
  3. Run the available claim and ad checks. Inspect their findings rather than treating the score as approval.
  4. Render a small pilot. Open each picture at full size and compare it with the actual reference.
  5. Inspect packaging, hands, product scale, legibility, qualifications and the intended action. Record individual decisions.

The illustrated ad makes preparation concrete through a familiar spoon and a visible sachet. Its generated staging is not evidence that a customer used the product. Native package print also needs scrutiny; a source photo does not establish every marketing claim printed on the pack.

7 Read an actual image example critically

A01 from the actual Grüns batch with preparation copy and a pouch beside a daily sachet

Headline: Your spoon can sit this out

Support: Grüns Original Adults comes as gummies. Open the pack and eat, without water or mixing.

Feed copy: Not interested in another mixing assignment? Grüns Original Adults is eaten as gummies, straight from the pack. No water needed. Your spoon has other things to do.

What works: The headline gives the picture a job. The support explains the literal product fact, while the feed copy develops the preparation choice. The outer pouch and daily sachet are distinguishable.

What to inspect: Read the fine pack text, check that hands and gummies match the references, and decide whether incidental food props confuse the intended message. A technically readable render can still need a tighter creative edit. This remains draft copy until a person accepts it.

8 Keep numeric copy and its conditions together

A22 from the actual Grüns batch illustrating a serving amount with its serving basis

Use a number only when the exact source supports the product, variant and basis shown. For the saved Grüns Low Sugar panel, 6 g dietary fiber belongs to one pack of 20 g. It is not a total for the outer pouch or a confirmed Sugar-Free value.

The same rule applies to pack duration. 28 daily packs means four weeks at one pack per day. Keep the condition near the duration, including in image overlays. A count does not establish that a buyer will follow a routine or experience an outcome.

When a number drives the ad, inspect both the written plan and the final pixels. A correct caption cannot rescue a misleading amount rendered on the image. If the qualification becomes too small to read, simplify the concept or remove the numeric claim.

9 Write video scripts before producing video

Script builder with explicit product awareness and persona choices

In Script builder, narrow the products and personas before choosing quantity. Its broad defaults are useful for exploration, but a focused batch is easier to evaluate. Pick an awareness stage, angle, offer and format that fit the same buyer decision.

Use ten genuinely different formats for a ten-script exploration: hands-only demonstration, desk situation, bag packing, stop motion, ingredient explanation, label walkthrough, split screen, version choice, sound-led preparation and measured product information.

For each script, ask for timings, spoken lines, visual actions, on-screen text and the supporting source. Keep them separate. On-screen words should help a sound-off viewer rather than duplicate a long spoken paragraph.

Example direction: Write a 30-second hands-only script about preparation. Show opening across the sachet's top, then pouring into a palm. Use the reviewed no-water and no-mixing fact. Do not imply a customer testimonial, a health outcome or a comparison test. End by inviting the viewer to inspect Original Adults.

Read it aloud at a natural pace. Budget pauses and physical actions. A script with timestamps is not a rendered video, and a script that passes factual checks can still be too rushed to film.

11 Build a landing page around the ad promise

CRO Studio intake with a saved ad or script optional and first-principles page planning

Go to Landing pages > CRO Studio. Choose the product and page type. Select a saved ad or script when the page should continue its argument. Use Start from first principles when building a new page from your existing brand evidence. The workflow audits the brief before building.

Write one buyer, one question, one promise, one useful next action and the proof available for it. Inspect the brand context before accepting the proposed changes. Inspiration can guide structure; it cannot supply your product's facts, offers or reviews.

Grüns page brief: Continue an ad about preparation. Show what the daily pack is, how it opens and what the outer pouch contains. Explain the pectin base within the reviewed source scope. Let the reader inspect the selected version's actual label. Use the real product photos. Omit current price, discounts, guarantees and customer quotes unless separately reviewed evidence supports them.

A useful section order is hero, product format, preparation, product details, selection questions and next action. Every section should answer a different buyer question. Extra sections that repeat the same convenience promise add length without adding help.

12 Check the page as a shopper would

The saved Grüns landing draft with editable sections checks and phone preview

Open Page quality and create a working copy from the saved page. Review the actual rendered content. Use the phone preview at 390 pixels and the desktop view. A good editor outline does not prove that the exported page is readable or that its purchase path works.

Ask whether you can identify the product, explain the promise, find support for it, understand the offer terms and reach the correct next step. Record the section or exact words that answered each question. Label your review as editorial unless an actual person completed the task.

Check image identity and crop, heading order, small type, button destination, variant selection and visible qualifications. Inspect the exported HTML as well as the editor. Check purchase terms again at implementation; a saved offer may be old.

The illustrated Grüns page is a draft with unresolved checks. It is a useful example of the workflow, not an approved launch page. Resolve the individual findings rather than optimizing only for its numerical score. Use controlled section variants to change one hypothesis while preserving accepted images, typography and layout.

15 Review the full article rather than its word count

A complete Grüns label comparison article shown in the actual Blog studio preview

The two final Grüns samples contain about 1100 prose words each. One explains the pack and preparation; the other develops a label-reading comparison with a worksheet. Their length is useful only because the sections perform different tasks.

Read the whole preview. Check that it answers the question near the start, develops explanations, includes a practical decision aid and ends with a relevant next step. Remove repetitive restatements and empty cautionary filler. Keep necessary material limits beside the fact they qualify.

Inspect every number, ingredient, quote and link against its exact evidence. The comparison article uses a saved Low Sugar panel and leaves Sugar-Free fields unresolved. Its source links identify the brand destination; the retained local panel is the evidence for the captured figures. That is different from verifying the live label today.

Review title, description, keyword fit, headings, anchor text, destination and proposed store URL. A proposed URL does not create a page. Save edits, rerun checks and review the current revision before handoff. Blog studio exports work for implementation; it does not publish to the store.

17 Use the specialist tools for a specific problem

Creative portfolio allocation controls distinguishing supported arguments from visual executions

Use Creative portfolio when variety is the problem. Review buyer needs and supported reasons, reserve slots, inspect audition boards and select a deliberate subset for Studio. An audition board shows staging and layout; it is not a finished generated scene or demonstration evidence.

Use Creative quality when the argument or evidence is weak. Its tools cover buying moments, awareness assumptions, execution readiness, scoped evidence, headline families, production coverage, duplicate candidates, feedback and reviewed handoffs.

Use Production lab when the production plan needs work. Its tabs cover opening and copy, carousel flow, video and sound, design and display, photos and visual world, and repair comparisons. Import a real local script or supply a complete body first.

Use Page quality for ad-to-page continuity, proof placement, offer clarity, information gained by each section, controlled edits and shopper tasks. Use Editorial quality for buyer questions, source-bound articles and revision review.

Some advanced tools expose JSON. Inspect their default request and field descriptions, change a small relevant part, then save and review the receipt. Do not mark a checklist complete merely to enable the next button.

18 Increase volume without creating repetitive work

Volume means outputs you can actually use after review. Track accepted distinct outputs as well as total renders. Forty images with the same argument and composition are a large file count with limited learning value.

A recommended operating cycle

  1. Review the source packet once for the intended product and market.
  2. Plan a small set of distinct buyer decisions. Merge semantically duplicate ideas.
  3. Make four pilot images and two scripts before authorizing a larger run.
  4. Fix shared faults in the brief and references. Avoid repairing the same mistake across forty outputs.
  5. Expand accepted directions into deliberate execution siblings and formats.
  6. Review every final item. Export its copy, source references and correct media version together.

For a 40-image request, the ten groups in this guide can each receive four executions. The groups themselves still need a semantic review. For ten scripts, vary the storytelling and production method as well as the hook. For two SEO articles, give them different reader decisions so they earn separate pages.

Volume: Reviewable outputs per measured hour. Variety: Distinct buyer questions, supported arguments and visual structures. Veracity: Correct source scope and a faithful final output. Improve all three by making the brief more precise, not by raising quantity alone.

19 Make the workflow faster and control spending

Production queue with saved article batches attempt cost and resource limits

Use Production queue and campaign review for checkpointed article batches and coordinated work. Article batches accept 1 to 20 complete briefs. Assembly preserves finished copy without a provider call; AI mode writes from source notes under the existing limits.

Use Resume unfinished articles for interrupted work. Inspect retained responses and use Recheck retained response when applicable. Do not start a duplicate batch simply because a result took longer than expected. An uncertain provider attempt may still have incurred cost.

For speed, choose product-scoped resources, review plans before images, reuse good photos, edit text in the existing image editor when only words need changing, and repair one visual detail when the scene should stay fixed. Enable text-free duplicates or extra aspect ratios only when you need them; they increase work and may increase generation charges.

The queue shows text, image, browser and CPU lanes. Current defaults are 4, 2, 2 and 2 respectively. These are implementation limits, not a Settings speed slider. Keep parallel work within the available lanes and measure the bottleneck before an administrator changes concurrency.

The app's text, image-count and video limits are separate. An unused dollar allowance does not override a reached image-count cap. Include retries, rejected attempts and held uncertain charges in a batch budget. The completed Grüns review stayed below the approved cumulative $20 cap; estimates are usage-based, not provider invoices.

When a claim or source changes, inspect the affected outputs and approvals again. Old screenshots or an accepted earlier version do not establish that the current text is approved. Export only the final version you actually reviewed.

22 Use a clear final acceptance standard

Before exporting, answer these questions for the actual final version.

  1. Does it address one identifiable buyer decision?
  2. Does the opening make a promise the body or page delivers?
  3. Can each factual statement be traced to the right product, variant, market and source?
  4. Do quantities retain their units, serving basis and material conditions?
  5. Does the final picture or script portray the product plausibly?
  6. Are copy and important qualifications readable at the intended size?
  7. Is the execution distinct, or deliberately labeled as a sibling?
  8. Does the next action lead to the correct product, page or implementation step?
  9. Have changed sources and unresolved findings been reviewed?
  10. Did a person inspect this individual output before accepting it?

A practical weekly rhythm

Review changed resources and missing evidence at the start of the week. Plan a small batch of buyer questions, scripts and image directions. Review pilots before expanding. Use the next session for focused revisions, landing-page continuity and article depth. Finish by exporting accepted work and recording a few precise lessons.

The fresh Grüns batch contains 40 distinct image files, ten scripts, one page and two full articles. Pixel review marked 24 images pass and 16 warn, with no failed final images. Those diagnostics do not replace copy approval or publication review. The useful goal is a growing library of accepted, varied, source-bound work.
