- [Data analyst](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/data-analyst): Give it a dataset and a question. Get evidence-based answers with trends, tests and correlations.
- [Data chart maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/data-chart-drawer): Turn your data into a clean chart. Give it a file or pasted rows and get a chart image or interactive plot, with the right chart type picked for you.

- [Dataset analysis and insight reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/turn-a-raw-dataset-into-a-checked-brief-of-top-insights): Share a data file and one clear question. Get a data profile, checked analysis and a ranked brief with actions and limits.
