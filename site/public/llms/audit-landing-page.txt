# Audit a landing page: AI Blocks

> 36 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [30-point landing page checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/landing-page-30-point-checker): Share your page and screenshots. Get a 30-point audit, a score and five fixes to make first.
- [A/B test traffic checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/ab-test-traffic-checker): Check if your site has enough visitors for an A/B test. Paste visits, orders and revenue to get the weeks needed and a readiness score out of 100.
- [App store listing optimiser](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/app-store-listing-optimiser): Give it your app listing. Get a scored audit and a rewritten title, keywords, description and screenshots plan.
- [Article-style ad readiness checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/advertorial-readiness-checker): Share live ads, spend and break-even results. Get a build-or-wait decision, checklist score and page plan.
- [Article-style ad story checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/advertorial-story-and-belief-checker): Share an article-style ad draft. Get story breaks, missing reasons to believe, targeted rewrites and a checklist score.
- [Button and link label reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/click-choice-clarity-checker): Check your menu and button labels for clarity. Get each choice rated, a score out of 100, and plain rewrites for weak labels.
- [Collection page fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/collection-page-fixer): Check your collection page for stock gaps and weak top products. Give it a screenshot and stock by size. You get swaps, fixes and a score.
- [Conversion problem finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/conversion-problem-finder): Share a page, goal and evidence. It flags possible friction and ranks tests to check.
- [Conversion research planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/conversion-research-planner): Plan your conversion research before you test. Paste your page and analytics. You get an order that finds where people drop and why.
- [Customer objection coverage checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/buyer-doubt-gap-finder): Share customer questions and page copy. Find missing or hidden answers, with a coverage score out of100.
- [Decision friction auditor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/decision-friction-auditor): Find where your sign-up or checkout page loses people. Get a friction audit of layout, defaults and step order, with a fix for each.
- [Email sequence results auditor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/email-sequence-auditor): Review email steps and results before a rewrite. Get protect, fix and unknown groups, proposed tests and score parts with data gaps.
- [Landing page conversion reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/landing-page-auditor): Share your page and screenshots. Get conversion problems ranked by impact, with recommended fixes and a score.
- [Landing page copy repair reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/landing-page-fix-loop): Find weak page sections and rewrite them using supplied facts. Recheck the changes and flag what copy cannot fix.
- [Landing page draft and test planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/build-a-landing-page-fix-it-and-prove-the-top-change): Draft and review a landing page. Plan a test of one change, then use real results to make the next call.
- [Landing page repair reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/fix-your-landing-pages-top-problems-and-check-again): Rank landing page problems, separate fixes from test ideas and review the updated page against a 30-point checklist.
- [Page drop-off test planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/page-drop-off-fix): Share page content, step counts and customer questions. Get the largest measured drop, answer gaps and a traffic-based test estimate.
- [Page issue sorter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/page-issue-sorter): Sort a long list of page problems into fix now, investigate, test or tracking, then rank them. Get a clear plan for your page issue list.
- [Page traffic and conversion reader](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/page-traffic-conversion): Find which Shopify product and collection pages get visits but few sales. Give your store address, get a ranked page traffic and conversion report.
- [Page usability test planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/page-usability-review): Share a page and the task users need to finish. Get usability concerns, clearer click choices and a small participant-test plan.
- [Popup copy and display planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/popup-optimiser): Share your visitor goal and current results. Get popup copy, display rules and test ideas suited to your page.
- [Product page auditor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-page-auditor): Check your product page for changes that lift sales. Give it a link or screenshot and get a ranked list of fixes, each with the research behind it.
- [Product page screenshot auditor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-page-screenshot-auditor): Check your product page screenshots for what stops shoppers buying. Get an evidence table, changes to test and a score out of 100.
- [Sales funnel drop-off finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/sales-funnel-drop-off-finder): Find the step where most visitors drop out before they buy. Get fixes sized from your own numbers and a page score out of 100.
- [Sales funnel repair planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/leaky-funnel-fixer): Find where your landing page, signup or checkout loses people. Get a ranked list of leaks and a test plan to fix your conversion rate.
- [Shopify redirect chain finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/redirect-chain-finder): Use your Shopify store domain to get a read-only report of chains and shared redirect targets.
- [Simulated user walkthrough reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-design-persona-walkthrough): Get a persona walkthrough of your web page. See what a visitor thinks at each scroll, where they doubt or leave, and the fixes ranked.
- [Site search basics checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/site-search-basics-checker): Paste your site address and page list. Get a short fix list for duplicate titles, a missing sitemap and dead-end 404 pages.
- [Store page experiment reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-where-your-page-loses-buyers-and-prove-the-fix): Review page behaviour, rank changes to test and check the real test data before deciding to ship, stop or keep running.
- [Store usability checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-friction-checker): Attach screenshots of your store pages. Get a ranked cut list and a score out of 100.
- [Thin page finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/thin-page-finder): Connect your Shopify store. Get a CSV of empty or short pages and posts, plus missing SEO fields.
- [Unanswered customer question finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/customer-question-finder): Find the customer questions your product page leaves unanswered. Paste reviews and phone screenshots. Get a doubt table, fixes and a score out of 100.
- [Website fix backlog builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/website-fix-backlog-builder): Give it your live site. Get a ranked list of fixes for conversion, usability, message and speed, each with evidence.
- [Website fix priority planner](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/fix-now-or-test-sorter): Share page issues and evidence. Get a ranked list of tracking problems, quick fixes, questions to investigate and changes to test.
- [Website growth planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/website-growth-guide): Give it your website, traffic and goal. Get a phased plan to capture more leads and grow sales.
- [Website issue and test readiness reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/page-problems-test-picks): Share page evidence, analytics and customer feedback. Get a research plan, ranked issues and a check of whether traffic supports testing.
