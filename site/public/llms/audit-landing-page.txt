# Audit a landing page: AI Blocks

> 36 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [A/B test traffic checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/ab-test-traffic-checker): Paste visits, orders and revenue. Get the test size, weeks needed and a readiness score out of 100.

- [Buyer doubt gap finder](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/buyer-doubt-gap-finder): Paste customer questions and your page text. Get each doubt matched to the page, plus a score out of 100.
- [Click choice clarity checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/click-choice-clarity-checker): Paste your menu and labels. Get a score out of 100, each choice rated and rewritten.
- [Collection page fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/collection-page-fixer): Paste your collection page and stock by size. Get which products to feature and a daily check.
