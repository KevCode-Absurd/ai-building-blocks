# Find products worth selling: AI Blocks

> 14 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Category and trend risk checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/category-and-trend-risk-checker): Name a category to enter. Get a score out of 100 on market size, trend risk and fit.
- [Competitor revenue estimator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/competitor-revenue-estimator): Give a rival's visits and price. Get low, mid and high monthly revenue, a score and a verdict.
- [Competitor saturation checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/competitor-saturation-checker): Give it a product name. Get store counts, rival strength, a score out of 100 and a go.
- [Crowded category white space finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/crowded-category-white-space-finder): Name a crowded category and its leaders. Get the gaps in price, reach, taste or buyer.
- [Find a product idea in buyer complaints and score it](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-a-product-idea-in-buyer-complaints-and-score-it): Give it a category. Get complaints that sell, the gap to own and a score out of 100.
- [Find a product that sells in a gap rivals miss](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-a-product-that-sells-and-a-gap-rivals-leave-open): Give it your market. Get proven products, a gap rivals leave open, a score out of 100 and a small first test.
- [New product line checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/new-product-line-checker): Name a product to add. Get CAC or LTV, a score out of 100, a test cap.
- [Pain point niche finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pain-point-niche-finder): Pick a market. Get 30 buyer problems, your best 5 and the brands already selling to them.
- [Proven product scout](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/proven-product-scout): Name your market. Get ad spy filters, a reading routine and a short list of proven products.
- [Rating gap product finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/rating-gap-product-finder): Name a category. Get pain points ranked by urgency and sub-categories with weak ratings but strong search.
- [Taster event planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/taster-event-planner): Give it your product versions and buyer. Get a one-day tasting plan, taster card and decision rule.
- [TikTok Shop breakout finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/tiktok-shop-breakout-finder): Attach TikTok Shop product graphs. Get a score out of 100 for each, and test or drop.
- [Untapped buyer finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/untapped-buyer-finder): Give it a crowded product. Get the buyer groups nobody targets, ranked, with a test for each.
- [Winning product scorer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/winning-product-scorer): Give it a product idea and its costs. Get a score out of 100 and a verdict.
