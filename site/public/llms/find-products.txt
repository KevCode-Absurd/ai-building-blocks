# Find products worth selling: AI Blocks

> 17 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Advertised product finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/proven-product-scout): Find proven products to sell. Name your market and get ad spy filters, an ad reading routine and a short list of five to ten products marked winner or fluke.
- [Buyer complaint product idea reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-a-product-idea-in-buyer-complaints-and-score-it): Rank sourced buyer complaints, compare gaps and score a chosen product using its actual price and costs.
- [Buyer problem and product idea finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pain-point-niche-finder): Name a market and your budget. Get 30 problem ideas, five picks and a plan to check products and competitors.
- [Category and trend risk checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/category-and-trend-risk-checker): Name a category to enter. Get a score out of 100 on market size, trend risk and fit.
- [Competitor revenue estimator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/competitor-revenue-estimator): Estimate a rival store's monthly revenue from its visits and price. Get a low, mid and high case, a score and a clear verdict.
- [Competitor saturation checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/competitor-saturation-checker): Check how crowded a product is before you test it. Give it rival ads from the Ad Library. You get a score out of 100 and a go, test fast or skip.
- [Crowded market opportunity finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/crowded-category-white-space-finder): Find gaps in a crowded product category by price, reach, taste or buyer. Give it the top brands and get ranked gaps, a position for each and a test.
- [New customer group test planner](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/open-buyer-group-finder): Share product facts and rival ads. Get buyer groups missing from that sample, ideas to test and spending stop rules.
- [New product line checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/new-product-line-checker): Share a new product, margin and launch budget. Assess its role, buyer overlap and sales channels, then draft a capped test.
- [Product candidate test planner](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/monthly-product-opportunity-scout): Review product candidates from collected evidence. Check costs and competition, then propose one small test or no pick.
- [Product idea viability scorer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/winning-product-scorer): Score a winning product idea before you buy stock or run ads. Give it your cost and price. You get 11 rule marks, a score out of 100 and a verdict.
- [Product market test planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-a-product-that-sells-and-a-gap-rivals-leave-open): Research products and rival gaps, check a chosen product's costs and plan a small test with a pass rule set in advance.
- [Product niche problem finder](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/product-niche-problem-picker): Name a market and your limits. Get 20 possible buyer problems, five picks, product ideas and searches to check demand.
- [Product opportunity reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/rating-gap-product-finder): Find product gaps where buyers search a lot but rate what exists low. Give it a category and get ranked pain points with proof links.
- [Taster event planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/taster-event-planner): Plan a one-day product tasting event for your food or drink. Get a cost line, a taster card and a pass rule set before the day.
- [TikTok Shop breakout finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/tiktok-shop-breakout-finder): Find TikTok Shop products worth testing. Attach your research graphs and get each one scored out of 100, with a clear test, cross-check or drop call.
- [Untapped buyer finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/untapped-buyer-finder): Find buyer groups your rivals miss for a crowded product. Give it rival ads. You get ranked groups and a small test for each.
