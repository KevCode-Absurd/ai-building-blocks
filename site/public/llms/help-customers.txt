# Help your customers: AI Blocks

> 30 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Angry customer reply checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/angry-customer-reply-checker): Paste the complaint and your draft reply. Get promises, facts and tone checked, plus a repaired draft.
- [Angry customer reply writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/angry-customer-reply-writer): Paste an angry message. Get a reply that names the feeling, fixes what you can and promises nothing false.
- [Chargeback evidence gatherer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/chargeback-evidence-gatherer): Tell it the dispute reason. Get what proof to collect, where to find it in Shopify, and whether to accept instead.
- [Clear your support inbox and turn fixes into help pages](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/clear-your-support-inbox-and-turn-fixes-into-help-pages): Give it your open tickets. Get them sorted, checked replies to send and a help page per fix.
- [Complaint reply promise checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/complaint-reply-promise-checker): Paste the complaint and your draft reply. Get each promise checked, tone flags and a repaired draft.
- [Complaint to story planner](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/complaint-to-story-planner): Paste a complaint and your fix. Get the one surprise that turns it into a story customers retell.
- [Customer inbox triage and reply drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/customer-inbox-triage-and-reply-drafter): Give it the new customer emails. Get them ranked, with a checked reply to approve for each. It never sends.
- [Customer question researcher](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/customer-question-researcher): Ask a customer question. Get an answer found across your docs, past tickets and chats, with each source named.
- [Customer reply and order checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/customer-reply-and-order-checker): Forward a customer email. Get order status checked and a reply in your voice. Refunds only on your yes.
- [Customer reply drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/customer-reply-drafter): Describe the customer's situation. Get a reply in the right tone, plus notes on what to check before you send it.
- [Customer review reply writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/customer-review-reply-writer): Give it your reviews and complaints. Get themes with quotes, a reply to each review, and customers at risk. Scrapes sites.
- [Escalation brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/escalation-brief-writer): Give it a stuck customer issue. Get a clear escalation brief for engineering or leadership, with impact and what was tried.
- [Feature request no writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/feature-request-no-writer): Paste a feature request you will not build. Get a no the customer accepts, with the real reason.
- [Get a stuck customer issue unstuck and close the loop](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/get-a-stuck-customer-issue-unstuck-and-close-the-loop): Give it the stuck issue. Get the facts, an escalation brief and a reply that keeps its promises.
- [Help article writer from support tickets](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/help-article-from-ticket-writer): Paste tickets about the same problem. Get a short help article in the customer's words, with its title.
- [Monthly help centre keeper](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/monthly-help-centre-keeper): Give it last month's support tickets each month. Get the questions your help pages miss, new articles drafted and stale ones fixed.
- [Public review reply desk](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/public-review-reply-desk): Give it each new batch of public reviews. Get a checked reply for each, with risky ones held and repeat complaints flagged.
- [Public review reply drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/public-review-reply-drafter): Paste your store reviews. Get a specific reply to each, with risky ones flagged and no refunds promised.
- [Refund and reorder processor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/refund-and-reorder-processor): Name the order and items. Get a full or partial refund, plus a replacement order if you want one. Moves real money.
- [Refund request reply writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/refund-request-reply-writer): Paste a refund request and your policy. Get a clear yes, no or partial reply that keeps the customer.
- [Reply to reviews safely and plan how to get more](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/reply-to-your-reviews-safely-and-plan-how-to-get-more): Give it your reviews. Get themes, safe replies and a plan to get more reviews.
- [Review reply batch writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/review-reply-batch-writer): Paste your store reviews. Get a specific public reply to each one and a list of the risky ones.
- [Review request and reply planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/review-request-and-reply-planner): Tell it about your reviews. Get when to ask, how to word the request, and how to answer a bad review.
- [Solved-ticket help page writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/solved-ticket-help-page-writer): Give it a solved ticket. Get a help article with steps, what you should see, and a fix for next time.
- [Support desk automation builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/support-desk-automation-builder): Tell it your help desk and tools. Get scripts, reply templates and a triage routine that drafts replies.
- [Support inbox priority sorter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/support-inbox-priority-sorter): Paste a support ticket. Get its category, P1 to P4 priority, owning team and any known duplicate.
- [Support ticket sorter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/support-ticket-sorter): Give it incoming tickets. Get each one classified, ranked by severity, routed and given a first reply.
- [Turn repeat customer questions into help pages people understand](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/turn-repeat-customer-questions-into-help-pages-people-understand): Give it the questions you answer every week. Get checked help pages a stranger can follow.
- [Voice assistant builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/voice-assistant-builder): Say what your voice assistant should do. Get a working agent that talks with customers, with tools and a web embed.
- [WhatsApp marketing planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/whatsapp-marketing-planner): Describe your business. Get a WhatsApp setup with broadcasts, automatic replies and support flows.
