- [Image maker and editor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/image-maker-and-editor): Describe a picture or give one to change. Get new images, variants or transparent cutouts.

- [Marketing image maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/marketing-image-maker): Say what image you need. Get a prompt, size and file plan for blog heroes, social graphics and mockups.
- [Multi-provider image maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/multi-provider-image-maker): Give it a prompt. Get an image from the image service you choose, in the size you pick.
- [Product demo video maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-demo-video-maker): Give it screenshots or a page. Get a short demo video or GIF that walks through the product.
- [Science diagram drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/science-diagram-drafter): Describe a diagram. Get a draft of a network, pathway or flowchart, reviewed and refined once or twice.
- [Slack GIF maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/slack-gif-maker): Describe the animation. Get a GIF sized and checked to work in Slack.
- [Stable Diffusion image maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/stable-diffusion-image-maker): Describe a picture. Get generated images, edits to your own photos or code for a custom image pipeline.
