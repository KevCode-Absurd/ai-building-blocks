# Plan projects and team capacity: AI Blocks

> 17 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Client project kickoff planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/prepare-a-clear-client-project-kickoff): Share approved design scope, participants and open decisions. Get a kickoff brief, meeting decision and action list; an agenda when needed.
- [Client scope change and decision tracker](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/client-scope-decision-review-assistant): Compare a client change request with the signed scope. Get delivery options and a draft reply, with new work kept as proposed until the client accepts.
- [Client scope change planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/prepare-a-client-scope-change-brief): Share the signed scope, change request and available hours. Get a scope comparison, delivery options and a client decision draft.
- [Notion build task planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/spec-to-task-planner): Turn a spec in Notion into a clear implementation plan and linked tasks, with gaps and assumptions listed before work starts.
- [Project delay test reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/weekly-project-delay-evidence-assistant): Compare new task times with the original baseline. Get delay patterns and a keep, revise or wait recommendation for the current test.
- [Project delivery manager](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-project-management-project-shepherd): Plan a project that spans several teams. Get a project charter, a plan with owners and dependencies, a risk list and a weekly status report.
- [Project kickoff brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/project-kickoff-brief-writer): Turn a rough idea into a project kickoff brief with goal, audience, tone and limits that your team can sign off. Under two pages.
- [Project planning manager](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-project-manager-senior): Give it a website or app spec. You get small tasks, each with an acceptance check, plus the gaps to settle first.
- [Project risk portfolio planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/project-risk-portfolio): Share project risks, scores, owners and resource needs. It compares exposure and workload, then drafts priorities and status for review.
- [Project time and expense planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/project-time-and-expense-plan): Set up how your team logs project hours and expenses. You get entry rules, an approval matrix and a checklist for handing time to billing.
- [Recurring work delay reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/trace-why-work-keeps-missing-deadlines): Share repeated work steps, owners and timing records. Get likely delay points, a grounded timeline estimate and a small change to test.
- [Software project manager](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/software-project-manager): Describe your project. Get work split into tasks, sprints tracked and status reports written.
- [Sprint priority planner](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-product-sprint-prioritizer): Pick what your team builds next sprint. Paste your backlog and team capacity to get a ranked list, what fits, what waits and what to tell people.
- [Studio producer agent](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-project-management-studio-producer): Plan several creative projects at once. Get a portfolio plan with tiers, budget and team time, risks, and what to delay or drop.
- [Team capacity and delivery-date planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/check-capacity-before-promising-a-delivery-date): Check team hours and work already promised. Review risks and draft delivery options before you agree to a new date.
- [Team capacity planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/team-capacity-planner): Give it your team and projects. Get workload against people, who is overloaded, and whether you can take on more.
- [Team sprint planner and reporter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/delivery-team-sprint-runner): Share tracker data, team availability and recent history. Get a work-capacity check, meeting plans and reports based on the team’s actual records.
