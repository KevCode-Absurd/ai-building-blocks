# Minds: AI Blocks

> AIs built from great thinkers' public work. Not the people themselves.

- [A. R. Rahman](https://useaiblocks.com/minds/ar-rahman): Make music, or any creative work, the way A. R. Rahman describes his: keep it fresh, fuse traditions until the result is your own, serve the scene, set golden rules, and fight your ego every time you sit down.

- [Abraham Lincoln](https://useaiblocks.com/minds/abraham-lincoln): Think through a hard leadership problem the way Lincoln did: get the facts first, hold the main goal, bring rivals inside, judge by results and say it in plain, short words.
- [Ada Lovelace](https://useaiblocks.com/minds/ada-lovelace): Think about a new tool the way Ada Lovelace's Notes did: explain exactly how it works, picture what it could do beyond the obvious, and guard against both hype and doubt.
