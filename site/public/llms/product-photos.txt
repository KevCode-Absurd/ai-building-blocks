# Make product photos: AI Blocks

> 29 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [A full product photoshoot from your phone photos](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/phone-product-photoshoot): Give it your product photos and brand look. You get 8 to 12 photos, each with a job and its own light.
- [A product gallery that answers shoppers' doubts](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/product-gallery-answers): Give it your product, photos, reviews and current gallery. You get a checked set of images, ready to upload.
- [Accessory try-on photo maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/accessory-try-on-photo-maker): Give it a shoe, bag, watch or necklace photo. Get a person wearing it. Your image model draws it.
- [AI product photo inspector](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/ai-product-photo-inspector): Give it an AI-made product photo. Get a risk rating, eight checks and lines to add to your prompt before you retry.
- [Fabric close-up maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/fabric-close-up-maker): Give it a clothing photo. Get close-ups of fabric, stitching and weave for the product page. Your image model draws them.
- [Fabric swap sample maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/fabric-swap-sample-maker): Give it a garment shape and a fabric swatch. Get a sample photo in the new fabric. Your image model draws it.
- [Fabric texture fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/fabric-texture-fixer): Give it a blurry clothing photo and a sharp product photo. Get clear, believable fabric. Your image model redraws it.
- [Flash snapshot product photo maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/flash-product-photos): Attach your product photos. Get 6 flash snapshot prompts from one real occasion.
- [Get product photos in three looks from phone photos](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/three-look-product-photos): Give it your website and product photos. Get a styled photo set, flash snapshots and a copy of a look you like.
- [Ghost mannequin photo maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/ghost-mannequin-photo-maker): Give it a clothing flat lay. Get a photo shaped as if worn by an invisible model. Your image model draws it.
- [Main product image designer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/main-product-image-designer): Give it a product photo and selling points. Get a main image with the copy laid on. Your image model draws it.
- [Marketplace photo rule checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/marketplace-photo-rule-checker): Give it a product photo. Get a pass or reject check for Amazon, TikTok Shop, Temu and Shopee rules, and a fix.
- [Missing product image finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/missing-product-image-finder): Get products and variants with no image or too few images, so no page goes live looking bare.
- [Model and scene swapper](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/model-and-scene-swapper): Give it a model photo. Get versions with other models and backgrounds, same clothes. Your image model draws them.
- [Outfit photo composer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/outfit-photo-composer): Give it up to eight single items. Get one photo of a model wearing the full outfit. Your image model draws it.
- [Product background swapper](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-background-swapper): Give it a white-background product photo. Get it placed in a real-looking scene with matching light. Your image model draws it.
- [Product detail image maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-detail-image-maker): Give it a product photo and selling points. Get detail page pictures with the text built in. Your image model draws them.
- [Product image spec writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-image-spec-writer): Attach your product photos. Get a sourced truth sheet, lock lines and the next photo to take.
- [Product page gallery planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-page-gallery-planner): Attach your product photos and facts. Get an 8-image gallery plan with sourced words and a gaps list.

- [Product photo batch maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-photo-batch-maker): Give it a spreadsheet of products. Get one matching photo per product, with a cost limit. Your image model draws them.
- [Product photo retoucher](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/product-photo-retoucher): Give it a phone photo of a product. Get a cleaner listing photo with fewer wrinkles. Your image model edits it.

- [Turn real photos into a product page gallery](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/photo-product-gallery): Give it your product photos and facts. You get about 8 checked images that each answer a shopper question.
- [Virtual try-on photo maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/virtual-try-on-photo-maker): Give it a clothing flat lay and a pose photo. Get a model-wearing shot. Your image model draws it, not Claude.
