# Keep your store safe: AI Blocks

> 13 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Bot and AI agent access planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/bot-and-ai-agent-access-planner): Plan which bots and AI agents to allow, check or block on your store. Give it your traffic and flows. You get a policy, limits and alerts.
- [Domain access and certificate reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/domain-and-certificate-watch-checker): Review supplied domain records and account access. Get possible takeover risks, renewal gaps, a house checklist score and proposed fixes.
- [Order fraud and bot-access planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/stop-fake-orders-card-testing-and-bot-abuse): Share order patterns, payment setup and incident evidence. Get proposed fraud rules, a review routine and a later bot-access plan.
- [Order risk review rule planner](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/risky-order-rule-writer): Share order numbers, payment disputes and review time. Get draft risk rules, checks of current defaults and limits to confirm with your payment provider.
- [Sign-in policy drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/sign-in-policy-drafter): Describe account access and sign-in methods without sharing passwords. Get ranked gaps, a checklist score and a draft sign-in policy.
- [Sign-in security checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-sign-in-security-checker): Describe how staff and customers sign in. Get gaps ranked, a score and a password and second-step policy.
- [Staff phishing awareness trainer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/staff-phishing-rules-briefer): Share your tools and team size. Get a one-page phishing rule sheet and a ten-minute drill with answers.
- [Store access security reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/quarterly-store-security-keeper): Review staff access, sign-in and app risks. Compare earlier findings and get the next security checks and fixes to consider.
- [Store account security reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/lock-down-your-store-logins-and-staff-against-takeover): Describe store accounts, staff access and apps without sharing secrets. Get gap priorities, a sign-in policy draft and phishing guidance.
- [Store app and script reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/cut-risky-apps-and-scripts-from-your-store): Review which store apps to keep, replace or remove, then inspect the scripts that remain after approved changes.
- [Store fraud rules planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/fraud-rules-setter): Share your order volume and payment setup. Get fraud rules, a review routine and a chargeback watch line.
- [Store script and app risk auditor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-script-and-app-risk-auditor): Check the scripts, apps and pixels on your store for risk. Get each one ranked, a score out of 100 and a list of what to remove and revoke.
- [Store security reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-security-checkup): Describe your admin team and apps. Get a ranked security fix list, a score and a quarterly routine.
