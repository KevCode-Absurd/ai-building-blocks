# Plan funding and ownership: AI Blocks

> 18 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Convertible loan interest calculator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/convertible-note-interest-checker): Share the signed loan terms. Get accrued interest, the balance that converts and the resulting share count.
- [Convertible note conversion checker](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/check-a-convertible-note-conversion): Calculate the amount a note can convert, compare its cap and discount prices, and check the resulting share count.
- [Convertible note conversion reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/note-conversion-revision-assistant): Review changed note terms, accrued interest and conversion prices. Get a sourced balance-to-shares bridge and unresolved price questions.
- [Convertible note price checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/cap-and-discount-conversion-checker): Share the signed lower-of terms, cap price, round price, discount and balance. Get the lower price and shares issued.
- [Deal funding gap checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/deal-funding-gap-checker): Share deal costs and funding sources. Check the closing cash gap and how rollover value is counted.
- [Debt funding and credit limit reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/maturity-funding-evidence-reviewer): Match debt due dates with confirmed funding. See cash gaps and credit limit issues without treating unused limits as cash.
- [Debt maturity funding checker](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/review-maturity-funding-and-credit-limits): Share debt dates, usable cash, confirmed loans and credit limits. Get a funding schedule, shortfalls and exposure checks.
- [Debt repayment funding planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/debt-maturity-funding-ladder): Share debt due dates, cash and confirmed funding. Get a repayment schedule with funding gaps shown.
- [Debt restructuring plan drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/debt-restructuring-plan-drafter): Give it your company's debts and cash flow. You get a draft debt restructuring plan with new terms for each creditor and a recovery comparison.
- [Down-round conversion and option-pool change checker](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/review-a-down-round-rights-change): Share charter terms, new issuance details and reserve targets. Get conversion-price reset checks, option-pool changes and unresolved share-count effects.
- [Down-round conversion price checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/weighted-average-antidilution-checker): Share charter terms and a qualifying share issue. Check the weighted-average reset price and revised conversion ratio.
- [Employee option pool top-up calculator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/option-pool-top-up-checker): Share share counts and the agreed pool target. Check extra reserved shares and dilution before new funding.
- [Funding round multiple analyst](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/funding-round-multiple-analyst): Share a software company. Get how its value multiple changed across funding rounds and what drove the shift.
- [Loan application document checker](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-loan-officer-assistant): Get a loan file checklist that shows missing documents, expiry dates, deadlines to track and questions for the reviewer. No credit decisions.
- [Post-money SAFE ownership calculator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/post-money-safe-ownership-checker): Share a signed cap-only SAFE and later dilution. Check ownership before and after a priced funding round.
- [SAFE ownership and funding checker](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/check-a-safe-into-priced-financing): Check SAFE ownership, the share reserve change and rights to join a funding round, using the actual signed terms.
- [SAFE ownership and share-pool reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/safe-financing-revision-assistant): Review a funded SAFE and later round changes. Check contract share counts, option pool effects and supported ownership figures.
- [Venture loan terms reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/venture-loan-terms-reviewer): Give it a startup loan offer. Get the cost, warrants and covenants explained, with points to question.
