# Sell on Amazon: AI Blocks

> 16 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Amazon brand registry planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-brand-registry-planner): Give it your brand name and listing. Get a trademark choice, a registry pack and tools to turn on.
- [Amazon fee and margin checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-fee-and-margin-checker): Give it price, landed cost and size. Get profit per unit, all fees and a score out of 100.
- [Amazon inbound stock planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-inbound-stock-planner): Give it sales, lead times and case sizes. Get how much to send, when, and a shipment checklist.
- [Amazon keyword picker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-keyword-picker): Give it your product and a keyword export. Get the phrases for your title, bullets and backend.
- [Amazon launch week planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-launch-week-planner): Give it your product and audience. Get a pre-launch and launch-day plan for early sales and reviews.
- [Amazon listing first draft writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/amazon-listing-first-draft-writer): Paste product facts and search phrases. Get an Amazon title, five bullets and backend keywords.
- [Amazon listing writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-listing-writer): Give it product facts and keyword tiers. Get an Amazon title, five benefit bullets and backend keywords.
- [Amazon PPC launch builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-ppc-launch-builder): Give it your product, keywords and daily budget. Get auto, keyword and competitor campaigns with bids and negatives.
- [Amazon product opportunity finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-search-gap-finder): Give it an interest or category. Get Amazon product ideas people search for that page 1 misses.
- [Amazon product viability scorer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-product-viability-scorer): Give it a product idea and costs. Get a score out of 100, the weakest test and a go or no-go.
- [Amazon reimbursement claim finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-reimbursement-claim-finder): Give it shipment and fee reports. Get a claim-readiness score and the claims to file.
- [Amazon unit profit checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/amazon-unit-profit-checker): Paste price, landed cost and Amazon fees. Get profit per unit and break-even ad cost.
- [Find an Amazon product gap and check it will pay](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-an-amazon-product-gap-and-check-it-will-pay): Give it a category. Get product ideas page 1 misses, a score out of 100 and a go or no-go.
- [Launch an Amazon product with sales from day one](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/launch-an-amazon-product-with-sales-from-day-one): Give it product facts and keywords. Get a listing, launch ads, a stock order and a launch week.
- [Monthly Amazon account review](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/amazon-monthly-account-keeper): Give it your Amazon fee, stock and sales reports. Get margins checked, restock sized and refund claims found.
- [Ship stock to Amazon and claim back what goes missing](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/ship-stock-to-amazon-and-claim-back-what-goes-missing): Give it your sales and product facts. Get a shipment plan, then the claims to file.
