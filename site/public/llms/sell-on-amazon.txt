# Sell on Amazon: AI Blocks

> 16 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Amazon brand registry planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-brand-registry-planner): Share your brand name and listing. Get a trademark choice, a registry pack and tools to turn on.
- [Amazon fee and margin checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-fee-and-margin-checker): Share price, costs, size and fee figures. Get profit per unit, break-even ad cost and a score against the source’s rules.
- [Amazon inbound stock planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-inbound-stock-planner): Give it sales, lead times and case sizes. Get how much to send, when, and a shipment checklist.
- [Amazon keyword picker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-keyword-picker): Give it your product and a keyword export. Get the phrases for your title, bullets and backend.
- [Amazon launch week planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-launch-week-planner): Plan your Amazon launch week with a dated pre-launch plan, a launch-day run sheet, a discount with its cost, and honest review steps.
- [Amazon listing first draft writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/amazon-listing-first-draft-writer): Write a first Amazon listing from your product facts and search phrases. You get a title, five bullets, a short description and backend keywords.
- [Amazon listing writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-listing-writer): Write an Amazon listing from your product facts: a title with the main phrase first, five benefit bullets and backend keywords, ready to paste.
- [Amazon margins, stock and claim review](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/amazon-monthly-account-keeper): Review Amazon reports for margins, restock needs and possible claims. Get a ranked action list using current fees and supporting records.
- [Amazon product gap and viability reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-an-amazon-product-gap-and-check-it-will-pay): Review what Amazon search results miss, then score a selected idea using supplier cost, size, weight and selling price.
- [Amazon product launch preparation](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/launch-an-amazon-product-with-sales-from-day-one): Share product facts, keyword data and stock details. Get listing drafts, ad plans, inbound-stock planning and a launch-week checklist.
- [Amazon product opportunity finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-search-gap-finder): Share an interest, category and any search data. Get ranked product-gap ideas and the evidence needed to test them.
- [Amazon product viability scorer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-product-viability-scorer): Share a product idea and costs. Get a score, the weakest test and a go or no-go.
- [Amazon reimbursement claim finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-reimbursement-claim-finder): Find Amazon reimbursement claims you can file. Give it 90 days of shipment and fee reports. You get a readiness score and a list of claims with proof.
- [Amazon Sponsored Products ad planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/amazon-ppc-launch-builder): Share product profit, keywords and budget. Get three Amazon ad campaign plans with bids, excluded search terms and stop rules.
- [Amazon stock shipment and reimbursement evidence planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/ship-stock-to-amazon-and-claim-back-what-goes-missing): Share product and shipment records. Get a stock plan, evidence checklist and possible claim gaps after the shipment closes.
- [Amazon unit profit checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/amazon-unit-profit-checker): Check your Amazon unit profit before you order stock. Paste price, landed cost and fees. You get profit per unit and break-even ad cost.
