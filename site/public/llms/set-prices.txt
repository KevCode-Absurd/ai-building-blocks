# Set your prices: AI Blocks

> 39 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Bulk price changer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/bulk-price-changer): Pick products and a percent or amount. Get every price changed at once, within limits. Changes live prices, so preview first.
- [Clearance discount planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/clearance-discount-planner): Give it your slow stock and past sales. Get when and how much to cut prices to sell out.
- [Digital product pricer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/digital-product-pricer): Tell it what you sell, such as a course or ebook. Get the price digits, labels and launch discount to use.
- [Discount and price change checker](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/discount-and-price-change-checker): Give it a planned sale or price change. Get the deepest discount your margin allows and the wording to use.
- [Discount sanity checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/discount-sanity-checker): Paste every discount you show. Get a consistency score out of 100, each clash, and one true offer line.
- [Discount wording planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/discount-wording-planner): Give it the discount you plan. Get the percent or amount form, sale price display and banner wording that look biggest.
- [ETF price gap calculator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/etf-price-gap-calculator): Give it an ETF ticker. Get how far its price sits above or below its holdings, and why. Collects data from websites.
- [Event ticket pricer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/event-ticket-pricer): Tell it about your class, workshop or event. Get ticket prices, early-bird terms and how to show seats and dates.
- [Fake sale price cleaner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/fake-sale-price-cleaner): Get strikethrough prices removed where there is no real discount. Changes live prices and the old values are not kept.
- [Free-to-paid flow designer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/free-to-paid-flow-designer): Describe your SaaS plans. Get upgrade moments, paywall design, win-back flows and churn prevention steps.
- [Hotel room pricing planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/hotel-room-pricing-planner): Give it your bookings and rates. Get room prices, overbooking limits and a channel mix that fill rooms at the best rate.
- [Membership plan pricer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/membership-plan-pricer): Tell it your plans or membership. Get tier prices, flat or pay-per-use advice, and ways to win upgrades.
- [Minimum viable price calculator](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/waterfall-price-setter): Share costs, sales targets and acquisition spend. Calculate the lowest viable price or identify a product that cannot pay.
- [Prepare your first freelance project quote](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/prepare-your-first-freelance-project-quote): Share the source files. Get a scoped proposal draft, price assumptions and a contract review list.
- [Price a course or ebook and write its launch emails](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/price-a-course-or-ebook-and-write-its-launch-emails): Give it your course or ebook. Get a price, honest discount wording and the launch email sequence.

- [Price digits and placement planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/price-display-planner): Give it a price you are weighing. Get whether to round it, how to price add-ons, and where to place it.
- [Price ending picker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/price-ending-picker): Paste a price and what you sell. Get round, just-below or exact digits, with the reason.
- [Price position planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/price-position-planner): Give it your costs and rival offers. Get a price for each product, response rules for rival discounts, and a rollout plan.
- [Price presentation planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/price-presentation-planner): Give it your product and prices. Get a tier and anchor layout that makes value clear without tricks.
- [Price rise letter writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/price-rise-letter-writer): Paste the old price, the new price and why. Get a customer letter that keeps most of them.

- [Pricing model designer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pricing-model-designer): Give it your product and price survey data. Get a pricing model, price points and good, better, best tiers.
- [Pricing strategist](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pricing-strategist): Describe your product and buyers. Get tiers, a value metric and a test plan for your prices.
- [Quarterly client rate reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/quarterly-client-rate-reviewer): Give it your hours and invoices by client each quarter. Get each client's real rate, who to raise and what to propose.
