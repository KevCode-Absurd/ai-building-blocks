# Speak and persuade: AI Blocks

> 40 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Annual letter writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/annual-letter-writer): Write an annual letter to your team or owners from your results. It puts the bad news first, owns mistakes, and says what you will do and stop.
- [Bad news update writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/bad-news-update-writer): Write a bad news update that opens with the loss, gives the real numbers and ends on next steps. Paste the facts of the setback to start.
- [Belief-led message writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/why-first-message-writer): Turn a pitch that opens with your product into a why-first message. Give your cause and audience, and get belief, how, then what.
- [Board deck builder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/board-deck-builder): Build a board deck outline from your quarter's numbers. Get the story, metrics, bad news and board asks laid out section by section.
- [Board meeting question coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/hard-questions-board-prep-coach): Share your board agenda, deck and weak spots. Get tough practice questions, number checks and answer outlines with clear next actions.
- [Case competition financial-assumption reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/stress-test-a-case-competition-recommendation): Share the full case, marking guide and draft recommendation. Get a challenged argument, financial checks and an editable deck when tools support it.
- [Case competition slide reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/case-competition-evidence-review-assistant): Check a changed case recommendation and its numbers. Review new assumptions and update the affected slides with supported tools.
- [Case-based pitch and talk writer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/build-a-talk-around-one-real-case): Use one real case to build a pitch story, explain the idea and put the stakes early in the talk.
- [Contact notes organizer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/contact-memory-card-writer): Turn rough notes from a first meeting into a one-page contact memory card with the exact name, facts, promises and your next contact date.
- [Conversation question planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/get-them-talking-plan): Plan a conversation that gets the other person talking. Give it a brief on them. You get a warm start, open questions and a close.
- [Funny speech planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/funny-speech-planner): Turn one true story into a funny speech or toast. You get a deadpan script with the pause marked and a check on what your room can take.
- [Honest thank-you note writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/honest-appreciation-note-writer): Share one thing you valued and a real detail. Get a short thank-you note that stays specific, truthful and free of requests.
- [Internal proposal pitch planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/idea-buy-in-pitch-planner): Plan how to get buy-in for your idea. Give it your idea and the people who decide. You get a story, a vision picture and an order to approach them.
- [Investor pitch script and email writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/investor-pitch-scriptwriter): Share startup facts, funding needs and target investors. Get pitch drafts, likely questions and practice notes.
- [Launch talk writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/launch-talk-writer): Turn a product launch into a two-minute talk. Give your product, audience and venue. Get a timed script with one story, a demo and a closing belief.
- [Lesson story and talk writer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/turn-your-lessons-into-funny-stories-and-a-short-talk): Share two or three lessons and real examples. Get short story drafts, checks that each serves one point and a five-to-ten-minute talk.
- [Lesson talk writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/lesson-talk-writer): Turn your own stories and lessons into a short talk, like a graduation speech. Get two or three repeatable lessons, a joke, a script and one next step.
- [Meeting brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/before-you-meet-brief-writer): Share who you will meet, your goal and public sources. Get a brief with supported talking points, a question and a small ask.
- [Meeting preparation and follow-up writer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/first-impression-plan): Plan a meeting with good questions. Record real details and promises, then draft a short thank-you with no request.
- [Narrative memo checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/narrative-memo-checker): Check your narrative memo before the meeting. Get a score out of 100, each weak spot and the three fixes that add the most points.
- [Numbers explainer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/figures-to-plain-pictures-writer): Turn the key figures in your talk into comparisons and counts of people your room can picture. Paste your figures and who will hear them.
- [Offline event experiment planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/traction-offline-events): Share your product, buyer and limits. Get a small workshop plan with an attendee group, agenda, cost cap and follow-up measures.
- [Persuasion and claim editor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/honest-persuasion-editor): Share your message and facts you can prove. Find supported, missing or false persuasion claims, then get a clearer rewrite and change notes.
- [Persuasion and trust reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/honest-persuasion-advisor): Share a page, pitch or product flow and its facts. Review proof, trust and urgency ideas, with truth checks and changes to consider.
- [Pitch arc planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pitch-arc-planner): Plan a sales or investor pitch outline that builds belief first, then asks for a clear decision. Give it your audience and goal.
- [Pitch deck presentation reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pitch-deck-checker-10-20-30): Check your pitch deck against the 10/20/30 rule. Paste your slides and talk time. Get a score out of 100 from five checks and fixes by slide.
- [Pitch feedback and revision tracker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/pitch-revision-log): Share your pitch and feedback. Get repeated doubts, changes you can make now, a revised draft and questions to ask next.
- [Pitch objection and answer writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/hardest-objections-answer-writer): Find the toughest objections to your pitch or argument. Paste it and your evidence, get fair answers, open gaps and a rewrite that admits its limits.
- [Presenter fit planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/deliverer-fit-planner): Share your brief and presenter. Get an approach that suits their strengths, with pitfalls and a standout moment.
- [Press outreach experiment planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/traction-public-relations): Share product facts and a dated news hook. Get a reporter research plan, evidence-backed pitch and small outreach test.
- [Public relations message planner](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-marketing-pr-communications-manager): Plan an announcement for the press. Get three key messages, a press release outline, media pitch angles and spokesperson answers to review.
- [Read-aloud speech editor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/read-aloud-speech-shaper): Turn your notes into a speech or poem made to be heard, with breath marks and a refrain. Use it to write a speech for a funeral or event.
- [Setback update drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/setback-update-drafter): Write a setback update that states the loss first, gives the numbers and ends on next steps. Paste the facts and who it is for.
- [Short pitch feedback planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/write-a-short-pitch-and-fix-it-after-every-no): Share the idea, audience and evidence. Get a short pitch, answers to hard questions and a revision log after real feedback.
- [Six-page narrative memo writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/six-page-narrative-memo-writer): Turn your press release, questions and numbers into a six-page narrative memo in full sentences, with the ask on page one and a writing schedule.
- [Speaking event outreach planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/traction-speaking-engagements): Share your expertise and buyer audience. Get an event shortlist, talk proposal and follow-up resource plan.
- [Speech tension planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/talk-tension-planner): Reorder your talk or pitch outline so the room knows the stakes in the first minute. Get a true clock, picture swaps and one real twist.
- [Tough audience speech planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/hostile-room-speech-planner): Share the audience’s concerns, your point and evidence. Plan a speech with honest common ground, a clear argument and one practical next step.
- [Visual numbers explainer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/number-to-picture-translator): Turn your key figures into pictures an audience can feel. Give it your numbers and your listeners. You get each one as a comparison or a count of people.
- [Witty line editor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/witty-line-punch-up): Punch up a toast, speech or bio with two or three sharp turned lines. Each stays true, and the rest of your text stays plain.
