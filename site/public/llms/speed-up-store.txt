# Make your store load fast: AI Blocks

> 14 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Drupal performance engineer agent](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-engineering-drupal-performance): Find why your Drupal site is slow. Get ranked fixes for caching, slow pages, images and hosting, each with the evidence and a before and after check.
- [Layout shift fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/layout-shift-fixer): Fix layout shift on your store. Give it your PageSpeed CLS report and theme code. You get the jumping element, its cause and the CSS fix.
- [Leftover app code finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/leftover-app-code-finder): Find leftover app code in your Shopify theme. Give it your theme files and removed apps. You get each file and line to delete, plus a clean score.
- [Shopify script and style loader](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/theme-script-and-style-loader): Share your Shopify theme, app embeds and fonts. Get safer load rules and code changes for scripts, styles and fonts.
- [Store app script reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/app-script-auditor): Give it your apps and speed report. Get each app marked keep, remove or delay, and a score.
- [Store image prep checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-image-prep-checker): Check your store images before upload. Get what to resize, crop and rename, plus alt text, gallery order and a score out of 100.
- [Store speed report analyst](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-speed-report-reader): Share PageSpeed reports for key pages. Get field scores where traffic allows, lab clues and three fixes.
- [Store website speed reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/speed-budget-and-sale-check): Plan your store's speed budget before a sale. Give it your speed results and sale date. You get limits, a test routine and a pre-sale checklist.
- [Website performance fix ranker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/speed-fix-ranker): Rank your store speed fixes by orders gained per hour of work. Give it your audit problems and sales numbers. You get a plan for this week and next month.
- [Website speed repair planner](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/speed-fix-week-planner): Share speed issues, traffic and sales figures. Get fixes ranked by effort and reach, with assumed gain ranges clearly marked.
- [Website speed report explainer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/speed-report-translator): Share PageSpeed results. Get plain-language metric checks, a separate checklist score, likely causes and fixes supported by the report.
- [WooCommerce speed and budget reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/speed-up-a-woocommerce-store-and-keep-it-fast): Share your PageSpeed report, hosting and store setup. Get ordered fixes, speed limits and a checklist for the next sale.
- [WooCommerce speed repair planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/self-hosted-store-speed-fixer): Speed up your WooCommerce store. Give it your hosting, plugins and speed report. You get fixes in a safe order, plus what never to cache.
- [WordPress performance reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/agency-engineering-wordpress-performance): Find why your WordPress site is slow. Get ranked fixes for plugins, caching, the database, images and hosting, each with a before and after check.
