- [Layout shift fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/layout-shift-fixer): Give it your CLS report and page. Get the shifting element found and the fix for each cause.
- [Leftover app code finder](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/leftover-app-code-finder): Give it your theme files and removed apps. Get each leftover script found and the lines to delete.
- [Monthly store speed keeper](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/monthly-store-speed-keeper): Give it this month's speed reports and app list. It compares with last month, flags new app scripts, and ranks the fixes.

- [Speed fix ranker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/speed-fix-ranker): Give it your speed problems and store numbers. Get fixes ranked by orders gained per hour of work.
- [Speed up a WooCommerce store and keep it fast](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/speed-up-a-woocommerce-store-and-keep-it-fast): Give it your PageSpeed report and setup. Get speed fixes in order and a budget that keeps the store fast.
- [Store image prep checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-image-prep-checker): Give it your image files and sizes. Get what to resize and rename, alt text and a score.
- [Store speed report reader](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-speed-report-reader): Paste your PageSpeed report. Get a field score out of 100, each page read, three fixes.
- [Theme script and style loader](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/theme-script-and-style-loader): Give it your theme.liquid, apps and fonts. Get scripts delayed or deferred, styles unblocked and fonts set safely.
