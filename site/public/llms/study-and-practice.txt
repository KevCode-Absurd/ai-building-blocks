# Study and check your understanding: AI Blocks

> 31 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Claude Code learning coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/claude-code-learning-coach): Check what you have tried in Claude Code and find your gaps. Get a short practice plan from a quick or deeper experience check.
- [Claude Code quiz coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/claude-code-quiz-coach): Take a scored Claude Code quiz on one lesson. You get feedback on each answer and a list of what to review. Ten questions per lesson.
- [Codebase learning planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/codebase-learning-map): Turn a code folder into a study map. Get ordered phases, what to know first and 2 or 3 paths that fit your goal and level.
- [Coding project coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/coding-project-coach): Learn to code by building a real project. Your AI sets small milestones, gives hints and checks your code, and you write every line yourself.
- [Debugging practice coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/debugging-practice-coach): Learn to debug your own code. Share the bug and what you tried, and this debugging practice coach guides you to find and prove the fix yourself.
- [Flashcard maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/flashcard-maker): Paste your notes or a chapter. Get flashcards with one question and one short answer each, grouped by topic.
- [Guided reasoning tutor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/guided-reasoning-tutor): Bring a problem and your current thinking. Work through focused questions, hints, a clear correction and a small practice task.
- [Learning level checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/learning-level-check): Find your real level in a technical topic. Answer a few short questions and get a starting level and first lesson to match.
- [Learning mistake coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/learning-mistake-coach): Fix a mistake you keep making while learning. Share your wrong answer and get a clear correction, a fresh example and a check question.
- [Learning session handover writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/learning-session-handoff): Turn your session notes into an updated learner profile and a handoff note, so your next learning session starts where this one stopped.
- [Mistake-based practice planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/build-a-practice-plan-from-common-mistakes): Review actual learner mistakes, write practice with checked answers and plan a later recall check.
- [Personal study planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/personal-study-notebook): Plan your self-study and get lesson notes, quizzes with honest feedback, review dates and a progress dashboard saved in your own notes folder.
- [Reading notes and recall planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/turn-saved-reading-into-notes-you-can-use): Share reading excerpts, existing notes and a question. Get linked notes, a sourced answer and recall questions to check your understanding.
- [Research paper comparison writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/research-literature-comparison): Compare several research papers on one topic. Get a literature map of shared findings, conflicts and open questions, with sources.
- [Research paper evidence reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/research-paper-evidence-review): Check how far to trust a research paper. Give it the paper and get a claims review with weak spots, limits and what would settle the question.
- [Research paper reading coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/research-paper-reading-guide): Get a research paper reading guide: the main idea, the results, and which parts to read closely. Share the paper and see what to focus on.
- [Spanish and Turkish writing tutor](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/spanish-and-turkish-writing-tutor): Get your Spanish or Turkish writing corrected, with the reason for each change and a log of repeat mistakes, for exam prep or daily practice.
- [Student code project reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/student-coding-project-review): Get a student coding project review: specific strengths, up to five ranked fixes with code references, and a next step for each.
- [Student reflection feedback reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/review-feedback-on-a-student-reflection): Share the project record, reflection, marking guide and real feedback. Get an evidence check, clearer feedback and revision prompts.
- [Study gap tracker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-gap-tracker): Turn your repeated mistakes into a study gap tracker. Get a short ranked list of learning gaps and what to practice first.
- [Study note diagram maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-note-diagrams): Turn a lesson concept into a clear study note diagram: a flowchart, table or simple drawing, with a caption, placed right beside your explanation.
- [Study notebook reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-notebook-review): Run a study notebook review: recall each old topic first, get honest feedback, and see new review dates for every lesson note that is due.
- [Study practice progress reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/study-plan-progress-review-assistant): Review actual practice answers and available study time. Keep what works and adjust the next practice for observed gaps.
- [Study review scheduler](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-review-scheduler): Run a spaced repetition review of your study cards. Get a short recall session, new review dates and a view of the week ahead.
- [Study revision coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-revision-coach): Plan your exam revision in one session. Share your topics, past mistakes and deadline, and get a ranked review plan that starts with your weakest topics.
- [Study roadmap and weekly session planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/choose-a-study-plan-you-can-keep): Map what you need to learn, choose practice methods and fit sessions with recall checks into your available time.
- [Study understanding checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-understanding-check): Check your understanding after you study. Share your notes or a chapter and get questions or a worked practice problem, plus feedback on each answer.
- [System design practice reviewer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/system-design-practice-review): Get a system design review that asks first, then ranks risks and gives a revision checklist. Share your design and see where it may break.
- [Technical topic explainer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/technical-topic-explainer): Explain a hard concept with an everyday analogy from a field you know. You get the analogy, where it breaks, and a check question.
- [Technical topic learning coach](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/technical-topic-deep-dive): Practice a technical topic you know a little about. Your AI guides you through how it works, its tradeoffs and failure cases, then you explain it back.
- [Topic learning roadmap planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/topic-learning-roadmap): Build a learning roadmap for any new topic. Share your goal and what you know. Get milestones, readings and checks for each step.
