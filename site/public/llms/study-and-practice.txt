# Study and check your understanding: AI Blocks

> 10 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Build a practice plan from common mistakes](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/build-a-practice-plan-from-common-mistakes): Share the source files. Get an error map, focused practice and a later recall check.
- [Choose a study plan you can keep](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/choose-a-study-plan-you-can-keep): Share the source files. Get a study roadmap, weekly sessions and a recall check.
- [Research literature comparison](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/research-literature-comparison): Share papers on one topic. Get a map of shared findings, conflicting claims and open questions with source references.
- [Research paper evidence review](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/research-paper-evidence-review): Share a paper and its supporting material. Get a claims review with evidence gaps, limits and questions to investigate.
- [Research paper reading guide](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/research-paper-reading-guide): Share a paper. Get its main idea, evidence and sections to read closely, with questions to test your understanding.
- [Review feedback on a student reflection](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/review-feedback-on-a-student-reflection): Share the source files. Get a rubric-linked feedback review and reflection revision prompts.
- [Study plan progress review assistant](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/study-plan-progress-review-assistant): A progress review, adjusted practice plan and the next understanding check.
- [Study understanding check](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/study-understanding-check): Share what you studied. Get questions, worked practice and feedback that shows what to review next.
- [Topic learning roadmap](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/topic-learning-roadmap): Share a topic and what you know. Get a reading path with clear milestones and checks for each step.
- [Turn saved reading into notes you can use](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/turn-saved-reading-into-notes-you-can-use): Share the source files. Get linked notes, a sourced answer and a small recall exercise.
