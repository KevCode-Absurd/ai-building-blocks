# Test changes to your store: AI Blocks

> 12 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Find the store change most worth testing](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/find-the-store-change-most-worth-testing): Give it your store numbers and what shoppers do on key pages. Get a profit baseline and a ranked list of tests.
- [Heatmap and recording reader](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/heatmap-and-recording-reader): Paste your heatmap and recording notes. Get the friction found, a test idea for each, and a friction score.
- [High-impact experiment planner](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/big-swing-test-picker): Give it your store size and page data. Get five bold tests your traffic can prove, in order.
- [Read a finished store test and pick the next one](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/read-a-finished-store-test-and-pick-the-next-one): Give it your test numbers and dates. Get a ship call, ranked ideas and the next bold tests.
- [Run one store test and know whether to ship](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/run-one-store-test-and-know-whether-to-ship-the-change): Give it your orders and traffic. Get a test sized to your store and a clear ship call.
- [Shopify test setup picker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/shopify-test-setup-picker): Give it your Shopify plan and test idea. Get what you can test, the right route, and a setup score.
- [Store test program runner](https://useaiblocks.com/agents/store-test-program-runner): Give it your store numbers and test results. It ranks ideas, plans one test, reads it, and picks the next.
- [Store test readiness checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-test-readiness-checker): Paste your orders, visits and the page to test. Get test, research or fix, plus a readiness score.
- [Store test verdict maker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-test-verdict-maker): Give it test numbers and dates. Get valid or not, the lift as a range, a ship call and a trust score.
- [Test idea ranker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/test-idea-ranker): Paste your research findings. Get each test written as a hypothesis and ranked by evidence and profit.
- [Test idea shortlist maker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/test-idea-shortlist-maker): Paste your test ideas and the evidence for each. Get a ranked shortlist and the one to run first.
- [Test result trust checker](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/test-result-trust-checker): Paste a finished test's numbers. Get whether to trust it, the lift as a range and a ship call.
