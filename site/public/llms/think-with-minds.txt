# Talk it through with great minds: AI Blocks

> 3 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Advisory decision review planner](https://useaiblocks.com/workflows/settle-a-hard-decision-with-a-council-then-commit-the-team): Share the decision, evidence and constraints. Get simulated advisory views, dissent, a checked action plan and a draft team decision note.
- [Council decision brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/advisory-council): Get an advisory council of great minds to argue your hard decision. You get a brief with the call, the strongest dissent and your first three steps.
- [Three-viewpoint decision brief writer](https://useaiblocks.com/prompts/three-mind-debate-brief): Describe a hard decision. Get three simulated viewpoints, counterarguments and a decision brief with first steps.
