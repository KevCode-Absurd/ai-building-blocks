# Write emails that get read: AI Blocks

> 16 free AI skills and workflows for this job. Each page links its SKILL.md.

- [Account-based outreach drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/account-based-outreach-drafter): Name a company or contact. Get an email, call script and LinkedIn message built from your Common Room data. You send them.
- [Cold email pre-send checker](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/cold-email-pre-send-checker): Paste a cold email and what you know of the prospect. Get claims tested, legal basics checked, and a tighter version.
- [Cold email writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/cold-email-writer): Name your buyer and offer. Get cold emails, follow-ups and a sending setup that gets replies.
- [CRM and email strategist](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/crm-and-email-strategist): Ask any email or CRM question. Get expert advice on audits, deliverability, flows, tests and measuring what email earns.
- [Customer email reply drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/customer-email-reply-drafter): Ask what needs a reply. Get unread customer emails sorted by priority, each with a draft reply. You send them.
- [Email backlog clearer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/email-backlog-clearer): Point it at your inbox. Get what needs you, drafted replies in your voice, and deadlines pulled from long threads.
- [Email mistake fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/email-mistake-fixer): Paste an email. Get small errors fixed in your own voice, and tracking bits removed from links if you ask.
- [Email program lead](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/email-program-lead): Say your email goal. Get flows, copy, deliverability fixes and compliance checks, with a hard stop before any real send.
- [Email sequence writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/email-sequence-writer): Say the goal. Get a welcome, nurture or win-back sequence with timing, subject lines and full emails.
- [Inbox delivery fixer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/inbox-delivery-fixer): Name your sending domain. Get SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and a plan to stop emails landing in spam.
- [New inquiry reply drafter](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/new-inquiry-reply-drafter): Give it new inquiries. Get each one qualified and answered with a draft reply and real meeting times. You send it.
- [Outbound email sequence writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/outbound-email-sequence-writer): Describe your prospect. Get a multi-step email sequence with timing and personal touches.
- [Own-voice outreach writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/own-voice-outreach-writer): Give it your prospects. Get emails and follow-ups that sound like you, each tied to something specific about them.
- [Slack message writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/slack-message-writer): Give it your point. Get a short, well-formatted Slack message, thread reply or canvas that leads with the news. Needs Slack.
- [Store email flow writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/store-email-flow-writer): Describe your store. Get ten email flows with full copy, who gets each one, and a year of campaigns.
- [Text message campaign writer](https://useaiblocks.com/skills/text-message-campaign-writer): Say your goal. Get SMS or WhatsApp flows for welcome, cart, win-back and promos, with consent rules.
