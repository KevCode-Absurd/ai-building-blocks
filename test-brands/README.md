# Public test cases

Each test case is built only from **saved public pages**: its site, product or pricing pages, FAQ, public reviews and the public Meta Ad Library. Every saved page records its URL and capture date.

Because the platform serves many kinds of digital business, every launch part is tested on more than one business type:

| Test case | Business type | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `gruns/` | Ecommerce / D2C | Primary: Creative Engine's tests already use it |
| `ag1/` | Ecommerce / D2C | Held-out check |
| `im8/` | Ecommerce / D2C | Extra held-out check |
| one software company | Software and apps | Proposed; chosen by the founder from a shortlist in Week 2 |
| one service or course business | Services / creators | Proposed; chosen by the founder from a shortlist in Week 2 |

Rules:
- The claims register uses verbatim quotes only, each with its source and date.
- No invented economics. Parts that need numbers use a separate `illustrative-economics.json`, labelled as not the brand's real figures.
- Every example carries the label: "Example made from public info about <brand>, captured <date>. Not affiliated."
- No private client data, ever.
