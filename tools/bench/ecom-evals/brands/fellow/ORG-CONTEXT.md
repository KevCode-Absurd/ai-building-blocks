# Fellow: Org context

Built 15 Nov 2026 from the brand's public site and press (see PUBLIC-FACTS.md for sources). Avatars, voice examples and rules marked (test) are test inputs for this eval, not the brand's own guidelines.

## Brand DNA

- Fellow makes premium coffee and tea gear for the home: kettles, grinders, a drip brewer, an espresso machine and drinkware [1].
- Heroes: Stagg EKG kettle (from $179.95), Ode Gen 2 grinder ($399.95), Espresso Series 1 (from $1,499.95) [1].
- It stands for careful, useful design that turns a daily ritual into a small moment of joy [2][4].
- People choose it for café-quality coffee at home without bulky, fussy machines [4].
- Jake Miller founded it in 2013 after a Kickstarter launch. It is based in San Francisco [4][5][7].

## Markets

- Main market: the US, priced in USD. Ships to all 50 states, US territories and military addresses [2][10].
- A country selector offers AUD and EUR in some regions. The footer lists a Thailand store [2][4].
- Own shops in San Francisco (Mission District) and Venice, Los Angeles [4].
- Retail: Williams Sonoma, Crate & Barrel [7], Best Buy [8], Amazon [9].
- Fellow Drops sells a weekly coffee by text in the continental US [3]. Free US shipping from $75 [10].

## Customers and avatars (test)

**Alex, 35 (test).** A coffee hobbyist who dials in pour over at home.
- Situation: owns a scale and tweaks the recipe daily.
- Wants: control and the same great cup every day.
- What stops him: gear that is not precise.
- In his words: "precise to the degree", "my morning ritual".

**Taylor, 29 (test).** A beginner upgrading from a drip machine.
- Situation: wants a nicer kitchen setup.
- Wants: gear that looks good and is simple to use.
- What stops them: the price, and fear that it is too fussy.
- In their words: "looks great on my counter", "I didn't know coffee could taste like this".

## Tone of voice and writing style

- Three adjectives: calm, precise, quietly playful.
- Reading level: grade 6 to 8 for customer copy.
- Sentences: headlines of two to four words with a full stop. Medium-length body sentences [1].
- Humour: light and rare [1].
- Words they favour: thoughtful, precise, clean, simple, considered [1].
- They speak to "you" and stress ease over specs. No hype outside promo banners [1].
- The one quote on file: "Espresso, evolved." [2]

| We say (test) | We never say (test) |
|---|---|
| Brew a better cup at home. | The ULTIMATE coffee machine EVER!!! |
| Built for your morning ritual. | Perfect coffee, guaranteed. |
| Designed in San Francisco. | Made in the USA. |
| Refurbished and ready to brew. | Eco-friendly, sustainable gear. |

Formatting: headlines end in a full stop. Plain CTAs such as Shop now (test). Exact product names, with a plus sign in colourways (Matte Black + Walnut) [1].

## How we speak to customers (test)

- **Ads:** one headline, one benefit, one product.
- **Email and SMS:** emails lead with one product or brew guide. Drops texts are short and say how to reply STOP [3].
- **Product pages:** how it feels to use, then specs and the warranty term for that product [12].
- **Support replies:** warm and step by step. Quote return fees and the gift card option exactly [11].

## Claims we can make / claims we cannot make

**We can say:**
- Designed in San Francisco [4].
- Free US shipping on orders of $75 or more [10].
- 30-day returns for a refund or gift card [11].
- 2-year warranty on named newer electrics, 1 year on the rest, in the US, its territories and Canada [12].
- Rebrew items are refurbished [1].

**We cannot say:**
- Made in the USA. Fellow does not say where products are made [4]. Rule: FTC Made in USA standard.
- Broad eco or sustainable claims, even for Rebrew [1]. Rule: FTC Green Guides.
- That gear is "safe" or UL or ETL certified. No marks are confirmed [12]. Rule: FTC truthful advertising, with CPSC oversight of hazards.
- Series 1 speed or performance numbers without test proof [6]. Rule: FTC claim substantiation.
- A warranty wider than the terms, or the Mulberry plan as Fellow's own [12]. Rule: Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act.
- "Save" against compare-at prices that were not real past prices [1]. Rule: FTC former-price guidance.
- Texts without consent and a STOP opt-out [3]. Rule: TCPA.

## Do's and don'ts (test)

**Do:** keep headlines to four words; name the exact product and colourway; use calm, natural light; link beginners to a brew guide; check prices against the store.

**Don't:** use caps lock or hype; claim where products are made; promise perfect coffee; mix up warranty terms; make green claims without proof.
