## Company

- **Founded:** 2013, by Jake Miller, who is founder and CEO [5][6].
- **Start:** The first product, the Duo Coffee Steeper, was funded on Kickstarter. Fellow later added the Stagg Pour-Over Kettle (2014) and Stagg EKG (2016) [4]. Entrepreneur reports the Kickstarter raised almost $200,000 [7].
- **HQ:** San Francisco, California [5][6]. Fellow says its products are designed in San Francisco [4]. The legal name in the site footer is Fellow Industries, Inc. [4].
- **Funding:** $30 million Series B in June 2022, led by NextWorld Evergreen, with Benchmark's Peter Fenton and angel investors also taking part [5]. No acquisition found.
- **Size signals:** Entrepreneur (Nov 2025) calls Fellow a nine-figure company, with no exact figure [7]. The 2022 release said the company had doubled in size every year since its first funding in 2014 [5].
- **Channels:**
  - Own site, US store in USD, with a country selector that also offers AUD and EUR in some regions [2].
  - Two Fellow Store + Playground shops: Mission District, San Francisco, and Abbot Kinney Blvd, Venice, Los Angeles [4]. The footer also lists a Thailand store [4].
  - Amazon: a Fellow brand store exists on amazon.com, and some listings show Fellow Industries Inc. as seller [9].
  - Retail partners: Williams Sonoma and Crate & Barrel [7]. Best Buy sells Stagg EKG Pro kettles [8]. Crate & Barrel product pages for Stagg EKG kettles exist [8].
  - Fellow Drops: a weekly coffee offer sold by text message (see Policies) [3].

## Policies

- **Shipping:** Free US shipping on orders of $75 or more. Orders ship from California in about 1 to 2 business days. Delivery usually takes up to 10 days after shipping. Ships to all 50 states, US territories and military addresses [10].
- **Returns:** 30 days from the purchase date, in original condition. You get a refund or a Fellow gift card. US return labels cost $15 for electric items and $5 for non-electric items, and the fee is waived if you take a gift card. Coffee, personalised items and Rebrew items are final sale [11].
- **Warranty:** 2 years on newer electrics (for example Stagg EKG Pro, Ode Gen 2, Opus 2, Aiden, Espresso Series 1, Clyde). 1 year on older electrics and on non-electrics. US customers can add a Mulberry extended plan for 3 or 5 years total on select electrics. Coverage applies only to products bought and used in the US, its territories or Canada [12].
- **Loyalty or subscription:** No points scheme seen. Fellow Drops texts members a hand-picked coffee every Tuesday. You reply to buy, and the card on file is charged only when you reply. It is not a subscription. Drops is open to the continental US [3].

## Positioning and customer

Fellow is for people who want café-quality coffee at home without bulky, fussy machines [4]. It targets both new brewers and serious hobbyists. Its product pages talk to everyone from champions to beginners [1]. The brand stands for careful, functional design that turns a daily ritual into a small moment of joy, under its trademarked tagline Everyday Magic [2][4]. Gear sits at the premium end of the home market, and Fellow pairs it with coffee from partner roasters and brew guides in its Academy [2][3].

## Voice

- **Three adjectives:** calm, precise, quietly playful.
- **Quote:** "Espresso, evolved." ([fellowproducts.com](https://fellowproducts.com/)) [2]
- **How they write:** Headlines are very short, often two to four words with a full stop. Body copy uses medium-length sentences and plain words such as thoughtful, precise, clean, simple and considered [1]. They add light humour now and then, for example promising a ridiculously good cup on the Stagg EKG page [1]. Copy speaks to the reader as "you" and stresses ease and confidence over technical specs. They avoid hype words and loud sales language outside promo banners.

## Claims and rules to watch

- **Electrical and heat safety:** Kettles, grinders, brewers and the espresso machine are electrical goods that heat water or steam. Safety claims need care, and the US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) oversees product hazards. Fellow lists safeguards in its warranty terms but no safety certification marks were confirmed [12].
- **Performance claims:** Series 1 claims (a patented Boosted Boiler, stability in under two minutes) are company statements [6]. Ads must have support for them under FTC rules on truthful advertising.
- **Warranty wording:** Terms differ by product (1 or 2 years), apply only in the US, its territories and Canada, and need proof of purchase. The Mulberry plan is a third-party add-on. The Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act and FTC rules apply to how warranties are described [12].
- **Made in:** Fellow says products are designed in San Francisco. It does not say where products are made [4]. Do not claim the products are made in the USA.
- **Sustainability:** Rebrew items are described as refurbished with the planet in mind [1]. Green claims fall under the FTC Green Guides, so avoid broad eco or sustainable claims without proof.
- **Coffee and matcha:** Food items. Origin and roast claims come from partner roasters [3].
- **Text marketing:** Drops and other offers run by SMS, so US text marketing rules (TCPA) and opt-out (reply STOP) matter [3].
- **Prices:** Rebrew and bundle pages show compare-at prices. FTC guidance on former-price claims applies to save messages [1].
