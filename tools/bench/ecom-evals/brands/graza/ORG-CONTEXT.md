# Graza: Org context

Built 15 Nov 2026 from the brand's public site and press (see PUBLIC-FACTS.md for sources). Avatars, voice examples and rules marked (test) are test inputs for this eval, not the brand's own guidelines.

## Brand DNA

- We sell olive oil in squeeze bottles: "Drizzle" for finishing ($21), "Sizzle" for cooking ($16) and "Frizzle" for high heat ($14) [1][2].
- We also sell refill cans, glass bottles, sprays, mayo and chips [2].
- Our big idea: good olive oil should be used every day, not saved for special meals [9].
- We split the job in two: one oil to cook with, one to finish with [9].
- We pitch fresh, single origin oil from Jaén, Spain, at a fair price [3][9].
- People choose us because the squeeze bottle makes pouring easy and fun [9][13].

## Markets

- US only, priced in US dollars [3].
- Our website sells one-off orders and subscriptions, up to 19% off [3][5].
- Retail: Whole Foods nationwide [8][9] and Target [10]. One source also names Costco and Walmart [11].
- More than 13,000 retailers in June 2024, per CNBC [6].

## Tone of voice and writing style

- **Three adjectives:** playful, casual, confident.
- **Reading level:** grade 6 to 8 for customer copy.
- **Sentences:** very short. Puns and rhymes welcome [1].
- **Humour:** warm and quick.
- **Words we favour:** kitchen words like punchy, mellow, crisping and searing. No lab or luxury words [2].
- **Our one line:** "Made for eating, never heating." [1]
- We answer doubts head on [2].

| We say (test) | We never say (test) |

| Squeeze it on everything. | Save it for a special occasion. |
| "Sizzle" is for cooking. Go ahead. | A rare, artisanal elixir. |
| Most of our oil comes from Jaén, Spain. | 100% Spanish, every drop, always. |
| "Frizzle" is our high heat blend. | "Frizzle" extra virgin olive oil. |

**Formatting habits:**
- Product names sit in quote marks: "Drizzle", "Sizzle", "Frizzle" [1].
- Headlines are short and punny. The blog is called the Glog [1].
- CTAs are short and casual, such as "Get squeezing" (test).

## How we speak to customers (test)

- **Ads:** Show the bottle in use in a real pan. One joke or rhyme, one job per oil.
- **Email and SMS:** Recipes and quick tips first. Free shipping is on orders over $47 [5].
- **Product pages:** What the oil is for, taste, origin and exact oil type. Smoke points as approximate figures [8].
- **Support replies:** Warm and fast. No returns on food, but if something goes wrong we put it right [3]. Point to hello@graza.co.

## Claims we can make / claims we cannot make

**We can say:**
- "Drizzle" and "Sizzle" are 100% extra virgin olive oil [2][3].
- Each bottle is single origin and single varietal [3].
- Most of our oil is Picual from Jaén, Spain [3].
- Non-GMO certified [3].
- Packed in facilities certified allergen free and gluten free [3].
- "Fancy" Mayo and "Garlic" Aioli use 100% olive oil [9][17].
- Bottles and cans are recyclable once caps and spouts are removed and cleaned [3]. Keep that condition.

**We cannot say:**
- That "Frizzle" is extra virgin. It blends olive pomace oil with EVOO [3][8]. Rule: FDA food identity rules and USDA olive oil grade standards. Avoid "naturally refined" too [16].
- "Organic". We are not USDA certified organic [3]. Rule: USDA organic rules.
- "100% Spanish" or "always from Jaén". Some oil comes from Portugal or Italy [3][6]. Rule: FTC and FDA bar misleading origin claims.
- That "Original" Mayo is 100% olive oil or 100% EVOO. It uses EVOO plus pomace oil [9]. Check each recipe before any "seed oil free" line. Rule: FDA labelling and FTC.
- "Recyclable" with no condition. Rule: FTC Green Guides.
- Health claims such as "heart healthy". None are in our sources. Rule: FDA health claim rules.

## Do's and don'ts (test)

**Do:**
1. Tell people to use olive oil every day.
2. Give each oil one job.
3. Show the bottle in real kitchens.
4. Keep "Frizzle" apart from EVOO copy.
5. Put product names in quote marks.

**Don't:**
1. Sound snobby.
2. Call any oil organic.
3. Promise one region for every bottle.
4. Quote funding or valuation numbers.
5. Promise free shipping below $47.
