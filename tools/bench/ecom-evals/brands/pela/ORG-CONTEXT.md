# Pela: Org context

Built 15 Nov 2026 from the brand's public site and press (see PUBLIC-FACTS.md for sources). Avatars, voice examples and rules marked (test) are test inputs for this eval, not the brand's own guidelines.

## Brand DNA

- Pela sells plant based phone cases with prints by named artists ($75 for printed iPhone 17 models), plus accessories [2][3].
- It stands for less plastic waste, and admits it is not perfect [2][7].
- People choose it for a nice looking case with less plastic waste [2].
- Founded by Jeremy Lang in 2011, based in Kelowna, BC, and a Certified B Corporation [2][6][7][18].

## Markets

- US store pelacase.com (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP), Canada store pelacase.ca (CAD), plus UK and EU stores [2][3][13].
- Free shipping over $75 in Canada and $85 USD in the US. US orders ship duty free from Arizona or BC [2][3][5].
- Best Buy Canada lists Pela cases [11].

## Customers and avatars (test)

**Hannah, 26 (test).** Eco-minded, buys gifts and cares about style.
- Situation: shops for herself and friends.
- Wants: a pretty case she feels good about.
- What stops her: dull designs, or a green story that feels fake.
- In her words: "love that it won't sit in a landfill", "the prints are so cute".

**Ben, 33 (test).** Protection first and skeptical of eco claims.
- Situation: has cracked screens before.
- Wants: proof the case protects.
- What stops him: fear that a plant based case is flimsy.
- In his words: "does it actually protect?", "survived three drops on concrete".

## Tone of voice and writing style

- Three adjectives: candid, playful, warm.
- Reading level: grade 6 to 8 for customer copy.
- Sentences: short, punchy lines in plain words [2].
- Humour: light self mockery [2].
- Words they favour: plants, artists, humans, Canada [2].
- Sale copy is loud. Standards talk stays in the FAQ [5].
- The one quote on file: "Honestly, we're just trying to be less evil." [2]

| We say (test) | We never say (test) |
|---|---|
| Made from plants. Designed by artists. | The healthiest phone case on the planet. |
| Base material composts in industrial facilities. | 100% compostable. Toss it anywhere. |
| Most cases are made in our own BC and Arizona plants. | Everything is Made in Canada. |
| Clear cases use recycled plastic. | All our cases are compostable. |

Formatting: short headlines, no jargon, poetic colour names such as London Fog [2], big simple numbers in deals [2][3].

## How we speak to customers (test)

- **Ads:** lead with the print, then one honest, qualified eco line.
- **Email and SMS:** friendly and self aware. Name the artist in series launches [19][20][21].
- **Product pages:** print and fit first, then qualified material facts [5].
- **Support replies:** warm and honest. Name the returns rule and handling fee [5][9].

## Claims we can make / claims we cannot make

**We can say:**
- Certified B Corporation [2][18].
- Over 80% of cases are made in our own plants in Kelowna, BC and Mesa, Arizona [5].
- The base material meets ASTM D6400 and EN 13432 for industrial composting (keep the qualifier) [5].
- Clear cases are not compostable [4][5].
- Pela 360 takes back old cases to compost, upcycle or recycle [5].
- $500 screen protection guarantee with a Pela case plus Pela screen protector [2].

**We cannot say:**
- "100% compostable" with no qualifier [2]. Rule: FTC Green Guides treat it as home compostable [14].
- "Compostable" or "home compostable" on US labels without a named certificate [5]. Rule: California AB 1201, stricter from 1 January 2026 [15].
- Any green claim in Canada without adequate and proper proof. Rule: Competition Act (Bill C-59) [16].
- "Prevented 100,049,717 plastic bags" without the method on file [2]. Rule: Competition Act and FTC Green Guides.
- "Healthiest phone case on the planet" [17]. Rule: superlatives need proof under both laws.
- Made in Canada for the whole range; some items come from the US and China [5]. Rule: Competition Act and FTC rules.
- Non-toxic, BPA free, plastic free packaging or child safety claims without test data and named standards [2][5]. Rule: FTC substantiation and the Competition Act.
- Climate Neutral (not on the site today).

## Do's and don'ts (test)

**Do:** name the artist behind each print; qualify every compost claim; show drop proof for skeptics; keep the candid voice; check claims per market.

**Don't:** say "100% compostable" alone; call clear cases compostable; use the plastic bag number; claim everything is Made in Canada.
