# Pela: public facts

Checked: 15 Nov 2026. Every fact below has a source. Anything not confirmed is listed under Not verified.

Pela still trades. One thing has changed: the domain pela.earth now sends visitors to lomi.com with a permanent (301) redirect [1]. Lomi is Pela's sister brand, a kitchen food recycler. The phone case shop lives at pelacase.com (US, with USD, CAD, EUR and GBP options) and pelacase.ca (Canada) [2][3]. There are also UK and EU stores [13]. Pela says it and Lomi are part of the same family [2].

## Company

- **Founded:** 2011, per a 2019 press release [6]. Pela's own story starts with founder Jeremy Lang on a beach in 2010, then about a year of work on the material [7].
- **Founders:** Jeremy Lang, an environmental consultant from Saskatchewan [6][7]. Matt Bertulli calls himself co-founder and CEO of Pela Case and Lomi [8].
- **HQ:** Kelowna, British Columbia [6]. Pela's older About page says the company started in Saskatoon [7].
- **Legal name on the site:** Open Mind Developments Corporation (footer copyright) [2][9].
- **Making things:** Over 80% of cases are made in Pela's own plants in Kelowna, BC and Mesa, Arizona. Clear cases and some accessories are made by partners in China [5].
- **Funding:** A $5M round in October 2019, led by Marcy Venture Partners (Jay-Z's fund) with Kensington Capital [6]. In 2022 the Canadian agency PacifiCan announced over $3.6M for Pela [10].
- **Size signals:** The site says over 2 million people love its products [2]. No revenue figure from a reputable source was found.
- **Channels:** Own stores in the US, Canada, UK and EU [2][3][13]. Best Buy Canada lists Pela cases [11]. A Pela blog post says it was in 63 Sunrise Records stores in Canada [12]. Another Pela post says most US, Australia and UK/EU orders ship from Amazon warehouses [13]. Both blog posts are undated.

## Policies

- **Shipping:** Free shipping over $75 on the Canada store [3]. Free standard US shipping over $85 USD [2][9]. US orders ship from Arizona or British Columbia, with no duties. Standard US delivery takes about 6 to 10 business days after 2 to 7 days of processing [5].
- **Returns:** The refund policy page gives 30 days from purchase and a happiness guarantee where the reason for return does not matter [9]. The FAQ gives more detail: a free replacement if a case is damaged within one year, a refund within 90 days, 90 days for accessories, and 30 days for gifts. A return handling fee applies. Custom case fees are not refundable [5].
- **Guarantee:** $500 screen protection guarantee when a Pela case and Pela screen protector are used together [2].
- **Take back:** Pela 360 takes back old Pela cases and other plastic cases to compost, upcycle or recycle [5].
- **Subscriptions and loyalty:** None found on the site [2][5].

## Positioning and customer

Pela sells to people who want a nice looking phone case without adding to plastic waste. The pitch mixes three things: plant based materials, art from named artists, and a company that admits it is not perfect but is trying. The buyer is likely an eco minded shopper who also cares about style and gifting. Pela leans on its Canadian making story and its Lomi sister brand to show a bigger waste mission [2][7].

## Voice

- **Three adjectives:** candid, playful, warm.
- **One quote:** "Honestly, we're just trying to be less evil." ([pelacase.com](https://pelacase.com/)) [2]
- **How they write:** Short, punchy lines. Plain words. Some light self mockery, such as a newsletter line saying emails suck but theirs do not [2]. They like the words plants, artists, humans and Canada. Product names use poetic colour names like London Fog, Seashell and Forest Floor [2]. Sale copy is direct and loud. They avoid technical jargon in headlines and save standards talk for the FAQ [5].

## Claims and rules to watch

- **100% compostable claim:** The store's page title calls the cases 100% compostable [2]. The FAQ says the base material meets ASTM D6400 and EN 13432 for industrial composting. It also says you can put the case in a backyard compost bin, but gives no time frame [5]. No third party certificate number (for example BPI or TÜV OK compost) is named [5].
- **US rules:** The FTC Green Guides say an unqualified "compostable" claim means it will break down safely and in a timely way in a home compost pile. If it only composts in industrial plants, the claim needs a qualifier [14]. California's AB 1201 ties "compostable" and "home compostable" labels to certification, and it adds stricter rules from 1 January 2026 [15].
- **Canada rules:** Bill C-59 got royal assent on 20 June 2024. It changed the Competition Act so green claims need adequate and proper proof. Private parties can also bring cases since June 2025. The Competition Bureau issued final guidance on 5 June 2025 [16].
- **Other claims to check:** non-toxic, zero forever chemicals and BPA free [2]; a superlative calling it the healthiest phone case on the planet [17]; prevented the equivalent of 100,049,717 plastic bags [2]; Made in Canada, while some products are made in the US and China [2][5]; plastic free packaging [5]; clear cases from recycled plastic [2].
- **Certifications stated:** Certified B Corporation (logo on site; the B Lab directory lists the company as Pela and Lomi) [2][18]. ASTM D6400 and EN 13432 for the base material [5]. Child safety standards in several countries, with no standard named [5].
- **Regulators:** US Federal Trade Commission; California CalRecycle; Competition Bureau Canada; EU and UK consumer bodies for the EU and UK stores.
