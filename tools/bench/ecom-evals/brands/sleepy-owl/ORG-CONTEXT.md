# Sleepy Owl Coffee: Org context

Built 15 Nov 2026 from the brand's public site and press (see PUBLIC-FACTS.md for sources). Avatars, voice examples and rules marked (test) are test inputs for this eval, not the brand's own guidelines.

## Brand DNA

- A homegrown Indian brand selling cold brew packs, hot brew bags, instant, ground and protein coffee, and matcha premix. Cold Brew Packs start at ₹699 for 5 [1][2].
- It stands for good coffee at home without machines or barista skills [10][11].
- People choose it for easy formats without café prices [10][11].
- Founded in 2016 in New Delhi by Ajai Thandi, Arman Sood and Ashwajeet Singh [9][10][11]. FY26 revenue was ₹82 crore, up 86% [8].

## Markets

- India only, priced in rupees (₹) on sleepyowl.co [1].
- Quick delivery via Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, Zepto and Dunzo in select cities, plus BigBasket and bulk orders [1][15].
- Delivery: 2 to 3 days in Delhi NCR, 5 to 7 elsewhere, none on weekends [3].
- Amazon, Flipkart and 15,000+ stores: branded content only [10].

## Tone of voice and writing style

- Three adjectives: cheeky, casual, confident.
- Reading level: grade 6 to 8 for customer copy.
- Sentences: taglines under six words; longer copy friendly and plain [1][2][11].
- Humour: slang, stretched spelling, emoji, mock warnings [1][2].
- Words they favour: smooth, chocolatey, easy, 100% Arabica, Kaapi [1][2][11].
- The one quote on file: "Brew overnight & Smooooth AF." [1]

## How we speak to customers (test)

- **Ads:** one format, one cheeky line. Sale price and MRP as on site.
- **Email and SMS:** short and playful. Promo SMS only to opted-in numbers, under TRAI rules.
- **WhatsApp:** opt-in only. Two or three lines, one link. For orders and restocks.
- **Product pages:** brew steps first, then caffeine, pack size and cups per pack [3][5].
- **Support replies:** quick and kind. Explain the 48-hour and 5-day windows and store credit refunds [4].

## Claims we can make / claims we cannot make

**We can say (matching pack labels):**
- About 425 mg caffeine per Cold Brew pack, 105 mg per Hot Brew bag, 72 mg per cup of instant [3][5].
- Each Cold Brew pack makes about 450 ml of concentrate [3].
- 15 g protein per Protein Coffee sachet [2].
- A 100 g instant jar makes 50 cups [2].

**We cannot say:**
- "India's best coffee brand" [1]. Rule: CCPA misleading ad rules and ASCI.
- "Grade A beans sourced directly from farms" without proof [1][11]. Rule: FSSAI Advertising and Claims Regulations, 2018 [20].
- New uses of natural, fresh, pure, original, authentic or real [1][2]. Rule: the same FSSAI rules [20].
- No added sugar, under 97 calories or no preservatives without label proof [2][5]. Rule: FSSAI claims rules [20].
- "100% recyclable, absolutely no plastic" for Sustain Packs without proof [16]. Rule: CCPA greenwashing guidelines and ASCI [21].
- Ready to drink coffee at 145 to 300 mg per litre without the high caffeine warning. Rule: FSSAI caffeinated beverage standard [17].
- Instant Filter Coffee without coffee and chicory percentages [2]. Rule: FSSAI coffee and chicory labelling [18][19].
- ₹100 crore revenue, profit or 15,000+ stores (branded content; conflicts with Entrackr) [8][10].

## Do's and don'ts (test)

**Do:** keep it short; show how easy the brew is; match numbers to the label; name delivery apps by city; keep humour kind.

**Don't:** claim to be India's best; make health or fitness promises; use restricted FSSAI words in new copy; say packs have no plastic without proof; use coffee snob jargon.
