# Sleepy Owl Coffee: public facts

Checked: 15 Nov 2026. Every fact below has a source. Anything not confirmed is listed under Not verified.

## Company

- **Founded:** 2016 [9][10].
- **Founders:** Ajai Thandi, Arman Sood and Ashwajeet Singh, three friends [10][11].
- **HQ:** New Delhi [9][10].
- **Funding:** A $500K seed round in April 2018 [12]. A $6.5M Series A in November 2021 [12], from Rukam Capital and DSG Consumer Partners [13]. About ₹12 crore raised in April 2026, which brings total investment to nearly $10M [9][14]. The Gauri Khan Family Trust put money in again in that round [14].
- **Revenue:** ₹82 crore in FY26, up 86% from ₹44 crore in FY25. Net loss ₹1.76 crore in FY26, and the company is near breakeven [8].
- **Channels:** Own site with direct to consumer orders [1]. The site links to Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, Zepto and Dunzo for quick delivery in select cities [1]. BigBasket lists its products [15]. An Inc42 BrandLabs feature (branded content) says it is in more than 15,000 retail stores, on Amazon and Flipkart, and does B2B bulk orders [10]. The site also has a Bulk Orders page [1].

## Policies

- **Shipping:** Orders usually leave within 24 hours. Delhi NCR takes 2 to 3 days. The rest of India takes 5 to 7 days. They do not ship on Saturday or Sunday [3]. The shipping policy page is blank, and no free shipping threshold is shown [6].
- **Cash on delivery:** Not stated on any page checked [1][3][6].
- **Returns:** Damaged or broken bottles must be reported within 48 hours, with photos. Quality issues, or not liking the taste, must be reported within 5 days. Refunds are store credit only, and only on the first box if you bought more than one. There are no exchanges on ready to drink, cold brew, hot brew or ground coffee bags. Damaged merch gets a replacement or store credit [4].
- **Subscriptions:** The menu has a Subscribe link, but that page returns a 404 not found error [7]. The cart code includes a subscription widget [1]. No live subscription offer was confirmed.
- **Loyalty:** None found. The cart suggests a 10% off coupon code [1].

## Positioning and customer

Sleepy Owl is a homegrown Indian coffee brand for people who want good coffee at home without machines or barista skills. It focuses on easy formats: overnight cold brew packs, dip bags, instant jars and sachets. The buyer is likely a young, urban coffee drinker who is moving up from basic instant coffee but does not want café prices [10][11]. It now pushes into protein coffee and matcha for health minded buyers [1][9].

## Voice

- **Three adjectives:** cheeky, casual, confident.
- **One quote:** "Brew overnight & Smooooth AF." ([sleepyowl.co](https://sleepyowl.co/)) [1]
- **How they write:** Very short taglines, often under six words. They use internet slang, stretched spelling and emoji in menus. They joke with mock warnings about strong coffee. They use the Indian word Kaapi for filter coffee. They favour real good coffee, smooth, chocolatey, easy and 100% Arabica. Longer copy is friendly and plain, with no coffee snob jargon [1][2][11].

## Claims and rules to watch

- **Caffeine:** The FAQ gives about 425 mg caffeine per Cold Brew pack (which makes about 3 cups), about 105 mg per Hot Brew bag, and about 72 mg per cup of instant coffee [3][5]. Copy must match the pack labels. Ready to drink coffee may need care with FSSAI's caffeinated beverage standard. That standard covers drinks with 145 to 300 mg caffeine per litre. It requires a high caffeine statement and a warning that the drink is not for children, pregnant or nursing women, or people sensitive to caffeine [17].
- **Coffee and chicory:** The Instant Filter Coffee is a blend of robusta and chicory [2]. FSSAI requires the coffee and chicory percentages on the label. An August 2025 amendment added a front of pack box. One report says the deadline moved to July 2027 [18][19].
- **Restricted words:** FSSAI's Advertising and Claims Regulations, 2018 limit words like natural, fresh, pure, original, authentic and real [20]. Sleepy Owl uses Original as a flavour name, and also uses freshly roasted, authentic South Indian Kaapi and real good coffee in its copy [1][2][11].
- **Health and nutrition claims:** Protein Coffee claims 15 g protein, no added sugar and under 97 calories per sachet [2]. A no preservatives claim is used on instant coffee [5]. These need label proof under FSSAI rules [20].
- **Superlatives and sourcing:** The site calls itself India's best coffee brand. It also says it uses Grade A beans sourced directly from farms. All three claims need proof [1][11].
- **Green claims:** Sustain Packs are called 100% recyclable with absolutely no plastic [16]. India's CCPA greenwashing guidelines were notified on 15 October 2024. ASCI green claims rules took effect on 15 February 2024 [21].
- **Certifications stated:** None found on the site [1]. A compliance page links to a PDF that could not be read [22].
- **Regulators:** FSSAI (food safety and labels), CCPA under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 (misleading ads and green claims), ASCI (ad self regulation).
