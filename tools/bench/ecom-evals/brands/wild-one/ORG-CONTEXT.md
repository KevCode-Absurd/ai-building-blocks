# Wild One: Org context

Built 15 Nov 2026 from the brand's public site and press (see PUBLIC-FACTS.md for sources). Avatars, voice examples and rules marked (test) are test inputs for this eval, not the brand's own guidelines.

## Brand DNA

- Wild One makes walk, travel, play and home gear for dogs, and since mid 2026 for cats [1][17].
- Heroes: Cushioned Dog Harness ($48), Hands-Free Rope Dog Leash ($48), Walk Kits ($64 to $105) [1].
- It puts function first and keeps designs simple but bold [8].
- People choose it for gear that works hard, looks good and matches [8].
- Founded in 2018 in New York by Minali Chatani, Bill Wells and Veronica Becchetti [6][7]. It supports 12 US rescue partners [9].

## Markets

- Main market: the US, priced in USD. Free standard US shipping from $90 [1][3].
- Ships worldwide by UPS. Import fees may apply [3][5]. EU buyers get 14 days to withdraw [4].
- Retail: Target since January 2021 [10], PetSmart [11], a store locator and a wholesale site [5].
- Amazon listings exist, but the seller is unclear [12].

## Tone of voice and writing style

- Three adjectives: playful, confident, design-led.
- Reading level: grade 6 to 8 for customer copy.
- Sentences: very short lines and headlines, often three to five words [2][8].
- Humour: light jokes and puns, then practical specs on product pages [1][8].
- Words they favour: Walk Kits, slang like IRL, colour names [1][2].
- The one quote on file: "Boring isn't in our DNA." [8]

## How we speak to customers (test)

- **Ads:** one colour field, one pet in the gear, five words or fewer.
- **Email and SMS:** lead with a new colour or matching set. SMS under two lines, with opt-out.
- **Product pages:** a fun first line, then fit points, sizing, care and specs [1][8].
- **Support replies:** friendly and direct. Explain the unused-item rule and chewing exclusion kindly [4][5].

## Claims we can make / claims we cannot make

**We can say:**
- 1-year warranty against manufacturer defects [5].
- 30-day returns on unused items [4].
- The Cushioned Dog Harness has three leash points [1].
- Made in Cambodia, China and the Philippines. Treats made in Canada [5].
- Poop bags are made with a corn starch blend [1].

**We cannot say:**
- That gear prevents injury, stops pulling or makes night walks safe, or pull strength beyond test results [1][8]. Rule: FTC truth in advertising and claim substantiation.
- "Compostable" or "biodegradable" poop bags [1]. Rule: FTC Green Guides, plus California's strict laws on those words.
- "Organic" treats unless certified [1]. Rule: USDA National Organic Program, with FDA and state feed rules for pet food.
- Made in USA [5]. Rule: FTC Made in USA standard.
- Airline-approved without the check-your-airline caveat [15].
- That the Breakaway Cat Collar works with a leash. It must not [1].
- Rescue counts or old pledges such as A Dog a Day [7].
- Savings against compare-at prices that were not real past prices [1]. Rule: FTC former-price guidance.

## Do's and don'ts (test)

**Do:** lead with style, then prove function; show real pets in the gear; link the size guide in harness messages; sell matching sets; keep the airline caveat.

**Don't:** make gear look like hiking kit; promise safety or zero pulling; call poop bags compostable; pair the breakaway collar with a leash; repeat old rescue numbers.
