## Policies

- **Shipping:** Free standard US shipping on orders of $90 or more. Ground takes 5 to 9 business days, expedited 2 to 4. Ships to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and internationally by UPS. International orders do not get free shipping and may owe import fees [3][5].
- **Shipping Protection:** An optional paid add-on for US orders. It covers lost, stolen or damaged parcels and includes a prepaid return label [5].
- **Returns:** 30 days from delivery for unused items. Trying on is fine, but items used outdoors, soiled or chewed are not accepted. You pay return shipping unless you bought Shipping Protection. Orders of $400 or more and limited editions are final sale. EU buyers get a 14-day right of withdrawal [4][5].
- **Warranty:** 1 year against manufacturer defects. It does not cover normal wear, dirt or damage done by the dog [5].
- **Loyalty or subscription:** No loyalty scheme seen on the site [2]. An ambassador page exists [2]. A 2019 report mentioned a treat subscription at 10% off, which was not seen on the site now [6].

## Positioning and customer

Wild One is for style-minded dog owners, and now cat owners, who want gear that works hard and looks good [8]. The brand puts function first and says it keeps designs simple but bold [8]. It sells matching sets in a wide palette, so owners can coordinate collar, leash, harness and bags. It also supports animal rescue through product and cash donations, adoption drives and volunteering [9].

## Voice

- **Three adjectives:** playful, confident, design-led.
- **Quote:** "Boring isn't in our DNA." ([wildone.com/pages/about](https://wildone.com/pages/about)) [8]
- **How they write:** Very short lines and headlines, often three to five words, with lots of exclamation marks in calls to action [2][8]. Light humour and puns, for example the poop bag page jokes about taking care of business [1]. They use internet slang such as IRL and call their bundles Walk Kits [2]. Product copy then turns practical, with specs like pull strength, fit points and care [1][8]. They talk to the owner directly as "you".

## Claims and rules to watch

- **Pet safety:** The Breakaway Cat Collar uses a buckle that opens under pressure and must not be used with a leash [1]. Harness claims such as anti-pull control (front clip) and leash pull strength (about 80 to 90 lbs) need care [1][8]. Avoid any claim that gear prevents injury or stops pulling.
- **Airline claims:** The Travel Carrier is sold as airline-approved, but the brand's own FAQ says to check each airline's rules first [15]. Copy should keep that caveat.
- **Reflective items:** Limeade Reflective items aid visibility. Do not claim they make night walks safe.
- **Green claims:** Poop bags are made with a corn starch blend [1]. Do not call them compostable or biodegradable without proof. The FTC Green Guides apply, and California has strict laws on those words.
- **Treats:** Organic Baked Treats are pet food. Organic claims fall under the USDA National Organic Program, and pet food labels under FDA and state feed rules [1].
- **Made in:** Products are made in Cambodia, China and the Philippines. Treats are made in Canada [5]. Do not claim made in USA.
- **Warranty and returns:** 1-year defect warranty that excludes chewing. Final sale over $400 [4][5].
- **Sale prices:** The site shows compare-at prices during sales. FTC rules on former-price claims apply [1].
- **Give-back numbers:** Older pledges (A Dog a Day, from 2022) were reported by Fortune in 2021 [7]. Do not repeat rescue counts unless the brand confirms them now.
