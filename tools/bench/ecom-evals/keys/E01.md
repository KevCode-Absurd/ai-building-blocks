# E01 key: Wild One Org context (tune, 3 signals)

## Signals
1. **Cat buyers are now a core customer.** Survey "for my cat" rose from 1% (May) to 31% (Oct). Cat reviews are 9 of 27 and all 4 to 5 stars. A great answer adds a cat owner avatar (first walk outside, safety, "she didn't wiggle out") and treats cats as part of the brand, not a side note.
2. **The voice that works is short and playful.** Emails 1 and 3 (short lines, one photo) got 3.9% and 4.6% clicks; email 2 ("unleash the ultimate", "best-in-class", long paragraphs) got 1.1% and 0.41% unsubscribes. A great answer makes that a written rule with the banned words.
3. **Customers repeat claims the brand cannot make.** Reviews say "airline approved" and "compostable" bags. The public facts say check each airline, and the bags are a corn starch blend. A great answer puts both on the cannot-say list with the reason.

## Decoys
- Chew complaints: 5 of 27 reviews, 1 to 2 stars. Warranty excludes chew damage. Right call: a support and warranty clarity note, not "chew proof" or "durable" as brand DNA.

## Right calls
- Real palette and fonts from PUBLIC-FACTS (black, white, limeade #DFF464, lavender #E7E5FF, periwinkle #CAC5FF, orange #FC4C02; Modern Era).
- Gift buyers (about 5 to 6%) can be noted but are not a main avatar.

## Wrong
- Inventing hex codes, fonts, certifications, rescue numbers or prices.
- "No-pull", "chew proof", "airline approved", "compostable" in the can-say list.

## Facts to check
- Cat share 1% to 31%. Email clicks 3.9%, 1.1%, 4.6%. Chew reviews 5 of 27.
