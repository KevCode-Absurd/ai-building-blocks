# E02 key: KraveBeauty Org context (held-out, 3 signals)

## Signals
1. **The buyer wants a simpler routine.** Survey: 44% simpler routine, 29% irritated or sensitive skin, 9% anti-aging. A great answer builds the avatars and DNA around fewer steps and calm skin.
2. **Real customers use drug-claim words.** "Healed my eczema", "repaired my damaged skin barrier, cured my rosacea". A great answer lists heal, cure, eczema, rosacea and repair-as-treatment as claims the brand cannot make (FDA drug claims), and keeps sunscreen claims inside the Drug Facts label.
3. **Urgency hurts this brand.** The July "SHOP NOW BEFORE IT'S GONE!!!" email got 1.6% clicks, 0.52% unsubscribes and 0.21% spam complaints; the calm emails got 3.4% and 2.9% with about 0.1% unsubscribes. A great answer writes an anti-urgency rule.

## Decoys
- Five incentivised giveaway reviews (5 to 7 Jul), all "anti-aging miracle". Right call: exclude or discount them; do not adopt the anti-aging angle.

## Right calls
- Palette from PUBLIC-FACTS (signature yellow-lime #EAF586, black, white, dark green #003333 and the rest); fonts ABC Diatype and Value Serif.
- Gaps named (HQ city, US retail).

## Wrong
- Positioning on anti-aging. Repeating "heals" or "cures". Fake certifications or study numbers.

## Facts to check
- 44% / 29% / 9%. July email 1.6% click, 0.52% unsubscribe, 0.21% spam.
