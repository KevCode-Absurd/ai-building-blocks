# E04 key: Taylor Stitch Meta audit (tune, 5 signals)

## Signals
1. **Women 65+ buy through single-image ads (the 9 to 10 insight).** In prospecting (ASC and Shirts and chinos), women 65+ on single image: $4,107 spend, 96 purchases, about $43 per purchase, AOV about $200. Prospecting overall: about $82. The same women on video: about $156 ($1,869, 12 purchases). A great answer finds this only in the breakdown, reads it as gift buying for a partner or son, and proposes a single-image gift-angle test (copy like "he'll wear it every weekend", free returns and easy exchanges up front), with a budget and a read date. Bonus: links it to the "Gift for him" static ad ($55 over 90 days).
2. **The best ad was paused.** "Year six Jack" video: $31,000 for 517 purchases over 90 days, about $60 each. Paused 24 Oct; ASC cost per purchase went 63, 66, 68, then 79, 86, 92. A great answer relaunches it (and new cuts of the hook) as the first, cheapest move.
3. **Retargeting is mostly existing customers.** Frequency 11.8; 78% of its Shopify orders are returning customers. Meta ROAS about 5.0 overstates new sales. Right call: cut or cap budget, exclude recent buyers, lower frequency.
4. **Meta overcounts.** Meta 2,920 purchases vs 1,882 Meta-tagged Shopify orders (about 64%). Real account CPA about $106 vs about $68 in Meta. Contribution per order is about $104 ($188 x 62% minus $9.50 shipping minus a little return cost), so the account is near break-even on first orders.
5. **Jackets ROAS hides returns.** Long Haul Jacket returns 34% (58% of those for boxy fit). Right call: hold Jackets, fix size guidance, then scale.

## Decoys
- Meme ad: 4.1% CTR, 6 purchases on $2,400 ($400 each). Do not scale.
- CPM up from $13.1 to $16.9: the normal Q4 rise, not a creative failure.

## Right calls
- A budget split that sums to about $200,000 for 30 days.
- Daily rules with numbers.

## Wrong
- Scaling retargeting or the meme ad. Taking Meta's count at face value. Cutting women or older buyers on the account average. Generic "test more creative".
