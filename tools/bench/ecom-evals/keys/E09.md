# E09 key: Pela Amazon listing (tune, 3 signals)

## Signals
1. **Search intent is eco plus model.** "compostable phone case iphone 17" converts at 13.9% and "eco friendly iphone 17 case" at 7.6%, vs 2.0% for "iphone 17 case". Right call: the title leads with brand, "Compostable Case for iPhone 17", then key features; drop gendered and filler words.
2. **MagSafe confusion.** 46 questions, 31 low reviews. The case has no built-in magnets; the MagSafe module adds them. Right call: say it plainly in bullet 1 or 2 and in an image; suggest a bundle or variation with the module.
3. **The main image hides the product in use.** Image 1 is the back of the case without a phone. Right call: case on the phone, plus a drop-test image (internal 6 ft test, no certificate claim) and a MagSafe answer image.

## Claims
- Remove "100% compostable", "biodegradable", "breaks down in your backyard". Say it meets industrial composting standards (ASTM D6400, EN 13432) and point to Pela 360. 9 reviews already report it did not break down at home.

## Decoys
- Price ($59.99 vs $21 average): not the fix; eco-intent buyers convert.

## Wrong
- Keeping the green claims. Cutting price. Inventing drop heights or certificates.
