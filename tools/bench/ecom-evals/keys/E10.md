# E10 key: Fellow Ode page CRO audit (tune, 3 signals)

## Signals
1. **The default variant is the $599.95 SSP version.** Landing on SSP: 3.1% add to cart; on core $399.95: 6.9%. Right call: make core the default and show SSP as an upgrade. Ship, then confirm with a test or a before and after read.
2. **Buyers cannot tell Ode from Opus.** 27% of exit answers ask about espresso or Opus; 18% of mobile sessions tap the static "Ode vs Opus" image; the FAQ never mentions espresso. Right call: a tappable, plain comparison (Ode is for brew; for espresso choose Opus) high on the page, and an FAQ answer.
3. **Mobile speed.** 9.2 MB autoplay hero video; mobile LCP 4.8 s vs 1.9 s desktop; mobile conversion 0.90% vs 2.60%. Right call: replace the hero video with a poster image or a light video that loads after.

## Decoys
- Blog bounce 81 to 86%: not this page's problem.
- "Waiting for Black Friday" (14%): not a reason to discount the page.

## Right calls
- Free shipping and warranty text moved up and made readable (now small grey text).
- A ranked list with effect estimates; quick ships vs tests separated.

## Wrong
- Leading with a discount, a full redesign, or generic "add trust badges".
