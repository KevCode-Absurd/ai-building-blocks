# E11 key: Pela Black Friday landing page (held-out, 2 signals)

## Signals
1. **Gift traffic, on phones.** 48% of sessions come from gift-angle ads, more than 90% on mobile. Right call: the hero leads with a "one for you, one for someone you love" style BOGO, mobile-first layout, short sections, sticky add to cart.
2. **Returns come from MagSafe and wrong models.** 41% MagSafe, 22% wrong model. Right call: a model picker near the top for both cases, a plain MagSafe line (no built-in magnets; add the module), maybe a module upsell.

## Claims
- Compostable claims qualified: industrial composting standards; no home-compost promise or time frame. Drop test stated as an internal 6 ft test.

## Right calls
- Proof from inputs only: "survived drops on concrete", "bought one for my sister too".
- Brand colours (#4C4C4C charcoal, #FEEFB8 butter, #432F2E buttons) and fonts (Lora headings, Poppins body).
- Offer math is safe: landed cost $9.40 per case, so a free second case is affordable.

## Wrong
- Invented star ratings, review counts or "100% compostable".
- A desktop-first long page with the offer below the fold.
