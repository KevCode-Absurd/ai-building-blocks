# E12 key: Sleepy Owl homepage (tune, 2 signals)

## Signals
1. **The best seller is buried.** Cold Brew Packs are 61% of revenue, the 5th bestseller card, reached by 29% of mobile sessions. The 4-slide carousel gets 4.1%, 1.2%, 2.0% and 0.6% clicks. Right call: a static hero for Cold Brew Packs with add to cart in the first screen; drop the carousel.
2. **Delivery time drives conversion.** Delhi NCR (2 to 3 days) converts at 4.0% vs 1.7% elsewhere (5 to 7 days); 1,800 searches for "delivery time". Right call: a pincode delivery estimate near the top and on product cards.

## Also expected
- Quick commerce buttons cause 34% of exits: move them lower and frame them (for example, "need it in 10 minutes?") rather than remove them.

## Decoys
- Matcha searches (1,500): not a homepage lead.

## Wrong
- Leading with the brand story or matcha. Hiding quick commerce completely without reasoning.
