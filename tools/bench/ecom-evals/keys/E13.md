# E13 key: KraveBeauty Klaviyo audit (tune, 4 signals)

## Signals
1. **Gmail spam over the limit.** The two "All subscribers (includes 180 days unengaged)" sends hit 0.38% and 0.41% Gmail spam; engaged sends are about 0.05%. Google's bulk sender limit is 0.3%. Right call: stop mailing the unengaged, add a sunset flow and re-permission, before the holiday sends.
2. **Replenishment beats discounts (segment by offer).** Refill buyers at day 40 to 50: "running low" with no discount converted at 19% ($13,260) vs 6% for 20% off ($3,020). Right call: a replenishment flow timed by size (80 mL at about day 35, 200 mL at about day 90), no code.
3. **The welcome flow is one email.** 38% of sign-ups come from the pop-up. Right call: a 3 to 5 email welcome with education and the routine.
4. **The abandoned checkout code sits in email 1.** 61% of flow orders come before email 2; 402 of 980 orders used the code ($1,870 given). Right call: no code in email 1; test a code in email 3 only.

## Decoys
- Opens of 56 to 61% are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection.

## Wrong
- Celebrating opens. A 20% offer for refill buyers. Sending more to all subscribers.
