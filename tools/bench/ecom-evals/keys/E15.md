# E15 key: Ritual retention flows (held-out, 3 signals)

## Signals
1. **The cliff is renewal 1.** 31% cancel at the first full-price charge, peaking when the reminder with the new price lands; later renewals are 6 to 11%. Right call: rewrite the pre-renewal email (why it is worth it, what changes, a skip or delay link), maybe a softer step up in price.
2. **Save offers differ by product (segment by offer).** Perimenopause: skip a month saves 44% vs 12% with 20% off. Women's 18+: 20% off saves 23% vs 14% for skip. Right call: product-specific save offers.
3. **The win-back discount barely works.** 4.6% vs 3.4% holdout: about 1.2 points, about 324 extra reactivations from 27,000 people, for $10,300 in discounts. Right call: stop it or test cheaper versions.

## Decoys
- "Too expensive" at 38% tempts blanket discounts; the data says skip or delay works for many.

## Wrong
- More discounts everywhere. Health claims beyond structure or function.
