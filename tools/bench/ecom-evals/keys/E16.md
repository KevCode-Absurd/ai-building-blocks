# E16 key: Wild One harness ad images (tune, 2 signals)

## Signals
1. **In use beats flat lay.** Dog wearing it on a city street with a product close-up inset: $27 per purchase, 1.9% CTR; flat lay $61; text-heavy $88. Right call: most of the 5 are in use, with a clear product inset or detail.
2. **Big dogs want in.** 214 comments ask about large sizes. Right call: at least one ad with a large dog (lab, shepherd) and "up to XL".

## Brand
- Colours from the Org context (limeade #DFF464, lavender #E7E5FF, black, white), Modern Era style type, the Wild One wordmark; best colours Limeade, Oxford, Lilac.

## Claims
- No "no-pull" or "stops pulling"; "front clip for more control" is fine.

## Image judging notes
- On brand: palette, type, logo. Product faithful: neoprene padded harness, three leash points. No errors: no extra legs, warped text or wrong logo. Phone readable: short, large on-image text.
