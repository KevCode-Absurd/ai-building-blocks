# E26 key: Taylor Stitch unit economics (held-out, 3 signals)

## Method used for this key
Per order: price x (1 minus product cost share) minus $9.50 shipping, $3.20 pick and pack, and fees (2.9% plus $0.30). A refund return adds $15 and writes off 8% of the product cost; an exchange keeps the sale and adds $27.70 ($15 + $9.50 + $3.20). Answers may use another sound method if they state it.

## Signals
1. **Small orders lose money.** Orders under $60: 2,900 orders, $41 average, about $7.70 each before marketing. Right call: a free shipping minimum (for example $75) or a small-order fee while keeping free returns, or bundle prompts.
2. **Carnegie Pant returns.** A 31% return rate costs about $17 per order versus a 12% rate. Contribution per Carnegie order is about $64. Right call: fit tools and size guidance, not dropping the product.
3. **Exchanges are a cost.** 30 to 45% of returns are exchanges at about $27.70 extra each. Right call: an instant exchange that ships the new size on scan, or size advice before purchase.

## Approximate contribution per order (this key's method)
- Jack $61, Chino $59, Carnegie $64, Long Haul $87, Fisherman $122, under-$60 accessories $8.

## Decoys
- A 63% gross margin looks healthy.

## Wrong
- Stopping at gross margin. Numbers that cannot be traced. Ending free returns outright with no math.
