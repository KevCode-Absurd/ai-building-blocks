# E27 key: Sleepy Owl COD (tune, 4 signals; India specialist)

## Signals
1. **COD is too big to drop.** About 52% of 10,200 orders are COD; RTO is about 26% of COD.
2. **Confirmation works.** WhatsApp confirm-or-cancel: RTO 14% vs 27%; 9% cancel before dispatch (cheap to lose). Right call: roll it out to all COD orders.
3. **The prepaid nudge works.** Rs 30 off: 31% switched vs 9%; checkout completion 61% vs 63% (a small cost to watch). Right call: keep it; test Rs 20 or a free gift.
4. **Tier 3 is the problem.** 79% COD, 41% RTO. Right call: partial prepaid, a COD fee or COD caps in tier 3; metros are fine.

## Decoys
- The courier says 60% "customer not available": mostly intent and confirmation, not only courier fault.

## Economics for checking
- An RTO costs about Rs 182 (Rs 120 return plus Rs 62 forward) plus the Rs 35 COD fee and some write-off.

## Wrong
- "Stop COD." Blaming only the courier.
