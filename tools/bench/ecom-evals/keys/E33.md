# E33 key: Wild One free shipping threshold (held-out, 0 signals)

## Right call
- Keep $90. Orders cluster just above it ($90 to $104: 6,900 of 20,500); 38% of $70 to $89 carts add an item to qualify; AOV is $96.
- Dropping to $49 would give free shipping to most of the 5,200 orders between $50 and $89 (about $8.90 each, about $46,000 over 90 days) and weaken add-on behaviour.
- At most, one small test: a better add-on rail near the threshold (poop bags, collars).

## Decoys
- A competitor at $49. 71% cart abandonment (a normal range).

## Wrong
- Dropping the threshold. Inventing a problem.

## False signals
- Any "hidden problem" claimed here is a false signal.
