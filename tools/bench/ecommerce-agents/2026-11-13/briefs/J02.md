# Job J02: TikTok ads launch plan for the first 30 days

Business: Bombas (bombas.com), a US apparel brand best known for socks, which donates an item for every item purchased.
Role wanted: TikTok ads media buyer.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Situation (test): the brand has never run TikTok ads. It has an organic TikTok account with modest reach and a few creator videos it has rights to use. Budget for the first 30 days: $30,000. Average order value $62. Gross margin after product cost 55%; shipping and payment fees $7 per order. Meta is the main paid channel today at a blended cost per first order of $38. The TikTok pixel is not yet installed; the store runs on Shopify. Market: US only.

What the owner wants: a launch plan they can hand to the team on Monday: account and tracking set-up, campaign structure, how much creative to make and what kind, budget phasing across the 30 days, the rules for killing and scaling, and how they will know on day 30 whether TikTok earned more budget.

Length: at most 1,400 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
