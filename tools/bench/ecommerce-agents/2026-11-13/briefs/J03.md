# Job J03: Eight Meta ad concepts for a cabin suitcase

Business: Mokobara (mokobara.com), an Indian travel brand selling luggage, backpacks and travel accessories online.
Role wanted: paid social creative strategist.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Product (test): a cabin-size hard-shell suitcase, polycarbonate shell, 360-degree spinner wheels, TSA-style lock, fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits, priced at ₹6,999. Warranty and returns as on the site. Main buyers: working professionals 25 to 40 in metro cities who fly for work and weekends. Current ads are clean product shots on colour backgrounds; the owner feels they all look the same and results are flat. Approved claims: the specs above. No competitor names in ads.

What the owner wants: eight distinct ad concepts for Meta (Feed and Reels), each with the angle, who it is for and how aware they are, the hook (first line or first three seconds), the format, what the visual shows, a draft of the primary text, and what the concept tests. Then say which three to make first and why.

Length: at most 1,500 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
