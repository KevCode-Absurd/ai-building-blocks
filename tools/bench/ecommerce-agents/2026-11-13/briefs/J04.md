# Job J04: Three UGC video ad scripts

Business: Olipop (drinkolipop.com), a US soda brand made with plant fiber and prebiotics, sold online and in stores.
Role wanted: video ad and UGC producer.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Brief (test): the store wants three creator-style video ads, 30 to 45 seconds each, for Meta Reels and TikTok, to sell a 12-can variety pack online. Buyers: adults who drink regular soda and want to cut sugar without giving up the soda moment. Claims the client approved for this test: "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours". No health or gut-health cure claims. The creators will be real paid creators the store hires; ads must follow the platforms' paid partnership disclosure rules.

What the owner wants: three ready-to-shoot scripts, each with a different angle, a scene-by-scene shot list with timing, spoken lines, on-screen text, the opening hook with two alternate hooks to test, and a short brief to send to the creator.

Length: at most 1,300 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
