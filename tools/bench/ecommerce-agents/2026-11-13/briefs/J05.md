# Job J05: Meta ad copy set for protein bars

Business: The Whole Truth (thewholetruthfoods.com), an Indian food brand known for listing ingredients on the front of its packs, selling protein bars, nut butters and more online.
Role wanted: ad copywriter.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Product (test): a box of 6 protein bars, 12 g protein per bar, no added sugar, sweetened with dates, ₹540 per box. Approved claims: the ones in the previous sentence and "ingredients listed on the front of the pack". No comparisons with named competitors. Audience: urban gym-goers and office workers 22 to 40 who distrust "healthy" snacks with hidden sugar.

What the owner wants: a launch-ready copy set for Meta: 6 primary texts (each a different angle), 6 headlines, 3 descriptions, every line within Meta's display limits, and which 4 combinations to launch first with what each one tests.

Length: at most 900 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
