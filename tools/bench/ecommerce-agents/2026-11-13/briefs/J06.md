# Job J06: Product page copy for a slim wallet

Business: Bellroy (bellroy.com), an Australian brand selling slim wallets, bags and accessories worldwide.
Role wanted: product page copywriter.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Product (test): Hide & Seek wallet, a slim leather bifold. Holds 5 to 12 cards, quick-access card slots, a hidden pocket for flat notes and spare cards, a coin pouch, premium leather, 3-year warranty, US$99. Top review themes from 400 reviews (test): "much thinner than my old wallet" (most common), "leather ages nicely", "takes a week to break in", "coin pouch is small", "hidden pocket is clever". Main reason for returns: buyers expected it to hold more cash.

What the owner wants: the full product page copy: product title and one-line subtitle, the 4 to 6 bullets above the fold, a short description, a spec and "fits" section, an FAQ of 5 questions, the SEO title and meta description, and the line under the add-to-cart button. It should cut the "holds less cash than expected" returns without hurting sales.

Length: at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
