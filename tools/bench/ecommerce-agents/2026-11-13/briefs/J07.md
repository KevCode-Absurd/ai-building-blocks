# Job J07: Welcome email series for new subscribers

Business: Minimalist (beminimalist.co), an Indian skin care brand known for naming active ingredients and their strength on the pack, selling online and on marketplaces.
Role wanted: email and SMS copywriter.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Situation (test): new subscribers join from a site pop-up that promises skin care tips and 10% off the first order. About 18% buy within 14 days. The store uses Klaviyo. Average first order ₹699. Best sellers: a niacinamide serum, a sunscreen and a salicylic acid face wash. Many new buyers are unsure which active suits their skin and fear irritation. No medical claims; no before-and-after promises.

What the owner wants: a 5-email welcome series for people who have not bought yet: send timing and exit rule, and for each email the subject line, preview text, full body copy, the call to action and what job the email does. Add one WhatsApp or SMS message if it fits and say where.

Length: at most 1,800 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
