# Job J08: Win-back programme for lapsed coffee buyers

Business: Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters (bluetokaicoffee.com), an Indian specialty coffee roaster selling beans, ground coffee and equipment online and in its cafes.
Role wanted: retention marketing lead.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Data (test, last 24 months of online orders): 60,000 customers. 12-month repeat rate fell from 34% to 28%. Median days between first and second order: 34. Average order ₹1,150; gross margin after product cost 58%; shipping and fees ₹110 per order. Subscriptions are 9% of revenue. Customer counts by last order: 0 to 45 days 11,000; 46 to 90 days 9,000; 91 to 180 days 14,000; 181 to 365 days 15,000; over 365 days 11,000. 62% have opted in to email, 40% to WhatsApp. The team can send email and WhatsApp; discounts above 15% need the founder's yes.

What the owner wants: a win-back programme: which groups to target and in what order, the offer logic for each, the message sequence and timing per channel, the first message drafts, how to measure true lift, and what it is worth in gross profit if it works.

Length: at most 1,500 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
