# Job J09: 90-day growth plan

Business: Brooklinen (brooklinen.com), a US direct-to-consumer brand selling sheets, bedding, towels and home goods online.
Role wanted: e-commerce growth lead.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Last 6 months versus the same 6 months a year ago (test): revenue down 4%. New customer orders down 11%; returning customer orders up 6%. Blended cost per first order up from $78 to $104. Average first order $188; gross margin after product cost 52%; shipping, fees and returns cost 14% of revenue. Paid mix: Meta 55%, Google 35%, other 10%. Site conversion rate flat at 2.1%. Email and SMS drive 22% of revenue. 18-month repeat rate 31%. Cash: comfortable, but the board wants contribution margin to improve this year, not just revenue.

What the owner wants: a 90-day plan: what is really causing the slowdown (separate what the numbers prove from what needs checking), the 5 to 7 moves ranked by gross profit impact and effort, who owns each, what to stop doing, and a weekly scorecard to run it.

Length: at most 1,600 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
