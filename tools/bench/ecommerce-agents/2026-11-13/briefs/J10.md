# Job J10: Bundles and free shipping threshold

Business: Plum Goodness (plumgoodness.com), an Indian vegan beauty brand selling skin, hair, body and makeup products online.
Role wanted: offer strategist.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Data (test): average order value ₹620. Order value spread: under ₹400 22%; ₹400 to 499 18%; ₹500 to 699 27%; ₹700 to 999 19%; ₹1,000 and up 14%. Free shipping today above ₹499; shipping costs the store ₹70 per order. Gross margin after product cost: skin care 68%, hair care 62%, body care 60%, makeup 55%. Top sellers: a vitamin C serum ₹599, a green tea face wash ₹345, a body lotion ₹425, a hair shampoo ₹399, a lip balm ₹199. 40% of orders are COD. Shoppers often buy one hero product only.

What the owner wants: three bundles to launch (contents, price, the job each does), a recommendation on the free shipping threshold with the money worked out, and how to test both safely.

Length: at most 1,300 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
