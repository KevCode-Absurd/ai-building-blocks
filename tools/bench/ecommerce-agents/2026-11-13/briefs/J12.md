# Job J12: WhatsApp sales and COD confirmation messages

Business: Snitch (snitch.co.in), an Indian men's fashion brand selling shirts, t-shirts, jeans and more online.
Role wanted: chat and WhatsApp sales lead.

Numbers marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures.

Situation (test): 60% of orders are cash on delivery; 25% of COD orders are refused at delivery (RTO), each costing about ₹180. Average order ₹1,300. The store has the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, with opt-in collected at checkout. 30% of carts are abandoned after the shipping step. Common chat questions: size and fit, delivery date, exchange policy, COD availability, order status. A team of 4 agents answers chats 10 am to 8 pm.

What the owner wants: ready-to-submit WhatsApp message templates (name, category, body, buttons) for COD order confirmation, a nudge to switch to prepaid, cart recovery, and delivery-day reminders; the flow and timing for each; short reply scripts for the 5 common questions; and the numbers to watch weekly.

Length: at most 1,400 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
