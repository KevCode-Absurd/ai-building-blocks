# Bombas TikTok ads: 30-day launch plan

**Goal:** learn, for $30,000 or less, whether TikTok can win first orders at or under the $38 Meta costs today.
**Dates:** Day 1 is Monday 16 November 2026. First spend Day 4 (19 Nov). Verdict Day 30 (Tue 15 Dec).

## 1. The numbers every rule uses

| Line | Working | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Profit per first order before ads | $62 x 55% = $34.10, minus $7 fees | $27.10 |
| Target cost per first order | Meta's current cost | $38 |
| Ceiling | 1.5 x $38 | $57 |
| Spend to judge one ad | 3 x $38 | $114 |
| Learning bar per ad group | 25 purchases x $38 / 7 days | $136 a day |

Meta already pays $10.90 more per first order than that order earns, so repeat orders carry the payback. TikTok gets the same $38 target.

## 3. Campaign structure

**Campaign 1, Test (from Day 4).** Sales objective, website, optimise to CompletePayment. Manual, not Smart+, so each concept gets a clean read. Lowest-cost bidding. US, 18+, otherwise broad. TikTok placement only. Customers excluded. Default attribution (7-day click, 1-day view), judged on Shopify orders.

- 4 ad groups at $150 a day, one per concept, 3 ads each.
- $150 clears the $136 learning bar. More, thinner groups would never leave learning.

**Campaign 2, Scale (from Day 11).** One broad ad group, winners only, moved in as the same post so likes and comments carry over. Starts at $500 a day.

Smart+ waits for month 2, as a side-by-side test.

## 4. Creative: what and how much

**Launch set: 12 ads, ready Day 3.**

| Ad group (concept) | Format | Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Creator posts | Spark Ads of the creator videos you own rights to | 3 |
| Sock drawer swap | Problem and fix: worn-out sock, then Bombas on foot | 3 hooks, one body |
| One bought, one given | Talking head on the giving promise | 3 hooks, one body |
| The gift they use | Holiday gift unboxing or slideshow | 3 hooks, one body |

Hooks differ only in words, on-screen text and first visual.

**Refresh: 6 new ads a week** (2 new concepts x 3 hooks) on Days 11, 18 and 25: 30 ads in the month. Brief from why ads failed: weak 2-second hold needs a new opener; few clicks, show the sock sooner; few carts, check the page. On Day 4, gift product to new creators for posts with Spark codes, ready by week 3.

**Every ad:** 9:16, captions on, brand seen in the first 2 seconds, most under 25 seconds, text in the safe zone. Sound from TikTok's Commercial Music Library or original audio only. Claims only from the current product page. Creator posts disclose the paid partnership. Any AI-made person or voice is labelled and never shown as a customer. Price and offer match the landing page, including any Black Friday offer.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Set-up | 1-3 (16-18 Nov) | $0 | $0 | $0 | |
| Test | 4-10 (19-25 Nov) | $600 | $0 | $4,200 | Test order passed |
| Prove | 11-17 (26 Nov-2 Dec) | $450 | $500 | $6,650 | Gate A |
| Scale 1 | 18-20 (3-5 Dec) | $450 | $650 | $3,300 | Gate B |
| Scale 2 | 21-23 (6-8 Dec) | $450 | $850 | $3,900 | Raise rule |
| Scale 3 | 24-26 (9-11 Dec) | $450 | $1,100 | $4,650 | Raise rule |
| Scale 4 | 27-30 (12-15 Dec) | $450 | $1,375 | $7,300 | Raise rule |
| **Total** | | | | **$30,000** | |

Steps are 25 to 31% raises, three days apart. A step that fails its rule holds; that money stays unspent.

**Gate A (Day 11).** Over Days 4 to 10, TikTok purchases and TikTok-tagged Shopify orders roughly agree. At least one ad has 3 or more purchases on its first $114. If not, skip Scale: Test stays at $600 a day with 6 new ads to Day 17. If Day 17 still fails, pause TikTok with $8,400 spent.

**Gate B (Day 18).** The Scale ad group's 7-day cost per first order is at or under $38. Between $38 and $57: hold at $950 a day and keep testing.

**Black Friday week.** Days 11 to 15 cover Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Cyber Monday. No raises then. Judge TikTok against Meta's cost per first order on the same days, not the $38 average.

## 6. Kill and scale rules

**Per ad:**
- No call before $50 spend or 1,000 impressions.
- Early health: click rate above 1%, average watch time above 25%. Below that, diagnose; do not pause yet.
- At $114 spend: 0 or 1 purchases, cut. 2, allow $38 more. 3 or more, move to Scale.
- Leave weaker ads alone inside an ad group meeting $38.
- Click rate down a third from its own best week: brief a refresh.

**Per ad group (rolling 7-day cost per first order):**

| Call | Rule |
|---|---|
| Raise 20 to 30% | At or under $38, out of learning, last raise 72+ hours ago |
| Hold | $38 to $57, still learning, or raised in the last 72 hours |
| Cut budget by a third | Over $57 on two reads in a row |
| Pause | Over $57 for 7 days after learning |

**After every raise,** divide the extra spend by the extra orders. Over $57: go back to the last budget, even if the average looks fine.

**Weekly,** correct TikTok's count: Shopify TikTok-tagged orders divided by TikTok-reported purchases. Divide TikTok's cost per purchase by that factor before any call.

**Daily, 15 minutes:** breakages first (spend, rejections, tracking, stock, checkout). Log each change with a prediction. Anything outside these rules needs the owner's yes.

## 7. Day 30: did TikTok earn more budget?

Work out TikTok's cost per first order three ways, for Days 4 to 30 and Days 16 to 30 (after the sale week):

1. **Shopify floor:** TikTok spend / TikTok-tagged first orders. It undercounts later, in-app and cross-device buyers.
2. **Survey:** TikTok spend / (share of first-order buyers answering "TikTok" x all first orders).
3. **Lift:** TikTok spend / (first orders a day minus the baseline), with Meta held steady. Sale weeks lift orders anyway; compare with last year's same weeks if available.

| Result | Call |
|---|---|
| Two reads at or under $38 | **Earned.** Month 2 at the Day 30 rate ($1,825 a day), raised in 20 to 30% steps while extra orders cost under $57. Under $27.10 on two reads: raise faster. |
| Reads disagree, or $38 to $57 | **Hold** at $950 a day; in January, switch TikTok off in a matched set of states for three weeks and compare. |
| Two reads over $57, not falling | **Stop.** Reuse the winning videos on Meta. |

## Assumptions

- Repeat orders justify Meta's $38. With no repeat data, $38 is the target rather than a lifetime value figure.
- Creator rights cover paid use. If files only, they run as normal ads from the brand profile.
- The team can film three short phone videos by Day 3.
- Meta spend stays as planned; any change is logged with its date.
- Website sales only; TikTok Shop is out of scope.
