# Olipop 12-can variety pack: three creator video ads for Reels and TikTok

Three angles: the afternoon soda habit kept, a blind taste test for doubters, and a ranking of the whole pack. Each runs 38 to 45 seconds at 9:16 and ends on drinkolipop.com.

## Assumptions

- No price, offer or flavour list was given. The ads state no price or discount, and creators name flavours as printed on their cans.
- Each creator drinks Olipop for at least two weeks before filming. Lines marked *own words* come from their real use.
- Viewers are in the US, so FTC endorsement rules apply.

## Rules for every script

**Claims, in this approved wording only:** "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours".

**Never say:** gut health, digestion, bloating, healthy, good for you, guilt-free, weight, sugar or fiber numbers, or another soda brand's name. No line suggesting the viewer has a health problem.

**Disclosure, every video:**
- On screen from 0:00 to 0:03: "Ad · Paid partnership with Olipop".
- Spoken once before 0:06 (lines below).
- Platform tools on: Instagram's Paid partnership label and TikTok's branded content disclosure. #ad first in the caption. "#gifted" or "#collab" alone is not enough.

**Hook test:** run each script's three hooks on the same body, at the same time, with equal budget. Pick on 3-second view rate, then cost per purchase.

## Creator brief (send with your script)

**The product:** Olipop, a soda with plant fiber and prebiotics. You are selling the 12-can variety pack at drinkolipop.com.

**Your job:** a 35 to 45 second video that sounds like you telling a friend. Drink Olipop for at least two weeks first, and only say what really happened. Setting, pace, humour and story are yours. Use a hook only if it is true for you.

**Must have:** the disclosure (on screen for the first 3 seconds, spoken once), the product in real use, the claim lines as written, and the drinkolipop.com call to action.

**Don't:** use health words, nutrition numbers or other brands' names. Use original sound or the platform's commercial music library, not trending tracks from a personal account.

**Framing:** 1080 x 1920, subject centred so a 4:5 crop works. Keep text and key action out of the top 14%, bottom 35% and 6% each side. Check TikTok with its own safe-zone overlay.

**Deliverables:**
- 1 edited video with captions burned in
- The main hook and both alternates, filmed separately
- All raw clips plus 10 to 15 seconds of b-roll, unedited

**Rights (proposed):** paid use on Meta and TikTok for 6 months from first run, plus organic reposts, signed before filming. Accept Olipop's Instagram partnership ad request and send a TikTok ad authorization code.

**Dates (proposed):** product ships day 0; draft due day 17; one revision round; final due day 21. We reply to drafts within 48 hours.

**Pay:** flat fee per video, agreed before filming.
