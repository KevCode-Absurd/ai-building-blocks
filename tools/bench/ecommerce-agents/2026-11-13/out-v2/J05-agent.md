# The Whole Truth protein bars: Meta launch copy set

**Launch these four first:** primary texts 1, 2, 3 and 4, each with headline "Ingredients on the front" and description "Box of 6 bars for ₹540". Only the primary text changes, so each result tells you which reason to buy works. Ad A against Ad B tests plain facts against the hidden sugar doubt. Ad C against Ad D tests the gym moment against the office moment.

Every line shows in full in Feed: primary texts are 125 characters or fewer (nothing hides behind "See more"), headlines and descriptions 27 or fewer, Meta's recommended length. Counts include spaces.

## How to run and read it

- One Sales campaign. Each ad gets its own ad set with the same budget and image, or use Meta's A/B test tool. One broad audience: ages 22 to 40, in the cities you deliver to.
- Turn off Meta's automatic text changes, so it does not rewrite the copy under test.
- Run each ad set for at least 7 days. Read results at ad set level on 7-day click, and check them against your store orders.
- Before you judge an ad set, let it spend at least 3 times your target cost per purchase. Then: 3 or more purchases, keep it. 2 purchases, give it one more target cost of spend. 0 or 1, cut it.
- If the budget cannot reach that in 7 days, judge on cost per add to cart, then confirm on purchases.
- A high click rate with no purchases is not a win. A near tie means keep the plainer ad.

**Round 2:** test the winning text with H4 and H6. Show texts 5 and 6 to product page visitors who did not buy.

## Lines kept out on purpose

- "Sugar-free" or "zero sugar": dates contain natural sugar, so only "no added sugar" is safe.
- Calling the bar "healthy" or "guilt-free", or any energy, muscle, weight or fitness result. None is approved.
- Taste promises beyond "sweet", named-brand comparisons, all-caps lines and countdowns.

## Assumptions

- The product page is live and in stock at ₹540. ₹90 a bar is ₹540 divided by 6.
- No reviews or sales figures were supplied, so none are used.
- The voice is blunt, plain and a little dry, to fit a brand built on showing what's in the pack.

## To confirm

1. The exact product page URL for the Shop Now button.
2. That the pack label says "no added sugar" in the same words used here.
3. Your target cost per purchase, which sets each ad set's test budget.
