## Description

Most wallets get thick because they try to carry everything. The Hide & Seek carries what you reach for every day and leaves the rest behind. That's how it stays slim.

Your daily cards sit in quick-access slots up front. Spare cards and a few notes go flat into a hidden pocket behind them, out of sight but easy to reach. Coins go in a small pouch so they don't roam free.

New leather is firm, so give it about a week to break in. After that, it keeps changing in a good way: reviewers often say the leather ages nicely.

**Not for you if** you pay in cash most days or carry a thick fold of notes. The hidden pocket shares its space with your spare cards, so there's only room for a little cash. One of our roomier wallets will serve you better.

## FAQ

**How much cash does it hold?**
A few notes, kept flat in the hidden pocket. That pocket also holds your spare cards, so the more cards you keep there, the less room for notes. If you carry a lot of cash, choose one of our roomier wallets.

**How many cards fit?**
5 to 12. Your daily cards go in the quick-access slots and the rest go in the hidden pocket. With 5 cards it's at its slimmest. With 12 it's full.

**Why does it feel stiff at first?**
New leather is firm. Reviewers often say it takes about a week to break in. After that it softens with use.

**How big is the coin pouch?**
Small. It's for a few coins, not a full day's change. Reviewers mention this too, so we'd rather you know before you buy.

**Will it last?**
Reviewers say the leather ages nicely, and every Hide & Seek comes with a 3-year warranty.
