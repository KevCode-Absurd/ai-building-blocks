# Minimalist welcome series: 5 emails for subscribers who have not bought

New buyers hold back for two reasons: they don't know which active suits their skin, and they fear irritation. Each email answers one of those questions while the 10% code stays in view. The code lasts 7 days, so the series runs for 6 days and ends with a true 24-hour reminder. The goal is to lift the 14-day purchase rate above today's 18%.

- **Trigger:** added to the pop-up list. **Flow filter:** Placed Order zero times over all time, so existing customers never get the offer.
- **Exit:** before every email, a split on "Placed Order since starting this flow". Anyone who buys leaves at once, WhatsApp included.
- **Pause:** skip a welcome email if the person started checkout in the last 48 hours, so nobody gets a welcome and a checkout reminder on the same day.
- **Settings:** Smart Sending off for this flow, UTM tracking on, standard footer with postal address and unsubscribe.

## Email 3: start without irritation (day 3)

**Job:** answer the fear of irritation with a clear, safe way to begin.
**Subject A:** How to start an active without upsetting your skin (50)
**Subject B:** New to actives? Start slow (26)
**Preview:** Patch test, one product at a time, and what to do if it stings.

Worried a new active will irritate your skin? It's the most common reason people wait. Here's how to start slowly.

1. **Patch test first.** Put a little on a small area, like the side of your neck, once a day for three days. No redness or itching? Go ahead.
2. **One new product at a time.** Give it about two weeks before you add the next.
3. **Go slow with leave-on actives.** Use a serum every other night at first. Move to daily once your skin feels comfortable.
4. **Wear sunscreen every morning,** especially once you use exfoliating actives like salicylic acid.
5. **Know when to stop.** A brief tingle can happen. Burning, redness that lasts, a rash or itching means rinse it off and stop. If it doesn't settle, see a dermatologist.

If you have a skin condition, use prescription skin care or are pregnant, ask your doctor before you add a new active.

Because every pack names the active and its strength, you always know exactly what you added.
