# Plum Goodness: three bundles and a ₹699 free-shipping line

## The answer in short

- **Launch three bundles**, all at ₹699 or more: Glow Duo ₹799, Face and Body Basics ₹699, Head-to-Toe Kit ₹1,599.
- **Move free shipping from ₹499 to ₹699** once a split test proves it. Planning case: about +₹11,700 gross profit per 1,000 orders, a 3.3% lift. It stops paying if more than 18% of ₹500 to 699 shoppers walk away.
- **Test bundles first (weeks 1 to 4), then the line (weeks 5 to 8)**, each a same-days split judged on gross profit per visitor.

Runner-up: a ₹599 line. It keeps the lone ₹599 serum shipping free and earns about half as much.

## What your order data says

All money is per 1,000 orders, as order volume was not given.

| Order value | Share | Free shipping today / at ₹699 |
|---|---|---|
| Under ₹400 | 22% | No / No |
| ₹400 to 499 | 18% | No / No |
| ₹500 to 699 | 27% | Yes / only if they add an item |
| ₹700 and up | 33% | Yes / Yes |

1. **600 of every 1,000 orders ship free.** At ₹70 each, that is ₹42,000, or ₹42 on every order you take. Gross profit today is about ₹3,54,800 (₹6,20,000 of sales at 64%, less ₹42,000 shipping).
2. **Four of your five top sellers sit under ₹499 alone**: face wash ₹345, shampoo ₹399, lotion ₹425, lip balm ₹199. With one-hero shopping, that is why 40% of orders fall below the line.
3. **₹400 to 499 is the densest band**: 18% of orders inside ₹100. They are close to free shipping and still stop: they need a ready-made second item, not a lower line.

## Three bundles to launch

Gross profit = price minus product cost minus ₹70 shipping (bundles ship free). "Versus today" is the same shopper buying the main single item; items under ₹500 pay their own shipping today.

| | Glow Duo | Face and Body Basics | Head-to-Toe Kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contents | Vitamin C serum + green tea face wash | Green tea face wash + body lotion | Serum, face wash, shampoo, body lotion, lip balm |
| Price | ₹799 (₹944 bought separately, save ₹145) | ₹699 (₹770 separately, save ₹71) | ₹1,599 (₹1,967 separately, save ₹368) |

| Stops paying if this share would have bought all items at full price anyway | 38% | 57% | 57% |

How to set them up:

- **Savings climb with box size**: 9%, 15%, 19%. Put the rupee saving in each title ("Glow Duo, save ₹145").
- **The Glow Duo is the default**: under add to cart on the serum page, and the bundle your ads name. Skin care's 68% margin can carry the routine discount.
- **Basics sits exactly on ₹699.** Show it on the face wash and lotion pages, and in any cart under ₹699.
- **The Kit** goes in a Kits collection and gifting emails.
- **Shampoo buyers get a cart prompt**: add the face wash (₹744 total) and it ships free.
- **Keep it honest.** "Save" figures use prices you actually charge. Show bundle prices and the shipping line on the product page, not first at checkout, as India's rules on hidden charges require.

At a ₹699 line, a lone serum order pays its own shipping and earns ₹407, so the Duo's edge narrows to ₹20; its job becomes keeping serum shoppers who balk at the fee.

## Free shipping: move the line to ₹699

A ₹500 to 699 order that loses free shipping does one of three things:

- adds the ₹199 lip balm and still ships free: **+₹109**
- pays the shipping fee: **+₹70**
- walks away: **minus ₹314** (a ₹600 order's ₹384 gross profit, less the ₹70 you no longer spend)

Planning case: 30% add, 60% pay, 10% leave. That is **+₹43 per affected order.**

| Line | Orders affected | If nobody leaves | Planning case | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹399 | 180 more ship free | minus ₹12,600 | Needs 58 extra orders (5.8%) just to break even | Reject |
| ₹599 | about 135 | +₹9,450 | +₹5,900 | Runner-up |
| ₹699 | 270 | +₹18,900 | +₹11,700 | Recommend, after the test |
| ₹799 | about 333 | +₹23,300 | Not trusted | Reject: serum plus lip balm is ₹798 and misses it |

Why ₹699:

- **Every key route clears it**: serum plus lip balm (₹798), shampoo plus face wash (₹744) and all three bundles.
- **Clear break-even**: it pays while fewer than 18% of the 270 affected shoppers leave (49 orders, about 5% of all orders). If 30% top up, that rises to 21%.
- **Planning case**: gross profit rises from ₹3,54,800 to ₹3,66,500.

The risk is your top seller: a lone ₹599 serum loses free shipping. The cart bar ("Add ₹100 for free shipping") and the Glow Duo catch that shopper. If serum-only orders fall sharply in the test, take ₹599 instead.

## How to test both safely

Run the tests one after the other. Each is a 50/50 split by visitor on the same days, via a split-test app or theme; failing that, split by states with matched past sales. Keep emails, ads and sales equal across arms, skip festival weeks, and never show two prices for one product.

| | Test 1: bundles | Test 2: free-shipping line |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks | 1 to 4 (full weeks) | 5 to 8 (full weeks) |
| Arm A | Today's pages | Free shipping at ₹499 |
| Arm B | All three bundles live | Free shipping at ₹699 (bundles live in both arms) |
| Main metric | Gross profit per visitor, after shipping | Same |
| Stop line | Orders per visitor more than 5% below arm A | Same, or COD delivered rate more than 2 points below arm A |
| Ship it if | Gross profit per visitor is higher in B after 4 weeks | Same, and serum-only orders hold up |

- **Gross profit per visitor, not order value**: it counts both the full-price buyer who took a bundle and the shopper who left.
- **Delivered orders, not placed ones.** 40% of orders are COD. Count a refused COD order as lost and charge it ₹140 (₹70 out, ₹70 back), since a higher line or bigger box can raise refusals.
- **Watch the band where the effect lives.** A 5% store-wide fall needs very high volume to see, so also track how many ₹500 to 699 carts reach checkout. There the break-even fall is 18%, far easier to spot. On low volume, run 6 to 8 weeks.

## Assumptions

1. Orders under the line pay a fee covering the ₹70 cost. A lower fee shrinks each saving by the gap.
2. Store-wide margin is 64%: your five top sellers, one of each, weighted by price. Margins are after product cost only.
3. Orders spread evenly within each band; the typical ₹500 to 699 order is ₹600.
4. The 30/60/10 split is a planning guess that test 2 replaces.
5. A bundle buyer would otherwise have bought the bundle's main single item.
6. A refused COD order's return leg costs ₹70, like the outbound leg.
