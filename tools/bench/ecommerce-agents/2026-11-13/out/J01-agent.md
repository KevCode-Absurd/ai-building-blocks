# Meta budget for the next 14 days: cut two, fix three, hold three

## The decision

- **Meta is not yet making money on first orders.** On Meta's own count it lost about ₹27,000 in the last 14 days. On Shopify's count (1,020 Meta-tagged orders, not the 1,425 Meta claims) it lost about ₹1.84 lakh.
- **No raises today.** Spend ₹30,700 a day (₹4.3 lakh). Today's ₹44,900 a day runs at ₹13.5 lakh a month, over budget.
- **₹1.3 lakh stays in reserve**, released in 20% steps only when ASC or Beard Kit beats target.
- **The biggest upside is the UGC ad.** It buys the cheapest clicks (₹7.90), but only 1.4% of clickers buy, against 3.8% in ASC. At ASC's buy rate it would cost about ₹210 an order.

## The line each ad set must beat

On a ₹899 order, profit per order placed is about **₹394**, after 38% product cost, ₹95 shipping and fees, 22% of COD orders refused at ₹160 each, and 2% prepaid returns. 89 of every 100 orders placed are kept.

- If every Meta purchase is real, break-even is ₹394 per purchase (ROAS 2.3).
- Shopify tags only 72% of Meta's claims. On that count, break-even is ₹282 (ROAS 3.2).
- UTM tags miss some real Meta orders, so the truth sits between. **Working target: 86% of break-even**, the midpoint: about ₹340 on a ₹899 order. Each ad set uses its own order value.

**ASC Broad, hold.** The only prospecting ad set that makes money on Meta's count: ₹39 an order. Its ₹344 cost is above its ₹329 target, so no raise yet. Add the UGC ads and the best Women and Gift ads here, so those products keep selling.

**Beard Kit LAL 1%, fix.** Makes ₹21 an order on Meta's count, but frequency 3.4 and the highest prospecting CPM (₹230) show the 1% pool is wearing out. Turn on Advantage+ audience with the lookalike as a suggestion. Remove refused COD buyers from the seed list, so Meta stops finding more like them.

**Catalog DPA, hold.** ₹95 under break-even on Meta's count, near break-even on Shopify's. Frequency 5.2 is the limit; add no money.

**Retargeting 30-day, cut to ₹1,500 a day.** Frequency 6.8 means each visitor saw about 7 ads in 14 days, about 15 a month: overspend on a warm audience. It overlaps DPA and ASC. Saves ₹34,000.

**Razor Starter Kit, fix.** Loses ₹97 an order even on Meta's count, over 205 purchases. Add a cost per result goal of ₹315 and cut to ₹4,000 a day. If it cannot spend half that at the goal for 4 days, pause it; ASC still sells the kit.

**New UGC test, fix.** Loses ₹167 an order, but CTR 1.9% and CPM ₹150 are the best in the account. The leak is after the click. Make the link open the product page of the item shown, at the price and offer in the video. Keep ₹3,600 a day for 7 more days; it is still learning.

**Women Body Razor, cut.** Loses ₹383 an order even on Meta's count. Clicks cost ₹26, twice ASC's ₹13, because the interest audience is narrow and costly. Yet 3.3% of clickers buy: the product sells, the audience fails. Its best ads move into ASC.

## Rules for the team, day to day

1. **Judge on the last 7 days, never one day.** Change an ad set at most once every 4 days; each change restarts Meta's learning.
2. **Raise.** Only ASC and Beard Kit draw on the reserve: +20% when 7-day cost per purchase is under target (ASC ₹329, Beard Kit ₹387), frequency is under 2.5 and Shopify Meta-tagged orders rose too. After each raise, divide extra spend by extra purchases; if that passes break-even, go back.
3. **Cut.** If 7-day cost per purchase is above break-even after spending 3 times that amount, halve the budget; if the next 7 days repeat, pause. Switch off any new ad with 0 or 1 purchases after ₹1,200.
4. **Fatigue.** Prospecting frequency over 2.5 in 7 days: add new ads before touching budget. Retargeting and catalog over 14 a month: cut budget.
5. **Monday truth check.** Shopify Meta-tagged orders divided by Meta purchases is 72% today. Outside 65% to 80%, reset every target. Every ad carries UTM tags.
6. **Refusals, fortnightly.** From courier data, read refusals by ad set on orders two weeks old. An ad set 10 points above the store's rate is cut first, whatever Meta's cost says.
7. **Limits.** Never above ₹40,000 a day. New ad sets, or anything outside these rules, need your yes.

## Assumptions

- ₹899 is revenue after GST and discounts. If it includes GST, profit per order falls to about ₹270 to ₹320 and every cut gets stronger.
- Every ad set has the same COD share and refusal rate; rule 6 will show which differ.
- Refused and returned stock is resold, so only ₹160 shipping is lost; a prepaid return costs the same.
- Each ad set's order value is its purchase value divided by purchases.
- Profit is first order only. Refill buys would raise break-even; no budget relies on them until measured.
