# Bombas TikTok ads: 30-day launch plan

## The plan on one screen

- **Goal:** learn whether TikTok buys new customers at or under Meta's $38 per first order, judged on Shopify orders, not TikTok's dashboard.
- **Budget:** $30,000 cap in three 10-day blocks of $700, $1,000 and $1,300 a day.
- **Build:** a manual Test campaign from day 1, creator videos as Spark Ads, a Scale campaign from day 11, and about 43 ads.
- **Day 30:** TikTok earns more budget if Shopify-verified cost per first order is $38 or less over days 15 to 30.

## The numbers every rule uses

| Line | Working | Per first order |
|---|---|---|
| Gross profit | $62 x 55% | $34.10 |
| Break-even after $7 shipping and fees | $34.10 - $7 | **$27.10** |
| Ceiling: Meta's cost today | brief | **$38** |
| Kill line for one ad | 3 x $38 | **$114** |

Every rule uses the $38 ceiling. Below $27.10, the first order pays for itself.

## 1. Account and tracking set-up (Monday to Friday, no spend)

**Launch gate (Friday):** all steps done, ads approved by TikTok, owner signs off the settings. Day 1 is the next Monday.

## 2. Campaign structure

**Settings for both campaigns:** Sales objective, website, optimize to CompletePayment. Lowest-cost bid. 7-day click, 1-day view attribution. US, age 18+, otherwise broad, existing customers excluded. TikTok placement only. Ad names: product_angle_format_opening_creator_date.

- **TT-Test, day 1:** a creator Spark ad group at $200 a day, plus 5 concept ad groups at $100 a day, 3 openings each.
- **TT-Scale, day 11:** one broad ad group. Winners move in as the same post, so likes and comments carry over.

**Why manual:** a new pixel has no purchase history, and each concept needs its own read. Runner-up: Smart+, TikTok's automated campaign, tested in month 2 once purchases are steady.

## 3. Creative: what to make and when

Every ad: 9:16, under 25 seconds, brand visible in the first 2 seconds, captions on, sound from TikTok's commercial music library. Each concept keeps one body and tests 3 openings, each changing one thing: spoken line, on-screen text or first shot.

**Wave 1, ready Friday:**

| Concept | Format | Who films | First 2 seconds |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Creator posts | Spark Ads | Your creators | Run the 3 to 5 strongest as posted |
| B. One bought, one given | Talking head | Staff | "Every item you buy, another one gets donated." |
| C. Drawer swap | Hands-only demo | Staff | Old socks go in the bin; a new pair comes out of the box |
| D. Gift moment | Unboxing | Staff | A pair handed over as a gift |
| E. Comment reply | Reply video | Staff | A real question from your organic comments; skip if none fits |
| F. Fit and color | Slideshow, no voice | Designer | Product close-up with one line of text |

**Wave 2, ready day 9:** 4 new concepts x 3 openings, briefed from where wave 1 losers lost viewers.
**Wave 3, ready day 19:** 4 new openings on each of the top 3 winners.

Post B to F on the Bombas account first so they run as Spark Ads.

**Rules:** claims only from the bombas.com product page. No invented reviews. Paid or gifted creators say "ad" aloud and on screen. Tick TikTok's AI-content box for any AI-made person, voice or scene.

## 4. Budget phasing

| Days | Daily cap | Test | Scale | Block total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 10 | $700 | $700 | $0 | $7,000 |
| 11 to 20 | $1,000 | $500 | $500 | $10,000 |
| 21 to 30 | $1,300 | $400 | $900 | $13,000 |
| **Total** | | **$16,000** | **$14,000** | **$30,000** |

- **Days 11 to 20 Test money:** 4 wave 2 groups at $100, plus $100 for wave 1 ads still waiting.
- **Day 11 step:** only if at least one ad is a "keep". If not, stay at $700 and run wave 2.
- **Day 21 step:** only if TT-Scale's 7-day cost per first order is $38 or less. If not, hold at $1,000; the unspent $3,000 goes back to Meta.
- TT-Scale needs about 25 purchases a week to leave learning: about $136 a day at $38.
- Signing this plan approves the day 11 and day 21 steps. Inside a block, Scale raises are paid for by cutting Test losers; the daily cap never moves.

## 5. Kill and scale rules

**Before any call:** at least $50 spend or 1,000 impressions per ad. Read rolling 3-day and 7-day numbers. No budget or audience edits to an ad group in its first 7 days; pausing a losing ad is allowed.

**Each ad, at $114 spend:** 0 or 1 purchase, kill. 2, allow $38 more, then kill if still under 3. 3 or more, keep and move to TT-Scale.

**Each ad group, on 7-day cost per first order:**

- $38 or less, out of learning, last raise held: raise budget 20 to 30%, then wait 48 to 72 hours.
- In learning, or raised in the last 72 hours: hold.
- Over $38 on two reads in a row: cut budget by a third.
- Over $38 for 7 days after learning: pause.

**After every raise:** extra spend divided by extra purchases. If over $38, go back to the last budget, even if the average looks fine.

**Store check, every Monday:** divide Shopify first orders with the TikTok UTM over 7 days by TikTok's reported purchases. Divide TikTok's cost per purchase by this factor: that is the cost per first order every rule uses. In week 1, use TikTok's purchases.

**Limits:** outside the planned steps, the media buyer may raise up to 30% a step within the block cap; anything more needs the owner's yes.

## 6. Day 30: did TikTok earn more budget?

Read days 15 to 30 only: TikTok spend divided by Shopify first orders tagged TikTok.

| Cost per first order | Call | Month 2 |
|---|---|---|
| $38 or less | Earned it | From $1,300 a day, raise 20 to 30% a week while extra purchases cost $38 or less. Run a state holdout to prove the sales are extra. |
| $38.01 to $57, and days 23 to 30 lower than days 15 to 22 | Hold | $1,000 a day, double creative tests, read again day 45 |
| Over $57, or not lower | Cut back | Stop scaling; return the budget to Meta |

$57 is 1.5 times the ceiling. Check three things before the call:

1. TikTok's share of "How did you hear about us?" answers is close to its share of paid spend.
2. Total paid spend per first order is no worse than the prior 30 days, or the same weeks last year if Black Friday falls inside.
3. If Meta's cost improved while TikTok looked weak, weigh that before cutting.

## Assumptions

1. Meta's $38 is counted on store first orders, and repeat orders cover its $10.90 gap. UTM tags undercount TikTok, so check 1 matters.
2. The 55% margin includes the donated item's cost. If not, redo every line.
3. Creator rights cover paid TikTok use for 60 days. Without a Spark code, run the video from the Bombas account with the "ad" disclosure.
4. Set-up takes one working week; day 1 is the Monday after hand-off.
