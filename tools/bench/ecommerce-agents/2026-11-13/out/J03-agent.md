# Eight Meta ad concepts for the Mokobara cabin suitcase

**The main bet:** your ads look the same because they are one idea: the product on a colour background. Meta groups near-identical ads and shows them to the same people, so more of that look buys little new reach. Each concept below changes the angle, the buyer moment or the format. All use only the approved specs and the ₹6,999 price.

**Make first:** concepts 2, 3 and 1 (reasons at the end).

## The concepts

### 1. Desk to departure
- **For:** metro professionals who fly out for weekends. Cold.
- **Hook (0 to 3 s):** a laptop shuts on an office desk. On screen: "Logged off. Flight at 8."
- **Format:** Reels 9:16, 15 seconds, plus a 4:5 Feed cut.
- **Visual:** four shots, one journey: desk, suitcase rolled out, cab, gliding through the terminal to the gate. Logo and price in the last two seconds.
- **Primary text (102 characters):** Logged off at 6. Flight at 8. One cabin bag, straight from your desk. Mokobara cabin suitcase, ₹6,999.
- **Headline (26):** Weekends start at the desk
- **Tests:** does a person, place and moment beat the flat product shot with cold buyers?

### 2. One finger
- **For:** people already comparing cabin suitcases. Solution aware.
- **Hook (0 to 3 s):** first frame is one finger on the handle. On screen: "One finger. That's the push."
- **Format:** Reels 9:16 and Feed 4:5, 8 to 10 seconds, one unbroken take, real speed.
- **Visual:** one finger pushes the case down a tiled corridor, turns it through a narrow gap, then spins it a full circle.
- **Primary text (122):** One finger is enough. 360-degree spinner wheels, polycarbonate shell, TSA-style lock. The Mokobara cabin suitcase, ₹6,999.
- **Headline (22):** Pushes with one finger
- **Tests:** does a live demo of one spec beat a still of the whole product?

### 3. Skip the belt
- **For:** work flyers on short trips who know the baggage-belt wait. Problem aware.
- **Hook:** the on-image line "Land. Walk straight out."
- **Format:** Feed static 4:5, with a 9:16 version for Stories and Reels.
- **Visual:** split frame. Left, in grey, travellers watching an empty baggage belt. Right, in your brand colour, one traveller walking past with the suitcase.
- **Primary text (112):** No baggage belt. No waiting. The Mokobara cabin suitcase fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits. ₹6,999.
- **Headline (21):** Skip the baggage belt
- **Tests:** in today's static format, does a real pain beat the product-only look? Only the idea changes.

### 4. Four checks
- **For:** shoppers comparing cabin bags. Solution aware.
- **Hook:** card 1, "Buying a cabin bag? Check these four."
- **Format:** Feed carousel, 6 cards, 4:5.
- **Visual:** card 1 is your current colour-background shot. Cards 2 to 5 each pair one close-up with one answer: "Fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits." "360-degree spinner wheels." "Polycarbonate hard shell." "Built-in TSA-style lock." Card 6: "₹6,999. Warranty and returns on the product page."
- **Primary text (95):** Four checks before you buy a cabin bag, answered in one swipe. Mokobara cabin suitcase, ₹6,999.
- **Headline (25):** Four checks, all answered
- **Tests:** do spec answers move people who are already shopping?

### 5. Out of sight
- **For:** business travellers who leave bags in hotel luggage rooms or cloakrooms. Problem aware.
- **Hook:** on-image line "Hotel luggage room. Cab boot. Cloakroom."
- **Format:** Feed static 4:5; panels stack for 9:16.
- **Visual:** three small photos of the case waiting in each place, one large close-up of the lock.
- **Primary text (110):** Hotel luggage rooms. Cloakrooms. Cab boots. The Mokobara cabin suitcase has a built-in TSA-style lock. ₹6,999.
- **Headline (24):** Locked while you're away
- **Tests:** does security pull, or do fit and wheels carry the category?

### 6. Two nights, one bag
- **For:** professionals who over-pack for short work trips. Problem aware.
- **Hook (0 to 3 s):** a creator holds up a pile of clothes. "Two nights in Mumbai. Will this fit?"
- **Format:** phone-shot 9:16, 20 to 30 seconds, by a team member or a paid creator with the partnership label on screen. Never presented as a customer.
- **Visual:** packs two shirts, a blazer, shoes and a toiletry bag in one take. Zips, locks, rolls away.
- **Primary text (116):** Two nights, two meetings, one cabin bag. Watch what fits in the Mokobara cabin suitcase, packed in one take. ₹6,999.
- **Headline (18):** Packed in one take
- **Tests:** does real use, filmed by a person, beat brand-made ads?

### 7. Hear it
- **For:** site visitors and people who watched earlier ads. Product aware.
- **Hook (0 to 3 s):** extreme close-up, the lock clicks shut. Caption: "Lock: click."
- **Format:** Reels 9:16, 12 seconds, close-ups only, original sound, no music.
- **Visual:** three captioned sounds: lock click, a knuckle tapping the shell, wheels rolling on stone.
- **Primary text (115):** Click. Tap. Glide. TSA-style lock, polycarbonate shell, 360-degree spinner wheels. Mokobara cabin suitcase, ₹6,999.
- **Headline (22):** Hear it before you buy
- **Tests:** does a sound-led ad hold Reels viewers longer than text-led ones?

### 8. Price first
- **For:** site visitors who did not buy. Most aware.
- **Hook:** "₹6,999" as the largest thing in the frame.
- **Format:** Feed static 4:5 and 9:16, in your current colour-background style.
- **Visual:** today's product shot, the price set large, three spec lines under it.
- **Primary text (101):** ₹6,999 for a hard-shell cabin suitcase that fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits. Mokobara.
- **Headline (22):** Cabin suitcase, ₹6,999
- **Tests:** does price up front lift purchases from warm visitors? The look stays; only the message changes.

## Make these three first

1. **One finger (2).** Cheapest to make: a 10-second phone take this week. It proves your most visible spec, claims nothing extra and works sound-off.
2. **Skip the belt (3).** Your designer can build it from one new photo. Same format as today's ads, so it shows cleanly whether the idea or the look is the problem.
3. **Desk to departure (1).** The cold concept. It tests whether a story reaches new buyers that product shots miss. A half-day shoot also gives stills for later.

Runner-up: Two nights, one bag; creators take longer to brief and ship to.

**Extra hooks for the first three:**
- One finger: "Watch it turn on the spot." / "No dragging. No tilting."
- Skip the belt: "Cabin bag only. No belt." / "Your bag flies with you."
- Desk to departure: "Friday, 6:02 pm." / "Straight from the office."

## How to run the test

- Each concept gets its own ad set with three hook versions. Keep your best current ad live as the control.
- Spend at least 3 times your target cost per purchase on each concept before you judge it, 5 times for a firm call.
- At 3 times: three or more purchases, keep; two, give it one more target cost; none or one, cut.
- Read on 7-day click, at ad set level, over at least 7 days. High clicks without purchases are not a win; check store orders before calling a winner.

## Assumptions

- No reviews or results were supplied, so proof comes from showing the product, not quoting customers.
- Cabin size and weight were not given, so no ad names a size, weight or airline, and copy keeps the word "most".
- Warranty and returns are pointed to, not restated.
- Target cost per purchase was not given, so the test rule uses multiples of it.
