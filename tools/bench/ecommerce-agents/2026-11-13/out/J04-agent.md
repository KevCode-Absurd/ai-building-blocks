# Olipop 12-can variety pack: three creator video ads

Three scripts for Meta Reels and TikTok at 9:16, one creator each. Each has its own buyer moment and proof: a daily habit, a blind taste test, a ranking of the pack.

**Assumptions I made**
- No price or offer was given, so no ad states one. Every ad links to the variety pack page on drinkolipop.com.
- Creators name only the flavors printed on the cans they receive.
- Each creator drinks the pack for two weeks before filming. "Your words" lines must be what they really found.
- Approved claims are used word for word, in US spelling ("flavors").

## Rules for all three ads

- **Claims:** only "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavors". Never gut health, digestion, "healthy", weight, energy or sugar numbers.
- **No "you" health questions.** Meta rejects lines implying the viewer's health.
- **Disclosure:** spoken and shown on screen from second 3 to 6, in the first seconds of every ad: "Ad: paid partnership with Olipop". Run as partnership ads on Meta; on TikTok, use the paid partnership setting and an ad authorization code.
- **Text placement:** keep text out of the top 14%, bottom 35% and right 6%. Check TikTok's safe-zone overlay.
- **No other brand logos.** "Classic soda" is a plain glass, or a can turned label-away.
- **End card (last 3s):** pack on counter, "Olipop 12-can variety pack at drinkolipop.com". Button: Shop now.

## Ad 2: The blind sip (35s)

**Buyer moment:** a friend who doubts less-sugar soda. **Stage:** solution aware. **Doubt:** "less-sugar soda tastes flat". **Creator:** films a friend who really holds that view.

| Time | Shot | Spoken | On-screen text |

| 3-6 | Same | "Paid ad with Olipop. My friend doesn't know which is which." | Ad: paid partnership with Olipop |
| 6-17 | Close on each sip. A: classic soda. B: closest Olipop flavor | Creator: "Which one's the less-sugar one?" Friend: unscripted. | A or B? |
| 17-25 | Creator turns the Olipop can to camera. Friend reacts | Unscripted. Keep the real reaction. | B = Olipop |

The friend's verdict is never scripted or reshot. No text claims people "can't tell the difference".

**Hooks to test**
1. Main, story, solution aware: "Blind test. My friend thinks less-sugar soda tastes flat."
2. Question, unaware: "Two sodas. One has less sugar. Guess which."
3. Bold, product aware: "Giving my friend Olipop without saying so."

**How to test:** run each ad's three hooks on the same body. Judge on 3-second hold, then purchases.
