# Olipop 12-Can Variety Pack: Three UGC Video Ad Scripts

## Assumptions

- Each ad runs 35 to 40 seconds, vertical, for Meta Reels and TikTok.
- The brief does not list the flavours in the pack. Creators name only the flavours printed on the cans they get. [Flavour] marks those spots.
- No price, discount or shipping offer was approved, so none is used.
- The three approved claims apply to every can in the pack.
- Reactions must be real. If a creator dislikes a flavour, they swap it for another. We never script fake praise.

## Rules for all three ads

**Approved claims (use these words only):** "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours".

**Never say or show:** gut health, digestion, bloating, detox, "healthy", "good for you", weight loss, sugar or calorie numbers, or any claim that it fixes or treats anything. No other soda brand's logo on screen.

**Disclosure:** Creators turn on the "Paid partnership" label (Instagram and Facebook) and the branded content toggle (TikTok) before posting, and tag Olipop. The brand then runs the posts as partnership ads or Spark Ads. As a backup, the caption starts with "#ad" and the first 3 seconds show "Paid partnership with Olipop" at the top of the frame.

**Text placement:** Keep on-screen text in the middle of the frame. The bottom fifth and right edge are covered by platform buttons.

## Script 1: "The Soda Swap" (habit angle)

**Idea:** You keep your daily soda moment. You just change the can.

**Opening hook:** "I'm not quitting soda. I'm just changing which one I grab."
**Alt hook A:** "If you drink a soda every day, try this before you quit."
**Alt hook B:** "My afternoon soda, with less sugar than classic soda."

| Time | Shot | Spoken line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Close-up: hand reaches into fridge, pauses, grabs an Olipop can. Other drinks unbranded or out of frame. | Hook line | "Not quitting soda. Swapping it." |
| 3-8s | Crack the can near the mic. Pour over ice. | (none, let the fizz play) | "Same fizz. Same moment." |
| 8-16s | To camera, turning the can to show the label. | "It's Olipop. Less sugar than classic soda, and it contains plant fiber and prebiotics." | "Less sugar than classic soda" / "Plant fiber + prebiotics" |
| 16-24s | First sip, honest reaction, one take. | "It's made with real fruit juice and botanical flavours. This [Flavour] still feels like my soda break." | "Real fruit juice + botanical flavours" |
| 24-32s | Variety pack on the counter. Pull out three different cans. | "I got the 12-can variety pack, so I'm not stuck with one flavour all week." | "12 cans. Mixed flavours." |
| 32-38s | Holding the can, small smile. | "Link's right here if you want to try the swap." | "Shop the variety pack" |

## Script 2: "The Skeptic's First Sip" (doubt angle)

**Idea:** A regular soda drinker expects a "better" soda to taste wrong, then tries it on camera.

**Opening hook:** "Soda with fiber in it? Okay, I'll be honest with you."
**Alt hook A:** "Rating this soda like someone who drinks the regular stuff every day."
**Alt hook B:** "Less sugar than classic soda usually means worse taste. Let's check."

| Time | Shot | Spoken line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Creator holds the can at arm's length, side-eye to camera. | Hook line | "Soda... with fiber?" |
| 3-10s | Close-up of the label, creator reads it. | "Contains plant fiber and prebiotics. Less sugar than classic soda. That's usually a bad sign for taste." | "Plant fiber + prebiotics" / "Less sugar than classic soda" |
| 10-14s | Pour into a glass. Fizz close-up. | (none) | "First sip. No edits." |
| 14-22s | Sip and react in one unbroken take. | Unscripted. Say what you honestly taste, e.g. "Oh. That actually tastes like [Flavour]." | "Verdict:" + creator's own word |
| 22-30s | Open a second can from the variety pack. | "They use real fruit juice and botanical flavours. I think that's why it still tastes like soda." | "Real fruit juice + botanical flavours" |
| 30-38s | To camera, pack in frame. | "If you drink regular soda and want less sugar, the variety pack is the easy way to find your flavour." | "Try the 12-can variety pack" |

## Script 3: "Ranking the Variety Pack" (pack and choice angle)

**Idea:** A fast taste ranking that shows off the whole pack and gives viewers a reason to comment.

**Opening hook:** "I ranked every flavour in the Olipop variety pack so you don't have to."
**Alt hook A:** "A 12-pack for people who still want their soda."
**Alt hook B:** "Which Olipop flavour is number one? Let's settle it."

| Time | Shot | Spoken line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Overhead: 12 cans lined up on a table. | Hook line | "Ranking every flavour" |
| 3-7s | Open the box, group cans by flavour. Quick cuts. | "Twelve cans, a few flavours. Let's go." | "12-can variety pack" |
| 7-25s | Quick cuts per flavour: sip, one honest line, place the can on a paper ranking board. | "[Flavour]: tastes like [honest note]. That's a [rank]." Repeat per flavour. | Flavour name + rank number |
| 25-32s | Holding the top-ranked can. | "Every one has less sugar than classic soda, real fruit juice and botanical flavours, and plant fiber and prebiotics." | "Less sugar than classic soda" / "Real fruit juice + botanicals" / "Plant fiber + prebiotics" |
| 32-38s | To camera, ranking board behind. | "Get the variety pack and tell me your number one in the comments." | "Shop the 12-can variety pack" |

## Brief to Send to Each Creator

**Goal:** Sell the 12-can variety pack online to adults who drink regular soda and want less sugar without giving up their soda.
**Your script:** [Script 1, 2 or 3 attached]. Spoken lines are a guide. Say them in your own words, but keep the three product claims word for word.

**You may say:** "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours".
**Do not say:** anything about gut health, digestion, bloating, detox, weight loss, being "healthy", sugar or calorie numbers, or prices and deals.

**Be honest:** Only praise flavours you actually like. If you don't like one, skip it and use another can.

**Disclosure:** Turn on the Paid partnership label (Instagram and Facebook) or the branded content toggle (TikTok), tag Olipop, and start your caption with "#ad".

**Filming:**
- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, natural daylight, phone is fine.
- Record clean audio with no music. We add licensed music later.
- Keep other drink brands out of frame.
- Hold the can so the label faces the lens on every claim line.
- Film the opening hook three times: the main hook and both alternates, all with the same body.

**Send us:** Raw clips, the full edit with captions, and a version with no on-screen text.

## How to Test the Hooks

Cut each script three ways: one per hook, with the same body after second 3. That gives nine ads. Launch each script's three versions together at equal budget. Judge hooks first on 3-second view rate and thumb-stop rate. Then judge on cost per purchase once each version has enough spend to compare.
