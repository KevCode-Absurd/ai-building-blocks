# Meta Launch Copy Set: The Whole Truth Protein Bars (Box of 6)

## Assumptions

- **Display limits.** Primary text 125 characters or fewer, so it shows in Feed without "See more". Headline 27 or fewer. Description 25 or fewer. Spaces and ₹ count.
- **Claims.** Only the approved ones. One worked number: ₹540 / 6 bars = ₹90 a bar.
- **Button:** Shop Now, linking to the box of 6 product page.
- **Visual:** one image showing the pack front, used on all 4 launch ads, so only the copy changes.

## First 4 ads to launch

Round 1 asks one question: **which reason to buy works best?** Each ad leads with a different angle, backed by a matching headline and description.

| A | P1 Transparency | H1 Ingredients on the front | D1 Sweetened with dates | Control. Does the brand's "nothing to hide" signature sell by itself? |

### How to run it

- **Setup.** Use Meta's A/B test tool, or 4 ad sets with equal budgets and the same audience. In one shared ad set, Meta pushes spend to one ad early and the test is unfair.
- **Audience.** Ages 22 to 40, major Indian cities, same for all 4.
- **What to read.** Cost per purchase first, link click-through rate second.
- **When to decide.** At least 7 days, and ideally 30 or more purchases per ad. If budget makes that slow, judge on add-to-cart rate instead.
- **Round 2.** If the winner was close, test it against P4 (office) and P6 (taste). Then test H5 and H6 against its headline, changing nothing else.

## Compliance checks before launch

- "12 g protein" always says "per bar" or "each", never per box.
- "No added sugar" is never shortened to "sugar-free" or "zero sugar".
- No line pairs "you" with a body or health state. Meta rejects that.
- No competitor is named or shown. P2 and P4 speak about hidden sugar in general.
- Never write "healthy" as our claim, or "guilt-free", weight loss or energy.
