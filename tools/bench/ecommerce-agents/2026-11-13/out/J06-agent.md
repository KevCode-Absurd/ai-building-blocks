# Hide & Seek wallet: product page copy

**The approach:** lead with what buyers praise most ("much thinner than my old wallet"). Then say plainly, wherever people decide, that it holds cards first and only a few flat notes. The cash limit is framed as the reason it stays slim, so it reads as a feature, not a warning.

## Bullets (above the fold)

- **Holds 5 to 12 cards.** Quick-access slots keep the cards you use every day within easy reach.
- **A hidden pocket for flat notes and spare cards.** Room for a few backup notes, out of sight. Reviewers call it clever.
- **A small coin pouch.** Keeps a few coins together without adding bulk.

- **3-year warranty.** On every Hide & Seek.

## Line under the add-to-cart button

Holds 5 to 12 cards and a few flat notes. 3-year warranty.

## Description

Your old wallet got thick for a reason: it tried to carry everything. Hide & Seek carries what you actually reach for.

Your everyday cards sit in quick-access slots. Spare cards and a few flat notes hide in a pocket you won't see until you need it. A small pouch keeps loose coins in one place.

It's slim on purpose. If you mostly pay by card and keep some cash for backup, it will feel like your pocket got lighter. If you carry a thick stack of notes every day, a wallet with a full-length note section will suit you better.

The leather starts out snug and takes about a week to break in. From there, reviewers say it ages nicely.

## Why this should cut returns without hurting sales

- **The promise leads.** Slim is the top review theme, so it opens the subtitle and first bullet. The cash limit never sits above it.
- **The limit is a choice, not a flaw.** "Built for cards" and "slim on purpose" tell card-first buyers this is for them, and tell cash-heavy buyers to look elsewhere before they buy, not after.
- **Every place a buyer decides repeats it:** the subtitle, bullet 3, the line under the button, "What fits" and the first FAQ.
- **The other gripes are named early too.** The break-in week and the small coin pouch arrive as expected, not as surprises.

**How to read the result:** compare the share of returns tagged "expected more cash" for 60 days before and after launch, alongside the add-to-cart rate. Fewer of those returns with a steady add-to-cart rate means it worked.

## Assumptions I made

- The hidden pocket is the only place for notes. There is no full-length note section.
- No star rating, reviewer names, dimensions, colours, shipping or returns terms were given, so none appear in the copy.
- Review lines restate the review themes you shared; none quote a named customer.
- The voice is plain and friendly; check it against your live pages.

## To confirm before publishing

1. Count how many notes the hidden pocket holds before it bulges, and replace "a few" with that number everywhere it appears.
2. Add dimensions, weight and colours to the specs table.
3. Show the star rating and review count from your review app beside the title.
4. Pick three real reviews with first names to sit beside the buy box: one on thinness, one on the hidden pocket, one on the break-in.
5. If you offer free returns or free shipping, add it to the line under the button.
