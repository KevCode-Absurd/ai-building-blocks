# Blue Tokai win-back programme

## Summary: start at day 68, hold offers to message 3, prove it with a holdout

- **Lapse line: 68 days with no order**, twice your 34-day median gap between first and second order. 49,000 of 60,000 customers (82%) have not ordered in 45 days.
- **Order:** an always-on day-68 flow first, then a one-off sweep of older groups, newest first.
- **Offers:** message 3 only: a gift for full-price buyers, 10% for the rest. Nothing above 15%, so no founder sign-off.
- **Proof:** a random 10% of each group gets nothing; lift is measured against them.
- **Worth:** working figure ₹7.9 lakh gross profit in year one (range ₹2.8 to ₹13.0 lakh), about 2,000 extra orders. It pays for itself at about 1 extra order per 100 contacted in the newest group.

**Runner-up I rejected:** one 15% code to all 49,000. It pays ₹173 to everyone who would have returned anyway and teaches the list to wait for codes.

Recent lapsers come first: earlier win-back converts best. Add the 181-365 group in slices of about 10% of your normal campaign volume.

The 0-45 band is not in win-back. A reorder reminder before the 34-day gap is the main fix for the repeat-rate fall from 34% to 28%; build it next.

**Inside each group, split by the order export:**

- **A. Valuable repeat:** 2 or more orders, top third by gross profit.
- **B. Repeat:** 2 or more orders, the rest.
- **C. One-time, full price.**
- **D. One-time, used a discount code.**
- **E. Fix first:** returned, refunded or complained. No marketing, only a note from support. If over 20% of a group is E, fix that issue before any offer goes out.
- **Cancelled subscribers:** email 2 offers a one-tap restart on current terms.

**This week:** your CRM executive flags A to E and draws the holdout by 20 Nov; your support lead clears group E. Before the 181-365 sends, check engaged campaigns still open at 30% or more.

## 2. Offer logic: every offer keeps at least two-thirds of the profit

Gross profit per order: ₹1,150 x 58% = ₹667, minus ₹110 shipping and fees = **₹557**.

| Offer (message 3 only) | Cost per redeemed order | Gross profit left | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|---|
| None | ₹0 | ₹557 | Everyone in messages 1 and 2; over-365 group throughout |
| Gift, up to ₹100 at product cost | up to ₹100 | ₹457 | A and C: they paid full price |
| 10% off coffee | ₹115 | ₹442 | B and D |
| 15% off coffee (test arm) | ₹173 | ₹385 | Half of B and D, 181-365 group only |

Codes end 7 days after message 3 at 11:59 pm IST, enforced in the store. No resets.

## 3. Sequence and timing per channel

Day 0 is day 68 (flow) or the send date (sweeps).

| Day | Channel | Job | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Email 1 | Their last coffee, one-tap reorder, what is new | None |
| 7 | Email 2, plus WhatsApp for opt-ins (10 am to 8 pm IST) | Brew help, a coffee pick, reply to a person; subscription for A and B | None |
| 14 | Email 3 | Group offer with end date, plus "email me less" | Group rule |
| 22 | Email 4, non-responders only | Stay or go; no click in 7 days means suppress from marketing email | None |

**Over-365 group:** email on day 0 and day 10 (last notice); suppress on day 17 if no click. No offer, no WhatsApp: blocks lower your number's quality rating. Suppressed people still get order emails and support.

**Exit at once** on any order, new support ticket or preference click. Opens do not count as engagement, as privacy features inflate them.

## 5. How to measure true lift

- **Holdout:** before the first send, pick 10% of each reachable group at random. They get normal campaigns, no win-back.
- **Measure:** share who order within 60 days of day 0, and gross profit per customer after offer costs. Incremental gross profit = (per contacted customer minus per held-out customer) x customers contacted. Ignore attributed revenue: it credits orders that would have come anyway.
- **Precision:** the 95% range is about ±1.4 points (91-180) and ±1.0 (181-365). The day-68 flow reaches 1,000 held-out customers around 20 Jan 2027; read it in late March.
- **Decision rule, set now:** whole range above zero, keep and scale. Range crosses zero, run another month. Whole range below zero, drop the offer and keep the no-offer messages.
- **Offer test:** in the 181-365 group, 15% beats 10% only if it earns more gross profit per contacted customer, not just more orders.
- **Cafes:** some "lapsed" online buyers may now buy in your cafes. The holdout cancels this out.

## Assumptions and to confirm

- ₹1,150 is net of tax and discounts; the ₹110 is your cost.
- Most WhatsApp opt-ins also opted in to email, so 62% are reachable.
- WhatsApp costs about ₹1 a message all in; confirm your provider's rate. Extra emails cost nothing.
- 40% of returning orders in contacted groups use the message-3 offer.
- Published win-back rates (12 to 20%) count attributed orders, so I planned lower.
