# Plum Goodness: three bundles and a new free shipping line

## The answer

- **Launch three bundles:** Glow Duo (₹849), Pick Any 2 (₹699) and Head-to-Toe Kit (₹1,369, lip balm free). Each earns ₹52 to ₹260 more per order than the single product it replaces, after shipping.
- **Move free shipping from above ₹499 to ₹699 and above,** after the bundles have run four weeks. In my cautious case this adds about ₹10,850 per 1,000 orders, about 3% on today's ₹349 per order. It stops paying if about one in five ₹500 to ₹698 buyers walk away rather than pay shipping.
- **Runner-up:** keep ₹499 and launch the bundles only. Safer, but it leaves up to ₹18,900 per 1,000 orders of shipping cost on the table.

## Assumptions I made

1. Below the free line, shoppers pay a ₹70 fee covering your cost. A lower fee shrinks the threshold saving in proportion.
2. The margins apply to the selling prices listed. Lip balm is counted as makeup (55%), the cautious choice.
3. A typical order earns 63% gross margin, since skin care leads your top sellers. So today's ₹620 order earns ₹391 gross profit, less ₹42 average shipping (60% of orders ship free × ₹70), which leaves ₹349.
4. Orders spread evenly inside each band. COD refusal losses are not in the sums (no rate given); the test tracks them.

## The three bundles

| Bundle | Contents | Price (full price) | Shopper saves | Gross profit after ₹70 shipping | Versus the single it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glow Duo | Vitamin C serum + green tea face wash | ₹849 (₹944) | ₹95 | ₹477 | +₹140 vs serum alone (₹337) |
| Pick Any 2 | Any two different: face wash, shampoo, body lotion | ₹699 (₹744 to ₹824) | ₹45 to ₹125 | ₹307 to ₹367 | +₹52 to +₹132 vs one item alone (₹235 to ₹255) |
| Head-to-Toe Kit | Serum + face wash + body lotion, lip balm free | ₹1,369 (₹1,568 with balm) | ₹199 (the balm) | ₹737 | +₹260 vs Glow Duo |

**Glow Duo turns the serum buyer into a routine buyer.** A serum alone (₹599) lands in your biggest band, ₹500 to ₹699, with 27% of orders. On the serum page, say "Add the face wash for ₹250 (usually ₹345)". Pre-select it there and name it in ads. It pays while at least 4 in 10 Duo buyers would otherwise have bought the serum alone: each adds ₹140, while each who would have paid full price for both costs ₹95.

**Pick Any 2 lifts the one-item buyer over the new line.** 40% of orders are under ₹500, mostly one ₹345 to ₹425 product. At exactly ₹699, any pair ships free. The weakest pair, shampoo and lotion, still earns ₹52 more than lotion alone. Show the rupee saving for the pair chosen, not an "up to" headline. Two of the same item are excluded: two face washes cost ₹690, less than the bundle.

**Head-to-Toe Kit moves the ₹700 to ₹999 buyer (19%) toward ₹1,000 and suits gift buyers.** The three products stay at normal prices; the balm costs you ₹90, not ₹199. Keep it this way: India's ad code allows "free" only when nothing but delivery is charged and no other price is raised.

**Placement.** A bundle picker under Add to cart on each hero product page. A cart bar showing the gap to free shipping, with a one-click lip balm (₹199) for carts of ₹500 to ₹698. Serum plus balm (₹798) earns ₹447 after shipping, against ₹337 for the serum alone.

## Free shipping: move the line to ₹699

Set the line from where orders bunch, not the average. Your densest band is ₹400 to ₹499, right under today's line; next is ₹500 to ₹699, where a lone serum lands. A ₹699 line sits ₹100 above the serum, so one ₹199 add-on gets those buyers over it.

**Per 1,000 orders:**

| Free shipping line | Orders shipped free | Shipping you pay | Saving if nobody changes | Orders newly charged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Above ₹499 (today) | 600 | ₹42,000 | ₹0 | 0 |
| ₹699 and above | 330 | ₹23,100 | ₹18,900 | 270 |
| ₹849 and above | 235 | ₹16,450 | ₹25,550 | 365 |

For the 270 newly charged orders at ₹699:

- Each one that pays the fee saves you ₹70.
- Each one that adds a ₹199 lip balm earns about ₹110 more.
- Each one that leaves costs ₹308 (₹378 gross profit on a ₹600 order, less ₹70 shipping).

**Cautious case** (70% pay, 20% add an item, 10% leave): 270 × (₹49 + ₹22 − ₹30.80) = **+₹10,854 per 1,000 orders.**
**Bad case** (70% pay, 10% add, 20% leave): 270 × (₹49 + ₹11 − ₹61.60) = **−₹432**, about break-even.

It breaks even at 21% leaving (18.5% if nobody adds an item). That is about 50 lost orders per 1,000, or 5% of all orders: the number the test watches.

**Why not ₹849.** It saves only ₹6,650 more but charges 95 more orders, each worth about ₹418 after shipping. If 16 of those 95 leave, the extra saving is gone. A serum buyer would also need ₹250 more, which only the face wash covers.

**Why test it.** ₹70 is 11.7% of a ₹600 order. In one practitioner's tests, shipping charges were harmless only under about 10% of order value.

## How to test both safely

**Ground rules.** One change at a time. Never show two prices for one product or bundle; test placement and wording only. Judge on gross profit per session after shipping, counting a COD order (40% of orders) only once delivered and paid.

**Can you split traffic?** Visitors per version = 16 × p × (1 − p) ÷ (p × change)². At 2% conversion and a 10% change, that is about 78,400 per version, or 5,600 a day for four weeks. With less traffic, run each phase as a dated switch against the four weeks before.

| Dates | Step | Who | What you read |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 to 20 Nov 2026 | Set up bundles, cart bar, and order tags for bundle, COD and delivered | Store manager | Test orders work |
| 23 Nov to 20 Dec 2026 | Phase 1: bundles live, line stays above ₹499. If your app allows, show the Glow Duo on the serum page to half of visitors | Owner | Bundle take rate, gross profit per session, Duo buyers new to the pair, COD refusals on bundles |
| 4 to 31 Jan 2027 | Phase 2: line moves to ₹699, bundles unchanged. Skipping 21 Dec to 3 Jan avoids holiday sales; add a week if you run a Republic Day sale | Owner | Gross profit per session against Phase 1; what ₹500 to ₹698 orders did |
| 1 Feb 2027 | Decide on the line | Owner | Stop rules below |

**Stop rules:**

- Go back to ₹499 at once if orders per session fall over 10% in any full Phase 2 week (twice the break-even loss).
- On 1 Feb, keep ₹699 only if gross profit per session after shipping beats Phase 1. If not, return to ₹499 and keep the bundles.
- End the Glow Duo if fewer than 4 in 10 of its buyers are new to the pair.
- If COD bundle orders are refused 5 points more often than COD single orders, make the kit's free balm prepaid only.

- Your sales mix, to replace the 63% blended margin.
