# Guide bench: jobs

Eight owner requests, typed the way a busy owner would. Each runs twice: plain Claude with only the Meta connector, and AI Blocks at full power. The same simulated owner (`OWNER-PROFILE.md`) answers both.

| Id | Family | The owner's first message |
|---|---|---|
| J1 | Creatives | Make me 10 new ad creatives for Gehna. |
| J2 | Account structure | Set up our Meta account structure for the next 3 months. We want more sales without wasting money. |
| J3 | Media-buying audit | Can you audit my Meta ads? I feel like we're wasting a lot of money. |
| J4 | CRO | Do a CRO audit of my website gehnaindia.com and tell me what to change. |
| J5 | Email and retention | I need a win-back email series for customers who haven't bought in a while. |
| J6 | Video scripts | Write video ad scripts for our daily wear chains. |
| J7 | AEO and SEO | How do we get Gehna to show up when people ask ChatGPT or Google for gold jewellery? |
| J8 | Big bet | What's one big bet that could double our revenue next year? |

Each first message is sent with one line of context that both arms get: "I'm from Gehna (gehnaindia.com). Our Meta ad account is 577226564072364."

## Rules for both arms

- Up to 4 turns: the arm may stop to ask the owner questions up to 3 times, then must deliver.
- Read only: no live change to any ad account, no sending, no publishing, no spending.
- Each arm writes only inside its own folder. It never opens the other arm's folder or this bench folder.
