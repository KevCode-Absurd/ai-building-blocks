# Job J01: Meta budget decisions for the next 14 days

**Business:** Bombay Shaving Company (bombayshavingcompany.com), an Indian grooming brand selling razors, shaving and skin care for men and women online across India.

**Role wanted:** Meta media buyer.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: confident, witty, straight-talking. We say "Shave better." We never say "the ultimate grooming experience."
- Reading level: grade 7. Light Hinglish is fine in ads when it sounds natural.
- Avoid: "painless", "zero cuts", "dermatologist approved", anything about skin colour or fairness, and shaming body hair.
- Show the product in use within 2 seconds, one benefit per ad, real people over models.
- We stand for grooming that is simple, honest and made for Indian skin and weather.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Rohan, 27, first job in Pune. Moved off his dad's old razor and wants a shave without burn. What stops him: he does not know which kit to buy, and cheap razors burned him before. In his words: "No razor burn after a week." "Didn't know a shave could feel this smooth." "Ordered COD, wanted to see it first."
- Ananya, 29, Bengaluru. Uses a body razor between salon visits and wants quick, no nicks, easy to travel with. What stops her: pink razors that feel flimsy and cost more. In her words: "Saves me so many salon trips." "No bumps on my legs." "Finally not a flimsy pink razor."
