# J01 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Women 25 to 34 is the real Women Body Razor audience.** The ad set averages ₹795 per purchase, but 25 to 34 costs ₹414 (₹24,000 / 58) while 18 to 24 costs ₹1,750 and 45 and over ₹1,500. A great answer does not cut the ad set on its average. It moves the money to women 25 to 34 (own ad set or age-led creative in Ananya's words), stops the 18 to 24 spend, and says what that does to cost per purchase.
2. **The best ad in the account was paused and never brought back.** "Dad's razor vs mine" ran at ₹250 per purchase (₹1,40,000 / 560), better than anything live. ASC cost per purchase climbed from ₹262 to ₹281 to ₹318 to ₹348 in the weeks after it was paused. A great answer relaunches it first (back into ASC as a fresh ad, plus 2 or 3 new cuts of the same hook) and calls it the cheapest fix in the account.
3. **The Razor Starter Kit is a COD trap.** It looks fine on Meta (₹463 per purchase, ROAS 1.83, low frequency) but 74% of its orders are COD and 36% of those are refused. Its contribution per purchase is about ₹271, so it loses roughly ₹190 per Meta-counted order before any attribution haircut. A great answer cuts it or fixes it (prepaid-only or part-prepaid offer, COD confirmation, block high-RTO pincodes) and does not scale it on CTR or frequency.

## What would delight me

- One clear verdict per ad set with a rupee reason, and a 14-day split that adds up to about ₹5,60,000.
- Relaunching the paused creator video as the first move, with a test of new versions of that hook.
- Splitting women 25 to 34 out instead of killing women's razors.
- Judging every ad set on Shopify-adjusted, after-RTO numbers, not Meta ROAS.
- Daily rules with numbers (for example: cut an ad after ₹X spend with no purchase; cap retargeting frequency; scale winners by 20% steps).

## What would annoy me

- Scaling the Razor Starter Kit because CTR and frequency look healthy.
- Taking Meta's 1,425 purchases at face value.
- Generic "test more creatives" or "improve targeting" advice with no rupee amounts.
- A budget table that does not add up or blows past the monthly budget.
- Jargon with no first step for Monday.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Meta claims 1,425 purchases; Shopify shows 1,020 Meta-tagged orders, about 72%. Meta cost per purchase understates real cost by about 40% (1 / 0.716 = 1.40).
- Contribution per order at ₹899 with 45% COD, 22% RTO and 2% prepaid returns is about ₹394 before ad spend. That is the break-even cost per real order.
- The 14-day budget at ₹12,00,000 a month is about ₹5,60,000. The last 14 days spent ₹5,80,000.
- Women 25 to 34 cost ₹414 per purchase; the Razor Starter Kit earns about ₹271 per purchase after its COD and RTO.
