# Job J02: TikTok ads launch plan for the first 30 days

**Business:** Bombas (bombas.com), a US apparel brand best known for socks, which donates an item for every item purchased.

**Role wanted:** TikTok ads media buyer.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: warm, upbeat, plain-spoken. We say "Comfort you notice by lunchtime." We never say "cheap socks" or "shop guilt-free."
- Reading level: grade 6 to 7.
- Avoid: "medical-grade", "cures foot pain", "best socks in the world", and any giving numbers other than the approved line "One purchased = one donated."
- Real feet on real days beat studio shots. Show the product in the first 2 seconds. Giving is shared as a fact, never as a guilt trip.
- We stand for comfort that lasts and for helping people experiencing homelessness.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Maria, 34, an ER nurse in Ohio who works 12-hour shifts. She wants socks that stay up and cushion her feet. What stops her: paying $15 or more for one pair of socks. In her words: "My feet don't ache by hour ten." "They don't slide down in my clogs." "Pricey, but I'm never going back."
- Tasha, 39, a mom of two in Texas who buys for the whole family. She wants socks that last through the wash. What stops her: the price, when kids outgrow everything. In her words: "My kids' socks actually stay on." "I like that a pair gets donated."

**Data (test):**

The brand has never run TikTok ads. Budget for the first 30 days: $30,000. Average order value $62. Gross margin after product cost 55%; shipping and payment fees $7 per order. New customers spend $88 on average in their first 90 days, first order included. Meta is the main paid channel today at a blended cost per first order of $38. The TikTok pixel is not yet installed; the store runs on Shopify. Market: US only.
