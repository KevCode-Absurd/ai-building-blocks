# J02 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **On-feet workers are the creator style that works.** The nurse and server videos got 186,000 and 92,000 views with 9.6 to 11.4 seconds of watch time. Every other style stayed under 16,000 views and 5.1 seconds. A great answer builds the creative plan around people who stand all day (nurses, servers, retail, warehouse), runs the two licensed videos as Spark Ads from day 1, notes the rights end in March, and briefs new creators in the same niche.
2. **Lead with the problem, close with the mission.** On Meta, problem-first hooks cost $29 per first order. Product-first costs $40, offer-first $42 and mission-first $51. A great answer opens TikTok ads with the foot problem in the customer's words and uses "One purchased = one donated" as the closing reason to feel good, not the opener. It also avoids leading with a discount.
3. **The day-30 bar has to come from the money.** A first order earns only $27.10 ($62 x 55% minus $7). Over 90 days a new customer earns about $38 ($88 x 55% minus about $10 in fees). Meta's $38 is roughly break-even at 90 days. A great answer sets TikTok's target at or below about $38 per first order on the same basis, checked with Shopify data, a post-purchase survey or a holdout, not TikTok-reported ROAS alone.

## What would delight me

- A Monday-ready set-up list: pixel and Events API through the Shopify app, event checks, a post-purchase "how did you hear about us" question.
- Day-1 Spark Ads using the nurse and server videos, plus a creator brief for 6 to 10 new on-feet workers.
- Hooks written in Maria's words ("My feet don't ache by hour ten").
- A phased budget that adds to $30,000, with kill and scale rules in dollars.
- A clear day-30 decision rule tied to the 90-day break-even.

## What would annoy me

- Generic TikTok advice ("be authentic", "jump on trends") with no link to our data.
- Opening ads with the donation line or a discount.
- Studio product videos as the main creative.
- Judging day 30 on platform ROAS or first-order profit alone.
- Giving numbers or health claims we did not approve.
