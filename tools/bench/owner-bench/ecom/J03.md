# Job J03: Eight Meta ad concepts for a cabin suitcase

**Business:** Mokobara (mokobara.com), an Indian travel brand selling luggage, backpacks and travel accessories online.

**Role wanted:** paid social creative strategist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: modern, confident, understated. We say "Built for the way you travel now." We never say "cheap", "unbreakable" or "lifetime."
- Reading level: grade 7. Short lines.
- Avoid: "lightest in India", "indestructible", any claim beyond the approved specs, and competitor names.
- Design leads. Clean colour, real travel moments, the product always clearly in frame.
- We stand for thoughtful design for the modern Indian traveller.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Karan, 31, a consultant in Mumbai who flies to client sites twice a month. He wants to travel cabin-only and be out of the airport fast. What stops him: worry that the bag will fail the gate check, and ₹6,999 for a cabin bag. In his words: "I never check in anymore." "The gate guy didn't even look at it." "Out of the airport before the belt starts."
- Meera, 28, a product designer in Bengaluru who takes solo weekend trips. She wants a bag she can handle alone that looks good. What stops her: scratches, and heavy bags she cannot lift. In her words: "I lifted it into the overhead bin myself, no asking for help." "So quiet at 5 am, no rattling." "People keep asking where it's from."

**Data (test):**

Product: a cabin-size hard-shell suitcase, polycarbonate shell, 360-degree spinner wheels, TSA-style lock, fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits, weighs about 2.6 kg, priced at ₹6,999. Warranty and returns as on the site. Approved claims: these specs only. No competitor names in ads.

Current ads are clean product shots on colour backgrounds. The owner feels they all look the same and results are flat.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me eight distinct ad concepts for Meta Feed and Reels. For each: the angle, who it is for and how aware they are, the hook (first line or first three seconds), the format, what the visual shows, a draft of the primary text, and what the concept tests. Then tell me which three to make first and why."

**Length:** at most 1,500 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
