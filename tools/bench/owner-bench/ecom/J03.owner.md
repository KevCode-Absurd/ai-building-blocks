# J03 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **"Quiet wheels" is the hook nobody is using.** It is the most common review theme (290 mentions), ahead of fit and looks, and no current ad mentions sound or glide. A great answer turns it into at least one concept, for example a sound-led Reel in a quiet airport at dawn, with a hook drawn from the review line.
2. **Women travelling solo are under-served and cheaper to win.** Women 25 to 40 get 18% of spend but convert at ₹1,058 per purchase against ₹1,615 for men, with a CTR of 1.4% against 0.8%. They are 22% of buyers and chose the bag because they can lift it alone (2.6 kg is an approved spec). A great answer gives this group its own concept in Meera's words and puts it in the first three.
3. **Dark-colour scratches are a live objection.** 85 reviews mention scuffs on dark shells. A great answer avoids close-ups of scuffed black shells, leads with lighter colours, or handles it honestly, and does not claim the shell is scratch-proof.

## What would delight me

- Eight concepts that are truly different in angle and format, not one idea in eight colours.
- Hooks lifted from real review and survey lines ("Out of the airport before the belt starts", "I lifted it myself").
- A clear awareness level and a single test variable for each concept.
- A first-three pick tied to the data: quiet wheels, women solo travellers, work flyers skipping the belt.
- Primary text in our voice: short, calm, no hype.

## What would annoy me

- More product-on-colour statics with a new headline.
- Claims beyond the specs: "lightest", "unbreakable", "scratch-proof", or a named airline's exact limit as a guarantee.
- Competitor names or "better than X" lines.
- Ignoring the women 25 to 40 numbers.
- Generic travel clichés ("Adventure awaits") instead of customer language.
