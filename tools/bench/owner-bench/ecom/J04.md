# Job J04: Three UGC video ad scripts

**Business:** Olipop (drinkolipop.com), a US soda brand made with plant fiber and prebiotics, sold online and in stores.

**Role wanted:** video ad and UGC producer.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: playful, nostalgic, warm. We say "A new kind of soda." We never say "healthy soda" or "guilt-free."
- Reading level: grade 6. Creators speak in their own words; scripts are guides, not word-for-word.
- Avoid: health, cure, gut-fix or weight claims; "zero sugar"; "diet"; naming other soda brands.
- Taste first, then what's inside. Bright, fun, real kitchens and desks, never a lab or a gym.
- We stand for bringing back the joy of soda with better ingredients.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Dana, 36, a project manager in Denver who drinks two regular sodas a day. She wants the fizz and the treat with less sugar. What stops her: diet soda taste, and paying more per can. In her words: "I need my 3 pm soda." "Diet soda tastes like chemicals to me." "I'm not paying $3 for fizzy water."
- Marcus, 42, a dad in Atlanta who packs drinks for his kids' games. He wants something the kids enjoy that he feels better about. In his words: "My kids think it's real soda." "I read the label in the store, then bought a case."

**Data (test):**

The store wants three creator-style video ads, 30 to 45 seconds each, for Meta Reels and TikTok, to sell a 12-can variety pack online: $35.99, so $3.00 a can, with 3 cans each of Vintage Cola, Strawberry Vanilla, Classic Root Beer and Orange Squeeze. Buyers: adults who drink regular soda and want to cut sugar without giving up the soda moment.

Approved claims: "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours". No health or gut-health cure claims. The creators are real paid creators the store hires; ads must follow each platform's paid partnership disclosure rules.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me three ready-to-shoot scripts, each with a different angle. For each: a scene-by-scene shot list with timing, the spoken lines, on-screen text, the opening hook plus two alternate hooks to test, and a short brief I can send to the creator."

**Length:** at most 1,300 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
