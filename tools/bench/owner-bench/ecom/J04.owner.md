# J04 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **"My 3 pm soda" is the hook.** The biggest comment theme (23%) is the afternoon soda habit, and Dana says it in her own words. A great answer builds at least one script around the 3 pm slump moment at a desk, uses the line or a close variant as the opening, and keeps the soda moment rather than selling "health".
2. **Taste tests win and label reading loses.** Blind taste tests cost $26 per purchase with a 33% view rate. Reading the label costs $44 at 19%, and founder story costs $51. The approved claims tempt a label-first script. A great answer opens with taste (a blind test or a swap), saves the claims for the middle, and does not make three label-led scripts.
3. **Price is the objection to answer.** 15% of comments push back on $3 a can. A great answer handles it inside a script (for example the cost of an afternoon coffee-shop drink, or the variety pack as a way to find a favourite) without a fake discount.

## What would delight me

- Three truly different angles: the 3 pm habit, a blind taste test, and a family or swap angle.
- Hooks taken from real comments, each with two testable alternates that change one thing.
- Shot lists with second-by-second timing that land in 30 to 45 seconds.
- Paid partnership disclosure written into each creator brief (Meta Paid Partnership label, TikTok branded content toggle).
- Creator briefs that give freedom on wording but list the exact approved claims and the banned words.

## What would annoy me

- Any gut-health, digestion, weight or "healthy" claim, or "zero sugar" or "diet".
- Three versions of "creator reads the label."
- Naming or showing another soda brand.
- Scripts too long to say in 45 seconds.
- Generic hooks ("You won't believe this soda!") instead of customer language.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- The variety pack is $35.99, which is $3.00 a can, 4 flavours x 3 cans.
- Cost per purchase by opening: taste test $26, swap $32, label $44, founder $51.
- Only three approved claims; disclosure is required on both platforms because creators are paid.
