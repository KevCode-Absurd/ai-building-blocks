# J05 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **The best angle was paused for tiredness, not failure.** "Flip the pack" cost ₹210 per purchase with a 2.1% CTR, far better than protein (₹340), no added sugar (₹290) or office snack (₹380). It stopped only because frequency hit 4.2. A great answer brings the transparency angle back in fresh wording (new primary texts and headlines on the same idea) and puts it in the first launch set, rather than dropping it.
2. **Taste is the missing hook.** The top review phrase, "Doesn't taste like a protein bar" (26%), plus "dense and chewy, not chalky" (14%), never appears in any past ad. A great answer writes at least one primary text and one headline around taste, close to the customers' own words, and tests it against transparency.
3. **₹90 a bar is the objection.** 9% of reviews push back on price for daily use. A great answer frames the price per bar honestly or against a real daily alternative, without a discount it was not given and without naming competitors.

## What would delight me

- Six primary texts that are truly different: transparency, taste, no added sugar, protein, the 4 pm snack, and price or value.
- Headlines and descriptions that respect the display limits, with character counts shown.
- A first-4 launch plan where each combination tests one thing, led by transparency and taste.
- Blunt, witty lines in our voice, using Aditya's and Priya's words.

## What would annoy me

- "Guilt-free", "healthy", "superfood" or claims beyond the approved list.
- Six versions of "12 g protein, no added sugar."
- Ignoring the paused winner or the taste reviews.
- Lines over Meta's limits, or no character counts.
- Comparisons that point at a named or obvious competitor.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- ₹540 per box of 6 is ₹90 per bar.
- Meta shows about the first 125 characters of primary text before "See more"; headlines should stay within about 40 characters and descriptions within about 30 to avoid truncation on most placements.
- Approved claims: 12 g protein per bar, no added sugar, sweetened with dates, ₹540 per box, ingredients listed on the front of the pack.
