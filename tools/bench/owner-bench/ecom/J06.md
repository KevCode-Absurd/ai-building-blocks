# Job J06: Product page copy for a slim wallet

**Business:** Bellroy (bellroy.com), an Australian brand selling slim wallets, bags and accessories worldwide.

**Role wanted:** product page copywriter.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: clever, calm, precise. We say "Slim your wallet." We never say "the last wallet you'll ever need" or "indestructible."
- Reading level: grade 8.
- Avoid: "sustainable" or "eco-friendly" without a specific fact behind it, "genuine leather" as a boast, and overstating capacity.
- Show how it carries, with exact numbers. Name the trade-offs honestly; it builds trust.
- We stand for carrying better with less.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Tom, 38, an engineer in Chicago replacing a thick bifold. He wants something slim that still holds his everyday cards. What stops him: US$99 for a wallet, and doubt that his cards will fit. In his words: "My old wallet was a brick in my back pocket." "I carry six or seven cards and a little cash."
- Jen, 34, buying a gift for her partner. She wants it to fit his things and look good for years. In her words: "Will his cards actually fit?" "I want it to look good in five years."

**Data (test):**

Product: Hide & Seek wallet, a slim leather bifold. Holds 5 to 12 cards, quick-access card slots, a hidden pocket for flat notes and spare cards, a coin pouch, premium leather, 3-year warranty, US$99. Fit: the note section takes about 10 notes laid flat. 10 mm thick with 6 cards, 14 mm with 12.

Reviews, 400 reviews:

| Theme | Mentions | Typical line |
|---|---|---|
| Much thinner than the old wallet | 142 | "Much thinner than my old wallet" |
| Leather ages nicely | 88 | "Looks better after a year" |
| Cards fit and it stays flat | 61 | "I carry 8 cards and it still closes flat" |
| Takes a week to break in | 54 | "Card slots are tight for the first week" |
| Hidden pocket is clever | 40 | "Love the hidden pocket" |
| Coin pouch is small | 33 | "Coin pouch fits a few coins only" |

Returns: 7.5% of orders. Reasons:

| Reason | Share of returns |
|---|---|
| Holds less cash than I expected | 44% |
| Card slots too tight | 21% |
| Changed my mind | 15% |
| Colour not as pictured | 12% |
| Fault | 8% |

63% of "card slots too tight" returns are sent back within 7 days of delivery.

Questions asked on the product page, last 90 days: "How much cash does it hold?" 31%; "Will it fit in a front pocket?" 24%; "How many cards, really?" 22%; other 23%.

**What the owner wants:** "Write the full product page copy: product title and a one-line subtitle, the 4 to 6 bullets above the fold, a short description, a spec and 'what fits' section, an FAQ of 5 questions, the SEO title and meta description, and the line under the add-to-cart button. It should cut the 'holds less cash than expected' returns without hurting sales."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
