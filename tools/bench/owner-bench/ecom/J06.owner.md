# J06 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Cash capacity must be a number above the fold.** "Holds less cash than I expected" is 44% of returns, and "How much cash does it hold?" is the top page question (31%). A great answer states "about 10 notes, laid flat" in a bullet and in the "what fits" section, says it is built cards-first, and frames the trade-off in our calm voice without scaring buyers off.
2. **The early "too tight" returns are a break-in problem.** 21% of returns say the card slots are too tight, and 63% of those come back within 7 days, which matches the review "tight for the first week." A great answer sets the expectation on the page (a bullet, an FAQ, or the line under the button: "Snug for the first week, then it settles") so buyers wait it out.
3. **"8 cards and it still closes flat" is the proof line.** It answers Tom's and Jen's main doubt and the "How many cards, really?" question. A great answer uses this customer phrase (or the 10 mm with 6 cards, 14 mm with 12 numbers) as a headline proof, and answers the front-pocket question.

## What would delight me

- A subtitle and bullets that lead with slimness and real numbers: cards, notes, millimetres.
- An FAQ built from the actual top questions: cash, front pocket, how many cards, break-in, warranty.
- A line under the add-to-cart button that cuts returns, such as the break-in note or the 3-year warranty.
- Honest handling of the small coin pouch.
- An SEO title within about 60 characters and a meta description within about 155.

## What would annoy me

- Vague capacity ("plenty of room for cash").
- Ignoring the break-in issue or the return reasons.
- Hype like "the last wallet you'll ever need", or eco claims with no facts.
- Copy that hides trade-offs, so returns rise.
- Generic leather-wallet copy that could be any brand's.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Holds 5 to 12 cards; about 10 notes laid flat; 10 mm with 6 cards, 14 mm with 12 cards.
- 3-year warranty; US$99.
- Return rate 7.5%; cash expectation is 44% of returns, about 3.3% of all orders.
