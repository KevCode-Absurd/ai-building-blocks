# Job J07: Welcome email series for new subscribers

**Business:** Minimalist (beminimalist.co), an Indian skin care brand known for naming active ingredients and their strength on the pack, selling online and on marketplaces.

**Role wanted:** email and SMS copywriter.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: clear, factual, calm. We say "Here's what's inside, and how much." We never say "miracle", "flawless" or "glass skin in 7 days."
- Reading level: grade 7.
- Avoid: medical claims (treats, cures, heals), before-and-after promises, "fairness", "whitening" or "lightening", and selling through fear.
- Name the active and its strength. One idea per email. Always explain how to start slowly.
- We stand for honest, effective skin care at fair prices.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Sneha, 24, in her first job in Pune. She has dark spots left from old breakouts and is new to actives. She wants a simple routine. What stops her: fear of irritation, and too many choices. In her words: "I don't know which serum is for me." "My skin is sensitive, I'm scared to try acids." "Every sunscreen makes me look grey."
- Arjun, 27, in Hyderabad, with oily skin. He wants the fewest steps that work. In his words: "Just tell me what to use." "I don't want a 10-step routine."

**Data (test):**

New subscribers join from a site pop-up that promises skin care tips and 10% off the first order. About 18% buy within 14 days. The store uses Klaviyo. Average first order ₹699. Best sellers: a niacinamide serum, a sunscreen and a salicylic acid face wash. Many new buyers are unsure which active suits their skin and fear irritation. No medical claims; no before-and-after promises.

The pop-up asks one quiz question. Answers from 9,400 sign-ups, last 60 days:

| Main skin concern | Share |
|---|---|
| Dark spots and uneven tone | 44% |
| Acne and oiliness | 26% |
| Dryness | 12% |
| Dullness | 11% |
| Sensitivity | 7% |

61% also ticked "I'm new to actives."

Today there is one welcome email, sent at sign-up. Results by product, last 60 days:

| Product | Price ₹ | Clicks | Orders after click |
|---|---|---|---|
| Niacinamide 10% serum | 599 | 2,900 | 232 |
| SPF 50 sunscreen | 399 | 3,600 | 79 |
| Salicylic acid 2% face wash | 299 | 1,700 | 116 |

Questions in chat on the sunscreen page: "Will it leave a white cast?" 38%; "Is it sticky in humid weather?" 27%; "Can I wear it under makeup?" 12%; other 23%. The most liked sunscreen review: "No white cast, even on my dusky skin."

**What the owner wants:** "Write a 5-email welcome series for people who haven't bought yet. Give me the send timing and the exit rule, and for each email the subject line, preview text, full body copy, the call to action, and the job the email does. Add one WhatsApp or SMS message if it fits, and say where."

**Length:** at most 1,800 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
