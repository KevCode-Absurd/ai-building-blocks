# J07 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Dark spots dominate, so the series should lead there.** 44% of sign-ups name dark spots and uneven tone, far ahead of acne (26%). A great answer builds the core of the series around a simple daily routine for uneven tone (sunscreen every morning plus niacinamide) in cosmetic, non-medical language, and uses the quiz answer to branch or personalise (Klaviyo profile property), with a lighter path for acne and oiliness.
2. **Sunscreen gets clicked but not bought.** It draws the most clicks (3,600) but only 2.2% order after clicking, against 8.0% for niacinamide and 6.8% for the face wash. The chat questions show why: white cast (38%) and stickiness (27%). A great answer gives one email (or a block) to these two fears, uses the review line "No white cast, even on my dusky skin" as proof, and does not just push sunscreen harder.
3. **Most are beginners afraid of irritation.** 61% are new to actives. A great answer teaches a slow start (patch test, start a few times a week, one new active at a time) and suggests a starter routine of 2 or 3 products instead of the whole range.

## What would delight me

- A clear timing plan (for example day 0, 1, 3, 6, 10 or so) with an exit rule on purchase and a reminder before the 10% code's deadline.
- Each email doing one job: welcome and code, the dark-spot routine, the sunscreen fears, beginner safety, last reminder with social proof.
- Subject lines in our calm voice that name the active and the strength.
- A WhatsApp or SMS message placed where it helps (for example the code reminder), only for people who opted in.

## What would annoy me

- "Brightening", "lightening", "fairness", "clears acne", "heals" or any before-and-after promise.
- Ignoring the quiz data and writing a generic welcome series.
- Pushing sunscreen without answering white cast and stickiness.
- Long emails that teach five actives at once.
- Fake urgency the brand did not approve.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Orders after click: niacinamide 8.0% (232 / 2,900), face wash 6.8% (116 / 1,700), sunscreen 2.2% (79 / 3,600).
- 44% of sign-ups name dark spots and uneven tone; 61% are new to actives.
- The offer is 10% off the first order; average first order ₹699, so the code is worth about ₹70 on a typical order.
