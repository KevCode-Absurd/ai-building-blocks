# Job J08: Win-back programme for lapsed coffee buyers

**Business:** Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters (bluetokaicoffee.com), an Indian specialty coffee roaster selling beans, ground coffee and equipment online and in its cafes.

**Role wanted:** retention marketing lead.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: warm, curious, expert but never snobby. We say "Tell us how you brew, and we'll suggest a coffee." We never say "gourmet" or "barista-level at home."
- Reading level: grade 7.
- Avoid: health claims about coffee or caffeine, "best coffee in India", and guilt lines such as "Did we do something wrong?"
- Name the farm or the roast, give one brew tip, and use one call to action per message.
- We stand for fresh specialty coffee from Indian farms.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Aarav, 33, works from home in Bengaluru and brews with a French press. He wants his coffee to taste the same every time. What stops him: running out and grabbing whatever is nearby. In his words: "I ran out, bought supermarket coffee, and just never came back." "I don't want to choose from 40 coffees again."
- Nisha, 29, in Delhi, an ex-subscriber. In her words: "My subscription came five days late twice, so I cancelled." "I'd come back if I could pick the delivery date."

**Data (test):**

Last 24 months of online orders: 60,000 customers. 12-month repeat rate fell from 34% to 28%. Median days between first and second order: 34. Average order ₹1,150; gross margin after product cost 58%; shipping and fees ₹110 per order. 62% have opted in to email, 40% to WhatsApp. The team can send email and WhatsApp; discounts above 15% need the founder's yes.

Subscriptions are 9% of revenue. 2,400 subscribers cancelled in the last 6 months. Reasons given: "Coffee arrived late, I ran out" 39%; "Too much coffee" 22%; "Price" 13%; "Moving or travelling" 9%; other 17%. On-time delivery for subscription orders was 95% before the courier change in June and 79% since.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me a win-back programme: which groups to target and in what order, the offer logic for each, the message sequence and timing per channel, the first message drafts, how to measure true lift, and what it's worth in gross profit if it works."

**Length:** at most 1,500 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
