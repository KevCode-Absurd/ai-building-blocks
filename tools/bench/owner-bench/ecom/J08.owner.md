# J08 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Many lapsed because their coffee disappeared.** 5,200 customers lapsed 91 to 365 days ago last bought the "Monsoon Dark" blend, which stopped 8 months ago; 4,300 of them sit in the 181 to 365 group, about 29% of that group. They did not leave over price. A great answer gives them their own track: name the blend, recommend the closest dark roast still on sale (or a sample), and lead with the match, not a discount.
2. **Subscription churn is a delivery problem.** 39% of cancellations say coffee arrived late, and on-time delivery fell from 95% to 79% after the June courier change. A great answer says to fix delivery first (or ship subscriptions earlier, or let customers pick dates and skip) before winning back the 2,400 ex-subscribers, and makes the win-back message about that fix, not a bigger discount. Otherwise they will lapse again.
3. **46 to 90 days is "at risk", not lost.** The median gap to a second order is 34 days, so the 9,000 customers at 46 to 90 days are the cheapest to bring back. A great answer starts there with a light reminder and little or no discount, and saves deeper offers for older groups within the 15% limit.

## What would delight me

- A ranked target order with group sizes, reachable counts (62% email, 40% WhatsApp) and the offer for each.
- A Monsoon Dark track with a draft message that names the blend and the closest match.
- An ex-subscriber track that leads with flexible dates and on-time delivery.
- A holdout group (for example 10%) per segment to measure true lift, with the minimum lift needed to break even.
- Gross profit worked out from ₹557 per order, net of the discount.

## What would annoy me

- One discount for everyone, or offers above 15% without flagging the founder's approval.
- Guilt lines or "We miss you" clichés.
- Ignoring the discontinued blend or the courier problem.
- No holdout, or lift claimed from redemptions alone.
- WhatsApp sent to people who did not opt in.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Gross profit per order: ₹1,150 x 58% minus ₹110 = ₹557.
- A 15% discount costs ₹172.50, leaving ₹384.50 per order; 10% leaves ₹442.
- Monsoon Dark buyers: 900 + 4,300 = 5,200 customers.
- On-time subscription delivery: 95% before June, 79% since.
