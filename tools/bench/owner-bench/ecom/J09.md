# Job J09: 90-day growth plan

**Business:** Brooklinen (brooklinen.com), a US direct-to-consumer brand selling sheets, bedding, towels and home goods online.

**Role wanted:** e-commerce growth lead.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: warm, witty, confident. We say "Made for the way you actually sleep." We never say "luxury for less" or "cheap."
- Reading level: grade 8.
- Avoid: "hotel-quality" or "organic" without proof, "best sheets on the internet", and fake urgency.
- Plain numbers, a light touch of humour, and no constant sitewide discounts.
- We stand for comfortable, well-made home essentials at an honest price.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Sarah, 34, just bought her first home in Philadelphia. She wants a bed that feels like a treat. What stops her: paying $200 or more for sheets she has not touched. In her words: "Like sleeping in a nice hotel, but it's my bed." "Worth it after a year of washing." "Returned the linen, it shrank."
- Mike, 45, a repeat customer in Austin who adds towels and a second sheet set. In his words: "I just reorder what I know works." "I'd buy more if I heard about new stuff."

**Data (test):**

Last 6 months versus the same 6 months a year ago: revenue down 4%. New customer orders down 11%; returning customer orders up 6%. Blended cost per first order up from $78 to $104. Average first order $188; gross margin after product cost 52%; shipping, fees and returns cost 14% of revenue. Paid mix: Meta 55%, Google 35%, other 10%. Site conversion rate flat at 2.1%. Email and SMS drive 22% of revenue. 18-month repeat rate 31%. Cash: comfortable, but the board wants contribution margin to improve this year, not just revenue.

Cost per first order by channel:

| Channel | Share of paid spend now | A year ago | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | 55% | $72 | $121 |
| Google | 35% | $88 | $92 |
| Other | 10% | $76 | $80 |

40% of Meta spend promotes the linen sheet set.

Email and SMS: 1.1 million email subscribers. Email sign-ups were 2.4% of new visitors a year ago and 0.8% now; the sign-up pop-up was removed in March to speed up the site. Only the welcome flow is live; the browse and cart abandonment flows were switched off in the January platform move and not rebuilt. Flows bring in 9% of email revenue; campaigns bring in the rest.

Return rate by product (units):

| Product | Share of revenue | A year ago | Now | Top reason now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic sheet set | 24% | 5% | 5% | Colour |
| Sateen sheet set | 20% | 6% | 6% | Feel |
| Linen sheet set | 16% | 7% | 18% | "Shrank after first wash" |
| Down comforter | 14% | 4% | 4% | Too warm |
| Towels | 10% | 3% | 4% | Colour |
| Everything else | 16% | 5% | 5% | Mixed |

**What the owner wants:** "Give me a 90-day plan. Tell me what is really causing the slowdown, and keep what the numbers prove apart from what still needs checking. Then the 5 to 7 moves ranked by gross profit impact and effort, who owns each, what to stop doing, and a weekly scorecard to run it."

**Length:** at most 1,600 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
