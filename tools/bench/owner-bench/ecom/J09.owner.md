# J09 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Meta is the slowdown, not paid as a whole.** Meta's cost per first order rose 68% ($72 to $121) while Google rose about 5% ($88 to $92). Meta carries 55% of spend, so it drives most of the blended jump from $78 to $104. And 40% of Meta spend pushes linen, the product with the returns problem. A great answer separates "proved" (Meta cost rise) from "to check" (creative fatigue, audience saturation, tracking), cuts or reworks Meta spend, and moves linen out of prospecting until returns are fixed.
2. **Email is switched half off.** Sign-ups fell from 2.4% to 0.8% of new visitors after the pop-up was removed, and the browse and cart abandonment flows have been off since January; flows bring in only 9% of email revenue. A great answer ranks restoring capture (a lighter, fast-loading form) and rebuilding the abandonment flows near the top: low effort, high margin, and it feeds the returning-customer growth that is already working.
3. **Linen returns tripled.** Linen returns rose from 7% to 18% with "Shrank after first wash" as the top reason, lifting the overall return rate from about 5.2% to 7.0%. A great answer treats it as a product or listing fix (supplier batch check, size and care guidance, shrinkage note) and counts the margin it recovers.

## What would delight me

- A short diagnosis that splits "proved" from "needs checking", with numbers.
- 5 to 7 moves ranked by gross profit and effort, each with an owner and a first-week step.
- Clear "stop" items: Meta prospecting on linen, and spend above a set cost per first order.
- A weekly scorecard with targets: Meta and Google cost per first order, email sign-up rate, flow revenue, linen return rate, contribution margin.
- Respecting the brand: no sitewide discount spiral.

## What would annoy me

- Generic "optimise the funnel" or "invest in brand" advice.
- Treating the paid rise as equal across channels.
- Discount-led growth that hurts contribution margin.
- Ignoring linen returns or the switched-off flows.
- No owners and no first steps.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Blended cost per first order with today's mix: about $104 (Meta $121, Google $92, other $80 at 55/35/10).
- First-order contribution: $188 x (52% minus 14%) = about $71, so each first order loses about $33 at $104 before repeat purchases.
- Weighted return rate: about 5.2% a year ago, about 7.0% now; linen alone adds about 1.8 points.
- Meta cost per first order rose about 68%; Google about 5%.
