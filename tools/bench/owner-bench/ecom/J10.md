# Job J10: Bundles and free shipping threshold

**Business:** Plum Goodness (plumgoodness.com), an Indian vegan beauty brand selling skin, hair, body and makeup products online.

**Role wanted:** offer strategist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: cheerful, friendly, honest. We say "100% vegan, always." We never say "chemical-free" or "toxin-free."
- Reading level: grade 6 to 7.
- Avoid: "fairness", "whitening", "miracle", fake countdown timers and "only 2 left" unless true.
- Bright, simple and clear on price. A bundle must make sense as a routine, not a clearance pile.
- We stand for vegan, cruelty-free beauty with good ingredients.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Ishita, 23, a student in Delhi on a tight budget. She buys one hero product at a time. What stops her: shipping fees and spending more than she planned. In her words: "I only buy one thing at a time because of shipping." "The vitamin C serum is the only thing I reorder."
- Kavya, 31, a marketing manager in Mumbai who wants a routine that works together. In her words: "Just tell me which face wash goes with the serum." "I'd buy a set if it actually saves me something."

**Data (test):**

Average order value ₹620. Order value spread: under ₹400 22%; ₹400 to 499 18%; ₹500 to 699 27%; ₹700 to 999 19%; ₹1,000 and up 14%. Free shipping today on orders of ₹499 and above; smaller orders pay ₹49. Shipping costs the store ₹70 per order. Gross margin after product cost: skin care 68%, hair care 62%, body care 60%, makeup 55%. Top sellers: a vitamin C serum ₹599, a green tea face wash ₹345, a body lotion ₹425, a hair shampoo ₹399, a lip balm ₹199. 40% of orders are COD. Shoppers often buy one hero product only.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me three bundles to launch (what's in each, the price, and the job each one does), a recommendation on the free shipping threshold with the money worked out, and how to test both safely."

**Length:** at most 1,300 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
