# J10 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Face wash + serum is the bundle customers already built.** It is the top pair (3,100 orders, 19% of multi-item orders), sits in skin care at 68% margin, and is worth ₹944 at list price, well above any likely threshold. A great answer makes it bundle number one (a "routine" set, priced with a modest saving, for example around ₹849), uses Kavya's words, and works out the margin.
2. **Small COD orders are where the money leaks.** COD orders under ₹500 are refused 29% of the time, against 17% for ₹500 to 999 and 10% at ₹1,000 and up. A great answer brings this into the threshold decision: for example a higher bar for free shipping on COD, a COD fee, or free shipping at a lower bar for prepaid only. It counts RTO cost (₹130) as well as the ₹70 shipping, and does not treat all orders the same.
3. **The lip balm is the natural add-on.** "Lip balm + any one item" is the second most common pair, and at ₹199 it is the right size to lift a ₹400 to 499 order over a higher threshold. A great answer uses it as a cart add-on or a small bundle, not a discounted main bundle.

## What would delight me

- Three bundles with clear jobs: raise order value (face wash + serum), build a routine in a second category (hair or body), and a low-price add-on to cross the threshold.
- The threshold maths shown: which order bands move, shipping saved or spent, RTO effect, and the margin per order.
- A safe test plan: one change at a time, a holdout or split by visitor, guardrail metrics (conversion rate, RTO, margin per visitor) and a run time.
- Bundle names and copy in Plum's cheerful voice.

## What would annoy me

- Deep discounts that wipe out the 68% skin care margin.
- Raising the threshold with no maths on the 27% of orders between ₹500 and 699.
- Ignoring COD and RTO.
- Random bundles that do not match how people already buy.
- "Chemical-free", fake timers or false scarcity.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Face wash + serum at list: ₹345 + ₹599 = ₹944; skin care gross margin at list about ₹642 before shipping.
- Raising the threshold to ₹699 puts the ₹500 to 699 band (27% of orders) below it.
- Free shipping costs ₹70 per order; each RTO costs ₹130; COD is 40% of orders.
- 31% of orders already have 2 or more items.
