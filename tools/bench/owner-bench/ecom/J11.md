# Job J11: Conversion test plan for a mattress product page

**Business:** Wakefit (wakefit.co), an Indian sleep and home brand selling mattresses, beds and furniture online.

**Role wanted:** conversion testing specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: friendly, direct, reassuring. We say "Try it for 100 nights." We never say "orthopaedic cure" or "best mattress in India."
- Reading level: grade 6 to 7.
- Avoid: medical claims such as back pain relief, "doctor recommended", and fake stock or timer messages.
- Mobile first. Price, delivery date and the trial should be clear before the first scroll. Plain words over foam jargon.
- We stand for good sleep at an honest price.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Rohit, 31, and Neha, 29, setting up a new flat in Pune. They browse on their phones, often from Instagram. What stops them: buying a mattress they cannot try, and not knowing when it will arrive. In their words: "What if it's too soft for us?" "When will it actually come? We move in on the 10th." "Which size fits our bed?"
- Deepa, 46, in Chennai, replacing an old spring mattress. She researches on a laptop and wants to pay in EMIs. In her words: "I want to see the EMI before I decide." "Too many sizes, I just need queen."

Error log, cart page, last 30 days: 4,300 "pop-up blocked" errors on the "Proceed to checkout" tap; 96% came from in-app browsers.

Average order ₹14,500. Gross margin after product cost 42%.

Orders by size: Queen 78 x 60 in, 36%; King 78 x 72, 22%; Single 72 x 36, 14%; Double 75 x 48, 9%; the other 26 sizes, 19% together.

Pre-sale chat topics: "When will it be delivered?" 34%; firmness 19%; "Will it fit my bed?" 17%; trial and returns 16%; EMI 14%.

Page notes: on mobile the size picker has 30+ size combinations in one long list; the price changes after size is chosen but the delivery date only appears at checkout; the 100-night trial is mentioned only in the FAQ at the bottom; reviews (4.5 stars, 20,000+) sit below six image tiles; EMI and COD options appear only at checkout; the hero image is a bedroom scene with the mattress small in frame.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me the five tests to run first, ranked. For each: the problem it fixes and the evidence, the change, the hypothesis, the main metric and a guardrail, the traffic split, how long it must run and the smallest effect it can detect. Also tell me what to ship without testing."

**Length:** at most 1,500 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
