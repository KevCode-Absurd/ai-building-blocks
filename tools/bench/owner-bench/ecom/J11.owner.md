# J11 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Checkout is broken in Instagram and Facebook in-app browsers.** Only 23.8% of in-app add-to-carts reach checkout, against 53.3% in Chrome and Safari, and the error log shows 4,300 "pop-up blocked" errors on the checkout tap, 96% in-app. This is a bug, not a test. A great answer puts "fix it now, no test" at the top of the ship list, sizes it (about 1,860 more checkouts and about 560 orders a month, worth about ₹34 lakh in gross margin a month), and says to fix it before starting A/B tests so mobile results are not polluted.
2. **Four sizes make 81% of orders.** Queen, King, Single and Double cover 81% of orders, yet mobile shows 30+ sizes in one list. A great answer tests (or ships) a picker that shows the top sizes first with "other sizes" behind a tap, plus a "measure your bed" helper.
3. **Delivery date is the top question.** 34% of pre-sale chats ask when it will arrive, but the date only shows at checkout. A great answer moves a pincode delivery-date check onto the product page near the price, alongside the 100-night trial, and treats this as a high-ranked test or a ship.

## What would delight me

- The in-app checkout fix called out first, with the money.
- Five tests ranked by expected impact and confidence, each tied to a line of evidence in the brief.
- Honest sample-size maths: with about 0.56% mobile conversion, most page tests need weeks and can only detect large lifts; using add-to-cart or checkout reach as the main metric where it fits, with orders as a guardrail.
- A short ship-without-testing list: the bug, trial near the price, reviews summary higher up.
- Copy in the brand's voice for the trial and delivery lines.

## What would annoy me

- Testing a button colour while checkout is broken for 40% of mobile traffic.
- Treating mobile as one group and missing the browser split.
- Run times or detectable effects that ignore the traffic numbers.
- Medical claims or "best mattress" lines.
- Ten small tests with no ranking.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- Mobile conversion 0.56% (2,350 / 420,000); desktop 1.44% (1,150 / 80,000).
- Add to cart to checkout: Chrome and Safari 53.3%; in-app 23.8%.
- Gross margin per order: ₹14,500 x 42% = ₹6,090.
- Top 4 sizes = 81% of orders.
