# Job J12: WhatsApp sales and COD confirmation messages

**Business:** Snitch (snitch.co.in), an Indian men's fashion brand selling shirts, t-shirts, jeans and more online.

**Role wanted:** chat and WhatsApp sales lead.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: bold, young, quick. We say "Fresh drop just landed." We never say "cheap" or "sasta maal."
- Reading level: simple English, grade 5 to 6. Light Hinglish is fine in chat.
- Avoid: fake urgency ("only 2 left" unless true), shaming COD buyers, and promising delivery dates the courier cannot keep.
- Short messages, one action per message, at most one emoji.
- We stand for fast, trend-led fashion for young Indian men.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Vikram, 22, a student in Indore buying from Snitch for the first time. He wants trendy shirts at a fair price. What stops him: fit worries, and he does not trust paying upfront. In his words: "COD hai na?" (COD is available, right?) "Other brands mein M fit hota hai, yahan?" (M fits me in other brands, what about here?) "Kab tak aayega?" (When will it arrive?)
- Arjun, 27, works in sales in Mumbai and orders often. In his words: "Just tell me when it ships." "Exchange easy hai toh I'll try the new fit." (If exchange is easy, I'll try the new fit.)

**Data (test):**

About 42,000 orders a month. 60% of orders are cash on delivery; 25% of COD orders are refused at delivery (RTO), each costing about ₹180. Average order ₹1,300. The store has the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, with opt-in collected at checkout. 30% of carts are abandoned after the shipping step. A team of 4 agents answers chats 10 am to 8 pm. Today the only WhatsApp message sent is "order placed."

RTO by pincode group, COD orders, last 90 days:

| Pincode group | Share of COD orders | RTO rate |
|---|---|---|
| Metro cities | 40% | 15% |
| Tier 2 cities | 29% | 23% |
| Tier 3 towns and rural | 31% | 39% |

Chats, last 30 days: 11,200 chats.

| Topic | Share of chats |
|---|---|
| Order status ("Where is my order?") | 44% |
| Size and fit | 21% |
| Exchange policy | 14% |
| COD availability | 11% |
| Delivery date | 10% |

31% of chats arrive between 8 pm and midnight, when no agent is online. Median first reply in working hours: 52 minutes.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me ready-to-submit WhatsApp message templates (name, category, body, buttons) for COD order confirmation, a nudge to switch to prepaid, cart recovery, and delivery-day reminders. Give me the flow and timing for each, short reply scripts for the 5 common questions, and the numbers to watch weekly."

**Length:** at most 1,500 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
