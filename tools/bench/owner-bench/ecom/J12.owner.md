# J12 owner notes (judges only)

## Signals in the data

1. **Tier 3 towns cause half the RTO cost.** Tier 3 and rural pincodes are 31% of COD orders but refuse 39% of them, about 3,050 RTOs and ₹5.5 lakh of the roughly ₹11.2 lakh monthly RTO cost. A great answer targets them harder: a COD confirmation with a firm reply window, a stronger prepaid nudge (or a small advance for COD) for that band, and a lighter touch for metros, instead of one flow for everyone.
2. **"Where is my order?" is 44% of chats because nobody tells customers.** The only message sent today is "order placed." A great answer adds shipped and out-for-delivery utility templates with tracking, and says this should cut chat volume sharply and free the 4 agents for size and exchange questions that drive sales.
3. **A third of chats arrive after hours.** 31% come between 8 pm and midnight with no one online. A great answer sets up an automated first reply with buttons (track order, size guide, exchange policy, COD check) so those chats get answered at once, and routes the rest to agents at 10 am.

## What would delight me

- Templates with correct categories: COD confirmation, shipping and delivery updates as Utility; prepaid nudge with an incentive and cart recovery as Marketing.
- Clear flows with timing (for example COD confirm within minutes of the order, auto-cancel or call if no reply by a set time).
- Reply scripts in a short, bold Snitch voice, with light Hinglish where natural.
- The RTO money worked out by pincode group.
- A weekly scorecard: COD confirm rate, prepaid switch rate, RTO by pincode group, cart recovery rate, chats per 100 orders, after-hours response time.

## What would annoy me

- Marketing content inside Utility templates.
- One generic flow that ignores pincode risk.
- Shaming or pushy COD messages, or fake urgency.
- Promising delivery dates we cannot keep.
- Missing the order-status chat load.

## Facts a careful answer gets right

- COD orders: about 25,200 a month; RTOs about 6,240 (24.8%); cost about ₹11.2 lakh a month at ₹180 each.
- Tier 3 and rural: about 7,800 COD orders, 39% RTO, about 3,050 RTOs, about ₹5.5 lakh a month.
- Business-initiated WhatsApp messages need opt-in and an approved template; order confirmations and delivery updates are Utility, offers are Marketing.
