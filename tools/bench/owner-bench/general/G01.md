# Job G01: A 90-day SEO plan

**Business:** Plausible Analytics (plausible.io), a lightweight, open source, privacy-friendly web analytics tool sold as a simple alternative to Google Analytics.

**Role wanted:** SEO strategist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Plausible's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: plain, direct, principled. We say "Plausible doesn't use cookies." We never say "best-in-class privacy-first analytics solution."
- Reading level: grade 8. Short sentences. Show the dashboard instead of describing it.
- Avoid: "100% GDPR compliant" as a guarantee, fear copy about fines, and any attack on a competitor's people.
- Comparison pages are fine when they are fair, factual and dated.
- No paid links, no guest-post swaps, no AI-spun articles at scale. We would rather publish less and keep it true.
- We stand for a web that doesn't track people, built by a small, independent team.

**Who we sell to (test):**

1. **Maya, solo founder** of a small SaaS or blog with 5,000 to 50,000 visits a month. She uses GA4 because it is free and dreads opening it. She wants numbers she can read in 30 seconds and no cookie banner. What stops her: fear of losing her old data and one more monthly bill.
   - "GA4 makes me feel stupid."
   - "I just want to know where people came from."
   - "Can I finally get rid of the cookie banner?"
2. **Tomas, agency developer** in the EU who runs 20 or more client sites. He wants one tool clients understand and he can defend to a data protection officer. What stops him: client sign-off and cost per site.
   - "My clients only look at three numbers anyway."
   - "I need something I can defend to a DPO."

Team and budget (test):
- One part-time writer: at most 4 new or fully rewritten pages a month.
- One developer day a month for site changes.
- No budget for links, ads or freelancers this quarter.
- Trial to paid conversion is 38% across all sources.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me a 90-day plan, month by month, ranked by what will grow trials most, not traffic. Tell me what to stop doing as well as what to start. Spell out the first week's tasks so the writer can begin on Monday. Every page you suggest should fit our capacity."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
