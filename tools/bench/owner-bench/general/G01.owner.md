# G01 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Comparison pages win while blog posts decay.** Blog clicks fell 37% (61,000 to 38,500) and turn 0.25% of clicks into trials. The 9 "vs" and "alternative" pages grew 74% (7,400 to 12,900) and convert 3.2%, producing 4.3 times the trials of 140 blog posts. Queries for "fathom vs plausible" and "umami vs plausible" show demand with no page. A great answer moves most of the writer's capacity to new and refreshed comparison pages and stops general blog posting.
2. **Cheap wins on pages that already rank near page one.** "how to remove cookie banner" (44,000 impressions, position 9.6, CTR 0.7%, a 2022 post) and "website analytics without cookies" (31,000 impressions, position 11.4) match Maya's own phrase "get rid of the cookie banner" and are high intent for Plausible. A great answer rewrites and retitles these first, linking them to trial, before writing anything new.
3. **Capacity limit.** 4 pages a month means about 12 pieces in 90 days, plus 3 developer days. No links or paid help. A great answer fits every task inside this and says what gets cut.

**What would delight me:**
- The first week is a real task list: rewrite the cookie banner post and title, retitle the cookies page, brief the Fathom comparison.
- Trial forecast per month, tied to the conversion rates in the data.
- A clear "stop" list: pause general marketing blog posts, consolidate or redirect decaying posts into the pages that convert.
- Fair, dated comparison pages written in our voice, with internal links from docs (docs pages already rank well, e.g. the WordPress doc at 21% CTR).
- A simple way to measure: trials by landing page, checked monthly.

**What would annoy me:**
- Generic SEO advice (build backlinks, do keyword research, improve Core Web Vitals) with no link to our numbers.
- Any plan that needs link buying, guest posts or AI content at scale.
- More than 12 pieces of content in 90 days, or a plan with no owner or first step.
- Planning to grow blog traffic as the goal.
- "100% GDPR compliant" claims or fear of fines in suggested titles.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Blog clicks down about 37%; comparison clicks up about 74%.
- Trial rate: comparison pages about 3.2%, blog about 0.25%.
- Capacity: 4 pages a month, about 12 in 90 days, 1 developer day a month.
- Trial to paid is 38%, so 100 extra trials is about 38 paying customers.
