# Job G02: Homepage hero and landing page copy for one segment

**Business:** Cal.com (cal.com), open source scheduling software that gives people and teams booking links, round robin scheduling and routing, often chosen as an alternative to Calendly.

**Role wanted:** Landing page CRO specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Cal.com's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: confident, open, practical. We say "Let people book you without the back and forth." We never say "revolutionary AI-powered meeting synergy."
- Reading level: grade 7 to 8.
- Avoid: "the only," "#1," uptime promises, and any claim about a competitor's prices or features.
- Copy principles: one idea per section, show a real booking page, numbers over adjectives, every section ends in one action.
- Calls to action: "Get started" for self-serve, "Book a demo" for teams. Never exclamation marks in buttons.
- We stand for open source scheduling you can own, with no lock-in.

**Who we sell to (test):**

1. **Priya, talent acquisition lead** at a company of 100 to 1,000 people. She coordinates 40 to 80 interviews a week across six interviewers. She wants candidates to book the right interviewer themselves and the load spread fairly. What stops her: fit with her hiring tools, IT security review, interviewers who don't keep calendars current.
   - "I spend half my Monday playing email tennis with candidates."
   - "I need round robin that actually knows who's on PTO."
   - "Security will ask where the data lives before I can buy anything."
2. **Dan, freelance coach.** He wants one professional booking link. What stops him: he already has a free link elsewhere.
   - "I just need one link for my email signature."
   - "I'm not paying for something I get free."

Current page (test):
- Hero: "Scheduling infrastructure for absolutely everyone." Button: "Get started."
- No recruiting example anywhere on the page. Routing forms appear in section 6 of 8.
- Our security and compliance page was visited by 48% of recruiting accounts that later paid. It is linked only from the footer.
- Homepage visit to signup: 3.1%. Demo form has 9 fields.

**What the owner wants:** "Pick the one segment our numbers say we should win, and tell me why in two lines. Write the hero for a version of the page aimed at them, then the full landing page: each section in order with the actual copy, the button text, and what proof goes where. Give me two hero versions to A/B test and the one metric that decides the winner."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
