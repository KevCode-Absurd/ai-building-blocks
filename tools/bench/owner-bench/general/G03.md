# Job G03: October investor update

**Business:** Supabase (supabase.com), an open source Postgres development platform that gives developers a database, authentication, storage, edge functions and realtime in one place.

**Role wanted:** Investor relations specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Supabase's real figures, investors or guidelines. Treat them as the company's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: candid, specific, builder-like. We say "Here's what went wrong and what we're doing about it." We never say "We're crushing it."
- Reading level: grade 9. Short paragraphs, numbers in a table.
- Avoid: hype words ("explosive," "insane"), forecasts without a range, any metric without its denominator.
- Principles: the three numbers that matter first, bad news in the first screen, every ask names a profile or person and a date.
- We stand for open source, developers first, and Postgres for everyone.

**Who reads it (test):**

1. **Lead investor partner.** Reads 40 updates a month, on a phone, between meetings. Wants to know if anything changed and what we need.
   - "Tell me the three numbers and what you need from me."
   - "I hate finding the bad news on page three."
2. **Angel investors who were devtools founders.** Happy to help, but only with a clear ask.
   - "I can make intros, but you have to name the kind of person."
   - "Show me the denominator."

Notes from the finance and product teams (test):
- 72% of projects created through AI app builders have no database activity after their first 7 days. Free-tier compute cost rose $0.9M in October.
- Orgs that arrive through AI app builders convert to paid within 30 days at 0.4%; orgs that sign up directly convert at 3.1%.
- Free projects are paused after 7 days of inactivity today. Engineering says pausing inactive AI-builder projects after 48 hours is a two-week change.
- The rest of the $2.2M rise in burn: $0.8M was a one-off deposit for next year's developer conference and $0.5M was six new hires.
- Customer A, an enterprise account worth 2.1% of October ARR, gave 90-day notice. They are moving workloads to their own servers. Last day of service: 31 January 2027.
- Enterprise ARR grew 9% month on month; three new enterprise customers signed in October.
- The VP Enterprise Sales role has been open for 5 months. Two finalists turned it down; both said they wanted a base in New York.
- Our September update said: "We expect to cross $125M ARR in October."
- Launch highlights: branching for databases left beta; a new usage dashboard shipped to all paid plans.

**What the owner wants:** "Draft the October investor update email. Lead with what matters, put the bad news where they'll see it, and say what we're doing about it. Include a small table, and end with no more than three specific asks."

**Length:** at most 700 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
