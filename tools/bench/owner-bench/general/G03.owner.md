# G03 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **The database growth headline hides a margin problem.** New databases rose 26%, but 61% came through AI app builders, 72% of those go idle within 7 days, and they convert at 0.4% vs 3.1%. Free-tier compute rose $0.9M, which explains the gross margin drop from 71% to 64%. A great answer does not lead with "2.4M databases" as a win. It explains the margin drop plainly and names the fix (pause inactive AI-builder projects after 48 hours, two weeks of work) with the expected effect.
2. **Customer A's notice is the bad news that must be up front.** 2.1% of $124.5M is about $2.6M ARR leaving by 31 January 2027, which will pull NRR down. Combined with the missed $125M ARR forecast from last month, a great answer states both early and plainly, before the good news.
3. **The stalled hire is the right ask.** The VP Enterprise Sales role is 5 months open, finalists wanted New York. Enterprise ARR is growing 9% a month, so this hire matters. A great answer asks investors for intros to enterprise sales leaders based in or willing to be in New York, and decides on (or asks advice about) a New York base.

**What would delight me:**
- Three headline numbers first: ARR $124.5M (+5.5%), gross margin 64% (down 7 points), runway.
- Bad news in the first screen: Customer A, the margin drop, the missed forecast.
- Burn explained: $2.2M rise is $0.9M compute, $0.8M one-off, $0.5M hires; so the underlying rise is smaller.
- Runway calculated correctly.
- Asks that are specific: a named profile, a city, a date.

**What would annoy me:**
- "We're crushing it" tone or hype words.
- Leading with database count as proof of momentum.
- Hiding Customer A or the forecast miss at the bottom, or not mentioning them.
- Vague asks like "intros to anyone helpful."
- Wrong runway or ARR math.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- ARR growth: $118.0M to $124.5M is +5.5%; the $125M forecast was missed by $0.5M.
- Customer A: about $2.6M ARR (2.1% of $124.5M), last day 31 January 2027.
- Runway at October burn: $205.7M / $6.3M is about 32.7 months; without the $0.8M one-off, about 37 months ($205.7M / $5.5M).
- Gross margin down 7 points, 71% to 64%.
