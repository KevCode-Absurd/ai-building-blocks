# Job G04: A 4-step cold email sequence for SaaS finance leads

**Business:** Chargebee (chargebee.com), subscription billing and revenue management software for businesses that charge on a recurring basis.

**Role wanted:** Sales playbook specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Chargebee's real figures, customers or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: clear, helpful, peer to peer. We say "Close your books faster." We never say "Hope this email finds you well" or "revolutionize your revenue."
- Reading level: grade 8.
- Avoid: guaranteeing compliance outcomes, fake "Re:" or "Fwd:" subject lines, fake urgency, criticizing any named competitor.
- Copy principles: under 120 words per email, plain text, one question and one ask per email, write like a finance person talking to a finance person.
- We stand for helping subscription businesses grow without billing getting in the way.

**Who we sell to (test):**

1. **Controller or Head of Finance** at a B2B SaaS company with 50 to 300 people. Owns month-end close. Revenue recognition lives in spreadsheets built on top of a billing setup engineering wrote years ago. Wants a faster close and numbers an auditor will accept. What stops them: fear of a migration, "billing belongs to engineering," and no time.
   - "Month-end is me and three spreadsheets for nine days."
   - "Every time sales changes pricing, I rebuild the rev rec model."
   - "I'm not touching billing in the middle of an audit."
2. **CFO at a company with 1,000 or more people.** Locked into an ERP and a long procurement process.
   - "Send it to my team."
   - "We just signed a three-year ERP contract."

**Data (test):**

Outbound pilot last quarter, 4,800 contacts, same 4-step sequence for everyone:

| Segment | Contacts | Open rate | Reply rate | Positive reply rate | Meetings booked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CFO, 1,000+ employees | 1,600 | 52% | 0.9% | 0.2% | 3 |
| CFO, 50 to 300 employees | 1,100 | 58% | 2.6% | 1.1% | 10 |
| Controller or Head of Finance, 50 to 300 employees | 1,300 | 61% | 6.8% | 3.4% | 38 |
| RevOps lead, 50 to 300 employees | 800 | 49% | 2.2% | 0.9% | 6 |

Reply rate by the trigger mentioned in the first line:

| First-line trigger | Reply rate |
|---|---|
| None (generic opener) | 1.8% |
| Raised funding in the last 90 days | 2.4% |
| Hiring a billing or revenue operations role | 3.1% |
| Changed their public pricing page in the last 60 days (new tiers or usage-based pricing) | 7.9% |

Reply rate by format of step 1:

| Step 1 format | Reply rate |
|---|---|
| Plain text, no links | 4.1% |
| Plain text with a case study link | 1.7% |
| Plain text with a calendar link | 1.2% |

Proof we may use (test): a 140-person SaaS company (unnamed, approved for anonymous use) cut its month-end close from 9 days to 4 after moving billing and revenue recognition to Chargebee.

List available: about 6,000 new finance contacts at SaaS companies, of which about 1,900 are Controllers or Heads of Finance at companies with 50 to 300 people. Our data tool flags pricing-page changes weekly.

**What the owner wants:** "Write the 4-step sequence for the people and the moment our pilot says work. For each step: send day, subject line, and body. Give two subject line options for step 1. Then tell me exactly who to load into the list, who to leave out, and what reply rate we should expect."

**Length:** at most 900 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
