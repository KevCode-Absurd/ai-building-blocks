# G04 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Controllers and Heads of Finance at 50 to 300 person SaaS companies are the segment.** They were 27% of contacts but booked 38 of 57 meetings (67%), with a 6.8% reply rate vs 0.9% for enterprise CFOs. A great answer writes the sequence for them, loads the roughly 1,900 matching contacts, and leaves out enterprise CFOs.
2. **The pricing-change trigger plus the customer's phrase is the hook.** A first line about a recent pricing page change replied at 7.9% vs 1.8% generic, and the persona says "Every time sales changes pricing, I rebuild the rev rec model." A great answer opens step 1 on that pricing change and the rev rec rebuild pain, and uses the weekly pricing-change flag to time sends.
3. **No links in step 1.** Plain text with no link replied at 4.1%; a case study link cut it to 1.7%, a calendar link to 1.2%. A great answer keeps step 1 link-free and puts the 9-to-4-day close proof in plain words in a later step, not as a link.

**What would delight me:**
- Each email under 120 words, one question, one ask, in a finance person's voice.
- Step 1 opens on their pricing change and the spreadsheet rebuild, not on Chargebee.
- The 9 days to 4 days proof used as a sentence, attributed to an unnamed 140-person SaaS company.
- Clear list rules: include Controllers and Heads of Finance at 50 to 300 person SaaS companies with a pricing change in the last 60 days first; exclude CFOs at 1,000+ companies.
- An honest expected reply rate based on the pilot, not a wish.

**What would annoy me:**
- One sequence for all four segments, or including enterprise CFOs.
- Fake "Re:" subject lines, "Hope this finds you well," "quick question" filler, fake urgency.
- Links or attachments in step 1.
- Promising ASC 606 or audit compliance as a guarantee.
- Emails over 120 words or with three asks.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Controllers at 50 to 300 person companies: 6.8% reply, 38 of 57 meetings.
- Pricing-page change trigger: 7.9% reply vs 1.8% generic.
- No-link step 1: 4.1% vs 1.7% with a case study link and 1.2% with a calendar link.
- The proof: close went from 9 days to 4, company unnamed, 140 people.
