# Job G05: Job post and interview scorecard for a customer support specialist

**Business:** 37signals (basecamp.com), the small, remote software company behind Basecamp, project management software for teams, and the HEY email service.

**Role wanted:** Recruitment marketing specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not 37signals' real figures, salaries or hiring guidelines. Treat them as the company's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: calm, candid, warm. We say "We work 40 hours a week, and we mean it." We never say "rockstar," "ninja," "fast-paced environment," or "work hard, play hard."
- Reading level: grade 8. Write like a person talking to a person.
- Avoid: degree requirements, inflated requirement lists, "competitive salary" without a number, perks used as a substitute for honesty.
- Principles: publish the salary, say plainly what the hard parts of the job are, explain the hiring steps and when people will hear back.
- We stand for calm work, clear writing over meetings, and people who stay for years.

**Who we want to reach (test):**

1. **Experienced support person at a fast-growing startup,** tired of quotas and deflection targets. Wants to solve problems properly and work somewhere calm.
   - "I'm tired of being measured on tickets per hour."
   - "I want to actually fix the customer's problem, not deflect it."
   - "Is it really remote, or remote until it isn't?"
2. **Career changer** from teaching or hospitality. A strong writer with no software support title.
   - "Every support job wants three years of Zendesk experience."
   - "I explain things to stressed people all day. Isn't that the job?"

**Data (test):**

Support team today: 14 people. Where they live: Americas 8, Europe 5, Asia-Pacific 1.

Tickets and response by time of day (UTC), last 90 days:

| Window (UTC) | Share of tickets | Median first response | Customer satisfaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:00 to 16:00 | 34% | 41 minutes | 95% |
| 16:00 to 00:00 | 39% | 35 minutes | 96% |
| 00:00 to 08:00 | 27% | 7.1 hours | 81% |

Last support hiring round (2025): one opening, 2,100 applications, a resume and cover letter only, three reviewers, 160 reviewer hours to reach a shortlist.

How well each hiring signal predicted first-year performance (customer satisfaction plus peer review) for our last 9 support hires:

| Signal at hiring | Correlation with first-year performance |
|---|---|
| Score on the written customer reply exercise (done at interview stage) | 0.62 |
| Interview impression | 0.11 |
| Prior experience with a support tool | 0.07 |
| Years in a support role | 0.04 |

Leavers: 3 of those 9 hires left within 12 months. Two said in exit interviews that "the weekend rotation wasn't clear when I applied."

Job facts (test):
- Salary: $82,000 a year, the same wherever you live.
- Fully remote. 40-hour weeks. 4-day weeks from May to August.
- Weekend rotation: one weekend in six, with two weekdays off in return.
- Tools: Basecamp and HEY for our own work; our own help desk tool.
- Hiring timeline: applications open for 2 weeks; everyone hears back within 3 weeks of closing.

**What the owner wants:** "Write the job post as we would publish it, and the scorecard the hiring team will use: what we score, the weight for each, and what a 1, 3 and 5 look like. Tell us how the application itself should work so we don't drown again."

**Length:** at most 1,100 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
