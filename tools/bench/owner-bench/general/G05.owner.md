# G05 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Writing predicts success; experience does not.** The written reply exercise correlates 0.62 with first-year performance; years in support 0.04 and tool experience 0.07. A great answer drops years-of-experience and tool requirements from the post, welcomes career changers, and weights writing heaviest on the scorecard. It also moves a short written customer reply into the application itself, which cuts the 160 reviewer hours and filters 2,100 applicants on the signal that matters.
2. **The overnight UTC gap.** 27% of tickets arrive 00:00 to 08:00 UTC, waiting a median 7.1 hours with 81% satisfaction, and only 1 of 14 support staff is in Asia-Pacific. A great answer says this hire should work Asia-Pacific hours (roughly UTC+8 to UTC+12, or hours that cover 00:00 to 08:00 UTC) and states it in the post.
3. **The weekend rotation must be stated plainly.** Two of three early leavers said it wasn't clear. A great answer puts "one weekend in six, two weekdays off in return" in the post near the top of the hard parts, not buried.

**What would delight me:**
- A post that sounds like us: salary of $82,000 in the first screen, honest hard parts, no buzzwords.
- An application with one short written exercise (a real-feeling customer email to answer) instead of a cover letter.
- A scorecard with weights, writing the largest, with plain 1, 3 and 5 examples.
- The timeline stated: open 2 weeks, everyone hears back within 3 weeks.
- Language that welcomes the career changer persona.

**What would annoy me:**
- "3+ years of support experience required" or "Zendesk experience required."
- "Rockstar," "fast-paced," "competitive salary," or perk lists.
- A post that hides the time zone need or the weekend rotation.
- A scorecard that weights "culture fit" or interview impression heavily.
- Generic HR template structure with no link to our data.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Writing exercise correlation 0.62; years in role 0.04.
- 27% of tickets in 00:00 to 08:00 UTC, median first response 7.1 hours, satisfaction 81%; 1 of 14 staff in Asia-Pacific.
- Salary $82,000, same everywhere; weekend rotation one in six with two weekdays off.
- Last round: 2,100 applications, 160 reviewer hours.
