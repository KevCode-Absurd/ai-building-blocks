# Job G06: Competitive analysis against Semrush, and what to do about it

**Business:** Ahrefs (ahrefs.com), an SEO toolset for keyword research, backlink analysis, site audits and rank tracking, with a free Ahrefs Webmaster Tools tier for site owners.

**Role wanted:** Competitive intelligence analyst.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Ahrefs' or Semrush's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: data-led, blunt, educational. We say "Here's the data." We never say "industry-leading" or "the best SEO tool on the market."
- Reading level: grade 9.
- Avoid: attacking Semrush or its people, claims we can't back with a dated source, "biggest" or "fastest" without numbers.
- Principles: show, don't tell; teach before we sell; every claim has a number and a date.
- We stand for useful tools and free education that make marketers better at their jobs.

**Who we sell to (test):**

1. **In-house SEO lead** at a company with a 1 to 5 person SEO team. Lives in backlink and keyword data.
   - "Ahrefs' link data is the one I trust."
   - "Why does going up one plan cost so much more?"
   - "I ran out of credits halfway through an audit."
2. **Agency owner** with 10 to 40 clients across SEO and paid search.
   - "My clients don't care about DR. They care about leads."
   - "I need one report I can send every Monday."
   - "I'm paying for two tools and I hate it."

**Data (test):**

New trials and losses by segment, last two quarters:

| Segment | Share of new trials | Trial to paid | Share of that segment's losses that went to Semrush | Top loss reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In-house SEO team | 38% | 24% | 18% | Price jump between plans |
| Agency, 10+ clients | 27% | 11% | 54% | Needs client reports and PPC research in one tool |
| Solo creator or blogger | 25% | 6% | 9% | Too expensive for their traffic |
| Enterprise | 10% | 31% | 19% | Procurement and security review |

Monthly customer churn by plan:

| Plan | Share of paying customers | Monthly churn | Top exit reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 44% | 7.9% | Hit usage limit mid-project (46%) |
| Standard | 35% | 3.1% | Budget cut (31%) |
| Advanced | 15% | 2.2% | Moved to Semrush (28%) |
| Enterprise | 6% | 0.9% | Consolidating vendors (40%) |

Survey of 600 SEOs who have used both tools in the last year, "Which tool is better at...":

| Area | Ahrefs | Semrush | About the same |
|---|---|---|---|
| Backlink data | 64% | 21% | 15% |
| Keyword data | 41% | 44% | 15% |
| Site audit | 45% | 38% | 17% |
| PPC and ads research | 8% | 71% | 21% |
| Client reporting | 12% | 66% | 22% |

Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free), last 90 days:
- 310,000 active free accounts.
- Average conversion to a paid plan within 90 days: 1.5%.
- Accounts with 3 or more verified sites on different domains: 21,000, converting at 9.2%.
- These multi-site accounts get the same onboarding emails as everyone else.

**What the owner wants:** "Tell me where we actually lose to Semrush and where we don't. Then give me no more than five moves, ranked by impact, each with an owning team, a first step this month and how we'll know it worked. I don't want a feature-by-feature checklist."

**Length:** at most 1,100 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
