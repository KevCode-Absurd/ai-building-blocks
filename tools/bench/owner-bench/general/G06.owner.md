# G06 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **We lose to Semrush mainly among agencies, and on reporting and PPC, not data quality.** 54% of agency losses go to Semrush; in-house and creator losses mostly don't. SEOs rate our backlink data far better (64% vs 21%) while Semrush wins PPC (71%) and client reporting (66%). A great answer says we should not chase PPC parity. It picks a focused response: a client reporting layer for SEO-led agencies, and accepting that PPC-first agencies are not our fight.
2. **Our biggest churn is self-inflicted, not competitive.** Lite is 44% of customers with 7.9% monthly churn, and 46% of Lite leavers hit a usage limit mid-project. The in-house persona also says "I ran out of credits halfway through an audit." A great answer ranks a pricing and limits fix (top-ups, warnings before the limit, a smoother step between Lite and Standard) at or near the top, as a cheaper win than any feature build.
3. **An under-used agency pipeline.** 21,000 free Webmaster Tools accounts verifying 3+ sites convert at 9.2% vs 1.5% average, yet get generic onboarding. A great answer turns them into an agency-specific nurture and trial, tied to the reporting move.

**What would delight me:**
- A short, clear "where we lose and where we win" section built from the numbers.
- Five or fewer moves, ranked, each with owning team, first step this month and a success metric.
- The Lite usage-limit fix identified as the fastest money.
- Agency reporting as a focused bet, with an honest view on what not to build.
- Claims about Semrush kept to the survey data, fair and dated.

**What would annoy me:**
- A feature-by-feature comparison table copied from public pages.
- "Build PPC tools to match Semrush" as the top move.
- Generic advice (improve onboarding, do more content) with no number behind it.
- Attacks on Semrush or claims like "biggest backlink index" with no source.
- More than five moves, or moves with no first step.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Agency losses to Semrush: 54%; in-house 18%; creators 9%.
- Lite: 44% of customers, 7.9% monthly churn, 46% of leavers cite usage limits.
- Multi-site Webmaster Tools accounts: 21,000, converting at 9.2% vs 1.5%.
- Backlink data preferred 64% to 21%; PPC research 8% to 71%; client reporting 12% to 66%.
