# Job G07: Launch plan for a major new app version

**Business:** Raycast (raycast.com), a productivity launcher for Mac that lets people open apps, run commands, manage windows, and use community-built extensions and AI from one search bar.

**Role wanted:** Product launch manager.

Numbers, notes and the launch itself are marked (test): this is a test scenario for this exercise, not Raycast's real plans, figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: crisp, playful, craft-obsessed. We say "Do more with your keyboard." We never say "game-changing AI-powered productivity revolution."
- Reading level: grade 8.
- Avoid: "10x productivity," "fastest" without a measured number, and promising anything that hasn't shipped.
- Principles: show it moving (short screen recordings), one keyboard shortcut per message, always credit the community extension authors.
- We stand for craft, speed, and a community that builds the tool with us.

**The launch (test scenario):** a major new version, rebuilt for speed, with a redesigned extension store and a new AI command builder. Launch date: Tuesday 8 December 2026. Existing settings, hotkeys and extensions should carry over automatically.

**Who we sell to (test):**

1. **Developer power user.** Has used launchers for years and has dozens of hotkeys.
   - "Raycast is the first thing I open in the morning."
   - "Please don't break my hotkeys."
   - "Will my extensions still work?"
2. **Product manager or designer, new to launchers.** Saw a teammate fly through their Mac.
   - "I watched a dev on my team do everything without the mouse and wanted that."
   - "There are so many extensions, I don't know where to start."

**Data (test):**

Our last major release (2025), by channel, first 14 days:

| Channel | New installs | Still active in week 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Product Hunt | 38,000 | 9% |
| Hacker News | 21,000 | 14% |
| Our X account | 17,000 | 22% |
| YouTube creator walkthroughs (6 videos) | 11,000 | 41% |
| Extension authors sharing with their followers | 6,500 | 38% |

Support after that release:
- 2,400 tickets in the first 10 days, handled by a support team of 3.
- 58% of tickets were about hotkeys or extension settings that didn't carry over after updating.
- Median first response in those 10 days: 31 hours.

Community and audience (test):
- 1,500 extension authors have published in the store. None were given early access last time.
- Email list: 420,000 existing users, 46% open rate on product emails.
- 9 YouTube creators have covered Raycast before with over 50,000 subscribers each.

Team (test): one launch manager, one designer, one video editor, 3 support staff, engineering in a code freeze from 1 December.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me a launch plan from today, Friday 13 November, to two weeks after launch: who we tell, in what order, on which channels, with what message, and what has to be true before we press the button. Rank the channels by what will bring users who stay. Then draft the launch-day post for X and the email to existing users."

**Length:** at most 1,200 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
