# G07 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Rank channels by retained users, not installs.** Product Hunt brought the most installs (38,000) but only 9% stayed, about 3,400 users. YouTube walkthroughs brought 11,000 installs at 41%, about 4,500 retained users, the most of any channel; extension authors kept 38%. A great answer ranks YouTube creators and extension authors first, X next, and treats Product Hunt and Hacker News as reach, not the main bet.
2. **The migration issue will repeat unless fixed before launch.** 58% of 2,400 tickets were about hotkeys or extension settings not carrying over, a 3-person team took 31 hours to respond, and the developer persona says "Please don't break my hotkeys." With a code freeze on 1 December, a great answer makes a carry-over check a launch gate before the freeze, prepares a help article and in-app message, and staffs support for launch week.
3. **An unused community asset.** 1,500 extension authors were never given early access, yet their shares retained at 38%. A great answer gives them early access, a share kit and credit on launch day, and gives the 9 creators early builds and time to make videos that go live on launch day.

**What would delight me:**
- A dated timeline from 13 November to 22 December with owners, fitting a small team and the 1 December code freeze.
- Clear "must be true before launch" gates: settings carry-over tested, help article live, creator videos scheduled, support rota set.
- Channels ranked by retained users, with the math.
- An X post and existing-user email that show one shortcut, credit authors, and directly answer "will my hotkeys and extensions still work?"
- No promises of unshipped features.

**What would annoy me:**
- Product Hunt as the centerpiece because it got the most installs.
- A generic launch checklist (press release, influencers, teaser campaign) with no link to our data.
- Ignoring the migration ticket problem or support capacity.
- "10x productivity" or "fastest launcher ever" claims.
- A plan that needs more people than we have.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Retained users: YouTube about 4,500, X about 3,700, Product Hunt about 3,400, Hacker News about 2,900, extension authors about 2,500.
- 58% of 2,400 tickets were migration issues; median first response 31 hours; 3 support staff.
- Launch Tuesday 8 December 2026; code freeze 1 December; 1,500 extension authors; 420,000 email users.
