# Job G08: Pricing recommendation

**Business:** Buffer (buffer.com), a social media tool that helps small businesses, creators and agencies plan, publish and analyze posts across their social channels.

**Role wanted:** SaaS pricing strategist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Buffer's real figures, prices or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: transparent, friendly, plain. We say "Here's exactly what you'll pay." We never say "Unlock premium power features!"
- Reading level: grade 7 to 8.
- Avoid: hidden fees, dark patterns, "limited time" pressure, surprise price rises.
- Principles: a pricing page anyone can understand in 10 seconds; explain every change publicly, with reasons.
- We stand for transparency and for small businesses that do their own marketing.

**Who we sell to (test):**

1. **Small business owner** running a cafe, salon or shop with 2 or 3 channels.
   - "I post when I remember, which is the problem."
   - "I don't want another subscription that creeps up."
2. **Freelance social media manager** with 4 to 8 clients.
   - "Every client I add, my Buffer bill goes up, and I can't always pass that on."
   - "I need clients to approve posts without me forwarding screenshots."
   - "I'd pay more for one flat price I can plan around."

**Data (test):**

Current pricing (test):
- Free: up to 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel.
- Essentials: $6 per channel per month, or $5 per channel per month billed yearly.
- Team: $12 per channel per month, or $10 billed yearly. Adds unlimited users and approval workflows.

Paying customers, October 2026:

| Plan | Channels | Customers | Avg channels | Monthly customer churn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | 1 to 3 | 41,000 | 2.1 | 3.2% |
| Essentials | 4 to 7 | 14,500 | 5.2 | 4.1% |
| Essentials | 8 or more | 3,900 | 11.0 | 9.6% |
| Team | 1 to 3 | 6,200 | 2.5 | 2.4% |
| Team | 4 to 7 | 7,100 | 5.4 | 2.1% |
| Team | 8 or more | 3,300 | 10.4 | 2.6% |

Exit survey, Essentials customers with 8 or more channels: "cost keeps rising with each channel I add" 52%; "I needed client approvals" 21%; "stopped managing social for clients" 14%; other 13%.

Billing:
- 19% of paying customers pay yearly.
- Monthly churn of yearly payers, averaged over 12 months: 0.9%. Monthly payers: 4.0%.
- The pricing page toggle defaults to monthly. The yearly option is not offered again after signup.

Our public commitment (test): when we change prices, existing customers keep their current price for at least 12 months, and get 60 days' notice before anything changes for them.

Finance view (test): the board wants net revenue growth from pricing to show within two quarters. Payment and infrastructure costs are about 9% of revenue at every plan.

**What the owner wants:** "Tell me what we should change in our pricing, if anything. One recommendation, not a menu. Show the revenue math, the risks, and exactly how we tell customers. Then tell me what we test first and what result would make us stop."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
