# Job G09: A 4-week content plan with 6 ready-to-post drafts

**Business:** Headspace (headspace.com), a meditation, sleep and mental health app with guided meditations, sleep content and short mindfulness exercises.

**Role wanted:** Content strategist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Headspace's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: warm, light, kind. We say "Take a breath. You've got this." We never say "Fix your anxiety in 5 minutes."
- Reading level: grade 6 to 7.
- Avoid: medical claims ("cure," "treat," "clinically proven" unless approved), shame, hustle language, "you should."
- Principles: one idea per post; friendly, rounded illustration with our orange; invite, don't instruct; end with one small thing the reader can do now.
- We stand for making mindfulness simple and welcoming for everyone.

**Who we're talking to (test):**

1. **Young professional, 25 to 34.** Busy days, racing mind at night. Has tried meditation apps before and dropped them.
   - "I can't switch my brain off at night."
   - "I lie there replaying every email from the day."
   - "I downloaded three meditation apps and quit all of them by day three."
2. **Parent, 35 to 44.** No time alone.
   - "Ten minutes to myself is a fantasy."
   - "I need something I can do in the car before school pickup."

Assets: about 300 hours of narrated sleep and meditation audio already cleared for use in social clips. Our editor can cut 4 clips a week.

Review rule (test): any post that mentions anxiety, depression, insomnia or any sleep or mental health condition needs clinical and legal review. Review takes 5 business days from submission, and approved posts can go live from the next business day. The earliest we can submit is Monday 16 November.

Capacity (test): 6 posts a week in total across Instagram and TikTok; one designer and one editor.

Plan dates: Monday 16 November to Sunday 13 December 2026.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me a 4-week plan: posts per week by channel, theme, format and time. Then write 6 drafts ready to post, each with caption, on-screen text, and a note for the designer or editor. Tell me what to stop posting, and how we'll know in four weeks if it worked."

**Length:** at most 1,200 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
