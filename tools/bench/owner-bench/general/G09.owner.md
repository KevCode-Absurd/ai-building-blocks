# G09 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Sleep content, posted in the evening, drives installs.** Sleep posts produce about 450 installs each vs about 95 for stress, 50 for focus and 40 for app news, and evening posts (20:00 to 22:00) get 2.2 times the saves. The young professional says "I can't switch my brain off at night." A great answer shifts the mix strongly toward sleep and winding down, posts in the evening, uses the customer's phrase as a hook, and cuts focus/productivity and app news.
2. **Narrator audio clips are the best format and barely used.** 5 posts averaged 380,000 reach and 5.9% saves vs 2.0% for carousels, with 300 hours of cleared audio and an editor who can cut 4 clips a week. A great answer makes narrator-clip videos the core format (up to 4 a week) and drops static quote cards.
3. **Review timing shapes week 1.** Anything naming insomnia, anxiety or a condition needs 5 business days from 16 November, so it cannot post before Monday 23 November. A great answer fills week 1 with posts that need no review (winding down, "switch your brain off," without naming conditions), submits reviewed posts on 16 November, and words drafts to avoid needless review.

**What would delight me:**
- A week-by-week table that fits 6 posts a week and 4 clips a week, with times in the evening.
- Drafts that use "switch my brain off" and "replaying every email" style hooks, in our warm voice, each ending with one small action.
- Clear flags on which drafts need review and when to submit them.
- A stop list: quote cards, app news posts, focus/productivity.
- A simple success measure: installs per post and save rate vs the 90-day baseline.

**What would annoy me:**
- Medical claims ("cures insomnia," "treats anxiety," "clinically proven").
- An even split across all themes, or morning posting.
- Generic wellness captions that could be any brand.
- A week 1 full of posts that need review.
- More posts than the team can make.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Installs per post: sleep about 450, stress about 95, kindness 100, focus 50, app news 40.
- Narrator clip videos: 380,000 average reach, 5.9% saves, only 5 posted.
- Evening posts: 2.2 times the save rate.
- Reviewed posts can't go live before Monday 23 November; capacity 6 posts a week, 4 clips a week.
