# Job G10: A 5-email onboarding series for new users

**Business:** Todoist (todoist.com), a to-do list and task manager made by Doist, used by individuals and teams on web, desktop and mobile.

**Role wanted:** Email marketing specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Todoist's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: calm, clear, encouraging. We say "Get it out of your head and into Todoist." We never say "Crush your goals!"
- Reading level: grade 7.
- Avoid: guilt ("You haven't opened Todoist in days!"), fake urgency, "productivity hacks," promises to change someone's life.
- Principles: one tip and one button per email; show the real screen; give typed examples in quotes, like "Pay rent every 1st"; subject lines under 45 characters.
- We stand for calm, focused work and a tool that gets out of your way.

**Who we're writing to (test):**

1. **Overwhelmed knowledge worker** who has tried several to-do apps.
   - "My to-do list lives in six different places."
   - "I set it all up perfectly on Sunday and give up by Wednesday."
   - "I don't need more features, I need to trust the list."
2. **Busy parent or student** who lives on their phone.
   - "I add stuff on my phone while I'm in line at the store."
   - "I'm hardly ever at a computer."

**Data (test):**

Signups July to September 2026: 180,000. Signup platform: mobile app 41%, web 46%, desktop app 13%.

30-day retention by what users did in their first 7 days:

| First 7 days | Share of signups | Still active at day 30 |
|---|---|---|
| Added 1 to 4 tasks only | 44% | 11% |
| Added 5 or more tasks | 31% | 29% |
| Created a recurring task by typing a date ("every Monday") | 14% | 52% |
| Signed in on a second device (phone plus computer) | 22% | 48% |
| Did both: recurring task and a second device | 8% | 67% |
| Created or joined a shared project | 6% | 34% |

(Rows overlap; a user can appear in more than one.)

Current onboarding series:

| Day | Email | Open rate | Click rate (web signups) | Click rate (mobile signups) | Unsubscribe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome and "add your first task" | 64% | 22% | 19% | 0.2% |
| 1 | Projects and labels | 51% | 6.1% | 4.8% | 0.4% |
| 2 | 10 keyboard shortcuts | 46% | 2.9% | 0.3% | 0.7% |
| 4 | Invite your team | 39% | 0.9% | 0.6% | 1.4% |
| 7 | Upgrade to Pro | 33% | 1.1% | 0.8% | 1.1% |

What our email tool can trigger on (test), until the second quarter of 2027:
- signup, with signup platform known;
- first task added;
- recurring task created;
- second device signed in;
- 3 days with no activity.

Other behaviors, such as labels used or projects shared, can't trigger or branch an email yet. Fixed send days are allowed.

**What the owner wants:** "Write our new 5-email series. For each email: the trigger or send day, subject line, preview text, body and button. Say what each email is meant to get the user to do, and how we'll know it worked. Tell us what to drop from the current series and why."

**Length:** at most 1,200 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
