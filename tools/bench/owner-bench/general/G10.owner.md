# G10 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Two habits predict retention: a typed recurring task and a second device.** Recurring task users retain at 52%, two-device users at 48%, both at 67%, vs 11% for light users. Shared projects reach only 34% and 6% of users. A great answer builds the series around these two actions (e.g. "type 'every Monday'" and "get Todoist on your phone/computer") and drops the "Invite your team" email, which also has the highest unsubscribe rate (1.4%).
2. **Mobile signups get desktop-only advice.** 41% sign up on mobile; the keyboard shortcuts email gets 0.3% clicks from them vs 2.9% from web. A great answer branches on signup platform (which the tool supports): mobile signups are pushed to add the computer as their second device, web and desktop signups to install the phone app, and shortcut tips go only to desktop users.
3. **Trigger limits shape the design.** Only signup (with platform), first task, recurring task, second device and 3 days inactive can trigger emails. A great answer uses these triggers to skip or swap emails (e.g. don't send the recurring-task email to people who already made one; use the 3-day-inactive trigger for a gentle, guilt-free nudge) and does not rely on triggers that don't exist.

**What would delight me:**
- Five emails, each with one tip and one button, with typed examples in quotes and short subject lines.
- A clear goal per email tied to the two habits, with a measure (share of signups reaching recurring task plus second device by day 7, and day 30 retention).
- Platform branching that uses the signup platform field.
- A guilt-free inactivity email in our calm voice.
- A short "what we dropped and why" with numbers.

**What would annoy me:**
- Keeping the team invite email or keyboard shortcuts for everyone.
- Guilt or urgency ("Don't fall behind!"), "productivity hacks," or "crush your goals."
- Feature tours with many links per email.
- Triggers the tool can't do, like "after the user creates a label."
- Subject lines over 45 characters.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Day 30 retention: both habits 67%, recurring 52%, second device 48%, shared project 34%, 1 to 4 tasks 11%.
- Mobile signups are 41%; shortcut email clicks 0.3% mobile vs 2.9% web.
- Invite email unsubscribe 1.4%, the highest in the series.
- Five allowed triggers, plus fixed send days.
