# Job H01: Stock reorder and cash plan for the wedding season and December sale

**Business:** SUGAR Cosmetics (sugarcosmetics.com), an Indian makeup brand selling lipsticks, eye, face and skin products online, on marketplaces and in retail stores.

**Role wanted:** e-commerce inventory and demand planner.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: bold, confident, playful. We say "Your shade, your rules." We never say "flawless skin" or "hide your flaws."
- Reading level: grade 6 to 7.
- Avoid: fake "only 2 left" messages, made-up sale end times, skin-whitening or "fairness" claims.
- Principles: never let a hero shade sell out before a big moment; tell customers the truth about restock dates.
- We stand for makeup made for Indian skin tones that lasts through long days and longer nights.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Tanvi, 26, an office worker in Mumbai with three weddings to attend this season. She buys online, usually one or two nude lipsticks at a time. What stops her: her shade being out of stock when she needs it. In her words: "My shade is always sold out right before a wedding." "I'll wait a week, not a month."
- Farah, 34, a freelance bridal makeup artist in Hyderabad. She buys 6 to 10 units of the same few shades for her kit. In her words: "If my shade's gone, I switch brands for the whole season." "I stock up before December."

**What the owner wants:** "Tell me what to order, how much and by when, line by line and for the lipstick shades that matter, inside the cash I have. Tell me what to do with the stock that isn't moving. I want one plan with dates that my ops lead can act on Monday, not a list of options."

**Length:** at most 900 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
