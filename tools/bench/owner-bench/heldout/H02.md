# Job H02: Google Shopping and Performance Max plan for the next 30 days

**Business:** Rothy's (rothys.com), a US brand selling washable shoes and bags knit from thread spun from recycled plastic bottles.

**Role wanted:** Google Ads and Shopping specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: warm, clear, quietly confident. We say "Made to walk, made to wash." We never say "eco-friendly" on its own or "guilt-free."
- Reading level: grade 7.
- Avoid: vague green claims without the fact behind them, "best shoes ever", and comparisons that name other brands.
- Principles: lead with comfort and washability, then the material story; product titles say what the shoe is and what it does.
- We stand for comfortable, long-lasting shoes made with less waste.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Claire, 38, a middle school teacher in Seattle who stands most of the day. What stops her: past flats that hurt by noon. In her words: "I need flats I can stand in for eight hours." "Can I really throw them in the wash?"
- Nina, 29, a consultant in New York who flies every week. What stops her: bags that look tired after a month. In her words: "They fold flat in my carry-on." "I want one bag that goes from the airport to the client."

Profit: after product cost, shipping, returns and payment fees, we keep 40% of each sale as contribution.

Merchant Center: 212 of 560 pointed flat variants have been disapproved since 21 October for "price mismatch". They are sizes 5 to 7.5. On our site, those sizes are 46% of pointed flat sales. The pointed flat is 34% of site revenue.

Current product titles look like: "The Pointed Flat | Black". Titles do not mention washable, comfort or size.

Budget: total Google spend stays at $240,000 for the next 30 days. The CFO will not approve more.

**What the owner wants:** "Our Google ROAS looks fine at 4.0 but profit doesn't feel like it. Tell me what is really working, what to cut, how to split the $240,000 next month, what to fix in the feed and titles, and what results I should expect. Give me the first week's actions in order."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
