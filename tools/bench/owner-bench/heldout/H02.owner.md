# H02 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Performance Max is living off brand searches.** 41% of its conversion value ($275,520) comes from brand terms for $14,700 of spend, with no brand exclusions. Without brand, Performance Max returns $396,480 on $195,300: a ROAS of about 2.0, below our break-even of 2.5 (40% contribution). A great answer adds brand exclusions so brand stays in the brand search campaign, judges each part against 2.5, and cuts or caps the broad "women's flats" spend ($64,000 at 1.2) and the weak "sustainable shoes" spend.
2. **The best seller is half switched off.** 212 of 560 pointed flat variants (sizes 5 to 7.5, 46% of its sales) have been disapproved for price mismatch since 21 October. This is a feed fix, not a budget question, and should be the first action of week one.
3. **Winners are starved.** Bags Shopping returns 4.8 and loses 38% of impressions to budget; "washable" (3.9) and "standing all day" (3.4) terms beat break-even. The customers' own words ("throw them in the wash", "stand in for eight hours") should go into titles and assets. A great answer moves the cut money here inside the same $240,000.

**What would delight me:**
- The 4.0 ROAS taken apart to show where profit is made and lost, against a 2.5 break-even.
- The feed fix first, with what to check so it does not happen again after a site price change.
- A new budget split that adds to exactly $240,000, with the working and an expected range of profit, not just ROAS.
- New title patterns using the customer's words, for example "Washable Pointed Flat, All-Day Comfort, Black".
- Clear rules for week two to four: what gets more money and what gets cut.

**What would annoy me:**
- Celebrating the 4.0 ROAS.
- Asking for more budget.
- Green claims in titles with no fact behind them.
- Ten tests with no order.
- Budget numbers that do not add up to $240,000.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Break-even ROAS: 1 / 0.40 = 2.5.
- Performance Max without brand: $672,000 - $275,520 = $396,480 value on $210,000 - $14,700 = $195,300 spend, ROAS about 2.03.
- Blended ROAS: $962,400 / $240,000 = 4.01.
- Disapproved: 212 of 560 variants, about 38%, covering 46% of pointed flat sales.
