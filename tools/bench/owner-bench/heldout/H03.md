# Job H03: Post-purchase plan to cut returns and "where is my order" tickets

**Business:** Lounge Underwear (loungeunderwear.com), a UK brand selling lingerie, loungewear and activewear online.

**Role wanted:** e-commerce post-purchase experience manager.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not the brand's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the store's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: confident, inclusive, playful. We say "Find your fit." We never say "flaws", "fix your shape" or "flattering for your body type."
- Reading level: grade 6 to 7.
- Avoid: body shaming, before and after language, promising a delivery day we cannot keep.
- Principles: short messages that answer the question before it is asked; one clear button per message.
- We stand for comfort and confidence for every body.

**Who we sell to (test):**
- Jess, 27, in Manchester, buying her first bra from us after seeing it on Instagram. What stops her: not knowing her size in a new brand. In her words: "I never know my size with a new brand." "If it doesn't fit, I just want it sorted quickly."
- Amira, 35, in London, a repeat buyer of loungewear sets, often as gifts. In her words: "Is it going to arrive before Friday?" "Swapping a size shouldn't mean a whole new order."

**Data (test):**

Last 90 days, UK orders:

| Category | Orders | Return rate | Returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bras | 30,000 | 31% | 9,300 |
| Loungewear sets | 22,000 | 22% | 4,840 |
| Leggings | 18,000 | 18% | 3,240 |
| Briefs | 14,000 | 9% | 1,260 |
| Total | 84,000 | 22% | 18,640 |

Bra returns by reason: too small 58%, too big 19%, changed mind 11%, arrived after the date I needed it 6%, other 6%.

Fit finder: the bra page has a fit finder quiz, linked in small text below the reviews. 11% of bra buyers used it. Their return rate was 14%. Buyers who did not use it returned 33%.

Returns portal: customers pick "Refund" or "Exchange". Refund is shown first and is pre-selected. 82% of returns are refunds. An exchange means placing a new order, then returning the first. Each return costs us £7.50 in postage and handling. Average order value £58.

Delivery and "where is my order" tickets:
- Standard UK delivery is 3 to 5 working days.
- The dispatch email is sent only when the courier first scans the parcel, on average 2.1 days after it leaves our warehouse.
- 9,800 "where is my order" tickets in 90 days. 62% arrive between day 3 and day 5 after the order.
- Each ticket costs us about £3.20 to answer.

Messages we send today: order confirmation, dispatch email, delivery confirmation. Nothing after delivery.

**What the owner wants:** "Give me a post-purchase plan for the next 60 days that cuts returns and 'where is my order' tickets. I want the messages with the copy written, what changes in the returns portal, the order to do things in, and the numbers I should expect."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
