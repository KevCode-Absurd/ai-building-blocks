# H03 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Bras are the returns problem, and fit is the cause.** Bras are 9,300 of 18,640 returns, and 58% of bra returns (about 5,390) say "too small". The fit finder cuts bra returns from 33% to 14% but only 11% of buyers see it, because it sits below the reviews. A great answer moves the fit finder up the page, puts it in the order confirmation for first-time bra buyers ("check your fit before it arrives"), and notes that fit finder users may differ from others, so the real gain should be measured, not assumed to be the full 19 points.
2. **The portal pushes refunds.** Refund is first and pre-selected, and an exchange means a new order. A great answer makes exchange (or a size swap) the first, one-step option, especially for "too small" and "too big", so a fit return keeps the sale.
3. **Most "where is my order" tickets are self-made.** The dispatch email waits for the first courier scan, about 2.1 days after the parcel leaves, so customers hear nothing on days 3 to 5, when 62% of tickets arrive. A great answer sends dispatch with tracking when the parcel leaves and a short day-3 update, a cheap fix worth about 6,000 tickets in 90 days.

**What would delight me:**
- The three fixes ranked by money saved, with the working.
- Each message written in our voice, short, with one button.
- A post-delivery message for first-time bra buyers that helps with fit before they decide to return.
- An honest expected range, not one perfect number.
- Order of work across the 60 days, starting with the cheapest fix.

**What would annoy me:**
- Generic "send a review request" flows that ignore returns.
- Hiding the refund option or making returns harder.
- Body-shaming or "flattering" language.
- Promising delivery dates the courier cannot keep.
- Ignoring the fit finder data.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Overall return rate: 18,640 / 84,000 = 22.2%. Bras are 50% of all returns.
- Bra "too small" returns: 58% x 9,300 = about 5,394.
- Return cost: 18,640 x £7.50 = £139,800 in 90 days.
- "Where is my order" tickets on days 3 to 5: 62% x 9,800 = 6,076, about £19,400 at £3.20 each; all tickets cost about £31,360.
