# Job H04: A new cancellation flow with save offers

**Business:** Babbel (babbel.com), a subscription language-learning app from Berlin with short lessons for learning a new language.

**Role wanted:** cancellation and save desk specialist.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Babbel's real figures, prices or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: encouraging, clear, warm. We say "Pick up where you left off." We never say "Are you sure you want to give up?"
- Reading level: grade 7.
- Avoid: guilt, fake deadlines, hiding the cancel button, more than one extra screen before someone can cancel.
- Principles: respect the learner's time; every screen shows a plain "Cancel my subscription" button.
- We stand for real conversations in a new language, learned in small steps.

**Who we sell to (test):**
1. **Lena, 33, a nurse in Leeds** learning Spanish before a move to Valencia next year.
   - "Night shifts ate my week. I'll be back."
   - "Don't make me hunt for the cancel button."
2. **Greg, 58, retired in Ohio**, learned Italian for a trip.
   - "We're back from Rome, I don't need it right now."
   - "Maybe next spring, for Portugal."

**Data (test):**

One market, October 2026:
- Active subscribers: 400,000. Cancellations started: 24,000 a month (6%).
- Average revenue per subscriber: £9 a month. The 12-month plan costs 45% less per month than the monthly plan.

Why people cancel (one required question in the flow):

| Reason | Share |
|---|---|
| "I don't have time right now" | 31% |
| "It's too expensive" | 22% |
| "I reached my goal or my trip is over" | 18% |
| "I'm not making progress" | 16% |
| "I'm switching to another app" | 8% |
| Other | 5% |

Current flow: after the reason, everyone sees the same offer, 50% off for 3 months. 9% accept it. 61% of those who accept cancel when the discount ends.

Other facts:
- In 2025 we tested a 1-month pause for people who chose "I don't have time right now". 34% took it. 70% of those who paused came back and were still paying 90 days later. The pause was switched off when we changed billing systems and never turned back on.
- Of people who chose "I'm not making progress", 72% never set a lesson reminder. Learners with a reminder complete 2.1 times as many lessons a week.
- Of people who cancelled with "my trip is over" last year, 40% subscribed again within 6 months at full price, with no offer.
- 64% of people choosing "It's too expensive" are on the monthly plan.

Rules: in our markets, cancelling must be as easy as signing up. Legal has asked for no more than one save screen.

**What the owner wants:** "Design the new cancel flow. Tell me what each reason sees, which offer goes to whom, the exact words on each screen, and how many more subscribers we keep each month and what that is worth. One flow my product team can build next sprint."

**Length:** at most 1,000 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
