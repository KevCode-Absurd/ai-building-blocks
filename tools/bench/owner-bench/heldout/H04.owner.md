# H04 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **The best save we ever had is switched off.** The 1-month pause for "no time" kept far more people than the discount: 7,440 "no time" cancellers a month x 34% x 70% is about 1,771 kept at full price, against about 842 kept by today's half-price offer across everyone. A great answer brings the pause back as the main save for "no time", in Lena's words.
2. **One offer for everyone wastes money.** 40% of "trip is over" cancellers come back within 6 months at full price with no offer, so a discount to them is lost margin. A great answer lets them go kindly, offers to pause or remind them before their next trip, and saves discounts for the reasons where they work (for "too expensive", a switch to the cheaper 12-month plan, since 64% of them pay monthly).
3. **"Not making progress" is a product problem.** 72% never set a lesson reminder, and reminders mean 2.1 times the lessons. A great answer offers to set a reminder and a short plan, not a discount.

**What would delight me:**
- One flow with a screen per reason, the exact words, and "Cancel my subscription" visible on every screen.
- The money: subscribers kept per month and the revenue, with working and an honest range.
- Respect for the one-save-screen rule and easy cancelling.
- A note on how we will know it works (what to watch in the first month).
- Copy in our voice that sounds like a friend, not a retention script.

**What would annoy me:**
- Guilt or "Are you sure?" copy, or extra screens.
- Keeping the 50% off for everyone.
- Discounts for people who will come back anyway.
- Revenue maths that counts discounted subscribers at full price.
- A plan with no build order for the team.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Cancellations by reason: no time 7,440; too expensive 5,280; trip or goal over 4,320; not progressing 3,840; switching 1,920; other 1,200 (total 24,000).
- Today's offer: 9% of 24,000 = 2,160 accept; 61% of them cancel later, so about 842 stay.
- Pause for "no time": 7,440 x 34% = about 2,530 pause; x 70% = about 1,771 still paying after 90 days.
- At £9 a month, 1,771 subscribers are worth about £15,900 a month.
