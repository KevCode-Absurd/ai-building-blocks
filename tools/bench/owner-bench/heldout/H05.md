# Job H05: Help centre fixes to cut support tickets

**Business:** Splitwise (splitwise.com), an app that helps friends, housemates and travel groups track shared expenses and settle up, with a free version and a paid Pro plan.

**Role wanted:** help centre documentation writer.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Splitwise's real figures or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: friendly, calm, plain. We say "Here's who owes what, and why." We never say "Simply" or "Just" before a step that confuses people.
- Reading level: grade 6.
- Avoid: blaming the user, support jargon such as "ticket" or "escalation" in articles, screenshots without words.
- Principles: answer in the first two lines; one task per article; numbered steps; the article title uses the words people search for.
- We stand for less awkward money talk between friends.

**Who we sell to (test):**
1. **Jordan, 24, shares a flat in Toronto with three friends.**
   - "Why does the app say I owe Alex when I never borrowed from Alex?"
   - "I deleted the rent by mistake. Help."
2. **Chiara, 41, organises group trips from Milan.**
   - "I paid in euros, they paid in pounds, and now nothing adds up."
   - "Where do I get my Pro refund?"

**Data (test):**

What we found in the tickets and search logs:
- 63% of "balances look wrong" tickets come from groups with "Simplify debts" turned on. "How balances work" does not mention Simplify debts.
- Top help centre searches with no click on any result: "undo delete" 2,300 a month, "restore expense" 1,700, "recover deleted" 900.
- 80% of Pro billing tickets come from people who paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Our team cannot refund those payments; only Apple or Google can.
- The in-app "Help" link opens the help centre home page, not the article for the screen the user is on.

Team: one writer, two weeks. Article changes can go live the same day. In-app link changes need one engineer for one day.

**What the owner wants:** "Tell me which help articles to fix, merge or write first, and write the top three in full, ready to publish. Tell me which in-app links to change. Then tell me how many fewer tickets to expect next month, with your working."

**Length:** at most 1,200 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
