# H05 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **The biggest topic has an article people read but that does not answer them.** "How balances work" gets 21,000 views but only 38% say it helped, and 63% of those tickets (about 2,960) come from groups using Simplify debts, which the article never explains. A great answer rewrites it to explain Simplify debts in the first lines, with Jordan's question as the heading, and links to it from the balance screen.
2. **People cannot find the delete article.** They search "undo delete", "restore expense" and "recover deleted" (4,900 searches a month with no click) while the article is called "Deleting expenses". A great answer retitles or adds a "Restore a deleted expense" article in their words, a cheap same-day fix.
3. **Most billing tickets are ones we cannot solve.** 80% of Pro billing tickets (about 2,000) are App Store or Google Play payments that only Apple or Google can refund. A great answer writes an article that tells people in the first line where to go, with the exact steps, and links it from the Pro screen.

**What would delight me:**
- A ranked list based on tickets we can remove, with working, not on article views.
- Three articles written in full in our voice, answer first, numbered steps.
- The currency conversion article noticed as a gap and planned.
- In-app links to the right article from the right screen.
- An honest expected drop, as a range.

**What would annoy me:**
- "Simply" or blame in the copy.
- Rewriting every article at once with one writer.
- Promising refunds we cannot give.
- A ticket forecast with no working.
- Long articles that bury the answer.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Tickets add up to 18,000; cost about 18,000 x $4.10 = $73,800 a month.
- Simplify debts: 63% x 4,700 = about 2,961 tickets.
