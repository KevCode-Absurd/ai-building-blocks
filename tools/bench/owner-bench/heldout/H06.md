# Job H06: Affiliate programme redesign for next year

**Business:** Podia (podia.com), a platform where creators sell online courses, digital downloads, webinars, coaching and memberships from one place.

**Role wanted:** affiliate marketing manager.

Numbers and notes marked (test) are test inputs for this exercise, not Podia's real figures, terms or guidelines. Treat them as the business's own.

**Brand notes (test):**
- Voice: friendly, plain, cheerful. We say "Everything you need to sell online, in one place." We never say "passive income" or "get rich."
- Reading level: grade 7.
- Avoid: income claims of any kind, "guaranteed", and anything an affiliate could repeat as a promise of earnings.
- Principles: treat partners as people we know by name; every rule fits on one page; disclose affiliate links.
- We stand for creators running a real business without a tech team.

**Who we sell to (test):**
1. **Renee, 36, a yoga teacher in Austin** moving her classes online.
   - "I just want to sell my course without stitching five tools together."
   - "I don't know where to start."
2. **Kwame, 31, a YouTuber in London** who teaches creators how to sell courses and earns from affiliate links.
   - "I recommend what I use, but your payouts are slow."
   - "Half my audience takes two months to decide, and I lose the credit."

**Data (test):**

Programme today:
- 30% recurring commission for the life of the customer.
- Credit window: 30 days from the affiliate's link click to the customer's first payment.
- Payouts by PayPal only, $100 minimum, paid 60 days after the month ends.

Last 12 months:
- 2,400 affiliates signed up. 140 brought at least one paying customer.
- Revenue from referred customers: $2,100,000. Commission paid: $630,000.
- The top 20 affiliates brought 71% of referred revenue. 17 of the 20 teach creators how to sell online.
- Average revenue per customer: $52 a month.
- Monthly churn: referred customers 3.1%; all other customers 4.6%.
- Median time from first visit to first payment, for customers who said in our signup survey that a creator sent them: 41 days. 58% of them paid after day 30 and were credited to no affiliate.
- Affiliate survey, 96 replies: payout speed and minimum were the top complaint (64% of comments).
- Two rival platforms pay 30% recurring; one pays 40% for the first 12 months.

Finance view (test): our finance lead wants to cut commission to 20% from January to save about $210,000 a year.

Team: one partner manager. Budget for new partner perks: $40,000 a year.

**What the owner wants:** "Redesign our affiliate programme for next year. Who we focus on, commission and tiers, what we give partners, and the first 30 days. Tell me straight whether cutting commission to 20% is right, with the numbers. One plan."

**Length:** at most 1,100 words. Deliver the finished work in one go; the owner is not available for questions, so state any assumption you make.
