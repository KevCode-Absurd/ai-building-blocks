# H06 owner notes (judges only)

**Signals in the data:**
1. **Referred customers are worth more, even after commission.** They churn 3.1% a month against 4.6%, so lifetime revenue is about $1,677 against $1,130. After 30% commission (about $503) a referred customer still nets about $1,174, more than a non-referred customer. A great answer says no to the blanket cut to 20%, shows this maths, and notes the risk of losing top affiliates to the rival that pays 40%.
2. **The programme is 20 people.** The top 20 bring 71% of referred revenue (about $1,491,000), and 17 of them teach creators to sell online. A great answer builds a top tier for them (named contact, early access, co-made content, faster payouts) with the $40,000 perk budget, rather than recruiting more of the 2,260 who never refer.
3. **The credit window cuts affiliates out.** The median path is 41 days and 58% of creator-sent customers pay after day 30, credited to no one. A great answer extends the window (for example to 90 days), a cheap fix that keeps partners like Kwame, and fixes payouts (lower minimum, faster schedule, more methods).

**What would delight me:**
- A straight answer on the 20% cut, with the lifetime maths.
- Tiers that reward the top 20 without raising cost across the board.
- The 30-day window and payout fixes in the first 30 days.
- A first-30-days plan the one partner manager can run, with dates.
- Partner rules that keep income claims out of affiliate content.

**What would annoy me:**
- Agreeing to cut commission to save $210,000 without looking at lifetime value.
- Recruiting hundreds more affiliates as the main plan.
- Income or "passive income" language.
- Maths that mixes monthly and lifetime numbers.
- A menu of programme models with no pick.

**Facts a careful answer gets right:**
- Lifetime revenue: $52 / 0.031 = about $1,677 (referred); $52 / 0.046 = about $1,130 (others).
- Lifetime commission at 30%: about $503; net about $1,174.
- Top 20: 71% x $2,100,000 = $1,491,000.
- The 20% cut saves $210,000 a year on today's base (10 points of $2,100,000).
