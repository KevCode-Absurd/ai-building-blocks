# Bombas on TikTok: 30-day launch plan

## The plan on one screen

- **Budget:** $30,000. Set-up (days 1 to 3): $0. Test (days 4 to 10): $5,250. Prove (days 11 to 20): $10,000. Scale (days 21 to 30): $14,750, released only if the day-20 read passes.
- **The line to beat: $38 per new customer.** That is Meta's blended cost today and our 90-day break-even. Under $27.10, TikTok pays back on the first order.
- **What leads:** shift workers on their feet. Our ER nurse and server posts held 11.4 s and 9.6 s average watch time; the studio video, 2.8 s. On Meta, problem-first hooks cost $28.97 per first order; mission-first, $50.85.
- **Bad news:** no pixel yet, so no spend before day 4.
- **This week:** install the TikTok app on Shopify, pass one test order, get Spark codes from both creators, shoot 8 staff videos.

**Assumptions:** creator fees sit outside the $30,000. Repeat orders carry the same $7 fee. Meta budget and offers stay flat for 30 days, so TikTok's effect can be read.

## 1. The numbers behind every rule

| Measure | Working | Value |
|---|---|---|
| First-order break-even | $62 x 55% minus $7 | $27.10 |
| 90-day break-even | $88 x 55% minus $7 x (88 / 62 orders) | $38.46 |
| Target cost per new customer | Meta parity, under $38.46 | $38 |
| Stop line | 1.5 x $38 | $57 |
| Learning bar per ad group | 25 purchases x $38 / 7 days | $136 a day |
| Ad kill spend | 3 x $38 | $114 |

**Projection, not a result:** $30,000 at $38 buys about 790 new customers; at $57, about 525.

## 3. Campaign structure

One manual campaign, so each ad gets a clean read. No Smart+ in month one.

| Setting | Choice |
|---|---|
| Objective | Sales, website, optimised to CompletePayment |
| Bidding | Lowest cost |
| Targeting | US, 25 and up, no interests (both buyers are in their 30s) |
| Exclusions | Existing customers |
| Placement | TikTok only |
| Attribution | 7-day click, 1-day view; judged on store orders |
| Landing page | The page Meta's problem-first ads use; no discount in the ad |
| Naming and tags | Group_Hook_Format_Date; utm_source=tiktok, utm_content = ad name |

**Three broad ad groups at $250 a day:** Shift (creator Spark ads, for Maria), Problem (our phone videos), Family (for Tasha). $250 clears the $136 learning bar and funds 4 ads to $114 each in a week.

## 4. Creative: how much and what kind

**28 ads:** 12 at launch, 8 on day 11, 8 on day 21. Later rounds add three on-feet creators (warehouse, teacher, line cook) and new openings on winners.

Every ad: product on feet in the first 2 seconds, 9:16, captions, under 25 seconds, text in the safe zone. Sound from TikTok's commercial library or original; our colour-swap trend sound is not cleared for ads. "One purchased = one donated." appears only as a closing fact.

| Group | Hook | First 2 seconds | On-screen line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shift | Sock check, hour 12 | Existing ER nurse post | Creator's words |
| Shift | Double-shift kit | Existing server post | Creator's words |
| Shift | Clogs test | Nurse walks a hallway in clogs | "They don't slide down in my clogs." |
| Shift | Hour ten | Server's feet out of shoes after close | "My feet don't ache by hour ten." |
| Problem | Feet after a double | Tired feet up on a break-room chair | "My feet after a double shift" |
| Problem | Lunchtime | Socks on at 7 a.m., cut to a noon walk | "Comfort you notice by lunchtime." |
| Problem | Worth $15? | One pair held to camera | "Pricey, but I'm never going back." |
| Problem | Still up at 5 | Ankle shot at day's end | "Still up at 5 p.m." |
| Family | Recess proof | Kid's feet running out the school door | "My kids' socks actually stay on." |
| Family | Laundry day | Pairs from the dryer, matched by size | "Laundry day, sorted." |
| Family | One drawer | Parent fills a drawer for everyone | "Socks for all of us." |
| Family | Where it goes | Pair on feet, then the donation shelf | "One purchased = one donated." |

Clogs test and Hour ten are new posts from the same two creators, run as Spark ads.

**Left out:** the studio spin (2.8 s watch), the sock-drawer makeover (4.0 s, 22 shares) and offer-first hooks (19% 3-second view rate on Meta, the lowest).

## 6. Kill and scale rules

| When | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ad, $100 spent | Under 4 s average watch and no purchase: cut. Our organic misses held 2.8 to 4.0 s; hits 9.6 to 11.4 s. |
| Ad, $114 spent | No purchase: cut |
| Ad, $190 spent | Cost per purchase over $57: cut |
| Ad group, first 7 days | No budget or targeting edits |
| Ad group, 7-day cost over $57 | Off; budget moves to the best group |
| Ad group, 3-day cost $38 or less, 10+ purchases | Raise 20 to 25%, at most every 3 days |
| Ad, $30 or less per purchase, 10+ purchases | Brief 3 new openings on the same body |
| Winner's cost up 30% on its first week, 3 days running | New hook, same body |

Daily: spend, rejections, TikTok purchases against Shopify orders.

## 7. Day 30: has TikTok earned more budget?

1. **Store tags:** TikTok spend / new customers tagged TikTok (a floor).
2. **Survey:** TikTok spend / (all new customers x share answering TikTok).
3. **Blended lift:** TikTok spend / (new customers in the 30 days minus the 30-day baseline). Meta stays flat, so the lift is TikTok's.

| Two reads agree | Month 2 |
|---|---|
| $38 or less | Earned it. Hold $1,625 a day (about $48,750 a month) and keep the step rule. |
| $38 to $57 | Fix month: $1,000 a day, new spend on new creators and hooks |
| Over $57, not falling | Stop; budget back to Meta's problem-first ads |
| Reads disagree | $1,000 a day until the holdout below settles it |

## Bolder move: a holdout from day 4

Leave matched states, about 10% of US orders, out of TikTok targeting. On day 30, compare their new customers a day with the rest, against baseline: a fourth, cleaner read. **First step:** on day 3 the media buyer picks the states from last-90-day orders. **Risk:** 10% less reach, and the read may be noisy, so it backs up the three reads.
