# Mokobara cabin suitcase: eight Meta concepts

**Make concepts 3, 1 and 4 first.** Last month, men 25 to 40 took 70% of spend (₹4,20,000 of ₹6,00,000) at ₹1,615 a purchase. Women 25 to 40 bought at ₹1,058 on just 18% of spend. Every current ad is the same product on colour, so none says what buyers told you: skip the belt, lift it alone, quiet wheels.

**Two bits of bad news shape the set.** 85 reviews say scuffs show on dark colours, and 60 ask for a front pocket. So lead with lighter colours, never close up on a dark shell, never imply scratch resistance, and never show a laptop going into the front.

## Rules for all eight

- **Sizes:** Reels 9:16 (1080 x 1920), with text clear of the top 14% and bottom 35%. Feed 4:5 (1080 x 1350). Carousel cards 1:1 (1080 x 1080).
- **Button:** Shop Now. **Link:** the cabin suitcase page on mokobara.com.
- **Claims:** the approved specs only. Always "fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits", never "fits every airline". Quiet, light and premium appear only as buyers' own review lines.
- **People on camera** are real buyers, or paid creators labelled as a paid partnership. Persona lines are used as brand copy, never as fake quotes.
- **Assumption:** each concept is one ad plus its two sizes; the price and product page stay as they are today.

## 1. Out before the belt

**First 3 seconds:** Arrivals hall. The belt is empty and a crowd waits. A man in work clothes rolls the suitcase past them toward the exit. On screen: "Out before the belt starts."
**Scenes:** 3 to 8 s, wheels gliding across the floor. 8 to 12 s, he lifts it into a cab with one hand. 12 to 15 s, product, name, price.
**Primary text:** Out of the airport before the belt starts. Flying for work twice a month? Go cabin-only. The Mokobara cabin suitcase fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits. 360-degree spinner wheels. About 2.6 kg. ₹6,999. Built for the way you travel now.
**Headline:** Skip the belt. Go cabin-only. (29)

## 2. No drama at the gate

**Primary text:** "Fit the overhead bin, no drama at the gate." A Mokobara buyer. Our cabin suitcase fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits. Polycarbonate shell, spinner wheels, TSA-style lock. Rated 4.6 stars from 1,150 reviews. ₹6,999.

## 3. Lifted it myself

**First 3 seconds:** A woman in a plane aisle lifts the suitcase into the overhead bin in one move. Nobody helps. On screen: "No asking for help."
**Scenes:** 3 to 6 s, she shuts the bin and sits. 6 to 10 s, product in a light colour, "About 2.6 kg", then price.
**Primary text:** No asking for help at the overhead bin. "Light enough to lift up myself." That's how a buyer put it. The Mokobara cabin suitcase weighs about 2.6 kg, with 360-degree spinner wheels for the long walk to the gate. ₹6,999.

## 4. The 5 am test (bolder concept)

**First 3 seconds:** A dark hallway at 5 am, a bedroom door ajar. The suitcase rolls across the tiles. On screen: "5 am. Sound on."
**Scenes:** 3 to 8 s, close on the wheels in soft light. 8 to 12 s, front door opens to a waiting cab; product, name, price. Record live sound with no edits and no music, so the demo is honest. Captions carry it on mute.
**Primary text:** 5 am. The house is asleep. Listen. "The wheels are so quiet, it just glides." A Mokobara buyer. Cabin suitcase, 360-degree spinner wheels, about 2.6 kg. ₹6,999.
**Headline:** Leave before anyone wakes. (26)
**What it would teach:** quiet wheels are your most-mentioned review theme (290), and no ad uses them. Run it with no age or gender split. Risk: on mute it is a quiet hallway, so the captions must do the work.

## Make these three first

1. **Concept 3, Lifted it myself.** Women 25 to 40 already buy at ₹1,058, 35% below men, with a 1.4% CTR on ads not made for them. Solo women are 22% of buyers, and "can lift it alone" is their top reason. Built for them, this audience has the most room to grow.
2. **Concept 1, Out before the belt.** Men 25 to 40 get 70% of spend at a 0.8% CTR. Work flyers are your largest group (34%), and their top reason is skipping the belt. No current ad says it. This is the biggest fix to the biggest line of spend.
3. **Concept 4, The 5 am test.** It uses your strongest unused proof and shows if a sound-led demo can sell without audience splits.

**How to read them:** give each about 15 purchases' worth of spend before calling it. That is about ₹24,000 for concept 1 (15 x ₹1,615), ₹16,000 for concept 3 (15 x ₹1,058) and ₹23,000 for concept 4 (15 x ₹1,515, last month's blended cost). Total ₹63,000, about a tenth of last month's spend. A concept wins if it beats its audience's current cost per purchase. Stop one early if it spends three times that cost with no purchase.
