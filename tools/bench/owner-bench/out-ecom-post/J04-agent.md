**The plan:** Lead with taste. In the last 60 days, blind taste test openers cost $26 a purchase. Label reads cost $44, yet got the most spend ($24,000). So no script opens on the label; it comes after the sip. Each script answers "$3 a can" on camera, since price is the top doubt in comments (15%).

**Hooks:** Main: "One of these is regular soda. Pick." Alt A: "I made my friend do a blind soda test." Alt B (if true): "Honestly, I couldn't tell which one was regular soda."

**Creator brief:** Film a blind taste test with a real friend at your table or desk. Pour both drinks into plain glasses off camera. Keep the real guess and reaction; film two or three friends and we use the best take. Read the two claim lines as written. Your call: who you test, the banter. Don't coach the guess or show another soda brand.

## Rules for every creator

- **Disclosure:** "Ad" on screen from the first frame, and said once in your own words in the first 12 seconds. Turn on the paid partnership label: a partnership ad on Meta, the branded content toggle on TikTok.
- **Say exactly:** "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours". Price: "$35.99 for 12" or "$3 a can".
- **Never say:** healthy soda, guilt-free, diet, zero sugar, gut health, weight, cure, or another soda brand. No labs or gyms. Taste first, then what's inside.
- **Deliver:** one 9:16 edit, 30 to 45 s, captions burned in, text clear of the top 15%, bottom 25% and right edge. Plus all three hooks as separate takes and all raw clips.
- **Rights and dates:** paid use on Meta and TikTok for 12 months, plus our organic reposts. You grant partnership ad access in your own app. First cut 7 days after the product arrives, one round of changes, final 3 days after notes. Flat fee per video, in your contract.

## Test plan

Same body, three hooks per script: nine ads, one change per pair. Projections from your 60-day numbers:

- Give each ad about $800, room for about 30 purchases at $26. Nine ads cost about $7,200, a tenth of the last 60 days' $72,000.
- After $300, cut any hook under a 19% 3-second view rate (the label-read level).
- After $800, keep ads under $32 a purchase. Scale any near $26.

**Bolder move:** The Script 1 creator answers three real "Vintage Cola or Strawberry Vanilla first?" comments with TikTok reply videos. It tests whether the flavour question (12%) sells packs. First step: pick three comments this week, names hidden. Risk: lower polish, so judge on cost per purchase, not views.
