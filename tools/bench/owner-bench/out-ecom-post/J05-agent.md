# Meta copy set: The Whole Truth protein bars, box of 6

**The call:** launch 4 new ads next to a copy of your live sugar ad, all on one image, so the words are the only thing that changes.

- **Bring back the front-of-pack angle.** It was your best ad by far (₹210 per purchase, 2.1% CTR). It stopped because people saw it 4.2 times, not because it stopped selling. It needs fresh words, not retirement.
- **Test taste.** "Doesn't taste like a protein bar" is your top review phrase (26% of 870 reviews), and no ad has ever led with it.
- **Name the price.** "₹90 a bar is a lot for every day" (9% of reviews) is Priya's real blocker. Saying it first is the bold bet.

**Assumptions:**
1. Limits used: 125 characters for primary text (whole text, so nothing hides behind "See more"), 27 for headlines and descriptions.
2. Test budget is ₹6,000 a day for 14 days, close to your last 90 days' rate of ₹6,333 a day. Target cost per purchase is ₹290, your best live ad.
3. Every ad sends people to the box-of-6 product page, with the Shop Now button.

## How to run and read it

- **Setup:** one Meta A/B test with 5 equal cells: ads A to D, plus a copy of your live "Sweetened only with dates" ad as the control. Same audience for all: urban India, 22 to 40. Run one image in all 5: the bar next to the front of the pack, in 4:5. Keep the text off the image.
- **Budget:** ₹1,200 a day per cell for 14 days. That's ₹16,800 a cell and ₹84,000 in all. At ₹290 per purchase, each cell gets about 58 purchases. At ₹380, it gets about 44. Both are enough to read.
- **Early cut:** stop any cell that spends ₹1,140 (3 × ₹380, your worst live cost) with no purchase.
- **Day 14 read:** an ad wins if its cost per purchase is 20% or more below the control's, with at least 40 purchases. Within 20%, it's a tie: keep the simpler ad running and read again at day 21.
- **Fatigue rule:** swap in a next-in-line ad when an ad's frequency passes 3.5 or its CTR falls 30% below its first week. Your old winner faded at 4.2. Act before that point.
- **Track orders:** give each ad its own UTM content tag (ad-a to ad-d, control) and check store orders against what Meta reports.
