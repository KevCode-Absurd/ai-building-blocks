## Description

**Lose the brick in your back pocket.**

Thick wallets get that way slowly. One more card, a few receipts, cash folded twice. The Hide & Seek keeps what you use and leaves out the rest.

Quick-access slots hold the cards you reach for every day. A hidden pocket keeps spare cards or flat notes out of sight. A small coin pouch takes a few coins.

It stays slim because it is honest about space. Notes lie flat, so it carries about 10 of them, not a fat fold. The card slots start snug and ease over the first week as the leather shapes to your cards. After that, the leather keeps changing with use. As one customer put it: "Looks better after a year."

## FAQ

**How much cash does it hold?**
About 10 notes, laid flat. That is the total, hidden pocket included. It is made for a little cash, not a thick fold, which is how it stays 10 mm thick with 6 cards. If you carry more cash than that, browse our other wallets.

**How many cards will it really hold?**
5 to 12. With 6 cards it is 10 mm (0.4 in) thick. With 12 it is 14 mm (0.55 in). One customer carries 8 and says it still closes flat.

**Will it fit in a front pocket?**
That depends on your pocket and how much you carry. Fewer cards make a thinner fold: 10 mm with 6 cards, 14 mm with 12. For front-pocket carry, keep it close to 6 cards and a few notes.

**Why do the card slots feel tight?**
New leather starts snug. Carry your usual cards in the slots every day and they ease within about a week.

**I'm buying it as a gift. Will it suit him for years?**
If he carries 5 to 12 cards and a little cash, it fits his things. The leather ages well with use: "Looks better after a year," one customer wrote. It also comes with a 3-year warranty.
