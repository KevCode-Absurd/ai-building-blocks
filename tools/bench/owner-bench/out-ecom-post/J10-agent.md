# Plum Goodness: three bundles and the free shipping line

## The decision

- **Launch three routine bundles:** Glow Routine at ₹999, Wash Day Duo at ₹749 and Body Duo at ₹749.
- **Keep free shipping at ₹499.** It sits where COD refusals drop from 29% to 17%, and keeps the serum, your reorder product, free to ship.
- **Offer a lip balm in the cart** under ₹499: +₹0.40 to 0.75 lakh a month (projection).
- **Test before switching for everyone:** bundles in weeks 1 to 4, then a ₹699 line against ₹499 in weeks 5 to 8.

**The bad news**

1. Bundle savings also go to shoppers who already buy these pairs at full price: up to ₹0.74 lakh a month. The bundles must win 136, 153 and 109 new orders a month to break even.
2. 40% of orders (about 6,880 a month) are under ₹499. Their COD refusals cost about ₹1.04 lakh a month in two-way shipping.
3. A ₹699 line could earn ₹2.35 lakh a month or lose ₹0.72 lakh. Only a test can tell.

**This week:** create the three bundle products, set up the 50/50 split, and check lip balm stock for up to 2,200 extra units a month.

**Basis:** about 17,200 orders a month (the pair table implies 16,000 multi-item orders in 90 days, which are 31% of orders). Gross profit is price times category margin, minus ₹70 shipping, plus any ₹49 fee.

- Assumption: lip balm earns the makeup margin (55%).
- Assumption: COD is 40% of orders at every value, and refused parcels go back to stock.
- Assumption: orders spread evenly inside each value band.

## 1. The three bundles

| Bundle | What's in it | Price | Its job | Gross profit per order | Break-even |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glow Routine | Green tea face wash, vitamin C serum, lip balm | ₹999 (worth ₹1,143, save ₹144) | Lead offer for ads. Answers "which face wash goes with the serum" for your top pair (3,100 orders). | ₹537, vs ₹337 for the serum alone | 136 new orders a month (+13% on today's pair) |
| Wash Day Duo | Shampoo, conditioner | ₹749 (worth ₹798, save ₹49) | Lifts the ₹399 shampoo order over ₹500: free shipping, and COD refusals fall from 29% to 17%. | ₹376, vs ₹226 for shampoo alone | 153 a month (+33%) |
| Body Duo | Body wash, body lotion | ₹749 (worth ₹800, save ₹51) | Gives the ₹425 lotion buyer a routine instead of a ₹49 fee. | ₹359, vs ₹234 for lotion alone | 109 a month (+36%) |

Break-even counts new bundle orders a month, from one-item shoppers, needed to pay for the saving given to current pair buyers. Each adds about ₹262, ₹149 or ₹140.

**Why these savings**

- **Glow Routine adds a lip balm instead of cutting the pair's price.** Today's pair buyer pays ₹55 more for a ₹199 lip balm, and you give up only ₹35. A ₹95 cut to ₹849 would cost ₹95 on all 1,033 pair orders a month and need 487 new orders. Lip balm is already your most common add-on.
- **The duos save about 6%.** A 10% cut to ₹719 would need 66% and 74% more pair orders just to break even.
- **"Worth" is the sum of today's real prices.** Each single product keeps its one price.

**Where they go:** a bundle picker under "Add to cart" on each item's page, plus a "Routines" collection.

## 2. Free shipping: keep ₹499

**Today, a month:** shipping costs ₹12.04 lakh (17,200 × ₹70). Fees bring back ₹3.37 lakh (6,880 × ₹49). Net cost is ₹8.67 lakh, about 8% of ₹1.07 crore in sales.

| Free shipping from | Orders that start paying ₹49 | Projected change a month |
|---|---|---|
| ₹499 (keep) | None | ₹0 |
| ₹599 | About 2,320 (orders from ₹499 to ₹598) | −₹0.37 lakh to +₹0.84 lakh |
| ₹699 | About 4,650, including every serum-only order | −₹0.72 lakh to +₹2.35 lakh |

The range assumes that of orders newly charged, 20 to 35% add an item, 55 to 60% pay and 10 to 25% leave.

**Why keep ₹499**

- **It sits on the refusal line.** COD refusals are 29% under ₹500 and 17% above. Free shipping at ₹499 already pulls people across.
- **₹599 is not worth it.** The gain is small, and shampoo plus lip balm (₹598) would fall a rupee short.
- **₹699 puts ₹49 on the serum.** Ishita reorders only the serum and buys one thing at a time because of shipping. Lost reorders would not show in one month's numbers. So test it first.

**Where the money is: the 6,880 orders under ₹499.** Carts from ₹300 to ₹498 get a one-click lip balm, which lifts every top seller over ₹500. Each lifted order earns about ₹60 more if prepaid and ₹91 more if COD, as fewer are refused. If 8 to 15% take it: +₹0.40 to 0.75 lakh a month (projection).

## 3. How to test both safely

| | Test 1: bundles | Test 2: free shipping line |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks | 1 to 4 | 5 to 8, with bundles live for all |
| Split | 50/50 by visitor, at random, same days | Same. If shipping rules can't split by visitor, split pin codes into two halves with matched sales over 90 days. |
| Arms | A: today's site. B: three bundles plus the lip balm cart offer. | A: ₹499. B: ₹699. |
| Main measure | Delivered gross profit per session. A refused COD order counts as no sale and −₹130. | Same |
| Also read | Bundle and pair orders per 1,000 sessions, B against A. The gap is new orders, to compare with break-even. | Serum-only orders per 1,000 sessions |
| Stop early if | After the first full week, B converts 10% below A, or B's COD delivered rate is 3 points lower | Same, or serum-only orders fall 15% |
| Roll out if | B wins on the main measure after 4 full weeks | B wins, and serum-only orders hold within 5% |

Each arm gets about 8,600 orders over four weeks.

**Rules for every test**

- Run full weeks with no sitewide sale. If a sale starts, pause the clock.
- Never show one product at two prices. Bundles are new products, and singles keep their price.
- No countdown timers, and no "only 2 left" unless it is true.
- Show the ₹49 fee and the free shipping line on the product page and cart, never first at checkout.

## 4. Bolder move: a ₹49 COD fee under ₹499

About 2,750 COD orders a month are under ₹499, and about 800 are refused. Charge ₹49 for COD under ₹499, shown on the product page. COD stays free from ₹499. Adding a lip balm then removes both fees.

- **Size (projection):** +₹0.68 to 1.08 lakh a month, if 30% switch to prepaid, 50 to 60% pay the fee and 10 to 20% leave.
- **First step:** run it as Test 3 in weeks 9 to 12, with the same split and stop rules.
- **Risk:** first-time buyers who only trust COD may leave. If new-customer orders fall more than 10%, stop.
