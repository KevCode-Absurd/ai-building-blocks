# Meta budget decisions: next 14 days

## The number that drives every call

A delivered ₹899 order leaves ₹462 after product cost (38%) and ₹95 fees. But 9.9% of orders are refused at the door (45% COD x 22% RTO) at ₹160 each, and about 1% are prepaid returns. So one order placed is worth **₹394**. That is your break-even cost per purchase on Meta's own count.

The catch: Meta claims 1,425 purchases, but Shopify shows 1,020 Meta UTM orders, 72% of the claim. If only those are real, break-even drops to **₹282**. The truth is in between, so I use two lines:

- **Safe line (₹282):** profitable even if Meta over-counts. Scale here.
- **Break-even line (₹394):** profitable only if Meta's count is right. Hold here.

Each ad set gets its own lines, because order value and COD mix change the math. Last 14 days: ₹5,80,000 spent at ₹407 per purchase. On Meta's count the account lost about ₹29,000 on first orders; on UTM numbers, about ₹1,85,000.

## Rules for the team, day to day

1. **Judge on 3-day cost per purchase, never one day.** No edits in the 3 days after a change, unless it spends ₹800 with zero purchases.
2. **Use each ad set's lines.** Under safe: scale. Between: hold. Above break-even for 3 days: fix creative or audience once, then cut.
3. **Scale by 20% at most, once every 3 days.** Never double a budget overnight.
4. **New ads:** pause any ad that spends ₹800 with no purchase.
5. **Never pause a winning ad to "refresh."** Add new ads beside it. Pausing the creator video cost about ₹65,000.
6. **Frequency limits:** prospecting above 3.5 means new creative. Retargeting or DPA above 7 means cut budget 20%.
7. **COD check every Monday** from the courier report. Any ad set above 60% COD or 25% RTO gets fixed: its break-even drops sharply.
8. **Attribution check every Monday:** Shopify Meta-UTM orders divided by Meta purchases. Today it is 72%. If it drops below 65%, lower every safe line by the same share.

## Assumptions

- Refused parcels and prepaid returns come back resellable, so only ₹160 shipping is lost.
- Break-even uses first-order profit only. Repeat blade and refill orders would raise it, but no repeat data was given.
- The 72% UTM match is applied evenly. Retargeting and DPA likely over-count more than prospecting.
- The budget assumes a 30-day month.
