# Bombas TikTok ads: 30-day launch plan

## The plan on one screen

- **Goal:** prove TikTok wins new customers at $38 or less each, level with Meta, on $30,000 over 30 days.
- **Dates:** set-up 16 to 20 November 2026. Day 1 is Monday 23 November, if tracking passes. Day 30 is 22 December; verdict on 23 December.
- **Lead with what sells:** on-feet workers, problem first. On Meta that hook cost $28.97 per first order; mission first cost $50.85. The nurse and server posts drew 13 and 7 times our median organic views.
- **Giving closes every ad as a fact:** "One purchased = one donated." Never as the opener.
- **No discount ads.** Unspent money is fine; budget rises only when the rules say so.

## 3. Campaign structure

**Test campaign, days 1 to 30.** Manual. Sales objective, website, optimise for purchase, lowest-cost bidding. US, age 18+, no interests, TikTok placement only, current customers excluded. Attribution 7-day click, 1-day view (default; check in Ads Manager).

A group needs about 25 purchases in 7 days to learn: 25 x $38 / 7 = $136 a day. So four groups get $200 each, not eight thin ones.

| Ad group | Concept | Ads | Daily |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Worker Spark | Nurse and server posts, run as posted | 2 | $200 |
| 2. Hour-ten sock check (Maria) | On-feet workers check socks after a shift | 3 hooks | $200 |
| 3. Laundry day (Tasha) | Family socks out of the wash, still paired, still on | 3 hooks | $200 |
| 4. Feet first | Sock on a real foot in second one | 3 hooks | $200 |

**Scale campaign, from day 15.** One broad ad group with every ad that passes the scale rule, on the same post IDs so likes carry over. Test keeps $300 a day for new ads.

Runner-up: automated Smart+ from day 1. It hides which hook works, so it waits for month two.

## 4. Creative: how much and what kind

**Volume:** 11 ads at launch (2 Spark, 9 new). Then 6 new ads in each of weeks 2, 3 and 4 (2 concepts x 3 hooks): 29 ads in 30 days. Next up: more on-feet jobs (teacher, barista) and an honest price hook: "Yes, they cost more. Here's hour ten."

| Group | Hook | First 2 seconds | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Scrub pull | Nurse lifts a scrub leg after a shift; sock still up | Hour ten. Sock check. |
| 2 | Clog test | Feet slip out of clogs; socks stay put | No sliding. Even in clogs. |
| 2 | Double shift | Server kicks off shoes after close | My feet after a double shift |
| 3 | Laundry pile | Hand pulls a matched pair from clean laundry | Out of the wash. Still a pair. |
| 3 | School pickup | Kid runs out of school, socks still up | Their socks actually stay on. |
| 3 | Whole crew | Four pairs of feet on one couch | Socks for the whole crew |
| 4 | Second one | Sock slides onto a foot | Comfort you notice by lunchtime. |
| 4 | Lunch check | Office worker wiggles toes at lunch | Lunchtime. Still comfy. |
| 4 | Cuff snap | Hands stretch the cuff; it snaps back | Made to stay up |

**Every ad:**

- 9:16, 15 to 25 seconds, captions on, text in the safe zone, ending on "One purchased = one donated."
- Socks on real feet in the first 2 seconds. No studio spins: our studio post held 2.8 seconds.
- Sound from TikTok's commercial music library or original voice. The colour-swap post's trend sound is not cleared for ads.
- Creators speak from their own use, not a review to read.
- Never: "cheap socks", "shop guilt-free", "medical-grade", "cures foot pain", "best socks in the world", or other giving numbers.
- No AI-made people or voices. Paid creators turn on the paid-partnership label.
- New creator deals include 12 months of paid usage and raw files, so winners outlive the March licences.

## 6. Kill and scale rules

**Kill** (media buyer checks daily at 10 a.m. Eastern and logs each call)

1. Ad spent $60, average watch time under 3 seconds: pause.
2. Ad spent $114 (3 x target), no purchase: pause.
3. Ad spent $228, cost per purchase above $57: pause.
4. Ad group above $57 per purchase after 7 days: off, budget to the best group.
5. TikTok shows zero or double purchases against Shopify's tagged orders for 24 hours: pause all, fix tracking.

**Hands off:** only kills in a group's first 7 days.

**Scale**

1. An ad with 10 or more purchases at $38 or less over 3 or more days moves to the scale campaign.
2. Each step in section 5 goes ahead only if the last 3 days ran at $38 or less. From $38 to $57, hold. Above $57, drop back one step.
3. Every Monday, log the gap between TikTok's purchase count and Shopify's TikTok-tagged orders.

## 7. Day 30: has TikTok earned more budget?

TikTok over-counts and tagged orders under-count. Two of three reads must agree.

| Read | How we work it out |
|---|---|
| A. Tagged orders | $30,000 / new Shopify customers with a TikTok UTM or click ID. A floor |
| B. Survey | TikTok's share of answers x all new customers. Compare with its 32% share of paid spend ($30,000 of $95,000) |
| C. Lift | New customers above baseline, adjusted by last year's jump over the same dates |

| Two reads show | New customers for $30,000 | Month two |
|---|---|---|
| $38 or less each | 790 or more | **Earned more.** Raise to $40,000 in weekly steps; add a Smart+ test |
| $38 to $57, or reads disagree | 527 to 789 | **Hold** at $30,000, fix creative, run a 4-week geo holdout |
| Above $57, no fall in the last 10 days | Under 527 | **Stop.** Keep posting organically |
