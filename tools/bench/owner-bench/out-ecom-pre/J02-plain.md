# Bombas TikTok Ads: 30-Day Launch Plan

## The numbers that set every rule

- **Profit on a first order:** $62 x 55% = $34.10, minus $7 fees = **$27.10**.
- **Profit on a new customer over 90 days:** $88 x 55% = $48.40, minus about $9.90 fees (about 1.4 orders) = **about $38.50**.
- **Target cost per first order: $38.** It matches Meta and pays back inside 90 days: we lose about $11 on the first order and earn it back on repeat buys.
- **What Meta already tells us about hooks:**

| Hook | Cost per first order |
|---|---|
| Problem first | $29 |
| Product first | $40 |
| Offer first | $42 |
| Mission first | $51 |

Organic TikTok agrees: on-feet worker posts held 11.4 s and 9.6 s; the studio spin held 2.8 s. We lead with real feet and real problems, not discounts or the mission.

**Assumptions:** the $30,000 is media only; creator fees are separate. "First order" means a Shopify new-customer order. The creator licences (to March) cover all 30 days.

## Week 0: account and tracking set-up (before any spend)

1. **Business Center.** Create it on a company email with two admins. One ad account in USD, US Eastern time, with a backup card.
2. **Pixel and Events API.** Install the TikTok Shopify sales channel app. Turn on the pixel, the Events API and advanced matching.
3. **Events.** Confirm ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout and CompletePayment fire with value and currency. Place two test orders. Check browser and server events share an event ID so purchases are not counted twice.
4. **Domain.** Verify bombas.com.
5. **Spark Ads.** Link the Bombas TikTok account. Get Spark codes from the nurse and server creators. Diary the March end date.
6. **Own tracking.** UTM tags on every ad. Add "TikTok" to a post-purchase "How did you hear about us?" survey.
7. **Comments.** Turn on keyword filters. One person checks daily.
8. **Lead time.** Upload launch ads by Day -2 so ad review does not eat Day 1.

## Campaign structure

**Campaign 1: TEST** (manual, optimise for CompletePayment, ad group budgets)
- AG1 Worker comfort: $200 a day. Concepts 1 and 2.
- AG2 Family: $200 a day. Concept 3.
- AG3 Real day plus mission: $200 a day. Concepts 4 and 5.
- All ad groups: US, 21+, broad, no interests. Creative does the targeting.
- Attribution: 7-day click, 1-day view.

**Campaign 2: SCALE** (opens Day 8; Smart+ or broad campaign budget, optimise for CompletePayment)
- Only ads that pass the scale rule. Reuse the same post IDs so likes and comments carry over.

**No retargeting in month one:** the pool is too small.

## Creative: how much and what kind

**Launch with 15 ads across 5 concepts. Then add 6 new ads each week from Week 2.** That is about 33 ads in 30 days.

Rules for every ad:
- Product on real feet in the first 2 seconds.
- 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds, captions on, native sound.
- Grade 6 to 7 words. Warm and plain.
- Giving appears only as "One purchased = one donated." Said as a fact, never as guilt.
- Never: "cheap socks", "shop guilt-free", "medical-grade", "cures foot pain", "best socks in the world", or any other giving number.
- Creators speak from their own use. We never script results they have not had.

| # | Concept | Ads | What it looks like | Hook idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shift check | 5 | 2 Spark Ads (nurse, server) plus 3 new on-feet workers: nurse, warehouse, teacher | "Sock check. Hour twelve." |
| 2 | The slide test | 3 | Worker in clogs or work boots, shot at start and end of shift | "Do your socks slide down in your clogs too?" |
| 3 | Family wash test | 3 | Parent with kids' socks after many washes | "These have been through a lot of laundry." |
| 4 | Real-day close-up | 2 | On-feet cushion and heel close-ups in a real home or workplace, not a studio | "Comfort you notice by lunchtime." |
| 5 | Mission as a fact | 2 | Worker hook first, then "One purchased = one donated" near the end | Same as Concept 1 |

**Handling price.** Price stops both Maria and Tasha, but do not lead with "20% off" ($42 per order on Meta vs $29 for problem-first). Answer price inside the ad: how many shifts or washes they last. Keep any offer on the landing page.

**Landing pages.** Worker ads go to the cushioned sock page, family ads to kids' and family packs (assumed to exist; else the best-sellers page).

**Skip** the studio spin, sock drawer and trend sound posts as ads. Watch times of 2.8 s to 4.0 s are too low.

**Weekly refresh:** 3 new hooks on the best concept, 2 new creators, 1 new concept. Own the rights to at least half, so winners are ready before the March licences end.

## Kill and scale rules

Check daily at 10 a.m.

**Kill an ad when:**
- It spends $76 (2x target) with no purchase.
- It has spent $150 or more and costs over $57 per order (1.5x target).
- Its 2-second view rate is in the account's bottom third after 5,000 impressions. Re-cut the opening and relaunch.

**Fix an ad group when:**
- Cost per order is over $50 after 7 days. Halve its budget and add new ads. Still over $50 three days later: pause.

**Scale an ad when:**
- It has 10+ purchases at $34 or less. Move it to SCALE and make 3 new hook versions for TEST.

**Scale a budget when:**
- An ad group or SCALE is at $34 or less for 3 days running. Raise budget 20%, then wait 48 hours. Never raise more than 20% at once.

**Refresh when:**
- Click-through rate falls 30% below its first 3 days and frequency tops 3.

## Day 30: has TikTok earned more budget?

Judge on Days 15 to 30. Days 1 to 14 are learning.

| Measure | Pass | Hold | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per first order, Days 15 to 30 | $38 or less | $39 to $45 | Over $45 |
| First orders in 30 days | 790 or more ($30,000 / $38) | 670 to 789 | Under 670 |
| Winning ads not on licensed posts | 3 or more | 1 to 2 | 0 |
| Store new customers vs prior 30 days, Meta spend flat | Up by at least 70% of TikTok-claimed orders | Up, but less | Flat |

The last row checks TikTok adds new customers rather than taking credit for Meta's. Keep Meta spend flat so it stays clean. Survey answers are a second check.

**What each result means:**
- **Pass on cost and new customers:** Month 2 goes to $45,000 (up 50%) plus a small retargeting ad group.
- **Hold:** $30,000 again, with 40% on new creative tests.
- **Fail:** $10,000 a month for creative testing only. Review on Day 60.
