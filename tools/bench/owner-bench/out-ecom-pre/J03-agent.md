# Eight Meta ad concepts for the Mokobara cabin suitcase

**The main bet:** your ads look alike because each one is the same picture, the bag on a colour background. These eight put the bag in real travel moments and give each buyer group its own reason to buy, taken from your reviews and survey.

**Make first:** concepts 4, 1 and 3. Reasons are at the end.

## Rules for every ad

- **Claims:** copy uses only the approved specs: polycarbonate shell, 360-degree spinner wheels, TSA-style lock, about 2.6 kg, fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits, ₹6,999. "Quiet", "light" and "looks" appear only as quoted reviews.
- **Page and button:** the product page, Shop Now. Description on every ad: "Cabin spinner. ₹6,999" (21 characters).
- **Sizes:** Feed 4:5 (1440 x 1800). Reels 9:16 (1440 x 2560), with text and logo out of the top 14%, bottom 35% and 6% each side. Captions burned into every video.
- **Look:** the bag in frame and in focus from the first second. Light shell colours on camera, since 85 reviews say scuffs show on dark ones. Never show a front laptop pocket; 60 reviews ask for one.

## The eight concepts

### 1. Out before the belt
- **For:** work flyers like Karan, men 25 to 40. Problem aware.
- **Angle:** time saved by flying cabin-only.
- **Hook (0 to 3 s):** a crowd waits at the baggage belt. He walks past, bag rolling beside him. On screen: "Out before the belt starts."
- **Format and visual:** Reel, 15 seconds, handheld. Belt, walk past, exit doors, bag into a cab boot. Plus 4:5.
- **Primary text:** "Out before the belt starts. Our cabin spinner fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits, so your bag stays with you. Land, walk out, get to the meeting." (Hook line: 121 characters.)
- **Headline:** Skip the baggage belt (21)
- **Tests:** whether a real airport scene lifts men 25 to 40 above a 0.8% click rate.

### 2. Will it pass the gate?
- **For:** Karan, solution aware: already cabin-only, choosing a bag.
- **Angle:** removes the gate-check fear.
- **Hook:** "Fit the overhead bin, no drama at the gate."
- **Format and visual:** static 4:5 and 9:16. The bag standing in an airline cabin sizer at a gate. On image: "Fits most Indian domestic cabin limits," with stars and the quote.
- **Primary text:** "\"Fit the overhead bin, no drama at the gate.\" 210 of our reviews say the bag fit the cabin with no gate trouble. Check your airline's limit, then skip the check-in queue."
- **Headline:** Sized for the cabin (19)
- **Tests:** naming the fear against concept 1's speed promise, same audience.

### 3. 5 am, no rattle
- **For:** everyone, led by solo women like Meera. Unaware.
- **Angle:** the sound of the wheels, your top review theme.
- **Hook:** a dark building corridor at 5 am. The only sound is a soft roll. On screen: "5 am. Building asleep."
- **Format and visual:** Reel, 10 to 12 seconds, no music, captions on. Low shot following the wheels across tiles, into the lift, out to a cab.
- **Primary text:** "\"The wheels are so quiet, it just glides.\" 290 of our reviews mention quiet, smooth wheels. 360-degree spinners for early flights and sleeping neighbours."
- **Headline:** Built for early flights (23)
- **Tests:** whether your biggest review theme, unused in ads, beats feature-led ads.

### 4. Into the bin, by myself
- **For:** women travelling solo like Meera, women 25 to 40. Problem aware.
- **Angle:** handle it alone.
- **Hook:** one unbroken take. She lifts the bag into the overhead bin, shuts it, sits. On screen: "No asking for help."
- **Format and visual:** Reel, 8 seconds, one take, plus 4:5. A plane aisle, or a cabin set if filming on board is not allowed.
- **Primary text:** "\"Light enough to lift up myself.\" About 2.6 kg. Up into the bin, no asking for help. Pack for the weekend and handle every step yourself."
- **Headline:** Made for solo weekends (22)
- **Tests:** whether women 25 to 40 can take more budget at or below their ₹1,058 cost per purchase.

### 5. "Where's it from?"
- **For:** couples on weekend trips, 29% of buyers, who chose it for looks. Solution aware.
- **Angle:** looks, with other people noticing.
- **Hook:** "People ask where it's from."
- **Format and visual:** static 4:5 and 9:16. A couple checking in at a hill-station homestay. Two bags in two colours by the desk, sharp in the foreground.
- **Primary text:** "\"People ask where it's from.\" Two cabin spinners, two colours, one long weekend. A TSA-style lock on each. Thoughtful design for the modern Indian traveller."
- **Headline:** Made for weekends for two (25)
- **Tests:** your current looks angle, but in a real moment instead of on a colour background.

### 6. 1,150 reviews in one swipe
- **For:** site visitors and video viewers. Most aware.
- **Angle:** proof in numbers, with the price.
- **Hook:** card 1: "4.6 stars from 1,150 reviews."
- **Format and visual:** carousel, 5 cards, 1:1. Card 1: the bag on a station platform with the rating. Cards 2 to 5: one theme each with its count and review line over a matching scene: wheels (290), cabin fit (210), lifting (160), looks (150).
- **Primary text:** "4.6 stars from 1,150 reviews. What reviewers mention most: quiet wheels, a cabin fit, and lifting it themselves. ₹6,999, with warranty and returns as on our site."
- **Headline:** 4.6 stars, 1,150 reviews (24)
- **Tests:** stacked proof against single benefits for warm visitors.

### 7. The gift that flies with them
- **For:** gift buyers, 15% of buyers, who chose it for brand and looks. Solution aware.
- **Angle:** a gift for someone who travels.
- **Hook:** "For the one who's always flying out."
- **Format and visual:** static 4:5 and 9:16. A living room: the bag with a ribbon, a hand on the handle, a boarding pass on top. Brand name clear.
- **Primary text:** "For the one who's always flying out. A cabin spinner they will use on every trip, at ₹6,999. Polycarbonate shell, ready for their next booking."
- **Headline:** A gift for every trip (21)
- **Tests:** whether a gift message opens a new buyer group at a workable cost.

### 8. Four details, fifteen seconds
- **For:** comparers who doubt ₹6,999, Karan's second worry. Product aware.
- **Angle:** design, shown up close.
- **Hook:** macro shot of the lock clicking shut. On screen: "Four details. 15 seconds."
- **Format and visual:** Reel, 15 seconds, one detail every 3 to 4 seconds. The shell in window light, the lock set, the wheels spun by hand, the bag on a luggage scale showing its real weight.
- **Primary text:** "Four details, fifteen seconds. Polycarbonate shell. TSA-style lock. 360-degree spinner wheels. About 2.6 kg. Built for the way you travel now."
- **Headline:** Every detail, considered (24)
- **Tests:** whether design detail answers the price doubt.

## Make these three first

1. **Concept 4.** Women 25 to 40 already buy at ₹1,058, about a third below men, yet get 18% of spend. Lifting it alone is their top survey reason and 160 review mentions.
2. **Concept 1.** Men 25 to 40 take 70% of spend, so a small gain there moves the most money. Their 0.8% click rate says today's ads do not stop them. Work flyers are your biggest group (34%), and skipping the belt is their top reason. If it gets clicks but few sales, make concept 2 next.
3. **Concept 3.** Quiet wheels are your biggest review theme, 290 mentions, and no current ad shows them. It fits every group.

## How to test them

- One campaign, four ad sets: the three concepts plus one current colour-background ad as control. Same dates, ages 25 to 40, past buyers excluded. Leave out 41 and over (₹2,188 a purchase).
- ₹1,100 a day per ad set for 7 days: ₹7,700 each, about five times your blended ₹1,515 cost per purchase. ₹30,800 in total, about 5% of last month's ₹6,00,000.
- Read on day 8, at ad set level, 7-day click. 6 or more purchases (under ₹1,515 each): keep and scale. 4 or 5: one more week. 3 or fewer: cut. Clicks without sales are not a win.
- Before scaling, check store orders agree with Meta.

## Assumptions and to confirm

- No discount, since the brief gives none. With no margin given, the bar is today's blended cost per purchase.
- Pull each review quote word for word before it runs, with the reviewer's permission where a name shows.
- Confirm the bag's exact size and which airline sizers it fits before the concept 2 shoot.
- Confirm light colours are in stock for concepts 3 and 4.
- Update the rating and count in concept 6 on launch day.
