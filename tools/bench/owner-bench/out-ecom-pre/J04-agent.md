# Olipop variety pack: three creator video ads

Three ready-to-shoot scripts for Meta Reels and TikTok, 9:16, each 35 to 45 seconds. All three sell the 12-can variety pack: $35.99, so $3.00 a can, with 3 cans each of Vintage Cola, Strawberry Vanilla, Classic Root Beer and Orange Squeeze.

Each ad leads with taste, then what's inside, then the offer. The angles come from your own numbers:

| Ad | Angle | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blind taste test | Your best opening: 33% 3-second views, $26 per purchase. 21% of comments say it tastes like real soda. |
| 2 | The 3 pm swap | Pour-and-swap opening: 28% and $32. "Afternoon soda habit" is your top comment theme (23%). It also answers price (15%). |
| 3 | Game-day cooler | For parents like Marcus. Uses "Kids and family" (9%) and "Which flavour first" (12%). |

The label read (19%, $44) and the brand story (17%, $51) were your weakest openings. So no ad opens on them. The label shows up mid-ad, after taste.

**Assumptions:** each creator has drunk Olipop for at least two weeks; the pack is live at $35.99; spoken lines are guides, so creators use their own words. Lines marked **exact** must be said or shown word for word.

## Ad 1: "Can you tell?" Blind taste test (40 s)

For: Dana, who thinks low-sugar swaps taste like chemicals. Setting: a bright home kitchen, creator and a real friend.

| Time | Shot | Spoken (guide) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Close: friend, eyes shut, two plain glasses | "One of these is a new kind of soda. Which one?" | Blind soda test. Can you tell? |
| 3-12 s | Two-shot: friend sips A, then B | Friend reacts in their own words | Glass A or glass B? |
| 12-18 s | Creator turns the Vintage Cola can to camera | Real reaction to the reveal | It's Olipop Vintage Cola |
| 18-27 s | Close on can, creator taps label | **Exact:** "It has less sugar than classic soda. And it contains plant fiber and prebiotics." | Less sugar than classic soda |
| 27-36 s | Creator opens the box, friend picks a second can | "Four flavours in here. She's going for Strawberry Vanilla." | 12 cans, 4 flavours |
| 36-40 s | Chest up, can in hand | **Exact:** "The variety pack is $35.99 for 12. Link's below." | $35.99 for 12 cans |

**Hooks to test**
- **Main:** the cold open above, friend mid-sip.
- **Alt A (story):** "I told my friend she'd never pass this soda test."
- **Alt B (call-out):** "If low-sugar soda always tastes off to you, try this blind."

**Creator brief:** Pour both glasses off camera. The second glass is a regular soda in a plain cup; never show or say its brand. Do not script the friend's guess. Film the real result, even if they get it right: "Okay, close, but I'd still drink it" is fine. Keep cuts every 3 seconds or so.

## Ad 2: "My 3 pm soda" swap (40 s)

For: Dana, who needs her afternoon soda and balks at $3 a can. Setting: a real desk, afternoon light, laptop open.

| Time | Shot | Spoken (guide) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Close: pouring regular soda from a plain cup, then stops | "Wait. Not today." | My 3 pm soda, swapped |
| 3-9 s | Creator at desk, slides the cup away, grabs Orange Squeeze | "Every day at 3 pm I need a soda. That part's not changing." | Every. Single. Day. |
| 9-17 s | Close: crack the can, pour over ice, sip | Honest taste words: fizz, flavour, how it compares to their old soda | Still tastes like soda |
| 17-25 s | Close on can | **Exact:** "It has real fruit juice and botanical flavours. Less sugar than classic soda." | Less sugar than classic soda |
| 25-35 s | Chest up, direct to camera | Price, said plainly: "Yes, it's about $3 a can. For my one soda a day, it's worth it to me." | $3.00 a can |
| 35-40 s | Box on desk, four flavours lined up | **Exact:** "Get the variety pack, 12 cans for $35.99. Link below." | 12 cans, $35.99 |

**Hooks to test**
- **Main:** the stopped pour above.
- **Alt A (call-out):** "If you have a 3 pm soda, this is for you."
- **Alt B (objection first):** "$3 a can? Here's why I pay it."

**Creator brief:** This is your real afternoon routine, so shoot it at your real desk. Say the price line only if you mean it; if not, say the price and skip the opinion. The old soda stays unbranded. Do not call it a habit you "fixed" or "quit".

## Ad 3: Game-day cooler (45 s)

For: Marcus, a parent who packs drinks for kids' games. Setting: kitchen counter, then the sideline or car trunk.

| Time | Shot | Spoken (guide) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Overhead: hands pack Olipop cans into a cooler | "Packing the game-day cooler. And yes, it's soda." | Game-day soda, packed |
| 3-12 s | Sideline: kids' hands grab cans after the game | Real kid reactions, unscripted | The flavour fight begins |
| 12-22 s | Creator holds up each can in turn | "Okay, ranking all four. Strawberry Vanilla, Vintage Cola, Root Beer, Orange Squeeze." Creator gives their real order. | Our ranking: (their real order) |
| 22-32 s | Close on label, creator talking | "I read the label first." **Exact:** "Less sugar than classic soda. Contains plant fiber and prebiotics." | Read the label |
| 32-40 s | Creator to camera at car trunk | Their own words on why it works for game days | 3 of each flavour |
| 40-45 s | Box in trunk | **Exact:** "12-can variety pack, $35.99. Link below." | 12 cans, $35.99 |

**Hooks to test**
- **Main:** the cooler pack above.
- **Alt A (question):** "Which flavour would you grab first?" Hold up two cans.
- **Alt B (story):** "I read the label in the store, then bought a case." Use only if it happened.

**Creator brief:** Use your real family's real ranking. Film kids from behind or hands only, unless every parent signs a release. Kids say only what they really say; never give them lines. No talk of health, energy or behaviour.

## For every creator

**Must say or show, word for word:** the approved claims ("contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours") and the price, "$35.99 for 12 cans".

**Never say:** "healthy soda", "guilt-free", "diet", "zero sugar", any gut, health, cure or weight claim, or any other soda brand's name. No lab or gym settings.

**Your freedom:** your own words, jokes and pace. Taste first, label second.

**Disclosure:** turn on the Paid partnership label on Instagram and TikTok. Say "ad" or show "#ad" in the first 3 seconds.

**Deliver:** one edited 9:16 video with captions burned in, all three hook takes, and all raw clips. Keep text out of the bottom fifth and right edge so platform buttons don't cover it. Frame centred so a 4:5 crop works.

**Terms to agree in writing before filming:** paid use on Meta and TikTok for 12 months (my proposed term), partnership ad access on Instagram and a TikTok ad code, one revision round, and the fee and dates you set.

## How to test the hooks

Run all three hooks per ad at the same time, on the same body, same budget. Read the 3-second view rate first, against your best 33%. Then call cost per purchase only after about 20 purchases per hook, my working rule. At your best past $26, that is about $520 per hook. Keep hooks at or under $32, your second-best past opening; cut the rest.
