# Three UGC video ad scripts: Olipop 12-can variety pack

**Placement:** Meta Reels and TikTok, vertical 9:16.
**Offer:** 12-can variety pack, $35.99 ($3.00 a can). Three each of Vintage Cola, Strawberry Vanilla, Classic Root Beer and Orange Squeeze.

## Assumptions

- Each script runs 38 to 40 seconds, so the editor can trim to 30.
- Every ad links to the variety pack page. There is no discount, so we state the price plainly.
- Openings follow your last 60 days. Blind taste test ($26 per purchase) and pour-and-swap ($32) lead. Label reading ($44) and founder story ($51) never open an ad. The label only shows up after the taste moment.
- "Regular soda" on camera means a plain cup or a can with the label turned away.

## Rules for every script

- Taste first, then what's inside.
- Use the approved claims word for word: "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours".
- Never say: healthy, guilt-free, diet, zero sugar, or anything about health, gut or weight. No other soda brand names or logos in shot.
- Real kitchens, desks and sidelines in daylight. No gyms, no lab coats.
- Disclosure: run as a partnership ad from the creator's handle. Turn on Meta's "Paid partnership" label and TikTok's branded content toggle. Start the caption with "#ad".
- Every ad ends on "A new kind of soda."
- Reactions must be real. If something does not happen on camera, do not say it did.

## Script 1: "Can you tell?" (blind taste test)

**Angle:** It tastes like real soda (21% of comments). **For:** Dana. **Length:** 38 s.

| Time | Shot | Spoken (guide) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Two plain cups on a kitchen counter. Friend with eyes shut. | Creator: "One is regular cola. One isn't. Go." | Can you tell which one? |
| 3-10 s | Friend sips cup A, then cup B. Stay on her face. | Friend reacts in her own words. | Cup A or Cup B? |
| 10-16 s | Friend makes her guess. Creator laughs. | Friend gives her real answer. | Her guess, as said |
| 16-22 s | Creator pulls a Vintage Cola can from behind her back. | "That one was Olipop Vintage Cola." | Vintage Cola |
| 22-30 s | Pour over ice, fizz close-up, then the can side. | "It has less sugar than classic soda. And it contains plant fiber and prebiotics." | Less sugar than classic soda. Plant fiber + prebiotics |
| 30-38 s | Open variety box, four flavours lined up. | "There are four flavours. Strawberry Vanilla is next." | 12 cans, 4 flavours, $35.99. A new kind of soda. |

**Hooks to test**
- Main: "One of these is regular cola. Can you tell?"
- Alt A: Start mid-sip, eyes shut. Friend: "Wait, which one is the real soda?"
- Alt B: Text only over the two cups: "Testing my two-sodas-a-day friend."

**Creator brief:** Film at your kitchen counter with a friend who drinks regular soda. Pour both drinks off camera into matching cups. Do not tell your friend which is which. Keep her honest reaction, even if she guesses right. If she does, pivot: "Okay, but which one do you want more of?" Say the two claims after the reveal, not before. Keep it bright and fun. Do not name the other cola.

## Script 2: "My 3 pm soda" (pour and swap)

**Angle:** Afternoon soda habit (23%) plus price (15%). **For:** Dana. **Length:** 38 s.

| Time | Shot | Spoken (guide) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Desk, laptop clock reads 2:59. Creator lifts a plain regular soda can. | "It's 3 pm. You know what that means." | My 3 pm soda... |
| 3-8 s | Stops, slides the can out of frame, picks up Strawberry Vanilla. | "But I swapped it." | ...got a swap |
| 8-16 s | Pours over ice. Fizz close-up. First sip, real reaction. | "Still fizzy. Still sweet. Still my treat." | Same fizz. Same 3 pm break. |
| 16-24 s | Holds the can to camera, taps the side. | "Less sugar than classic soda. Real fruit juice and botanical flavours." | Less sugar than classic soda |
| 24-32 s | Leans in, honest tone. | "Is it $3 a can? Yes. It's my one soda of the day, so I want a good one." | $35.99 for 12. $3 a can. |
| 32-38 s | Variety box on the desk, four cans out. | "Twelve cans, four flavours. Start with this one." | A new kind of soda. Shop the variety pack. |

**Hooks to test**
- Main: "It's 3 pm. You know what that means."
- Alt A: Creator about to sip a regular soda, stops: "Wait. Not that one."
- Alt B: Text over the desk shot: "POV: your 3 pm soda, with less sugar."

**Creator brief:** Film at your real desk mid-afternoon. Show the swap in one smooth move. Hide the regular soda label. Talk about the price head on. It costs $3 a can, so say why it's worth it to you in your own words. Only say "I used to drink two a day" if that is true for you. Pick the flavour you like best for the final shot.

## Script 3: "Cooler check" (game day family)

**Angle:** Kids and family (9%) plus which flavour first (12%). **For:** Marcus. **Length:** 40 s.

| Time | Shot | Spoken (guide) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Dad at the kitchen counter, cooler open, holds up Orange Squeeze. | "My kids think this is regular soda." | Game day cooler check |
| 3-10 s | Kids each grab a can from the variety box. Quick cuts. | Kids call out flavours in their own words. | Which flavour first? |
| 10-18 s | Sideline or park bench after the game. Kids sip and react. | Dad says what really happens, like who grabs which flavour. | Real reactions |
| 18-27 s | Dad alone, turns the can, reads the side. | "I read the label first. Less sugar than classic soda. Real fruit juice and botanical flavours." | Less sugar than classic soda |
| 27-35 s | Dad holds up the box. | "Twelve cans, three of each flavour. So nobody fights." | Vintage Cola, Strawberry Vanilla, Classic Root Beer, Orange Squeeze |
| 35-40 s | Dad cracks a Vintage Cola for himself. | "And one for me." | A new kind of soda. $35.99 for 12. |

**Hooks to test**
- Main: "My kids think this is regular soda."
- Alt A: Kids blind taste test at the counter. Dad: "Real soda or not? Go."
- Alt B: Text over cans dropping into the cooler: "Packing drinks for game day."

**Creator brief:** Film your own family on a real game day. Get a signed parent consent for every child on camera. Let the kids pick and react freely. Do not script their lines. The label moment comes after the kids, never first. Say the claims as written. Do not say anything about what the soda does for the kids' bodies. End on you enjoying one too.

## How to test the hooks

Shoot each script once, then cut three versions that differ only in the first 3 seconds. Run all nine at the same budget. After about a week, compare 3-second view rate and cost per purchase. Keep the winning hook for each angle and use it to brief the next round of creators.
