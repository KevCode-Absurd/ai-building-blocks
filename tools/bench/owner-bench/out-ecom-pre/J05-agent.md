# Meta copy set: The Whole Truth protein bars, box of 6

Your best ad was never beaten. It wore out. "Flip the pack" had your top CTR (2.1%) and lowest cost per purchase (₹210). You paused it at frequency 4.2, so the message still works. People just saw it too often.

So the first test brings that message back in new words. It runs against taste: the line in more than 1 in 4 of your 870 reviews, and an angle no ad has tried yet.

## Launch these 4 first

Two pairs. Inside each pair, only the primary text changes. The headline, description, image and audience stay the same, so the result tells you which message sells.

| Ad | Copy | Pair | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | P1 + H2 + D1 | Gym buyer | Does your best message still win in new words? Its old mark to beat is ₹210 a purchase. |
| A2 | P2 + H2 + D1 | Gym buyer | Does taste proof beat label honesty? Taste answers the "tastes like chalk" fear, and no past ad used it. |
| A3 | P5 + H3 + D1 | Daily snack buyer | Does the 4 pm moment work once it has review proof? Your old office ad had none and cost ₹380 a purchase. |
| A4 | P6 + H3 + D1 | Daily snack buyer | Does naming ₹90 a bar up front help or hurt? 9% of reviews call it a lot for every day. This ad faces that head on. |

P3 and P4 wait because their angles are already live (₹290 and ₹340 a purchase). Keep them as spares for when an ad tires.

## Test plan

1. Proposed budget: give each ad its own ad set at ₹1,500 a day for 7 days. That's ₹6,000 a day for all four, close to the ₹6,333 a day your four ads averaged over the last 90 days (₹5,70,000 ÷ 90).
2. Each ad spends ₹10,500 in the week. That buys about 36 purchases at ₹290, or about 27 at ₹380, so you can judge on purchases, not clicks.
3. On day 7, compare cost per purchase at ad set level. At or under ₹290 (your best live ad): keep. Over ₹380 (your worst): cut. In between: run 7 more days.
4. Pause the ₹340 and ₹380 ads to fund this. Keep the ₹290 "No added sugar" ad live as your control.
5. Check frequency every week. When a winner passes 2.5, add a spare primary text as a new ad. Don't wait for 4.2 again.

## Assumptions

- Meta display limits used: 125 characters of primary text before "See more", and about 27 for headlines and descriptions. Placements vary, so check the preview.
- Target cost per purchase is ₹290, your best live ad today. If your margin allows more, raise the keep line.
- All four ads use the same 4:5 image of the pack front and the same broad audience, ages 22 to 40.
