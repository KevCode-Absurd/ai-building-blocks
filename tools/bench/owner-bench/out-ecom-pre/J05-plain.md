# Meta copy set: The Whole Truth protein bars (box of 6)

## Assumptions

- **Display limits.** I wrote to the limits where Meta does not cut text in Feed: primary text up to 125 characters, headline up to 27, description up to 27. Every primary text below fits whole in 125, so nothing hides behind "See more". Counts include spaces and punctuation.
- **Claims.** I only use the approved claims: 12 g protein per bar, no added sugar, sweetened with dates, ₹540 per box, ingredients listed on the front of the pack. ₹90 a bar is ₹540 ÷ 6.
- **Review quotes.** Taste and texture lines appear only as quotes from our own 870 reviews, with the share stated. They are customer words, not brand claims. If legal says no to quoting reviews, drop primary texts 2 and 3 and use the backups at the end.
- **Budget.** No budget was given. I assume an equal split across the 4 launch ads.

## What the past 90 days tell us

- **Flip the pack is the winner, and it is tired, not broken.** It had the best CTR (2.1%) and the lowest cost per purchase (₹210). It was paused at frequency 4.2 after 7 weeks. That is fatigue. The angle needs new words, not a funeral.
- **Every active ad costs ₹80 to ₹170 more** per purchase: no added sugar ₹290, protein ₹340, office snack ₹380.
- **Our best proof has never run.** The top review phrase, "Doesn't taste like a protein bar" (26%), was never an angle. Nor was texture (14%).
- **Price is the stated objection.** 9% of reviews say "₹90 a bar is a lot for every day". The daily-habit office ad is our most expensive.

## The 4 combinations to launch first

| Ad | Primary | Headline | Description | What it tests |

| A | P1 | H1 | D3 | **Can the old winner come back?** Same front-of-pack angle, new words. If it lands near ₹210 again, the angle was fatigued, not finished. |

Why these four:
- **A vs C** shows if the win came from the fact or from who the line speaks to.
- **B** is the biggest untested bet: the phrase 1 in 4 buyers chose on their own.
- **D** answers our weakest segment's own objection instead of repeating "4 pm snack, sorted" at ₹380.

Held for round two: P4 is close to the active ₹290 ad, so it teaches little now. P3 runs next if B wins.

## How to read the results

- **Judge on cost per purchase, not CTR.** CTR shows interest. Cost per purchase shows money.
- **Bar to beat: ₹290,** the best active ad today. Under it earns more budget. Near ₹210 is a new winner.
- **Watch frequency.** The last winner wore out at 4.2. Swap in spare lines from above before an ad passes 3.5.
- **Then turn off the weakest active ad.** If D beats ₹380, pause "Your 4 pm snack, sorted".
