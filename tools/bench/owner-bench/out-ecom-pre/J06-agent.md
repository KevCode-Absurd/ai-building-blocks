## Description

If your wallet feels like a brick in your back pocket, start here. Hide & Seek is a slim leather bifold for what most days need: your everyday cards, a little cash and a spare card or two, tucked out of sight.

It stays slim because it holds less. With 6 cards it's 10 mm thick, and 142 of our 400 reviewers say it's much thinner than their old wallet. Notes lie flat, about 10 of them, so there's no bulge. If you carry a thick fold of cash or a pocket of coins, this isn't your wallet.

New card slots are snug. Reviewers say they break in over the first week, so give it a week of daily use before you judge the fit. The leather wears in, not out: 88 reviewers say it ages nicely. A 3-year warranty comes with it.

## FAQ

**How much cash does it hold?**
About 10 notes, laid flat in the note section. The hidden pocket also takes flat notes and spare cards. It isn't built for a thick fold of cash, and the coin pouch fits a few coins only.

**Will it fit in a front pocket?**
That depends on the pocket, so here are the numbers. With 6 cards it's 10 mm (0.4 in) thick. Full, with 12 cards, it's 14 mm (0.55 in). For the flattest front-pocket carry, keep it near 6 cards.

**How many cards, really?**
5 to 12. It's slimmest at about 6 cards. 61 reviewers say their cards fit and it still closes flat, with lines like "I carry 8 cards and it still closes flat."

**The card slots feel tight. Is that normal?**
Yes. The leather slots are snug when new, and 54 reviewers say they break in over the first week. Load the cards you carry every day and use it daily for a week before you decide.

**Will it still look good in a few years?**
Leather changes with use, and 88 of 400 reviewers say it ages nicely, with lines like "Looks better after a year." It also comes with a 3-year warranty. Buying it as a gift? Check that he carries 5 to 12 cards and only a little cash.
