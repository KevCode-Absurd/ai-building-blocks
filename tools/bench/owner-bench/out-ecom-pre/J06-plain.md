## FAQ

**How much cash does it hold?**
About 10 notes, laid flat in the note section. The hidden pocket takes one more flat note if you want a spare. Notes should not be folded, or the wallet gets thicker. If you carry a lot of cash, this wallet is not the best fit.

**How many cards will it really hold?**
5 to 12. One owner wrote, "I carry 8 cards and it still closes flat." At 12 cards it is 14 mm thick, so it holds them, but it is no longer at its slimmest.

**My cards feel too tight. Is that normal?**
Yes. The slots are made snug so cards don't slide out. They ease in over about a week of daily use. Keep your cards in the wallet during that week and the leather will shape to them.

**Will it fit in a front pocket?**
It is 10 mm thick with 6 cards, so it sits flat in a back pocket or jacket. Front-pocket fit depends on your trousers and how many cards you carry. Keep it to 6 cards for the slimmest carry.

**Is it a good gift?**
Yes, if they carry cards and a little cash. It holds 5 to 12 cards, and the leather is made to age well, with a 3-year warranty behind it. If they carry a lot of cash or coins, choose a larger wallet.
