# Meta budget: decisions for the next 14 days

## The decision

Meta lost money on first orders in the last 14 days. On the orders Shopify can tie to Meta, the loss was ₹1,85,300. Even on Meta's own count it was ₹28,600. Four ad sets lose on both counts, and your main engine broke on one move: the "Dad's razor vs mine" creator video was paused 18 days ago, and ASC's cost per purchase rose from ₹281 to ₹348.

So: relaunch that video today, cut three ad sets, fix three, hold two. Base spend is ₹3,45,600, plus an ₹84,000 reserve that only ASC can earn. ₹1,30,400 of the ₹5,60,000 stays unspent: every ad set it could go to loses money on your store's numbers today.

Bad news to plan for: about 20% fewer Meta orders (1,139 against 1,425) on 40% less spend. Projected first-order result: a ₹24,400 loss on the store count to a ₹1,03,100 profit on Meta's, if ASC returns to its weeks 1 to 3 average of ₹271 a purchase.

**Day 1 moves, in order:**
1. Relaunch "Dad's razor vs mine" inside ASC from its original post, plus 2 fresh cuts with a new hook and first frame.
2. Set Razor Starter Kit, Beard Kit and Gift Sets to zero.
3. Women Body Razor: ages 25 to 34 only, ₹1,800 a day.
4. Retargeting: 7-day add-to-cart and checkout only, buyers excluded, ₹2,000 a day.

## Rules for the team, every day

1. **One basis.** Meta claims 1,425 purchases; Shopify has 1,020 orders with a Meta UTM. The lines allow for that gap, so judge Ads Manager costs against the line, never ROAS.
2. **Recheck the gap every Monday:** Shopify Meta-UTM orders divided by Meta purchases, last 7 days. If it moves more than 5 points from 0.72, move every line by the same share. Keep the 7-day click, 1-day view setting.
3. **Read windows.** ASC on 3 days (about 40 purchases a day), every other ad set on 7. Never act on one day. Raise at most 20%, once every 3 days.
4. **Never pause a winner to "refresh" creative.** Add new ads beside it. That one pause cost about ₹44,700. Inside ASC, leave weaker ads on while ASC is under its line.
5. **Check refusals weekly.** Use the courier report by UTM. An ad set whose refusals pass 25% of its COD orders cannot be raised, whatever Meta shows. A prepaid order earns ₹450; a COD order earns ₹325.
6. **Hard cap.** Never above ₹5,60,000 for the fortnight. Reserve goes to ASC only.

## The money behind this

- **Profit per delivered order** at ₹899: ₹899 minus ₹342 product cost (38%) minus ₹95 shipping and fees = ₹462.
- **Profit per placed order,** which is what Meta counts: prepaid, 98% kept, ₹450. COD, 78% delivered and ₹160 lost per refusal, ₹325. Store mix at 45% COD, ₹394. Each ad set's line uses its own order value, COD share and refusal rate, times 0.72.
- **Store count** = Meta purchases × 0.72 (1,020 ÷ 1,425).
- **Projection** on ₹3,45,600: 1,139 Meta purchases, about 815 store orders. Ad sets other than ASC stay at last fortnight's cost.

**Assumptions:**
- The 14-day budget is ₹5,60,000: ₹12,00,000 × 14 ÷ 30.
- Meta over-claims by the same ratio in every ad set, as no tagged orders by ad set exist. Retargeting and catalog are probably worse than shown.
- A prepaid return costs ₹160 and the sale, like a refusal. Profit is first order only, as the brief has no repeat data.
