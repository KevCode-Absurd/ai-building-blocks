# Bombas on TikTok: 30-day ads launch plan

## The plan on one screen

- **Decision:** one TikTok Sales campaign, US only, built on what already sells: real people on their feet at work, problem first. $800 a day for Days 1 to 14, then up to $1,000 a day split by results. $30,000 in total.
- **Target:** $38 or less per new customer, read on Shopify orders. That matches Meta's $38 and is what a new customer earns you over 90 days.
- **Bad news:** TikTok pays back on the first order only at $27.10 or less. At $38 we lose $10.90 per first order and earn it back within 90 days (projection). No money is spent until the pixel passes a real test order.
- **Projection:** at $38, the $30,000 buys about 790 new customers; at $57, about 526.
- **First moves this week:** the owner opens the ad account, the store lead installs the TikTok app on Shopify, and the creator lead asks the nurse and server creators for Spark Ads codes.

## The numbers behind the target

| Measure | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Profit on a first order | $62 x 55% minus $7 | $27.10 |
| Profit per new customer over 90 days (projection) | $88 x 55% minus $7 x 1.42 orders | about $38.46 |
| Allowance per new customer | 90-day break-even, same as Meta | $38 |
| Cut line | 1.5 x $38 | $57 |
| Spend needed to judge one ad | 3 x $38 | $114 |
| Lowest daily budget per ad group | 25 purchases x $38 over 7 days | $136 |

**What your data decides:**

1. **Creator posts of workers on their feet lead.** The nurse post had 186,000 views, 11.4 seconds of watch time and 2,900 shares. The server post had 92,000. That is 13 and 7 times your middle post. Both run as Spark Ads from Day 1 with half the early budget.
2. **Problem first is your cheapest hook.** On Meta it cost $29 per first order against $40 to $51 for the rest, with the best 3-second view rate (31%). About 70% of new ads open on it.
3. **Mission, offer and studio shots do not open ads.** Mission first cost $51 on Meta. Offer first cost $42 with the lowest view rate (19%), and 20% off cuts first-order profit from $27.10 to $14.70. The studio spin held viewers 2.8 seconds. Giving closes every ad as a plain fact.

## 2. Campaign structure

One campaign: Sales objective, website, optimised to purchase, lowest-cost bidding. US, English, broad: no age floor, no interests. TikTok placement only. Attribution 7-day click, 1-day view. Budgets per ad group, built by hand rather than Smart+ so each ad reads cleanly. Smart+ can follow once winners are clear.

| Ad group | Who it's for | Ads at launch | Added Day 8 | Daily, Days 1 to 14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creators on shift (Spark) | Maria | Nurse and server posts | 6 new worker creator posts | $400 |
| Shift workers (brand-made) | Maria | 4 problem-first videos | Recuts of what's selling | $250 |
| Family | Tasha | 3 videos | 2 parent creator posts | $150 |

Every group clears the $136 floor; $650 of $800 backs the proven style.

## 3. Creative: how much and what kind

**About 30 ads in 30 days:** 9 at launch, 8 on Day 8, then 6 a week in Weeks 3 and 4. About 70% show on-feet workers; the rest speak to Tasha. Filming, not money, is the limit: $30,000 can judge 263 ads at $114 each. The creative lead films 7 videos in set-up week on real staff or customers on real days. The creator lead signs 6 on-feet workers (such as a line cook or warehouse picker) and 2 parents, each with a 60-day Spark code.

| Hook | For | First 2 seconds | Opening line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hour ten | Maria | Clogs come off after a shift, socks on feet | "My feet don't ache by hour ten." |
| Clog check | Maria | Walk-in shot, sock still up at the ankle | "They don't slide down in my clogs." |
| Pricey, but | Maria | Socks pulled on in a break room | "Pricey, but I'm never going back." |
| Stays on | Tasha | Kids running, socks still on | "My kids' socks actually stay on." |
| Laundry day | Tasha | Pairs pulled from the dryer | "I like that a pair gets donated." |

**Rules for every ad:**
- Socks on real feet in the first 2 seconds. No studio shots.
- 9:16, captions on, text in the safe zone, commercial-library sound, most under 25 seconds.
- End card: "Comfort you notice by lunchtime." Then, word for word: "One purchased = one donated."
- Never "medical-grade", "cures foot pain", "best socks in the world", "cheap socks" or "shop guilt-free".
- Creator posts carry TikTok's paid partnership label. No AI-made people or voices.
- Weeks 3 and 4: of each 6 new ads, 4 put new openers on the best seller's body and 2 are new ideas.

## 4. Budget phases

| Phase | Days | Daily | Total | How it's split |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Launch and learn | 1 to 7 | $800 | $5,600 | $400 / $250 / $150 |
| First read | 8 to 14 | $800 | $5,600 | Same; new ads join Day 8 |
| Scale or cut | 15 to 30 | $1,000 | $16,000 | By Day 14 calls |
| Reserve | 18 to 30 | Varies | $2,800 | Release rule below |
| **Total** | | | **$30,000** | |

The reserve (9%) goes to the best ad group in 20% steps once two reads agree it costs $38 or less per new customer. If no group earns Scale on Day 14, stay at $800 a day.

## 5. Rules for killing and scaling

**How we read cost:** TikTok spend divided by TikTok-tagged Shopify new-customer orders. From Day 7 we log the gap between tagged orders and TikTok's own purchase count. TikTok's number never sets a call alone.

| Call | When (7-day store numbers) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Cut an ad | $114 spent with no store purchase | Off |
| Cut a group | Two reads agree cost is over $57, not falling | To zero; money to the best group |
| Trim | $38 to $57 and falling | Down 20% |
| Hold | Reads disagree, or under 7 days since a change | No change |
| Scale | Two reads agree $38 or less | Up 20% every 3 to 4 days |

- Days 4 to 7: no edits while TikTok learns, except cutting an ad.
- Early flags after $50 or 1,000 impressions: click rate under 1%, or average watch under a quarter of the video. They shape the next opener, never kill an ad alone.

## 6. How we'll know on Day 30

We judge Days 15 to 30 on three reads:

1. **Store:** cost per new customer on tagged Shopify orders.
2. **Survey:** TikTok's share of "How did you hear about us?" answers against its share of paid spend.
3. **Blended:** paid cost per new customer across all channels against the baseline.

| Verdict | Test | Month 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Earned more | Store read $38 or less, a second read agrees, and at least 3 ads at or under $38 | Keep the Day 30 daily spend and keep the 20% steps |
| Hold | $38 to $57 and falling, or reads disagree | $30,000 again, mostly on the best group |
| Stop | Over $57 on two reads | Pause paid; keep creators posting |

Three winners, not one: the creator licences end in March, and a single ad will tire.

## Assumptions

- The 30 days and $30,000 count from Day 1, the first day of spend.
- Repeat orders carry the same 55% margin and $7 fees.
- The March licences cover both creator posts as paid ads for all 30 days.
