# Mokobara cabin suitcase: eight Meta concepts for Feed and Reels

The main bet: stop showing the bag on a colour. Show the jobs people bought it for: skip the belt, a quiet roll, lift it alone.

Women 25 to 40 buy at ₹1,058 a purchase, about 35% below men at ₹1,615, on 18% of spend, so they get three concepts. Men 25 to 40 take 70% of spend at a 0.8% link CTR, so they get three built on why work flyers buy. Make concepts 4, 1 and 2 first.

**Assumptions**
- The "typical lines" in your review table are word-for-word review quotes.
- Every ad links to the cabin suitcase product page on mokobara.com, priced at ₹6,999.
- The range has a lighter shell colour. Women's concepts use it, since reviewers say scuffs show on black.

**Build rules for all eight**
- Reels: 9:16 at 1440 x 2560. Keep text and logo out of the top 14%, bottom 35% and 6% each side. Burn in captions.
- Feed: 4:5 at 1440 x 1800. Carousel: 1:1 at 1080 x 1080.
- Logo in the first second or top of the image. The bag sharp and in frame in every shot.
- Button: Shop Now. Headlines under 27 characters. Hook inside the first 125 characters.
- Cast people play travellers, never customers. Review lines appear only as text, labelled "Mokobara review".
- Show no front pocket; 60 reviews wish it had one.
- No concept targets everyone 41 and over: ₹2,188 a purchase is your highest.

## 1. Out before the belt (Reels, 15 s)

**Hook, 0 to 3 s:** In arrivals, the belt has not started. A man rolls the suitcase past it to the exit. On screen: "Out before the belt starts."

**Scenes:** 3 to 7 s, earlier: into the overhead bin. 7 to 11 s: through arrivals, the belt crowd behind. 11 to 15 s: the cab rank. End card: "Mokobara cabin suitcase. ₹6,999."

**Primary text:**
Out of the airport before the belt starts.
The Mokobara cabin suitcase fits most Indian domestic airline cabin limits.
360-degree spinner wheels. TSA-style lock. ₹6,999.

**Headline:** Skip the baggage belt (21)

**Tests:** whether the time-back outcome lifts men's link CTR above today's 0.8%.

## Make these three first

1. **Concept 4, So quiet at 5 am.** Quiet wheels is your top review theme (290 mentions), and a silent product shot cannot show it. It goes to women 25 to 40, your cheapest buyers at ₹1,058.
2. **Concept 1, Out before the belt.** Men 25 to 40 get 70% of spend at ₹1,615 a purchase. Work flyers are your biggest group (34%), and they buy to skip the belt. This puts that reason in the first second.
3. **Concept 2, No drama at the gate.** It answers the fear that stops Karan in a buyer's words (210 mentions). A single image is the fastest to make. Beside concept 1, it shows whether men respond to time saved or fear removed.

Watch link CTR by audience in week one, against today's 0.8% for men and 1.4% for women.
