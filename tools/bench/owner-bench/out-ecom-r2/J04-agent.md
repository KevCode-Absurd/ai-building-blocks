## Send with every brief

- **Your words.** Only lines marked "Exact" are word for word. Say only what you really noticed.
- **Say only these product facts:** "contains plant fiber and prebiotics", "less sugar than classic soda", "real fruit juice and botanical flavours". You can call it "a new kind of soda."
- **Never say:** healthy soda, guilt-free, diet, zero sugar, or anything about gut, health, cures or weight. Never name or show another soda brand.
- **Look:** bright real kitchens and desks. No gym, no lab. Keep text in the middle of the frame; leave the bottom fifth and right edge clear for app buttons.
- **Disclosure:** "Paid partnership with Olipop" on screen for the first 3 seconds. "#ad" at the start of the caption. The spoken line where the script marks it, never during a sip. Turn on the paid partnership label in TikTok and Instagram.
- **Deliverables:** one edited 9:16 video, 35 to 40 seconds, captions burned in; all three hook takes; all raw clips plus 15 seconds of pours and cans; framed so a 4:5 crop works.
- **Rights:** paid use on Meta and TikTok for 90 days from first run, plus organic reposts. Grant partnership ad access in your own app.
- **Timing:** first cut due 7 days after the pack arrives, then one round of changes.
