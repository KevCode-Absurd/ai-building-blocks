# Meta copy set: The Whole Truth protein bars (box of 6, ₹540)

**The main bet:** bring back "flip the pack" with new words, and lead a new ad with taste. Flip the pack was your cheapest angle at ₹210 per purchase. It stopped because people saw it too often (frequency 4.2), not because the idea failed. Taste is your top review theme (26% say it "doesn't taste like a protein bar"), and no ad has used it yet.

Every primary text fits in 125 characters, so nothing hides behind "See more". Every headline and description fits in 27 characters. Counts include spaces.

**P2. Taste, in a customer's words** (for Aditya's chalk worry) · 122

**P3. No added sugar** (for both: "Healthy snacks always hide sugar somewhere") · 119
> No added sugar. Not even a little. The Whole Truth protein bar is sweetened with dates. Don't trust us? Read the front.

**P4. The price, said first** (for Priya's price worry) · 123
> Yes, it's ₹90 a bar. That buys 12 g protein, no added sugar and every ingredient on the front. The Whole Truth, ₹540 for 6.

**P5. The 4 pm moment** (for Priya: "I need something at 4 pm that isn't a samosa") · 118
> 4 pm. You want something sweet that isn't a samosa. The Whole Truth protein bar: no added sugar, sweetened with dates.

**P6. The training week** (for Aditya, who trains five days a week) · 101
> Train 5 days a week? A box of The Whole Truth bars has 6, so you get one spare. 12 g protein in each.
