## Description

"My old wallet was a brick in my back pocket." If that sounds familiar, Hide & Seek is the fix. It's a slim leather bifold that carries what you use every day and leaves the bulk behind.

Your daily cards sit in quick-access slots. Spare cards and flat notes go in a hidden pocket. A small coin pouch holds a few coins.

Slim means trade-offs, and we'd rather name them now. Cash lies flat, so the note section suits about 10 notes, not a folded wad. The coin pouch is small. The card slots start tight and need about a week of use to ease. After that, one owner puts it simply: "I carry 8 cards and it still closes flat."

US$99, with a 3-year warranty.

## FAQ

**How much cash does it hold?**
About 10 notes, laid flat in the note section. It isn't made for a thick fold of cash. Keeping notes flat is part of what keeps the wallet slim.

**How many cards will it really hold?**
5 to 12. With 6 cards it's 10 mm thick, and with 12 it's 14 mm. One owner carries 8 and says it "still closes flat."

**Why are the card slots so tight?**
They start snug so your cards stay put. Give them about a week of daily use and they ease. If they feel tight on day one, that's normal.

**Will it fit in a front pocket?**
It's 10 mm thick with 6 cards and 14 mm with 12. For the slimmest front-pocket carry, keep to fewer cards and a little flat cash.

**Will it still look good in a few years?**
The leather ages with use. "Looks better after a year" is a common line in our reviews. It's also covered by a 3-year warranty.
