# Bundles and free shipping: the plan

## The decisions

- **Launch three routine duos, built from pairs your shoppers already buy together:** Vitamin C Duo ₹899, Wash Day Duo ₹749, Body Duo ₹749. Each saves about ₹50 and ships free.
- **Keep free shipping at ₹499.** A ₹699 line could bring in up to ₹2.25 lakh a month in fees. But it puts a ₹49 fee on 27% of orders, including every lone vitamin C serum, your top reorder. It loses money if more than 1 in 8 of those shoppers walk away.
- **Bad news:** about ₹1 lakh a month goes on refused COD parcels under ₹500, where 29% are refused. All three bundles sit above ₹500, where refusals drop to 17%.
- **This week:** build the three bundles at one price for everyone, add the cart shipping bar, and start the bundle test. The threshold test follows in Week 5.

Assumptions: lip balm earns the makeup margin (55%), the lowest, so its numbers are cautious. COD is 40% of orders at every order value. A refused parcel comes back sellable, so it costs only the ₹130 shipping.

- Face wash alone to Vitamin C Duo: +₹309.
- Shampoo alone to Wash Day Duo: +₹156.
- Lotion alone to Body Duo: +₹134.

Together, the three need about 520 extra bundle orders a month to cover their savings: 3% of your roughly 17,000 monthly orders.

## Free shipping threshold: keep ₹499

| Line | What changes | Money a month | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹399 | 3,060 orders (₹400 to ₹499) stop paying ₹49 | Loses ₹1.5 lakh. Needs about 1,060 extra orders (6% more) to cover it | No |
| ₹499 (today) | Nothing | Baseline | **Keep** |
| ₹699 | 4,590 orders (₹500 to ₹699) start paying ₹49 | Up to +₹2.25 lakh if nobody leaves. Loses money if more than 1 in 8 leave | Test only, on new shoppers |

**Why ₹499 is the right line.** It sits just above your busiest band: 18% of orders fall between ₹400 and ₹499. It also sits below the serum's ₹599 price, so your hero product ships free on its own. Ishita says shipping is what stops her. A ₹699 line would charge her on the one product she reorders. A ₹399 line gives away fees on low orders that COD shoppers refuse 29% of the time.

**Help shoppers cross the line.** Add a cart bar: "You're ₹X away from free shipping." Under it, offer one-click lip balm at its full ₹199. Lip balm plus one item is already your second most common pair (1,600 orders). A face wash cart goes from ₹394 with shipping to ₹544 shipped free. You earn about ₹60 more on that order, with no discount.

## How to test both safely

Run the tests one after the other. The bundles change your order value spread, and the threshold test must read the new one.

**Test 1: bundles (Weeks 1 to 4)**

- **Split:** 50/50, at random by visitor. Group A sees the bundle boxes and cart nudges. Group B sees today's pages.
- **One price for all:** the bundles also sit in a "Routines" collection both groups can reach. No shopper ever sees two prices for the same set.
- **Main measure:** gross profit per session, after product cost, shipping and refused COD parcels.
- **Guardrails:** conversion rate, COD delivered rate, and serum orders per session.
- **Keep or drop:** keep a bundle if Group A's profit per session is higher. Drop any bundle whose orders in A (bundle plus full-price pair) are no higher than B's pair orders. That bundle only gives away its saving.

**Test 2: threshold (from Week 5, at least 4 full weeks)**

- **Split:** 50/50 among new visitors. Shoppers with a past order always keep ₹499, so serum reorders are never charged.
- **Arms:** ₹499 against ₹699. The cart bar shows the right line in each arm.
- **Main measure:** gross profit per session.
- **Guardrails:** orders per session between ₹500 and ₹699, and COD delivered rate.
- **Size:** run until each arm has about 1,000 orders between ₹500 and ₹699. With fewer, you cannot see a 1 in 8 drop.
- **Stop early:** if those band orders fall by more than 1 in 5 by Week 2, end the test and keep ₹499.
- **Switch rule:** move new shoppers to ₹699 only if profit per session is higher and band orders fall by less than 1 in 8. Otherwise keep ₹499 and close the question.

No fake countdowns or "only 2 left" lines in either test.

## Appendix: the working

**Monthly orders.** 3,100 pair orders ÷ 19% = about 16,300 multi-item orders in 90 days. ÷ 31% = about 52,600 orders. The other pairs give 48,400 to 51,600. We use about 17,000 a month.

**Product cost** = price × (1 − margin): serum ₹192, face wash ₹110, shampoo and conditioner ₹152 each, lotion ₹170, body wash ₹150, lip balm ₹90.

**Profit per order (prepaid)** = price − product cost − ₹70 shipping, plus ₹49 if under ₹499. Vitamin C Duo: 899 − 302 − 70 = ₹527. Serum alone: 599 − 192 − 70 = ₹337.

**COD orders** = delivered share × profit − refusal rate × ₹130, blended 60% prepaid and 40% COD.

**Break-even** = monthly pair orders × saving ÷ gain per move-up. Vitamin C Duo: 1,033 × ₹45 ÷ ₹177 = 263.

**₹699 line.** 27% × 17,000 = 4,590 orders × ₹49 = ₹2.25 lakh. A serum order earns ₹337 today and ₹386 with the fee, so it breaks even when 49 ÷ 386 = 12.7% leave.

**₹399 line.** 18% × 17,000 = 3,060 × ₹49 = ₹1.5 lakh lost. A new ₹399 shampoo order shipped free earns about ₹142 blended, so 1.5 lakh ÷ 142 = about 1,060 orders.

**Refused COD under ₹500.** 6,800 orders × 40% COD × 29% × ₹130 = ₹1.03 lakh a month.
