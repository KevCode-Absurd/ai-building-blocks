## Two heroes to A/B test

Only the H1 and subhead change. Everything else stays the same.

**Version A: the time it saves.** The hero above.

**Version B: fair load across the team**
- **H1:** Round robin interviews that skip whoever is on PTO.
- **Subhead:** Candidates book themselves. Cal.com spreads interviews evenly across your panel and only offers times when someone is free.

**The one metric that decides it:** qualified starts per visitor. That is page visitors who book a demo or sign up and choose "Team: recruiting," divided by unique visitors to the page.

**How to run it**
- Split traffic 50/50, in full weeks, at least two.
- At a start rate near today's 3.1%, you need about 13,400 visitors per version to spot a 20% lift at 95% confidence and 80% power. Weeks needed = 26,800 divided by weekly page visitors. Do not stop early.
- The higher rate wins if the result holds at 95% confidence. If not, ship A: it carries our core line.
- Later, check 60-day paid rate by version. It does not decide the test; it flags a hero that brings weaker teams.

**Projection:** if this page lifts recruiting signups 10% to 30%, that is 210 to 630 more per 90 days, or about 150 to 460 more paid seats at today's 9.4% and 7.8 seats.
