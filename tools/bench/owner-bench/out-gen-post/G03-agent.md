*Confidential: for Supabase investors only.*

Hi all,

**The three numbers**

- **ARR: $124.5M**, up $6.5M (5.5%) from $118.0M. In September we wrote: "We expect to cross $125M ARR in October." We missed by $0.5M.
- **Gross margin: 64%**, down 7 points from 71%. Free-tier compute cost rose $0.9M.
- **Net burn: $6.3M**, up from $4.1M. Cash is $205.7M: 33 months at October's burn, 37 months without a one-off deposit.

**What we need:** intros to a New York sales leader (by 30 November), a devtools founder who fixed free-tier economics (by 11 December), and an infrastructure lead who moved Postgres in-house (by 15 December). Details at the end.

**Here's what went wrong and what we're doing about it**

- **Customer A is leaving.** An enterprise account worth 2.1% of October ARR (about $2.6M) gave 90-day notice. They are moving workloads to their own servers. Last day is 31 January 2027, so the loss shows in February ARR. We will keep full service to the end and ask for an exit review.
- **Idle free projects are eating margin.** AI app builders created about 1.46M of our 2.4M new databases, up 48% from about 0.99M. Direct databases barely moved (0.91M to 0.94M). But 72% of AI-builder projects have no database activity after their first 7 days, and we keep free projects running until 7 days of inactivity. We believe most of the $0.9M compute rise comes from them. They also convert to paid within 30 days at 0.4%, against 3.1% for direct signups. **Fix:** pause inactive AI-builder projects after 48 hours. It ships by 27 November. We will report the compute saving in the November update.
- **Burn rose $2.2M.** Compute was $0.9M (fix above). A one-off deposit for next year's developer conference was $0.8M. Six new hires were $0.5M. Without the deposit, burn was $5.5M.
- **We missed our own forecast.** We gave you one number. From now on, every forecast comes with a range.
- **VP Enterprise Sales is open after 5 months.** Two finalists said no; both wanted a base in New York. The role is now based in New York.
