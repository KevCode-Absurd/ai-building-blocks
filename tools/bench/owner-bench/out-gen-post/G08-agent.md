# Pricing recommendation: add one flat Agency plan, and stop hiding yearly billing

## The decision

Add one new plan: **Agency, $99 a month or $990 a year.** It covers up to 25 channels, client approvals and 2 team members. Clients approve as free guests. Every current plan and price stays exactly as it is.

Also show yearly next to monthly, with the exact amount charged, on the pricing page and in billing settings. It changes no price, so it ships to everyone on 1 December.

- **Revenue (projection):** mid case +$28,600 MRR by month 6, about 1.3% on today's $2.21M. That is +$128,000 revenue over two quarters, +$117,000 after the 9% cost. Range: +$4,300 to +$50,500 MRR.
- **Bad news:** the low case is near zero, mostly from Team customers moving down. Yearly billing won't lift revenue inside two quarters. No rise for existing customers can count in the board's window, because of our 12-month promise.
- **This week:** set up Agency in billing as a hidden plan, and split accounts for the test below.

## Why this, and not a price rise

| Signal | Number | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials with 8+ channels leaks fastest | 9.6% monthly churn: 374 customers and $23,900 MRR lost each month | 5% of customers lose more MRR than 41,000 small shops ($16,000) |
| The same size on Team stays | 2.6% churn at 10.4 channels | Approvals keep these accounts |
| Why they leave | 52% "cost keeps rising", 21% "needed client approvals" | 73% of exits are reasons Agency answers |
| Yearly payers stay | 0.9% vs 4.0% monthly churn, yet only 19% pay yearly | Our best retention tool is hidden after signup |

Freelancers asked for "one flat price I can plan around," plus approvals. Agency costs more than the typical Essentials 8+ bill ($64), but it stops rising with each client until 25 channels.

## Revenue math

Basis: monthly revenue per account at today's 19% yearly mix. Essentials 8+ is $63.91, Team 8+ $120.85, Agency $95.87. Each Essentials upgrade adds $31.96 a month; each Team move-down loses $24.98. The six months include the 8-week test, when half of accounts see Agency.

| Case | Essentials 8+ who upgrade | Their churn after | Team 8+ who move down | MRR at month 6 | Revenue over 6 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 15% | 5.0% | 20% | +$4,300 | +$15,800 |
| Mid | 25% | 3.5% | 10% | +$28,600 | +$128,200 |
| High | 35% | 2.6% | 5% | +$50,500 | +$224,800 |

New freelancers joining straight onto Agency are extra and not counted.

**Yearly, honestly:** per channel, a monthly payer at 4.0% churn brings $58.09 over 12 months; a yearly payer brings $60 on day one. That is 3% more in year one but 8% less in the first six months ($30 vs $32.59). It wins only if switchers would otherwise churn above 3.4% a month. Check local rules on how yearly totals and renewals must be shown.

## Risks

1. **Team move-downs.** Team accounts with 9+ channels and 2 or fewer members pay less on Agency. The 2-member limit is the fence. If close to half of Team 8+ moved down, the mid-case gain is gone.
2. **Too few upgrades.** At 5% upgrades, the plan only breaks even.
3. **Yearly may flatter itself.** Loyal people may pick yearly, not the other way round.

## How we tell customers

Nobody's price goes up, so the 60-day notice does not apply. We still explain every step in public, so a test plan never looks like a hidden deal.

**Pricing page, during the test:** "We're trying a new Agency plan with some customers. If it works, we'll open it to everyone and explain why here."

**Email to test-group Essentials customers with 8+ channels:**

> Subject: One flat price for client work
> Your Buffer bill grows with every channel you add. Customers like you told us that is hard to plan around.
> So we made Agency: up to 25 channels, with client approvals built in.
> Here's exactly what you'll pay: $99 a month, or $990 a year.
> Your current plan and price stay the same unless you choose to switch.

**Public post at full launch:** what is new, why (the exit survey reasons, in plain words) and what did not change (every existing price).

## What we test first, and when we stop

**Test:** split the 7,200 accounts with 8+ channels, and new visitors, 50/50 at random. Only the test half sees Agency. Run 1 December 2026 to 25 January 2027.

**Main measure:** MRR per account at week 8, churned accounts counted as zero, test against holdout. Also track Essentials 8+ churn, Team move-downs and revenue per new visitor.

**Stop if any of these happens:**
- By week 4, more than 20% of test-group Team 8+ accounts have moved down.
- At week 8, fewer than 10% of test-group Essentials 8+ accounts have upgraded.
- At week 8, MRR per account in the test group is not above the holdout.

If we stop, everyone on Agency keeps it at the price they chose. We close it to new sign-ups and say why in public.

## Bolder move: lock the price for yearly payers

Promise in public: "Pay yearly, and your price won't go up while you keep renewing." It answers the shop owner who fears a creeping bill. For every 1,000 monthly customers who move to yearly, about 284 more would still be paying at month 12 (39% vs 10% lost a year), if the churn gap holds for switchers. First step: test it on new visitors after the Agency readout. Risk: we give up future rises on that group, so set yearly prices right first.

**Assumptions:** the 19% yearly mix holds in every group and on Agency. Agency reuses Team's approval workflow. The two quarters run from 1 December.
