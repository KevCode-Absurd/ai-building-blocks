# Headspace social plan: 16 November to 13 December 2026

**The decision:** lead with sleep, voiced by our narrators. Narrator clips averaged 380,000 reach and 5.9% saves, against 130,000 and 2.0% for carousels, yet we posted only 5 in 90 days. Sleep posts brought 450 installs each; stress-at-work posts brought 95. So: 4 narrator clips and 2 carousels a week, half on sleep, nearly all at 20:00 to 22:00.

**Bad news:** stress at work, our most-posted theme (22 posts), is weak for installs. And sleep posts nearly triple, so expect some fatigue.

**First move:** the editor cuts week 1's four clips by Monday 16 November, 18:00; the designer finishes both carousels by Wednesday 18 November, 12:00.

**Review:** no post names a sleep or mental health condition, so none needs clinical and legal review. If an edit adds one, it cannot go live before Tuesday 24 November.

Assumption: a post is one publish on one channel, so 6 a week means 3 on Instagram and 3 on TikTok.

## Six drafts for week 1

**1. Mon 16 Nov, 21:00, Instagram Reel**
- On-screen: "Brain still on at bedtime?" / "Let someone else talk for a minute." / "Breathe with the voice."
- Caption: Some nights your mind keeps going after the lights go out. That's okay. Here's one quiet minute from our sleep library. Take a breath. You've got this. Lower the volume and press play. More wind-downs in the Headspace app, link in bio.
- Editor: 30 to 45 seconds of a wind-down track, opening on a breath cue. Orange bedroom illustration, burned-in captions.

**2. Tue 17 Nov, 14:15, TikTok**
- On-screen: "Parked before school pickup?" / "You have one minute." / "Engine off. Breathe along."
- Caption: Ten minutes alone can feel like a fantasy. One minute in a parked car? That counts. Rest your hands on the wheel and breathe along with this voice from our app. Then go get your people.
- Editor: 45 seconds of guided breathing. Show a parked car, keys out, never moving.

**3. Wed 18 Nov, 20:30, Instagram carousel**

- Caption: Lying there, rereading every email in your head? That happens. Park it on paper tonight and let it rest until morning. Try it now, before bed.

**4. Thu 19 Nov, 21:00, TikTok**

**5. Fri 20 Nov, 21:00, Instagram Reel**

**6. Sun 22 Nov, 20:30, TikTok photo carousel**

## How we'll know it worked

Give every post its own link or code so installs read per post.

| Measure | Past, per 4 weeks | Target (projection) |
|---|---|---|
| Installs from post links and codes | about 3,400 | 4,800 to 6,600 |
| Average save rate per post | about 2.0% | 3.4% or higher |

Working: past installs 10,900 × 28/90 days = 3,391. High case: 12 sleep posts × 450, plus 4 after-work × 95, plus 8 parent and kindness posts × 100 = 6,582. Low case: sleep posts drop a third, to 300 each, from fatigue = 4,782. Save rate: each theme's past rate × its planned posts, divided by 24.

- **Mon 30 Nov:** a slot under 1.5% saves two weeks running becomes a sleep carousel. Parent clips saving under half the evening rate move to 20:30.
- **Mon 14 Dec:** 4,800 installs or more means it worked; keep the mix in January. 3,400 to 4,800: keep sleep and narrators, rework the rest. Under 3,400: it failed.

**Bolder move:** in weeks 3 and 4, swap the Sunday carousel for a 21:00 "Lights down" TikTok live stream: 20 minutes of cleared sleep audio over a slow orange illustration. It must beat the carousel's roughly 100 installs, tracked with one pinned code. First step: by Friday 27 November, check the audio clearance covers live streams; if not, skip it. Risk: weaker tracking.
