# Plausible: 90-day SEO plan, 16 Nov 2026 to 14 Feb 2027

**The plan in one line:** spend the writer's 12 page slots on comparison pages and the two buying pages already near the top, and stop writing general blog posts.

**Why:** in October, 9 comparison pages brought 412 trials. 140 blog posts brought 96. Since May the blog lost 22,500 clicks while every other group grew.

## What to stop

1. **New marketing tips and privacy news posts.** 0.7 trials per post a month (96 ÷ 140), against 45.8 per comparison page (412 ÷ 9).
2. **Judging SEO by clicks.** Report trials by page group each month instead.
3. **Chasing the blog decline post by post.** The 22,500 lost clicks were worth about 56 trials at the blog rate. Month 3 tagging picks the few posts worth saving.
4. **Touching pages that already win.** /vs-matomo (9.0% click, position 2.8) and /docs/wordpress-integration (21.4%, position 1.3) get fact checks only.

## Week one: the writer's tasks

**Monday 16 Nov.** Export 3 months of Search Console searches for /vs-google-analytics. Note the top 10 by impressions and the top one by clicks. List every claim the page makes about Google Analytics.

**Tuesday 17 Nov.** Check each claim against Google's public pages and our docs. Note source and date. Drop what you cannot source. Outline:
1. The answer in 2 lines: who should switch, and who should stay on Google Analytics.
2. A dashboard screenshot.
3. A dated comparison table.
4. "What happens to my old data?"
5. Cookies, in plain words.
6. Price and trial link.

**Wednesday 18 Nov.** Draft at grade 8, with this title and description:
- Title: "Google Analytics Alternative Without Cookies | Plausible" (56 characters)
- Description: "Plausible is a simple, open source alternative to Google Analytics. Plausible doesn't use cookies. See the dashboard, then start a trial."

**Thursday 19 Nov.** Voice check: no "100% GDPR compliant", no fear about fines, nothing about Google's people. Owner review.

**Friday 20 Nov.** Publish with "Last checked 20 Nov 2026". Record the baseline: 58,000 impressions, 2,030 clicks, 3.5%, position 4.2. Start /vs-fathom: list Fathom's facts from its own pages, dated.

## Rules to cut, keep or scale

Check each page 4 weeks after it goes live.

- **Rewritten page:** if its click rate is below the old one, put back the old title and description. Keep the new body.
- **New comparison page:** not in the top 20 by week 6? Add links from the 3 strongest comparison pages.
- **Scale:** if a new comparison page turns 3.2% or more of its clicks into trials, give the next spare slot to another rival.
- **Quarter goal:** comparison plus home, pricing and features pages reach 1,171 trials in the last 28 days (October's 1,102 plus 69).

## Assumptions

- October trial rates hold, and page 4 converts like a comparison page.
- The writer can publish and edit titles.
