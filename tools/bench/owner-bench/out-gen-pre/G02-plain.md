# Cal.com landing page for recruiting teams

## The segment: Team recruiting and interviews

Recruiting is 5.5% of signups (2,100 of 38,000) but brings 57% of new paid seats (about 1,540 of 2,717). Each recruiting signup is worth 0.73 paid seats (9.4% x 7.8), over four times the next best segment, team sales demos at 0.17. They also activate fastest: 71% book within 7 days. Yet the current page never shows them a recruiting example.

**Assumptions:** this page lives at its own URL (for example cal.com/recruiting) for recruiting ads, search and a "For recruiting" link on the homepage. Facts I do not have (hiring tools, security details, a customer quote) are marked in [brackets]. Fill them with real material or cut them.

**Subhead:** Share one link. Candidates pick a time. Round robin spreads interviews fairly across your panel and skips anyone who is busy or off.

## Section 2. The problem

**Headline:** Stop spending Monday on back and forth.

**Copy:** Every interview takes three or four emails to land. At 60 interviews a week, that is your week gone. With Cal.com, the candidate books in one step and the invite lands on the right calendar.

**Proof:** "41% of people who looked for a scheduling tool said the reason was too much back and forth over email." (Our survey, 1,240 answers.)

## Section 3. Round robin that shares the load

**Headline:** Share interviews fairly across your team.

**Copy:** Add your interviewers once. Cal.com checks each calendar before it offers a slot, then rotates bookings so no one gets buried. If someone blocks time off, they drop out of the rotation until they are back.

**Proof:** screenshot of a round robin event with six interviewers and a booking count next to each name. Stat: "88% of paid recruiting teams set up round robin in their first 30 days."

**Button:** Book a demo

## Two heroes to A/B test

Both keep the same booking page proof and "Book a demo" button, so the test isolates the message.

**Version A: time saved.** The hero in section 1.

**Version B: fair load**
- Headline: Interviews shared fairly across your team, booked by candidates.
- Subhead: Round robin checks every interviewer's calendar and rotates bookings, so no one gets buried. Candidates book in one step.

**Why these two:** A leads with the biggest reason people look for a tool (back and forth, 41%). B leads with the feature paid recruiting teams use most (round robin, 88%). We learn which one moves Priya.

## The one metric that decides the winner

**Qualified team starts per visitor:** the share of page visitors who either book a demo or sign up with "Team: recruiting and interviews" and get a first booking within 7 days.

Paid seats arrive at 60 days, too slow to call a test, and raw signups can reward the wrong people. This counts only recruiting teams who reach real use or a sales talk, which is where paid seats come from.

Split traffic 50/50 for at least two full weeks. Call it only at 95% confidence, then confirm with paid seats at day 60.
