## What went wrong, and what we're doing about it

**We missed our own forecast.** We gave one number with no range. From now on, every forecast will have a range.

**AI app builders bring volume, not revenue yet.** New databases rose 26%, from 1.9M to 2.4M. AI app builders created 61% of them (about 1.46M), up from 52% (about 0.99M). Databases created directly grew only 2.6%. But:

- 72% of projects created through AI app builders have no database activity after their first 7 days.
- Orgs from AI app builders convert to paid within 30 days at 0.4% (4 in 1,000). Direct sign-ups convert at 3.1% (31 in 1,000).
- We pause a free project only after 7 days of inactivity, so idle projects keep using compute.

*Fix:* we will pause inactive AI-builder projects after 48 hours. It is a two-week change, due by 30 November. Our November update will show whether compute cost and gross margin moved.

**Burn rose $2.2M.** All of it:

- $0.9M: free-tier compute.
- $0.8M: one-off deposit for next year's developer conference.
- $0.5M: six new hires.

Without the deposit, October burn was $5.5M.

**Customer A is leaving.** This enterprise account is 2.1% of October ARR (about $2.6M). They gave 90 days' notice and are moving workloads to their own servers. Last day: 31 January 2027. From November we will show ARR with and without them.

**VP Enterprise Sales is still open after 5 months.** Two finalists turned it down. Both wanted a base in New York. We are now hiring for New York.
