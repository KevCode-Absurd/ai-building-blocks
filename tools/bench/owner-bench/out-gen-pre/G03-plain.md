**Subject:** Supabase October: $124.5M ARR (missed $125M), margin 64%, burn $6.3M, three asks

Hi all,

## The three numbers

- **ARR: $124.5M**, up $6.5M (5.5%) from $118.0M. In September we said we'd cross $125M in October. We missed by $0.5M.
- **Gross margin: 64%**, down 7 points from 71%. Free-tier compute cost rose $0.9M.
- **Net burn: $6.3M**, up from $4.1M. Cash is $205.7M. That is 33 to 37 months of runway (at $6.3M a month, or $5.5M without a one-off deposit).

## What went wrong

**1. AI app-builder traffic is costing us more than it earns.** 61% of October's 2.4M new databases (about 1.46M) came through AI app-builder partners, up from 52% of 1.9M. Direct-created databases grew only 2.6%, so nearly all our growth came from this channel. But 72% of projects created through AI app builders have no database activity after their first 7 days. And only 0.4% of orgs from this channel convert to paid within 30 days, against 3.1% for orgs that sign up directly. On about $10.4M of monthly revenue, that extra $0.9M is roughly 9 points of margin, more than the 7-point drop.

**2. We are losing Customer A.** This enterprise account is 2.1% of October ARR (about $2.6M). They gave 90-day notice and are moving to their own servers. Last day: 31 January 2027.

**3. We missed our own forecast.** That is on us. From now on, every forecast we send will come with a range.

**4. VP Enterprise Sales is still open after 5 months.** Two finalists turned it down. Both wanted a base in New York.

## What we're doing about it

- **Pausing idle AI-builder projects after 48 hours, not 7 days.** A two-week engineering change that cuts the idle window by 5 of 7 days. We will report the real saving in November.
- **Opening the VP role to a New York base.** Both finalists asked for it.
- **Learning from Customer A before they leave.** Is this one account or a pattern? Ask 2 helps.
