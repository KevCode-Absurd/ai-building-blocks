# Pricing recommendation: add one flat plan for people who manage clients

## The recommendation

Add one new plan, **Agency: $80 a month, or $800 a year, for up to 15 channels.** It has one user, and clients approve posts as free approvers who can't publish. Nothing else changes. No existing customer's price moves.

Why: your biggest leak is Essentials customers with 8 or more channels. They leave at 9.6% a month, about 374 customers and $23,900 of monthly revenue. In exit surveys, 73% of them say the per-channel cost or missing client approvals is the reason. Today their only step up is Team, which doubles the bill (11 channels: $66 becomes $132 a month).

Runner-up: offer yearly billing again after signup. It cuts revenue per switcher by a sixth now and only pays back after about 12 months, missing the board's window.

## The new pricing page

| | Essentials | **Agency (new)** | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| For | A shop or cafe with a few channels | Freelancers with clients | Teams with several staff |
| Monthly | $6 per channel | $80 flat, up to 15 channels | $12 per channel |
| Yearly | $5 per channel a month | $800 a year | $10 per channel a month |
| Users | As today | 1, plus free client approvers | Unlimited |
| Approvals | No | Client approvals | Full approval workflows |

The page asks "How many channels?" and highlights the plan that costs you least. Costs are 9% at every plan, so Agency keeps $72.80 of each $80. Cost is not the limit; Team customers moving down is.

## Revenue math

From your October numbers, blending in the 19% who pay yearly:

- Essentials 8+: 3,900 customers at about $63.90 each, $249,200 a month.
- Team 8+: 3,300 customers at about $120.85 each, $398,800 a month.
- An 11-channel Essentials customer who moves to Agency pays $14 more a month. At 9.6% churn they stay about 10 months ($690). At 4.1% churn, about 24 months ($1,950).
- Each Team 8+ customer who moves down costs about $43 a month.

First six months after launch, existing customers only:

| Case | Essentials 8+ who switch | Their monthly churn | Team 8+ who move down | 6-month revenue change | Monthly gap at month 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 15% (585) | 6.0% | 20% (660) | -$104,000 | -$13,600 |
| Mid | 30% (1,170) | 4.1% (Essentials 4 to 7 today) | 10% (330) | +$57,000 | +$17,500 |
| High | 45% (1,755) | 2.6% (Team 8+ today) | 5% (165) | +$194,000 | +$48,800 |

The mid case adds about $41,000 in the first five months, inside the board's window if we launch on 8 February, and about $177,000 by month 12. New sign-ups are upside. The mid case breaks even if about 1 in 6 Team 8+ customers move down. That number decides everything, so we check it first.

## Risks

1. **Team customers move down.** Each costs $43 a month. The one-user limit protects us; the data check shows how much.
2. **The churn drop is a guess.** Exit surveys say why people left, not what keeps them. The test and a 90-day read will tell us.
3. **Big Essentials accounts pay less.** At 14 or 15 channels, $84 or $90 drops to $80.
4. **Client approvals may need product work.** If approvers need paid seats today, delay the test. Don't test a plan missing its main reason.
5. **Trust.** A new plan can look like an upsell. So nobody is moved, and Agency's price is held for 12 months.

## How we tell customers

No price changes unless a customer chooses, so the 60-day notice rule isn't triggered. We still explain it publicly.

1. **Public post on launch day:** "Why we added a flat plan. When people with 8 or more channels left Essentials, more than half said the cost kept rising with each channel. Agency is $80 a month for up to 15 channels, with client approvals. Nobody's price is changing. If you're happy where you are, do nothing."
2. **Email to Essentials customers with 8 or more channels:**
   - Subject: A flat price if you manage clients
   - "Hi {first name}, you have {channel count} channels on Essentials, which costs {current price} a month and goes up with each channel you add. From 8 February you can choose Agency instead: $80 a month, or $800 a year, for up to 15 channels, and your clients can approve posts themselves. Your price stays exactly the same unless you switch. If Essentials is cheaper for you, stay on it. Here's why we made it: {link to post}."
3. **Team customers** see Agency on the pricing page and in the post. We don't hide it.

## What we test first, and when we stop

**Step 1, data check by 27 November.** Count Team 8+ accounts with one active user. **Stop if it is more than 1 in 6:** the user limit won't protect us, so rethink the Agency price first.

**Step 2, new-visitor test, 1 December 2026 to 25 January 2027.**
- Logged-out visitors only, split 50/50: today's page against the page with Agency.
- Measure: revenue per new paying customer with 8 or more channels, over their first 60 days, after the 9% costs. Also watch plan mix and cancellations.
- Run 8 weeks; extend to 12 if either arm has fewer than 300 such sign-ups. Anyone shown Agency can keep it.
- **Keep it** if the Agency arm earns at least as much per sign-up.
- **Stop early** if, from week 4, the Agency arm earns 10% or more less for two weeks running. **Stop at the end** if it earns less at all.

**Step 3, launch 8 February, check 8 May.** If more than 1 in 6 Team 8+ customers have moved down, or switchers churn above 6% a month, close Agency to new sign-ups. Current Agency customers keep it at their price.

## Assumptions

- The yearly share (19%) is the same on every plan.
- Switchers average 11 channels, like Essentials 8+ today. Above 15 channels, people use Team.
- The board's two quarters are January to June 2027.
- Check local rules where prices must include tax.
