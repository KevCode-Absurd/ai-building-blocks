# Pricing recommendation: add one flat-price Agency plan

## The recommendation

Add one new plan, **Agency: $79 a month, or $66 a month billed yearly. Up to 15 channels, 2 users, and client approvals included.**

Change nothing else. Free, Essentials and Team keep today's prices. Nobody's bill goes up.

## Why this, and why now

Our biggest leak is one group: Essentials customers with 8 or more channels.

| Group | Monthly revenue | Monthly churn | Revenue lost each month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials, 8+ channels | $249k (11% of total) | 9.6% | $24k (29% of all churned revenue) |
| All other paying groups | $1,963k | 2.1% to 4.1% | $58k |

They tell us why. 52% left because "cost keeps rising with each channel I add." Another 21% needed client approvals, which only Team has, at double the price per channel. That is 73% of exits tied to two things one plan can fix: a bill that stops growing, and approvals.

Freelancers said: "I'd pay more for one flat price I can plan around." Team 8+ customers, who already get approvals, churn at only 2.6%.

Why $79: an 11-channel Essentials customer pays about $64 today. $79 is $15 more, but the price stops moving and approvals come with it. The same 11 channels on Team cost about $121. The 2-user limit keeps Team as the plan for real teams.

## The revenue math

How I got today's numbers: average price per channel is $5.81 on Essentials and $11.62 on Team, assuming the 19% yearly mix holds in every group. Agency averages $76.53 on the same mix. Total revenue today is about $2.21M a month.

**Base case assumptions (stated, not proven):**
- Half of Essentials 8+ customers switch (1,950 people).
- Their churn drops from 9.6% to 5.0%. That is still about double the Team 8+ rate.
- 15% of Team 8+ customers move down to Agency (495 people), each paying $44 less.
- No new customers counted. Any new signups are upside.

| | Day of switch | Month 6 |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials switchers pay more | +$24.6k | |
| Essentials switchers stay longer | | +$41.7k vs. no change |
| Team customers move down | -$21.9k | -$18.7k |
| **Net monthly revenue** | **+$2.7k** | **+$23.0k** |

At month 6 that is about $276k a year in added revenue. After the 9% payment and infrastructure cost, about $251k. The gain comes mostly from keeping customers, so it grows each month, inside the board's two quarters.

**Downside case:** only 30% of Essentials 8+ switch, their churn only falls to 7%, and 30% of Team 8+ move down. Net at month 6: **-$20.3k a month.**

**The number that matters most:** in the base case, the plan loses money if more than about 1 in 3 Team 8+ customers move down. That is why the test below watches it closely.

## Risks

1. **Team customers move down.** The main risk. The 2-user limit is the guard. Hiding Agency from Team customers would break our principles.
2. **Fewer Essentials customers switch than expected.** The test tells us before we commit.
3. **A cliff at 15 channels.** A 16-channel customer must move to Team. Watch how many Agency customers sit at 13 to 15 channels before adding a bigger tier.
4. **A busier pricing page.** One line keeps the 10-second test: "Managing clients? One flat price."

## How we tell customers

Nothing changes for any current customer unless they choose it. So our 12-month price hold and 60-day notice are not triggered. We still explain it publicly, with reasons.

- **Public post on our blog:** what Agency costs, who it is for, and the real reason: "Customers with many channels told us their bill kept climbing. Many left because of it. This plan fixes that."
- **Email and in-app note** to Essentials customers with 8+ channels:

> **A flat price for client work**
> You manage a lot of channels with us. Many people like you told us the bill keeps creeping up as you add clients. So we made a plan for it.
> Agency is $79 a month, or $66 a month if you pay yearly. That covers up to 15 channels, 2 users, and client approvals. Your clients can approve posts from a link. No screenshots.
> Here's exactly what you'd pay: today you pay [$X]. On Agency you'd pay $79.
> You don't have to do anything. Your current plan and price stay the same. If you switch and it's not right, you can switch back.

- **No deadlines, no "limited time,"** no auto-switching. Each email shows the customer's own current bill next to the Agency price.
- **Offer yearly at the switch,** shown clearly as a choice. Yearly payers churn far less (0.9% vs. 4.0% a month), and today we never offer it after signup.

## What we test first

**An 8-week early-access test with existing customers.** Nothing is hidden. Agency launches for everyone afterward if it passes.

- Invite a random half of Essentials 8+ customers (about 1,950). The other half is the control group.
- Invite a random 20% of Team 8+ customers (about 660). This measures the downgrade risk honestly.
- Measure: share who switch, monthly churn of switchers vs. control, and Team downgrades.

**Go to public launch (week 10) if all three hold:**
- 35% or more of invited Essentials 8+ customers switch.
- Switchers churn at 6% a month or less.
- 15% or fewer of invited Team 8+ customers move down.

**Stop if any one of these happens:**
- Fewer than 20% of invited Essentials 8+ customers switch by week 8.
- Switchers churn no better than 7% a month, measured against the control group.
- More than 20% of invited Team 8+ customers move down.

If we stop, early-access customers keep Agency at $79 for at least 12 months, as our price promise says. We explain publicly what we learned.
