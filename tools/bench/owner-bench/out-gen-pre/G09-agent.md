# Headspace social plan: 16 November to 13 December 2026

**The call:** go all in on winding down at night. More sleep posts, more narrator clips, all posted 20:00 to 22:00.

**Why, from your last 90 days:**
- Sleep posts earned 450 installs each (6,300 ÷ 14). Other themes earned 40 to 100.
- Narrator clips got 2.5 times the reach and 3 times the saves of any other format, though on only 5 posts. This month tests it.
- Evening posts got 2.2 times the saves of morning posts. That's when your audience lies awake replaying emails.
- Kindness is second on saves and shares.

## Week 1: six drafts, ready to post

### 1. Mon 16 Nov, TikTok, 20:30. Sleep, narrator clip
**On-screen text:** "Still replaying today's emails?" then "The day is done. Your mind can catch up."
**Caption:** Your day is over, even if your mind hasn't heard yet. Here's one quiet minute to let it land. The emails will be there in the morning. Want to try something small? Breathe out a little longer than you breathe in.
**Editor note:** 45 to 60 seconds from a wind-down meditation. Open on the voice. Captions over a rounded orange night-sky loop.

### 2. Tue 17 Nov, Instagram, 21:00. Kindness, carousel
**On-screen text:**
1. Quit a meditation app by day three?
2. You're in good company.
3. Stopping isn't failing. It's a pause.
4. There's nothing to catch up on.
5. One breath counts. It really does.
6. Want to try one now? Breathe in slowly. Breathe out even slower. Welcome back.

**Caption:** Starting again is part of it, for all of us. Just one breath, whenever you're ready. Take a breath. You've got this.
**Designer note:** One rounded character: sitting, standing, then waving on slide 6. Our orange on a soft background.

### 3. Wed 18 Nov, TikTok, 20:30. Small pauses, narrator clip
**On-screen text:** "Ten minutes to yourself? A fantasy." then "One minute in the parked car? Maybe." End card: "Only when you're parked."
**Caption:** Early for school pickup? A parked car can be a tiny quiet room. Engine off, eyes soft. One minute is plenty. Want to try it now? One slow breath, right where you're sitting.
**Editor note:** 60 seconds of an eyes-open breathing exercise; skip any clip that says "drift off." Never show driving: draw a parked car.

### 4. Thu 19 Nov, Instagram Reels, 21:00. Sleep, narrator clip
**On-screen text:** "Your shoulders went to work today too." then "Let them come home."
**Caption:** We carry the day in our bodies. Here's a moment to notice where, and let it soften. Try this now: let your shoulders drop, just a little.
**Editor note:** 45 seconds from a body scan that mentions shoulders. If yours names another body part, change line 1 to match.

### 5. Fri 20 Nov, TikTok, 20:30. Kindness, narrator clip
**On-screen text:** "What would you say to a friend who had your week?" then "Say it to yourself."
**Caption:** You'd never tell a friend they didn't do enough. Tonight, you get the same kindness. Here's a minute of it. Try this now: put a hand on your chest and say, "That was a lot."
**Editor note:** 45 to 60 seconds from a self-compassion meditation.

### 6. Sun 22 Nov, Instagram, 21:00. Sleep, carousel
**On-screen text:**
1. Monday already in your head?
2. Grab any scrap of paper.
3. Write the first thing you'll do tomorrow. Just one line.
4. Now it lives on the paper, not in your head.
5. It'll be there in the morning.
6. Tonight is just for tonight.

**Caption:** Sunday nights can feel like Monday mornings. One line on paper gives your mind a place to set it down. Want to try? Write that line now, before you scroll on.
**Designer note:** Night palette, with our orange as lamp light.
