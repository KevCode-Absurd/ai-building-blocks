# Headspace social plan: 16 November to 13 December 2026

## The short version

- **Lean into sleep.** Sleep posts brought 450 installs per post. Kindness brought 100, stress at work 95, focus 50 and app news 40.
- **Lead with our narrators.** Narrator audio videos reached 380,000 with a 5.9% save rate, nearly three times any other format. The editor's 4 clips a week fill 4 of our 6 slots.
- **Post in the evening.** Posts at 20:00 to 22:00 got 2.2 times the saves of morning posts.

## Six drafts (week 1)

For every post, add its tracking link to the bio and burn the on-screen text in large type.

### 1. Mon 16 Nov, 21:00, TikTok, sleep, narrator video

**On-screen text:** "Still replaying today's emails?" / "You can set them down for tonight." / "Breathe out, slow and long."

**Caption:** The inbox will still be there tomorrow. Tonight, it's just you and this breath. Want to try? Take one slow breath out before you scroll on. #sleep #winddown #mindfulness

**Editor note:** 25 to 30 seconds of a wind-down passage about letting go of the day. Show an orange laptop closing, then a moon rising. First text within 1 second.

### 2. Tue 17 Nov, 20:30, Instagram, kindness, carousel

**On-screen text (5 slides):** "Stopped meditating by day three? You're in good company." / "Missing a day isn't failing. It's just a day." / "Starting again counts. Every time." / "One breath is a whole practice." / "Want to try one now? In... and out. Done."

**Caption:** Lots of us start, stop, and start again. That's how habits grow. If you'd like, take one breath right now. It counts. Save this for the next time you begin again.

**Designer note:** Rounded orange characters, lots of white space, one line per slide. Slide 1: a friendly figure beside a stack of three phones.

### 3. Wed 18 Nov, 14:30, TikTok, kindness (parents), narrator video

**On-screen text:** "Early for school pickup?" / "Engine off. This minute is yours." / "Feel your hands. Feel your breath." / "That was your minute."

**Caption:** Ten minutes alone can feel like a dream. One minute in a parked car might fit. Next time you're early for pickup, turn the engine off and listen along.

**Editor note:** 45 to 60 seconds of a short breathing passage. Show a parked orange car near a school gate, engine clearly off. Never show driving.

### 4. Thu 19 Nov, 21:00, TikTok, sleep, narrator video

**On-screen text:** "Brain still busy at bedtime?" / "No need to stop the thoughts." / "Let them drift by, like clouds." / "Just listen and breathe."

**Caption:** A busy mind at night is so common. There's nothing to solve right now. Notice a thought, then let it float on. Want to try? Close your eyes for your next three breaths.

**Editor note:** 30 seconds of a passage about noticing thoughts. Clouds drift across an orange sunset that fades to night. No music under the voice.

### 5. Fri 20 Nov, 20:30, Instagram, stress at work, carousel

**On-screen text (5 slides):** "Week's over. Is your mind still at work?" / "Here's a tiny closing-time moment." / "Think of one thing that went okay." / "Think of one thing that can wait until Monday." / "Breathe out. The weekend can start."

**Caption:** Work can follow us home. A small closing-time moment helps it stay put. One thing that went okay. One thing that can wait. One long breath out. Want to share your "went okay" thing in the comments?

**Designer note:** An orange door slowly closes across the slides. On the last slide, a figure steps outside.

### 6. Sun 22 Nov, 21:00, Instagram Reel, sleep, narrator video

**On-screen text:** "Sunday night, thinking about Monday?" / "Monday can wait till Monday." / "Tonight is just tonight." / "Rest well."

**Caption:** Sunday nights can fill up with Monday plans. Tonight, let them wait. Listen along, take one long breath out, and let the week start when it starts.

**Editor note:** 30 to 45 seconds of a sleep passage. A calendar page folds shut under a rounded moon. Cover frame: "Monday can wait."

## How we'll know it worked

**Baseline (last 90 days):** 10,900 installs from 76 posts, about 3,390 per four weeks. Save rate 2.1%, share rate 1.0% (average per post).

**If each theme matches its past results,** this mix gives about 6,580 installs (12 × 450 + 8 × 100 + 4 × 95), a 3.4% save rate and a 1.7% share rate.

**Success on 14 December:**
- At least 5,000 installs from post links and codes, about 1.5 times baseline.
- Average save rate of 3.0% or higher.
- Narrator videos hold a 4% save rate or higher across 16 posts. The 5.9% came from only 5 posts.

**Check after week 2:** If narrator videos average under 3% saves, swap one a week for a text-on-screen video. If the Wednesday test gets less than half the evening save rate, move it to 20:30.

## Assumptions

- "6 posts a week" means 6 in total, each on one channel. That matches our past pace.
- The editor's 4 clips cover all video. Times are the audience's local time.
