# Todoist onboarding series: 5 emails

## The idea behind it

Users who type a repeating task in week one keep 52% at day 30. A second device: 48%. Both: 67%. Today only 14%, 22% and 8% of signups get there. Users who add just 1 to 4 tasks keep 11%.

So the series teaches three habits: empty your head, let Todoist hold the repeats, keep the list on phone and computer. A calm check-in catches people who drift. Pro comes last, only to people already getting value.

**Assumptions:** the email tool can skip an email when a tracked event already happened. Desktop-app signups get the web version. Screenshots are the real, current screen for the reader's platform.

## Email 1: Welcome

- **Trigger:** Signup, sent right away.
- **Subject:** Get it out of your head
- **Preview:** Start with five things. It takes two minutes.

> Welcome to Todoist.
>
> You probably have a lot on your mind. Let's move some of it here, so you don't have to hold it.
>
> **Today's tip:** add five tasks, fast. Don't sort them. Just type them as they come:
> - "Call the dentist"
> - "Buy milk"
> - "Send the report Friday"
>
> Todoist spots words like "Friday" and sets the date for you.
>
> *[Screenshot: Quick Add with "Send the report Friday" typed and the date picked up.]*

- **Button:** Add five tasks
- **Goal:** add 5 or more tasks in week one.
- **Worked if:** signups adding 5+ tasks in 7 days rise above 31%.

## Email 2: Let Todoist remember the repeats

- **Trigger:** First task added. Send the next morning, never on signup day. Skip if the user already has a recurring task.
- **Subject:** Let Todoist remember the repeats
- **Preview:** Type "every Monday" once. That's it.

> Some tasks come back again and again. Rent. Bins. The weekly report. You shouldn't have to keep them in your head.
>
> **Today's tip:** type how often, right in the task.
> - "Pay rent every 1st"
> - "Take out the bins every Tuesday"
> - "Team update every weekday at 9am"
>
> Todoist sets the repeat. When you check it off, the next one appears on its own.
>
> *[Screenshot: "Pay rent every 1st" in Quick Add, with the repeat icon showing.]*

- **Button:** Add a repeating task
- **Goal:** create a recurring task.
- **Worked if:** signups with a recurring task in 7 days rise above 14%.

## Email 3: Your list on a second device

- **Trigger:** Day 3, fixed. Skip if a second device has signed in. Two versions by signup platform.

**Web and desktop signups**
- **Subject:** Your list, in your pocket
- **Preview:** Add things the moment you think of them.

> Ideas show up at odd times, like in line at the store. If your list isn't with you, it ends up back in your head.
>
> **Today's tip:** get Todoist on your phone and sign in with this email. Your tasks are already there. Add something on one device and it shows on the other.
>
> Next time a thought hits, type "Buy birthday card Saturday" right where you are.
>
> *[Screenshot: the same list on a computer and a phone, side by side.]*

- **Button:** Get the phone app

**Mobile signups**
- **Subject:** Your list, on a bigger screen
- **Preview:** Plan your week where you can see all of it.

> Your phone is great for catching things fast. A bigger screen shows the whole week at once.
>
> **Today's tip:** open todoist.com on any computer and sign in with this email. Everything you added on your phone is already there.
>
> Try adding "Plan next week Sunday 7pm" while you're there.
>
> *[Screenshot: the Upcoming view on the web, with tasks added from a phone.]*

- **Button:** Open Todoist on the web
- **Goal:** sign in on a second device.
- **Worked if:** the second-device rate in 7 days rises above 22%. Report it by signup platform.

## Email 4: A calm check-in

- **Trigger:** 3 days with no activity. Send once, in the first 30 days.
- **Subject:** Start again with one thing
- **Preview:** Your list is right where you left it.

> Lists get busy. Weeks get busy. That's normal.
>
> **Today's tip:** if your list feels like too much, pick one task for today. Type "today" at the end, like "Reply to Sam today." Leave the rest for later.
>
> One clear next step is enough.
>
> *[Screenshot: the Today view with a single task in it.]*

- **Button:** Add one task for today
- **Goal:** come back and add or finish one task.
- **Worked if:** more recipients are active within 3 days than in a holdout group that gets no check-in.

## Email 5: Pro, for people it fits

- **Trigger:** Day 14. Send only to users who created a recurring task or signed in on a second device. Others get no Pro email in this series.
- **Subject:** When you're ready for a bit more
- **Preview:** Pro is here if you need it. No rush.

> Two weeks in, your list is starting to carry the load for you.
>
> If you want more, Todoist Pro adds things like reminders at a set time, so a task can nudge you when it matters.
>
> **Today's tip:** add a time to a task, like "Call Mom Sunday 6pm." With Pro, Todoist reminds you at 6pm.
>
> *[Screenshot: a task with a reminder set for 6pm.]*

- **Button:** See what Pro adds
- **Goal:** upgrade to Pro.
- **Worked if:** upgrades by day 30 beat the old day-7 email, with fewer unsubscribes than its 1.1%.

## How we'll know the series worked

- **Main number:** the share of signups with both a recurring task and a second device in week one. Today it is 8%.
- **Holdout:** keep 10% of new signups on the old series for one month. At about 60,000 signups a month, that is around 6,000 people, enough to compare day-30 retention.
- **Guardrail:** no email should pass 0.4% unsubscribes, the old day-1 level.

## What to drop and why

- **Day 1, Projects and labels.** Structure before habit. That is the "perfect on Sunday, gone by Wednesday" trap. Clicks were low (6.1% web, 4.8% mobile), and labels can't trigger an email, so we can't measure it.
- **Day 2, 10 keyboard shortcuts.** Phones have no shortcuts, and 41% of signups start on mobile. Mobile clicks were 0.3%, unsubscribes 0.7%. Ten tips also breaks the one-tip rule.
- **Day 4, Invite your team.** Worst unsubscribe rate (1.4%), clicks under 1%. Shared projects reach 6% of users and keep 34%, well below repeats (52%) or a second device (48%). Many parents and students have no team. Bring it back for users with both habits once shared projects can trigger emails in 2027.
- **Day 7, Upgrade to Pro.** It asked for money before the habit formed, with 1.1% unsubscribes. It moves to day 14, for engaged users only.
- **Keep, but rewrite: Day 0 welcome.** Your strongest email (64% opens). It now asks for five tasks, not one, because 1 to 4 tasks leads to 11% retention.
