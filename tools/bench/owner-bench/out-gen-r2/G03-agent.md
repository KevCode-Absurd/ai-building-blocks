**What went wrong and what we're doing about it**

**1. AI app builders grow our database count, not our revenue.** About 1.46M of October's 2.4M new databases came through AI app builders, including about 476,000 of the month's 500,000 increase. But 72% of those projects show no database activity after their first 7 days. Within 30 days, 0.4% of orgs that arrive this way convert to paid, against 3.1% of direct sign-ups. We pause idle free projects after 7 days, so we pay for a week of compute on most of them.

*What we're doing:* we plan to pause inactive AI-builder projects after 48 hours, a two-week change for engineering. We will report free-tier compute and gross margin for the first full month after it ships. We also plan to judge AI-builder partners by paid conversion, not databases created.

**2. Burn rose $2.2M.** The split: $0.9M free-tier compute, $0.8M one-off deposit for next year's developer conference, $0.5M for six new hires. Without the deposit, burn was $5.5M, about 37 months of runway.

**3. Customer A is leaving.** They are moving workloads to their own servers. The loss hits ARR when service ends on 31 January 2027. *What we're doing:* we plan to ask their engineering lead for a review before they leave, to learn what drove the move.

**4. VP Enterprise Sales is still open.** Two finalists turned us down. Both wanted a base in New York. *What we're doing:* we plan to open the role to candidates based in New York.

**What went well**

- Enterprise ARR grew 9% month on month, with three new enterprise customers signed.
- Database branching left beta.
- A new usage dashboard shipped to all paid plans.
