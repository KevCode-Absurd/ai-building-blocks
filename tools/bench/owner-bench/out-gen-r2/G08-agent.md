# Pricing recommendation: add a flat-price Agency plan

**The decision:** Add one plan for people who manage social for clients. **Agency: $79 a month, or $790 a year, for up to 15 channels, 1 user, with client approvals.** Free, Essentials and Team stay exactly as they are. No current customer's price changes.

**Why:** Essentials customers with 8+ channels are 5% of paying customers, but they lose us $23,900 of monthly revenue every month. No group loses more, not even Essentials 1 to 3 channels, with ten times the customers. Their churn is 9.6% a month, against 2.6% for Team customers of the same size. 73% of those who leave name the two things Agency fixes: a bill that rises with each channel (52%) and no client approvals (21%).

**Bad news first:** this only works if these customers pay a little more for a price that stops moving. The test proves that before anything goes public. Some Team customers may move down, and Essentials accounts above 13 channels will pay less.

**This week:** build Agency as an invite-only plan and pick the random test group.

## The plan

| Plan | For | Price | Reason to step up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | Your own business | $6 a channel a month, $5 yearly | Start here |
| Team | Teams with several people | $12 a channel a month, $10 yearly | Unlimited users |
| **Agency (new)** | **Freelancers managing clients** | **$79 a month, $790 a year, up to 15 channels** | **Flat price, client approvals** |

- Yearly is two months free, like our other plans.
- Clients approve posts from a link, without a paid seat.
- The 1-user limit keeps Team as the plan for teams.
- 15 channels leaves the average 8+ customer (11 channels) room for four more.

## Revenue math

Monthly revenue today is about $2.21 million. Costs are 9% on every plan, so each $1 kept is worth $0.91.

**For every 1,000 customers who move from Essentials (8+ channels) to Agency, over two quarters (projection):**

| | Stay on Essentials | Agency, churn unchanged at 9.6% | Agency, churn 4.1% | Agency, churn 2.6% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly revenue per customer | $63.91 | $76.50 | $76.50 | $76.50 |
| Revenue over 6 months | $302,000 | $362,000 | $414,000 | $430,000 |
| Gain | | +$60,000 | +$112,000 | +$128,000 |
| Monthly revenue in month 6 | $34,900 | $41,800 | $59,500 | $65,300 |

Agency churn is the one assumed input: 4.1% matches Essentials 4 to 7; 2.6% matches Team 8+, who already have approvals. After costs, the middle case is about $102,000.

**What can cost us:**
- **Team customers moving down.** A Team 8+ customer pays about $120.85 a month; on Agency, $76.50. Each downgrade loses about $249 over six months. In the middle case, 1,000 switchers cover about 450 Team downgrades. That sets the stop rule below.
- **Large Essentials accounts.** Above 13 channels, Agency is cheaper. A 15-channel account drops from $90 to $79. We tell them anyway.

**Why not lead with yearly billing:** yearly payers churn 0.9% against 4.0%, but partly because of who chooses yearly. A $6 channel paid monthly brings in about $58 over 12 months; paid yearly, $60. It pays back only around month 11, after the board's window. It is the next test. Agency shows both prices side by side from day one.

## The test we run first

**Who:** a random half of the 3,900 Essentials customers with 8+ channels (about 1,950) gets the invite. The other half is our control and gets it eight weeks later.

**How long:** 8 weeks, decide in week 9. That leaves the second quarter for growth to show.

**Goal metric:** revenue per customer in each group at week 8, counting cancels and switches.

**Go:** the invited group earns more per customer than control, and no stop rule below fires.

**Stop, and don't launch, if any of these happen:**
- At week 8, the invited group earns the same or less per customer than control.
- At the week 4 check, the invited group's churn is higher than control's.
- Fewer than 10% of invited customers (about 195) switch.

**After launch:** for 60 days, if more than one Team customer moves to Agency for every three Essentials customers who do, take Agency off the page and rethink the user limit. People already on Agency keep it.
