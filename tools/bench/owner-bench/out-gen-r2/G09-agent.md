# Headspace on Instagram and TikTok: 16 November to 13 December 2026

## The plan in short

- **Lead with sleep, told by our narrators.** Sleep posts brought 450 installs each in the last 90 days (6,300 from 14). Kindness, the next best, brought 100. Narrator clips reached 380,000 and saved at 5.9%, against 150,000 and 1.8% for text videos. So 15 of 24 posts are sleep, 16 are narrator clips.
- **Post at night.** Posts at 20:00 to 22:00 saved 2.2 times as often as morning posts. All posts go out at 20:30 or 21:00, except the parent post at 14:00, when parents wait in the car before school pickup.
- **Bad news: no sleep post can go live before Tuesday 24 November,** because sleep posts need clinical and legal review. Week 1 is six kindness posts.
- **First move, Monday 16 November:** submit the 10 sleep posts for weeks 2 and 3 (3 are drafted below). Submit week 4's on 23 November. That leaves time for rewrites, even across US Thanksgiving.

Assumptions: review checks the words (caption, on-screen text, clip transcript), so scripts go in before the editor cuts. Five business days start the day after you submit, so a Monday submission goes live the next Tuesday. Install data is not split by app, so posts split evenly.

## What to stop

- **App news and features:** 40 installs a post, 0.6% saves. Sleep posts name the app at the end instead.
- **Focus and productivity:** 50 installs a post, 1.1% saves, and it pulls toward hustle talk.
- **Stress at work by day:** 22 posts at 95 installs each. Its best line, replaying emails, moves to the night sleep slot.
- **Static quote cards:** 70,000 reach, 0.7% saves.
- **Morning posts, and text videos** (fine at 1.8% saves, but the editor's 4 clips go to narrator clips).

## How we'll know it worked

Main measure: installs from post links and codes, one per post. The old pace was about 3,400 per 4 weeks (10,900 in 90 days).

Projection: 7,650 installs (15 sleep posts at 450, 9 kindness at 100): about 600 in week 1, then 2,350 a week. If sleep posts earn half their old average at triple the volume, it is about 4,300.

On Monday 14 December, it worked if:
- installs reach 4,300 or more (1.25 times the old pace);
- sleep narrator clips save at 4.8% or more, sleep's old average.

One rule along the way: if the 14:00 parent posts in weeks 1 and 2 save less than week 1's evening kindness posts, move week 4's to 21:00.
