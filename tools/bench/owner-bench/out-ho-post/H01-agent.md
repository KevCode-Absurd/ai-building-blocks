# Stock and cash plan: 14 November to mid-January

**Decision.** Spend ₹1.17 crore, not ₹1.86 crore. 76% goes to the five hero matte shades, partly on rush so it lands before the sale. Foundation and kohl go on standard lead time. Nothing else gets ordered.

**Bad news.** Even after re-cartoning, the top 5 shades have 2.4 weeks of stock. They run out around 1 December. A rush order placed Monday lands 7 December. We cover that 6-day gap, about 3,300 units, with honest back-orders.

**Monday:** place the rush order and start re-cartoning the top 5 shades.

All spent the week of 16 November; ₹2.54 lakh stays free to 31 December. Split orders 1, 3 and 5 by each shade's share of recent sales, at least 2,000 per shade. A matte shade with 25% of top-5 sales gets 6,000 rush and 5,500 standard.

**The top-5 maths.** They sell 3,770 a week (58% of 26,000 over 4 weeks). Rush: 3,300 back-orders plus 16,965 for 7 to 27 December (sale week at 2.5 times) plus one week of buffer = 24,000. Standard: 41 days of sales, 28 December to 7 February = 22,000; the rush buffer covers until it lands 1 January. All ordered lines then last to about 12 February.

**Why not the team draft.** It is ₹66 lakh over budget. Nothing arrives until after the sale. Ordering matte in proportion to stock sends 82% of matte money to shades that bring 42% of sales.

## The 1 to 7 December gap

- **Site team, from 25 Nov:** when a top-5 shade drops under 300 units, switch it to back-order on our own site with the real date: "Your shade is coming back. Order now and we ship by 9 Dec. That's a real date."
- **Warehouse lead, 7 to 10 Dec:** ship back-orders first, then stock marketplaces and stores for the sale.
- No "only 2 left" or countdown timers.

## Stock that isn't moving

**Glitter palette:** 19,500 units, ₹60.45 lakh at cost. ₹54.4 lakh of it is beyond 12 weeks of cover.
- No more orders. Keep about 2,000 at full price.
- Sale week: a "Party pair" of one palette plus one liquid lipstick for ₹1,299 (normally ₹1,998, cost ₹470). Palette alone ₹899 (cost ₹310).
- From 2 Jan: if under 1,500 palettes sold in sale week, list 8,000 on marketplaces at ₹649, our floor.
- Target: 3,000 sold by 31 January.

**Liquid lipstick:** 25 weeks of cover, ₹97.6 lakh at cost. It still sells, so no solo discount. It anchors the Party pair, a lip offer that spares the hero shades, and falls to about 8 weeks of cover by 1 March.

## Bolder move: pro pre-book

Artists like Farah stock up before December and switch brands if a shade is gone. From 18 November, let pros pre-pay for 6 or 10 of a top-5 shade, shipping by 9 December. Cap: 2,000 units, inside the rush buffer, worth up to ₹15.98 lakh before tax and fees. **First step:** site team builds the page Monday. **Risk:** it eats the sale buffer past the cap.

## Assumptions

- Sales hold at the last 4 weeks' pace through January; sale week sells 2.5 times normal.
- The 52,000 matte units include the 6,400 on hold. Orders are paid when placed.
