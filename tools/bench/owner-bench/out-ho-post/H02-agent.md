# Rothy's Google plan: next 30 days

## The short answer

Google makes money only on people who already search "Rothy's". Brand terms (Search plus Performance Max, or PMax) brought $479,520 of your $962,400 conversion value for $26,700. Everything else spent $213,300 to bring back $482,880: a ROAS of 2.26. (ROAS is conversion value divided by ad spend.) You keep 40% of each sale, so break-even ROAS is 2.5. Non-brand Google lost about $20,100 last month. Brand hides it inside the 4.0.

**Next month:** cut $146,300 of loss-making spend, commit $136,000, and hold $104,000 back for campaigns earning a ROAS of 3.0 or more.

**Expected:** profit after ad spend rises from $144,960 to about $198,000 (range $174,000 to $218,000). **Bad news:** Google-reported revenue falls about 13%, to about $835,000. Most of what you lose was sold below cost.

Assumptions: conversion value is order revenue and your 40% already covers returns; the agency's category split is an estimate but good enough to act on; $240,000 is a ceiling, and unspent money stays as profit.

**Reserve rule:** each Monday from day 8, add 20% to a campaign's daily budget if its last 7 days show non-brand ROAS of 3.0 or more and it spent its full budget. That is break-even plus a 20% margin. Expect $40,000 or more to stay unspent.

We change several things at once on purpose: testing each separately costs about $2,000 a day ($59,948 ÷ 30). Judge the month on store totals; PMax's own ROAS will drop once brand is excluded.

## Fix the feed and titles

**1. Price mismatch, first.** 212 pointed flat variants, sizes 5 to 7.5, have been disapproved since 21 October. Those sizes are 15.6% of site revenue (46% × 34%), and Google cannot show them. Rough upside at that share of non-brand PMax value: $61,900 a month, or $24,700 profit before ads.

Compare one disapproved variant's page price with its feed price. Usual causes: a sale or size price the feed missed, or a page showing one price for every size. Make each size's page and structured data show its own price. Refresh the feed at least every 6 hours, then request re-review. Hold extra pointed flat budget until approved.

**2. Titles.** What the shoe is, then what it does, then colour and size, brand last. Searched words in the first 70 characters:

"Women's Washable Pointed Flats, Comfortable All-Day Work Flats, Black, Size 6, Rothy's"

Bags: bag type, "Washable", the use people search (travel, work), colour, brand. Descriptions open: "Made to walk, made to wash. Comfortable for long days on your feet. Knit from thread spun from recycled plastic bottles." Load through a supplemental feed: half the styles on day 4, the rest on day 18, and compare clicks per impression.

## Rules for the month

- PMax non-brand ROAS under 2.5 for 14 days: cut its budget 20%.
- New-customer orders down over 15% two weeks running: restore $1,000 a day of broad spend nationally.
- Brand Search losing over 10% of impressions to budget: raise it.

## Bolder move: test whether the cut spend builds brand

The 1.2 may miss buyers who return later through brand search. From day 10, run a second PMax with images and video, brand excluded, in one half of the states at $1,000 a day. Compare total site revenue in each half. Keep it next month only if the "on" half earns at least $52,500 more ($21,000 × 2.5). First step: split the states on day 6. Risk: if the 1.2 is right, the test costs about $10,900 in profit.
