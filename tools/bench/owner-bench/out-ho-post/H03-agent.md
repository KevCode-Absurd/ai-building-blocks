# Lounge Underwear: 60-day post-purchase plan

**The decision:** fix three things, in this order. Email buyers the day their parcel leaves us, not 2.1 days later at the courier scan. Put the fit finder, whose users return 14% of bras against 33%, where bra buyers choose a size. Make "Swap my size" the first choice in the returns portal when the reason is fit.

**What to expect once all three are live** (projections from your last 90 days):

| Measure | Today | Day-60 target |
|---|---|---|
| "Where is my order" tickets | 762 a week | 478 to 573 a week |
| Bra return rate | 31% | 27.3% to 28.6% |
| Returns that are exchanges | 18% | 35% to 45% |

Each month: 810 to 1,215 fewer tickets (£2,590 to £3,890 saved), 243 to 369 fewer bra returns (£1,820 to £2,770 saved), and 1,056 to 1,678 refunds turned into swaps.

**Bad news:** the plan runs through Christmas. Gift orders will push tickets up and returns into January. Judge results against a holdout, not last quarter.

**First move this week:** build the "On its way" email (message 2) on the warehouse leave time.

## The messages

Dates in curly brackets are the order date plus 3 to 5 working days. We never promise a single day.

**1. Order confirmation (updated, sent at once)**
Subject: Got it. Your order's in.
Preview: Due by {latest date}.
Body: Thanks for your order. It's due between {earliest date} and {latest date}. We'll email you the moment it leaves us.
Button: View my order
*First-time bra buyers get this instead of the button above:* New to our sizes? Your size elsewhere may not be your size here. Our fit finder tells you in a few quick questions.
Button: Find your fit

**2. On its way (new, sent when the parcel leaves the warehouse)**
Subject: It's on its way
Preview: Your tracking link wakes up soon.
Body: Your order has left us and is due by {latest date}. Tracking starts when the courier first scans it. That usually takes about 2 days, so a quiet link is normal.
Button: Track my parcel

**3. Still on track (new, working day 3, only if the courier has scanned it and it is not delivered)**
Subject: Still on track for {latest date}
Preview: Nothing you need to do.
Body: Quick update. Your parcel is with the courier. If it hasn't arrived by the end of {latest date}, reply here and we'll chase it for you.
Button: Track my parcel
*No courier scan by day 3? The warehouse team checks the order instead.*

**4. Delivered (updated, sent on the delivery scan)**
Subject: It's here. Time to try it on.
Preview: Not quite right? Swapping is easy.
Body: Your order has arrived. Try it on today, tags still on. Not the right fit? Our fit finder tells you which size to try, and you can swap in one step. No new order needed.
Button: Find your fit

Messages 2 and 3 stop on the delivery scan. All stop if the order is cancelled.

## Returns portal changes

1. Ask "Why are you sending it back?" first.
2. For "too small" or "too big", show **Swap my size** first and pre-selected, with the fit finder's suggested size. Heading: "Let's find your fit." Below it, a clear link: "I'd rather have a refund."
3. A swap is one step: pick the size, print the label. The new size ships on the return's courier scan.
4. For other reasons, show Refund and Swap side by side, neither pre-selected.
5. If the new size is out of stock, offer the refund straight away.

## How the numbers work

- **Tickets:** 62% of tickets (6,076) come on days 3 to 5. Messages 2 and 3 remove 40% to 60% of them. That is 2,430 to 3,646 fewer per 90 days, at £3.20 each.
- **Bra returns:** fit finder use rises from 11% to 30% or 40%: 5,700 to 8,700 more users. Each returns 9.5 points less, half the 19-point gap. That saves 542 to 827 returns. Clear delivery dates cut a third to half of the 558 "arrived too late" returns: 186 to 279 more. Total: 728 to 1,106 per 90 days, at £7.50 each.
- **Swaps:** today 82% of returns are refunds. A fit-first portal lifts exchanges 17 to 27 points across 18,640 returns. At the £58 average order, that keeps up to £61,000 to £97,000 of sales a month.

## Bolder move

**Ship the swap first.** Send the new size when the return is booked, about 2 days sooner. It could lift the exchange share another 5 to 10 points: 932 to 1,864 more swaps per 90 days. First step: two weeks in January for repeat buyers swapping a bra size. Risk: some first items never come back, so charge if not posted within 14 days.

**Assumptions:**
- The warehouse system can trigger an email when a parcel leaves. It already records that time.
- Only half the fit finder gap comes from the quiz itself. Keen buyers are likelier to use it.
- Each swap is valued at the £58 average order, so the sales figure is a ceiling.
