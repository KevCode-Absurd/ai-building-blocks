## 2. The top three articles, ready to publish

### Why do I owe someone I never borrowed from?

Your group has **Simplify debts** turned on. It cuts the number of payments in the group. The total you owe stays the same. Only the person you pay changes.

**An example.** Jordan owes Sam $50. Sam owes Alex $50. That would take two payments. With Simplify debts on, Jordan pays Alex $50 and Sam pays nothing. Everyone ends up even.

**See who owes what, and why**

1. Open the group.
2. Tap **Balances**. You will see each person's total.
3. Tap the settings gear.
4. Turn off **Simplify group debts**.

You will now see who owes whom, expense by expense. This changes the view for everyone in the group, so tell them first. You can turn it back on any time.

**If a total still looks wrong,** open each expense and check **Paid by** and the split. One wrong payer can move every balance.

Still stuck? Write to us from the link below.

### Undo delete: restore a deleted expense

You can bring back a deleted expense from **Activity**. It returns with the same amount and split, and balances update right away.

1. Tap **Activity** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Find the line that says the expense was deleted, such as "You deleted Rent".
3. Tap that line to open the expense.
4. Tap **Restore**.

The expense is back in the group, and balances include it again.

**Can't find it?** Activity shows the newest changes first. Scroll down to the day it was deleted.

**Changed an expense by mistake?** Open it and scroll down to see what changed, then put the old numbers back.

Still stuck? Write to us from the link below.

### How do I get a refund for Splitwise Pro?

If you paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play, ask Apple or Google for the refund. They took the payment, so only they can refund it. If you paid on splitwise.com, write to us and we will help.

**Not sure where you paid?** Your receipt email came from Apple, Google or Splitwise.

**Paid through Apple**

1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in.
2. Under **I'd like to**, choose **Request a refund**.
3. Pick your reason and tap **Next**.
4. Choose Splitwise Pro and tap **Submit**.

**Paid through Google Play**

1. Go to play.google.com and sign in.
2. Tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Budget & history**.
3. Find Splitwise Pro and choose **Report a problem**.
4. Choose your reason and tap **Submit**.

Apple or Google will email you their answer.

**Good to know:** a refund may not stop the next payment. To stop it, cancel the subscription too.
