# Podia affiliate programme for 2027: one plan

**Decision: do not cut commission to 20%.** Keep 30% recurring for the life of the customer. Fix what partners complain about: slow payouts and lost credit. Focus on creators who teach other creators to sell online.

**The bad news:** this plan costs more than today, not $210,000 less. A 90-day credit window adds about $62,000 to $89,000 of commission in year one (projection below). Faster payouts also pull $52,500 of cash forward once.

**First move this week:** the partner manager calls each top-20 partner by name, before any email goes out.

## Why 20% is the wrong cut

| Point | Number |
|---|---|
| Lifetime revenue, referred customer ($52 a month, 3.1% churn) | $1,677 |
| Lifetime revenue, customer who came alone (4.6% churn) | $1,130 |
| Referred customer after 30% commission for life | $1,174, still more |
| Saving finance expects | $210,000 a year |
| Saving if we keep the lifetime promise and cut only new referrals | About $38,000 in year one |
| Referred revenue we can lose before the cut costs money | $262,500 to $350,000 |
| Top-20 partners (average $74,550 each) who must leave to wipe out the saving | 4 or 5 |

The full $210,000 only comes from cutting commission on customers partners already brought. That breaks a written promise.

One rival pays 40% for the first year. Two pay 30% recurring. Our 17 creator-educators can switch with one video, and their uncredited buyers leave with them. The programme is not overpaying. It is under-crediting.

## Who we focus on

1. **Creator-educators:** YouTubers, newsletter writers and coaches who teach people like Renee to sell courses. They are 17 of our top 20, plus the next 20 earners. The partner manager's time goes here.
2. **Everyone else** (2,260 brought no one) gets a self-serve welcome. We stop open recruiting and invite named creators instead.

## Commission and tiers

Same rate in both tiers. The rate was not the complaint; payouts were, in 64% of survey comments.

| | Affiliate (anyone approved) | Partner (invited) |
|---|---|---|
| Commission | 30% of every payment, for the customer's life | Same |
| Credit window | 90 days from click to first payment | Same |
| Payout | Monthly, 30 days after month end | Monthly, 15 days after month end |
| Minimum | $50 | $25 |
| Method | PayPal or bank transfer | Same, and we pay the fee |

**Who is a Partner:** the top 40 by referred revenue, reviewed quarterly, plus anyone who brings 10 new paying customers in a quarter.

**Why 90 days:** the median creator-sent buyer pays on day 41, and 58% pay after day 30. Kwame's audience takes two months, so 60 days is too tight.

**Window cost (projection):** we gain at least 1,250 referred customers a year (3,365 paying referred customers x 3.1% churn x 12). If 65% to 75% of creator-sent buyers pay by day 90, we credit 690 to 980 more a year: $62,000 to $89,000 of commission in year one. These buyers pay us anyway. We pay so their creators keep sending them.

## The rules, on one page

- You earn 30% of every payment a customer makes, for as long as they stay.
- You get credit if they pay within 90 days of clicking your link.
- We pay on new customers who keep their plan. Refunds come off your next payout.
- We pay monthly, 30 days after the month ends, from $50. Partners: 15 days, from $25.
- No buying through your own link, no search ads on "Podia", no coupon or cashback sites.
- Say you are paid, before the link: "Ad: I earn a commission if you buy Podia through my link." In the US, "affiliate link" alone is not enough. In the UK, "aff" or "spon" is not enough. Mark website links rel="sponsored".
- Talk about Podia, not money: "Everything you need to sell online, in one place." Never promise earnings or results. Never say "passive income" or "guaranteed."
- We give 30 days' notice before rules change. Commission already earned stays yours.

Our lawyer finalises these terms; our accountant advises on payout tax forms.

## Bolder move: credit by "Who sent you?"

Our signup survey already asks if a creator sent the customer. From February, add Partner names to that question. If a buyer picks a Partner and no link took credit, that Partner gets the 30%. First step: test it with the top 20 for one quarter. Risk: disputes when link and answer disagree. Rule: the link wins.

**Assumptions:**
- The programme is roughly flat, so at least 1,250 new referred customers a year.
- 65% to 75% of creator-sent buyers pay by day 90. We know 42% by day 30 and half by day 41.
- Gross margin is not given. The low break-even figure uses revenue; the high one assumes 80% gross margin.
