# Stock and cash plan: wedding season and the December sale

**The plan in short.** Your top 5 matte crayon shades sell 58% of matte units but hold only 9,100 units. At 3,770 a week, they sell out around 30 November. A standard order placed Monday lands 28 December, after the sale. So on Monday: re-carton the held stock, place a rush order for the top 5 (lands 7 December) and a standard order (lands 28 December). Total ₹1.14 crore, inside your ₹1.2 crore, with ₹5.87 lakh held back.

- **Owner:** ops lead sends both orders Monday. Each day the rush order waits, stock lands closer to the sale.
- **Shade split:** if the top 5 do not sell equally, split by each shade's share of the last 4 weeks' sales, at least 2,000 each.
- **Foundation and kohl check before sending:** weeks of cover = units on hand ÷ weekly sales. A shade needs about 8 weeks today to last to 28 December through the sale. Move any ordered shade under 8 weeks to the rush order (foundation ₹299, kohl ₹80.50). Worst case adds ₹4.17 lakh, paid from the held-back money.

## 2. Why not the team draft

- ₹1.86 crore is ₹66 lakh more than you have.
- It all arrives 28 December. The top 5 shades would be gone from about 30 November through the sale.
- Ordering by current stock sends 82% of matte units to the 19 shades that sell 42%.

**How we handle the one-week gap (owner: e-commerce lead):**
- Pause paid ads on the top 5 shades until 7 December. Move that money to liquid lipstick nudes.
- From 30 November, the product page says: "Your shade is back on 7 December. Pre-order now and we ship by 9 December." It shows the closest liquid lipstick nude as a swap. No "only 2 left" and no countdown clocks.
- Email makeup artists by 25 November: pre-order the top 5 now, shipped 9 December. Pros stock up before December and switch brands if they miss out.

## 5. The January order

Cash returns mid-January. Place the next order on Friday 15 January.
- If the top 5 sold under 2,500 a week on average in the weeks of 28 December and 4 January, order on standard lead time (lands 26 February).
- Otherwise order rush (lands 5 February).
- Foundation and kohl last to about 12 February. Add them to the same order.

**Reorder point** (the stock level that triggers an order): 8 weeks of sales. That is 6 weeks of lead time plus 2 weeks of buffer.

## 6. Monday check, every week (owner: ops lead)

1. Top 5 shades: units and days left, by shade.
2. Manufacturer confirms ship dates. If the rush slips, change the restock date on the site that day.
3. Glitter palettes: units left against the 15,000 line.
4. From 15 January, any line or shade where stock plus open orders falls under 8 weeks of sales gets an order.

## Assumptions

- The last 4 weeks' rate is normal and holds to February. The sale week is 2.5 times normal for every line.
- The 6,400 held units are inside the 52,000 on hand.
- Orders are paid when placed and stock is sellable the day it lands.
- No foundation sales by shade were given, so the order goes to the 4 shades with the least cover.
- Every gap customer pre-orders. Fewer pre-orders leaves more for the sale.
