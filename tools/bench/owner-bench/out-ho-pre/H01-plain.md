So most of the cash goes to those five shades: a rush order for the sale and a standard order for January. Foundation and kohl get a small top-up. Total: ₹1.19 crore, inside our ₹1.2 crore.

We will not use the ₹1.86 crore draft. It is ₹66 lakh over budget. It splits matte orders by current stock, so most money goes to the 19 slow shades that already have 15 weeks of cover. And on standard lead time, nothing lands before the sale.

## The one gap we cannot close

Even on rush, the top 5 shades are out from about 1 to 8 December. We handle it honestly:

- **Slow the run.** Pause ads and promos on the top 5 until 9 December. Cap orders at 4 per shade on our site and marketplaces.
- **Protect bridal artists.** Open a pro pre-order now, filled first from rush stock. Artists like Farah stock up before December and switch brands for the season if our shade is gone.
- **Tell the truth.** Sold-out pages show the real date and a notify button: "Your shade is back on 9 December. Tap Notify me and we'll tell you the minute it lands." No "only 2 left." No countdown timers. Tanvi will wait a week, and this is a week.
- **Show real alternatives.** Our nearest matte nude and our liquid nudes.

## Stock that isn't moving

**Held matte stock (6,400 units).** Re-carton from Monday, top 5 units first. All of it is back on sale by 20 November. Without it, the top 5 run out around 23 November.
