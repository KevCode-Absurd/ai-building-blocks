## Feed and titles

**Price mismatch first.** 212 of 560 pointed flat variants, sizes 5 to 7.5, are disapproved. Those sizes are 46% of pointed flat sales, and the pointed flat is 34% of site revenue. So about 15.6% of site revenue cannot show in Shopping or Performance Max. Make each variant's feed price match its own page price exactly, including any sale price. Then request a review; Google re-checks over hours or days. Repeated mismatches can put the account at risk, so time Black Friday price changes to the minute in the feed.

**Titles second, once disapprovals clear.** Pattern: product type, washable, comfort use, colour, size, material, brand. Searched words go in the first 70 characters.

- Now: "The Pointed Flat | Black"
- New: "Women's Pointed Toe Flat, Machine Washable, Comfortable Work Flat, Black, Size 6, Recycled Knit, Rothy's" (104 characters)
- Bags: bag type, machine washable, colour, recycled knit, Rothy's.

Description opening: "A comfortable pointed toe flat for long days at work. It is machine washable. Knit from thread spun from recycled plastic bottles." No slogans or "eco-friendly" in the feed.

Roll out in two halves matched by impressions, half B four weeks after half A. Keep the change if contribution per product rises.
