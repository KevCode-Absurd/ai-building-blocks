# Lounge Underwear: 60-day post-purchase plan

**In short.** Most "where is my order" tickets come from a 2-day silence before the courier's first scan. Most bra returns are size problems that our portal turns into refunds. Fix the silence first, then the swap, then help buyers find their fit.

**What to expect per 90 days, once live:**

| Measure | Today | Target |
|---|---|---|
| "Where is my order" tickets | 9,800 | 6,762 |
| Bra return rate | 31% | 28.7% |
| All returns | 18,640 | 17,956 |
| Swap share of returns | 18% | 35% |
| Refunds | 15,285 | 11,671 |
| Cost saved (tickets and return postage) | | £14,852 |
| Sales kept (refunds avoided x £58) | | about £209,600 |

## 1. Why it happens

- 6,076 tickets (62%) arrive on days 3 to 5. The dispatch email waits about 2.1 days for the courier's first scan, so buyers see no movement.
- 77% of bra returns are size: too small (5,394) or too big (1,767). Fit finder users return 14%, everyone else 33%. Only 11% find the link under the reviews.
- A swap means a new order plus a return, and Refund is pre-selected. So 82% of returns become refunds.

## 2. The order to do things in

- **Days 1 to 10:** send emails 1, 3 and 4. Show the delivery date range at checkout too. Move the fit finder link next to the bra size picker: "Not sure? Find your fit in 2 minutes."
- **Days 11 to 25:** rebuild the returns portal; run it 50/50 against the old one. Switch on emails 2 and 5, holding out 10% of buyers.
- **Days 26 to 60:** read weekly. Move everyone to the winning portal on day 46. Final read on day 60.

## 3. The messages

One button each. Fields in {curly_brackets} fill from the order. Today's delivery confirmation stays as it is.

**1. Order confirmation (rewrite).** Sent at order.
- Subject: Got it, {first_name}. Here's when it lands
- Preview: Arriving {date_from} to {date_to}.
- Body: Thanks for your order! Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days, so expect it between {date_from} and {date_to}. We'll email you when it leaves us.
- Button: View my order

**2. Fit check (new).** Bra orders where the fit finder was not used. Sent 30 minutes after order. Stops once packed.
- Subject: Quick one: is {size} your size with us?
- Preview: 2 minutes now saves a swap later.
- Body: New brand, new sizing. Totally normal to wonder. Our fit finder takes 2 minutes. If it suggests a different size, change your order before we pack it. No new order needed.
- Button: Find your fit

**3. Packed (new).** Sent when the parcel leaves our warehouse.
- Subject: Packed and heading to the courier
- Preview: Tracking goes live in about 2 days.
- Body: Your order has left our warehouse. The courier usually scans it within 2 days, and then tracking goes live. Until then it may say "no updates yet". That's normal. You're still on for {date_from} to {date_to}.
- Button: See my order status

**4. With the courier (today's dispatch email, rewritten).** Sent at first courier scan.
- Subject: Tracking's live!
- Preview: Follow it all the way to your door.
- Body: The courier has your parcel. Tap below to follow it.
- Button: Track my parcel

**5. How's the fit? (new).** Sent 2 days after delivery for bras, loungewear sets and leggings. Skipped if a return has started.
- Subject: How's the fit, {first_name}?
- Preview: Wrong size? Swap it in a few taps.
- Body: We hope it feels like you. Too snug or too roomy? Swap it for another size. No new order. We send your new size as soon as the courier scans your return.
- Button: Swap my size

## 4. Returns portal changes

1. Ask the reason first.
2. Then one screen, nothing pre-selected. Swap first, refund right below it, same size text:
   - **Swap my size.** "We send your new size as soon as your return is scanned. No new order."
   - **Refund to my original payment.**
3. For "too small" or "too big", show "Not sure which size? Find your fit" above the size list.
4. Ship the swap on the courier's first scan of the return. That's the "sorted quickly" Jess wants.
5. A swap creates no new order and no new delivery charge. That answers Amira's "Swapping a size shouldn't mean a whole new order."

## 5. The numbers, worked

Per 90 days, UK orders.

- **Tickets.** Assume the Packed email and date range halve the 6,076 day 3 to 5 tickets: 3,038 fewer, leaving 6,762. Saving: 3,038 x £3.20 = £9,722.
- **Fit finder.** Assume use rises from 11% to 35% of bra buyers: 7,200 more users. Early users chose the quiz themselves, so new users close half the 19-point gap: 9.5 points. 7,200 x 9.5% = 684 fewer bra returns, leaving 8,616 (28.7%). All returns: 17,956. Saving: 684 x £7.50 = £5,130.
- **Swaps.** Assume swap share rises from 18% to 35%. Refunds fall from 15,285 (82% x 18,640) to 11,671 (65% x 17,956): 3,614 fewer. At the £58 average order, about £209,600 kept. Item prices may be lower.
- **Cost saved:** £9,722 + £5,130 = £14,852.

Not counted: fewer "arrived too late" returns (558 bra returns today), and the cost of sending each swap, which the data does not give.

**How we'll know.** Track weekly: day 3 to 5 tickets per 1,000 orders (72 today), fit finder use, swap share. Guardrail: time to refund. Returns lag orders, so day 60 reads first-month orders only. Swap share under 25% by day 46 means reworking the swap wording.
