# Lounge Underwear: 60-day post-purchase plan

## Where the cost sits

- **Returns:** 18,640 in 90 days at £7.50 each = £139,800. Bras are half of them (9,300). 77% of bra returns are about size: 5,394 too small and 1,767 too big.
- **"Where is my order" tickets:** 9,800 at £3.20 = £31,360. 62% (6,076) land on day 3 to 5. The parcel has left us, but the courier has not scanned it, so the customer hears nothing for 2.1 days.

The plan: stop the silence first (fast and cheap), then get bra buyers into their size, then make a swap easier than a refund.

## Assumptions

- Your warehouse system can trigger an email when a parcel leaves you, not only at the courier's first scan.
- Orders can be changed for about 2 hours before picking. If not, drop the size-switch line in message 2 and keep the quiz link.
- A swap ships the new size free. The £7.50 return cost is the same for a swap or a refund.
- The rates below are my estimates. Prove them with a 10% hold-out group on today's messages.

## The messages

One button each. Bracketed dates come from the 3 to 5 working day window: always a range, never one day.

**1. Order confirmed** (all orders, replaces today's email)
Subject: Got it, [Name]!
Body: We're packing your order now. It should arrive [Tue 18] to [Thu 20 Nov]. We'll email you when it leaves us.
Button: **View my order**

**2. Fit check** (first bra order, fit quiz not used, 10 minutes after ordering)
Subject: Quick check: is that your size?
Body: New brand, new sizes. Lots of people aren't sure of theirs with us yet. Our fit quiz takes a few minutes. If it suggests a different size, you can switch for free in the next 2 hours.
Button: **Find your fit**

**3. On its way** (when the parcel leaves our warehouse)
Subject: Your order has left us
Body: Your parcel is with [courier] now. Tracking can stay quiet for a day or two until their first scan. That's normal. It should arrive [Tue 18] to [Thu 20 Nov].
Button: **Track my parcel**

**4. Nearly there** (morning of day 3 if not yet delivered, email and SMS)
Subject: Your order is nearly there
Body: Still on track for [Tue 18] to [Thu 20 Nov]. Latest update: [tracking status]. Nothing for you to do.
Button: **Track my parcel**

**5. Delivered** (replaces today's email)
Subject: It's here. Time to try it on
Body (bras): Try it with the band on the loosest hook. The band should feel snug and stay level. The cups should sit smooth, with no gaps and no spilling over. Not quite right? Swap your size for free. No new order needed.
Body (everything else): Try it on and get comfy. Wrong size? Swap it for free. No new order needed.
Button: **Swap my size**

**6. How's the fit?** (bra orders, 5 days after delivery)
Subject: How's the fit, [Name]?
Body: Comfort beats the number on the tag. If it digs in, gaps or rides up, swap for a different size. It's free and takes a few taps.
Button: **Swap my size**

## Returns portal changes

1. **Ask the reason first.** "Why is it going back?" comes before the refund or swap choice.
2. **Size reasons lead to a swap.** For "too small" or "too big", show **Swap size** first and pre-selected, with the next size up or down already filled in. Refund stays on the same screen, one tap away. We don't hide it.
3. **One-step swap.** No new order. The new size ships as soon as the courier scans the return.
4. **Other reasons stay neutral.** For "changed mind" or "arrived too late", show both options with nothing pre-selected.
5. **Feed reasons back weekly.** If one style runs small, add a sizing note to its product page.

## One change before purchase

Quiz users return 14% of bras; non-users return 33%. Move the fit quiz from small text under the reviews to a **Find your fit** button next to the size picker.

All figures are per 90 days, once everything is live.
