# Babbel cancel flow: one screen, one fair option per reason

**Recommendation:** replace the 50%-off-for-everyone screen with one screen that asks the reason and shows the one option that fits it. Bring back the 1-month pause for "no time". Offer the 12-month plan to monthly payers who say "too expensive". Offer a lesson reminder to "not making progress" learners who have none. Everyone else gets a clean, thankful exit.

**Result:** of each month's 24,000 cancel starts, about 2,170 still pay at day 90, against about 840 today. That is **about 1,330 more subscribers kept each month**, worth **about £10,700 more revenue a month**.

## The flow

1. **Entry.** Account > Subscription > "Cancel my subscription", next to the plan details.
2. **Screen 1: reason and option.** The required reason question. Tapping a reason opens its option card on the same screen. "Cancel my subscription" sits at the bottom the whole time. This is the only save screen.
3. **Screen 2: confirmation.** The cancel is done. No offer here.

Cancelling takes three taps: Cancel, pick a reason, Cancel. Words in [brackets] are filled in by the app.

## Exact words

**Screen 1 heading:** "Before you go, what's the main reason?"
**Helper line:** "Pick one. It helps us make Babbel better."
**Button, always visible:** "Cancel my subscription"
**If no reason is picked:** "Please pick one reason first."

**No time:**
"Life gets busy. Take a month off instead. You won't be charged, and your progress stays saved. Your plan starts again on [restart date]. Pick up where you left off."
Button: "Pause for 1 month"

**Too expensive, monthly plan:**
"Our 12-month plan costs 45% less each month than your monthly plan. That's [12-month price] for the year. Your progress comes with you."
Button: "Switch to 12 months"

**Too expensive, 12-month plan:**
"Thanks for telling us. Your plan stays active until [end date], and you won't be charged again."

**Goal reached or trip over:**
"Well done. You put in the work, and it showed. Your progress stays saved for your next trip."
Tick box, unticked: "Send me one email in 4 months, in case I'm planning a new trip."

**Not making progress, no reminder:**
"Small steps add up. Learners with a daily reminder finish about twice as many lessons a week. Pick a time, and we'll nudge you once a day."
Button: "Set my reminder" (time picker opens in place)

**Not making progress, reminder already set:**
"Thanks for telling us. It helps us make lessons better."

**Switching to another app, or Other:**
"Thanks for learning with us. We hope you keep talking."

**Screen 2, confirmation:**
"Your subscription is cancelled.
You can keep learning until [end date]. You won't be charged again. We've emailed you a copy.
Changed your mind? Restart any time and pick up where you left off."
Button: "Restart my subscription"
Optional box: "What could we have done better?"

**After an offer is taken:** "You're on a break until [restart date]." / "You're on the 12-month plan." / "Done. We'll remind you at [time] each day."

**Pause-ending email, 7 days before:** "Your break ends on [restart date], and your plan starts again then. Need longer? You can cancel in Settings in three taps."

**How safe is this?** The pause alone keeps 1,771, which is 928 more than today's whole flow, and it rests on our own 2025 test. The other two rows are planning guesses.

## Assumptions

- The 2025 pause results repeat: 34% take it, 70% still pay at day 90.
- Today's 9% take rate and 39% stay rate hold for every reason.
- The switch and the reminder are each taken at today's 9%. Switchers pay up front, so all still pay at day 90. Reminder takers stay at today's 39%.
- A switcher is worth £4.95 a month, 45% below the £9 average. This undercounts, since monthly payers pay above average. Everyone else kept is worth £9.

## How to measure it

Track weekly by reason: shown, taken, still paying at day 30 and 90, cancels completed, and complaints about cancelling. Judge the switch and reminder rows after 90 days. If either keeps fewer than 50 people a month, replace it with a clean exit.

## To confirm before launch

- Billing can pause a plan for one month and restart it on a set date.
- The 12-month plan is paid up front, and the app can show its real price on the card.
- Progress stays on a cancelled account, so learners can pick up where they left off.
- Legal checks the flow against this market's cancellation rules. UK subscription rules are expected from January 2027.
