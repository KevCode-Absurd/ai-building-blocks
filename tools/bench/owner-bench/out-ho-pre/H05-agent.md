## 2. The top three articles, ready to publish

### Why does Splitwise say I owe someone I never borrowed from?

Your group probably has **Simplify debts** turned on. It combines debts so the group makes fewer payments. The total you owe stays the same. Only who you pay changes.

**An example**

- Jordan owes Sam $50 for groceries.
- Sam owes Alex $50 for internet.
- With Simplify debts on, Splitwise says Jordan pays Alex $50, and Sam pays nothing.

Everyone ends up even, with one payment instead of two. Here's who owes what, and why.

**To see each person's original debts**

1. Open the group.
2. Tap the **gear icon** to open the group settings.
3. Turn off **Simplify group debts**.

You'll now see what you owe each person directly. Nothing is deleted, and you can turn it back on any time. This changes the view for everyone in the group, so let your group know first.

**Still looks wrong?** Open each recent expense and check who paid and how it was split. If a number still doesn't add up, contact us with the group name and the amount that looks wrong.

Related: How balances work · Recording a payment

### Undo delete: how to restore a deleted expense

Deleted an expense by mistake? You can bring it back from **Activity**. It returns to the group, and everyone's balances update.

1. Tap **Activity** at the bottom of the app.
2. Find the line that says the expense was deleted, such as "You deleted Rent".
3. Tap that line to open the expense.
4. Tap **Restore**.

You'll see the expense back in the group, with the same amount and split as before.

**Changed it, not deleted it?** Open the expense, tap **Edit**, and set it back the way it was.

**Can't find it?** Scroll further down **Activity**. Older changes are lower down. If it still isn't there, contact us with the group name and the name of the expense.

Related: Deleting expenses · How balances work

### How do I get a refund for Splitwise Pro?

It depends on where you paid. If you paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play, only Apple or Google can refund you. If you paid on the Splitwise website, we can help.

**Step 1: Find out where you paid**

1. Search your email for your Splitwise Pro receipt.
2. Look at who sent it: Apple, Google Play or Splitwise.

**If Apple sent it**

1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Choose **Request a refund**.
3. Pick Splitwise Pro and send your request.

**If Google Play sent it**

1. Open your **Order history** in Google Play.
2. Find Splitwise Pro.
3. Choose **Request a refund**.

Apple and Google decide on these refunds. The money went through them, so we can't return it.

**If Splitwise sent it**

Contact us from the email address on your Splitwise account. Tell us you'd like a Pro refund, and we'll look at it with you.

**Don't want Pro to renew?** A refund doesn't stop renewal. Cancel your subscription in the same place you paid.
