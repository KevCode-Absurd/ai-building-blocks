## Article 1, ready to publish

# Why does it say I owe someone I never borrowed from?

Your group probably has **Simplify debts** turned on. It cuts down the number of payments, so you may pay a different friend than the one who paid for you. The total you owe stays the same.

**An example**

Jordan owes Sam $30. Sam owes Alex $30. With Simplify debts on, Jordan pays Alex $30 and Sam pays nothing. Everyone is even, with one payment instead of two.

**Check if Simplify debts is on**

1. Open the group.
2. Tap the gear icon at the top to open group settings.
3. Look at **Simplify group debts**. If the switch is on, your balances are simplified.

**See who owes whom for each expense**

1. Follow the steps above.
2. Turn **Simplify group debts** off.
3. Go back to the group. You now see each debt between each pair of people.

This changes the view for everyone in the group, so let them know first. You can turn it back on at any time.

**Still looks wrong?**

Open the group and check each expense: who paid, and how it was split. A split that's off or a payment that wasn't recorded is the usual cause. To change one, tap the expense, then tap **Edit**.

## Article 2, ready to publish

# How to undo a deleted expense

You can bring back a deleted expense. Find it in your activity and tap **Restore**.

**On the app**

1. Tap **Activity** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Find the line that says the expense was deleted.
3. Tap it to open the expense.
4. Tap **Restore**.

The expense goes back into the group. Balances update for everyone.

**On splitwise.com**

1. Open the group.
2. In **Recent activity**, click the deleted expense.
3. Click **Undelete expense**.

**Changed an expense by mistake?**

1. Open the expense.
2. Scroll down to see its history. It shows each change and who made it.
3. Tap **Edit** and put back the old amount or split.
4. Tap **Save**.

The history shows every change, so nothing is lost for good.

## Article 3, ready to publish

# Get a refund for Splitwise Pro

How you get a refund depends on how you paid. If you paid on an iPhone or Android phone, Apple or Google holds your payment, and only they can refund it.

**Not sure how you paid?** Look for your receipt email. It comes from Apple, Google or Splitwise.

**Paid on an iPhone or iPad**

1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Choose **Request a refund**.
3. Pick a reason, then pick Splitwise Pro.
4. Tap **Submit**. Apple will email you with its answer.

**Paid on an Android phone**

1. Go to play.google.com and sign in.
2. Open **Order history** from your profile.
3. Find Splitwise Pro and choose **Request a refund**.
4. Follow the steps. Google will email you with its answer.

**Paid on splitwise.com**

Use the **Contact us** form and choose **Pro billing**. We'll reply by email.

**Stop the next payment**

Deleting the app does not cancel Pro.
- iPhone: Settings, then your name, then **Subscriptions**, then Splitwise, then **Cancel Subscription**.
- Android: Play Store, then your profile, then **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**, then Splitwise, then **Cancel**.
