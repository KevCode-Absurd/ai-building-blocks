# Podia affiliate programme for 2027

**The short answer:** Keep 30% recurring for life. Do not cut to 20%. The top partner complaint is slow payouts, not the rate. And creators get no credit for 58% of the customers they send. Fix those, and spend the $40,000 on the 40 partners who matter most.

## Should we cut to 20%? No. Here are the numbers.

| | Number | How I got it |
|---|---|---|
| Months a referred customer stays | 32.3 | 1 ÷ 3.1% monthly churn |
| Months any other customer stays | 21.7 | 1 ÷ 4.6% |
| Revenue from a referred customer | $1,677 | $52 × 32.3 |
| Revenue from any other customer | $1,130 | $52 × 21.7 |
| Referred customer, after 30% commission | $1,174 | $1,677 × 70% |
| Top 20 partners, revenue each | $74,550 a year | 71% × $2,100,000 ÷ 20 |

Why the cut is wrong:

1. **The saving breaks a promise.** The $210,000 is 10% of $2.1 million. Most of that revenue comes from customers partners already sent us under a "for life" deal. Cutting only new customers saves about $38,000 next year, not $210,000.
2. **It is easy to lose.** At 20% we keep 80 cents of each referred dollar instead of 70. So the cut only pays if referred revenue drops by less than 12.5% ($262,500). Four of our top 20 leaving would wipe it out. Two rivals already pay 30% recurring. Our top partners teach creators how to pick a platform, so they will notice.
3. **Referred customers are our best customers.** They stay 49% longer. Even after commission, one brings in more revenue ($1,174) than a customer from anywhere else brings in total ($1,130).

**What would change my mind:** referred churn rising to match other customers, or fewer than 15 of the top 20 still sending customers by July.

## Who we focus on

- **First: creators who teach creators to sell online.** 17 of our top 20 do this. Kwame is the model. Their viewers are people like Renee, who wants one tool and does not know where to start.
- **Second: the other 120 active partners.** Clear rules and fast pay help the good ones grow.
- **Not a focus: the 2,260 who never sent a customer.** One friendly email, no chasing.

## Commission and tiers

Same rate for everyone. The top tier gets more time, no minimum and a person who knows them.

| | Member | Partner |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Anyone approved | 5+ paying customers in the last 12 months, or invited. Capped at 40, so one manager can know each by name |
| Commission | 30% of every payment, for the life of the customer | Same |
| Credit window | 60 days from click to first payment | 90 days |
| Payout | By the 15th of the next month | By the 15th of the next month |
| Minimum | $25 | No minimum |
| Paid by | PayPal or bank transfer | Same |
| Extras | Partner kit and approved wording | Named contact, perks below, early look at new features |

Why these changes:
- **Window.** Customers a creator sends take a median 41 days to pay. Today 58% pay after day 30, and nobody gets credit.
- **Payout.** This was 64% of survey comments. At $100, a partner with one customer waits about 7 months for a first payout. At $25, it is about 2 months.

**The cost to watch:** we will now pay on customers creators send who pay after day 30. Today they cost nothing. The review below tracks it.

**Runner-up:** 40% for the first 12 months for Partners, to match one rival's headline. I passed on it. It costs more, and the survey points at payouts, not the rate.

## The rules on one page

- Commission is 30% of each payment a referred customer makes, for as long as they stay.
- Credit goes to the last partner link clicked within 60 days (90 for Partners).
- We pay by the 15th of each month for the month before. A refunded payment comes off your next payout.
- No credit for buying through your own link.
- No coupon or cashback sites. No search ads on the word "Podia."
- Put "Ad" or "paid link" right next to every Podia link, and say it out loud in videos. "Affiliate link" alone is not enough.
- Talk about what Podia does. Never promise what anyone will earn. No "passive income," "get rich" or "guaranteed."
- We give 30 days' notice before any rule changes. Commission already earned stays yours.
