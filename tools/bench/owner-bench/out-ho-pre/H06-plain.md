# Podia affiliate programme: plan for next year

## The straight answer: do not cut to 20%

Keep 30% recurring for life. Fix what partners complain about: slow payouts and lost credit.

The numbers:

- **The saving only works if revenue holds.** At 30% we keep 70 cents of each referred dollar. At 20% we keep 80 cents. So the cut breaks even if referred revenue falls by 12.5%, from $2,100,000 to $1,837,500. That is a drop of only $262,500.
- **Four people can wipe it out.** The top 20 affiliates bring 71% of referred revenue, about $1,491,000, or $74,550 each on average. Lose 4 of them to a rival and, once their flow of new customers moves, the cut costs more than it saves.
- **They are the most likely to leave.** 17 of the 20 teach creators how to sell online. They know every rival's terms.
- **We would become the lowest payer.** Per referred customer ($52 a month, 3.1% monthly churn), first-year commission is $158 at our 30% today, $106 at 20%, and $211 at the rival's 40%.
- **Referred customers are our best customers.** They churn at 3.1% a month against 4.6% for everyone else. A referred customer brings about $1,677 over their life ($52 ÷ 0.031). After 30% commission we keep $1,174. Any other customer brings about $1,130 ($52 ÷ 0.046) before we spend anything to win them.
- **It breaks a promise.** The $210,000 figure means cutting pay on customers already earned "for life."

Finance is right to protect margin. It just isn't in the rate.

## Who we focus on

1. **Creator educators (core).** The top 20, by name, plus 30 more like them. Their audiences are full of people like Renee, and they think like Kwame.
2. **The active middle.** The other 120 affiliates who brought at least one customer (about $609,000, 29% of referred revenue). We help the best of them move up.
3. **The 2,260 who brought no one.** One automated email series. No manager time.

## Commission and tiers

| | Affiliate | Partner |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Anyone approved | Invited, or 10+ active paying customers referred |
| Commission | 30% recurring for life | 40% for each new customer's first 12 months, then 30% for life |
| Credit window | 90 days from link click | 90 days from link click |
| Payout timing | 30 days after month end | 15 days after month end |
| Support | Monthly group call, email | Named contact, monthly one-to-one |

Both tiers: $50 minimum, PayPal or bank transfer.

**Why 90 days.** For customers a creator sent us, the median time to pay is 41 days, and 58% paid after day 30 and earned no one anything. That is Kwame's complaint in one number.

**What it costs:**

- **Partner year-one rate.** With a steady flow, about 31% of a customer's lifetime revenue comes in their first 12 months (1 minus 0.969^12). Ten extra points on 31% of $1,491,000 is about **$46,000 a year**, rising only if partners bring more new customers.
- **90-day window.** This may be the biggest cost. Part of it pays for sales we already get. Every 10% rise in credited referred revenue adds about **$63,000** in commission. I still recommend it: it is fair, our best partners ask for it, and those customers beat average even after commission. We review it at day 90.
- **Faster payouts.** Not a new cost. Moving from 60 to 30 days pulls one month of commission forward once: about **$52,500** of cash ($630,000 ÷ 12).

## The one-page rules

1. Disclose every affiliate link, every time, in plain words.
2. Never promise or hint at income, earnings or results. No "passive income," no "get rich," no "guaranteed."
3. Do not bid on "Podia" in search ads.
4. No commission on your own account.
5. Credit goes to the last affiliate link clicked within 90 days.
6. Refunded payments come off your next payout.
7. We pay 30 days after month end (Partners: 15 days), from $50, by PayPal or bank transfer.
8. Questions? Your partner contact answers within two working days.

Our approved line for affiliates to use: "Podia is everything you need to sell online, in one place."

## The first 30 days

**Days 1 to 7**
- Get sign-off from finance on the cost lines above.
- Partner manager calls each of the top 20, by name, before any email goes out.
- Confirm bank transfer and new payout dates with finance.

**Days 8 to 14**
- Publish the one-page rules and new terms.
- Switch tracking to 90 days. Test it with five real partner links.
- Invite the 20 to the Partner tier and offer the content fund.

**Days 15 to 21**
- Email all affiliates: "Good news. From January you get paid sooner, and you get credit when people take up to 90 days to decide."
- Send the partner kit. Start the series for dormant sign-ups.

**Days 22 to 30**
- List 30 creator educators we don't work with yet. Contact the first 10 personally.
- Make the first payout on the new schedule and check it lands.

## How we'll know it works (review at day 90)

- All 20 top affiliates still active.
- Share of creator-sent customers credited to an affiliate rises from 42% toward 70%.
- Affiliates with at least one paying customer rise from 140 to 180.
- Payout complaints fall by half in the next survey.
- Commission stays near 30 to 32% of referred revenue.

If credited revenue rises but new referred customers don't, we revisit the window.

## Assumptions

- Revenue per customer and churn stay at today's levels. Lifetime values are internal only, never shared with affiliates as earnings examples.
- The $210,000 saving assumes the cut covers all referred revenue, including existing customers.
- The rival's 40% stops after 12 months.
- One partner manager can carry 20 named Partners plus group support.
