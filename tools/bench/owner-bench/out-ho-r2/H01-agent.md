# Stock and cash plan: 16 November to 31 January

## The decision

Place four orders on Monday 16 November for ₹1.16 crore of your ₹1.2 crore, and re-carton the held lipsticks. ₹87.75 lakh of it goes to the top 5 matte crayon shades, on rush and standard. Add foundation stick and kohl eyeliner on standard lead time. Order no liquid lipstick, no glitter palettes and none of the other 19 matte shades. Keep ₹4.07 lakh in reserve.

## Bad news first

The top 5 matte shades will sell out around 1 December, whatever we order. They have 9,100 units, counting the held ones, and sell 3,770 a week. Rush stock takes 21 days, so an order placed Monday lands 7 December. That is a gap of about 6 days: roughly 3,200 units, or ₹25.6 lakh in sales at today's pace (projection). The plan turns that gap into pre-orders with a true ship date.

The team draft would not fix this. It costs ₹1.86 crore, ₹66 lakh over cash. It lands everything on 28 December, after the sale. And it gives the top 5 shades about 18% of the matte order, though they make 58% of sales.

## Why these amounts

- **Top 5 rush (25,000):** covers 1 to 27 December. Pre-orders from the gap (3,200) + 7 to 11 December (2,700) + sale week at 2.5 times (9,400) + 19 to 27 December (4,800) = 20,100. That leaves about 4,900 units, or 9 days, of buffer, enough even if the sale runs 3.5 times a normal week.
- **Top 5 standard (20,000):** covers 28 December to about 7 February (22,100 units) with the rush buffer.
- **Foundation (8,000):** 1,800 a week. Runs out around 13 January. Need to 7 February plus 7 days' buffer: about 8,400.
- **Kohl (10,000):** 2,200 a week. Runs out around 13 January. Need to 7 February plus 7 days' buffer: about 10,200.
- **Other 19 matte shades:** 42,900 units at 2,730 a week leaves about 8,000 on 31 January. No order.

1. The 52,000 matte units on hand include the 6,400 held units.
