## Article 1

**Why does Splitwise say I owe someone I never borrowed from?**

Your group probably has **Simplify debts** turned on. It cuts the number of payments in a group, so you may pay a different friend than the one you shared a cost with. The total you owe stays the same.

**Here's who owes what, and why**

For example:
- You owe Sam $50 for groceries.
- Sam owes Alex $50 for internet.
- With Simplify debts on, Splitwise shows one payment: you pay Alex $50.

Everyone ends up even, with one payment instead of two.

**See what you owe each person directly**

1. Open the group.
2. Tap the settings icon at the top.
3. Turn off **Simplify debts**.

You will now see each debt between two people. Your total does not change. This setting is for the whole group, so tell your group before you change it.

**If a number still looks wrong**

The usual cause is an expense with the wrong person under **Paid by**. Open the expense, tap **Edit**, and fix who paid.

**Still stuck?** Contact us and tell us the group name.

## Article 2

**Undo a delete: restore a deleted expense**

*Search words: undo delete, restore expense, recover deleted expense*

Deleted an expense by mistake? You can bring it back. Deleted expenses stay in your **Activity** list, and you can restore them from there.

1. Tap **Activity** at the bottom of the app.
2. Find the entry for the deleted expense.
3. Tap it to open the expense.
4. Tap **Restore**.

You will see the expense back in its group, and everyone's balances update.

**Edited it by mistake instead?** Open the expense, tap **Edit**, and change it back.

**A common mix-up:** deleted expenses do not show in the group's expense list. Look in **Activity**.

**Still can't find it?** Contact us with the group name and roughly when the expense went missing.

*Related: Deleting expenses. How balances work.*

## Article 3

**Get a refund for Splitwise Pro**

Who can refund you depends on where you paid. If you paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play, only Apple or Google can give the refund. If you paid on the Splitwise website, we can help.

**Check where you paid**

Find your receipt email. It came from Apple, Google or Splitwise.

**Paid through Apple (iPhone or iPad)**

1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Choose **Request a refund**.
3. Pick Splitwise Pro and say why.

Apple emails you its answer.

**Paid through Google Play (Android)**

1. Open your order history at play.google.com.
2. Find Splitwise Pro.
3. Choose **Request a refund**.

Google emails you its answer.

**Paid on splitwise.com**

Contact us with the email address on your account. Our team will look at it and reply.

**Good to know:** cancelling Pro stops the next payment. It does not ask for a refund. If you want both, do both.

**Still stuck?** Contact us and tell us where you paid.
