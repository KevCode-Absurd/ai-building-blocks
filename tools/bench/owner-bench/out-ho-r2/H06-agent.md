# Podia affiliate programme: one plan for next year

## The decision

Keep commission at 30% recurring for the life of the customer. Do not cut to 20%. Fix what partners actually complain about: the credit window and payouts. Put the partner manager's time and the $40,000 perks budget on the people who teach creators to sell online.

**Why the 20% cut is wrong, in numbers**

- The cut saves $210,000 only if referred revenue holds at $2,100,000. Today we keep 70% of it, $1,470,000. At 20% we need $1,837,500 of referred revenue to keep the same. If referred revenue falls more than 12.5% ($262,500), the cut loses money.
- The top 20 affiliates bring 71% of referred revenue, about 3.55% each. Lose four of them (14.2%) and the saving is gone.
- A rival pays 40% for 12 months. At our referred churn of 3.1%, a customer pays about $528 in year one. An affiliate earns about $211 of that at the rival, $158 with us at 30%, and $106 at 20%. At 20% we pay half the rival's rate, to the people who tell creators where to go.
- Referred customers stay longer: 3.1% monthly churn against 4.6%. That is about $1,677 of lifetime revenue against $1,130. After $503 of commission, a referred customer still brings $1,174, which is $44 more than a customer who found us alone.

**The bad news, said plainly**

- The 90-day window will raise the commission bill, because we will start paying for customers creators already send us. Every $100,000 of referred revenue it credits costs $30,000 and leaves us $70,000. Budget commission as 30% of referred revenue, not a fixed sum.
- Faster payouts mean the switch month pays two months at once: about $52,500 of extra cash that month. That is timing, not new cost.
- The rival still out-pays us in year one. Over a customer's life we pay more: about $503 against $211, if their customers stay like ours.

**First move this week:** the partner manager writes to each top-20 affiliate by name with the new terms, before anyone else hears.

## Who we focus on

**Creator educators**, the people who teach creators how to sell online. They are 17 of our top 20.

- Each top-20 affiliate averages about 120 paying customers. The other 120 active affiliates average about 8. Of 2,400 sign-ups, 2,260 brought nobody.
- The partner manager knows every Partner by name: the top 20 now, 40 by year end.
- Recruit 10 new creator educators this year, funded below.
- Their audience is people like Renee, teachers moving online who don't want to stitch five tools together. Partner content should show one real course set up in one place.
- Anyone else can still join, with the monthly email and partner kit.

## Commission and tiers

Everyone gets the same terms: **30% recurring for the life of the customer, a 90-day credit window from click to first payment, monthly payouts 30 days after month end, a $25 minimum, PayPal or bank transfer.**

| Tier | Who | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate | Anyone we approve | Monthly email, partner kit |
| Partner | 25+ active paying customers, or invited by the partner manager | Named contact, early previews, the perks below |

**One rate.** A 5-point raise for the top 20 would cost about $74,550 a year, nearly twice the perks budget. Perks do more for less.

**90 days.** The median creator-sent customer takes 41 days to pay. 58% paid after day 30 and earned their creator nothing. Creators like Kwame say half their audience takes two months. 90 days covers that.

**$25 and 30 days.** Payouts drew 64% of survey comments. One customer earns an affiliate $15.60 a month, so two customers now mean a payout every month. At $100 it took seven. Paying 30 days after month end still leaves at least 30 days to catch refunds.

## The rules (one page)

1. Disclose every affiliate link upfront: "I earn a commission if you buy through my link."
2. No income claims. Never say "passive income", "get rich" or "guaranteed", and never quote earnings. Use our lines, like "Everything you need to sell online, in one place."
3. Commission is paid on paid, kept payments from new customers. Refunds come off before payout.
4. No self-referrals. No coupon or cashback sites.
5. No paid search ads on the word "Podia".
6. Credit goes to the last affiliate link clicked within 90 days before the first payment.
7. Rate changes get 60 days' notice. Commission already earned is always paid.

Our lawyer finalises the terms before they go live. Payout tax questions go to our accountant.

**What to watch:** the share of creator-sent customers credited to an affiliate (58% were missed last year) and whether all top 20 are still sending customers.

**Assumptions**

- We have no margin figure, so the maths uses revenue. Counting margin raises the break-even drop (about 16.7% at an 80% margin, or five top-20 partners). The answer stays the same.
- Most refunds come within 30 days of a payment. If ours run longer, lengthen the payout hold to match.
- If our affiliate tool cannot add bank transfer yet, make the other changes on Day 10 anyway.
