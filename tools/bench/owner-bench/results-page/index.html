Blind test · 20 judges from Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek and Gemini

Our agents against plain Opus, judged like an owner

Each job went to two writers: an AI Blocks agent with its pack, and plain Claude Opus with only the brief. Twenty judges saw both outputs in a random order with no names, scored them the way the business owner would, and picked one. The anonymous runs go further: both outputs are put into one plain format and the judges get no rubric, only "which would you rather use?"
