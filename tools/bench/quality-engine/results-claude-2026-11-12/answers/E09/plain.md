# Creative Brief: Fall Seasonal Menu, Lapsed App Win-Back

**Client:** Sweetgreen | **Owner:** [Your name], Brand Marketing | **Date:** [Date] | **Agency:** [Agency name]

## Background
Our fall menu launches [launch date] with [warm bowl 1], [warm bowl 2] and [seasonal salad]. It's our best reason this season to bring back app customers who've drifted away.

## Objective
Win back 8% of lapsed app customers (no order in 60+ days) within 30 days of launch. *(Hypothetical exercise target, not company data.)*
**Measured by:** app orders from the lapsed segment, tracked against a [holdout group / baseline rate].

## Audience
Weekday lunch customers in cities who order through the Sweetgreen app and haven't ordered in 60 or more days. They already know us and liked us enough to download the app. Something pulled them away: cooler weather, routine, price, or simply forgetting. They need a fresh, specific reason to come back, not a reminder that we exist.

## Single-minded message
**Fall is here, and so is a warm reason to come back for lunch.**

## Reasons to believe *(verify each before use)*
- New for fall: [warm bowl 1], [warm bowl 2] and [seasonal salad], available [dates].
- [Ingredient or sourcing fact for the fall menu, confirmed by culinary/sourcing team.]
- [Prep fact, e.g. how and where items are made, confirmed by ops.]
- Ordering ahead in the app gets you [confirmed app benefit, e.g. pickup timing].
- [Offer, if approved: amount, terms, end date.]

## Mandatories
- Sweetgreen logo and current brand guidelines.
- Menu item names exactly as approved by culinary: [final names].
- Approved food photography only. Show the real build.
- Pricing, offers and availability lines reviewed by legal; "at participating locations" where needed.
- Every app push and email deep-links to the fall menu in the app.
- Email: unsubscribe and legal footer. Push: within character limits per OS.
- No health or nutrition claims beyond what's approved.

## Tone
Warm, welcoming and a little playful. Like a friend saying "you'll want to try this." Never guilt-trippy ("we miss you" is fine once; "where did you go?" is not).

## Deliverables
| Channel | What we need |
|---|---|
| App push | 3 messages: launch, mid-campaign reminder, final nudge (2 variants each to test) |
| Email | 2 emails: launch and reminder; subject line + preheader variants |
| Instagram | 3 feed posts, 4 Stories frames, 1 Reel (15–30s) |
| In-store | Window poster, menu board insert, counter card, pickup-shelf tag ([sizes per store specs]) |
