# Creative Brief: Fall Seasonal Menu, Lapsed App Win-Back

**Client:** sweetgreen | **Owner:** [Your name], Brand Marketing | **Date:** [Date] | **Agency:** [Agency name]

## Background
Our fall menu launches [launch date] with [warm bowl 1], [warm bowl 2] and [seasonal salad].

## Objective
Win back 8% of lapsed app customers within 30 days of launch. *(Hypothetical exercise target, not company data.)*
- **Win-back:** one app order within 30 days of the first send, from a customer with no order in 60+ days when we pull the list.
- **Holdout:** 10% of the group gets no push or email, so we can see what the campaign added.
- **Exit rule:** anyone who orders stops getting win-back push and email.

## Audience
Weekday lunch customers in cities who order through the sweetgreen app and haven't ordered in 60 or more days. They liked us enough to download the app, then routine, price or cooler weather pulled them away. They need a fresh, specific reason to come back, not a reminder that we exist.

## Single-minded message
**Fall is here, and so is a warm reason to come back for lunch.**

## Reasons to believe *(verify each before use)*
- New for fall: [warm bowl 1], [warm bowl 2] and [seasonal salad], available [dates].
- [Ingredient or sourcing fact for the fall menu, confirmed by culinary/sourcing.]
- SG Rewards members earn 10 points per eligible $1 on app orders, and in store by scanning the app (sweetgreen.com SG Rewards FAQ, Nov 2026).
- [Offer, if approved: amount, terms, end date.]

## Mandatories
- Brand name in lowercase: sweetgreen. Current logo and guidelines.
- Menu item names exactly as approved by culinary.
- Approved food photography only. Show the real build.
- Push and email deep-link to the fall menu in the app; signage carries a QR code to it.
- Email shows a member's points balance and expiry date where they have points. Points expire 180 days after they post, so this is a real reason to order now.
- Legal reviews pricing, offers and availability; "at participating locations" where needed.
- Email: unsubscribe and legal footer. Push: within OS character limits.
- No health or nutrition claims beyond what's approved.

## Tone
Warm, welcoming and a little playful, like a friend saying "you'll want to try this." Never guilt-trippy: "we miss you" once is fine; "where did you go?" is not.
