# E09 working notes (sweetgreen fall menu creative brief)

Kit: write-emails, lead Holly, card lifecycle-email, depth team (specialist parts integrated by lead + plain). The deliverable is a one-page agency creative brief, not an email series, so the flow build table is out of scope. Applicable card must-haves: trigger/segment/exit rule, metric with a holdout (failure mode 5: no holdout), true reasons to act now, no fake urgency. Team depth: the lead integrated the parts in one context. No separate specialist subagents were used.

## Grounding (checked 2026-11-12)
- sweetgreen.com/sg-rewards-faq: "You can earn 10 points for every eligible $1 spent excluding taxes, tips, fees". Points are earned on app and online orders and in store by scanning the app QR code. "Points expire 180 days after they are deposited in your account."
- investor.sweetgreen.com release 2026-09-02 "Fall's Best Dressed" (Fall Harvest: Autumn Harvest Bowl, Maple Glazed Salmon, Roasted Bacon Brussels; "available now at sweetgreen locations nationwide"). The brand's copy writes the name lowercase ("at sweetgreen"). The real menu isn't used, because the brief asks for placeholder items.
- Context: SG Rewards replaced Sweetpass in April 2025 (Restaurant Dive, 2025-04-03). Q4 2025 same-store sales fell 11.5% (NRN, Feb 2026). Background only, not in the brief.

## Expert edge
1. Current client fact: SG Rewards earns 10 points per eligible $1 on app orders and in-store app scans. Now a verified reason to believe in place of plain's unverified "[app benefit]" placeholder.
2. True reason to act now, from the same source: points expire 180 days after they post. Most of a 60+ day lapsed member's points are still live, so a mandatory makes the email show each member's balance and expiry date. This replaces generic urgency.
3. Card must-haves plain missed: a defined win-back (one app order within 30 days of first send, 60+ days lapsed at list pull), a 10% push/email holdout (a planning figure the client can tune) and an exit rule (orderers stop getting win-back push and email). Also a lowercase "sweetgreen" brand-name mandatory and a signage QR code to the app menu.
Removed for length: the prep-fact placeholder, the Background second sentence and "or simply forgetting".
