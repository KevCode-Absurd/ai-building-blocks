# Men's Better Sweater® Fleece Jacket

It looks like a sweater and wears like a fleece. The sweater-knit face is neat enough for the office, and the soft fleece inside is what you'll notice on the walk there.

- **Fit:** Regular fit, so not slim and not boxy, with room for a layer underneath. Full-length zip, stand-up collar, two zippered handwarmer pockets and a zippered chest pocket.
- **Fabric:** 100% recycled polyester fleece, dyed with a low-impact process.
- **How it's made:** Fair Trade Certified™ sewn, which means the people who made it earned a premium for their work.
- **Care:** Machine wash warm, tumble dry low. Don't bleach or iron. Like any sweater knit, it will pill a little over time.
- **Repairs:** Covered by our Ironclad Guarantee. If a zip or seam gives out, Worn Wear can repair it so you keep wearing it.
