# SOP: Worn Wear Trade-Ins at the Register

**Patagonia Pacific Northwest stores** | For all register staff, including seasonal hires

## The rules

- We take **clean Patagonia apparel in working condition.**
- **The trade-in app sets the credit.** You never set, change or promise an amount.
- **Broken zipper or big stain?** It goes in the **repair bin**, not trade-in.
- **No swimwear. No underwear.** Ever.
- **Up to 10 items per customer, per visit.**

## Steps at the register

1. Greet the customer. Ask: "How many items are you trading in today?"
2. If they have more than 10, ask them to pick their top 10. The rest can come back on another visit.
3. Open the trade-in app.
4. Check one item at a time. Do not batch.
5. Look at the label. Is it Patagonia? If not, decline it.
6. Is it clothing? We take apparel only. Is it swimwear or underwear? If so, decline it.
7. Check that it is clean. No dirt, no smell, no pet hair.
8. Check that it works. Test every zipper, snap and button.
9. Check for stains in good light. Look at cuffs, collar and front.
10. Broken zipper or big stain? Tell the customer it goes to the repair bin (see script below). If they want to keep it, hand it back.
11. Item passes? Scan or enter it in the app. Read the credit to the customer exactly as the app shows it.
12. Repeat for each item. Then finish the trade-in in the app so the credit is issued.
13. Give the customer their receipt. Tell them the credit works in store and online.
14. Tag and bag the items right away (see below). Do not leave them on the counter.

## What to say when you decline an item

Keep it short and kind. Blame the rule, not the item or the person.

- **Not Patagonia:** "We can only take Patagonia gear for Worn Wear. Thanks for bringing it in."
- **Swimwear or underwear:** "We can't take swimwear or underwear for trade-in. That's a rule for every store."
- **Not clean:** "This one needs a wash first. Bring it back clean and we'll take another look."
- **Broken zipper or big stain:** "This one can't be traded in, but it can go to our repair bin. Want me to put it there?"
- **Over 10 items:** "We take up to 10 items per visit. Pick your top 10 and bring the rest next time."
- **Customer asks for more credit:** "The app sets the amount. I can't change it."

If a customer is upset, stay calm. Call a lead. Do not argue and do not change the credit.

## How to tag and bag accepted items

1. Print the trade-in tag from the app for each item.
2. Attach the tag to the care label. Do not pin or punch through fabric.
3. Fold the item. Put all items from one customer in one trade-in bag.
4. Put the app receipt copy in the bag.
5. Seal the bag. Write the date and your initials on it.
6. Put the bag in the trade-in bin behind the register.
7. Repair items go in the repair bin. Never mix the two bins.

## End-of-day count

1. Pull the day's trade-in report from the app.
2. Count every tagged item in the trade-in bin.
3. Your count must match the app's item count.
4. Count the repair bin items. Write that number down too.
5. Fill in the count sheet: date, trade-in count, app count, repair count, your name.
6. Counts don't match? Do not fix it yourself. Tell the closing manager before you leave.
7. Move the sealed bags to the back room trade-in shelf.

**Questions?** Ask your lead. When in doubt, don't accept the item.
