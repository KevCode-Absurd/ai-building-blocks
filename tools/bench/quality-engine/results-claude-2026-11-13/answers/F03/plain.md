**Identified: Slow loading and board errors for some Jira Cloud sites in the EU**

Since 14:05 UTC, some Jira Cloud customers in the EU region have seen slow page loads and errors when opening boards. About 8% of EU sites are affected. Confluence and Jira Service Management are not affected.

We have found the cause. A problem occurred while one of our databases was switching over to its backup. Our engineers are now rolling back a recent configuration change to restore normal service.

We have found no data loss.

We will post the next update within 30 minutes.
