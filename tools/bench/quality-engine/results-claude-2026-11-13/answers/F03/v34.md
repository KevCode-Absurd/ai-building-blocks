**Title:** Slow page loads and board errors for some Jira Cloud sites in the EU
**Status:** Identified
**Affected component:** Viewing content (Partial outage)

Since 14:05 UTC, some Jira Cloud customers in the EU region are seeing slow page loads and errors when opening boards. About 8% of EU sites are affected. Confluence and Jira Service Management are not affected.

We have identified the cause. A problem occurred while one of our databases was switching over to its backup. Our engineers are now rolling back a configuration change to restore normal service.

We have found no data loss.

We will post the next update within 30 minutes.
