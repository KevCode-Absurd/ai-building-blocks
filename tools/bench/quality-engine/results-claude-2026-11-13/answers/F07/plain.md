# Wise Business pricing: frequently asked questions

**Is there a monthly fee?**
No. There's no monthly or annual subscription for a Wise Business account. You pay a one-time setup fee, then you only pay when you use a feature that has a fee, such as sending money.

**What does the one-time £50 setup fee cover?**
The £50 fee covers getting your business verified and your account set up, including the account details you use to get paid. You pay it once, when you open the account. There's nothing to renew.

**What is the mid-market rate?**
It's the midpoint between the rates banks use to buy and sell a currency, and it's the rate you'll usually see on Google or Reuters. We convert your money at this rate and show our fee separately. That way, you can see exactly what you're paying instead of finding it hidden in a marked-up rate.

**Why does the fee change depending on the currency?**
Moving money costs different amounts on different routes. Some currencies have cheap, fast local payment systems, and others need more steps or partner banks to reach the recipient. Our fee reflects what it costs us to move money on that route, so it varies by currency.

**How is the fee for a transfer worked out?**
Most transfers have a small fixed fee plus a percentage of the amount you convert. Both depend on the currencies and how you pay. You'll see the full fee and the amount your recipient gets before you confirm anything.

**How do volume discounts work?**
Once your business sends more than £20,000 in a month, a lower percentage fee applies automatically to the amount above that level. You don't need to apply or negotiate for it. The discount shows up in the fee you see before you confirm each transfer.

**Does it cost anything to receive money?**
Receiving money in many currencies using your local account details is free. Some ways of getting paid, such as USD wire transfers or SWIFT payments, have a fixed fee. You can check the fee for each currency and payment type on our pricing page.

**Are there any hidden fees?**
No. We show the fee and the exchange rate before you confirm a payment, and you can check every fee on our pricing page. Bear in mind that a sender's bank, or an intermediary bank on a SWIFT payment, may charge its own fees, which we don't control.

**How do your fees compare with my bank's?**
The simplest way to find out is to compare a real transfer. Look at the total your bank charges, including any markup on its exchange rate, and compare it with the fee and mid-market rate we show you for the same amount. Our fees are always shown up front, so you can make a like-for-like comparison.

**Can your fees change?**
Yes, our fees can change, for example when the cost of moving money on a route changes. The fee shown when you set up a transfer is the one you pay for that transfer. Our pricing page always shows our current fees.
