# Wise Business pricing: frequently asked questions

**Is there a monthly fee?**
No. There's no monthly or annual subscription for a Wise Business account. You pay a one-time setup fee, then you only pay when you use a feature that has a fee, such as sending money.

**What does the one-time £50 setup fee cover?**
The £50 fee gives you every Wise Business feature, including account details to get paid in 22 currencies. You pay it once, when you open the account, and there's nothing to renew. See the [full price list](https://wise.com/gb/pricing/business).

**What is the mid-market rate?**
It's the midpoint between the rates banks use to buy and sell a currency, and it's the one you can check on Google. We convert your money at this rate and show our fee separately, so you can see exactly what you're paying. Nothing is hidden in a marked-up rate.

**Why does the fee change depending on the currency?**
It costs more to move money on some routes than on others. For example, moving money between pounds, euros and US dollars costs less than sending to many other currencies. Our fee follows what each route costs us, so the percentage varies by currency.

**How is the fee for a transfer worked out?**
Our fee is a percentage of the amount you send, with a minimum fee on small transfers. The percentage depends on the currencies, and the cost can depend on how you pay. You'll see what your transfer costs when you set it up, before you confirm. Check any route on our [sending fees page](https://wise.com/gb/pricing/business/send-money).

**How do volume discounts work?**
Once your business sends or converts more than £20,000 in a calendar month, a lower fee applies automatically to the amount above £20,000. Only transfers between two different currencies count, so a GBP to GBP payment doesn't add to your total. Your total resets on the first day of each month. See [how volume discounts work](https://wise.com/help/articles/2977942/how-much-does-it-cost-to-send-large-transfers).

**Does it cost anything to receive money?**
Receiving local payments is free in AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, HUF, NZD, PHP, SGD and USD. Swift payments and USD wires have a small fixed fee per payment, for example 6.11 USD for a USD wire. Fees for each currency are on our [pricing page](https://wise.com/gb/pricing/business).

**Are there any hidden fees?**
No. We show the fee and the exchange rate before you confirm a payment. The bank or card provider you pay with may add its own charges, which we don't control.

**How do your fees compare with my bank's?**
Compare a real transfer. Look at the total your bank charges, including any markup on its exchange rate. Then compare it with the fee and mid-market rate we show you for the same amount.

**Can your fees change?**
Yes, our fees can change. Our [pricing page](https://wise.com/gb/pricing/business) always shows our current fees. You'll also see what each transfer costs when you set it up, before you confirm.
