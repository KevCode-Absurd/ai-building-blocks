# Wise Platform: Q3 Board Update

## Headline summary

Platform had a strong revenue quarter. Revenue was **£41m**, up **9%** on Q2 and **38%** year on year. Partner volume reached **£6.2bn**.

Two things did not go to plan, and we want to be clear about both.

- **Take rate fell from 0.71% to 0.66%.** Our largest partners are growing fastest, and they pay the lowest rates. Revenue grew more slowly than volume because of this. We expect this mix effect to continue.
- **One partner launch slipped from August to November.** The cause was the partner's own compliance review. But we committed to August in our last update, and we missed it.

We signed **4 new bank partners**. **2 are live.** The other 2, plus the delayed launch, are the main delivery job for the next two quarters.

**What the take-rate drop cost us.** Had the take rate held at 0.71%, Q3 volume would have produced about **£44.0m** of revenue. So the mix shift cost about **£3m** in the quarter. Put another way, volume grew roughly 17% on Q2, but revenue grew only 9%.

## What went well

**Revenue growth held up.** 38% growth year on year is strong for a business at this size.

**Volume growth was strong.** Partner volume grew roughly 17% on Q2, to £6.2bn. Larger partners are moving more of their cross-border flows to Wise. That is the core of the Platform strategy.

**Signings were healthy.** Four new bank partners in one quarter adds to our base and to future volume.

**Two new partners went live.** Half of this quarter's signings are already live.

## What didn't go well

**The launch slip.** One partner launch planned for August moved to November. The delay came from the partner's own compliance review.

That explanation is true, but it is not enough. We gave the board an August date we did not control. We also did not have early warning of the review. We should have seen it coming, and we should not have presented the date as firm. Two changes follow from this:

- From now on, we will show launch dates as "partner-confirmed" or "Wise estimate", and only treat partner-confirmed dates as commitments.
- We will start compliance work with each partner's compliance team at signing, not after the build is done.

**The take-rate decline.** The drop from 0.71% to 0.66% is a mix effect. Our biggest partners get volume-based pricing, and they are growing faster than the rest.

Still, this is a real trend, not a one-quarter blip. If large partners keep growing faster, the blended take rate will keep falling. Revenue will grow more slowly than volume. We have not yet set a floor on how low we are willing to go. We think we should (see Decision 1).

**Signed is not live.** Of the 4 partners signed this quarter, only 2 are live. Together with the delayed launch, we have 3 partners whose revenue is in our plans but not yet in our numbers.

## Key risks

**1. Continued take-rate pressure.** Large partners have bargaining power, and renewals give them a chance to use it. If blended take rate keeps falling at this quarter's pace, strong volume growth may no longer turn into strong revenue growth.

**2. Partner concentration.** The take-rate drop tells us our largest partners are a growing share of volume. A pause, a delay or a lost renewal at one of them would hit revenue hard. This pack does not yet show top-partner share, and it should (see Ask 3).

**3. Launch timing we don't control.** Go-live depends on each partner's own compliance, risk and IT schedules. The August slip will not be the last. Forecasts that assume on-time launches will be too high.

**4. Delivery capacity.** Taking 3 pending partners live while still signing new ones puts strain on the same integration and partner-success teams. If we stretch too thin, more launches could slip.

## Decisions and asks for the board

**Decision 1: Agree a pricing guardrail.**
We propose to keep volume-based pricing for our largest partners. It is why they are moving more flows to us. But we also propose a floor on the blended Platform take rate. Our proposal is **0.60% for the next four quarters**. Any new deal or renewal that would push the forecast below that comes back to the board before signing.
*We are asking the board to approve the principle and the 0.60% floor.*

**Decision 2: Put go-lives ahead of new signings for the next two quarters.**
We propose that the partner team focuses on getting the 2 signed partners and the delayed launch live before chasing more new signings. That means fewer new signings in Q4 and Q1 than the current plan assumes. In return, more signed revenue reaches our numbers sooner.
*We are asking the board to agree this trade-off and the lower signings target that comes with it.*

**Ask 3: Add new measures to the board pack from next quarter.**
To judge the take-rate and concentration risks properly, we propose to add:
- the share of volume and revenue from our top 5 partners
- blended take rate against the floor in Decision 1
- time from signing to live, for each partner
- launch dates marked as partner-confirmed or Wise estimate

*We are asking the board to confirm these are the right measures, or tell us what to add.*
