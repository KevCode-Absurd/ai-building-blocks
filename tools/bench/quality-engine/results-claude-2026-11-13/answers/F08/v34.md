# Wise Platform: Q3 Board Update

## Headline summary

Revenue was **£41m**, up **9%** on Q2 and **38%** year on year, on partner volume of **£6.2bn**. Revenue grew about half as fast as volume, because our take rate fell from **0.71%** to **0.66%**.

Two things did not go to plan, and we want to be clear about both.

- **Take rate fell from 0.71% to 0.66%.** Larger partners grew their share of volume, and they pay lower rates. We expect this mix effect to continue.
- **One partner launch slipped from August to November.** The cause was the partner's own compliance review. Its revenue now starts three months later than we planned.

We signed **4 new bank partners**. **2 are live.** The other 2, plus the delayed launch, are the main delivery job for the next two quarters.

**What the take-rate drop cost us.** Had the take rate held at 0.71%, Q3 volume would have produced about **£44.0m** of revenue. So the mix shift cost about **£3m** in the quarter. Put another way, volume grew roughly 17% on Q2, but revenue grew only 9%.

## What went well

**Revenue grew.** Revenue was up 9% on Q2 and 38% on Q3 last year.

**Volume grew faster.** Partner volume grew roughly 17% on Q2, to £6.2bn. Our larger partners led that growth.

**Signings were healthy.** Four new bank partners in one quarter adds to our base and to future volume.

**Two new partners went live.** Half of this quarter's signings are already live.

## What didn't go well

**The launch slip.**
- What happened: one partner launch planned for August moved to November.
- Cause: the partner's own compliance review.
- Our part: we planned around a date that depended on another firm's review.
- Fix: from now on, we will show launch dates as "partner-confirmed" or "Wise estimate", and only treat partner-confirmed dates as commitments. We will also start working with each partner's compliance team at signing.

**The take-rate decline.**
- What happened: blended take rate fell from 0.71% to 0.66%.
- Cause: a mix effect. Larger partners pay lower rates, and they are growing faster than the rest.
- Why it matters: this is a trend, not a one-quarter blip. If large partners keep growing faster, revenue will keep growing more slowly than volume.
- Fix: a pricing guardrail (Decision 1).

**Signed is not live.**
- What happened: of the 4 partners signed this quarter, only 2 are live. With the delayed launch, 3 partners are signed but not yet earning.
- Cause: go-live depends on each partner's compliance, risk and IT schedules, and on our integration capacity.
- Fix: put go-lives ahead of new signings for two quarters (Decision 2).

## Key risks

**1. Continued take-rate pressure.** Renewals with large partners could push rates lower still. If blended take rate keeps falling at this quarter's pace, strong volume growth may no longer turn into strong revenue growth.

**2. Partner concentration.** The take-rate drop tells us our largest partners are a growing share of volume. A pause, a delay or a lost renewal at one of them would hit revenue hard. This pack does not yet show top-partner share, and it should (see Ask 3).

**3. Launch timing we don't control.** Go-live depends on each partner's own compliance, risk and IT schedules. The August slip will not be the last. Forecasts that assume on-time launches will be too high.

**4. Delivery capacity.** Taking 3 pending partners live while still signing new ones puts strain on the same integration and partner teams. If we stretch too thin, more launches could slip.

## Decisions and asks for the board

**Decision 1: Approve a pricing guardrail, at this meeting.**
Our largest partners keep their lower rates. But no new deal or renewal priced below the lowest rate any live partner pays today should be signed without board approval.
*We ask the board to approve this rule today. We will bring a proposed floor for the blended take rate, with partner-level economics, to the next board meeting.*

**Decision 2: Put go-lives ahead of new signings for Q4 and Q1, at this meeting.**
The partner team would focus on getting the 2 signed partners and the delayed launch live before chasing more signings. That means fewer new signings in Q4 and Q1. In return, more signed revenue reaches our numbers sooner.
*We ask the board to agree this trade-off today. We will bring the revised signings target to the next board meeting.*

**Ask 3: Agree new measures for the board pack, starting with the Q4 update.**
To judge the take-rate and concentration risks properly, we propose to add:
- the share of volume and revenue from our top 5 partners
- blended take rate, with the floor once agreed
- time from signing to live, for each partner
- launch dates marked as partner-confirmed or Wise estimate

*We ask the board to confirm this list, or add to it, before the Q4 pack is drafted.*
