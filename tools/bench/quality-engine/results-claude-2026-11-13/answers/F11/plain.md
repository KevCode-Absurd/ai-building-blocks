# We're changing how we travel for work from 1 January

Let's start with the honest part. Some of these changes take away things many of you value. A bigger seat on a long morning flight. The freedom to fly when the train is slower. We know that, and we're not going to pretend otherwise.

Here is what's changing, and why.

## What's changing

From **1 January**:

1. **Flights under 5 hours are economy for everyone.** That includes VPs and the leadership team. No one is exempt because of their title.
2. **Rail is required for trips under 4 hours** wherever a train route exists. If the train gets you there in under 4 hours, you take the train.
3. **Every team gets a yearly travel carbon budget.** You'll be able to see it, and how much your team has used, on a new dashboard.
4. **Exceptions need a director's sign-off.** If a trip really can't follow these rules, your director can approve it.

Flights of 5 hours or more follow our current travel policy.

## Why we're doing this

There are two reasons, and we want to name both.

**Our climate targets.** The LEGO Group has committed to cut our absolute carbon emissions by 37% by 2032, compared with 2019, and to reach net zero by 2050. Business travel is part of that footprint. Since 2024, part of the bonus for salaried colleagues has been linked to our emissions, and business travel is one of the areas counted. So this is a promise we've already tied to how we're rewarded. It's hard to keep it while travel stays the same.

**Cost.** Travel is expensive, and business class on short flights costs a lot more for a few hours of comfort. Money we save here can go to the work that matters to children and fans.

We could have framed this only as a climate step. That wouldn't be fully honest. It's both.

## What this means for you

- **Booking a short flight?** From 1 January, book economy if the flight is under 5 hours.
- **Trip under 4 hours by train?** Book the train. Use the time to work, read or rest.
- **Planning your team's travel?** Check the carbon budget dashboard before you plan big trips, events or offsites. Treat the budget like any other team budget.
- **Have a trip that doesn't fit?** Talk to your director early. Don't wait until the day before.
- **Already travel a lot for your role?** Talk with your manager about which trips could be video calls, or combined into one.

Nothing changes before 1 January.

## FAQ

**Does this really apply to VPs?**
Yes. Flights under 5 hours are economy for every colleague, at every level. A rule that only applies to some of us wouldn't feel fair, and it wouldn't work.

**What if the train just doesn't work for my trip?**
Sometimes it won't. Maybe the connections don't line up, or you have a health or family need. That's what exceptions are for. Explain the situation to your director and ask for sign-off before you book.

**Where do I see my team's carbon budget?**
On the travel carbon dashboard: [link]. It shows your team's yearly budget and how much has been used so far. Every team can see its own.

**Is this really about the climate, or about saving money?**
Both. Travel is a part of our footprint we can act on now, and cutting it also saves money. We'd rather say so plainly than pretend it isn't.
