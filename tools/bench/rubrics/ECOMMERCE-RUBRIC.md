# The e-commerce owner rubric

Use the nine owner dimensions in OWNER-RUBRIC.md, plus the store-owner checks below for the type of job. These checks are how a store owner reads the work: they feed the scores. They are not separate tick boxes.

## Every e-commerce job

- **Money first.** Decisions rest on gross profit per order (after product cost, shipping, payment fees, COD refusals and returns), not on ROAS or revenue alone, and the working is shown.
- **The right customer.** It names the segment the data says is winning (an age band, gender, region, device, source or product) and builds for it. It doesn't spread effort evenly.
- **Our brand.** Copy uses our voice and our customers' words, avoids our banned words and claims, and keeps to our reading level.
- **India and US reality.** COD, RTO, UPI, WhatsApp, GST and regional language where the store is in India. FTC, platform and consent rules where it applies.
- **Can the team do it.** It fits the budget, the stock, the cash and the people in the brief.

## Media buying (Meta, Google, TikTok)

- Thorough: it checks tracking (platform against store orders, the gap and its cause) before trusting any number.
- It reads every cut the data allows: audiences, ages, placements, formats (static, video, carousel), messages and concepts.
- It finds what worked before and was paused, and says whether to relaunch it.
- Each ad set gets a decision (scale, hold, fix or cut) with the money reason, a budget split, and day-to-day rules with numbers.
- It respects learning-phase and budget-per-cell reality, and uses two reads that agree before a verdict.

## Creative concepts, ad copy and scripts

- **Variety:** genuinely different angles, formats and hooks, not one idea reworded. At least one fresh idea.
- **Persona:** each concept says who it is for and speaks to them in their words; the hook comes from real customer language where the brief gives it.
- **Usable:** scripts flow naturally when read aloud, fit the time, and could be shot or run with a few tweaks. Copy fits the platform's limits.
- **On brand:** voice, words, claims and reading level, with no banned claims and correct disclosure (paid partnership, AI-made people).
- **For image creatives (when the job includes images):**
  - brand colours and fonts are right, and the logo and product are faithful;
  - text is legible at phone size;
  - there are no visual errors (extra fingers, warped text, impossible objects);
  - the formats vary;
  - it would stop the target customer and fits the store's current design.

## Product pages, landing pages and emails

- It leads with the outcome the customer wants, in their words, and answers the real objections from reviews and returns.
- It matches the brand's voice and design principles, and the copy reads naturally.
- Each email or section has one job, and the series has a clear exit rule.
- It is ready to paste: no placeholders, and claims are within what is proven.

## Retention, offers and pricing

- It targets the segments the data shows, with the offer sized to margin, and protects full-price buyers.
- Lift is measured against a holdout. Before-and-after comparisons are not accepted as lift.
- It says what it is worth in gross profit, with the working.

## Strategy and growth plans

- It separates what the numbers prove from what needs checking.
- Moves are ranked in one unit tied to the owner's goal, effort is named, and it says what to stop.
- The first week is concrete, and a weekly scorecard has targets.

## Conversion testing

- The problems are tied to evidence in the funnel data, with the biggest leak first.
- Sample size and duration are realistic for the traffic, and there is a guardrail metric.
- It says what to ship without testing.

## Chat and WhatsApp sales

- Templates are ready to submit, in the right category with opt-in, written in the brand's voice.
- It targets the cost driver in the data (RTO band, top question) and sizes prepaid nudges to the real saving.
